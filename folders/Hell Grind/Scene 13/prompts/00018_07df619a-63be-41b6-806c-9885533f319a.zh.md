## 原文 (source)
8K IMAX. Photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera. No 3D render, no game engine. Genre: Modern action blockbuster. References: cinematography of Emmanuel Lubezki and Roger Deakins. No CGI-heavy feel, no game-cutscene aesthetic. Lighting: Only natural light and practicals — camera always on the shadow side of subjects, backlit contre-jour with atmospheric haze throughout. Key light comes only from the sky, windows, or on-set fixtures. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent. Camera: Physical cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. No frame-interp smoothing. Realism: Photorealistic surfaces with real-world wear, organic asymmetry, and real material behavior — skin shows pores, vellus, asymmetric moles, capillary flush with pore-shadow matching on-set light; corner vignette and edge chromatic aberration from physical lens. Gravity, inertia, and follow-through respected — mass has real weight, every object casts correct contact shadows. No render-look, no floating or duplicated props. Continuity: All characters, props, environment, and lighting stay identical across every frame and every cut. No identity drift, no appearance changes. Acting: Hollywood-grade — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with practical catch-lights and micro-saccades, visible breath and chest rise, pre-speech micro-swallow, inter-beat micro-expressions. No playing to camera. Composition: Thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one, no static figures. Technical: 60fps smooth motion, 8K detail. No jitter, no identity drift. Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music, no subtitles.

Ten black-and-white patrol cars <<<image_2>>> already standing on  the wide boulevard — emergency lights strobing red and blue across wet asphalt. More cars keep arriving: from the left, from the right, from a side street — converging from multiple directions, tires screeching into position. Officers <<<image_3>>> pour out of vehicles and sprint toward the shattered museum entrance in formation — boots striking wet stone, the mass of them moving at once.

Camera: Wide establishing shot, slightly elevated — museum facade filling upper frame, multiple arriving police vehicles converging in foreground and midground from several vectors. Slow locked push forward 3%.

Environment: Evening urban exterior, overcast sky. Wet stone plaza. Overlapping strobing emergency lights from 10+ vehicles reflecting on wet asphalt. Perimeter tape already in place.

New arrivals still in motion while already-stationed officers hold position. Puddle reflections from overlapping light sources. Scale reads as overwhelming force. Consistent vehicle colors and uniform details. Smooth locked frame.

## 中文对照 (zh)
8K IMAX。写实摄影——以实体电影摄影机（physical motion-picture camera）拍摄的真实影像。非 3D 渲染，非游戏引擎。类型：现代动作大片。参考：Emmanuel Lubezki 与 Roger Deakins 的电影摄影。无厚重 CGI 质感、无游戏过场动画美学。灯光：仅自然光与实际光源（practical light）——摄影机始终位于被摄主体阴影侧，全程逆光背光（contre-jour）配大气雾感。主光仅来自天空、窗户或现场布设的灯具。色彩：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀色。摄影机：实体电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。无帧插值平滑。写实：写实表面带真实磨损、有机不对称与真实材质行为——皮肤可见毛孔、汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称痣、毛细血管潮红，毛孔阴影贴合现场光；实拍镜头带来的边角暗角与边缘色差。遵循重力、惯性与随动——质量有真实分量，每个物体投下准确的接触阴影。无渲染感、无漂浮或重复道具。连贯性：所有角色、道具、环境与灯光在每一帧、每一个镜头（cut）间完全一致。无身份漂移、无外观变化。表演：好莱坞级——反应前的微停顿、精准视线、带眼神光（catch-light）的湿润活眼与微扫视（micro-saccade）、可见的呼吸与胸口起伏、说话前的微吞咽、节拍间的微表情。不对镜头表演。构图：三分法 + 黄金比例。每个人从第一帧起即在运动，无静态人影。技术：60fps 平滑运动，8K 细节。无抖动、无身份漂移。音频：仅环境音效（SFX only）。无音乐、无字幕。
