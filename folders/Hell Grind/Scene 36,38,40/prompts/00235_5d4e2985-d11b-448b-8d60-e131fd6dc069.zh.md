## 原文 (source)

Edit this image. Convert this photographic shot into a SIMPLE LIT 
3D CLAY RENDER PREVIEW in the style of an untextured Unreal Engine 
viewport or a 3D animation pre-visualization clay render. The 
composition stays exactly the same, all geometry stays exactly the 
same, only the appearance is converted from photoreal textured to 
untextured grey clay shaded geometry.

THE LOOK:
   • Convert all surfaces to a uniform matte grey clay material — 
     same flat neutral grey shading on everything in the scene 
     (walls, floor, ceiling beams, columns, characters, training 
     equipment, hanging banners, weapons rack, all objects)
   • The grey is a medium neutral grey, like default 3D viewport 
     clay material, slightly darker on shadowed surfaces and 
     slightly lighter on lit surfaces purely from the lighting, but 
     the underlying material color is the same uniform grey 
     everywhere
   • NO textures, NO patterns on any surface, NO wood grain, NO 
     stone texture, NO fabric pattern, NO color of any kind, NO 
     reds NO blues NO yellows, NO ornament details, NO writing or 
     scripts on banners or scrolls, all surface detail removed
   • Geometry edges remain visible and crisp showing the form of 
     each object, simple geometric shapes readable
   • The characters become smooth grey clay figures — same poses, 
     same positions, same proportions, same robes silhouettes, but 
     all rendered as featureless clay, no faces detail, no skin 
     tone, just grey clay form
   • Surfaces have soft simple shading from the lighting, no specular 
     highlights, no reflections, no glossy surfaces, matte clay 
     throughout

THE LIGHTING:
   • Preserve the exact lighting setup of the original — same light 
     sources, same direction, same intensity, same shadows
   • The bright light coming through the windows on the right stays 
     in the same position and direction
   • The dim ambient light and shadow areas stay in the same places
   • Soft shadows cast by the clay figures and objects fall in the 
     same positions
   • The lighting is purely illuminating the grey clay surfaces, 
     creating value contrast through shading only, NOT color

THE COMPOSITION:
   • Same camera angle, same framing, same aspect ratio
   • Same positions of all elements — line of monks on the left, 
     single monk facing them, weapons rack on the right, ceiling 
     beams overhead, hanging bags, prayer banners across the room, 
     window light pouring in, dust/atmospheric haze
   • Same depth of field
   • Same atmospheric haze present in the original is also visible 
     in the clay version as soft grey volumetric haze

OVERALL FEEL:
A clean professional pre-visualization clay render or untextured 
3D viewport snapshot, the kind of greyscale layout pass used in 
animation pre-production or virtual production previs, all geometry 
and lighting readable but stripped of all surface detail and color. 
Like a Pixar layout pass, like an Unreal Engine clay material 
preview, like a professional 3D animation previs lighting test.

NOT photorealistic, NOT colorful, NOT textured, NOT painted, NOT 
illustration, NOT cartoon, NOT toy. A professional 3D clay render 
preview with proper lighting and clean geometry.
