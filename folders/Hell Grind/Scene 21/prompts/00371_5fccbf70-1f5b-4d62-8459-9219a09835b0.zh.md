Smooth steady dolly motion at slow walking pace in BOTH shots. Style: documentary architectural cinematography, reportage realism, National Geographic / Architectural Digest visual language. Both shots must read as real cinema camera dollies captured on location. Total duration: 6–8 seconds, with a single hard cut at the midpoint between the two shots.
