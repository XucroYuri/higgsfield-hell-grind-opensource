## 原文 (source)

— Cathedral-scale stone sanctum chamber, deep stone columns receding into shadow on both sides, oil lamp glow along floor edges, faint overhead light shaft through a ceiling oculus, mid-distance back wall visible. Cool grey-blue ambient light in the upper volume, warm pools of lamp-light along the floor. SCENE REF — space only.

SPATIAL LAYOUT
Each shot is a still locked-off composition somewhere in the sanctum aftermath. No characters in frame.

SCENE CONTEXT
The chamber after the kill. Stone falls, dust settles, cracks spread. A monastery breathing out the violence. Useful as cutaways between the bigger story beats.

STRICT
All three are static locked-off shots. NO characters in any frame. Composition off-center on each shot — avoid centering subjects. Cool sanctum ambient with warm low lamp pools.

The wide shot frames the polished floor of the sanctum. Small/medium stone chunks fall from above-frame, bounce once or twice with hard ringing impacts, skid across the stone, settle.

The medium shot frames the lower portion of a far sanctum column. A thin pale fracture line APPEARS at the base near the floor and slowly TRAVELS upward along the column surface like a living crack, branching once or twice, climbing several feet before stopping. Faint stone dust falls from the new crack.

The macro close-up frames the polished stone floor at near-pavement level. Three or four small stone shards roll into frame from one side, scratching faint white grooves into the polished surface, decelerating, settling against tiny dust mounds.

WIDE 35mm — STATIC, low-angle on the polished sanctum floor of in the aftermath. From above-frame, small and medium stone chunks RAIN DOWN onto the polished stone, bouncing once or twice with hard ringing impacts, skidding across the polished surface, settling. Dust drifts down with them. Composition slightly off-center — a single oil lamp at lower-RIGHT corner of frame casting a warm pool, the rest of the floor cold blue-grey. Hold for several seconds. Background: column bases of the sanctum in shadow.

Audio: stone clatter, ringing impacts, slow dust drift.

MEDIUM 50mm — STATIC, slightly off-center, framed on the lower portion of a far sanctum column of. Column placed on the RIGHT third of frame, the floor cuts the lower edge. Stone surface texture visible in detail. Out of nowhere a thin pale fracture line APPEARS at the base of the column near the floor — and slowly TRAVELS upward along the surface of the column like a living crack, branching once or twice, climbing several feet before stopping. Faint stone dust falls from the new crack along its path. Hold the held crack frame for a beat. Cool stone color, warm lamp glow at the floor edge.

Audio: low stone-strain creak, faint dust hiss.

CU 85mm — STATIC, low-angle macro on the polished sanctum stone floor of. Surface fills the frame at near-pavement level. Three or four small stone shards ROLL slowly into frame from the RIGHT, scratching faint white grooves into the polished black stone surface, decelerating, settling against tiny dust mounds. Dust drifts down across the frame in slow particle layers, settling around the shards. Composition off-center — shards aligned along the lower-RIGHT diagonal, leaving the upper-LEFT mostly empty. A tiny oil lamp glow flickers on the surface at the frame edge.

Audio: small stone scrape, slow dust hiss, distant chant from above muffled.

## 中文对照 (zh)

—— 大教堂尺度的石质圣殿大厅，两侧厚实的石柱渐隐入阴影，油灯光沿地板边缘流淌，头顶一道微弱光柱透过天花板的天窗射入，中景后墙可见。上部空间弥漫冷灰蓝的环境光，地板上是温暖的油灯光晕。场景参考——仅空间。

空间站位
每一镜都是圣殿余波中某处的静态锁位构图。画面中无角色。

场景情境
杀戮之后的大厅。碎石坠落，尘土落定，裂隙蔓延。一座呼出杀伐之气的修道院。可充当大段故事节拍之间的转场插入镜头。

硬规则
三个镜头均为静态锁位。任何画面都绝无角色。每镜构图偏离中心——避免把主体放在正中。清冷的圣殿环境光配低矮的暖油灯光晕。

宽景拍摄光洁的圣殿地板。画面上方落下中小块碎石，以清脆的撞击声弹跳一两次，在地板上滑行，落定。

中景拍摄远处一根圣殿石柱的下段。柱基近地板处出现一道纤细的浅色裂隙线，像一条活生生的裂纹沿柱面缓缓向上蔓延，分叉一两次，攀爬数英尺后止住。新裂痕处落下细微石尘。

微距特写以近乎路面的高度拍摄光洁的圣殿地板。三、四块小石片从一侧滚入画面，在光滑表面刮出细微的白痕沟槽，逐渐减速，抵着极小的尘丘停下。

宽景 35mm——静态，以低角度拍余波圣殿的光洁地板。画面上方，中小块碎石如雨般砸落在光洁石面上，以清脆的撞击声弹跳一两次，在光滑表面滑行，落定。石尘随它们一同飘落。构图中度偏离中心——画面右下角一盏油灯投下一方暖光晕，其余地板为冷调的蓝灰。保持数秒。背景：圣殿的柱基隐没在阴影中。

音频：碎石撞击声、清脆的撞击响、缓慢的扬尘。

中景 50mm——静态，中度偏离中心，取景于远处一根圣殿石柱的下段。石柱置于画面右三分之一处，地板切过下缘。石面纹理清晰可见。冷不丁地，柱基近地板处出现一道纤细的浅色裂隙线——沿柱面缓缓向上蔓延，像一条活生生的裂纹，分叉一两次，攀爬数英尺后止住。新裂痕沿途落下细微石尘。将停住的裂隙画面保持一拍。冷调石色，地板边缘是暖油灯光。

音频：低沉的石料绷紧吱响、细微的石尘嘶声。

特写 85mm——静态，低角度微距，拍光洁的圣殿石地板。近乎路面的高度让表面填满整幅画面。三、四块小石片自右侧缓缓滚入画面，在光洁的黑色石面上刮出细微的白痕沟槽，逐渐减速，抵着极小的尘丘停下。石尘以缓慢的粒子层飘落过画面，在石片四周落定。构图偏离中心——石片沿右下对角线排列，让左上区域大片留空。画面边缘地表有一小点油灯光忽明忽暗地闪烁。

音频：小石片的刮擦声、缓慢的石尘嘶声、从上方传来的远处吟唱被压抑模糊。

风格：8K IMAX。照片级写实——绝无3D渲染，绝无游戏引擎，绝无游戏过场运镜审美。
