The body is solid carved grey stone — crystals exist ONLY at the mouth-rupture, not anywhere inside the body. Through-column medium as the body topples, breaks apart mid-fall (no crystals visible inside), slams the floor and crumbles to nothing — dust and smoke fill the frame entirely.
