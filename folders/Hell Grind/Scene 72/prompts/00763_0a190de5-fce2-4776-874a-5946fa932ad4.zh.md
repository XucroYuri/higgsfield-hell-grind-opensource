## 原文 (source)

Environment design sheet of the small corner building at the center of the street fork. White background. Multiple orthographic views arranged cleanly: front elevation, left side elevation, right side elevation, and a 3/4 perspective view. Each view clearly labeled.
The building is 2–3 stories, boxy and worn. Ground floor: open shopfront with roll-up shutter, glass display case, canvas awning, wooden crates outside. Upper floors: patched concrete walls, mismatched window frames, wall-mounted AC units, exposed pipes, a water tank and scraggly vegetation on the rooftop. External metal staircase on the side. Blue tarps and improvised repairs visible on the upper levels.
All signage is blank, removed, or replaced with faded paint remnants and sun-bleached rectangles where signs used to be. No readable text, no writing of any kind on the building.
Materials: raw concrete with staining and cracks, rusted metal shutters and railings, chipped ceramic tile at street level, weathered wood, corroded drain pipes. Every surface has weight, grit, and age. No plastic textures, no smoothness, no clean surfaces — everything is tactile, rough, and lived-in.
Surrounding context shown minimally — adjacent buildings cropped at the edges to establish the street corner context, towering high-rises faintly indicated behind to show scale.
Style: 8K concept art environment sheet. Photorealistic material rendering. Natural diffused lighting, no dramatic shadows. Muted warm color palette — concrete greys, faded creams, oxidized reds, weathered blues. No 3D render smoothness, no game engine aesthetic.
