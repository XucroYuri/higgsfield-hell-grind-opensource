Shot on anamorphic cine optics — natural anamorphic depth-of-field falloff and oval bokeh, slight horizontal compression of background highlights, BUT NO ANAMORPHIC LENS FLARES, no horizontal streak flares, no blue-line artifacts. Lighting: only natural light and practicals, contre-jour with atmospheric haze. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant aged-hardwood blacks/reds, secondary cool slate-blue moonlight, accent warm amber butter-lamps. 180° shutter motion blur, photorealistic skin (pores, sweat-shine, capillary flush, micro-saccades), corner vignette and edge chromatic aberration. Gravity and contact shadows respected. Hollywood-grade acting — micro-pauses, no playing to camera. 60fps, 2.39:1, no jitter, no identity drift. Environmental SFX only — no music, no subtitles, no on-screen text.

LOCATION (held across all shots): a vast ancient sacred prayer hall, built entirely of intricately carved dark-stained hardwood — densely ornamented columns, a second-storey carved gallery balcony around all four walls, coffered hardwood ceiling, a single rectangular skylight casting a cool blue-white moonlight shaft down through heavy haze with visible god-rays. Polished dark grey flagstone floor. AT THE FAR END: a colossal seated dark-stone Buddha, ~6m tall, on a stepped pedestal, in front of it a long altar cluttered with bronze ceremonial vessels, candelabras and rows of butter-lamps flooding the front third of the hall in warm amber glow. The middle of the hall is empty negative space.

CHARACTERS (sweat-shined, dust-powdered, breathing hard):

The mustache and the matte-red nose stripes catch progressively warmer light with each step. Hold on his profile walking — cold purpose, no ceremony. End on a held beat as he settles into a stop. SHOT 5 — ROCO TURNS BACK TO JAX, THREATENING, CLOSE-UP. Tight chest-up close-up on Roco, anamorphic ~75mm-equivalent, shallow oval-bokeh DoF, lens at chest height (~1.4m), 1.2m from him, faint handheld breath. Roco starts the shot in 3/4 angle (where shot 2 left him, mid-retreat with his head turned toward Jax). SHOT 6 — JAX RELENTS, HANDS UP, CLOSE-UP. Same tight chest-up close-up framing on Jax as shots 3 and 4 — anamorphic ~75mm-equivalent, shallow oval-bokeh DoF, slight low-angle, 1.2m from him, faint handheld breath. Jax in clean profile facing screen-left toward Roco off-screen.

Jax's eyes hold on Roco off-screen for a beat, the wind going out of him. He breaks first — his shoulders drop with a defeated exhale, his head bobs once, eyes briefly rolling. He concedes, voice tired and quick, the two words clipped together in defeat: "Okay, okay!" The first one resigned, the second slightly higher and almost pleading — yes-I'll-do-it-just-don't-look-at-me-like-that. His mouth pulls down at the corners. The amber light flickers on his lower lip, glasses catch a tiny crescent of warm light. Hold a beat — hands still up, mouth in a tight line, body language reading reluctance from head to toe. Audio: defeated double "okay, okay" close on mic with natural hall reverb, a long resigned exhale through the nose, distant lamp flutter, deep hall tone; final beat only his breath and ambience.
