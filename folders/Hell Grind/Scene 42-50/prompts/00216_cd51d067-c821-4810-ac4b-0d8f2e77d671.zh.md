Anamorphic ~85mm-equivalent, very shallow oval-bokeh DoF — his eye razor-sharp, the corridor behind him dissolved into soft cool-and-warm bokeh with hard god-rays in the haze. A sharp explosive inhale through the nose, his nostrils flaring, a vein at his temple pulsing visibly, capillary flush blooming hot red across his cheekbones and the line of his scar. His brow drops hard, jaw clenching, lips peeling back from his teeth. His mouth opens wide and he ROARS the command in Tibetan — a hard, aggressive, full-volume battle-shout, voice cracking with fury as it tears out of his throat. Spit visible at the corner of his mouth flecking outward, neck tendons bulging hard, the muscle along the side of his jaw cording. The capillary flush deepens to red. SHOT 2 — THE DOOR TAKES THE HITS, MEDIUM-CLOSE WITNESS-STYLE. Heavy aggressive HANDHELD throughout — witness-style, the camera planted close to the door from the outer corridor side at chest height (~1.4m), framing a medium-close shot of the carved sealed door filling most of the frame. The team and the beam are off-screen behind camera, charging from the corridor — we see only the door taking the punishment. Anamorphic ~35mm-equivalent for wide immersive read, moderate oval-bokeh DoF. Cool god-rays cut across the upper portion of frame from a window to one side, dust thick in the air, the warm amber floor-lamp throwing faint flicker on one edge of the doorframe.

Frame composition: the carved dark-hardwood double-door fills the frame — deeply carved concentric panels in aged hardwood, two heavy iron strap-hinges visible along one side, the seam where the two leaves meet running vertically down the center. The cool window light grazes the relief of the carving, picking out every cut in the wood. Dust hangs thick in the air around the door from earlier action. The door is sealed, intact at the start of the shot.

We hear the team's run-up off-screen behind camera — the thunder of eight pairs of sandals sprinting flagstone, the beam's wood groan as it accelerates, the synchronized inhale of eight men launching. Then the iron-banded round flat end of the massive beam BURSTS into frame from one side at full speed, lancing across the lower-middle portion of frame, and SLAMS into the dead center of the carved door at full force — a thunderous WOODEN BOOM, the entire frame jolts violently with the concussion, the door shudders hard in its frame, dust EXPLODES outward from the impact point in a thick cloud blasting back into camera, splinters fly from the carved relief, the iron strap-hinges flex audibly under the deep metal stress. The door HOLDS. The beam recoils back out of frame as the team off-screen rears back. The handheld shake settles slightly between hits.

We hear the team's run-up again off-screen — sandals thunder, leader's furious off-screen bark, eight men sprinting at full power. The beam's leading end BURSTS into frame again and SLAMS into the door — second impact, even harder, BOOM — dust roars outward in a wall, the carved panels visibly stress, the central seam of the door begins to crack with a sharp wood-snap, fine splinters spray. Beam recoils.

Third run-up off-screen, leader's bark, sprinting thunder. The beam's end LANCES in and SLAMS — BOOM — third impact, the deepest yet, the carved face of the door cracking audibly along the central seam, a hairline split running from top to bottom, splinters spraying into the cool god-rays, the iron hinges groaning loud, the door visibly bowing inward.

Fourth run-up off-screen — louder, harder. The beam's end SLAMS in at maximum velocity — BOOM — fourth impact, monstrous, the door cracks hard down the center seam, a clear vertical split running from top to bottom, the iron strap-hinges visibly bending and tearing from the wood, large splinters and chunks of carved hardwood blasting outward toward camera, the heavy beam itself reverberating with the impact — but the door, against all the punishment, still HOLDS by a thread.

End on a held beat after the fourth hit — the beam still wedged into the cracked center of the door, the door split and bowed and stressed and bleeding splinters and dust into the cool god-rays, the iron hinges tortured but still holding. Audio: synchronized off-screen sprint thunder before each charge, beam's wood groan under acceleration, iron bands chime, then the massive WOODEN BOOM of each impact with deep sub-bass and long corridor reverb tail close on mic, dust roar, splintering crackle, door hinges flexing with progressively louder metallic stress; the cycle repeats four times, each impact louder and with more wood-stress crackle than the last, leader's furious off-screen bark between each charge; final beat held with off-screen panting from eight men, faint tick of stressed wood, deep corridor sub-bass dying, dust hiss, the cracked door creaking on its tortured hinges.
