8K IMAX, photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera (no 3D render, no game engine). Modern action blockbuster — cinematography in the lineage of Lubezki and Deakins. Documentary verité style — shot on long telephoto cine lens with heavy lens compression, operator-imperfect framing, micro-recompositions, focus hunts that miss and snap back. Heavy aggressive HANDHELD — violent vertical bounce, lateral whip-corrections, witness-style instability transmitting the scene's urgency. Anamorphic cine optics — natural anamorphic depth-of-field falloff and oval bokeh, slight horizontal compression of background highlights, BUT NO ANAMORPHIC LENS FLARES, no horizontal streak flares, no blue-line artifacts. Lighting: only natural light and practicals, contre-jour with atmospheric haze. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant aged-hardwood blacks/reds, secondary cool slate-blue moonlight, accent warm amber butter-lamps. 180° shutter motion blur, photorealistic skin (pores, sweat-shine, capillary flush, micro-saccades), corner vignette and edge chromatic aberration. Gravity, mass and contact shadows respected. Hollywood-grade acting — micro-pauses, no playing to camera, deadpan working-tradesman comedic register. 60fps, 2.39:1, no jitter, no identity drift. Environmental SFX only — no music, no subtitles, no on-screen text.

LOCATION: a vast ancient sacred prayer hall built entirely of intricately carved dark-stained hardwood — densely ornamented red-and-black columns on a grid, second-storey carved gallery balcony, coffered hardwood ceiling, single rectangular skylight casting a cool blue-white moonlight shaft down through heavy haze with visible god-rays. Polished dark grey flagstone floor. DEEP IN THE FRAME (where Jax runs toward): the colossal seated dark-stone Buddha, ~6m tall, on a stepped pedestal, with the long altar table cluttered with cast-bronze ceremonial vessels and rows of butter-lamps with live amber flames flooding warm glow far in the deeper plane — the placed grey-tan plastic explosive lump on the lower carved robe just above the pedestal lip in the deep distance. BEHIND CAMERA: the cool moonlit hall stretching back toward the off-screen entry door under sporadic battering ram impacts.

CHARACTER:
— JAX: lean Black man, early 30s, very short bleached-yellow buzz-cut, clear wraparound safety glasses, sweat at temples; black panel-paneled hooded bomber with charcoal-grey inserts and yellow zipper-pulls (hood down), black tee, olive cargo trousers, black combat boots, fingerless gloves; sweat-darkened patch over right ribs as if injured. Sweat-shined, dust-powdered, breathing hard. He moves like a tradesman on a deadline — efficient, fussy, unbothered.

SHOT — BACK-OF-JAX MEDIUM CLOSE-UP, HE STOPS, LOOKS BACK OVER SHOULDER, TURNS, SPRINTS TOWARD CAMERA, LOCKED-OFF FRAME. Documentary verité long telephoto cine lens (anamorphic ~135mm-equivalent), heavy lens compression that flattens his back against the deeper plane of the hall, very shallow oval-bokeh DoF — Jax razor-sharp, the deeper hall dissolved into thick layered cool-and-warm bokeh in compressed depth. Tight medium close-up framing on Jax from BEHIND, capturing his back from waist-up — the back of his bleached-yellow buzz-cut, his neck, his shoulders in the black bomber, the back panel of the jacket with charcoal-grey inserts and yellow zipper-pulls visible. Camera at chest height (~1.4m), 3m behind him on the long telephoto. Camera LOCKED OFF in a single fixed position — does NOT track, does NOT follow, does NOT pan with him. The framing stays planted throughout. Heavy aggressive HANDHELD throughout — violent vertical bounce of the operator's body weight, lateral whip-corrections, focus hunts that miss and snap back, witness-style instability cranked maximum, the long lens amplifying every micro-shake. Cool moonlight catches the back of his head and shoulders from above-back, warm amber altar bokeh balls glowing soft far ahead of him in the deeper plane (the off-screen Buddha he's running toward in the far depth of frame).

The shot OPENS on Jax already at hurrying pace mid-stride away from camera in clean back-view, running deeper into the frame AWAY from camera toward the warm altar bokeh in the far distance — body forward, jacket flapping at his shoulders, boots slamming flagstone, the back of his head bobbing with each stride, the carved hardwood columns of the hall passing on either side of him in compressed bokeh.

Then — at the next stride — he STOPS. Body locks hard, foot planting on the flagstone, the handheld shake JOLTING violently with the sudden brake, his shoulders hitching with the residual momentum, his head settling. He stands in back-view facing deeper into frame, breath sharp, body still angled forward in the residual sprint posture.

His head TURNS back over his right shoulder — looking BACK toward camera (toward the entry door behind camera) — his profile coming into view from behind as his face rotates into 3/4-back angle visible past his right shoulder, the warm altar glow now glancing the side of his face from the far depth of frame ahead, his clear safety glasses reflecting a faint amber crescent. His eyes lock back over his shoulder past camera toward the off-screen entry door behind, evaluating the situation behind him for a held beat — head tilted, brow visible furrowing in the readable portion of his profile, the deadpan tradesman pause of a guy assessing his window of time. The handheld shake violent over his held looking-back face, the long lens compression amplifying every jolt. A small wordless tongue-click visible at the corner of his profile mouth.

Then DECISION — fast, decisive. A small sour exhale, his expression sharpening. He PIVOTS hard — body rotating fully toward camera, shoulders snapping around, head leading the rotation back FORWARD through profile through 3/4-frontal as he turns to face camera fully. His face comes around — sweat-shined, jaw set, glasses catching a faint amber crescent, eyes locking forward past camera at the off-screen entry door behind, the brisk fussy commitment of a guy who has decided to make the second trip back.

Then he MOVES. He breaks INTO A HARD SPRINT toward camera — running directly forward at the lens, the locked-off framing letting him close the distance toward the lens at full speed. His body forward, arms pumping, boots SLAMMING flagstone in heavy cathedral-reverb thuds, jacket flapping with the speed, his face filling more of the frame stride by stride as he closes — the warm amber altar bokeh in the deep background fading behind him as he leaves it, the cool moonlight from above-back catching the crown of his hair, dust particles drifting past him through the cool god-rays. The CAMERA DOES NOT MOVE. The locked frame holds while he closes the distance, his form growing larger in the compressed long-lens framing as he sprints toward camera. His face becomes readable as he approaches — fussy purposeful focus, jaw tight, eyes locked past camera at the off-screen objective. The handheld shake jolts violently throughout, the operator's body weight transmitting heavy bounce into the long lens, focus hunting once across his face and snapping back as he closes.

Final beat: Jax sprints close to the lens, his form filling much of the locked frame, the warm bleed gone behind him, the cool moonlight haze around him heavy. He is about to pass camera. End on his hard sprint at close range, the locked frame held, the violent handheld shake continuing. An off-screen WOODEN BOOM lands distantly from camera-back-direction — he doesn't flinch, eyes locked forward, sprint unbroken. Audio: his hurrying footfalls slamming flagstone away from camera in cathedral reverb at the start, the sudden stopping footfall and the rustle of his bomber jacket as his body locks, his sharp quiet inhale through his nose as he turns his head, his small tongue-click of decision, his held controlled breath, his sour resigned exhale as he commits, the rustle of his jacket as his body pivots toward camera, his renewed hard sprinting footfalls slamming flagstone TOWARD camera growing louder and closer with each stride, his fast quick breath approaching the mic, the rustle of his jacket flapping hard, the off-screen WOODEN BOOM landing distantly with deep sub-bass and corridor-distance reverb, cast-bronze altar vessels chiming faintly with the tremor in the deeper plane, butter-lamp flutter receding behind him as he closes toward camera, distant combined Roco-and-Rein voices and grunts of effort bleeding faintly off-screen across the hall with louder urgency, the operator's heavy breath close on mic, deep cathedral room tone.

场景：一座广阔古老的圣祷大殿，完全以精雕深染硬木建造——密饰红黑立柱网格排列，二层雕饰画廊阳台，方格硬木天花板，一扇矩形天窗将一道冷白蓝月光柱穿过浓重雾霭垂落、可见神光。打磨光亮的深灰石板地。画面深处（Jax 跑向之处）：巨大坐姿深色石佛高约 6 米、立于台阶基座上、其下长供桌堆满铸铜礼器与一排排燃着琥珀色火焰的酥油灯、远远地在深处平面灌出温暖光辉——基座唇缘上方佛像下段雕饰佛袍上贴着那块灰褐色塑胶炸药团块、在深远之处。摄影机后方：被冷月照亮的殿厅向画外入口门延展、间歇挨着撞门木桩的冲击。

角色：

镜头——JAX 背后中距特写，他停住、回头过肩看、转身、朝镜头冲刺、锁死构图。纪实性长焦电影镜头（约等效 135mm 变形宽银幕），强镜头压缩把他的后背压平贴进大殿较深平面、极浅椭圆虚化景深——Jax 锐利清晰、较深大殿融进压缩纵深中层层叠叠的冷暖虚化。以 JAX 背后的紧贴中距特写框定、捕捉他自腰以上的后背——漂黄寸头的后脑、后颈、黑夹克里的双肩、夹克后片带炭灰嵌片与可见的黄色拉链头。镜头在胸口高度（约 1.4 米）、长焦端在他身后 3 米。镜头锁死在一个固定位置——不跟拍、不追随、不随他摇移。全片构图始终钉住不动。全程沉重激进手持——运镜者体重的猛烈垂直颠簸、横向甩鞭修正、失焦又弹回的追焦、目击式不稳拉到极限、长焦放大每一丝微动。冷月光自上方后侧接住他后脑与双肩、暖琥珀色供桌虚化光球在他前方较深平面绵软发亮（画框深远之处他正跑向的画外佛像）。

随即——在下一步——他停住。身体猛锁、脚钉在石板上、手持抖动随这记急刹猛烈剧颠、他的双肩随惯性余量一耸、头落定。他背对镜头站着、面朝画框更深处、气息短促、身体仍在残余的冲刺姿态中前倾。

随即决定——快、果断。一声小小的酸苦吐气、他的表情锐化。他猛然后转——身体完全转向镜头、双肩啪地转过来、头引领着转身自侧影再过 3/4 正面、直到他完全面朝镜头。他的脸转过来——汗光、下颌咬定、眼镜接住一枚微弱琥珀月牙、双眼越过镜头向前锁定背后的画外入口门、是那个决定要再跑一趟回去的老手艺人的利落挑剔劲。

随即他动身。他爆发成朝镜头的一次大力冲刺——径直朝镜头奔跑、锁死构图任他以全速拉近镜头的距离。他的身体前倾、双臂摆动、作战靴以沉重的教堂混响闷响狠砸石板、夹克被速度鼓动、随他逼近、他的面孔每跨一步填满更多画面——他离开深背景时身后的暖琥珀供桌虚化淡去、上方后侧冷月光接住他发顶、尘粒穿过冷调神光在他身旁飘过。摄影机不动。锁定构图撑住、任他拉近距离、他的身形在压缩长焦构图中越变越大、朝他向镜头冲刺。逼近中他的面孔可读了——挑剔而专注、下颌咬紧、双眼越过镜头锁定画外目标。全程手持抖动猛烈颠簸、运镜者体重把重重弹跳传导进长焦、追焦在他逼近时掠过他脸一次又弹回。

末拍：Jax 冲近镜头、他的身形填满锁定构图的大半、身后的暖溢光已消失、他四周冷月雾霭浓重。他就要越过镜头。收尾于他近距处的大力冲刺、锁定构图撑着、猛烈手持抖动持续。一声画外木质轰响自镜头后方方向遥远落下——他不退缩、双眼锁定前方、冲刺不打断。音频：开场他疾步远离镜头踏过石板的沉重脚步映教堂混响、身体猛锁时骤停的脚步与轰炸机夹克的窸窣、他转头时透过鼻子的短促轻吸、他决定的无声咂舌、他屏住的克制气息、他敲定时那声酸苦认命的吐气、他身体转向镜头时夹克的窸窣、他重新大力冲刺、朝镜头踏过石板的脚步每跨一步越响越近、他急促的呼吸逼近麦克风、夹克猛鼓的窸窣、那场画外木质轰响遥远落下带深沉低音与走廊距离般混响、深处平面铸铜供桌礼器随震颤微微叮当、酥油灯焰摆在他朝镜头接近时作身后退隐、远处 Roco 与 Rein 交叠的话语与用力鼻哼以更响的急迫隐约穿过大殿从画外渗入、运镜者近在麦前的重喘息、深沉教堂室内氛围。
