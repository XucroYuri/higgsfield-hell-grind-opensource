8K IMAX, photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera (no 3D render, no game engine). Modern action blockbuster — cinematography in the lineage of Lubezki and Deakins. Documentary verité style throughout — shot on long telephoto cine lens with heavy lens compression, operator-imperfect framing, micro-recompositions, focus hunts that miss and snap back. Heavy aggressive HANDHELD — violent vertical bounce, lateral whip-corrections, witness-style instability transmitting the scene's urgency. Anamorphic cine optics — natural anamorphic depth-of-field falloff and oval bokeh, slight horizontal compression of background highlights, BUT NO ANAMORPHIC LENS FLARES, no horizontal streak flares, no blue-line artifacts. Lighting: only natural light and practicals, contre-jour with atmospheric haze. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant aged-hardwood blacks/reds, secondary cool slate-blue moonlight, accent warm amber butter-lamps. 180° shutter motion blur, photorealistic skin (pores, sweat-shine, capillary flush, micro-saccades), corner vignette and edge chromatic aberration. Gravity, mass and contact shadows respected. Hollywood-grade acting — micro-pauses, no playing to camera, deadpan working-tradesman comedic register. 60fps, 2.39:1, no jitter, no identity drift. Environmental SFX only — no music, no subtitles, no on-screen text.

LOCATION: a vast ancient sacred prayer hall built entirely of intricately carved dark-stained hardwood — densely ornamented red-and-black columns on a grid, second-storey carved gallery balcony, coffered hardwood ceiling, single rectangular skylight casting a cool blue-white moonlight shaft down through heavy haze with visible god-rays. Polished dark grey flagstone floor. AT SCREEN-RIGHT (the direction Jax came from and will return to): the colossal seated dark-stone Buddha, ~6m tall, on a stepped pedestal, with the long altar table cluttered with cast-bronze ceremonial vessels and rows of butter-lamps with live amber flames flooding warm glow, the placed grey-tan plastic explosive lump visible on the lower carved robe just above the pedestal lip — the spot Jax stuck it. Off-screen far at screen-LEFT: the massive sealed entry door under sporadic battering ram impacts.

CHARACTER:
— JAX: lean Black man, early 30s, very short bleached-yellow buzz-cut, clear wraparound safety glasses (faintly fogged at lower edge), sweat at temples; black panel-paneled hooded bomber with charcoal-grey inserts and yellow zipper-pulls (hood down), black tee, olive cargo trousers, black combat boots, fingerless gloves; sweat-darkened patch over right ribs as if injured. Sweat-shined, dust-powdered, breathing hard. He moves like a tradesman on a deadline — efficient, fussy, unbothered.

SHOT — JAX RUNS IN PROFILE, STOPS, TURNS, RUNS BACK. Strict clean side-on profile to Jax's line of travel — camera 90 degrees to his run, anamorphic ~135mm-equivalent long telephoto, heavy lens compression, very shallow oval-bokeh DoF. Lens at chest height (~1.4m), 5m off-axis from his line on the long lens. Heavy aggressive HANDHELD throughout — violent vertical bounce of every operator footfall, lateral whip-corrections, focus hunts that miss and snap back, witness-style instability cranked hard, the long lens amplifying every micro-shake. Cool moonlight shaft cutting down across the upper portion of frame in heavy haze, warm amber altar bokeh balls glowing soft at screen-right (the off-screen Buddha behind him), dust drifting heavy in the air. The carved hardwood columns pass behind him in the deeper plane as soft compressed vertical rhythms in cool bokeh.

The shot OPENS on Jax already mid-sprint in clean profile facing screen-LEFT (running away from the off-screen Buddha which sits behind him at screen-right) — body forward, arms pumping, boots slamming flagstone, jacket flapping with the speed, glasses faintly fogged from the pace. The operator tracks parallel with him as he runs across the frame, the warm bokeh receding behind him. He is hurrying — the brisk fussy retreat of a guy who thinks the work is done.

Then — at the next stride — he STOPS. Body locks hard mid-sprint, the boot planting, knees flexing to absorb the momentum, the handheld shake JOLTING violently with the sudden brake. The carved bokeh behind him settles. He stands in profile facing screen-LEFT, breath sharp, body still angled forward in the residual sprint posture.

His head TURNS sharply BACK over his right shoulder toward the off-screen Buddha at screen-RIGHT — the rotation pulling his face from clean profile through 3/4-back angle. The CAMERA TURNS WITH HIM — the operator pivoting smoothly along with his head turn, the lens swinging from his profile through 3/4-back to follow his line of sight, the framing reorienting through the turn, the warm altar bokeh of the off-screen Buddha now sliding into view at screen-right of frame as the operator finishes the pivot. The camera ends repositioned 90 degrees from where it started — now framing Jax in clean profile facing screen-RIGHT (the new direction, toward the off-screen Buddha), his eyes locked off-screen-right on the placed plastic, the warm altar glow flooding the front of his face from screen-right ahead of him, the cool moonlight haze now behind him at screen-left.

He STARES off-screen-right at the placed plastic for a held beat — head tilted fractionally, brow furrowing, lips pursing in the small sour pout of dissatisfaction, the deadpan tradesman annoyance of a guy who knows the placement is wrong and he has to fix it. A small wordless tongue-click. The corner of his mouth pulls down. The handheld shake violent over his held looking face, the long lens compression amplifying every jolt.

Then DECISION. A small sour exhale through his teeth. His expression sharpens. He BOLTS — breaking back into a hard sprint forward in clean profile facing screen-RIGHT now, running BACK toward the off-screen Buddha and the placed plastic at the same brisk hurried pace, retracing his exact path in reverse. Boots slamming flagstone, body forward, jacket flapping, the warm altar glow brightening on his face as he closes the distance toward the warm source. The operator tracks parallel with him as he runs across the frame in the new direction, the warm bokeh balls of the altar now growing larger and brighter at screen-right ahead of him as he closes. He sprints across the frame in profile, the camera holding pace with him, the carved hardwood columns now passing behind him in the opposite direction. End on Jax in profile mid-sprint toward the off-screen Buddha at screen-right, the warm altar glow blooming bright on his face, the placement waiting ahead. An off-screen WOODEN BOOM lands distantly throughout — Jax doesn't flinch.
