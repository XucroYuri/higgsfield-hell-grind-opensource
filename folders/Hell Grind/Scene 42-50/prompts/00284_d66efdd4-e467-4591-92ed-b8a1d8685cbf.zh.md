Gravity and contact shadows respected. Hollywood-grade acting — micro-pauses, no playing to camera. 60fps, 2.39:1, no jitter, no identity drift. Environmental SFX only — no music, no subtitles, no on-screen text.

LOCATION (held across all shots): a vast ancient sacred prayer hall, built entirely of intricately carved dark-stained hardwood — densely ornamented columns, a second-storey carved gallery balcony around all four walls, coffered hardwood ceiling, a single rectangular skylight casting a cool blue-white moonlight shaft down through heavy haze with visible god-rays. The middle of the hall is empty negative space.

Jax hangs forward off the grip — arms loose dangling, head tipped forward, boots trailing 10–15cm above the deck, full bodyweight suspended; bomber bunched at the shoulders. SHOT 1 — STRICT PROFILE WIDE, ENTRY + REIN LOCKS THE DOOR, ONE CONTINUOUS HANDHELD TAKE. Strict clean side-on profile to the riders' line of travel — camera stays its ground, does NOT track them, never pans to follow. Locked at chest height (~1.4m), 9m back from the centerline of the entry door, framing a side-on view: the open carved double-door at far screen-left (threshold beyond reading as solid pitch-black void, no detail past the doorframe), the rest of the hall opening to screen-right with the cool moonlight shaft cutting down through haze and warm altar glow bleeding from far off-screen right. Anamorphic 35mm-equivalent, oval bokeh on the practical light sources, deeper read of the columns and back wall.

Then Roco's deck BURSTS out of the void through the open doorway at high speed (~8 m/s), hovering ~1m above the floor, traveling left-to-right across the frame in clean side-on profile — Roco riding the rear in a low athletic stance holding Jax aloft by the armpits, Jax limp in front of him, boots trailing in the air, body rocking with the motion. They sweep past camera in clean profile and continue toward screen-right, motion-blurred silhouettes cutting through the moonlight shaft, then exit screen-right while still at speed — off-screen we hear the thrusters flare as Roco brakes, dust catching the warm light from that direction.

The camera holds its strict profile geometry on the doorway. A half-beat after the men clear the frame, Rein lunges into shot from screen-left foreground — she had been just outside the camera's view — and slams both hands onto the inner face of the massive carved hardwood door. Shoulder-driving the wood with her full bodyweight, head down, jaw clenched, she swings the door shut. The void of the threshold pinches to nothing as the door seats with a deep wooden BOOM. Without pause she grabs the heavy iron locking bar from its rest beside the door, lifts it with both hands — forearms cording with the weight — and drops it across into the iron brackets on the inner face with a hard metallic CLANG. She steps back a half-step, palms still resting on the bar, head dropping forward, panting hard, a single drop of sweat tracking down her temple. The handheld shake gentles slightly as the action settles.

End on this held beat — door locked, Rein recovering with her back partly to camera, faint reverb tail dying away, faint thruster idle hum from off-screen right, deep cathedral room tone. SHOT 2 — LOW-ANGLE HALF-PROFILE CLOSE-UP, ROCO LOWERS JAX (JAX FOREGROUND, GAZE UP). Verité handheld doc — operator-imperfect framing, focus hunts, occasional flare from altar lamps.
