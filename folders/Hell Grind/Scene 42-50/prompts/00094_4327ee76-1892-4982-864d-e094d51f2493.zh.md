8K IMAX, photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera (no 3D render, no game engine). Modern action blockbuster — cinematography in the lineage of Lubezki and Deakins. Documentary verité style — shot on long telephoto cine lens with heavy lens compression, operator-imperfect framing, micro-recompositions, focus hunts that miss and snap back. Heavy aggressive HANDHELD — violent vertical bounce, lateral whip-corrections, witness-style instability transmitting the scene's urgency. Anamorphic cine optics — natural anamorphic depth-of-field falloff and oval bokeh, slight horizontal compression of background highlights, BUT NO ANAMORPHIC LENS FLARES, no horizontal streak flares, no blue-line artifacts. Lighting: only natural light and practicals, contre-jour with atmospheric haze. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant aged-hardwood blacks/reds, secondary cool slate-blue moonlight, accent warm amber butter-lamps. 180° shutter motion blur, photorealistic skin (pores, sweat-shine, capillary flush, micro-saccades), corner vignette and edge chromatic aberration. Gravity, mass and contact shadows respected. Hollywood-grade acting — micro-pauses, no playing to camera, deadpan working-tradesman comedic register. 60fps, 2.39:1, no jitter, no identity drift. Environmental SFX only — no music, no subtitles, no on-screen text.

LOCATION: a vast ancient sacred prayer hall built entirely of intricately carved dark-stained hardwood — densely ornamented red-and-black columns on a grid, second-storey carved gallery balcony, coffered hardwood ceiling, single rectangular skylight casting a cool blue-white moonlight shaft down through heavy haze with visible god-rays. Polished dark grey flagstone floor. AT THE FAR END BEHIND THE CAMERA: the colossal seated dark-stone Buddha, ~6m tall, on a stepped pedestal, with the long altar table cluttered with cast-bronze ceremonial vessels and rows of butter-lamps with live amber flames flooding warm glow, the placed grey-tan plastic explosive lump on the lower carved robe just above the pedestal lip — the spot Jax stuck it (he is running TOWARD this off-screen behind camera). DEEPER BACKGROUND BEHIND HIM: the carved hardwood columns and cool moonlit haze of the hall stretching back toward the distant off-screen entry door under sporadic battering ram impacts.

CHARACTER:
— JAX: lean Black man, early 30s, very short bleached-yellow buzz-cut, clear wraparound safety glasses (faintly fogged at lower edge), sweat at temples; black panel-paneled hooded bomber with charcoal-grey inserts and yellow zipper-pulls (hood down), black tee, olive cargo trousers, black combat boots, fingerless gloves; sweat-darkened patch over right ribs as if injured. Sweat-shined, dust-powdered, breathing hard. He moves like a tradesman on a deadline — efficient, fussy, unbothered.

SHOT — LOW-ANGLE CLOSE-UP, JAX SPRINTS BACK TOWARD CAMERA. Documentary verité long telephoto cine lens (anamorphic ~135mm-equivalent), heavy lens compression that flattens Jax against the carved hardwood architecture and cool moonlight haze receding behind him in compressed depth, very shallow oval-bokeh DoF — Jax razor-sharp when in focus, the deeper hall dissolved into thick layered cool-and-warm bokeh sliding behind him. Camera planted LOW on the flagstones (~0.4m height) pushing UP at a steep low angle, framed as a tight chest-up close-up but with the low-angle making him read tall and monumental against the carved coffered hardwood ceiling and the cool moonlight haze far above. Lens 4m from his approach line. Heavy aggressive HANDHELD throughout — violent vertical bounce, lateral whip-corrections, focus hunts that miss his face and snap back, the long-lens compression amplifying every shake.

The shot OPENS with Jax already at full sprint in the deeper plane, running TOWARD camera — body forward, arms pumping, boots slamming flagstone hard, jacket flapping with the speed, glasses faintly fogged from the pace. From the low angle his sprinting form looms tall, knees lifting into frame on each stride, the carved coffered ceiling and cool moonlight haze rising vast behind and above him, dust drifting heavy through the god-rays, the warm amber altar glow pouring onto his front face from off-screen behind camera (the off-screen Buddha he's running toward) — flooding his cheeks, the lenses of his clear safety glasses, the underside of his jaw in flickering live amber, his sweat-shined skin glistening wet with the warm light. Cool moonlight from above-back catches the crown of his bleached-yellow buzz-cut and rim-lights his shoulders.

He CLOSES the distance toward camera — the long-lens compression making the approach feel slowed and inevitable while the speed of his sprint reads urgent in his body language and breath. His face fills more of the frame stride by stride, the warm altar glow brightening on his features. His brow is set in fussy purposeful focus, jaw tight, eyes locked off-screen-down at the camera's level on the off-screen placed plastic ahead of and below him, the deadpan tradesman commitment of a guy returning to fix the work. His glasses catch brighter amber reflections as he closes. His bomber jacket flaps loud at his shoulders.

The handheld shake jolts violently with every pounding footfall and with the operator's struggle to hold the long-lens framing steady at this low angle, the focus hunting once across his face and snapping back as he draws closer. He keeps coming — boots SLAMMING flagstone in cathedral-reverbing thuds. His face fills a third of the frame, then half, the warm light pouring onto him hot. He doesn't break pace. His final stride brings him close to the lens. End on Jax mid-sprint at the closest point of his approach, his face filling much of the frame in the low-angle close-up, the warm altar bleed flooding him, the off-screen plastic waiting just past camera. An off-screen WOODEN BOOM lands distantly from far behind him at the entry end — he doesn't flinch, eyes locked forward toward the off-screen Buddha. Audio: his hard sprinting footfalls slamming flagstone in deep cathedral reverb close on mic, growing louder as he closes, the rustle of his bomber jacket flapping hard with the speed, his fast urgent breath through his teeth, the rasp of his sweat-soaked tee against his skin, the off-screen WOODEN BOOM landing far behind him with deep sub-bass and corridor-distance reverb, cast-bronze altar vessels chiming faintly with the tremor close on mic from the off-screen altar behind camera, butter-lamp flutter close on mic from the altar behind camera, distant combined Roco-and-Rein voices and grunts of effort bleeding faintly off-screen across the hall with louder urgency now, the operator's heavy breath close on mic at low angle, deep cathedral room tone.
