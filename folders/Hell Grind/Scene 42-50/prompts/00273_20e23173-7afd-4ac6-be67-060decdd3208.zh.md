8K IMAX, photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera (no 3D render, no game engine). Modern action blockbuster — cinematography in the lineage of Lubezki and Deakins. Shot on anamorphic cine optics — natural anamorphic depth-of-field falloff and oval bokeh, slight horizontal compression of background highlights, BUT NO ANAMORPHIC LENS FLARES, no horizontal streak flares, no blue-line artifacts. Lighting: only natural light and practicals, contre-jour with atmospheric haze. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant aged-hardwood blacks/reds, secondary cool slate-blue moonlight, accent warm amber butter-lamps. 180° shutter motion blur, photorealistic skin (pores, sweat-shine, capillary flush, micro-saccades), corner vignette and edge chromatic aberration. Gravity and contact shadows respected. Hollywood-grade acting — micro-pauses, no playing to camera. 60fps, 2.39:1, no jitter, no identity drift. Environmental SFX only — no music, no subtitles, no on-screen text.

LOCATION (held across all shots): a vast ancient sacred prayer hall, built entirely of intricately carved dark-stained hardwood — densely ornamented columns, a second-storey carved gallery balcony around all four walls, coffered hardwood ceiling, a single rectangular skylight casting a cool blue-white moonlight shaft down through heavy haze with visible god-rays. Polished dark grey flagstone floor. AT THE FAR END: a colossal seated dark-stone Buddha, ~6m tall, on a stepped pedestal, in front of it a long altar cluttered with bronze ceremonial vessels, candelabras and rows of butter-lamps flooding the front third of the hall in warm amber glow. The middle of the hall is empty negative space.

CHARACTERS (sweat-shined, dust-powdered, breathing hard):
— ROCO: lean Latino-Asian, late 20s, mid-length messy black hair plastered to forehead, dark mustache, two parallel matte-red painted stripes across the bridge of his nose; black hooded windbreaker with charcoal-grey panel inserts (hood down), black tee, black cargo pants with olive thigh-strap rigs, fingerless gloves with red bandana on right wrist, black combat boots.

GROUP STAGING (held across all shots): the two of them stand a couple of meters apart, facing each other across the vast empty hall floor. The colossal seated stone Buddha rises off-screen behind Roco at the altar end (warm amber butter-lamp glow washes Roco from his back, cool moonlight from above falls on the front of his profile). Jax faces the opposite direction (warm altar glow on the front of his profile, cool moonlight on his back). Both stand in the cool middle zone of the hall.

SHOT 1 — ROCO ORDERS, PROFILE CLOSE-UP. Tight chest-up close-up on Roco in strict clean side-on profile facing screen-left (toward Jax off-screen), anamorphic ~75mm-equivalent, shallow oval-bokeh DoF — Roco razor-sharp, the deep hall behind him dissolved into soft cool bokeh with faint warm amber bleed from the altar far behind him reading on the back of his neck. Camera at chest height (~1.4m), 1.2m from him, faint handheld breath. Cool moonlight catches the front of his profile from camera-front; warm amber rim from the altar far behind him glows on the back curve of his hair and neck. The two matte-red painted stripes across the bridge of his nose visible clean in profile.

Roco's gaze locked off-screen left on Jax, jaw set hard, mustache firm. A small pre-speech swallow, throat moving in profile. He speaks low, controlled, in a no-arguments commanding tone — first the name, sharp and grounded: "Jax." A half-beat pause, his eyes hardening. Then the order, even flatter and more direct, almost gentle in its certainty: "Blow it." His lips close on the consonant; he doesn't blink. Hold a beat — locked profile, locked gaze, no give, no tension in the body, just immovable resolve. Audio: dialogue delivered low and commanding close on mic with the natural deep reverb of the hall on the consonants, distant butter-lamp flutter, deep cathedral room tone; final beat only his breath and ambience.

SHOT 2 — JAX TURNS TO ROCO, INCREDULOUS, CLOSE-UP. Tight chest-up close-up on Jax in clean profile facing screen-right (toward Roco off-screen), anamorphic ~75mm-equivalent, shallow oval-bokeh DoF, slight low-angle, lens at chest height (~1.4m), 1.2m from him, faint handheld breath. Warm amber altar glow from screen-right washes the front of his profile, flickering live across the lenses of his clear safety glasses, his cheekbone and lower lip; cool moonlight rims the back of his close-cropped yellow buzz-cut.

Jax's head turns sharply from gazing forward to looking screen-right at Roco, his gaze landing wide-eyed behind the clear lenses, eyebrows up high, mouth dropping open in pure disbelief. A beat of stunned silence, mouth still hanging open. Then he throws out a single word, voice cracked with how absurd this is, half exclamation half plea: "Bro." A short stunned head-shake, glasses catching a flicker of warm amber. He continues incredulously, the words tumbling out fast: "What do you mean, blow it?" His voice climbs on the second half. He pauses for a beat, breath sharp through his teeth. Hold on his face — wide-eyed, sweat-shined, disbelieving. Audio: dialogue delivered close on mic in incredulous escalating alto-tenor with cathedral reverb on the words, distant lamp flutter, deep hall tone.

SHOT 3 — TELEPHOTO MEDIUM-CLOSE FROM BEHIND JAX, ROCO + BUDDHA IN FRAME. Long telephoto cine lens (anamorphic ~135mm-equivalent), heavy compression, very shallow oval-bokeh DoF. Camera positioned behind Jax over his right shoulder at upper-back height (~1.5m), 2m from him so Jax's back, the curve of his right shoulder and the edge of his close-cropped yellow buzz-cut sit in soft out-of-focus silhouette across the immediate foreground left side of frame. The focus pulls deep past Jax onto Roco standing in the middle distance facing camera-Jax, Roco sharp in the deeper plane with the warm altar glow washing the back of him and cool moonlight on his front. Behind Roco, telephoto compression collapses depth so the colossal seated stone Buddha looms huge in soft focus directly behind him, the half-lidded carved stone face reading clearly through the haze, warm amber butter-lamp flames blooming as oval bokeh balls along the bottom edge of frame from the altar table at the Buddha's base. Heavy handheld breath, faint focus hunt that resolves on Roco.

Jax's silhouetted right arm rises into the foreground frame, his hand and pointing index finger jabbing across toward Roco — emphatic, exasperated. He continues his line of dialogue, picking up the thought, his voice landing on each word with theatrical objection: "That's BUDDHA." The word "Buddha" lands on a hard emphasized beat, his pointing finger trembling slightly. Then: "I can't blow up Buddha!" — the words escalating in pitch, the second "Buddha" almost cracking. Then the kicker, half-whispered half-shouted, his voice cracking on the verb: "He'll curse me!" His pointing finger holds steady on Roco, with the towering stone Buddha right behind Roco as the visual punchline of the line. Roco in the deeper plane doesn't react — face flat, jaw set, unmoved, just looking back at Jax. Hold a beat — Jax's pointing finger trembling in the foreground, Roco unmoved, Buddha towering between/behind them in the haze. Audio: Jax's dialogue delivered close on mic in incredulous escalating tone with the natural deep reverb of the hall on each emphasized word, distant lamp flutter, faint incense crackle, deep hall tone.

SHOT 4 — ROCO REPEATS THE NAME, PROFILE CLOSE-UP. Same framing as shot 1 — tight chest-up close-up on Roco in strict clean profile facing screen-left, anamorphic ~75mm-equivalent, shallow oval-bokeh DoF, lens at chest height, 1.2m from him, faint handheld breath. Roco hasn't moved an inch. His gaze still locked off-screen left on Jax, jaw still set, even harder now. A single beat of held silence. Then he says one word, low and final, no patience left, an order and a warning compressed into a single syllable: "Jax." His lips close on the consonant. He doesn't blink. The amber rim catches the firm line of his mustache. Hold on his profile — locked, immovable, the word doing all the work. Audio: the single word delivered low close on mic with natural deep reverb on the consonant, distant lamp flutter, deep hall tone; final beat only breath and ambience.

SHOT 5 — JAX RELENTS, CLOSE-UP. Same tight chest-up close-up framing on Jax as shot 2 — anamorphic ~75mm-equivalent, shallow oval-bokeh DoF, slight low-angle, 1.2m from him, faint handheld breath. Jax's eyes hold on Roco off-screen right for a beat, mouth still open from his rant, the wind out of him. He breaks first — his shoulders drop with a defeated exhale, his head bobs once, eyes rolling skyward briefly. Both his hands rise into the lower edge of frame in a small surrender gesture, palms out. He concedes, voice tired and quick, the words clipped together in defeat: "Okay, okay!" The two words come out in a rapid pair, the second one slightly higher and resigned. Then his head turns away from Roco — gaze traveling slowly screen-right and then UP, eyes leaving Roco and traveling up toward the off-screen Buddha (now further back behind Roco), head tipping back, chin lifting, the awe returning to his face but now mixed with dread. Warm amber lamp-light flickers live across the underside of his jaw and the lenses of his glasses as his face turns upward. Hold on his face — gazing up at the Buddha he's been ordered to blow up, equal parts fear, awe, and resignation. Audio: his defeated double "okay, okay" close on mic with natural hall reverb, a long resigned exhale, distant lamp flutter, deep hall tone; final beat only his breath and ambience.
