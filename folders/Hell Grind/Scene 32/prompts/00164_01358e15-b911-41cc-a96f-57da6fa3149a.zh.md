ROKO comes through the carved doorway first at speed — then EXECUTES A HARD PANIC-BRAKE: his board's discs SURGE BRIGHT (red-amber-purple flare, point-source only — no trails), body pitches slightly forward with the deceleration then corrects, board comes to a dead stop floating just inside the threshold. REIN partly visible behind/beside on RIGHT. All three in a perfect OH-SHIT FREEZE — motionless except for micro body-sway from residual bump momentum that slowly damps out over 2–3 seconds. Steam drifts around and past them. The monk's blurred figure continues at the barrel in the soft background, unaware.

Audio: burst of board disc surge hum as Roko brakes (disc sound peaks on flare, no squeal) → soft dull thump as Jaxx bumps Roko → smaller soft thud from Rein's board stopping → then total silence. Steam hiss. Distant drip. Held stone-room quiet.
