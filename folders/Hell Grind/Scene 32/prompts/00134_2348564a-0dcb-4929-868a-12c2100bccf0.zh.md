MEDIUM SHOT — VIGOROUS WASH:
The monk sits in the barrel, upper body above the rim. He SCOOPS water with the copper ladle and POURS it hard over his shoulder or arm — generous splash, water running fast down his skin, steam bursting from the contact. The other hand SCRUBS that area IMMEDIATELY and with real force — not a gentle rub, a thorough committed scrub, the kind of a man who means business with hygiene. He works up and down his arm, across his shoulder, into his neck. At one point he lifts the ladle HIGH and pours the full scoop directly over the top of his head — water cascades down over his bald scalp, runs through his white beard in streams, steam bursting up around his head. Then scoops again and repeats on another area. The rhythm is purposeful and absorbed — not meditative slowness, but focused physical effort. He hums a low Tibetan melody under his breath throughout, continuous and automatic, the way a person hums when their mind is fully elsewhere. Wet beard sways with each scrubbing motion. Steam rolls off the water surface and the splashed skin around him.

WORLD LOCK（世界观锁定）：
— 构图（硬规则）：僧侣与木桶占据画面左三分之二——僧侣居中于该区域、中景、胸口以上越出桶沿。拱形门洞位于画面右侧三分之一，全程柔焦/失焦。摄影机静止——完全不动。

SHOT（单镜）——单次连续长镜头，约 5–7 秒。无剪切。摄影机完全静止。

MEDIUM SHOT（中景）——用力清洗：
僧侣坐于桶中，上半身越出桶沿。他用铜长柄勺舀水并用力泼向自己的肩或手臂——水花大方泼下、水沿肌肤快速流下、接触处蒸汽喷涌。另一手立即搓洗该处并真下力气——不是轻柔擦拭，而是彻底投入的搓洗，一个对卫生认真之人的样子。他上上下下顺着手臂、横过肩头、探入脖颈地搓。某一刻他高高举起长柄勺，把满满一勺水直接泼到自己头顶——水从秃顶倾泻而下，穿白须成股流下，蒸汽在头顶四周猛喷。然后再舀、再在另一处重复。节奏专注而沉浸——不是冥想式的缓慢，而是聚焦的体力投入。他全程低声哼着一支藏族小调，连绵而自动，一个人心思全然不在时的那种哼法。湿胡须随每次搓洗动作摇摆。蒸汽从水面与溅湿的肌肤上滚涌开来。
