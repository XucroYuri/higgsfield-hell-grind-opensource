COUNT LOCK: exactly 3 riders (Rein, Jaxx, Roko), exactly 1 leopard. No duplicates, no clones.

SPATIAL: leopard FOREGROUND lower-LEFT, gaze toward right side of frame. Cloud-shrouded peak deep background, RIGHT. Camera low and rooted near the leopard. Riders enter from the UPPER-RIGHT EDGE of frame, already back-to-camera, already heading away toward the peak — they cross through the RIGHT HALF of frame VERY LOW (a few feet above the ground), shrinking rapidly into the depth. Only their backs and rear-three-quarter silhouettes are ever visible.

ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT — single oner, no cuts.

BEAT ORDER (strict): (1) leopard licks paw, calm. (2) AUDIO CUE — a low board-hum swells in from off-frame upper-right, rising fast. (3) Leopard reacts to the sound — ears flatten, body cowers — BEFORE the riders are visible in frame. (4) Riders blast into frame from upper-right at high speed and tear away toward the peak. (5) Camera takes kinetic jolt at the pass moment. (6) Leopard, already cowered, just keeps tracking them with its eyes as they recede. (7) Camera micro-pans to the cloud-shrouded peak; riders are tiny specks.

CAMERA: settled and rooted, near-static, only subtle live breath. Camera does NOT follow the leopard's gaze and does NOT tilt up. Two exceptions: (1) at the exact moment of the rider pass, a brief kinetic JOLT-shake as if hit by the boards' magnetic pressure-wave, then immediate re-settle; (2) at the very end, one quiet slow micro-pan toward the cloud-shrouded peak.

Open settled on the leopard. The leopard tips its head down to its forepaw and gives it two or three calm unhurried licks with its rough pink tongue, eyes half-lidded.

THEN we hear a low board-hum swell in from off-frame, rising fast, coming from the upper-right. The leopard reacts to the SOUND — head lifts mid-lick, ears prick and then flatten fully back, body stiffens and presses lower and flatter against the ground in a scared-wary cower, shoulders dropping, eyes wide and locked on the upper-right where the sound is coming from. Head does NOT turn away. The cower is complete BEFORE any rider is visible.

ONLY THEN — AT HIGH SPEED, extreme velocity, whip-fast, frame-blur, gone in under a second — three riders BLAST into frame from upper-right edge: Rein, Jaxx, Roko in a tight V, knees bent, weight low, arms making micro-balance corrections, jackets snapping hard in the slipstream, hair lashed flat backward, loose straps and bandanas whipping. Their slipstream lifts a small puff of powder off the ground in their wake.

At the moment of the pass the camera takes its kinetic jolt, then re-settles. The leopard — already pressed flat — does not flinch further; gaze stays locked, head smoothly tracking the trio as they shrink into the depth.

After the riders are deep in frame, the camera performs its small slow micro-pan toward the cloud-shrouded peak — the riders are tiny specks near the peak in the haze, still shrinking.

Style: 8K cinema, hyperreal. Lighting: strictly natural — overcast high-altitude diffuse daylight, soft cool ambient with a low contre-jour rim, soft atmospheric haze. Color: 60:30:10 — 60% cool grey-blue snow and stone, 30% warm cream leopard fur, 10% steady drive-ring point-glows (purple / amber / red — point glows only, NO trails, NO neon streaks, NO rainbow smears). Fur: individual guard hairs catch backlight, micro-snow glitter, visible breath from the leopard's nose, rough pink tongue during the lick. Motion: 180° shutter blur — riders smear naturally on the pass, no video staccato; 60fps. Physics: gravity respected, contact shadows correct, hair/fur/fabric delays behind motion, snow puff carries weight and inertia.

Audio: no music. Environmental SFX only — wind, soft tongue-rasp during the lick, then a low rising board-hum that swells in from off-frame BEFORE the riders are visible (this hum is what triggers the leopard's cower), then peaks with a hard Doppler shift as the riders pass, then fades. Snow hiss, soft chuff from the leopard.

Avoid: jitter, identity drift, mannequin riders, frozen poses, big camera moves, camera following the leopard's gaze, camera tilting up, riders crossing through the LEFT half of the frame, riders facing or approaching camera, riders turning around mid-shot, frontal hero shot, riders flying away from the peak, leopard standing up, leopard fleeing, leopard turning its head away, leopard breaking eye contact, leopard reacting only AFTER the riders pass, leopard cower starting after riders enter frame, riders entering frame before the leopard has cowered, duplicate riders, four+ riders, two leopards, locked-off mechanical stillness.

CAMERA（摄影机）：定住扎根、近静态、仅微妙活呼吸。摄影机不跟随豹视线、不上仰。两处例外：(1) 在骑手通过的精确刹那、一次短暂动态 JOLT 震、如被板磁压波击中、然后立即回落；(2) 在最后、一次安静的慢微移朝云雾巨峰。

以豹定场开场。豹头朝下弯向前爪、用粗糙粉舌缓慢不紧不慢地舔两或三下、眼睛半眯。

然后我们听到一阵低闷板嗡从画外涌入、快速渐升、来自画面右上。豹对该声音反应——舔到一半头抬、耳先竖起再完全后压、身体僵住并往下压得更平、成惊怯的畏缩、肩下沉、眼睛瞪大锁向声音传来的右上。头不转开。在任何骑手可见之前、畏缩已完成。

骑手深入画面后、摄影机朝云雾缭绕的巨峰做小慢微移——骑手是雾中巨峰附近的小点、仍在下缩。
Style（风格）：8K 电影、超写实。Lighting（灯光）：严格自然——阴天高海拔漫射日光、柔和冷光环境 + 低角度逆光背光边光、柔和大气雾。Color（色彩）：60:30:10——60% 冷调灰蓝雪与石、30% 暖调奶白豹毛、10% 稳定驱动环点光（紫/琥珀/红——仅点光、无光尾、无霓虹拖痕、无彩虹拖影）。Fur（毛发）：单根针毛接住逆光、微雪晶闪烁、豹鼻可见呼吸、舔爪时粗糙粉舌。Motion（运动）：180° 快门模糊——骑手通过时自然拖影、无视频顿挫；60fps。Physics（物理）：重力被尊重、接触阴影正确、发/毛/织物在运动后延迟、雪蓬携重量与惯性。
