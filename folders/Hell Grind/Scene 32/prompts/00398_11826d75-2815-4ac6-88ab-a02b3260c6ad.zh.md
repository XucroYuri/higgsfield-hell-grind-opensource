Only their backs and rear-three-quarter silhouettes are ever visible.

Two exceptions: (1) at the exact moment of the rider pass, a brief kinetic JOLT-shake as if hit by the boards' magnetic pressure-wave, then immediate re-settle; (2) at the very end, one quiet slow micro-pan toward the cloud-shrouded peak.

Open settled on the leopard. The leopard tips its head down to its forepaw and gives it two or three calm unhurried licks with its rough pink tongue, eyes half-lidded. As the last lick ends, ears prick, body stiffens, head lifts, eyes sharpen toward the upper-right (head does not turn away). A tense beat.

Then AT HIGH SPEED — extreme velocity, whip-fast, frame-blur, gone in under a second — three riders BLAST into frame from upper-right edge: Rein, Jaxx, Roko in a tight V, knees bent, weight low, arms making micro-balance corrections, jackets snapping hard in the slipstream, hair lashed flat backward, loose straps and bandanas whipping. Their slipstream lifts a small puff of powder off the ground in their wake.

The INSTANT the FIRST rider clears past — without waiting for the other two — the leopard IMMEDIATELY reacts: ears flatten fully back, shoulders drop, body presses lower and flatter against the ground in a scared-wary cower. The leopard NEVER breaks eye contact, NEVER turns its head off the riders — gaze stays locked on them the whole time, head smoothly tracking as the trio recedes.

At that same moment the camera takes its kinetic jolt, then re-settles. After the riders are deep in frame, the camera performs its small slow micro-pan toward the cloud-shrouded peak — the riders are tiny specks near the peak in the haze, still shrinking.

Style: 8K cinema, hyperreal. Lighting: strictly natural — overcast high-altitude diffuse daylight, soft cool ambient with a low contre-jour rim, soft atmospheric haze. Color: 60:30:10 — 60% cool grey-blue snow and stone, 30% warm cream leopard fur, 10% steady drive-ring point-glows (purple / amber / red — point glows only, NO trails, NO neon streaks, NO rainbow smears). Fur: individual guard hairs catch backlight, micro-snow glitter, visible breath from the leopard's nose, rough pink tongue during the lick. Motion: 180° shutter blur — riders smear naturally on the pass, no video staccato; 60fps. Physics: gravity respected, contact shadows correct, hair/fur/fabric delays behind motion, snow puff carries weight and inertia.

Audio: no music. Environmental SFX only — wind, soft tongue-rasp during the lick, low rising board hum that swells, Dopplers past, fades, snow hiss, soft chuff from the leopard.

ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT（一镜）——单次长镜头、无切。

CAMERA（摄影机）：定住扎根、近静态、仅微妙活呼吸。摄影机不跟随豹视线、不上仰。两处例外：(1) 在骑手通过的精确刹那、一次短暂动态 JOLT 震、如被板磁压波击中、然后立即回落；(2) 在最后、一次安静的慢微移朝云雾巨峰。

以豹定场开场。豹头朝下弯向前爪、用粗糙粉舌缓慢不紧不慢地舔两或三下、眼睛半眯。最后一舔结束时、耳竖起、身体僵住、头抬起、眼睛朝右上锐利起来（头不转开）。一个紧绷的节拍。

第一个骑手刚擦过——不等另外两个——豹立即反应：耳朵完全后压、肩下沉、身体往地面压得更低更平、成惊怯的畏缩。豹绝不中断目光接触、绝不把头转离骑手——视线全程锁住、头顺畅追踪三人后退。

同一刹那摄影机受到动态颠震、然后回落。骑手深入画面后、摄影机朝云雾缭绕的巨峰做小慢微移——骑手是雾中巨峰附近的小点、仍在下缩。
