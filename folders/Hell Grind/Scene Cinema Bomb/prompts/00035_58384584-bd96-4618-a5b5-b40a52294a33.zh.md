Duration: 5 seconds. Aspect ratio: 21:9. Two timed shots. R2V mode.

Use Image 1 as the location reference: a police operations centre with a curved bank of large wall monitors showing maps and CCTV. Use Image 2 <<<image_2>>> as the dispatcher identity and costume reference. Use Image 3 <<<image_1>>> as the background officers identity and costume reference.

To the right of the room there is a window with horizontal blinds and a vintage brass desk lamp with a green shade on a side desk. Wall has framed sheriff plaques. Polished light grey floor. Cool desaturated blue-grey palette with warm tungsten accents from desk lamps. Low-key practical lighting.

Character lock for the dispatcher: A young female SCPD police officer matching Image 2. Dark brown hair pulled into a tight low bun. Slim build. Grey SCPD uniform shirt with an SCPD patch on the chest pocket and on the shoulder. Body camera or microphone clipped to her chest. Dark utility belt. Black headset with a boom microphone over her right ear. Calm focused expression.

Background officers lock: Two SCPD officers from Image 3 stand in the background of Shot 1. They wear matching grey SCPD uniforms. They face the back wall of monitors. They never enter the foreground.

The dispatcher sits at a desk in the center of the frame. She is framed from behind and slightly to her right side, so we see the back of her head, her right shoulder and her right ear with the headset. Her tight low bun is visible. Three monitors stand on her desk in front of her. The central monitor shows a city map. A red rectangular tag on the map reads "PASSADE MALL". A red map pin marks the location. A vintage desk lamp on her left throws warm tungsten light onto the desk. In the deep background, two SCPD officers in grey uniforms stand in front of the curved monitor wall, looking at maps. The room has a cool desaturated blue-grey palette with warm tungsten accents. 50mm lens, mild compression. Locked-off camera with subtle handheld micro-shake. She works at her keyboard — only her hands and head move slightly.

Shot 2, 1.5 to 4.0 seconds — Extreme close-up on the dispatcher speaking into the headset:
Extreme close-up on the dispatcher's face from a three-quarter angle on her right side. Framed tight from her chin to her cheekbone. Her lips, jaw, neck and the boom microphone of her headset fill the frame. Her dark uniform collar visible at the bottom of frame. Background fully in bokeh — soft cool blue-grey blur. She speaks into the headset with calm urgent dispatch cadence.
At 1.7 seconds she speaks: "All units. Possible bomb at Passade Mall. Code three. Move."
135mm telephoto lens. Very shallow depth of field. Locked-off camera with subtle handheld micro-shake. Only her lips, jaw and breathing move.

Aesthetics: ARRI ALEXA, 35mm film grain, anamorphic 21:9 letterbox, low-key cinematic lighting with warm tungsten practicals from desk lamps and cool blue-grey ambient from monitors. Shallow depth of field on the ECU.

Continuity: The dispatcher's identity, hair, uniform and headset stay identical across both shots. The room layout stays consistent.

SFX: Shot 1 quiet room tone, soft keyboard typing, distant radio chatter, low hum of monitors. Shot 2 her clear voice through the headset, faint radio crackle behind her line. No music.

Final frame: Shot 2 at 4.0 seconds — ECU on her mouth just after the final word "Move", lips closing, jaw set.

地点锁定：一个警察调度中心。远端墙面有一整排弧形、两行排列的壁挂显示器，显示带红色标记的地图、闭路电视画面与航拍影像。显示器墙前是一排带网面办公椅与台灯的操作工位。房间右侧有一扇带横向百叶的窗户，侧桌上放着一盏复古黄铜绿罩台灯。墙面挂着镶框的治安官徽牌。抛光浅灰色地面。冷调低饱和蓝灰色调，配以台灯带来的暖调白炽光点缀。低调的实用光源布光。

调度员角色锁定：一位年轻女性，SCPD 警员，与 Image 2 一致。深棕色头发挽成紧贴的低发髻。身材纤瘦。灰色 SCPD 制服衬衫，胸前口袋与肩部有 SCPD 臂章。胸口夹着执法记录仪或麦克风。深色武装腰带。黑色头戴式耳机，右侧耳边带有吊杆麦克风。平静而专注的表情。

背景警员锁定：Image 3 中的两名 SCPD 警员站在镜头 1 的背景里。他们穿着同款灰色 SCPD 制服。他们面朝后方的显示器墙。他们从不进入前景。

镜头 1，0.0 至 1.5 秒——调度员与房间的中宽景：
调度员坐在画面中央的一桌工位。她从背后偏右侧被取景，因此我们能看见她的后脑、右肩以及带耳机的右耳。她紧贴的低发髻清晰可见。她面前桌上有三台显示器。中央显示器显示一张城市地图。地图上一个红色长方形标签写着「PASSADE MALL」。一个红色地图图钉标出该位置。她左侧一盏复古台灯将暖调白炽光洒在桌上。在远景深处，两名穿灰色制服的 SCPD 警员站在弧形显示器墙前，看着地图。房间是冷调低饱和蓝灰色调，配以暖调白炽光点缀。50mm 镜头，轻微压缩。锁定机位，带细微的手持微振。她敲击键盘——只有她的手与头微微移动。

镜头 2，1.5 至 4.0 秒——调度员对着耳机说话的极特写：
以她右侧的四分之三角度对调度员面部极特写。取景紧绷，从下颌到颧骨。她的嘴唇、下颌、脖颈与耳机吊杆麦克风充满画面。画面底部可见她深色制服领口。背景完全虚化——柔和冷调蓝灰的模糊。她以平静而急切的调度节奏对着耳机说话。
在 1.7 秒时她说：「各单位注意。Passade 购物中心可能有炸弹。三号代码。行动。」
135mm 长焦镜头。极浅景深。锁定机位，带细微的手持微振。只有她的嘴唇、下颌与呼吸在动。

美学：ARRI ALEXA，35mm 胶片颗粒，变形宽银幕 21:9 上下黑边（letterbox），低调电影布光，带台灯暖调白炽光实用光源与显示器冷调蓝灰环境光。极特写（ECU）采用浅景深。

连续性：调度员在两个镜头间的身份、头发、制服与耳机保持一致。房间布局保持一致。

SFX（音效）：镜头 1 为安静的室内环境音、柔和键盘打字声、远处电台对讲声、显示器低鸣。镜头 2 是她透过耳机传来的清晰声音、台词背后微弱的电台杂音。无音乐。

收尾帧：镜头 2 的第 4.0 秒——刚说完最后一词「Move（行动）」后她嘴部的极特写（ECU），双唇合拢、下颌绷定。
