PROMPT — NanoBanana — SCPD EOD TECHNICIAN

@scpd_eod — Soul City Police Department EOD bomb disposal technician, full-body shot. Adult male, heavy build hidden under suit. Wearing a full heavy bomb suit, colour-matched to SCPD tactical units:

- Dark charcoal grey EOD blast suit, multi-layered armoured construction, articulated panels at shoulders, elbows, knees and hips. Reinforced rectangular chest plate, groin plate, thigh plates, shin plates — all in the same dark charcoal grey, no red trim, no coloured seams. Quilted ballistic fabric visible between rigid plates, faintly lighter charcoal in tone. Black SCPD eagle badge embroidered on the left chest plate — same eagle silhouette as worn on SCPD tactical vests. Stencilled white "SCPD EOD" text across the back of the chest plate.
- Heavy bomb suit helmet — dark charcoal grey shell, large clear curved ballistic visor across the front, internal ventilation grilles on the sides. Bold white "SCPD" lettering stencilled across the front top of the helmet shell, just above the visor — same font and weight as SCPD tactical helmets. Black SCPD eagle decal on the right side of the helmet. Inside the visor: black balaclava covering the lower face up to the bridge of the nose, only eyes visible. Faint headset boom mic and earpiece inside the helmet. Wide rigid neck collar attached to the helmet, sealing into the suit.
- Heavy padded gauntlets, black with dark charcoal grey articulated knuckle plates, gripping a small black EOD defuser kit (rectangular hard case with wire cutters, an X-ray probe, a pair of insulated pliers visible).
- Heavy reinforced black tactical boots, thick rubber soles, fastened with rigid buckles up the shin plate, matching SCPD tactical unit footwear.
- Web of cable management running along the suit's seams — radio cable, oxygen line, all in black braided sheathing.

Standing still in a confident wide stance, body angled at 3/4 to the camera, facing slightly screen-right. Helmet visor faces toward camera, head turned just slightly off-axis. Defuser kit held in the right gauntlet at hip level, left arm relaxed at side. Posture grounded, weight evenly distributed.

CAMERA: Full-body shot from feet to top of helmet, framed slightly low-angle (camera at chest height, looking marginally up) so the technician reads as imposing. Anamorphic 50mm-equivalent lens, mild barrel character, shallow depth of field, plain neutral seamless backdrop softly out of focus.

LIGHTING: Soft diffused key light from upper-right, gentle ambient fill from below, no direct sun, no hard shadows. Camera on the SHADOW side — left side of the suit holds soft falloff, right side catches gentle key. Helmet visor catches one soft rectangular highlight from the key.

PALETTE 60:30:10: 60% dark charcoal grey suit and helmet · 30% black gauntlets, boots, balaclava, and SCPD eagle badge · 10% white "SCPD" stencil on helmet and "SCPD EOD" stencil on back

GRADING: cinematic photo-realism, desaturated charcoal-and-grey base, fine grain. Crisp armour textures — visible weave on quilted panels, scuffs and micro-scratches on the rigid plates, dust on the lower legs and boots.

PROMPT ——NanoBanana——SCPD EOD 排爆专家

@scpd_eod ——Soul City 警局排爆（EOD）炸弹处理专家，全身镜头。成年男性，敦实体格藏在排爆服下。身穿全套重型排爆服，颜色与 SCPD 战术单位匹配：

- 深炭灰排爆防爆服（EOD blast suit），多层装甲构造，肩部、肘部、膝部与胯部有关节式面板。加固矩形胸甲板、裆部甲板、大腿甲板、护胫甲板——均为同款深炭灰，无红色饰边、无彩色缝线。刚性甲板之间可见绗缝防弹布料，炭灰色调略浅。左胸甲板上绣有黑色 SCPD 鹰徽——与 SCPD 战术背心上佩戴的为同一鹰形轮廓。胸甲板背部横贯白色丝印「SCPD EOD」文字。
- 重型排爆服头盔——深炭灰壳体，正面大型透明弧形防弹面罩，两侧内部通风格栅。头盔壳体正面顶部、面罩正上方丝印醒目的白色「SCPD」字样——与 SCPD 战术头盔相同的字体与字重。头盔右侧黑色 SCPD 鹰徽贴标。面罩内侧：黑色巴拉克拉瓦头套覆盖下脸直至鼻梁，仅露双眼。头盔内侧可见幽淡的头戴式耳机吊杆麦克风与耳机。连接头盔的宽大硬质护颈领，密封进排爆服。

- 厚重加衬护腕（gauntlets），黑色配深炭灰关节式指节板，握着一只小型黑色排爆排除器工具盒（EOD defuser kit，矩形硬壳盒，内可见剪线钳、一枚 X 光探针、一把绝缘钳）。

- 重型加固黑色战术靴，厚橡胶鞋底，护胫板处缀刚性鞋扣固定，与 SCPD 战术单位鞋靴匹配。

- 沿排爆服接缝排布的电缆管理线网——电台线、供氧管路，均为黑色编织护套。

以一个自信的宽站姿静止站立，身体成 3/4 角度朝向摄影机，微微面向画面右侧。头盔面罩朝向摄影机，头仅略偏离轴线。排除器工具盒握于右护腕中、位于胯部高度，左臂松弛垂于身侧。姿态沉稳，重心均匀分布。

摄影机（CAMERA）：自脚至头盔顶的全身镜头，略带低角度取景（摄影机位于胸部高度，略微仰拍），使专家显得气势慑人。等效变形宽银幕 50mm 镜头，轻微桶形畸变特性，浅景深，纯中性无缝背景柔和失焦。

布光（LIGHTING）：右上柔和的漫射主光，下方柔和环境补光，无直射太阳，无硬阴影。摄影机位于阴影侧——排爆服左侧持柔和衰减，右侧承接柔和主光。头盔面罩承接主光的一处柔和矩形高光。

调色 60:30:10：60% 深炭灰排爆服与头盔 · 30% 黑色护腕、靴、头套与 SCPD 鹰徽 · 10% 头盔上白色「SCPD」丝印与背部「SCPD EOD」丝印

调色处理（GRADING）：电影级照片写实，去饱和的炭灰基调，细腻颗粒。明晰的装甲质感——绗缝面板上可见织纹、刚性甲板上的擦痕与细微划痕、小腿与靴上的灰尘。
