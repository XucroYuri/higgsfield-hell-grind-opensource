PROMPT — NanoBanana — SCPD EOD TECHNICIAN

@scpd_eod — Soul City Police Department EOD bomb disposal technician. Adult male, average build hidden under the suit. Standing in a confident wide stance, body squared toward camera, walking forward one step. Bare hands, no gauntlets — wrists and forearms exposed. Posture grounded, weight forward, head slightly tilted down so the visor faces camera.

FULL HEAVY EOD BOMB SUIT — dark charcoal grey throughout, with black armoured plates and black accents:

- HELMET: rounded military-style EOD helmet shell, dark charcoal grey, sitting low on the head. Large curved ballistic visor across the front, smoke-tinted at the top fading to clear in the centre, framed by a thick rubber gasket. Small ventilation grilles on both sides of the helmet shell. Two stubby tactical clip-mounts on the helmet crown. Bold white "SCPD" lettering stencilled across the front of the helmet, just above the visor. Black SCPD eagle decal on the right side of the helmet shell. Inside the visor: lower face wrapped in a black balaclava, only eyes visible.

- THROAT AND NECK COLLAR: massive flared protective collar wrapping around the neck and lower jaw, made of dark charcoal grey heavy quilted ballistic fabric. The collar splits into wide upturned petal-flaps that rise up around the helmet — one large flap on the front of the throat, two side flaps curving up toward the ears, one rear flap behind the neck. Black trim along every edge of every flap. The collar attaches at the shoulders and seals into the helmet's neck ring, leaving no gap at the throat.

- TORSO JACKET: long-sleeved dark charcoal grey heavy quilted ballistic jacket, oversized and padded. Visible horizontal stitching lines across the chest, shoulders, and back from quilting channels. Padded shoulder caps. Black reinforced patches on the elbows. Internal radio cable running from the right shoulder up into the helmet.

- CHEST ARMOUR PLATE: large rectangular black hard-shell chest plate strapped over the front of the jacket from sternum to lower abdomen, attached with thick black velcro and webbing straps. The plate has a recessed black molle panel in the centre, two vertical access pouches with black flaps, and small embroidered badges on the upper-right corner. Black SCPD eagle badge on the upper-left of the chest plate. White stencilled "SCPD EOD" across the back of the chest plate (visible from behind).

- WAIST: thick black tactical waist belt buckled at the front, low-mounted black utility pouch on the left hip, radio pouch on the right hip. Belt cinches the jacket at the waist.

- TROUSERS: dark charcoal grey heavy quilted ballistic trousers, baggy and oversized, padded throughout. Visible quilted stitching channels running vertically down the thighs. Black reinforced patches on the front of the thighs.

- KNEE PROTECTION: large black hard-shell knee plates strapped over the front of each knee, trapezoidal shape, secured with two horizontal black straps above and below each knee.

- SHIN GUARDS: long black hard-shell shin plates running from below the knee to the top of the foot, secured to the legs with two black straps wrapping around each calf, and black canvas overlays at the ankles. Quilted dark charcoal grey fabric still visible behind the shin plates.

- FOOTWEAR: heavy black EOD overboots — rounded toe-cap, thick rubberised black uppers covering the entire foot and ankle, no laces visible, low matte sheen. The overboots fit over the trousers and seal at the lower shin.

- HANDS: bare hands, exposed forearms below the rolled-back sleeves of the jacket, no gloves. Right hand relaxed at side, left hand slightly raised.

CAMERA: Full-body shot from feet to top of helmet, framed slightly low-angle (camera at chest height, looking marginally up) so the technician reads as imposing. Anamorphic 50mm-equivalent lens, mild barrel character, shallow depth of field, plain neutral seamless backdrop softly out of focus.

LIGHTING: Soft diffused key light from upper-right, gentle ambient fill from below, no direct sun, no hard shadows. Camera on the SHADOW side — left side of the suit holds soft falloff, right side catches gentle key. Helmet visor catches one soft rectangular highlight from the key.

PALETTE 60:30:10: 60% dark charcoal grey quilted suit, helmet shell, neck collar, jacket, trousers · 30% black chest plate, knee plates, shin plates, overboots, balaclava, belt and webbing · 10% white "SCPD" helmet stencil, white "SCPD EOD" back stencil, black SCPD eagle badge on chest

GRADING: cinematic photo-realism, desaturated charcoal-and-grey base, fine grain. Crisp armour textures — visible quilted stitching channels on the suit, weave of ballistic fabric, scuffs and micro-scratches on the hard plates, dust on the lower legs and overboots.

PROMPT ——NanoBanana——SCPD EOD 排爆专家

@scpd_eod ——Soul City 警局排爆（EOD）炸弹处理专家。成年男性，中等体型被排爆服遮盖。以一个自信的宽站姿站立，身体正向朝向摄影机，向前迈步一步。双手赤裸，无护腕——手腕与前臂外露。姿态沉稳，重心前倾，头微微下低，使面罩朝向摄影机。

全套重型排爆（EOD BOMB）服——周身深炭灰色，配黑色装甲板与黑色装饰：

- 头盔（HELMET）：圆润军用风格排爆头盔壳体，深炭灰色，低低地扣在头上。正面一块大型弧形防弹面罩，顶部烟色渐降为中央透明，由厚橡胶密封圈框住。头盔壳体两侧各有小通风格栅。头盔顶部两个短小战术夹具座。头盔正面、面罩正上方丝印醒目的白色「SCPD」字样。头盔壳体右侧黑色 SCPD 鹰徽贴标。面罩内侧：下脸以黑色巴拉克拉瓦头套包裹，仅露双眼。

- 咽喉与护颈领（THROAT AND NECK COLLAR）：宽大喇叭状护颈领，环抱颈部与下颌，由深炭灰重型绗缝防弹布料制成。护颈领裂分为宽大的上翻瓣状护片，上抬环绕头盔——喉部正面一大片，两侧护片弯向上抵向双耳，颈后一片。每片护片的每条边缘都有黑色饰边。护颈领固定于肩部，嵌入头盔颈部密封圈，咽喉处不留缝隙。

- 躯干外套（TORSO JACKET）：深炭灰重型绗缝防弹长袖外套，不合身、敦实厚重。绗缝槽使胸口、肩膀与背部可见横向明线走线。加衬的肩垫。肘部黑色加固补丁。内置电台线从右肩蜿蜒向上接入头盔。

- 胸甲板（CHEST ARMOUR PLATE）：大型矩形黑色硬壳胸甲板，自胸骨至下腹束于外套正面，以厚黑尼龙搭扣（velcro）与织带束紧。甲板中央有内凹黑色 molle 面板，两个带黑色盖板的竖向收纳袋，右上角小型刺绣徽章。胸甲板左上方黑色 SCPD 鹰徽。胸甲板背面横贯白色丝印「SCPD EOD」（从背后可见）。

- 腰部（WAIST）：正面束扣的厚黑战术腰带，左胯低位黑色多功能工具袋，右胯电台袋。腰带在外套腰间将其收紧。

- 长裤（TROUSERS）：深炭灰重型绗缝防弹裤，宽松肥大、通体加衬。可见绗缝明线走线沿大腿垂向而下。大腿正面黑色加固补丁。

- 护膝（KNEE PROTECTION）：每侧膝前束带的大型黑色硬壳护膝板，梯形轮廓，膝上膝下各以两道横向黑色束带固定。

- 护胫（SHIN GUARDS）：自膝下延伸至脚掌的长度护胫硬壳板，以绕小腿的两道黑色束带固定躯干，脚踝处黑色帆布覆盖层。护胫板后方仍可见深炭灰绗缝布料。

- 鞋靴（FOOTWEAR）：重型黑色排爆外套靴——圆头鞋尖，厚橡胶化黑色鞋面覆盖整足与脚踝，不见鞋带，低哑光光泽。外套靴套于长裤之外，在小腿下部密封。

- 双手（HANDS）：双手赤裸，外套外翻袖口下方前臂外露，不戴手套。右手松弛垂于身侧，左手略抬。

摄影机（CAMERA）：自脚至头盔顶的全身镜头，略带低角度取景（摄影机位于胸部高度，略微仰拍），使专家显得气势慑人。等效变形宽银幕 50mm 镜头，轻微桶形畸变特性，浅景深，纯中性无缝背景柔和失焦。

布光（LIGHTING）：右上柔和的漫射主光，下方柔和环境补光，无直射太阳，无硬阴影。摄影机位于阴影侧——排爆服左侧持柔和衰减，右侧承接柔和主光。头盔面罩承接主光的一处柔和矩形高光。

调色 60:30:10：60% 深炭灰绗缝排爆服、头盔壳体、护颈领、外套、长裤 · 30% 黑色胸甲板、护膝、护胫、外套靴、头套、腰带与织带 · 10% 白色「SCPD」头盔丝印、白色「SCPD EOD」背部丝印、胸前黑色 SCPD 鹰徽

调色处理（GRADING）：电影级照片写实，去饱和的炭灰基调，细腻颗粒。明晰的装甲质感——排爆服上可见的绗缝明线、防弹布料织纹、硬壳板上的擦痕与细微划痕、小腿与外套靴上的灰尘。
