Duration: 7 seconds. Aspect ratio: 21:9. Two timed shots. R2V mode.

Use Image 1 as the location reference: a police operations centre with a curved bank of large wall monitors. Use Image 2 <<<image_2>>> as the dispatcher identity and costume reference. Use Image 3 <<<image_1>>> as the background officers identity and costume reference.

Location lock: A police operations centre. The far wall has a long curved bank of two rows of wall-mounted monitors displaying maps with red markers, CCTV feeds and aerial imagery. The wall monitors sit at standing eye level, the centre of each screen about 1.5 metres from the floor. A row of operator stations with mesh office chairs sits in front of the monitor wall. Each station has its own desk monitors at sitting eye level. Vintage brass desk lamps with green shades stand on every station. To the right of the room there is a window with horizontal blinds and a vintage desk lamp on a side desk. Wall has framed sheriff plaques. Polished light grey floor. Cool desaturated blue-grey palette with warm tungsten accents from desk lamps. Low-key practical lighting.

Character lock for the dispatcher: A young female SCPD police officer matching Image 2. Dark brown hair pulled into a tight low bun. Slim build. Grey SCPD uniform shirt with an SCPD patch on the chest pocket and shoulder. Body camera or microphone on her chest. Dark utility belt. Black headset with boom microphone over her right ear. Her face shows anxious focus — brow slightly furrowed, jaw tight, lips pressed together between phrases. She is professional but the weight of the situation is visible on her face. She knows it is real and dangerous.

Background officers lock: Five SCPD officers from Image 3 are visible in Shot 1 across the room. They wear matching grey SCPD uniforms. Two officers sit at neighbouring operator stations working at their keyboards. One officer walks slowly through the deep midground from screen-left to screen-right carrying a folder. Another officer walks slowly through the background from screen-right to screen-left holding a coffee mug. One officer stands in the deep background in front of the wall monitors, looking at the map.

Monitor reflections lock: The dispatcher's desk monitors reflect cool blue-white glow onto her face, her cheekbone, the side of her neck and her headset. The polished desk surface reflects the maps as soft warped highlights. The wall monitors behind the room cast scattered blue-white shimmer onto the polished floor. In Shot 2, soft reflected blue-white light from the monitors plays on her right cheek and on the boom microphone of her headset.

Shot 1, 0.0 to 2.0 seconds — Medium wide on the dispatcher and the room:
The dispatcher sits at a desk in the centre of the frame, framed from behind and slightly to her right side. We see the back of her head, her right shoulder and her right ear with the headset. Her low bun is visible. Her shoulders are slightly hunched in tension. Three monitors stand on her desk in front of her at her eye level. The central monitor shows a city map. A red rectangular tag on the map reads "PASSADE MALL". A red map pin marks the location. A vintage brass desk lamp on her left throws warm tungsten light onto the desk. The room is alive — two officers sit at neighbouring stations working, one officer walks slowly through the midground left-to-right carrying a folder, another walks right-to-left in the deep background holding a coffee mug, one officer stands in front of the wall monitors. Cool blue-white glow from the monitors plays on the polished grey floor and on the chairs. 50mm lens, mild compression. Handheld camera with gentle eight-centimetre slow push-in toward the dispatcher's desk plus four-centimetre lateral drift to camera-right across the shot.

Shot 2, 2.0 to 7.0 seconds — Extreme close-up on the dispatcher speaking the warning:
Extreme close-up on the dispatcher's face from a three-quarter angle on her right side. Framed tight from her chin to her cheekbone. Her lips, jaw, neck and the boom microphone fill the frame. Her dark uniform collar visible at the bottom of frame. Background fully in bokeh with shimmering reflected light from the monitors. Soft reflected blue-white glow on her right cheek and on the boom microphone.
Her face carries anxious seriousness — jaw tight, lips pressing together between phrases, faint quickening of breath. Her voice is low, controlled, weighted with the seriousness of what she is reporting.
At 2.4 seconds she begins to speak into the headset with anxious controlled urgency. She fully delivers the line, pressing her lips tight before key words. She speaks: "All units, Possible bomb at Passade Mall. Suspects armed. Respond code three. Move."
Brief sharp inhale before the word "bomb". Tight jaw on "code three". Final word "Move." spoken quieter, with weight.
135mm telephoto lens. Very shallow depth of field. Handheld camera with gentle four-centimetre lateral drift to camera-right plus a slow three-centimetre push-in toward her lips across the shot. Only her lips, jaw, breathing and the boom microphone move.

Aesthetics: ARRI ALEXA, 35mm film grain, anamorphic 21:9 letterbox, low-key cinematic lighting with warm tungsten practicals from desk lamps and cool blue-white ambient from monitors. Reflections from monitors are visible on her face, her headset, the polished desk and the floor. Shallow depth of field on the ECU.

Continuity: The dispatcher's identity, hair, uniform and headset stay identical across both shots. Her anxious serious expression stays consistent. The room layout stays consistent. Background officers stay in their motion paths without entering the foreground or crossing the dispatcher.

SFX: Shot 1 quiet room tone, soft keyboard typing, distant radio chatter, low hum of monitors, footsteps of the walking officers on the polished floor, soft murmured voices. Shot 2 her clear low voice through the headset weighted with anxiety, faint radio crackle behind her line, sharp inhale before "bomb", distant office hum. No music. No subtitles.

Final frame: Shot 2 at 7.0 seconds — ECU on her mouth just after the final word "Move", lips pressed tight, jaw still locked, reflected blue-white glow on her cheek.

## 中文对照 (zh)

时长：7 秒。宽高比：21:9。两段定时镜头。R2V 模式。

以 Image 1 作为地点参考：一个警察调度中心，内有一整排弧形的大型墙面显示器。以 Image 2 <<<image_2>>> 作为调度员的身份与服装参考。以 Image 3 <<<image_1>>> 作为背景警员的身份与服装参考。

地点锁定：一个警察调度中心。远端墙面有一整排弧形、两行排列的壁挂显示器，显示带红色标记的地图、闭路电视画面与航拍影像。墙面显示器位于站立平视高度，每块屏幕中心距地面约 1.5 米。显示器墙前是一排带网面办公椅的操作工位。每个工位都有自己的桌面显示器，位于坐姿平视高度。每个工位上都有一盏复古黄铜绿罩台灯。房间右侧有一扇带横向百叶的窗户，侧桌上放着一盏复古台灯。墙面挂着镶框的治安官徽牌。抛光浅灰色地面。冷调低饱和蓝灰色调，配以台灯带来的暖调白炽光点缀。低调的实用光源布光。

调度员角色锁定：一位年轻女性，SCPD 警员，与 Image 2 一致。深棕色头发挽成紧贴的低发髻。身材纤瘦。灰色 SCPD 制服衬衫，胸前口袋与肩部有 SCPD 臂章。胸口佩戴执法记录仪或麦克风。深色武装腰带。黑色头戴式耳机，右侧耳边带有吊杆麦克风。她的面容带着焦虑的专注——眉头微蹙、下颌绷紧、说话间隙双唇抿紧。她专业干练，但局势的重量清楚地显现在她脸上。她明白这是真实的、危险的。

背景警员锁定：镜头 1 中可见 Image 3 中的五名 SCPD 警员，散布在房间里。他们穿着同款灰色 SCPD 制服。两名警员坐在相邻工位操作键盘。一名警员手拿文件夹，从画面左向右缓慢穿过深处中景。另一名警员手持咖啡杯，从画面右向左缓慢穿过背景。一名警员站在远景里显示器墙前方，看着地图。

显示器反射锁定：调度员的桌面显示器将冷调蓝白光辉映在她的面颊、颧骨、脖子侧与耳机上。抛光桌面将地图的柔和扭曲高光反射出来。房间后方的墙面显示器在抛光地面上投下散落的蓝白微光。在镜头 2 中，显示器发出的柔和反射蓝白光落在她右侧脸颊与耳机吊杆麦克风上。

镜头 1，0.0 至 2.0 秒——调度员与房间的中宽景：
调度员坐在画面中央的一桌工位，从背后偏右侧取景。我们看见她的后脑、右肩以及带耳机的右耳。她的低发髻清晰可见。她的双肩因紧张而微微佝偻。她面前桌上有三台显示器，与她平视等高。中央显示器显示一张城市地图。地图上一个红色长方形标签写着「PASSADE MALL」。一个红色地图图钉标出该位置。她左侧一盏复古黄铜台灯将暖调白炽光洒在桌上。房间充满动感——两名警员坐在相邻工位操作，一名警员手拿文件夹缓慢穿过中景自左向右行走，另一名手持咖啡杯在远景深处自右向左行走，一名警员站在墙面显示器前。显示器的冷调蓝白光辉洒在抛光灰色地面与座椅上。50mm 镜头，轻微压缩。手持摄影机以八厘米的缓慢推近缓缓逼近调度员的工位，并辅以四厘米向右横向漂移贯穿画面。

镜头 2，2.0 至 7.0 秒——调度员念出警告的极特写：
以她右侧的四分之三角度对调度员面部极特写。取景紧绷，从下颌到颧骨。她的嘴唇、下颌、脖颈与吊杆麦克风充满画面。画面底部可见她深色制服领口。背景完全虚化为焦外（bokeh），带有显示器反射光的闪烁微光。柔和反射的蓝白光落在她右脸颊与吊杆麦克风上。
她的面容带着焦虑的严肃——下颌绷紧、说话间隙双唇抿紧、呼吸微微加快。她的嗓音低沉、克制，承载着所通报之事的严峻分量。
在 2.4 秒时她开始以紧张而克制的急切对着耳机说话。她完整念出台词，在关键词语前用力抿紧双唇。她说：「各单位注意，Passade 购物中心可能有炸弹。嫌疑人携带武器。以三号代码响应。行动。」
说出「bomb（炸弹）」一词前有一次短暂急促的吸气。说到「code three（三号代码）」时下颌绷紧。最后一词「Move（行动）」说得更轻，带着分量。
135mm 长焦镜头。极浅景深。手持摄影机以四厘米向右横向漂移，加三厘米缓慢推近朝向她的嘴唇贯穿画面。只有她的嘴唇、下颌、呼吸与吊杆麦克风在动。

美学：ARRI ALEXA，35mm 胶片颗粒，变形宽银幕 21:9 上下黑边（letterbox），低调电影布光，带台灯暖调白炽光实用光源与显示器冷调蓝白环境光。她面部、耳机、抛光桌面与地面都能看到显示器的反射。极特写（ECU）采用浅景深。

连续性：调度员在两个镜头间的身份、头发、制服与耳机保持一致。她焦虑严肃的表情保持连贯。房间布局保持一致。背景警员保持在各自的运动路径上，不进入前景，也不越过调度员。

SFX（音效）：镜头 1 为安静的室内环境音、柔和键盘打字声、远处电台对讲声、显示器低鸣、走动的警员在抛光地板上的脚步声、柔和的低声交谈。镜头 2 是她透过耳机传来的低沉清晰、承载着焦虑的声音，台词背后微弱的电台杂音、说出「bomb（炸弹）」前的一次急促吸气、远处办公室的嗡鸣。无音乐。无字幕。

收尾帧：镜头 2 的第 7.0 秒——刚说完最后一词「Move（行动）」后她嘴部的极特写（ECU），嘴唇用力抿紧、下颌仍紧锁、脸颊上反射着蓝白光辉。
