## 原文 (source)

Multi-shot sequence connected by hard cuts, 12 seconds total. Three 
internal shots separated by clean hard cuts (no fade, no dissolve).

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY — APPLIES TO EVERY INTERNAL SHOT]

FORMAT: Anamorphic 2.39:1 aspect ratio. 35mm film grain throughout. 
Shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic lenses. Horizontal anamorphic lens 
flares from candle flames.

COLOR PALETTE: Cool desaturated base — deep blue-blacks, charcoal 
grays, slate stone tones. Punctuated by warm orange candle pools at 
floor level. Dust beige on gear and skin. Color temperature mix: 
~5500K cool blue ambient from above, ~2700K warm candle pools from 
below. NO bright saturated colors except the small red bandana on 
Roco's wrist, red nose bandage, red bear keychain.

LIGHTING: Single dominant cool blue shaft from a high opening in 
cave ceiling. Hundreds of small clay oil lamp candles glowing warm 
orange at column bases. High-contrast chiaroscuro. Heavy mist and 
dust particles catching light. Deep negative space.

CAMERA: Organic handheld throughout. Always alive — operator breathing, 
slight bounces, micro-sways. NEVER static, NEVER smooth gimbal, NEVER 
stabilized. The camera is a witness in the temple, present and 
breathing.

LOCATION: Massive ancient cave-temple. Monolithic stone columns 
rising on both sides into darkness. Intricate Hindu-Buddhist carvings 
on weathered stone walls. Heavy mist in air. Stone floor with cracked 
weathered slabs, scattered ancient prayer beads and broken stone 
fragments. The colossal seated guardian statue not yet revealed in 
this prompt — only mist and depth at the far end.

CHARACTERS:
- ROCO (CENTER): messy shaggy black hair, faint mustache, olive skin, 
  small red adhesive bandage strip on nose, dust on face, intense 
  brown eyes. Dark weathered tactical jacket with shevron panels 
  covered in dust, dark cargo pants, dark boots, red bandana tied at 
  right wrist, fingerless tactical gloves. Small red bear keychain 
  hanging from back belt loop. Lean athletic build.
- JAXX (RIGHT): short bleached blonde hair, dark skin, clear safety 
  goggles pushed up on forehead, small healing scar on right temple. 
  Dark tactical jacket with gray paneling, olive cargo pants, dark 
  boots, dark fingerless tactical gloves. Lean athletic build.
- REIN (LEFT): short silver-and-black two-toned bobbed hair, scar on 
  right cheekbone. Dark gray weathered tactical jacket with multiple 
  chest pockets, black turtleneck, dark cargo pants, tan tactical 
  thigh holster on right leg. Slim athletic build.

All three covered in fine gray dust from travel. Their gear shows 
wear, scuff marks, weathering.

REFERENCES: In the style of Denis Villeneuve and Greig Fraser ("Dune"), 
Roger Deakins' chiaroscuro. Real cinematic weight, NOT CGI-fantasy, 
NOT clean digital, NOT video-game-render.

## 中文对照 (zh)

多镜头序列由硬切（hard cuts）连接，总时长 12 秒。三个内部镜头以干净的硬切分隔（无淡入淡出，无溶接）。

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY — APPLIES TO EVERY INTERNAL SHOT]（视觉一致性——适用于每个内部镜头）

FORMAT（格式）：变形宽银幕 2.39:1 宽高比。全程 35mm 胶片颗粒。以 Arri Alexa 配变形宽银幕镜头拍摄。来自蜡烛火焰的水平向变形宽银幕镜头光斑（anamorphic lens flares）。

COLOR PALETTE（色彩基调）：冷调去饱和基底——深蓝黑、炭灰、板岩灰调。由地面层暖橙烛光池点缀。装备与皮肤上覆尘埃米黄。色温混杂：上方约 5500K 冷蓝环境光、下方约 2700K 暖烛光池。除 ROCO 手腕的红色小头巾、红色鼻贴、红色小熊钥匙扣外，无任何明亮的饱和色。

LIGHTING（灯光）：单一主导的冷蓝光柱自洞窟天花板一处高开口垂下。数千盏小陶土油灯蜡烛在柱基燃着暖橙光。高反差明暗对比（chiaroscuro）。浓雾与抓光的微尘粒子。深邃的负空间。

CAMERA（摄影机）：全程有机手持。始终保持「活」——操机员呼吸、轻微弹跳、细微摇动。绝不停滞，绝不用平滑云台，绝无稳定化。摄影机是庙中的一位见证者，在场而呼吸着。

LOCATION（场景地点）：巨型古老洞窟-庙宇。整石巨柱两侧升入黑暗。风化石墙上繁复的印度-佛教雕饰。空气中浓雾。石板地面带裂纹风化的板面，散落古老的念珠与破碎的石块。这位巨硕坐姿守护者雕像本段中尚不揭示——远处尽头只有雾与纵深感。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
- ROCO（中央）：凌乱蓬松的黑发、淡胡须、橄榄色皮肤、鼻梁一条小型红色创可贴、脸上带灰、专注的棕色眼睛。深色风化的战术夹克带雪佛龙面板、蒙满灰尘，深色工装裤，深色靴子，右手腕系红色头巾，露指战术手套。后腰带环挂着红色小熊钥匙扣。瘦削运动体型。
- JAXX（右）：短漂白金发、深色皮肤、透明护目镜推到额头、右太阳穴一道愈合的小疤。深色战术夹克带灰色面板、橄榄色工装裤、深色靴子、深色露指战术手套。瘦削运动体型。
- REIN（左）：短银黑双色波波头、右颧骨有道疤。深灰色风化的战术夹克带多个胸前口袋、黑色高领衫、深色工装裤、右腿米色战术大腿枪套。瘦削运动体型。

三人身上都覆着旅途沾染的细灰。他们的装备显露出磨损、擦痕、风化。

REFERENCES（参考）：丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦与格雷格·弗莱瑟（〈沙丘〉）、罗杰·戴金斯明暗对比风格。真实的电影质感，非 CG 奇幻、非干净数字、非游戏渲染。
