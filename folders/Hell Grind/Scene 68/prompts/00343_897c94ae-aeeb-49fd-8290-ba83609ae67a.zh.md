## 原文 (source)

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY — APPLIES TO EVERY SHOT IN THIS SCENE]

FORMAT: Anamorphic 2.39:1 aspect ratio. 35mm film grain throughout.
Shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic lenses. Horizontal anamorphic lens
flares from any bright light sources.

COLOR PALETTE: Cool desaturated base — deep blue-blacks, charcoal
grays, slate stone tones. Punctuated by warm orange candle light at
floor level. Dust beige and dirty muted earth tones on characters'
gear and skin. NO bright saturated colors except: red bandana on
Roco's wrist, red nose bandage on Roco, occasional red bear keychain.
Once the statue activates — deep blood-red glowing lines emerge as
the only saturated color. Color temperature mix: ~5500K cool blue
ambient from above, ~2700K warm candle pools from below.

LIGHTING SIGNATURE: Single dominant cool blue shaft from above
(skylight from cave opening), countless small warm candle pools at
floor level along columns. High-contrast chiaroscuro. Heavy mist and
dust particles in air catching light. Deep negative space — most of
frame falls into darkness, faces are sculpted by directional light.

GRAIN AND TEXTURE: Pronounced 35mm film grain, slight softness in
shadows. Subtle film halation around bright candles. Slight gate
weave for organic feel. NOT digital, NOT clean.

CAMERA LANGUAGE: Organic handheld throughout the entire scene. Always
alive — operator breathing, slight bounces from steps, micro-sways.
NEVER static dead camera, NEVER smooth gimbal, NEVER stabilized. The
camera is a witness inside the temple, present and breathing with
the characters.

LOCATION CONSISTENCY: Massive ancient cave-temple. Monolithic stone
columns rising on both sides into darkness. Intricate Hindu-Buddhist
carvings on weathered stone. Heavy mist hanging in air. Stone floor
with cracked weathered slabs, scattered ancient prayer beads and
broken stone fragments. Single shaft of blue light from a high
opening in the cave ceiling. Hundreds of small clay oil lamp candles
flickering at column bases. Deep in the temple — the colossal seated
guardian statue holding a small cross-legged monk on its open palms.

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY:
- ROCO: messy shaggy black hair, faint mustache, olive skin, small
 red adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose, dust on
 face. Dark weathered tactical jacket with shevron panels covered in
 dust, dark cargo pants, dark boots, red bandana tied at right wrist,
 fingerless tactical gloves. Small red bear keychain hanging from
 back belt loop. Build: lean, athletic.
- JAXX: short bleached blonde hair, dark skin, clear safety goggles
 pushed up on forehead (or pulled down over eyes during action),
 small healing scar on right temple. Dark tactical jacket with gray
 paneling, olive cargo pants, dark boots, dark fingerless tactical
 gloves. Build: lean, athletic.
- REIN: short silver-and-black two-toned bobbed hair, scar on right
 cheekbone, faint scar on right brow. Dark gray weathered tactical
 jacket with multiple chest pockets, black turtleneck, dark cargo
 pants, tan tactical thigh holster on right leg. Build: slim,
 athletic.

All three covered in fine gray dust from travel. Their gear shows
wear, scuff marks, weathering. They look like a tested team.

REFERENCES: Cinematography in the style of Denis Villeneuve and
Greig Fraser ("Dune"), Roger Deakins' use of negative space and
chiaroscuro, the sacred-scale framing of "Blade Runner 2049" temple
sequences. NOT video-game-render, NOT CGI-fantasy, NOT clean. Real
cinematic weight.

NEGATIVE BLOCK (apply across all shots):
no music, no score, no instruments, no smooth gimbal, no static dead
camera, no stabilization, no clean digital look, no aerial drone
shots, no fade between shots, no dissolve, no soft transitions, no

no daylight, no removal of Roco's red nose bandage, no Jaxx without
his goggles available, no Rein without scar, no facial hair drift
on Roco (mustache stays consistent), no character identity drift,

yet (until later acts), no statue movement yet, no glowing lines on
statue yet (statue dormant in act 1), no weapons drawn yet, no
combat yet, no transformation yet, no text, no captions, no logos.

## 中文对照 (zh)

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY — APPLIES TO EVERY SHOT IN THIS SCENE（视觉一致性——适用于本场每一个镜头）]

FORMAT（画幅）：变形宽银幕 2.39:1 宽高比。全程 35mm 胶片颗粒。以 Arri Alexa 配变形宽银幕镜头拍摄。任何明亮光源都会产生横向变形镜头耀斑。

COLOR PALETTE（调色板）：冷去饱和基调——深蓝黑、炭灰、石板色调。以地面层的暖橙色烛光点缀其间。角色装备与皮肤上呈灰尘米色与脏兮兮的柔和土色。绝无明亮饱和的色彩，除非：Roco 腕上的红色头巾、Roco 鼻上的红色创可贴、偶尔的红色小熊钥匙扣。一旦雕像激活——深血色发光线条浮现，成为唯一的饱和色彩。色温混合：上方约 5500K 冷蓝环境光，下方约 2700K 暖烛光池。

LIGHTING SIGNATURE（灯光印记）：自上方射下的一束主导冷蓝色光柱（来自洞穴开口的天窗）、地面层沿石柱无数小小的暖烛光池。高反差明暗对照（chiaroscuro）。空气中浓重的雾气与灰尘微粒捕捉光线。深层的负空间——画面大半落入黑暗之中，面部由指向性光线雕凿成形。

GRAIN AND TEXTURE（颗粒与质感）：显著的 35mm 胶片颗粒，阴影中略带柔和。明亮蜡烛周围有细微的胶片光晕（halation）。略有片门游动（gate weave）以营造有机感。绝非数字感、绝非干净。

CAMERA LANGUAGE（摄影机语言）：整个场景全程有机手持。始终鲜活——摄影师呼吸、脚步带来的轻微起伏、细微摇摆。绝不静止呆滞的摄影机、绝不平滑云台、绝不稳定化。摄影机是神庙内的一个目击者，与角色们一同在场、一同呼吸。

LOCATION CONSISTENCY（地点一致性）：巨大的古老洞穴神庙。整块巨石石柱在两侧向黑暗升起。风化石材上有繁复的印度教—佛教雕刻。空气中悬着浓重的雾气。石地面带开裂风化的石板、散落的古老念珠与破碎的石片。洞穴天花板上高高的开口射下一束蓝光。数百盏小小的陶土油灯蜡烛在柱脚闪烁。神庙深处——巨大的坐姿守护者雕像，在它张开的掌心上托着一位盘腿的小僧侣。

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY（角色一致性）：
- ROCO：凌乱蓬乱的黑发、淡淡的胡须、橄榄色皮肤、鼻梁上横贴一条小小的红色胶布创可贴、脸上沾尘。深色风化的战术夹克带人字形（shevron）面板、覆满灰尘，深色工装裤、深色靴子、右手腕系着红色头巾、无指战术手套。后腰带上挂着一个小红色小熊钥匙扣。体形：精瘦、矫健。
- JAXX：短漂白金发、深色皮肤、前额推着透明护目镜（或动作时拉下遮住眼睛）、右太阳穴有一道正在愈合的小疤痕。深色战术夹克带灰色面板、橄榄绿工装裤、深色靴子、深色无指战术手套。体形：精瘦、矫健。
- REIN：短银黑双色波波头短发、右颧骨有一道疤痕、右眉有一道隐约的疤痕。深灰色风化的战术夹克带多个胸前口袋、黑色高领毛衣、深色工装裤、右腿上套着棕褐色战术大腿枪套。体形：纤细、矫健。

三人因赶路而覆满细腻的灰色灰尘。他们的装备显出磨损、擦痕、风化。他们看起来像一支久经考验的队伍。

REFERENCES（参考）：摄影风格参照丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦与格雷格·弗雷泽（"沙丘" Dune）、罗杰·狄金斯对负空间与明暗对照（chiaroscuro）的运用、《银翼杀手2049》神庙段落的圣尺度取景。绝非电子游戏渲染、绝非 CG 奇幻、绝非干净。要有真实的电影质感。
