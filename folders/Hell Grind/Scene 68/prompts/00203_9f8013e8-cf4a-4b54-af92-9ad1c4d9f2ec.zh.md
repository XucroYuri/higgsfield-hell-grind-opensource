Ancient sanctum location b-roll, three cuts, 12 seconds total, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera. No 3D render, no game engine. References: Emmanuel Lubezki, Roger Deakins. Lighting: cold pale ambient from natural rock fissure above as dominant key, warm amber oil-lamps along columns as practicals, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets as distant secondary wash. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant deep cool blue-grey / secondary saturated red / accent warm amber. Heavily desaturated. Physical anamorphic cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. Corner vignette, edge chromatic aberration. 60fps, 8K. Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music, no subtitles.

WORLD: vast cavern-sanctum carved from natural rock. Towering carved stone columns with ancient carved script along their surfaces, warm amber oil-lamps mounted on columns, irregular flagstone floor, atmospheric haze patchy. High in side wall — sacred carved stone ledge with single candle, elderly monk in dark maroon robes seated in meditation, eyes closed, mouth open in sustained low guttural throat-tone.

Cut A (0-4s): Three-quarter handheld slow lateral drift along carved stone column surface, 100mm macro, f/1.4. Camera drifting slowly laterally past the column — the ancient carved stone surface passing through razor-thin focus, deep relief sculpture visible, ancient carved script running vertically along the shaft, moss growing in the carved letter crevices, dust accumulation across the stone surface. Warm amber oil-lamp mounted on the column visible at frame edge with halation bloom casting warm accent. Cold pale ambient from above catching stone edges in contre-jour cold rim. Dust particles drifting slowly through the lamp beam visible. Frame trembles with operator's slow walking motion. Audio: dust hissing softly past the column surface, lamp flame flickering subtly in atmospheric draft, deep ambient cavern drone, monk's distant throat-tone faint.

Cut B (4-8s): Three-quarter handheld slow tilt upward from flagstone floor to ceiling, 35mm, f/2. Camera beginning at floor level on the irregular flagstone — the ancient stone floor in razor-thin focus with real-world weathering, cracks, moss between flagstone joints, debris settled. Camera operator tilts slowly upward — the frame traveling from floor through columns rising on either side, through the atmospheric haze, up toward the high irregular cavern ceiling lost in shadow above. Cold pale daylight shaft visible cutting down from the natural rock fissure above, dust particles in the beam catching the light as they drift slowly downward. The scale of the vertical space revealed through the tilt. Audio: ambient cavern drone, deep low resonance of the space, atmospheric haze hissing softly.

Cut C (8-12s): Three-quarter handheld close-up on the monk's ledge from below looking up, 85mm, f/1.4. Camera looking up at the high sacred carved stone ledge on the side wall far above — the ledge visible at height with the single warm amber candle flame burning in a small stone bowl, the elderly monk in dark maroon robes seated cross-legged in meditation, eyes closed, mouth open in sustained low guttural throat-tone, motionless. The rock overhang above sheltering him. The vast dark space of the chamber below visible in shallow blur behind him. Cold pale ambient catching the ledge in contre-jour rim, warm candle casting warm amber light across the monk's face from below. Frame trembles handheld. Audio: the monk's sustained low guttural throat-tone resonating from the elevated position, candle flame barely audible, deep ambient cavern drone.

## 中文对照 (zh)

古代圣地（sanctum）场景 b-roll（穿插镜头），三个分镜，总长 12 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX。写实摄影，以实体电影摄影机拍摄。非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎。参考：Emmanuel Lubezki、Roger Deakins。Lighting（灯光）：上方天然岩石裂隙渗下的冷调苍白环境光为主光，沿柱的暖琥珀色油灯为布景灯（practicals），守护者眼眶溢出的饱和红光为远处次级光洗。Color（色彩）：60:30:10——主色调深冷蓝灰 / 次色调饱和红 / 点缀暖琥珀。重度去饱和。实体变形宽银幕电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。角落暗角、边缘色差。60fps，8K。Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（SFX only）。无音乐、无字幕。

WORLD（世界）：由天然岩石凿成的巨大洞窟圣地（cavern-sanctum）。高耸的石雕柱沿表面刻满古代凿刻文字，柱上装着暖琥珀色油灯，不规则石板（flagstone）地面，大气雾感分布不均。侧墙高处——一块神圣的石雕搁台，上有一支蜡烛，一位身着深栗色僧袍的老僧盘坐冥想，双目闭合，嘴微张，持续发出低沉喉音诵唱（throat-tone）。

Cut A（0-4s）：三分之三手持缓慢横向漂移，沿石雕柱表面移动，100mm 微距，f/1.4。摄影机缓慢横向漂移掠过石柱——古代石雕表面穿过极薄对焦（razor-thin focus），深浮雕清晰可见，古代凿刻文字沿柱身纵向延伸，雕刻字缝里长着苔藓，石面堆积着灰尘。柱上安装的暖琥珀色油灯在画幅边缘可见，光晕（halation）漫射投射出暖色点缀。上方冷调苍白环境光以逆光背光（contre-jour）冷色边缘光（rim）描出石边。灯束中可见细尘缓缓飘动。画面随操作员缓慢步行动作而颤动。Audio（音频）：细尘轻擦柱面咝咝作响，灯焰在大气气流中微微闪烁，深邃的环境洞窟嗡鸣，僧侣远处微弱的喉音。

Cut B（4-8s）：三分之三手持缓慢仰摇，从石板地面摇向天花板，35mm，f/2。摄影机从地面层的凹凸石板上起——古老石地面呈极薄对焦，带着真实的自然风化、裂缝、石板接缝间的苔藓、沉降的碎屑。操作员缓缓向上摇——画面从地面穿行于两侧升起的立柱之间，穿过大气雾感，朝上进入隐没于上方阴影的崎岖洞穴高顶。上方自然岩缝里可见一束冷调白昼光切下，光束中细尘缓缓下坠时被光点亮。仰摇揭示出这片垂直空间的尺度。Audio（音频）：环境洞窟嗡鸣，空间深邃的低频共鸣，大气雾感轻轻咝响。

Cut C（8-12s）：三分之三手持特写，从下方仰视僧侣的搁台，85mm，f/1.4。摄影机仰视侧墙高处那座神圣石雕搁台——搁台在高处可见，上一只在小小的石碗里燃烧的暖琥珀色烛焰，老僧身穿深栗色僧袍盘腿坐禅，双目闭合，嘴微张，持续发出低沉喉音诵唱，纹丝不动。上方岩檐为他遮护。他身后下方巨大的黑暗洞室空间隐没在浅景深虚化里。冷调苍白环境光以逆光背光（contre-jour）边缘光勾出搁台，暖烛从下方把暖琥珀色光投过僧侣面庞。画面手持颤动。Audio（音频）：僧侣那从高处回响的低沉喉音诵唱，几乎听不见的烛焰轻响，深邃环境洞窟嗡鸣。
