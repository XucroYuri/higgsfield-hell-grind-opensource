## 原文 (source)

Multi-shot sequence connected by hard cuts, 12 seconds total,
anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, shallow-to-medium depth of field,
in the style of Denis Villeneuve's "Dune" — sacred scale and intimate
observation. Three internal shots separated by clean hard cuts.

[CHARACTERS — CONSISTENT ACROSS ALL SHOTS]
<<<image_1>>> - ROCO: messy shaggy black hair, faint mustache, dust on face, small
 red adhesive bandage strip on nose, dark weathered tactical jacket
 with shevron panels, dark cargo pants, red bandana on right wrist,
 fingerless gloves, red bear keychain on back belt.
 <<<image_2>>> - JAXX: bleached blonde hair, dark skin, clear safety goggles on
 forehead, dark tactical jacket with gray panels, olive cargo pants,
 fingerless gloves.
<<<image_3>>> - REIN: silver-and-black bobbed hair, scar on right cheekbone, dark
 gray cropped tactical jacket, black turtleneck, dark wide cargo
 pants, thigh holster.
All dusty.

[LOCATION — ALL SHOTS]
<<<image_4>>> Cave-temple interior, stone columns and carved walls visible, mist
in the air, candles flickering at column bases, dust on the floor.

SHOT A — REIN GRABS THE SKATEBOARD — 0.0s to 3.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
⚠️ CAMERA OPERATION — HEAVY HANDHELD, BODY-PRESENT, OPERATOR CHASING: the camera is clearly carried on a human body — operator's gait, breath, weight transfer all visible in the frame's micro-motion. Frame is ALWAYS ALIVE in the operator's hands, never locked off, never on a tripod, never on a gimbal-smooth float. Reference operation: SAVING PRIVATE RYAN battlefield, CHILDREN OF MEN one-takes, THE REVENANT chase sequences, ROGUE ONE corridor handheld. Operator MOVES with the action — runs when the actor runs (matched gait, frame trembling on each footfall), drops when the actor drops, holds his weight low when the action goes to ground. Constant subtle reframing as he tracks the performer. Every frame has visible handheld signature: small unpredictable jitters, drift correction, weight settling. Camera operation is RAW and PHYSICAL — never aerial, never sweeping crane, never stabilized.

[LIGHT]
Cool blue ambient from above mixed with warm orange candle pools at
floor level, deep shadows, dust particles drifting in mixed light.
LOW-KEY HIGH CONTRAST — crushed deep blacks dominate the corridor, only the torch-pools and the wet floor reflections carry highlight detail. Faces sit largely in shadow with only flickering rim from torch passes. Atmospheric perspective — the corridor's depth dissolves into smoky torch-haze.

Composition: rule of thirds + golden ratio. The descending slab anchors the deep frame as a vertical / dropping graphic block, the gap underneath as the dramatic focal aperture.

⚠️ COLOR PALETTE — INHERITED FROM REFERENCE: tonal direction only — warm torchlight orange/amber as the only chromatic source, deep crushed-charcoal shadow dominant, wet stone reading as cold neutral grey-black where unlit. Do NOT impose a teal/orange Hollywood grade, do NOT push toward green, do NOT add blue daylight. Faithful to the location reference.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Skateboard <<<image_5>>> lies abandoned on dusty stone floor, deck face up,
 fine pale dust covering the grip tape, faint candle glow on the wood.
1.0s — Rein's boots and lower legs rush into frame from the right,
 she crouches sharply, her hand enters frame and snatches the board
 by the trucks.
2.0s — She lifts the skateboard out of frame in one fast motion,
 dust kicked up where the board was lying.
3.0s — Empty patch of stone floor remains, dust still settling,
 outline of the board visible in the dust.

[CAMERA]
Active organic handheld tight close-up, anamorphic 35mm lens,

[LIGHT]

4.5s — Her face fills more of the frame, mouth opens wide, she shouts

6.0s — She is almost on top of camera, scar on her cheekbone catching

[CAMERA]
Active organic handheld, anamorphic 35mm wide lens, medium shot
positioned directly in front of the three characters, slight low
angle. Stone wall fills the background behind them, columns
suggested at frame edges. Medium depth of field.

[LIGHT]
Cool blue ambient from above mixed with warm orange candle pools at
floor level, warm rim catching the edges of jackets, deep shadows
on the wall behind, dust drifting through the mixed light.

[ACTION]
6.0s — Roco lies on the dusty stone floor, on his back, head turned
 slightly to one side, eyes half-open, dust on his jacket and face.
 Jaxx and Rein crouch on either side of him, Rein dropping the
 skateboard beside her.
7.5s — Jaxx and Rein each grab one of Roco's forearms with both
 hands, brace their legs, exchange a brief glance.
9.0s — They PULL Roco up together in one coordinated lift. He comes
 off the floor heavy, head lolling forward, then catching itself.
10.5s — Roco finds his feet between them, knees buckling once, then
 holding. Jaxx and Rein keep their grip on his arms, supporting him.
12.0s — All three stand in medium shot, Roco swaying slightly upright
 between Jaxx and Rein, dust falling from his jacket, the stone
 wall behind them in soft focus.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, CONTINUOUS ACROSS ALL THREE SHOTS]
No music. Scrape of the skateboard trucks lifted off stone, Rein's
sharp boots running on stone, her single shouted "ROCO!" echoing
through the cave, soft crackle of candle flames, distant drip of
water, low ambient cave resonance, creak of tactical gear, grunt of
effort as Jaxx and Rein lift Roco, his heavy exhale as he comes
upright, their measured breathing.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no aerial shot, no fade
between shots, no dissolve between shots, no other people, no monk,
no statue, no glowing red lines, no transformation, no weapons drawn,
no combat, no removal of red nose bandage, no b-roll inserts of
candles or columns without characters, no detail shots without
characters in frame, no text, no captions, no logos.

[CAMERA（摄影机）]
⚠️ 摄影机操作——重度手持、身体在场、操作员追拍：摄影机明显由一具人体托载——操作员的步态、呼吸、重心转移都在画面的微观运动里清晰可见。画面始终在操作员手中保持「活着」，绝不平移锁定（locked off），绝不架在三脚架上，绝无云台般平滑浮漂。参考操作：《拯救大兵瑞恩》战场、<人间之子>（CHILDREN OF MEN）一镜到底、<荒野猎人>（THE REVENANT）追击段落、《侠盗一号》（ROGUE ONE）走廊手持。操作员随动作而动——演员跑他跟着跑（步幅匹配，每一下落步画面都随之震颤）、演员伏低他跟着伏低、动作坠向地面时他压低重心。追拍表演者时不断进行细微的重新取景。每一帧都带可视的手持特征：难以预料的小抖动、漂移矫正、重心落定。摄影机操作是原始而物理的——绝不航拍、绝不横扫摇臂、绝不稳定化。

[LIGHT（灯光）]
上方冷蓝环境光与地面层暖橙色烛光池相交汇，深重的阴影，混合光线中细尘飘荡。低调高反差（LOW-KEY HIGH CONTRAST）——碾至深黑（crushed deep blacks）主导走廊，只有火炬光池与湿地板反光承托高光细节。面孔大半隐在阴影里，只有火炬扫过时带起一闪一闪的边缘光。大气透视——走廊的纵深溶解进烟雾般的火炬尘霭。

Composition（构图）：三分法 + 黄金比例。下落的石板（descending slab）作为垂直/下坠的图形块锚定纵深的画面，其下方空隙则成为戏剧性的焦点孔径。

⚠️ COLOR PALETTE（色彩基调）——继承自参考图（仅色调方向）：暖火炬光的橙色/琥珀色是唯一的彩色来源，深碾碳黑阴影为主，未受光处的湿石读作冷中性灰黑。不得强加 teal/orange 好莱坞调色，不得推向绿色，不得加入蓝色日光。忠实于场景参考。
