## 原文 (source)

Ancient sanctum location b-roll set three, five cuts, 16 seconds total, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera. No 3D render, no game engine. References: Emmanuel Lubezki, Roger Deakins. Lighting: cold pale ambient from natural rock fissure above as dominant key, warm amber oil-lamps along columns as practicals, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets as distant secondary wash. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant deep cool blue-grey / secondary saturated red / accent warm amber. Heavily desaturated. Physical anamorphic cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. Corner vignette, edge chromatic aberration. 60fps, 8K. Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music, no subtitles.

WORLD: vast cavern-sanctum carved from natural rock. Towering carved stone columns with ancient carved script, warm amber oil-lamps, irregular flagstone floor, atmospheric haze patchy. High in side wall — sacred carved stone ledge with single candle, elderly monk seated in meditation. 30-meter guardian seated at far end, eye-sockets pouring saturated red light. Distant guardian's deep red hum audible throughout.

Cut A (0-3s): Three-quarter handheld slow push-in toward the guardian's massive seated hands with monk, 85mm, f/1.4. Camera at elevated position walking slowly forward toward the guardian's cupped stone palms resting in his lap — the massive carved stone hands growing in frame as camera approaches, the cupped palms the size of a small bed, weathered ancient stone with deep relief and moss in crevices visible across the hand surfaces. The elderly monk in dark maroon robes seated cross-legged in the cupped palms, tiny against the scale of the stone hands, eyes closed, throat-tone sustained, completely motionless. Warm candle pinpoint from the high ledge above casting minimal warm accent from above. Cold pale ambient from above as dominant light. Lag focus pulling toward the monk as camera approaches. Frame breathes with walking pace. Audio: monk's sustained throat-tone resonating from his position in the guardian's hands, guardian's deep red hum from his body nearby, deep ambient cavern drone.

Cut B (3-6s): Three-quarter handheld slow lateral drift along the guardian's massive stone sword across his back, 50mm, f/1.8. Camera positioned behind the guardian, drifting slowly laterally along the length of the colossal sword visible across his back — the 7-meter blade passing through frame as camera drifts, the carved stone blade surface with ancient script etched along its length visible in razor-thin focus, dust accumulation across the blade surface, the carved ancient script running the length of the blade. The guardian's massive back visible in shallow blur behind the sword. Cold pale ambient from above catching the blade edges in contre-jour rim light. Frame breathes with lateral drift. Audio: guardian's deep red hum from his body close to camera, deep ambient cavern drone, atmospheric haze hissing.

Cut C (6-9s): Three-quarter handheld slow tilt upward along guardian's body from seated position toward empty eye-sockets, 35mm, f/2. Camera at floor level beside the seated guardian, tilting slowly upward along his massive body — the carved stone legs and lower torso in the lower frame as camera begins, then tilting upward revealing his chest plate in carved relief, then his shoulders and neck, then arriving at his severe carved stone face with the two EMPTY EYE-SOCKETS pouring saturated red light at the upper frame — the red light filling each socket as smooth featureless glow, no eye structure, only red. The camera tilt is slow and deliberate — a journey from floor level to face level, the scale of his body passing through frame emphasizing his impossible height. Frame breathes with operator's tilt. Audio: guardian's deep red hum sustained and present close to camera, the hum intensifying marginally as frame arrives at eye-socket level, deep ambient cavern drone.

Cut D (9-12s): Three-quarter handheld slow lateral drift through atmospheric haze catching cold daylight shaft, 24mm wide, f/2.8. Camera walking slowly laterally through the chamber — the cold pale daylight shaft from the natural rock fissure above visible cutting diagonally through the atmospheric haze, dust particles drifting through the beam catching the light and glowing briefly as they pass through it. The beam itself visible as a defined shaft of cold light in the dark chamber, the surrounding chamber in cool shadow. Carved stone columns visible in shallow blur on either side. The atmospheric haze around the beam visible as patchy density — denser pockets and clearer areas drifting organically. The simple beauty of natural light entering ancient stone architecture. Frame breathes with lateral drift. Audio: atmospheric haze hissing softly, dust drifting audibly barely, deep ambient cavern drone, monk's distant throat-tone very faint.

Cut E (12-16s): Three-quarter handheld slow push-in toward the ancient staircase carved into the cliff-face leading to the monk's ledge, 50mm, f/1.8. Camera walking slowly forward toward the narrow ancient stone staircase carved from the rock face — the staircase growing in frame as camera approaches, the carved stone steps visible with real-world weathering and wear from centuries of use, moss on the steps, dust accumulation, the staircase spiraling upward along the cliff-face toward the monk's ledge above out of frame top. Cold pale ambient from above casting minimal light down the staircase. The monk's ledge barely visible at the top of the staircase in shallow blur above. The ancient staircase communicating centuries of ritual use — built for humans, not for the guardian. Frame breathes with walking pace. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, monk's throat-tone from above becoming marginally clearer as camera approaches the base of the staircase, atmospheric haze hissing.

PACING: each set — 16-second five-cut b-roll with slow deliberate camera movement throughout. Each cut unique subject — no repeated subjects across all three sets.

## 中文对照 (zh)

远古圣殿地点技术景别·第三组（b-roll set three），五个镜头，共 16 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX。写实摄影，实拍电影摄影机完成。非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎。参考：Emmanuel Lubezki、Roger Deakins。Lighting（灯光）：上方天然岩缝洒下的冷白环境光为主导主光，沿石柱的暖琥珀油灯为内置灯具（practicals），守卫眼窝的饱和红为远方次色红晕。Color（色彩）：60:30:10——主色深冷蓝灰 / 次色饱和红 / 点缀色暖琥珀。高度去饱和。实体变形宽银幕电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。四角暗角、边缘色差。60fps，8K。Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（SFX only）。无音乐、无字幕。

WORLD（世界）：一座从天然岩体中开凿的宏大洞窟圣殿。高耸的雕刻石柱带古代刻字、暖琥珀油灯、不规则石板地、斑驳大气雾感。侧墙高处——神圣的雕刻石台上燃着一支烛火，一位老僧坐禅其间。30 米高的守卫端坐于远端尽头，眼窝倾泻饱和红光。守卫的深红低鸣自远处始终可闻。

镜头 A（0-3 秒）：四分之三手持，缓缓向守卫那巨大端坐、手捧僧人的双掌推近，85mm，f/1.4。摄影机在略高的位置，稳步缓慢走向守卫搭在膝上那捧成碗状的石掌——随着摄影机靠近，那巨大雕刻石手在画面中渐大，那捧成碗的掌心有小床般大小，风化的古石带深浮雕、缝隙间苔藓可见于整个手面。穿深酒红色僧袍的老僧盘腿坐在那捧成碗的掌心里，在石手的尺度下渺小无比，双目闭合，喉音持续，纹丝不动。上方高台上那支暖蜡烛头自上方投下极弱暖色点缀。上方的冷白环境光为主导光。随着摄影机靠近，跟焦（lag focus）向老僧处拉动。画面随行走步伐呼吸。音频：老僧持续喉音自守卫手心的方位传来共鸣、守卫身边躯体传来深红嗡鸣、深沉的环境洞窟低鸣。

镜头 B（3-6 秒）：四分之三手持，沿守卫悬在背上的巨大石剑缓慢横向漂移，50mm，f/1.8。摄影机位于守卫身后，沿那把横悬于其背的巨剑之长度缓慢横向漂移——随着摄影机移动，7 米长的剑刃掠过大画面，剑刃雕石表面带沿其全长的古代刻字，以剃刀般浅的焦点可见、刃面上落满积尘、雕刻的古代铭文横贯整个剑身。守卫的庞然背脊在剑后以浅虚化可见。上方冷白环境光照在剑缘上，形成逆光背光（contre-jour）轮廓光。画面随横向漂移呼吸。音频：守卫贴近摄影机处身体传来深红嗡鸣、深沉的环境洞窟低鸣、大气雾感嘶鸣。

镜头 C（6-9 秒）：四分之三手持，沿守卫躯体自端坐处缓缓向上仰摇，朝向空眼窝，35mm，f/2。摄影机在端坐守卫身旁的地面层，沿他庞大的身体缓缓向上仰摇——开始时雕刻石腿与下躯干位于画面下部，接着仰摇揭示他带浮雕的胸甲，再是他的双肩与颈，最后抵达他那严峻的雕刻石脸、两个空洞的眼窝在画面上部倾泻饱和红光——红光填满每个眼窝，成为平滑无特征的光晕，没有眼的构造，只有红光。这次仰摇缓慢而蓄意——自地面层向面部层的一段旅程，他身体的大小穿越大画面，强调其不可能的体高。画面随操作员的仰摇呼吸。音频：守卫深红嗡鸣贴近摄影机、持续而现前，当画面抵达眼窝高度时嗡鸣微微增强，深沉的环境洞窟低鸣。

镜头 D（9-12 秒）：四分之三手持，穿过大气雾感缓慢横向漂移，捕捉冷日光光束，24mm 广角，f/2.8。摄影机在洞窟中缓慢横移——上方天然岩缝洒下的冷白日光光束可见，斜切过大气雾感，尘埃粒子在光线中漂移、穿过时捕捉到光、短暂发亮。光束本身在暗洞窟中可见为一道轮廓分明的冷光柱，四围洞窟处于冷影中。两侧可见浅虚化的雕刻石柱。光束周围的大气雾感呈斑驳密度——有的更浓、有的更清，有机地飘动。自然光进入远古石构建筑那份朴素的美。画面随横向漂移呼吸。音频：大气雾感轻轻嘶鸣、尘埃飘动几乎听不见、深沉的环境洞窟低鸣、老僧远处喉音极其微弱。

镜头 E（12-16 秒）：四分之三手持，缓缓向凿进岩壁、通往老僧石台的古老台阶推近，50mm，f/1.8。摄影机稳步缓慢走向那凿自岩面的狭长古石阶——随着摄影机靠近，台阶在画面中渐大，雕刻石级可见，带数个世纪使用后真实的自然风化与磨损、阶上苔藓、积尘，台阶沿岩壁盘旋而上、通往上方画框顶部之外的老僧石台。上方冷白环境光洒下极弱的光照亮台阶。老僧的石台在台阶顶端、上方浅虚化处几乎不可见。这座古阶诉说着数世纪的仪式使用——为人而建，而非为守卫。画面随行走步伐呼吸。音频：深沉的环境洞窟低鸣、自上方传来的老僧喉音随摄影机接近台阶底部而略微清晰、大气雾感嘶鸣。

PACING（节奏）：每一组——16 秒五个镜头的技术景别，全程摄影机动作缓慢而蓄意。每个镜头主题独特——三组之间绝不重复主题。
