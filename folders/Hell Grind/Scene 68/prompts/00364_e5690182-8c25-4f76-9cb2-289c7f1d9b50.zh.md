## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 10 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, anamorphic 100mm telephoto starting close-up then drifting 
to medium, very shallow depth of field, in the style of intense 
character beats.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, very close on Roco's face. Gentle breathing 
motion. After the dialogue, camera drifts back slightly to a medium 
shot as Roco starts walking. Operator alive, present, observing.

[LIGHT]
Cool blue ambient light from above hitting one side of Roco's face, 
warm candle glow from below catching his jaw and chin. Strong dramatic 
contrast. Dust particles around him.

[CHARACTER — Roco focus]
Messy shaggy black hair, faint mustache, olive skin with dust 
streaks, small red adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his 
nose, intense focused brown eyes, jaw set. Same dark tactical jacket. 
Jaxx and Rein soft in bokeh on either side, slightly behind him.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Tight close-up on Roco's face, eyes locked ahead on the 
 statue (off-frame), processing what he sees.
1.5s — His jaw tightens. He breathes in slowly. Decision settling.
2.5s — Roco speaks, low and steady: "Let's go."
3.5s — He takes the first step forward, walking past the camera. 
 Camera tracks back with him, drifting from close-up to medium 
 shot.
5.0s — Now we see the full team moving forward together — Roco 
 leading, Jaxx and Rein on either side, all walking toward 
 the colossal statue ahead.
7.0s — Camera continues pulling back as they approach the statue 
 in measured pace. The statue grows bigger in their POV.
9.0s — They are close enough now to see the small monk on its hands 
 clearly — cross-legged, eyes closed, motionless.
10.0s — Frame ends on the team mid-stride, statue and monk visible 
 ahead, tension building.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Roco's slow controlled breath, his low voice "Let's go" 
spoken with calm intent, the scrape of his boot starting forward, 
the synchronized boots of all three resuming their walk, faint cave 
ambient hum, distant candle crackle, the distant statue silent.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no looking at camera, 
no shouting, no whisper, no broad emotion, no smiling, no fear, no 
glowing red lines on statue (still dormant), no statue moving, no 
monk moving, no monk singing yet, no transformation, no removal of 
red nose bandage, no facial morphing, no text, no captions.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单一连续手持镜头，10 秒，变形宽银幕 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，变形宽银幕 100mm 长焦镜头，先特写随后后拉至中景，极浅景深，紧张的内心人物节拍风格。

[CAMERA]（摄影机）
细微的有机手持，极近地贴住 ROCO 的脸。轻柔的呼吸律动。对白结束后，随着 ROCO 开始行走，摄影机略微后拉至中景。操机员在场、活生生、在观察。

[LIGHT]（灯光）
来自上方的冷蓝环境光照亮 ROCO 脸的半侧，下方的暖烛光辉打亮他的下颌与下巴。强烈戏剧性对比。他周围的微尘粒子。

[CHARACTER — Roco focus]（角色——ROCO 中心）
凌乱蓬松的黑发、淡胡须、橄榄色皮肤带尘痕、横跨鼻梁的一条小型红色创可贴、专注的棕色眼睛、下颌绷紧。同款深色战术夹克。JAXX 与 REIN 在他身后两侧与微微侧面，柔焦于虚化（bokeh）中。

[ACTION]（动作）
0.0s——极紧凑的 ROCO 脸特写，双眼锁向前方的雕像（画外），消化着眼前所见。
1.5s——他的下颌收紧。缓缓吸气。决定落定。
2.5s——ROCO 开口，低沉而稳：「走。」
3.5s——他迈出第一步向前，走过摄影机。摄影机随他后移，从特写漂向中景。
5.0s——此刻我们看见整支团队一同前进——ROCO 打头，JAXX 与 REIN 分列两侧，都朝前方那座巨硕的雕像走去。
7.0s——他们以克制的步速接近雕像，摄影机继续后拉。雕像在他们的视线里越变越大。
9.0s——此刻已近得足以清晰看见它手掌上的小僧人——结跏趺坐、双目紧闭、纹丝不动。
10.0s——画面结束于团队正步行中，雕像与僧人可见于前方，张力在攀升。

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]（声音——仅剧情内音）
无音乐。ROCO 缓慢克制的呼吸、他以平静意图说出那句低沉的「走」、他靴子向前迈出的刮擦声、三双靴子的同步脚步重新行走、隐约的洞穴环境嗡鸣、远处的蜡烛噼啪、远处雕像静默无声。

[NEGATIVE]（反面清单）
无音乐，无慢动作，无平滑云台，不看镜头，不喊叫，不低语，无夸张情绪，不笑，无恐惧，雕像上无发光红线（仍休眠），雕像不动，僧人不移动，僧人尚不吟唱，无变身，不得摘除红色鼻贴，无脸部形变，无文字，无字幕。
