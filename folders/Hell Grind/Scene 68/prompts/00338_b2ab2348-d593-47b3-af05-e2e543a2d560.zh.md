## 原文 (source)

Multi-shot sequence connected by hard cuts, 12 seconds total. Four 
internal shots separated by clean hard cuts (no fade, no dissolve). 
Every shot uses non-frontal directorial framing.

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY]

FORMAT: Anamorphic 2.39:1. 35mm film grain. Arri Alexa with anamorphic 
lenses. Horizontal anamorphic lens flares from candles.

COLOR PALETTE: Cool desaturated — deep blue-blacks, charcoal grays, 
slate stone. Warm orange candle pools. Dust beige on gear. ~5500K 
cool blue ambient, ~2700K warm candles. Monk's burgundy red robe 
and orange sash the only saturated colors besides Roco's red bandana, 
nose bandage, red bear keychain, and faint purple of Rein's hoverboard. 
STATUE STILL DORMANT — no glowing red lines yet in this prompt.

LIGHTING: Single dominant cool blue shaft from cave opening. Hundreds 
of warm candle pools. High-contrast chiaroscuro. Heavy mist and dust. 
Faint pale beam touching the monk on the statue's hands.

CAMERA: Organic handheld throughout. Operator breathing, slight 
bounces. NEVER static, NEVER smooth gimbal.

LOCATION: Massive ancient cave-temple. Monolithic stone columns 
rising into darkness. Heavy mist. The colossal seated guardian statue 
holding the small monk on its open palms. Team standing some distance 
from statue.

CHARACTERS:
- THE MONK <<<image_5>>>: elderly Asian monk in his 60s-70s, short cropped gray 
  hair (not bald), deeply weathered face with profound wrinkles, 
  calm serene expression, dark brown ancient eyes. Deep burgundy red 
  Tibetan-style monk robe wrapped across the body, orange cloth sash 
  at waist, dark gray inner garment visible at collar, simple worn 
  sandals on bare feet. Sitting cross-legged in lotus position on the 
  colossal stone hands. NO glasses.
- ROCO (CENTER): messy shaggy black hair, faint mustache, red bandage 
  on nose, dark tactical jacket with shevron panels, red bandana on 
  right wrist, red bear keychain.
- JAXX (RIGHT): bleached blonde hair, dark skin, clear safety goggles 
  on forehead, scar on right temple, dark tactical jacket with gray 
  panels, olive cargo pants.
- REIN (LEFT): silver-and-black bobbed hair, scar on right cheekbone, 
  dark gray tactical jacket, black turtleneck, dark cargo pants, 
  thigh holster. Carrying weathered longboard hoverboard with purple 
  circuit lines, copper-coil thrusters underneath, faint purple 
  underglow.

REFERENCES: Denis Villeneuve and Greig Fraser ("Dune") sacred scale 
through framing. NEVER frontal, NEVER centered hero shots.

## 中文对照 (zh)

多镜头序列由硬切（hard cuts）连接，总时长 12 秒。四个内部镜头以干净的硬切分隔（无淡入淡出，无溶接）。每个镜头均采用非正面导演式取景。

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY]（视觉一致性）

FORMAT（格式）：变形宽银幕 2.39:1。35mm 胶片颗粒。Arri Alexa 配变形宽银幕镜头。来自蜡烛的水平向变形宽银幕镜头光斑。

COLOR PALETTE（色彩基调）：冷调去饱和——深蓝黑、炭灰、板岩灰。暖橙烛光池。装备上尘埃米黄。约 5500K 冷蓝环境光、约 2700K 暖蜡烛。除 ROCO 的红色头巾、鼻贴、红色小熊钥匙扣及 REIN 悬滑板的淡紫色外，僧人的酒红僧袍与橙色腰巾是唯一的饱和色。**雕像仍休眠**——本段中雕像上无发光红线。

LIGHTING（灯光）：洞窟口垂下的单道主导冷蓝光柱。数百处暖烛光池。高反差明暗对比（chiaroscuro）。浓雾与尘埃。淡淡苍白光束落在雕像掌心的僧人身上。

CAMERA（摄影机）：全程有机手持。操机员呼吸、轻微弹跳。绝不停滞，绝不用平滑云台。

LOCATION（场景地点）：巨型古老洞窟-庙宇。整石巨柱升入黑暗。浓雾。巨硕坐姿守护者雕像张开的手掌托着小小的僧人。团队站在距雕像一段距离处。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
- THE MONK <<<image_5>>>（僧人）：60-70 多岁的年长亚洲僧人，灰色短发（非秃顶）、深深刻蚀风化的脸带深重皱纹、安详平静的表情、深褐色的古老眼睛。深酒红藏式僧袍裹身、腰间橙色布腰巾、衣领处可见深灰内衬、赤足穿简朴磨损的凉鞋。结跏趺坐（莲花坐）于巨石掌上。不戴眼镜。
- ROCO（中央）：凌乱蓬松的黑发、淡胡须、鼻上红色创可贴、带雪佛龙面板的深色战术夹克、右手腕红色头巾、红色小熊钥匙扣。
- JAXX（右）：漂白金发、深色皮肤、额头透明护目镜、右太阳穴有道疤、带灰色面板的深色战术夹克、橄榄色工装裤。
- REIN（左）：银黑波波头、右颧骨有道疤、深灰色战术夹克、黑色高领衫、深色工装裤、大腿枪套。带着紫色电路线、下方铜线圈推进器的风化长板悬滑板，带淡紫色底光。

REFERENCES（参考）：丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦与格雷格·弗莱瑟（〈沙丘〉）透过取景营造的神圣尺度。绝不正面，绝不居中英雄镜头。
