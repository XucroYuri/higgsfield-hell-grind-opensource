## 原文 (source)

Multi-shot sequence connected by hard cuts, 12 seconds total, 
anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, deep focus, in the style of 
Denis Villeneuve's "Dune" — overwhelming sacred scale. Two internal 
shots separated by clean hard cut.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT A — TEAM WALKS DEEPER, MEDIUM — 0.0s to 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
Organic handheld, anamorphic 35mm lens, operator walking slowly 
behind the team. Slight bounces from steps.

[LIGHT]
Cool blue ambient from above, warm candle pools running along columns.

[LOCATION]
Cave-temple, monolithic columns rising on both sides, mist, weathered 
stone floor, candles at column bases.

[CHARACTERS]
Same three: Roco center, Jaxx right, Rein left. Walking forward in 
loose formation, alert. Same outfits as before.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Three figures from behind, walking forward in formation into 
 the temple. Camera tracks slowly behind them.
2.0s — They pass between two massive columns, candle pools warm at 
 their boots.
5.0s — They keep walking, getting deeper. Camera maintains pace.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT AT 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT B — EXTREME WIDE REVEAL — 5.0s to 12.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
Static-ish handheld, anamorphic 24mm ultra-wide lens, EXTREME wide 
framing. Operator stands still, slight breathing motion.

[LIGHT]
The single dramatic shaft of cool blue light pouring down from a 
high opening in the cave ceiling, bathing the colossal seated 
guardian statue in ethereal glow. Candle pools running in lines 
toward the statue. Mist in deep space, dust particles catching light.

[LOCATION]
The colossal seated guardian statue REVEALED at the far end of the 
temple — massive, ancient, holding a small monk figure cross-legged 
on its open palms. Statue dominates the back of the frame.

[ACTION]
5.0s — EXTREME wide shot suddenly. Three figures TINY in lower middle 
 of frame, dwarfed by cathedral of stone columns rising on both 
 sides, the colossal seated guardian statue at the far end with 
 a tiny monk figure on its hands.
7.0s — Hold. Candles flicker. Dust drifts. The statue silent, 
 motionless, ancient.
10.0s — A single small movement — Roco takes one step forward toward 
 the statue. Jaxx and Rein remain still.
12.0s — Frame ends on Roco mid-step, the team about to follow him 
 into the depth.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, CONTINUOUS]
No music. Synchronized echo of three pairs of boots on stone in shot 
A, then in shot B — vast cave ambient hum, distant water drip echoing, 
soft crackle of countless candles, faint whisper of mist, Roco's 
single boot step on stone reverberating in the vast space.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no aerial drone shot, no 
fade between shots, no dissolve, no glowing red lines on statue (still 
dormant), no monk's face visible (too far), no statue moving, no 
transformation, no combat, no weapons drawn, no other people, no 
text, no captions, no logos.

## 中文对照 (zh)

多镜头序列，以硬切（hard cuts）相连，共 12 秒，变形宽银幕 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，深焦点，致敬丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦《沙丘》的风格——压倒性的神圣体量。两个内部镜头以干净利落的硬切分隔。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT A（镜头 A）——队伍向纵深进发，中景——0.0s 至 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]（摄影机）
有机手持，anamorphic 35mm 镜头，操作员缓慢跟在队伍身后行走。随步伐有轻微弹跳。

[LIGHT]（灯光）
上方冷蓝环境光，沿石柱排列的一汪汪暖烛光光池。

[LOCATION]（场景）
洞穴寺庙，两侧高耸的整块巨石柱，雾气，风磨的石地，柱基处燃着烛火。

[CHARACTERS]（角色）
还是那三人：Roco 居中、Jaxx 在右、Rein 在左。以松散的队形向前行进，机警警觉。穿着与之前相同的装束。

[ACTION]（动作）
0.0s——自背后看去三道身影，排着队形前进，走入寺庙。摄影机缓慢跟在他们身后。
2.0s——他们穿行于两根巨大的石柱之间，靴边一汪汪暖烛光光池。
5.0s——他们继续行走，越走越深。摄影机保持着步速。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT AT 5.0s（5.0 秒硬切）
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT B（镜头 B）——极广角揭示——5.0s 至 12.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]（摄影机）
近乎静止的手持，anamorphic 24mm 超广角镜头，极广角取景。操作员静静站立，带轻微呼吸感的动作。

[LIGHT]（灯光）
那道主导性的冷蓝光柱自洞窟天花板的高处开口倾泻而下，将坐姿的庞然守护者雕像沐浴在幽灵般的光辉中。一汪汪烛光光池排成行，通向雕像。深处空间中浮着雾气，粉尘粒子捕捉着光线。

[LOCATION]（场景）
坐姿的庞然守护者雕像在寺庙尽头被「揭示」——硕大、古老，摊开的双掌上托着一个小小的、盘腿而坐的武僧身形。雕像主宰着画面的后部。

[ACTION]（动作）
5.0s——镜头骤变为极广角。三道渺小至极的身影出现在画面下方中央，被两侧如教堂般高耸的林立石柱衬得微不足道，尽头的坐姿守护者雕像掌上托着一个微小的武僧身形。
7.0s——持定。烛火闪烁。尘土飘荡。雕像沉默、纹丝不动、古老。
10.0s——一个细微的小动作——Roco 朝雕像迈进了一步。Jaxx 与 Rein 静止不动。
12.0s——画面定格于 Roco 迈到半途，队伍即将随他踏入深处。

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, CONTINUOUS]（音效——仅剧情内音，连贯）
无音乐。镜头 A 中三双靴子踏在石上同步的回响，随后镜头 B 中——宽阔的洞穴环境低鸣，隐远的滴水回响，无数烛火柔和的噼啪，雾气细微的耳语，Roco 一记靴步踏在石上、在辽阔空间中回荡。

[NEGATIVE]（反面指令 / 不得清单）
无音乐，无慢动作，无顺滑云台，无航拍无人机镜头，镜头之间无淡入淡出（fade），无叠化（dissolve），雕像上不得有发光红线（依然沉睡），武僧的脸不得可见（太远），雕像不得移动，不变身，不战斗，不拔兵刃，无其他人，无文字，无字幕，无商标。
