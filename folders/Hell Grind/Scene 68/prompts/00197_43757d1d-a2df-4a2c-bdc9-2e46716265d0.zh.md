## 原文 (source)

Ancient sanctum location b-roll set one, five cuts, 16 seconds total, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera. No 3D render, no game engine. References: Emmanuel Lubezki, Roger Deakins. Lighting: cold pale ambient from natural rock fissure above as dominant key, warm amber oil-lamps along columns as practicals, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets as distant secondary wash. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant deep cool blue-grey / secondary saturated red / accent warm amber. Heavily desaturated. Physical anamorphic cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. Corner vignette, edge chromatic aberration. 60fps, 8K. Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music, no subtitles.

WORLD: vast cavern-sanctum carved from natural rock. Towering carved stone columns with ancient carved script, warm amber oil-lamps, irregular flagstone floor, atmospheric haze patchy. High in side wall — sacred carved stone ledge with single candle, elderly monk seated in meditation. Distant guardian's deep red hum audible throughout.

CAMERA: fully locked static on tripod for every cut. Zero camera movement. Zero operator shake. Zero breathing motion. Perfectly still frame throughout each cut. All motion in frame comes only from the environment — dust drifting, flame flickering, atmospheric haze moving, ambient particles floating. Pure observational locked frame.

Cut A (0-3s): Locked static wide shot looking down the colonnade into deep perspective, 35mm, f/2. Camera locked completely still on tripod. The colonnade in perfect static frame — columns flanking both sides receding in deep perspective, warm amber oil-lamps flickering on each column as small practical lights receding into darkness, their halation bloom catching the carved stone. Cold pale daylight shaft cutting diagonally through atmospheric haze, dust particles drifting slowly through the beam. The distant guardian's seated form as a massive dark silhouette at the far end, two faint red points of his eye-sockets deep in the perspective blur. Flagstone floor receding below. All motion environmental only — dust drifting, lamp flames flickering subtly, atmospheric haze shifting. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, atmospheric haze hissing, oil-lamps flickering, monk's distant throat-tone faint, guardian's deep red hum distant.

Cut B (3-6s): Locked static medium close on a single oil-lamp mounted on a column surface, 85mm, f/1.4. Camera locked completely still. Razor-thin focus on the warm amber oil-lamp flame — flame burning with subtle flicker in atmospheric drafts, warm amber halation bloom around the flame catching the carved stone column beside it, heat distortion above the flame visible subtly. Carved stone column surface in shallow blur behind, deep relief and ancient script visible. Dust particles drifting slowly through the warm beam. Cold blue-grey ambient contrasting with warm amber flame. All motion from flame flicker and drifting dust only. Audio: lamp flame flickering audibly in atmospheric draft, faint pop of flame, dust hissing softly, deep ambient cavern drone.

Cut C (6-9s): Locked static extreme close-up on flagstone floor with debris, 100mm macro, f/1.4. Camera locked completely still at floor level. Razor-thin focus on the irregular flagstone surface in extreme detail — ancient stone with real-world weathering, deep cracks between joints, moss growing in the crevices, dust accumulation settled across the surface, small debris pieces settled on the floor. Fine dust particles drifting slowly downward and landing on the flagstone surface with near-invisible motion. Cold pale ambient from above casting minimal light across the floor. Warm amber lamp glow from off-frame catching one flagstone edge. All motion from drifting settling dust particles only. Audio: dust settling onto flagstone with near-inaudible soft whisper, deep ambient cavern drone resonating through the stone floor, monk's throat-tone distant.

Cut D (9-12s): Locked static medium shot on the monk's ledge from across the chamber, 85mm, f/1.4. Camera locked completely still. The high sacred carved stone ledge visible at height with the single warm amber candle flame burning in a small stone bowl, the elderly monk in dark maroon robes seated cross-legged in meditation, eyes closed, mouth open in sustained low guttural throat-tone, body completely motionless. Rock overhang above sheltering him. Cold pale ambient catching ledge in contre-jour rim, warm candle casting amber light across the monk's face from below. The vast dark chamber space in shallow blur below and around him. All motion from candle flame flicker only — monk perfectly still. Audio: monk's sustained low guttural throat-tone resonating across the chamber, candle flame barely audible, deep ambient cavern drone.

Cut E (12-16s): Locked static medium close on ancient carved script on a column surface, 50mm, f/1.8. Camera locked completely still at three-quarter angle to the column. Ancient carved script running vertically along the column shaft visible clearly in frame — deep carved letters filled with shadow, moss growing in the deepest cuts of the inscriptions, dust accumulation settled into the carved channels. Cold pale ambient from above catching the column in contre-jour raking light, the raking light revealing the depth of the carved relief and script dramatically. Warm amber oil-lamp glow from off-frame catching the opposite side of the column with warm accent. Fine dust particles drifting slowly past the column surface. All motion from drifting dust particles only. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, atmospheric haze hissing softly, monk's distant throat-tone, guardian's deep red hum very faint from far across chamber.

WORLD（世界）：由天然岩石凿成的巨型洞窟-圣所。高耸的雕刻石柱带古老刻文，暖琥珀油灯，不规则石板地面，大气雾霭斑驳。侧壁高处——一块神圣的雕花石崖带一支蜡烛，年长僧人结跏趺坐冥想。全程可闻守护者远处的深红嗡鸣。

CAMERA（摄影机）：每个镜头都在三脚架上**完全锁定静止**。零摄影机移动。零操机员抖动。零呼吸律动。每个镜头全程画面完美静止。画面中的一切运动只来自环境——尘埃飘浮、火焰摇曳、大气雾霭流动、环境中粒子浮游。纯观察式的锁定画面。

Cut A（0-3s）【镜头 A】：锁定静止的广角镜头，沿柱廊望入深邃透视，35mm，f/2。摄影机在三脚架上完全锁定不动。柱廊处于完美的静止画面——两侧石柱退入深邃透视，每根柱上暖琥珀油灯作为小小的实用光点退入黑暗，其辉散（halation）泛光抓住雕花石。冷白日光柱斜斜切穿大气雾霭，微尘粒子缓缓飘过光束。远处的守护者坐姿作为远端巨大的暗色剪影，两粒红点是他眼窝深处于透视虚化之中。石板地面在下方退去。一切运动仅环境性——尘埃飘浮、灯焰细微摇曳、大气雾霭移转。Audio（音频）：深沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣、大气雾霭嘶嘶、油灯摇曳、僧人远处喉音微弱、守护者深红嗡鸣遥远。

Cut B（3-6s）【镜头 B】：锁定静止的中特写，对准柱面上一盏油灯，85mm，f/1.4。摄影机完全锁定不动。极浅焦点落在暖琥珀油灯火焰上——火焰在气流中细微摇曳，火焰周围暖琥珀辉散（halation）泛光抓住旁侧的雕花石柱，火焰上方有可微见的空气热畸变。身后的雕花柱面处于浅虚，深浮雕与古代刻文可见。微尘粒子缓缓飘过暖光束。冷蓝灰环境光与暖琥珀火焰相对照。一切运动仅来自火焰摇曳与飘浮微尘。Audio（音频）：灯焰在气流中可闻地摇曳、火焰微弱的噼啪、尘埃轻轻嘶嘶、深沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣。

Cut D（9-12s）【镜头 D】：锁定静止的中景镜头，从厅堂对面看僧人崖台，85mm，f/1.4。摄影机完全锁定不动。高处那块神圣雕花石崖可见，一支暖琥珀烛焰在小小的石碗中燃烧，年长僧人深暗酒红僧袍、结跏趺坐冥想、双目紧闭、嘴张开维持低沉喉音、身体完全纹丝不动。上方岩檐庇护着他。冷白环境光以逆光轮廓光（contre-jour rim）抓住崖台，暖烛自下方把琥珀光洒过僧人的脸。他下方与四周的广阔幽暗厅堂空间处于浅虚。一切运动仅来自烛焰摇曳——僧人纹丝不动。Audio（音频）：僧人持续低沉喉音回响过厅堂、烛焰几不可闻、深沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣。

Cut E（12-16s）【镜头 E】：锁定静止的中特写，对准柱面上古老的雕刻文字，50mm，f/1.8。摄影机与柱成 3/4 角度完全锁定不动。沿柱身竖向刻的古代刻文清晰可见于画面中——深刻的字母填满阴影，铭文最深凹处生长苔藓，积尘沉降进雕凿的沟槽。上方冷白环境光以擦扫光（raking light）抓住石柱，擦扫光戏剧性地揭示浮雕与刻文的深度。画外暖琥珀灯辉以暖点缀抓住石柱另一侧。细小微尘缓缓飘过柱面。一切运动仅来自飘浮微尘。Audio（音频）：深沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣、大气雾霭轻轻嘶嘶、僧人远处喉音、守护者深红嗡鸣自厅堂极远处非常微弱。

PACING（节奏）：16 秒五镜头地点 B-roll。所有镜头完全锁定静止——摄影机从不移动。全程仅环境运动。柱廊纵深透视带远方守护者剪影（0-3s）、暖琥珀中近景油灯火焰（3-6s）、石板地面微距细节（6-9s）、高崖台上僧人（9-12s）、擦扫光下古代刻文中特写（12-16s）。
