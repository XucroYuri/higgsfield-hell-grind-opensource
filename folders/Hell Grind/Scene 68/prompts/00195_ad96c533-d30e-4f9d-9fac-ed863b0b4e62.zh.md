Ancient sanctum location b-roll set three, five cuts, 16 seconds total, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera. No 3D render, no game engine. References: Emmanuel Lubezki, Roger Deakins. Lighting: cold pale ambient from natural rock fissure above as dominant key, warm amber oil-lamps along columns as practicals, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets as distant secondary wash. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant deep cool blue-grey / secondary saturated red / accent warm amber. Heavily desaturated. Physical anamorphic cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. Corner vignette, edge chromatic aberration. 60fps, 8K. Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music, no subtitles.

WORLD: vast cavern-sanctum carved from natural rock. Towering carved stone columns with ancient carved script, warm amber oil-lamps, irregular flagstone floor, atmospheric haze patchy. High in side wall — sacred carved stone ledge with single candle, elderly monk seated in meditation. 30-meter guardian seated at far end. Distant guardian's deep red hum audible throughout.

CAMERA: fully locked static on tripod for every cut. Zero camera movement. Zero operator shake. Perfectly still frame throughout each cut. All motion in frame from environment only.

Cut A (0-3s): Locked static medium shot on the ancient staircase carved into the cliff-face, 50mm, f/1.8. Camera locked completely still facing the narrow ancient stone staircase carved from the rock face — the carved stone steps visible with real-world weathering and centuries of wear, moss on the steps, dust accumulation settled across the stone, the staircase spiraling upward along the cliff-face toward the monk's ledge out of frame above. Cold pale ambient from above casting minimal light down the staircase, the upper steps in slight darkness. The monk's candle glow barely visible as a faint warm amber point far above at the top of the staircase in deep blur. The staircase communicating centuries of ritual use. All motion from atmospheric haze drifting past only. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, monk's throat-tone from above barely audible, atmospheric haze hissing softly.

Cut B (3-6s): Locked static medium close on the guardian's massive stone sword blade surface, 85mm, f/1.4. Camera locked completely still facing a section of the colossal 7-meter carved stone sword — the blade surface in razor-thin focus, ancient script etched along the blade length visible in deep carved channels, dust accumulation settled into the carved script channels, the stone surface with real-world weathering and organic asymmetry. Cold pale ambient from above catching the blade surface in contre-jour raking light, the raking light revealing the depth of the etched script dramatically. The blade edge in sharp cold rim light against the dark chamber behind it. All motion from fine dust particles drifting past the blade surface only. Audio: guardian's deep red hum from his body nearby, deep ambient cavern drone, atmospheric haze hissing.

Cut C (6-9s): Locked static wide shot of the daylight shaft cutting through atmospheric haze between columns, 35mm, f/2. Camera locked completely still facing across the chamber — the cold pale daylight shaft from the natural rock fissure above visible cutting diagonally through the atmospheric haze between two columns, the beam defined and visible as a shaft of cold light in the dark chamber. Dust particles drifting slowly through the beam catching the light and glowing briefly as they pass. The columns flanking the beam in shallow blur on either side. The surrounding chamber in cool shadow. All motion from dust drifting through the beam only — the beam itself steady. Audio: atmospheric haze hissing softly, dust drifting barely audible, deep ambient cavern drone, monk's distant throat-tone very faint.

Cut D (9-12s): Locked static medium shot looking up at the guardian's chest plate in three-quarter angle, 50mm, f/1.8. Camera locked completely still at floor level looking up at the guardian's massive carved stone chest plate — the chest plate filling much of the frame, the ancient armor carved in deep relief into the stone surface, individual armor plate details visible, carved decorative elements running across the chest surface, moss in the deepest crevices of the carved relief, dust accumulation across the surface. Cold pale ambient from above catching the upper edges of the chest plate in contre-jour cold rim light. Saturated red light from his eye-sockets far above washing downward across the chest plate surface as warm raking light from above. The chest plate extending beyond the frame edges on both sides — too wide to contain. All motion from atmospheric haze drifting past only. Audio: guardian's deep red hum emanating from his body filling the frame, deep ambient cavern drone, monk's distant throat-tone.

Cut E (12-16s): Locked static medium close on a column base where flagstone meets stone, 100mm macro, f/1.4. Camera locked completely still at floor level facing the base of a column — the junction between column base and ancient flagstone floor in razor-thin focus, the carved stone column base with centuries of weathering, moss growing at the base where stone meets floor, dust accumulation settled thick around the base, small debris pieces settled on the flagstone around it. The column base carved with ancient decorative relief at its lowest section. Cold pale ambient from above casting minimal light at floor level. Warm amber lamp glow from off-frame lamp above casting faint warm accent downward onto the column base. Fine dust particles drifting slowly past and settling at the base. All motion from drifting settling dust particles only. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone resonating through the stone floor, monk's distant throat-tone, guardian's deep red hum very faint, dust settling with near-inaudible whisper.

PACING: 16-second five-cut b-roll. All cuts fully locked static. Ancient staircase medium (0-3s), guardian's sword blade script close (3-6s), daylight shaft between columns wide (6-9s), guardian's chest plate medium from below (9-12s), column base at floor level macro (12-16s).

STYLE（风格）:8K IMAX。写实摄影，以实体胶片电影机拍摄。非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎。参考:埃马纽埃尔·罗泽基、罗杰·迪金斯。Lighting（灯光）:上方天然岩缝透下的冷白环境光作为主导基调、沿柱的暖琥珀色油灯作为现场实用光、守护者眼窝远处饱和的红作为远处次要红外罩。Color（色彩）:60:30:10——主导深冷蓝灰 / 次要饱和红 / 点缀暖琥珀。重度去饱和。实体变形宽银幕电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。角落暗角、边缘色差。60fps、8K。Audio（音频）:仅环境音效（SFX only）。无音乐、无字幕。

WORLD（世界）:由天然岩石开凿而成的辽阔洞穴圣殿。高大雕花石柱带古老刻字、暖琥珀色油灯、不规则石板地面、大气雾感成片。侧墙高处——一座神圣雕花石台、上一支孤烛、年迈僧侣静坐冥想。最远端坐着 30 米高的守护者。远处守护者深沉的红光嗡鸣全程可闻。

CAMERA（摄影机）:每一切都完全锁定在三脚架上静止。摄影机零移动。操作员零晃动。每个切镜头全程画面绝对静止。画内一切运动只来自环境本身。

Cut A（切 A）（0-3s）:锁定静止中景，落在凿入崖壁的古老阶梯上，50mm，f/1.8。摄影机完全锁定静止、正对自岩壁凿出的狭窄古老石阶——雕凿石阶清晰可见、带真实世界的风化与数百年磨损、阶上生苔、石上积了厚尘、阶梯沿崖壁螺旋上升、通向画面外上方僧侣的石台。上方冷白环境光只投下微光到阶梯上、上层台阶略显暗淡。僧侣的烛光几乎不可见，化作楼梯顶端远处深景深中一个幽淡的暖琥珀色小点。这座阶梯诉说着数百年仪式的使用。所有运动仅来自掠过的雾气。音频:深沉的环境洞穴低频、上方僧侣喉音几乎不可闻、大气雾感轻柔嘶鸣。

Cut B（切 B）（3-6s）:锁定静止中近景，落在守护者庞巨石剑的剑刃表面上，85mm，f/1.4。摄影机完全锁定静止、正对那柄 7 米量级雕花石剑的一段——剑刃表面剃刀般极薄对焦、沿剑身刻制的古老字铭可见于深凿的刻槽中、积尘灌进刻字槽、石面带真实世界的风化与有机不对称。上方冷白环境光以逆光斜掠光兜住剑刃表面、那斜掠光戏剧性地揭示出刻字铭文的深度。剑刃边缘在身后幽暗大厅前化作锐利的冷色轮廓光。所有运动仅来自掠过剑面的细尘粒。音频:守护者近旁身体传来深邃的红光嗡鸣、深沉环境洞穴低频、大气雾感嘶鸣。

Cut C（切 C）（6-9s）:锁定静止总镜头，落在那道切过柱间大气雾的日光柱上，35mm，f/2。摄影机完全锁定静止、正对大厅——上方天然岩缝透下的冷白日光柱可见、斜斜切过两柱之间的大气雾、在幽暗大厅中化作一道轮廓分明、清晰可见的冷光柱。尘埃微粒缓缓穿过光柱、掠过时接住光、短暂发光。两柱以浅景深夹在光柱两侧。周围大厅笼于冷色调阴影。所有运动仅来自穿柱飘动的尘埃——光柱本身稳定不动。音频:大气雾感轻柔嘶鸣、尘埃飘动几乎不可闻、深沉环境洞穴低频、僧侣幽远的喉音极微弱。

Cut D（切 D）（9-12s）:锁定静止中景，以三分之四角度仰望守护者的胸甲，50mm，f/1.8。摄影机完全锁定静止、置于地面级仰望守护者雄浑的雕花石胸甲——胸甲充满大半画面、古老装甲以深浮雕刻入石面、单个甲板细节清晰可见、雕凿的装饰元素横过胸口、雕浮最深裂隙中生苔、表面积尘。上方冷白环境光以逆光冷轮廓光兜住胸甲上缘。他上方远处眼窝的饱和红光自上方斜掠暖光、向下漫过胸甲表面。胸甲两侧越过画框边缘——宽到画框盛不下。所有运动仅来自掠过的雾气。音频:守护者周身散发、充满画面的深沉红光嗡鸣、深沉环境洞穴低频、僧侣幽远喉音。

Cut E（切 E）（12-16s）:锁定静止中近景，落在石板与岩石相接的柱基上，100mm 微距，f/1.4。摄影机完全锁定静止、置于地面级正对一根石柱的柱基——柱基与古老石板地面相接处剃刀般极薄对焦、雕花石柱基经数百年风化、石与地相接处柱根生苔、柱基四周积了厚尘、周边石板上落着零星小碎块。柱基最低一段以古老装饰浮雕雕凿。上方冷白环境光只投下微光到地面层。画外上方一盏油灯洒下淡淡的暖琥珀光、向下投到柱基上作微暖点缀。细尘粒缓缓飘过、落定在柱基处。所有运动仅来自飘动沉降的尘埃。音频:深沉环境洞穴低频透过石地板共振、僧侣幽远喉音、守护者深沉红光嗡鸣极微弱、尘埃沉降耳语般几乎无声。

PACING（节奏）:16 秒五切 b-roll。所有切全数锁定静止。古老阶梯中景（0-3s）、守护者剑刃刻字近景（3-6s）、柱间日光柱大全（6-9s）、自下仰望守护者胸甲中景（9-12s）、地面层柱基微距（12-16s）。
