## 原文 (source)

Ancient sanctum location b-roll set one, five cuts, 16 seconds total, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera. No 3D render, no game engine. References: Emmanuel Lubezki, Roger Deakins. Lighting: cold pale ambient from natural rock fissure above as dominant key, warm amber oil-lamps along columns as practicals, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets as distant secondary wash. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant deep cool blue-grey / secondary saturated red / accent warm amber. Heavily desaturated. Physical anamorphic cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. Corner vignette, edge chromatic aberration. 60fps, 8K. Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music, no subtitles.

WORLD: vast cavern-sanctum carved from natural rock. Towering carved stone columns with ancient carved script, warm amber oil-lamps, irregular flagstone floor, atmospheric haze patchy. High in side wall — sacred carved stone ledge with single candle, elderly monk seated in meditation, eyes closed, mouth open in sustained throat-tone. Distant guardian's deep red hum audible throughout.

Cut A (0-3s): Three-quarter handheld slow push-in down the colonnade into deep perspective, 35mm, f/2. Camera walking slowly forward — the colonnade opening in deep perspective as camera advances, columns flanking both sides with warm amber oil-lamps flickering as small practical lights receding into darkness. Cold pale daylight shaft cutting diagonally through atmospheric haze ahead, dust particles drifting through the beam. The distant guardian's seated form as a massive dark silhouette at the far end, his two eye-sockets visible as two faint red points deep in the perspective blur growing marginally larger as camera pushes in. Flagstone floor receding below. Frame breathes with walking pace. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, atmospheric haze hissing, oil-lamps flickering, monk's distant throat-tone faint, guardian's deep red hum distant.

Cut B (3-6s): Three-quarter handheld slow lateral drift past an oil-lamp mounted on a column, 85mm, f/1.4. Camera walking slowly sideways — the oil-lamp passing through frame, razor-thin focus on the warm amber flame flickering in atmospheric drafts, halation bloom around the flame catching the carved stone beside it, heat distortion above the flame visible subtly. Carved stone column surface scrolling laterally in shallow blur behind. Dust particles drifting through the warm beam. Cold blue-grey ambient contrasting with warm amber flame. Frame breathes with lateral walk. Audio: lamp flame flickering audibly, faint pop of flame, dust hissing, deep ambient cavern drone.

Cut C (6-9s): Three-quarter handheld slow tilt down from column mid-height to flagstone floor, 100mm macro, f/1.4. Camera beginning with ancient carved script in razor-thin focus at mid-height, tilting slowly downward — carved stone surface scrolling upward through frame, passing column base, arriving at flagstone floor in extreme detail. Floor reveals real-world weathering, deep cracks between joints, moss in crevices, dust accumulation, small debris settled. Single contemplative downward journey from inscription to floor. Frame breathes with operator throughout. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, monk's throat-tone distant, dust settling with near-inaudible whisper.

Cut D (9-12s): Three-quarter handheld slow push-in toward monk's ledge from across chamber, 85mm, f/1.4. Camera walking slowly forward — the ledge growing marginally larger, the elderly monk in dark maroon robes visible in meditation, eyes closed, mouth open in sustained throat-tone, completely motionless. Single warm amber candle flame beside him. Rock overhang above sheltering him. Cold pale ambient catching ledge in contre-jour rim, candle casting warm amber across his face from below. Lag focus pulling gently toward monk as camera approaches. Frame breathes with walking pace. Audio: monk's sustained throat-tone growing marginally clearer as camera approaches, candle barely audible, deep ambient cavern drone.

Cut E (12-16s): Three-quarter handheld slow lateral drift combined with slight pull-back revealing sanctum depth, 50mm, f/1.8. Camera walking sideways while stepping slightly backward — column drifting laterally through frame as camera moves away, ancient carved script visible in razor-thin focus as it passes. Frame opening up as camera pulls back — more sanctum visible, columns appearing in shallow blur on both sides, chamber depth revealed. Atmospheric haze visible in opening field. Distant guardian's eye-sockets briefly visible as two faint red points deep in background as frame opens. Frame breathes with combined movement. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, atmospheric haze hissing, monk's distant throat-tone, guardian's deep red hum very faint emerging as frame opens.

## 中文对照 (zh)

古老圣所地点 B-roll 第一套，五个镜头，总时长 16 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX。写实摄影，以实体电影摄影机拍摄。非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎。References（参考）：埃曼纽尔·卢贝兹基、罗杰·戴金斯。Lighting（灯光）：上方天然岩缝的冷白环境光作为主导主光、沿柱的暖琥珀油灯作实用光、守护者眼窝的饱和红光作远处次级洗。Color（色彩）：60:30:10——主导深冷蓝灰 / 次级饱和红 / 点缀暖琥珀。重度去饱和。物理变形宽银幕电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。暗角、边缘色差。60fps，8K。Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（SFX only）。无音乐、无字幕。

WORLD（世界）：由天然岩石凿成的巨型洞窟-圣所。高耸的雕刻石柱带古老刻文、暖琥珀油灯、不规则石板地面、大气雾霭斑驳。侧壁高处——一块神圣的雕花石崖带一支蜡烛、年长僧人结跏趺坐冥想、双眼紧闭、嘴张开维持喉音。全程可闻守护者远处的深红嗡鸣。

Cut A（0-3s）【镜头 A】：3/4 手持沿柱廊缓缓推近入深邃透视，35mm，f/2。摄影机缓慢向前走——柱廊随摄影机前进在深透中打开、两侧石柱带暖琥珀油灯作为小小的实用光点退入黑暗、细微摇曳。前方冷白日光柱斜切大气雾霭、微尘粒子飘过光束。远端守护者的坐姿作为巨大的暗色剪影、他两只眼窝在深透虚化中作为两粒微淡红点可见、随摄影机推近而略略变大。石板地面在下方退去。画面随行走步调呼吸。Audio（音频）：深沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣、大气雾霭嘶嘶、油灯摇曳、僧人远处喉音微弱、守护者深红嗡鸣遥远。

Cut B（3-6s）【镜头 B】：3/4 手持缓慢侧向漂移、掠过一盏柱面油灯，85mm，f/1.4。摄影机缓慢侧向走——油灯掠入画面、极浅焦点落在气流中摇曳的暖琥珀火焰、火焰周围辉散（halation）泛光抓住旁侧雕花石、火焰上方有可微见的空气热畸变。身后的雕花柱面在浅虚中横向滚动。微尘粒子飘过暖光束。冷蓝灰环境光与暖琥珀火焰相对照。画面随侧向行走呼吸。Audio（音频）：灯焰可闻地摇曳、火焰微弱的噼啪、尘埃嘶嘶、深沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣。

Cut C（6-9s）【镜头 C】：3/4 手持从柱中段缓慢向下俯摇至石板地面，100mm 微距，f/1.4。摄影机开场以极浅焦点落在中段的古代刻文上、缓缓向下俯摇——雕花石面向上滚过画面、掠过柱基、抵达石板地面极尽细节。地面显露出真实风化、接缝间深裂纹、裂隙中的苔藓、积尘、沉降的小碎屑。一次自铭文到地面的沉思式下行之旅。画面全程随操机员呼吸。Audio（音频）：深沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣、僧人远处喉音、尘埃近乎不可闻地低语沉降。

Cut D（9-12s）【镜头 D】：3/4 手持自厅堂对面缓缓推近僧人的崖台，85mm，f/1.4。摄影机缓慢向前走——崖台微微变大、年长僧人深暗酒红僧袍可见于冥想、双眼紧闭、嘴张开维持喉音、完全纹丝不动。他身旁一支暖琥珀烛焰。上方岩檐庇护着他。冷白环境光以逆光轮廓光（contre-jour rim）抓住崖台、暖烛自下方把琥珀光洒过他的脸。摄影机接近时跟焦迟滞轻柔地向僧人拉。画面随行走步调呼吸。Audio（音频）：摄影机接近时僧人的持续喉音微微变得更清晰、烛光几不可闻、深沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣。

Cut E（12-16s）【镜头 E】：3/4 手持缓慢侧向漂移结合轻微后拉揭示圣所纵深，50mm，f/1.8。摄影机侧向行走同时略微后退——石柱随摄影机退后横向掠过画面、古代刻文极浅焦点清晰可见地掠过。画面随摄影机后拉而打开——更多圣所可见、两侧浅虚中石柱出现、厅堂纵深被揭示。打开的视野中大气雾霭可见。画面打开时守护者的眼窝短暂可见为深背景中的两粒微淡红点。画面随结合的运动呼吸。Audio（音频）：深沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣、大气雾霭嘶嘶、僧人远处喉音、画面打开时守护者深红嗡鸣极微弱地浮现。
