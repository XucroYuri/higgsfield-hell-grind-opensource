## 原文 (source)

Multi-shot sequence connected by hard cuts, 12 seconds total, 
anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, shallow-to-medium depth of field, 
in the style of Denis Villeneuve's "Dune" — sacred scale and intimate 
observation. Three internal shots separated by clean hard cuts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT A — TEAM HELPS ROCO UP — 0.0s to 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
Active organic handheld, anamorphic 35mm wide lens, slight low angle. 
Operator positioned in front of the team. Frame: medium three-shot.

[LIGHT]
Cool blue ambient from above mixed with warm orange candle pools at 
floor level, deep shadows.

[LOCATION]
Cave-temple interior, stone columns and carved walls visible, mist 
in the air, candles flickering at column bases.

[CHARACTERS]
- ROCO (CENTER): messy shaggy black hair, faint mustache, dust on 
  face, small red adhesive bandage strip on nose, dark weathered 
  tactical jacket with shevron panels, dark cargo pants, red bandana 
  on right wrist, fingerless gloves, red bear keychain on back belt.
- JAXX (RIGHT): bleached blonde hair, dark skin, clear safety goggles 
  on forehead, dark tactical jacket with gray panels, olive cargo 
  pants.
- REIN (LEFT): silver-and-black bobbed hair, scar on right cheekbone, 
  dark gray tactical jacket, black turtleneck, dark cargo pants, 
  thigh holster.

All dusty.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Roco kneeling on one knee on stone floor, dust around him. 
       Jaxx and Rein flanking, reaching down — each grabbing one of 
       his forearms.
1.5s — They PULL Roco up. He rises, finds balance, shakes his head 
       once to clear it.
3.0s — All three slowly look around, scanning the space. Heads 
       turning, taking in the scale.
5.0s — Their gazes drift upward, jaws slightly slack.

HARD CUT AT 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT B — DETAIL OF CANDLES — 5.0s to 8.5s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld extreme close-up, 50mm lens, very shallow 
depth of field. Low angle near the stone floor.

[LIGHT]
Warm orange candle flame glow as primary light, blue ambient falling 
away in background, dust particles in warm light.

[ACTION]
5.0s — Extreme close-up on small clay oil lamp candles at the base 
       of a stone column. Multiple flames flickering in a row, casting 
       warm orange light on carved stone.
7.0s — Camera holds, candles flicker, dust drifting through warm 
       light.
8.5s — Frame steady on the candles glowing in deep blue darkness.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, looking up along the carved stone column

[LIGHT]
Warm candle glow at the base, deep blue mist above, the column 
disappearing into darkness.

[ACTION]
8.5s — Frame at the base of the column, carved Hindu-Buddhist details

10.0s — Camera tilts slowly upward, following the column rising.

12.0s — Frame ends looking up into the vast dark space above, the 
        column vanishing into the misty heights.

## 中文对照 (zh)

多镜头序列由硬切（hard cuts）连接，总时长 12 秒，变形宽银幕 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，浅至中等景深，丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦式「沙丘」风格——神圣尺度与亲密观察并存。三个内部镜头以干净的硬切分隔。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT A —— 团队扶 ROCO 起身 —— 0.0s 至 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]（摄影机）
主动式有机手持，变形宽银幕 35mm 广角镜头，轻微低角度。操机员位于团队前方。取景：中景三人镜头（three-shot）。

[LIGHT]（灯光）
上方冷蓝环境光与地面层暖橙烛光池相混，深阴影。

[LOCATION]（场景地点）
洞窟-寺庙内部，石柱与雕花墙可见，空气中有雾，柱基蜡烛摇曳。

[CHARACTERS]（角色）
- ROCO（中央）：凌乱蓬松的黑发、淡胡须、脸上带灰、鼻梁一条红色小创可贴、带雪佛龙面板的深色风化战术夹克、深色工装裤、右手腕红色头巾、露指手套、后腰带红色小熊钥匙扣。
- JAXX（右）：漂白金发、深色皮肤、额头透明护目镜、带灰色面板的深色战术夹克、橄榄色工装裤。
- REIN（左）：银黑波波头、右颧骨有道疤、深灰色战术夹克、黑色高领衫、深色工装裤、大腿枪套。

全都沾满灰尘。

[ACTION]（动作）
0.0s——ROCO 单膝跪在石板地面上、周围带尘。JAXX 与 REIN 分列两侧、俯身向下——各抓住他一条前臂。
1.5s——他们**拉** ROCO 起身。他站起来、找到平衡、甩了一下头让自己清醒过来。
3.0s——三人缓缓环视、扫视空间。转头、领会那片尺度。
5.0s——他们的目光向上飘去、下巴微松。

5.0s HARD CUT（硬切）
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]（摄影机）
细微有机手持极端特写，50mm 镜头，极浅景深。贴近石板地面的低角度。

[LIGHT]（灯光）
暖橙烛焰光辉作为主光，背景中蓝环境光渐退，暖光中微尘粒子。

细微有机手持，低角度沿雕花石柱向上看。摄影机缓缓向上仰摇。

[LIGHT]（灯光）
柱基暖烛辉光、上方深蓝雾、石柱消入黑暗。
