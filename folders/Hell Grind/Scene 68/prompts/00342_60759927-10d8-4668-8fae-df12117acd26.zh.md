## 原文 (source)

Multi-shot sequence connected by hard cuts, 12 seconds total. Four 
internal shots separated by clean hard cuts (no fade, no dissolve).

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY]

FORMAT: Anamorphic 2.39:1. 35mm film grain. Arri Alexa with anamorphic 
lenses. Horizontal anamorphic lens flares from candles.

COLOR PALETTE: Cool desaturated — deep blue-blacks, charcoal grays, 
slate stone. Warm orange candle pools at floor. Dust beige on gear. 
~5500K cool blue ambient, ~2700K warm candles. Monk's burgundy red 
robe and orange sash the only saturated colors besides Roco's red 
bandana, nose bandage, red bear keychain. STATUE STILL DORMANT — 
no glowing red lines yet in this prompt.

LIGHTING: Single dominant cool blue shaft from cave opening. Hundreds 
of warm candle pools. High-contrast chiaroscuro. Heavy mist and dust. 
Faint pale beam touching the monk on the statue's hands.

CAMERA: Organic handheld throughout. Operator breathing, slight 
bounces. NEVER static, NEVER smooth gimbal.

LOCATION: Massive ancient cave-temple. Monolithic stone columns 
rising into darkness. Heavy mist. Stone floor. The colossal seated 
guardian statue at center, holding the small monk on its open palms. 
The team standing some distance from the statue, looking up.

CHARACTERS:
- THE MONK: elderly Asian monk in his 60s-70s, short cropped gray 
  hair (not bald), deeply weathered face with profound wrinkles, 
  calm serene expression, dark brown ancient eyes. Deep burgundy red 
  Tibetan-style monk robe wrapped across the body, orange cloth 
  sash at waist, dark gray inner garment visible at collar, simple 
  worn sandals on bare feet. Sitting cross-legged in lotus position 
  on the colossal stone hands. NO glasses.
- ROCO (CENTER): messy shaggy black hair, faint mustache, olive 
  skin, red bandage on nose, dark tactical jacket with shevron 
  panels, red bandana on right wrist, red bear keychain.
- JAXX (RIGHT): bleached blonde hair, dark skin, clear safety goggles 
  on forehead, scar on right temple, dark tactical jacket with gray 
  panels, olive cargo pants.
- REIN (LEFT): silver-and-black bobbed hair, scar on right cheekbone, 
  dark gray tactical jacket, black turtleneck, dark cargo pants, 
  thigh holster.

REFERENCES: Denis Villeneuve and Greig Fraser ("Dune") sacred scale, 
Blade Runner 2049 temple sequences. NOT CGI-fantasy.

SHOT A — TEAM STANDING BEFORE STATUE — 0.0s to 2.5s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 35mm wide lens, low angle from behind the team. They stand 
in foreground, statue and monk visible far ahead.
[ACTION]
0.0s — Three figures from behind, standing still some distance from 
       the colossal statue, looking up. Temple silent.
1.5s — Roco steps slightly forward, head tilted up. Rein and Jaxx 
       still flanking, alert.
2.5s — They wait. Silence.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT — SHOT B — CLOSE-UP MONK BEGINS TO SING — 2.5s to 6.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 100mm telephoto, very shallow focus, slight low angle on 
the monk. Subtle organic handheld.
[ACTION]
2.5s — Tight close-up on the elderly monk's serene weathered face 
       on the stone hands, eyes closed in meditation, deep wrinkles 
       in faint pale light, motionless.
3.5s — His lips slowly part. A deep low resonant tone begins — the 
       first low note of the mantra emerging from him, building from 
       silence into a deep guttural drone vibrating through the cave.
6.0s — Frame ends on the monk's open mouth, the low note 
       reverberating, his expression still serene, eyes still closed.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT — SHOT C — TEAM REACTS — 6.0s to 9.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 50mm lens, medium three-shot of the team from the front, 
chest level. Subtle handheld.
[ACTION]
6.0s — Roco, Jaxx, and Rein in frame, faces lit by cool blue and 
       warm candle. The low mantra fills the cave around them. They 
       tense — heads slightly turning, listening.
7.5s — Roco's jaw tightens. His hand drifts toward his belt. Rein's 
       hand moves to her thigh holster. Jaxx pulls the safety goggles 
       down over his eyes in one quick motion.
9.0s — Frame ends on the team in combat-ready stance.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT — SHOT D — DUST FALLING FROM CEILING — 9.0s to 12.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 35mm lens, slight low angle looking up into the dark heights 
of the cave. Subtle organic handheld.
[ACTION]
9.0s — Looking up into the misty cave ceiling. Deep mantra continues 
       to vibrate through the space. Small streams of stone dust begin 
       to fall from above, catching the blue shaft of light.
10.5s — More dust falls. A small pebble loosens and tumbles down 
        through the shaft of blue light, glittering as it falls.
12.0s — Frame ends as more debris begins to descend, the mantra 
        deepening.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, CONTINUOUS]
No music, no score, no instruments. Vast cave ambient hum, soft

cave, growing in volume, shaking the air. Faint sound of falling 
stone dust and small pebbles tumbling.

## 中文对照 (zh)

多镜头序列由硬切（hard cuts）衔接，总时长 12 秒。四个内部镜头由干净的硬切分隔（无淡入淡出、无溶解转场）。

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY（视觉一致性）]

FORMAT（格式）：变形宽银幕 2.39:1。35mm 胶片颗粒。Arri Alexa 配合变形宽银幕镜头。来自蜡烛的水平向变形宽银幕镜头眩光（lens flares）。

COLOR PALETTE（色彩基调）：冷调去饱和——深蓝黑、炭灰、板岩石色。地面层的暖橙色烛光光池。装备上的尘土米色。约 5500K 冷调蓝环境光、约 2700K 暖调蜡烛。武僧的酒红色僧袍与橙色腰带是除 Roco 的红色头巾、鼻贴、红色小熊钥匙扣之外仅有的饱和色彩。雕像仍处休眠（STATUE STILL DORMANT）——本 prompt 中尚无发光的红线。

LIGHTING（灯光）：来自洞穴开口的单一主导冷调蓝光束。数百处暖烛光池。高对比度的明暗对照（chiaroscuro）。厚重的雾气与尘土。一道微弱的苍白光柱触及雕像掌上的武僧。

CAMERA（摄影机）：全程有机手持。摄影师呼吸、轻微起伏。绝不静止、绝不平滑云台。

LOCATION（场景）：巨大的远古洞穴神庙。独石（monolithic）石柱耸入黑暗。厚重的雾气。石质地面。殿堂中央坐落着庞大的守护者雕像、在张开的手掌上托着那位小武僧。队伍站在距雕像一定距离处、仰望着。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
- THE MONK（武僧）：年迈的亚洲老僧，约六七十岁，短发灰色寸发（并非秃顶）、面容被岁月深深刻蚀、皱纹深重、神情平静安详、深褐色苍老的眼睛。深酒红色的藏式僧袍裹身、腰间橙色布腰带、衣领处露出一件深灰色内袍、赤足穿着简单的旧凉鞋。他以莲坐姿（lotus position）盘腿坐在那巨大的石掌上。绝无眼镜。
- ROCO（中央）：凌乱蓬松的黑发、淡淡的胡须、橄榄色皮肤、鼻上红贴、深色战术外套带 V 形肩章片、右手腕系红色头巾、红色小熊钥匙扣。
- JAXX（右侧）：漂浅的金发、深色皮肤、额头架着透明护目镜、右太阳穴有一道疤、深色战术外套带灰色拼接片、橄榄色工装裤。
- REIN（左侧）：银黑相间的齐肩短发、右颧骨有一道疤、深灰色战术外套、黑色高领衫、深色工装裤、大腿枪套。

REFERENCES（参考）：丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦（Denis Villeneuve）与格雷格·弗雷泽（Greig Fraser）（《沙丘》Dune）的神圣尺度、《银翼杀手 2049》的神庙序列。绝非 CGI 奇幻。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA（摄影机）] 35mm 广角镜头，从队伍后方低角度。他们立于前景、雕像与武僧在远处清晰可见。

硬切（HARD CUT）— SHOT B（镜头 B）— 特写：武僧开始吟唱 — 2.5s 至 6.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA（摄影机）] 100mm 长焦、极浅对焦、对武僧轻微低角度。细微有机的手持。
[ACTION（动作）]

3.5s — 他的嘴唇缓缓张开。一道深沉低回的共鸣音开始——咒语（mantra）的第一个低音从他那升起，从寂静中积聚成一道低沉喉音的嗡鸣、震颤贯穿整个洞穴。
6.0s — 画面以武僧张开的嘴结束、低音在回响、他的表情依然安详、眼睛依然闭着。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[ACTION（动作）]
6.0s — Roco、Jaxx 与 Rein 在画面中、面容被冷蓝与暖烛光照亮。那道低沉咒语灌满他们四周的洞穴。他们绷紧——微微转头、倾听。
7.5s — Roco 的下颚绷紧。他的手朝他的腰带悄然移去。Rein 的手伸向大腿枪套。Jaxx 一个干脆的动作把护目镜拉下拉过眼睛。
9.0s — 画面以队伍处于戒备战斗姿态而结束。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

9.0s — 仰望着雾蒙蒙的洞穴天花板。深沉的咒语继续在整个空间里震颤。细小的石尘流开始自上而下坠落、捕捉到那道蓝色光柱。
