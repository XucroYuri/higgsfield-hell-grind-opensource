## 原文 (source)

Multi-shot sequence connected by hard cuts, 12 seconds total. Four 
internal shots separated by clean hard cuts (no fade, no dissolve). 
Every shot uses non-frontal angle — directorial framing only.

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY]

FORMAT: Anamorphic 2.39:1. 35mm film grain. Arri Alexa with anamorphic 
lenses. Horizontal anamorphic lens flares from blue shaft and candles.

COLOR PALETTE: Cool desaturated — deep blue-blacks, charcoal grays, 
slate stone. Warm orange candle pools. Dust beige on gear. ~5500K 
cool blue ambient, ~2700K warm candles. NO saturated colors except 
small red bandana on Roco's wrist, red nose bandage, red bear keychain, 
and faint purple circuit lines / purple underglow on Rein's hoverboard.

LIGHTING: Single dramatic shaft of cool blue light from high cave 
opening. Hundreds of warm candle pools at column bases. High-contrast 
chiaroscuro. Heavy mist, dust in shaft of light.

CAMERA: Organic handheld throughout. Operator breathing, slight 
bounces. NEVER static, NEVER smooth gimbal. Top-down shot has 
handheld presence — slight breathing.

LOCATION: Massive ancient cave-temple. Monolithic stone columns 
rising on both sides into darkness. Hindu-Buddhist carvings. Heavy 
mist. Stone floor with cracked slabs, prayer beads, stone fragments. 
Far end — colossal seated guardian statue, dormant, holding the monk 
on its open palms. NO glowing lines yet.

CHARACTERS:
- ROCO (CENTER): messy shaggy black hair, faint mustache, olive skin, 
  red bandage on nose, dark tactical jacket with shevron panels, dark 
  cargo pants, red bandana on right wrist, fingerless gloves, red 
  bear keychain on back belt.
- JAXX (RIGHT): bleached blonde hair, dark skin, clear safety goggles 
  on forehead, scar on right temple, dark tactical jacket with gray 
  panels, olive cargo pants.
- REIN (LEFT): silver-and-black bobbed hair, scar on right cheekbone, 
  dark gray tactical jacket, black turtleneck, dark cargo pants, 
  thigh holster. Carrying weathered longboard-style hoverboard with 
  purple circuit lines on dark gray distressed wood top, circular 
  electromagnetic thrusters underneath with copper coils and warm 
  orange cores, faint purple energy underglow, DIY repairs visible.

REFERENCES: In the style of Denis Villeneuve and Greig Fraser ("Dune") 
— sacred scale through framing. Roger Deakins' use of foreground 
elements and silhouettes. NEVER frontal, NEVER centered hero shots.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT A — LOW PROFILE THROUGH COLUMN BASE — 0.0s to 3.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 35mm lens, EXTREME LOW ANGLE near the stone floor, planted 
at the base of a massive column. Foreground: rough weathered stone 
of the column edge dominating the LEFT side of frame in deep 
out-of-focus blur. Through the gap, we see the team walking deeper 
into the temple in profile from the SIDE, NOT frontal. Candle pools 
glowing warm at floor level. Subtle handheld breathing.
[ACTION]
0.0s — Frame opens with the column edge in foreground blur, the team 
       just entering frame from the right, walking left-to-right in 
       profile, slow steps. Rein's hoverboard catches a faint purple 
       glow as she passes.
2.0s — They walk through the gap, dust trailing, their silhouettes 
       passing column after column behind them.
3.0s — Frame ends as Roco's profile passes the column edge, his face 
       briefly catching warm candle light from below.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT — SHOT B — TOP-DOWN OVERHEAD — 3.0s to 6.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 24mm wide lens, EXTREME high overhead angle looking straight 
down. Subtle organic handheld presence — slight breathing, NOT smooth 
drone.
[ACTION]
3.0s — Top-down overhead shot of the entire temple. Rows of monolithic 
       stone columns in parallel, hundreds of warm candle flames forming 
       glowing lines along their bases. Shaft of cool blue light cutting 
       through center. Three TINY figures walking along the central 
       aisle in formation, Rein's hoverboard a small purple-glowing dot. 
       Dust trailing. Colossal guardian statue at far end, head and 
       shoulders visible from above, monk a tiny figure on its hands.
5.0s — Hold. Candles flicker. Mist drifts. Figures continue, 
       imperceptibly small.
6.0s — Frame ends, scale fully read.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT — SHOT C — DUTCH ANGLE BETWEEN COLUMNS — 6.0s to 8.5s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 24mm ultra-wide lens, EXTREME LOW ANGLE with strong DUTCH 
TILT (camera rotated ~15 degrees), framing two massive columns 
diagonally cutting the frame. Organic handheld.
[ACTION]
6.0s — Two colossal stone columns dominate the frame at a diagonal, 
       receding into misty heights, the shaft of blue light slashing 
       between them at an angle. Carved details, candles glowing warm 
       at the base.
7.0s — The team's silhouettes pass through the lower right of frame, 
       walking between the columns, tiny against towering stone. 
       Rein's hoverboard purple underglow catches as they pass.
8.5s — Frame holds on the empty diagonal corridor, columns rising 
       into darkness, the unease of the dutch angle hanging.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT — SHOT D — OVER-SHOULDER SILHOUETTE OF ROCO — 8.5s to 12.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 50mm lens, OVER THE SHOULDER from behind Roco's left side, 
shallow focus. Roco's head and shoulder dominate the LEFT FOREGROUND 
of the frame as a dark backlit silhouette, slightly out of focus. 
The vast temple opens beyond him in deep focus — the colossal statue 
revealed at the far end through this point-of-view framing. Subtle 
handheld breathing.
[ACTION]
8.5s — Roco's silhouette in foreground left — his messy hair, the 
       outline of his shoulder, a fragment of his red bandana 
       wrist visible at frame edge. Beyond him, the temple opens up — 
       the colossal seated guardian statue revealed at the far end 
       in the shaft of blue light, holding the small monk on its 
       open palms.
10.5s — Roco's head turns very slightly — taking in the scale. His 
        breathing visible in his shoulders.
12.0s — Frame ends on the silhouette frame composition — Roco's 
        weighted presence in foreground, the impossible scale of 
        the statue and monk far ahead through his POV.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, CONTINUOUS]
No music, no score. Synchronized echo of three pairs of boots on 
stone reverberating in vast space, very faint electromagnetic hum 
of Rein's hoverboard thrusters at idle, distant water drip echoing, 
soft crackle of countless candles, faint whisper of mist, low cave 
ambient hum, their breathing — in the final shot Roco's breathing 
slightly more present.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no instruments, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, 
no static dead camera, no stabilization, no clean digital look, no 
aerial drone (top-down must be handheld-feel), no fade between shots, 
no dissolve, no soft transition, no frontal centered hero shots, no 
direct head-on framing of characters, no symmetrical eye-level medium 
shots, no saturated colors except specified reds and faint purple, 
no bright sun, no daylight, no glowing red lines on statue (DORMANT), 
no statue moving, no transformation, no combat, no weapons drawn, no 
Rein flying yet, no hoverboard activated to full thrust, no removal 
of red nose bandage, no character identity drift, no facial hair 
drift on Roco, no clean futuristic hoverboard, no text, no captions, 
no logos.

## 中文对照 (zh)

多镜头序列由硬切（hard cuts）连接，总时长 12 秒。四个内部镜头以干净的硬切分隔（无淡入淡出，无溶接）。每个镜头均采用非正面角度——仅导演式取景。

[VISUAL CONSISTENCY]（视觉一致性）

FORMAT（格式）：变形宽银幕 2.39:1。35mm 胶片颗粒。Arri Alexa 配变形宽银幕镜头。来自蓝光柱与蜡烛的水平向变形宽银幕镜头光斑。

COLOR PALETTE（色彩基调）：冷调去饱和——深蓝黑、炭灰、板岩灰。暖橙烛光池。装备上尘埃米黄。约 5500K 冷蓝环境光、约 2700K 暖蜡烛。除 ROCO 手腕的红色小头巾、红色鼻贴、红色小熊钥匙扣及 REIN 悬滑板的淡紫电路线/紫色底光外，无任何饱和色。

LIGHTING（灯光）：来自洞窟高位开口的一道戏剧性冷蓝光柱。柱基数百处暖烛光池。高反差明暗对比（chiaroscuro）。浓雾、光柱中的尘埃。

CAMERA（摄影机）：全程有机手持。操机员呼吸、轻微弹跳。绝不停滞，绝不用平滑云台。俯拍镜头带手持在场感——轻微呼吸。

LOCATION（场景地点）：巨型古老洞窟-庙宇。两侧整石巨柱升入黑暗。印度-佛教雕饰。浓雾。石板地面带龟裂板面、念珠、石头碎片。远端——巨硕坐姿守护者雕像、休眠、张开的双掌托着僧人。此刻无发光线条。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
- ROCO（中央）：凌乱蓬松的黑发、淡胡须、橄榄色皮肤、鼻上红色创可贴、带雪佛龙面板的深色战术夹克、深色工装裤、右手腕红色头巾、露指手套、后腰带红色小熊钥匙扣。
- JAXX（右）：漂白金发、深色皮肤、额头透明护目镜、右太阳穴有道疤、带灰色面板的深色战术夹克、橄榄色工装裤。
- REIN（左）：银黑波波头、右颧骨有道疤、深灰色战术夹克、黑色高领衫、深色工装裤、大腿枪套。带着深灰做旧木面、紫色电路线的风化长板式悬滑板、下方圆电磁推进器带铜线圈与暖橙核心、淡紫能量底光、可见 DIY 维修痕迹。

REFERENCES（参考）：丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦与格雷格·弗莱瑟（〈沙丘〉）——透过取景营造的神圣尺度。罗杰·戴金斯对前景元素与剪影的运用。绝不正面，绝不居中英雄镜头。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT A —— 穿过柱基的低姿态 —— 0.0s 至 3.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 35mm 镜头，近石地板的**极端低角度**，栽在一根巨柱基部。前景：柱缘粗糙风化石材在深失焦虚化中主导画面**左侧**。透过间隙，我们看到团队以**侧影**（从侧面、非正面）走入寺庙深处。烛光池在地面高度暖洋洋地发光。细微的手持呼吸。
[ACTION]（动作）
0.0s——画面开场柱缘在前景虚化中、团队刚自右侧入画、以侧影自左向右走、脚步缓慢。REIN 滑过的瞬间其悬滑板捕到一点淡紫光辉。
2.0s——他们穿过间隙、尘埃拖尾、他们的剪影身后一柱接一柱掠过。
3.0s——画面结束于 Roco 的侧影掠过柱缘、他的脸瞬间自下方捕捉到暖烛光。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT —— SHOT B —— 俯拍顶视 —— 3.0s 至 6.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 24mm 广角镜头，极端高空俯角垂直向下看。细微的有机手持在场感——轻微呼吸、非平滑无人机。
[ACTION]（动作）
3.0s——整座寺庙的俯拍顶视镜头。成排整石巨柱平行、数百簇暖烛火沿其基部形成发光的线条。一道冷蓝光柱切穿中央。三个**微小**的人影沿中央通道列队行走、REIN 的悬滑板是一个小小的紫光点。尘埃拖尾。远端巨硕守护者雕像、自上方可见头与肩、僧人是它掌上的小小人影。
5.0s——保持。蜡烛闪烁。雾气飘移。人形继续、渺小得不可察觉。
6.0s——画面结束、尺度完全读出。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT —— SHOT C —— 柱间的荷兰角 —— 6.0s 至 8.5s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 24mm 超广角镜头、带强烈**荷兰倾斜（DUTCH TILT）**的极端低角度（摄影机旋转约 15 度）、取景使两根巨柱对角切分画面。有机手持。
[ACTION]（动作）
6.0s——两根巨硕石柱以对角线主导画面、退入雾霭高处、蓝光柱以角度斜劈在它们之间。雕饰细节、烛光在柱基暖洋洋地燃。
7.0s——团队的剪影穿过画面右下方、在柱间行走、衬着高耸石石**渺小**。REIN 悬滑板的紫底光在他们经过时被捕捉。
8.5s——画面停留在空荡的对角走廊上、柱列升入黑暗、荷兰角的不安悬着。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT —— SHOT D —— ROCO 的过肩剪影 —— 8.5s 至 12.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[CAMERA] 50mm 镜头，自 ROCO 左肩后方**过肩（OVER THE SHOULDER）**，浅焦点。ROCO 的头与肩作为暗色逆光剪影主导画面**左侧前景**、略失焦。他身外巨硕寺庙在深焦点中展开——巨硕雕像透过这俯视取景在远端被揭示。细微的手持呼吸。
[ACTION]（动作）
8.5s——ROCO 在前景左侧的剪影——他凌乱的头发、肩膀的轮廓、画面边缘可见他腕部红头巾的一角。他身外、寺庙展开——巨硕坐姿守护者雕像在蓝光柱中于远端被揭示、张开的双掌托着小小的僧人。
10.5s——ROCO 的头极轻微转动——领会着那尺度。他的呼吸在肩头可见。
12.0s——画面结束于剪影框构图——ROCO 在前景中带着分量的在场、透过他的视点、雕像与僧人那不可能的尺度远在前方。

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, CONTINUOUS]（声音——仅剧情内音，持续）
无音乐、无配乐。三双靴子在石头上的同步回声在广阔空间回响、REIN 悬滑板推进器怠速时极微弱的电磁嗡鸣、远处滴水回响、无数蜡烛轻柔的噼啪、薄雾微弱的呢喃、低沉的洞穴环境嗡鸣、他们的呼吸——末镜中 Roco 的呼吸略微更在场。

[NEGATIVE]（反面清单）
无音乐、无配乐、无乐器，无慢动作，无平滑云台，无静止的死摄像机，无稳定化，无干净的数字质感，无航拍无人机（俯拍必须保持手持感），镜头间无淡入淡出，无溶接，无柔和转场，无正面居中的英雄镜头，无正对直拍人物的取景，无对称的眼平中景镜头，除指定的红色与淡紫色外无饱和色，无明亮的太阳，无日光，雕像上无发光红线（休眠），雕像不动，无变身，无战斗，无拔武器，REIN 尚不飞，悬滑板不激活到全力推进，不得摘除红色鼻贴，无角色身份漂移，ROCO 无胡须漂移，无干净的未来感悬滑板，无文字，无字幕，无标识。
