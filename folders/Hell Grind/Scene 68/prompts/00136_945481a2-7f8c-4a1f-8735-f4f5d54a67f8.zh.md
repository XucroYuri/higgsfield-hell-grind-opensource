## 原文 (source)

[CAMERA]
⚠️ CAMERA OPERATION — HEAVY HANDHELD, BODY-PRESENT, OPERATOR CHASING: the camera is clearly carried on a human body — operator's gait, breath, weight transfer all visible in the frame's micro-motion. Frame is ALWAYS ALIVE in the operator's hands, never locked off, never on a tripod, never on a gimbal-smooth float. Reference operation: SAVING PRIVATE RYAN battlefield, CHILDREN OF MEN one-takes, THE REVENANT chase sequences, ROGUE ONE corridor handheld. Operator MOVES with the action — runs when the actor runs (matched gait, frame trembling on each footfall), drops when the actor drops, holds his weight low when the action goes to ground. Constant subtle reframing as he tracks the performer. Every frame has visible handheld signature: small unpredictable jitters, drift correction, weight settling. Camera operation is RAW and PHYSICAL — never aerial, never sweeping crane, never stabilized.

[LIGHT]
Cool blue ambient from above mixed with warm orange candle pools at
floor level, deep shadows, dust particles drifting in mixed light.
LOW-KEY HIGH CONTRAST — crushed deep blacks dominate the corridor, only the torch-pools and the wet floor reflections carry highlight detail. Faces sit largely in shadow with only flickering rim from torch passes. Atmospheric perspective — the corridor's depth dissolves into smoky torch-haze.

Composition: rule of thirds + golden ratio. The descending slab anchors the deep frame as a vertical / dropping graphic block, the gap underneath as the dramatic focal aperture.

⚠️ COLOR PALETTE — INHERITED FROM REFERENCE: tonal direction only — warm torchlight orange/amber as the only chromatic source, deep crushed-charcoal shadow dominant, wet stone reading as cold neutral grey-black where unlit. Do NOT impose a teal/orange Hollywood grade, do NOT push toward green, do NOT add blue daylight. Faithful to the location reference.

[LIGHT]
Cool blue ambient from above mixed with warm orange candle pools at

[STAGING — STRICT]
Roco lies FACE DOWN on his stomach in the CENTER of the frame on the
dusty stone floor, head turned slightly to one side, cheek against
the stone, arms loose beside his body.
JAXX is on the RIGHT of Roco (camera-right, screen-right).
REIN is on the LEFT of Roco (camera-left, screen-left).
Rein keeps the skateboard in her LEFT hand throughout the entire
shot — she does not put it down.

[ACTION]
6.0s — Roco lies prone on the dusty stone floor at the foot of the
  carved wall, face down, cheek against the stone, dust on his jacket
  and in his hair, arms slack beside him. Jaxx crouches on Roco's
  right side, Rein crouches on Roco's left side with the skateboard
  still gripped in her left hand.
7.5s — Jaxx slides his hands under Roco's right arm and shoulder.
  Rein hooks her free right hand under Roco's left arm and shoulder,
  skateboard still in her left. They brace their legs and exchange
  a quick glance.
9.0s — They HEAVE Roco up together in one coordinated lift, peeling
  him off the floor. He rolls forward as he rises, head hanging,
  legs dragging at first then planting under him. Jaxx grins, says
  clearly: "Damn! Bro, that was close! Get up man, c'mon!"
10.5s — Roco finds his feet between them, swaying, knees buckling
  once then holding. Jaxx, smiling, gives Roco a friendly clap on
  the shoulder with his free hand. Rein keeps her grip on Roco's
  left arm, skateboard still in her left hand at her side.
12.0s — All three stand in medium shot directly in front of the
  carved stone wall — Jaxx right, Roco center swaying upright with
  head still lolling slightly, Rein left with the skateboard. Dust
  falls from Roco's jacket, the carved doorway visible behind them
  in soft focus.

[NEGATIVE additions for this shot]
Roco is never lying on his back, Roco is always face down before
being lifted, Roco never sits up on his own.

[CAMERA（摄影机）]
⚠️ 摄影机操作——重度手持、随身在场、操作员追逐跟拍：摄影机明显由真人身体携带——操作员的步态、呼吸、重心转移都可见于画面的细微运动中。画面在操作员手中始终是「活的」，绝不锁定机位、绝不架在三脚架上、绝无云台般平滑的漂浮感。参考操作：SAVING PRIVATE RYAN（《拯救大兵瑞恩》）的战场、CHILDREN OF MEN（《人类之子》）的一镜到底、THE REVENANT（《荒野猎人》）的追逐段落、ROGUE ONE（《侠盗一号》）走廊手持。操作员随动作而动——演员跑他就跑（步态同步，每一步落地画面都颤抖），演员伏身他就伏身，动作落到地面时他放低重心。追拍表演者时不断地进行细微重构图。每一帧都有可见的手持痕迹：微小不可预知的抖动、漂移纠偏、重心落定。摄影机操作是原始而物理的——绝不航拍、绝不横扫摇臂、绝不增稳。

[LIGHT（灯光）]
来自上方的冷蓝环境光，与地面层暖橙色烛光光源相混合，深重的阴影，尘埃粒子在混合光中漂移。低基调高对比——压垮的深黑主宰整条走廊，只有火炬光斑与湿滑地面的反光保留高光细节。面孔大多处于阴影中，只被路过的火炬带出闪烁的轮廓光。大气透视——走廊的纵深消融进烟雾般的火炬霾气中。

Composition（构图）：三分法 + 黄金比例。沉落的石板作为纵深画面中一块垂直/下坠的图形块把它锚定住，其下方的空隙成为戏剧性的焦点孔径。

⚠️ COLOR PALETTE（调色板）——继承自参考图：仅作色调方向参考——暖色火炬光橙/琥珀是唯一的有彩色来源，压垮的深炭黑阴影为主导，未照亮处湿滑石材读作冷中性灰黑。不得强加青橙好莱坞调色，不得推向绿色，不得添加蓝色日光。忠实于场景参考图。

[STAGING（走位）——STRICT（硬规则）]

9.0 秒——他们一起把 Roco 猛地抬起来，一次协调的托举，把他从地板上揭起。起身时他向前一滚，头耷拉着，双腿先拖着再落到脚下。Jaxx 咧嘴一笑，清晰地开口：「Damn! Bro, that was close! Get up man, c'mon!」（该死！兄弟，刚才真险！起来，哥们，快点！）
10.5 秒——Roco 在他们之间站起身，身体摇晃，膝盖先一软随即撑住。Jaxx 笑着，用空出来的手在 Roco 肩上亲切拍了拍。Rein 仍扶着 Roco 的左臂，滑板仍在身侧左手。
12.0 秒——三人在雕刻石墙正前方以中景站定——Jaxx 在右，Roco 居中摇摇晃晃地站直、头仍有几分耷拉，Rein 持滑板在左。灰尘从 Roco 的夹克上落下，凹入式门道在他们身后以软焦点可见。
