strip on the nose, in black compression t-shirt, black tactical cargo pants, 
khaki thigh rig, black combat boots, black fingerless gloves, red bandana

 abstract patch or icon, roughly 5cm tall, embroidered or printed in

 dot eyes, OR small red devil face, OR small red flame, OR small red 
 star. Original generic design, not branded

 NOT any branded logo, NOT Hello Kitty, NOT any IP character. No 
 readable brand text, no logos
