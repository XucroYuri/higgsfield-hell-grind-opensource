Edit this Roko character image. Transform the LEFT ARM (the arm on 
the right side of the front view, the arm on the left side of the 
back view) from a normal human arm into a CRYSTAL SWORD WEAPON arm 
in the same material and style as the red crystal shield reference.

THE TRANSFORMATION:
The left arm transforms partway down the forearm — the upper arm 
and elbow remain normal human flesh under the jacket sleeve. 
Approximately at the mid-forearm point, the flesh and bone visibly 
transition into red translucent crystal material, the transition 
zone shows the flesh being absorbed and crystallized — small red 
crystal spikes emerging from the skin near the transition, the 
flesh becoming translucent reddish crystal where it meets the 
weapon, fingers of crystal growing out of where the wrist used to 
be, a clear visible boundary between living tissue and crystal 
matter.

From that transition point onward, the entire forearm and hand are 
replaced by a long CRYSTAL SWORD BLADE in the same material as the 
reference shield image — deep translucent red crystal, jagged 
crystalline spikes erupting from the surface of the blade, internal 
fractures and bubbles visible in the translucent crystal, the same 
hardened glass-like material with rough jagged crystalline edges.

THE BLADE SHAPE:
Long elongated sword-like form extending from the transition point, 
about 80 to 100 centimeters long total from the forearm transition 
to the tip, the blade is roughly the shape of a jagged crystal 
sword — narrower than the shield reference, more elongated, but 
covered in the same chaotic crystalline spike formations along its 
edges and surface. The blade has a clear pointed tip at the far 
end, ready to thrust or slash. Where a normal sword would have a 
hilt, the crystal seamlessly merges with the flesh of the forearm, 
no man-made hilt or grip — this is a biological transformation, 
the weapon IS his arm.

CRYSTAL MATERIAL — match the reference exactly:
 • Same deep translucent red crystal color
 • Same jagged spike formations erupting from the central mass
 • Same internal fracture patterns and bubbles visible in the 
 translucent material
 • Same glass-like hardened crystalline texture
 • Same dark blood-red core with brighter red reflective edges
 • Same chaotic organic crystal growth pattern, NOT geometric, 
 NOT regular, NOT cut and polished gemstone
 • Same studio lighting catching the crystal facets

PRESERVE EXACTLY everything else:
— The right arm remains a normal human arm with the black fingerless 
 tactical glove, the red bandana on the right wrist, intact
— Face, hair (dark messy black hair, mustache), expression
— Skin tone, body proportions, height
— The black-and-grey shell jacket with graphic panels (note the

 torn or pushed up at the mid-forearm point where the crystal 
 emerges, the upper arm portion of the jacket sleeve remains 
 intact)
— Black t-shirt underneath
— Black tactical cargo pants
— Black boots
— Belt and thigh-rig with tactical pouches
— Red devil plush keychain on the back hip (visible in back view)
— The red bandage strip across the nose
— Three-view layout: front, back, close-up

For the close-up view: show a portrait close-up but with the 
transformed crystal sword arm raised partially into the frame near 
his face/shoulder so the crystal weapon and the transition zone are 
visible alongside the close-up portrait, the close-up shows both 
his face and the upper portion of the crystal blade with the 
transition zone clearly readable.

For the back view: the crystal sword arm is visible from behind, 
hanging at his side or held outward from the body, the same blade 
visible from the reverse angle, the same transition zone visible at 
the forearm.

LIGHTING: Same neutral grey studio backdrop, same soft three-point

specular highlights on the spike facets, the crystal reads as a 
striking visual element against the dark outfit and grey backdrop.

Photorealistic, ARRI Alexa LF film still aesthetic, real practical
