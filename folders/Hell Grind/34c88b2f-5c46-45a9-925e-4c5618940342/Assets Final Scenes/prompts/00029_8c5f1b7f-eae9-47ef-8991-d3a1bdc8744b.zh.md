injury detail. Apply the following changes while keeping

centimeters long, slightly diagonal, clean cut edges through 
the fabric

shut and scarred over, no longer actively bleeding — the way

settled in place

fabric, just a small wet glossy dark patch right at and 
around the slit, the stain matches the size and shape of the

no pooling, no spreading stains, no blood on the back panel, 
no blood on the chest above the wound, only the contained wet 
patch right at the wound

Keep everything else in the image identical and untouched.
