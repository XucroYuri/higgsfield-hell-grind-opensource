## 原文 (source)

Edit this image. The character has post-combat damage that is currently 
massively overdone. Apply these reductions consistently across all 
three views — front, back, close-up. The goal is to dial DOWN the 
damage to a realistic restrained level, NOT to remove it.

CHANGE 1 — REMOVE WHITE CHUNKS FROM HAIR:
Remove all the large white chunky debris pieces currently scattered 
through the hair (they look like cottage cheese or popcorn — these 
are incorrect and must be removed completely). Hair should just be 
disheveled, slightly dusty, slightly damp. Maybe one or two TINY dark 
grey small stone fragments visible if any, but mostly just messy 
sweat-and-dust hair, NO white chunks, NO chunky pieces, NO clumps.

CHANGE 2 — DRAMATICALLY REDUCE BLOOD AND FACIAL INJURIES:
The face currently has way too many cuts and way too much blood — 
this reads as a horror movie victim, not a realistic post-combat 
character. Reduce to:
   • ONE small cut above the right eyebrow, about 2cm, thin pinkish-red 
     line with a single faint dried blood trail (NOT the current 
     dramatic gash with thick red streaks)
   • ONE small scrape on the left cheekbone, abraded skin, subtle pink 
     reddish area, NO blood streaks
   • Faint dust smudge on the bridge of the nose where the bandage is
   • Remove ALL the other facial cuts and abrasions currently visible 
     on the cheeks, jaw, neck, and chin
   • Remove all the dramatic blood streaks running down the face
   • The face should read as: slightly bruised and dusted, one small 
     forehead cut, one cheek scrape — that's it
   • NO blood on the chin, neck, or shirt
   • NO multiple cuts clustered together
   • The overall face should look TIRED and DUSTED, not BLOODIED

CHANGE 3 — TONE DOWN THE CHALKY WHITE DUST:
The current dust on the jacket and pants is too white, too chalky, 
too floury — it looks like flour or chalk, not stone dust. Adjust:
   • Reduce overall dust density by about 40 percent — less coverage, 
     more selective placement
   • Shift the dust color from chalky white to muted grey-tan, the 
     color of weathered Himalayan limestone — warm grey with slight 
     beige tone, NOT pure white, NOT chalky
   • Keep dust mostly concentrated on shoulders, upper chest, knees, 
     and forearms — areas of natural impact
   • Lighten or remove the heavy white streaking on the pant legs and 
     thigh area where it currently looks like grease stains
   • The dust should look like a powder layer, not painted-on chalk

CHANGE 4 — RESTORE THE GLOVES IN FRONT VIEW:
The fingerless tactical gloves on the hands appear to have lost 
definition or look incorrect in the front view. Restore: black 
fingerless tactical gloves on both hands, knuckle padding visible, 
clearly readable as the original gloves from the source image, lightly 
dusted but intact and unchanged in design.

CHANGE 5 — CLEAN UP THE BOOTS AND PANT HEMS:
The current pant hems and boots have excessive frayed/destroyed 
appearance. Reduce to: pants slightly scuffed at the hems but not 
shredded, boots dusty and scuffed but not falling apart. Functional 
gear, not destroyed costume.

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
— Identity, face structure, mustache, hair color, body proportions
— Pose: same standard reference stance in all views
— Three-view layout: front, back, close-up
— The red bandage strip across the nose
— All clothing items: shell jacket with graphic panels, t-shirt, cargo 
  pants, thigh

## 中文对照 (zh)

编辑这张图片。角色的战后损伤目前被过度放大。在全部三个视图——正面、背面、特写中一致应用这些削减。目标是把损伤降到写实克制的水准，而非移除它。

变更1 —— 移除头发中的白色大块：
移除当前散布在头发中的所有大片白色块状碎屑（它们看起来像白软干酪或爆米花——这些是错误的，必须彻底移除）。头发应当只是凌乱、轻度蒙尘、轻度湿润。至多可见一两个极小的深灰小石粒，但大体上只是脏湿的乱发，无白色大块、无块状碎屑、无成块。

变更2 —— 大幅减少血与面部损伤：
当前脸上伤口和血都太多了——这读起来像恐怖片受害者，而非写实的战后角色。减少为：
   • 右眉上一道约2厘米的小伤口，细浅粉红色线，带单道淡淡干血痕（而非当前那道带厚红色条纹的戏剧性大口子）
   • 左颧骨上一小块擦伤，皮肤磨损，微粉红发红的区域，无血迹
   • 绷带处的鼻梁上淡淡的尘污
   • 移除当前面颊、下颌、颈部与下巴上可见的所有其他面部伤口与擦伤
   • 移除所有顺脸而下的戏剧性血迹
   • 脸部应读起来为：轻微瘀伤蒙尘、一道额头小伤口、一块面颊擦伤——就这些
   • 下巴、颈部或短袖上无血
   • 无成簇聚在一起的多处伤口
   • 整张脸应看起来疲惫而蒙尘，而非血污

变更3 —— 压淡白垩般的白色灰尘：
当前夹克与裤子上的灰尘太白、太白垩、太面粉状——看起来像面粉或白垩，而非石尘。调整：
   • 将整体灰尘密度大约降低40%——更少的覆盖、更有选择的放置
   • 将灰尘颜色从白垩白调为淡灰褐色，风化喜马拉雅石灰岩的颜色——带一点米色的暖灰，绝非纯白、绝非白垩状
   • 让灰尘主要集中留在肩部、上胸、膝盖与前臂——自然受冲击的区域
   • 减轻或移除裤腿上与大腿区域当前看起来像油脂污渍的厚重白色条纹
   • 灰尘应看起来像粉末层，而非涂上的白垩

变更4 —— 还原前视图中的手套：
前视图中手上的露指战术手套似乎失去了轮廓或看起来不正确。还原：双手上黑色露指战术手套、可见指节垫，清晰可读为源自素材图的原始手套，轻蒙尘但完好且设计不变。

变更5 —— 清理靴子与裤脚：
当前裤脚与靴子的过度破旧/破烂观感。减少为：裤子裤脚轻微磨损但未破碎，靴子蒙尘有磨损但不散架。可用的装备，而非毁坏的戏服。

严格保持：
— 身份、面部结构、胡子、发色、身体比例
— 姿态：所有视图中相同的标准参考站姿
— 三视图布局：正面、背面、特写
— 鼻梁上的红色绷带条
— 所有服装物品：带图形拼接面板的壳夹克、短袖、工装裤、腿挂装备
