purple hoodie, mesh top, black flared jeans). Change outfit on all three

waist. Ends well above navel. Deep armholes exposing side torso and hint of 
bra band.

JACKET: Add olive green cropped military field jacket worn open and pulled 
DOWN off both shoulders, draped around upper arms and elbows — neither 
shoulder on her shoulders, exposing collarbone, shoulders, upper chest, 
full bust line. Collar standing at back of neck, chest pockets visible, 
gathered cuffs with strap details, washed military cotton.

BELLY SCAR: Add a single long HORIZONTAL scar running across her bare 
lower stomach — running sideways from one side of the abdomen to the other, 
crossing horizontally just above or across the navel area, parallel to the 
waistband line. Roughly 15-20 cm long, clean straight line.
The scar is RECENT and FRESH — angry pinkish-red color, slightly raised 
and inflamed edges, the surrounding skin shows mild redness. Looks like 
a recently healed wound, weeks old not years — visible, prominent, 
clearly readable on the skin. Not bleeding, not an open wound, but 
a fresh recent scar still in the early healing phase.
Visible on both front view and close-up wherever the bare stomach is shown.

PANTS: Replace flared jeans with VERY WIDE floor-length BLACK MILITARY 
CARGO PANTS — matte black ripstop military cotton, mid-rise on widest hip 
point, waistband cinched tight by belt creating narrow waist vs wider hip 
silhouette. Fabric hugs upper hip and seat snugly before flaring wide from 
mid-thigh down — pant fabric conforms to round shape of rear in front and 
back views. Three fingers of bare lower stomach exposed (showing the fresh 
horizontal belly scar). LARGE bellows-style 3D milspec pockets standing 
out from fabric: two big front-thigh pockets with button flaps, two 
lower-leg pockets near knees, small pen-pockets on side seams. Visible 
stitching, reinforced seams, double-fabric knee panels, ankle drawstrings 
left untied. Floor-length, hem covers most of shoe. NOT tucked, NOT cuffed, 
NOT tapered. Authentic milspec combat trousers aesthetic, not denim, not 
streetwear.

BELT: Dark brown leather belt, antique gold buckle, threaded through loops, 
pulled snug to cinch waist. Worn weathered leather.

THIGH RIG (LEFT thigh only, right thigh clean): Sleek khaki/olive military 
webbing main panel strapped to upper left thigh over the cargo. Two khaki 
nylon thigh straps with black plastic buckles, one vertical khaki strap 
connecting up to the belt. Mounted: exactly FOUR small khaki ripstop nylon

SNEAKERS: Replace canvas sneakers with low-profile vintage trainers — warm 
taupe/grey-beige suede upper, darker grey mesh and panel inserts, flat 
white midsole (NOT chunky, NOT platform), white rubber outsole, round toe,

POSE:

疤痕是新近的、新鲜的——微血管的粉红色，边缘微凸且发炎，周围皮肤微红。看起来像刚愈合几周的伤口而非数年的——可见、醒目、皮肤上清晰可读。不出血、不是开放伤口，而是一道仍处于早期愈合阶段的新鲜近期疤痕。
