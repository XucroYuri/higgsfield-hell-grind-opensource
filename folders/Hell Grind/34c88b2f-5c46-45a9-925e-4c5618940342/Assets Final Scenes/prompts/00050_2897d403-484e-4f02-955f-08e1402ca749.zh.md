## 原文 (source)

Edit this character reference sheet image to produce a 
cinematic death-moment version of the character — show him in 
the final seconds of life after taking a fatal sword wound 
during a film fight scene, mortally injured but still on his 
feet. Apply the following changes while keeping everything 
else identical:

CHANGE 1 — FATAL SWORD WOUND ON ABDOMEN: Add a major sword 
wound to the character's abdomen, visible in all three panels. 
The wound is a deep diagonal slash across his lower torso / 
stomach area, cut by a blade. Specifically:

— A long jagged diagonal slash through the lower part of the 
tactical jacket and the inner black shirt at the abdomen, the 
fabric cut clean through with torn ragged edges
— Heavy dark crimson blood saturating the surrounding fabric, 
the jacket and shirt soaked dark red around the wound, blood 
spreading outward in an organic stain pattern
— Long blood streaks running down from the wound onto the 
belt area and the front of the cargo pants, blood pooling 
into the fabric of the pants
— Through the slash in the fabric, a glimpse of the wounded 
abdomen flesh visible — deep open cut, fresh blood welling 
up, cinematic but realistic, not gore-spectacle
— Some blood spatter and droplets on the chest of the jacket 
above the wound, indicating he was struck while standing
— In the front view panel: the slash and bloody saturated 
fabric clearly readable on the lower torso
— In the back view panel: a smaller corresponding bloody 
patch on the back suggesting partial through-and-through, 
plus blood streaks running down the back of the pants
— In the close-up portrait panel: blood spatter visible on 
the jacket front near the chest collar area, framing the 
character's dying face

CHANGE 2 — DYING PALE FACE: Show the character's face in the 
final moments of life from blood loss and shock — clearly 
visible in the close-up portrait panel and faintly in the 
back view if his head turns:

— Skin tone shifted toward pale ashen grey, his rich dark 
skin drained of color and vitality, sallow and bloodless 
look, the warmth gone from his complexion
— Lips slightly parted, slightly blue-grey from oxygen 
deprivation
— Eyes glassy, distant, slightly unfocused — the gaze of 
someone in the final moments, no longer fighting, looking 
through rather than at
— Faint cold sweat sheen across the skin
— Slight darkening and hollowness around the eyes
— A trickle of dark blood at the corner of his mouth from 
internal bleeding, running down his chin
— Small flecks of blood on the chin and lower lip
— Eyebrows relaxed — not angry, not afraid — quiet 
resignation, peaceful acceptance of death
— Cinematic film death moment, dignified, grounded
— Maintain the green tribal war paint stripes across the 
nose and cheeks (still visible but with subtle blood smudges 
mixing into them) and the clear protective safety glasses 
still on his face

The character's posture remains the same as in the original 
character sheet — front facing in the left panel, back facing 
in the middle panel, close-up portrait in the right panel — 
he is still standing upright using the last of his strength, 
maybe with the very slightest weakening suggested, but not 
slumped, not falling, not crouched, the same poses preserved.

Keep everything else in the image identical and untouched. 
Preserve in all three panels: the same character identity 
(Black male with bleached blonde short buzz cut hair, the same 
facial features and bone structure, the same green tribal war 
paint stripes across nose and cheeks in the close-up, the 
same clear protective glasses, the existing scratches/scars 
on the temple and cheek), the same body and physique, the 
same costume design (black and grey tactical hooded jacket 
with the geometric panel design and orange tag detail, olive 
green cargo pants, black tactical pistols holstered on both 
thighs with the black strap rigs, black fingerless tactical 
gloves, black tactical boots), the same posture and stance 
in each panel, the same neutral light grey studio background 
in all three panels, the same vertical panel layout, the same 
soft studio product photography lighting, the same color 
palette and color grading, the same realism and texture detail 
throughout.

Only the two specified changes (fatal sword wound on abdomen 
with heavy blood saturation, dying pale face with cinematic 
death indicators) are made. Everything else stays exactly as 
is.
