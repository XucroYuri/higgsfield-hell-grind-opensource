Edit this character reference sheet image to produce a subtle 
post-mortem version of the character with restrained realistic 
injury detail and the protective glasses removed. Apply the 
following three changes while keeping everything else 
identical:

CHANGE 1 — NARROW WET SWORD WOUND ON ABDOMEN WITH BLEEDING: 
Add a narrow sword-shaped wound to the character's lower 
abdomen, visible in the front view panel:

— A narrow elongated slit in the jacket fabric at the lower 
abdomen, sword-blade shape — narrow and linear, about 6-8 
centimeters long, slightly diagonal, clean cut edges through 
the fabric
— The wound underneath has closed and sealed shut, no longer 
actively gushing — but the surrounding fabric is freshly 
soaked
— A wet glossy dark blood stain centered on the wound itself, 
the immediate fabric around the slit darkly saturated and 
visibly wet with subtle sheen and reflective quality, blood 
fully soaked into the cloth around the cut
— Blood streaks running downward from the wound — several 
trails of fresh wet blood streaking down the lower jacket, 
crossing the belt area, and continuing onto the upper part 
of the olive cargo pants below the belt, the streaks 
irregular and following gravity, like blood that ran down 
the body before stopping
— A few darker droplets and runs at the bottom edges of the 
streaks where blood collected
— The blood looks fresh and wet on the fabric, not crusty 
dried blood, glossy and dark crimson with subtle reflective 
quality
— No blood elsewhere — no spatter on the chest above the 
wound, no blood on the back panel, no pooling on the ground, 
only the wound stain and the downward streaks from gravity

CHANGE 2 — BARELY PERCEPTIBLE PALE FACE WITH BLOOD FROM 
MOUTH: Show the character's face with extremely subtle hints 
of lifelessness plus substantial blood from the mouth, 
visible in the close-up portrait panel:

— Skin tone almost identical to the original, just the very 
faintest possible hint of cooler less vital tone, barely a 
shift, anyone glancing would not notice — NOT pale, NOT 
ashen, NOT grey, only the slightest cooling
— Lips almost identical to the original color, just the very 
slightest hint of cooler tone, barely perceptible — NOT 
blue, NOT grey, NOT corpse-colored, only a tiny touch less 
warm than alive
— Substantial blood from the corner of the mouth: a clear 
visible trail of dark fresh blood running from the corner of 
one lip down across the chin and toward the lower jaw, the 
trail thicker and more saturated than a faint trickle, 
clearly readable as bleeding from the mouth, fresh and wet
— Additional blood smudged on the lips themselves, dark 
fresh blood coating part of the lower lip and the corner of 
the mouth, like he's been bleeding from inside
— A few small drops of blood on the chin and near the jaw 
line
— Some faint blood smudges where he might have unconsciously 
moved his lips or where blood pooled at the corner
— Possibly a faint blood smudge crossing into one of the 
green war paint stripes near the cheek
— Everything else on the face stays exactly the same — same 
expression, same gaze direction, same green tribal war paint 
stripes, same hair, same existing scratches on the temple 
and cheek

CHANGE 3 — REMOVE PROTECTIVE GLASSES: Remove the clear 
protective safety glasses from the character's face entirely 
in the close-up portrait panel (and in any other panel where

no red pressure marks where the frames sat, no shadow line 
from glasses

The character's posture and stance remain exactly the same 
as in the original character sheet — front facing in the 
left panel, back facing in the middle panel, close-up

Keep everything else in the image identical and untouched.
