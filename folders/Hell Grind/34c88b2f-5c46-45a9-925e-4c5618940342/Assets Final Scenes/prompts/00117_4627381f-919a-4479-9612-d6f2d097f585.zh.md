Edit this image. Apply post-combat temple-collapse damage to all three 
views — front, back, and close-up. The character has just survived a 
major fight inside the monastery during which the building partially 
collapsed around him. Apply ALL of the following changes consistently 
across all three views.

CHANGE 1 — STONE DUST EVERYWHERE:
Cover the entire outfit and exposed skin with a layer of pale grey-beige 
stone dust (dust from collapsed weathered monastery stone, NOT brown 
mud, NOT soil dirt — this is pulverized stone, light grey-tan in color):
   • Heaviest dust accumulation on the shoulders, upper chest, and 
     hood of the jacket — as if dust rained down from above
   • Dust streaked across the front and back of the jacket, settled 
     into the seams and panel joins, accumulated in the graphic panel 
     recesses
   • Pants: heavy dust on the thighs and knees especially, lighter 
     accumulation on the lower legs, dust caked into the cargo pocket 
     creases and the thigh-rig fabric
   • Boots: caked with stone dust and small fragments
   • Face and neck: pale dust film on the cheekbones, forehead, jawline, 
     and temples, lighter dust in the hair
   • Hands and gloves: dust on the knuckles and forearms
   • The dust reads as fresh — recently settled, not weathered-in, 
     slightly powdery

CHANGE 2 — TATTERED CLOTHING:
Add subtle damage to the jacket and pants — slightly tattered, NOT 
destroyed, the gear is still functional:
   • Jacket: a small tear or rip on one sleeve near the elbow (1-2cm, 
     fabric pulled and frayed, dark backing visible through), a few 
     surface scuffs and snags on the chest panels and shoulder area, 
     one small tear at the hem of the jacket revealing a hint of the 
     dark inner lining, slight dirt-and-tear marks where fabric has 
     scraped against rough stone
   • Pants: scuff marks and abrasion on both knees from contact with 
     stone floor (worn through fabric in places, lighter color showing 
     through), a small tear on one cargo pocket, frayed edges along 
     the lower hem of one leg, dust-caked scrapes on the thighs
   • The damage is consistent with crawling out of rubble — surface 
     wear, small tears, no large gaping holes, no destroyed sections
   • The thigh-rig and tactical gear remain intact and functional, 
     just dusted

CHANGE 3 — SKIN ABRASIONS AND MINOR CUTS:
Add fresh combat injuries — minor, surface-level, the kind from being 
hit by debris or scraping against stone:
   • A small fresh cut about 2-3cm above the right eyebrow, pinkish-red 
     line, slight dried blood trail down the temple (subtle, not 
     dramatic, like a small forehead wound from falling debris)
   • A scrape on the left cheekbone — abraded skin, slightly raised, 
     reddish-pink area about 3cm
   • A small scrape on the jawline or chin
   • Light scratches on the neck and exposed forearms (where visible 
     between glove and sleeve in front view)
   • A small scrape across the bridge of the nose, supplementing the 
     existing red bandage strip (the bandage is still in place but now 
     dirty and partially askew)
   • The injuries are SURFACE-LEVEL and FRESH — not dramatic gore, no 
     heavy bleeding, just the kind of marks you get from a building 
     coming down on you, traces of dried and drying blood mixed with 
     stone dust

CHANGE 4 — MESSY HAIR WITH DEBRIS:
   • Hair is now disheveled, more chaotic than in the original studio 
     image, strands sticking out at irregular angles
   • Visible flecks of stone dust and small bits of debris caught in 
     the hair — tiny stone fragments, a few small wood splinters
   • Hair partially sweat-damp and stone-dust-coated, slightly matted 
     in places
   • The mustache also lightly dusted

CHANGE 5 — SUBTLE EXHAUSTION IN THE FACE (close-up only):
The close-up view shows slightly more drawn features — faint shadows 
under the eyes, a tired but alert expression, slightly parted lips 
suggesting recovered breathing. NOT collapsed, NOT defeated, just the 
look of someone who just walked out of a major fight and is still 
running on adrenaline. The body language in front and back views 
remains the same standard reference stance.

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
— Identity, face, features, eyes, mustache, expression structure
— Skin tone underneath the dust (still readable as the same person)
— Hair color: same dark messy black hair, just more chaotic and dusted
— Pose: identical to original — relaxed neutral standing stance, arms 
  at sides in front and back views
— The red bandage strip across the nose (still present, now dirtier 
  and slightly askew but in place)
— ALL clothing items: same black-and-grey shell jacket with graphic 
  panels, same black t-shirt underneath, same black tactical cargo 
  pants, same black fingerless tactical gloves, same black boots, 
  same belt, same thigh-rig, same tactical pouches
— Red bandana on the right wrist (now slightly dirty but still red 
  and clearly visible)
— Red devil plush keychain hanging at the back hip (visible in back 
  view, now lightly dusted but still red and clearly readable)
— Tactical pouches and rig configuration — all intact, all in same 
  position, just dusted
— Three-view layout: front, back, close-up
— Grey backdrop, lighting setup, shadows, color temperature, ARRI 
  film grain, exact same studio aesthetic as original

The character should read as: same person, same outfit, same pose, 
same studio shoot — but having just walked off the set of a temple

Photorealistic, real photography, ARRI Alexa LF anamorphic film still

变更1 —— 处处石尘：
用一层淡灰米色石尘覆盖整套服装与暴露的皮肤（坍塌的风化寺庙石的灰尘，绝非棕色泥、绝非泥土——这是粉碎的石料，淡灰褐色的颜色）：
   • 灰尘积累最重的在夹克的肩部、上胸与兜帽上——如同灰尘自上方落下
   • 灰尘横过夹克前身与后身，沉降进接缝与拼接结合处，积聚在图形拼接面板的凹陷处
   • 裤子：大腿与膝盖尤其积尘重，小腿上积尘较轻，工装口袋折痕与腿挂装备布料中积尘
   • 靴子：糊着石尘与小碎石
   • 脸与颈：颧骨、额头、下颌线与太阳穴上有浅色尘膜，头发中蒙尘较轻
   • 手与手套：指节与前臂上有尘
   • 灰尘读起来是新鲜的——刚沉降不久，绝非风化已久，略微呈粉质

变更2 —— 破烂的服装：
为夹克与裤子添加细微损伤——略为破烂，而非毁坏，装备仍可用：
   • 夹克：一只衣袖肘部附近一个小裂口（1-2厘米，布料被扯起并毛边，透过可见深色衬背）、胸前拼接面板与肩部区域几处表面磨损与挂痕、夹克下摆一道小裂口露出一丝深色内衬、布料蹭过粗石处有淡淡的脏污与撕裂痕

变更3 —— 皮肤擦伤与轻微划伤：
添加新的战斗损伤——轻微、表面级、那种被碎屑击中或蹭过石面造成的：
   • 右眉上方一道约2-3厘米的小新伤口，浅粉红色线，太阳穴处淡淡的干血痕（微妙，不戏剧性，如同坠落碎屑造成的小额头伤）

   • 下颌线或下巴上一小块擦伤
   • 颈部与暴露的前臂（前视图中手套与衣袖之间可见处）上轻微的划痕
   • 鼻梁上一小块擦伤，补充既有红色绷带条（绷带仍在原位，但现在脏了、略显歪斜）
   • 损伤是表面级且新鲜的——绝非戏剧性的血淋淋，无大量出血，只是那种建筑塌在你身上会留下的痕迹，混着石尘的干涸与干燥中的血迹

变更4 —— 带碎屑的凌乱头发：
   • 头发现在凌乱，比原始影棚图像更狂乱，发丝以不规则的角向外支棱
