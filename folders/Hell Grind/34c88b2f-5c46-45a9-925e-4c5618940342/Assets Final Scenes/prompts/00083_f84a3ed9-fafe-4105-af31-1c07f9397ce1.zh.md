## 原文 (source)

Edit this character sheet image to remove the AI-generated slop look 
and make it look like real photoreal cinema reference photography. 
Apply the following deslop adjustments while preserving the 
character identity, costume, weapons, three-view layout, and overall 
composition.

DESLOP ADJUSTMENTS:

ADD NATURAL SKIN TEXTURE on the visible skin (face, neck, hands):
The current skin looks digitally smooth and airbrushed. Add 
realistic skin texture — visible pores, fine subtle skin imperfections, 
slight skin tone variation, natural film grain in the skin pixels, 
slight subtle redness in nose and cheeks, very fine peach fuzz at 
the hairline, real skin photographic quality. NOT plastic, NOT 
porcelain, NOT digitally smooth. Real human skin photographed on 
35mm film.

ADD WEATHERING TO THE BLACK ARMOR:
The current armor looks too clean and rendered. Add subtle realistic 
practical wear — fine micro-scratches and scuffs across the armor 
plates from use, slight dust film on flat surfaces, occasional 
small dings or impact marks, very subtle dark grime in the panel 
seams, faint smudges of oil and grease in mechanical joint areas, 
the metallic sheen reduced to matte where worn. The armor reads as 
real practical prop with use marks, NOT pristine factory-fresh CG 
shell. Keep the dark color and design exactly the same, only add 
weathering texture.

CINEMATIC DIMENSIONAL LIGHTING:
The current lighting looks flat and uniform. Apply dramatic studio 
cinema lighting with depth — soft key light from upper side casting 
gentle directional shadows across the body, soft fill light from 
the opposite side reducing shadow darkness without eliminating it, 
subtle rim light along the silhouette edges separating the figure 
from background, the lighting should have dimensional falloff with 
brighter highlights on raised surfaces and gentle shadow in 
recesses, classic three-point cinema portrait lighting. Soft warm 
key, neutral fill, subtle cool rim. NOT flat catalog lighting, NOT 
uniform illumination.

ADD ORGANIC 35MM FILM GRAIN throughout the entire image — fine 
authentic film grain texture across all surfaces, the grain 
slightly more visible in shadow areas, organic random grain 
pattern, the kind of grain from real Kodak motion picture film 
stock. NOT digital noise, NOT clean rendered output. Real film 
texture quality.

NATURAL HAIR TEXTURE:
The current hair looks like a 3D rendered hair card model. Make 
the hair read as real photographed hair — natural strand variation 
with stray loose strands, fine flyaways at the edges, soft natural 
falloff between hair tones, slight movement and life in the hair, 
not a smooth uniform helmet shape. Real hair photographed.

SUBTLE POSE NATURALIZATION:
The current pose is rigidly pristine catalog stance. Add very 
subtle natural human posture variation — slight asymmetric weight 
distribution, micro-relaxation in the shoulders, slight natural 
hand positions holding the weapons with weight readable, very 
subtle movement-life in the stance. NOT stiff mannequin, NOT 
catalog pose. Subtle human naturalism without changing the overall 
three-view standing arrangement.

WEAPONS WITH WEIGHT:
The weapons currently look attached to the hands without physical 
weight. Make the hands grip the weapons naturally with finger 
position showing the weight, slight downward pull on the arms from 
weapon mass, the weapons resting realistically in the grip rather 
than floating attached.

CHANGE BACKDROP TO NEUTRAL GREY:
Replace the current pale backdrop with a neutral medium grey 
seamless studio backdrop — solid neutral grey color (medium-tone 
grey, not too light, not too dark, classic studio reference 
photography grey backdrop). Add very subtle atmospheric gradient — 
slightly darker at the top, slightly lighter at the bottom, very 
subtle tonal variation, slight depth-of-field softness, the kind 
of backdrop a real film production studio photograph would have. 
NOT pure flat color, NOT pale white, neutral medium grey with 
subtle gradient.

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
— Character identity, face structure, features, expression
— Hair color, style, length, bob cut
— Eye makeup but slightly toned down to read more cinematic less 
  stylized
— Costume and armor design exactly as is, only weathering added
— All weapons positions and design
— Three-view layout: front, back, close-up
— Same exact composition and panel arrangement
— Same exact aspect ratio
— Body proportions and pose general arrangement

Photorealistic cinema reference photography, ARRI Alexa LF 
Panavision Primo 50mm anamorphic lens, real practical costume and 
prop photography, set photography quality, the photographic look 
of a real character reference shoot for a Denis Villeneuve or 
Guillermo del Toro production. Not CGI, not 3D render, not video 
game asset, not concept art, not stylized, not slop, not AI-
generated look, not pristine catalog look, not digital perfection.

去AI感调整：

当前皮肤看起来数字化平滑、像被空气刷平过。添加写实的皮肤纹理——可见的毛孔、细微而微妙的皮肤瑕疵、轻微的肤色变化、皮肤像素中的自然胶片颗粒、鼻子与面颊中轻微微妙的泛红、发际线处极细的汗毛、真实皮肤的照片质感。绝非塑料感、绝非瓷器感、绝非数字化平滑。在35毫米胶片上拍摄的真实人类皮肤。

为黑色盔甲增添做旧：
当前盔甲看起来太干净、太像渲染物。添加微妙而写实的实用磨损——盔甲甲片上细微的微划痕与磨损痕迹、平面上淡淡的尘膜、偶发的细小凹痕或撞击痕迹、拼缝处非常微妙的深色污垢、机械关节区域的淡淡油污与油脂痕迹、磨损处的金属光泽已减为哑光。盔甲读起来是有使用痕迹的真实实用道具，而非崭新的出厂级CG外壳。深色与设计完全保持原样，只增添做旧纹理。

在全图范围内加入有机的35毫米胶片颗粒——所有表面上一层精细真实可信的胶片颗粒纹理，阴影区域的颗粒略为明显，有机随机的颗粒纹样，那种来自真实 Kodak 电影胶片的颗粒。绝非数字噪点、绝非干净渲染输出。真实胶片的纹理质感。

自然头发纹理：
当前头发看起来像3D渲染的头发卡片模型。让头发读起来是真正被拍摄的头发——自然的发丝变化，夹杂零散散落的发丝，边缘处细细的飞散发丝，发色之间柔和的自然过渡，头发中带着一丝运动与活力，并非光滑均匀的头盔形。真实被拍摄的头发。

带分量的武器：
当前武器看起来像无视物理分量地贴附在手上。让双手自然地握持武器，手指位置显示出分量，武器质量对手臂产生轻微的向下拉扯，武器写实地安放在握持中，而非漂浮贴附。
