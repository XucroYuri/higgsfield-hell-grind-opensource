## 原文 (source)

Edit this character reference sheet image to produce a subtle 
post-mortem version of the character with restrained realistic 
injury detail. Apply the following changes while keeping 
everything else identical:

CHANGE 1 — NARROW HEALED-OVER SWORD WOUND ON ABDOMEN: Add a 
narrow sword-shaped wound to the character's lower abdomen, 
visible primarily in the front view panel:

— A narrow elongated slit in the jacket fabric at the lower 
abdomen, sword-blade shape — narrow and linear, about 6-8 
centimeters long, slightly diagonal, clean cut edges through 
the fabric
— The wound underneath has already closed and dried, sealed 
shut and scarred over, no longer actively bleeding — the way 
a fatal wound looks once the body has stopped, the flesh 
settled and dried in place
— A modest contained dark blood stain around the slit on the 
jacket fabric, dried dark crimson spreading just slightly 
outward from the cut, not soaked through, not saturated, just 
a stained patch like dried blood on cloth
— Subtle and grounded, the wound is small and quiet — could 
be missed at a glance but clearly there on close inspection
— No additional blood anywhere else on the costume, no 
spatter, no streams, no pooling

CHANGE 2 — VERY SUBTLE PALE / LIFELESS FACE: Show the 
character's face with restrained subtle signs of death, 
visible in the close-up portrait panel:

— Skin tone slightly paler than in the original, the warmth 
of his complexion just slightly drained — NOT ashen, NOT 
grey, NOT corpse-pale, just a noticeable shift toward less 
vital, like cold skin
— Lips slightly bluer / cooler than alive, a faint tinge of 
blue-grey at the lips, NOT corpse-blue, just a barely 
perceptible cool shift
— A small thin trickle of dark dried blood from the corner 
of one lip running a short way down toward the chin, just a 
single faint trail, restrained, no smearing, no pooling
— Everything else on the face stays the same — same 
expression, same gaze direction, same green tribal war paint 
stripes, same clear protective glasses, same hair, same 
existing scratches on the temple and cheek

The character's posture and stance remain exactly the same as 
in the original character sheet — front facing in the left 
panel, back facing in the middle panel, close-up portrait in 
the right panel — he stands upright as in the original, no 
slumping, no leaning, no signs of collapse.

Keep everything else in the image identical and untouched. 
Preserve in all three panels: the same character identity 
(Black male with bleached blonde short buzz cut hair, same 
facial features and bone structure, same green tribal war 
paint stripes across nose and cheeks in the close-up, same 
clear protective glasses, the existing scratches and scars on 
the temple and cheek), the same body and physique, the same 
costume design (black and grey tactical hooded jacket with 
geometric panel design and orange tag detail, olive green 
cargo pants, black tactical pistols holstered on both thighs 
with black strap rigs, black fingerless tactical gloves, 
black tactical boots), the same posture and stance in each 
panel, the same neutral light grey studio background in all 
three panels, the same vertical panel layout, the same soft 
studio product photography lighting, the same color palette 
and color grading, the same realism and texture detail 
throughout.

Only the two specified changes (narrow sealed sword wound 
with modest contained blood stain on abdomen, very subtle 
paler skin with slightly bluer lips and thin trickle of dried 
blood from mouth) are made. Everything else stays exactly as 
is.
