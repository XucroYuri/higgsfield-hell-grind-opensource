7. BURN ZONES — large areas of charred flesh (CRITICAL):
Major sections of his body have been BURNED BLACK by hellfire — not small scattered patches, but large continuous burnt zones covering significant areas of skin and shield. The burns look old, healed but never recovered. The fire reshaped him.

ZONE DISTRIBUTION (asymmetric, dramatic):

ZONE 1 — UPPER BODY DIAGONAL (largest burn area):
- Sweeps diagonally across the front of the torso from the right shoulder, down across the chest, ending at the lower-left ribcage
- Covers approximately 30-40% of the front torso
- Includes parts of the bone shield (where the shield in this zone is blackened) and the bare skin around the shield edges
- The right shoulder, including the base of the shoulder spikes, is heavily charred — the bone spikes themselves emerge from blackened tissue, with the lower portions of the spikes also blackened, transitioning to natural bone color higher up

ZONE 2 — LEFT FLANK AND HIP:
- Wraps around the left side from the lower ribs down to the hip
- Visible from both front and back panels
- Covers part of the bone shield's left side and continues down onto the bare flesh of the hip
- Edge of the loincloth meets this zone — the leather may be slightly singed where it touches the burnt skin

ZONE 3 — RIGHT THIGH AND CALF:
- Large burn covers the front and outer side of the right thigh, extending down to the right calf
- Bare pink flesh is replaced with charred black tissue across this zone
- The body hair in this area is completely gone (burned away)

ZONE 4 — UPPER BACK AND RIGHT SHOULDER BLADE (visible in BACK panel):
- Large burnt zone across the upper back, particularly around the right shoulder blade
- Affects part of the back shield, the bases of several spine spikes, and the surrounding flesh
- The spine spikes in this area have blackened bases, transitioning to natural color toward the tips
- Connects visually to Zone 1 (the upper body diagonal) when viewed from the side

ZONE 5 — LEFT FOREARM (smaller zone):
- The outer side of the left forearm is burnt, from elbow halfway to the wrist
- The arm hair in this zone is gone

BURN ZONE TEXTURE:
- The skin in burnt zones is CHARRED BLACK — deep matte black, the color of carbonized wood or burnt meat
- Surface is CRACKED and IRREGULAR — like a dried lakebed pattern, with deep fissures running across the burnt area
- Inside the cracks: deeper red or pink underlying tissue is faintly visible, suggesting the burn went deep but living tissue survives beneath
- The boundary between burnt zone and unburnt flesh is IRREGULAR and ORGANIC — not a clean line. Edges show:
  • Gradient from charred black through dark scorched-brown into reddish angry skin into the natural pink/dark-red base
  • Some flaking/peeling at the boundaries where the dead tissue is sloughing off
  • Slightly raised burn ridges where the skin healed unevenly
- Three-dimensional carbonized texture with hammered/cracked relief — NOT a flat black paint

BURN ZONES INTERACTION WITH OTHER ELEMENTS:
- BONE SHIELD inside burn zones: the dark red shield turns matte black-charcoal, but the rib segment structure is still readable underneath the charring
- BONE SPIKES inside burn zones: bases blackened, transitioning to natural bone color toward the tips. Some spikes look damaged — possibly cracked or broken from heat
- METAL VEINS inside burn zones: the metal is heat-tarnished, darker than usual, with possible bluish heat-discoloration
- BODY HAIR in burn zones: completely absent (burned away). Surrounding pink flesh still has hair
- The burn zones are the only areas where ALL elements (skin, shield, spikes, hair, metal) are unified by the same destruction

NARRATIVE READ:
The burn zones tell a clear story — he stood in fire and did not die. The fire took half of him. The other half remained. This is what survival in hell looks like.

## 中文对照 (zh)

7. BURN ZONES（烧灼区）——大面积的焦黑肉（CRITICAL，关键）：
他身体的大块部分已被地狱火【烧成焦黑】——不是零散的小焦斑，而是覆盖皮肤与盾上大片面积的、大段连续的烧灼区。烧伤看起来很旧，愈合过但从未复原。火重塑了他。

ZONE DISTRIBUTION（区域分布，不对称、戏剧性）：

ZONE 1（区域一）——UPPER BODY DIAGONAL（上体斜带，最大烧伤区）：
- 自右肩起、斜向刷过躯干前侧，横过胸口，止于左下肋笼。
- 覆盖前躯干约 30-40%。
- 包括骨盾的一部分（此区内的盾被烧黑）与盾缘周围的裸皮。
- 右肩（含肩刺根部）严重焦化——骨刺本身自焦黑组织中突出，刺的下部也变黑，向高处过渡为原生骨色。

ZONE 2（区域二）——LEFT FLANK AND HIP（左肋侧与胯）：
- 自下肋绕左侧延伸到胯。
- 正、背两栏均可见。
- 覆盖骨盾左侧的一部分，并继续向下落到胯的裸肉上。
- 缠腰布边缘与这一区相接——皮革与烧焦皮肤接触处可能略微烧糊。

ZONE 3（区域三）——RIGHT THIGH AND CALF（右大腿与小腿）：
- 一大片烧伤覆盖右大腿前侧与外侧面，向下延伸到右小腿。
- 此区内裸粉色肉被焦黑组织取代。
- 此区体毛完全消失（被烧掉）。

ZONE 4（区域四）——UPPER BACK AND RIGHT SHOULDER BLADE（上背与右肩胛，背面栏可见）：
- 上背一大片烧灼区，尤以右肩胛周围为甚。
- 影响部分背盾、数根脊柱刺的根部以及周围血肉。
- 此区脊柱刺根部变黑，向尖端过渡为自然色。
- 从侧面看时与区域一（上体斜带）视觉相连。

ZONE 5（区域五）——LEFT FOREARM（左前臂，较小区）：
- 左前臂外侧面被烧，从肘到腕一半的距离。
- 此区的臂毛消失。

BURN ZONE TEXTURE（烧灼区纹理）：
- 烧灼区的皮肤【焦黑】——深哑光黑，碳化木或烧焦肉的颜色。
- 表面【开裂且不规则】——如干涸的湖床图案，有深裂纹横过烧灼区。
- 裂缝内部：隐约可见更深的红或粉底层组织，表明火烧得深，但活组织在下面存活。
- 烧灼区与未烧肉之间【不规则且有机】——不是干净线。边缘呈现：
  · 从焦黑、穿过暗焦棕、进入发红疼痛的皮肤、再进入天然粉／深红基色的渐变。
  · 边界处有些脱屑／剥落，那是死组织正被剥脱。
  · 皮肤愈合不均处有微隆的烧灼脊。
- 【三维】碳化纹理并带锤击／开裂浮雕——【不是】一层平面黑漆。

BURN ZONES INTERACTION WITH OTHER ELEMENTS（烧灼区与其他元素的交互）：
- 烧灼区内的【骨盾】：深红盾变成哑光黑炭，但焦炭下面仍可读出肋段结构。
- 烧灼区内的【骨刺】：根部变黑，向尖端过渡为自然骨色。有些刺看起来受损——可能因热而开裂或折断。
- 烧灼区内的【金属矿脉】：金属被热氧化，比平时更深，可能带微蓝的热变色。
- 烧灼区内的【体毛】：完全缺失（被烧掉）。周围粉色血肉仍有毛。
- 烧灼区是【唯一】所有元素（皮肤、盾、刺、毛、金属）被同一种毁灭统一起来的区域。

NARRATIVE READ（叙事解读）：
烧灼区讲出一个清晰的故事——他站在火中而没有死。火夺走了他一半。另一半留下。在地上，这就是在地狱中幸存的样子。
