## 原文 (source)

Take the attached image. Keep absolutely everything identical — same character, same face, same outfit, same dirt, same scratches, same wet hair, same poses, same three-panel layout, same backdrop, same expression, same horizontal line position on the nose, same skin color of that line. Do not change anything else.

Fix only the TEXTURE of the horizontal line on the bridge of the nose.

CURRENT PROBLEM:
The line currently looks like a fresh cut or a split in the skin — sharp, dark, like the skin is just opened. This is wrong. It should look like fully matured scar tissue from an injury that healed over a decade ago.

WHAT REAL OLD SCAR TISSUE LOOKS LIKE (this is what you must render):

- It is NOT a recessed cut, NOT a dark slit, NOT a line of shadow.
- It is a band of FIBROUS TISSUE that has replaced the original skin in that area
- The tissue is SLIGHTLY RAISED above the surrounding skin surface — a low gentle bump, not a cut. Light catches the top of this bump softly
- Or alternatively: a slight soft DEPRESSION with smooth gradient edges, never sharp
- The surface of the scar tissue is SMOOTHER than the surrounding skin — it does NOT have visible pores or peach fuzz on it. The absence of pores is the key visual signal that this is scar tissue
- The scar tissue catches light SLIGHTLY DIFFERENTLY than normal skin — has a faint subtle sheen because fibrous tissue reflects light more uniformly than pored skin
- EDGES: not perfectly straight. Real scars have slightly irregular edges where the wound pulled together — micro-variations in width along the length
- Soft transition between scar tissue and normal skin — gradient, not a sharp border
- Same warm tan color as surrounding skin (you already got this right, keep it)

HOW TO IMAGINE IT:
Look at photographs of real people who have old facial scars from childhood — boxers, soldiers, people who fell as kids. The scar is visible because:
1. It has no pores while the surrounding skin does
2. It has a slight raised or smooth quality that catches light differently  
3. Its edges are slightly irregular and softly fading
4. It is NOT visible as a dark line — it is visible as a SUBTLE TEXTURE CHANGE

It should be the kind of scar a viewer notices on second look, then realizes "oh, that's an old scar from years ago" — not the kind that screams "fresh wound."

REMOVE THESE WRONG QUALITIES:
- The sharp dark line look (split / cut appearance)
- Any dark shadow inside the line that looks like an opening
- Any sense that the skin is currently broken or recently opened
- Any sharp edges between the line and the rest of the skin

Keep absolutely everything else in the image identical. Only the surface texture and reflectivity of this one horizontal line on the nose changes.

## 中文对照 (zh)

取下附带的这张图片。把所有东西保持得【绝对一致】——相同的角色、相同的脸、相同的服装、相同的污垢、相同的划痕、相同的湿发、相同的姿态、相同的三栏版式、相同的背景、相同的表情、鼻上那道水平线的相同位置、那条线相同的肤色。除此之外不要更改任何内容。

只修鼻梁上那道【水平线】的【质感（TEXTURE）】。

CURRENT PROBLEM（当前问题）：
眼下这条线看起来像一道新割的口子或皮肤上的一道裂口——锐利、黝黑，仿佛皮肤刚刚被切开。这是不对的。它应当看起来像一场十多年前就已愈合的伤所留下的、已完全成熟的瘢痕组织。

WHAT REAL OLD SCAR TISSUE LOOKS LIKE（你必须渲染出的真实陈旧瘢痕组织）：
- 它【不是】凹陷的口子、不是黝黑的裂缝、不是一条阴影线。
- 它是一带【纤维组织】，取代了该处的原生皮肤。
- 该组织【微微隆起】于周围皮肤表面之上——一个低缓的隆起，而非一道切口。光线柔和地照在此隆起的顶部。
- 又或是：一处略微柔和的【凹陷】，边缘为平滑的渐变，绝不锐利。
- 瘢痕组织的表面比周围皮肤【更光滑】——其上【没有】可见毛孔或细绒。毛孔的缺失是判断这是瘢痕组织的关键视觉信号。
- 瘢痕组织接受光线的方式与正常皮肤【略有不同】——带一丝微弱的反光，因为纤维组织比多毛孔皮肤反射光线更均匀。
- 边缘：并非完美笔直。真实的瘢痕边缘略微参差，那是伤口收拢之处——宽度沿长度有细微变化。
- 瘢痕组织与正常皮肤之间是柔和过渡——渐变，而非锐利边界。
- 与周围皮肤相同的暖棕褐色（这点你已经做对了，保持它）。

HOW TO IMAGINE IT（如何想象）：
去看真实的人带着自幼留下的陈旧面部疤痕的照片——拳击手、军人、幼时跌倒过的人。这道疤之所以可见，因为：
1. 它没有毛孔，而周围皮肤有。
2. 它带一点隆起或平滑的质感，接光方式不同。
3. 它的边缘略微参差、柔和地淡出。
4. 它【不是】作为一道黝黑的线可见——它是作为一处【细微的质感变化】可见。

它应当成为观者看第二眼才注意到的疤，然后意识到"哦，那是多年前留下的旧疤"——而不是那种直喊"新伤口"的疤。

REMOVE THESE WRONG QUALITIES（去除这些错误的质感）：
- 锐利的黝黑线外观（裂口／切口的外观）。
- 线内任何看起来像开口的黝黑阴影。
- 任何皮肤当下破损或近来被切开的观感。
- 这条线与皮肤其余部分之间任何锐利的边界。

图片中其他一切【原样不变】。只有鼻子上这道水平线的表面质感与反光度变化。
