## 原文 (source)

Take the attached image. Keep absolutely everything identical — same character, same face, same outfit, same dirt, same scratches on body, same wet hair, same poses, same three-panel layout, same backdrop, same expression. Do not change anything in the image.

Fix only one specific area: the bridge of the nose.

Currently there is a red or pink mark on the bridge of his nose. This is wrong. Replace it with a thin diagonal line that looks like part of his natural skin — a permanent feature he has had since childhood, fully integrated into his face like any natural skin line.

Describe it this way: imagine a thin diagonal indentation in the skin across the bridge of the nose, like a faint crease or fold that has always been there. The color of this line is exactly the same color as the surrounding skin — same warm tan tone, no color difference. The line is visible only because it is slightly recessed into the skin, catching shadow along its length, not because it has any different color.

Think of it like a deep wrinkle on someone's forehead, or like the natural line at the corner of someone's mouth. Just a structural feature of the face. Same skin color throughout. The only thing that makes it visible is the tiny shadow inside the indentation.

Critical color rules — the line must be:
- The exact same warm tan color as the surrounding nose skin
- Not red, not pink, not brown, not gray, not silver, not purple, not any color different from his face
- No coloring at all that distinguishes it from skin tone
- Visible through subtle shadow only, not through pigment

Critical texture rules:
- Smooth integrated skin surface, like a natural face crease
- No scab texture
- No raised tissue
- No different surface quality from the rest of the nose
- Skin looks completely intact and undamaged

Keep the rest of the image absolutely unchanged. Only this one detail on the nose changes.

## 中文对照 (zh)

取下附带的这张图片。把所有东西保持得【绝对一致】——相同的角色、相同的脸、相同的服装、相同的污垢、相同的身体划痕、相同的湿发、相同的姿态、相同的三栏版式、相同的背景、相同的表情。不要更改图片中的任何内容。

只修一个特定的区域：鼻梁。

眼下他鼻梁上有一道红色或粉色的印记。这是不对的。把它替换成一道细【对角】线，看起来像是他天生皮肤的一部分——一个他从小就有的永久特征，像任何自然的皮肤纹路一样，完全融入他的脸。

这样描述它：想象鼻梁皮肤上其其一道细窄的对角凹痕，像一道一直就存在的浅褶或折痕。这条线的颜色与周围皮肤【完全相同】——同样的暖棕褐色，无任何色差。这条线之所以可见，仅仅是因为它微微凹陷进皮肤，沿其长度捕捉阴影，而不是因为它有什么不同的颜色。

把它想象成某人额头上的一道深皱纹，或想象成嘴角处那道自然的纹路。纯然是脸部的一个结构特征。整条线肤色相同。唯一让它可见的，是凹痕内的那一丝微小阴影。

Critical color rules（关键配色规则）——这条线必须：
- 与周围鼻部皮肤完全相同的暖棕褐色。
- 不是红色、不是粉色、不是棕色、不是灰色、不是银色、不是紫色，不是与他脸不同的任何颜色。
- 完全没有把它与肤色区分开的着色。
- 仅通过细微阴影可见，而非通过色素。

Critical texture rules（关键质感规则）：
- 平滑融合的皮肤表面，像自然的脸部褶痕。
- 无结痂质感。
- 无隆起的组织。
- 与鼻子其余部分无不同的表面质感。
- 皮肤看起来完全完整、未受损。

图片其余部分保持【绝对不变】。只有鼻子上这一处细节变化。
