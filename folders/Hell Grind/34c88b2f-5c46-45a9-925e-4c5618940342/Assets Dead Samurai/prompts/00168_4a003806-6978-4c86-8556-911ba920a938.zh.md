## 原文 (source)

Take the attached image and convert it from AI render quality to real photograph quality. Keep EVERYTHING identical — same character, same face, same identity, same outfit, same dirt, same scratches, same wet hair, same poses, same three-panel layout, same gray backdrop, same expression, same nose scar, same all visible marks. Do not remove anything. Do not change composition.

The single goal: replace AI rendering with photographic realism.

WHAT IS WRONG NOW:
The current image looks like CGI. The skin looks like wax or rubber. Edges have bright AI sharpening halos. Hair strands are over-separated like vector art. The whole image has the "uncanny valley" plastic quality of AI generation.

WHAT TO DO:
Process this image as if a professional retoucher took an AI render and made it look like an actual photograph captured on a Hasselblad camera with natural studio lighting.

SKIN must show:
- Real visible pores everywhere on the face — cheeks, forehead, nose, chin, neck
- Natural color variation — slight pink in the cheeks, slight warmth at the nose tip and ears, cooler tones in shadows, NOT a uniform tan
- Fine peach fuzz visible at the jawline and cheekbones catching light
- Real subsurface scattering — slight translucency at thin areas (nostrils, eyelids, ear edges)
- Tiny imperfections scattered naturally — small spots, freckle marks, dry patches, blemishes
- Texture even in shadow zones — never smooth, never plastic
- Mustache and stubble showing individual hair follicles, not a painted shape
- Lips with natural cracking texture, not a smooth surface

EDGES must be:
- Naturally soft, no bright halos along hair, shoulders, jawline, clothes
- Hair softened — wet matted hair photographed at f/2.8, not vector illustration with each strand separately defined
- Remove the small white particle dots currently in the hair (they look like rendering artifacts)

Output: a real photograph, not an AI image. Indistinguishable from a film still or editorial portrait. The transformation is in surface quality and texture only — character and content stay identical.

## 中文对照 (zh)

取下附带的这张图片，把它从 AI 渲染（render）质感转换为真实照片质感。把所有东西保持得【绝对一致】——相同的角色、相同的脸、相同的身份、相同的服装、相同的污垢、相同的划痕、相同的湿发、相同的姿态、相同的三栏版式、相同的灰色背景、相同的表情、相同的鼻疤、所有可见的印记都相同。不要删掉任何东西。不要改变构图。

唯一目标：用摄影式写实取代 AI 渲染。

WHAT IS WRONG NOW（眼下哪里不对）：
当前图像看起来像 CGI。皮肤看起来像蜡或橡胶。边缘有亮晃晃的 AI 锐化光晕。发丝被过度分离，像矢量插画。整张图带有 AI 生成特有的"恐怖谷"塑料质感。

WHAT TO DO（怎么做）：
把这张图像当作一位专业修图师拿到一幅 AI 渲染图、把它修得像哈苏（Hasselblad）相机配自然影棚灯光拍出的真实照片那样处理。

SKIN 皮肤必须具备：
- 整张脸到处可见真实毛孔——脸颊、额头、鼻子、下巴、脖颈。
- 自然的颜色变化——脸颊微带粉、鼻尖和耳尖略带暖意、阴影处色调偏冷，而【不是】均匀的棕褐。
- 下颚线和颧骨处可见的细小桃绒绒毛，接住光线。
- 真实的次表面散射（subsurface scattering）——薄处（鼻翼、眼皮、耳缘）略带半透明。
- 自然散布的微小瑕疵——小斑点、雀斑印记、干燥皮块、瑕疵。
- 即使在阴影区也有纹理——绝不光滑、绝不塑料。
- 胡子和胡茬须显示一个个毛发毛囊，而非一个涂出来的形状。
- 嘴唇带自然的干裂纹理，而非光滑表面。

EDGES 边缘必须：
- 自然柔和，发际、肩、下颚线、衣物周围无亮晕。
- 头发被柔化——如同一张在 f/2.8 下拍出的湿漉漉的贴发，而非每根发丝各自分离的矢量插画。
- 去除头发里目前那些小白色颗粒点（它们看起来像渲染瑕疵）。

输出：一张真实照片，而非一幅 AI 图像。与电影剧照或社论人像难以区分。转变只发生在表面质感与纹理层面——角色与内容保持完全相同。
