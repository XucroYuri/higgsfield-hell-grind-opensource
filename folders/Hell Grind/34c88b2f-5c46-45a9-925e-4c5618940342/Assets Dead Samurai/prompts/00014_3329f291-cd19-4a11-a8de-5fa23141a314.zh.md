## 原文 (source)

Using the attached image as the exact base character, generate a new character sheet showing this same monster in his BATTLE-DAMAGED STATE — after a brutal fight where he was struck repeatedly by a sword and survived, but barely. Same pose, same layout (three vertical panels on pure black background), same lighting setup, same identity. ONLY add the damage described below.

LAYOUT (same as reference):
Three vertical panels on pure black background. Panel 1: Full body FRONT, slight low angle, hunched stance. Panel 2: Full body BACK, same stance, full back damage visible. Panel 3: FACE close-up, 3/4 angle, intense eye contact.

PRESERVE FROM REFERENCE (do not change):
- Same face, same skull-nose cavity, same lipless grin, same yellow-green eyes
- Same body proportions, same hunched stance, same height
- Same loincloth and leather armor pieces
- Same digitigrade legs and clawed hands
- Same dark red bone shield base structure
- Same samurai topknot hairstyle
- Same right arm sword (half bone half steel)
- Same overall color palette: dark red flesh-armor, bone, black steel
- Same lighting setup

DAMAGE PASS — add the following injuries:

1. BROKEN SHOULDER SPIKES (CRITICAL):
All large bone spikes on both shoulders are now BROKEN. Not bent, not cracked — SHATTERED.
- Most spikes are snapped off at varying lengths down their original height — some broken halfway, some near the base, some leaving only jagged stumps
- The break points are RAGGED and IRREGULAR — not clean cuts. Bone fragments and splinters at each broken end
- The broken bone surfaces show INTERIOR bone structure — paler bone marrow color contrasting with the dark red exterior
- Some metal tips that were on the spike points are now GONE (broken off with the spike), some metal tips are still attached to broken segments lying conceptually on the ground (do not show ground debris, just imply through missing tips)
- A few smaller spikes may remain mostly intact, but the dominant primary spikes are all damaged
- Dried blood crusted around the break points where the bone connects to flesh — the breaks were violent, the body bled

2. BROKEN SPINE SPIKES (visible in BACK panel):
The mohawk-like spine spike row is also damaged.
- Most of the larger spine spikes are SNAPPED OFF at various heights
- Same ragged break pattern as the shoulder spikes
- Some spikes leaning at unnatural angles where they cracked but didn't fully break off
- Dried blood streaks running down the back from the broken spike bases
- The silhouette from BACK panel is dramatically reduced — the bristling mohawk is now a row of jagged stumps

3. SHATTERED CHEST SHIELD (CRITICAL):
The ribbed bone shield on the chest is BROKEN OPEN by sword strikes.
- Multiple deep CRACKS running across the rib segments — some segments completely shattered, exposing the dark interior beneath
- One large IMPACT FRACTURE near the center-right of the chest — a roughly fist-sized hole where the shield was punctured through. Inside the hole: torn flesh, exposed inner bone structure, dark blood
- Several rib segments are BROKEN and DISLODGED — pieces missing, jagged edges where shield material has been ripped away
- Cracks branching out from the impact points like spider webs
- The shield no longer reads as protective armor — it reads as a broken cage
- Dried and fresh blood seeping from the cracks and the impact hole
- The structure is still recognizable but compromised

4. SWORD CUT ON RIGHT SHOULDER (NEW WOUND):
A clean diagonal SWORD SLASH across the right shoulder where the sword-arm meets the body.
- Cut runs from upper shoulder down toward the chest at about 30 degrees
- Length: about 15-20 cm
- DEEP cut — the blade went through the bone shield edge and into the underlying flesh
- The wound is OPEN — flesh parted along the cut, dark interior tissue visible
- BLEEDING — fresh red blood flowing from the wound, running down the chest and the upper arm
- Some dried blood crust at the upper end (the wound is hours old, still bleeding from movement)
- The bone shield around the cut has been sliced clean — you can see the cross-section of the shield material at the edges of the wound
- This is the critical wound — the one that finally hurt him

5. BLOODY MOUTH AND TEETH (CRITICAL — face close-up):
His mouth and teeth are now COVERED IN BLOOD.
- Fresh bright red blood smeared across the lipless grin
- Blood between every tooth in the rows of yellow-stained teeth — dark red filling the gaps
- Blood dripping from the corners of the mouth down the jaw
- Blood on the chin, with thin streams running down the neck
- Some blood splatter on the cheek and lower face
- The blood is FRESH — wet, glistening, recently spilled (not dried brown)
- Source ambiguity: he may have bitten something (or someone), or the blow that broke his shield made him cough blood from internal damage
- Eyes show pain and rage simultaneously — same yellow-green color, but more intense

6. ADDITIONAL DAMAGE ACROSS THE BODY:
- Multiple smaller cuts and abrasions across the bone shield and limbs
- Dried blood streaks running down the body from various wounds
- Some scuff marks and dirt on the surface — he fought, fell, got back up
- The overall surface looks BEATEN — like he just walked off a battlefield he barely won

DAMAGE TEXTURE LAW:

BROKEN BONE SURFACES:
- Show interior bone structure — paler creamy bone marrow color where the bone has been freshly broken
- Jagged irregular fracture edges, never clean cuts
- Splinters and fragments at break points

FRESH BLOOD:
- Bright crimson red, wet, glistening
- Catches strong specular highlights — the wettest surfaces in the image
- Flows naturally with gravity — vertical drips, pooling in low points
- Concentrated at: mouth, chin, neck, sword-cut on shoulder, around shield impact hole

DRIED BLOOD:
- Darker brown-red, matte, crusted
- Visible at older injury sites: around broken spike bases, edges of older wounds
- Contrasts with fresh blood — old vs new

EXPOSED INTERIOR:
- Where the chest shield is broken through, the interior is dark — torn dark red flesh, glimpse of inner bone structure
- Subsurface scattering on the exposed flesh — slightly translucent at thin tissue
- The interior must look organic, alive, and wounded — not hollow

LIGHTING (preserve from reference, but emphasize damage):
- Same three-point cinematic setup as reference
- Key light from upper-left catches: the freshly cut shoulder wound, the bloody mouth (highlighting wet blood), the broken spike stumps (bringing out paler interior bone)
- Cool fill from the right reveals the depth of the chest shield impact hole and the ragged bone fractures
- Specular hits on FRESH BLOOD become some of the brightest points in the image — wet blood reflects strongly
- Subsurface scattering visible on exposed wound interiors
- The damage must read clearly under this lighting — the broken parts catch the light differently than the intact parts

NARRATIVE TONE:
This is the moment AFTER the battle. He survived, but his body bears the cost. The bristling spear-tipped silhouette is now a broken stump-line. The shield that made him invulnerable is cracked open. Blood paints his teeth from a strike that hurt him deep. He still stands — but he is no longer the unbeatable monster from before. He is wounded, bleeding, and dangerous because of it.

RENDER:
Photorealistic creature design at Weta Workshop / Guillermo del Toro / practical effects level. Same shaders as the reference image — skin SSS, hard surface bone, metallurgical steel, charred carbon on the burned half — plus additional shaders for fresh wet blood (high specular, slight SSS) and exposed interior tissue. Sharp focus across all panels. 85mm for full body, 105mm for face close-up. NOT video game CGI — like a real beaten creature photographed on set.

## 中文对照 (zh)

以所附图片为精确基底角色，生成一张新的角色三视图，展示这位同样的怪物处于他的【战斗受损状态（BATTLE-DAMAGED STATE）】——经过一场惨烈战斗，他数次被刀剑击中并幸存下来，却几乎濒死。相同的姿态、相同的版式（纯黑背景上的三个竖版分栏）、相同的灯光布置、相同的身份。只添加上下文描述的损伤。

LAYOUT（版式，与参考图一致）：
纯黑背景上的三个竖版分栏。分栏1：全身【正面】，略带低角度，佝偻站姿。分栏2：全身【背面】，同一站姿，背部损伤完整可见。分栏3：【面部】特写，3/4 角度，强烈的眼神接触。

PRESERVE FROM REFERENCE（自参考图保留，不改变）：
- 相同的脸、相同的骷髅鼻窝、相同的无唇咧嘴笑、相同的黄绿色眼睛。
- 相同的身体比例、相同的佝偻站姿、相同的身高。
- 相同的缠腰布和皮革铠甲部件。
- 相同的趾行腿（digitigrade legs）与带爪的手。
- 相同的深红色骨骼盾基座结构。
- 相同的武士顶髻发型。
- 相同的右臂剑（半骨半钢）。
- 相同的整体配色：深红肉甲、骨、黑钢。
- 相同的灯光布置。

DAMAGE PASS（损伤处理）——添加以下伤势：

1. BROKEN SHOULDER SPIKES（破裂的肩刺——CRITICAL，关键）：
两肩上的所有大型骨刺如今都【断裂】。不是弯折、不是开裂——而是【碎裂】。
- 多数尖刺沿其原本高度以不同长度被折断——有些断到一半，有些近乎齐根，有些只留下参差的根桩。
- 断裂点【参差且不规则】——不是干净切口。每个断裂端都有骨碎片和裂片。
- 断裂的骨面呈现【内部骨结构】——更苍白的骨髓色，与深红外表形成对比。
- 原本附在刺尖上的某些金属尖现在【没了】（随刺一同断掉），有些金属尖仍附着在断裂的骨段上，概念上落在地上（不显示地面碎屑，只通过缺失的尖头来暗示）。
- 少数较小的刺可能大体完好，但主导性的主刺全部受损。
- 骨与肉相接处的断裂点周围结了干血痂——断裂是暴力的，身体流过血。

2. BROKEN SPINE SPIKES（破裂的脊柱刺——BACK 背面分栏中可见）：
莫霍克似的脊柱刺排也受损了。
- 多数较大的脊柱刺在各异高度被【折断】。
- 与肩刺相同的参差断裂样式。
- 有些刺朝不自然的角度歪坍，那是在开裂但没有全断之处。
- 干涸的血痕自断裂的刺根沿背流下。
- 【背面】分栏的剪影被大幅缩减——原本直竖的莫霍克如今是一排参差的根桩。

3. SHATTERED CHEST SHIELD（碎裂的胸盾——CRITICAL，关键）：
胸口带肋的骨盾被剑斩【劈开】。
- 多条深【裂纹】横过肋段——有些段完全碎裂，露出其下黝黑的内腔。
- 胸口近中央偏右一处大【冲击骨折】——一个约拳头大小、盾被凿穿的洞。洞内：撕裂的血肉、外露的内部骨结构、暗血。
- 数个肋段【断裂并移位】——有碎片缺失，盾材被扯离之处露出参差的边缘。
- 裂纹自冲击点如蛛网般四散。
- 盾不再读作防护甲——它读作一具破笼。
- 干涸与新鲜的血从裂纹和冲击洞口渗出。
- 结构仍可辨认，但已受损。

4. SWORD CUT ON RIGHT SHOULDER（右肩刀伤——NEW WOUND，新伤口）：
一道干净斜向的【剑斩】，横过右肩、剑臂与身体相接之处。
- 切口自肩上部以约 30° 向下朝胸口切去。
- 长度：约 15-20 厘米。
- 深切口——刀刃穿过了骨盾边缘，切入底层血肉。
- 伤口【敞开】——血肉沿切口分离，可见黝黑的内层组织。
- 【流血】——新鲜红血自伤口涌出，沿胸口和上臂流下。
- 切口上端有些干血痂（伤口已有数小时，仍随活动在流血）。
- 切口周围的骨盾被整齐切开——你可在伤口边缘看到盾材的横断面。
- 这是关键伤口——那一道终于伤到他的伤口。

5. BLOODY MOUTH AND TEETH（血污的嘴与牙齿——CRITICAL，关键，面部特写）：
他的嘴和牙齿如今【沾满血】。
- 新鲜亮红血糊在无唇咧嘴笑上。
- 一排排黄染牙齿的每个牙缝间都有血——深红填满缝隙。
- 血自嘴角滴下，沿下颌流落。
- 下巴上有血，细流流下脖颈。
- 脸颊和下半脸有些血迹飞溅。
- 血是【新鲜的】——湿润、晶莹，刚刚溅出（不是干褐）。
- 来源模糊：他可能咬了什么东西（或什么人），或者是震碎他盾的那一击令他从内部损伤中咳出血。
- 眼神同时显出痛苦与狂怒——相同的黄绿色，但更为强烈。

6. ADDITIONAL DAMAGE ACROSS THE BODY（周身额外损伤）：
- 骨盾和肢体上有多道更小的切口与擦伤。
- 干涸血痕自各伤口沿身体流下。
- 表面有些擦痕和污垢——他打斗过、跌倒过、又爬了起来。
- 整体表面看起来【饱经捶打】——像他刚从一场勉强赢下的战场上走下来。

DAMAGE TEXTURE LAW（损伤质感法则）：

BROKEN BONE SURFACES（断裂的骨面）：
- 呈现内部骨结构——骨刚断裂处显更苍白的乳白骨髓色。
- 参差不规则（jagged）的断裂边缘，绝非干净切口。
- 断裂点处有裂片和碎片。

FRESH BLOOD（新鲜血）：
- 亮绯红，湿润，晶莹。
- 捕捉强烈镜面高光——全图最湿的表面。
- 顺重力自然流淌——垂直滴落，在低点积成洼。
- 集中于：嘴、下巴、颈、肩上的剑切开、盾冲击洞口周围。

DRIED BLOOD（干涸血）：
- 更深的棕红，哑光，结痂。
- 见于较老的伤处：断裂刺根周围、较老伤口的边缘。
- 与新鲜血形成对比——旧与新。

EXPOSED INTERIOR（外露内部）：
- 胸盾穿透处，内里是黝黑的——撕裂的深红血肉、一丝内部骨结构。
- 外露血肉上有次表面散射——薄组织处略半透明。
- 内里必须看起来有机、鲜活、受伤——而非中空。

LIGHTING（灯光，自参考图保留，但强调损伤）：
- 与参考图相同的三分点电影布光。
- 左上主光捕捉：刚刚切开的肩伤、血污的嘴（凸显湿血）、断裂的刺桩（衬出更苍白的内部骨）。
- 右侧冷色补光揭示胸盾冲击洞与参差骨裂的纵深。
- 新鲜血上的镜面高光成为全图最亮的一些点——湿血强烈反光。
- 外露伤口内里可见次表面散射。
- 损伤必须在此灯光下清晰可读——破损部分与完好处以不同方式接光。

NARRATIVE TONE（叙事基调）：
这是战斗【之后】的时刻。他活了下来，但他的身体付出了代价。原本枪尖密布的剪影如今成了一排破损的根桩。那面令他刀枪不入的盾裂开了。血为他那排牙齿涂上颜色，来自一道伤他很深的斩击。他依然站着——但他不再是之前那个不可战胜的怪物。他受伤、流血，也因此而危险。

RENDER（渲染）：
写实生物设计，Weta Workshop／Guillermo del Toro／实拍特效级。与参考图相同的着色器——皮肤 SSS、硬质表面骨、冶金钢、炙烧半边的焦炭——外加新的鲜湿血着色器（高镜面、轻微 SSS）与外露内部组织着色器。所有分栏锐利对焦。全身用 85mm、面部特写用 105mm。绝非游戏 CGI——像一头真的被揍得不成样子的生物在片场被拍摄下来。
