## 原文 (source)

Using the attached reference image as the exact base character, generate a 
character sheet showing this same monster in his FINAL EVOLVED FORM.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
LAYOUT — match reference exactly:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
Three vertical panels on pure black background (#000000), studio lighting, 
photorealistic CGI render quality, same cinematic key light from upper-left, 
same neutral gray ground plane.

- Panel 1 (left): Full body, FRONT view, slight low angle, hunched stance
- Panel 2 (center): Full body, BACK view, same stance, spine fully visible  
- Panel 3 (right): FACE close-up, 3/4 angle, intense eye contact with camera

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
PRESERVE FROM REFERENCE (do not change):
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- Same height and body mass — DO NOT make him taller or bulkier overall
- Same hunched silhouette and crouched posture
- Same facial structure: exposed skull-nose cavity, lipless grin with rows 
  of yellow-stained teeth, sunken yellow-green eyes with red sclera
- Same skin tone palette: raw pinkish-red flesh with dark venous undertones
- Same loincloth (torn leather wrap at the hips)
- Same digitigrade legs and elongated clawed hands
- Same face — this must read as the SAME character, just transformed

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
TRANSFORM (final-form upgrades):
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

1. SKIN — denser, armor-like
   The flesh has hardened into natural bone-plate armor. Surface reads as 
   calcified, leathery, with visible chitinous bone-ridges fused under the 
   skin across chest, shoulders, forearms, and thighs. Like the skeleton 
   pushed outward and became external armor. Keep the same red-flesh tone 
   but matte and stone-like, less wet.

2. SPINE SPIKES — new growth
   Add a NEW row of bone spikes erupting along the entire spine, from base 
   of skull down to tailbone. These are smaller and sharper than the 
   shoulder spikes, jagged like broken vertebrae pushing through. Visible 
   clearly in the BACK panel.

3. HAIR — samurai topknot
   Long black hair pulled up into a HIGH SAMURAI TOPKNOT (chonmage-style) 
   on the crown of the head. Tight wrap at the base, loose tail falling 
   back. Hair is greasy, matted, with strands escaping. Replaces the 
   loose hanging hair from the reference.

4. RIGHT ARM — the bone-sword limb (CRITICAL DETAIL — FOCAL POINT)
   His right arm has transformed into a living BONE-BLADE WEAPON, 
   fundamentally different from the left:
   
   • SHAPE: The arm tapers from the shoulder into an elongated, blade-like 
     forearm — the bone itself has grown into the form of a massive sword. 
     The "hand" is no longer a hand but the continuation of the bone-blade, 
     ending in a sharp pointed tip. Reaches past the knee when standing.
   
   • MATERIAL: Bone-and-metal hybrid. The base is dense charcoal-black bone 
     — calcified, organic, with visible texture of fused vertebrae and 
     calcium ridges along its length.
   
   • METAL INCLUSIONS: Raw iron and dull-steel veins are fused INTO the 
     bone structure — not coating it, but embedded through it like ore 
     trapped in stone. The metal shows as:
       - Jagged streaks running along the blade's length
       - Cluster patches at stress points (elbow joint, blade spine)
       - Sharp metallic shards protruding from the cutting edge
       - Dull industrial sheen, NOT polished — looks ancient, unrefined, 
         like meteoric iron grown into the bone
   
   • EDGE: The blade has a clear cutting edge running its length, 
     serrated and irregular — bone teeth interlocked with metal fragments. 
     Looks like it was forged in hell, not crafted.
   
   • CONTRAST: Against his organic red-flesh left arm, this weaponized 
     limb reads as ALIEN, UNBREAKABLE, WEAPONIZED. This visual contrast 
     is the centerpiece of the front panel.
   
   • LIGHTING: Hard specular highlights catch ONLY the metal inclusions, 
     making them glint coldly while the bone stays matte. This separation 
     of materials is what sells the effect.

TEXTURE LAW (applies to ALL elements above):
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
All bone and flesh surfaces must remain ORGANIC, LIVING, and NATURAL — 
even after the transformation. This rule overrides any tendency toward 
stylization or "armor look."

- BONE (spikes, plates, spine ridges, right-arm blade):
   - Real bone texture: porous, slightly uneven, with hairline cracks, 
     calcium ridges, faint discoloration (yellowed at stress points, 
     darker in crevices)
   - Looks GROWN, not sculpted — asymmetrical, irregular, alive
   - Matte finish with subtle subsurface translucency at thin edges
   - NO polished surfaces, NO smooth fantasy-armor look, NO ceramic shine

- FLESH (skin, muscle, exposed tissue):
   - Real human/animal skin texture: pores, fine wrinkles, stretch marks, 
     scars, vein networks under the surface
   - Subsurface scattering — light penetrates the skin slightly, especially 
     at thin areas (ears, fingers, around eyes)
   - Wet/oily sheen ONLY in natural places: eyes, gums, inside the mouth, 
     fresh wounds — the rest stays matte and breathing
   - Visible imperfections: dirt in creases, dried blood, old scarring, 
     uneven pigmentation
   - The body must look like it BREATHES and BLEEDS

- HAIR:
   - Individual strands visible, greasy, matted with sweat and grime
   - NOT CGI-clean, NOT shampoo-commercial — looks unwashed, real

- OVERALL FEEL:
   - Practical creature effects aesthetic (Guillermo del Toro / 
     The Witcher / Pan's Labyrinth) — NOT video-game cinematic gloss
   - Every surface should feel like you could touch it and feel warmth, 
     texture, imperfection
   - The monster is EVOLVED, not MANUFACTURED

## 中文对照 (zh)

以所附参考图为精确基底角色，生成角色三视图，展示这同一个怪物的【最终进化形态（FINAL EVOLVED FORM）】。

LAYOUT（版式——与参考图完全对齐）：
纯黑背景（#000000）上的三个竖版分栏，影棚灯光，写实 CGI 渲染质感，相同的左上电影主光，相同的中性灰地面。
- 分栏1（左）：全身【正面】视图，略带低角度，佝偻站姿。
- 分栏2（中）：全身【背面】视图，同一站姿，脊柱完整可见。
- 分栏3（右）：【面部】特写，3/4 角度，与镜头强烈眼神接触。

PRESERVE FROM REFERENCE（自参考图保留，不改变）：
- 相同身高与体量——【不要】让他整体变得更高或更强壮。
- 相同佝偻剪影与蹲伏姿态。
- 相同面部结构：外露骷髅鼻窝、带几排黄染牙齿的无唇咧嘴笑、带红色巩膜的深陷黄绿色眼睛。
- 相同肤色盘：粗糙粉红红色血肉伴深色静脉底色。
- 相同缠腰布（臀部破损皮革裹布）。
- 相同趾行腿（digitigrade legs）与延长带爪的手。
- 相同的脸——必须读作同一个角色，只是被异变。

TRANSFORM（异变——最终形态升级）：

1. SKIN（皮肤）——denser（更密）、armor-like（如铠甲）：
血肉已硬化成天然骨板甲。表面读作钙化、如皮革，胸、肩、前臂、大腿皮肤下可见融合的几丁质骨脊。仿佛骨架被推出去、变成了外甲。保持相同的红肉色调，但哑光、如石，更少湿润。

2. SPINE SPIKES（脊柱尖刺）——new growth（新生）：
沿整条脊柱（从头颅基部到尾骨）新爆出一排骨刺。它们比肩刺更小更锐利，如断裂的椎骨穿出。在【背面】分栏中清晰可见。

3. HAIR（头发）——samurai topknot（武士顶髻）：
黑色长发拢成一个【高位武士顶髻（chonmage-style）】扎在头顶。基部紧缠，散尾向后垂落。油腻、纠结、有发丝散出。取代参考图中垂散的头发。

4. RIGHT ARM（右臂）——the bone-sword limb（骨剑肢体，CRITICAL DETAIL——FOCAL POINT，关键细节——焦点）：
他的右臂已异变成一件【活体骨刃武器】，与左臂根本不同：
- 形状：手臂自肩部削成延长的、刃状前臂——骨头本身长成了巨剑的形态。"手"已不再是手，而是骨刃的延续，以锐利尖点收尾。站直时长过膝盖。
- 材质：骨与金属的混成。基底是致密的炭黑骨——钙化、有机，沿其长度可见融合椎骨与钙质棱脊的纹理。
- 金属内含物：生铁与暗淡钢脉【熔入】骨结构——不是包裹它，而是像矿石困在石头里一样嵌穿它。金属显现为：
   - 沿刃长的参差条纹。
   - 高应力点（肘关节、刃脊）的簇状斑块。
   - 自刃口凸出的尖锐金属片。
   - 暗淡工业化光泽，【不是】抛光——看起来古老、未经锤炼，如长进骨里的陨铁。
- 锋刃：刃身沿全长一道清晰的切割刃，锯齿状、不规则——骨齿与金属碎片互锁。看起来是地狱里锻出来的，不是打制的。
- 对比：对照他有机的红肉左臂，这条武器化肢体读作【异类、坚不可摧、武器化】。这一视觉对比是正面分栏的中心。
- 灯光：硬朗镜面高光【只】捕捉金属内含物，令其冷冷地闪光而骨保持哑光。这种材质分离正是效果成立的关键。

TEXTURE LAW（质感法则，适用于以上所有元素）：
所有骨与血肉表面在异变之后也必须保持【有机、鲜活、自然】。此法则凌驾于任何走向简约化或"铠甲观感"的倾向。

- BONE（骨——尖刺、板、脊柱脊、右臂刃）：
   - 真实骨的纹理：多孔、略不均匀，带发丝裂纹、钙脊、细微褪色（应力点发黄、裂缝中更深）。
   - 看去是【长出来的】，不是【雕刻的】——不对称、不规则、活。
   - 哑光饰面，薄缘带细微次表面半透明。
   - 无抛光表面、无光滑奇幻铠甲观感、无陶瓷光泽。

- FLESH（血肉——皮肤、肌肉、暴露组织）：
   - 真实人类／动物皮肤纹理：毛孔、细纹、妊娠纹、疤、表面下血管网。
   - 次表面散射——光线微微穿透皮肤，尤在薄处（耳、指、眼周）。
   - 湿润／油光【只】在自然处：眼、牙龈、口内、新伤口——其余保持哑光、呼吸。
   - 可见瑕疵：皱褶里积污、干血、旧疤、色素不均。
   - 身体必须看起来会【呼吸】、会【流血】。

- HAIR（头发）：
   - 可见一根根发丝、油腻、被汗和污垢纠住。
   - 非 CGI 光洁、非洗发水广告——看起来未洗、真实。

- OVERALL FEEL（整体感受）：
   - 实拍生物特效美学（Guillermo del Toro／猎魔人／潘神的迷宫）——【不是】电子游戏电影油光。
   - 每个表面都应让你觉得可触碰，能感到温暖、纹理、残缺。
   - 这怪物是【进化】的，不是【制造】的。

RENDER SPECIFICATIONS（渲染规格）：
写实生物设计，AAA 游戏电影级／Weta Workshop 质量，高细节皮肤着色器并在肉区带次表面散射、金属臂内含物带硬镜面，所有三个分栏锐利对焦，以 85mm 镜头等效拍摄，无动态模糊，无背景元素，页面上无文字或标签。
