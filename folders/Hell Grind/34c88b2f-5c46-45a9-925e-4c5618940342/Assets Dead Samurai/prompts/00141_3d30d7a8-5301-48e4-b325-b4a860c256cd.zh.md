## 原文 (source)

Using the attached reference image as the exact base character, generate a character sheet showing this same monster in his FINAL DEMONIC WAR FORM — a creature that has walked through hell and brought hell back with him.

LAYOUT (match reference exactly):
Three vertical panels on pure black background (#000000), studio lighting, photorealistic CGI render, cinematic key light from upper-left, neutral gray ground plane.
- Panel 1: Full body FRONT, slight low angle, hunched stance
- Panel 2: Full body BACK, same stance, full back armor visible
- Panel 3: FACE close-up, 3/4 angle, intense eye contact

PRESERVE FROM REFERENCE:
- Same height and body mass — DO NOT make him taller or bulkier
- Same hunched silhouette and crouched posture
- Same facial structure: exposed skull-nose cavity, lipless grin with rows of yellow-stained teeth, sunken yellow-green eyes with red sclera
- Same loincloth (torn leather wrap at hips)
- Same digitigrade legs and elongated clawed hands
- Same sparse body hair across forearms, legs, chest, shoulders
- Same face — must read as the SAME character, just transformed

THE MATERIAL TRANSFORMATION (core concept):
His body is no longer flesh and bone as separate layers. They have FUSED. Skin has hardened and merged with the underlying skeleton, calcifying into a single armored organic surface. What was once soft flesh is now a ribbed living shield — bone, tendon, and skin pressure-cooked into one material. This is not armor he wears. This is what his body has become.

TRANSFORM (final demonic form upgrades):

1. BODY COLOR — RED, not pink:
The pink-flesh tone of the reference is gone. New color is DEEP CRIMSON RED — the color of cooked meat, dried blood, internal organs. Bordeaux red, dark wine red, oxblood. NOT bright cartoon red, NOT orange — a deep saturated meat-red that reads as VERY ALIVE and VERY WRONG. Veins underneath are darker, almost black, branching across the surface.

2. RIBBED SHIELD TORSO — full armor (CRITICAL):
The entire torso (chest, sides, abdomen, AND back) is covered in a SEGMENTED RIBBED SHIELD made of fused bone-and-skin:
- Visible HORIZONTAL RIB-LIKE SEGMENTS running across chest and abdomen — each segment slightly raised, with recessed groove between them
- The ribs are not separate bones poking through skin — they are PART of the skin, like the skin grew thick and hard along the rib lines
- Same ribbed armor wraps around the sides and continues across the BACK — full circumferential coverage
- Surface harder than the rest of the body, slightly darker red where armor is thickest
- Where armor segments meet, deep grooves cast shadow — strong silhouette definition
- ORGANIC — slightly asymmetric, irregular spacing between ribs, alive

3. SPINE SPIKES — new growth:
New row of bone spikes erupting along the entire spine, from base of skull down to tailbone. Smaller and sharper than the shoulder spikes, jagged like broken vertebrae pushing through the spine ridge of the back armor. Same deep red as the body, with bone-white tips. Visible clearly in the BACK panel.

4. BLACK METAL INCLUSIONS — embedded throughout:
Veins of dark metal — black iron, oxidized steel — run THROUGH the armored surfaces, fused into the bone-skin like ore in stone:
- Streaks along the rib segments of the torso
- Cluster patches at high-stress points (shoulder spikes base, knee plates, knuckles)
- Embedded in spine spikes (metal cores running through the bone)
- Sharp metallic shards protruding from existing shoulder spikes
- Dull industrial sheen, NOT polished — looks ancient, primordial
- Contrast: deep red flesh-armor + black metal veins = blood and iron

5. BURN MARKS — point damage from hellfire:
Across the body, distributed asymmetrically, several BURNT PATCHES where skin has been charred black by intense fire. These are POINT damage areas, not whole zones:
- Approximately 4-6 burn spots across the entire body
- LOCATIONS (distribute naturally):
 • One large patch on right shoulder/upper arm (palm-sized)
 • One on left side of ribcage (smaller)
 • One across right thigh (medium)
 • One on lower back or hip
 • One on left forearm
- TEXTURE: charred black flesh, cracked surface like burnt wood or carbonized meat. Cracks may show DEEP RED underneath where burn went into living tissue
- EDGES: irregular, with surrounding skin slightly darker and angrier near burn, fading back to deep red body color
- These burns look OLD — healed but never recovered. Permanent black scarring fused into the armor surface
- Some burns intersect with ribbed armor and metal veins — hellfire touched everything

6. HAIR — samurai topknot:
Long black hair pulled up into a HIGH SAMURAI TOPKNOT (chonmage-style) on the crown. Tight wrap at base, loose tail falling back. Greasy, matted, strands escaping. Replaces the loose hanging hair from the reference.

7. RIGHT ARM — bone-sword limb (FOCAL POINT):
Right arm transformed into a living BONE-BLADE WEAPON, fundamentally different from left:
- SHAPE: Tapers from shoulder into elongated blade-like forearm. The "hand" is the continuation of the bone-blade, ending in a sharp pointed tip. Reaches past the knee when standing
- MATERIAL: Same dark red bone-skin as the body but harder and denser. Visible texture of fused vertebrae and calcium ridges along its length
- METAL CORE: Heavy black metal veins run THROUGH the blade structure — more concentrated than elsewhere on the body. Jagged streaks along blade's length, cluster patches at elbow joint, sharp metallic shards from cutting edge
- EDGE: Clear cutting edge running its length, serrated and irregular — bone teeth interlocked with metal fragments
- LIGHTING: Hard specular highlights catch ONLY metal inclusions, glinting coldly while bone-flesh stays matte
- Weapon-arm may have one or two burn marks — even his weapon was scorched

TEXTURE LAW (organic horror, never CGI gloss):

DEEP RED ARMORED FLESH (torso, limbs):
- Real organic surface — pores, micro-wrinkles, stretch lines, vein networks under surface
- Subsurface scattering — light penetrates slightly at thinner areas (between rib segments, at joints)
- MATTE finish overall, very subtle wet sheen only at joints and creases
- Slightly wet/glistening look in deep grooves between rib segments — like dried blood collected there
- NO ceramic shine, NO polished surface, NO video game armor

BONE ELEMENTS (spikes, blade):
- Bone-white only at the very tips of spikes and blade edge
- Otherwise integrated with deep red — calcified red-brown bone, not clean white
- Porous, slightly uneven, with hairline cracks
- Looks GROWN, not sculpted

METAL VEINS:
- Embedded INTO the surface, not coating it
- Dark iron-black with subtle warm rust-touched undertones
- Catches hard specular only at most exposed points
- Some pieces jagged shards, some smooth veins

BURN SCARS:
- Charred black surface with CRACKED texture like dried lava or burnt wood
- Cracks may glow with slightest hint of deep red where tissue beneath shows
- NOT smooth black, NOT painted — three-dimensional carbonized texture
- Around each burn, surrounding skin slightly darker, angrier red

BODY HAIR:
- Sparse, wiry dark hair across forearms, lower legs, chest, upper back
- Some hairs grow between rib segments and around metal veins
- Coarse, animal-like, NOT grooming hair
- Survives near burn marks but absent ON the burns (hair burned away)

HEAD HAIR:
- Greasy black topknot, individual strands, matted, real
- NOT shampoo-clean

OVERALL FEEL:
- Practical creature effects aesthetic — Guillermo del Toro / Pan's Labyrinth / The Witcher
- Every surface touchable, warm, imperfect
- Monster EVOLVED through suffering — animal, not statue, not video game character

LIGHTING & MOOD:
- Cinematic key light from upper-left — strong but not harsh
- Subtle red rim light from below or behind — gives deep red armor faint glow at edges, like body is hot
- Deep shadows in rib grooves and around burns
- Black background absorbs stray light — silhouette reads strong
- Metal inclusions are the ONLY cold-colored elements — everything else is warm red, warm bone, warm shadow

RENDER:
Photorealistic creature design / Weta Workshop quality, high-detail organic shader with subsurface scattering on flesh-armor, hard specular on metal inclusions, sharp focus across all three panels, 85mm lens equivalent, no motion blur, no background elements, no text or labels.

## 中文对照 (zh)

以所附参考图为精确基底角色，生成角色三视图，展示这同一个怪物的【最终魔神战争形态（FINAL DEMONIC WAR FORM）】——一个走过地狱、并把地狱随身带回来的生物。

LAYOUT（版式，与参考图完全对齐）：
纯黑背景（#000000）上的三个竖版分栏，影棚灯光，写实 CGI 渲染，左上方电影主光，中性灰地面。
- 分栏1：全身【正面】，略带低角度，佝偻站姿。
- 分栏2：全身【背面】，同一站姿，背部铠甲完整可见。
- 分栏3：【面部】特写，3/4 角度，强烈的眼神接触。

PRESERVE FROM REFERENCE（自参考图保留）：
- 相同身高与体量——【不要】把他做成整体更高或更壮。
- 相同佝偻剪影与蹲伏姿态。
- 相同面部结构：外露骷髅鼻窝、带几排黄染牙齿的无唇咧嘴笑、带红色巩膜的深陷黄绿色眼睛。
- 相同缠腰布（臀部破损皮革裹布）。
- 相同趾行腿（digitigrade legs）与延长带爪的手。
- 相同稀疏体毛，遍布前臂、腿、胸口、肩。
- 相同的脸——必须读作同一个角色，只是被异变了。

THE MATERIAL TRANSFORMATION（材质异变，核心概念）：
他的身体不再是分层的肉与骨。它们已经【融合】。皮肤已硬化并与底层骨骼结合，钙化成单一的装甲有机表面。原本柔软的血肉如今是一面带肋的活盾——骨、腱、皮肤被高压锻成一整块材质。这不是他【穿】的铠甲。这是他身体【变成】的东西。

TRANSFORM（异变——最终魔神形态升级）：

1. BODY COLOR（身体颜色）——RED（红），不是 pink（粉）：
参考图的粉色肉色调【消失】。新颜色是【深绯红】——熟肉、干血、内脏器官的颜色。酒红、深酒红、牛血红。不是明亮的卡通红、不是橙色——一种深饱和的肉红，读作【极其鲜活、极其不对劲】。底下的血管更深、几乎发黑，成枝状横过表面。

2. RIBBED SHIELD TORSO（肋状盾躯干）——full armor（全甲，CRITICAL，关键）：
整个躯干（胸、侧、腹、【以及】背）覆盖一幅由骨与皮肤融合而成的【分段肋状盾】：
- 可见的水平【肋状分段】横过胸与腹——每段微隆起，段间带下陷凹槽。
- 这些肋【不是】刺破皮肤的一块块独立骨——它们是【皮肤的一部分】，仿佛皮肤沿着肋线长厚了、长硬了。
- 同一肋甲绕到侧身、继续横过【背部】——完整周向覆盖。
- 表面比身体其余部分更硬，铠甲最厚处红得略深。
- 铠甲段相接处，深沟投下阴影——清晰的剪影定义。
- 有机——略带不对称、肋间间距不规则、活。

3. SPINE SPIKES（脊柱尖刺）——新生：
一排新的骨刺沿整条脊柱爆出，从头颅基部一直长到尾骨。比肩刺更小更锐利，如断裂的椎骨穿出，刺破背部甲的脊。与身体同深红，骨白色尖端。在【背面】分栏中清晰可见。

4. BLACK METAL INCLUSIONS（黑色金属内含物）——全程嵌入：
深色金属矿脉——黑铁、氧化钢——【贯穿】装甲表面，像石头中的矿石一样熔进骨皮：
- 沿躯干肋段呈条纹。
- 在高应力点（肩刺根、膝甲、指节）呈簇状斑块。
- 嵌入脊柱刺（贯穿骨的金属芯）。
- 从既有肩刺上突出的尖锐金属片。
- 暗淡工业化光泽，【不是】抛光——看起来古老、原始。
- 对比：深红肉甲 + 黑色金属脉 = 血与铁。

5. BURN MARKS（烧伤痕）——来自地狱火的点状损伤：
遍布全身、不对称分布，有若干处被烈焰烧成焦黑的【烧灼斑块】。这些是【点状】损伤区，而非整片区域：
- 全身约 4-6 处烧伤点。
- 位置（自然地分布）：
  · 右肩／上臂一处大斑（手掌大小）。
  · 左侧肋笼一处（更小）。
  · 右大腿横跨一处（中等）。
  · 下背或胯一处。
  · 左前臂一处。
- 纹理：焦黑的肉、开裂表面如烧焦的木头或碳化的肉。裂缝可能在下方显出【深红】，那是火烧进活组织之处。
- 边缘：不规则，烧伤附近的周围皮肤略深、更"怒气冲冲"，渐变回深红体色。
- 这些烧伤【很旧】——愈合但从未复原。永久性黑色疤痕熔进装甲表面。
- 有些烧伤与肋甲和金属脉相交——地狱火触及了一切。

6. HAIR（头发）——武士顶髻：
黑色长发拢成一个【高位武士顶髻（chonmage-style）】扎在头顶。根部紧缠，散尾向后垂落。油腻、纠结、有发丝散出。取代参考图中垂散的头发。

7. RIGHT ARM（右臂）——bone-sword limb（骨剑肢体，FOCAL POINT，焦点）：
右臂异变成了一件【活体骨刃武器】，与左手根本不同：
- 形状：自肩部削成延长的剑刃状前臂。"手"是骨刃的延续，以尖锐的尖点收尾。站直时长过膝盖。
- 材质：与身体相同的深红骨皮，但更硬、更密。沿其长度可见融合椎骨与钙质棱脊的纹理。
- 金属芯：粗重黑色金属脉【贯穿】刃身结构——比身体其他任何地方都更集中。沿刃长的参差条纹、肘关节处的簇状斑块、自刃口伸出的尖锐金属片。
- 锋刃：沿全长一道清晰的刃口，锯齿状、不规则——骨齿与金属碎块互锁。
- 灯光：硬朗镜面高光【只】捕捉金属内含物，令其冷冷地闪光，而骨肉保持哑光。
- 武器臂可能带一两处烧伤——连他的武器都被烧灼过。

TEXTURE LAW（质感法则，有机恐怖，绝不 CGI 油亮）：

DEEP RED ARMORED FLESH（深红装甲血肉——躯干、肢体）：
- 真实有机表面——毛孔、微小皱纹、拉伸纹、皮表下血管网。
- 次表面散射——光线在较薄处（肋段之间、关节处）微微穿透。
- 整体【哑光】饰面，只有关节和褶皱处带极微弱的湿泽。
- 肋段间深槽里有略微湿/发亮的外观——像那里积了干血。
- 无陶瓷光泽、无抛光表面、无游戏铠甲。

BONE ELEMENTS（骨元素——尖刺、刃）：
- 只在尖刺和刃口的最尖端呈骨白色。
- 其余为大面积的有机红棕骨——多孔、不均、钙质纹理。
- 看去是【长出来的】，而非【雕刻的】——不对称、不规则、活。
- 薄缘处带细微次表面。

METAL INCLUSIONS（金属内含物——装甲表面各处）：
- 嵌在骨内，氧化铁黑——非涂覆。
- 高应力点更明显。
- 只在最凸出点捕捉坚硬冷冽镜面光。
- 锈蚀与微蓝的氧化色调。

BURNS（烧伤）：
- 碳化的焦黑表面，干涸裂缝状纹理——如烧黑的木头。
- 裂纹露出下层深红活组织。
- 三维碳化纹理，绝非一层平面漆。

BODY HAIR（体毛）：
- 稀疏、硬挺、硬直的深色毛，遍布裸露的装甲肉——前臂、小腿、下背。
- 皮肉相熔处可被烧掉。

BODY OIL / MOISTURE（体油／潮气）：
- 细微、天然、动物般的湿度在体表，非湿亮汗渍。

OVERALL FEEL（整体感受）：
- 实拍生物特效美学，Guillermo del Toro／潘神的迷宫／猎魔人。
- 每一层都读作由活肉所生——铠甲不是贴合皮肤，【它已经是】皮肤。
- 红色是主宰色——一处深色谋杀般的绯红。

LIGHTING & MOOD（灯光与氛围）：
- 左上方电影主光——强烈但不刺眼。
- 下方或后方细微红色轮廓光——捕捉暗盾边缘和剑的骨侧。
- 深沟与烧伤周围深阴影。
- 黑色背景吞噬杂散光——剪影强烈。
- 剑臂的钢与金属脉是【唯一】例外冷色元素——其余皆暖。

RENDER（渲染）：
写实生物设计／Weta Workshop 质量，肉上高性能细节有机着色器并带次表面散射，骨盾与剑骨侧用硬质表面着色、冶金着色器用于剑臂钢侧与金属尖，所有三个分栏锐利对焦，85mm 镜头等效，无动态模糊，无背景元素，无文字或标签。
