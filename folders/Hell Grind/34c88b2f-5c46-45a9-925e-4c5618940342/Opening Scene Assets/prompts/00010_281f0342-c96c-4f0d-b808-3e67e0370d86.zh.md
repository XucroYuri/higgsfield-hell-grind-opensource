## 原文 (source)

Photorealistic top-down product shot of three different 
modern mainstream candy items on a muted grey concrete 
surface. A variety mix — one chocolate bar, one small candy 
snack pouch, and one lollipop — all in glossy plastic-foil 
commercial packaging with bold saturated solid colors and 
simple abstract decorative graphic patterns, modern candy 
aisle visual style. All wrappers are COMPLETELY BLANK of 
any text, writing, letters, numbers, words, logos, brand 
marks, or typography of any kind — only pure abstract 
decorative shapes and solid color fields. Real practical 
photography aesthetic.

Item 1 — CHOCOLATE BAR: glossy bright RED plastic-foil 
wrapper rectangular chocolate bar with simple yellow 
abstract curved shape elements scattered across the 
wrapper, no central emblem, shiny reflective surface, 
standard candy bar proportions.

Item 2 — CANDY SNACK POUCH: glossy bright BLUE plastic 
snack-size foil pouch bag of small candies, upright or 
slightly slumped pouch shape with sealed crinkly top edge, 
solid blue base color with simple white wavy line elements 
and orange dot accents across the pouch, no central emblem, 
shiny reflective plastic surface, modern commercial candy 
bag aesthetic.

Item 3 — LOLLIPOP: a single modern round candy lollipop on 
a white plastic stick, fully wrapped in clear plastic 
twisted at both ends like classic lollipop wrapping, the 
candy inside visible as a glossy bright YELLOW-ORANGE 
spherical or disc-shaped hard candy with subtle internal 
swirl pattern, simple modern lollipop appearance.

Items laid flat on the surface with slight casual 
placement, slightly offset and not perfectly aligned, like 
real items placed down.

Soft natural overhead daylight, gentle shadows, slight 
focal falloff, painterly cinematic still life.

Palette: cool grey neutral background, bold saturated 
candy-bright colors — vivid red, vivid blue, vivid yellow-
orange — as dominant chromatic accents, glossy plastic-
foil packaging finish.

Photorealistic ARRI Alexa LF anamorphic, organic 35mm Kodak 
Vision3 250D grain, painterly cinematic film still.

NO branded packaging, NO logos, NO brand marks, NO emblems, 
NO readable text, NO writing, NO letters, NO words, NO 
numbers, NO typography, NO labels, NO copyrighted brands, 
NO artisan craft aesthetic, NO matte paper, NO digital 3D 
render.
