## 原文 (source)

A young child's very messy unfinished hand-drawn pencil and 
colored pencil drawing on slightly crumpled aged white paper, 
extremely naive childlike art style as if drawn by a 5-6 
year old child in kindergarten. Photographed flat from 
directly above as a top-down scan-style shot, the paper 
occupying the full frame, soft even daylight, paper texture 
visible, faint eraser smudges and small finger marks on the 
page.

The drawing shows an epic battle scene — a brave RED KNIGHT 
fighting a HUGE TERRIFYING MONSTER.

The RED KNIGHT is a small stick-figure character on the left 
side of the page, wearing bright red armor scribbled in red 
pencil going way outside the lines, a simple helmet with a 
visor slit for eyes, holding a tall sword raised high above 
his head drawn in shaky grey pencil, a small triangle shield 
in his other hand, tiny legs in a running pose, a small red 
cape flowing behind him.

The HUGE MONSTER takes up most of the right side of the 
page, drawn much bigger than the knight, dark green scribbled 
body with rough scribbly scales and bumps, huge round googly 
eyes with black dot pupils, an open mouth full of jagged 
triangle teeth, two big curved horns on its head, sharp 
claws on outstretched arms reaching toward the knight, a 
long scribbly tail, parts of the monster left in pencil 
outline without color yet.

Action elements scattered around — wobbly zigzag action 
lines indicating speed, a few small star shapes for impact, 
small orange and yellow flame scribbles around the sword 
tip, the word "BAM!" written in shaky uneven capital letters 
near the monster.

Background: a wonky castle shape in the distance with simple 
square towers in grey pencil, a yellow sun with a smiley 
face in one corner, scribbly green grass at the bottom of 
the page, a couple of small clouds.

Clearly unfinished — some parts brightly scribble-colored, 
others still in pencil outline only, coloring messy and goes 
outside the lines in classic young child way, eraser 
smudges, wobbly proportions, inconsistent figure sizes, 
endearing imperfect quality. Authentic young child's drawing 
aesthetic, drawn by a real small kindergarten-age child with 
imagination, very imperfect, charming.

NO professional illustration, NO polished art, NO digital 
art, NO anime style, NO clean lines, NO consistent 
proportions, NO adult drawing skill.
