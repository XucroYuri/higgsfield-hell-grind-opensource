Photorealistic top-down product shot of three different 
modern mainstream commercial candy items on a muted grey 
concrete surface, looking exactly like real supermarket 
candy products with full authentic commercial wrapper design 
language — sophisticated multi-color graphics, photographic-
style candy imagery on the wrappers, gradient color 
transitions, curved diagonal banner graphics, drop shadow 
design elements, realistic plastic wrapper textures and 
sheen — but with NO readable text, NO writing, NO letters, 
NO words, NO numbers, NO logos, NO brand marks, NO 
typography of any kind, only design imagery and graphics. 
Real practical photography aesthetic.

Item 1 — CHOCOLATE BAR: a rectangular candy bar in a 
sophisticated multi-color glossy plastic-foil wrapper, dark 
warm brown base color with a bold red diagonal banner 
graphic crossing the wrapper, golden accent shapes, a small 
photographic-style image of a chocolate bar cross-section 
with caramel and nougat layers visible on one side of the 
wrapper, subtle drop-shadow design effects, looking exactly 
like a real commercial chocolate bar from the supermarket.

Item 2 — CANDY SNACK POUCH: a small upright candy snack-
size pouch bag with sealed crinkly top, sophisticated multi-
color glossy plastic foil — a vibrant rainbow gradient base 
with photographic-style imagery of small colorful candy 
pieces scattered across the front, looking exactly like a 
real commercial small candy bag from the supermarket.

Item 3 — LOLLIPOP: a single modern round candy lollipop on 
a white plastic stick, wrapped in printed glossy plastic 
twisted at both ends, the wrapper showing sophisticated 
photographic imagery of swirled rainbow candy with gradient 
colors, looking exactly like a real commercial lollipop from 
the supermarket.

Items laid flat on the surface with slight casual placement, 
slightly offset, like real items placed down. Subtle 
realistic plastic wrapper crinkles, soft reflective sheen, 
authentic packaging detail.

Soft natural overhead daylight, gentle shadows, slight focal 
falloff, painterly cinematic still life.

Palette: cool grey neutral background, sophisticated 
commercial candy wrapper colors with multiple color 
combinations per item, photographic-style candy imagery 
adding visual depth, glossy plastic-foil packaging.

Photorealistic ARRI Alexa LF anamorphic, organic 35mm Kodak 
Vision3 250D grain, painterly cinematic film still.

NO readable text, NO writing, NO letters, NO words, NO 
numbers, NO logos, NO brand marks, NO typography, NO 
copyrighted brands, NO single solid color flat wrappers, NO 
simple abstract shapes only, NO toy-like fake candy 
appearance, NO digital 3D render.
