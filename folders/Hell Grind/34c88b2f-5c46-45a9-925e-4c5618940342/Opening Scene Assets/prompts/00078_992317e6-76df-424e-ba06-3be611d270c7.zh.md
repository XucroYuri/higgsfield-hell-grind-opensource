## 原文 (source)

A young child's very messy unfinished hand-drawn pencil and 
colored pencil drawing on slightly crumpled aged white paper, 
extremely naive childlike art style as if drawn by a 5-6 year 
old child in kindergarten, four cute simple character figures 
standing across the page with wobbly stick-figure-like 
proportions and disproportionately large round heads on small 
bodies, drawn with shaky uneven lines, scribbly messy 
coloring that goes way outside the outlines, charming amateur 
quality. Their names written in shaky uppercase kindergarten 
handwriting below each, some letters backwards or 
inconsistent in size. Photographed flat from directly above 
as a top-down scan-style shot, the paper occupying the full 
frame, soft even daylight, paper texture visible, faint 
eraser smudges and small finger marks on the page.

REIN on the left — a simple stick-figure girl with a huge 
round head, short straight silver-grey bob hair scribbled in 
grey pencil with darker streaks, a red squiggly scar mark 
over her left eye, two dot eyes and a tiny mouth, a wobbly 
purple zip hoodie shape scribbled in purple way outside the 
lines, black pants only in uncolored outline. "REIN" below 
in shaky capitals.

LULU next — a simple stick-figure girl with a big round head, 
brown hair with bangs drawn as messy brown scribbles, two 
dot eyes and small smile, a blue tank top shape colored 
roughly in blue with an orange stripe at the waist, baggy 
pants partially scribbled in dark color, tiny circle earrings 
as careful dots. "LULU" below in shaky capitals.

JAX next — a simple stick-figure boy with a big round head, 
dark brown skin colored in brown pencil with the color going 
outside the head outline, bright yellow bleached buzz-cut 
hair as a small yellow scribbled oval on top, large black 
sunglasses with two tiny spike points carefully drawn on his 
head, a camo jacket attempted as chaotic green-brown-grey 
scribbles with little patch shapes, much of the jacket left 
uncolored outline. "JAX" below in shaky capitals, the J 
slightly backwards.

ROKO on the right — a simple stick-figure boy with a big 
round head, messy black hair as wild scribbly lines on top, 
a clear red dot on his nose, a tiny mustache as two small 
lines, a grey tank top in light scribbly pencil, baggy red 
pants colored in red going outside the lines, a black strap 
across his chest. "ROKO" below in shaky capitals.

Clearly unfinished — some parts brightly scribble-colored, 
other parts still only in pencil outline with no color yet, 
all coloring messy and goes outside the lines in classic 
young child way, eraser smudges, small mistakes throughout, 
inconsistent figure sizes (one slightly bigger than another), 
the whole drawing endearing and emotionally tender. 
Background scattered with childlike doodles — a wobbly 
yellow sun with a smiling face in one corner, a few small 
uneven hearts, a fluffy cloud, a small rainbow arc, all 
drawn in classic kindergarten naive style.

Authentic young child's drawing aesthetic, drawn by a real 
small child, very imperfect, endearingly amateur, wobbly 
shapes, like a real drawing made by a kindergarten-age child 
in an orphanage drawing their older friends with love.

NO professional illustration, NO polished art, NO digital 
art, NO anime style, NO adult drawing skill, NO clean lines, 
NO consistent proportions, NO careful coloring.
