EDIT: SAME empty chamber. Move the camera HARD to the right side of the hall — lens almost touching the right wall — then rotate 45 DEGREES LEFT (yaw only) to look diagonally across the hall toward the far end.

The RIGHT-WALL columns dominate the right edge of frame, very large in foreground, like you're standing right beside them. The LEFT WALL is far across the hall, small and distant. Floor seams sweep from lower-right diagonally up toward upper-left. Strong perspective convergence.

CRITICAL — HORIZON STAYS LEVEL. Columns vertical, floor flat-horizontal. NO Dutch angle. NO tilt. NO roll. ONLY horizontal yaw rotation.

Same stone, same light shaft, same oil lamps along both walls, same haze, same atmosphere. Empty hall, no characters.

## 中文对照 (zh)

编辑：同一空房间。将摄影机果断移至大厅右方——镜头几乎贴到右墙——然后向左水平旋转 45 度（仅偏航 yaw），斜向望向大厅远端。

右侧墙（RIGHT-WALL）的立柱占据画面右缘，在前景中显得非常高大，仿佛你就站在它们旁边。左墙（LEFT WALL）远在大厅对岸，小而遥远。地面接缝从右下斜向上扫向左上。透视纵深强烈。

关键——地平线保持水平。立柱垂直，地面平直。不得有荷兰角（Dutch angle）、不得倾斜（tilt）、不得滚转（roll）。只允许水平偏航旋转。

同样的石材、同样的光柱、沿两墙同样的油灯、同样的雾霾、同样的氛围。空无一人的大厅，无任何角色。
