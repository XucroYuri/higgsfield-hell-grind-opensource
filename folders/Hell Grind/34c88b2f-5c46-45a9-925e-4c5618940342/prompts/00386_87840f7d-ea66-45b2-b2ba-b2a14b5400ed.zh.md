CHARACTER DESIGN PROMPT — COMBAT TRAINING ROBOT
Full-body character design sheet. Front view + back view + 3/4 fighting stance view.
Overall silhouette: Humanoid bipedal combat robot. Height approximately 190–200 cm. Build: lean but dense — not bulky, built for speed and precision over raw mass. No outer shell or armour plating — the entire structure is exposed skeletal exoskeleton.
Head: Smooth matte black dome skull — no face, no facial features except the jaw structure (exposed mechanical mandible with visible tooth-like metal ridges). Single oversized glowing red eye centred in the forehead — not a lens, a deep red luminous orb that pulses faintly. No nose, no ears, no external sensors visible.
Torso: Exposed ribcage framework — individual curved rib struts visible, connected to a central sternum column. Between the ribs: negative space showing the internal chamber where the hydraulic fuel reservoir is housed (partially visible as a dark black liquid-filled cylinder). Spine: fully exposed segmented vertebral column running the full length of the back — each vertebra is a distinct machined component. No skin, no panels covering the structure.
Arms: Skeletal arm assembly — upper arm has two parallel structural tubes (radius/ulna equivalent), exposed elbow joint with rotating pivot. Forearms taper to three-fingered clawed hands — each finger terminates in a curved blade claw (12–15 cm, sharp, matte black). The claws can retract partially but are never fully sheathed.
Legs: Reverse-jointed (digitigrade) lower legs — the knee bends forward, the ankle is elevated, weight carried on a narrow three-toed foot platform. This gives a predatory, spring-loaded stance. Upper thigh: solid structural panels. Lower leg: exposed cable bundles and hydraulic pistons.
Material: All components matte black with subtle carbon-fibre surface texture — catches specular highlights at edges only. No chrome, no polished surfaces. Hydraulic fuel lines run externally along the limbs — black rubberized tubing, visible at every joint junction. When damaged, these lines sever and leak viscous black hydraulic fluid.
Damage state (secondary design): Same robot with combat damage — one arm missing at the shoulder (clean mechanical shear, fuel line spraying black fluid), skull cracked revealing internal components, ribcage partially crushed (one side caved inward), red eye flickering.
Fighting stance (3/4 view): Weight shifted forward onto left foot, both arms raised and slightly forward, claws extended, skull angled down — predatory crouch. Red eye burning at full intensity.
Technical: 8K detail. No background. Grey neutral ground plane for shadow reference only. Hard practical side-lighting from left catching all skeletal edges — deep shadow on right. Photorealistic material rendering — matte black absorbs light, only structural edges and the red eye emit/reflect.

## 中文对照 (zh)

角色设计提示词——格斗训练机器人
全身角色设计表。正面视图 + 背面视图 + 3/4 格斗姿态视图。
总体轮廓（Overall silhouette）：人形双足格斗机器人。身高约 190–200 厘米。体格：精瘦却致密——不笨重，为速度与精准而非蛮力而造。无外壳或装甲板——整个结构是暴露的骨架状外骨骼。
头（Head）：光滑哑光黑色圆顶颅骨——无脸、无五官，唯除下颚结构（外露机械下颌，带可见的齿状金属脊）。一只超大、发光的红色眼睛居中于前额——不是镜头，而是一个微微脉动的深红发光球体。无鼻子、无耳朵、无可见外部传感器。
躯干（Torso）：暴露的肋笼框架——可见独立弯曲肋条，连接到中央胸骨柱。肋骨之间：负空间显示出容纳液压燃料储箱的内部腔室（部分可见为一根深色黑色充液体圆柱）。脊柱：完全暴露的分段脊椎，贯穿背部全长——每节椎骨都是一个独立机加工部件。无皮肤、无覆盖结构的覆板。
手臂（Arms）：骨架状手臂组件——上臂有两条平行结构管（相当于桡骨/尺骨），外露肘关节带旋转枢轴。前臂渐收为三指的爪状手——每根手指终止于一个弧形刀状利爪（12–15 cm，锐利，哑光黑）。利爪可部分收回，但绝不完全收纳。
腿（Legs）：反向关节（趾行式）小腿——膝盖前屈，脚踝抬高，重量承载在窄窄的三趾足平台上。这赋予其掠食性、蓄满弹簧的姿态。大腿上部：实心结构面板。小腿：外露的缆线束与液压活塞。
材质（Material）：所有组件哑光黑带微妙的碳纤维表面质感——仅在边缘捕捉镜面高光。无镀铬、无抛光表面。液压燃料管线沿四肢外露走线——黑色包胶管，在每一处关节结合处可见。损坏时，这些管线断裂并泄漏粘稠的黑色液压油。
损坏状态（Damage state，次级设计）：同一机器人带战斗损伤——一条手臂在肩部缺失（干净机械剪切、燃料管线喷射黑色液体）、颅骨开裂露出内部组件、肋笼部分被压碎（一侧向内塌陷）、红色眼睛忽闪。
格斗姿态（Fighting stance，3/4 视图）：重心前移压到左脚上，双臂抬起略向前，利爪伸出，颅骨下倾——掠食性蹲伏。红色眼睛以满强度燃烧。

技术（Technical）：8K 细节。无背景。灰色中性地面平面仅作阴影参考。来自左侧的硬朗实用侧光捕捉所有骨架边缘——右侧深阴影。写实摄影材质渲染——哑光黑吸收光线，只有结构边缘与红色眼睛发光/反射。
