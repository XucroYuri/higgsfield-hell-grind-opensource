## 原文 (source)

Shot on 35mm film, real abandoned Pizza Chain restaurant interior, pitch black night. Red vinyl booth seats along the walls, tables with checkered red and white tablecloths, thick layer of dust on every surface. Small kitchen pass-through window on the back wall, impenetrable darkness inside the kitchen. A single orange sodium streetlamp outside the window casting one narrow beam of warm amber light into the room — illuminating only a fragment of a dusty table, the edge of a booth seat, a patch of floor. Everything else swallowed by deep teal-black darkness. Hard shadows. Cold teal and deep black dominate, with one isolated pool of warm amber from the streetlamp. Dust particles visible only inside that beam of light. Film grain, available light only, no flash. handheld, documentary urban exploration photography.

PHOTOREALISM — REAL PHOTOGRAPH WITH SHARP TEXTURES AND REALISTIC LIGHT:

CAMERA & LENS:
- Shot on Arri Alexa 65 / RED V-Raptor 8K / Sony Venice 2 8K large-format cinema sensor with Zeiss Supreme Prime or Cooke S7/i Anamorphic lens
- Native large-format depth of field with gentle focus falloff, foreground tack-sharp, background slightly soft
- Real lens characteristics: subtle chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, mild barrel distortion at frame edges, slight corner vignetting, occasional faint anamorphic flare on bright highlights
- Authentic photographic grain and sensor noise, more visible in shadows, calibrated to scene lighting (ISO 200–400 in bright light, 800–1600 in moderate, 1600–3200 in low-light interiors)

TEXTURE QUALITY — TACK-SHARP, NOT MUSHY:
- Razor-sharp micro-detail across the entire frame — every individual texture element clearly resolved
- Stone surfaces: rough granular porous texture with visible mineral grain, individual aggregate, chisel marks on carved stone, weathering pits, dust accumulation in crevices
- Wood surfaces: deep visible wood grain with raised fibers, individual plank seams, lacquer crackle, knots, age cracks, axe and chisel marks
- Metal surfaces: hammered hand-tooled texture with individual hammer dimples, etched or engraved relief, oxidation patina, scratches, soot or grease buildup
- Fabric: visible weave, individual threads readable, faded dye, fraying at edges, dust embedded in fibers
- Paint: brushstrokes visible, paint cracking and flaking, age patina, color shifts, edges curling where paint peels
- Glass: real refraction, edge highlights, dust on surface, micro-scratches, smudges
- Skin (if present): pores, fine peach fuzz, capillary network, subsurface scattering, asymmetric blemishes — never airbrushed

LIGHTING — PHYSICALLY REAL:
- Light behaves with real-world physics: realistic falloff with distance, proper bouncing onto surfaces, accurate shadow rolloff into ambient
- Shadow rolloff goes into deep cool blue-grey, NEVER pure black; highlights stay textured, NEVER blown to pure white clipping
- Surfaces show realistic specular response based on actual material — matte stone diffuses softly, polished metal has tight specular reflections, wet stone has mirror-like sheen, dry rough wood barely reflects
- Sources of light have visible direction and physical anchors — nothing glows mysteriously without an explainable source
- Air carries volume — atmospheric haze, dust motes, smoke, breath, depending on the scene
- Color temperature is consistent and natural — daylight cool grey, tungsten warm but never neon orange, fire flickering warm red-orange but never neon yellow
- Mixed lighting reads as cinematographically intentional — cool key + warm practicals create dimensional separation
- NO ambient occlusion that looks painted-on, NO flat global illumination, NO impossible bounce light, NO HDR over-exposure, NO golden-hour saturation unless the scene actually is golden hour

NEGATIVE DIRECTIVES — CRITICAL:
- NOT a render, NOT 3D, NOT CGI, NOT illustration, NOT painting, NOT matte painting, NOT video game still, NOT concept art
- NO CGI smoothness, NO uncanny perfection, NO over-clean surfaces, NO plastic-looking materials, NO rubber-looking skin
- NO Unreal Engine look, NO Octane render look, NO Blender Cycles look, NO 3D render look
- NO AI-smoothed surfaces, NO low-res blur, NO mushy textures, NO soapy surfaces, NO painted-on details, NO flat shaded planes
- NO over-saturation, NO HDR halos, NO heavy color grading, NO Instagram filter, NO clarity slider abuse
- NO uncanny symmetry, NO repeated identical pattern tiling, NO suspiciously perfect alignment. REAL-WORLD IMPERFECTIONS (always present at high resolution):
- Dust accumulation visible grain by grain on flat surfaces
- Soot and smoke staining on upper walls and ceilings (interiors) or on weathered exteriors
- Water stains, mineral deposits, salt bloom on stone
- Wax drips, candle soot, grease around lamps
- Faded peeling paint with edges curling and underlayer showing through
- Mismatched repair patches with different age and color
- Individual scratches, gouges, dings from use
- Cobwebs in dark forgotten corners (when atmospheric)
- Asymmetric weathering — one side worn more than the other based on light/wind exposure
- Natural micro-variation across every surface — no two square inches identical

FINISHING:
- Faint cinema diffusion (Tiffen Black Pro-Mist 1/8 — minimal strength) — subtle creamy halation only around the brightest highlights, preserves overall sharpness
- Color science of a real cinema camera — natural shadow rolloff, smooth highlight clipping behavior, real color rendition based on the dominant light source
- Cinematography reference: Roger Deakins / Greig Fraser — sharp but organic, photographic, never artificial
- Architectural sharpness reference (for built environments): Hélène Binet / Iwan Baan — crisp resolution of every architectural detail

The final image must read unmistakably as a real photograph captured on professional cinema equipment, NOT as any kind of digital render or AI generation.

## 中文对照 (zh)

以 35mm 胶片拍摄，真实废弃的披萨连锁餐厅（Pizza Chain）室内，漆黑深夜。沿墙是红色乙烯基卡座软椅，铺着红白格纹桌布的餐桌，每个表面都覆盖厚厚一层灰。后墙上有一个小型厨房递餐窗口，厨房内部是穿透不透的黑暗。窗外一盏橙色钠路灯，向屋内投射出一束窄窄的暖琥珀色光线——只照亮落灰餐桌的一角、卡座座椅的边缘、一小片地面。其余一切都被深邃的青-black 黑暗吞噬。硬朗阴影。冷青色与深黑主导，路上路灯形成唯一一片孤立的暖琥珀色光池。只有在光束内可见灰尘微粒。胶片颗粒，仅用现场光，无闪光灯，手持拍摄，纪录片式城市探险摄影。

写实摄影（PHOTOREALISM）——具有锐利质感与写实光线的真实照片：

摄影机与镜头（CAMERA & LENS）：
- 以 Arri Alexa 65 / RED V-Raptor 8K / Sony Venice 2 8K 大画幅电影传感器拍摄，配 Zeiss Supreme Prime 或 Cooke S7/i 变形宽银幕镜头
- 原生大画幅景深，焦点柔和过渡，前景锐利清晰，背景略柔
- 真实的镜头特性：高对比边缘的轻微色差、画面边缘的轻微桶形畸变、轻微暗角、明亮高光上偶尔的素淡变形宽银幕光晕（anamorphic flare）
- 真实的摄影颗粒与传感器噪点，暗部更明显，依据场景照明校准（明亮光下 ISO 200–400，中度光 800–1600，低光室内 1600–3200）

质感质量（TEXTURE QUALITY）——极为锐利，而非绵软（TACK-SHARP, NOT MUSHY）：
- 全画面的锐利微细节——每一处独立质感元素都清晰分辨
- 石头表面：粗糙颗粒状多孔质感，可见矿物纹理、独立骨料、雕琢石头上的凿痕、风化坑洞、裂隙中的积尘
- 木头表面：深层可见木纹带隆起纤维、独立板缝、漆面龟裂、结疤、岁月裂纹、斧凿痕迹
- 金属表面：手工锤打锻造质感带独立锤痕、蚀刻或浮雕、氧化锈斑、刮痕、烟灰或油污积聚
- 布料：可见织纹、独立线缕可辨、褪色染料、边缘磨损抽丝、纤维中嵌入灰尘
- 油漆：可见笔触、油漆开裂剥落、岁月包浆、色调变化、剥落边缘卷曲
- 玻璃：真实折射、边缘高光、表面灰尘、微刮痕、污渍
- 皮肤（如有）：毛孔、细腻桃绒、毛细血管网、次表面散射、不对称瑕疵——绝不过度磨皮

照明（LIGHTING）——物理真实（PHYSICALLY REAL）：
- 光遵循现实物理：随距离的写实衰减、正确地弹射到表面上、精确的阴影滚落进入环境光
- 阴影滚落进入深冷蓝灰色，绝不落入纯黑；高光保持带质感，绝不吹到纯白过曝
- 表面基于实际材质显示写实镜面响应——哑光石柔和漫射，抛光金属紧密的镜面反射，湿石如镜面光泽，干粗木几乎不反射
- 光源有可见方向与物理锚点——没有任何东西在缺乏可解释来源的情况下神秘发光
- 空气承载体积——视场景有大气雾霭、尘埃微粒、烟雾、呼吸
- 色温一致自然——日光的冷灰、白炽灯的暖但不呈霓虹橙、火焰跳动的暖红橙但绝不呈霓虹黄
- 混合光读起来是电影化的有意为之——冷主光 + 暖现实灯具营造体积分离
- 不得有看起来像画上去的环境光遮蔽（ambient occlusion）、不得有平的全局照明、不得有不可能的反弹光、不得有 HDR 过曝、不得有黄金时刻饱和度（除非场景确实是黄金时刻）

负面指令（NEGATIVE DIRECTIVES）——关键（CRITICAL）：
- 非渲染、非 3D、非 CGI、非插画、非绘画、非哑光绘画、非游戏静帧、非概念艺术
- 不得有 CGI 平滑感、不得有诡异完美、不得有过度干净的表面、不得有塑料质感材质、不得有橡胶质感皮肤
- 不得有 Unreal Engine 观感、不得有 Octane 渲染观感、不得有 Blender Cycles 观感、不得有 3D 渲染观感
- 不得有 AI 平滑表面、不得有低分辨率模糊、不得有绵软质感、不得有皂感的表面、不得有画上去的细节、不得有平的阴影平面
- 不得过度饱和、不得有 HDR 光晕、不得有重度调色、不得有 Instagram 滤镜、不得滥用清晰度滑块
- 不得有诡异对称、不得有重复的相同图案平铺、不得有可疑的完美对齐。

现实世界的瑕疵（REAL-WORLD IMPERFECTIONS，高分辨率下始终存在）：
- 平面表面上逐粒可见的积灰
- 上墙与天花板（室内）或风化的外墙上的烟灰与烟渍
- 水渍、矿物沉积、石头上的盐析
- 蜡滴、烛烟、灯具周围的油污
- 褪色剥落的油漆，边缘卷曲，下层显现
- 年代与颜色各异的错配修补块
- 使用造成的独立刮痕、凿痕、凹痕
- 幽暗遗忘角落里的蛛网（当氛围需要时）
- 不对称风化——基于光照/风向，一侧比另一侧磨损更甚
- 每一处表面的自然微观差异——没有两平方英寸相同

收尾（FINISHING）：
- 轻微的电影感柔化（Tiffen Black Pro-Mist 1/8——最小强度）——仅在最亮高光周围有微妙的奶油状光晕（halation），保持整体锐度
- 真实电影摄影机的色彩科学——自然阴影滚落、平滑的高光裁切表现、基于主光源的真实色彩还原
- 摄影参考：Roger Deakins / Greig Fraser——锐利但有机、摄影感、绝不人工
- 建筑锐度参考（针对建成环境）：Hélène Binet / Iwan Baan——每一处建筑细节的清晰分辨率

最终图像必须无可质疑地读作以专业电影设备拍摄的真实照片，而非任何形式的数字渲染或 AI 生成物。
