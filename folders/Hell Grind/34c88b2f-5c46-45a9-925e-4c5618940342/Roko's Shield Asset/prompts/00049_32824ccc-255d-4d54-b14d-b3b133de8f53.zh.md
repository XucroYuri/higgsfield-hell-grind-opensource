## 原文 (source)

Edit the image: same character same pose same back view 
composition, but the crystalline shield is significantly 
SMALLER in scale.

CHANGE — shield size only:
- The crystalline shield is now MUCH SMALLER overall, 
roughly head-sized to shoulder-width in spread (NOT torso-
tall, NOT body-engulfing)
- The dense crystal mass is compact and contained, smaller 
volume of clustered spikes, the shield reads as a defensive 
crystal formation in front of his hands but does NOT dwarf 
his body
- The crystal cluster only extends a moderate distance 
beyond his hands, not radiating outward to fill the entire 
frame
- Crystal spikes are correspondingly shorter and the overall 
cluster is more compact, contained, controlled — like a 
small defensive crystal burst rather than a giant aura
- Same DENSE COMPACT 3D MASS form (hedgehog cluster, NOT 
flat fan), just at a smaller size
- Same translucent deep red crystalline material

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
- The back view camera angle (behind the character)
- Character pose: arms extended forward at chest height
- Character appearance: messy black hair, grey ribbed tank 
top, red baggy pants, black sling-strap bag with red kitty 
plushie keychain
- Crystal armor on hands and partial forearms only (NOT 
shoulders, NOT upper arms)
- Grey backdrop, cinematic studio prop photography, 
lighting, color grade

ONLY CHANGE: shield size — significantly reduced. 
Everything else (pose, camera, character, crystal armor 
coverage, shield form, backdrop, grade) preserved 
identically.

## 中文对照 (zh)

构图，但水晶护盾在尺度上显著 SMALLER（更小）。

CHANGE（改动）—— 仅改变护盾尺寸：
- 水晶护盾整体现在明显更小，展开约头部大小至肩宽（绝非躯干之高，
绝非吞没身体）
- 致密的晶质块体紧凑而收敛，簇状尖刺体积更小，护盾读作他双手前方
一面防御性的水晶构造，但不压倒他的身体
- 晶簇仅向双手之外延伸一段中等距离，不向外辐射铺满整个画面
- 水晶尖刺相应更短，整体晶簇更紧凑、更收敛、更受控——宛如一小簇防御性
水晶爆开，而非一个巨大的光环
- 保持同样的 DENSE COMPACT 3D MASS（致密紧凑三维块体）形态（刺猬状晶簇，绝非
扁平扇面），只是处于更小的尺寸
- 保持同样的半透明深红晶质材质

PRESERVE EXACTLY（原封不动保留）——
- 背面视角机位（位于角色身后）
- 角色姿势：双臂在胸部高度向前伸展
- 角色外观：凌乱黑发、灰色罗纹背心、红色宽松裤、黑色斜挎包挂红色小猫毛绒
挂饰钥匙链

- 灰色背景、电影级棚拍道具摄影、布光、调色

ONLY CHANGE（唯一改动）——护盾尺寸：显著缩小。
其余一切（姿势、机位、角色、水晶护甲覆盖、护盾形态、背景、调色）原样保留。
