## 原文 (source)

Edit the image: keep ABSOLUTELY everything identical EXCEPT 
one single change — the crystal armor coverage on his arms.

THE ONLY CHANGE:
- The crystalline armor is reduced to cover ONLY his hands 
(palms and fingers), stopping cleanly at his wrist line
- His forearms from wrist to elbow are bare skin, no crystal 
on them
- His upper arms stay bare as they already are

EVERYTHING ELSE STAYS 100% THE SAME:
- The crystalline shield — exact same size, shape, form, 
density, position, crystal pattern, every spike, the entire 
shield mass is identical to current image, do not modify the 
shield in any way
- Camera angle (back view) identical
- Character pose identical
- His hair, face, tank top, pants, sling-bag, kitty 
keychain, every clothing detail identical
- Grey backdrop identical
- Lighting, color, grade, shadows all identical
- Image composition identical

The only pixels that should change are the crystal coverage 
on his arms between the elbow and wrist — those areas become 
bare skin. Nothing else changes.
