horizon line in the center-right area. Each fire is a discrete

NO mud, NO scorched ground. Just clean snow with flames on top.

should NOT spread broadly into the existing fog haze, just 
specific columns rising from specific fire locations.

darker overcast cloud cover with deep grey-purple cloud masses,

horizon reflecting the distant fires. Do NOT change the fog at 
ground level, only the upper sky cloud quality changes.

PRESERVE EXACTLY:

— Same exact film grain and photographic quality
— No fire in foreground close to camera
— No dirt or mud added anywhere
— No new shapes or forms pulled from the fog
— No phantom trees, structures, or objects in the haze
— Same exact texture, lighting, and grain as input image

Photorealistic, real practical location photography, ARRI Alexa 
anamorphic film still aesthetic, two specific fire patches placed 
in the middle distance with smoke columns, otherwise the image 
exactly as input. Match the texture, grain, and lighting quality 
of the input image. Not CGI, not stylized, not phantom shapes in 
fog, not added detail in haze, clean targeted edit only.
