Edit this winter landscape image. Add post-battle ground fires in 
the deep background of the frame, far back at the horizon line, 
matching the reference screenshot where flames spread horizontally 
on the ground.

CRITICAL ATMOSPHERIC PRESERVATION:
The thick atmospheric haze and fog density must remain EXACTLY as 
it is. Do NOT pull shapes or forms out of the fog, do NOT add tree 
silhouettes or hidden objects in the haze, do NOT introduce phantom 
shapes or artifacts. Treat the fog as a fixed atmospheric layer.

ADD COMPACT GROUND FIRES — small horizontal flame patches on the 
ground, NOT long stretches, NOT campfires, NOT vertical pyres. Each 
fire is a small contained patch of low spreading flames covering a 
modest area of snow, like a small fuel spill burning on the ground, 
horizontal but compact, wider than tall but not extended in length.

Three small ground fire patches FAR BACK in the deep background 
near the horizon line at varied positions:
   • One small fire at the horizon line on the right side
   • One small fire slightly closer back left of the frame
   • One faint distant fire near the center-left horizon, barely 
     visible orange glow through fog

All fires small, distant, softened by atmospheric haze, low 
horizontal flames hugging the snow surface in compact patches.

The snow around stays clean white. NO dirt, NO mud, NO scorched 
ground.

Small smoke wisps rising from each fire patch, distant smoke 
softened by atmospheric haze, not large smoke columns.

ADJUST THE SKY ONLY to slightly darker stormy quality — heavier 
darker overcast cloud cover with deep grey-purple cloud masses, 
subtle warm orange undertones at the horizon reflecting the 
distant fires below.

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
— The existing dense atmospheric fog and haze, untouched
— All existing foreground elements: snow, rocks, vegetation, 
  dried red bushes
— Clean white snow throughout
— Same composition, framing, aspect ratio
— Same falling snow particles
— Same film grain
— No fire close to camera, no fire in foreground or middle distance
— No phantom shapes in fog
— Same exact texture and lighting as input image

Photorealistic, real practical location photography, ARRI Alexa 
anamorphic film still aesthetic, three small compact distant ground 
fires at the deep background horizon, otherwise the image exactly 
as input. Match the texture, grain, and lighting of the input 
image. Not CGI, not stylized, not long fires, not extended fire 
stretches, not campfires, small compact distant fire patches only.

编辑这张冬日风景图。在画面深远背景、远离地平线处添加战后地面火，匹配参考截图，即火焰在地面水平蔓延。

关键的大气保全：
浓厚的天空雾霭与雾密度必须保持完全不变。不要从雾中拉扯出形状或形态，不要在雾中增加树的剪影或隐藏物体，不要引入幻影形状或伪影。把雾当作一层固定的大气层处理。

添加紧凑的地面火——地面上一小块一小块紧凑的水平火焰斑，绝非长条的延展、绝非篝火、绝非竖直火堆。每一处火焰都是地面上小面积向上或向前滋长的低矮可控火焰斑块，覆盖一小块不大面积的地域，如同一小摊洒落在地面燃烧的燃料，水平但紧凑，宽于高但不在地上延展长度。

三处小地面火斑块位于深邃远景、近地平线的不同位置：
   • 地平线右侧一处小火

所有火都小、远、被大气雾霭柔化，低矮水平的火焰贴着雪面、呈紧凑斑块。

周围的雪保持洁净纯白。无尘土、无泥浆、无烧焦地面。

仅把天空调到稍微更暗的风暴质感——更重更暗的阴云覆盖，带深灰紫色云块，地平线处微妙温暖的橙色余调，反射着下方远处的火。

写实摄影、真实的实拍地点摄影，ARRI Alexa 变形电影剧照美学，深邃背景地平线处三处紧凑遥远地面小火，否则图像严格如输入。匹配输入图像的纹理、颗粒与光线。绝非 CGI、绝非风格化、绝非长火、绝非延展火条、绝非篝火，只有小、紧凑、遥远的火斑。
