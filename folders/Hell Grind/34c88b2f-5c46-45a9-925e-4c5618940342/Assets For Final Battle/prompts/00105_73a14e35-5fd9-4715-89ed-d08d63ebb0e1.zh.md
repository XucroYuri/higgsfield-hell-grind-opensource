## 原文 (source)

Edit this winter landscape image. Move the camera position to the 
side so we view the same exact scene from a different angle, AND 
shift the foreground hill mound so it is no longer dominating the 
foreground. The camera shifts left or right by a moderate amount, 
creating a diagonal three-quarter perspective view of the location 
instead of the current straight-on frontal view.

DESCRIBE THE NEW CAMERA ANGLE VISUALLY:
The camera is no longer pointing straight at the tree from the 
front. Instead the camera has moved sideways approximately 35-45 
degrees around the tree, and now points at the tree from the side-
front diagonal. As a result:

   • The tree is no longer perfectly frontal — we now see it from 
     a diagonal angle showing its front and one side simultaneously
   • The branches that were flat profile from the frontal view now 
     reveal depth and thickness from the new angle
   • One side of the tree trunk is visible alongside the front, 
     showing the trunk's three-dimensional cylindrical shape
   • The exposed roots at the base are now seen from a side-front 
     angle, showing their gnarled three-dimensional structure
   • The wind-shaped canopy is now angled in the frame
   • The horizon line and background atmosphere reposition slightly 
     to reflect the new camera position

REMOVE THE FOREGROUND HILL MOUND FROM THE FOREGROUND:
The current image has a prominent rocky hill mound dominating the 
left foreground area near the camera. This foreground mound should 
be removed from the close foreground or shifted far back into the 
middle distance through the camera angle change. The new foreground 
should be open snow ground extending toward the tree, no large 
prominent rocky mound right in front of the camera. Any rock 
formations that remain in the scene should be repositioned naturally 
in the middle distance or background, smaller and farther away from 
camera, no longer blocking the foreground space.

The foreground area is now clean open snow with the small dried 
red bushes and ornamental grasses scattered across it, no large 
foreground rocky mound, the eye flows naturally from the open snow 
foreground back to the tree as the central subject.

The composition is no longer dominated by a foreground mound — 
it's a diagonal cinematic angle with open foreground leading to 
the tree, like a documentary cinematographer walked sideways and 
forward past the original mound, repositioning to a cleaner 
diagonal view of the scene.

PRESERVE THE EXACT LOCATION AND OTHER ELEMENTS:

The tree stays identical — same exact tree with same bark, same 
branches, same dried red leaves, same root structure. Just shown 
from the new diagonal angle.

The smaller scattered rocks and stones farther in the scene remain 
in their natural distribution but reposition through the camera 
shift. Some rocks may end up in the middle distance instead of 
foreground.

The dried red bushes, ornamental grasses, and small vegetation 
remain in the scene, repositioned naturally for the new camera 
angle, scattered across the open snowy ground.

The atmospheric conditions stay identical — same dense fog and 
haze, same overcast grey-blue sky, same falling snow particles, 
same diffuse cold winter light quality, same color grade with cool 
blue-grey tones and dried red accents, same contrast and exposure 
levels, same cinematic mood.

The photographic quality stays identical — same film grain, same 
anamorphic aesthetic, same widescreen character.

DO NOT:
— Do NOT change the tree itself (shape, branches, leaves, roots, 
  bark) — just show it from the new angle
— Do NOT replace the tree with a different tree
— Do NOT keep the large foreground rocky mound dominating the 
  close foreground
— Do NOT change the type or character of vegetation, just 
  reposition naturally
— Do NOT change the snow color or texture quality
— Do NOT change the sky or atmospheric mood
— Do NOT change the lighting direction or quality
— Do NOT change the color grade or palette
— Do NOT change the film grain or photographic quality
— Do NOT add new objects to the scene beyond what was originally 
  there
— Do NOT pull phantom shapes from the fog or haze

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
— The tree identity completely
— Vegetation character (bushes, grasses)
— Snow appearance and texture
— Atmospheric haze and fog
— Sky and clouds
— Lighting and color
— Film grain and photographic texture
— Aspect ratio
— Cinematic mood

The result: same winter plateau location with the same tree and 
atmospheric elements, viewed from a diagonal three-quarter side 
angle, AND with the prominent foreground rocky mound removed from 
the close foreground (shifted back into middle distance or out of 
frame), giving a cleaner open foreground leading to the tree.

Photorealistic, real practical location photography, ARRI Alexa 
anamorphic film still aesthetic. Match the texture, grain, color 
grade, and lighting quality of the input image exactly. Same 
location, diagonal three-quarter side angle, foreground mound 
removed, every other element preserved. Not CGI, not stylized, 
not a different tree, not a different location.

视觉上描述新机位：
镜头不再从正面正对树。相反，镜头已横向移动约 35-45 度绕树而转，如今从侧前方斜向对着树。结果：

   • 树不再完全正对正面——如今我们从斜向角度观看它，同时显示其正面与一个侧面
   • 正面视角下呈平面外形的枝条，如今从新角度显现出深浅与粗细
   • 树干的一个侧面与正面同时可见，显现出树干的三维圆柱形
   • 基部裸露的根系如今从侧前方角度观看，显现出盘结的三维结构
   • 迎风树冠如今在画面中倾斜
   • 地平线与背景氛围略微重排，以反映新的机位

把前景土丘从前景移开：
当前图有一块醒目的岩石土丘占据靠近镜头的前景区域。通过机位变化，这块前景土丘应从近前景中被移除，或远远推到中距离处。新的前景应是向树延伸的开阔雪地，镜头正前方不应再有大块醒目的岩石土丘。场景中任何残留的岩石形态都应自然地重新安置在中距离或背景处，更小、离镜头更远，不再遮挡前景空间。

前景区域如今是干干净净的开阔积雪，点缀着零星的干枯红叶小灌木与观赏性草丛，没有大块前景岩石土丘，视线从开阔雪地自然流回作为中心主体的树。

构图不再被前景土丘主导——它是一个带开阔前景、通向树的斜向电影角度，好比一位纪实摄影师侧向并向前走过原来的土丘，重新落位到更干净的斜向视角。

干枯红叶灌木、观赏性草丛与小植被留在场景中，为新机位自然重排，散布在开阔雪地上。

大气条件保持相同——相同的浓雾与雾霭、相同的灰蓝阴天天空、相同的落雪颗粒、相同的漫射冷冬光质感、相同的冷蓝灰调与干红点缀的色调、相同的反差与曝光级数、相同的电影氛围。

严格保留：
