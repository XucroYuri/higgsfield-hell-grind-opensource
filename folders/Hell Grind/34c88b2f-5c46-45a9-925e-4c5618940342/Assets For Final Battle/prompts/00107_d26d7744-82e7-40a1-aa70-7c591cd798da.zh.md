stormy overcast sky. Preserve everything else in the image exactly

sky, cold dramatic atmosphere.

its branches and leaves visible against the darker dramatic 
backdrop, the tree appearing more striking and atmospheric against 
the stormy sky than against the previous calm overcast.

 lighting stays the same as input even though the sky is darker)
— Color grade and palette of the foreground and tree

— Do NOT change the atmospheric ground-level fog and haze

The result: same exact winter landscape with the same exact tree, 
foreground, atmosphere, and lighting on the ground, but with a 
dramatic stormy overcast sky replacing the previous calm overcast — 
deep grey-blue brooding cloud masses with visible structure and 
depth.

Photorealistic, real practical photography quality, match the 
texture, grain, and base aesthetic of the input image. Not CGI, 
not stylized, dramatic stormy overcast sky replacement, everything 
else preserved.
