visible in the cloud structure, heavier and darker than the current 
calm overcast, oppressive atmospheric weight, dramatic post-storm 
or pre-storm mood. Deep grey-blue palette, no warm tones, cold 
dramatic atmosphere.

The atmospheric haze and soft fog quality of the original image

same depth-of-distance fade where far elements become softer

exact same haze density and quality as the input image, do NOT 
remove the haze, do NOT make the image too clear, do NOT eliminate

maximum fidelity.
