Edit this winter landscape image. Four changes in this edit, all 
applied together: change the camera angle to a diagonal three-
quarter side view, remove the foreground rocky mound, change the 
sky to dramatic stormy overcast, add distant background fires from 
old battle aftermath, and enhance the cinematic detail throughout 
the image with natural cinematic quality (not over-sharpened) while 
preserving the same atmospheric haze quality as the original.

CHANGE 1 — CAMERA ANGLE TO DIAGONAL THREE-QUARTER SIDE VIEW:
The camera is no longer pointing straight at the tree from the 
front. The camera has moved sideways approximately 35-45 degrees 
around the tree, and now points at the tree from the side-front 
diagonal. As a result:

   • The tree is no longer perfectly frontal — we now see it from 
     a diagonal angle showing its front and one side simultaneously
   • The branches reveal depth and thickness from the new angle
   • One side of the tree trunk is visible alongside the front, 
     showing the trunk's three-dimensional cylindrical shape
   • The exposed roots are seen from a side-front angle showing 
     three-dimensional structure
   • The wind-shaped canopy is now angled in the frame
   • The horizon line and background atmosphere reposition slightly 
     to reflect the new camera position

The composition is a diagonal cinematic angle, like a documentary 
cinematographer walked 35 steps to one side and re-framed the 
same scene.

CHANGE 2 — REMOVE THE FOREGROUND ROCKY MOUND:
The current image has a prominent rocky hill mound dominating the 
left foreground. This foreground mound is removed from the close 
foreground or shifted far back into the middle distance through 
the camera angle change. The new foreground is open snow ground 
extending toward the tree, no large prominent rocky mound right 
in front of the camera. Any rock formations that remain are 
repositioned naturally in the middle distance or background, 
smaller and farther away, no longer blocking the foreground space.

The foreground area is now clean open snow with small dried red 
bushes and ornamental grasses scattered across it, the eye flows 
naturally from the open snow foreground back to the tree.

CHANGE 3 — DRAMATIC STORMY OVERCAST SKY:
Replace the current sky with a heavy stormy overcast sky — dense 
brooding storm clouds in deep grey and slate blue tones, dark moody 
cloud masses with natural soft cloud texture, volumetric depth 
and layered cloud formations, turbulent atmospheric movement, 
heavier and darker than the current calm overcast, oppressive 
atmospheric weight, dramatic post-storm or pre-storm mood. Deep 
grey-blue palette, no warm tones, cold dramatic atmosphere.

CHANGE 4 — ADD DISTANT BACKGROUND FIRES FROM OLD BATTLE AFTERMATH:
Add subtle distant fires in the deep background near the horizon 
line — fires from a battle that happened long ago, calm sustained 
burn rather than fresh intense flames. The fires appear as long 
horizontal lines of low burning flames stretching across portions 
of the deep background — like long trails where fuel or fire spread 
linearly across the ground and continues to smolder and burn. Low 
horizontal lines of orange-red flame visible far back near the 
horizon, NOT vertical, NOT campfire shapes, NOT compact patches — 
extended horizontal lines of low flame across the deep distance.

Two or three such horizontal fire lines distributed asymmetrically 
in the deep background:
   • One longer line of low flames in the deep background on one 
     side of the frame
   • One shorter horizontal flame line in another part of the deep 
     background
   • Optionally one fainter line near the horizon, barely visible 
     through the haze

The fires are far enough back that they read as distant — small in 
scale, softened by atmospheric haze, fading into the fog density. 
NOT in the foreground, NOT in the middle ground, ONLY in the deep 
background near the horizon. Faint thin smoke wisps rising slowly 
from the fire lines, dissolving quickly into the existing 
atmospheric haze, NOT large smoke columns.

The fires are subtle distant elements in the background, not 
foreground subjects. The snow around the fires stays clean white, 
no scorched ground, no dirt, no mud, just clean snow with distant 
flame lines and faint smoke wisps.

PRESERVE THE ATMOSPHERIC HAZE EXACTLY AS THE ORIGINAL:
The atmospheric haze and soft fog quality of the original image 
must remain EXACTLY as it is — same gentle atmospheric softness 
across the deep distance, same subtle veil over the background, 
same depth-of-distance fade where far elements become softer 
through the haze, same cool cinematic atmospheric quality. Keep 
the exact same haze density and quality as the input image, do NOT 
remove the haze, do NOT make the image clearer than original.

ENHANCE DETAIL WITH NATURAL CINEMATIC QUALITY (not over-sharpened):
The image should have the natural detailed quality of a real 
anamorphic cinema frame — detailed but with soft natural falloff, 
organic photographic quality, NOT digitally crisp or over-sharpened. 
Think of how a real Roger Deakins anamorphic frame on Kodak motion 
picture film looks — there is detail and definition in every 
element, but the detail is rendered through soft anamorphic optics 
and organic film grain, with natural micro-contrast rather than 
digital edge sharpness.

Tree quality:

PRESERVE EXACTLY:

The result: same winter plateau location with the same tree,

## 中文对照 (zh)

编辑这张冬日风景图。本次共四项修改，一并应用：把机位改为斜向四分之三侧视角、移除前景的岩石土丘、把天空改为戏剧性的风暴阴天、添加来自旧日战事余烬的远处背景火焰，并全程以自然的电影质感（不过度锐化）增强画面细节，同时保留与原文相同的大气雾霭质感。

修改 1——改为斜向四分之三侧视角：
镜头不再正对着树从正面直射。镜头已在原地侧移约 35-45 度绕树而转，如今从侧前方斜向对着树。结果：

   • 树不再完全正对正面——如今我们从斜向角度观看它，同时显示其正面与一个侧面
   • 枝条从新角度显现出深浅与粗细
   • 树干的一个侧面与正面同时可见，显现出树干的三维圆柱形
   • 裸露根系从侧前方角度观看，显现出三维结构
   • 迎风树冠如今在画面中倾斜
   • 地平线与背景氛围略微重排，以反映新的机位

构图是一个斜向的电影角度，好比一位纪实摄影师走到一侧 35 步，重新框住同一场景。

修改 2——移除前景岩石土丘：
当前图有一块醒目的岩石土丘占据左前景。通过机位变化，这块前景土丘从近前景中被移除，或远远推到中距离处。新的前景是向树延伸的开阔雪地，镜头正前方不再有大块醒目的岩石土丘。任何残留的岩石形态都自然地重新安置在中距离或背景处，更小、更远，不再遮挡前景空间。

前景区域如今是干干净净的开阔积雪，点缀着零星的干枯红叶小灌木与观赏性草丛，视线从开阔雪地自然流向树。

修改 3——戏剧性风暴阴天：
把当前天空替换为厚重的风暴阴天——深灰与蓝灰石板色调中浓稠幽郁的风暴云，带自然柔和云质纹理的幽暗阴沉云块，体积的纵深与层叠的云形，湍动的空气运动，比当前平静的阴天更重更暗，压抑的大气重量，暴风雨后或暴风雨前那种戏剧性的氛围。深灰蓝色调配、没有暖色调，冷峻戏剧性的大气。

修改 4——添加来自旧日战事余烬的远处背景火焰：
在近地平线处的深远背景中，添加微妙而遥远的火焰——一场很久以前发生的战斗留下此后持续静燃的余烬火，而非新鲜猛烈的火焰。这些火表现为一道道低燃火焰的水平长线，横贯深远背景的若干段落——如同燃料或火沿地面呈直线蔓延、仍在持续阴燃燃烧的长长轨迹。橙色转红的低矮火焰线在遥远近地平线处可见，绝非竖直形、绝非篝火形、绝非紧凑的斑块——是横贯深远距离的延展水平低火焰线。

两条或三条这样的水平火线不对称地分布在深远背景中：
   • 画面一侧深远背景中较长的一条低火焰线
   • 深远背景另一处较短的一条水平火焰线
   • 可选地，地平线附近一条更淡的线，透过雾霭勉强可见

这些火位于足够的纵深，读作遥远之物——尺度小、被大气雾霭柔化、渐渐消融在雾气密度之中。绝不出现在前景、绝不出现在中景，只出现在近地平线的深远背景。细薄的淡烟缕从火线上缓缓升起，很快散入现有的大气雾霭之中，绝非巨大的烟柱。

这些火是背景中微妙的遥远元素，而非前景主体。火旁的积雪保持洁净纯白，没有烧焦的土面、没有尘土、没有泥浆，只是干净的雪配着遥远的火焰线与淡烟缕。

严格按原始保留大气雾霭：
原始图中的大气雾霭与柔和薄雾质感必须保持原样——深远处同样和缓的大气柔和度、背景上同样的细微面纱、远处元素经雾霭而渐柔的同样距离衰减、同样冷峻的电影大气质感。保持与输入图完全相同的雾霭密度与质感，不要移除雾霭，不要让画面比原始更清晰。

树的质感：
   • 叶片与枝条以自然的分辨感清晰可读，干枯红叶带自然的颜色变化与柔和边缘细节，非像素级锐利但画得干净
