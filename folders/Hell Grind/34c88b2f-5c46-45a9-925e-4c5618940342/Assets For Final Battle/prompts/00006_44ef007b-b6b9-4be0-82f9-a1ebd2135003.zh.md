canopy. Do not change anything else in this image.

Repaint the existing leaves on the canopy to these specific colors:
- Most leaves across the canopy: deep crimson red
- The existing cluster of lighter leaves in the upper right of 
  the canopy: pure creamy white frost-pale color
- A small patch of leaves at the lower left of the canopy: 
  amber-yellow

Only modify the COLOR of the existing leaves. Do not add new 
leaves. Do not remove any leaves. Do not change the canopy shape, 
size, density, silhouette, or position. Do not change the trunk, 
branches, or roots in any way. Do not change the sky, the fires, 
the haze, the foreground, the snow, the bushes, the grasses, the 
rocks, the lighting, or anything else in this image.

This is a hyper-local color adjustment to the existing leaves 
only. Maximum 5 percent pixel change. Preserve the entire image 
exactly except for the leaf colors.
