Edit this image with two minimal local changes only. Preserve 
everything else exactly as it is — composition, the tree's trunk 
and roots, foreground rocks and grasses and bushes, sky, clouds, 
warm horizon glow, atmospheric haze, snow texture, mood, color 
grade, and overall lighting all unchanged.

CHANGE 1 — add more fires to the background only: add additional 
small fires scattered across the right side mid-ground and far 
horizon at varied distances, more burning patches visible through 
the haze, additional thin smoke columns rising from each new fire 
and merging with the existing mist. Keep the existing fires as 
they are. Do not add any fires near the tree base or in the 
foreground — only across the right plain at mid-distance and 
toward the horizon.

CHANGE 2 — refine the tree's canopy foliage to match an ancient 
Japanese maple more closely: slightly denser and more abundant 
canopy with predominantly deep red foliage filling out, the 
smaller distinct cluster of creamy white frost-pale leaves in the 
upper right of the canopy made more prominent and clearly visible, 
keep the small amber-yellow patch at the lower left. Only adjust 
the leaves themselves — do not change the trunk, branches, root 
system, silhouette, or position of the tree.

Maximum 10% change overall. Output a single unified photograph 
matching the existing aspect ratio.
