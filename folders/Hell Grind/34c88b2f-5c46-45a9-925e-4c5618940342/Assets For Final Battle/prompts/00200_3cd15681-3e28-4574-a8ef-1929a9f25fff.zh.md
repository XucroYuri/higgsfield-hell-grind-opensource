## 原文 (source)

Edit this winter landscape image. Add post-battle aftermath fire and 
smoke matching the reference screenshot — burning ground patches 
along the left and right edges of the frame, smoke columns rising, 
darker stormy sky atmosphere. Keep all existing elements of the 
landscape exactly the same.

ADD ON THE LEFT AND RIGHT EDGES OF THE FRAME:
Patches of ground fire burning on the snow at multiple positions 
along the left edge of the frame and right edge of the frame, the 
fire patches scattered organically, some larger flame areas with 
visible orange-red flames burning bright through dark smoke, some 
smaller fire patches with low flames and smoldering embers. The 
fires are positioned on the snowy ground, the snow around the fire 
patches melted into dark wet ground revealing scorched dirt 
underneath, ash and burnt vegetation around the fire patches. The 
fires distribute along both edges of the frame, leaving the central 
area of the composition relatively clear of fire.

ADD SMOKE COLUMNS:
Rising columns of dark grey-brown smoke ascending from each fire 
patch, the smoke trailing upward into the sky, some smoke columns 
thick and dense from larger fires, some thin and wispy from smaller 
fires, the smoke drifting and curling as it rises. The smoke 
gathers in the middle distance forming a layer of atmospheric haze 
across the landscape from all the burning patches.

ADJUST THE SKY:
Darken the sky slightly into a more dramatic stormy quality matching 
the reference screenshot — heavy overcast with darker storm clouds, 
the cloud cover more pronounced and moody, deep grey-purple cloud 
masses with subtle warm orange undertones reflecting from the fires 
below. The sky reads as post-battle moody atmosphere, NOT the 
gentle peaceful overcast of the original. The lighting on the 
landscape catches subtle warm fire glow on the snow nearest the 
flames, the rest of the landscape in cool blue-grey atmospheric 
tones contrasting with the warm fire light.

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
— All existing landscape elements: snow, rocks, vegetation patches, 
  dried red bushes and grass, any tree if present
— Same exact composition and framing
— Same exact aspect ratio
— Same atmospheric haze and falling snow particles
— Same overall winter setting
— Same exact film grain and photographic quality
— Same exact cool grey-blue snow palette in areas not affected by 
  fire

Photorealistic, real practical location photography of a winter 
battlefield aftermath, real fire effects on practical location, 
ARRI Alexa anamorphic film still aesthetic, dramatic post-battle 
mood. Match the texture, grain, and lighting quality of the input 
image. Not CGI, not stylized, not exaggerated, not horror, real 
battle aftermath with fires on snow.
