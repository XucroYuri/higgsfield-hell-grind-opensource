## 原文 (source)

Edit this winter landscape image. Add post-battle aftermath fire 
and smoke in the middle distance of the frame, matching the 
reference screenshot where fires burn on the ground in the midground 
area at distance from camera. Keep all existing elements exactly 
the same. The snow stays clean and white throughout, no dirt, no 
mud, no scorched ground.

ADD FIRE PATCHES IN THE MIDDLE DISTANCE:
Patches of ground fire burning on the clean snow positioned in the 
middle distance of the frame — NOT in the foreground close to 
camera, NOT filling the edges of the immediate viewer space, but 
back in the landscape on the horizon line and middle distance. The 
fires are visible at distance from camera, scattered organically 
across the midground area on both the left side and right side of 
the frame, some at the horizon line itself, some in the middle 
distance between foreground and horizon. Distance perspective 
makes the fires look smaller and farther away rather than 
dominating the foreground.

The fire patches show visible orange-red flames burning through 
faint dark smoke, the flames simply burning on top of the snow 
surface like the reference screenshot, the snow around the fire 
patches stays clean white snow with maybe a slight subtle warm 
glow tint nearest the flames. NO dirt, NO mud, NO melted scorched 
ground, NO ash spreading, NO burnt vegetation around the fires. 
Just clean snow with flames burning on top of it.

ADD SMOKE COLUMNS rising from each midground fire — dark grey-brown 
smoke columns ascending from each fire patch into the sky, the 
smoke trailing upward and drifting, smoke columns visible at 
varying distances giving depth, thinner more atmospheric at the 
horizon. The smoke gathers and contributes to the atmospheric haze 
of the deep distance.

ADJUST THE SKY slightly into more dramatic moody quality matching 
the reference — heavier darker overcast with subtle warm orange 
undertones reflecting from the distant fires below, deep grey-purple 
cloud masses, post-battle stormy atmosphere.

LIGHTING: The foreground stays cool blue-grey winter palette as in 
the original, the warm fire glow comes only from the distant 
midground fires not from the foreground, subtle warm fire light 
reflected on the snow near the distant fires, the foreground 
landscape stays cool and clean and untouched by warm light or dirt.

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
— All existing foreground elements: snow, rocks, vegetation patches, 
  dried red bushes and grass, any tree if present
— Clean white snow throughout the landscape
— Same exact composition and framing
— Same exact aspect ratio
— Same atmospheric haze and falling snow particles
— No fire in foreground, no fire close to camera
— No dirt or mud added anywhere
— Sam

## 中文对照 (zh)

编辑这张冬日风景图。在画面中景添加战后余烬之火与烟，匹配参考截图，即远离镜头的火焰在中景地面燃烧。让所有现有元素严格保持相同。雪全程保持洁净纯白，无尘土、无泥浆、无烧焦地面。

在中景添加火斑：
地面之火在洁净的雪上燃烧，位于画面中景——绝不在贴近镜头的前景，绝不满溢贴近观者的即时空间边缘，而是退到风景深处、在地平线处及中景。火在离镜头可见的距离处燃烧，有机地散布在左、右两侧的中景区域，有些正中地平线，有些介于前景与地平线之间的中距离。距离透视使火看起来更小、更远，而不主宰前景。

火斑显出可见的橙红色火焰透过微弱的暗烟燃烧，火焰就简单地像参考截图一样在雪面之上燃烧，火斑周围的雪保持洁净纯白，或许最贴近火焰处带有极轻微的温暖光晕色调。无尘土、无泥浆、无融化烧焦的地面、无蔓延的灰烬、无火焰周围烧焦的植被。只是干净的雪，火上顶着一层。

添加从每一处中景火升起的烟柱——由每一处火斑向上空升起的暗灰褐色烟柱，烟向上拖曳飘移，烟柱在变化不一的距离处可见、给出纵深，地平线处更细、更有大气感。烟聚集起来，汇入深远距离的大气雾霭。

把天空略调到更戏剧性的幽暗质感以匹配参考——更重更暗的阴云，带从下方远处火反射而来的微妙温暖橙色余调，深灰紫色云块，战后风暴般的大气。
