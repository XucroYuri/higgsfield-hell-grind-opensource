## 原文 (source)

Edit this winter landscape image. Change the sky to a dramatic 
stormy overcast sky AND enhance the cinematic detail and quality 
throughout the image. Preserve everything else in the image exactly 
as it is.

CHANGE THE SKY TO DRAMATIC STORMY OVERCAST:
Replace the current sky with a heavy stormy overcast sky — dense 
brooding storm clouds in deep grey and slate blue tones, dark moody 
cloud masses with rich detailed cloud texture, volumetric depth 
and layered cloud formations, turbulent atmospheric movement 
visible in the cloud structure, heavier and darker than the current 
calm overcast, oppressive atmospheric weight, dramatic post-storm 
or pre-storm mood. Deep grey-blue palette, no warm tones in the 
sky, cold dramatic atmosphere.

ENHANCE CINEMATIC SKY DETAIL:
The clouds should have rich volumetric detail — visible vapor 
texture, soft edge transitions between cloud layers, internal 
cloud shading from darker lower portions to lighter upper portions, 
wisps and tendrils of cloud detail, the kind of cloud structure a 
Roger Deakins anamorphic frame captures with full tonal range and 
fine atmospheric detail. NOT flat painted clouds, NOT smooth 
featureless grey. Real photographic cloud detail with depth and 
nuance, like clouds shot on Kodak motion picture film through 
anamorphic lenses. Multiple cloud layers visible giving depth — 
foreground darker storm clouds, middle layer of grey cloud mass, 
distant lighter sky breaks at horizon.

ENHANCE TREE DETAIL AND SHARPNESS:
The tree's leaves and branches should retain crisp detail — every 
individual leaf cluster sharp and defined, branch silhouettes 
clean and precise against the new sky, the dried red leaves with 
visible texture and edge detail, bark texture on the trunk and 
branches preserved with cinematic micro-detail, no blur or softness 
on the tree itself. The tree silhouette reads strikingly against 
the stormy sky with full detail and definition.

ENHANCE OVERALL IMAGE QUALITY:
Apply cinematic detail enhancement throughout — fine micro-texture 
in the snow surface revealing crystalline detail and subtle drift 
patterns, sharp foreground vegetation detail in the dried red 
bushes and ornamental grasses, the rock textures showing weathered 
mineral detail, distant elements properly sharp at their depth 
plane, clean photographic clarity throughout. Subtle organic 35mm 
Kodak film grain texture overlaid as final layer. Anamorphic film 
still aesthetic quality with proper micro-contrast and tonal range.

PRESERVE EVERYTHING ELSE EXACTLY:
— The tree completely (shape, branches, leaves, bark, roots) — 
  silhouette unchanged, just sharper detail and reading against 
  new stormy sky
— Foreground snow, vegetation, rocks, dried red bushes, ornamental 
  grasses — same elements with sharpened detail
— Middle distance and background terrain
— Atmospheric haze and fog at ground level
— Falling snow particles
— Lighting direction and quality on the landscape
— Color grade and palette of the foreground and tree
— Composition, framing, aspect ratio
— Camera angle exactly as input

DO NOT:
— Do NOT change the tree or any of its details (only sharpen)
— Do NOT change the foreground or vegetation
— Do NOT change the lighting on the landscape
— Do NOT change the color grade of non-sky areas
— Do NOT change the atmospheric ground-level fog and haze
— Do NOT add new elements
— Do NOT pull phantom shapes from the fog or clouds
— Do NOT add bright lightning or storm effects
— Do NOT lose detail or soften the image, only enhance detail
— Do NOT over-process or look digitally enhanced

The result: same exact winter landscape with the same exact tree, 
foreground, atmosphere, and lighting on the ground, but with a 
dramatic stormy overcast sky featuring rich volumetric cloud detail, 
AND enhanced cinematic detail throughout the image — sharp tree 
foliage, crisp foreground vegetation, full micro-detail and 
photographic clarity, anamorphic film still quality.

Photorealistic, real practical photography quality, ARRI Alexa LF 
Panavision Primo anamorphic 35mm at T2.0, Roger Deakins 
cinematography, organic 35mm Kodak film grain, full cinematic 
detail and tonal range, sharp anamorphic film still aesthetic. Not 
CGI, not stylized, not blurred, not soft, not low-detail, not 
digitally over-processed, real cinema reference quality with 
dramatic stormy sky and rich cinematic detail throughout.

把天空改为戏剧性风暴阴天：
把当前天空替换为厚重的风暴阴天——深灰与蓝灰石板色调中浓稠幽郁的风暴云，带着丰富细腻云质纹理的幽暗阴沉云块，体积的纵深与层叠的云形，云的构造中可见湍动的空气运动，比当前平静的阴天更重更暗，压抑的大气重量，暴风雨后或暴风雨前那种戏剧性的氛围。深灰蓝色调配，天空没有暖色调，冷峻戏剧性的大气。

增强天空的电影细节：

严格保全其他一切：
— 树完全保全（形状、枝条、叶片、树皮、根）——剪影不变，只是更锐利的细节并顶衬新风暴天可读

— 地景光线的方向与质感

— 机位严格如输入

不要：
— 不要改变树或其任何细节（只锐化）
— 不要改变前景或植被
— 不要改变地景的光线
— 不要改变非天空区域的色调
— 不要改变地面层大气薄雾与雾霭
