Edit this winter landscape image. Add post-battle aftermath fire

reference screenshot where fires burn on the ground in the midground

the same.

ADD FIRE PATCHES IN THE MIDDLE DISTANCE:

distance of the frame — NOT in the foreground close to camera, NOT 
filling the edges of the immediate viewer space, but back in the 
landscape on the horizon line and middle distance. The fires are 
visible at distance from camera, scattered organically across the 
midground area on both the left side and right side of the frame, 
some at the horizon line itself, some in the middle distance 
between foreground and horizon. Fires positioned where the snowy 
ground meets the foggy distance. Distance perspective makes the 
fires look smaller and farther away rather than dominating the 
foreground.

The fire patches show visible orange-red flames burning through dark 
smoke, the snow around the fire patches melted into dark wet ground 
revealing scorched dirt underneath, ash and burnt vegetation around 
each fire patch. Multiple fire patches at varying distances 
distributed along the midground edges of the composition.

ADD SMOKE COLUMNS rising from each midground fire — dark grey-brown 
smoke columns ascending from each fire patch into the sky, the 
smoke trailing upward and drifting, smoke columns visible at 
varying distances giving depth, some thicker and denser in closer 
midground positions, some thinner and more atmospheric at the 
horizon. The smoke gathers and contributes to the atmospheric haze 
of the deep distance.

the reference — heavier darker overcast with subtle warm orange

cloud masses, post-battle stormy atmosphere. NOT gentle peaceful 
overcast.

LIGHTING: The foreground stays cool blue-grey winter palette as in 
the original, the warm fire glow comes only from the distant 
midground fires not from the foreground, subtle warm fire light 
reflected on the snow near the distant fires, the foreground 
landscape stays cool and untouched by warm light.

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
— All existing foreground elements: snow, rocks, vegetation patches, 
  dried red bushes and grass, any tree if present
— Same exact composition and framing
— Same exact aspect ratio
— Same atmospheric haze and falling snow particles
— Same cool grey-blue snow palette in foreground and middle areas
— No fire in foreground, no fire close to camera
— Same exact film grain and photographic quality

Photorealistic, real practical location photography of a winter 
battlefield aftermath, real fire effects in middle distance on 
practical location, ARRI Alexa anamorphic film still aesthetic, 
dramatic post-battle mood with distant fires. Match the texture, 
grain, and lighting quality of the input image. Not CGI, not 
stylized, not exaggerated, not foreground fire, not close-up fire, 
distant midground fires only.
