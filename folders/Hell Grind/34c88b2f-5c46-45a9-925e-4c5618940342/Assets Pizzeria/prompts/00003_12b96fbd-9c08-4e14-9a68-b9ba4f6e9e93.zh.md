## 原文 (source)

Ultra-realistic cinematic night street scene with gritty cyberpunk atmosphere driven by environment, dense, chaotic and worn urban setting.
COMPOSITION & CAMERA:
— street-level, slightly low angle
— medium-close framing, corner pizzeria dominates foreground
— deep layered background with dense buildings
— overpass / bridge crossing behind or side
— 35mm anamorphic lens, slight handheld motion
MAIN SUBJECT — PIZZERIA:
— corner storefront on FIRST FLOOR, embedded in building
— wraps around the corner with two glass sides
— interior clearly visible: empty, warm light ON
— simple tables, chairs
— glass slightly dirty, reflections visible
— MAIN SIGN:
— “PIZZA” neon sign in English
— warm red/orange glow, visible light bloom
SURROUNDING BUILDINGS:
— varied heights (разноэтажные): mid-rise + tall towers
— tightly packed, heavy density
— white concrete facades, heavily worn
— irregular vertical red stripe patterns (faded, broken, uneven, partially missing)
— heavy aging:
— peeling paint
— water stains
— grime, mold streaks
— rust patches
— balconies cluttered
— AC units everywhere
— pipes, vents
WIRES & INFRASTRUCTURE (CRITICAL):
— dense overhead cable network across the scene
— thick cable bundles between buildings
— hanging wires, chaotic intersections
— poles, junction boxes, transformers
— cables catching neon light
NEON SIGNAGE ON BUILDINGS (STRICT COUNT):
— EXACTLY:
— 1 LARGE vertical neon sign (English)
— 2 MEDIUM neon signs (English)
— plus:
— 1 SMALL neon sign with Asian characters (accent only)
— placement:
— mounted on building facades or corners
— clearly visible in frame
— style:
— glowing red/orange with slight green/cyan accents
— light spill onto walls, cables, street
— slightly uneven brightness
— English examples:
— “HOTEL”, “BAR”, “OPEN”, “MARKET”
STREET:
— wet asphalt with strong reflections
— reflections of neon signs + PIZZA sign
— puddles, grime
— one car in background
LIGHTING:
— strong contrast: warm interior vs cool blue night
— neon reflections visible on surfaces
— subtle green/cyan accent glow
— light haze catching light
ATMOSPHERE:
— dense, heavy, slightly oppressive
— gritty urban cyberpunk realism
— not clean, not minimal
NEGATIVE / HARD RULES:
— no clean modern buildings
— no missing wires
— no missing neon signs
— no excessive Asian text
— no uniform building heights
— no empty environment
PRIORITY:
dense wires and urban chaos
visible neon lighting and reflections
corner pizzeria + interior light
varied building heights
worn red stripe architecture
FINAL ENFORCER:
— ensure dense cables, visible neon signage, and worn buildings dominate the frame while keeping Asian characters minimal

— 街面高度，微低机位
— 中近景取景，街角披萨店占据前景主体
— 纵深感强烈的多层背景，建筑密集
— 天桥 / 立交桥在后方或侧方横穿
— 35mm 变形宽银幕镜头，轻微手持晃动
主体 — 披萨店（MAIN SUBJECT — PIZZERIA）：
— 位于一楼的街角店面，嵌于建筑之中
— 环绕街角展开，两个玻璃面
— 室内清晰可见：空空荡荡，暖色灯光亮起（warm light ON）
— 简单的桌椅
— 玻璃略脏，可见反光
— 主招牌（MAIN SIGN）：

— 暖红/橙色光晕，可见光晕扩散（light bloom）

— 紧凑排列，高密度
— 白色混凝土立面，严重磨损
