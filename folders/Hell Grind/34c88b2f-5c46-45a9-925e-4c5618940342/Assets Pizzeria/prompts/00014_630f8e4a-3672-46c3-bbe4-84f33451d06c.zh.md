## 原文 (source)

PROMPT (EN, FINAL — varied heights + strong neon + corner pizzeria):
Ultra-realistic cinematic night street scene with grounded cyberpunk lighting, realistic dense urban environment.
COMPOSITION & CAMERA:
— street-level, slightly low angle
— camera placed closer to subject (medium-close framing)
— corner pizzeria dominates foreground
— deep perspective with layered city behind
— elevated bridge / overpass visible in background or side
— 35mm anamorphic lens feel, slight handheld
MAIN SUBJECT — PIZZERIA (STRICT):
— corner storefront on FIRST FLOOR of a building (not standalone)
— wraps around corner with two glass sides
— interior visible: empty, warm light ON
— simple tables and chairs
— slightly worn, realistic
— MAIN SIGN:
— “PIZZA” in English, bright neon, clearly readable
— warm red/orange glow with visible bloom
SURROUNDING BUILDINGS (VERY IMPORTANT):
— varied heights (разноэтажные):
— some 5–7 floors
— some 10–15 floors
— some taller towers behind
— dense stacking, not uniform skyline
— white concrete facades, worn and dirty
— irregular vertical red stripe patterns (faded, broken, uneven)
— balconies, AC units, cables
NEON SIGNAGE ON BUILDINGS (FORCED VISIBILITY):
— EXACTLY:
— 1 LARGE vertical neon sign (English words)
— 2 MEDIUM neon signs (English words)
— optional:
— max 1 small Asian neon sign (very secondary)
— all neon signs:
— clearly visible in frame
— mounted on building facades
— glowing strongly (red/orange + slight green/cyan accents)
— casting light onto nearby surfaces
— example English words:
— “HOTEL”, “OPEN”, “BAR”, “MARKET”, “LIQUOR”
STREET:
— wet asphalt with strong reflections
— reflections of neon + PIZZA sign
— one car in background or side
LIGHTING:
— strong contrast: warm interior vs cool night
— neon reflections clearly visible
— slight haze catching light
— soft streetlights
ATMOSPHERE:
— dense, heavy urban environment
— subtle cyberpunk through lighting only
— not clean, not minimal
NEGATIVE / HARD RULES:
— no uniform building heights
— no missing neon signs
— no dark pizzeria interior
— no standalone pizzeria
— no clean modern architecture
— no excessive Asian signage
PRIORITY:
corner pizzeria + interior light
neon signage visibility (English dominant)
varied building heights
red stripe architecture
bridge depth
FINAL ENFORCER:
— ensure varied building heights and clearly visible neon signage in English

PROMPT（EN，FINAL — 错落高度 + 强烈霓虹 + 街角披萨店）：
— 超写实电影感夜景街道场景，接地气的赛博朋克灯光，写实密集的城市环境。
构图与摄影机（COMPOSITION & CAMERA）：
— 街面高度，微低机位
— 摄影机更贴近主体（中近景取景）
— 街角披萨店占据前景主体
— 纵深感强烈的多层城市背景
— 后方或侧方可见高架桥 / 立交桥
— 35mm 变形宽银幕镜头感，轻微手持
主体 — 披萨店（MAIN SUBJECT — PIZZERIA，严格）：
— 位于某栋建筑的一楼街角店面（非独立建筑）
— 环绕街角展开，两个玻璃面
— 室内可见：空空荡荡，暖色灯光亮起
— 简单的桌椅
— 略磨损、写实
— 主招牌（MAIN SIGN）：
— 英文“PIZZA”，明亮霓虹，清晰可读
— 暖红/橙色光晕，可见光晕扩散
周边建筑（SURROUNDING BUILDINGS，非常重要）：
— 高低错落（разноэтажные）：
— 部分 5–7 层
— 部分 10–15 层
— 后方有更高的塔楼
— 密集堆叠，非单一天际线
— 白色混凝土立面，磨损且脏污
— 不规则的竖条纹红色图案（褪色、破损、高低不齐）
— 阳台、空调外机、线缆
建筑上的霓虹招牌（NEON SIGNAGE ON BUILDINGS，强制可见）：
— 严格为：
— 1 个大型竖式霓虹招牌（英文单词）
— 2 个中型霓虹招牌（英文单词）
— 可选：
— 至多 1 个小型亚洲文字霓虹招牌（极次要）

— 画面内清晰可见
— 安装在建筑立面上
— 强烈发光（红/橙色 + 少许绿/青色点缀）
— 向邻近表面投下光线
— 示例英文单词：
— “HOTEL”、“OPEN”、“BAR”、“MARKET”、“LIQUOR”
街道（STREET）：
— 湿沥青路面，强烈反光

— 不得有黑暗的披萨店室内
— 不得有独立式披萨店
— 不得有干净现代的当代建筑
— 不得有过量亚洲招牌
优先级（PRIORITY）：
街角披萨店 + 室内灯光

红条纹建筑
桥梁纵深
