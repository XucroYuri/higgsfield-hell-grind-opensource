## 原文 (source)

Ultra-realistic cinematic night scene, shot like a high-end film still, grounded urban realism with subtle cyberpunk energy.
COMPOSITION & CAMERA:
— low street-level angle, slightly looking up
— pizzeria in foreground (rule of thirds), clearly dominant
— massive high-rises framing the shot in background
— 35mm film look, slight anamorphic feel (subtle horizontal stretch, light streaks)
— shallow depth of field but environment still readable
— natural handheld micro-shake
— strong perspective lines from street and cables
MAIN SUBJECT — PIZZERIA:
— small 2-story corner building at intersection
— worn concrete facade, chipped paint, stains, грязь, следы времени
— ground floor: full glass facade wrapping the corner
— empty interior, clearly visible: simple tables, chairs, counter, slightly outdated
— warm tungsten lighting inside (soft but rich)
— glass slightly dirty with realistic reflections
SIGNAGE (FOCAL POINT):
— bright neon “PIZZA” sign in English
— strong red/orange glow, slightly blooming (film halation)
— glow reflecting in glass and on wet ground
— smaller Asian character signage around, secondary
SURROUNDING BUILDINGS (KEY DETAIL):
— tall residential high-rises like in reference 2
— white concrete with vertical faded red stripes
— BUT heavily worn: cracks, dirt streaks, peeling paint, rust, patched areas
— balconies cluttered, AC units, cables, signs of life
— dense urban layering, chaotic but believable
— one high-rise has vertical red neon Asian characters
LIGHT ACCENTS (IMPORTANT DETAIL):
— random windows lit with warm light
— one or two windows glowing green (distinct accent)
— some flickering or uneven lighting in windows
— small scattered neon signs across street (red/orange/green tones)
STREET & GROUND:
— wet asphalt with cinematic reflections (not mirror, slightly diffused)
— reflections of neon, windows, and PIZZA sign
— worn sidewalks, dirt, small puddles
— no crowd, but environment feels alive
LIGHTING (CINEMATIC):
— contrast: warm pizzeria interior vs cold blue night
— neon reds and occasional green accents influencing scene
— soft streetlight glow from above
— volumetric haze catching light subtly
— realistic shadow falloff, no overexposure
COLOR GRADING:
— cinematic, slightly desaturated base
— reds (neon) and warm interior pop
— green window accents stand out subtly
— filmic highlight rolloff, soft contrast
— slight grain, halation on bright lights
ATMOSPHERE:
— dense urban night, not empty emotionally
— grounded, lived-in, немного «грязный реализм»
— subtle cyberpunk через детали, не через перегруз
IMPORTANT:
— pizzeria = foreground hero
— buildings MUST look worn and heavy, not clean
— white + red stripe architecture preserved
— green window accents visible but not dominant
— “PIZZA” sign bright but integrated in cinematic lighting
— looks like a real movie frame, NOT CGI
