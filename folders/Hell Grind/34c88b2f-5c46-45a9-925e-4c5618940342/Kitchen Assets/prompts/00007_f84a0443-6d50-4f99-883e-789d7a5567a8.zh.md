Ultra-wide cinematic photo of a completely dark, abandoned commercial pizza kitchen at night. No light sources visible in the frame — total darkness, only the faintest residual ambient night glow seeping through the pass-through counter gap from the unlit dining hall. The entire scene is swallowed in deep shadow, surfaces barely readable.
Heavy industrial stainless steel prep tables, scratched and stained. A large commercial pizza oven against the back wall — cold, dead, unlit. Overhead hood exhaust, grease-coated gunmetal. Exposed pipes and conduit along raw concrete walls. Cracked ceramic tile floor.

Mounted beside it: a biometric security scanner, a faint red diode the only pinpoint of light in the entire frame, casting almost nothing, just a whisper of red on the steel surface next to it.
