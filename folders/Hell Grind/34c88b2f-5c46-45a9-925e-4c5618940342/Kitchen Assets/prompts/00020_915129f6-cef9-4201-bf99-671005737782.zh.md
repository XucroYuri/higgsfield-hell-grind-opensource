## 原文 (source)

Photorealistic ultra-wide cinematic interior shot of a dark, gritty commercial pizza kitchen, shot from a low angle. The kitchen is located in the back of a closed, abandoned-feeling pizzeria. No windows — the only ambient light bleeds faintly through a pass-through counter opening from the dimly lit dining hall beyond, casting cold teal-green underlit glow across stainless steel surfaces.
Heavy industrial stainless steel prep tables dominate the center, surfaces scratched and stained with years of use. A large commercial pizza oven sits against the back wall — cast iron and blackened steel, pilot light barely glowing orange-amber. Overhead, a low industrial hood exhaust system, grease-coated, dark gunmetal. Exposed pipes and conduit run along raw concrete walls. Cracked ceramic tile floor, wet and reflective, puddles of murky water catching faint light.
Shelving units along the walls hold dusty metal pans, forgotten aluminum trays, and industrial containers. A single flickering fluorescent tube light mounted under the hood throws harsh cold white light in a narrow strip across the prep station, the rest of the room fading into deep shadow.
Color palette: desaturated teal shadows, cold steel greys, deep blacks, faint amber from the oven glow, sickly fluorescent white stripe. Atmosphere: oppressive, claustrophobic, post-closure decay. Anamorphic lens, cinematic grain, depth of field, ultra detailed, 8K.

## 中文对照 (zh)

低角度拍摄的一座黑暗、粗粝的商业披萨厨房的写实风格（photorealistic）超宽电影级内景镜头（interior shot）。厨房位于一家关门、感觉被遗弃的披萨店的后部。没有窗户——唯一的环境光微弱地从后方昏暗就餐大厅的传菜口（pass-through counter）开口渗出，在不锈钢表面投下一片冰冷的青绿色（teal-green）底部逆光。
中央是厚重的不锈钢备料台（prep tables），表面因多年使用而划痕累累、污渍斑斑。靠后墙有一台大型商业披萨烤箱——铸铁与发黑钢材，长明火（pilot light）几乎不发光地泛着橙琥珀色。头顶是一套低矮的工业抽油烟罩（hood exhaust），覆满油脂、暗沉的枪灰色。毛坯混凝土墙上布满裸露的管道与线管（conduit）。开裂的瓷砖地面，湿润而有反光，一滩滩浑浊的水捉住微光。
沿墙的搁架单元上放着蒙尘的金属烤盘、被遗忘的铝托盘和工业容器。罩下方装有一根单一闪烁的荧光灯管（fluorescent tube），在备料区投下一道狭窄刺目的冷白光线，房间其余部分淡入深重阴影。
Color palette（配色）：去饱和的青绿色阴影、冰冷钢灰、深黑、来自烤箱光的微弱琥珀、病态的荧光白条带。Atmosphere（氛围）：压抑、幽闭、关店后的衰败。Anamorphic lens（变形宽银幕镜头）、电影颗粒、景深、极尽细节、8K。
