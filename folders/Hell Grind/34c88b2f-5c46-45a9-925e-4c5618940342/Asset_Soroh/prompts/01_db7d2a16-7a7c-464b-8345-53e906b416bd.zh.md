Edit the character sheet in this image. Apply ALL of the following changes 
together while keeping the three-view layout (front, back, close-up) intact.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHANGE 1 — REMOVE THE IRON ARM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Remove all blackened iron armor from the arm currently wearing it in the 
front view — shoulder pauldron, forearm plating, articulated metal joints, 
clawed metal fingers. Remove all orange and amber fire glow between the 
iron joints. The iron armor and orange fire are completely gone.

That arm returns to the exact same size and proportions as the other arm — 
same shoulder width, same bicep, same forearm thickness, same hand size, 
same elongated clawed fingers. No bulk, no extra volume, no armor shape. 
Both arms are now natural purple-blue limbs of identical size.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHANGE 2 — APPLY CHARRED ZONE (~30% OF BODY)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Apply a charred transformation to a contained zone covering roughly 30% 
of the visible body, located on the OPPOSITE side from where the iron 
armor used to be.

Simple rule: the arm that previously had iron armor becomes a normal 
purple-blue arm. The OPPOSITE arm — the one that was already purple-blue 
in the original — that entire arm and the body next to it becomes the 
charred zone.

The charred zone includes (anatomically, regardless of viewing angle):
— the entire arm that was originally purple-blue
— that same shoulder and that side of the upper chest
— the lower portion of the torso on that same anatomical side
— the lower part of the neck on that same anatomical side

In the FRONT view the charred zone appears on one side of the frame.
In the BACK view the charred zone appears on the OPPOSITE side of the 
frame, because the body is rotated 180 degrees — same anatomical side 
of the body = mirrored side of the frame. Both views must show the 
charring on the same anatomical side of the character.

The face stays completely untouched — fully purple-blue skin on both 
halves, no charring anywhere on the face. The charring starts below the neck.

The charred zone has organic irregular edges — fading and breaking into 
the surrounding purple-blue skin with cracked transitions, not a clean 
straight line. It looks like the burn spread naturally across that part 
of the body and stopped.

The charred area is the character's own flesh burnt black — the skin 
and tissue itself is scorched, deeply scarred and cracked, like flesh 
that was set on fire long ago and never healed. Not armor, not stone, 
not a sleeve — the actual living body has become this charred flesh 
in this zone, while keeping all natural anatomy and proportions unchanged.

Texture of the charred flesh: real burn-scar surface, blackened crusted 
skin with deep fissures running across the muscle anatomy. Layered burnt 
skin, peeling edges around the deepest cracks, exposed raw tissue showing 
dark inside the fissures. Real practical SFX makeup applied directly on 
the actor's skin with prosthetic burn appliances — not a separate sculpted 
piece, not armor.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHANGE 3 — BLUE LAVA IN THE CRACKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Inside the deepest cracks of the charred zone, deep blue lava glows from 
within — the color of a gas flame or glacier core. Dim and contained, 
visible only inside the fissures, never spilling onto the surface, 
breathing slowly. Brightest in the deepest fissures across the chest, 
forearm, and the back of the hand. The lava reads as the character's 
own internal fire showing through where the flesh has cracked open.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CHANGE 4 — EYE GLOW COLOR
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Change the eye glow color from orange-amber to the same deep blue as 
the lava in the charred zone — matching internal light source. Keep the 
glow subtle and faint, same intensity as before. Both eyes change color. 
Apply this change in all three views (front, back if visible, close-up).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The close-up portrait on the right side of the image currently shows the 
character's bare shoulders and chest with no drape. Add the black silk robe 
to the close-up.

The robe in the close-up must match the same fabric, color, weight, and 
style as the robe seen in the full-body front view — heavy black silk, 
asymmetric drape, matte deep black fabric with soft directional highlights 
along the folds. It should drape across one shoulder and down across the 
chest in the same asymmetric way as the front view, fabric folds visible,

## 中文对照 (zh)

编辑本图内的角色表（character sheet）。一次性应用以下全部改动，同时保持三视图排布（前、背、特写）完整不变。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
改动 1 —— 移除铁臂（REMOVE THE IRON ARM）
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

移除前视图中当前穿戴铁臂那只手臂上的全部乌黑铁甲——肩甲（pauldron）、前臂甲片、金属活动关节、金属利爪手指。移除铁关节之间发光的橙色与琥珀色火焰。铁甲与橙色火焰彻底消失。

该手臂恢复到与另一只手臂完全相同的尺寸与比例——同样肩宽、同样的肱二头肌、同样的前臂粗细、同样的手部大小、同样修长的利爪手指。无鼓胀、无额外体积、无甲胄形状。双臂如今都是尺寸一致的天然紫蓝色肢体。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
改动 2 —— 施加焦炭灼烧区（约占身体的 30%）
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

对一处受约束的区域施加焦炭灼烧转化，覆盖可见身体约 30%，位于原先铁甲所在的相反一侧。

简单规则：原先有铁甲的那只手臂变成正常的紫蓝色手臂。相反一侧的手臂——即原图中本来就是紫蓝色的那只——整条手臂及其旁侧的身体变成灼烧区。

灼烧区包括（按解剖部位计，与观察角度无关）：
— 原来就是紫蓝色的整条手臂
— 同一侧肩膀与那一侧胸部上方
— 同一解剖侧躯干的下部
— 同一解剖侧颈部的下部

在前视图中，灼烧区出现在画面的某一侧。在后视图中，灼烧区出现在画面的相反一侧，因为身体旋转了 180 度——同一解剖侧 = 画面的镜像侧。两个视图都必须在角色的同一解剖侧显示灼烧。

面部保持完全不动——两半边都是全紫蓝色皮肤，面部任何位置都不出现灼烧。灼烧从颈部以下才开始。

灼烧区有自然不规则的边缘——淡出并破碎融入周围紫蓝色皮肤，带皲裂的过渡，而非一条干净的直线。看起来像是烧伤在那部分身体上自然蔓延后停止。

灼烧区是角色自身的血肉被烧成黑色——皮肤与组织本身被灼焦、深留疤痕并皲裂，像是很久以前被点燃却从未愈合的血肉。不是甲胄、不是石头、不是袖套——真身活体在这一区域中变成了这种灼烧血肉，同时保持所有天然解剖与比例不变。

灼烧血肉的质感：真实的烧伤疤痕表面，焦黑结痂的皮肤，深裂隙横跨肌肉解剖。层叠的烧焦皮肤，最深裂纹周围剥落的边缘，裂隙内露出深色的裸组织。真实实用的 SFX 特效化妆直接施加在演员皮肤上，配以假体烧伤器具——不是独立雕塑件、不是甲胄。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
