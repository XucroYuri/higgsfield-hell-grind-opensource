## 原文 (source)

Cinematic character sheet, grey background (#808080), three views — 
front, back, close-up face.

Tall demon king, athletic build, broad shoulders, regal stance.
Deep purple-blue skin — real living skin under cinema lighting, 
natural pores, subtle veins, tonal variation across the body, 
organic surface like real flesh. 

Surface details may include subtle markings, faint scarring, 
or quiet imperfections — but only where they serve the character. 
A single meaningful scar, a faint ritual mark, the right kind of weathering — 
chosen with restraint, not stacked. The skin should feel inhabited, 
not decorated.

Bald — no hair. Sharp angular facial features, hollow eyes with faint glow.

Demonic horned crown — ornate dark obsidian crown fused to the skull,
curved horns rising from it like a king's crown, not random spikes.
Model decides the exact shape of the crown and horns — 
make it stylish, sovereign, unique.

Long flowing black robe over the entire body, asymmetric drape, 
heavy fabric falling past the feet. Model decides cut and structure.
Bare purple-blue chest visible where the robe opens.
Bare feet, clawed, no shoes.

Long powerful arms, purple-blue skin, ending in elongated clawed fingers.
No weapons. No jewelry. No medallions.

Subtle faint eye glow only — body glow restrained or absent. 
Any surface details (markings, scarring, weathering) used sparingly 
and intentionally — never the full demon-cliché stack.
The figure should read as a king first, a demon second.

NOT CGI. NOT illustration. NOT concept art.
Hyperrealistic practical creature, real actor in prosthetic makeup. 
ARRI LF. Film grain. 16:9.

## 中文对照 (zh)

电影感角色表（character sheet），灰色背景（#808080），三视图——前、背、特写面部。

高大的魔王（demon king），健壮体魄，宽肩，威严站姿。深紫蓝色皮肤——影院灯光下真实的活体皮肤，天然毛孔、隐约血管、全身色调变化，有机表面如同真实血肉。

表面细节可以包括隐约的纹样、淡疤痕或安静的不完美——但只在它们为角色服务之处。一道有意义的疤、一抹淡仪式印记、恰到好处的风霜——克制地选择，不堆叠。皮肤应令人感到被栖居过，而非被装饰。

光头——无毛发。棱角分明的面部特征，带微光的凹陷双眼。

恶魔角冠（demonic horned crown）——与颅骨相融的华丽黑曜石冠，弯角自其上翘起如王者之冠，而非杂乱尖刺。冠冕与犄角的确切形状由模型决定——做得出众、威严、独一无二。

全身披垂坠的黑色长袍，不对称垂坠，厚重布料垂过脚面。剪裁与结构由模型决定。长袍敞开处可见裸露的紫蓝色胸部。赤足，有爪，无鞋。

修长有力的双臂，紫蓝色皮肤，末端是修长的利爪手指。无武器。无首饰。无护身符。

仅微弱若有似无的眼部发光——身体发光被抑制或不存在。任何表面细节（纹样、疤痕、风霜）都克制而有意地使用——绝不堆满恶魔式陈词滥调。这个人物应该读作：首先是王者，其次是恶魔。

非 CGI。非插画。非概念艺术。超写实的实用活物，真实演员配假体化妆。ARRI LF。胶片颗粒。16:9。
