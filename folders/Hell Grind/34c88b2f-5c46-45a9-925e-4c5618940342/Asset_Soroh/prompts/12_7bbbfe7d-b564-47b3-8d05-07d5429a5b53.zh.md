## 原文 (source)

Cinematic character sheet, grey background (#808080), three views — 
front, back, close-up face.

Tall demon king, athletic build, broad shoulders, regal stance.
Deep purple-blue skin — real living skin under cinema lighting, 
natural pores, subtle veins, tonal variation across the body, 
organic surface like real flesh. 

A single seamless vertical seam runs the full length of the body — 
from the crown of the head down through the center of the face, 
through the chest and abdomen, continuing down between the legs. 
A thin precise raised line, slightly ritualistic, as if the body 
was assembled from two perfect halves. Subtle, not gory.

Where one section of skin has peeled back along the torso, 
underneath is more skin — same purple-blue, but a different shade, 
slightly darker or warmer. The figure has layers. Used once, not all over.

Bald — no hair anywhere. Sharp angular facial features, strong cheekbones, 
narrow lips. Hollow eyes with faint ember-orange glow inside the sockets — 
the only light on the body besides the iron arm.

Demonic horned crown — ornate dark obsidian crown fused to the skull,
curved horns rising from it like a king's regalia, not random spikes.
Model decides the exact shape of the crown and horns — 
make it stylish, sovereign, unique.

One arm is replaced from the shoulder down by a heavy blackened iron limb — 
forged segments, articulated like industrial armor, ending in clawed metal fingers. 
Between the iron segments and along its joints, ember-orange fire glows from within — 
the same fire that lives behind the eyes, escaping through this one limb. 
The other arm is normal — purple-blue skin, elongated clawed fingers.

Long flowing black silk robe over the body, asymmetric drape, 
heavy fabric falling past the feet, drifting slightly. 
Model decides cut and structure. Bare purple-blue chest visible 
where the robe opens — the vertical seam runs straight down through it. 
Bare feet, clawed, no shoes.

No weapons. The iron arm is the weapon. No medallions, no jewelry, 
no surface markings beyond the seam, the peeled skin layer, 
and the iron arm's contained fire.

The figure should read as a king first, a demon second, 
something assembled and barely holding together third.

NOT CGI. NOT illustration. NOT concept art. NOT plastic, NOT mannequin.
Hyperrealistic practical creature, real actor in prosthetic makeup. 
ARRI LF. Film grain. 16:9.

## 中文对照 (zh)

电影感角色表（character sheet），灰色背景（#808080），三视图——前、背、特写面部。

高大的魔王（demon king），健壮体魄，宽肩，威严站姿。深紫蓝色皮肤——影院灯光下真实的活体皮肤，天然毛孔、隐约血管、全身色调变化，有机表面如同真实血肉。

一道无缝的垂直接缝自头顶向下贯穿面部中央，再穿过胸部与腹部，继续延伸至两腿之间。一道细而精准的凸起线，略带仪式感，仿佛这具身体是由两半完美拼合而成。微妙，不血腥。

沿躯干某处皮肤剥起之处，底下是另一层皮肤——同样紫蓝，但色调略有不同，稍深或稍暖。这个人物有层次。仅使用一次，不遍布全身。

光头——任何地方无毛发。棱角分明的面部特征、高颧骨、窄唇。凹陷的眼窝中带有微弱的余烬橙色光——除铁臂之外身体上唯一的光。

恶魔角冠（demonic horned crown）——与颅骨相融的华丽黑曜石冠，弯角自其上翘起如王者华饰，而非杂乱尖刺。冠冕与犄角的确切形状由模型决定——做得出众、威严、独一无二。

一只手臂自肩以下被一条厚重的乌黑铁肢所替换——锻造分段，如工业甲胄般铰接，末端是金属利爪手指。在铁分段之间及其关节沿线，余烬橙色的火焰自内而外发光——正是眼睛背后那股火，透过这唯一一条肢体逸出。另一只手臂正常——紫蓝色皮肤、修长的利爪手指。

全身披垂坠的黑色长丝袍，不对称垂坠，厚重布料垂过脚面，微微飘动。剪裁与结构由模型决定。长袍敞开处可见裸露的紫蓝色胸部——垂直接缝自其中径直向下。赤足，有爪，无鞋。

无武器。铁臂即为武器。无护身符、无首饰、除接缝、剥起的皮肤层与铁臂内敛的火焰之外无表面纹样。

这个人物应该读作：首先是王者，其次是恶魔，第三是某种被拼合而成、勉强维系在一起的存在。

非 CGI。非插画。非概念艺术。非塑料、非人体模型。超写实的实用活物，真实演员配假体化妆。ARRI LF。胶片颗粒。16:9。
