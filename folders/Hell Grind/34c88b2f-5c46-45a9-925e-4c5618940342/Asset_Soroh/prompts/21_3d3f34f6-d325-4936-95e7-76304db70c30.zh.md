Cinematic character reference sheet for film production. SAME IDENTICAL CHARACTER 
in all 7 views. Neutral grey seamless backdrop (#808080), no labels, no text.

LAYOUT: Large frontal hero portrait on the right half (50% of frame). 
Six smaller reference views on the left half arranged in 2 rows of 3:
top row — full body front, full body 3/4 angle, full body side profile.
bottom row — full body back view, close-up of crowned head from front, 
close-up of clawed hand resting on hip.

HERO PORTRAIT (right half, dominant):
Frontal medium shot from chest up, looking directly at camera.
85mm lens, T1.8, dramatic low-key cinematic lighting from front-left, 
deep rim light from behind catching the horns and shoulders. 
Sharp focus on eyes with clear catchlights. Shallow depth of field.

POSE — IMPERIAL:
Shoulders pulled back, chin slightly raised, weight evenly grounded. 
One fist clenched at his side, the other hand resting on his hip. 
The bearing of a sovereign who has ruled for centuries. 
Absolute stillness, absolute confidence.

CHARACTER — DEMON KING:
Towering powerful figure, 7 feet tall. Broad shoulders, deep chest, 
muscular and dense — the build of an ancient warlord, not a wraith. 
Skin a deep blood-crimson, slightly translucent over the sternum and forearms 
revealing dark bone structure beneath. 
Glowing molten cracks running across the torso, neck and biceps — 
ember-orange light bleeding through fissures in the flesh, like cooling lava. 
Brightest along the sternum and throat, dimming toward the limbs.

CROWN:
Massive blackened iron crown fused into the base of the horns, 
three forward-facing prongs rising from the brow, set with rough 
obsidian and smoldering ember stones glowing faintly red. 
The crown does not sit on the head — it has grown into the bone, 
ancient and inseparable.

HORNS:
Two enormous curved horns rising up and outward from the temples — 
dark stone-grey, thick at the base, heavily ridged and weathered, 
each curving back and slightly outward in a powerful sweep. 
Bands of blackened gold wrapping the base of each horn.

HEAD:
Bald crown beneath the iron diadem. Long heavy white hair flowing 
from the back of the skull down past the shoulders, slightly lifted 
as if by static. Sharp pointed ears, each pierced with heavy black iron rings. 
Tribal rune-tattoos in dark grey ink etched across the bald scalp 
and down the cheekbones — sharp angular sigils, ancient script, 
branching like roots. 
Eyes — glowing molten gold-amber, no whites, slight inner fire. 
Strong cheekbones, narrow lips set in calm regal expression.

UPPER BODY ARMOR:
Massive blackened gold pauldrons on both shoulders — 
ridged, embossed with deep rune-circles glowing faintly red, 
sharp spike accents pointing back. 
Heavy bracers of the same blackened gold on both forearms, 
inscribed with ritual sigils, fitted over the wrists. 
Chest left bare between the pauldrons, displaying the runic seal-circles 
burned into the pectorals — complex circular geometries, ancient script along the rims. 

ROYAL ADORNMENTS:
Heavy chain of black iron links draped across the chest, 
suspending a massive obsidian medallion centered on the sternum — 
the medallion's core glowing with the same molten ember light as his cracks. 
Several thick rings on the clawed fingers — black metal, set with dark stones.

CLOAK:
Heavy black fur mantle cascading from the right pauldron only, 
falling behind the back like a royal train, lining of deep crimson silk 
visible at the inner edge. Asymmetric, regal.

LOWER BODY:
Heavy royal kilt of thick black leather, segmented into vertical strips, 
each strip mounted with a blackened gold plate engraved with a single 
rune-sigil glowing faintly red. Wide ceremonial belt of black leather 
with a massive central buckle shaped like a horned skull in blackened gold. 
The kilt falls to mid-thigh.

LEGS:
Bare crimson skin from mid-thigh down, same molten cracks running along the calves. 
Tall black leather greaves on both shins, segmented and rune-etched. 
Clawed bare feet with bone-white nails, planted firmly.

HANDS:
Long powerful arms ending in elongated clawed fingers, sharp black nails, 
crimson skin shifting to bone-white at the fingertips.

NO weapons. The king does not carry — the king commands.

REALISM SPEC:
National Geographic meets cinematic movie quality. Real actor in 
practical makeup and prosthetics, Stan Winston / Weta Workshop level 
creature design. 
Hyperrealistic skin texture — pores, micro-imperfections, subsurface scatter. 
Real leather grain on the kilt, real fur on the cloak, real metal patina 
on the gold armor, real bone density on the horns and crown stones, 
real molten glow from the cracks (practical LED lighting embedded in prosthetics).

NOT anime. NOT illustration. NOT concept art. NOT CGI. NOT digital painting. 
ARRI Alexa LF, ARRI color science, film still aesthetic. 
35mm film grain, no oversharpening, no AI smoothing. 
Dramatic low-key cinematic key light on hero portrait, 
even soft light on the six reference views. 
16:9 aspect ratio.

## 中文对照 (zh)

电影级角色参考表，供影视制作使用。七个视图均为同一角色、完全一致。中性灰色无缝背景（#808080），无标签、无文字。

布局（LAYOUT）：右半边（画面的 50%）为大幅正面主角肖像（hero portrait）。左半边为六个较小的参考视图，按 2 行 3 列排列：
上行——全身前、全身 3/4 角度、全身侧面。
下行——全身后视图、戴冠头部的正面特写、叉腰的利爪手特写。

主角肖像（HERO PORTRAIT，右半边，主体）：
自胸部以上的正面中景，直视镜头。85mm 镜头，T1.8，自左前方而来的戏剧性低调电影布光，正后方的深轮廓光（rim light）勾出犄角与双肩。双眼锐利对焦，带清晰眼神光（catchlight）。浅景深。

姿态——帝王式（POSE — IMPERIAL）：
双肩后收，下巴微抬，重心均匀落地。一只拳攥于身侧，另一只手叉在腰上。一位已统治数个世纪君主的威仪。绝对静止，绝对的自信。

角色——魔王（CHARACTER — DEMON KING）：
高耸有力的身影，7 英尺高。宽肩、深胸，肌肉强健而密实——位古代战将的体魄，而非鬼魅。皮肤呈深血红，在胸骨与前臂处微微半透明，透出底下的深色骨骼结构。
发光的熔浆裂纹横贯躯干、颈部与肱二头肌——余烬橙色的光自血肉裂隙渗出，如冷却的熔岩。沿胸骨与喉部最亮，向四肢渐暗。

冠冕（CROWN）：
巨大的乌黑铁冠融入犄角根部，自眉额升起三个朝前的尖棱，镶嵌粗糙黑曜石与幽幽泛红的冒烟余烬石。冠冕并非戴于头上——它已长入骨骼，古老而不可分离。

犄角（HORNS）：
两只巨大的弯角自太阳穴向上向外翘起——深石灰色，根基粗厚，棱纹深刻而饱经风霜，每只都以有力的扫势向后并略微向外弯曲。乌金色带（band）缠绕每只犄角根部。

头部（HEAD）：
铁质头环（diadem）之下的光秃头顶。长而浓密的白色长发自脑后流过肩头，如被静电微微扬起。尖耳，各穿刺沉重的黑色铁环。深灰色墨水的部族符文刺青蚀刻于光秃头皮并向下蔓延至颧骨——尖锐的棱角符文、古老文字，如根系般分叉。双眼——发光的熔金琥珀色，无眼白，微带内焰。高颧骨，窄唇凝成平静威严的表情。

上半身甲胄（UPPER BODY ARMOR）：
双肩巨大的乌金肩甲（pauldron）——带棱纹，压印深深的符文圆圈、幽幽泛红，尖锐的尖刺饰向后指。同种乌金锻造的厚重护臂（bracer）箍于双前臂，刻有仪式符文，扣于腕间。双肩甲之间的胸口裸露，展示烙入胸肌的符文环印——复杂的圆形几何，边缘环绕古老文字。

王室饰物（ROYAL ADORNMENTS）：
沉重的乌黑铁环链横跨胸前，悬垂一件巨大的黑曜石护身符居中于胸骨——护身符核心发出与他裂纹相同的熔融余烬光。利爪手指上数枚粗戒指——黑金属，镶嵌深色宝石。

披风（CLOAK）：
自右侧肩甲单边倾泻而下的厚重黑色毛皮斗篷，如王室长裾般坠于身后，内缘可见深绯红丝质衬里。不对称、威严。

下半身（LOWER BODY）：
厚实黑皮的重型王室短裙，纵向分段成竖条，每一条都嵌一块乌金甲片，其上刻一个幽幽泛红的符文。黑色皮革的宽典礼腰带，中央一件巨大的乌金独角骷髅形带扣。短裙垂至大腿中部。

腿部（LEGS）：
自大腿中部以下为裸露的绯红皮肤，同样的熔浆裂纹沿小腿延伸。双胫高筒黑色皮革护胫（greave），分段并刻符文。有爪赤足，白骨般的指甲，稳稳踏地。

双手（HANDS）：
修长有力的双臂，末端是修长的利爪手指、尖锐的黑色指甲，绯红皮肤在指尖渐变为骨白色。

无武器。王不携器——王下令。

写实规范（REALISM SPEC）：
《国家地理》（National Geographic）与电影级画质结合。真实演员配实用化妆与假体，Stan Winston / Weta Workshop 级别的生物造型。超写实皮肤质感——毛孔、微观瑕疵、次表面散射。短裙上真实的皮革纹理，披风上真实的毛皮，金甲上真实的金属包浆，犄角与冠石上真实的骨质密度，裂纹处真实的熔融发光（假体中嵌入的实用 LED 灯光）。

非动漫。非插画。非概念艺术。非 CGI。非数字绘画。ARRI Alexa LF，ARRI 色彩科学，厚重胶片静帧美学。电影静帧（Movie still）。35mm 胶片颗粒，无过度锐化、无 AI 柔化。主角肖像用戏剧性低调电影主光，六个参考视图用均匀柔光。16:9 画幅。
