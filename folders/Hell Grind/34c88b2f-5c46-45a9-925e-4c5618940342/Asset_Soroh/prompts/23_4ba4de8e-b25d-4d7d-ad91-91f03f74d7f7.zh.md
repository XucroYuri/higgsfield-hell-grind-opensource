Cinematic character reference sheet for film production. SAME IDENTICAL CHARACTER 
in all 7 views. Neutral grey seamless backdrop (#808080), no labels, no text.

LAYOUT: Large frontal hero portrait on the right half (50% of frame). 
Six smaller reference views on the left half arranged in 2 rows of 3:
top row — full body front, full body 3/4 angle, full body side profile.
bottom row — full body back view, close-up of horned head from front, 
close-up of clawed hands holding sword hilt.

HERO PORTRAIT (right half, dominant):
Frontal medium shot from chest up, looking directly at camera. 
85mm lens, T1.8, soft cinematic key light from front-left, subtle rim light. 
Sharp focus on eyes with clear catchlights. Shallow depth of field.

CHARACTER — DEMON KING:
Tall imposing figure, 7 feet, lean and powerful. Exposed musculature 
in deep blood-red tones — visible sinew, anatomy, traces of bone structure 
through translucent crimson skin, organic and ancient. 
Glowing molten cracks running across torso and shoulders — dim ember-orange 
light bleeding through fissures in the flesh, like cooling lava.

Bald angular skull, two massive curved horns rising up and slightly outward 
from the temples — bone-white, sharp as blades, one meter long each. 
Long white hair flowing from the back of the skull down past the shoulders, 
heavy and unkempt. Sharp pointed ears with simple iron hoop earrings on the left.
Tribal rune-like tattoos in dark grey ink etched across the bald scalp 
and down the cheekbones — sharp angular geometric symbols, ancient script.
Eyes — glowing molten gold-amber, no whites, slight inner fire. 
Strong cheekbones, narrow lips, faint scar tracing from temple to jaw.

Bare torso displaying ribcage anatomy in red bone visible beneath sinew. 
Faded crimson runic seals burned into the chest and shoulders. 
Lean muscular build, long arms ending in skeletal clawed fingers — 
elongated phalanges, sharp black nails.

Lower body wrapped in a heavy weathered linen loincloth, 
deep beige and dust-stained, layered ropes and cords binding it at the waist 
with hanging bone charms and small clattering pendants. Frayed edges, ancient fabric.

Right shoulder bears a single battered iron pauldron — dented, scarred, 
secured by leather straps across the chest. Tattered black cloak fragment 
hanging from that shoulder, ragged at the edges.

Strapped diagonally across the back — a collection of seven swords 
of different lengths and origins, hilts protruding above the shoulders 
at varied angles. Worn handles wrapped in dark cloth and leather. 
A long katana-like blade sheathed at the left hip, hand resting on it.

Barefoot, clawed feet with bone-white nails.

REALISM SPEC:
National Geographic meets cinematic movie quality. Real actor in 
practical makeup and prosthetics, Stan Winston level creature design. 
Hyperrealistic skin texture — pores, micro-imperfections, subsurface scatter. 
Real fabric weave, real weathered leather, real metal patina, 
real bone density on horns.

NOT anime. NOT illustration. NOT concept art. NOT CGI. NOT digital painting. 
ARRI Alexa LF, ARRI color science, film still aesthetic. 
35mm film grain, no oversharpening, no AI smoothing. 
Soft cinematic key light on hero portrait, even soft light on side views.
16:9 aspect ratio.

主角肖像（HERO PORTRAIT，右半边，主体）：
自胸部以上的正面中景，直视镜头。85mm 镜头，T1.8，自左前方而来的柔和电影主光，微妙的轮廓光（rim light）。双眼锐利对焦，带清晰眼神光（catchlight）。浅景深。

光秃的棱角颅骨，两只巨大的弯角自太阳穴向上并略微向外翘起——骨白色，锋利如刃，各长一米。长而浓密的白色长发自脑后垂过肩头，厚重而凌乱。尖耳，左侧戴简单的铁圈耳环。深灰色墨水的部族式符文刺青蚀刻于光秃头皮并向下蔓延至颧骨——尖锐的棱角几何符号、古老文字。双眼——发光的熔金琥珀色，无眼白，微带内焰。高颧骨，窄唇，自太阳穴至下颚有一道淡淡疤痕。

裸露的躯干展现肋骨架解剖，筋腱下可见红色骨骼。胸口与双肩烙入褪色的绯红符印。精瘦肌肉线条，修长双臂末端是骷髅般的利爪手指——修长的指骨、尖锐的黑色指甲。
