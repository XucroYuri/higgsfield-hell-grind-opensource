## 原文 (source)

Cinematic character reference sheet for film production. SAME IDENTICAL CHARACTER 
in all 7 views. Neutral grey seamless backdrop (#808080), no labels, no text.

LAYOUT: Large frontal hero portrait on the right half (50% of frame). 
Six smaller reference views on the left half arranged in 2 rows of 3:
top row — full body front, full body 3/4 angle, full body side profile.
bottom row — full body back view, close-up of crowned head from front, 
close-up of clawed hand resting on hip.

HERO PORTRAIT (right half, dominant):
Frontal medium shot from chest up, looking directly at camera.
85mm lens, T1.8, dramatic low-key cinematic lighting from front-left, 
deep rim light from behind catching the crown horns and shoulders. 
Sharp focus on eyes with clear catchlights. Shallow depth of field.

POSE — IMPERIAL:
Shoulders pulled back, chin slightly raised, weight evenly grounded. 
One fist clenched at his side, the other hand resting on his hip. 
Absolute stillness, sovereign confidence.

CHARACTER — DEMON KING:
Towering powerful figure, 7 feet tall. Broad shoulders, deep chest, 
muscular and dense — the build of an ancient warlord. 

Skin — deep violet-blue, rich royal indigo with subtle cooler highlights 
on muscle ridges, slightly desaturated. Matte finish, no shine. 
Smooth skin texture overall — clean, not detailed with veins or cracks. 
The body reads as a single sculpted form.

CROWN:
Massive demonic crown of blackened iron sitting heavy on the bald head. 
Two enormous curved horns rising from the crown itself (not from his skull) — 
dark forged metal, thick at the base, sweeping up and outward, 
slightly ridged but clean, not over-textured. 
The crown wraps the brow with three forward-facing spikes rising between the horns. 
A single piece of regalia — the crown IS the silhouette.

HEAD:
Completely bald. No hair anywhere. Smooth violet-blue scalp.
Sharp pointed ears, each with a single heavy black iron ring. 
Strong cheekbones, narrow closed lips, calm regal expression.
Eyes — softly glowing molten gold-amber, the only light source on the body. 
Subtle inner glow, not blazing.

UPPER BODY:
Bare violet-blue chest. Lean dense musculature, defined but not exaggerated. 
Twin pauldrons of blackened iron on both shoulders — clean curved forms, 
matte black, no engravings, no glow, just powerful silhouette. 
Heavy black iron bracers on both forearms, simple smooth forms. 

CLOAK:
Heavy black fur mantle cascading from the right pauldron only, 
falling behind the back like a royal train, lining of deep crimson silk 
visible at the inner edge. Asymmetric, regal. Heavy and present.

LOWER BODY:
Long royal kilt of thick black leather, falling to the knee. 
Wide ceremonial belt of black leather with a single horned skull buckle 
in blackened iron. No glowing runes. No engravings. Clean material reads.

LEGS:
Bare violet-blue skin from the knee down. Tall black iron greaves on both shins, 
clean smooth forms. Clawed bare feet, dark nails.

HANDS:
Long powerful arms ending in elongated clawed fingers with dark nails. 
Skin remains uniform violet-blue down to the fingertips. No rings, no jewelry.

No weapons. No medallion. No chains. No glowing runes anywhere on the body or armor. 
The figure is read by silhouette, color, and presence — not by detail.

REALISM SPEC:
Real actor in practical makeup and prosthetics, Stan Winston / Weta Workshop 
level creature design. 
Realistic skin texture — pores and natural surface, but skin reads smooth overall, 
no exaggerated wrinkles or veining. Real leather grain on the kilt, 
real fur on the cloak, real forged iron patina on the crown and armor.

NOT anime. NOT illustration. NOT concept art. NOT CGI. NOT digital painting. 
ARRI Alexa LF, ARRI color science, film still aesthetic. 
35mm film grain, no oversharpening, no AI smoothing. 
Dramatic low-key cinematic key light on hero portrait, 
even soft light on the six reference views. 
16:9 aspect ratio.

## 中文对照 (zh)

电影级角色参考表，供影视制作使用。七个视图均为同一角色、完全一致。中性灰色无缝背景（#808080），无标签、无文字。

布局（LAYOUT）：右半边（画面的 50%）为大幅正面主角肖像（hero portrait）。左半边为六个较小的参考视图，按 2 行 3 列排列：
上行——全身前、全身 3/4 角度、全身侧面。
下行——全身后视图、戴冠头部的正面特写、叉腰的利爪手特写。

主角肖像（HERO PORTRAIT，右半边，主体）：
自胸部以上的正面中景，直视镜头。85mm 镜头，T1.8，自左前方而来的戏剧性低调电影布光，正后方的深轮廓光（rim light）勾出冠冕犄角与双肩。双眼锐利对焦，带清晰眼神光（catchlight）。浅景深。

姿态——帝王式（POSE — IMPERIAL）：
双肩后收，下巴微抬，重心均匀落地。一只拳攥于身侧，另一只手叉在腰上。绝对静止，帝王般的自信。

角色——魔王（CHARACTER — DEMON KING）：
高耸有力的身影，7 英尺高。宽肩、深胸，肌肉强健而密实——一位古代战将的体魄。

皮肤——深紫蓝，浓郁的王室靛蓝，在肌肉棱线上带微妙的冷调高光，略微去饱和。哑光饰面，无光泽。整体平滑的皮肤质感——干净，不以血管或裂纹细化。身体读作单一雕塑形体。

冠冕（CROWN）：
一顶沉重的乌黑铁质巨型恶魔冠，压于光头之上。两只巨大的弯角自冠冕本身（而非头颅）翘起——深色锻造金属，根基粗厚，向上向外扫起，略带棱纹而干净，不过度纹理。冠冕以三个朝前的尖刺包裹眉额，尖刺升起于两角之间。单件华饰——冠冕即轮廓本身。

头部（HEAD）：
完全光头。任何地方无毛发。光滑的紫蓝色头皮。尖耳，各戴一只沉重的黑色铁环。高颧骨，紧闭的窄唇，平静威严的表情。双眼——柔柔发光的熔金琥珀色，是身体上唯一的光源。微妙的内光，不炽烈。

上半身（UPPER BODY）：
裸露的紫蓝色胸部。精瘦密实的肌肉，分明而不夸张。双肩一对乌黑铁肩甲（pauldron）——干净的弧形形体，哑光黑，无雕花、无发光，只有有力的轮廓。双前臂戴厚重的黑色铁护臂（bracer），简洁平滑的形体。

披风（CLOAK）：
自右侧肩甲单边倾泻而下的厚重黑色毛皮斗篷，如王室长裾般坠于身后，内缘可见深绯红丝质衬里。不对称、威严。厚重而有存在感。

下半身（LOWER BODY）：
厚实黑皮的长款王室短裙（kilt），垂至膝部。浅宽的黑皮典礼腰带，配一件乌黑铁质独角骷髅带扣。无发光符文。无雕花。干净的材质质感。

腿部（LEGS）：
自膝部以下为裸露的紫蓝色皮肤。双胫高筒黑色铁护胫（greave），干净平滑的形体。有爪赤足，深色指甲。

双手（HANDS）：
修长有力的双臂，末端是深色指甲的修长利爪手指。皮肤保持均匀紫蓝直至指尖。无戒指、无首饰。

无武器。无护身符。无锁链。身体或甲胄任何位置都无发光符文。这个人物以轮廓、色彩与气场来识读——而非细节。

写实规范（REALISM SPEC）：
真实演员配实用化妆与假体，Stan Winston / Weta Workshop 级别的生物造型。写实的皮肤质感——毛孔与自然表面，但整体读作平滑，无夸张的皱纹或血管。短裙上真实的皮革纹理，披风上真实的毛皮，冠冕与甲胄上真实的锻造铁锈色包浆。

非动漫。非插画。非概念艺术。非 CGI。非数字绘画。ARRI Alexa LF，ARRI 色彩科学，胶片静帧美学。35mm 胶片颗粒，无过度锐化、无 AI 柔化。主角肖像用戏剧性低调电影主光，六个参考视图用均匀柔光。16:9 画幅。
