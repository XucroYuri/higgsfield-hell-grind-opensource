## 原文 (source)

Cinematic character reference sheet for film production. SAME IDENTICAL CHARACTER 
in all 7 views. Neutral grey seamless backdrop (#808080), no labels, no text.

LAYOUT: Large frontal hero portrait on the right half (50% of frame). 
Six smaller reference views on the left half arranged in 2 rows of 3:
top row — full body front, full body 3/4 angle, full body side profile.
bottom row — full body back view, close-up of crowned head from front, 
close-up of clawed hand resting at his side.

HERO PORTRAIT (right half, dominant):
Frontal medium shot from chest up, looking directly at camera.
85mm lens, T1.8, dramatic low-key cinematic lighting from front-left, 
deep rim light from behind catching the crown horns and the silk edge. 
Sharp focus on eyes with clear catchlights. Shallow depth of field.

POSE — IMPERIAL:
Shoulders pulled back, chin slightly raised, weight evenly grounded. 
Arms relaxed at his sides. The bearing of a sovereign. 
Absolute stillness, sovereign confidence.

CHARACTER — DEMON KING:
Towering powerful figure, 7 feet tall. Broad shoulders, deep chest, 
lean and dense — the build of a sculpted warlord, refined not bulky. 

SKIN:
Deep violet-blue, rich royal indigo with subtle cooler highlights 
on the muscle ridges. Matte finish, no shine, no veins, no cracks, 
no glow on the body. Smooth uniform skin reading as a single sculpted form. 
The body is the only color in the image — everything else is black.

CROWN:
Demonic crown of polished black obsidian, fitted to the bald skull as if grown there. 
Two large curved horns rising from the crown itself (not from his skull) — 
black obsidian, smooth and tapered, sweeping up and outward in clean elegant arcs, 
like single sculpted forms. No ridges, no texture noise, just pure shape. 
Faint dark glassy reflections along the curves. 
The crown wraps the brow as a single seamless band, three subtle pointed peaks 
rising between the horns. One unified piece — couture, not forged.

HEAD:
Completely bald. Smooth violet-blue scalp. No tattoos, no markings.
Sharp pointed ears, bare. Strong cheekbones, narrow closed lips. 
Calm, regal, almost still expression. 
Eyes — softly glowing molten gold-amber, the only light source on the body. 
Subtle inner glow, contained, not blazing.

UPPER BODY:
Bare violet-blue chest. Lean dense musculature, defined but understated. 

Sculpted obsidian armor — couture-grade, like Iris van Herpen. 
Twin pauldrons of polished black obsidian flowing over the shoulders 
in fluid organic curves, no rivets, no plating seams, no engravings. 
Each pauldron reads as a single liquid form that hardened into stone, 
gently extending down the upper arm before tapering off. 
Matching obsidian bracers wrap the forearms in the same fluid sculpted geometry — 
seamless, smooth, almost wet-looking in their reflective black surface.

CLOAK:
Long flowing black silk cape attached at both shoulders beneath the pauldrons. 
Floor-length, extending well past the feet, drifting slightly as if caught 
in a gentle wind. Heavy fluid drape, deep matte black silk that catches 
soft directional highlights along the folds. No lining, no trim, no embroidery — 
pure sculptural fabric. The cape is dramatic and architectural, 
shaping the silhouette into a tall narrow column.

LOWER BODY:
Long sculpted obsidian skirt-armor flowing from a fitted waist piece, 
reaching mid-calf — single-form construction, no segments, no plates, 
just smooth obsidian curves following the line of the body, slightly tapered. 
Like a hardened liquid garment. No belt, no buckle.

LEGS & FEET:
Bare violet-blue skin from mid-calf down. Clawed bare feet with dark nails. 
No greaves, no boots — the obsidian skirt-armor stops mid-calf and gives way 
to bare clawed feet for contrast.

HANDS:
Long powerful arms ending in elongated clawed fingers with dark nails. 
Skin remains uniform violet-blue down to the fingertips. No rings.

No weapons. No medallion. No chains. No engravings. No glow on the armor or cloak. 
Read by silhouette, by the contrast of violet-blue skin against unbroken black, 
by the line of the silk. Couture, not fantasy.

REALISM SPEC:
Real actor in practical makeup and prosthetics. Avant-garde fashion meets 
Stan Winston creature design — the silhouette belongs in a Rick Owens runway 
as much as in a film. 
Realistic skin texture — pores and natural surface, but skin reads smooth overall. 
Real silk drape and weight on the cape, real polished obsidian on the armor 
with subtle dark glassy reflections, no plastic shine.

NOT anime. NOT illustration. NOT concept art. NOT CGI. NOT digital painting. 
ARRI Alexa LF, ARRI color science, film still aesthetic. 
35mm film grain, no oversharpening, no AI smoothing. 
Dramatic low-key cinematic key light on hero portrait, 
even soft light on the six reference views. 
16:9 aspect ratio.

## 中文对照 (zh)

电影级角色参考表，供影视制作使用。七个视图均为同一角色、完全一致。中性灰色无缝背景（#808080），无标签、无文字。

布局（LAYOUT）：右半边（画面的 50%）为大幅正面主角肖像（hero portrait）。左半边为六个较小的参考视图，按 2 行 3 列排列：
上行——全身前、全身 3/4 角度、全身侧面。
下行——全身后视图、戴冠头部的正面特写、垂于身侧的利爪手特写。

主角肖像（HERO PORTRAIT，右半边，主体）：
自胸部以上的正面中景，直视镜头。85mm 镜头，T1.8，自左前方而来的戏剧性低调电影布光，正后方的深轮廓光（rim light）勾出冠冕犄角与丝袍边缘。双眼锐利对焦，带清晰眼神光（catchlight）。浅景深。

姿态——帝王式（POSE — IMPERIAL）：
双肩后收，下巴微抬，重心均匀落地。双臂放松垂于身侧。王者气度。绝对静止，帝王般的自信。

角色——魔王（CHARACTER — DEMON KING）：
高耸有力的身影，7 英尺高。宽肩、深胸，精瘦而密实——一座雕塑般战将的体魄，精练而不臃肿。

皮肤（SKIN）：
深紫蓝，浓郁的王室靛蓝，在肌肉棱线上带微妙的冷调高光。哑光饰面，无光泽、无血管、无裂纹、身体无发光。平滑统一的皮肤读作单一雕塑形体。身体是画面中唯一的颜色——其他一切都是黑色。

冠冕（CROWN）：
抛光黑曜石的恶魔冠，贴合光头头颅，仿佛天生如此。两只巨大的弯角自冠冕本身（而非头颅）翘起——黑曜石，平滑收细，以干净优雅的弧线向上向外扫起，如单一的雕塑形体。无棱纹、无纹理杂音，只有纯粹形状。沿弧线的微弱暗色玻璃般反光。冠冕如一条无缝的环带包裹眉额，两角之间升起三个微妙的尖峰。一整块统一的部件——是高级定制（couture），而非锻造而成。

头部（HEAD）：
完全光头。光滑的紫蓝色头皮。无纹身、无纹样。尖耳，裸露。高颧骨，紧闭的窄唇。平静、威严、几近静止的表情。双眼——柔柔发光的熔金琥珀色，是身体上唯一的光源。微妙的内光，内敛而不炽烈。

上半身（UPPER BODY）：
裸露的紫蓝色胸部。精瘦密实的肌肉，分明而不张扬。

雕塑般的黑曜石甲胄——高级定制级别，如 Iris van Herpen。一对抛光黑曜石肩甲（pauldron）以流动的有机曲线覆盖双肩，无铆钉、无甲片接缝、无雕花。每片肩甲读作一泓硬化为石头的流态形体，沿上臂轻柔下延后收细。相配的黑曜石护臂（bracer）以同样流动的雕塑几何包裹前臂——无缝、平滑，在反光黑色表面中几近湿润之感。

披风（CLOAK）：
垂坠的黑色长丝披风，在肩甲之下系于双肩。及地长度，远垂过脚面，如被微风轻拂般微微飘动。厚重的流态垂坠，深哑光黑丝，沿褶裥捕捉柔和定向高光。无衬里、无滚边、无刺绣——纯粹雕塑感的布料。披风戏剧而有建筑感，将轮廓塑成一道高而窄的立柱。

下半身（LOWER BODY）：
自贴合腰件倾泻而下的长款雕塑黑曜石裙甲，及至小腿中部——单一形体构造，无分段、无甲片，只有沿身体线条延伸的平滑黑曜石曲线，略微收细。如一件硬化的流态衣袍。无腰带、无带扣。

腿部与脚（LEGS & FEET）：
小腿中部以下为裸露的紫蓝色皮肤。带深色指甲的有爪赤足。无护胫、无靴——黑曜石裙甲止于小腿中部，让位给裸露的有爪赤足以作对比。

双手（HANDS）：
修长有力的双臂，末端是深色指甲的修长利爪手指。皮肤保持均匀紫蓝直至指尖。无戒指。

无武器。无护身符。无锁链。无雕花。甲胄或披风上无发光。以轮廓识人，以紫蓝肌肤映衬无断裂的纯黑，以丝袍的线条识人。高级定制，而非奇幻。

写实规范（REALISM SPEC）：
真实演员配实用化妆与假体。先锋时尚邂逅 Stan Winston 生物造型——这一轮廓既属于 Rick Owens 的秀场，也同样属于一部电影。写实的皮肤质感——毛孔与自然表面，但整体读作平滑。披风上真实的丝质垂坠与分量，甲胄上真实的抛光黑曜石配微妙暗色玻璃反光，无塑料光泽。

非动漫。非插画。非概念艺术。非 CGI。非数字绘画。ARRI Alexa LF，ARRI 色彩科学，胶片静帧美学。35mm 胶片颗粒，无过度锐化、无 AI 柔化。主角肖像用戏剧性低调电影主光，六个参考视图用均匀柔光。16:9 画幅。
