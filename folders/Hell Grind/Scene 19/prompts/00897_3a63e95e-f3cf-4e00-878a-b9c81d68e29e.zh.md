## 原文 (source)

A two-shot sequence inside an underground brutalist research base atrium carved into black volcanic rock. Both shots are slow continuous straight-line camera dolly fly-throughs along the central axis of the same location, at constant eye-level height, smooth steady motion, no shake, no roll, no pan, no zoom — pure forward translation like a rail-mounted dolly. ——— SHOT 1 (first half of the video, ~3–4 seconds) ——— Camera flies FORWARD from the elevator zone toward the giant LED screen. Opening frame: camera at the elevator end of the atrium, framed like the TOP view of the reference — looking forward along the central walkway, two rows of empty research workstations flanking the path (empty ergonomic black office chairs, PC monitors, ancient stone tablets and pottery fragments under articulated microscopes, small precision robotic arms, scanning equipment), brushed steel and tempered glass railings, multi-level cantilevered concrete balconies and glass-walled laboratory pods with illuminated interiors on left and right, jagged black volcanic basalt rock framing the cavern. Closing frame: camera arrives directly under the giant LED screen, screen filling the upper portion of the frame, displaying a clean schematic minimalist world map in dark blue-green tones with bright glowing city dots and thin curved golden-amber connection lines, soft cool emissive bloom washing the foreground concrete, recessed brutalist concrete portal visible beneath the screen. ——— HARD CUT ——— ——— SHOT 2 (second half of the video, ~3–4 seconds) ——— Camera flies FORWARD from the screen zone back toward the elevator. Opening frame: camera positioned right under the giant LED screen, framed like the BOTTOM view of the reference — looking forward along the same central walkway in the opposite direction, the same two rows of workstations now flanking the path from the reverse angle, the same lab pods sliding past in mirrored parallax. Closing frame: camera arrives at the elevator end of the atrium — a large polished stainless steel elevator with two sliding metal doors meeting in the middle centered on the back wall, framed by massive brutalist concrete pillars and jagged black volcanic basalt rock, soft warm tungsten downlight pooling on the steel doors. ——— APPLIED TO BOTH SHOTS ——— Lighting consistent throughout: cool blue-grey ambient palette, warm tungsten interior lab glow from the pods, practical LED downlights, fluorescent panels inside pods, realistic falloff, natural bounce, subtle volumetric haze and real dust particles drifting in the deep cavern air catching the light as the camera moves. Materials: real poured architectural concrete with formwork seams, tie-rod holes, water staining, micro-cracks, weathered patina; real volcanic basalt with sharp fracture facets, mineral grain, dust-filled crevices; real brushed stainless steel railings and elevator; real tempered glass with smudges and reflections; real polished concrete floor with reflective sheen. Camera: simulated ARRI Alexa cinema camera, 28mm prime lens, f/4, slight natural film grain, real cinematic dynamic range, mild lens vignetting, subtle bloom on highlights, accurate documentary color science. Smooth steady dolly motion at slow walking pace in BOTH shots. Style: documentary architectural cinematography, reportage realism, National Geographic / Architectural Digest visual language. Both shots must read as real cinema camera dollies captured on location. Total duration: 6–8 seconds, with a single hard cut at the midpoint between the two shots. Negative: no people, no humans, no figures, no faces, no hands, no shadows of people, all chairs unoccupied, no camera shake, no zoom, no rotation, no pan, no tilt, no handheld motion, no CG look, no 3D render look, no AI artifacts, no morphing, no flickering, no warping geometry, no shifting architecture, no changing materials between frames, no transition effects other than the hard cut.
