## 原文 (source)

LOCATION PROMPT:

Dense urban street, daytime. Tall white residential tower blocks with 
large bold vertical lines red painted across their facades — massive 
 red panels covering entire sections of each 
building, symmetric and graphic. Buildings rise 20+ floors. Dense 
cluster of mid-rise commercial buildings at street level — white painted 
facades , shop awnings, overhead cables and power 
lines crossing between buildings at multiple levels. Street wide and busy 
— cars, taxis, pedestrians on sidewalks. Trees along the central median. 
Atmospheric haze in the distance between buildings.

FOREGROUND: a standalone telephone booth on the sidewalk — classic 
rectangular glass and metal frame, door slightly ajar. Inside: a telephone 
handset hanging on its cord, swinging slightly. The booth positioned 
LEFT-OF-CENTER at the edge of the sidewalk, the dense urban street 
stretching behind it.

No Chinese characters, no hieroglyphs, no Asian script anywhere — 
on buildings, signs, storefronts, or any surface. All signage either 
blank, abstract, or in Latin characters only. Strictly no text in any 
Asian language anywhere in the frame.

Photorealistic. Cinematic. 8K. Natural daylight — overcast sky, grey sky. Physical 
cine lens — corner vignette, slight chromatic aberration. No CGI feel.

## 中文对照 (zh)

LOCATION PROMPT（场景提示）：

密集的城市街道，白天。高大洁白的住宅板楼，外立面上绘有粗大醒目的红色竖条——巨大的红色色块覆盖每栋建筑的整段立面，对称且极具图形感。楼高 20+ 层。街道层面是一簇密集的中高层商业建筑——白色粉刷立面、店铺遮阳篷，多层级的架空电缆与输电线横跨于楼间。街道宽阔繁忙——汽车、计程车、人行道上的行人。中央绿化带沿线有树木。楼间远处有大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。

FOREGROUND（前景）：一独立电话亭位于人行道上——经典长方形玻璃金属框架，门微开。内里：一部电话听筒挂在话线上，微微晃动。电话亭位于画面左侧偏中（LEFT-OF-CENTER）、人行道边缘，身后延伸着密集的城市街道。

全画面任何地方不得出现中文、象形文字或任何亚洲文字——无论建筑、招牌、店面前还是任何表面。所有标识要么留白、抽象，要么仅用拉丁字母。画面内严格不得出现任何亚洲语言文字。

写实摄影。电影感。8K。自然日光——阴天，灰蒙蒙的天空。实体电影镜头——边角暗角（vignette），轻微色差（chromatic aberration）。无 CGI 感。
