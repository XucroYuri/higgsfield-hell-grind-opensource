## 原文 (source)

LOCATION PROMPT:

Urban embankment, daytime. Wide pedestrian promenade along a narrow 
river — stone or concrete paving, low railing at the water's edge. 
The river is small and dark, running through the city. In the 
BACKGROUND: a bridge spanning the river — solid concrete urban 
bridge, cars crossing it. Beyond the bridge: tall white residential 
tower blocks with bold vertical red stripe accents running the full 
height of each building — clean geometric vertical lines of red 
against white facades, rising 20+ floors. Dense overhead cables 
and power lines crossing between buildings. Atmospheric haze in 
the distance.

FOREGROUND: a standalone telephone booth positioned directly against 
the embankment wall — flush against it, classic rectangular glass 
and metal frame, door slightly ajar. Inside: a telephone handset 
hanging on its cord, swinging slightly. The booth LEFT-OF-CENTER. 
River visible behind it, bridge in the middle distance, tower 
blocks on the horizon.

No Chinese characters, no hieroglyphs, no Asian script anywhere — 
on buildings, signs, or any surface. All signage either blank, 
abstract, or in Latin characters only. Strictly no text in any 
Asian language anywhere in the frame.

Photorealistic. Cinematic. 8K. Overcast sky — heavy grey clouds, 
no direct sunlight, flat diffused ambient light. No shadows. 
Physical cine lens — corner vignette, slight chromatic aberration. 
No CGI feel.

## 中文对照 (zh)

LOCATION PROMPT（场景提示）：

城市堤岸，白天。沿着一小段河流的宽阔步行道（promenade）——石质或混凝土铺装，水边有一道低栏杆。河水小且暗沉，穿城而过。BACKGROUND（背景）中：一座横跨河面的桥梁——坚固的混凝土城市桥梁，有车辆驶过。桥梁更远处：高大洁白的住宅板楼，饰有贯穿各楼全高的粗重竖直红色条带——白色立面上干净利落的几何竖直线条，楼高 20+ 层。楼间密布横跨的架空电缆与输电线。远处有大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。

FOREGROUND（前景）：一独立电话亭直接贴着堤岸墙面摆放——与墙面齐平，经典长方形玻璃金属框架，门微开。内里：一部电话听筒挂在话线上，微微晃动。电话亭位于画面左侧偏中（LEFT-OF-CENTER）。身后可见河流，中景为桥梁，地平线上为高耸板楼。

全画面任何地方不得出现中文、象形文字或任何亚洲文字——无论建筑、招牌还是任何表面。所有标识要么留白、抽象，要么仅用拉丁字母。画面内严格不得出现任何亚洲语言文字。

写实摄影。电影感。8K。阴天——厚重灰云，无直射阳光，平整柔和的漫射环境光。无阴影。实体电影镜头——边角暗角（vignette），轻微色差（chromatic aberration）。无 CGI 感。
