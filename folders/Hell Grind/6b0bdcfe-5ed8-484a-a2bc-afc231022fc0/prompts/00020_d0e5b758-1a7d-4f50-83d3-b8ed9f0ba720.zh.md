## 原文 (source)

LOCATION PROMPT:

Narrow urban alley, daytime. Tight passage between buildings — 
cracked concrete or stone pavement underfoot, walls of old 
residential buildings on both sides, overhead cables strung 
between them. The alley opens at the far end onto a pedestrian 
riverside walkway — a narrow promenade running alongside a small 
dark river. The river visible in the BACKGROUND through the alley 
opening — calm, dark water, stone embankment on both sides. 
Pedestrians walking along the riverside path in the distance. 
Beyond the river: tall white residential tower blocks with bold 
vertical red stripe accents running the full height of each 
building — clean geometric vertical lines of red against white 
facades, rising 20+ floors. Dense overhead cables and power lines 
crossing between buildings above.

FOREGROUND: a standalone telephone booth positioned against the 
alley wall — flush against it, classic rectangular glass and metal 
frame, door slightly ajar. Inside: a telephone handset hanging on 
its cord, swinging slightly. The booth LEFT-OF-CENTER. The alley 
stretches behind it toward the river and walkway visible at the 
far end.

No Chinese characters, no hieroglyphs, no Asian script anywhere — 
on buildings, signs, or any surface. All signage either blank, 
abstract, or in Latin characters only. Strictly no text in any 
Asian language anywhere in the frame.

Photorealistic. Cinematic. 8K. Overcast sky — heavy grey clouds, 
no direct sunlight, flat diffused ambient light. No shadows. 
Physical cine lens — corner vignette, slight chromatic aberration. 
No CGI feel.
