## 原文 (source)

LOCATION PROMPT:

Dense urban street, daytime. Tall white residential tower blocks with 
large bold, red vertical lines painted across their facades — massive 
diagonal and rectangular red panels covering entire sections of each 
building, asymmetric and graphic. Buildings rise 20+ floors. Dense 
cluster of mid-rise commercial buildings at street level — white painted 
facades with red signage boards, shop awnings, overhead cables and power 
lines crossing between buildings at multiple levels. Street wide and busy 
— cars, taxis, pedestrians on sidewalks. Trees along the central median. 
Atmospheric haze in the distance between buildings.

FOREGROUND: a standalone telephone booth positioned directly against 
the building wall on the sidewalk — flush against the wall, classic 
rectangular glass and metal frame, door slightly ajar. Inside: a 
telephone handset hanging on its cord, swinging slightly. The booth 
LEFT-OF-CENTER, the dense urban street stretching behind it.

No Chinese characters, no hieroglyphs, no Asian script anywhere — 
on buildings, signs, storefronts, or any surface. All signage either 
blank, abstract, or in Latin characters only. Strictly no text in any 
Asian language anywhere in the frame.

Photorealistic. Cinematic. 8K. Natural daylight — clear sky, strong 
directional sunlight from above-right casting hard shadows. Physical 
cine lens — corner vignette, slight chromatic aberration. No CGI feel.

## 中文对照 (zh)

LOCATION PROMPT（场景提示）：

密集的城市街道，白天。高大洁白的住宅板楼，外立面上绘有粗大醒目的红色竖线——巨大的斜向与矩形红色色块覆盖每栋建筑的整段立面，不对称且极具图形感。楼高 20+ 层。街道层面是一簇密集的中高层商业建筑——白色粉刷立面配红色招牌板、店铺遮阳篷，多层级的架空电缆与输电线横跨于楼间。街道宽阔繁忙——汽车、计程车、人行道上的行人。中央绿化带沿线有树木。楼间远处有大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。

FOREGROUND（前景）：一独立电话亭直接贴着人行道上的建筑墙面摆放——与墙面齐平，经典长方形玻璃金属框架，门微开。内里：一部电话听筒挂在话线上，微微晃动。电话亭位于画面左侧偏中（LEFT-OF-CENTER），身后延伸着密集的城市街道。

全画面任何地方不得出现中文、象形文字或任何亚洲文字——无论建筑、招牌、店面前还是任何表面。所有标识要么留白、抽象，要么仅用拉丁字母。画面内严格不得出现任何亚洲语言文字。

写实摄影。电影感。8K。自然日光——晴空，右上方的强方向性阳光投下生硬阴影。实体电影镜头——边角暗角（vignette），轻微色差（chromatic aberration）。无 CGI 感。
