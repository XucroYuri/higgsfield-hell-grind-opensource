## 原文 (source)

LOCATION PROMPT:

Under a large urban bridge, daytime. The bridge structure overhead — 
massive concrete support columns, beams, and the underside of the 
bridge deck visible above. Below: dark water — a river or canal 
running beneath the bridge, surface choppy and grey. Tall white 
residential tower blocks visible on both riverbanks — bold vertical 
red stripe accents running the full height of each building, clean 
geometric vertical lines of red against white facades. Buildings 
rise 20+ floors on both sides. Dense overhead cables and power lines 
crossing between structures. Wet concrete embankment or narrow 
walkway alongside the water's edge.

FOREGROUND: a standalone telephone booth positioned directly against 
one of the bridge's concrete support columns — flush against it, 
classic rectangular glass and metal frame, door slightly ajar. 
Inside: a telephone handset hanging on its cord, swinging slightly. 
The booth LEFT-OF-CENTER, the water and bridge structure stretching 
behind it.

No Chinese characters, no hieroglyphs, no Asian script anywhere — 
on buildings, signs, or any surface. All signage either blank, 
abstract, or in Latin characters only. Strictly no text in any 
Asian language anywhere in the frame.

Photorealistic. Cinematic. 8K. Overcast sky — heavy grey clouds, 
no direct sunlight, flat diffused ambient light. No shadows. 
Physical cine lens — corner vignette, slight chromatic aberration. 
No CGI feel.

## 中文对照 (zh)

LOCATION PROMPT（场景提示）：

大型城市桥梁之下，白天。上方为桥梁结构——可见巨大的混凝土桥墩、梁体以及桥面底部。下方：暗色水体——一条从桥下流过的河流或运河，水面起伏、呈灰色。两岸可见高大洁白的住宅板楼——贯穿各楼全高的粗重竖直红色条带，白色立面上干净利落的几何竖直线条。两侧楼房均高 20+ 层。结构之间密布横跨的架空电缆与输电线。沿水边是湿漉漉的混凝土堤岸或窄步道。

FOREGROUND（前景）：一独立电话亭直接贴着某一座混凝土桥墩摆放——与桥墩齐平，经典长方形玻璃金属框架，门微开。内里：一部电话听筒挂在话线上，微微晃动。电话亭位于画面左侧偏中（LEFT-OF-CENTER），身后延伸着水体与桥梁结构。

全画面任何地方不得出现中文、象形文字或任何亚洲文字——无论建筑、招牌还是任何表面。所有标识要么留白、抽象，要么仅用拉丁字母。画面内严格不得出现任何亚洲语言文字。

写实摄影。电影感。8K。阴天——厚重灰云，无直射阳光，平整柔和的漫射环境光。无阴影。实体电影镜头——边角暗角（vignette），轻微色差（chromatic aberration）。无 CGI 感。
