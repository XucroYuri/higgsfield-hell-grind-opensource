<<<61e5ffdd-7613-43f5-831f-002c71a5789c>>> Snow-covered Japanese plain at the foot of Mt Fuji. Heavy overcast sky. Dense pale-grey fog swallows the horizon. Heavy snowfall throughout — thick visible flakes fill the frame. Foreground: dry susuki grass tufts with puffy beige seed-heads, small crimson-leaf shrubs poking through snowdrifts, mossy grey rock outcrops, scattered fallen red leaves.

A dense ring of 300 surrounds a central position — black-lacquered samurai armor, iron-masked faces all angled inward. Snow rims their helmets and shoulders.

MEDIUM SHOT — camera at standing height looking across the wall of iron masks. The camera DOLLIES IN aggressively, moving 3 to 4 meters forward through the crowd. At the exact moment the dolly begins, the front rank of samurai BREAKS — a wave shattering. All 300 EXPLODE into a full sprint simultaneously, silent. Heavy snow kicks up from their running feet in dense white bursts. Iron masks angle forward, all bodies in motion toward a central unseen target ahead of camera. The dolly push-in collides with the incoming sprint — the crowd presses toward the lens as the lens presses into the crowd. At the rear of the mass, the lone gnarled Japanese tree is barely visible as a ghostly shape through the fog, Mt Fuji a faint cone silhouette behind it.

No Roco. No Monster. Only the wave of armored bodies and the roar of snow beneath running feet. Soft flat overcast light. Cold grey-blue palette. Photorealistic. 24fps. 16:9.
