## 原文 (source)

Cinematic four-part sequence with hard cuts between parts.

PART ONE (5s): Extreme close-up macro of a lean man's left hand, framed center-left, surrounding edges in deep shadow void. The left hand is bare with no glove and no bandana, pale weathered skin with subtle vein detail. Hand starts open palm-down, fingers spread. Faceted deep crimson-red translucent crystal grows organically across the skin from wrist outward, forming layered armor plates over knuckles and fingers — the crystal emerges seamlessly through the skin without breaking it, no blood, no wounds, no torn skin, like the crystal is naturally manifesting through the flesh as a clean transformation. By the end of the growth, between the four knuckles, four short crystal blades push up cleanly each five centimeters long, sharp, faceted, deep crimson-red, like Wolverine claws but shorter and crystalline. Hand clenches into a fist by the end, four short blades extended. Single hard top-light, deep cold shadow void around. Camera near-static, tiny push-in, subtle handheld drift.

HARD CUT.

PART ONE-B (2s): Tighter extreme close-up macro on just the back of the closed fist showing only the four short crystal blades extending upward between the knuckles. The blades dominate the frame as four faceted deep crimson-red translucent shards catching cold directional light, refracting subtle prismatic highlights through their facets. Skin around the blades clean, no blood, no damage. Camera holds completely still on the blades. Single hard top-light. Subtle handheld drift.

HARD CUT.

PART TWO (4s): Medium-wide of the same man, waist up, centered. Stained black t-shirt, dark tactical cargo pants. Right hand grips a long curved bordeaux-and-dark-steel blade pointed downward, a thick crimson-red bandana knotted around the right wrist over a black tactical fingerless glove. Left arm with crystallized hand and four short knuckle blades drawing back behind his shoulder in a coiled throwing motion. Wet dark hair across forehead, dark circles, thin moustache, light stubble, fresh pink cut on right cheek. Around him at varying distances three or four ghost samurai in weathered lamellar steel armor with patina, suji-kabuto helmets, bare exposed skulls instead of faces, deep crimson-red light glowing softly from inside their eye sockets like embers, all turned toward him, motionless. Time freezes: blizzard snow particles hang suspended in mid-air, only the man and his coiling left arm move in real time. Slow lateral thirty-degree arc tracking with handheld drift.

HARD CUT.

PART THREE (3s): Ultra-low-angle tracking parallel to a single deep crimson-red faceted crystal blade flying through the air at high speed across the snowy moor. Blade five centimeters long, sharp, spinning slightly, refracting cold light. Behind the blade a thin tapering wake of fine red crystal dust. Camera holds the blade steady in the right third of frame while the snowy battlefield streaks past in heavy motion blur. Ahead in the left third a single ghost samurai with skull face and crimson-red glowing eye sockets comes into focus, the blade heading toward his head. High-speed lateral tracking with motion blur and handheld drift.

HARD CUT.

PART FOUR (5s): Medium shot of three or four ghost samurai scattered across the snowy moor, each pierced by a single deep crimson-red crystal blade in skull, throat, or chest. Foreground lower-left third: dry crimson-red leaves around a snow-covered grey rock. Each samurai at a different stage of dissolution, simultaneously: closest one (right third) just beginning to dissolve, armor and bones streaming downward as ribbons of grey ash dust, eye sockets dimming. Mid-distance samurai already half-dissolved, lower body converted to rising columns of grey ash drifting upward in slow motion, upper torso still holding shape. Deeper background samurai a roughly humanoid cloud of swirling grey ash, holding silhouette before dispersing. Furthest samurai fully dispersed, only his katana, suji-kabuto helmet, and lamellar pieces remain on the snow as evidence. Grey ash settles onto the white snow as dark particles. Camera slow pull-back about one meter with handheld drift.

ALL PARTS SHARE: dense cold blizzard with horizontal blowing snow at moderate-to-strong intensity (except Part Two where snow freezes mid-air). Cold pale grey-blue overcast atmospheric light, no sun, no horizon. Low-key chiaroscuro. Palette: cold pale blue-white snow, grey weathered rocks, bone-grey ash, dark weathered armor; deep crimson-red on the crystal armor, knuckle blades, flying blade, embedded blades, dimming samurai eye sockets, foreground leaves, and the bandana on the right wrist in Part Two — all in the same red color family. Shot on Alexa Mini LF, spherical lenses (100mm macro for Part One and One-B, 50mm for Part Two, 65mm for Part Three, 50mm for Part Four), shallow depth of field, heavy 35mm film grain, ultra-cinematic widescreen, photorealistic VFX-quality. No music, no soundtrack. Ambient sound: layered low rumble of crystal growth and glassy crystal chime as the crystal forms in Part One, no skin tearing or bone crunch sounds; pure glassy chime as the camera holds on the blades in Part One-B; sudden silence with one slow heartbeat and clothing rustle in Part Two; sharp high-pitched whistle of cutting air in Part Three; four layered low resonant exhale-like sounds at different tonalities as each samurai disperses in Part Four; howling wind underneath all parts.

Style references: Roger Deakins macro precision, Cronenberg organic transformation but clean and bloodless, Zack Snyder bullet-time and projectile tracking, Andrei Tarkovsky poetic dissolution, Denis Villeneuve cold beauty.

Total duration: 19 seconds (5+2+4+3+5).

## 中文对照 (zh)

电影感四段式序列，段与段之间用硬切衔接。

PART ONE（5 秒）：一位清瘦男子左手的极特写微距，构图居中偏左，周围边缘陷入深邃阴影虚空。左手无手套、无头巾，裸露皮肤苍白饱经风霜，带有细微血管纹理。手起初掌心向下摊开，五指张开。多面体深绯红色半透明水晶从手腕向外在皮肤上有机地生长，在指节和手指上形成层叠的铠甲片——水晶无缝地穿过皮肤冒出而不撕裂它，无血、无伤口、无撕裂的皮肤，仿佛水晶作为一种洁净的转化天然地透过血肉显化。生长结束时，在四个指节之间，四枚短晶刃干净地向上翘出，每枚五厘米长、锋利、多面、深绯红色，像 Wolverine 的利爪但要更短且是水晶质。手在结束时攥成拳头，四枚短刃伸出。单一硬顶光，四周深邃冷色阴影虚空。机位近乎静止，极轻微推近，细微手持漂移。

HARD CUT（硬切）。

PART ONE-B（2 秒）：更贴近的极特写微距，只对准闭合拳头的手背，仅展示四枚短晶刃在指节间向上延伸。刀刃占据画面，成为四枚多面深绯红色半透明晶片，承接冷向光，透过各切割面折射出细微棱镜高光。刀刃周围皮肤洁净，无血、无损伤。机位在刀刃上完全静止。单一硬顶光。细微手持漂移。

HARD CUT（硬切）。

PART TWO（4 秒）：同一男子的中全景，腰部以上，居中。染污的黑色 T 恤、深色战术工装裤。右手握着一柄弯曲的波尔多红与深钢色长剑，剑尖朝下，一条厚实的绯红头巾（bandana）系在右手腕上，压在黑色战术露指手套之上。左臂带着结晶的手和四枚短指节刃，向后拉过肩头呈盘绕投掷姿态。湿漉漉的深色头发搭在前额，黑眼圈，稀疏胡须，轻浅的胡茬，右颊一道新割的粉色伤口。他周围不同距离处有三到四名鬼武士（Ghost Samurai），身着带铜绿的饱经风霜层状钢甲（lamellar），戴尖顶兜（suji-kabuto），以裸露骷髅为面，深绯红光从他们眼窝内如余烬般柔和地亮起，全都转向他，一动不动。时间凝滞：暴风雪雪粒悬停空中，只有男子和他盘绕的左臂实时移动。缓慢横向三十度弧线跟拍，带手持漂移。

HARD CUT（硬切）。

PART THREE（3 秒）：超低角度跟拍，与一枚深绯红色多面晶刃平行，晶刃高速穿越雪原在空中飞行。刀刃五厘米长、锋利、微微旋转，折射冷光。刀刃身后拖着一条逐渐变细的细红晶尘尾迹。机位将刀刃稳持在画面右三分之一处，雪原战场在身后以重重运动模糊飞掠。前方左三分之一处，一名骷髅脸、绯红发光眼窝的鬼武士进入对焦，刀刃正朝他头部袭来。高速横向跟拍，带运动模糊与手持漂移。

HARD CUT（硬切）。

PART FOUR（5 秒）：三名或四名鬼武士散布于雪原的中景，每名都被一枚深绯红色晶刃刺中头骨、喉咙或胸膛。前景左下三分之一：一簇干枯绯红叶片围着一块覆雪灰色岩石。每名武士同时处于不同的化解阶段：最近的一名（右三分之一）刚开始化解，铠甲与骨骼化为灰色灰烬丝带向下流淌，眼窝逐渐黯淡。中距武士已化解一半，下半身化为缓慢上升的灰色灰烬柱向上飘浮，上半身仍保持形状。更远处背景武士是盘旋灰色灰烬形成的近似人形云团，在消散前保持剪影。最远一名武士已完全化解，雪上只留下他的 katana、suji-kabuto 头盔和层状甲片作为遗迹。灰色灰烬以深色颗粒落回白雪上。机位缓慢拉远约一米，带手持漂移。

ALL PARTS SHARE（各段共用）：浓密冷暴风雪，水平飞舞的雪以中到强度吹扫（PART TWO 除外，那里雪在空中冻结）。冷色调苍白灰蓝阴云大气光，无太阳，无地平线。低调明暗对比（low-key chiaroscuro）。调色：冷调青白风雪、灰色风化岩、骨灰灰色、深色风化铠甲；水晶甲、指节刃、飞刃、镶嵌刃、渐暗的武士眼窝、前景叶片、以及 PART TWO 里右腕头巾上的深绯红色——全属同一红色系。用 Alexa Mini LF 拍摄，球面镜头（PART ONE 与 ONE-B 用 100mm 微距，PART TWO 用 50mm，PART THREE 用 65mm，PART FOUR 用 50mm），浅景深，厚重 35mm 胶片颗粒，超电影感宽银幕，照片级写实 VFX 质感。无音乐、无配乐。环境声：PART ONE 水晶成形时，水晶生长低沉的叠加轰鸣与玻璃质水晶清鸣，无皮肤撕裂或骨骼碎裂声；PART ONE-B 机位定格刀刃时只有纯净玻璃质清鸣；PART TWO 突然静默，伴随一声缓慢心跳与衣物窸窣；PART THREE 一声尖锐高亢的破空哨音；PART FOUR 每名武士消散时四道不同音调的低沉共鸣吐息般声音；所有段落下方持续呼啸的风声。
