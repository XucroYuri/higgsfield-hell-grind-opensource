Cinematic four-part sequence with hard cuts between parts.

PART ONE (5s): Extreme close-up macro of a lean man's left hand, framed center-left, surrounding edges in deep shadow void. The left hand is bare with no glove and no bandana, pale weathered skin with subtle vein detail. Hand starts open palm-down, fingers spread. Faceted deep crimson-red translucent crystal grows organically across the skin from wrist outward, forming layered armor plates over knuckles and fingers — the crystal emerges seamlessly through the skin without breaking it, no blood, no wounds, no torn skin, like the crystal is naturally manifesting through the flesh as a clean transformation. By the end of the growth, between the four knuckles, four short stubby crystal spikes push up cleanly each only about three centimeters long roughly the length of a finger joint, sharp pointed, faceted, deep crimson-red, like short crystalline claws not blades, compact and stubby not long. Hand clenches into a fist by the end, four short stubby spikes extended barely longer than the knuckles themselves. Single hard top-light, deep cold shadow void around. Camera near-static, tiny push-in, subtle handheld drift.

HARD CUT.

PART ONE-B (2s): Tighter extreme close-up macro on just the back of the closed fist showing only the four short stubby crystal spikes extending upward between the knuckles. The spikes dominate the frame as four faceted deep crimson-red translucent shards each only about three centimeters long, compact, stubby, sharp at the tips, catching cold directional light, refracting subtle prismatic highlights through their facets. Skin around the spikes clean, no blood, no damage. Camera holds completely still on the spikes. Single hard top-light. Subtle handheld drift.

HARD CUT.

PART TWO (4s): Medium-wide of the same man, waist up, centered. Stained black t-shirt, dark tactical cargo pants. Right hand grips a long curved bordeaux-and-dark-steel blade pointed downward, a thick crimson-red bandana knotted around the right wrist over a black tactical fingerless glove. Left arm with crystallized hand and four short stubby knuckle spikes drawing back behind his shoulder in a coiled throwing motion. Wet dark hair across forehead, dark circles, thin moustache, light stubble, fresh pink cut on right cheek. Time freezes: blizzard snow particles hang suspended in mid-air, only the man and his coiling left arm move in real time. Slow lateral thirty-degree arc tracking with handheld drift.

PART THREE (3s): Ultra-low-angle tracking parallel to a single short stubby deep crimson-red faceted crystal spike flying through the air across a short distance of snowy moor. Spike only about three centimeters long, sharp pointed, spinning slightly, refracting cold light. Behind the spike a thin tapering wake of fine red crystal dust. Camera holds the spike steady in the right third of frame while the snowy ground streaks past in heavy motion blur for only a brief flight covering a short close range. Ahead in the left third a single ghost samurai with skull face and crimson-red glowing eye sockets is already very close in immediate striking distance about five to seven meters from the spike's origin point, the spike heading toward his head and reaching him quickly within the duration of the shot. High-speed lateral tracking with motion blur and handheld drift.

HARD CUT.

PART FOUR (5s): Medium shot of three or four ghost samurai pressed in close around the area at five to ten meter distance from the original throwing point, each pierced by a single short stubby deep crimson-red crystal spike in skull, throat, or chest. Foreground lower-left third: dry crimson-red leaves around a snow-covered grey rock. Each samurai at a different stage of dissolution, simultaneously: closest one (right third) just beginning to dissolve, armor and bones streaming downward as ribbons of grey ash dust, eye sockets dimming. Mid-range samurai already half-dissolved, lower body converted to rising columns of grey ash drifting upward in slow motion, upper torso still holding shape. Slightly further samurai a roughly humanoid cloud of swirling grey ash, holding silhouette before dispersing. Furthest samurai (still within close range) fully dispersed, only his katana, suji-kabuto helmet, and lamellar pieces remain on the snow as evidence. Grey ash settles onto the white snow as dark particles. Camera slow pull-back about one meter with handheld drift.

ALL PARTS SHARE: dense cold blizzard with horizontal blowing snow at moderate-to-strong intensity (except Part Two where snow freezes mid-air). Cold pale grey-blue overcast atmospheric light, no sun, no horizon. Low-key chiaroscuro. Palette: cold pale blue-white snow, grey weathered rocks, bone-grey ash, dark weathered armor; deep crimson-red on the crystal armor, knuckle spikes, flying spike, embedded spikes, dimming samurai eye sockets, foreground leaves, and the bandana on the right wrist in Part Two — all in the same red color family. Ambient sound: layered low rumble of crystal growth and glassy crystal chime as the crystal forms in Part One, no skin tearing or bone crunch sounds; pure glassy chime as the camera holds on the spikes in Part One-B; sudden silence with one slow heartbeat and clothing rustle in Part Two; sharp high-pitched whistle of cutting air in Part Three; four layered low resonant exhale-like sounds at different tonalities as each samurai disperses in Part Four; howling wind underneath all parts.

电影感四段式序列，段与段之间用硬切衔接。

PART ONE（5 秒）：一位清瘦男子左手的极特写微距，构图居中偏左，周围边缘陷入深邃阴影虚空。左手无手套、无头巾，裸露皮肤苍白饱经风霜，带有细微血管纹理。手起初掌心向下摊开，五指张开。多面体深绯红色半透明水晶从手腕向外在皮肤上有机地生长，在指节和手指上形成层叠的铠甲片——水晶无缝地穿过皮肤冒出而不撕裂它，无血、无伤口、无撕裂的皮肤，仿佛水晶作为一种洁净的转化天然地透过血肉显化。生长结束时，在四个指节之间，四枚短钝的晶刺干净地向上翘出，每枚仅约三厘米长、约等于一个手指节长度，尖端尖锐、多面、深绯红色，像短促的水晶利爪而非刀刃，紧凑短钝而不长。手在结束时攥成拳头，四枚短钝的晶刺伸出，仅比指节本身略长。单一硬顶光，四周深邃冷色阴影虚空。机位近乎静止，极轻微推近，细微手持漂移。

HARD CUT（硬切）。

PART ONE-B（2 秒）：更贴近的极特写微距，只对准闭合拳头的手背，仅展示四枚短钝晶刺在指节间向上延伸。尖刺占据画面，成为四枚多面深绯红色半透明晶片，每枚仅约三厘米长、紧凑、短钝、尖端锋利，承接冷向光，透过各切割面折射出细微棱镜高光。尖刺周围皮肤洁净，无血、无损伤。机位在尖刺上完全静止。单一硬顶光。细微手持漂移。

HARD CUT（硬切）。

PART TWO（4 秒）：同一男子的中全景，腰部以上，居中。染污的黑色 T 恤、深色战术工装裤。右手握着一柄弯曲的波尔多红与深钢色长剑，剑尖朝下，一条厚实的绯红头巾（bandana）系在右手腕上，压在黑色战术露指手套之上。左臂带着结晶的手和四枚短钝指节刺，向后拉过肩头呈盘绕投掷姿态。湿漉漉的深色头发搭在前额，黑眼圈，稀疏胡须，轻浅的胡茬，右颊一道新割的粉色伤口。他周围在五到十米距离处紧贴着三到四名鬼武士（Ghost Samurai），身着带铜绿的饱经风霜层状钢甲（lamellar），戴尖顶兜（suji-kabuto），以裸露骷髅为面，深绯红光从他们眼窝内如余烬般柔和地亮起，全都转向他，一动不动，在画面中处于近前景深度清晰可辨而非远处。时间凝滞：暴风雪雪粒悬停空中，只有男子和他盘绕的左臂实时移动。缓慢横向三十度弧线跟拍，带手持漂移。

HARD CUT（硬切）。

PART THREE（3 秒）：超低角度跟拍，与一枚短钝的深绯红色多面晶刺平行，尖刺穿越雪原短暂距离在空中飞行。尖刺仅约三厘米长、尖端尖锐、微微旋转，折射冷光。尖刺身后拖着一条逐渐变细的细红晶尘尾迹。机位将尖刺稳持在画面右三分之一处，雪原地面在身后以重重运动模糊飞掠，仅为覆盖短近距离的一次短暂飞行。前方左三分之一处，一名骷髅脸、绯红发光眼窝的鬼武士已经非常近，处于即时打击距离，距尖刺原点约五到七米，尖刺朝他头部袭来，并在本镜时长内迅速抵达。高速横向跟拍，带运动模糊与手持漂移。

HARD CUT（硬切）。

PART FOUR（5 秒）：三名或四名鬼武士紧贴地围绕在原投掷点五到十米距离处的中景，每名都被一枚短钝深绯红色晶刺刺中头骨、喉咙或胸膛。前景左下三分之一：一簇干枯绯红叶片围着一块覆雪灰色岩石。每名武士同时处于不同的化解阶段：最近的一名（右三分之一）刚开始化解，铠甲与骨骼化为灰色灰烬丝带向下流淌，眼窝逐渐黯淡。中距武士已化解一半，下半身化为缓慢上升的灰色灰烬柱向上飘浮，上半身仍保持形状。稍远一点的武士是盘旋灰色灰烬形成的近似人形云团，在消散前保持剪影。最远一名武士（仍在近距离范围内）已完全化解，雪上只留下他的 katana、suji-kabuto 头盔和层状甲片作为遗迹。灰色灰烬以深色颗粒落回白雪上。机位缓慢拉远约一米，带手持漂移。
