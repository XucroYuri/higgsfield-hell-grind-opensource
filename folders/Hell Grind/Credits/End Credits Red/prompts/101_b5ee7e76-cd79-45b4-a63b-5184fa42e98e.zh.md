<<<image_1>>> Style Reference — oil painting battle scene] <<<image_2>>> Red stone artifact design reference] Animated oil painting on glass — each frame is a NEWLY PAINTED frame — the entire canvas repainted from scratch every frame — brushstrokes appear, disappear, reshape between frames — edges boil and crawl — surfaces ripple with living paint. Strictly fully oil-painted style — no photographic feel — no smooth digital rendering — no realistic textures — thoroughly painted handmade — every facet is a visible brushstroke — edges loose and painterly with no clean outlines. Thick impasto. Deep crimson, vermillion, dark carmine, burnt sienna, and black palette over near-black undertone. 12fps throughout. Slow motion — solemn and visceral. One continuous unbroken 15-second shot — no cuts. Camera rotating continuously — no zoom-ins.

An abstract human heart floats at frame center in pure black oil-painted void — no environment — absolute darkness. The heart is NOT anatomically detailed — NOT photorealistic — an impressionistic painted suggestion of a heart — a few bold impasto masses of crimson, carmine, and deep maroon implying the general shape — no fine detail, no veins, no arteries — abstract, symbolic — ridged wet paint, not flesh. It glows from within with deep warm red painted light — crimson light-beams radiate into the surrounding darkness. Red ember-like painted particles drift off the heart's surface — smoke-like impasto wisps trail from its edges into the void. Foreground bokeh brushstrokes float soft and out-of-focus between the camera and the heart — adding cinematic depth.

The heart subtly pulses twice — not dramatic contractions but gentle breathing swells — the impasto surface barely shifts, a soft compression and release — two faint waves of red light ripple outward — the camera keeps rotating through both pulses without pausing — the beats feel like something felt rather than seen.

Camera rotates slowly around the heart — the impasto ridges catch the red self-light differently from each angle. Eastern-style crimson cloud-brushstrokes sweep through the void — broad curling strokes of deep crimson and dark carmine spiraling like traditional cloud scrolls — flickering and reshaping frame to frame.

The heart begins to dissolve — the impasto brushstrokes detach from its form — peeling away — drifting free into the void — the abstract shape melts down into a swirling cloud of loose red brushstrokes. The dissolution feels inevitable — organic mass losing its hold — weight shifting from soft to weightless.

The loose brushstrokes reassemble — pulled inward — rebuilding into a NEW FORM — the red stone artifact from the reference. Stroke by stroke the artifact takes shape — crimson deepens to dark red stone — the soft wet-paint texture calcifies into dense dry impasto that reads as carved stone — the artifact's shape emerges from the reference — still all painted, all impasto — but now describing stone instead of flesh. The transition carries a shift in weight — the artifact lands into its form with a sense of mass and permanence the heart never had.

The artifact fully forms — deep red stone in heavy impasto — glowing steady from within — the light is constant, mineral, ancient. The Eastern cloud-brushstrokes catch the red light and spiral around the completed artifact. Camera continues rotating — the artifact slowly turning — every impasto ridge catching red self-light. The heart has become stone.

Then — a single amber-red painted streak shoots out from the artifact — a long impasto brushstroke trailing light — streaking across the black void. Camera chases the amber red streak — hard side-slide — surging upward. The streak multiplies — more oil brushstrokes appear in mirrored symmetry around it — forming a tunnel of amber red and dark impasto symmetrical brushstrokes curving into the void. Camera plunges into this symmetrical brushstroke tunnel — as if passing through an oil-painted wormhole — vortex brushstroke walls flying past the lens — the tunnel deepens.

12fps throughout. Oil painting style. Thick impasto. Camera rotating continuously. No zoom-ins. No photorealism. Abstract — pure black void. 15s.

## 中文对照 (zh)

<<<image_1>>> 风格参考——油画战斗场景] <<<image_2>>> 红色石质法器设计参考] 玻璃板上动画化色的油画——每一帧都是「新绘制的」帧——整个画布每一帧都从头重绘——笔触在帧与帧之间出现、消失、重塑——边缘翻涌（boil）并爬动——表面被活的颜料所涟漪起伏。严格全油画风格——无照片感——无平滑数字渲染——无写实质感——彻底手绘手工感——每一个切面都是可见笔触——边缘松散而具绘画感，无干净轮廓。厚涂（impasto）。深红（crimson）、朱红（vermillion）、暗胭脂红（dark carmine）、焦赭（burnt sienna）与黑色构成的上层色调，铺陈在近黑的底色之上。全程每秒 12 帧。慢动作——肃穆而直击感官。一个连续不间断的 15 秒镜头——无剪辑。镜头持续旋转——无推近（zoom-in）。

一颗抽象的人心悬浮在画面中央，置身于纯黑油画虚空中——无环境——绝对黑暗。这颗心并非解剖学上精雕细刻——并非照片写实——而是一种印象派的绘画化心脏暗示——几团深红、胭脂红与深栗色（deep maroon）的大胆厚涂团块，暗示出大致轮廓——无精细细节、无血管、无动脉——抽象、象征——隆起的湿颜料，而非血肉。它从内部发出深邃暖红的绘画之光——深红光束辐射进周围黑暗。红色余烬般的绘画颗粒从心脏表面飘散——烟缕般的厚涂细丝自其边缘拖入虚空。前景虚化（bokeh）笔触柔软而失焦地漂浮在镜头与心脏之间——增添电影深度。

心脏微妙地搏动两次——不是戏剧性的收缩，而是轻柔的呼吸式膨起——厚涂表面几乎不动，只是一次柔和的压缩与舒张——两道微弱的红光波向外涟漪扩散——镜头在两次搏动中持续旋转而不停顿——这搏动更像是被「感觉」到而非「看见」到的东西。

镜头缓缓绕心脏旋转——厚涂的隆起脊从不同角度以不同的方式捕捉红色自光。东方式深红云状笔触扫过虚空——宽阔翻卷的深红与暗胭脂红笔触，如传统云纹般盘旋——逐帧闪烁、重塑。

心脏开始消解——厚涂笔触自其形体脱落——层层剥离——飘离入虚空——抽象形体融化成一团旋动的松散红色笔触云。这一消解显得不可避免——有机团块正失去维系——重量自柔软转向失重。

松散的笔触重新聚合——向内被牵引——重筑成一个「新的形体」——来自参考图的红色石质法器。笔触一点一点地砌出法器——深红渐深为暗红石质——柔软的湿颜料质地石化（calcifies）成致密干涩的厚涂，读作雕刻的石——法器的形体自参考图中浮现——仍是全绘画、全厚涂——但如今描绘的是石头而非血肉。转场承载着一份重量的转移——法器落入自身形体时带着一种心脏从未有过的质量感与恒久感。

法器完全成型——厚重厚涂中的深红石——内部发出稳定之光——这光恒定、矿物感、古老。东方云状笔触捕捉红光，环绕着成形的法器盘旋。镜头持续旋转——法器缓缓转动——每一道厚涂隆起脊都捕捉着红色自光。心脏已化为石头。

然后——一道琥珀红色的绘画光迹从法器激射而出——一道长长的厚涂笔触拖着光——划过黑色虚空。镜头追猎那道琥珀红光迹——硬性横移——向上猛冲。光迹增殖——更多油画笔触在其周围镜像对称地出现——形成一道琥珀红与暗色厚涂笔触相绕成对的隧道，弯入虚空。镜头一头扎进这道对称笔触隧道之中——仿佛穿过一条油画绘成的虫洞——旋涡状笔触壁从镜头前飞掠而过——隧道不断加深。

全程每秒 12 帧。油画风格。厚涂。镜头持续旋转。无推近。无照片写实。抽象——纯黑虚空。15 秒。
