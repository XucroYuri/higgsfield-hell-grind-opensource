<<<image_1>>> Style Reference — oil painting Red stone artifact design reference.

Animated oil painting on glass — each frame is a NEWLY PAINTED frame — the entire canvas repainted from scratch every frame — brushstrokes appear, disappear, reshape between frames — edges boil and crawl — surfaces ripple with living paint. Strictly fully oil-painted style — no photographic feel — no smooth digital rendering — no realistic textures — thoroughly painted handmade — every facet is a visible brushstroke — edges loose and painterly with no clean outlines. Thick impasto. Deep crimson, vermillion, dark carmine, burnt sienna, and black palette over near-black undertone. 12fps throughout. Slow motion — solemn and visceral. One continuous unbroken 15-second shot — no cuts. Camera rotating continuously — no zoom-ins.

The red stone artifact from the reference floats at frame center in pure black oil-painted void — no environment — absolute darkness. The artifact is rendered in heavy impasto — deep red stone surface built from thick layered brushstrokes matching the reference design — it glows from within with steady warm red painted light — crimson light-beams radiate into the surrounding darkness. Red ember-like painted particles drift off its surface — smoke-like impasto wisps trail from its edges into the void. Foreground bokeh brushstrokes float soft and out-of-focus between the camera and the artifact — adding cinematic depth. The camera never stops — always moving, always rotating around the artifact.

As the camera rotates — painted veins begin to creep across the artifact's stone surface — dark crimson and carmine impasto strokes growing outward from the cracks in the stone — organic, alive — the veins spread slowly like roots or tendrils — thick painted lines of deep red and purple wrapping around the artifact's form — threading through its carved edges — pulsing faintly with a subtle living warmth. The veins multiply — branching and splitting — each vein a single visible impasto brushstroke — they entwine the artifact tighter and tighter — gradually covering the stone surface — the hard mineral form becomes enmeshed in a web of painted organic matter. The camera keeps rotating — revealing the veins from every angle — they reshape and flicker frame to frame.

The veins thicken and spread further — wrapping the artifact so completely that the stone beneath begins to disappear — the entire form softens — the sharp carved edges of the artifact round out under the mass of painted veins — the shape swells slightly — becoming rounder, more organic — the veins envelope the artifact until the whole object vaguely resembles a heart — not a precise anatomical heart but an abstract suggestion — a rough, vein-covered, pulsing painted mass that reads as something between stone and organ — ancient mineral becoming something alive. The camera never stops rotating — Eastern-style crimson cloud-brushstrokes sweep through the void around the vein-wrapped form — broad curling strokes spiraling like traditional cloud scrolls — flickering and reshaping frame to frame.

The vein-wrapped artifact subtly pulses — a faint organic swell — the painted veins contract gently and release — a soft wave of deep red light ripples outward into the black void — the camera keeps rotating through the pulse without pausing. The form glows warmer — the veins throb with quiet painted life — stone and flesh merged into one object — ancient and alive at once.

Then — a single amber-red painted streak shoots out from the artifact — a long impasto brushstroke trailing light — streaking across the black void. Camera chases the amber red streak — hard side-slide — surging upward. The streak multiplies — more oil brushstrokes appear in mirrored symmetry around it — forming a tunnel of amber red and dark impasto symmetrical brushstrokes curving into the void. Camera plunges into this symmetrical brushstroke tunnel — as if passing through an oil-painted wormhole — vortex brushstroke walls flying past the lens — the tunnel deepens.

12fps throughout. Oil painting style. Thick impasto. Camera rotating continuously. No zoom-ins. No photorealism. Abstract — pure black void. 15s.

## 中文对照 (zh)

<<<image_1>>> 风格参考——油画红色石质法器设计参考。
 <<<image_2>>> 红色石质法器设计参考。

玻璃板上动画化色的油画——每一帧都是「新绘制的」帧——整个画布每一帧都从头重绘——笔触在帧与帧之间出现、消失、重塑——边缘翻涌（boil）并爬动——表面被活的颜料所涟漪起伏。严格全油画风格——无照片感——无平滑数字渲染——无写实质感——彻底手绘手工感——每一个切面都是可见笔触——边缘松散而具绘画感，无干净轮廓。厚涂（impasto）。深红（crimson）、朱红（vermillion）、暗胭脂红（dark carmine）、焦赭（burnt sienna）与黑色构成的上层色调，铺陈在近黑的底色之上。全程每秒 12 帧。慢动作——肃穆而直击感官。一个连续不间断的 15 秒镜头——无剪辑。镜头持续旋转——无推近（zoom-in）。

来自参考图的红色石质法器悬浮在画面中央，置身于纯黑油画虚空中——无环境——绝对黑暗。法器以厚重厚涂呈现——深红石质表面由匹配参考设计的厚重层叠笔触构筑——它从内部发出稳定的暖红绘画之光——深红光束辐射进周围黑暗。红色余烬般的绘画颗粒从其表面飘散——烟缕般的厚涂细丝自其边缘拖入虚空。前景虚化（bokeh）笔触柔软而失焦地漂浮在镜头与法器之间——增添电影深度。镜头从不停止——始终移动，始终围绕法器旋转。

随着镜头旋转——绘画的血管开始爬过法器的石质表面——暗红与胭脂红的厚涂笔触自石层裂缝向外生长——有机、鲜活——血管像根须或卷须般缓缓蔓延——深红与紫色的厚重绘画线条缠绕法器形体——穿过其雕刻边缘——以细微的生命暖意隐隐搏动。血管增殖——不断分枝与分裂——每一根血管都是一道可见的厚涂笔触——它们把法器缠绕得越来越紧——逐渐覆盖石质表面——坚硬矿物形体陷入一幅绘画有机物的网中。镜头持续旋转——从每个角度揭示血管——它们逐帧重塑、闪烁。

血管加粗并进一步蔓延——把法器包裹得如此彻底，以至于底下的石质开始消失——整个形体变软——法器锐利的雕刻边缘在大量绘画血管下趋于圆润——形状轻微膨胀——变得更圆、更有机——血管层层包裹法器，直到整个物体隐约形似一颗心脏——不是精确的解剖学心脏，而是抽象的暗示——一坨粗糙、布满血管、搏动的绘画团块，介于石头与脏器之间——古老的矿物渐成活物。镜头永不停歇地旋转——东方式深红云状笔触扫过缠满血管形体周围的虚空——宽阔翻卷的笔触如传统云纹般盘旋——逐帧闪烁、重塑。

裹满血管的法器微微搏动——一阵细微的有机膨缩——绘画的血管轻轻收缩又舒张——一道柔和深红光的波向黑色虚空外扩散——镜头在搏动中持续旋转而不停顿。形体发光更暖——血管以安静的绘画生命搏动——石头与血肉融为同一物——古老与鲜活并存。

然后——一道琥珀红色的绘画光迹从法器激射而出——一道长长的厚涂笔触拖着光——划过黑色虚空。镜头追猎那道琥珀红光迹——硬性横移——向上猛冲。光迹增殖——更多油画笔触在其周围镜像对称地出现——形成一道琥珀红与暗色厚涂笔触相绕成对的隧道，弯入虚空。镜头一头扎进这道对称笔触隧道之中——仿佛穿过一条油画绘成的虫洞——旋涡状笔触壁从镜头前飞掠而过——隧道不断加深。

全程每秒 12 帧。油画风格。厚涂。镜头持续旋转。无推近。无照片写实。抽象——纯黑虚空。15 秒。
