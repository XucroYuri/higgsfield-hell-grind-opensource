## 原文 (source)

Принял. В этом промпте артефакт в трёх состояниях: у бедра в medium-framing → подъём руки с артефактом → extreme close-up macro. При palm-sized размере держание меняется — не "gripped firmly", а зажат между большим и указательным пальцами за бронзовое основание, кристалл наружу, чтобы он читался во всех трёх ракурсах. В CUT 1 артефакт у бедра виден как маленький блик. В CUT 3 macro-lens тянется на 25-30см и артефакт заполняет кадр (это нормально работает с 5см объектом), но пальцы по краям кадра будут крупнее относительно артефакта.

🔥 SCENE NOTE 🔥
Three-cut SLOW-MOTION sequence on — quiet aftermath. <<<b45115de-a4a5-4333-88a0-ec4c8209d302>>> walks a few weighted steps and STOPS, then slowly RAISES his right hand with <<<fdbacf4c-db7d-4715-96b8-b62583316e80>>>, then we cut to a CLOSE on the artifact itself. All cuts in HIGH-SPEED 96fps captured and played back at 24fps (4x slow-motion) — heavy, dreamlike, weighted with grief. Continuous emotional through-line: the survivor with the cost of his dead friends in his hand, deciding to look at what he carries.

🔥 LOOK PRESET 🔥
Cold post-storm grey daylight, diffused, no hard sun. Photoreal Kodak 500T pushed cold, light film grain. 60:30:10 cold grey-white snow + grey sky / dark stone-grey rock + DARK RED-BROWN frozen brush / saturated accents (dark blood crimson, bronze artifact, RED bandana wrist-wraps). Vinterberg-handheld doc-style measured (camera handheld at high-speed capture, gentle natural shake preserved through slow-mo as smooth weighted drift). 96fps capture played at 24fps. 180° shutter at native 24fps equivalent — beautiful smooth motion blur. Lubezki × Deakins. 8K IMAX. Real physics. NO LUT, no oversharpening.

★ ARTIFACT SELF-LUMINESCENCE (contained, not radiating):

The crystal core has a SOFT INNER GLOW — visibly self-luminescent inside the crystal facets, gently shimmering and breathing.
Glow pulses GENTLY in slow-mo stretched rhythm (slow swell up, slow ebb down, ~5-6s per visible cycle in slow-motion). Quiet, contained, NOT a bright burst.
THE GLOW IS CONTAINED TO THE CRYSTAL ITSELF. NO practical light cast on surrounding surfaces. NO warm pool on snow. NO warm underlight on Roko's hand, wrist, face, torso, or clothing. NO prismatic rainbow refractions onto snow. NO volumetric haze. NO halo or bloom into air.
Cold ambient daylight remains the ONLY light source acting on Roko, his body, the snow, the surrounding scene. The artifact's glow is a visible inner fire of the crystal alone — readable when looking AT the artifact, but not affecting the lit world around it.
Bronze claw setting reads as it normally would in cold daylight — dark patinated bronze, no warm catch from the inner glow.
The artifact is small (palm-sized, ~5cm tall) — at medium framing the inner glow reads as a tiny bright pinpoint at his side, only fully readable at the close framings.
