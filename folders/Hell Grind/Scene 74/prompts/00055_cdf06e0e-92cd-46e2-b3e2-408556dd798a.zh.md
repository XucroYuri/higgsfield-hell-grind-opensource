Duration: 14 seconds. Aspect ratio: 16:9. Three timed shots in sequence, all framed in close-up to medium close-up — never wide, never full body.

Right hand bare with no glove, pale weathered skin with subtle vein detail. Left hand wears a black tactical fingerless glove with a loose red bandana wrapped around the left wrist. Olive-grey-green heavy cargo pants. Dark combat boots. Multiple cuts and abrasions on face, dirt and grime, dark dried blood at temple and jawline, fresh blood at split lip, tears wet on his cheeks throughout all three cuts.

Cold post-battle grey daylight, no hard sun, low light from heavy storm clouds. Photoreal Kodak 500T pushed cold, light film grain. Cinematography in the style of Lubezki crossed with Deakins meets Lars von Trier Dogme realism. 8K IMAX. Real physics. No LUT, no oversharpening. Optics clean throughout — no lens flares, no anamorphic streaks, no bursts. The maximum framing is medium close-up showing torso and head — never feet, never full body, never landscape. The intimacy of the close framing is maintained through every beat including the action and the collapse.

CUT 3 —— 颤抖的双手，头向前抬起——中特写四分之三低角—— 5 秒

Roko 跪着、锚定在画面正中、x=50%。框取约从下躯干与腰上至头顶、他的躯干与头占据画面高度 70-80%。摄影机置于胸高、约雪面上方 140 厘米、因他跪着、摄影机在他脸的高度或略高、成轻微低角、四分之三右前透视。50mm 中特写框取——画面下缘是他的下躯干/腰、他的躯干填满画面正中、他的脸在上中部、呈四分之三角、他头上与周围的柔焦背景仅在浓重散焦中可见。无脚、无跪雪膝——框取停留在躯干与头上。f/2.0 的浅景深、焦点拉移偶发滞后。
