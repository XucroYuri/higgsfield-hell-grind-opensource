SCENE CONTEXT
THE KILL. After all the mockery and dialogue, Roco delivers the final death-strike to Monster. CUT 1 sets the standoff and shows Roco wind up from a lowered sword position into a huge cock and drive the blade down into Monster's mouth, head pinned to tree. CUT 2 jumps to a close-up on Monster's head at the moment of impact: his eyes — previously hidden in deep shadow or amused — are now WIDE ALIVE AND SCARED, the audience reads genuine terror as he realizes this is his death. Then in an instant, dark blood-red crystal spikes (the sword's own material) punch through his skull from the inside in 5-10 places — forehead, top of skull, temples, cheeks, eye sockets, jaw — creating exit-wound holes. The crystals do not glow, do not make their own sound, only the meaty/bony thud of them punching through bone is heard. Blood pours from each puncture hole. The eyes go fixed and dimmed; consciousness ends. Then his body begins to disintegrate — converting into dark black ash particles that stream away horizontally in the blizzard wind. The crystal-spike halo eruption + bone-spike halo + flesh + bone all dissolve. Eventually only the tree remains, with Roco's sword still embedded vertically in the trunk where Monster's mouth was — a thin red streak in the gnarled bark, the storm still raging around it. Tone: snow-deadened, blizzard-howling, mythic death — the body becoming nothing but ash carried away by the wind, the weapon staying as the only mark on the tree.
