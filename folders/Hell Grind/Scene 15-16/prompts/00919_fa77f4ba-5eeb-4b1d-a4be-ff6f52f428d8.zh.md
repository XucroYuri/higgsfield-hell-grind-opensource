## 原文 (source)

8K IMAX. Photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera. No 3D render, no game engine. Genre: Modern action blockbuster. References: cinematography of Emmanuel Lubezki and Roger Deakins. No CGI-heavy feel, no game-cutscene aesthetic. Lighting: Only natural light and practicals — camera always on the shadow side of subjects, backlit contre-jour with atmospheric haze throughout. Key light comes only from the sky, windows, or on-set fixtures. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent. Camera: Physical cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. No frame-interp smoothing. Realism: Photorealistic surfaces with real-world wear, organic asymmetry, and real material behavior — skin shows pores, vellus, asymmetric moles, capillary flush with pore-shadow matching on-set light; corner vignette and edge chromatic aberration from physical lens. Gravity, inertia, and follow-through respected — mass has real weight, every object casts correct contact shadows. No render-look, no floating or duplicated props. Continuity: All characters, props, environment, and lighting stay identical across every frame and every cut. No identity drift, no appearance changes. Acting: Hollywood-grade — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with practical catch-lights and micro-saccades, visible breath and chest rise, pre-speech micro-swallow, inter-beat micro-expressions. No playing to camera. Composition: Thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one, no static figures. Technical: 60fps smooth motion, 8K detail. No jitter, no identity drift. Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music, no subtitles. Scene 1: A woman <<<image_2>>> enters the pizzeria first; a group of young people—a guy <<<image_3>>>, another guy <<<image_4>>>, and a girl <<<image_5>>>—follow her; the woman walks with a confident stride; The group of young people looks beaten down; they are dejected; and seem very upset about something; the pizzeria is almost empty, with only a few people sitting and eating inside, who pay no attention to the main characters.
Scene 2: The woman and the group of young people enter the kitchen and walk through it, where several chefs are cooking; the characters are puzzled as to what they are doing here and where they are going.
Scene 3: The girl approaches a gray metal elevator and places her hand on the palm-scanning ID reader; the scanner lights up green and the elevator doors open; the young people look at each other. 
Fourth scene: all the characters enter the elevator; the elevator moves; 
Fifth scene: a wide shot with a top-down view of all the characters in the elevator.

## 中文对照 (zh)

8K IMAX。超写实——使用物理电影摄影机实拍。非3D渲染，非游戏引擎。类型：现代动作大片。参考：Emmanuel Lubezki与Roger Deakins的摄影语言。绝非CGI重度感，绝非游戏过场动画美学。光线：仅自然光+实景光源（practicals）——摄影机始终位于角色阴影侧，逆光（contre-jour），全场景持续大气薄雾。主光仅来自天空、窗户或现场实景灯具。色彩：60:30:10——主色 / 副色 / 点缀色。摄影机：物理电影镜头。180°快门运动模糊。禁帧间插值平滑。真实感：照片级真实表面——有真实磨损、有机不对称、真实材质行为；皮肤显现毛孔、绒毛、不对称痣斑、毛细血管潮红，毛孔级阴影方向与现场光严格一致；物理镜头自带边角渐晕与边缘色差。尊重重力、惯性及动作随动——质量具真实重量，每个物体投射正确接触阴影。禁渲染感、禁漂浮或重复道具。连贯性：每一帧与每一次剪辑之间所有角色、道具、环境与光线完全一致——禁身份漂移、禁外观改变。表演：好莱坞级——反应前微停顿、精准视线、带实景眼神光与微跳视的湿润活泼眼睛、可见呼吸与胸腔起伏、开口前微吞咽、情绪节拍间的不自主微表情。禁对镜头表演。构图：三分法+黄金比例。画面中每个人从第一帧起即在运动，禁静止人物。技术：60fps平滑运动，8K细节。禁抖动、禁身份漂移。音频：仅环境SFX。无音乐，无字幕。
