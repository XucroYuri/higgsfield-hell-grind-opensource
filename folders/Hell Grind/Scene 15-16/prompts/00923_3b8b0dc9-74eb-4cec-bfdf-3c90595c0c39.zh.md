## 原文 (source)

8K IMAX. Photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera. No 3D render, no game engine. Genre: Modern action blockbuster. References: cinematography of Emmanuel Lubezki and Roger Deakins. No CGI-heavy feel, no game-cutscene aesthetic. Lighting: Only natural light and practicals — camera always on the shadow side of subjects, backlit contre-jour with atmospheric haze throughout. Key light comes only from the sky, windows, or on-set fixtures. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent. Camera: Physical cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. No frame-interp smoothing. Realism: Photorealistic surfaces with real-world wear, organic asymmetry, and real material behavior — skin shows pores, vellus, asymmetric moles, capillary flush with pore-shadow matching on-set light; corner vignette and edge chromatic aberration from physical lens. Gravity, inertia, and follow-through respected — mass has real weight, every object casts correct contact shadows. No render-look, no floating or duplicated props. Continuity: All characters, props, environment, and lighting stay identical across every frame and every cut. No identity drift, no appearance changes. Acting: Hollywood-grade — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with practical catch-lights and micro-saccades, visible breath and chest rise, pre-speech micro-swallow, inter-beat micro-expressions. No playing to camera. Composition: Thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one, no static figures. Technical: 60fps smooth motion, 8K detail. No jitter, no identity drift. Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music, no subtitles. A wide, anamorphic shot taken from a low angle at street level. A narrow city alley between dilapidated brick buildings, night, streetlights, puddles on the cracked asphalt. A black car with heavily tinted windows—large, aggressive—sharply turns into the alley from the main street, its headlights cutting through the gray haze. It rolls forward and brakes in front of the entrance to a run-down pizzeria: a faded sign, peeling paint; a flickering neon sign. No people in sight. The car comes to a stop. The engine dies. The headlights go out. Deep silence. An atmosphere of secrecy—this place doesn’t want to be found. Cinematic feature film, anamorphic 2.39:1 aspect ratio, shot on ARRI Alexa 65, desaturated color grading, deep shadows, film grain.

## 中文对照 (zh)

8K IMAX。超写实——使用物理电影摄影机实拍。非3D渲染，非游戏引擎。类型：现代动作大片。参考：Emmanuel Lubezki与Roger Deakins的摄影语言。绝非CGI重度感，绝非游戏过场动画美学。光线：仅自然光+实景光源（practicals）——摄影机始终位于角色阴影侧，逆光（contre-jour），全场景持续大气薄雾。主光仅来自天空、窗户或现场实景灯具。色彩：60:30:10——主色 / 副色 / 点缀色。摄影机：物理电影镜头。180°快门运动模糊。禁帧间插值平滑。真实感：照片级真实表面——有真实磨损、有机不对称、真实材质行为；皮肤显现毛孔、绒毛、不对称痣斑、毛细血管潮红，毛孔级阴影方向与现场光严格一致；物理镜头自带边角渐晕与边缘色差。尊重重力、惯性及动作随动——质量具真实重量，每个物体投射正确接触阴影。禁渲染感、禁漂浮或重复道具。连贯性：每一帧与每一次剪辑之间所有角色、道具、环境与光线完全一致——禁身份漂移、禁外观改变。表演：好莱坞级——反应前微停顿、精准视线、带实景眼神光与微跳视的湿润活泼眼睛、可见呼吸与胸腔起伏、开口前微吞咽、情绪节拍间的不自主微表情。禁对镜头表演。构图：三分法+黄金比例。画面中每个人从第一帧起即在运动，禁静止人物。技术：60fps平滑运动，8K细节。禁抖动、禁身份漂移。音频：仅环境SFX。无音乐，无字幕。
一个宽阔、变形的宽银幕（anamorphic）低角度街面镜头。一条位于破败砖楼之间的狭窄城市巷道，夜晚，路灯，开裂沥青上的积水。一辆深色车胎大而具有侵略性的黑色轿车，从主街急转驶入巷道，车灯穿破灰色雾气。它向前滚动，在一家破旧披萨店入口前刹车：褪色招牌、剥落的油漆、闪烁的霓虹灯。画面中人影全无。汽车停住。引擎熄灭。车灯关闭。深刻的寂静。一种隐秘的气氛——这里并不想被找到。电影感剧情片，变形宽银幕2.39:1画幅，以ARRI Alexa 65拍摄，去饱和调色，深重阴影，胶片颗粒。
