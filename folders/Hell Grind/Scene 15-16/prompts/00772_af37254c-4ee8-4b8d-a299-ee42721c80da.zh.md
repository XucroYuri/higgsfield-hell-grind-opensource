## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles. [SHOT 1]
The shot is from Gendelphina’s waist up. The camera is positioned next to the closed elevator doors, looking inside.
GENDELPHINA — in the foreground, center, facing us, holding a cane. She is silent.
ROKO (behind and to the left of Gendelphina): his face is angry and tense; he’s looking at the floor. His jaw is clenched. Hands: clenches his fist → unclenches it → clenches it again. He sways slightly nervously in place—from left to right.
JACKS (behind and to the right of Gendelphina): Leaning with his entire back against the right wall. Arms crossed over his chest. Staring at the floor. Not looking at anyone.
RAIN enters last (hasn’t taken her place yet)—with her back to the camera, she walks (to the right of Gendelphina) across the foreground into the background, and stands behind Gendelphina on the left.
As soon as Rain stands up, Gendelphina begins to speak; during her line, she turns her head slightly toward Roko:
“They say the Gods forged six artifacts of unimaginable power.”
Roko: turns her head toward Gendelphina—suspicion and anger. The swaying does not stop.
[SHOT 2]
Close-up. A matte black panel with a biometric scanner; Gendelphina’s palm rests on the panel.
① A green line scans her palm—tracing the exact outline of her palm and each finger (a high-tech green outline on the panel’s screen)
② The entire surface of the panel lights up green—confirming successful identity verification
The green light fades. The panel returns to black. The hand withdraws.
Gendelphina’s voice off-screen: “Three were taken by demons.”
[SHOT 3]
Close-up of Gendelphina’s face (up to her neck). We see her face; three figures are out of focus behind her. The elevator starts moving—the camera jerks once.
ROKO (off-focus, left rear): looks at Gendelphina—intently, suspiciously. Doesn’t take his eyes off her. Blinks periodically.
JACKS (off-focus, right rear): isn’t looking at her—eyes on the floor. His arms are still crossed. He leans back.
RAIN (back left, to the right of Roko): isn’t looking at her—stares straight ahead into the void. She’s scared; she holds her one lowered arm (near the elbow) and strokes it, calming herself.
Gendelphina—ends her monologue with the line:
“Three were hidden on Earth.”

Color（色彩）：60:30:10——主色 / 副色 / 点缀色。
Camera（摄影机）：物理电影镜头。180°快门运动模糊。
Skin（皮肤）：毛孔级写实——汗毛、不对称痣、毛细血管潮红、毛孔阴影匹配现场光。
Acting（表演）：好莱坞级——反应前微停顿、精准视线、带眼神光的湿润活眼、可见呼吸与胸腔起伏。
Physics（物理）：遵循重力与惯性——质量有真实重量、正确接触阴影。禁漂浮道具。
Composition（构图）：三分法+黄金比例。每个人从第一帧开始在运动。
Continuity（连贯性）：每一刀（cut）之间角色、道具、环境完全一致。无身份漂移。
Technical（技术）：24fps流畅运动。8K细节。禁抖动。
Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（SFX）。无音乐。No subtitles. 无字幕。[SHOT 1]
镜头从甘德尔菲娜（Gendelphina）腰部以上取景。摄影机位于关闭的电梯门旁，向内取景。
GENDELPHINA（甘德尔菲娜）——前景、居中、面向我们，手持手杖。她沉默不语。
ROKO（罗科）(甘德尔菲娜身后偏左)：他的脸愤怒而紧绷；他低头看着地板。下颚紧咬。双手：握拳 → 松开 → 再握拳。他原地略显紧张地摇晃——从左到右。
JACKS（贾克斯）(甘德尔菲娜身后偏右)：整个后背靠在右墙上。双臂交叉于胸前。盯着地板。不看任何人。
RAIN（雷恩）最后一个进场（还没站好位）——背对镜头，她从（甘德尔菲娜右侧）跨过前景走向背景，站到甘德尔菲娜身后的左侧。
雷恩一站好，甘德尔菲娜便开始说话；在说这句台词时，她微微把头转向罗科：
"据说诸神锻造了六件拥有难以想象之威力的神器。"
罗科：把头转向甘德尔菲娜——怀疑与愤怒。摇晃没有停止。
[SHOT 2]
特写。一块带生物识别扫描仪的哑光黑色面板；甘德尔菲娜的手掌按在面板上。

② 整个面板表面亮起绿色——确认身份核验成功

甘德尔菲娜面部特写（到她的颈部）。我们看到她的脸；她身后三个身影虚焦。电梯开始移动——摄影机猛地颠了一下。
ROKO（虚焦，左后方）：看着甘德尔菲娜——专注而怀疑。目不转睛。不时眨眼。
