僵尸-武士army crowd必须VISIBLE in EVERY single shot——无论镜头景别（WS/MS/CU/ECU/macro CU都一样必须可见）。背景绝不允许是empty fog或empty snow plain或empty sky——deep background MUST always读为"a continuous dark band of out-of-focus humanoid crowd silhouettes through fog"。这条规则overrides一切深度of field、bokeh、framing decisions——always reserve the deep background for the crowd silhouettes。

【每个景别下army的rendering规则】（强制per-shot要求）：
- 16mm WS（wide shot）：背景中可见200-400 sharp清晰僵尸figures肩并肩堆积形成solid black silhouette wall围绕arena 360度，在中-远景；前景包含决斗主角；远景军队人群fills the full width and 30-50%的frame height作为thick continuous dark band。
- 35mm MS（medium shot）：背景bokeh区域可见100-200 mid-defocused僵尸figures filling背景the full width of the frame作为continuous dark crowd band，high-density packed silhouettes填充画面下半部分to upper-third area。决不能是empty grey fog——总是visible crowd silhouettes through fog。
- 85mm CU / macro CU / ECU（特写）：即使foreground subjects filling 60-80%的frame，剩余20-40%的background bokeh必须show 50-150 heavily-out-of-focus dark humanoid blob silhouettes作为continuous dark crowd band across the entire deep-background。Bokeh blobs应该read as undeniably人形剪影（不是random noise，不是empty fog）——viewer立刻能认出"那是a crowd of soldiers in the deep background through fog"。
- 85mm fly-through shots：前景sharp samurai剪影（武器、骷髅马、yari、katana）crossing the frame as motion-blurred dark silhouettes；mid-ground包含战斗主角；deep background仍然show defocused crowd silhouettes。
- POV / OTS shots：over-the-shoulder的background frame区域必须包含crowd silhouettes作为defocused dark band。
- 任何ground-level / low-angle shots：从低角度仰视时背景crowd silhouettes are EVEN more prominent作为tall dark figures looming against the storm-cloud sky。

【army density and visibility specs】：
