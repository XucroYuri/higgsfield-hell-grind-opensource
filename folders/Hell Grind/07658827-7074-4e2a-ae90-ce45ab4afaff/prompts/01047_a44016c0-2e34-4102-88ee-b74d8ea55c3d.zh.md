21:9 · 8K · 60fps · 15s · 10 numbered shots · ALL probe-lens macro / extreme close-up · ALL slow-motion 4-6x · ALL low-key cave-dark
Style: low-key cave-dark cinematography (Deakins "1917" tunnel scenes / "Sicario" night-vision / "Apocalypse Now" cave interior aesthetic — pitch-dark interior with thin light slivers).

⚠⚠⚠ THE SUBJECT OF THIS PROMPT IS NOT ROCO ⚠⚠⚠

The subject of every frame is the UNDERSIDE OF A PILE OF ZOMBIE-SAMURAI BODIES — the camera is buried at the bottom of the pile filming UPWARD into the dense ceiling of stacked corpses pressing down. Roco is barely visible at the edge of frame as a tiny dark glint of crystal armor — he is NOT the subject. He is a buried prisoner whose existence is implied through one or two small dark fragments at the frame edge. Treat this prompt as if filming a horror-movie shot of a victim buried alive at the bottom of a writhing corpse-pile, where the audience sees mostly the corpse-pile crushing down and barely glimpses the victim.

⚠⚠⚠ CAMERA POSITION (mandatory in every shot) ⚠⚠⚠
The camera is a Laowa 24mm Probe-Lens (long thin macro barrel) lying on the snow next to Roco's body, threaded sideways into the gap beneath 3-4 stacked layers of zombie samurai. The lens is pointed UPWARD into the underside of the pile — looking up at the bellies / chests / arms / skulls / lamellar plates of the zombies above. The camera does NOT look at Roco; it looks at the zombies pressing down above him. Roco's crystal armor is at the frame's edge or out of focus — never centered.

⚠⚠⚠ WHAT FILLS THE FRAME (every frame, every shot) ⚠⚠⚠

Frame TOP 70-85% — DENSE ZOMBIE-BODY CEILING IMMEDIATELY ABOVE THE LENS:
- 20+ zombie-samurai bodies visible in the frame, stacked in 3-4 overlapping layers above the lens.
- We see the UNDERSIDES of bodies pressed down toward the camera: bellies in tattered samurai lamellar (dark brown / blackened plates with faded red lacing), exposed bone ribcages, dangling bone arms with skeletal fingers reaching down, fanged skulls hanging upside-down with mouths grinding, dirty rusted-iron kabuto helmets, ragged hakama cloth draping down, frayed lacing falling, severed-looking bone limbs flopping.
- Multiple zombies per frame layer; multiple layers stacked above.
- Faint orange-red eye-socket glows scattered across the upper frame from the various zombie skulls in deep shadow.
- The body ceiling in continuous micro-motion: bones flexing, hands clenching, teeth grinding, bodies shifting, new bodies dropping in from above frame.
- Frame top = a dense low ceiling of intertwined corpses pressing down, blocking the view upward. No sky / daylight / open air.

Frame BOTTOM 5-15% — CORNER OF ROCO + COLD GREY-BLUE SNOW:
- A SMALL CORNER at the bottom edge of frame: a tiny 3-8cm dark-burgundy / near-black sliver of Roco's <<<1d58fa3e-7359-4376-bb67-ea43d20c8fdb>>> crystal armor barely visible (the edge of his helmet, or the edge of his chestplate, or the edge of his gauntlet — never the whole armor, only a corner).
- Beside the crystal sliver: a small slice of cold grey-blue compacted snow Roco is lying on (he is on his back on the snow ground, the bottom layer of the pile).
- The crystal is HEAVILY UNDER-EXPOSED — the darkest element in the frame, almost black, only ONE tiny red glint per frame on a single facet edge or fresh crack-line.

Frame MID-FRAME — ONE ZOMBIE ATTACKING THE CRYSTAL:
- ONE zombie attacker is in razor-sharp macro focus, lit by a thin cold-grey light sliver leaking through a gap in the body ceiling. This attacker is the only sharply-focused element of the frame.
- The attacker is doing one of: GNAWING (fanged skull mouth chewing the dark crystal sliver, teeth grinding, broken teeth tumbling), SCRATCHING (skeletal bone fingers clawing at crystal, fingernails dragging, fingers breaking off), HITTING (bone fist pounding crystal, knuckles cracking).
- The attacker's lit detail is the visual focus — it sits AGAINST the dark Roco crystal sliver at the bottom edge of frame.

⚠⚠⚠ LIGHTING (every frame) ⚠⚠⚠

Inside the pile is BLACK like a coal mine at night. Daylight CANNOT reach Roco — the dense pile of bodies overhead absorbs all daylight before it gets down to his level. The only illumination is THIN COLD-GREY LIGHT SLIVERS leaking through narrow body-to-body gaps for half a second at a time before the pile shifts and the gap closes.
- ~85-90% of every frame = DEEP SHADOW / NEAR-BLACK / UNDER-EXPOSED.
- ~10% = ONE small lit zombie attacker detail.
- ~5% = faint orange-red glows from zombie eye-sockets (heavily blurred bg) + faint red glint from a single crystal facet edge / fresh crack on Roco's armor.
- Histogram heavily left-shifted — almost everything in shadows / blacks.
- Roco's crystal is the darkest red on screen — wet raw liver in a dark cellar, near-monochrome black with one faint glint per frame.

⚠⚠⚠ MOTION (every frame, every shot) ⚠⚠⚠

Every frame is in deep slow-motion 4-6x (captured at 240-360fps, played at 60fps). Every motion slowed:
- Snowflakes drift slowly through rare light gaps.
- Cloth and lacing lift slowly.
- Bone hands squeeze in slow continuous pressure.
- Teeth grinding moves frame-by-frame.
- Crystal cracks propagate visibly in slow-motion.
- Broken teeth and bone fragments tumble slowly.
- Roco's struggles (legs kicking / hips bucking / gauntlet flexing / helmet rocking) all in slow-motion against the pile.

⚠⚠⚠ FRAMING (every shot) ⚠⚠⚠

Every shot is a probe-lens macro / extreme close-up. The lens is buried inside the pile, subject 2-15cm from front element. Shallow DoF f/2.8-f/4 — only a 2-5cm slice in focus, everything else dissolves into creamy bokeh of dark zombie-body shapes + faint orange-red eye-socket glows + faint blurred red crystal glints.

⚠⚠⚠ SOUND ⚠⚠⚠

Continuous: through the helmet — Roco's heavy labored gasping + low guttural rage-growl. Outside — slow muffled bone-on-crystal thuds, slow grinding teeth on crystal, fingernails dragging, bones cracking, broken teeth tumbling, the muffled rustle of stacked bodies above.

⚠⚠⚠ ROCO IS ACTIVELY FIGHTING TO BREAK FREE — VISIBLE IN EVERY SHOT ⚠⚠⚠

Roco is NOT a passive prisoner. In every single shot we see CONCRETE PHYSICAL RESISTANCE from Roco fighting against the pile. Each shot must show at least one (often several) of these specific acts of resistance, slowed down in the macro frame:

(a) HEAD-TURN AWAY FROM TEETH:
When zombie jaws clamp on his helmet or chew at his neck-seam, Roco's helmet TWISTS and turns away from the bite — head wrenched sharply to the side, helmet scraping against the snow as he tries to pull his head out from under the chewing teeth. The bite-zombie's grip slips for half a second, drags across the helmet surface (leaving a tooth-scratch trail), then re-clamps deeper. Roco's neck strains visibly through the gorget — chest crystal heaving with the effort. We see the helmet sliver at frame bottom JERK violently sideways, sometimes 5-8cm of motion, before the head-pinners force it back flat against the snow.

(b) ARMS WRENCHING TO BREAK GRIPS:
When zombie hands clamp on his <<<1d58fa3e-7359-4376-bb67-ea43d20c8fdb>>> gauntlets / forearms / shoulders, Roco's arm WRENCHES hard outward and side-to-side trying to break the grip. We see his gauntlet pull straight out from the clamping bone hand — knuckles go white inside the gauntlet, the gauntlet rotating at the wrist trying to unscrew the zombie's grip; sometimes the gauntlet briefly succeeds in pulling the bone hand off (one zombie hand pried off and thrown sideways) before three more zombie hands re-clamp from new angles. The arm visibly extends 5-10cm out of the pile in the surge, then is dragged back in by re-clamping zombies. His fingers in the gauntlet flex into a fist, then claw outward, then grip back at any zombie surface they can reach (a bone wrist, a lamellar plate, a kabuto edge) trying to pry.

(c) LEGS KICKING TO BREAK FREE:
When zombie hands clamp on his thighs / knees / shins / ankles, Roco's legs KICK and JERK violently — knee-plates pop upward 5-10cm into the corpse-ceiling above before being forced flat by leg-pinners; ankles twist trying to free his foot from the ankle-grip; sometimes a leg breaks free for a half-second (knee snapping straight up out of the pile) before three fresh zombies dive bodily onto the freed leg and re-pin it; his armored boot drives upward into a zombie's belly above; calf muscles strain visibly through the leg-armor as he tries to push his weight up.

(d) BODY TWISTING TO ROLL OUT:
Roco's torso TWISTS hard side-to-side trying to roll out from under the pile — shoulders wrenching upward and to the side, hips arching off the snow, his whole body trying to corkscrew sideways out of the pinned position. We see his back lift 3-5cm off the snow during the twist; the corpse-ceiling above visibly RIPPLES with the twist as bodies shift; then the pile clamps tighter and his body is forced flat onto the snow again. The chest crystal HEAVES with each twist attempt.

(e) HIPS BUCKING UPWARD:
Roco's hips arch upward against the body-weight piled on his torso — pelvis lifts briefly off the snow before the pile crushes back down. The buck is a desperate maximum-effort motion — every buck sees the corpse-ceiling above pulse upward 2-4cm before re-compressing.

(f) FINGERS PRYING:
His gauntlet fingers in macro-CU shots are seen actively prying — fingers digging under bone wrists, into seams of zombie lamellar, gripping skull jaws to push them off. Sometimes a gauntlet finger crushes a bone wrist (visible bone-dent / crack); sometimes it pries a zombie hand off an inch before the hand re-clamps.

PILE PULSE (mandatory in every shot — visible in macro):
With every act of Roco's resistance, the entire CORPSE-CEILING above visibly PULSES UPWARD a few cm (the body-mass ripples up as Roco surges from below) and then RE-COMPRESSES tighter as the zombies clamp down harder. This pulse-and-compress cycle repeats throughout every shot — we see at least 2-3 visible pile-pulses per shot.

THE EFFORT IS FUTILE BUT TOTAL:
Every act of resistance ends in being re-pinned — every freed limb is re-grabbed by 3+ fresh zombies, every bucking torso is crushed back flat, every twisted body is forced supine again. But the EFFORT is continuous and at maximum — Roco never gives up, never stops fighting; the audience always sees him struggling against the pile in every single frame.
