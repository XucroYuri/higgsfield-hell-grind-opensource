cushion. The stack should look small and unobtrusive on the 
floor, not dominating the composition, occupying only a small

small detail in the scene, not a large prop.

produce a diptych. Do not produce a side-by-side comparison. 
Do not produce a before-after split. Do not change the aspect 
ratio of the target scene. Output only the modified target 
scene as a single integrated image.

and untouched. Preserve: the exact composition and camera

its rumpled throw blanket, the existing books and coffee mug 
already on the floor beside the couch (keep these in their 
original positions and original sizes), the mannequin with the 
partially assembled feminine tactical armor suit, the 
workbench with armor pieces and tools, the cleaned concrete 
floor surface, the loose cable, the warm workbench lamp glow, 
all overhead recessed ceiling lights, all shadows and dark 
corners, the cool blue-grey color palette, deep low-key 
cinematic lighting, near-black darks, the entire mood and 
atmosphere, the ARRI cinematic look, the Kodak film grain.

Only the new small modest stack of books from the reference 
is added to the floor in front of the couch, at proper 
realistic small scale. Everything else stays exactly as is.
