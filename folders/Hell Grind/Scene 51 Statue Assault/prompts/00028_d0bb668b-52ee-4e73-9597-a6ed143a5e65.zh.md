## 原文 (source)

A young Black man in his 20s is pressed hard against 
a massive ancient stone column, back flat against it, 
shoulders tight. Short bright golden-yellow natural hair. 
Tactical clear wraparound visor glasses. Black tactical 
bomber jacket with orange accent details. Olive green 
cargo pants. Black fingerless gloves.

The column vibrates — a heavy impact somewhere offscreen 
sends dust and small debris raining down from above. 
He flinches slightly with each impact, eyes wide, 
jaw set tight. Then he turns his head toward the fight 
offscreen and shouts with sharp urgency — mouth wide open, 
neck muscles tense — but doesn't move from behind 
the column. Confident but cornered energy.

Setting: underground ancient temple corridor. 
Massive dark stone columns. Warm amber torchlight 
flickering from wall sconces. Heavy dust and debris 
falling from ceiling. Particles catching the light.

Camera: handheld, natural breathing rhythm, 
subtle urgent micro-shake — as if the operator 
is also reacting to nearby impacts. 
Telephoto 85–100mm. Shallow depth of field — 
face and upper body in sharp focus, 
column foreground edge soft, 
corridor behind dissolved into warm amber bokeh.
Angle: side profile, slightly low at 15 degrees, 
tilted 5 degrees — dutch tilt tension.
Camera presses slightly closer as he shouts.

Lighting: flickering warm amber torchlight 
casting from the left, deep reactive shadows 
on right side of face. Light flickers slightly 
from offscreen explosion flash.

Cinematic realism. Photorealistic. 
High tension atmosphere. Film grain.
No jitter artifacts. No limb distortion. 
Maintain exact character appearance throughout.

## 中文对照 (zh)

一位 20 多岁的年轻黑人男子，被用力压在一根巨大的古老石柱上，背贴平它，肩头紧绷。短而明亮的金色自然发。战术透明环绕式护目镜眼镜（visor glasses）。带橙色点缀细节的黑色战术轰炸机夹克。橄榄绿工装裤。黑色露指手套。

石柱震动——画面外某处一记重撞击使尘土与小碎石自上方如雨洒落。他随每次撞击微微退缩，双眼睁大、下颚紧咬。然后他转头朝向画面外的打斗，带着尖锐的急切大喊——嘴巴大张、颈部肌肉绷紧——但不离开石柱身后。自信却受困的气场。

Setting：地下古庙走廊。巨大的暗色石柱。壁灯中摇曳的温暖琥珀色火炬光。厚重尘土与碎屑自天花板坠落。颗粒捕捉着光线。

Camera：手持，自然的呼吸节奏，微妙而急切的微抖动——仿佛操作员也在对近旁的撞击作出反应。长焦 85–100mm。浅景深——脸与上半身在锐利对焦中，石柱前景边缘虚化，身后走廊溶解为暖琥珀色散景。角度：侧面侧身，略微低机位 15 度，倾斜 5 度——歪斜镜头（dutch tilt）的紧张感。他大喊时摄影机微微更贴近。

Lighting：摇曳的温暖琥珀色火炬光自左侧打来，他面部右侧深重的反应阴影。光线自画面外爆炸闪光中微微闪烁。

电影式写实。写实摄影（Photorealistic）。高度紧张的氛围。胶片颗粒。无抖动伪影、无肢体畸变。全程保持完全一致的角色外观。
