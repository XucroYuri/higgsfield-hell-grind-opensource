Rain uses falling debris cloud as cover to fly toward the monk's ledge, guardian punches through the cloud, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Cuarón's Children of Men dust-haze action, Villeneuve's Dune Part Two scale combat. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, accent saturated blue-violet. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic skin, fabric, matte-black hoverboard, weathered ancient stone, dense dust haze. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking through dust.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, MASSIVE ceiling debris cascading creating dense dust column.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall. Scale through Rain's body small against the colossal stone fist that punches through the dust column near her — fist the size of multiple Rains.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 2.
Refer to the elderly East Asian monk in dark maroon robes from Image 3.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, MASSIVE column of cascading dust from the ceiling fills the air between the chamber's center and the side wall ledge, the monk on the ledge in deep blurred background visible through the dust.

Shot 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Rain entering the dust column on hoverboard, 85mm, f/1.4. Operator handheld at twelve-meter elevation tracking her flight. Rain rides the board into the dense cascading dust cloud rising from massive ceiling debris, her body becoming partially obscured by the swirling dust, only her silhouette and the purple thruster glow readable through the cloud. She uses the cover — angling the board sharply toward the monk's ledge. Her right hand extended with saturated BLUE-VIOLET magnetic field active, parting the dust around her with the field. Her face determined, eyes hard. Audio: the WHOOSH of dense dust around her, thruster scream muted by the cloud, her controlled fast breath, debris falling.

Shot 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Rain mid-flight through dust, 100mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on her dust-streaked face, eyes forward, hair flying through the dust. She is closer to the ledge than ever — the carved stone of the high platform visible through the dust at frame edge in shallow blur, the maroon robes of the seated monk visible. She is going to make it. Then in real time a COLOSSAL CARVED STONE FIST punches through the dust cloud from the side — the stone fist the size of multiple Rains erupting through the dust like a missile, dust EXPLODING outward from the fist's passage, the fist headed toward Rain's flight path in front of her. The cracked carved stone surface of the fist visible in shallow blur. Audio: the WHOOSH of the colossal fist punching through dust, dust eruption sound, her sharp gasp of recognition, the colossus's deep red hum spiking close.

Shot 3 (10-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Rain evading the punch, 50mm, f/1.8. The colossal fist passes through the air in deep blur where Rain just was — Rain has tilted the hoverboard SHARPLY downward at the last moment, her body angled almost vertically as she dives BELOW the fist's path, the fist sweeping past above her with WHOOSH of mass, the wake of the fist pushing dust and her hair violently. She rights the board lower, the monk's ledge now FURTHER above her — she has been pushed down by the evasion. She glances UP at the ledge, frustrated — close but missed again. Audio: the WHOOSH of the colossal fist passing above her, her sharp evasion grunt, the wake's air-rush, her thrusters re-firing for recovery, her frustrated exhale.

PACING: 15-second cover-and-counter. Rain enters dust column for cover (0-5s), almost reaches ledge when fist punches through (5-10s), evades by diving below, pushed off-course again (10-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

Rain 借助坠落的碎石云团作掩护，飞向武僧所在的岩架，守护巨人挥拳穿透云团，15 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影，变形宽银幕电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Cuarón《人类之子》的尘雾行动，Villeneuve《沙丘·第二部》的宏大尺度战斗。Lighting（灯光）：上方惨白冷光，守护巨人眼窝的饱和红光，悬浮滑板推进器的饱和紫光，Rain 磁力手掌的饱和蓝紫光，温暖琥珀色油灯。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰为主，主导饱和红，点缀饱和蓝紫。重度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实皮肤、布料、哑光黑悬浮滑板、饱经风化的古石、浓密尘雾。Continuity（连贯性）锁定。绝不正面——四边形取景。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig，剧烈抖动，为穿尘而做的身体驱动的跟随。

CHAMBER TREMOR（洞室震颤）：剧烈摇晃，天花板大量碎石倾泻，汇成浓密尘柱。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护巨人尺度）：20 米高。通过近旁的撞穿尘柱的巨拳侧面衬托 Rain 的渺小——拳头的大小相当于好几个 Rain。

引用图 1 中那名头发银白的女人、眼旁有疤、穿着灰色连帽夹克。
引用图 2 中那块哑光黑、推进器核心发着紫光的悬浮滑板。
引用图 3 中那名身着深绛红僧袍、年迈的东亚武僧。

Setting（设定）：洞窟圣殿，从天花板倾泻而下的大量灰尘在洞室中央与侧壁岩架之间弥漫开来，武僧在岩架上，位于深虚化的背景中，透过灰尘依稀可见。

Shot 1（0-5s）：四边形手持中景近景，Rain 驾悬浮滑板进入尘柱，85mm，f/1.4。摄影师在十二米高度手持跟拍她的飞行。Rain 驾板冲入由天花板大量碎石扬起的浓密倾泻尘云，身体被旋舞的尘雾部分吞没，透过云团只能辨识出她的轮廓与紫色的推进器光芒。她利用遮蔽——在云中急转朝向武僧的岩架。她的右手前伸，掌心涌动着饱和蓝紫的磁力场，以磁场拨开身体四周的尘雾。她神情坚定、目光冷硬。Audio（音效）：周遭浓尘的呼呼声，推进器的尖啸被云团压低，她克制的急促呼吸，碎石坠落。

Shot 2（5-10s）：切至四边形手持极特写，Rain 穿尘飞行中，100mm，f/1.4。极浅对焦在她的蒙尘脸颊上，目光向前，发丝在尘中飞散。她比任何时候都更接近岩架——透过灰尘可见画框边缘那高台雕石的浅虚化，以及端坐武僧的绛红色僧袍。她就要成功了。就在这时，一只巨大的雕纹石拳实时从侧面穿透尘云——拳头大小相当于数个 Rain，如同导弹般冲开尘埃破云而来，拳过之处灰尘向外爆散，拳头正朝向前方 Rain 的飞行路径。拳上开裂的雕纹石面呈浅虚化可见。Audio（音效）：巨拳破尘的呼呼声、尘爆的声响、她认出危机时的锐利抽气声、巨人身旁飙升的深红嗡鸣。

Shot 3（10-15s）：切至四边形手持中景近景，Rain 躲避这一拳，50mm，f/1.8。巨拳从 Rain 刚在的位置深虚化地横扫而过——Rain 已在最后一刻将悬浮滑板急骤向下倾压，身体近乎竖直地潜入拳路下方，巨拳带着庞然之物的呼呼声从她上方扫过，拳的尾流猛烈地推动着尘雾与她的发丝。她压低将板回正，武僧的岩架如今更在她上方——这一避让把她推了下来。她沮丧地向上瞥向岩架——又近了却再度落空。Audio（音效）：巨拳从她上方掠过的呼呼声、她利落的躲闪闷哼、尾流的气流急冲、推进器重新点火以求恢复、她沮丧的长吐气。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒的掩护与反击。Rain 进入尘柱作掩护（0-5s），几乎够到岩架时拳头破云而出（5-10s），潜入下方避让、再度被推离航线（10-15s）。
