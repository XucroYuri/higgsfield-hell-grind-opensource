The open palm is flat and steady, like a platform — fingers relaxed and slightly spread.
The other hand rests calmly palm-up on the lap (relaxed meditation position).
Object on the palm:

The monk is carved from the same weathered grey stone with green moss patches — same material as the giant statue.

The monk sits upright and centered on the palm, calm and serene, contrasting with the giant's stern face.
Preserve everything else exactly:
Same stone material with weathered grey texture and green moss patches in the same locations on the giant
Same armored shoulders, robes, and belt with geometric pattern
Same bald head, stern facial expression, and furrowed brow
Same cross-legged seated pose of the giant
Same lighting, camera angle, background, and three-view composition (front view, back view, close-up portrait)
