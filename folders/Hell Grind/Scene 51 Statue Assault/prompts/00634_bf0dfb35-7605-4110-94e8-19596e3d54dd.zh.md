## 原文 (source)

Edit the statue's pose so it offers an open palm at ground level for a monk to sit on. Keep everything else identical.
Modify the seated stone warrior statue as follows:
Hand/arm pose:
Remove the sword entirely.
One arm extends forward and downward, reaching out from the body so that the open palm rests flat on the ground in front of the statue, at floor level.
The palm faces upward, fingers relaxed, slightly spread, and fully open — forming a flat, stable platform resting on the ground.
The arm is extended naturally forward (not stiffly), elbow slightly bent, shoulder relaxed, as if the giant is calmly offering his hand down to the floor for someone to step onto or sit upon.
The hand should be large enough and positioned at ground level so a human-sized person could comfortably sit on the palm.
The other hand rests calmly palm-up on the lap in a relaxed meditation position.
Important spatial detail:
The extended palm is at the same ground level as the base of the statue — flat on the floor, not floating in the air.
Leave the palm empty (no monk on it yet — the palm is offered, ready to receive).
Preserve everything else exactly:
Same stone material with weathered grey texture and green moss patches in the same locations
Same armored shoulders, robes, and belt with geometric pattern
Same bald head, stern facial expression, and furrowed brow
Same cross-legged seated pose of the giant
Same lighting, camera angle, background, and three-view composition (front view, back view, close-up portrait)
Same sculptural style and proportions of the main statue
Only the arm/hand position and the absence of the sword should change. The extended hand must be carved from the same mossy stone, matching the rest of the statue's material and weathering.

## 中文对照 (zh)

编辑雕像的姿势，使其在地面高度伸出一只张开的手掌，供一位僧侣坐上去。其余一切保持不变。

按如下方式修改这件端坐的石武士雕像：
手/手臂姿势：
完全移除宝剑。
一只手臂向前向下伸展，从身体探出，使张开的手掌平放在雕像前方的地面上，处于地板高度。
手掌朝上，手指放松、微微分开、完全张开——形成一个平放在地面上的平坦、稳定的平台。
手臂自然向前伸展（不僵硬），手肘微微弯曲，肩部放松，仿佛这位巨人正平静地把他的一只手递到地面，供某人踩上去或坐上去。
手掌应足够大，并位于地面高度，让一个真人大小的人可以舒适地坐在掌心上。
另一只手平静地掌心向上放在膝上，呈放松的冥想姿势。
重要的空间细节：
伸出的手掌与雕像底座处于同一地面高度——平放在地板上，而非悬在空中。
让手掌空着（上面还没有僧侣——手掌是递出的，准备承接）。
其余一切完全保持不变：
同样的石材，带有风化的灰色纹理和同在相同位置的绿色苔藓斑块
同样的装甲肩部、长袍与几何图案腰带
同样的光头、严峻的面部表情与紧锁的眉头
同样盘腿端坐的巨人姿势
同样的灯光、机位、背景与三视图构图（正面视图、背面视图、特写肖像）
主雕像同样的雕塑风格与比例
只有手臂/手的位置和移除长剑应改变。伸出的手必须用同样的覆有苔藓的石材雕刻，与雕像其余部分的材质和风化程度相匹配。
