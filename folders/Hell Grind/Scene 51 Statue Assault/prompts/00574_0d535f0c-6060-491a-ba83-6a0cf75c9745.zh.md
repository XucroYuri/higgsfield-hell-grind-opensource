Vast ancient sanctum carved from natural rock, recovery and forward motion sequence, 12 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, deep cool blue-grey palette with warm amber oil-lamp accents, dramatic chiaroscuro, no music. Inspired by Children of Men and The Revenant — handheld kinetic intimacy, close-up driven acting.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.
Refer to the man with dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 3.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 4.

Setting: vast cavern-sanctum carved from natural rock, towering carved stone column-faces receding into darkness on both side walls, irregular flagstone floor, two parallel lines of small oil-lamps flickering warm amber along the walls, ceiling lost in shadow far above, dust-hazed atmosphere. Roco lies face-down on the flagstones near the side wall, dust-covered, body parallel to the wall. Jax kneels beside him, jacket scuffed, glasses slightly askew. Rain stands a few meters away, the hoverboard held at her side in her left hand, watching them.

Shot 1 (0-2s): Handheld extreme low-angle close on Roco's face pressed against the flagstone, 100mm long lens, razor-thin focus on his closed eye. Camera lies on the floor inches from his face. Dust-streaked cheek against cold stone. His eyelid flutters, then opens — pupil dilating in the dark, slowly focusing. A faint twitch in his brow as awareness returns. He exhales softly, his breath fogging against the flagstone in the cold air. Frame breathes with intimate handheld drift. Off-screen above: Jax's voice, low, close, almost a whisper: "Hey. Hey, look at me." Roco's eye tracks up toward the voice. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drop with chamber reverb, Roco's slow ragged inhale magnified by closeness, the soft creak of Jax's jacket leaning in, faint oil-lamp crackle.

Shot 2 (2-3.5s): Cut to handheld extreme close on Jax's face above Roco, 100mm, three-quarter angle from below. Jax is leaning down into frame, glasses askew on his nose, brow tight with concern, dust-streaked. His eyes search Roco's off-frame face, scanning for response. He says again, quieter: "There you are." A small relieved exhale. Then his expression shifts to focused — he's about to act. Camera breathes intimately, the proximity to his face giving us his concern in detail. Audio: his lines with chamber reverb, his exhale of relief, his measured breath, ambient drone underneath.

Shot 3 (3.5-6.5s): Cut to handheld low-rising shot beside the two men, 50mm wider lens. Jax reaches down, grips Roco's shoulder firmly, then takes his forearm in a strong clasp — fingers locking around the wrist, the kind of grip that says I have you. He pulls. Roco grunts, pushes up onto his hands, then his knees, dust falling from his jacket. Jax shifts his weight back, using his body as counterbalance, and HAULS Roco upward to his feet in one continuous motion. The camera RISES WITH THEM continuously, operator physically standing. Roco staggers on his feet, leans heavily into Jax's shoulder for balance — face buried briefly against Jax's jacket, eyes squeezed shut, catching his breath. Jax's hand goes to the back of Roco's neck, holding him steady, the other hand still gripping Roco's arm. He gives him a beat. "Easy. Easy." Roco nods against him, recovering. Frame drifts and corrects, alive. Audio: scrape of Roco's grip on Jax's jacket, Jax's grunt of effort as he hauls, scrape of Roco's boots finding flagstone, Roco's heavy exhale into Jax's shoulder, Jax's "Easy" lines low and close, both breathing heavily, ambient drone, chamber reverb deepening.

Shot 4 (6.5-8s): Cut to handheld medium close on Rain, 100mm, three-quarter profile. She holds the hoverboard at her side, purple thruster glow soft accent. Her face is unreadable in the cold light — calm but watchful, eyes locked on the two men in deep blurred background. The cold distant light catches the scar by her eye and the front of her face. A small flicker of relief crosses her expression as she sees Roco upright. She speaks, voice low and direct, with chamber reverb: "You okay?" Camera breathes around her, drifts subtly closer. Audio: her controlled breathing, the question with long reverb tail, ambient drone, distant oil-lamp crackle.

Shot 5 (8-9.5s): Cut to handheld extreme close-up on Roco's face, 100mm, dust-streaked, the red bandage on his nose visible, eyes still adjusting. He pushes himself slightly upright off Jax's shoulder, finds his own balance. He is breathing heavily but steady now. He doesn't look at Rain. His eyes go forward, into the dark sanctum off-screen — fixing on something distant. His jaw sets. After a beat, his answer comes flat, decided, with chamber reverb: "Let's move." Frame breathes intimately on his face. Audio: his line with strong reverb tail, his breath settling, ambient drone.

Shot 6 (9.5-12s): Cut to handheld medium tight three-shot on the trio from a low front angle, 50mm. Roco center, just steady, eyes forward. Jax right of frame, takes his hand off Roco's neck and gives his shoulder one firm pat — confirming he's good — and steps back to clear his path. Rain left of frame, hoverboard at her side. Roco takes the first step forward into the chamber, then a second — boots solid on flagstone. Jax falls in beside him on his right. Rain a beat behind, the hoverboard swinging slightly with her stride. Camera dollies backward at their pace, framing them tight from waist-up walking toward camera. Behind them in deep shallow-focus blur: the dark interior of the sanctum receding, oil-lamp lines stretching away. Frame drifts with operator's breath, walking with them. Audio: Jax's pat on Roco's shoulder, Roco's first boot striking flagstone, then second, Jax falling in step, Rain joining a beat later — three pairs of boots in staggered rhythm with chamber reverb, breathing settling into walking pattern, ambient drone underneath.

CAMERA: long lens 50mm-100mm. Shots 1, 2, 4, 5 are 100mm (close-ups, the emotional core). Shots 3, 6 are 50mm wider (the physical action of standing, the motion of walking). Handheld throughout, alive, breath-driven, the operator a fourth presence. Clean direct cuts. Frame constantly drifts, reactive but never jittery. Shallow depth of field f/1.8-f/2. Cold blue-grey palette, warm oil-lamp accents, soft purple glow accents from the hoverboard's thruster cores. The clip is built around character close-ups — four of the six shots are 100mm intimate framings letting us read every micro-expression.

ACTING:
- Roco: comes back to consciousness slowly, physically diminished. His exchange with Jax is wordless but tactile — leans in, accepts help, nods. His "Let's move" is the moment he reclaims himself: looking forward, jaw setting, decision before recovery is complete.
- Jax: the caregiver in this beat. Concerned but functional. His "Hey, look at me" and "Easy. Easy." are quiet, intimate, brotherly. His final shoulder pat is a small physical confirmation: you're back, we're moving.
- Rain: the watcher. She doesn't intervene physically — she lets Jax handle Roco. Her "You okay?" is a check-in, not an emergency. Her relief is private, contained. She holds the hoverboard the whole time as if it's not even there — it's an extension of her, not a prop she's noticing.

AUDIO: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drops with chamber reverb, oil-lamp crackles, breathing and clothing rustle, all with significant chamber reverb. Tactile sounds: scrape of grip, Jax's grunt of effort hauling Roco, Roco's exhale into Jax's jacket, Jax's pat on Roco's shoulder, three pairs of boots in staggered rhythm. Dialogue: Jax's "Hey. Hey, look at me," Jax's "There you are," Jax's "Easy. Easy," Rain's "You okay?", Roco's "Let's move" — all with strong reverb tails. No music.

PACING: intimate and slow in the recovery (Shots 1-3), close watchful beat from Rain (Shot 4), Roco's reclaiming of himself (Shot 5), then the shift into forward motion (Shot 6). Six-beat structure. The clip is held together by character close-ups and physical contact — the audience reads the relationship between the three of them through faces, hands, and small tactile gestures more than through dialogue.

## 中文对照 (zh)

从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔古老圣殿（sanctum），恢复与前行动作序列，12 秒，16:9 anamorphic，写实摄影（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，深冷的蓝灰基调辅以暖琥珀色油灯点缀，戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro），无音乐。灵感源自《人类之子》（Children of Men）与《荒野猎人》（The Revenant）——手持（handheld）动能化的亲密感、以特写驱动表演。

参照 Image 1 中那位银白浅发、眼角带疤、灰色连帽夹克的女性。
参照 Image 2 中那位漂金寸头、深肤色、透明眼镜、深色夹克的男性。
参照 Image 3 中那位深色乱发、留须、鼻梁贴红色绷带、深色连帽夹克的男性。
参照 Image 4 中那架哑光黑悬浮滑板（hoverboard），配备泛紫色光芒的推进器核心。

置景（Setting）：从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔洞窟圣殿，两侧墙上刻着高耸的石柱面，渐隐没入两侧的黑暗，地面为不规则的石板（flagstone）地，沿墙有两列平行的小油灯摇曳着暖琥珀色光，天花板在头顶高处隐没于阴影，尘土弥漫。Roco 面朝下躺在侧墙旁的石板地上，满身尘土，身体与墙平行。Jax 跪在他身旁，夹克磨旧，眼镜略微歪斜。Rain 站在几米开外，左手把悬浮滑板拿在身侧，注视着他们。

镜头 1（0-2 秒）：手持极低机位特写，对准 Roco 紧贴在石板地上的脸，100mm 长焦，对他紧闭的眼做剃刀般极薄的焦点。摄影机躺在离他脸数英寸的地面上。沾满尘土的侧颊贴着冰冷的石头。他的眼皮颤动，随即睁开——瞳孔在黑暗中扩张，缓缓对焦。意识回归时他眉心有一下微微的抽动。他轻轻呼出一口气，呼吸在冷空气中对着石板地泛起雾气。画面随亲密的持握漂移而起伏。画面之外上方：Jax 的声音，低、近、几乎耳语：「Hey. Hey, look at me.」Roco 的眼睛循声向上。Audio：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响（chamber reverb）的水滴声，Roco 缓慢粗重的吸气因贴近而被放大，Jax 俯身时夹克轻柔的窸窣，轻微的油灯噼啪声。

镜头 2（2-3.5 秒）：切至手持极特写，对准 Roco 上方 Jax 的脸，100mm，从下方来的四分之三角度。Jax 俯下身进入画面，鼻梁上的眼镜歪斜，眉头紧绷透着关切，满身尘土。他的目光在画面外 Roco 的脸上搜寻，寻找回应。他又说了一遍，更轻些：「There you are.」一小口释然的气息。随后他的表情转为专注——他正要行动。摄影机亲密地起伏，靠近他脸的距离把那份关切细致地呈现给我们。Audio：他的台词带室内混响，他释然的呼气，他沉着的呼吸，底下是环境低鸣。

镜头 3（3.5-6.5 秒）：切至两名男性身旁手持低机位升起的镜头，50mm 更广的镜头。Jax 探下手，稳稳抓住 Roco 的肩，再以有力的锁握握住他的前臂——手指扣锁在腕部，那是种「我抓住你了」的握法。他拉。Roco 发出一声闷哼，撑到手上，再撑到膝上，尘土从夹克上簌簌落下。Jax 后移重心，用自己的身体作平衡，在一气呵成的动作中把 Roco 猛拉站起。摄影机随他们一气呵成地升起，操作员确实站起身。Roco 脚下踉跄，重重靠进 Jax 的肩里寻求平衡——脸短暂地埋进 Jax 的夹克，双眼紧闭，平复着呼吸。Jax 的手移到 Roco 后颈，稳住他，另一只手仍握着 Roco 的手臂。他给他一拍。「Easy. Easy.」Roco 靠着他说着话点着头，缓过来。画面漂移又校正，生气勃勃。Audio：Roco 抓在 Jax 夹克上的刮擦声，Jax 拉拽时用力的闷哼，Roco 靴底在石板地上的刮擦声，Roco 沉重的呼气呼进 Jax 的肩里，Jax 那两句「Easy」低而近，两人呼吸沉重，环境低鸣，室内混响渐深。

镜头 4（6.5-8 秒）：切至手持中景特写，对准 Rain，100mm，四分之三侧面。她把悬浮滑板拿在身侧，紫色推进器光晕作柔和点缀。她的脸在冷光中难以读懂——平静却警觉，目光锁定在纵深处模糊的两个男人身上。那种遥远而冷的光掠过她眼角那道疤与前脸。见她看到 Roco 直立起来，一丝释然的闪念掠过她的神情。她开口，声音低而直接，带室内混响：「You okay?」摄影机在她周围起伏，微妙地渐渐靠近。Audio：她受控的呼吸，带悠长混响尾音的问句，环境低鸣，远处油灯的噼啪声。

镜头 5（8-9.5 秒）：切至手持极特写，对准 Roco 的脸，100mm，满身尘土，鼻上的红色绷带清晰可见，眼睛仍在适应。他借 Jax 的肩微微撑起身来，自己站稳。他呼吸沉重，但此刻平稳。他没有看 Rain。他的目光向前，落入画面外幽暗的圣殿——锁定了某处远处。他的下颌绷紧。顿了一拍后，他的回答平平地、决然地传来，带室内混响：「Let's move.」画面在他脸上散发着亲密的气息。Audio：他的台词带强烈的混响尾音，他的呼吸渐趋平稳，环境低鸣。

镜头 6（9.5-12 秒）：切至手持中景紧凑三人镜头，自低正前方角度，50mm。Roco 居中，刚站稳，目光向前。Jax 在画面右侧，把手从 Roco 的脖颈上拿开，在他肩上干脆地拍了一下——确认他没事——然后退开，给他让出路。Rain 在画面左侧，悬浮滑板在身侧。Roco 向前迈出进入厅堂的第一步，然后是第二步——靴子稳稳踏在石板地上。Jax 在他的右侧跟上并肩。Rain 落后一拍，悬浮滑板随她的步伐轻轻摆动。摄影机以他们的步速向后移动（dolly），从腰际以上紧凑地框住他们朝摄影机走来。在他们身后以极深浅焦的模糊中：圣殿幽暗的深处退去，油灯的连排向远方延伸。画面随操作员的呼吸漂移，与他们并肩而行。Audio：Jax 拍在 Roco 肩上的轻拍，Roco 的第一只靴子踏在石板地上，然后是第二只，Jax 跟上脚步，Rain 一拍之后加入——三双靴子以错落有致的节奏带室内混响，呼吸渐入走路的节律，底下是环境低鸣。

CAMERA：长焦 50mm-100mm。镜头 1、2、4、5 为 100mm（特写，情感核心）。镜头 3、6 为更广的 50mm（站起的身体动作、行走的动作）。全程手持，生气勃勃、由呼吸驱动，操作员是第四位在场者。干脆利落的直接切。画面持续漂移，灵敏却绝不抖动。浅景深 f/1.8-f/2。冷蓝灰基调，暖琥珀油灯点缀，悬浮滑板推进器核心泛起的柔紫色光辉点缀。本片段围绕角色特写搭建——六个镜头里有四个是 100mm 的亲密取景，让我们读到每一个微表情。

ACTING（表演）：
- Roco：缓慢回归意识，身体上虚弱无力。他与 Jax 的交流无声却充满触觉——靠过去、接受帮助、点头。他的「Let's move」是他重新夺回自己的那一刻：看向前方、下颌绷紧、决断先于康复完成。
- Jax：这一拍的照料者。关切却又高效。他的「Hey, look at me」与「Easy. Easy.」安静、亲密、像兄弟。「他的最后一拍肩头，是一个小小的身体确认：你回来了，我们走。」
- Rain：观望者。她不在身体上介入——让 Jax 去料理 Roco。她的「You okay?」是一次探问，而非紧急呼救。她的释然是私密、收敛的。她从头到尾拿着悬浮滑板，仿佛它根本不在——它是她的一种延伸，而不是她在注意的道具。

AUDIO：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响的水滴声，油灯噼啪声，呼吸与衣物沙沙，全都带显著的室内混响。触觉声响：握持的刮擦声，Jax 拉动 Roco 时用力的闷哼，Roco 呼进 Jax 夹克的呼气，Jax 拍在 Roco 肩上的轻拍，三双靴子错落有致的节奏。台词：Jax 的「Hey. Hey, look at me,」Jax 的「There you are,」Jax 的「Easy. Easy,」Rain 的「You okay?」、Roco 的「Let's move」——全部带强烈的混响尾音。无音乐。

PACING：恢复过程（镜头 1-3）亲密而缓慢，Rain 那拍（镜头 4）是贴近警觉的关注，Roco 重新夺得自己（镜头 5），随后转向前行动作（镜头 6）。六拍结构。本片段以角色特写与身体接触收束在一起——观众通过面孔、双手与微小的触觉姿态，而非通过台词，去读懂三人之间的关系。
