Jax's slingshot misfires from chamber tremor, plastid bomb arcs up toward upper darkness, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig at chest height, body-driven tracking. EXTREME trembling INTENSIFYING for the misfire moment.

Jax loaded with the plastid ball in the slingshot pouch, drawing back the elastic to full tension, eye closed behind glasses for aim, body steady. He mutters: "...one shot, one shot..." He aims at the guardian off-frame above, finger ready to release. Audio: the creak of taut elastic, his held breath, chamber rumble, off-frame guardian deep red hum.

Shot 2 (7-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Jax mid-release, 85mm, f/1.4. As he releases the elastic, a HEAVY chamber TREMOR hits — the floor jolting hard under his feet from the guardian's nearby step, his body jerked sideways at the moment of release, the slingshot recoiling at an off-angle, his aim THROWN. He stands frozen in disbelief at his missed shot. Audio: the elastic SNAP, the heavy chamber WHUMP from the tremor jolt, his sharp inhale of recognition, the bomb's whistle ascending, his quiet "...shit", chamber settling.

PACING: 15-second misfire setup. Aiming with intent (0-7s), tremor jolts aim, bomb fires wide upward toward darkness (7-15s).
