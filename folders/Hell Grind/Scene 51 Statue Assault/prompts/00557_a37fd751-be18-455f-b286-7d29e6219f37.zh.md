## 原文 (source)

Vast ancient sanctum carved from natural rock, recovery and forward motion sequence at the sealed doorway, 12 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, deep cool blue-grey palette with warm amber oil-lamp accents, dramatic chiaroscuro, no music. Inspired by Children of Men and The Revenant — handheld kinetic intimacy, close-up driven acting.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.
Refer to the man with dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 3.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 4.

Setting: vast cavern-sanctum carved from natural rock, towering carved stone column-faces receding into darkness on both side walls, irregular flagstone floor, two parallel lines of small oil-lamps flickering warm amber along the walls, ceiling lost in shadow far above, dust-hazed atmosphere from the recent slab impact. The action happens RIGHT AT THE SEALED DOORWAY at the entrance end of the sanctum — the massive stone slab is fully closed behind the trio, filling the back wall as a flat dark mass, dust still settling around its base. Roco lies face-down on the flagstones at the foot of the slab, just inside the threshold, body angled, dust-covered. Jax is already crouched beside him, hand on his shoulder, having rushed over. Rain is crouched a half-meter behind Jax, hoverboard set down on the flagstone beside her, leaning in close.

Shot 1 (0-2s): Handheld extreme low-angle close on Roco's face pressed against the flagstone right at the foot of the sealed slab, 100mm long lens, razor-thin focus on his eye. Camera lies on the floor inches from his face. Dust-streaked cheek against cold stone. His eye is OPEN, conscious, alert — catching his breath after the slide. Pupil sharp in the dark, jaw tight. His breath comes ragged but controlled, fogging faintly against the flagstone in the cold air. Behind him, the sealed slab fills the deep background as a dark mass, dust still drifting in the air around it. Off-screen above, Jax's voice — dust-rattled and a little wild with adrenaline: "Damn, bro — that was close!" Roco's eye flicks up toward the voice, sharp and present. Frame breathes with intimate handheld drift, more agitated than calm. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, the dying reverb of the slab impact still fading, dust settling, Roco's controlled ragged breath, Jax's voice with chamber reverb, faint oil-lamp crackle.

Shot 2 (2-3.5s): Cut to handheld close on Jax above Roco, 85mm, three-quarter angle. Jax's face dust-streaked, eyes wide behind his glasses, brow lifted with adrenalized relief — he's smiling-not-smiling, the way you do when something almost killed your friend. He huffs out a laugh that isn't really a laugh, shaking his head once. Behind Jax's shoulder in shallow-focus blur, Rain leans in close, her face tight with worry, dust-streaked, eyes locked on Roco. He claps Roco's shoulder firmly, says: "Get up, man, c'mon." His hand goes to grip Roco's forearm. Audio: Jax's first line tail with chamber reverb, his huffed laugh, his second line urgent and warm at once, both Jax and Rain breathing audibly fast with adrenaline, ambient drone underneath, the slab's dying reverb still in the air.

Shot 3 (3.5-6.5s): Cut to handheld low-rising shot beside the group at the doorway, 50mm wider lens. Jax grips Roco's forearm — fingers locking around the wrist. Rain moves in from the other side, picks up the hoverboard from the floor in her left hand, and grips Roco's other forearm with her right. They HAUL him up together, one on each side, fast and urgent. Roco grunts, pushes off the floor with his core, comes up onto his knees, then to his feet in one continuous motion, dust falling from his jacket. He doesn't stagger — he lands solid, balanced, takes his own weight immediately. The slab fills the dark background behind them, sealed and silent. Jax holds Roco's arm a beat longer, scanning his face fast, eyes still wide with adrenaline. Rain holds his other arm worried, eyes searching him. Roco shrugs off both their grips at once with a controlled motion — not rough, just done. He stands on his own. Frame more agitated than smooth, alive with adrenaline. Audio: scrape of grips, Jax's grunt of effort, Rain's small sound of effort with him, scrape of Roco's boots finding flagstone, all three breathing heavy and fast, ambient drone, dust still settling.

Shot 4 (6.5-8s): Cut to handheld medium close on Rain, 100mm, three-quarter profile. The sealed slab is visible behind her in shallow-focus blur as a flat dark mass. She holds the hoverboard at her side now, breathing hard from the haul, dust-streaked. Her face is tight with worry — she watches Roco's face off-frame, eyes searching it, brow knit. Her breath catches once before she speaks, voice low and uneven, the worry plain, with chamber reverb: "You good?" Camera breathes around her, drifts subtly closer. Audio: her ragged breathing, the question with audible worry, long reverb tail, ambient drone, the slab's last reverb fading.

Shot 5 (8-9.5s): Cut to handheld extreme close-up on Roco's face, 100mm, dust-streaked, the red bandage on his nose visible. He's already standing on his own, breathing controlled — faster than rest, but level. His face is closed off, jaw set, eyes hard. He doesn't look at Rain. He doesn't acknowledge the worry. His eyes go forward, past her, fixing on something distant in the sanctum off-screen. His answer comes flat, cold, dismissive of the question itself, with chamber reverb: "Let's move." Frame breathes intimately on his face — the lack of warmth visible in his stillness. Audio: his line clipped, no softness in it, his breath, ambient drone.

Shot 6 (9.5-12s): Cut to handheld medium tight three-shot on the trio from a low front angle, 50mm. The sealed slab is visible behind them in deep shallow-focus blur. Roco center, eyes forward, already starting to step away from the doorway into the chamber — moving before the others, leading. Jax right of frame, exhales a small "shit, okay" under his breath at Roco's coldness, exchanges a fast look with Rain off-frame — eyebrows up: what's with him? Rain left of frame, hoverboard at her side, her eyes still on Roco's profile with worry, then she pulls herself together and falls in. Roco takes the first step into the chamber away from the slab. Jax falls in on his right. Rain a beat behind, the hoverboard swinging slightly with her stride. Camera dollies backward at their pace, framing them tight from waist-up walking toward camera, the sealed slab receding behind them. Frame drifts with operator's breath, walking with them. Audio: Roco's first boot striking flagstone (sharp, decisive), Jax's muttered "shit, okay," Jax falling in step a beat later, Rain joining last — three pairs of boots in staggered rhythm with chamber reverb, breathing settling, ambient drone underneath.

CAMERA: long lens 50mm-100mm. Shots 1, 4, 5 are 100mm intimate close-ups. Shot 2 is 85mm close. Shots 3 and 6 are 50mm wider. Handheld throughout, more agitated and adrenalized than calm. Clean direct cuts. Frame constantly drifts, reactive but never jittery. Shallow depth of field f/1.8-f/2. Cold blue-grey palette, warm oil-lamp accents, soft purple glow accents from the hoverboard's thruster cores. The sealed stone slab fills the background of multiple shots, anchoring the action at the doorway.

ACTING:
- Roco: conscious from the first frame, catching his breath after the slide. Lands solid when they pull him up, takes his own weight immediately, shrugs off their grips. Cold and closed off: doesn't look at Rain, doesn't soften, "Let's move" is dismissive. Already mission-mode while the others are still emotionally caught up. Leads forward without waiting.
- Jax: adrenalized, loose, the kind of guy who diffuses tension by being himself. "Damn, bro — that was close!" with a huffed laugh, then "Get up, man, c'mon." while clapping his shoulder. His worry comes through energy, not gravity. The muttered "shit, okay" in Shot 6 is the same character — the way he reads Roco's coldness, processes it, and moves with it. He's the one who'll say what everyone's thinking.
- Rain: visibly worried throughout. Dropped the hoverboard immediately when Roco came through. Hauls with Jax. Her "You good?" is uneven, the worry audible. Eyes linger on Roco's face. She's the one carrying the worry seriously while Jax processes through banter.

AUDIO: deep ambient cavern drone, the dying reverb of the slab impact through the early shots, dust settling sounds, oil-lamp crackles, breathing and clothing rustle. Tactile sounds: scrape of two grips on Roco's forearms, Jax's clap on his shoulder, grunts of effort hauling, Roco's exhale, three pairs of boots starting in staggered rhythm. Dialogue: Jax's "Damn, bro — that was close!" with huffed laugh, Jax's "Get up, man, c'mon.", Jax's muttered "shit, okay," Rain's "You good?" (uneven, worried), Roco's "Let's move" (cold, clipped, dismissive) — all with strong chamber reverb tails. No music.

PACING: kinetic and adrenalized through the haul (Shots 1-3) — fast, urgent, Jax letting off steam through banter, Rain carrying the worry. Shifts in Shot 4 with Rain's worried question. Shot 5 lands the tonal contrast — Roco cold while she's still scared. Shot 6: Jax mutters his read of the situation, the trio falls into formation behind Roco's lead.

## 中文对照 (zh)

从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔古老圣殿（sanctum），于封死门道处的恢复与前行动作序列，12 秒，16:9 anamorphic，写实摄影（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，深冷的蓝灰基调辅以暖琥珀色油灯点缀，戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro），无音乐。灵感源自《人类之子》（Children of Men）与《荒野猎人》（The Revenant）——手持（handheld）动能化的亲密感、以特写驱动表演。

参照 Image 1 中那位银白浅发、眼角带疤、灰色连帽夹克的女性。
参照 Image 2 中那位漂金寸头、深肤色、透明眼镜、深色夹克的男性。
参照 Image 3 中那位深色乱发、留须、鼻梁贴红色绷带、深色连帽夹克的男性。
参照 Image 4 中那架哑光黑悬浮滑板（hoverboard），配备泛紫色光芒的推进器核心。

置景（Setting）：从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔洞窟圣殿，两侧墙上刻着高耸的石柱面，渐隐没入两侧的黑暗，地面为不规则的石板（flagstone）地，沿墙有两列平行的小油灯摇曳着暖琥珀色光，天花板在头顶高处隐没于阴影，因方才石板冲击而尘土弥漫。动作就发生在圣殿入口端的封死门道正处——巨大的石板门已在小队三人身后完全关闭，以一片平直的暗色巨块填满后墙，底座四周尘土仍在沉降。Roco 面朝下躺在石板地脚的石板地面上，身体斜躺着，满身尘土。Jax 已经蹲在他身旁，手搭在他肩上，先前疾冲过来。Rain 蹲在 Jax 身后半米处，悬浮滑板放在身侧的石板地上，俯身靠近。

镜头 1（0-2 秒）：手持极低机位特写，对准 Roco 紧贴在封死石板脚下石板地的脸，100mm 长焦，对他的眼睛做剃刀般极薄的焦点。摄影机躺在离他脸数英寸的地面上。沾满尘土的侧颊贴着冰冷的石头。他的眼是睁开的，清醒、警觉——滑行后正平复呼吸。在黑暗中瞳孔锐利，下颌紧扣。他的呼吸粗重却有节制，在冷空气中对着石板地泛起淡淡雾气。在他身后，封死的石板门作为大片暗色巨块填满纵深背景，四周尘土仍在空中飘落。画面之外上方传来 Jax 的声音——被灰尘弄得沙哑、又因肾上腺素而有些狂野：「Damn, bro — that was close!」Roco 的眼睛朝那声音的方向锐利而清醒地一瞥。画面随亲密的持握漂移而起伏，躁动甚于平静。Audio：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，石板冲击的奄奄回响仍在消散，尘土沉降，Roco 受控的粗重呼吸，Jax 带室内混响（chamber reverb）的声音，轻微的油灯噼啪声。

镜头 2（2-3.5 秒）：切至手持特写，对准 Roco 上方的 Jax，85mm，四分之三角度。Jax 的脸沾满尘土，镜片后的双眼圆睁，眉毛因肾上腺素式的释然而挑起——他在似笑非笑，就像有人差点要了你朋友的命时你做出的那种表情。他呼出一声其实算不得笑的轻笑，摇了一下头。在 Jax 肩后浅焦模糊处，Rain 靠得很近，她的脸因担忧而紧绷，满身尘土，目光锁定在 Roco 身上。他用力拍了拍 Roco 的肩，说道：「Get up, man, c'mon.」他的手伸去握住 Roco 的前臂。Audio：Jax 第一句台词的尾音带室内混响，他那声呼出的轻笑，他的第二句既急迫又温暖，Jax 与 Rain 两人都因肾上腺素呼吸得明显急促，底下是环境低鸣，石板奄奄的回响仍残留在空气中。

镜头 3（3.5-6.5 秒）：切至门道旁手持低机位升起的镜头，50mm 更广的镜头。Jax 握住 Roco 的前臂——手指扣锁在腕部。Rain 从另一侧介入，左手从地上抬起悬浮滑板，右手握住 Roco 的另一只前臂。他们一同把他拉起，一边一个，快速而急迫。Roco 发出一声闷哼，用核心发力撑离地面，跪起，再在一气呵成的动作中站到脚上，尘土从他夹克上簌簌落下。他没有踉跄——落地稳固、重心平稳，随即承受起自己的体重。石板以封死而静默的形状填满他们身后的黑暗背景。Jax 多握了 Roco 手臂一拍，快速扫视他的脸，眼里肾上腺素未褪的圆睁未歇。Rain 担忧地握着他另一只手臂，目光在他身上搜寻。Roco 以一个受控的动作同时抖开他们两人的握持——不算粗鲁，只是到此为止。他靠自己站稳。画面躁动多于平稳，充满肾上腺素。Audio：握持的摩擦声，Jax 用力时的闷哼，Rain 与之同步的细小用力声，Roco 靴底在石板地上的刮擦声，三人呼吸沉重而急促，环境低鸣，尘土仍在沉降。

镜头 4（6.5-8 秒）：切至手持中景特写，对准 Rain，100mm，四分之三侧面。封死的石板在她身后以浅焦模糊的一片暗色巨块可见。她现在把悬浮滑板拿在身侧，用力拉拽后气喘吁吁，满身尘土。她的脸因担忧而绷紧——她注视着画面外 Roco 的脸，目光搜寻，眉头紧蹙。她开口前呼吸顿了一下，声音低而起伏，担忧显而易见，带室内混响：「You good?」摄影机在她周围起伏，微妙地渐渐靠近。Audio：她粗重的呼吸，带可闻担忧的问句，悠长的混响尾音，环境低鸣，石板最后的回响渐渐消散。

镜头 5（8-9.5 秒）：切至手持极特写，对准 Roco 的脸，100mm，满身尘土，鼻上的红色绷带清晰可见。他已经靠自己站定，呼吸受控——比平时快，却平稳。他的脸近乎封闭，下颌绷紧，目光冷硬。他没有看 Rain。他不理会那份担忧。他的目光向前，越过她，锁定在圣殿画面外某处远处。他的回答平平地、冷冷地、对问题本身近乎轻蔑地传来，带室内混响：「Let's move.」画面在他脸上散发着亲密的气息——那份缺乏的温度在他的静止中清晰可见。Audio：他的台词干脆利落，毫无柔软，他的呼吸，环境低鸣。

镜头 6（9.5-12 秒）：切至手持中景紧凑三人镜头，自低正前方角度，50mm。封死的石板在他们身后以极深浅焦的模糊可见。Roco 居中，目光向前，已经开始从门道朝厅堂内迈步——比旁人先动，领在前头。Jax 在画面右侧，对 Roco 的冷淡低声呼出一句「shit, okay」，与画面外的 Rain 飞快交换一眼——眉毛上扬：他怎么了？Rain 在画面左侧，悬浮滑板在身侧，她的目光仍带着担忧停留在 Roco 的侧影上，随后她打起精神，跟了上去。Roco 迈出离石板进入厅堂的第一步。Jax 在他的右侧跟上。Rain 落后一拍，悬浮滑板随她的步伐轻轻摆动。摄影机以他们的步速向后移动（dolly），从腰际以上紧凑地框住他们朝摄影机走来，封死的石板在他们身后退去。画面随操作员的呼吸漂移，与他们并肩而行。Audio：Roco 的第一只靴子踏在石板地上（尖锐、果决），Jax 咕哝的「shit, okay」，Jax 一拍之后跟上脚步，Rain 最后加入——三双靴子以错落有致的节奏带室内混响，呼吸渐趋平稳，底下是环境低鸣。

CAMERA：长焦 50mm-100mm。镜头 1、4、5 为 100mm 的亲密特写。镜头 2 为 85mm 特写。镜头 3 与 6 为更广的 50mm。全程手持，躁动与肾上腺素多于平稳。干脆利落的直接切。画面持续漂移，灵敏却绝不抖动。浅景深 f/1.8-f/2。冷蓝灰基调，暖琥珀油灯点缀，悬浮滑板推进器核心泛起的柔紫色光辉点缀。封死的石板门填满多个镜头的背景，将动作锚定在门道处。

ACTING（表演）：
- Roco：从第一帧起便有意识，滑行后正平复呼吸。被拉起时落地稳固，随即承受自己的体重，抖开他们的握持。冷淡而封闭：不看 Rain，不软化，「Let's move」轻蔑决绝。当旁人仍陷在情绪中时，他已进入任务模式。不等候便向前领路。
- Jax：肾上腺素充沛、松弛，是那种靠做自己来化解紧张的人。「Damn, bro — that was close!」配一声呼出的轻笑，接着一边拍肩一边说「Get up, man, c'mon.」他的担忧通过能量而非沉重感流露。镜头 6 里那句咕哝的「shit, okay」是同一个角色——他读出 Roco 的冷淡，消化它，并随之而动。他是那个会把众人所想说出来的人。
- Rain：全程明显担忧。Roco 闯过来时她立刻丢下悬浮滑板。与 Jax 一同拉拽。她的「You good?」起伏不定，担忧可闻。目光在 Roco 脸上流连。她是以认真的方式背负担忧的那个人，而 Jax 则用调侃来消化。

AUDIO：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，前几镜里石板冲击的奄奄回响，尘土沉降的声响，油灯噼啪声，呼吸与衣物沙沙。触觉声响：两处握持在 Roco 前臂上的刮擦、Jax 拍在他肩上的声响、拉拽时用力的闷哼、Roco 的呼气、三双靴子以错落有致的节奏起步。台词：Jax 的笑配「Damn, bro — that was close!」、Jax 的「Get up, man, c'mon.」、Jax 咕哝的「shit, okay」、Rain 的「You good?」（起伏、担忧）、Roco 的「Let's move」（冷淡、干脆、轻蔑）——全部带强烈的室内混响尾音。无音乐。

PACING：拉拽过程中（镜头 1-3）动能充沛、充满肾上腺素——快速、急迫，Jax 借调侃释放压力，Rain 背负担忧。镜头 4 里随 Rain 那句担忧的问句而转折。镜头 5 落定基调反差——他冷淡，而她仍惊魂未定。镜头 6：Jax 咕哝出自己的研判，小队在 Roco 的带领下结成队形。
