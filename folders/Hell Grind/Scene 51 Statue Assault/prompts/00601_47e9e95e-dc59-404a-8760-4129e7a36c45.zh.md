## 原文 (source)

Vast ancient sanctum interior, first-arrival awe sequence, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, ambient temple drone only — no music.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.
Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 3.

VISUAL STYLE: cinematic anamorphic, long-lens telephoto cinematography, shallow depth of field f/2, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from massive shafts of god-rays piercing down from openings high above, deep blacks with halated warm gold highlights on stone, atmospheric dust motes drifting through the light shafts, heavily desaturated palette with warm amber accents. Inspired by Villeneuve's Dune temple interiors and Snyder's Watchmen — vast, sacred, weighted.

LOCATION: a colossal stone sanctum, ceiling lost in shadow far above, massive stone columns lining the sides covered in ancient carved script, polished stone floor with faint geometric inlays, drifting dust haze illuminated by vertical light shafts. At the far end of the chamber, hundreds of meters away: a colossal seated stone statue of an ancient figure, its scale so vast that a single human MONK seated cross-legged on the statue's open upturned palm appears as a tiny silhouette. The monk is unmoving, meditating. Soft warm candlelight glows around the monk's small figure on the palm.

CHARACTERS: Rain, Jax, and Roco have just stepped through an entrance at the near end of the sanctum. They stand close together, dwarfed by the space. Rain holds a sleek hoverboard tucked under her left arm. All three are dust-covered from their previous passage, jackets scuffed, faces tired but alert.

Shot 1 (0-3s): EXTREME WIDE establishing shot from low-angle behind the three characters at the threshold. Camera at floor level, looking past the trio's silhouettes into the vast sanctum. The three figures occupy only the lower-left quarter of the frame as small silhouettes; the rest of the frame is the immense interior — columns receding into haze, light shafts cutting down, the distant statue barely readable at the far end. The monk on the palm is a single point of warm light. Camera holds completely still. Audio: the heavy thud of the entrance behind them sealing off, then a long silence broken only by deep ambient temple drone, distant air moving, a single drop of water somewhere. Their breathing audible, hushed.

Shot 2 (3-5.5s): SLOW PUSH-IN on a medium-wide three-shot from the front, low angle looking up past them toward the ceiling implied above. The three faces tilt upward in unison — heads slowly lifting, eyes tracking up the height of the columns, mouths slightly parted. Rain in the center, hoverboard held loose at her side now. Roco to her right, Jax to her left. The light shafts catch their faces in moving slats of gold as dust drifts through. Camera pushes in 30cm over the duration of the shot, very slow. Audio: continued temple drone, their breathing, the soft creak of Rain's jacket as she shifts.

Shot 3 (5.5-7.5s): EXTREME CLOSE-UP on Roco's face in profile, eyes wide, pupils tracking upward then settling forward. A single dust mote crosses the focal plane in front of his eye. He swallows. He speaks, voice low, almost a whisper, the reverb of the sanctum carrying it: "Is this the place?" The line lingers in the air, echoing off distant stone. Camera completely still. Shallow focus, his eye sharp, the rest of his face falling into soft blur.

Shot 4 (7.5-9.5s): MATCHING EXTREME CLOSE-UP on Rain's face, three-quarter angle. Her gaze is forward, fixed on the distant statue, calm and certain in contrast to Roco's wonder. A faint, almost imperceptible nod. She answers, voice quiet but firm, also reverberating: "That's right." She does not look at Roco when she says it. Her hand tightens on the hoverboard at her side. Camera completely still. The light catches the scar by her eye.

Shot 5 (9.5-12s): WIDE LOW-ANGLE tracking shot from the floor as the three begin walking forward into the sanctum. Camera mounted low, dollying backward at their walking pace, keeping them framed in the lower third of the image with the colossal statue and the tiny monk visible in the deep background between Rain and Roco. The scale crushing — the figures small, the statue immense, the monk a distant flame. Their footsteps echo softly on polished stone. Rain center, hoverboard now held in front of her at chest height, unconsciously. Roco left, Jax right, both still glancing upward at the columns as they walk. Slow, weighted pace — not striding, almost reverent.

Shot 6 (12-15s): EXTREME WIDE high-angle shot from near the ceiling, looking down. The three characters are tiny figures crossing the vast polished floor, leaving faint dust trails in the light shafts. The statue dominates the lower half of the frame, the monk on its palm a single warm pinpoint. The geometric inlays of the floor become visible from this height — concentric patterns the characters are unknowingly walking across. Camera completely still, holding for the full 3 seconds. Audio: temple drone deepens, their distant footsteps barely audible, the suggestion of the monk's slow breathing carried across the impossible distance.

CINEMATOGRAPHY: long lens 85mm-200mm equivalent throughout, no handheld shake — this is contemplative, weighted, reverent. All camera moves are slow and deliberate (push-in, dolly back, hold). Most shots are completely still. Shallow depth of field f/2. Strong vertical god-rays from above with visible volumetric haze. Color palette: deep cool shadows, warm amber highlights, desaturated overall.

AUDIO: deep ambient temple drone underneath everything, distant water drops, faint wind in high spaces, characters' breathing and quiet footsteps with heavy reverb, two whispered lines of dialogue with significant natural reverb tail. NO MUSIC. NO SCORE.

PACING: slow, weighted, reverent. Hold every shot longer than feels comfortable. Awe through stillness, scale through silhouette.

## 中文对照 (zh)

辽阔古老圣殿内部，初次抵达的敬畏镜头，15 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕（anamorphic）2.39:1，写实摄影，厚重 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，仅环境式寺庙氛围声（temple drone）——无音乐。

参照 Image 1 中那位银色金发、眼下有疤、着灰色连帽夹克的女子。
参照 Image 2 中那位漂白黄色寸头、深色皮肤、戴透明防护镜、着深色夹克的男人。
参照 Image 3 中那位深色乱发、淡胡须、鼻上有红色绷带、着深色连帽夹克的男人。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：电影感变形宽银幕，长焦远摄摄影，浅景深 f/2，戏剧性明暗对比，来自高处开口直射而下的大幅神迹光束（god-rays），深邃阴影与石上晕开的光晕暖金亮点，飘移的大气尘埃在光束中浮动，重度去饱和色板带暖琥珀点缀。灵感来自 Villeneuve 的《沙丘》神庙内部与 Snyder 的《守望者》——浩瀚、神圣、有重量。

场景（LOCATION）：一座巨型的石造圣殿，天花板消失在遥远上方的阴影里，巨型石柱沿两侧排开，覆有古老刻凿的铭文，光亮的石地带有隐约的几何镶嵌，被垂直光束照亮的飘尘薄雾。在厅堂远端、数百米之外：一尊巨型石质神祇坐像，其尺度之巨，使得一位盘坐在雕像张开朝上的掌心上的小小人形僧侣只是一抹微小剪影。僧侣静止不动，正在冥想。柔和的暖烛光在掌上僧侣的小小身形周围环绕。

角色（CHARACTERS）：Rain、Jax 与 Roco 刚从圣殿近端的入口走进来。他们紧挨而立，被空间衬托得渺小。Rain 把一块流线型悬浮板夹在左腋下。三人因先前穿行而满身尘土、夹克磨损，面容疲惫却警觉。

第 1 镜（0-3 秒）：极宽的全景定场镜头，低机位，自三人身后的门槛处。摄影机位于地面高度，越过三人的剪影望向辽阔的圣殿。三个身影在画面中只占左下四分之一，作为微小的剪影；画面其余部分是浩瀚的内部——柱群没入烟霭、光束切割而下、远方雕像在远端尽头勉强可读。掌上的僧侣是一点温暖的光。摄影机完全静止持住。音频：身后入口封合的重重闷响，然后是长久的寂静，只被深邃的环境寺庙声打破，远处气流移动，某处落下一滴水。他们的呼吸可闻，低声屏息。

第 2 镜（3-5.5 秒）：缓慢推近，对准一张中宽三人镜，自前方，低机位仰视越过他们望向上面隐含的天花板。三张脸齐向上仰——头部缓缓抬起，目光沿柱子的高度上溯，嘴微微张开。Rain 居中，悬浮板此刻松散地垂在身侧。Roco 在她右侧，Jax 在她左侧。尘埃飘过时，光束以流动的金色光板扫过他们的脸。摄影机在镜头时长内以极其缓慢的速度推近 30 厘米。音频：持续的寺庙声，他们的呼吸，Rain 移身时夹克轻微的窸窣。

第 3 镜（5.5-7.5 秒）：极特写拍 Roco 的脸，侧面，双眼睁大，瞳孔向上追踪后落定于前。一粒尘埃横过他眼前的对焦面。他咽了口唾沫。他开口，声音低沉，近乎耳语，被圣殿的混响托送："Is this the place?（就是这里吗？）" 话语悬在空气中，在远处石壁上回响。摄影机完全静止。浅对焦，他的眼睛锐利，脸其余部分落入柔焦。

第 4 镜（7.5-9.5 秒）：匹配极特写拍 Rain 的脸，四分之三角度。她的目光向前，定在远方雕像上，与 Roco 的惊叹相对照的平静与笃定。一个隐约、几乎不可察觉的颔首。她应答，声音轻而坚定，同样带着回响："That's right.（没错。）" 她说话时不看 Roco。她身侧的手收紧了悬浮板。摄影机完全静止。光线捕捉到她眼旁的疤。

第 5 镜（9.5-12 秒）：当三人开始向圣殿深处走去时，自地面低机位的宽全景跟踪镜头。摄影机装得低、以他们的步速后退移动，使他们保持在画面下三分之一，而巨型雕像与微小的僧侣在 Rain 与 Roco 之间的深远背景中可见。尺度压人——身影微小、雕像浩瀚、僧侣如一点遥远火焰。他们的脚步在光亮的石地上轻轻回响。Rain 居中，悬浮板如今不自觉地举在胸前高度。Roco 居左，Jax 居右，两人行走时仍在抬头瞥望石柱。步速缓慢、沉重——不是大步，而是近乎虔敬。

第 6 镜（12-15 秒）：近天花板处的极宽高机位镜头，俯视向下。三个角色是穿越辽阔光亮地面的微小身影，在光束中留下细微的尘迹。雕像主导着画面上半部，掌上僧侣是一点温暖微光。自这一高度，地面几何镶嵌清晰可见——同心图案，三个角色正不知不觉地走过。摄影机完全静止，持住整整 3 秒。音频：寺庙声加深，他们遥远的脚步几乎听不见，僧侣缓慢呼吸的暗示渡越这不可能的远距被托送而来。

摄影（CINEMATOGRAPHY）：全程长焦，等效 85mm-200mm，无手持抖动——这是沉思式、有重量、虔敬的。所有运镜皆缓慢而审慎（推近、后移、持住）。多数镜头完全静止。浅景深 f/2。自上而下的强烈垂直神迹光束，带可见的体积雾。色板：深邃冷影、暖琥珀高光、整体去饱和。

音频（AUDIO）：一切之下深邃的环境寺庙声，远处水滴，高处空间中隐约的风，角色带重混响的呼吸与轻脚步，两句带明显自然混响尾音的低语对白。无音乐。无配乐。

节奏（PACING）：缓慢、有重量、虔敬。每镜都停得比感觉舒适更久。以静止表达敬畏，以剪影表达尺度。
