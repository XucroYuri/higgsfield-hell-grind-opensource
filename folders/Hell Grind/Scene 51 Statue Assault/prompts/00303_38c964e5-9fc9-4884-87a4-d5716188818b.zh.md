## 原文 (source)

Stone guardian's massive backhand sends crystal-armored Roco flying across chamber, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men kinetic impacts, Villeneuve Dune Part Two scale combat, Snyder 300. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with combat damage cracks, weathered ancient carved stone with script. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking matching Roco's flight. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling, flagstone floor, carved stone columns flanking the side walls.

Shot 1 (0-4s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco standing his ground, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco crystal-armored visible from side angle, sword and shield raised, body in defensive stance. The colossal stone HAND of the guardian descends from above out of frame — massive carved stone palm the size of a small car coming sideways through the air toward Roco. The hand IMPACTS Roco from the side with deep WHUMP — Roco's crystal-armored body LAUNCHED sideways through the air horizontally, body pitched, sword and shield still in hands, dust trailing behind him. Audio: WHOOSH of the colossal hand approaching, deep WHUMP of impact, his sharp mineral grunt of pain, his body flying through air.

Shot 2 (4-8s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close tracking Roco mid-flight, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco's body horizontal in flight through the chamber air, dust trailing, the chamber background passing rapidly through frame, his armored body spinning slowly, his crystal collar concealing his face. He travels meters across the chamber in trajectory toward a stone column in deep blur. Audio: WHOOSH of his body through air, his mineral grunt of pain, the chamber rumble passing through frame.

Shot 3 (8-12s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco's impact, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco's crystal-armored body SLAMS into a carved stone column-face with thunderous CRACK — his armored back impacting the weathered ancient stone, the carved column cracking from the impact, dust eruption, several relief fragments breaking off the column. His body slides DOWN the column to the flagstone at its base, body sliding heavily against the stone, coming to rest crumpled on his side at the column base. Audio: thunderous CRACK of his body on the column, dust eruption, his deep mineral grunt of pain, his body sliding down stone.

Shot 4 (12-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Roco lying on the flagstone, 100mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on his crystal armor — visible CRACKS now spreading across his chest plate from the multiple impacts, deeper red glow leaking through the new fractures, several shoulder spikes broken off and missing. He moves slowly, struggling to push himself up on one armored hand, mineral resonance heavy and labored. Audio: his deep labored mineral breath, the crackling sound of his damaged armor moving, dust hissing as he struggles, distant guardian's deep red hum continuing.

PACING: 15-second four-shot beating. Hand impacts and launches Roco (0-4s), mid-flight across chamber (4-8s), impact on column (8-12s), Roco lying damaged trying to rise (12-15s).

石质守护者威力巨大的反手一击将水晶装甲的 Roco 抽飞穿越密殿，15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》激烈撞击、Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》尺度战斗、Snyder《300 勇士》。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 红宝石水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光，暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶带战斗损伤裂纹、风化古老雕花石面带铭文。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持，极度颤抖，以身体驱动的跟拍贴合 Roco 的飞行轨迹。绝不正面——保持四分之三角度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，天花板碎片纷纷坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，手有小汽车般大小。

参见这名男子：完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），尘雾在震颤，石板地面，雕花石柱排列于侧墙两旁。

Shot 1（0-4 秒）：四分之三手持中近景，Roco 坚守阵脚，50mm，f/1.8。水晶装甲的 Roco 从侧面角度可见，剑与盾高举、身体成防御架势。守护者巨大的石 HAND 自画面上方降下——有小汽车般大小的巨型雕花石掌侧向划过空气朝 Roco 而来。巨掌以深沉 WHUMP 从侧面撞中 Roco——Roco 的水晶装甲身体被水平侧向击飞、身体倾斜、剑与盾仍握在手中、身后尘土拖曳。音频：巨掌逼近的 WHOOSH、撞击的深沉 WHUMP、他尖锐的痛苦矿物闷哼、他的身体飞掠空气。

Shot 2（4-8 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，跟拍 Roco 空中飞行，50mm，f/1.8。Roco 的身体水平飞掠过密殿的空气、尘土拖曳、密殿的背景迅速滑过画面、他的装甲身体缓慢旋转、水晶护颈（collar）遮掩着他的面容。他在深虚化中朝着一座石柱的航向上横越数米。音频：他身体掠空的 WHOOSH、他痛苦矿物般的闷哼、密殿的隆隆自画面中呼啸而过。

Shot 3（8-12 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，对准 Roco 的撞击，50mm，f/1.8。Roco 的水晶装甲身体轰然撞上一座雕花石柱的柱面，发出雷鸣般的 CRACK——其装甲的背狠狠撞上风化的古老石块、柱面因撞击而开裂、尘土迸发、数块浮雕碎片自柱身崩落。他的身体沿柱身朝柱脚的石板地面滑落、身体沉重地碾过石面、最终蜷侧停于柱脚。音频：他身体撞柱的雷鸣 CRACK、尘土迸发、他深沉的痛苦矿物闷哼、他的身体沿石面滑落。

Shot 4（12-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持极特写，Roco 躺在石板地上，100mm，f/1.4。极薄焦点落在他的水晶铠甲上——多次撞击后他的胸甲处可见 CRACKS 裂纹正在蔓延、更深红的微光自新的裂隙中渗出、数根肩刺断裂缺失。他缓缓移动、挣扎着想用一只装甲手撑起身、矿物共鸣沉重而吃力。音频：他深沉吃力的矿物呼吸、受损铠甲移动时的噼啪声、他挣扎时尘土嘶嘶作响、远方守护者深沉的红光嗡鸣持续着。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒四镜头击打。巨掌撞击并击飞 Roco（0-4 秒），横越密殿的空中飞行（4-8 秒），撞上石柱（8-12 秒），Roco 带伤躺下试着起身（12-15 秒）。
