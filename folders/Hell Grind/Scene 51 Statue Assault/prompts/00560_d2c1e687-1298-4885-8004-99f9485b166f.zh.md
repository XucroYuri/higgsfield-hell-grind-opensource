## 原文 (source)

Vast ancient sanctum carved from natural rock, recovery and forward motion sequence, 12 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, deep cool blue-grey palette with warm amber oil-lamp accents, dramatic chiaroscuro, no music. Inspired by Children of Men and The Revenant — handheld kinetic intimacy, close-up driven acting.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.
Refer to the man with dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 3.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 4.

Setting: vast cavern-sanctum carved from natural rock, towering carved stone column-faces receding into darkness on both side walls, irregular flagstone floor, two parallel lines of small oil-lamps flickering warm amber along the walls, ceiling lost in shadow far above, dust-hazed atmosphere. Roco lies face-down on the flagstones a couple of meters from the side wall, dust-covered, body angled. Jax kneels beside him, jacket scuffed. Rain stands a few meters back, the hoverboard held at her side in her left hand, watching, ready to move.

Shot 1 (0-2s): Handheld extreme low-angle close on Roco's face pressed against the flagstone, 100mm long lens, razor-thin focus on his eye. Camera lies on the floor inches from his face. Dust-streaked cheek against cold stone. His eyelid flutters, then opens — pupil dilating in the dark. A faint twitch in his brow as awareness returns. He exhales softly. Off-screen above, Jax's voice low and close: "Hey. With me?" Roco's eye tracks up toward the voice. Frame breathes with intimate handheld drift. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drop with chamber reverb, Roco's slow ragged inhale magnified by closeness, faint oil-lamp crackle.

Shot 2 (2-3.5s): Cut to handheld close on Jax leaning down toward Roco from above, 85mm, three-quarter angle. Jax's face is dust-streaked, brow tight, eyes scanning Roco's off-frame face. He sees the response, exhales a small relieved breath. His expression shifts to focused — about to act. He reaches down toward Roco's forearm. Audio: his line tail with chamber reverb, his exhale of relief, his measured breath, ambient drone underneath.

Shot 3 (3.5-6.5s): Cut to handheld low-rising shot beside the two men, 50mm wider lens. Jax grips Roco's forearm in a strong clasp — fingers locking around the wrist. He shifts his weight back, plants his foot, and HAULS Roco upward. Roco grunts, pushes off the floor with his free hand, comes up onto his knees, then to his feet in one continuous motion, dust falling from his jacket. He staggers on landing, catches Jax's shoulder briefly with his free hand for balance — a quick brace, not a collapse. Jax's other hand comes up to steady Roco's elbow. They stand like that for a beat — Roco hand on Jax's shoulder, Jax hand under Roco's elbow — eyes meeting. Roco gives a small nod: I'm here. The camera RISES WITH THEM continuously, operator standing as they stand. Frame drifts and corrects, alive. Audio: scrape of grip, Jax's grunt of effort as he hauls, scrape of Roco's boots finding flagstone, Roco's heavy exhale, both breathing heavily, ambient drone, chamber reverb deepening.

Shot 4 (6.5-8s): Cut to handheld medium close on Rain, 100mm, three-quarter profile. She holds the hoverboard at her side, purple thruster glow soft accent. Her face is calm but watchful, eyes locked on the two men in deep blurred background. The cold light catches the scar by her eye. A small flicker of relief crosses her expression as she sees Roco upright. She speaks, voice low and direct, with chamber reverb: "You good?" Camera breathes around her, drifts subtly closer. Audio: her controlled breathing, the question with long reverb tail, ambient drone, distant oil-lamp crackle.

Shot 5 (8-9.5s): Cut to handheld extreme close-up on Roco's face, 100mm, dust-streaked, the red bandage on his nose visible. He's let go of Jax's shoulder, found his own balance. Breathing heavily but steady now. His eyes go forward, past Rain, fixing on something distant in the sanctum off-screen. His jaw sets. His answer comes flat, decided, with chamber reverb: "Let's move." Frame breathes intimately on his face. Audio: his line with strong reverb tail, his breath settling, ambient drone.

Shot 6 (9.5-12s): Cut to handheld medium tight three-shot on the trio from a low front angle, 50mm. Roco center, eyes forward. Jax right of frame, gives Roco's shoulder a brief firm tap — good, we go — and steps back to clear his path. Rain left of frame, hoverboard at her side, already shifting her weight forward. Roco takes the first step into the chamber. Jax falls in on his right. Rain a beat behind, the hoverboard swinging slightly with her stride. Camera dollies backward at their pace, framing them tight from waist-up walking toward camera. Behind them in deep shallow-focus blur: the dark interior of the sanctum receding, oil-lamp lines stretching away. Frame drifts with operator's breath, walking with them. Audio: Jax's tap on Roco's shoulder, Roco's first boot striking flagstone, Jax falling in step, Rain joining a beat later — three pairs of boots in slightly staggered rhythm with chamber reverb, breathing settling into walking pattern, ambient drone underneath.

CAMERA: long lens 50mm-100mm. Shots 1, 4, 5 are 100mm intimate close-ups. Shot 2 is 85mm close. Shots 3 and 6 are 50mm wider (physical action and walking). Handheld throughout, alive, breath-driven, the operator a fourth presence. Clean direct cuts. Frame constantly drifts, reactive but never jittery. Shallow depth of field f/1.8-f/2. Cold blue-grey palette, warm oil-lamp accents, soft purple glow accents from the hoverboard's thruster cores.

ACTING:
- Roco: returns slowly, accepts the help with a brief brace and a nod rather than collapsing into it — keeps his dignity. His "Let's move" is the moment he reclaims himself: eyes forward, jaw setting, decision before full recovery.
- Jax: caregiver and operator. His "Hey. With me?" is direct and intimate, not panicked. His haul is muscular, efficient. The shoulder tap at the end is the verbal "we're good" without words.
- Rain: the watcher. Doesn't intervene physically — lets Jax handle it. Her "You good?" is a check-in. Her relief is private, contained. She holds the hoverboard as an extension of her body, never noticing it.

AUDIO: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drops with chamber reverb, oil-lamp crackles, breathing and clothing rustle. Tactile sounds: scrape of grip, Jax's grunt hauling Roco, Roco's exhale, Jax's tap on Roco's shoulder, three pairs of boots in staggered rhythm. Dialogue: Jax's "Hey. With me?", Rain's "You good?", Roco's "Let's move" — all with strong chamber reverb tails. No music.

PACING: six-beat structure — Roco waking (Shot 1), Jax registering and reaching (Shot 2), the haul-up with mutual brace (Shot 3), Rain's check-in (Shot 4), Roco's decision (Shot 5), forward motion (Shot 6). Held together by close-ups and physical contact — the audience reads the trio's relationship through faces, hands, and small tactile gestures.

## 中文对照 (zh)

从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔古老圣殿（sanctum），恢复与前行动作序列，12 秒，16:9 anamorphic，写实摄影（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，深冷的蓝灰基调辅以暖琥珀色油灯点缀，戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro），无音乐。灵感源自《人类之子》（Children of Men）与《荒野猎人》（The Revenant）——手持（handheld）动能化的亲密感、以特写驱动表演。

参照 Image 1 中那位银白浅发、眼角带疤、灰色连帽夹克的女性。
参照 Image 2 中那位漂金寸头、深肤色、透明眼镜、深色夹克的男性。
参照 Image 3 中那位深色乱发、留须、鼻梁贴红色绷带、深色连帽夹克的男性。
参照 Image 4 中那架哑光黑悬浮滑板（hoverboard），配备泛紫色光芒的推进器核心。

置景（Setting）：从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔洞窟圣殿，两侧墙上刻着高耸的石柱面，渐隐没入两侧的黑暗，地面为不规则的石板（flagstone）地，沿墙有两列平行的小油灯摇曳着暖琥珀色光，天花板在头顶高处隐没于阴影，尘土弥漫。Roco 面朝下躺在距侧墙几米远的石板地上，满身尘土，身体斜躺着。Jax 跪在他身旁，夹克磨旧。Rain 站在几米开外，左手把悬浮滑板拿在身侧，注视着，随时准备行动。

镜头 1（0-2 秒）：手持极低机位特写，对准 Roco 紧贴在石板地上的脸，100mm 长焦，对他的眼睛做剃刀般极薄的焦点。摄影机躺在离他脸数英寸的地面上。沾满尘土的侧颊贴着冰冷的石头。他的眼皮颤动，随即睁开——瞳孔在黑暗中扩张。意识回归时他眉心有一下微微的抽动。他轻轻呼出一口气。画面之外上方，Jax 的声音低而近：「Hey. With me?」Roco 的眼睛循声向上。画面随亲密的持握漂移而起伏。Audio：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响（chamber reverb）的水滴声，Roco 缓慢粗重的吸气因贴近而被放大，轻微的油灯噼啪声。

镜头 2（2-3.5 秒）：切至手持特写，对准从上方朝 Roco 俯下身的 Jax，85mm，四分之三角度。Jax 的脸沾满尘土，眉头紧锁，目光在画面外 Roco 的脸上搜寻。他看到回应，呼出一小口释然的气息。他的表情转为专注——正要行动。他伸手朝 Roco 的前臂探去。Audio：他的台词的尾音带室内混响，他释然的呼气，他沉着的呼吸，底下是环境低鸣。

镜头 3（3.5-6.5 秒）：切至两名男性身旁手持低机位升起的镜头，50mm 更广的镜头。Jax 以一个有力的锁握握住 Roco 的前臂——手指扣锁在腕部。他后移重心，踩实脚，向上猛拉 Roco。Roco 发出一声闷哼，用那只空着的手撑离地面，跪起，再在一气呵成的动作中站到脚上，尘土从他夹克上簌簌落下。落地时他踉跄了一下，用空着的手短暂扶住 Jax 的肩稳住平衡——一个快速的撑持，而非坍倒。Jax 的另一只手抬起来稳稳托住 Roco 的肘部。他们就那样站了一拍——Roco 的手搭在 Jax 肩头，Jax 的手托在 Roco 肘下——四目相对。Roco 微微点了个头：我在这儿。摄影机随他们一气呵成地升起，操作员随他们一同站起身。画面漂移又校正，生气勃勃。Audio：握持的刮擦声，Jax 拉拽时用力的闷哼，Roco 靴底在石板地上的刮擦声，Roco 沉重的呼气，两人呼吸沉重，环境低鸣，室内混响渐深。

镜头 4（6.5-8 秒）：切至手持中景特写，对准 Rain，100mm，四分之三侧面。她把悬浮滑板拿在身侧，紫色推进器光晕作柔和点缀。她的脸平静却警觉，目光锁定在纵深处模糊的两个男人身上。冷光掠过她眼角那道疤。见她看到 Roco 直立起来，一丝释然的闪念掠过她的神情。她开口，声音低而直接，带室内混响：「You good?」摄影机在她周围起伏，微妙地渐渐靠近。Audio：她受控的呼吸，带悠长混响尾音的问句，环境低鸣，远处油灯的噼啪声。

镜头 5（8-9.5 秒）：切至手持极特写，对准 Roco 的脸，100mm，满身尘土，鼻上的红色绷带清晰可见。他已经松开 Jax 的肩，自己站稳了。呼吸沉重，但此刻平稳。他的目光向前，越过 Rain，锁定在圣殿画面外某处远处。他的下颌绷紧。他的回答平平地、决然地传来，带室内混响：「Let's move.」画面在他脸上散发着亲密的气息。Audio：他的台词带强烈的混响尾音，他的呼吸渐趋平稳，环境低鸣。

镜头 6（9.5-12 秒）：切至手持中景紧凑三人镜头，自低正前方角度，50mm。Roco 居中，目光向前。Jax 在画面右侧，在 Roco 肩上干脆地轻拍一下——好，我们走——然后退开，给他让出路。Rain 在画面左侧，悬浮滑板在身侧，已经前移重心。Roco 迈出进入厅堂的第一步。Jax 在他的右侧跟上。Rain 落后一拍，悬浮滑板随她的步伐轻轻摆动。摄影机以他们的步速向后移动（dolly），从腰际以上紧凑地框住他们朝摄影机走来。在他们身后以极深浅焦的模糊中：圣殿幽暗的深处退去，油灯的连排向远方延伸。画面随操作员的呼吸漂移，与他们并肩而行。Audio：Jax 拍在 Roco 肩上的轻拍，Roco 的第一只靴子踏在石板地上，Jax 跟上脚步，Rain 一拍之后加入——三双靴子以略错落有致的节奏带室内混响，呼吸渐入走路的节律，底下是环境低鸣。

CAMERA：长焦 50mm-100mm。镜头 1、4、5 为 100mm 的亲密特写。镜头 2 为 85mm 特写。镜头 3 与 6 为更广的 50mm（身体动作与行走）。全程手持，生气勃勃、由呼吸驱动，操作员是第四位在场者。干脆利落的直接切。画面持续漂移，灵敏却绝不抖动。浅景深 f/1.8-f/2。冷蓝灰基调，暖琥珀油灯点缀，悬浮滑板推进器核心泛起的柔紫色光辉点缀。

ACTING（表演）：
- Roco：缓慢回归，以短暂的撑持与一个点头而非瘫倒去接受帮助——他保住了自己的尊严。他的「Let's move」是他重新夺回自己的那一刻：目光向前、下颌绷紧、决断先于完全康复。
- Jax：照料者兼操作者。他的「Hey. With me?」直接而亲密，并不惊慌。他拉人是结实而高效的。结尾的肩上一拍，是不言而喻的「我们没事」。
- Rain：观望者。她不在身体上介入——让 Jax 去处理。她的「You good?」是一次探问。她的释然是私密、收敛的。她把悬浮滑板当作自己身体的延伸般拿着，浑然不觉。

AUDIO：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响的水滴声，油灯噼啪声，呼吸与衣物沙沙。触觉声响：握持的刮擦声，Jax 拉动 Roco 时用力的闷哼，Roco 的呼气，Jax 拍在 Roco 肩上的轻拍，三双靴子错落有致的节奏。台词：Jax 的「Hey. With me?」、Rain 的「You good?」、Roco 的「Let's move」——全部带强烈的室内混响尾音。无音乐。

PACING：六拍结构——Roco 苏醒（镜头 1）、Jax 觉察并伸手（镜头 2）、带相互撑持的拉起（镜头 3）、Rain 的探问（镜头 4）、Roco 的决意（镜头 5）、前行动作（镜头 6）。以特写与身体接触收束在一起——观众通过面孔、双手与微小的触觉姿态去读懂这支三人小队之间的关系。
