Stone guardian advances on retreating crystal-armored Roco, finally pins him with sword, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient carved stone. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking INTENSIFYING with each guardian step, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, flagstone floor with carved stone debris, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-4s): Three-quarter handheld medium close tracking Roco retreating backward, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco crystal-armored steps back across flagstone, sword and shield raised in defensive stance, crystal armor showing combat damage cracks. Behind him in deep blur the colossal stone leg takes a thunderous STEP forward — WHUMP impact shaking the floor, dust eruption. Roco accelerates retreat. Audio: his heavy footfalls, the colossal WHUMP of guardian's step, his mineral breath of effort.

Shot 2 (4-8s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco parrying, 85mm, f/1.4. The colossal stone sword sweeps in horizontally from above out of frame — Roco raises his ruby crystal sword to PARRY the strike. The colossal blade meets his crystal sword with thunderous CRACK and saturated red flash, the crystal sword visibly cracking from the impact, Roco's body forced backward several steps from the force, his armored boots scraping across the flagstone. Audio: WHOOSH of the colossal blade, thunderous CRACK of parry, his mineral grunt of effort, the SCRAPE of his boots on stone.

Shot 3 (8-12s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on Roco from behind low, 35mm, f/2. Roco crystal-armored stepping back, his armored heel CATCHES on a chunk of fallen carved stone debris on the flagstone — he STUMBLES backward, body pitching off-balance, sword and shield flailing for stability, knee buckling. He falls to one knee on the broken flagstone, briefly disoriented. The guardian's massive form fills the deep frame above him, advancing closer. Audio: the SCRAPE of his armored heel catching debris, his mineral grunt of imbalance, his hard landing on knee, the colossus's deep red hum advancing.

Shot 4 (12-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on Roco kneeling, 35mm, f/2. Roco still on one knee, looking UP. The guardian's colossal stone HAND descends from above gripping the colossal sword, raising it overhead — both massive carved stone hands hold the 5-meter blade vertically aloft, the blade descending toward Roco's position — but the shot CUTS at the moment the blade begins its downward arc, leaving the blade frozen at the apex of its raise, Roco frozen looking up at it. Audio: deep stone-on-stone CLAMP of both hands gripping hilt, the colossus's deep red hum spiking with execution intent, Roco's strained mineral breath, sustained tension at the apex moment.

PACING: 15-second four-shot guardian advance. Roco retreats while guardian steps forward (0-4s), Roco parries colossal sword forced backward (4-8s), Roco trips on debris falls to knee (8-12s), guardian raises sword overhead for execution as shot cuts (12-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

石质守护者向撤退中的水晶装甲 Roco 逼近、最终以剑将他钉住，15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》、Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 红宝石水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光，暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶、风化古老雕花石面。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持，极度颤抖，以身体驱动的跟拍。绝不正面——保持四分之三角度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，且随守护者每一步而增强，天花板碎片纷纷坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，5 米长的利剑。

参见这名男子：完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），带雕花石质碎片的石板地面，尘雾在震颤。

Shot 1（0-4 秒）：四分之三手持中近景，跟拍 Roco 向后退却，50mm，f/1.8。水晶装甲的 Roco 在石板地上后退一步，剑与盾高举成防御架势，水晶铠甲可见战斗损伤的裂纹。他身后深处虚化中巨大的石腿轰然 STEP 踏出一步——WHUMP 的撞击震得地面颤动、尘土迸发。Roco 加速后退。音频：他沉重的脚步声、守护者踏步时巨大的 WHUMP、他用力时矿物的呼吸。

Shot 2（4-8 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，Roco 格挡，85mm，f/1.4。巨大的石剑自画面上方水平横扫而入——Roco 高举红宝石水晶剑欲 PARRY 格挡这一击。巨刃撞上他的水晶剑、发出雷鸣般的 CRACK 与饱和红光一闪，水晶剑因这一击而明显开裂、Roco 的身体被力道逼退数步、装甲战靴在石板地上刮擦。音频：巨刃的 WHOOSH、格挡的雷鸣 CRACK、他用力时的矿物闷哼、战靴在石上刮擦的 SCRAPE。

Shot 3（8-12 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低机位，从 Roco 背后的低处对准他，35mm，f/2。水晶装甲的 Roco 正在退步，他的装甲鞋跟 CATCHES 勾住石板上的一块掉落雕花残石——他向后 STUMBLES 踉跄、身体失衡后仰、剑与盾在空中乱舞以求稳住、膝盖打弯。他单膝跌跪在碎裂的石板上、短暂地晕头转向。守护者庞大的身形在他上方的深虚中占满画面、愈逼愈近。音频：他装甲鞋跟勾住碎石的 SCRAPE、他失衡的矿物闷哼、他膝盖重重落地、巨像深沉的红光嗡鸣在逼近。

Shot 4（12-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低机位，Roco 跪地，35mm，f/2。Roco 仍单膝跪地、抬眼 UP 仰望。守护者巨大的石 HAND 自上方伸下握住巨剑、将剑高举过顶——两只巨型雕花石手将这柄 5 米长的剑刃垂直高举在空中、剑刃正朝 Roco 所在之处降下——但镜头在剑刃开始下切弧线的瞬间 CUTS 切走，令剑刃定格在举高的顶点、Roco 怔怔地仰望着它。音频：双手握柄时石头相扣的深沉 CLAMP 声、巨像深沉的红光嗡鸣随着行刑的意图而陡然拔高、Roco 勉力的矿物呼吸、顶点瞬间持续的紧绷。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒四镜头守护者逼近。Roco 后退而守护者踏前（0-4 秒），Roco 格挡巨剑被逼后退（4-8 秒），Roco 被碎石绊倒跌跪（8-12 秒），镜头切走之际守护者高举巨剑作行刑之状（12-15 秒）。
