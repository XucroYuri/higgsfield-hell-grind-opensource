## 原文 (source)

Rain on hoverboard flies toward monk's distant ledge, colossal stone sword slams down between them, Rain recoils, 5 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient from above, saturated purple from hoverboard thrusters, warm amber from candle on monk's ledge in distance. Color: deep cool blue-grey, accent purple, accent warm amber. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic skin, fabric, matte-black hoverboard, weathered ancient carved stone with script, monk's deep maroon robes. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld at Rain's elevation from her left side, three-quarter angle, frame trembling.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 2.
Refer to the elderly East Asian monk in dark maroon robes from Image 3.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling. Rain mid-flight in chamber air at twelve-meter elevation. The carved stone ledge with the seated monk in deep maroon robes meditating in shallow blurred background several meters ahead of Rain's position, candle burning beside him.

Single shot (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Rain from her left side, 50mm, f/1.8.

At 0-2s: Rain visible from chest-up in three-quarter side view on the matte-black hoverboard mid-flight, body crouched forward into low riding stance, silver-blonde hair flying back, jacket flaring, both arms extended for balance NO HANDLEBARS. Purple thrusters BLAZE behind the board. She rides forward through chamber air at high speed, the carved stone ledge with the monk visible in shallow blurred background several meters ahead of her — she has not yet reached it, distance between them. Her eyes locked forward on the ledge, face determined. Audio: thrusters scream, her controlled fast breath, the monk's distant sustained throat-tone audible from ahead, chamber rumble.

At 2-3s: The COLOSSAL STONE SWORD slams down from above into the chamber air between Rain and the ledge — 5-meter blade descending vertically into the empty space ahead of her flight path, weathered ancient stone with carved script filling the middle of frame as a wall of stone, the blade plunging through the air directly across Rain's trajectory several meters in front of her, dust streaming from its descent. The monk's ledge now hidden behind the blade in the deep frame. Rain still meters away from where the blade has arrived. Audio: massive WHOOSH of the descending blade, the rising rumble of its mass, her sharp inhale of recognition.

At 3-5s: Rain THROWS her body backward and to the left, the hoverboard lurching back and downward in a sharp recoil, body angled away from the blade, thrusters firing in reverse for the evasion. The colossal blade remains in the middle of frame as a wall blocking her path completely, the monk hidden behind it in deep blur. Rain emerges further away from the ledge than she started, the path completely blocked, breathing hard. Audio: thrusters firing in reverse, her sharp grunt of evasion, the blade's pressure GROAN as it hangs in the air, dust hissing, the monk's throat-tone continuing unbroken from behind the blade.

PACING: 5-second flight blocked. Rain flies toward distant ledge with monk in shallow blur ahead (0-2s), colossal sword slams down between them in midair (2-3s), Rain recoils backward, path completely blocked (3-5s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实电影特效。anamorphic 电影镜头、35mm 胶片颗粒、暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》、Villeneuve《沙丘2》。Lighting（灯光）：上方冷白环境光、hoverboard 推进器饱合的紫色光、远处武僧岩台上烛火暖琥珀色光。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰色，点缀紫，点缀暖琥珀。重度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实的皮肤、织物、磨砂黑 hoverboard、风化古老雕刻石并刻有铭文，武僧深绛红僧袍。60fps、8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持，置于 Rain 高度、位于她左侧，四分之三角度，画面颤抖。

CHAMBER TREMOR（洞厅震颤）：剧烈晃动，碎石下落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，5 米长剑。

参考 Image 1 中银白长发、眼旁有疤、身披灰色连帽夹克的女人。
参考 Image 2 中带紫色发光推进器核心的磨砂黑 hoverboard。
参考 Image 3 中身着深绛红僧袍的东亚老僧。

Setting（场景）：洞穴圣殿（cavern-sanctum），粉尘雾气震颤。Rain 凌于洞厅空气中、处于十二米高度飞行中。雕刻石台与盘坐沉思的深绛红僧袍老僧，处于 Rain 前方数米的浅模糊背景中，身旁燃着一支蜡烛。

Single shot（单一镜头）(0-5s)：从 Rain 左侧看她的四分之三手持中近景，50mm、f/1.8。

0-2s 期间：镜头中 Rain 胸部以上可见，四分之三侧面，踩在磨砂黑 hoverboard 上凌空飞行，身体前倾成低伏骑行姿态，银白长发向后飞扬，夹克鼓张，双臂平举保持平衡，无车把（NO HANDLEBARS）。板尾紫色推进器绽放（BLAZE）光芒。她高速穿过洞厅空气向前飞行，雕刻石台与老僧在她前方数米的浅模糊背景中可见——她尚未抵达，两者间仍有距离。她的双眼锁定前方的石台，神情坚定。Audio（音频）：推进器嘶鸣、她克制而急促的呼吸、前方传来武僧持续不断的高亢喉音、洞厅的隆隆声。

2-3s 期间：巨型石剑（COLOSSAL STONE SWORD）自上方猛然落下，砸入 Rain 与石台之间的洞厅空气——5 米剑刃垂直下劈，落入她飞行路线前方的空旷空间，风化古老雕刻石连同刻写的铭文填满画面中央，如一道石墙，剑刃穿过她前方数米的空气直坠，落在她飞行轨迹的正前方，落势扬起的尘土随之下泄。武僧的石台此刻被剑刃遮蔽，隐入画面深处。Rain 距剑刃落点仍有数米之遥。Audio（音频）：剑刃下落时掀起巨大呼啸声（WHOOSH）、其质量带起的滚滚隆隆声、她辨认出来时尖锐的吸气。

3-5s 期间：Rain 猛地将身体向后、向左甩出，hoverboard 猛烈后挫并朝下方倾斜，身体扭转躲开剑刃，推进器反向喷发以作规避。巨剑仍横亘画面中央，如一道石墙彻底阻断她的去路，武僧被掩在剑后，处于深重模糊之中。Rain 退离石台比起点更远，去路完全被封死，大口喘息。Audio（音频）：推进器反向喷发、她躲闪发出的短促闷哼、剑刃悬停空中的压力呻吟与碾压声（GROAN）、尘埃嘶嘶作响、剑后武僧的喉音不间断持续。

PACING（节奏）：5 秒受阻飞行。Rain 朝远处岩台疾飞，前方武僧浅模糊可见（0-2s），巨剑在半空猛然砸落两人之间（2-3s），Rain 向后急剧后缩，去路完全被封死（3-5s）。
