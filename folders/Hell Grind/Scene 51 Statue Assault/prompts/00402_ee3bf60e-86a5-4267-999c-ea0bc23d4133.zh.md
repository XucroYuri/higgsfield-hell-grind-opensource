Rain dodges guardian's swinging stone arm with hoverboard tilt, 5 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

GUARDIAN SCALE: the swinging stone arm the size of multiple Rains.

Single shot (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld medium close tracking Rain mid-flight, 50mm, f/1.8. Rain stands on hoverboard feet-locked in low stance, knees bent, arms extended outward for balance, NO HANDLEBARS, body controlling the board through weight shift. The guardian's COLOSSAL CARVED STONE ARM swings laterally through the chamber air toward her — massive ancient stone arm the size of multiple Rains sweeping through frame from one side, stone hand with fingers spread, the arm massive enough to fill the deep frame. Rain reacts fast — she throws her body weight DOWNWARD and to one side, the hoverboard TILTING sharply with her body, the board angling almost vertically as she ducks beneath the swinging arm, her body parallel to the stone surface above her, thrusters firing for the dive. The colossal arm sweeps THROUGH where she just was, missing by inches, WHOOSH of mass close.
