## 原文 (source)

Vast ancient sanctum carved from natural rock, awakening and orientation sequence, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, ambient temple drone only — no music.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.
Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 3.

VISUAL STYLE: cinematic anamorphic, long-lens telephoto cinematography 85mm-200mm equivalent, very shallow depth of field f/1.8-f/2, deep cool blue-grey palette with isolated warm amber accents from candle flames, dramatic chiaroscuro — most of the frame in near-black shadow, single cold light shaft from natural rock fissure above falling on the distant statue. Heavily desaturated. Inspired by Cuarón's Children of Men opening sequences, Lubezki's The Revenant, Iñárritu's Birdman — handheld kinetic intimacy, camera always alive.

LOCATION: vast sanctum carved from natural rock, deep cavern-corridor approximately fifteen meters wide and forty meters long, high irregular natural rock ceiling lost in shadow far above. Carved stone column-faces flanking both side walls. Irregular flagstone floor. Two parallel lines of small iron candle-stands flickering warm amber along both side walls. At the far end: a colossal seated stone warrior statue, bald, muscled, ancient armor, severe carved face, hands cupped together in his lap. A single shaft of cold pale daylight from above falls on the statue. On the statue's cupped hands, four meters above the floor, sits a small living MONK in dark robes, shaved head, cross-legged in meditation, motionless, eyes closed. A small candle flame beside him.

CHARACTERS: Rain, Jax, and Roco have just regained awareness near the entrance of the sanctum, dust-covered, jackets scuffed from previous passage. Roco lies face-up on the flagstone floor, semi-conscious, just blinking awake. Jax is already up on his feet beside him — he was the first to recover. Rain is on her knees a few meters away, hoverboard on the floor beside her, just beginning to push herself up.

Shot 1 (0-2s): HANDHELD LOW-ANGLE on Roco lying on the flagstones, 100mm long lens, very shallow focus. Camera lies near floor level beside him, looking down the length of his body — his face in three-quarter profile in foreground, dust-covered, eyes blinking open slowly, brow furrowed in confusion. The cold dark interior of the sanctum recedes behind him in shallow-focus blur, soft amber candle bokeh at the edges. Frame breathes with subtle handheld drift. Then from above frame, Jax's open hand enters into the shot — palm down, fingers extended, reaching toward Roco. Jax's voice comes from off-screen above: a single low syllable, "Hey." Roco's eyes track up to the hand. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drop with chamber reverb, Roco's slow ragged inhale, the soft creak of Jax's jacket as he leans down, faint candle crackle.

Shot 2 (2-5s): HANDHELD LOW-RISING tracking shot beside the two men, 85mm. The camera is at floor level as Roco reaches up and grasps Jax's forearm — their hands clasp, a firm grip — and Jax pulls. Roco rises with the pull, the camera RISING WITH HIM in a continuous handheld lift, the operator physically standing as Roco stands. Roco comes up to a crouch, then to his feet, leaning briefly against Jax's shoulder for balance before steadying. Jax keeps a hand on Roco's upper arm, watching him to see he's stable. The frame drifts and corrects constantly — alive, breath-driven, the operator's body transferring directly into the camera. As they rise, the sanctum reveals itself behind them in shallow-focus blur — the parallel candle lines coming into view on either side, the dark interior receding. Roco's face is dust-streaked, eyes squinting open, brow still furrowed. Jax beside him in profile, glasses askew, watching Roco's face. Audio: the soft scrape of fabric as Roco grips Jax's arm, a small grunt of effort from Jax, the scrape of Roco's boots as he finds balance, both their breathing, ambient drone underneath.

Shot 3 (5-7s): WHIP-PAN HANDHELD across the chamber to Rain, 100mm — fast lateral camera movement with motion blur, settling on her in medium close. She is pushing up from one knee to standing on her own, hoverboard still on the floor beside her, her hand braced against the flagstone for the last moment before releasing. Her head is already lifted — she scans forward into the sanctum, eyes tracking across the dark interior, taking it in. A strand of silver-blonde hair falls across her face; she does not move it. The cold distant light catches the scar by her eye and the edge of her cheekbone — the rest of her face in candle warm-shadow. Camera breathes around her, drifts subtly, almost circles her without committing to a full move. Audio: her controlled breathing, the soft drag of her jacket as she rises, the candle line whispers, ambient drone.

Shot 4 (7-9s): WHIP-PAN HANDHELD back across the chamber to Jax and Roco standing together, 85mm — fast camera swing with heavy motion blur, settling on them in medium two-shot. Roco is steadier now, has stepped slightly away from Jax, one hand pressed to his own temple as he blinks hard. Jax is beside him, glasses now straightened with a practiced flick of his finger, his head lifted — he is looking forward and up, eyes widening as he registers the sanctum interior. He exhales a half-syllable of involuntary surprise, almost a "huh," with chamber reverb. Roco follows Jax's gaze, his hand drops from his temple, his face shifts from confusion to slow awe. Camera drifts and holds, alive. Audio: Jax's "huh," Roco's slow exhale, the ambient drone deepening as the chamber's reverb becomes audible to them.

Shot 5 (9-11.5s): HANDHELD ROVING WIDE-MEDIUM on Roco, 85mm — but the camera is in motion the entire shot. It starts behind his right shoulder as he turns slowly to take in the sanctum, and tracks AROUND him in a partial arc — camera moves left-to-right behind him as he himself rotates, so we see what he sees in a moving reveal. The frame catches: the carved stone column-faces on the left wall, the candle line stretching away into darkness, the deep flagstone corridor receding, and then — as the camera completes its arc and Roco's gaze settles forward — the colossal statue at the far end with its single cold shaft of daylight, the meditating monk a tiny silhouette on its cupped hands, a warm pinpoint of candle beside him. The reveal is earned through the camera's movement matching Roco's body. Frame drifts hard with handheld breath, the operator's footsteps faintly transmitting into the image. Audio: his slow turning footsteps, his breathing, the chamber's depth becoming audible in a slow swelling reverb — distance is suddenly heard as well as seen.

Shot 6 (11.5-13.5s): HANDHELD MEDIUM on Rain, 100mm — she is now standing fully. The camera is alive around her, drifting, never locked. She bends down, picks up the hoverboard from the flagstones in one smooth motion — the camera dips with her body, then rises with her as she straightens. She tucks the hoverboard under her left arm. Her gaze is forward, fixed on the distant statue — calm, certain, knowing. The cold distant light catches the front of her face faintly, candle warmth on her side. Behind her in shallow focus blur, Roco's silhouette is visible standing also, looking the same direction. Camera continues breathing, drifts a hair closer. Audio: her movements with the hoverboard, the soft tap of her boots resettling, ambient drone, distant water drop.

Shot 7 (13.5-15s): HANDHELD OVER-THE-SHOULDER from behind Roco, 200mm extreme long lens. Roco's shoulder and the back of his head fill the left foreground in soft blur — and the distant statue with the meditating monk dominates the deep background, compressed close by the long lens. Roco's voice comes intimate and low, almost a whisper, with chamber reverb across the depth: "Is this the place?" A beat. Then Rain's voice, quieter still, off-screen from his right: "That's right." The camera drifts subtly during the exchange, frame breathing. Audio: his line, the reverb tail, her line, a longer reverb tail dying into ambient drone, distant candle crackle.

CINEMATOGRAPHY: long lens 85mm-200mm throughout. Handheld constant motion — the camera is never still. Shots 1-2 are rising handheld (floor to standing, with Jax helping Roco up). Shots 3-4 are whip-pans connecting Rain and the two men. Shot 5 is a roving arc around Roco matched to his rotation. Shots 6-7 are alive medium-close shots with continuous breath-driven drift. The operator is always present, always moving, always breathing through the frame. Reactive to each character's motion — when they rise, camera rises; when they turn, camera turns; when they settle, camera settles but still drifts. Very shallow depth of field f/1.8-f/2. Cold blue-grey palette, warm candle accents, single cold shaft on the distant statue.

AUDIO: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drops with chamber reverb, soft individual candle crackles, characters' breathing and clothing rustle, the scrape of Roco's boots and Jax's effort grunt as Jax pulls Roco up — all with significant chamber reverb. Two whispered lines of dialogue at the end with strong reverb tail. Jax's "Hey" in Shot 1 and his involuntary "huh" of surprise in Shot 4. NO MUSIC. NO SCORE.

PACING: kinetic and alive in the rising sequence (Shots 1-4) — quick whip-pans, tight cuts, breath-driven momentum. Slows into reverence in Shot 5's reveal. Settles into intimate dialogue in Shots 6-7. The clip moves from incapacity to recovery to awe to certainty — the camera carries the audience through this arc by literally rising with them, and the opening tactile contact between Jax and Roco grounds the trio as a team before the space takes over.

## 中文对照 (zh)

雕凿于天然岩石的辽阔古老圣殿，醒来与定位镜头，15 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕（anamorphic）2.39:1，写实摄影，厚重 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，仅环境式寺庙氛围声（temple drone）——无音乐。

参照 Image 1 中那位银色金发、眼下有疤、着灰色连帽夹克的女子。
参照 Image 2 中那位漂白黄色寸头、深色皮肤、戴透明防护镜、着深色夹克的男人。
参照 Image 3 中那位深色乱发、淡胡须、鼻上有红色绷带、着深色连帽夹克的男人。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：电影感变形宽银幕，长焦远摄摄影（等效 85mm-200mm），极浅景深 f/1.8-f/2，深邃冷调蓝灰色系，点缀以烛火产生的孤立暖琥珀色，戏剧性明暗对比（chiaroscuro）——画面大部分处于近黑阴影之中，一条来自上方天然岩石裂缝的冷光柱落在远处的雕像上。重度去饱和。灵感来自 Cuarón 的《人类之子》开场、Lubezki 的《荒野猎人》、Iñárritu 的《鸟人》——手持动态亲密感，摄影机始终是活的。

场景（LOCATION）：雕凿于天然岩石的辽阔圣殿，深邃的洞穴走廊约十五米宽、四十米长，高高的不规则天然岩顶消失在遥远上方的阴影中。两侧墙壁刻有石柱面。不规则石板地。两侧墙边各有一排小小的铁制烛台，暖琥珀色的烛火闪烁。在远端尽头：一尊巨型的端坐石武士雕像，光头、肌肉贲张、披古甲、面孔严肃刻凿，双手在膝上合拢相扣。一束来自上方的冷白昼光落在雕像上。在距地面四米高的合拢双掌上，坐着一位小小的活僧侣（MONK），着深色僧袍、剃度光头、盘腿冥想，静止不动、双眼闭合。他身侧有一小簇烛火。

角色（CHARACTERS）：Rain、Jax 与 Roco 刚刚在圣殿入口附近恢复意识，满身尘土，夹克磨损。Roco 面朝上躺在石板地上，半昏迷，正要眨醒。Jax 已站起身来在他身边——他是最先恢复的。Rain 跪在几米开外，悬浮板放在地上，正要撑身而起。

第 1 镜（0-2 秒）：手持低机位拍 Roco 躺在石板上，100mm 长焦，极浅对焦。摄影机贴近地面躺在他身边，沿他身体纵轴望过去——他的脸在前景呈四分之三侧面，沾着尘土，双眼缓慢眨开，眉头因困惑而紧锁。圣殿寒冷昏暗的内部在他身后以浅景深虚化后退，边缘有柔和琥珀色的烛光焦外光斑。画面随微妙的手持漂移而呼吸。随后自画面上方，Jax 张开的手掌入画——掌心向下、手指伸直，探向 Roco。Jax 的声音自画外上方传来：一个简短的低声音节："Hey。" Roco 的目光向上追向那只手。音频：深邃的洞穴环境声，远处水滴带室堂混响，Roco 缓慢粗重的吸气，Jax 俯身时夹克轻微的窸窣，隐约的烛火噼啪。

第 2 镜（2-5 秒）：手持低起步跟踪镜头，伴随两名男子，85mm。Roco 伸手握住 Jax 的前臂——两掌相握、牢牢一扣——Jax 用力拉。Roco 随拉拽起身，摄影机以连续的手持抬升随他上升，操作员在 Roco 起身时同步站起。Roco 先成蹲姿，再站直，短暂靠在 Jax 肩上维持平衡，随即稳住。Jax 一手仍按在 Roco 的上臂，看着他确认他站稳了。画面持续漂移并不断修正——活的、随呼吸而动，操作员的身体直接传进摄影机。他们起身时，圣殿在身后以浅景深虚化展开——两侧平行的烛火线进入视野，昏暗的内部后撤。Roco 的脸沾满尘土、双眼眯睁、眉头仍紧锁。Jax 在他身侧呈侧面，眼镜歪斜，看着 Roco 的脸。音频：Roco 抓握 Jax 手臂时织物的轻蹭，Jax 用力时的轻哼，Roco 找平衡时靴子的刮擦，两人的呼吸，其下环境声。

第 3 镜（5-7 秒）：手持甩镜（whip-pan）横掠厅堂到 Rain，100mm——带运动模糊的快速横向移动，定在她身上作中近景。她正从单膝撑按起身站直，悬浮板仍在地上，手按着石板作最后一下用力才松开。她的头已扬起——向前扫视着圣殿，目光扫过昏暗的内部，将其收入眼中。一缕银金发垂过她的脸；她不去拨开它。远处冷光捕捉到她眼旁的疤与颧骨边缘——她脸的其余部分处于烛火暖影之中。摄影机绕她呼吸、微妙漂移、几乎环绕她却不做一次完整的移动。音频：她克制的呼吸，起身时夹克的轻拖，烛火线的细语，环境声。

第 4 镜（7-9 秒）：手持甩镜横掠回厅堂，拍 Jax 与 Roco 并肩而立，85mm——带厚重运动模糊的快速摄影机摆动，定在他们身上作中景双人镜。Roco 现在已稳得多，略微离开 Jax，一手按在自己太阳穴，用力眨眼。Jax 在他身旁，眼镜已被手指娴熟地扶正，头扬起——正向前上方望着，双眼睁大，辨认着圣殿内部。他呼出半声不自觉的惊呼，近乎一声"huh"，带厅堂混响。Roco 循着 Jax 的目光看去，手从太阳穴落下，神情由困惑转为缓慢的敬畏。摄影机漂移持住，是活的。音频：Jax 的"huh"，Roco 缓慢的呼气，厅堂混响为二人所闻时环境声渐深。

第 5 镜（9-11.5 秒）：手持游移广角-中景拍 Roco，85mm——但整镜摄影机都在运动中。它从他右肩后方开始，随着他缓身环顾圣殿，摄影机以一段弧线绕他追踪——摄影机在他身后自左向右移动，配合他自己的转动，以移动中的揭示让我们看到他之所见。画面捕捉到：左墙上刻凿的石柱面，深入黑暗的烛火线，深远后撤的石板走廊，然后——当摄影机完成弧线、Roco 的目光定于前方——远端尽头的巨型雕像与它唯一的冷昼光柱，双掌上冥想的僧侣是一枚微小剪影，他身侧有一枚温暖的烛光点。这揭示通过摄影机的移动与 Roco 的身体相配合而获得。画面随手持呼吸而剧烈漂移，操作员的脚步声微弱地传进画面。音频：他缓慢转身的脚步声，他的呼吸，厅堂的纵深在缓慢隆起的混响中变得可闻——距离突然既被看见又被听见。

第 6 镜（11.5-13.5 秒）：手持中景拍 Rain，100mm——她现在完全站立。摄影机在她周围是活的，漂移着，从不锁死。她俯身，以单一流畅动作从石板地提起悬浮板——摄影机随她身体下沉，随她站直再升起。她把悬浮板夹在左腋下。她的目光向前，定在远方雕像上——平静、笃定、了然。远处冷光隐约照到她脸的前方，烛火暖光在她身侧。在她身后浅景深虚化里，Roco 的剪影也可见地站着，望向同一方向。摄影机继续呼吸，微微移近一丝。音频：她拿悬浮板的动作，靴子重新落定的轻响，环境声，远处水滴。

第 7 镜（13.5-15 秒）：手持过肩镜，自 Roco 身后，200mm 极长焦。Roco 的肩膀与后脑填满左侧前景，呈柔焦——远处带冥想僧侣的雕像主导深远背景，被长焦压缩得近在咫尺。Roco 的声音亲昵而低沉，近乎耳语，纵深间带厅堂混响："Is this the place?（就是这里吗？）" 一顿。随后是 Rain 的声音，更轻，自他右侧画外："That's right.（没错。）" 交流间摄影机微妙漂移，画面呼吸。音频：他的台词、混响尾音，她的台词、更长的混响尾音没入环境声，远处烛火噼啪。

摄影（CINEMATOGRAPHY）：全程长焦 85mm-200mm。手持持续运动——摄影机从不静止。第 1-2 镜为上升手持（从地面到站直，Jax 搀扶 Roco 起身）。第 3-4 镜为连接 Rain 与两名男子的甩镜。第 5 镜为绕 Roco 的游移弧线，与他自身的转动相匹配。第 6-7 镜为活跃的中近景，带持续随呼吸而动的漂移。操作员始终在场、始终移动、始终透过画面呼吸。对每个角色的动作都回应——他们起身，摄影机起身；他们转身，摄影机转身；他们定住，摄影机定住但仍漂移。极浅景深 f/1.8-f/2。冷调蓝灰、暖烛色点缀、远方雕像上唯一的冷光柱。

音频（AUDIO）：深邃的洞穴环境声，远处水滴带室堂混响，零星的独簇烛火噼啪，角色的呼吸与衣物窸窣，Jax 拉起 Roco 时他靴子的刮擦与用力闷哼——均带明显室堂混响。结尾两句低语对白带强烈混响尾音。第 1 镜 Jax 的"Hey"，第 4 镜他不自觉的惊呼"huh"。无音乐。无配乐。

节奏（PACING）：起身段（第 1-4 镜）动感而有生气——快速的甩镜、紧凑的剪辑、随呼吸而动的势头。第 5 镜的揭示中缓入虔敬。第 6-7 镜落定于亲密对白。片段自无力经由恢复走向敬畏再到笃定——摄影机通过随他们一同物理升起，把观众载过这一弧线，而开场 Jax 与 Roco 之间的触觉接触在空间接管之前就确立了三人组为一个团队。
