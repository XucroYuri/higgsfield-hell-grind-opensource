## 原文 (source)

Edit the statue's hand pose while keeping everything else identical.
Modify only the hands and arms of the seated stone warrior statue. Remove the sword entirely. Change the hand position so that the statue appears to be holding something in cupped hands:
Both hands held together in front of the lower chest / upper lap area, centered along the vertical midline of the statue.
The lower hand is positioned palm-up, fingers slightly curved inward.
The upper hand rests palm-up directly on top of the lower hand, palms stacked and cupped together to form a shallow bowl shape.
Fingers of both hands curve gently upward, creating the impression that the statue is carefully holding or offering an invisible object — as if cradling something precious.
The hands should be raised slightly away from the body (not resting flat on the lap), elbows bent, forearms angled upward at a gentle slope toward the cupped hands.
Leave the cupped space empty — no object inside, just the gesture of holding.
Preserve everything else exactly:
Same stone material with weathered grey texture and green moss patches in the same locations
Same armored shoulders, robes, and belt with geometric pattern
Same bald head, stern facial expression, and furrowed brow
Same cross-legged seated pose
Same lighting, camera angle, background, and three-view composition (front view, back view, close-up portrait)
Same sculptural style and proportions
Only the hand/arm positions and the absence of the sword should change. The hands must be carved from the same mossy stone, matching the rest of the statue's material and weathering.

## 中文对照 (zh)

编辑雕像的手部姿势，其余一切保持不变。

只修改这件端坐的石武士雕像的双手与双臂。完全移除长剑。改变手部位置，使雕像看起来像在用合拢的手掌托着什么东西：
双手合拢举起，位于下胸 / 上膝部位前方，沿雕像垂直中线居中。
下方的手掌心向上，手指微微向内弯曲。
上方的手掌心向上直接叠放在下方手上，掌心相叠并合拢，形成一个浅浅的碗形。
双手的手指微微向上弯曲，营造出雕像正在小心地托着或献出一件无形之物的印象——仿佛正捧着一样珍贵的东西。
双手应略微抬离身体（而非平放在膝上），手肘弯曲，前臂微微向上倾斜，朝向合拢的双手。
让合拢的空间保持空虚——里面没有物体，只有托物的姿态。
其余一切完全保持不变：
同样的石材，带有风化的灰色纹理和位于相同位置的绿色苔藓斑块
同样的装甲肩部、长袍与几何图案腰带
同样的光头、严峻的面部表情与紧锁的眉头
同样的盘腿端坐姿势
同样的灯光、机位、背景与三视图构图（正面视图、背面视图、特写肖像）
同样的雕塑风格与比例
只有手/手臂的位置和移除长剑应改变。双手必须用同样的覆有苔藓的石材雕刻，与雕像其余部分的材质和风化程度相匹配。
