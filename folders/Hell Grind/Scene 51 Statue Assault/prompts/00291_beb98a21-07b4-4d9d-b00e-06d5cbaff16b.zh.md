## 原文 (source)

Stone guardian dismisses crystal-armored Roco, three iconic beats, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300, Cuarón Children of Men. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient carved stone with relief detail. 60fps, 8K with brief slow-motion at impact moments.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, body-driven tracking. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

GUARDIAN SCALE: The guardian is a towering carved stone figure, roughly ten times Roco's height. His foot reaches Roco's shoulder height. His hand is large enough to swat Roco like an insect.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, weathered ancient carved stone columns, flagstone floor, dust haze trembling.

Beat 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld low-angle, 35mm, f/2. Roco crystal-armored visible in foreground striking the towering carved stone leg. He draws his ruby crystal sword back with both hands and DRIVES it forward into the stone shin — saturated red flash on impact, dust eruption, his maximum mineral roar of effort. The leg towers above him, the rest of the guardian's body extending up out of frame. The carved stone barely chipped. The leg does not move. Sustained held moment — Roco frozen with sword embedded in stone, the towering leg motionless, indifferent. Audio: his roar, deep CRUNCH of crystal into stone, dust eruption, the deep red hum from above unbroken.

Beat 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close, 50mm, f/1.8. The guardian's massive carved stone HAND descends from above into the frame — the hand large enough to fill half the frame as it approaches Roco. With brief subtle slow-motion stretch as the hand approaches. Roco at floor level visible smaller than the descending palm. Then return to full speed — the palm IMPACTS Roco with thunderous WHUMP, Roco's body LAUNCHES sideways through the air, dust trailing. The guardian's hand returns up out of frame without urgency. Audio: grinding stone of the hand descending, brief silence in slow-motion, then thunderous WHUMP at full volume, his sharp grunt, WHOOSH of body flying.

Beat 3 (10-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco's body flies through chamber air. He SLAMS into a carved stone column with thunderous CRACK, body crumpling, sliding down to the flagstone, coming to rest at the column's base. The towering guardian visible in deep blurred background, only his legs and lower torso in frame, the rest extending up out of frame, already turning attention elsewhere. Audio: thunderous CRACK of body on column, dust eruption, his deep groan of pain, body sliding, the deep red hum continuing in deep blur unaffected.

PACING: 15-second three-beat humiliation. Roco strikes leg with full force to no effect (0-5s), guardian's hand swats him aside (5-10s), Roco crashes into column and crumples (10-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

石质守护者打发掉水晶装甲的 Roco，三个标志性节拍（iconic beats），15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》、Snyder《300 勇士》、Cuarón《人类之子》。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 红宝石水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光，暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶、风化古老雕花石面带浮雕细节。60fps，8K，撞击瞬间带有短暂慢动作。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持，以身体驱动的跟拍。绝不正面——保持四分之三角度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，碎片坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：守护者是一座高耸的雕花石像，约有 Roco 的十倍之高。他的脚可及 Roco 的肩高。他的手掌大到足以像拍虫一般把 Roco 拍开。

参见这名男子：完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），风化古老雕花石柱，石板地面，尘雾在震颤。

Beat 1（0-5 秒）：四分之三手持低机位，35mm，f/2。水晶装甲的 Roco 可见于前景，正砍向高耸的雕花石腿。他双手将红宝石水晶剑向后收蓄，然后狠狠 DRIVES 向前刺入石质胫骨——落地时饱和红光一闪、尘土迸发、他爆发出最大力度的矿物咆哮。石腿在他上方高耸而立，守护者身体的其余部分一直延伸出画框顶端。雕花石面几乎未被削破。石腿纹丝不动。定格瞬间延续——Roco 僵在原地、剑嵌于石中，高耸的石腿巍然不动、漠不关心。音频：他的咆哮、水晶切入石头深处的 CRUNCH 声、尘土迸发、自上方传来的深沉红光嗡鸣毫无中断。

Beat 2（5-10 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，50mm，f/1.8。守护者巨大的雕花石 HAND 自上方降入画面——这只手大到手在逼近 Roco 时足以填满画面的一半。手接近时带有一阵短暂轻微的慢动作拉伸。地面高度的 Roco 露为身影，比这根正在降下的巨掌更小。随即恢复全速——巨掌以雷鸣般的 WHUMP 撞中 Roco，Roco 的身体侧向飞掠空，尘土拖曳。守护者的手不慌不忙地缩回画框上方。音频：手掌降下时碾磨的石声、慢动作中的短暂静默、随即全音量下雷鸣般的 WHUMP、他尖锐的闷哼、身体飞掠的 WHOOSH。

Beat 3（10-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，50mm，f/1.8。Roco 的身体飞掠过密殿的空气。他轰然撞上一座雕花石柱，发出雷鸣般的 CRACK 声，身体瘫软、沿石柱滑落至石板地面、停在柱脚。深处虚化的背景中可见高耸的守护者，画面中只有他的腿与下躯干，其余部分一直延伸出画框顶端、已把注意力转向别处。音频：身体撞柱的雷鸣 CRACK、尘土迸发、他深沉的痛苦呻吟、身体滑落、深处虚化中深沉的红光嗡鸣依旧在持续、不受影响。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒三拍羞辱。Roco全力出招砍腿却毫无效果（0-5 秒），守护者的手将他拍开（5-10 秒），Roco 撞上石柱瘫软（10-15 秒）。
