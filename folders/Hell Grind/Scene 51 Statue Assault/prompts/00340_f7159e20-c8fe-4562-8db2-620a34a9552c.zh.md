## 原文 (source)

Roco blocks guardian's vertical sword strike with crystal shield, then guardian shoves him aside, side profile, 7 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300 side profile combat. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient carved stone, flagstone with real fracture mechanics. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, chaotic verite shake, lag focus. STRICT SIDE PROFILE — camera locked at the side of Roco capturing the action in 90-degree profile. 50mm lens at chest height, f/1.4.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword, hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze, flagstone floor.

Single shot (0-7s): Strict side profile shoulder-mounted handheld medium, 50mm, f/1.4. Roco fully crystal-armored in 90-degree side profile facing frame right.

At 0-1.5s: The colossal stone sword visible in deep blur descending fast from above through the upper frame. Audio drops — only blade whistle.

At 1.5-3s: From Roco's left palm a CRYSTAL SHIELD MATERIALIZES — faceted ruby crystal forming out of nothing at the level of his crystal collar. Sword IMPACTS shield half a centimeter from his head — saturated red FLASH, deafening CRACK, dust and crystal fragments erupting in profile across frame. Audio returns full volume. Shield CRACKS in fracture lines. Flagstone beneath his boots SHATTERS, sinking five centimeters. Roco drops to one knee in profile, right hand braced behind.

At 3-7s: The guardian's MASSIVE FREE STONE HAND descends from above sweeping LATERALLY from frame right. Palm IMPACTS the shield sideways with deep WHUMP. Roco LAUNCHES sideways through the air to frame left, body horizontal, cracked shield still in his left gauntlet. He SLAMS into flagstones at frame left with deep CRACK, body skidding to rest. Dust settling.

PACING: 7-second single shot. Sword falls (0-1.5s), shield materializes and impacts, Roco to knee (1.5-3s), guardian shoves Roco sideways across frame (3-7s).
