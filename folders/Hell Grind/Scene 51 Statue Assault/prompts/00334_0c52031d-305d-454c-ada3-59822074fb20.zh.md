Rain on hoverboard flies from distance toward monk's high ledge from behind, colossal stone sword cuts her off, she dives to evade, 5 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

Color: deep cool blue-grey, accent purple, accent warm amber. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic skin, fabric, matte-black hoverboard, weathered ancient carved stone with script, monk's deep maroon robes. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld behind Rain over her right shoulder, slightly elevated. Frame trembling.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 2.
Refer to the elderly East Asian monk in dark maroon robes from Image 3.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze, weathered ancient rock cliff wall in the distance with carved stone ledge twelve meters up.

Single shot (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld over-the-shoulder rear view, 50mm, f/1.8.

At 0-3s: Rain visible from behind on the matte-black hoverboard, body crouched forward into low riding stance, silver-blonde hair flying back toward camera, jacket flaring back, both arms extended for balance NO HANDLEBARS. Purple thrusters BLAZE behind the board pointed toward camera. She rides forward through the chamber, the monk's distant ledge growing closer in deep blur ahead with the small warm amber candle glow visible. Audio: thrusters scream, her controlled fast breath, the monk's distant throat-tone, chamber rumble.

At 3-3.5s: The MASSIVE COLOSSAL CARVED STONE SWORD suddenly enters frame from the side sweeping into Rain's flight path — the 5-meter blade fills the entire middle and upper portion of frame as a wall of weathered ancient stone with carved script, completely blocking the path to the monk, the blade close enough that Rain is just meters from impacting it. The sword pauses at the apex of its sweep momentarily.

At 3.5-5s: Rain THROWS the hoverboard sharply DOWNWARD — her body and the board angling almost vertical in a steep dive, her silhouette dropping out of the upper-center frame to the lower frame, thrusters firing for the dive maneuver. The colossal stone blade remains in the upper portion of frame having missed her by inches as she drops below it. She emerges below the blade still riding forward at lower altitude. Audio: sudden massive WHOOSH of the colossal blade entering frame, her sharp inhale of recognition, the thrusters firing for the evasion dive, the wake of the blade above shoving her hair down.

PACING: 5-second cut-off and evasion. Rain rides forward toward distant monk (0-3s), colossal sword sweeps in massively across her path filling frame (3-3.5s), Rain dives sharply downward to evade below the blade (3.5-5s).
