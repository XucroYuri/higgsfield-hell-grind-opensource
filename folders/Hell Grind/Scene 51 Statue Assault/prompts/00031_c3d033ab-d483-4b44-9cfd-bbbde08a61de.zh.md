## 原文 (source)

A young Black man in his 20s stands with his back pressed 
flat against a massive ancient stone column. Short bright 
golden-yellow natural hair. Tactical clear wraparound visor 
glasses. Black tactical bomber jacket with orange accent 
details. Olive green cargo pants. Black fingerless gloves.

He turns his head slightly to the side, mouth open, shouting 
something offscreen to the left. One hand rests loosely 
against the column surface. Body language: casual, 
unbothered — like he has all the time in the world.

Setting: underground ancient temple corridor. Dark stone 
architecture. Warm amber torchlight from wall-mounted 
torches. Fine dust particles drifting in the air. 
Deep shadows between columns.

Camera: handheld, natural breathing motion, subtle organic 
sway. Telephoto lens, 85–100mm feel. Shallow depth of field — 
man in sharp focus, column edge soft in foreground, 
corridor behind fully blurred into warm bokeh.
Camera angle: side profile, slightly low, 
looking up at roughly 15 degrees. 
Slow natural drift left as he shouts.

Lighting: warm amber torchlight from the left side, 
deep shadow on the right half of his face and body.

Cinematic realism. Photorealistic. 
Film grain, natural motion blur on edges.
No camera shake artifacts. No jitter. 
No distortion on face or hands.
Maintain exact character appearance throughout.

一位 20 多岁的年轻黑人男子背贴平一根巨大的古老石柱站立。短而明亮的金色自然发。战术透明环绕式护目镜眼镜（visor glasses）。带橙色点缀细节的黑色战术轰炸机夹克。橄榄绿工装裤。黑色露指手套。

他微微侧头，嘴巴张着，向画面外左侧呼喊着什么。一只手松松地搭在石柱表面上。身体语言：随意、若无其事——仿佛他有着全世界的时间。

Setting：地下古庙走廊。暗色石质建筑。来自壁挂火炬的温暖琥珀色火炬光。细微的尘土颗粒在空中飘荡。石柱之间深影。

Camera：手持，自然的呼吸动作，微妙的有机摇摆。长焦镜头，85–100mm 的手感。浅景深——男子锐利对焦，石柱边缘于前景虚化，身后走廊完全模糊为暖色散景。摄影机角度：侧面侧身，略微低机位，约以 15 度抬望。他大喊时缓慢自然地向左侧漂移。

电影式写实。写实摄影（Photorealistic）。胶片颗粒，边缘自然的运动模糊。无摄影机抖动伪影、无抖动、无面部或手部畸变。全程保持完全一致的角色外观。
