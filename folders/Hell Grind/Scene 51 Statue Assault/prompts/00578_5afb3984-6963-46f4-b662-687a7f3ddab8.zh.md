## 原文 (source)

Vast ancient sanctum carved from natural rock, awakening sequence, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, deep cool blue-grey palette with warm amber oil-lamp accents, dramatic chiaroscuro, no music. Inspired by Children of Men and The Revenant — handheld kinetic intimacy.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.
Refer to the man with dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 3.
Refer to the elderly East Asian monk in dark maroon robes from Image 4.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 5.

Setting: vast cavern-sanctum, fifteen meters wide, forty meters long, towering carved stone column-faces on both side walls receding into darkness, irregular flagstone floor, two parallel lines of small oil-lamps flickering warm amber along the walls, ceiling lost in shadow far above. At the far end on the central axis: a colossal seated stone warrior statue, bald, muscled, ancient armor, hands cupped in his lap. A cold pale daylight shaft falls from a fissure above onto the statue. On the statue's cupped hands four meters above the floor: the elderly monk seated cross-legged in meditation, motionless, eyes closed, a small candle beside him. Roco lies face-down on the flagstones near the side wall at the entrance end. Jax stands beside him, recovered first. Rain stands a few meters away, holding the hoverboard at her side in her left hand.

Shot 1 (0-2s): Handheld extreme low-angle close on Roco's face pressed against the flagstone, 100mm long lens, shallow focus on his eye. Camera lies on the floor inches from his face. His dust-streaked cheek presses against cold stone. His eye opens slowly, focusing. Jax's open hand enters from above frame, reaching toward Roco's shoulder. Jax's voice off-screen: a low "Hey." Roco's eye tracks up toward the hand. Frame breathes with intimate handheld drift. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drop with chamber reverb, Roco's slow ragged inhale, faint oil-lamp crackle.

Shot 2 (2-5s): Handheld low-rising shot beside the two men, 50mm wider lens. Jax kneels, grips Roco's shoulder, takes his forearm in a strong clasp, pulls. Roco pushes up onto hands, then knees, then rises to his feet — continuous motion. Camera RISES WITH HIM, operator standing as Roco stands, rotating slightly to reveal the sanctum interior — towering column-faces extending up out of frame, oil-lamp lines stretching into deep darkness toward the distant statue, the cold daylight shaft visible in deep background. Roco leans briefly on Jax's shoulder. Frame drifts and corrects, alive. Audio: scrape of grip, Jax's grunt, scrape of boots, both breathing heavily, ambient drone, chamber reverb deepening.

Shot 3 (5-7s): Cut to handheld medium close on Rain, 100mm, three-quarter profile. She holds the hoverboard at her side, purple thruster glow as soft accent. Dust-streaked face, eyes tracking slowly UPWARD as she takes in the chamber's height. Cold distant light catches the scar by her eye. Behind her in shallow-focus blur, column-faces rise out of frame. Eyes tracking up, then settling forward toward Roco's blurred silhouette in deep background, she speaks quietly with chamber reverb: "You okay?" Frame breathes around her. Audio: her breathing, ambient drone, her line with long reverb tail.

Shot 4 (7-9s): Cut to high-angle extreme wide from far above near the dark ceiling, looking straight down at the trio, 50mm. The three appear as small figures on the vast flagstone floor — Roco and Jax close together, Rain to their right, all dwarfed by the chamber. Parallel oil-lamp lines stretch toward the distant statue at frame center, the cold daylight shaft falling on it, the tiny pinpoint of the monk's candle visible on its cupped hands. Trio looks small, fragile. Camera holds with subtle handheld drift. In the final beat all three tilt their heads back to look up toward camera — small dust-streaked faces lifting toward the unreachable ceiling. Audio: distant footsteps and breathing, ambient drone with deeper chamber reverb at this height, the chamber's enormous emptiness audible.

Shot 5 (9-11s): Cut to handheld medium two-shot on Roco and Jax, 100mm. Roco has stepped slightly away from Jax, hand pressed to his temple, blinking. Jax beside him, glasses just straightened, head lifted, looking forward and up. Roco follows Jax's gaze, hand drops from temple, face shifts from confusion to slow awe. In deep background between their heads, the colossal statue is clearly visible — cold daylight shaft on its severe carved face, the tiny silhouette of the elderly monk on its cupped hands, the warm pinpoint of his candle. Both men's eyes track upward toward the monk, mouths slightly parted. Jax exhales a half-syllable, "huh," with chamber reverb. Audio: their breathing, ambient drone, faint oil-lamp crackles, faint suggestion of the monk's slow breathing carried by chamber reverb across the distance.

Shot 6 (11-13s): Cut to handheld roving wide on Roco, 35mm wider lens, camera in motion the entire shot. Starts behind his right shoulder, tracks AROUND him in a partial arc as he rotates, so we see what he sees in a moving reveal. Frame catches: towering column-faces on the left wall extending up out of frame, oil-lamp line stretching into vanishing-point darkness, deep corridor receding, then — as the camera completes its arc — the colossal statue at the far end with the meditating monk on its cupped hands. Reveal earned through camera matching his body. Frame drifts hard with handheld breath. Audio: his turning footsteps, his breathing, chamber depth audible in swelling reverb.

Shot 7 (13-15s): Cut to handheld medium tight two-shot on Rain and Roco, 100mm. They stand close together, Rain holding the hoverboard at her side. Rain's eyes are on Roco, checking him, concerned. Roco does not look at her — eyes forward, fixed on the distant statue, awe settling into resolve. After a beat, his answer comes flat and decided with chamber reverb: "Let's move." Rain's eyes flick from his face to the distant statue, then back to him — she nods once, small. Behind them in shallow-focus blur, Jax's silhouette is already turned forward, ready. Audio: his line, reverb tail, distant oil-lamp crackle.

CAMERA: long lens 35mm-200mm. Handheld throughout, alive, breath-driven, the operator a fourth presence. Clean direct cuts (not whip-pans) connect characters. Frame constantly drifts, reactive but never jittery. Shallow depth of field f/1.8-f/2. Wider lenses (35mm, 50mm) used for scale-revealing shots; longer lenses (100mm) for character close-ups. Scale through vanishing-point oil-lamp geometry, vertical column tops cropped out of frame, the high-angle extreme wide of Shot 4.

AUDIO: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drops with chamber reverb, oil-lamp crackles, breathing and clothing rustle, all with significant chamber reverb. Dialogue: Jax's "Hey," Rain's "You okay?", Jax's "huh," Roco's "Let's move" — all with strong reverb tails. Faint suggestion of the monk's slow breathing carried across distance in Shot 5. No music.

## 中文对照 (zh)

从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔古老圣殿（sanctum），苏醒序列，15 秒，16:9 anamorphic，写实摄影（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，深冷的蓝灰基调辅以暖琥珀色油灯点缀，戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro），无音乐。灵感源自《人类之子》（Children of Men）与《荒野猎人》（The Revenant）——手持（handheld）动能化的亲密感。

参照 Image 1 中那位银白浅发、眼角带疤、灰色连帽夹克的女性。
参照 Image 2 中那位漂金寸头、深肤色、透明眼镜、深色夹克的男性。
参照 Image 3 中那位深色乱发、留须、鼻梁贴红色绷带、深色连帽夹克的男性。
参照 Image 4 中那位身着深绛红僧袍的年长东亚僧人。
参照 Image 5 中那架哑光黑悬浮滑板（hoverboard），配备泛紫色光芒的推进器核心。

置景（Setting）：辽阔的洞窟圣殿，宽十五米，长四十米，两侧墙上刻着高耸的石柱面，渐隐没入两侧的黑暗，地面为不规则的石板（flagstone）地，沿墙有两列平行的小油灯摇曳着暖琥珀色光，天花板在头顶高处隐没于阴影。中央轴线的远端：一尊巨型的坐姿石雕战士像，光头、肌肉毕现、身披古老铠甲，双手合拢搁在膝上。一道惨白的冷日光线从上方的一道裂隙照落，落在雕像上。在雕像离地面四米高合拢的双手上：那位年长僧人端坐盘腿入定，一动不动，双眼紧闭，身旁点着一支小蜡烛。Roco 面朝下躺在入口端侧墙旁的石板地上。Jax 站在他身旁，先一步恢复过来。Rain 站在几米开外，左手把悬浮滑板拿在身侧。

镜头 1（0-2 秒）：手持极低机位特写，对准 Roco 紧贴在石板地上的脸，100mm 长焦，对他的眼做浅焦。摄影机躺在离他脸数英寸的地面上。他沾满尘土的侧颊贴着冰冷的石头。他的眼缓缓睁开，对焦。Jax 摊开的手从画面上方探入，朝 Roco 的肩伸去。Jax 的声音在画面外：一句低低的「Hey.」。Roco 的眼睛循着手向上望去。画面随亲密的持握漂移而起伏。Audio：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响（chamber reverb）的水滴声，Roco 缓慢粗重的吸气，轻微的油灯噼啪声。

镜头 2（2-5 秒）：手持低机位升起的镜头，两名男性身旁，50mm 更广的镜头。Jax 跪下，抓住 Roco 的肩，以有力的锁握握住他的前臂，拉动。Roco 撑到手上，再到膝上，然后站起身——一气呵成的动作。摄影机随他一起升起，Roco 站起时操作员站起身，同时微微旋转，揭示圣殿内部——高耸的石柱面向上延伸出画面，油灯连排向深重的黑暗朝着远处那尊雕像延伸，惨白的日光线束在纵深处可见。Roco 短暂地靠在 Jax 的肩上。画面漂移又校正，生气勃勃。Audio：握持的刮擦声，Jax 的闷哼，靴子的刮擦声，两人呼吸沉重，环境低鸣，室内混响渐深。

镜头 3（5-7 秒）：切至手持中景特写，对准 Rain，100mm，四分之三侧面。她把悬浮滑板拿在身侧，紫色推进器光晕作柔和点缀。满脸尘土，目光缓缓向上升移，她打量着厅堂的高度。冷而遥远的光掠过她眼角那道疤。在她身后以浅焦模糊中，石柱面向上延伸出画面。目光上移，随后向前落到纵深处 Roco 模糊的剪影上，她轻声开口，带室内混响：「You okay?」画面在她周围起伏。Audio：她的呼吸，环境低鸣，她的台词带悠长的混响尾音。

镜头 4（7-9 秒）：切至自遥远上方幽暗天花板附近的高机位超广角，垂直向下俯视这支小队，50mm。三人作为辽阔石板地上的细小身影而显现——Roco 与 Jax 靠得很近，Rain 在他们右侧，全被厅堂衬得渺小。平行的油灯连排朝画面中央那座遥远的雕像延伸，惨白的日光线束落在它身上，僧人那支蜡烛的微小光点在其合拢的双手上可见。小队显得渺小、脆弱。摄影机以微妙的手持漂移稳住。最后一拍中，三人全都仰起头，向摄影机看上来——沾满尘土的小脸朝着那不可触及的天花板抬升。Audio：远处的脚步声与呼吸，此处高度带更深室内混响的环境低鸣，厅堂那无边空旷在回响中清晰可闻。

镜头 5（9-11 秒）：切至手持中景双人镜头，对准 Roco 与 Jax，100mm。Roco 已经轻轻远离 Jax，手按在自己太阳穴上，眨着眼。Jax 在他身旁，刚扶正眼镜，抬起头，向前向上望去。Roco 追随 Jax 的视线，手从太阳穴上落下，脸从困惑转向缓慢的敬畏。在两人头部之间纵深的背景里，那尊巨像是清晰可见的——惨白的日光线束落在它严酷的刻面上，年长僧人小小的剪影在它合拢的双手上，他那支蜡烛的温暖光点。两名男性的目光都朝上方的僧人望去，嘴唇微微张开。Jax 呼出一截半音节，「huh」，带室内混响。Audio：他们的呼吸，环境低鸣，轻微的油灯噼啪声，僧人缓慢呼吸的隐隐声息被室内混响隔着距离遥遥传来。

镜头 6（11-13 秒）：切至手持游移全景（roving wide），对准 Roco，35mm 更广的镜头，整个镜头摄影机都在运动中。从他右肩后开始，在他转身时以一段弧线绕着他横向追踪，于是我们在一个移动的揭示中看到他所见之物。画面捕捉：左墙上高耸的石柱面向画面外延伸，油灯连排延伸入灭点（vanishing-point）般的黑暗，幽深的走廊退去，随后——当摄影机完成弧线——远端那尊巨像，以及它合拢双手上那位入定的僧人。这揭示是通过摄影机贴合他的身体而挣得的。画面随手持呼吸剧烈漂移。Audio：他转身的脚步声，他的呼吸，厅堂的深度在渐浓的回响中可闻。

镜头 7（13-15 秒）：切至手持中景紧凑双人镜头，对准 Rain 与 Roco，100mm。他们站得很近，Rain 把悬浮滑板拿在身侧。Rain 的目光落在 Roco 身上，查看他，神色担忧。Roco 没有看她——目光向前，锁定在远方那尊雕像上，敬畏正沉淀为决意。顿了一拍后，他的回答平平地、决然地传来，带室内混响：「Let's move.」Rain 的目光从他脸上转而投向远处那尊雕像，再转回他身上——她轻轻点了一下头。在他们身后以浅焦模糊中，Jax 的剪影已经转身向前，准备就绪。Audio：他的台词，混响尾音，远处油灯的噼啪声。

CAMERA：长焦 35mm-200mm。全程手持，生气勃勃、由呼吸驱动，操作员是第四位在场者。干脆利落的直接切（而非甩镜/whit-pans）连接各角色。画面持续漂移，灵敏却绝不抖动。浅景深 f/1.8-f/2。更广的镜头（35mm、50mm）用于揭示尺度的镜头；更长的镜头（100mm）用于角色特写。尺度通过灭点油灯的几何、画面外裁切掉的石柱顶部、镜头 4 的高机位超广角得以传达。

AUDIO：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响的水滴声，油灯噼啪声，呼吸与衣物沙沙，全都带显著的室内混响。台词：Jax 的「Hey,」Rain 的「You okay?」、Jax 的「huh,」Roco 的「Let's move」——全部带强烈的混响尾音。镜头 5 里僧人缓慢呼吸的隐隐声息隔着距离遥遥传来。无音乐。
