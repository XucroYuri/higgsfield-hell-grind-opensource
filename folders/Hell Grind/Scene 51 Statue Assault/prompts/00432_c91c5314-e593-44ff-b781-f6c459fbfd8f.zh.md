Crystal-armored Roco climbs guardian's leg and strikes the knee, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, EXTREME trembling, body-driven vertical tracking, operator climbing in elevation with the action.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling. Roco crystal-armored ascending the guardian's massive carved stone leg.

Shot 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco mid-jump, 50mm, f/1.8. Operator handheld tracking him from a side angle. Roco crystal-armored leaping upward from flagstone toward the guardian's massive carved stone shin in deep blur — his armored body airborne, sword sheathed back into his gauntlet, both crystal hands extending forward. He impacts the carved stone of the shin and his ruby crystal forearm spikes EMBED into ancient cracks in the weathered stone, his crystal-clawed gauntlets gripping. He hangs on the shin, body pressed against the colossal leg. Audio: his mineral grunt of effort, the CRACK of crystal embedding into stone, the chamber tremor, distant guardian hum.

Shot 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Roco climbing up the guardian's shin, 85mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on his crystal gauntlets gripping carved stone — reaching up, embedding spikes, pulling himself higher. Operator on Easyrig rising vertically with him as he ascends, the carved stone passing downward through frame as he climbs upward. He passes the calf, reaches the back of the knee, finds purchase in a deep ancient crack of the carved stone. Mineral grunts of effort. Audio: CRACK of crystal embedding into stone repeatedly with each grip, his effortful grunts, scraping of armor against stone.

Shot 3 (10-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Roco at the back of the guardian's KNEE, 85mm, f/1.4. He has reached the joint — vulnerable point where carved stone meets carved stone in the colossus's leg. He grows the ruby crystal sword from his right gauntlet again — the sword extending outward in real time with crystalline crackling. He drives it into the joint of the back of the knee with both crystal hands — saturated red FLASH on impact, the sword sinking deeper than previous strikes into the gap between armor plates of the carved leg, the joint spraying stone fragments and ancient dust. Mineral roar from his sealed body. The guardian's entire leg in deep blur SHUDDERS from the strike, the leg beginning to BUCKLE slightly. Audio: the deep CRUNCH of crystal driving into the joint, his roar of effort, the SHUDDER of the colossal leg, debris cascading, the colossus's deep red hum spiking.

PACING: 15-second vertical climb attack. Jump and grip onto shin (0-5s), climb up to knee (5-10s), driving strike into the knee joint (10-15s).

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影，变形宽银幕电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Snyder《斯巴达三百勇士》、Villeneuve《沙丘·第二部》的垂直尺度战斗。Lighting（灯光）：上方惨白冷光，守护巨人画面上方出画的眼窝红光、以及 Roco 红宝石水晶装甲的饱和红光，温暖琥珀色油灯。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰为主，主导饱和红，重度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实半透红宝石水晶、带裂纹与青苔的饱经风化古石。Continuity（连贯性）锁定。绝不正面——四边形取景。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig，剧烈抖动，身体驱动的垂直跟随，摄影师随动作攀升高度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（洞室震颤）：剧烈摇晃，碎石坠落。

引用图 1 中那名全身包覆半透明红宝石色刻面水晶装甲的男人，他肩部有翼状尖刺、前臂有刺、锯齿状护颈遮蔽面容。

Setting（设定）：洞窟圣殿，尘雾震颤。Roco 水晶装甲正向上攀爬守护巨人巨大的雕纹石腿。

Shot 1（0-5s）：四边形手持中景近景，Roco 跃起之中，50mm，f/1.8。摄影师手持从侧面角度跟拍他。Roco 水晶装甲自石板腾跃而起、扑向深虚化中守护巨人巨大的雕纹石胫——装甲身体凌空而起，剑已收回护手内，双水晶手臂前伸。他撞上石胫的雕纹石面，红宝石水晶前臂的尖刺插入风化古石上的古代裂纹，水晶利爪护手抓紧。他攀在石胫上，身体贴住巨腿。Audio（音效）：他矿物质的使力闷哼、水晶嵌入石中的碎裂声、洞室震颤、远处守护巨人的嗡鸣。

Shot 2（5-10s）：切至四边形手持极特写，Roco 攀上守护巨人的胫骨，85mm，f/1.4。极浅对焦在他的水晶护手抓紧雕纹石面上——向上探出、嵌入尖刺、把身体拉得更高。摄影师在 Easyrig 上随他攀升而垂直上升，随着他向上攀爬，雕纹石面自画面中向下掠过。他越过小腿、抵达膝后，在雕纹石上一道深古裂纹里寻得落脚点。矿物质的使力闷哼。Audio（音效）：随着每次握持，水晶多次嵌入石中的碎裂声、他用力的闷哼、装甲刮擦石面的声响。

Shot 3（10-15s）：切至四边形手持极特写，Roco 位于守护巨人的膝后，85mm，f/1.4。他抵达到那个关节——巨腿雕纹石与雕纹石相接的薄弱点。他再度自右手护手长出血宝石水晶剑——剑身带着水晶的劈啪脆响实时向外延展。他双水晶手将其刺入膝后的关节——撞击处饱和红闪光迸溅，剑比先前的每次刺击都刺得更深，没入雕纹巨腿甲片之间的缝隙，关节处喷出石片碎片与古老尘埃。封闭的身体里传出矿物咆哮。浅虚化中守护巨人的整条巨腿因这一击而震颤，巨腿开始微微弯折。Audio（音效）：水晶刺入关节时深沉的嘎吱脆响、他拼尽全力的咆哮、巨腿的震颤、碎石倾泻、巨人身畔飙升的深红嗡鸣。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒的垂直攀爬攻击。跃起攀上胫骨（0-5s），爬至膝部（5-10s），刺击膝关节（10-15s）。
