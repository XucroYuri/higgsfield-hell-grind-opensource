Cold open — Roco's last-second entry into the sanctum, 5 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, ambient drone only — no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.

VISUAL STYLE: cinematic anamorphic, long-lens telephoto cinematography 50mm-85mm equivalent, very shallow depth of field f/2, deep cool blue-grey palette inside the sanctum with warm amber torch accents in the corridor visible behind Roco, dramatic chiaroscuro — most of the frame in deep shadow, the descending stone slab in the upper background of the frame silhouetted against the warm corridor beyond. Heavily desaturated. Inspired by Cuarón's Children of Men single-take action sequences, Iñárritu's Birdman handheld immersive intimacy, Gaspar Noé's Climax kinetic body-camera work.

LOCATION: the threshold between the corridor and the sanctum interior. Camera lies near floor level inside the sanctum, looking BACK toward the corridor — Roco runs/dives TOWARD the camera. Visible through the descending gap behind him: the corridor, with iron torch brackets flickering warm amber light at intervals along both walls, dust-hazed atmosphere, walls covered in carved script. The massive stone slab is descending from above in the upper portion of the frame, the doorway-sized gap rapidly closing — currently about a meter and a half tall and shrinking. The sanctum side of the threshold (where the camera lies) is dark, cool flagstone floor receding behind camera in deep shadow.

CHARACTER: Roco runs at full sprint down the corridor toward the closing gap and the camera, dust-covered, jacket scuffed, breath ragged, eyes locked forward toward camera lens, arms pumping with effort.

Single shot (0-5s): HANDHELD LOW-ANGLE FRONTAL TRACKING shot, camera at floor level inside the sanctum, retreating slowly backward as Roco approaches and dives toward it. 50mm wider lens, very shallow focus. The frame composition: descending stone slab fills the upper portion of the image, the corridor with its warm amber torch flames glowing through the closing gap behind Roco, Roco himself in the lower-center of the frame approaching camera. Frame trembles intensely with kinetic handheld motion.

At 0-1.5s: Roco appears in the corridor beyond, sprinting at full speed toward camera, his body charging forward, boots pounding flagstones, jacket flaring behind him. The torch flames in the corridor walls glow warm amber on either side of him, the corridor's depth visible behind. The slab descends in the upper frame, gap shrinking. Camera retreats backward at a rate roughly matching his approach, holding him in frame. Frame shakes with motion.

At 1.5-2.5s: Roco reaches the threshold and LAUNCHES into a horizontal dive — body going flat, hands extended forward toward camera, jacket flaring. He passes under the descending slab in this beat, the slab passing inches above his back. The camera continues retreating, low to the floor, holding the dive in frame — Roco diving directly toward the lens, the corridor with torches receding behind him as he crosses into the sanctum. Color temperature in the frame shifts as he crosses — warm corridor light fading from his body, cool sanctum shadow taking over.

At 2.5-3.5s: Roco's diving body slides forward on the sanctum flagstones, sliding TOWARD camera, dust kicking up around him. Camera continues retreating slightly to keep him in frame, then comes to rest as he stops sliding. Behind him in the deep background, the slab continues descending, the warm corridor with its torches still visible through the shrinking gap. Roco comes to a stop face-down on the flagstones, head turned to one side, arms splayed.

At 3.5-4.5s: Camera holds steady on Roco's face-down body in close low-angle frontal composition. Behind him: the slab grinds the final distance and SLAMS onto the flagstones with a heavy stone-on-stone impact, sealing the corridor and cutting off the warm torch light. The frame's color shifts as the warm light is extinguished — only cool sanctum shadow remains. The camera shakes hard with the impact. Dust plumes from the slab settling around Roco. Then Roco SLOWLY LIFTS HIS HEAD from the flagstones — he pushes up just slightly, head tilting up, eyes opening, looking forward toward camera. His face is dust-streaked, the red bandage on his nose visible, eyes alert but exhausted, breath ragged.

At 4.5-5s: Camera holds on Roco's face in close low-angle. He is looking forward, into the dark sanctum beyond camera, taking in where he is. His expression: a beat of recognition, of arrival, of "I made it." Frame breathes subtly, alive with handheld drift. Then cut to black.

CINEMATOGRAPHY: 50mm wider lens for full-body kinetic approach AND the descending slab AND the warm corridor in the same composition. Frontal angle — camera lies on the sanctum flagstones looking back at the corridor, Roco runs toward camera, dives toward camera, slides toward camera. Handheld throughout — the operator retreats backward as Roco approaches, then settles as he lands. Frame trembles intensely with kinetic motion during the approach, settles in the final beat to hold Roco's face. Critical color temperature shift: warm amber from corridor torches visible in frame until the slab seals at 4 seconds, then only cool sanctum shadow remains. Heavy motion blur on torch streaks behind Roco during the run, on the dust plumes during the slide. Shallow depth of field f/2. The reveal of his lifted face in the final beat is the emotional center of the shot.

AUDIO: the deep grinding rumble of the descending stone slab continuing throughout — heavy, mechanical, inexorable. Roco's ragged sprinting breath approaching closer to camera as he runs forward. His pounding boots on flagstone, fast rhythm. The grunt of effort as he launches into the dive. The whoosh of air as Roco dives toward camera. The friction-scrape of his body sliding across flagstone, close. The final SLAM of the slab onto the floor at 4 seconds — heavy stone-on-stone impact with significant chamber reverb propagating into the sanctum interior, abruptly cutting off the corridor's distant ambience. A beat of dust-haze silence after the slam. Roco's heavy exhale as he lifts his head, very close to camera. His ragged breath in the silence. NO MUSIC. NO SCORE.

PACING: kinetic and immersive in the approach and dive (0-3.5s) — camera retreating before Roco's charge transmits speed and danger to the audience. Settles at 3.5-5s — the slab slams, the silence, Roco lifting his head, his face revealed for the first time clearly. The clip ends on his arrived expression in the dust-haze, setting up the awakening of clip 1.

## 中文对照 (zh)

冷开场（cold open）——Roco 最后一刻闯入圣殿，5 秒，16:9 anamorphic 宽银幕（2.39:1），写实摄影（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，仅环境低鸣——无音乐。

参照 Image 1 中那位深色乱发、淡须、鼻梁贴红色绷带、深色连帽夹克的男性。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：电影感的 anamorphic，50mm-85mm 等效长焦远摄摄影，极浅景深 f/2，圣殿内部为深冷的蓝灰基调，辅以 Roco 身后可见走廊里的暖琥珀火把点缀，戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）——画面大部分处于深阴影中，画面上方背景里正在下落的石板门以剪影映衬在更远处温暖的走廊之上。高度去饱和。灵感源自 Cuarón 的《人类之子》（Children of Men）单镜头动作序列、Iñárritu 的《鸟人》（Birdman）手持沉浸式亲密、Gaspar Noé 的《高潮》（Climax）动能化的身体摄影。

地点（LOCATION）：走廊与圣殿内部之间的门槛处。摄影机躺在圣殿内部贴近地面的位置上，回望向走廊——Roco 朝摄影机跑来/扑来。透过他身后正在下落的缝隙可见：走廊，两侧墙上一段段铁制火把托架摇曳着暖琥珀光，尘土弥漫的氛围，墙面上满布刻写的文字。上方的巨型石板门正从上方下落，出现在画面上方区域，门大小的缝隙正迅速收窄——目前约一米半高，仍在缩小。门槛的圣殿一侧（摄影机所躺之处）幽暗、冷调，石板地面在摄影机后方隐入深阴影。

角色（CHARACTER）：Roco 以全速冲刺沿走廊朝正在收窄的缝隙与摄影机奔来，满身尘土，夹克磨旧，呼吸粗重，目光锁定朝摄影机镜头向前，双臂奋力摆动。

单镜头（0-5 秒）：手持低机位正面追踪镜头，摄影机位于圣殿内地面高度，在 Roco 接近并向它扑来时正缓慢地后退。50mm 更广的镜头，极浅焦。画面构图：下落中的石板门填满画面上方，走廊连同温暖琥珀色的火把火焰透过 Roco 身后正在收闭的缝隙发光，Roco 本人位于画面下部中央朝摄影机接近。画面随动能化的手持运动剧烈颤动。

0-1.5 秒：Roco 出现在更前方的走廊里，以全力朝摄影机冲刺，身体前冲，靴子重重踏在石板地上，夹克在身后翻飞。走廊墙上的火把火焰在他两侧泛着暖琥珀光，走廊的纵深在身后可见。石板在上方画面中下落，缝隙在收窄。摄影机以大致与他接近速度相称的速率后退，把他留在画面中。画面随运动而晃动。

1.5-2.5 秒：Roco 抵达门槛并跃起成一记水平飞扑——身体放平，双手朝摄影机前伸，夹克翻飞。在这一拍中他自下落中的石板门下穿过，石板自他背上越过数英寸。摄影机继续后退，贴着地面，把飞扑留在画面中——Roco 径直朝镜头扑来，随他穿过进入圣殿，带火把的走廊在他身后退去。他穿过的瞬间画面色温发生转变——他身上温暖的走廊光渐消，圣殿冷调的阴影接管过来。

2.5-3.5 秒：Roco 飞扑的身体在圣殿石板地上向前滑行，朝摄影机滑来，尘土在他身边扬起。摄影机继续略微后退以把他留在画面中，随后在他停止滑动时稳住。在他身后纵深的背景中，石板继续下落，带火把的温暖走廊透过正在收窄的缝隙仍可见。Roco 脸朝下停下，头转向一侧，双臂摊开。

3.5-4.5 秒：摄影机以致近景低机位正面构图持在 Roco 脸朝下的身体上。他身后：石板碾过最后一段距离，轰然砸在石板地上，发出沉重而石压石（stone-on-stone）的撞击，封死走廊，切断温暖的火把光。随着暖光被熄灭，画面色彩转变——只剩下圣殿冷调的阴影。摄影机随撞击大力晃动。尘土扬尘从石板上落下，在 Roco 周围沉降。随后 Roco 慢慢把头从石板地上抬起——他只是微微撑起，头仰起，眼睁着，朝摄影机前看。他的脸沾满尘土，鼻上的红色绷带可见，目光警觉却疲惫，呼吸粗重。

4.5-5 秒：摄影机以致近景低机位持在 Roco 脸上。他朝前看，落入摄影机后方的幽暗圣殿，打量着身处何地。他的表情：一瞬的认出、抵达、一种「我成功了」的意味。画面微妙地呼吸，充满手持漂移的生气。随后切黑。

摄影（CINEMATOGRAPHY）：50mm 更广的镜头，使全身能动的接近、下落中的石板与温暖的走廊落入同一幅构图。正面角度——摄影机躺在圣殿石板地上回望走廊，Roco 朝摄影机跑来、朝摄影机扑来、朝摄影机滑来。全程手持——随着 Roco 接近，操作员后退，随后在他落地时落定。接近时画面随动能运动剧烈颤动，最后一拍落定以持住 Roco 的脸。转折性的色温转变：画面中来自走廊火把的温暖琥珀色一直可见，直到 4 秒时石板封死，此后只余圣殿冷调的阴影。奔跑中 Roco 身后火把拖影的重运动模糊，滑动中尘土扬尘的重运动模糊。浅景深 f/2。最后一拍中他抬起脸的揭示是镜头的情绪中心。

AUDIO：下落石板门那深沉的碾磨轰鸣全程持续——沉重、机械、不可避免。Roco 粗重的冲刺呼吸随着他向前奔跑而向摄影机越来越近。他踩在石板地上的靴子，节奏快速。他跃起飞扑时用力的闷哼。Roco 朝摄影机扑来时空气的嗖然之声。他身体刮擦着石板滑过的摩擦声，贴近。4 秒时石板最终砸落地板的那记轰然——沉重而石压石的撞击，带显著室内混响传入圣殿内部，骤然切断走廊远方的环境声。砸落后尘土迷蒙中静默的一拍。Roco 抬头时沉重的呼气，离摄影机很近。寂静中他粗重的声音气息。无音乐、无配乐（NO MUSIC. NO SCORE.）。

PACING：接近与飞扑（0-3.5 秒）动能充沛、沉浸——摄影机在 Roco 的冲锋前后退，向观众传递速度与危险。3.5-5 秒落定——石板砸落、寂静、Roco 抬头、他的脸第一次被清楚地揭示。短片在他抵达后的神情中收束，落入尘土迷蒙，为短片 1 的苏醒做了铺垫。
