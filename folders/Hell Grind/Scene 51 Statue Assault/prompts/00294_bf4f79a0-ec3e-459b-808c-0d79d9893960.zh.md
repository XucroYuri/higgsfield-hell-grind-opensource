## 原文 (source)

Stone guardian effortlessly defeats crystal-armored Roco in three iconic beats, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Villeneuve Dune Part Two scale combat, Snyder 300 iconic combat moments, Cuarón Children of Men kinetic verite. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor (chromatic kinship), warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with combat damage cracks, weathered ancient carved stone with script. 60fps, 8K with brief subtle slow-motion stretches at impact moments.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, body-driven tracking. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword, hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, flagstone floor, dust haze trembling.

Beat 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle, 35mm wider lens, f/2. Operator at floor level. Roco crystal-armored visible in foreground at floor level, his entire body in the frame the size of one section of carved armor relief on the colossus. The colossal stone leg and lower torso fill the entire vertical frame extending up out of frame top. Roco draws his ruby crystal sword back with both hands and DRIVES it forward with all his strength into the colossal stone shin — saturated red flash on impact, dust eruption, his maximum mineral roar of effort. The carved stone barely chipped. The guardian's massive form does not move, does not look down, does not register the strike. Sustained moment held — Roco frozen with his sword embedded a few inches in the stone, the colossus motionless above. Audio: his roar of effort, deep CRUNCH of crystal into stone, dust eruption, the colossus's deep red hum continuing unbroken, indifferent.

Beat 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on the guardian's massive carved stone hand descending, 50mm, f/1.8. The colossal hand the size of a small car descends slowly with crushing weight toward Roco off-frame below — the hand moving with deliberate massive slowness, no urgency, casual dismissal. The hand's open palm faces down. With brief subtle slow-motion stretch as the hand reaches Roco. Then sudden return to full speed — the hand IMPACTS Roco off-frame with thunderous WHUMP, Roco's crystal-armored body LAUNCHES into frame from below being swatted sideways through the air at high velocity, body horizontal in flight, dust trailing, his mineral grunt of pain. Audio: deep grinding stone of the hand descending slowly, brief silence in the slow-motion stretch, then thunderous WHUMP of impact at full volume, his sharp grunt, the WHOOSH of his body flying.

Beat 3 (10-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco mid-flight then crashing, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco's crystal-armored body flies through the chamber air, body spinning slowly. He SLAMS into a carved stone column with thunderous CRACK, the column-face cracking from the impact, his body crumpling and sliding down the column to the flagstone. He comes to rest at the column's base, ruby crystal armor showing fresh deep cracks across the chest, body crumpled, mineral resonance heavy and labored. Camera holds on his crumpled form, dust settling around him. The colossal guardian visible far in the deep blurred background untouched, the empty red eye-sockets pouring saturated red light, already turning attention elsewhere. Audio: his body flying through air, thunderous CRACK of body on column, dust eruption, his deep mineral groan of pain, his body sliding down stone, chamber rumble, the colossus's deep red hum continuing in deep blur unaffected.

PACING: 15-second three-beat humiliation. Roco's maximum-effort strike to no effect (0-5s), guardian's casual hand swats him aside in slight slow-motion (5-10s), Roco crashes into column and crumples (10-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

石质守护者以三个标志性节拍（iconic beats）毫不费力地击败水晶装甲的 Roco，15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》尺度战斗、Snyder《300 勇士》标志性战斗瞬间、Cuarón《人类之子》激烈写实纪录（kinetic verite）。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 红宝石水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光（色彩同源），暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶带战斗损伤裂纹、风化古老雕花石面带铭文。60fps，8K，撞击瞬间带有短暂轻微的慢动作拉伸。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持，以身体驱动的跟拍。绝不正面——保持四分之三角度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，天花板碎片纷纷坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，5 米长的利剑，手有小汽车般大小。

参见这名男子：完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），石板地面，尘雾在震颤。

Beat 1（0-5 秒）：四分之三手持极低机位，35mm 广角镜头，f/2。拍摄者置于地面高度。水晶装甲的 Roco 可见于前景的地面高度，画面中他的整个身体只有巨像上的一块雕花铠甲浮雕般大小。巨大的石腿与下躯干占满整个垂直画面、一直延伸出画框顶端。Roco 双手将红宝石水晶剑向后收蓄，倾尽全力狠狠 DRIVES 向前刺入巨大的石质胫骨——落地时饱和红光一闪、尘土迸发、他爆发出最大力度的矿物咆哮。雕花石面几乎未被削破。守护者庞大的身形纹丝不动，不低头、对这次出招毫无察觉。定格瞬间延续——Roco 僵在原地、剑嵌进石块数英寸、巨像在他上方巍然不动。音频：他用力出招的咆哮、水晶切入石头深处的 CRUNCH 声、尘土迸发、巨像深沉的红光嗡鸣继续着、毫无中断、漠不关心。

Beat 2（5-10 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，对准守护者巨大的雕花石手降下，50mm，f/1.8。这只有小汽车般大小的巨手带沉重碾轧之力缓缓降向画框下方离框的 Roco——手以刻意沉重的迟缓移动、毫无紧迫感、像在随手打发。手张开掌心朝下。手触到 Roco 时带有一阵短暂轻微的慢动作拉伸。随即骤然恢复全速——手以雷鸣般的 WHUMP 在框外撞中 Roco，Roco 的水晶装甲身体自下方被弹进画面、以高速侧向凌空拍飞、身体水平飞掠、尘土拖曳、他痛苦矿物般的闷哼。音频：手缓缓降下时深层碾磨的石声、慢动作拉伸中的短暂静默、随即全音量下雷鸣般的撞击 WHUMP、他尖锐的闷哼、他身体飞掠的 WHOOSH。

Beat 3（10-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，Roco 在空中飞行随后坠落，50mm，f/1.8。Roco 的水晶装甲身体飞掠过密殿的空气，身体缓慢旋转。他轰然撞上一座雕花石柱，发出雷鸣般的 CRACK 声，柱面因撞击而开裂，他的身体瘫软、沿石柱滑落至石板地面。他在柱脚停下，红宝石水晶铠甲胸口现出新的深裂纹，身体瘫软，矿物共鸣沉重而吃力。摄影机锁定他瘫软的身形，尘土在他周围落定。深处虚化的背景中可见庞大的守护者依然毫发无损，空荡的红色眼窝倾泻出饱和红光、已把注意力转向别处。音频：他的身体飞掠过空气、身体撞柱的雷鸣 CRACK、尘土迸发、他深沉矿物般的痛苦呻吟、身体沿石面滑落、密殿隆隆、深处虚化中巨像深沉的红光嗡鸣依旧在持续、不受影响。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒三拍羞辱。Roco 倾尽全力的出招毫无效果（0-5 秒），守护者在轻微慢动作中随手一拍将他扇开（5-10 秒），Roco 撞上石柱瘫软（10-15 秒）。
