## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco parries colossal stone sword, guardian shoves him back with hand, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Snyder's 300, Villeneuve's Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient from above, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient stone. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading with each guardian action.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, scale through extreme low-angles where body parts fill or exceed vertical frame.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on the impact moment, 85mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on the ruby crystal SHIELD as the colossal carved stone BLADE descends into it — massive saturated red FLASH at the impact point, the blade's weathered carved stone meeting the translucent ruby crystal facets of the shield. The shield CRACKS visibly across multiple facets, several fragments breaking off and flying outward, the impact zone glowing saturated red. Roco's armored body forced down to one knee in foreground, the colossal blade pressing down on his shield, the carved stone surface filling the upper portion of frame. Mineral roar of effort behind his crystal collar. Audio: thunderous CRACK of stone-on-crystal impact, SHATTER of crystal fragments, his roar of effort, sustained pressure GROAN of the colossal blade.

Shot 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle, 35mm, f/2. Operator at floor level. Roco visible kneeling in foreground holding shield up, the colossal sword now retreating upward and out of frame as the colossus lifts it. Behind Roco the guardian's body extends upward filling the entire vertical frame — torso, shoulders, the head with empty red eye-sockets visible at frame top staring down at Roco. The colossus's MASSIVE STONE HAND (the size of a small car) descends through frame from above — not the sword arm but the OFF-HAND, the open palm coming down to SHOVE Roco backward. The hand fills the upper portion of frame, scale of the palm equivalent to multiple Rocos, descending toward the kneeling figure. Audio: the massive WHOOSH of the colossal hand descending through air, deep stone movement, Roco's sharp inhale.

Shot 3 (10-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close tracking Roco being thrown backward, 50mm, f/1.8. The guardian's stone palm impacts Roco's torso off-frame at frame edge — the impact visible as Roco's crystal-armored body LAUNCHES backward through the air across the chamber, body horizontal in flight, sword and damaged shield still in hand, dust trailing. He flies through the trembling air, then SLAMS into the flagstone floor with a deep CRACK, his armored body skidding across the stone, kicking up flagstone fragments. He comes to rest several meters back, chest plate visibly cracked from the shove, body still. Then he begins to push himself up on his crystal hands, mineral resonance growling from his sealed body. Audio: the deep WHUMP of the palm impact off-frame, his armored body flying through air, the IMPACT of his body slamming on flagstone, the SKID across stone, his low mineral growl rising.

PACING: 15-second escalating exchange. Sword impact on shield with damage (0-5s), guardian's other hand descends to shove (5-10s), Roco thrown backward and rises again (10-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

水晶装甲的 Roco 格挡住巨大的石剑，守护巨人用手掌把他推得踉跄后退，15 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影，变形宽银幕电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Snyder《斯巴达三百勇士》、Villeneuve《沙丘·第二部》。Lighting（灯光）：上方惨白冷光，守护巨人眼窝与 Roco 装甲的饱和红光，温暖琥珀色油灯。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰为主，主导饱和红，重度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实半透红宝石水晶、饱经风化的古石。Continuity（连贯性）锁定。绝不正面——四边形取景。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig，剧烈抖动，身体驱动的跟随。

CHAMBER TREMOR（洞室震颤）：剧烈摇晃，守护巨人的每次动作都让天花板碎石倾泻。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护巨人尺度）：20 米高，通过极低角度确立尺度，身体部位填满或超出垂直画框。

引用图 1 中那名全身包覆半透明红宝石色刻面水晶装甲的男人，他肩部有翼状尖刺、前臂有刺、锯齿状护颈遮蔽面容。

Setting（设定）：洞窟圣殿，尘雾震颤。

Shot 1（0-5s）：四边形手持极特写，命中瞬间，85mm，f/1.4。极浅对焦在红宝石水晶盾上周，巨大的雕纹石刃劈落其上——撞击点迸出漫天的饱和红闪光，饱经风化的刃面石与盾面的半透红宝石水晶刻面交汇。盾面多个刻面可见开裂，数枚碎片迸裂飞出，撞击区泛着饱和红光。Roco 的装甲身体在前景中被压至单膝跪地，巨刃压在他的盾上，雕纹石面填满画面上半部。水晶护颈后传出他拼尽全力的矿物咆哮。Audio（音效）：石击水晶时雷霆般的碎裂声、水晶碎片碎裂迸飞声、他拼尽全力的咆哮、巨刃压盾时持续的受压呻吟。

Shot 2（5-10s）：切至四边形手持极低角度，35mm，f/2。摄影师在地面层。Roco 在前景可见、单膝跪地举盾，巨剑此刻向上回撤、溜出画面，因为巨像正把它抬起。Roco 身后守护巨人的身体向上延伸、填满整幅垂直画面——躯干、双肩，以及那只带着空荡红色眼窝的头颅在画面上方低头俯视 Roco。巨像巨大的石掌（大小如一辆小汽车）自上方穿入画面——不是握剑的那只手，而是另一只手，张开的手掌向下压来，欲将 Roco 向后猛推。巨掌填满画面上半部，掌面足有多个 Roco 那么大，朝跪地身影压下。Audio（音效）：巨掌凌空下压时庞大的呼呼声、深沉的石头移动声、Roco 锐利的吸气。

Shot 3（10-15s）：切至四边形手持中景近景，跟随 Roco 被甩得向后飞，50mm，f/1.8。守护巨人的石掌在画框边缘、画外击中 Roco 的躯干——撞击可见于 Roco 的水晶装甲身体被凌空甩向洞室之后，身体在飞行中横直，剑与受损的盾仍在手中，尘雾拖曳。他飞过震颤的长空，随后重重砸落在石板上，发出一声深沉的碎裂声，装甲身体在石面上滑出，扬起石屑。他停在数米开外，胸甲在那一推下清晰可见地开裂，身体静止。随后他开始在水晶双手上撑身而起，封闭的身体里传出矿物共鸣的低吼。Audio（音效）：画外石掌击中的深沉闷响、他装甲身体凌空飞射之声、身体砸落石板的撞击声、在石面上的滑擦声、他渐起的低沉矿物咆哮。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒的步步升级交锋。巨剑劈盾造成损伤（0-5s），守护巨人另一只手落下推击（5-10s），Roco 被甩向后飞、再度起身（10-15s）。
