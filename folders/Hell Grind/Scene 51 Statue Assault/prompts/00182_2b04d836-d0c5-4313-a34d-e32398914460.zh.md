Roco and guardian fight, guardian throws Roco down, raises sword to execute, Roco shouts and guardian freezes mid-strike then turns away to monk, 11 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men kinetic verite, Villeneuve Dune Part Two violent impacts and decisive movement. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with combat damage, weathered ancient carved stone with script and moss. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, body-driven tracking. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

GUARDIAN SCALE: towering, ten times Roco's height, 5-meter sword, hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, flagstone floor, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-3s): Three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle, 35mm, f/2. Roco crystal-armored swings ruby crystal sword in powerful diagonal arc into the colossal stone leg, saturated red flash on impact. The guardian's MASSIVE STONE HAND descends and BACKHANDS Roco — colossal palm impacts him with deep WHUMP, his body LAUNCHED sideways, slamming into flagstone with thunderous CRACK, body skidding to rest on his side. Audio: CRACK of crystal on leg, WHOOSH of colossal hand, deep WHUMP of impact, his mineral grunt, CRACK of body on flagstone.

Shot 2 (3-5s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on the guardian raising sword above Roco, 24mm wide, f/2. The towering colossus stands over Roco's fallen position visible at floor level in foreground. Both massive carved stone HANDS grip the colossal sword OVER his head, the 5-meter blade raised vertically to its apex above. The guardian holds the blade aloft for the execution strike, body poised to bring it down on Roco. Audio: deep stone-on-stone CLAMP of grip, the colossus's deep red hum spiking with execution intent, chamber rumble, Roco's strained mineral breath from below.

Shot 3 (5-7s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Roco on the flagstone, 100mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on Roco's crystal-armored upper body — his head and shoulders visible from his side, body half-risen on one armored elbow, looking up at the colossal blade above off-frame. He SHOUTS through his crystal collar with all his strength, voice resonating mineral and muffled with deep crystalline reverb but the words clear, voice carrying with command authority through the chamber: "RAIN! THE MONK!" Audio: his shouted mineral-resonant voice carrying the order, his strained mineral breath, the colossus's deep red hum from above.

Shot 4 (7-9s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on the guardian, 24mm wide, f/2. The towering colossus visible holding the colossal sword aloft above his head — but FROZEN mid-strike, the blade held at its apex but no longer descending, the body rigid. IMMEDIATELY upon hearing Roco's shout the guardian's execution motion has STOPPED — the sword paused at the top of its raise. The colossus then begins TURNING in real time — deep grinding stone-on-stone of his body rotating in place even with the sword still held aloft, dust cascading from his joints, the empty red eye-sockets pivoting away from Roco's direction toward the direction of the monk's distant ledge. Once turned, the guardian LOWERS the sword to his side and begins WALKING toward the monk, heavy deliberate footsteps moving away from Roco, each step a colossal WHUMP, the colossus receding. Audio: the sound of the held aloft sword paused, deep grinding rotation of his body turning, dust pouring from joints, the grinding stone of the sword lowering to his side, colossal WHUMPS of receding footsteps, deep red hum shifting direction.

Shot 5 (9-11s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco still on the flagstone, 85mm, f/1.4. Roco visible half-risen on one elbow on the broken stone where he expected to die. He turns his head SLOWLY following the receding direction of the guardian — his crystal-armored head rotating with mineral effort, tracking the colossus walking away from him in deep blurred background. He breathes one deep mineral exhale of confusion — he was a second from death, instead the guardian abandoned him mid-execution. His armored hand loosely holding the sword now resting on the flagstone beside him. Audio: his deep mineral exhale of confusion, receding WHUMPS of guardian's footsteps growing slightly distant, dust hissing, the colossus's deep red hum shifting direction.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Roco 与守护者交战，守护者将 Roco 打翻在地，举剑行刑，Roco 呐喊，守护者在劈砍中途僵住，随后转身走向僧侣，11 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：卡隆《人类之子》的动态写实、维伦纽瓦《沙丘第二部》的暴力冲击与果断的移动。Lighting（灯光）：冷灰环境光，来自守护者眼眶与 Roco 红宝石水晶战甲的饱和红色，温暖琥珀色油灯。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰主调，红色为主导点缀，重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实的半透明红宝石水晶带战斗损伤，风化古老凿刻石带铭文与青苔。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持（shoulder-mounted handheld），以躯体驱动的追踪。绝不正面机位——使用四分之三侧面角。

CHAMBER TREMOR（殿堂震颤）：剧烈摇晃，碎屑坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者尺度）：高耸入云，为 Roco 身高的十倍，剑长 5 米，手掌有小型汽车大小。

参照图 1 中那位完全包裹在半透明红宝石色刻面水晶战甲内的男人，带肩部翼刺、前臂刺、遮住面孔的锯齿状护颈。

Setting（场景）：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），石板地面，震动的尘雾。

镜头 1（0-3 秒）：四分之三手持极低角度，35mm，f/2。身着水晶甲的 Roco 以强大的对角弧线挥动红宝石水晶剑劈向巨大的石腿，撞击瞬间爆出饱和红色闪光。守护者的巨大石手自上压下，反手扇向 Roco——巨大的掌心以深沉的重击声（WHUMP）击中他，他的身体横向飞出，以雷鸣般的脆裂声（CRACK）撞上石板，身体滑行侧卧停住。音效（Audio）：水晶击腿的脆裂声（CRACK），巨手挥来的呼啸声（WHOOSH），沉重重击声（WHUMP），他的矿物质闷哼，身体撞上石板的脆裂声（CRACK）。

镜头 2（3-5 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低角度，聚焦守护者在 Roco 上方举剑，24mm 广角，f/2。高耸的巨像俯视着倒在地上的 Roco，其位置在前景地面层可见。两只巨大的凿刻石手在头顶握住那把巨剑，5 米长的剑身垂直高举至上方最高点。守护者将剑悬于行刑一击，身躯蓄势要把剑劈向 Roco。音效（Audio）：深沉的石对石握柄咬合声（CLAMP），巨像深沉的红鸣随着行刑意图骤然拔高，殿堂轰鸣，下方 Roco 紧绷的矿物质呼吸。

镜头 3（5-7 秒）：切至四分之三手持极特写，对准石板上的 Roco，100mm，f/1.4。极薄聚焦于 Roco 身着水晶甲的上半身——头部与双肩自侧方可见，身子单臂拄着一只甲肘半撑起，仰望着画框上方那把巨大的剑身。他竭尽全力透过水晶护颈呐喊，声音以矿物质质感共振，既闷又沉，带深沉的结晶混响（crystalline reverb），但字字清晰，以号令般的威势传遍殿堂：「RAIN！THE MONK！」音效（Audio）：他呐喊的矿物质共振之声回荡着这道号令，他紧绷的矿物质呼吸，上方巨像深沉的红鸣。

镜头 4（7-9 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低角度，对准守护者，24mm 广角，f/2。高耸的巨像仍可见高举巨剑于头顶——却在劈砍中途僵住，剑悬于最高点不再下落，身躯僵立。一听到 Roco 的呐喊，守护者的行刑动作已立刻停下——剑悬停于高举的顶端。巨像随后实时开始转身——石对石的深沉碾磨声，身躯原地转动，即使剑仍高举，关节处尘土倾泻，空洞的红色眼眶自 Roco 的方向转向僧侣远处岩台的方向。转定之后，守护者收剑至身侧并开始朝僧侣走去，沉重而刻意的脚步移离 Roco，每一步都是巨重声（WHUMP），巨像渐行渐远。音效（Audio）：高举的剑悬停之声，身躯转身的深沉碾磨旋转声，关节喷涌的尘土，巨剑落向身侧的碾磨石声，渐行渐远脚步的巨重声（WHUMP），深沉的红鸣改变方向。

镜头 5（9-11 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，对准仍躺在石板上的 Roco，85mm，f/1.4。Roco 可见单肘半撑起，在那片他曾以为自己将要死去的碎石之上。他缓缓转头，追随守护者远去的身影——水晶甲裹覆的头部以矿物质的费力转动，追踪着巨像在深度虚化的背景中从他身边走远。他呼出一口深沉的矿物质混淆之息——距死亡仅一秒之遥，守护者却在中执行刑时将他抛下。他那只带甲的手松松握着剑，剑现已横放在身旁的石板上。音效（Audio）：他深沉的矿物质混淆之息，守护者渐行渐远的脚步声（WHUMP）略微拉开距离，尘土嘶鸣，巨像深沉的红鸣改变方向。

PACING（节奏）：11 秒的「行刑-呐喊-转身-困惑」。Roco 交战，守护者将他打翻在地（0-3 秒），守护者举剑行刑 Roco（3-5 秒），Roco 呐喊「RAIN！THE MONK！」（5-7 秒），守护者僵于劈砍中途随后转身走向僧侣（7-9 秒），Roco 困惑地注视（9-11 秒）。
