## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco strikes guardian uselessly while guardian effortlessly beats him down, montage sequence, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300 close kinetic combat. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with growing combat damage, weathered ancient carved stone with script. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld throughout, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword, hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, flagstone floor, dust haze trembling.

Beat 1 (0-3s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco striking the guardian's leg, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco swings ruby crystal sword in powerful diagonal arc into the colossal stone leg in shallow blur — saturated red flash, sparks of crystal on stone, his mineral grunt of effort. The carved stone barely chipped. The guardian's massive form in deep blur does not react — the colossus continues his motion uninterrupted, the empty red eye-sockets unmoved.

Beat 2 (3-6s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on the guardian's foot raising, 35mm, f/2. Roco visible mid-strike at frame edge in foreground. The colossal stone FOOT lifts and SWEEPS sideways through the air — the foot the size of Roco's body smashing into him laterally with deep WHUMP. Roco LAUNCHES sideways through the air, body horizontal, dust trailing. Audio: deep grinding stone of foot lifting, WHUMP of impact, his mineral grunt of pain, body flying.

Beat 3 (6-9s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco hitting the flagstone, 50mm, f/1.8. His armored body SLAMS into the floor with thunderous CRACK, body skidding across stone, kicking up flagstone fragments, coming to rest crumpled on his side. He pushes himself up shakily on one armored hand. Audio: thunderous CRACK of body on stone, dust eruption, his deep mineral grunt, his body sliding, dust hissing.

Beat 4 (9-12s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on Roco rising for another attack, 35mm, f/2. Roco crystal-armored half-risen on one knee, lunges forward swinging his sword again at the colossal leg in shallow blur — second strike, saturated red flash, his roar of effort. The leg untouched. The guardian still does not react, the colossus continuing combat motions disconnected from Roco's strikes. Audio: his roar of attack, crystal-on-stone CRACK, the colossus's deep red hum unbroken.

Beat 5 (12-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on the colossal hand descending, 85mm, f/1.4. The guardian's massive stone hand descends from above and BACKHANDS Roco across the face — colossal palm impacts him with deep WHUMP, his crystal-armored body whipped sideways through the air, body spinning from the force, sword and shield flying from his grip. He SLAMS into a carved stone column in deep blur with thunderous CRACK, body crumpling at the column's base. Audio: WHOOSH of backhand, deep WHUMP of impact, his sharp mineral grunt of pain, his weapons clattering off, the CRACK of his body on column, dust eruption, his deep groan of pain.

PACING: 15-second five-beat one-sided beating. Roco strikes leg uselessly (0-3s), guardian's foot launches Roco sideways (3-6s), Roco slams onto flagstone (6-9s), Roco strikes again uselessly (9-12s), guardian's backhand sends him into a column (12-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

水晶装甲的 Roco 徒劳地攻击守护者，而守护者不费吹灰之力将他击倒，蒙太奇序列（montage sequence），15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》、Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》、Snyder《300 勇士》的近距离激烈格斗。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 的宝石红水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光，暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶带渐进的战斗损伤，风化古老雕花石面带铭文。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：全程肩扛手持（shoulder-mounted handheld），极度颤抖，以身体驱动的跟拍。绝不正面——保持四分之三角度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，天花板碎片纷纷坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，5 米长的利剑，手有小汽车般大小。

参见完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹的这名男子，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），石板地面，飞扬的尘雾在震颤。

Beat 1（0-3 秒）：四分之三手持中近景，Roco 砍向守护者的腿，50mm，f/1.8。Roco 将红宝石水晶剑以有力的斜弧挥入浅淡虚化的巨型石腿——饱和红光一闪，水晶撞石迸出火星，他发出矿物般的用力低吼。雕花石面几乎未被削破。深处虚化中守护者的庞大身形毫无反应——巨像继续他未中断的动作，空荡的红色眼窝一动不动。

Beat 2（3-6 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低机位，对准守护者抬起的脚，35mm，f/2。Roco 可见于前景画框边缘处正在出招。巨大的石质 FOOT 抬起并侧向横扫过空气——这只脚有 Roco 的整个身体般大小，横向狠狠撞进他体内，发出沉闷的 WHUMP 声。Roco 侧向划过空气被击飞，身体呈水平，尘土拖曳。音频：抬脚时粗重碾磨的石声、撞击的 WHUMP、他痛苦矿物般的闷哼、身体飞掠。

Beat 3（6-9 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，Roco 摔落在石板地上，50mm，f/1.8。他的装甲身体轰然砸向地面，发出雷鸣般的 CRACK 声，身体在石面上滑行，掀起石板碎片，最后侧身蜷缩停在原地。他用一只装甲手颤巍巍地撑起身来。音频：身体砸石的雷鸣 CRACK、尘土迸发、他深沉矿物般的闷哼、身体滑动、尘土嘶嘶作响。

Beat 4（9-12 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低机位，Roco再度起身发起进攻，35mm，f/2。水晶装甲的 Roco 单膝半起，向前扑出，再次挥剑劈向浅淡虚化中的巨大石腿——第二击，饱和红光一闪，他用力咆哮出声。石腿毫发无损。守护者依旧毫无反应，巨像继续着他与 Roco 出招互不相干的战斗动作。音频：他进攻的吼声、水晶撞石的 CRACK、巨像深沉的红光嗡鸣毫无中断。

Beat 5（12-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，巨型手掌向下压来，85mm，f/1.4。守护者巨大的石手自上方降下，反手一巴掌（BACKHAND）扇过 Roco 的面门——巨大的手掌以深沉 WHUMP 撞中他，他水晶装甲的身体被横向抽飞，身体因冲击而旋转，剑与盾自他手中脱飞。他重重撞进深虚化中的一座雕花石柱，发出雷鸣般的 CRACK 声，身体蜷缩在柱脚。音频：反手挥出的 WHOOSH、撞击的深沉 WHUMP、他尖锐的矿物般痛苦闷哼、武器铛啷坠地、身体撞柱的 CRACK、尘土迸发、他深沉的痛苦呻吟。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒五拍单方面碾压。Roco 徒劳砍腿（0-3 秒），守护者的脚将 Roco 侧向击飞（3-6 秒），Roco 砸上石板（6-9 秒），Roco 再度徒劳出招（9-12 秒），守护者反手将他扇入石柱（12-15 秒）。
