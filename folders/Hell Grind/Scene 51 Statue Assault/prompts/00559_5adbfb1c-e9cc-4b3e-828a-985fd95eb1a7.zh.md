## 原文 (source)

Vast ancient sanctum carved from natural rock, recovery and forward motion sequence, 12 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, deep cool blue-grey palette with warm amber oil-lamp accents, dramatic chiaroscuro, no music. Inspired by Children of Men and The Revenant — handheld kinetic intimacy, close-up driven acting.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.
Refer to the man with dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 3.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 4.

Setting: vast cavern-sanctum carved from natural rock, towering carved stone column-faces receding into darkness on both side walls, irregular flagstone floor, two parallel lines of small oil-lamps flickering warm amber along the walls, ceiling lost in shadow far above, dust-hazed atmosphere. Roco lies face-down on the flagstones a couple of meters from the side wall, dust-covered. Jax kneels beside him, jacket scuffed. Rain crouches a meter behind Jax, hoverboard set down on the flagstone beside her, one hand braced on the floor, leaning forward toward Roco — she has just dropped to crouch, watching, tense.

Shot 1 (0-2s): Handheld extreme low-angle close on Roco's face pressed against the flagstone, 100mm long lens, razor-thin focus on his eye. Camera lies on the floor inches from his face. Dust-streaked cheek against cold stone. His eyelid flutters, then opens — pupil dilating in the dark. A faint twitch in his brow as awareness returns. He exhales softly. Off-screen above, Jax's voice low and close: "Roco. Hey." Roco's eye tracks up toward the voice. Frame breathes with intimate handheld drift. Audio: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drop with chamber reverb, Roco's slow ragged inhale magnified by closeness, faint oil-lamp crackle.

Shot 2 (2-3.5s): Cut to handheld close on Jax leaning down toward Roco from above, 85mm, three-quarter angle. Jax's face dust-streaked, brow tight, eyes scanning Roco's off-frame face. He sees the response, exhales a small relieved breath. His expression shifts to focused — about to act. He reaches down toward Roco's forearm. In shallow-focus blur behind Jax's shoulder, Rain's silhouette is visible crouched close, leaning in, watching. Audio: his line tail with chamber reverb, his exhale of relief, Rain's quiet exhale of relief mixing with his, ambient drone underneath.

Shot 3 (3.5-6.5s): Cut to handheld low-rising shot beside the two men, 50mm wider lens. Jax grips Roco's forearm — fingers locking around the wrist. He shifts his weight back, plants his foot, and HAULS Roco upward. Roco grunts, pushes off the floor with his free hand, comes up onto his knees, then to his feet in one continuous motion, dust falling from his jacket. He staggers on landing, catches Jax's shoulder briefly with his free hand for balance. Jax's other hand comes up to steady Roco's elbow. Rain rises with them in the same continuous motion, picking up the hoverboard from the floor as she stands, stepping into the frame from behind Jax — her hand goes to Roco's other arm, briefly, just above the elbow, a steadying touch as he finds his feet. The three of them in a tight knot for a beat — Roco between Jax and Rain, both supporting him without making it heavy. Roco gives Jax a small nod, then Rain a small nod. The camera RISES WITH THEM continuously, operator standing. Frame drifts and corrects, alive. Audio: scrape of grip, Jax's grunt of effort, Rain's small exhale as she rises and steps in, scrape of Roco's boots finding flagstone, Roco's heavy exhale, all three breathing heavily, ambient drone, chamber reverb deepening.

Shot 4 (6.5-8s): Cut to handheld medium close on Rain, 100mm, three-quarter profile. Her hand is still resting just above Roco's elbow in the foreground (Roco's arm visible blurred at the edge of frame). She holds the hoverboard at her side in her other hand, purple thruster glow soft accent. Her face is dust-streaked, brow soft with worry now that the action is over — the relief showing more openly. The cold light catches the scar by her eye. Her eyes are on Roco's face off-frame, searching it. She speaks, voice low and warm, the worry audible underneath, with chamber reverb: "You good?" A beat. Her hand gives his arm a small squeeze before she lets go. Camera breathes around her, drifts subtly closer. Audio: her controlled breathing with a faint waver of held-back worry, the question soft and close with long reverb tail, the small fabric-creak of her hand squeezing his arm, ambient drone, distant oil-lamp crackle.

Shot 5 (8-9.5s): Cut to handheld extreme close-up on Roco's face, 100mm, dust-streaked, the red bandage on his nose visible. He's let go of Jax's shoulder, Rain's hand has just left his arm. He turns his head briefly to meet Rain's eyes off-frame — gives her a small reassuring half-smile, the kind that says I'm here, don't worry — then his eyes go forward, past her, fixing on something distant in the sanctum off-screen. His jaw sets. His answer comes flat but warm at the edges, with chamber reverb: "Let's move." Frame breathes intimately on his face. Audio: his line with strong reverb tail, his breath settling, ambient drone.

Shot 6 (9.5-12s): Cut to handheld medium tight three-shot on the trio from a low front angle, 50mm. Roco center, eyes forward. Jax right of frame, gives Roco's shoulder a brief firm tap — good, we go — and steps back to clear his path. Rain left of frame, hoverboard at her side, eyes lingering on Roco's profile for half a second longer than Jax's before she shifts her weight forward. Roco takes the first step into the chamber. Jax falls in on his right. Rain a beat behind, the hoverboard swinging slightly with her stride. Camera dollies backward at their pace, framing them tight from waist-up walking toward camera. Behind them in deep shallow-focus blur: the dark interior of the sanctum receding, oil-lamp lines stretching away. Frame drifts with operator's breath, walking with them. Audio: Jax's tap on Roco's shoulder, Roco's first boot striking flagstone, Jax falling in step, Rain joining a beat later — three pairs of boots in slightly staggered rhythm with chamber reverb, breathing settling into walking pattern, ambient drone underneath.

CAMERA: long lens 50mm-100mm. Shots 1, 4, 5 are 100mm intimate close-ups. Shot 2 is 85mm close. Shots 3 and 6 are 50mm wider. Handheld throughout, alive, breath-driven, the operator a fourth presence. Clean direct cuts. Frame constantly drifts, reactive but never jittery. Shallow depth of field f/1.8-f/2. Cold blue-grey palette, warm oil-lamp accents, soft purple glow accents from the hoverboard's thruster cores.

ACTING:
- Roco: returns slowly, accepts the help with brief braces and small nods. His "Let's move" is decision but he gives Rain a half-smile before turning forward — the smile is the warmth, the line is the resolve.
- Jax: caregiver and operator. "Roco. Hey." direct and intimate, using the name to bring him back. His haul is muscular, efficient. The shoulder tap at the end is the verbal "we're good" without words.
- Rain: she crouches close as he wakes (not standing back), she rises with the men and steps in to steady his other arm, she squeezes his arm before letting go, her "You good?" is soft with audible worry underneath, her eyes linger on him longer than Jax's before they move. The hoverboard set down beside her at the start tells us she dropped it to be near Roco — she chose him over carrying it. She is the team's leader and a person who is scared for him; both can be true at once.

AUDIO: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drops with chamber reverb, oil-lamp crackles, breathing and clothing rustle. Tactile sounds: scrape of grip, Jax's grunt hauling Roco, Roco's exhale, Rain's exhales of relief, the squeeze of her hand on his arm, Jax's tap on Roco's shoulder, three pairs of boots in staggered rhythm. Dialogue: Jax's "Roco. Hey.", Rain's "You good?" (soft, worried), Roco's "Let's move" — all with strong chamber reverb tails. No music.

PACING: six-beat structure — Roco waking (Shot 1), Jax registering with Rain visible behind him watching (Shot 2), the haul-up where Rain joins the support physically (Shot 3), Rain's worried check-in with hand on his arm (Shot 4), Roco's reassuring half-smile then decision (Shot 5), forward motion with Rain's lingering look (Shot 6). The trio is now visibly a unit of three caring for each other, not two men and an observer.

## 中文对照 (zh)

从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔古老圣殿（sanctum），恢复与前行动作序列，12 秒，16:9 anamorphic，写实摄影（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，深冷的蓝灰基调辅以暖琥珀色油灯点缀，戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro），无音乐。灵感源自《人类之子》（Children of Men）与《荒野猎人》（The Revenant）——手持（handheld）动能化的亲密感、以特写驱动表演。

参照 Image 1 中那位银白浅发、眼角带疤、灰色连帽夹克的女性。
参照 Image 2 中那位漂金寸头、深肤色、透明眼镜、深色夹克的男性。
参照 Image 3 中那位深色乱发、留须、鼻梁贴红色绷带、深色连帽夹克的男性。
参照 Image 4 中那架哑光黑悬浮滑板（hoverboard），配备泛紫色光芒的推进器核心。

置景（Setting）：从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔洞窟圣殿，两侧墙上刻着高耸的石柱面，渐隐没入两侧的黑暗，地面为不规则的石板（flagstone）地，沿墙有两列平行的小油灯摇曳着暖琥珀色光，天花板在头顶高处隐没于阴影，尘土弥漫。Roco 面朝下躺在距侧墙几米远的石板地上，满身尘土。Jax 跪在他身旁，夹克磨旧。Rain 蹲在 Jax 身后一米处，悬浮滑板放在身侧的石板地上，一只手撑地，身体朝 Roco 前倾——她刚刚蹲下，注视着，紧绷着。

镜头 1（0-2 秒）：手持极低机位特写，对准 Roco 紧贴在石板地上的脸，100mm 长焦，对他的眼睛做剃刀般极薄的焦点。摄影机躺在离他脸数英寸的地面上。沾满尘土的侧颊贴着冰冷的石头。他的眼皮颤动，随即睁开——瞳孔在黑暗中扩张。意识回归时他眉心有一下微微的抽动。他轻轻呼出一口气。画面之外上方，Jax 的声音低而近：「Roco. Hey.」Roco 的眼睛循声向上。画面随亲密的持握漂移而起伏。Audio：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响（chamber reverb）的水滴声，Roco 缓慢粗重的吸气因贴近而被放大，轻微的油灯噼啪声。

镜头 2（2-3.5 秒）：切至手持特写，对准从上方朝 Roco 俯下身的 Jax，85mm，四分之三角度。Jax 的脸沾满尘土，眉头紧锁，目光在画面外 Roco 的脸上搜寻。他看到回应，呼出一小口释然的气息。他的表情转为专注——正要行动。他伸手朝 Roco 的前臂探去。在 Jax 肩后浅焦模糊处，Rain 的身影可见，蹲得很近，俯身注视着。Audio：他的台词的尾音带室内混响，他释然的呼气，Rain 那口与他交织的、安静的释然气息，底下是环境低鸣。

镜头 3（3.5-6.5 秒）：切至两名男性身旁手持低机位升起的镜头，50mm 更广的镜头。Jax 握住 Roco 的前臂——手指扣锁在腕部。他后移重心，踩实脚，向上猛拉 Roco。Roco 发出一声闷哼，用那只空着的手撑离地面，跪起，再在一气呵成的动作中站到脚上，尘土从他夹克上簌簌落下。落地时他踉跄了一下，用空着的手短暂扶住 Jax 的肩稳住平衡。Jax 的另一只手抬起来稳稳托住 Roco 的肘部。Rain 与他俩在同一段连贯的动作中站起身来，起身时从地上拾起悬浮滑板，从 Jax 身后步入画面——她的手搭到 Roco 的另一只手臂上，短暂地，刚过肘部，一个稳住他的触碰，帮他站稳脚。三人短暂地抱成一团——Roco 在 Jax 与 Rain 之间，两人都在扶他，却不显沉重。Roco 朝 Jax 微微点了个头，又朝 Rain 微微点了个头。摄影机随他们一气呵成地升起，操作员站起身。画面漂移又校正，生气勃勃。Audio：握持的刮擦声，Jax 用力时的闷哼，Rain 起身迈步时的一声轻浅呼气，Roco 靴底在石板地上的刮擦声，Roco 沉重的呼气，三人呼吸沉重，环境低鸣，室内混响渐深。

镜头 4（6.5-8 秒）：切至手持中景特写，对准 Rain，100mm，四分之三侧面。她的手仍搭在画面前景里 Roco 的肘部略上方（Roco 的手臂在画面边缘以模糊可见）。她另一只手把悬浮滑板拿在身侧，紫色推进器光晕作柔和的点缀。她的脸沾满尘土，动作结束后眉头因担忧而微软——那份释然流露得更明显。冷光掠过她眼角那道疤。她的目光落在画面外 Roco 的脸上，搜寻着。她开口，声音低而温暖，担忧在底下可闻，带室内混响：「You good?」顿了一拍。她松手前，手在他手臂上轻轻握了一下。摄影机在她周围起伏，微妙地渐渐靠近。Audio：她受控的呼吸里带着一丝压住担忧的微颤，问句轻柔而贴近，带着悠长的混响尾音，她握他手臂时的微小布料窸窣，环境低鸣，远处油灯的噼啪声。

镜头 5（8-9.5 秒）：切至手持极特写，对准 Roco 的脸，100mm，满身尘土，鼻上的红色绷带清晰可见。他已经松开 Jax 的肩，Rain 的手刚离开他的手臂。他短暂地转过头，与画面外的 Rain 目光相接——朝她露出一个小小的、令人安心的半笑，那种说着「我在这儿，别担心」的笑——随后他的目光向前，越过她，锁定在圣殿画面外某处远处。他的下颌绷紧。他的回答平平地传来，却带出一丝暖意，带室内混响：「Let's move.」画面在他脸上散发着亲密的气息。Audio：他的台词带强烈的混响尾音，他的呼吸渐趋平稳，环境低鸣。

镜头 6（9.5-12 秒）：切至手持中景紧凑三人镜头，自低正前方角度，50mm。Roco 居中，目光向前。Jax 在画面右侧，在 Roco 肩上干脆地轻拍一下——好，我们走——然后退开，给他让出路。Rain 在画面左侧，悬浮滑板在身侧，她的目光比 Jax 多在 Roco 的侧影上停留了半秒，才把重心前移。Roco 迈出进入厅堂的第一步。Jax 在他的右侧跟上。Rain 落后一拍，悬浮滑板随她的步伐轻轻摆动。摄影机以他们的步速向后移动（dolly），从腰际以上紧凑地框住他们朝摄影机走来。在他们身后以极深浅焦的模糊中：圣殿幽暗的深处退去，油灯的连排向远方延伸。画面随操作员的呼吸漂移，与他们并肩而行。Audio：Jax 拍在 Roco 肩上的轻拍，Roco 的第一只靴子踏在石板地上，Jax 跟上脚步，Rain 一拍之后加入——三双靴子以略错落有致的节奏带室内混响，呼吸渐入走路的节律，底下是环境低鸣。

CAMERA：长焦 50mm-100mm。镜头 1、4、5 为 100mm 的亲密特写。镜头 2 为 85mm 特写。镜头 3 与 6 为更广的 50mm。全程手持，生气勃勃、由呼吸驱动，操作员是第四位在场者。干脆利落的直接切。画面持续漂移，灵敏却绝不抖动。浅景深 f/1.8-f/2。冷蓝灰基调，暖琥珀油灯点缀，悬浮滑板推进器核心泛起的柔紫色光辉点缀。

ACTING（表演）：
- Roco：缓慢回归，以短暂的扶手与微微点头接受帮助。他的「Let's move」是决断，但他在转向向前之前先朝 Rain 露出一个半笑——那笑容是温度，这句台词是决意。
- Jax：照料者兼操作者。「Roco. Hey.」直接而亲密，用名字把他唤回来。他拉人是结实而高效的。结尾的肩上一拍，是不言而喻的「我们没事」。
- Rain：他醒来时她蹲得近近的（而不是站在后面），她起身随男人们一同揽住他另一只手臂，松手前在他手臂上握了握，她的「You good?」轻柔，下面带着可闻的担忧，他们的目光在她身上比 Jax 停留更久才移开。开场时放在她身旁的悬浮滑板告诉我们：她为了靠近 Roco 把它丢下了——她选择了他，胜过扛着它。她是小队的领袖，也是一个为他担心的人；两者可以同时成立。

AUDIO：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响的水滴声，油灯噼啪声，呼吸与衣物沙沙。触觉声响：握持的刮擦声，Jax 拉动 Roco 时用力的闷哼，Roco 的呼气，Rain 释然的呼气，她握在他手臂上的那一下，Jax 拍在 Roco 肩上的轻拍，三双靴子错落有致的节奏。台词：Jax 的「Roco. Hey.」、Rain 的「You good?」（轻柔、担忧）、Roco 的「Let's move」——全部带强烈的室内混响尾音。无音乐。

PACING：六拍结构——Roco 苏醒（镜头 1）、Jax 觉察而 Rain 在他身后可见地注视着（镜头 2）、Rain 在身体上加入扶助的拉起（镜头 3）、Rain 手搭在他手臂上的担忧探问（镜头 4）、Roco 安抚的半笑继而决意（镜头 5）、Rain 留恋一瞥中的前行动作（镜头 6）。队伍如今清晰可见地是一个彼此照料的三人组，而非两个男人加一个旁观者。
