## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco retreats and reassesses the guardian, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Cuarón's Children of Men kinetic retreat sequences, Villeneuve's Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient from above, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets above out of frame and from Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with cracks visible from previous combat damage, weathered ancient stone. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling. Roco crystal-armored retreating away from the guardian.

Shot 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld medium close tracking Roco running backward, 50mm, f/1.8. Operator handheld tracking laterally with him at chest height. Roco visible from three-quarter side view, his crystal armor showing CRACKS across the chest and shoulder from previous combat — fissures in the ruby crystal with red glow leaking through more strongly, several shoulder spikes broken or chipped. He runs backward away from the guardian, sword and shield still in hand, his armored gait visible — heavy, fast, retreating. The guardian's massive leg in deep blur receding behind him. Audio: his heavy footfalls on flagstone, mineral resonance breath, chamber rumble.

Shot 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco taking cover behind a fallen piece of carved stone debris, 85mm, f/1.4. He drops behind the cover, back against the stone, breathing hard through sealed armor. Mineral resonance heavy. He looks down at his cracked crystal chest plate — his armored hand touches the largest fissure, ruby crystal flakes coming off under his touch, red glow leaking from within. He is damaged. He looks back UP toward the guardian off-frame above, his concealed face turning, the empty red eye-sockets of the colossus reflecting in the facets of his collar. Audio: his heavy mineral breathing, the flake of crystal under his touch, chamber tremor, distant guardian hum.

Shot 3 (10-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Roco's gauntlet hand gripping the sword tighter, 100mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on the ruby crystal sword and his crystal-gloved hand gripping it. The grip TIGHTENS, crystal articulating against crystal with sharp sounds, knuckle facets shifting. Crystal mineral resonance from his body deepening — gathering for another attack. The sword's saturated red glow strengthens slightly as if responding to his will. Then he RISES from cover — body ascending from behind the cover, sword raised, ready. Cut on his rising silhouette. Audio: crystalline articulation of his tightening grip, the deepening mineral resonance, his rising breath, the surge of intent.

PACING: 15-second retreat-and-reset sequence. Running retreat with damaged armor visible (0-5s), behind cover assessing damage and looking back at threat (5-10s), gathering will and rising for another attack (10-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

水晶装甲、已受损的 Roco 撤退并重新评估守护巨人，15 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影，变形宽银幕电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Cuarón《人类之子》的动态撤退片段、Villeneuve《沙丘·第二部》。Lighting（灯光）：上方惨白冷光，守护巨人画面上方出画的眼窝红光、以及 Roco 红宝石水晶装甲的饱和红光，温暖琥珀色油灯。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰为主，主导饱和红，重度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实半透红宝石水晶、带有先前战斗损伤处可见的裂纹，饱经风化的古石。Continuity（连贯性）锁定。绝不正面——四边形取景。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig，剧烈抖动，身体驱动的跟随。

CHAMBER TREMOR（洞室震颤）：剧烈摇晃，碎石坠落。

引用图 1 中那名全身包覆半透明红宝石色刻面水晶装甲的男人，他肩部有翼状尖刺、前臂有刺、锯齿状护颈遮蔽面容。

Setting（设定）：洞窟圣殿，尘雾震颤。Roco 水晶装甲正向后撤退、远离守护巨人。

Shot 1（0-5s）：四边形手持中景近景，跟随 Roco 倒退奔跑，50mm，f/1.8。摄影师手持在胸口高度与他横向并行。Roco 从四边形侧面可见，他的水晶装甲胸膛与肩膀上因先前的战斗显现裂纹——红宝石水晶上的裂缝中红光更灼烈地渗出，数枚肩刺折断或缺损。他倒退奔跑、远离守护巨人，剑与盾仍在手中，他装甲性的步伐清晰可见——沉重、迅捷、且退。守护巨人深虚化的巨腿在他身后退去。Audio（音效）：他在石板上沉重的脚步声、矿物共鸣的呼吸、洞室轰鸣。

Shot 2（5-10s）：切至四边形手持中景近景，Roco 躲在一块倒落的雕纹石碴后，85mm，f/1.4。他扑到掩体后，背靠石面，隔着封闭装甲剧烈喘息。矿物共鸣沉重。他低头看向开裂的水晶胸甲——装甲手掌抚过最大的那条裂缝，指尖过处红宝石水晶的晶体碎片簌簌剥落，红光自内里渗出。他已经受伤了。他重新抬眼望向画面上方出画的守护巨人，掩藏的脸转动，巨像空荡的红色眼窝映现在他护颈的刻面上。Audio（音效）：他沉重的矿物业呼吸、指尖下水晶碎片的剥落声、洞室震颤、远处守护巨人的嗡鸣。

Shot 3（10-15s）：切至四边形手持极特写，Roco 的护手把剑握得更紧，100mm，f/1.4。极浅对焦在红宝石水晶剑与他水晶护手套住的手上。握持收紧，水晶抵着水晶发出锐利的嘎响，指节刻面错动。他体内的水晶矿物共鸣愈发深沉——为下一次进攻蓄力。剑上的饱和红光微微增强，仿佛回应他的意志。随后他从掩体后起身——身体自掩体后腾起，长剑高举、蓄势待发。切在他腾起的剪影上。Audio（音效）：他收紧握持时水晶的脆响、愈见深沉的矿物共鸣、他渐起的呼吸、意图的涌动。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒的撤退与重整序列。带着可见损伤的装甲身体向后撤退奔跑（0-5s），在掩体后评估损伤并回望威胁（5-10s），聚拢意志、起身再度出击（10-15s）。
