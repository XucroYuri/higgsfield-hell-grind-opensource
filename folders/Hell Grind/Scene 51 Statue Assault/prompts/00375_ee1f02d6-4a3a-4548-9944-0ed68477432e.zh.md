## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco fakes overhead strike, attacks low, guardian parries with stone sword, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Snyder's 300 close kinetic combat, Villeneuve's Dune Part Two duels. Lighting: cold pale ambient from above, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient stone with carved script on the colossal blade. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking matching Roco's combat motion.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall. Scale through the colossal carved stone sword the guardian wields — a 5-meter blade dwarfing Roco's 1-meter ruby sword.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-7s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco mid-feint, 50mm, f/1.8. Operator handheld at chest height tracking him. Roco crystal-armored stands in low ready stance, ruby sword raised, body angled. He commits to an OVERHEAD swing — the ruby sword arcing upward then forward in a high overhead trajectory toward the guardian off-frame above, body following through hard, telegraphing the strike. Then mid-motion he ABORTS — the swing pulled back at the last moment, his body pivoting downward, sword redirecting in an arc. He drops his stance and drives the sword in a LOW thrust toward the guardian's lower body off-frame, body now angled completely opposite from the original swing, the ruby blade coming in fast at hip-level of the colossus. Audio: WHOOSH of the overhead arc, his mineral grunt of the redirect, the WHOOSH of the low thrust, his focused breath through sealed armor.

Shot 2 (7-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on the impact moment, 85mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on the moment Roco's ruby sword meets the guardian's COLOSSAL CARVED STONE BLADE — the colossus has swung his 5-meter sword down to PARRY Roco's low thrust, the massive ancient stone blade meeting the small ruby blade with a thunderous CRACK and saturated red FLASH at the impact point. Roco's ruby sword RECOILS off the colossal stone blade, his arm jerked backward by the force, his body forced backward several steps. The colossal stone sword visible filling the deep blurred background, weathered carved stone with ancient script, the parry holding firm, the colossus's mass behind the blade. Roco recovers his balance, sword still in hand, the feint defeated. He resets his stance, the colossal blade in deep blur withdrawing for the next exchange. Audio: thunderous CRACK of stone-on-crystal parry, saturated red flash sound, his mineral grunt of recoil, the colossal stone blade's deep grinding withdrawal, dust cascading from the impact, his deep recovery breath.

PACING: 15-second feint-and-parry. Overhead feint redirected to low thrust (0-7s), guardian's colossal blade parries the low strike, Roco recoils (7-15s).

参照 Image 1 中那名全身被半透明红宝石红切面水晶护甲包裹的男子，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮住面部的锯齿状领甲。

场景（Setting）：石窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），尘埃雾气震颤。

镜头 2（7-15 秒）：切至四分之三侧面手持极特写拍撞击的一刻，85mm，f/1.4。焦点窄到极致，落在 Roco 的红宝石剑迎上守护者巨大的雕刻石刃的那一刻——巨神已经挥下他那 5 米长的剑来 PARRY 格挡 Roco 的低刺，巨大的古石刃迎上细小的红宝石剑，撞击点传来轰然 CRACK 与饱和红闪光。Roco 的红宝石剑在巨剑石刃上 RECOILS 弹开，手臂被那股力量向后扯动，身体被迫倒退数步。巨大的石剑可见地填满深虚化的背景，是带着远古铭文的风化刻石，这一挡稳稳镇住，巨神的质量全压在这柄剑上。Roco 找回平衡，剑仍握在手中，佯攻被击破。他重新凝住站姿，深虚化中的巨刃抽回，准备下一轮交锋。音效：石撞晶格挡的轰然 CRACK 声，饱和红闪光的声响，他向后弹开时矿物般的哼声，巨石的研磨抽回声，撞击处尘土成片泻落，他深沉的恢复呼吸。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒的佯攻与格挡。高举佯攻转而变作低位端刺（0-7 秒），守护者的巨刃格挡下这一记低位攻击，Roco 向后弹开（7-15 秒）。
