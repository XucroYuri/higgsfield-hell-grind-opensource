Vast ancient sanctum carved from natural rock, awakening and orientation sequence, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.
Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 3.
Refer to the elderly East Asian monk with short grey hair, weathered face, dark maroon robe over earth-toned undergarment from Image 4.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with carbon fiber surface, dual circular thruster cores glowing soft purple from Image 5.

VISUAL STYLE: cinematic anamorphic, long-lens telephoto cinematography 35mm-200mm equivalent, shallow depth of field f/1.8-f/2, deep cool blue-grey palette with warm amber accents from oil-lamps lining the floor on both side walls, dramatic chiaroscuro — most of the frame in near-black shadow, single cold pale daylight shaft falling from a natural rock fissure high above onto a colossal seated stone warrior statue at the far end. Heavily desaturated. Inspired by Cuarón's Children of Men, Lubezki's The Revenant, Iñárritu's Birdman — handheld kinetic intimacy, monumental scale through composition.

LOCATION: a vast sanctum carved from natural rock — deep cavern-corridor approximately fifteen meters wide and forty meters long, high irregular natural rock ceiling lost in shadow far above. Carved stone column-faces flanking both side walls, towering and receding into darkness. Irregular flagstone floor. Two parallel lines of small iron oil-lamp stands flickering warm amber along both side walls, stretching toward a vanishing point. At the far end on the chamber's central axis: a colossal seated stone warrior statue, bald, muscled, ancient armor, severe carved face, hands cupped together in his lap. Cold pale daylight shaft from above falls on him. On the statue's cupped hands at lap-height four meters above the floor: a small living elderly East Asian monk in dark maroon robes, short grey hair, seated cross-legged in meditation, motionless, eyes closed. A small candle flame beside him.

CHARACTERS: Roco lies face-down on the flagstones near the side wall of the sanctum at the entrance end, body parallel to the wall, dust-covered, jacket scuffed. Jax is on his feet beside Roco, having recovered first. Rain stands a few meters away, the matte-black hoverboard with glowing purple thruster cores held in her left hand at her side, dust-covered.

Shot 1 (0-1.5s): HANDHELD LOW-ANGLE on Roco lying face-down against the side wall of the sanctum, 85mm long lens, very shallow focus. Camera at floor level beside him, his head turned to one side, dust-streaked cheek pressed against cold flagstone, eyes blinking open in confusion. The carved stone column-face of the side wall rises behind him in shallow-focus blur, oil-lamps glowing warm amber at the wall's base. Frame breathes with handheld drift. Jax's open hand enters from above frame, reaching toward Roco's shoulder. Jax's voice off-screen above: a low "Hey." Roco's eyes track up. Audio: ambient cavern drone, distant water drop with chamber reverb, Roco's slow ragged inhale, Jax's jacket creak, faint oil-lamp crackle.

Shot 2 (1.5-4.5s): HANDHELD LOW-RISING shot beside the two men, 50mm wider lens. Jax kneels, grips Roco's shoulder, takes his forearm in a strong clasp, and pulls. Roco pushes up onto hands, then knees, then with Jax's pull rises to his feet. The camera RISES WITH HIM continuously, operator physically standing as Roco stands, AND rotating slightly to reveal the sanctum interior behind them — the towering column-faces extending up out of frame into shadow, the parallel oil-lamp lines stretching away into deep darkness toward the distant statue, the cold daylight shaft visible far in the background. The 50mm lens shows both men clearly AND the chamber's enormity in one frame. Roco leans briefly against Jax's shoulder for balance. Frame drifts and corrects, alive. Audio: the scrape of Roco's grip on Jax's arm, Jax's grunt of effort, scrape of Roco's boots on flagstone, both breathing heavily, ambient drone, chamber reverb deepening as space opens audibly.

Shot 3 (4.5-6.5s): CUT to HANDHELD MEDIUM CLOSE on Rain, 100mm. She stands holding the hoverboard at her side in her left hand, purple thruster glow as soft accent points on her body. Three-quarter profile, dust-streaked, calm and alert. Her eyes track slowly UPWARD — head tilting back as she takes in the height of the chamber. Cold distant light catches her face faintly, oil-lamp warmth on her side. Behind her in shallow-focus blur, the column-faces rise out of frame — vertical immensity implied through what is cropped. Camera breathes around her, drifts subtly. Eyes still tracking up, she speaks quietly with chamber reverb: "You okay?" Audio: her controlled breathing, jacket drag, ambient drone, distant water drop, her line with reverb tail.

Shot 4 (6.5-8.5s): CUT to HIGH-ANGLE WIDE from far above near the dark ceiling, looking down at the trio, 50mm. The three appear as small figures on the vast flagstone floor — Roco and Jax close together left of center, Rain to their right, all dwarfed by the chamber. Parallel oil-lamp lines stretch down both side walls toward the distant colossal statue at the far end of frame, vanishing-point geometry pulling the eye through depth. Cold daylight shaft falls on the statue at frame center. Frame mostly darkness with oil-lamp lines as warm amber threads. Trio looks small, alone, fragile — three figures occupying a tiny portion of the vast frame. Camera holds with subtle handheld drift transmitted from operator's high vantage. Audio: distant footsteps and breathing, ambient drone with deeper chamber reverb at this height, chamber's enormous emptiness audible.

Shot 5 (8.5-10.5s): CUT to HANDHELD MEDIUM TWO-SHOT on Roco and Jax, 100mm. Roco has stepped slightly away from Jax, hand pressed to his temple, blinking hard. Jax beside him, glasses just straightened with a finger flick, head lifted, looking forward and up toward the distant end of the chamber. Roco follows Jax's gaze, hand drops from temple, face shifts from confusion to slow awe. In deep background between their two heads, the colossal statue is visible clearly for the first time — the cold daylight shaft on it, the tiny silhouette of the elderly monk seated cross-legged on its cupped hands, warm pinpoint of candle beside him. Both men's eyes track upward, mouths slightly parted. Camera drifts and holds. Audio: their breathing, ambient drone deepening, faint oil-lamp crackles, faint suggestion of the monk's slow steady breathing carried by chamber reverb.

Shot 6 (10.5-13s): CUT to HANDHELD ROVING WIDE on Roco, 35mm wider lens, camera in motion the entire shot. Starts behind his right shoulder as he turns to take in the sanctum, tracks AROUND him in a partial arc — left-to-right behind him as he rotates, so we see what he sees in a moving reveal. Frame catches: towering carved column-faces on the left wall extending up out of frame into shadow, oil-lamp line stretching into vanishing-point darkness, deep flagstone corridor receding endlessly, then — as the camera completes its arc and Roco's gaze settles forward — the colossal statue at the far end, the meditating elderly monk in dark maroon robes a small silhouette on its cupped hands, warm pinpoint of candle beside him. Reveal earned through camera matching Roco's body. The 35mm wider lens shows Roco AND the enormity together. Frame drifts hard with handheld breath, operator's footsteps faintly transmitting. Audio: his slow turning footsteps, his breathing, chamber's depth audible in a slow swelling reverb.

Shot 7 (13-15s): CUT to HANDHELD MEDIUM TIGHT TWO-SHOT on Rain and Roco, 100mm. They stand close together, Rain holding the hoverboard at her side, purple thruster glow soft accent. Rain's eyes are on Roco, checking him. Roco does not look at her — eyes forward, fixed on the distant statue. After a beat, his answer comes flat and decided with chamber reverb: "Let's move." Frame breathes. Behind them in deep shallow-focus blur, Jax's silhouette already turned forward, ready. Audio: his line, reverb tail dying into ambient drone, distant oil-lamp crackle.

CINEMATOGRAPHY: long lens 35mm-200mm. Shot 1 is 85mm. Shot 2 is 50mm wider (rising with Roco AND revealing scale). Shots 3, 5, 7 are 100mm. Shot 4 is 50mm high-angle wide (chamber's full scale). Shot 6 is 35mm wider (roving arc). Handheld throughout — alive, breath-driven, operator a fourth presence. CUTS (not whip-pans) connect the characters — clean direct cuts, each character their own moment. Frame constantly drifts, never locks. Reactive but never jittery. Shallow depth of field f/1.8-f/2. Cold blue-grey palette, warm oil-lamp accents, single cold shaft on the distant statue, soft purple glow accents from the hoverboard's thruster cores when in frame. Scale communicated through wider lens choices in Shots 2, 4, 6, vanishing-point oil-lamp geometry, vertical implication of column tops cropped out of frame, high-angle wide of Shot 4.

AUDIO: deep ambient cavern drone, distant water drops with chamber reverb, soft oil-lamp crackles, characters' breathing and clothing rustle, scrape of Roco's boots and Jax's effort grunt as Jax pulls him up — all with significant chamber reverb. Jax's "Hey" in Shot 1, Rain's "You okay?" in Shot 3, Roco's "Let's move" in Shot 7 — all with strong reverb tails. Chamber's vast empty reverb signature deepens through the clip. Faint suggestion of the monk's slow steady breathing carried by chamber reverb in Shot 5. NO MUSIC.

PACING: kinetic and alive in the rising sequence (Shots 1-2) — physical effort, tactile help between Jax and Roco, scale revealed through movement. Cuts (Shots 3-5) give each character their own discovery moment with Rain's question landing in her upward gaze. Shot 4's high-angle wide is the architectural breath — trio as small figures in immense space, scale established in one image. Shot 6 is the personal reveal — Roco's perspective on the statue and the meditating monk. Shot 7 is the dialogue beat — care, decision, forward motion.

从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔古老圣殿（sanctum），苏醒与定向序列，15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 宽银幕（2.39:1），写实摄影（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，无音乐。

参照 Image 1 中那位银白浅发、眼角带疤、灰色连帽夹克的女性。
参照 Image 2 中那位漂金寸头、深肤色、透明防护镜、深色夹克的男性。
参照 Image 3 中那位深色乱发、淡须、鼻梁贴红色绷带、深色连帽夹克的男性。
参照 Image 4 中那位灰白短发、饱经风霜面庞、身着土地色系内衣外罩深绛红僧袍的年长东亚僧人。
参照 Image 5 中那架哑光黑、炭纤维表面的悬浮滑板（hoverboard），配双圆形推进器核心泛出柔和的紫色光。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：电影感的 anamorphic，35mm-200mm 等效长焦远摄摄影，浅景深 f/1.8-f/2，深冷的蓝灰基调，辅以两侧墙脚沿地排列的油灯散发的暖琥珀点缀，戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）——画面大部分处于近乎全黑的阴影中，一道惨白的冷日光线从高处一条天然岩缝中落下，照在远端那尊巨型坐姿石雕战士像上。高度去饱和。灵感源自 Cuarón 的《人类之子》（Children of Men）、Lubezki 的《荒野猎人》（The Revenant）、Iñárritu 的《鸟人》（Birdman）——手持（handheld）动能化的亲密感，以构图传达巨物的尺度。

地点（LOCATION）：一座从天然岩石中凿出的辽阔圣殿——深长的洞窟走廊，约宽十五米、长四十米，高高的不规则天然岩顶在头顶高处隐没于阴影。两侧墙旁刻着石柱面，高耸并渐隐没入黑暗。地面为不规则的石板（flagstone）地。两侧墙旁两列平行的小铁制油灯架摇曳着暖琥珀光，朝一个灭点延伸。厅堂中央轴线的远端：一尊巨型坐姿石雕战士像，光头、肌肉毕现、身披古老铠甲、容貌刻痕严酷，双手合拢搁在膝上。惨白的冷日光线从上方落在他身上。在雕像合拢的双手上，离地面四米高的膝部高度处：一位年长的东亚僧人，身着深绛红僧袍、灰白短发，盘腿端坐入定，一动不动，双眼紧闭。身旁有一小簇蜡烛火焰。

角色（CHARACTERS）：Roco 面朝下躺在圣殿入口端侧墙旁的石板地上，身体与墙平行，满身尘土，夹克磨旧。Jax 站在 Roco 身旁，先一步恢复过来。Rain 站在几米开外，左手把哑光黑、泛着紫色推进器核心光的悬浮滑板拿在身侧，满身尘土。

镜头 1（0-1.5 秒）：手持低机位，对准 Roco 脸朝下趴在圣殿侧墙旁，85mm 长焦，极浅焦。摄影机在他身旁的地面高度，他的头转向一侧，沾满尘土的侧颊紧贴冰冷的石板地，眼睛困惑地眨着睁开。侧墙刻着石柱面的墙在他身后以浅焦模糊中升起，油灯在墙基泛着暖琥珀光。画面随手持漂移而起伏。Jax 摊开的手从画面上方探入，朝 Roco 的肩伸去。Jax 的声音在画面外上方：一句低低的「Hey.」。Roco 的眼睛循着向上望去。Audio：洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响（chamber reverb）的水滴声，Roco 缓慢粗重的吸气，Jax 夹克的窸窣，轻微的油灯噼啪声。

镜头 2（1.5-4.5 秒）：手持低机位升起，两名男性身旁，50mm 更广的镜头。Jax 跪下，抓住 Roco 的肩，以有力的锁握握住他的前臂，拉动。Roco 撑到手上，再到膝上，再借 Jax 的拉动站起身。摄影机随他一起升起，Roco 站起时操作员确实站起身，同时微微旋转以揭示他们身后圣殿的内部——高耸的石柱面向画面外延伸入阴影，平行的油灯连排向深重的黑暗朝着远处那尊雕像伸去，惨白的日光线束在远方背景中可见。50mm 镜头在同一帧里既清楚呈现两名男性，又呈现厅堂的广袤。Roco 短暂地靠在 Jax 的肩上寻求平衡。画面漂移又校正，生气勃勃。Audio：Roco 抓在 Jax 手臂上的刮擦声，Jax 用力的闷哼，Roco 靴子踏在石板地上的刮擦声，两人呼吸沉重，环境低鸣，空间敞开时室内混响随之渐深。

镜头 3（4.5-6.5 秒）：切至手持中景特写，对准 Rain，100mm。她站着，左手把悬浮滑板拿在身侧，紫色推进器光晕在她身上化作柔和的光点。四分之三侧面，满脸尘土，平静而警觉。她的目光在缓慢向上升移——头向后仰，她打量着厅堂的高度。冷而遥远的光轻轻掠过她的脸，油灯的暖光在她身侧。在她身后以浅焦模糊中，石柱面向画面外升起——那种竖向的庞然是通过被裁切的部分暗示出来的。摄影机在她周围起伏，微妙地漂移。目光仍在向上移，她轻声开口，带室内混响：「You okay?」Audio：她受控的呼吸，夹克的拖曳，环境低鸣，远处的水滴声，她的台词带混响尾音。

镜头 4（6.5-8.5 秒）：切至自遥远上方幽暗天花板附近的高机位全景（WIDE），向下俯视这支小队，50mm。三人作为辽阔石板地上的细小身影而显现——Roco 与 Jax 在中线左侧靠得很近，Rain 在他们右侧，全被厅堂衬得渺小。平行的油灯连排顺着两侧墙脚朝画面远端那座巨像延伸，灭点几何把视线牵引着穿过纵深。惨白的日光线束落在画面中央的雕像上。画面大部分是黑暗，只有油灯连排化作温暖琥珀色的丝线。小队显得渺小、孤单、脆弱——三个身影只占据了辽阔画面的一小部分。摄影机以其与操作员高机位相连的微妙手持漂移稳住。Audio：远处的脚步声与呼吸，此处高度带更深室内混响的环境低鸣，厅堂那无边空旷清晰可闻。

镜头 5（8.5-10.5 秒）：切至手持中景双人镜头，对准 Roco 与 Jax，100mm。Roco 已经轻轻远离 Jax，手按在自己太阳穴上，使劲眨着眼。Jax 在他身旁，刚用手一记指尖轻拨扶正眼镜，抬起头，朝厅堂遥远的尽头向前向上望去。Roco 追随 Jax 的视线，手从太阳穴上落下，脸从困惑转向缓慢的敬畏。在两人头部之间纵深的背景里，那尊巨像第一次清晰可见——惨白的日光线束落在它上面，年长僧人盘腿端坐的小小剪影在它合拢的双手上，暖色蜡烛的光点在他身旁。两名男性的目光都朝上方望去，嘴唇微微张开。摄影机漂移并稳住。Audio：他们的呼吸，环境低鸣渐深，轻微的油灯噼啪声，僧人缓慢平稳呼吸的隐隐声息被室内混响遥传而来。

镜头 6（10.5-13 秒）：切至手持游移全景（roving wide），对准 Roco，35mm 更广的镜头，整个镜头摄影机都在运动中。从他右肩后开始，在他转身打量圣殿时以一段弧线绕着他追踪——在他自己旋转时从他身后自左向右移动，于是我们在一个移动的揭示中看到他所见之物。画面捕捉：左墙上高耸的刻面石柱向画面外延伸入阴影，油灯连排延伸入灭点般的黑暗，幽深的石板走廊无尽退去，随后——当摄影机完成弧线、Roco 的目光向前落定时——远端那尊巨像，身着深绛红僧袍、入定的年长僧人在它合拢的双手上化作一个小小剪影，他身旁那支蜡烛温暖的光点。这揭示是通过摄影机贴合 Roco 的身体而挣得的。35mm 更广的镜头既呈现 Roco，也呈现那份广袤。画面随手持呼吸剧烈漂移，操作员的脚步声隐约传透。Audio：他缓慢转身的脚步声，他的呼吸，厅堂的深度在一种缓慢渐浓的回响中可闻。

镜头 7（13-15 秒）：切至手持中景紧凑双人镜头，对准 Rain 与 Roco，100mm。他们站得很近，Rain 把悬浮滑板拿在身侧，柔和的紫色推进器光晕作点缀。Rain 的目光落在 Roco 身上，查看他。Roco 没有看她——目光向前，锁定在远方那尊雕像上。顿了一拍后，他的回答平平地、决然地传来，带室内混响：「Let's move.」画面起伏。在他们身后以极深浅焦的模糊中，Jax 的剪影已经转身向前，准备就绪。Audio：他的台词，混响尾音消散入环境低鸣，远处油灯的噼啪声。

摄影（CINEMATOGRAPHY）：全程长焦 35mm-200mm。镜头 1 为 85mm。镜头 2 为更广的 50mm（随 Roco 升起并揭示尺度）。镜头 3、5、7 为 100mm。镜头 4 为 50mm 高机位全景（厅堂全尺度）。镜头 6 为更广的 35mm（游移弧线）。全程手持——生气勃勃、由呼吸驱动，操作员是第四位在场者。切（而非甩镜/whip-pans）连接各角色——干脆利落的直接切，每个角色各有其独特时刻。画面持续漂移，从不锁定。灵敏却绝不抖动。浅景深 f/1.8-f/2。冷蓝灰基调，暖琥珀油灯点缀，远方雕像上那一道冷光柱，悬浮滑板推进器核心在入画时泛起的柔紫色光辉。尺度通过镜头 2、4、6 中更广的镜头选择、灭点油灯的几何、画面外裁切掉的石柱顶部的竖向暗示、以及镜头 4 的高机位全景而传达。

AUDIO：幽深的洞窟环境低鸣，远处带室内混响的水滴声，柔和的油灯噼啪声，角色的呼吸与衣物沙沙，Roco 靴子的刮擦以及 Jax 拉起他时用力的闷哼——全都带显著的室内混响。镜头 1 中 Jax 的「Hey」、镜头 3 中 Rain 的「You okay?」、镜头 7 中 Roco 的「Let's move」——全部带强烈的混响尾音。厅堂那空廓而广袤的回响印记于短片过程中渐深。镜头 5 中僧人缓慢平稳呼吸的隐隐声息被室内混响遥传而来。无音乐（NO MUSIC）。

PACING：升起序列（镜头 1-2）动能充沛、生气勃勃——身体上的费力、Jax 与 Roco 之间触觉上的搀扶，尺度通过移动得以揭示。切（镜头 3-5）给每个角色各自的发现时刻，Rain 的问题落在她上扬的目光中。镜头 4 的高机位全景是建筑性的呼吸——三人化作宏大空间中的细小身影，尺度在一个画面中确立。镜头 6 是私人的揭示——Roco 对那座雕像与那位入定僧人的视角。镜头 7 是对话的拍子——关切、决断、前行动作。
