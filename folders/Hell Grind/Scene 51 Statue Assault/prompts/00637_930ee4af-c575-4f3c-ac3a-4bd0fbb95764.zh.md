## 原文 (source)

Edit the statue's hand pose while keeping everything else identical.
Modify only the hands and arms of the seated stone warrior statue. Remove the sword entirely. Change the hand position to a meditation mudra (Dhyana mudra):
Both hands resting in the lap, centered along the vertical midline slightly in front of the statue.
The back hand (lower) rests palm-up on the lap/legs.
The front hand (upper) rests palm-up directly on top of the lower hand, palms stacked.
Both palms face upward, fingers relaxed and slightly curved.
Optionally, the thumb tips of both hands lightly touch each other to form a soft oval — classic meditation posture.
Preserve everything else exactly:
Same stone material with weathered grey texture and green moss patches in the same locations
Same armored shoulders, robes, and belt with geometric pattern
Same bald head, stern facial expression, and furrowed brow
Same cross-legged seated pose
Same lighting, camera angle, background, and three-view composition (front view, back view, close-up portrait)
Same sculptural style and proportions
Only the hand/arm positions and the absence of the sword should change. The hands themselves must be carved from the same mossy stone, matching the rest of the statue's material and weathering.

## 中文对照 (zh)

编辑雕像的手部姿势，其余一切保持不变。

只修改这件端坐的石武士雕像的双手与双臂。完全移除长剑。将手部姿势改为冥想手印（Dhyana mudra/禅定印）：
双手置于膝上，沿雕像垂直中线居中，略微位于雕像前方。
靠后的手（下方）掌心向上放在膝/腿上。
靠前的手（上方）掌心向上直接叠放在下方手上，掌心相叠。
两只手掌均朝上，手指放松、微微弯曲。
可选地，双手的指尖轻轻相触，形成一个柔和的椭圆——经典的冥想姿态。
其余一切完全保持不变：
同样的石材，带有风化的灰色纹理和位于相同位置的绿色苔藓斑块
同样的装甲肩部、长袍与几何图案腰带
同样的光头、严峻的面部表情与紧锁的眉头
同样的盘腿端坐姿势
同样的灯光、机位、背景与三视图构图（正面视图、背面视图、特写肖像）
同样的雕塑风格与比例
只有手/手臂的位置和移除长剑应改变。双手必须用同样的覆有苔藓的石材雕刻，与雕像其余部分的材质和风化程度相匹配。
