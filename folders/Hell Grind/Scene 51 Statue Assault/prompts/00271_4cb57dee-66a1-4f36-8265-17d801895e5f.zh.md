STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, physical cine camera, anamorphic lens, 180° shutter motion blur, heavy 35mm grain, corner vignette, edge chromatic aberration. Color 60:30:10 — dominant deep cool blue-grey shadow / secondary warm amber practicals / accent soft cool purple from hoverboard thrusters. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin depth of field f/1.4-f/2.8 throughout, focus used as dramatic tool. Photorealistic skin with pores, vellus, asymmetry, capillary flush. Real mass, real shadows, real follow-through. Continuity locked across all frames — no identity drift. Audio: environmental SFX only, no music, no subtitles.

All vertical lifts, lateral drifts, forward pushes, and circular arcs achieved by operator physically walking, rising, leaning, turning. Frame breathes constantly with operator's breath, weight shifts, footstep transmission, balance corrections. INTENSE handheld shake on dialogue close-ups. The top-down shot achieved by Easyrig arm extended overhead angled straight down.
