## 原文 (source)

Jax rolls between columns, loads slingshot with plastid bomb, fires, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Cuarón's Children of Men kinetic. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from off-frame guardian, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, accent amber. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic skin, fabric, weathered ancient carved stone columns, classic Y-frame wooden slingshot, grey plastid material. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig at chest height tracking laterally. EXTREME trembling.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, towering carved stone column-faces flanking the side walls, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-7s): Three-quarter handheld medium close tracking Jax mid-roll, 50mm, f/1.8. Operator handheld at chest height tracking him laterally. Jax rolls fast across the open space between two carved stone columns — body tucked, jacket flaring, dust kicking up under him, he comes out of the roll up onto one knee behind the second column for cover. His glasses askew, he straightens them with a practiced finger flick, dust-streaked. He immediately bites off a piece of plastid from his wad and spits the formed ball into his palm. He pulls a classic Y-frame wooden slingshot from his belt with his other hand. He mutters: "...c'mon big boy..." Audio: his roll on flagstone, his quick breath, the spit into palm, dust falling, his muttered words, off-frame guardian hum.

Shot 2 (7-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Jax aiming the slingshot from behind the column, 85mm, f/1.4. He has loaded the grey plastid ball into the slingshot's pouch. He draws back the elastic with his right hand to full tension, his arm muscles taut, his left arm holding the Y-frame steady, one eye closed behind his glasses for aim. He looks toward the guardian off-frame, calculating the shot. He releases — the elastic SNAPS forward launching the plastid ball out of frame, the slingshot recoiling in his hand. He immediately reaches back to bite another piece of plastid as he watches the shot off-frame, ready to reload. Then off-frame the bomb DETONATES — orange-yellow FLASH visible at frame edge, dust eruption. He mutters: "...yeah..." with grim satisfaction. Audio: the elastic SNAP of release, the bomb's whistle through air off-frame, his immediate bite into plastid, the explosion BOOM with chamber reverb, his muttered word.

PACING: 15-second roll-and-shoot. Roll between columns to new cover and prepare bomb (0-7s), aim slingshot, fire, detonation (7-15s).

STYLE：8K IMAX 写实摄影，anamorphic 电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Cuarón 的《人类之子》动感段落。灯光：冷白环境光，来自画外守护者的饱和红，温暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深冷蓝灰色，主导红，点缀琥珀色。高度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实的皮肤、布料、风化的古老刻石柱、经典 Y 形木质弹弓、灰色 plastid 材质。连贯性锁定。绝不用正面——用四分之三角度。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig 于胸口高度横向跟拍。极剧烈颤抖。

CHAMBER TREMOR（殿堂震颤）：剧烈摇晃，碎屑落下。

参照 Image 1 中那名漂白黄发寸头、深色皮肤、透明眼镜、深色夹克的男人。

场景：洞穴圣堂，侧墙两侧立有高耸的刻石柱面，尘雾震颤。

Shot 1（0-7s）：四分之三手持中近景跟踪翻滚中的 Jax，50mm，f/1.8。摄影师手持于胸口高度横向跟拍他。Jax 在两根刻石柱之间开阔空间快速翻滚——身体蜷缩，夹克翻飞，尘在他身下扬起，他自翻滚中起身单膝跪在第二根柱后寻求掩护。眼镜歪斜，他用熟练一指弹拨正，尘土斑驳。他立刻自胶团咬下一块 plastid，将成形的球啐进掌心。另一只手从腰间抽出一把经典 Y 形木质弹弓。他低语：「……来吧，大家伙……」音频：他在石板上的翻滚，他急促的呼吸，往掌心啐，尘土落下，他的低语，画外守护者嗡鸣。

Shot 2（7-15s）：切至四分之三手持中近景于柱后持弹弓瞄准的 Jax，85mm，f/1.4。他已将灰色 plastid 球装入弹弓弹兜。右手将弹性绳拉至满张力，手臂肌肉绷紧，左臂稳住 Y 形架，眼镜后闭起一目瞄准。他望向画外守护者，计算弹道。他释放——弹性绳 SNAP 前弹，将 plastid 球推出画外，弹弓在他手中回弹。他一边望着画外的弹道，一边立刻回手再咬一块 plastid 以准备装填。随后画外炸弹爆炸——画面边缘可见橙黄 FLASH，尘爆。他带着冷酷的满意低语：「……成了……」音频：释放时弹性绳的 SNAP，画外炸弹穿空而过的哨声，他即时的咬食 plastid，带殿堂回响的爆炸 BOOM，他的低语。

PACING：15 秒的翻滚与射击。滚过石柱至新掩护并准备炸弹（0-7s），瞄准弹弓，发射，爆炸（7-15s）。
