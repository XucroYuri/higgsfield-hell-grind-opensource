## 原文 (source)

Rain reaches for monk on ledge, colossal stone sword slams down between them, Rain recoils backward, 5 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient from above, saturated purple from hoverboard thrusters glowing below, warm amber from candle on monk's ledge. Color: deep cool blue-grey, accent purple, accent warm amber. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic skin, fabric, matte-black hoverboard, weathered ancient carved stone with script, monk's deep maroon robes. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld at Rain's elevation from her left side, three-quarter angle, frame trembling.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 2.
Refer to the elderly East Asian monk in dark maroon robes from Image 3.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling. Rain hovering on board at the level of the carved stone ledge twelve meters up the cliff wall. The ledge with the monk seated cross-legged in deep maroon robes meditating, candle burning beside him. A torch glowing warm amber on the cliff wall behind the ledge.

Single shot (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Rain from her left side, 50mm, f/1.8.

At 0-2s: Rain visible hovering on the matte-black hoverboard at ledge level, body crouched forward toward the ledge, right arm extended reaching toward the monk, silver-blonde hair, dust-streaked, jacket, purple thrusters glowing below the board on weathered stone surface beside the ledge. The monk visible in shallow blurred background on the carved stone ledge to her right, deep maroon robes, eyes closed, sustained throat-tone, candle beside him. Audio: thrusters humming, her controlled breath, the monk's sustained throat-tone close, chamber rumble.

At 2-3s: The COLOSSAL STONE SWORD slams down from above into the gap between Rain and the monk — 5-meter blade descending vertically into the space between them, weathered ancient stone with carved script filling the middle of frame as a wall of stone, the blade impacts the carved stone surface of the ledge with a deafening CRACK, dust erupting, separating Rain from the monk completely. Audio: massive WHOOSH of the descending blade, THUNDEROUS CRACK of stone meeting stone, dust eruption, her sharp inhale.

At 3-5s: Rain THROWS her body backward and to the left, the hoverboard lurching back and away from the blade, her body angled backward in a sharp recoil, arms flying out for balance, thrusters firing in reverse for the evasion. The colossal blade remains embedded in the ledge filling the right portion of frame as a wall, dust still erupting from its impact, the monk now hidden behind the blade. Rain emerges several meters away from the ledge, breathing hard, the path completely blocked. Audio: thrusters firing in reverse, her sharp grunt of evasion, the blade's continuing PRESSURE GROAN against the stone, dust hissing, the monk's throat-tone continuing unbroken from behind the blade.

PACING: 5-second guardian intervention. Rain reaches for monk (0-2s), colossal sword slams down between them (2-3s), Rain recoils backward away from the blade (3-5s).

PACING（节奏）：5 秒守护者干预。Rain 伸手伸向武僧（0-2s）、巨剑猛然砸落两人之间（2-3s）、Rain 向后急剧后缩、退离剑刃（3-5s）。
