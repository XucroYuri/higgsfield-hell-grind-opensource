## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco leaps onto guardian's torso, struck off, lands hard, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient carved stone. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking matching Roco's flight.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling, flagstone floor.

Shot 1 (0-4s): Three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle, 35mm, f/2. Roco crystal-armored launches from flagstone in foreground in a powerful upward leap toward the guardian's massive torso filling the deep frame. Sword raised in his right hand. He impacts the carved chest plate, his crystal forearm spikes embedding into ancient cracks of the stone, body pressed against the colossal torso. Audio: his mineral leap grunt, the CRACK of crystal embedding into stone, chamber rumble.

Shot 2 (4-8s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Roco on the chest, 100mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on Roco clinging to the carved stone armor, driving the ruby sword DOWN into the chest plate — saturated red flash, sparks of crystal and stone, the carved relief cracking from his strike. He digs the blade in deeper, his mineral grunt of effort. Audio: deep CRUNCH of crystal driving into stone, dust eruption, his roar.

Shot 3 (8-12s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on the colossal stone hand descending, 85mm, f/1.4. The guardian's massive carved stone hand the size of a small car SWATS Roco from the chest — colossal palm impacts him from the side with deep WHUMP, his crystal-armored body LAUNCHED off the chest, body horizontal in flight through the air, sword still in hand, dust trailing. Camera tracks his flight. Audio: WHOOSH of the colossal hand, deep WHUMP of impact, his sharp mineral grunt of pain, body flying through air.

Shot 4 (12-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco mid-flight slamming into a carved stone column, 50mm, f/1.8. His armored body impacts the column with thunderous CRACK, the carved stone column-face cracking from the impact, body sliding down the column to the flagstone at its base, coming to rest on his knees against the column base, breathing hard with mineral resonance. Audio: thunderous CRACK of his body on stone column, dust eruption, his mineral grunt of pain, his body sliding down stone, his mineral breath of recovery.

PACING: 15-second four-shot leap and crash. Leap onto chest (0-4s), drive sword in (4-8s), guardian's hand swats him off (8-12s), slam into column (12-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

水晶装甲的 Roco 跃上守护者的躯干、被击落、重重坠地，15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》、Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》、Snyder《300 勇士》。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 红宝石水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光，暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶、风化古老雕花石面。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持，极度颤抖，以身体驱动的跟拍贴合 Roco 的飞行轨迹。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，天花板碎片纷纷坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，手有小汽车般大小。

参见这名男子：完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），尘雾在震颤，石板地面。

Shot 1（0-4 秒）：四分之三手持极低机位，35mm，f/2。水晶装甲的 Roco 自前景的石板地上发力 LLAUNCHES 高高跃起，冲向占满深虚画面的守护者巨大躯干。右手中剑高举。他撞上雕花的胸甲，前臂水晶尖刺嵌入石头的古老裂隙、身体紧贴庞大的躯干。音频：他跃起时矿物的闷哼、水晶嵌入石中的 CRACK、密殿隆隆。

Shot 2（4-8 秒）：切至四分之三手持极特写，Roco 攀在胸口，100mm，f/1.4。极薄焦点落在攀附于雕花石甲的 Roco 身上，他将红宝石剑狠狠捅进胸甲——饱和红光一闪、水晶与石头的火星四溅、雕花浮雕因他这一击而开裂。他更深入地刺入剑刃、用力时发出矿物的闷哼。音频：水晶刺入石头深处的 CRUNCH 声、尘土迸发、他的咆哮。

Shot 3（8-12 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，巨型石手降下，85mm，f/1.4。守护者有小汽车般大小的巨型雕花石手 SWATS 把 Roco 自胸口拍落——巨大的手掌以深沉 WHUMP 从侧面撞中他、他水晶装甲的身体被从胸口弹飞、身体水平掠过空气、剑仍握在手中、尘土拖曳。摄影机跟拍他的飞行。音频：巨手的 WHOOSH、撞击的深沉 WHUMP、他尖锐的痛苦矿物闷哼、身体飞掠空气。

Shot 4（12-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，Roco 在空中飞行时轰然撞上一座雕花石柱，50mm，f/1.8。他装甲的身体撞上石柱、发出雷鸣般的 CRACK、雕花石柱的柱面因撞击而开裂、身体沿柱身滑落至柱脚的石板地面、最后跪在柱脚、以矿物共鸣艰难喘息。音频：他身体撞上石柱的雷鸣 CRACK、尘土迸发、他痛苦矿物般的闷哼、身体沿石面滑落、他恢复时矿物的呼吸。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒四镜头跃击与坠落。跃上胸口（0-4 秒），刺入剑刃（4-8 秒），守护者的手将他拍落（8-12 秒），撞上石柱（12-15 秒）。
