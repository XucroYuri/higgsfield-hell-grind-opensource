Stone guardian pins crystal-armored Roco against the floor with sword pressing down, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with combat damage cracks, weathered ancient carved stone. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, EXTREME trembling, body-driven. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, flagstone floor with combat damage, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-4s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco on his back on the flagstone, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco crystal-armored fallen on his back, ruby crystal sword still in his right hand, shield in his left hand raised desperately above his chest. The colossal stone SWORD descends from above toward him — 5-meter blade plunging vertically. Roco raises shield to block. Audio: his strained mineral breath, the WHOOSH of the descending colossal blade, the colossus's deep red hum.

Shot 2 (4-8s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on the impact, 100mm, f/1.4. The colossal stone blade IMPACTS Roco's ruby shield — saturated red flash, thunderous CRACK, the shield CRACKS deeply in branching fracture lines but holds, the blade pressing down. Roco's body pinned beneath the pressure, his chest and shoulders forced back against the flagstone, his armored back cracking the stone beneath him. Audio: thunderous CRACK of impact, sustained PRESSURE GROAN of stone on crystal, his mineral roar of strain, the flagstone cracking beneath his back.

Shot 3 (8-12s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on the sustained pressure, 85mm, f/1.4. Roco visible from the side flat on his back, the colossal blade pressing DOWN on his crystal shield with tremendous weight, his right arm with the sword pinned to the floor unable to counter, his left arm with the shield trembling violently from the pressure, his body shaking, the cracks in his shield deepening, the shield about to break. Audio: continued PRESSURE GROAN, the shield's fractures audibly extending, his mineral roar building, his strained breath through teeth.

Shot 4 (12-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Roco's crystal collar, 100mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on his concealed face within the high jagged crystal collar — his armored body shaking under the sword's pressure, the deep red glow of his crystal pulsing erratically with his strain, the shield in shallow blur visibly cracking further. Audio: his strained mineral breath at maximum effort, the cracking shield, the colossus's deep red hum sustained pressure, the sustained groan of stone on crystal.

PACING: 15-second four-shot pin-down. Roco fallen blocks descending sword (0-4s), impact pins him to floor (4-8s), sustained pressure on shield (8-12s), close-up on his straining body (12-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

石质守护者用剑向下压制、将水晶装甲的 Roco 压在地板上，15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》、Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》、Snyder《300 勇士》。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 红宝石水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光，暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶带战斗损伤裂纹、风化古老雕花石面。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持，极度颤抖，以身体驱动。绝不正面——保持四分之三角度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，天花板碎片纷纷坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，5 米长的利剑。

参见这名男子：完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），带战斗损伤的石板地面，尘雾在震颤。

Shot 1（0-4 秒）：四分之三手持中近景，Roco 仰躺在石板地上，50mm，f/1.8。水晶装甲的 Roco 仰面倒下，红宝石水晶剑仍在右手，左手的盾绝望地高举过胸前。巨大的石质 SWORD 自上方朝他降来——5 米长的剑刃垂直直落。Roco 举盾格挡。音频：他勉力呼吸的矿物气息、巨刃降下的 WHOOSH、巨像深沉的红光嗡鸣。

Shot 2（4-8 秒）：切至四分之三手持极特写，对准撞击，100mm，f/1.4。巨刃 IMPACTS 劈中 Roco 的宝石红盾——饱和红光一闪、雷鸣般的 CRACK、盾面以多线分叉的纹路 CRACKS 深深开裂但仍撑住、剑刃继续向下压制。Roco 的身体被压在这份压力之下、胸与双肩被死死抵回石板、其装甲的背把身下的石头压出裂纹。音频：雷鸣般的撞击 CRACK、石头压水晶持续的 PRESSURE GROAN（受压呻吟）、他勉力时矿物般的咆哮、身下石板开裂。

Shot 3（8-12 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，持续的压制，85mm，f/1.4。Roco 从侧面可见、平平地仰面躺着，巨刃以惊人的重量向下 PRESSING 压着他的水晶盾，他握剑的右臂被抵死在地上无法反击，握盾的左臂因压力剧烈颤抖、他的身体在颤动、盾上裂纹加深、盾即将碎裂。音频：持续的 PRESSURE GROAN、盾的裂隙声声清晰延伸、他的矿物咆哮逐渐积聚、他咬紧牙关的勉力呼吸。

Shot 4（12-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持极特写，对准 Roco 的水晶护颈（collar），100mm，f/1.4。极薄焦点落在他高耸锯齿状护颈（collar）内被遮掩的面孔上——他的装甲身体在剑刃的压力下颤抖、水晶内部深红的光芒随他用力而紊乱地搏动、浅虚化中的盾可见进一步开裂。音频：他以最大努力勉力挤出的矿物呼吸、盾碎裂声、巨像深沉的红光嗡鸣持续着压制、石头压水晶持续的低吟。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒四镜头压制。Roco 倒地格挡降下的剑（0-4 秒），撞击将他钉在地板（4-8 秒），盾上压力持续（8-12 秒），他勉力身体的特写（12-15 秒）。
