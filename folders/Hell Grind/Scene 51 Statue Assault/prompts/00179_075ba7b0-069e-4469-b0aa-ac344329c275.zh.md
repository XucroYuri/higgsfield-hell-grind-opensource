Roco and guardian fight, guardian throws Roco down, raises sword to execute, then suddenly lowers it and walks away to monk, Roco watches in confusion, 9 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men kinetic verite, Villeneuve Dune Part Two violent impacts. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with combat damage, weathered ancient carved stone with script and moss. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, body-driven tracking. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

GUARDIAN SCALE: towering, ten times Roco's height, 5-meter sword, hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, flagstone floor, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-3s): Three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle, 35mm, f/2. Roco crystal-armored swings ruby crystal sword in powerful diagonal arc into the colossal stone leg, saturated red flash on impact. The guardian's MASSIVE STONE HAND descends and BACKHANDS Roco — colossal palm impacts him with deep WHUMP, his body LAUNCHED sideways, slamming into flagstone with thunderous CRACK, body skidding to rest on his side. Audio: CRACK of crystal on leg, WHOOSH of colossal hand, deep WHUMP of impact, his mineral grunt, CRACK of body on flagstone.

Shot 2 (3-5s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on the guardian raising sword above Roco, 24mm wide, f/2. The towering colossus stands over Roco's fallen position visible at floor level in foreground. Both massive carved stone HANDS grip the colossal sword OVER his head, the 5-meter blade raised vertically to its apex above. The guardian holds the blade aloft for the execution strike, body poised to bring it down. Audio: deep stone-on-stone CLAMP of grip, the colossus's deep red hum spiking with execution intent, chamber rumble.

Shot 3 (5-7s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle, 24mm wide, f/2. The towering colossus visible holding the colossal sword aloft above his head, the blade still at apex. Then without any external cause the guardian's body STOPS — the sword paused at the apex, body rigid, the execution motion abandoned. The colossus then LOWERS the colossal sword from its raised position to his side with deep grinding stone, the blade descending without striking. Audio: the held aloft sword paused, deep grinding stone of the sword lowering to his side, the colossus's deep red hum continuing.

Shot 4 (7-9s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on the guardian's body walking away, 50mm, f/1.8. The guardian visible from lower torso to lower thigh, body extending out of frame top. He begins WALKING in a different direction — heavy deliberate footsteps moving away from Roco's position toward the direction of the monk's distant ledge, each step a colossal WHUMP shaking the chamber. In foreground at frame edge Roco visible on the flagstone half-risen on one elbow watching the guardian walk away from him with deep mineral exhale of confusion, his armored hand loosely holding the sword resting on the flagstone beside him. Audio: colossal WHUMPS of receding footsteps, deep red hum shifting direction, Roco's deep mineral exhale of confusion, dust hissing.

PACING: 9-second execution-stop-pivot-confusion. Roco fights, guardian throws him down (0-3s), guardian raises sword to execute (3-5s), guardian without external cause stops mid-strike and lowers sword (5-7s), guardian walks away to monk as Roco watches confused (7-9s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

Roco 与守护者交战，守护者将 Roco 打翻在地，举剑行刑，随后突然收剑走向僧侣，Roco 困惑地注视，9 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：卡隆《人类之子》的动态写实、维伦纽瓦《沙丘第二部》的暴力冲击。Lighting（灯光）：冷灰环境光，来自守护者眼眶与 Roco 红宝石水晶战甲的饱和红色，温暖琥珀色油灯。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰主调，红色为主导点缀，重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实的半透明红宝石水晶带战斗损伤，风化古老凿刻石带铭文与青苔。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持（shoulder-mounted handheld），以躯体驱动的追踪。绝不正面机位——使用四分之三侧面角。

CHAMBER TREMOR（殿堂震颤）：剧烈摇晃，碎屑坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者尺度）：高耸入云，为 Roco 身高的十倍，剑长 5 米，手掌有小型汽车大小。

参照图 1 中那位完全包裹在半透明红宝石色刻面水晶战甲内的男人，带肩部翼刺、前臂刺、遮住面孔的锯齿状护颈。

Setting（场景）：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），石板地面，震动的尘雾。

镜头 1（0-3 秒）：四分之三手持极低角度，35mm，f/2。身着水晶甲的 Roco 以强大的对角弧线挥动红宝石水晶剑劈向巨大的石腿，撞击瞬间爆出饱和红色闪光。守护者的巨大石手自上压下，反手扇向 Roco——巨大的掌心以深沉的重击声（WHUMP）击中他，他的身体横向飞出，以雷鸣般的脆裂声（CRACK）撞上石板，身体滑行侧卧停住。音效（Audio）：水晶击腿的脆裂声（CRACK），巨手挥来的呼啸声（WHOOSH），沉重重击声（WHUMP），他的矿物质闷哼，身体撞上石板的脆裂声（CRACK）。

镜头 2（3-5 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低角度，聚焦守护者在 Roco 上方举剑，24mm 广角，f/2。高耸的巨像俯视着倒在地上的 Roco，其位置在前景地面层可见。两只巨大的凿刻石手在头顶握住那把巨剑，5 米长的剑身垂直高举至上方最高点。守护者将剑悬于行刑一击，身躯蓄势待劈。音效（Audio）：深沉的石对石握柄咬合声（CLAMP），巨像深沉的红鸣随着行刑意图骤然拔高，殿堂轰鸣。

镜头 3（5-7 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低角度，24mm 广角，f/2。高耸的巨像仍可见高举巨剑于头顶，剑身仍悬于最高点。随后在没有任何外部诱因的情况下，守护者的身躯停下——剑悬停于高点上，身躯僵立，行刑动作被弃。巨像随后自高举位置以深沉碾磨的石声将那把巨剑落向身侧，剑身下落却未劈砍。音效（Audio）：高举的剑悬停，巨剑落向身侧的深沉碾磨石声，巨像深沉的红鸣继续。

镜头 4（7-9 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，对准守护者走开的身躯，50mm，f/1.8。守护者自下躯干至大腿可见，身躯延伸出画框顶部。他开始朝另一个方向行进——沉重而刻意的脚步离开 Roco 的位置，走向僧侣远处岩台的方向，每一步都是撼动殿堂的巨重声（WHUMP）。在前景画框边缘，Roco 可见于石板上，单肘半撑起，带着深沉的矿物质混淆之息看着守护者从他身边走开，他那只带甲的手松松握着剑，剑横放在身旁的石板上。音效（Audio）：渐行渐远脚步的巨重声（WHUMP），深沉的红鸣改变方向，Roco 深沉的矿物质混淆之息，尘土嘶鸣。

PACING（节奏）：9 秒的「行刑-停顿-转身-困惑」。Roco 交战，守护者将他打翻在地（0-3 秒），守护者举剑行刑（3-5 秒），守护者在无外部诱因下停在劈砍中途并收剑（5-7 秒），守护者走向僧侣而 Roco 困惑地注视（7-9 秒）。
