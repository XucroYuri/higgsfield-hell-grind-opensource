## 原文 (source)

Edit the statue's hand pose while keeping everything else identical.
Modify only the hands and arms of the seated stone warrior statue. Remove the sword entirely. Change the hand position to the following gesture:
One hand (palm pressed flat against the chest center) in a vertical namaste/anjali mudra position — fingers pointing upward, palm facing the body, held at the center of the chest.
The other hand held in front of the torso, open palm facing upward, fingers relaxed and slightly curved, positioned at the center of the lap/lower chest area, as if offering or receiving something.
The two hands should be aligned along the vertical center line of the statue — one above (namaste at chest) and one below (open palm at lap), both centered.
Preserve everything else exactly:
Same stone material with weathered grey texture and green moss patches in the same locations
Same armored shoulders, robes, and belt with geometric pattern
Same bald head, stern facial expression, and furrowed brow
Same cross-legged seated pose
Same lighting, camera angle, background, and three-view composition (front view, back view, close-up portrait)
Same sculptural style and proportions
Only the hand/arm positions and the absence of the sword should change. The hands themselves should be carved from the same mossy stone, matching the rest of the statue's material and weathering.

## 中文对照 (zh)

编辑雕像的手部姿势，其余一切保持不变。

只修改这件端坐的石武士雕像的双手与双臂。完全移除长剑。将手部位置改为下述手势：
一只手（掌心平贴于胸中央）呈垂直合十礼（namaste/anjali mudra）手印——手指朝上，掌心朝向身体，保持在胸中央。
另一只手置于躯干前方，张开的手掌朝上，手指放松、微微弯曲，位于膝中央 / 下胸区域，仿佛在奉献或承接某物。
双手应沿雕像的垂直中心线对齐——一只在上（胸前的合十礼），一只在下（膝上的张开掌心），均居于正中。
其余一切完全保持不变：
同样的石材，带有风化的灰色纹理和位于相同位置的绿色苔藓斑块
同样的装甲肩部、长袍与几何图案腰带
同样的光头、严峻的面部表情与紧锁的眉头
同样的盘腿端坐姿势
同样的灯光、机位、背景与三视图构图（正面视图、背面视图、特写肖像）
同样的雕塑风格与比例
只有手/手臂的位置和移除长剑应改变。双手必须用同样的覆有苔藓的石材雕刻，与雕像其余部分的材质和风化程度相匹配。
