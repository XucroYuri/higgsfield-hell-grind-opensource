Stone guardian raises and brings down colossal sword in vertical strike, 5 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300 iconic combat. Photorealistic weathered ancient carved stone with carved script on the blade and deep relief on the body. 60fps, 8K.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

The colossal carved stone SWORD raised vertically in both hands of the guardian — the 5-meter blade with ancient carved script on the surface filling the upper portion of frame, raised at apex above the colossus's head, the body extending up out of frame top. The sword held at apex for a beat. Then the colossus DROPS the sword in a powerful vertical strike — both stone arms swinging the blade downward fast through the chamber air, the colossal blade plunging with massive momentum toward the floor below. The blade exits frame bottom heading toward the ground with thunderous WHOOSH. Audio: deep stone-on-stone CLAMP of grip, the colossus's deep red hum spiking with intent, then massive WHOOSH of the colossal blade descending at speed, chamber rumble.

PACING: 5-second vertical sword strike from extreme low-angle.
