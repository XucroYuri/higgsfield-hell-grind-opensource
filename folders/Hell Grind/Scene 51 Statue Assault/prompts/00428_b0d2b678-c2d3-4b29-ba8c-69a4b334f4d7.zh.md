## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco slides under guardian's foot, guardian raises sword to strike, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Snyder's 300, Villeneuve's Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient from above, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient stone. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading with each guardian action.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, scale through extreme low-angles where body parts fill or exceed vertical frame.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle tracking Roco's slide, 35mm, f/2. Operator handheld at floor level. Roco visible in foreground sliding low across flagstone, body horizontal, ruby crystal armor scraping with sparks. He slides UNDER the guardian's massive carved stone FOOT in mid-blur — the foot taller than Roco's full standing body, looming above his sliding form, the colossal toes the size of his torso passing above. The carved stone leg extends upward out of frame top. Audio: SCRAPE of crystal on flagstone, sparks, his held breath, the colossal foot's deep stone presence above.

Shot 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle further from the guardian, 35mm wider lens, f/2. Operator at floor level. Roco visible in foreground rolling out of his slide and coming up on one knee, sword raised. Behind him in shallow blur, the guardian's body extends upward filling the entire vertical frame — the calves, thighs, lower torso visible, the upper body and head out of frame top, scale established. The colossus's massive carved stone ARM is visible mid-frame REACHING UP across his back, his stone hand gripping the hilt of the colossal greatsword sheathed across his shoulders. The guardian DRAWS THE SWORD — the five-meter blade emerging from the back-scabbard with deep stone-on-stone grinding rasp, the immense blade rising into the air above and behind the colossus. Roco watches from below, body tensing. Audio: deep stone-on-stone GRIND of the sword drawing from scabbard, dust cascading from the blade's emergence, his held breath, the colossus's deep red hum spiking.

Shot 3 (10-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on Roco at floor level looking up, 35mm, f/2. Operator at floor level. Roco visible in foreground crouched, sword and shield raised. High above him, the guardian's COLOSSAL FIVE-METER STONE BLADE is now raised overhead in the colossus's stone grip — the blade visible at frame top vertically, the script-etched ancient stone catching cold ambient light, the tip near the cavern ceiling, the blade's mass dominating the upper portion of frame. The blade BEGINS to descend — the colossus starting the swing downward toward Roco, the blade tilting forward through frame, mass and momentum building. Roco reacts fast, raising shield to brace. Audio: the deep WHOOSH of the colossal blade beginning its arc through air, the rising rumble of its mass, his sharp inhale, chamber tremor.

PACING: 15-second escalation. Slide under foot (0-5s), guardian draws colossal sword (5-10s), guardian begins downward swing (10-15s — cliffhanger before impact, leaves room for next shot to handle the strike).

## 中文对照 (zh)

水晶装甲的 Roco 从守护巨人的脚下滑过，守护巨人举剑欲击，15 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影，变形宽银幕电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Snyder《斯巴达三百勇士》、Villeneuve《沙丘·第二部》。Lighting（灯光）：上方惨白冷光，守护巨人眼窝与 Roco 装甲的饱和红光，温暖琥珀色油灯。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰为主，主导饱和红，重度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实半透红宝石水晶、饱经风化的古石。Continuity（连贯性）锁定。绝不正面——四边形取景。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig，剧烈抖动，身体驱动的跟随。

CHAMBER TREMOR（洞室震颤）：剧烈摇晃，守护巨人的每次动作都让天花板碎石倾泻。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护巨人尺度）：20 米高，通过极低角度确立尺度，身体部位填满或超出垂直画框。

引用图 1 中那名全身包覆半透明红宝石色刻面水晶装甲的男人，他肩部有翼状尖刺、前臂有刺、锯齿状护颈遮蔽面容。

Setting（设定）：洞窟圣殿，尘雾震颤。

Shot 1（0-5s）：四边形手持极低角度，跟随 Roco 的滑之动作，35mm，f/2。摄影师手持在地面层。Roco 在前景可见，横身滑过低矮的石板，红宝石水晶装甲刮擦出火星。他从中位虚化中滑过守护巨人巨大的雕纹石脚下——石脚比立立的 Roco 全身还高，悬立在他滑过的身形之上，巨趾大若他的躯干、自上方掠过。雕纹石腿向上延伸出画面顶部。Audio（音效）：水晶刮擦石板的声响、火星、他屏住的呼吸、上方石脚深沉的石化存在感。

Shot 2（5-10s）：切至四边形手持极低角度，离守护巨人更远，35mm 更广镜，f/2。摄影师在地面层。Roco 在前景可见，从滑姿中滚出、单膝撑起，长剑高举。他身后浅虚化中守护巨人的身体向上延伸、填满整幅垂直画面——小腿、大腿、下躯干可见，上身与头部在画面上方出画，尺度得以确立。巨像巨大的雕纹石臂在画面中部可见，绕其背部向上探出，石手握着横挎于肩背的巨刃劈砍剑的剑柄。守护巨人拔出巨剑——五米长的锋刃自后背剑鞘而出，发出深沉的石触石碾磨声，巨刃升入巨像上方与身后的长空中。Roco 从下方望着，身体绷紧。Audio（音效）：巨剑出鞘时深沉的石触石碾磨声、锋刃现世时尘埃簌簌洒落、他屏住的呼吸、巨人身畔飙升的深红嗡鸣。

Shot 3（10-15s）：切至四边形手持极低角度，地面层的 Roco 自下仰视，35mm，f/2。摄影师在地面层。Roco 在前景可见、蹲伏，剑盾高举。在他高处的守护巨人。手中，五米长的巨大石刃如今高举当空——巨刃在画面上方竖直可见，镌着铭文的古石在惨白冷光下清晰可辨，刃尖即将触及洞窟顶部，巨刃的体量笼罩着画面上半部。巨刃开始下落——巨像朝着 Roco 挥刃向下，巨刃穿过画面向前倾压，体量与势头不断累积。Roco 反应神速，举盾格挡。Audio（音效）：巨刃凌空开始劈弧时深沉的呼呼声、其庞大体量渐起的轰鸣、他锐利的吸气、洞室震颤。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒的步步升级。从脚下滑过（0-5s），守护巨人拔出巨剑（5-10s），守护巨人开始下劈（10-15s——命中前的悬念，为下一镜处理这一击留下空间）。
