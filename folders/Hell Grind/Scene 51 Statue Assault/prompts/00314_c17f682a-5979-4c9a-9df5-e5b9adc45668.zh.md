Jax behind carved stone column retches mini plastid bombs and fires slingshot at guardian, 10 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

Single shot (0-10s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Jax behind a carved stone column, 85mm, f/1.4. Jax in cover, dust-streaked, glasses on, classic Y-frame wooden slingshot in his left hand, body crouched.

At 0-3.5s: Jax cups his right palm in front of his open mouth. His throat contracts briefly — he RETCHES a small grey plastid pellet the size of a walnut into his palm, smaller than his usual bombs. The pellet WHISTLES off-frame, distant BOOM of small detonation echoes back. He mutters: "...one..." Audio: light retch, slap of pellet into palm, elastic SNAP, pellet whistle off-frame, small distant BOOM, his muttered word.

At 3.5-7s: He retches a SECOND pellet — quick, lighter effort than for larger bombs. Loads, draws, fires. Another distant small BOOM off-frame. His muttered: "...two..." His face concentrated, the rhythm building. The guardian's deep red hum continues unwavering off-frame, no reaction to either impact. Audio: retch, slap, snap, whistle, BOOM, his muttered word.

At 7-10s: Third pellet — retch, palm, load, fire. Third small BOOM off-frame. Jax mutters: "...three..." He continues immediately to retch a fourth pellet, prepping it in his palm. The guardian still unreactive in deep blur. His face tightening with the realization that the small pellets aren't doing damage. Audio: retch-slap-snap-whistle-BOOM cycle, the colossus's deep red hum continuing unbroken, his muttered word, the start of the next retch.

PACING: 10-second three-pellet cycle. First retch-load-fire (0-3.5s), second cycle (3.5-7s), third cycle and start of fourth (7-10s).
