## 原文 (source)

Jax behind carved stone column retches and shapes large plastid bomb, 10 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient, warm amber oil-lamps trembling on the column, distant saturated red from off-frame guardian, distant flashes of off-frame combat. Color: deep cool blue-grey, accent warm amber from lamps, distant red wash. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic skin, fabric, weathered ancient carved stone with moss, grey plastid material with rough hand-formed wet texture. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld at chest height close on Jax, body-driven, frame trembling.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling, dust drifting.

Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, weathered ancient carved stone column with moss in deep crevices, dust haze.

Single shot (0-10s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Jax crouched behind a carved stone column, 85mm, f/1.4. Jax pressed against the carved stone column for cover, dust-streaked, glasses gripped, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark jacket dust-covered, body crouched low.

At 0-3s: Jax cups his right palm in front of his open mouth. His throat and chest CONTRACT visibly — neck cords tightening, body heaving with effort — and he RETCHES a fully-formed grey plastid ball out from his mouth into his waiting palm. The plastid ball lands wet, fist-sized. He breathes hard from the effort, recovers. He immediately begins to retch AGAIN — chest contracting, more effort — and a second plastid ball comes up into his palm joining the first. He kneads the two together with his fingers, pressing them into a single larger mass. Audio: his retching sounds from chest and throat, wet slaps of plastid into palm, his strained breath, distant chamber rumble, distant off-frame combat sounds — guardian's deep red hum, crystal-on-stone CRACKS far away.

At 3-7s: He retches a THIRD plastid ball, then a FOURTH — both joining the growing mass in his palm. The bomb is now too large for one hand — he uses both hands to shape it, kneading and compressing, the grey wet mass becoming a dense fist-and-a-half-sized lump. His face is concentrated, focused, hands working the material into a tight ball. He mutters through teeth: "...special for you, big guy... Sweat on his brow from the repeated retching effort. Audio: continued retching cycles, wet plastid sounds, his concentrated breath, his muttered words, distant combat continuing.

At 7-10s: Jax retches a FIFTH and final plastid ball — the largest yet — and forces it into the mass. He kneads the now-massive plastid bomb between both hands with focused effort, compressing it into a dense round mass approximately the size of a small melon. Wet grey surface, rough texture from his hand-shaping. He holds it up briefly to inspect — his glasses reflecting the saturated red glow from off-frame. He nods grimly to himself. Audio: final retching effort, the plastid being compressed audibly with wet squelches, his recovered breath, his muttered word: "...try this on..."

PACING: 10-second bomb assembly. First two retches and combine (0-3s), third and fourth additions, kneading begins (3-7s), final fifth piece, final compression and inspection (7-10s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

Jax 躲在石刻石柱后干呕，捏出一枚大型塑胶炸弹（plastid bomb），10 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影，变形宽银幕电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 胶片颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》(Children of Men)、Villeneuve《沙丘2》(Dune Part Two)。Lighting（灯光）：冷调苍白环境光，石柱上暖琥珀色油灯摇曳，画面外守护者远处传来饱和红光，画面外战斗的远处闪光。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰色，以油灯为主的暖琥珀色点缀，远处红晕。重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实的皮肤、织物、带青苔的风化刻石，灰色塑胶材料带粗糙手塑的湿润肌理。连贯性锁定。绝不正对——四分之三角度。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持，位于胸口高度贴近 Jax，由身体驱动，画面颤动。

CHAMBER TREMOR（厅室震颤）：剧烈晃动，碎屑坠落，尘土飘荡。

参照 Image 1 中留黄色寸头、深色皮肤、无框眼镜、深色夹克的男人。

Setting（场景设定）：洞窟圣殿，深裂缝中带青苔的风化刻石石柱，尘雾。

单镜头（0-10s）：四分之三手持中近景，拍摄蹲在石刻石柱后的 Jax，85mm，f/1.4。Jax 贴紧石刻石柱借以掩护，满面尘土，攥着眼镜，留黄色寸头，深色夹克覆尘，身躯蜷低蹲伏。

0-3s：Jax 将右掌弓起置于微张的嘴前。他的喉与胸可见地收缩（CONTRACT）——颈部筋脉绷紧，身躯因用力而起伏——他干呕（RETCHES）出一枚成型完整的灰色塑胶球，从口中落入等待的掌中。塑胶球湿漉漉地落下，拳头大小。他因用力而粗喘，缓过气来。他立刻再次干呕（AGAIN）——胸部收缩，更加用力——第二枚塑胶球上涌落入掌中，与第一枚汇合。他用手指将两枚揉在一起，压成一大团。Audio（声音）：他自胸至喉的干呕声，塑胶落入手掌的湿响，他紧绷的呼吸，远处厅室隆隆，远处画面外的战斗声——守护者低沉红光嗡鸣、远处水晶对石 CRACK 碎裂声。

3-7s：他干呕出第三枚塑胶球，随后第四枚——二者都汇入掌中不断增大的那团。如今这枚炸弹大得单手难以握住——他改用双手塑形、揉捏挤压，灰湿的胶团变成一个紧实的、一个半拳头大小的疙瘩。他神情专注、凝神，双手把材料收拾成一枚紧实的圆球。他咬着牙咕哝：“……专门为你准备的，大块头……” 一次又一次干呕的用力让他额头沁出汗水。Audio（声音）：持续的干呕循环，湿黏的塑胶声，他屏息的呼吸，他咕哝的话语，远处战斗仍在继续。

7-10s：Jax 干呕出第五枚、也是最后一枚塑胶球——迄今最大的一枚——并将其揉进胶团。他以专注的用力在双手之间揉捏这枚如今大得惊人的塑胶炸弹，将其压实成大约小甜瓜大小的紧实圆团。湿灰的表面，手塑的粗糙纹理。他短暂举起查看——他的眼镜反射出画面外传来的饱和红光。他对自己沉着脸点了点头。Audio（声音）：最后一次干呕的用力，塑胶被压实发出可闻的湿响，他缓过来的呼吸，他咕哝的一句话：“……试试这个……”

PACING（节奏）：10 秒的炸弹组装。前两次干呕并合并（0-3s），第三、四次加入，开始揉捏（3-7s），最后第五枚，最终压实与查看（7-10s）。
