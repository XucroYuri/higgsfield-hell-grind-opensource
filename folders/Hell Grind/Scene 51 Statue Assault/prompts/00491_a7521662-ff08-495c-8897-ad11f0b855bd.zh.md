## 原文 (source)

The statue's body is covered in a NETWORK OF FRESH CRACKS that have just opened across the stone — fine hairline fractures branching like lightning across the chest plate, the shoulders, the carved armor, the cupped hands, the knees, the face. Some cracks are thin filaments, others are wider splits where stone fragments have shaken loose. Dust drifts visibly from the cracks. Small stone shards lie at the base of the pedestal. The cracks are real fracture mechanics — branching, propagating from stress points, asymmetric, organic. INSIDE EVERY CRACK, a deep saturated INCANDESCENT RED LIGHT pours outward from within the stone — as if the statue's body contains a furnace. The red glow is the only fully-saturated color in the image. The cracks illuminate the surrounding stone surface with warm red rim light. The red light pulses faintly with implied breath. The deeper cracks glow brighter, the hairline fractures glow dimmer — depth of glow varies with crack width. THE EYES ARE THE FOCAL POINT. Both stone eye-sockets have CRACKED OPEN — the carved stone eyelids fractured along their seams, split open. What is revealed in each socket is NOT an eye. There is no eyeball. No pupil. No iris. No sclera. No whites. No visible eye structure of any kind. Each socket is a HOLLOW CAVITY filled with pure incandescent saturated red light pouring outward as if from a furnace inside the statue's skull. The light is featureless, smooth, uniform red glow filling each socket — no shape behind it, no form, just SOLID RED LIGHT FROM EMPTINESS. The red light from each empty socket casts visible beams through atmospheric dust haze as saturated red shafts cutting through the air. The empty glowing sockets are deeply unsettling — the statue has awakened but there is nothing alive looking out, only pure light pouring from a hollow inside the skull.

## 中文对照 (zh)

雕像的身躯覆满一层刚刚在石面打开的崭新裂纹（NETWORK OF FRESH CRACKS）——细腻的发丝状裂缝如闪电般在胸甲、双肩、雕饰的铠甲、捧合的双手、膝盖与面容之上分叉。有些裂纹是细丝，另一些是更宽的裂口，石片已在其中震松。尘土可从裂纹中肉眼可见地飘离。小石屑散落在台座基部。裂纹遵循真实的断裂力学——分叉、自应力点扩展、不对称、有机。在每一条裂纹内（INSIDE EVERY CRACK），一种深饱和、白炽的红色光（INCANDESCENT RED LIGHT）自石料内部倾泻而出——仿佛雕像体内藏着一座熔炉。红色内光是画面中唯一完全饱和的颜色。裂纹以暖红色边缘光映亮周围的石面。红光以隐约的呼吸感微微脉动。更深的裂纹更亮，发丝状裂纹较暗——光的浓度随裂纹宽度而变化。双眼是焦点（THE EYES ARE THE FOCAL POINT）。两只石质眼窝都已裂开（CRACKED OPEN）——雕成的石质眼皮沿接缝碎裂、劈开。每一只眼窝中显露的并非眼睛（NOT an eye）。没有眼球。没有瞳孔。没有虹膜。没有巩膜。没有眼白。没有任何可见的眼球结构。每一只眼窝都是一个空心的凹陷（HOLLOW CAVITY），填满纯白炽的饱和红光倾泻而出，仿佛来自雕像头骨内的一座熔炉。这光毫无特征、平滑、均匀的红色内光填满每一只眼窝——其背后没有形状，没有形体，只有自空洞（EMPTINESS）而来的固态红光（SOLID RED LIGHT）。自每一只空洞眼窝射出的红光以饱合红色光束穿越大气尘雾，在空气中切割出可光的光柱。空洞发光的眼窝令人深深不安——雕像已然苏醒，但注视着外面的并不是任何活物，唯有纯粹的光自头骨内的空洞涌出。
