Stone fragment strikes massive bronze bell, the bell rings, guardian freezes, 10 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Villeneuve's Dune Part Two ritual moments. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets in deep blurred background dimming during the freeze. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red dimming. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic ancient bronze bell with patina and verdigris, iron chains, weathered ancient carved stone face. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, body-driven, EXTREME trembling SUBSIDING during the freeze moment.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking SUBSIDES dramatically as the guardian freezes.

Setting: upper chamber space of the cavern-sanctum, the massive bronze ritual bell suspended on iron chains, the guardian's massive carved stone face visible in deep blurred background lower in the chamber.

Shot 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on the bronze bell at the moment of impact, 85mm, f/1.4. The stone fragment STRIKES the lower curve of the massive bronze bell — sharp impact, the fragment shattering on contact and falling away in pieces. The bell's bronze surface vibrates visibly at the impact point. Then the bell BEGINS TO RESONATE — a deep enormous bronze TONE rings out, the bell's surface trembling with the resonance, dust shaking off its surface in waves. The bell's deep tone fills the chamber with a single sustained note that overwhelms all other sound. The iron chains supporting the bell tremble visibly with the resonance. The bell's bronze patina catches the cold ambient light from above. The note sustains, deep and pure. Audio: the sharp metallic-on-bronze IMPACT of the fragment striking the bell, then the COLOSSAL BRONZE BELL RING — deep, enormous, sustained, filling and dominating the chamber, drowning out everything else.

Shot 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on the guardian's carved stone face in lower chamber, 100mm, f/1.4. The empty red eye-sockets pouring saturated red light. As the bell's tone fills the chamber, the guardian FREEZES — the head stops moving mid-motion, the red glow from the eye-sockets FLICKERS once, dimming briefly, then steadying at lowered intensity. The carved stone face holds rigid, paralyzed by the sound. The chamber's tremor subsides dramatically as the colossus stops moving — relative quiet falls, only the bell's sustained ring filling the air. Audio: the bell's sustained ring continuing through the cut, the chamber's rumble subsiding, the guardian's deep red hum dropping in volume, sudden relative quiet under the bell's sustained tone, the monk's distant throat-tone audible from above harmonizing with the bell.

PACING: 10-second impact and freeze. Fragment hits bell, bell rings (0-5s), guardian freezes, chamber goes quiet (5-10s).

石块碎片击中庞大青铜钟，钟声响起，守护者僵住，10 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE：8K IMAX 写实摄影，anamorphic 电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Villeneuve 的《沙丘2》仪式时刻。灯光：冷白环境光，来自深模糊背景中守护者眼窝的饱和红光在僵住时刻逐渐转暗。色彩：深冷蓝灰色，主导饱和红变暗。高度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实的古青铜钟，带锈斑与铜绿，铁链，风化的古老刻石面孔。连贯性锁定。绝不用正面——用四分之三角度。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig，身体驱动，极剧烈颤抖在僵住时刻渐次平息。

CHAMBER TREMOR（殿堂震颤）：剧烈摇晃在守护者僵住时显著平息。

场景：洞穴圣堂的上层空间，庞大青铜仪式钟悬挂于铁链上，守护者巨大的刻石面孔在下方殿堂的深模糊背景中可见。

Shot 1（0-5s）：四分之三手持中近景于青铜钟撞击瞬间，85mm，f/1.4。石块碎片击中庞大青铜钟的下缘曲线——锐利撞击，碎片接触即碎裂并在碎片中散落。钟的青铜表面在撞击点可见地振动。随后钟开始共振——一道深沉的庞然青铜音响起，钟表面随共振震颤，尘土呈波状从其表面震落。钟的深沉音以一道持续的单一音符灌满整个殿堂，压倒其他一切声响。支撑钟的铁链在共振中可见地颤动。钟的青铜锈斑捕捉上方的冷白环境光。音符持续，深沉而纯净。音频：碎片击钟的锐利金属撞青铜 IMPACT，随后的是巨量青铜钟响——深沉、庞然、持续，填满并主导整个殿堂，将其余一切淹没。

Shot 2（5-10s）：切至四分之三手持极特写于下方殿堂的守护者刻石面孔，100mm，f/1.4。空洞的红眼窝倾泻出饱和红光。当钟声灌满殿堂，守护者僵住——头部在半空中停住，眼窝的红色辉光闪烁一次，短暂变暗，随后以降低的强度稳定下来。刻石面孔僵持不动，被声音麻痹。当巨躯停止移动，殿堂的震颤显著平息——相对的寂静降临，只有钟的持续鸣响充盈空气。音频：钟的持续鸣响贯穿切换，殿堂轰鸣渐息，守护者的深沉红嗡低声下消。钟的持续音之下的突发相对寂静，上方僧侣的远距离喉音与钟声相和。

PACING：10 秒的击钟与僵住。碎片击中钟，钟鸣响（0-5s），守护者僵住，殿堂归于寂静（5-10s）。
