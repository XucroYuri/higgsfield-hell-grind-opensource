## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco parries the colossal stone sword with crystal shield, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Snyder's 300, Villeneuve's Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient from above, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets above out of frame and from Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient stone. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, EXTREME trembling, body-driven movement tracking violent impacts.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling. Roco crystal-armored facing the guardian's incoming colossal carved stone sword.

Shot 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco from the side, 50mm, f/1.8. Operator handheld at chest height. Roco's armored body visible in three-quarter side view, sword and shield raised. In deep blurred background the guardian's COLOSSAL CARVED STONE SWORD descends through frame from above — the massive blade five meters long arcing down toward Roco, its weathered carved stone surface and ancient script catching cold ambient light, tip moving fast through dust haze. Roco braces, raises ruby crystal shield up to meet the strike. Audio: the WHOOSH of the colossal blade descending through air, the rising rumble of its mass, Roco's sharp held breath, chamber tremor.

Shot 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on the impact, 85mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on the moment the colossal stone blade meets Roco's ruby crystal SHIELD with a thunderous CRACK — massive saturated red FLASH at the impact point, ruby crystal facets of the shield CRACKING from the force, several fragments breaking off the shield's edge and flying outward, the shield holding overall but visibly damaged, the entire impact zone glowing saturated red with the energy. Roco's armored body forced down to one knee from the force, the stone sword pressing against the shield. His mineral resonance growl audible behind the crystal collar with effort. Sparks, debris, dust eruption. Audio: thunderous CRACK of stone-on-crystal impact, the SHATTER of crystal fragments, his effortful growl, the sustained pressure GROAN of the colossal sword pressing on the shield.

Shot 3 (10-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco recovering from the parry, 85mm, f/1.4. He pushes the colossal sword off his shield with a final shove — the blade lifts away, the colossus drawing it back. Roco rises from his knee, ruby crystal shield now visibly cracked along its edge with several fragments missing, the shield still functional but weakened. He shifts his stance, sword and damaged shield raised, breathing hard with mineral resonance through sealed armor. The guardian's leg in deep blur as the colossus repositions. Audio: the GRINDING release of the colossal sword lifting away, his heavy mineral breathing, chamber rumble, the colossus's deep red hum repositioning.

PACING: 15-second parry sequence. Sword incoming (0-5s), impact and shield holds with damage (5-10s), recovery with damaged shield (10-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

水晶装甲的 Roco 以水晶盾格挡住巨大的石剑，15 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影，变形宽银幕电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Snyder《斯巴达三百勇士》、Villeneuve《沙丘·第二部》。Lighting（灯光）：上方惨白冷光，守护巨人画面上方出画的眼窝红光、以及 Roco 红宝石水晶装甲的饱和红光，温暖琥珀色油灯。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰为主，主导饱和红，重度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实半透红宝石水晶、饱经风化的古石。Continuity（连贯性）锁定。绝不正面——四边形取景。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig，剧烈抖动，跟踪猛烈撞击的身体驱动移动。

CHAMBER TREMOR（洞室震颤）：剧烈摇晃，碎石坠落。

引用图 1 中那名全身包覆半透明红宝石色刻面水晶装甲的男人，他肩部有翼状尖刺、前臂有刺、锯齿状护颈遮蔽面容。

Setting（设定）：洞窟圣殿，尘雾震颤。Roco 水晶装甲正面迎向守护巨人挥来的巨大雕纹石剑。

Shot 1（0-5s）：四边形手持中景近景，从侧面拍 Roco，50mm，f/1.8。摄影师手持在胸口高度。Roco 的装甲身体从四边形侧面可见，剑盾高举。深虚化的背景中守护巨人巨大的雕纹石剑自上方穿入画面——五米长的巨刃划弧劈向 Roco，刃面上饱经风化的雕纹石与古老铭文捕捉着惨白冷光，刃尖高速穿过尘雾。Roco 稳住身形，举起红宝石水晶盾迎向这一击。Audio（音效）：巨刃凌空劈下的呼呼声、其庞大体量渐起的轰鸣、Roco 屏住的锐利呼吸、洞室震颤。

Shot 2（5-10s）：切至四边形手持极特写，撞击瞬间，85mm，f/1.4。极浅对焦在巨大石刃撞上 Roco 红宝石水晶盾、发出雷霆般碎裂声的那一刻——撞击点迸出漫天的饱和红闪光，盾面的红宝石水晶刻面因受力而开裂，多枚碎片自盾缘断裂飞出，盾整体撑住了、但可见受损，整片撞击区域被能量映得饱和通红。Roco 的装甲身体被力道压至单膝跪地，石剑抵住盾面。水晶护颈后隐约传出他拼尽全力的矿物共鸣低吼。火星、碎石、尘爆。Audio（音效）：石击水晶时雷霆般的碎裂声、水晶碎片的碎裂迸飞声、他用力的低吼、巨剑压盾时持续的受压呻吟。

Shot 3（10-15s）：切至四边形手持中景近景，Roco 自格挡中恢复，85mm，f/1.4。他最后猛地一推把巨剑从盾上卸开——巨刃抬起离去，巨像将其收回。Roco 自跪地中起身，红宝石水晶盾如今沿缘可见开裂、缺了数枚碎片，盾仍堪用但已受削弱。他调整架势，剑与受损的盾高举，隔着封闭装甲以矿物共鸣剧烈喘息。深虚化中守护巨人的巨腿随着巨像重新站位而移动。Audio（音效）：巨剑抬起离去的碾磨释放声、他沉重的矿物业呼吸、洞室轰鸣、巨像重新站位的深红嗡鸣。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒的格挡序列。巨剑劈至（0-5s），撞击、盾带损撑住（5-10s），带损盾下的恢复（10-15s）。
