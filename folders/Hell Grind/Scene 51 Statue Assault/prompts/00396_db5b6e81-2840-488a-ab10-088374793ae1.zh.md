Jax fires multiple plastid bombs from slingshot in rapid succession, guardian flinches, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

Photorealistic skin, fabric, weathered ancient carved stone, classic Y-frame slingshot, grey plastid material. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig at chest height. EXTREME trembling.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall. Scale through the colossal carved leg in deep blur reacting to detonations, the leg the size of a building.

Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-7s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Jax firing in rapid succession from behind a column, 85mm, f/1.4. Jax in cover, dust-streaked, glasses focused. He moves fast — bite, spit into palm, load slingshot, draw, release — first plastid ball LAUNCHES out of frame with elastic SNAP. He immediately repeats — bite, spit, load, draw, release — second ball LAUNCHES. Then third — bite, spit, load, draw, release — third ball launches. The rhythm is mechanical and fast, his hands working in practiced sequence, muttering through teeth: "...one, two, three, fucker..." Audio: three rapid sequences of bite-spit-snap-launch, his fast breath, his muttered count, plastid balls whistling off-frame.

Shot 2 (7-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on the guardian's massive carved stone leg, 35mm wider lens, f/2. Operator at floor level looking up. The three plastid balls IMPACT the leg in rapid succession — three bright orange-yellow FLASHES blooming on the carved stone surface in quick rhythm, each detonation producing dust eruption, shrapnel, smoke clouds. The third detonation hits a vulnerable point — at the back of the knee joint — and the leg visibly SHIFTS, the colossus taking a small backstep to absorb the cumulative force, the entire leg moving laterally with a deep grinding sound. Dust cascades heavily from the impacts and the step. Audio: three rapid BOOMS in succession with chamber reverb, dust eruptions, shrapnel pinging on stone, the guardian's deep grinding step backward, debris cascading.

PACING: 15-second machine-gun fire. Three rapid bomb launches from slingshot (0-7s), three detonations on guardian's leg, colossus takes a small backstep (7-15s).

STYLE：8K IMAX 写实摄影，anamorphic 电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Cuarón 的《人类之子》。灯光：冷白环境光，来自画外守护者的饱和红作为暖洗光，温暖琥珀色油灯，多次爆炸的橙黄闪光。色彩：深冷蓝灰色，主导红，点缀琥珀色与橙色闪光。高度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。写实的皮肤、布料、风化的古老刻石、经典 Y 形弹弓、灰色 plastid 材质。连贯性锁定。绝不用正面——用四分之三角度。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig 于胸口高度。极剧烈颤抖。

CHAMBER TREMOR（殿堂震颤）：剧烈摇晃，碎屑落下。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者尺度）：20 米高。通过在深模糊背景中因爆炸而反应的庞大刻石腿体现尺度，这条腿足有一栋建筑大小。

参照 Image 1 中那名漂白黄发寸头、深色皮肤、透明眼镜、深色夹克的男人。

场景：洞穴圣堂，尘雾震颤。

Shot 1（0-7s）：四分之三手持中近景于柱后快速连射的 Jax，85mm，f/1.4。Jax 藏身掩护，尘土斑驳，眼镜专注。他动作飞快——咬一口、往掌心啐、装弹入弹弓、拉弦、释放——第一枚 plastid 球随弹性绳 SNAP 射出画外。他立刻重复——咬、啐、装、拉、放——第二枚射出。接着第三枚——咬、啐、装、拉、放——第三枚弹出。节奏机械而快速，双手以熟练序列运作，透过牙缝低声数着：「……一、二、三，去你的……」音频：三组快速的咬啐啪放序列，他急促的呼吸，他低声的数数，plastid 球在画外啸鸣。

Shot 2（7-15s）：切至四分之三手持极低机位拍摄守护者庞大的刻石腿，35mm 更广镜头，f/2。摄影师在地面高度仰视。庞大刻石腿自上填满整幅垂直画面并延伸出画面上缘，远处画外的空洞红眼窝向下倾泻饱和红光。三枚 plastid 球快速连续击中腿部——三道明亮的橙黄 FLASH 在刻石表面以疾速节拍绽放，每次爆炸都产生尘爆、弹片、烟云。第三次爆炸击中一处脆弱点——膝盖后方关节——腿部可见地移动，巨躯踏出一小步以吸收累积的力道，整条腿发出一声低沉碾磨移动。粉尘自冲击与脚步重重倾泻。音频：三声带殿堂回响的连续 BOOM，尘爆，弹片叮叮打在石上，守护者退步的沉重碾磨声，碎屑倾泻。

PACING：15 秒的机枪式射击。自弹弓快速三连发射（0-7s），三次爆炸落于守护者腿部，巨躯踏出一小步（7-15s）。
