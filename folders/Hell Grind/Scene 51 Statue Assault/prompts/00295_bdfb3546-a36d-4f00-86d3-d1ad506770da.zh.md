## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco and stone guardian exchange sword strikes, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300 close kinetic combat. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor (chromatic kinship), warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient carved stone with script. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld throughout, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking matching combat. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-4s): Three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle, 35mm, f/2. Roco crystal-armored in foreground lunges forward with ruby crystal sword, swinging upward in a diagonal arc toward the guardian's leg. The colossal stone leg fills the deep frame above. Sword strikes the carved stone of the leg with deafening CRACK and saturated red flash, dust eruption. Audio: Roco's mineral grunt, crystal-on-stone CRACK, dust hissing, chamber rumble.

Shot 2 (4-8s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco from side, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco recovers from the strike, stepping back into defensive stance, sword and shield raised. The colossal stone SWORD descends from above into the frame — 5-meter blade plunging vertically toward Roco. He raises his ruby crystal SHIELD upward to meet it. The stone blade IMPACTS the shield with thunderous CRACK and saturated red flash, dust eruption. Roco's body forced down by the impact. Audio: WHOOSH of the descending blade, his sharp inhale, the THUNDEROUS CRACK of impact, the shield groaning under pressure.

Shot 3 (8-12s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on the impact zone, 100mm, f/1.4. Razor-thin focus on the colossal stone blade pressing down on Roco's ruby shield — the ruby crystal of the shield CRACKS visibly in branching fracture lines from the contact point, dust streaming, the ancient carved stone of the blade weathered and massive. The pressure sustains. Audio: sustained PRESSURE GROAN of stone on crystal, dust hissing, his strained mineral breath.

Shot 4 (12-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco breaking the bind, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco PUSHES the shield upward with effort, throwing the colossal blade off — the stone blade lifts away from his shield. Roco immediately swings his crystal sword in a counter-strike at the colossus's leg in deep blur — saturated red flash on impact, sparks of dust, his mineral grunt of effort. Audio: the GRINDING release of the stone blade, his mineral roar of counter-attack, the CRACK of his strike, chamber rumble.

PACING: 15-second four-shot dueling exchange. Roco strikes leg (0-4s), guardian counters with overhead sword, Roco blocks with shield (4-8s), sustained pressure on shield in close-up (8-12s), Roco breaks bind and counter-strikes (12-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

水晶装甲的 Roco 与石质守护者互换剑击，15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》、Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》、Snyder《300 勇士》近距离激烈格斗。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 红宝石水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光（色彩同源），暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导饱和红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶、风化古老雕花石面带铭文。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：全程肩扛手持，极度颤抖，以身体驱动的跟拍贴合战斗节奏。绝不正面——保持四分之三角度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，天花板碎片纷纷坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，5 米长的利剑。

参见这名男子：完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），尘雾在震颤。

Shot 1（0-4 秒）：四分之三手持极低机位，35mm，f/2。前景中水晶装甲的 Roco 以红宝石水晶剑向前扑出，朝守护者的腿挥出一道斜向上升的弧线。巨大的石腿在深处虚化中占满上方画面。剑以震耳欲聋的 CRACK 与饱和红光一闪劈中腿上的雕花石面，尘土迸发。音频：Roco 矿物般的闷哼、水晶撞石的 CRACK、尘土嘶嘶作响、密殿隆隆。

Shot 2（4-8 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，从侧面对准 Roco，50mm，f/1.8。Roco 自这一击中恢复，撤步回防架势，剑与盾高举。巨大的石质 SWORD 自上方降入画面——5 米长的剑刃垂直朝 Roco 直落。他高举红宝石水晶 SHIELD 迎上去。石刃劈中盾面，发出雷鸣般的 CRACK 与饱和红光一闪、尘土迸发。Roco 的身体被这一击压矮。音频：剑刃降下的 WHOOSH、他尖锐的吸气、雷鸣般的撞击 CRACK、盾在重压下嘎吱作响。

Shot 3（8-12 秒）：切至四分之三手持极特写，对准撞击区，100mm，f/1.4。极薄焦点落在这柄巨刃压着 Roco 的宝石红盾上——盾的红宝石水晶在触点处以多线分叉的纹路明显 CRACKS 开裂、尘土流泻、剑刃上古老雕花的石块风化而厚重。压力持续。音频：石头压水晶持续的 PRESSURE GROAN（受压呻吟）、尘土嘶嘶作响、他勉力呼吸的矿物气息。

Shot 4（12-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，Roco 挣脱钳制，50mm，f/1.8。Roco 用力将盾向上一顶、把巨刃甩开——石刃自他的盾面上抬起移开。Roco 随即挥动他的水晶剑，朝深虚化中巨像的腿发起一记反攻——落地时饱和红光一闪、扬起尘土火花、他用力时矿物般的闷哼。音频：石刃脱开时嘎吱的释放、他反攻的矿物咆哮、他一击中的 CRACK、密殿隆隆。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒四镜头对剑互换。Roco 砍腿（0-4 秒），守护者以上方剑还击、Roco 举盾格挡（4-8 秒），盾上压力持续的特写（8-12 秒），Roco 挣脱钳制并反攻（12-15 秒）。
