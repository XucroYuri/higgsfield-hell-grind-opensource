## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco fallen on floor, stone guardian stomps and kicks, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with combat damage cracks, weathered ancient carved stone. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld at floor level mostly, EXTREME trembling. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking INTENSIFYING with each guardian movement, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, foot taller than Roco's body.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, flagstone floor with combat damage, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-4s): Three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on Roco crawling on flagstone, 35mm, f/2. Roco crystal-armored on his side, struggling to push himself up, armor cracked across the chest and shoulders, ruby crystal sword still in his right hand but loose, dust covering him. He pushes himself up slowly with mineral effort. Above him in deep blur the colossal stone leg of the guardian visible advancing closer with each step. Audio: his labored mineral breath, his armor scraping on flagstone, the WHUMP of the colossus's nearby footstep, dust falling.

Shot 2 (4-8s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on the colossal stone foot raised above Roco, 35mm, f/2. The guardian's massive carved stone FOOT lifts off the flagstone in real time — taller than Roco's body rising up through the air with deep grinding stone, the carved sole visible as it ascends, dust cascading from the underside. The foot rises high, then begins to DESCEND — massive mass coming down toward Roco who is half-risen. Roco sees the foot, his crystal collar tilting upward in recognition. Audio: deep grinding stone of the foot lifting, the heavy mass shifting, dust pouring, his sharp mineral inhale.

Shot 3 (8-12s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close tracking Roco rolling, 50mm, f/1.8. Roco rolls his armored body sideways across the flagstone, the colossal stone foot SLAMMING down behind him with thunderous WHUMP at frame edge — the floor visibly cracking outward from the impact, dust EXPLOSION rising. Roco continues rolling, dust covering him, his body kicking up flagstone fragments. He comes to rest several meters away on his side, the colossal foot now stamped on the flagstone where he just was. Audio: thunderous WHUMP of the foot impact, flagstone cracking, dust eruption, his mineral grunt of effort, his roll on stone.

Shot 4 (12-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on Roco half-risen, 35mm, f/2. Roco crystal-armored on one knee on the broken flagstone, sword and damaged shield in hands trying to rise. The guardian's colossal STONE HAND descends from above into the frame — massive carved stone palm the size of a small car coming down toward Roco, fingers spread to GRAB him. The hand fills the upper frame closing on his position. Cut as the hand closes around his torso. Audio: his attempted mineral grunt to rise, the WHOOSH of the colossal hand descending, his sharp inhale of recognition, the deep grinding stone of the colossus reaching down.

PACING: 15-second four-shot ground-game beating. Roco crawling, guardian advancing (0-4s), guardian raises foot (4-8s), Roco rolls as foot slams (8-12s), guardian's hand reaches to grab him (12-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

水晶装甲的 Roco 倒在地上，石质守护者踩踏踢打，15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》、Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》、Snyder《300 勇士》。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 红宝石水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光，暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶带战斗损伤裂纹、风化古老雕花石面。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：肩扛手持，大多置于地面高度，极度颤抖。绝不正面——保持四分之三角度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，且随守护者每一次动作而增强，天花板碎片纷纷坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，脚比 Roco 的整个身体还高。

参见这名男子：完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），带战斗损伤的石板地面，尘雾在震颤。

Shot 1（0-4 秒）：四分之三手持极低机位，Roco 在石板地上爬行，35mm，f/2。水晶装甲的 Roco 侧身躺着，挣扎着撑起身来，铠甲在胸口与双肩处开裂，红宝石水晶剑仍在右手但已松脱，尘土覆满他周身。他以矿物的用力缓缓撑起身来。他上方深处虚化中守护者巨大的石腿可见每一步都在更近地逼近。音频：他勉力呼吸的矿物气息、铠甲在石板上刮擦、巨像临近脚步的 WHUMP、尘土洒落。

Shot 2（4-8 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低机位，巨型石脚抬升在 Roco 上方，35mm，f/2。守护者巨大的雕花石 FOOT 实时抬起离开石板——比 Roco 身体还高的巨脚带着深层碾磨的石声升过空中、雕花的脚底在上升中可见、碎片自底部簌簌洒落。巨脚升至高处、随即开始 DESCEND 下降——巨大的重压朝半起身的 Roco 罩下来。Roco 看见这只脚，水晶护颈（collar）向上倾侧以示察觉。音频：脚抬起时深层碾磨的石声、沉重质量的推移、尘土倾泻、他尖锐的矿物吸气。

Shot 3（8-12 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，跟拍 Roco 翻滚，50mm，f/1.8。Roco 将装甲身体侧向滚过石板，巨大的石脚在他身后以雷鸣般的 WHUMP 在画框边缘轰然踏下——地面自撞击点明显向外开裂、尘 EXPLOSION 喷涌而起。Roco 继续翻滚、尘土罩满他、身体掀起石板碎片。他侧身停在数米开外，此刻那只巨脚正踏在他方才所在之处。音频：脚落地的雷鸣 WHUMP、石板开裂、尘土迸发、他用力时的矿物闷哼、他在石上翻滚。

Shot 4（12-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低机位，Roco 半起身，35mm，f/2。水晶装甲的 Roco 单膝跪在碎裂的石板上，手持剑与已损伤的盾试图站起。守护者巨大的石 HAND 自上方降入画面——有小汽车般大小的巨型雕花石掌朝 Roco 压来、张开手指欲 GRAB 抓他。巨掌填满画面上方、朝他所在的位置收拢。巨掌合拢住他躯干的瞬间切走。音频：他试图起身时挤出的矿物闷哼、巨掌降下的 WHOOSH、他察觉时尖锐的吸气、巨像俯身时深层的碾磨石声。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒四镜头地面战击打。Roco 爬行、守护者逼近（0-4 秒），守护者抬脚（4-8 秒），巨脚踏下之际 Roco 翻滚（8-12 秒），守护者的手伸来要抓住他（12-15 秒）。
