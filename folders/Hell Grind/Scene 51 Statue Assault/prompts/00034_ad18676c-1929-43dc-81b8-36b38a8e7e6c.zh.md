## 原文 (source)

Jax behind carved stone column retches and shapes large plastid bomb, 10 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient, warm amber oil-lamps trembling on the column, distant saturated red from off-frame guardian, distant flashes of off-frame combat. Color: deep cool blue-grey, accent warm amber from lamps, distant red wash. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic skin, fabric, weathered ancient carved stone with moss, BLACK plastid material with rough hand-formed wet texture and deep glossy black color glistening with moisture. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld at chest height close on Jax, body-driven, frame trembling.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling, dust drifting.

Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, weathered ancient carved stone column with moss in deep crevices, dust haze.

Single shot (0-10s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Jax crouched behind a carved stone column, 85mm, f/1.4. Jax pressed against the carved stone column for cover, dust-streaked, glasses gripped, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark jacket dust-covered, body crouched low.

At 0-3s: Jax cups his right palm in front of his open mouth. His throat and chest CONTRACT visibly — neck cords tightening, body heaving with effort — and he RETCHES a fully-formed BLACK plastid ball out from his mouth into his waiting palm. The plastid ball lands wet, fist-sized, deep glossy black with glistening moisture. He breathes hard from the effort, recovers. He immediately begins to retch AGAIN — chest contracting, more effort — and a second BLACK plastid ball comes up into his palm joining the first. He kneads the two together with his fingers, pressing them into a single larger black mass. Audio: his retching sounds from chest and throat, wet slaps of plastid into palm, his strained breath, distant chamber rumble, distant off-frame combat sounds — guardian's deep red hum, crystal-on-stone CRACKS far away.

At 3-7s: He retches a THIRD black plastid ball, then a FOURTH — both joining the growing black mass in his palm. The bomb is now too large for one hand — he uses both hands to shape it, kneading and compressing, the wet glossy black mass becoming a dense fist-and-a-half-sized lump. His face is concentrated, focused, hands working the material into a tight ball. He mutters through teeth: "...one big one... one big one..." Sweat on his brow from the repeated retching effort. Audio: continued retching cycles, wet plastid sounds, his concentrated breath, his muttered words, distant combat continuing.

At 7-10s: Jax retches a FIFTH and final BLACK plastid ball — the largest yet — and forces it into the mass. He kneads the now-massive black plastid bomb between both hands with focused effort, compressing it into a dense round mass approximately the size of a small melon. Wet deep glossy black surface, rough texture from his hand-shaping, glistening with moisture. He holds it up briefly to inspect — his glasses reflecting the saturated red glow from off-frame, the black mass deep and glossy in his hands. He nods grimly to himself. Audio: final retching effort, the plastid being compressed audibly with wet squelches, his recovered breath, his muttered word: "...okay..."

PACING: 10-second bomb assembly. First two retches and combine (0-3s), third and fourth additions, kneading begins (3-7s), final fifth piece, final compression and inspection (7-10s).
