Jax sprints out from cover and throws plastid bomb at close range, guardian's hand swipes near him, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Cuarón's Children of Men. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig tracking, body-driven motion with Jax. EXTREME trembling.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall. Scale through the colossal stone hand swiping at floor-level, the hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-7s): Three-quarter handheld medium close tracking Jax sprinting out from a column, 50mm, f/1.8. Operator handheld tracking laterally with his run. Jax sprints fast across open space toward the guardian's leg in deep blurred background — committing to a close-range attack. He has a freshly formed plastid bomb in his right palm, spitting briefly into his palm to "arm" it as he runs, kneading it tighter. His other hand holds his glasses to his face against the wind. Mutters through teeth: "...fuck it, fuck it..." Audio: his pounding boots on flagstone, his fast breath, his muttered words, chamber rumble.

Shot 2 (7-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on Jax mid-throw, 35mm wider lens, f/2. Operator at floor level looking up. Jax visible in foreground in mid-stride, his right arm winding up and HURLING the plastid bomb upward toward the guardian's torso off-frame. The bomb arcs upward out of frame top. He immediately tries to dive sideways and roll away. But the guardian's MASSIVE STONE HAND descends through frame from above — colossal hand the size of a small car SWIPING laterally close to where Jax stands, the hand passing over him with WHOOSH of mass, the wake shoving him sideways violently as he completes his dive-roll. He tumbles across flagstone, the bomb DETONATING off-frame above with bright orange-yellow FLASH visible at frame top, dust eruption raining down. He comes up dust-covered, glasses askew, alive. Mutters: "...holy shit, holy shit..." Audio: WHOOSH of the colossal hand passing close, the BOOM of the detonation off-frame, dust raining down, his sharp grunts of evasion, his muttered words.

PACING: 15-second close-range strike. Sprint out of cover with armed bomb (0-7s), throw upward, evade colossal hand swipe, bomb detonates above (7-15s).

场景：洞穴圣堂，尘雾震颤。

Shot 1（0-7s）：四分之三手持中近景跟踪冲出石柱的 Jax，50mm，f/1.8。摄影师手持横向跟拍他的奔跑。Jax 快速穿过开阔空间冲向深模糊背景中守护者的腿——决意发起近距离攻击。他右掌中有一颗刚成形的 plastid 炸弹，奔跑时短暂往掌心啐口「上膛」，将其捏得更紧。另一只手按着眼镜以抵风。透过牙缝低语：「……豁出去了，豁出去了……」音频：他靴子踏在石板上的重砸声，他急促的呼吸，他的低语，殿堂轰鸣。

PACING：15 秒的近距离攻击。带已上膛炸弹冲出掩护（0-7s），向上抛掷，闪避庞大石手的挥扫，炸弹在上方爆炸（7-15s）。
