## 原文 (source)

Rain weaves the hoverboard between carved stone columns at high speed, guardian smashes a column to block her path, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Cuarón's Children of Men kinetic sequences, Villeneuve's Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient from above, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets, saturated purple from hoverboard thrusters, warm amber oil-lamps along the columns. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, accent saturated purple. Heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic skin, fabric, matte-black hoverboard, weathered ancient carved stone columns with column-faces. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, EXTREME trembling, body-driven tracking through column gaps.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall. Scale through the colossal stone fist that smashes a column — fist the size of multiple Rains shattering ancient stone.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, scar by her eye, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the matte-black hoverboard with purple-glowing thruster cores from Image 2.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, towering carved stone column-faces flanking the side walls, dust haze trembling.

Shot 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on Rain weaving between columns, 50mm, f/1.8. Operator handheld at six-meter elevation tracking her flight. Rain on the hoverboard threading through the carved stone columns at high speed — board angled sharply, body leaning hard into each turn, knees flexing, hands controlling the board. The carved column-faces pass close on either side of her in shallow blur as she weaves through them, ancient script and weathered stone surfaces flashing past. Her face determined, eyes forward, she is using the columns as natural obstacles between herself and the guardian. Audio: thrusters scream, the WHOOSH of close columns passing, her controlled breath, distant guardian deep red hum.

Shot 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on Rain emerging from the columns, 35mm wider lens, f/2. Operator at floor level looking up as Rain bursts from between two column
