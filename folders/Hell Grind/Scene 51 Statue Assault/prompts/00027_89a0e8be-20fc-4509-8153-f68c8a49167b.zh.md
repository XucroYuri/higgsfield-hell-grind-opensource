## 原文 (source)

A young Black man in his 20s, back pressed against 
a massive ancient stone column. Short bright golden-yellow 
natural hair. Tactical clear wraparound visor glasses. 
Black tactical bomber jacket with orange accent details. 
Olive green cargo pants. Black fingerless gloves.

He exhales sharply through his nose, tilts his head 
slightly forward, and spits a thick glossy black substance 
into his open cupped palm. The motion is practiced, 
efficient — zero hesitation, zero disgust. 
Like a mechanic reaching for a tool. 
He watches it land in his hand with calm focus.
Eyes down on the substance, jaw relaxed.

The black substance is dense, glossy, 
slightly viscous — catches the torchlight 
with a faint wet sheen.

Dust and small debris continue falling 
from the ceiling around him. 
Distant heavy impacts shake the frame subtly.

Camera: handheld, natural breathing motion, 
slow organic drift. Telephoto 100–135mm. 
Starts on his face in sharp focus — 
then slow rack focus down to his cupped hand 
as the black substance lands in the palm.
Angle: 3/4 rear side — we see his jawline, 
shoulder, and the hand in front of him. 
Face not fully visible, action is the focus.

Lighting: warm amber torchlight from the left, 
deep shadow on right. The black substance 
catches a glint of torchlight in his palm.

Cinematic realism. Photorealistic.
No camera shake artifacts. No jitter.
No limb distortion. No face distortion.
Maintain exact character appearance throughout.

## 中文对照 (zh)

一位 20 多岁的年轻黑人男子，背紧贴一根巨大的古老石柱。短而明亮的金色自然发。战术透明环绕式护目镜眼镜（visor glasses）。带橙色点缀细节的黑色战术轰炸机夹克。橄榄绿工装裤。黑色露指手套。

他透过鼻子急促地呼气，头微微前倾，向摊开的掌心吐出一团稠厚光泽的黑色物质。这个动作熟练、高效——零犹豫、零厌恶。如同一名机修工伸手去取工具。他用平静专注的目光看着它落在掌中。眼神低垂看着那物质，下颚放松。

那团黑色物质稠密、光泽、微微粘稠——捕捉到火炬光，带着淡淡的湿润光泽。

尘土与小碎石仍从他四周的天花板不断落下。远处沉重的撞击隐隐撼动着画面。

Camera：手持，自然的呼吸动作，缓慢的有机漂移。长焦 100–135mm。从他脸上锐利对焦开始——然后当黑色物质落掌时，缓慢下移焦点推拉（rack focus）到合拢的手掌。角度：四分之三背面侧面——我们看见他的下颌线、肩膀与他身前的手。脸不全然可见，动作是焦点。

Lighting：左侧温暖的琥珀色火炬光，右侧深影。那团黑色物质在他掌中捕捉到一丝火炬光。

电影式写实。写实摄影（Photorealistic）。无摄影机抖动伪影、无抖动、无肢体畸变、无面部畸变。全程保持完全一致的角色外观。
