## 原文 (source)

Stone guardian uses different body parts to beat down crystal-armored Roco who fights uselessly, montage sequence, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with progressing combat damage, weathered ancient carved stone. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld throughout, EXTREME trembling. NEVER frontal — three-quarter.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, ceiling debris cascading.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword, hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, flagstone floor, dust haze trembling.

Beat 1 (0-3s): Three-quarter handheld medium close on the colossal hand grabbing Roco, 50mm, f/1.8. The guardian's massive carved stone HAND closes around Roco crystal-armored — colossal stone fingers the size of his torso curling around his body, gripping him tight. Roco crushed in the grip, his ruby crystal sword arm pinned, body squeezed. He struggles uselessly. Audio: deep grinding stone of fingers closing, the crackle of crystal armor under pressure, his strained mineral roar.

Beat 2 (3-6s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco being hurled, 50mm, f/1.8. The colossal hand violently THROWS Roco — releasing him through the air, his body horizontal in flight, sword and shield still in hands, dust trailing. He SLAMS into the flagstone meters away, body skidding across stone. Audio: WHOOSH of his body through air, IMPACT of his body on stone, dust eruption, his mineral grunt of pain.

Beat 3 (6-9s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Roco striking the guardian's torso, 100mm, f/1.4. Roco half-risen, drives his ruby crystal sword into the colossal stone chest plate — saturated red flash on impact, crystal sinking briefly into ancient stone. The carved chest barely scratched. The guardian's massive form in deep blur shows no reaction, no sign of pain. Audio: his mineral roar of effort, the deep CRUNCH of crystal driving into stone, dust eruption, the colossus's deep red hum unwavering.

Beat 4 (9-12s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle on the colossal knee, 35mm, f/2. The guardian DROPS to one knee toward Roco — the colossal carved stone KNEE descending from above to crush, the knee the size of a small house. Roco rolls sideways at the last moment, the colossal knee SLAMMING into the flagstone next to him with thunderous WHUMP, the floor cracking outward, dust eruption. Roco rolls to the side dust covered. Audio: WHOOSH of the descending knee, thunderous WHUMP of impact, flagstone cracking, dust eruption, his sharp grunt of evasion.

Beat 5 (12-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco kneeling weak, 85mm, f/1.4. The colossal stone SHIN sweeps in laterally — massive ancient carved stone leg KICKING Roco from the side. The shin impacts him with deep WHUMP, his armored body launched sideways across the chamber, sword flying from his hand, body horizontal in flight trailing dust. He skids to rest meters away, body crumpled on his side. Audio: WHOOSH of the shin sweeping in, deep WHUMP of impact, his mineral grunt of pain, his body flying, his weapons clattering, his body skidding.

PACING: 15-second five-beat varied beating. Hand grabs Roco (0-3s), thrown to floor (3-6s), Roco strikes torso uselessly (6-9s), guardian's knee drops nearly crushing Roco (9-12s), shin kicks him across the chamber (12-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

石质守护者用不同的身体部位击打无望反抗的水晶装甲 Roco，蒙太奇序列（montage sequence），15 秒，16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影（photorealistic），变形电影镜头（anamorphic cine lens），35mm 颗粒，暗角（vignette）。参考：Cuarón《人类之子》、Villeneuve《沙丘二部曲》、Snyder《300 勇士》。灯光：寒冷苍白的环境光，守护者眼窝与 Roco 红宝石水晶铠甲散发的饱和红光，暖琥珀色油灯。色彩：深邃冷调蓝灰、主导红色、重度去饱和。极薄景深 f/1.4。写实半透明红宝石水晶带渐进的战斗损伤、风化古老雕花石面。60fps，8K。

CAMERA（摄影机）：全程肩扛手持，极度颤抖。绝不正面——保持四分之三角度。

CHAMBER TREMOR（密殿震颤）：剧烈摇晃，天花板碎片纷纷坠落。

GUARDIAN SCALE（守护者比例）：20 米高，5 米长的利剑，手有小汽车般大小。

参见这名男子：完全被半透明红宝石红多面水晶铠甲包裹，带肩部翼刺、前臂尖刺、遮掩面部的高耸锯齿状护颈（collar），源自 Image 1。

设定：洞窟圣殿（cavern-sanctum），石板地面，尘雾在震颤。

Beat 1（0-3 秒）：四分之三手持中近景，巨手抓住 Roco，50mm，f/1.8。守护者巨大的雕花石 HAND 收拢合住水晶装甲的 Roco——他躯干般大小的巨指环绕其身体合拢、把他紧紧攥住。Roco 被攥得变形，红宝石水晶剑的手臂被夹住，身体被挤压。他无望地挣扎。音频：手指合拢时深层碾磨的石声、受压铠甲噼啪作响、他勉力挤出的矿物咆哮。

Beat 2（3-6 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，Roco 被抛掷，50mm，f/1.8。巨手猛烈地 THROWS 抛掷 Roco——把他抛过空气、身体水平飞掠、剑与盾仍握在手中、尘土拖曳。他砸落到数米外的石板地面，身体在石面上滑行。音频：他身体掠空的 WHOOSH、身体砸石的撞击、尘土迸发、他痛苦矿物般的闷哼。

Beat 3（6-9 秒）：切至四分之三手持极特写，Roco 砍向守护者的躯干，100mm，f/1.4。Roco 半身抬起，将红宝石水晶剑刺入巨大的石质胸甲——落地时饱和红光一闪、水晶短暂嵌入古老石块。雕花的胸口几乎未被擦破。深处虚化中守护者庞大的身形毫无反应、毫无痛楚的迹象。音频：他用力出招的矿物咆哮、水晶刺入石头深处、尘土迸发、巨像深沉的红光嗡鸣毫不动摇。

Beat 4（9-12 秒）：切至四分之三手持极低机位，对准巨膝，35mm，f/2。守护者朝 Roco 放下单膝——这只巨大的雕花石 KNEE 自上方压下欲碾碎一切、膝盖有小屋般大小。Roco 在最后一刻侧向翻滚，巨膝轰然砸落在他身旁的石板地面、发出雷鸣般的 WHUMP，地板向外开裂、尘土迸发。Roco 满身尘土地滚到一旁。音频：膝部降下的 WHOOSH、撞击的雷鸣 WHUMP、石板开裂、尘土迸发、他闪避时尖锐的闷哼。

Beat 5（12-15 秒）：切至四分之三手持中近景，Roco 虚弱地跪地，85mm，f/1.4。巨大的石质 SHIN 侧向横扫——这条古老厚重雕花的石腿从侧面 KICKING 踢中 Roco。胫骨以深沉 WHUMP 撞中他，他装甲的身体被侧向击飞过密殿、剑自手中脱飞、身体水平飞掠拖着尘土。他滑行数米后停下，侧身瘫软在地。音频：胫骨扫入的 WHOOSH、撞击的深沉 WHUMP、他痛苦矿物般的闷哼、他的身体飞掠、武器铛啷坠地、身体滑行。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒五拍花样击打。手抓住 Roco（0-3 秒），掷向地面（3-6 秒），Roco 徒劳砍向躯干（6-9 秒），守护者落膝险些压碎 Roco（9-12 秒），胫骨将他踢飞越密殿（12-15 秒）。
