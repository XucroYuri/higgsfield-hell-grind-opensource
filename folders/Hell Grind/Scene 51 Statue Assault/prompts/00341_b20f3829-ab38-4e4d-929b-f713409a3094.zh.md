## 原文 (source)

Roco blocks guardian's vertical sword strike with crystal shield, then guardian shoves him aside, side profile, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 35mm grain, vignette. References: Cuarón Children of Men, Villeneuve Dune Part Two, Snyder 300 side profile combat. Lighting: cold pale ambient, saturated red from guardian's eye-sockets and Roco's ruby crystal armor. Color: deep cool blue-grey, dominant saturated red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal, weathered ancient carved stone, flagstone with real fracture mechanics. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA: shoulder-mounted handheld, chaotic verite shake, lag focus. STRICT SIDE PROFILE — camera locked at the side of Roco capturing the action in 90-degree profile. 50mm lens at chest height, f/1.4.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

GUARDIAN SCALE: 20 meters tall, 5-meter sword, hand the size of a small car.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze, flagstone floor.

Single shot (0-15s): Strict side profile shoulder-mounted handheld medium, 50mm, f/1.4. Roco visible in 90-degree side profile from his right side standing on flagstone, fully crystal-armored.

At 0-3s: Roco stands in profile facing forward (frame right), the colossal stone sword visible in deep blur descending from above through the upper portion of frame, the colossus's body filling the deep frame. The sword falls vertically. Audio drops to silence — only blade whistle and chamber rumble underneath.

At 3-5s: From Roco's left palm in mid-frame a CRYSTAL SHIELD MATERIALIZES — faceted ruby crystal forming out of nothing, growing rapidly, solidifying just in time at the level of his crystal collar. The sword reaches the shield. Sword IMPACTS shield in profile half a centimeter from his head — saturated red FLASH, deafening CRACK, dust and crystal fragments erupting outward in profile across the frame. Audio returns at full volume. Shield CRACKS in branching fracture lines.

At 5-10s: Camera holds on Roco in profile. Flagstone beneath his boots SHATTERS in fan-pattern, sinking five centimeters. Roco drops to one knee in profile, his right armored hand braced behind him on the broken flagstone. The colossal sword still pressing down on the shield from above, his body shaking with sustained pressure.

At 10-15s: The guardian's MASSIVE FREE STONE HAND descends from above sweeping LATERALLY toward Roco from the right side of frame — the colossal palm coming sideways through the air. Palm IMPACTS the shield sideways with deep WHUMP. Roco LAUNCHES sideways through the air to the left across the frame in profile, body horizontal in flight, cracked shield still in his left gauntlet. He SLAMS into flagstones at the left side of frame with deep CRACK, body skidding to rest on his side. Camera holds the side profile angle as he comes to rest, dust settling.

PACING: 15-second single shot in side profile. Sword falls in upper frame (0-3s), shield materializes and impacts (3-5s), Roco on knee with sustained pressure (5-10s), guardian's free hand sweeps Roco sideways across frame (10-15s).

Setting（场景）：洞穴圣殿（cavern-sanctum），粉尘雾气，石板地。

Single shot（单一镜头）(0-15s)：严格侧面轮廓肩扛手持中景，50mm、f/1.4。画面中 Roco 于石板地上自其右侧可见、处于 90 度侧面轮廓，完整水晶覆甲。

0-3s 期间：Roco 侧身而立、面朝前方（画面右侧），庞大石剑在深糊中清晰可见、自上方降下、穿过画面上部，巨人身形填满画面深处。剑刃竖直垂落。音频降至死寂——只剩剑刃劈风声与底层的洞厅隆隆声。

3-5s 期间：Roco 的左掌前、画面中部，一面水晶盾（CRYSTAL SHIELD）凭空成形（MATERIALIZES）——多面红宝石水晶自他开始成形、飞速长大、堪堪于他水晶领口同高处凝实。剑刃触及盾牌。剑刃在距他头部仅半厘米处从中侧面击中（IMPACTS）盾牌——饱合的红光爆闪（FLASH）、震耳欲聋的脆响（CRACK）、尘土与水晶碎片自侧面轮廓中向外迸溅、横穿画面。音频恢复满额音量。盾牌现出分叉放射的裂纹。

10-15s 期间：守护者庞大的自由石手（MASSIVE FREE STONE HAND）自上方降下、自画面右侧横向扫向 Roco——庞大的掌心侧着穿过空气而来。掌心以深沉的"嘭"声（WHUMP）从侧面命中盾牌。侧面轮廓中，Roco 被横向打飞（LAUNCHES）穿画面飞向左侧，飞行中身体水平，开裂的盾牌仍攥在左手甲中。他以一声深沉的脆响（CRACK）撞（SLAMS）入画面左侧的石板地，身体滑行，侧身停下。摄影机保持侧面轮廓角度直至他落定，尘土落下。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒单一镜头、侧影贯穿全片。剑刃于画面上部落下（0-3s）、盾牌成形并受击（3-5s）、Roco 在持续压力下单膝跪地（5-10s）、守护者的空手扫得 Roco 横向飞过画面（10-15s）。
