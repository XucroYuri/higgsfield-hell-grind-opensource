## 原文 (source)

Crystal-armored Roco slides between guardian's legs and strikes from below, 15 seconds, 16:9 anamorphic 2.39:1.

STYLE: 8K IMAX photorealistic, anamorphic cine lens, 180° shutter, 35mm grain, vignette, chromatic aberration. References: Lubezki, Deakins, Snyder's 300, Villeneuve's Dune Part Two. Lighting: cold pale ambient from above, saturated red glow from guardian's eye-sockets above out of frame and from Roco's ruby crystal armor, warm amber oil-lamps. Color: deep cool blue-grey shadow, dominant saturated red, heavily desaturated. Razor-thin DOF f/1.4. Photorealistic translucent ruby crystal with refraction, weathered ancient stone. Continuity locked. NEVER frontal — three-quarter. 60fps, 8K.

CAMERA SUPPORT: handheld Easyrig, EXTREME trembling, body-driven low-angle tracking.

CHAMBER TREMOR: violent shaking, debris falling.

Refer to the man fully encased in translucent ruby-red faceted crystal armor with shoulder wing-spikes, forearm spikes, jagged collar concealing his face from Image 1.

Setting: cavern-sanctum, dust haze trembling. Roco crystal-armored at floor level, the guardian's massive stone legs framing the chamber from his calves down.

Shot 1 (0-5s): Three-quarter handheld extreme low-angle tracking Roco mid-slide, 35mm wider lens, f/2. Operator handheld at floor level. Roco visible in foreground sliding low across the flagstone — his body horizontal, ruby crystal armor scraping against the stone floor with sparks, sword held ready in right gauntlet, shield held back. He slides UNDER the guardian's massive carved stone foot in deep blurred background — the colossal foot looming above him filling the upper frame, the empty red eye-sockets even higher above out of frame casting red light down. Dust and sparks trail his slide. Camera tracks his lateral slide motion. Audio: the SCRAPE of crystal on flagstone, sparks, his held breath through sealed armor, chamber rumble, the colossal foot's deep grinding above.

Shot 2 (5-10s): Cut to three-quarter handheld extreme close-up on Roco coming up from the slide on one knee, 85mm, f/1.4. Now positioned BEHIND the guardian's lower leg from his angle, three-quarter view of Roco rising on one knee, ruby crystal sword raised. He drives the sword UPWARD in a powerful thrust into the back of the guardian's stone calf in deep blur — saturated red FLASH on the impact, the sword piercing slightly into the weathered carved stone where ancient cracks meet, deeper than previous surface strikes. Crystal shavings, stone fragments, dust eruption. Mineral grunt of effort from sealed body. Audio: the CRACK and CRUNCH of crystal driving into stone, deeper impact than surface strikes, sparks, his deep effort grunt.

Shot 3 (10-15s): Cut to three-quarter handheld medium close on Roco recoiling and rolling away, 50mm, f/1.8. He pulls the sword from the stone with effort — strands of red crystal residue clinging to the blade — and rolls backward across the flagstone, coming up into a low crouch with sword and shield ready. The guardian's leg in deep blur shifts as the colossus reacts to the strike, dust cascading from the wound. Roco's armored silhouette in foreground watching the leg, ready for retaliation. Audio: the WET-MINERAL pull of sword from stone, his roll on flagstone, the guardian's massive groaning shift in response, dust cascading.

PACING: 15-second three-shot under-attack. Slide under foot with sparks (0-5s), upward thrust into back of calf (5-10s), pull-out and defensive roll (10-15s).

## 中文对照 (zh)

水晶装甲的 Roco 从守护巨人的双腿间滑过，自下方向上刺击，15 秒，16:9 变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

STYLE（风格）：8K IMAX 写实摄影，变形宽银幕电影镜头，180° 快门，35mm 颗粒感，暗角，色差。参考：Lubezki、Deakins、Snyder《斯巴达三百勇士》、Villeneuve《沙丘·第二部》。Lighting（灯光）：上方惨白冷光，守护巨人画面上方出画的眼窝红光、以及 Roco 红宝石水晶装甲的饱和红光，温暖琥珀色油灯。Color（色彩）：深冷蓝灰阴影为主，主导饱和红，重度去饱和。极浅景深 f/1.4。带折射的写实半透红宝石水晶、饱经风化的古石。Continuity（连贯性）锁定。绝不正面——四边形取景。60fps，8K。

CAMERA SUPPORT（摄影机支撑）：手持 Easyrig，剧烈抖动，身体驱动的低角度跟随。

CHAMBER TREMOR（洞室震颤）：剧烈摇晃，碎石坠落。

引用图 1 中那名全身包覆半透明红宝石色刻面水晶装甲的男人，他肩部有翼状尖刺、前臂有刺、锯齿状护颈遮蔽面容。

Setting（设定）：洞窟圣殿，尘雾震颤。Roco 水晶装甲在地面层，守护巨人巨大的石腿自其小腿以下框住洞室。

Shot 1（0-5s）：四边形手持极低角度，跟随 Roco 滑行之中，35mm 更广镜，f/2。摄影师手持在地面层。Roco 在前景可见、横身滑过低矮的石板——身体水平，红宝石水晶装甲刮擦石面迸出火星，右护手中剑蓄势待发，盾收于身后。他从深虚化背景中、守护巨人巨大的雕纹石脚下滑过——巨脚赫然悬立在他上方、填满画面上半部，更高处出画之外的空荡红色眼窝向下投下红光。尘埃与火星拖曳在他滑行的身后。摄影机跟随他横向的滑行动作。Audio（音效）：水晶刮擦石板的声响、火星、他隔着封闭装甲屏住的呼吸、洞室轰鸣、上方石脚的深沉碾磨。

Shot 2（5-10s）：切至四边形手持极特写，Roco 自滑姿单膝撑起，85mm，f/1.4。此刻他位于守护巨人小腿之后，从他的角度，Roco 单膝撑起的四边形视角清晰可见，红宝石水晶剑高举。他起身将剑奋力向上猛刺，刺入深虚化中守护巨人的石质小腿后侧——撞击处饱和红闪光迸溅，剑在风化古石上古老裂纹交汇之处微微刺入、比先前历次表面刺击更深一分。水晶碎屑、石片碎片、尘爆。封闭的身体里传出矿物质的使力闷哼。Audio（音效）：水晶刺入石中的碎裂与嘎吱声，比表皮刺击更深，火星、他用力的深沉闷哼。

Shot 3（10-15s）：切至四边形手持中景近景，Roco 撤剑滚身退开，50mm，f/1.8。他用劲把剑从石中拔出——刃上黏挂着几缕红色水晶残丝——随即沿石板向后翻滚，起身进入一个低矮蹲伏的架势，剑盾待命。深虚化中守护巨人的巨腿随着巨像对攻击作出反应而移位，尘雾自伤口倾泻。Roco 装甲的身影在前景守着那条腿，戒备着报复。Audio（音效）：剑离石时湿而矿物的拔脱声、他在石板上的翻滚声、守护巨人回应中庞大的呻吟移位、尘雾倾泻。

PACING（节奏）：15 秒的三镜下层偷袭。带火星的脚下滑过（0-5s），向上俯刺小腿后侧（5-10s），拔剑闪退（10-15s）。
