## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. NOTE: this shot focuses on a stone-carved statue rather than human skin — preserve photorealistic stone-surface realism: weathered porous granite-grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in the carved crevices, fine hairline cracks, fine dust accumulation, micro-shadow definition.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, visible breath in any human characters. The stone statue is animated with weight and intent but its face does not express through skin or eyes — its menace reads through head angle, silhouette, the deliberate stillness of stone catching cold light, and the faint shifting of dust on its surface.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this shot): Witness-style documentary camera, amateur vérité aesthetic — as if filmed raw on a consumer camera. Despite the amateur look the rendering remains 8K photorealistic. CLOSE-UP framing of a 20-meter colossus from a side angle in profile — implies camera is positioned at significant elevation relative to ground (e.g. on a raised stone ledge or upper level of the hall) so it can capture the giant's upper body in tight close-up. Operator stationary — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed moves: TILT (vertical rotation) only — used once to tilt down from face to sword. VIOLENT handheld shake throughout, intensifying on impacts. Imperfect raw framing.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS:
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior, captured here in close-up. Bald stone head, heavy menacing brow ridge, deep-set carved eyes (stone, no living iris), thick set jaw, expressionless stone face. Stone armored cuirass over tunic, wide stone belt with relief pattern, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in his right hand, left hand empty. Weathered grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in carved crevices, fine cracks across the surface. Moves with massive crushing weight, slow and deliberate.

SCENE 51E — GIANT CLOSE-UP STRIKE (single continuous shot).

Sub-beat A — FACE CLOSE-UP: Open on the GIANT's stone head and shoulders in tight CLOSE-UP, captured from a SIDE PROFILE angle. The bald carved head fills the upper portion of frame, heavy brow ridge in profile, deep-set carved eye-socket reading toward the depth of the hall, expressionless stone mouth set hard. Cold rim light catches the contour of his profile against haze, mossy patina visible in the deep crevices of his ear and neck. Held for a held beat — silent, monumental, terrifying for its lack of expression. Frame trembles violently with handheld shake.

Sub-beat B — TILT DOWN TO SWORD: Camera TILTS DOWN from the face, panning rotationally past the stone shoulder, the chest cuirass, the belt — landing on his MASSIVE STONE SWORD gripped in his right hand at hip-level. The sword fills frame in tight close-up, weight of the blade unmistakable, fine cracks running its length, edge worn but lethal. The hand grips the hilt, knuckles of stone tightening as he prepares.

Sub-beat C — FIRST STRIKE: From the sword close-up, we hold as he RAISES the blade in a slow methodical windup — the sword lifts up out of frame at the top. Held at the apex briefly. Then the blade SLAMS down through frame in a slow heavy arc, smashing into the column beside him at sword-level. Bone-deep concussive impact — a LARGE CHUNK of stone breaks off the column where the blade strikes, fragments spray outward, dust explodes from point of contact. Frame jolts violently with the shockwave.

Sub-beat D — FROZEN MICRO-PAUSE: For a fraction of a second — perhaps half a beat — the giant FREEZES completely still, blade embedded against the broken column, his form a silent stone statue caught mid-action. Even the violent handheld shake of the camera quiets into a stillness for that micro-moment. Dust drifts in the air around him. The stone face in close-up reads as utterly empty, a death-mask of stillness. The world holds its breath.

Sub-beat E — SECOND STRIKE: Then he MOVES again — pulls the blade back, raises it once more in another slow methodical windup, and brings it DOWN a SECOND time on the same column or another adjacent column. Another bone-deep impact, more stone shears off, dust billows again. Frame jolts violently again.

Sub-beat F — ROAR: After the second impact, the Giant tilts his huge stone head BACK in close-up profile, mouth gaping wide in stone, and unleashes a deep guttural ROAR upward at the ceiling. Stone face contorted in a death-mask howl, dust shaking loose from his shoulders and the cracks of his armor. Camera shake reaches its peak intensity.

Throughout: tight close-up profile of the giant, ground-elevated witness POV, planted operator, violent vérité handheld shake (peaking at the roar). Stone-surface photorealism is critical — preserve weathered granite, patina, dust, cracks. Tilt used once between face and sword. Two distinct strikes with a frozen micro-pause between them. The contrast between the stillness of stone and the violence of action carries the menace.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Style: 8K IMAX。写实电影级（Photorealistic）——无 3D 渲染、无游戏引擎、无游戏过场动画审美。
摄影指导：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
灯光：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机位于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
色彩：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀色。
摄影机：实体电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
皮肤：毛孔级真实感——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称胎记、毛细血管潮红、与棚内光匹配的毛孔阴影。注意：本镜头聚焦于石雕巨像而非人类皮肤——请保持写实石材表面的真实感：风化多孔的花岗岩灰石、刻槽内的苔绿灰蚀斑、细微发丝裂缝、细尘堆积、微阴影层次。
演技：好莱坞级——反应前微型停顿、精准视线与眼线、任何人形角色的可见呼吸。石雕像以重量与意念被驱动，但其面部不经由皮肤或眼睛传达情绪——它的威胁感通过头部角度、剪影、石材承接冷光时的刻意静止，以及其表面微尘的浮移而呈现。
物理：遵守重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量，正确的接触阴影。无悬浮道具。
构图：三分法（rule of thirds）+ 黄金比例（golden ratio）。每个角色从头帧开始即移动。
连续性：角色、道具、环境在每一处切换间完全一致。无身份漂移。
技术：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动（No jitter）。
音频：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（本镜头）：见证式纪录摄影机，业余真实（vérité）审美——如同一台消费级摄影机裸拍。尽管是业余观感，渲染仍为 8K 写实电影级。以侧角度、轮廓朝向对一座 20 米巨像进行特写取景——暗示摄影机相对于地面处于显著高度（例如立于凸起的石台或巨厅上层），因此能以紧凑特写捕捉巨像的上半身。操作者静止——不行走、不推轨推摄、不跟拍、不环绕。允许的运镜：仅 TILT（垂直旋转）——使用一次，从面部下移倾斜至剑。全程剧烈手持抖动，撞击时加剧。不完美的裸拍构图。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
凿刻于山体内部的宏伟石厅。巨大华丽立柱。石板地板。油灯在柱基处发光。一道冷白自然光柱自岩石天花板的裂隙倾泻而下。全程尘粒与大气雾感。

角色 / 主体：
石巨像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨硕雕刻石战士，此处以特写捕捉。光秃石首、厚重威慑眉脊、深陷刻凿之眼（石质，无活体虹膜）、粗壮下颌、无表情石面。罩衫外覆石质盔甲胸甲、宽石腰带上带浮雕纹样、缠裹手腕。右手执一柄长石剑，左手空置。风化灰石、刻槽内的苔绿灰蚀斑、表面细密裂纹。以沉重碾压力移动，缓慢而审慎。

场景 51E——巨像特写挥砍（单一连续镜头）。

子节拍 A——面部特写：以巨像的石首与双肩紧凑特写开场，自侧面轮廓角度捕捉。光秃刻凿的头占据画面上部大半，轮廓处见厚重眉脊，深陷刻凿的眼窝朝向巨厅深处凝视，无表情的石口硬挺闭合。冷色轮廓光（rim light）勾勒其轮廓衬于雾霭之中，耳部与颈部的深沟侵蚀可见苔绿蚀斑。定格一个节拍——沉默、恢弘、因其毫无表情而令人恐惧。画框随手持抖动剧烈震颤。

子节拍 B——倾斜下移至剑：摄影机自面部向下 TILT，旋转滑过石肩、胸甲、腰带——落定于他右手在髋部高度紧握的那柄巨硕石剑。剑以紧凑特写填满画框，剑身重量无可置疑，细裂沿剑身延伸，刃口磨损却致命。手握剑柄，石质指节在他蓄势之际攥紧。

子节拍 C——首次挥砍：自剑的特写，我们停留，他缓慢有条不紊地抬剑蓄势（windup）——剑于画面上端抬出画外。在顶点短暂定格。随后剑身以缓慢沉重的弧线贯穿画面猛劈而下，砸进他身旁齐剑高的立柱。骨深震撼的撞击——剑刃击打处一大块碎石自柱上崩落，碎片向外飞溅，接触点爆发尘雾。画框随冲击波剧烈震荡。

子节拍 D——冻结微型停顿：在极短的一瞬——约半拍——巨像完全静止冻结，剑身嵌于破损立柱，其形态如静止的沉默石像被定格于动作之中。连摄影机剧烈的手持抖动也在那一微瞬间归于宁静。灰尘在他周身空气中飘浮。特写中的石面读来全然空洞，一具静止的死亡面具。世界屏息凝息。

子节拍 E——第二次挥砍：随后他再度动作——抽回剑刃，以又一次缓慢有条不紊的蓄势重新举起，并再次劈下于同一根或相邻的立柱。又一次骨深震撼的撞击，更多石料崩落，尘土再度翻涌。画框再次剧烈震荡。

子节拍 F——咆哮：在第二次撞击后，巨像在特写轮廓中将巨大的石首向后仰，石口大张，朝着天花板发出低沉浑厚的怒吼。石面扭曲成死亡面具般的嚎叫，尘土从他肩头与盔甲裂纹震落。摄影机抖动达到峰值。

全程：巨像的紧凑特写侧面轮廓、地面高度见证者 POV、扎稳的操作者、剧烈的真实手持抖动（在咆哮处达到峰值）。石面写实度至关重要——保持风化花岗岩、蚀斑、尘垢、裂纹。面部与剑之间使用一次倾斜。两次清晰挥砍，其间夹一次冻结的微型停顿。石头之静与动作之暴之间的对比承载起那份威胁感。
