CAMERA RIG (this scene): MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld throughout in EVERY single shot — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections, frame nearly destabilized at all times. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed within a shot: rack focus, minor reframing pan/tilt rotation, lens-driven zoom. Even in close-ups the frame trembles intensely without exception.

TREMOR RULE: When tremors hit, camera shake intensifies and a low rumble shudders the environment, but NO dust cascades, NO debris falls, NO pebbles rain into frame. Atmosphere stays clean during dialogue.

Both hold their gaze upward, listening to the order settle. Visible breath, chest rises.

SHOT 5 — JAX AND ROKO SILENT PACT (close two-shot, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld, 2-SECOND HOLD).
Framing: Tight close two-shot framed on JAX in frame-LEFT and ROKO in frame-RIGHT, both pressed to the column. Cold rim light defines their profiles facing each other. Background: dark column stone and hazy depth.
Action: At the start of the shot, both men's heads are still angled up from the previous beat. Then in sync, they LOWER their gazes from the ceiling and SLOWLY TURN their heads to look at EACH OTHER across the column face. Eyes meeting — JAX's gaze finding Roko's, Roko's gaze finding Jax's. Held. Two full seconds of silence. No words. The look between them carries weight: assessment, acknowledgment, a wordless agreement that this is the plan and they are committing. Jax's brow lifts faintly under the safety glasses, an almost-imperceptible nod-energy in his expression. Roko's jaw sets harder, eyes returning the lock with calm conviction. Catch-light in both pairs of wet eyes, breath visible, chest rises. The pact is silent and complete. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout — maximum handheld jitter even during this intimate held beat, frame never stabilizing. No falling dust or debris.
