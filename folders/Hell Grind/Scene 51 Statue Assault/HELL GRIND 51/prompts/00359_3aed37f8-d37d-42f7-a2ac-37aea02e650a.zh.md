## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld throughout in EVERY single shot — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections, frame nearly destabilized at all times, the most extreme shake of the entire scene. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed within a shot: rotational pan/tilt to follow action, lens-driven zoom, rack focus. Even in close-ups the frame trembles intensely without exception.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Atmospheric haze throughout. Rugged cave-rock walls climb toward the ceiling fissure where the bell hangs.

CHARACTERS:
JAX — Black man, mid-20s, bleached yellow buzz-cut hair, clear wraparound safety glasses, fresh red graze on his forehead. Black tactical hooded jacket with grey paneling, olive cargo pants, fingerless gloves, brown belt with yellow zipper accents.
BELL — antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern, small clapper hanging beneath. Hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored deep into the rock of the ceiling fissure.

PROPS:
JAX's SLINGSHOT — heavy-duty modern tactical slingshot, machined dark metal frame, reinforced wrist brace forearm support, heavy elastic bands, leather pouch. Compact but powerful weapon, capable of launching dense projectiles at high velocity.
PLASTIQUE EXPLOSIVE — small lump of pliable putty-like military explosive, off-white/beige in color. Activated by being warmed and shaped — Jax chews/works the lump with his teeth and tongue to soften it, then molds it into a dense ball-shaped charge before loading it into the slingshot pouch.

SCENE 51H — JAX'S SHOT (three shots, single prompt, in this exact order). EVERY SHOT FEATURES MAXIMUM VIOLENT HANDHELD SHAKE WITHOUT EXCEPTION.

SHOT 1 — JAX CHEWS THE PLASTIQUE BALL (50mm CU on Jax behind column, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Tight close-up on JAX, back pressed flat against the side of a stone column, body tucked into cover. The vertical mass of the column behind him fills the background. Cold rim light from above catches the contour of his face, safety glasses reflecting faint cold light. Sweat at his temple, fresh graze on forehead.
Action: Jax's right hand is raised in front of his face, holding a small lump of off-white plastique explosive between his thumb and index finger. He works the LUMP with his teeth — biting into it, chewing the pliable putty to warm and soften it, jaw working methodically. His tongue rolls the material in his mouth. Then he removes it from his mouth, rolls it between his fingers, and presses/molds it into a dense BALL-SHAPED CHARGE roughly the size of a marble. He holds the finished ball up in front of his eyes for a beat, examining it — calm, focused, professional. Eyes locked on the small ball, satisfied. Visible breath. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout — maximum handheld jitter even in this tight close-up, frame never stabilizing. The contrast: chaos outside the column, quiet preparation here.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — JAX LEANS OUT AND FIRES THE SLINGSHOT (medium close-up, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Medium close-up on Jax at the column edge, planted camera at eye-level. The vertical mass of the column on frame-LEFT, Jax leaning out from cover into frame-CENTER-RIGHT. Hall depth visible in shallow soft-focus background.
Action: Jax raises his SLINGSHOT — heavy-duty tactical model, dark metal frame, his left forearm braced through the wrist support, the leather pouch loaded with the plastique ball. He LEANS OUT past the column edge, exposing his upper body. He extends his left arm forward fully gripping the slingshot frame, raises his right hand to draw the elastic bands BACK, the heavy bands stretching taut, tension visible in his arm muscles, jaw clenched, safety glasses reflecting the cold light. He sights down the line, eyes narrowing in aim, micro-pause for the calibration. Then he RELEASES — fingers letting go, the elastic bands SNAP forward with brutal speed, the plastique ball LAUNCHING out of the pouch and shooting away through frame in a streak of motion blur. Jax recoils slightly from the release, shoulder absorbing the snap. His eyes track the projectile's flight off-frame, breath held. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout — maximum handheld jitter, frame nearly destabilized.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — PROFILE CU OF BELL, CHARGE MISSES, EXPLOSION CLIMAX (single continuous shot, profile CU with rack focus to background, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Profile close-up on the BELL hanging from its chain in the ceiling fissure — bronze body in sharp focus filling frame-CENTER, ornate engraved relief bands visible across its patinated surface, the small clapper hanging beneath. Cold pale light rakes across the bronze. Background behind the bell: the rugged cave-rock wall in soft focus.

Sub-beat A — BELL HANGING: The bell hangs still in the cold light, swaying very faintly from distant tremors.

Sub-beat B — CHARGE MISSES: Suddenly the PLASTIQUE BALL streaks INTO frame from frame-RIGHT at high velocity — small dark projectile cutting through the air past the bell, MISSING the bronze body by a margin, motion blur smearing the projectile across frame. The ball continues past the bell out toward the cave-rock wall in the background.

Sub-beat C — RACK FOCUS TO WALL: Camera RACK FOCUS shifts — sharpness pulls off the bell in foreground (it goes soft, becoming a blurred bronze shape on frame-LEFT) and racks deep onto the cave-rock wall behind, which comes into sharp focus, the ball mid-flight toward the wall now in the new sharp plane.

Sub-beat D — EXPLOSION CLIMAX: The plastique ball IMPACTS the rock wall — instantaneous ignition. The frame is DOMINATED by the EXPLOSION as the focal climax of the entire shot. A massive burst of orange-yellow FIRE erupts outward from the impact point, expanding into a chaotic billowing FIREBALL filling much of the frame, white-hot core at center, flames licking outward, thick black SMOKE plumes mushrooming up. Heavy chunks of rock SHATTER and BLOW OUTWARD from the wall in violent shrapnel, fragments tumbling through frame with mass-correct trajectories. Dust and debris CLOUD upward into the cold light shaft. The cold rock face FRACTURES — crater carved into the wall, hairline cracks racing outward. The blurred bell in foreground left REVERBERATES VIOLENTLY from the shockwave, swinging on its chain. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY at peak intensity, jolting hard on the explosion impact, the operator clearly thrown by the blast.

The explosion is the headline of the shot — visually overwhelming, the climax. The framing holds on the fireball as it billows. A clean miss — but the destruction is enormous. The charge hit nothing important: just rock.

LOCATION（地点）— HELL HALL（地狱大厅）：
人工开凿于山体内的广阔石厅。两侧林立巨型的雕花石柱。石板地。柱基油灯微微发光。一道苍白冷光自远处高空的岩顶裂隙直落而下。全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。粗犷的洞岩壁攀升向悬钟的天顶裂隙。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
JAX（杰克）— 黑人男性，二十过半，漂白金寸头、透明的环绕式护目镜、前额一道新鲜红色擦伤。黑色战术连帽夹克配灰色嵌板、橄榄绿工装裤、露指手套、带黄色拉链装饰的棕色腰带。
BELL（钟）— 古董青铜钟，深斑锈绿青铜表面、文字与图案的华丽凸起浮雕带、钟下悬一小钟锤。由一条深入天顶裂隙岩石中的重型铁链悬吊。

PROPS（道具）：
JAX（杰克）的弹弓（SLINGSHOT）— 重型现代战术弹弓、机械加工的深色金属框架、加固的腕托前臂支撑、重型弹性皮筋、皮革兜袋。紧凑却威力强大的武器、能以高速度发射高密度弹丸。
塑胶炸药（PLASTIQUE EXPLOSIVE）— 一小团可塑的油灰状军用炸药、米白/米色。通过加热与塑形来激活 — Jax（杰克）用牙与舌啮嚼/摆弄那团材料将其软化、再把它搓成致密球形装药后装入弹弓兜袋。

SCENE 51H — JAX'S SHOT（杰克的一击）（三个镜头，单一提示词，按此顺序严格排列）。每一个镜头都必须呈现最大幅度剧烈手持晃动，绝无例外。

镜头 1 — JAX（杰克）啮嚼塑胶球（柱后 JAX 上 50mm 特写，最大幅度剧烈手持）。
取景：JAX（杰克）紧特写、背被平贴按压在一面石柱侧、身体藏入掩体。身后巨柱的垂直体量填满背景。上方寒冷轮廓光勾住他面部的轮廓、护目镜映着微弱的冷光。鬓角有汗、前额新鲜擦伤。
动作：Jax（杰克）的右手举在脸前、拇指与食指捏着一小团米白塑胶炸药。他用牙摆弄那团材料 — 咬进去、咀嚼可塑油灰以加热软化为它、下颌有条不紊地咬合。舌头在嘴里翻卷那团材料。随后把它从嘴里取出、指间搓动、按压/塑形成约莫一颗弹珠大小的致密 BALL-SHAPED CHARGE（球状装药）。他把做好的球举到眼前顿看一拍、审视它 — 冷静、专注、专业。双眼锁住小球、心满意足。可见的呼吸。全程摄影机剧烈晃动 — 即便在这紧特写中、最大幅度手持抖动、画面从未稳定。反差：柱外一片混乱、此处静默备战。

切。

镜头 2 — JAX（杰克）探身开弓弹射（中近景、最大幅度剧烈手持）。
取景：柱缘旁 Jax（杰克）的中近景、视平线固定摄影机。巨柱的垂直体量居画面 LEFT（左）侧、Jax（杰克）自掩体探入画面 CENTER-RIGHT（中右）。大厅纵深在浅景深柔和失焦背景中可见。
动作：Jax（杰克）举起他的弹弓 — 重型战术型号、深色金属框架、左前臂穿过腕托支撑、皮革兜袋已装入塑胶球。他 LEANS OUT（探出）柱缘、露出上身。他完全向前伸出左臂握紧弹弓框架、抬起右手向后抽出弹性皮筋 BACK（向后）、沉重的皮筋绷得笔直、手臂肌肉可见地绷紧、下颌紧咬、护目镜映着冷光。他沿力线瞄准、双眼眯起对焦、微顿校准。随后他 RELEASE（松手）— 手指松开、弹性皮筋以狂暴速度 SNAP（弹射）向前、塑胶球自兜袋 LAUNCHING（飞出）、化作一道运动模糊划过画面射离。Jax（杰克）因这次释放微微后坐、肩部吸收回弹。他的双眼追踪弹丸在框外的飞行、屏息。全程摄影机剧烈晃动 — 最大幅度手持抖动、画面几乎失稳。

切。

镜头 3 — 钟的侧影特写、装药落空、爆炸高潮（单一连续镜头、侧影特写配焦点变换到背景、最大幅度剧烈手持）。
取景：悬在天顶裂隙铁链上的 BELL（钟）侧影特写 — 青铜钟体对焦锐利填满画面中央、斑驳表面可见华丽浮雕带、钟下悬小钟锤。苍白冷光倾斜扫过青铜。钟后背景：粗犷的洞岩壁、柔和失焦。

分拍 A — 钟悬垂：钟在冷光中静悬、因远处震动而微微轻晃。

分拍 B — 装药落空：骤然间、塑胶球以高速度自画面 RIGHT（右）侧入画 — 一枚小小的深色弹丸切过空气掠过钟、与青铜体 MISSING（失之交臂）、运动模糊把它拖过画面。球越过钟继续飞向背景中的洞岩壁。

分拍 C — 焦点变换到墙壁：摄影机 RACK FOCUS（焦点变换）切换 — 清晰度自前景的钟上抽离（它变软、化作画面左缘一片模糊的青铜形影）、转而焦点鲜明地落在身后洞岩壁上、其进入锐利合焦、此刻向墙飞去的球正处于新的清晰焦平面上。

分拍 D — 爆炸高潮：塑胶球 IMPACTS（命中）岩壁 — 瞬时起爆。画面被这场 EXPLOSION（爆炸）DOMINATED（主宰）、成为整枚镜头的焦点高潮。大片橘黄色 FIRE（火焰）自命中点向外炸开、膨胀成一团混乱翻涌、填满画面大半的 FIREBALL（火球）、核心白热、火舌向外舔舐、浓黑 SMOKE（烟）柱蘑菇状上翻。大块岩石 SHATTER（碎裂）并化作猛烈破片向外 BLOW OUTWARD（迸飞）、碎块以符合质量的轨迹翻滚过画面。尘土与碎片 CLOUD（弥漫）上升汇入冷光柱。冷岩面 FRACTURES（开裂）— 壁上凿出弹坑、发丝般细纹向外急窜。前景左侧模糊的钟被冲击波震得 REVERBERATES VIOLENTLY（剧烈震颤）、沿链摆动。摄影机在峰值强度下剧烈晃动、爆炸冲击时猛地一沉、操作员显然被爆轰抛撼。

这场爆炸是本镜头的头号焦点 — 视觉上压倒一切、是高潮。取景停驻在翻滚的火球上。一次干净落空 — 但破坏力惊人。装药没有命中任何要紧的东西：只有岩石。
