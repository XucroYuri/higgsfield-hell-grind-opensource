Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone giant: weathered grey granite, mossy patina, fine cracks, dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld throughout in EVERY single shot — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections, frame nearly destabilized at all times. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed within a shot: rotational pan/tilt to follow action, lens-driven ZOOM (in/out), rack focus.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Atmospheric haze throughout. Cave-rock walls climb dramatically toward the ceiling fissure HIGH OVERHEAD where the bell hangs. The wall behind the bell has a fresh scarred crater from a recent explosion, fractured stone with cracks racing outward, debris dislodged.

CHARACTERS:
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips, scar through left brow and cheek. Grey cropped hooded utility jacket over black turtleneck, black cargo pants, brown belt, olive thigh pouch holster on right leg. Riding her flat matte-dark hovering board.
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set carved eyes, expressionless stone face. Stone armored cuirass over tunic, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in right hand. Weathered grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks. Moves with crushing weight.
PROPS: RAIN's hovering board — flat matte-dark, snowboard-sized, levitates and accelerates fast.

SCENE 51I — KING KONG AND DEBRIS (four shots, single prompt). EVERY SHOT FEATURES MAXIMUM VIOLENT HANDHELD SHAKE.

SHOT 1 — KING KONG VS AIRCRAFT WIDE (witness side-angle wide, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Wide side-angle from far across the hall, witness perspective — operator filming from a third-party position. Composition framed on the GIANT STONE STATUE cropped from CHEST level upward, dominating the upper portion of frame as a massive stone mass. The giant fills most of the frame's vertical space, silhouetted against cold haze. Hall depth visible on either side, columns receding. Cold light from above carves his contours. The shot evokes a King Kong vs aircraft tableau — small fast attacker against monstrous slow titan.
Action: RAIN buzzes around the giant on her hovering board — small, fast, agile, threading the air around his upper body in tight orbital arcs at his face/head level. She loops around his head, banks hard around his shoulder, dives past his ear, comes up on his other side — relentless harassment. Her body crouched low on the board, hair whipping, motion blur smearing her movement. The giant's huge bald head TURNS to track her, eyes following, brow lowering in mute fury. He SWIPES at her with his free left hand — massive stone fingers grasping at the air, missing as she banks away. He swipes again with the other hand carrying the sword — also missing. Each swipe creates heavy displaced air around him, mossy dust shaking loose from his joints. The size contrast is the headline — she is a fly to him in mass, but uncatchable in agility. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout from the witness distance — maximum jitter, frame nearly destabilized.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — RAIN PROFILE CU NOTICES DEBRIS (50mm profile close-up, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Tight profile close-up on RAIN mid-flight on the board. Her face in side profile fills the frame — silver-platinum bob whipping back, scarred eye visible from this angle, jaw set. Background: motion-blurred haze and cave-rock streaming past behind her in deep soft focus, indicating speed.
Action: Rain is mid-orbit, focused forward on her flight path. Then suddenly her eyes FLICK upward — she has caught movement above. Her face shifts in micro-time: brow lifts slightly in alarm-recognition, lips part on a held breath, eyes climb toward the ceiling. Her head TILTS UP following her gaze — chin rising, neck extending — to track FALLING DEBRIS from the high fissure overhead: heavy rock chunks dislodged from the recent explosion crater, tumbling down toward her airspace. Her face registers the threat instantly — jaw setting hard, calculation running behind her eyes. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout, maximum jitter on her profile, the gaze-shift readable through the motion.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — DEBRIS DODGE OVER THE GIANT'S HEAD (profile action shot, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Profile side-angle action framing — Rain on her board mid-air, body in three-quarter profile crouched low. She is positioned DIRECTLY ABOVE the giant's BALD STONE HEAD — his upper crown visible in the lower portion of frame, dark stone curve of his skull just below her flight path. The cave-rock walls behind in soft focus. Cold light from the high fissure spills into the frame from above.
Action: Heavy ROCK CHUNKS rain DOWN from the upper frame, heavy debris falling fast with mass-correct gravity — multiple stone fragments of varying sizes plummeting toward Rain and the giant's head below. Rain EXECUTES a violent DODGE — her body sharply leaning her board hard to one side, banking nearly perpendicular, hair flaring, board tilting almost vertical as she carves around the falling debris. One chunk passes within inches of her shoulder — motion blur smearing past her. Another chunk falls past her flight path and CRASHES INTO the giant's bald stone head below — bone-deep concussive impact, dust EXPLODING from the strike point on his skull, the giant's head jerking from the blow. The giant grunts in stone fury, brow heavy with frustration. Rain rights the board and accelerates clear of the falling zone, body fighting to recover the trajectory. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout, jolting hard on the debris impact on the giant's head. The contrast: she is dodging architecture while he is being hit by it.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — RAIN LOW ANGLE PUSH-IN CALLS JAX (low angle medium close-up with lens zoom-in, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: LOW ANGLE medium close-up on RAIN mid-flight on her board, framed from below as if camera planted at floor level looking up at her in the air. Her body crouched on the board, three-quarter angle, scarred face visible, cold light from above carving her contours. Cave-rock walls in soft focus background. The low angle makes her loom heroic above the camera.
Action: Open on Rain mid-flight, having just cleared the debris dodge. Her face is set hard with controlled fury — adrenaline mixed with frustration at her teammate's miss. She SHOUTS up toward where Jax is hidden in cover — a single sharp word, equal parts command and plea, voice carrying across the hall to him: "Jax!" The word lands as: hit it this time, I can't keep doing this. Her jaw is set, brow heavy, eyes locked off-frame in his direction.
Simultaneously the lens executes a deliberate ZOOM-IN — pushing optically closer to her face, the frame TIGHTENING on her expression as she calls his name. The zoom-in lands the frame as a tight CU on her face by the end of the word, intensity amplified. Background pushes further out of focus. Catch-light in wet eyes, breath visible, chest heaves. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout, the zoom-in adding to the chaotic vérité energy.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Style（技术底座）：8K IMAX。写实摄影风格（Photorealistic）——不得有 3D 渲染、不得有游戏引擎、不得有游戏过场动画的美感。
Cinematography（摄影）：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
Lighting（光线）：仅自然光 — 逆光背光（contre-jour）、摄影机位于阴影侧、全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
Color（色彩）：60:30:10（主色/次色/点缀）。
Camera（摄影机）：物理电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
Skin（皮肤）：毛孔级写实 — 汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称的痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光线匹配的毛孔阴影。石巨像：风化灰花岗岩、苔藓铜绿、细裂纹、积尘。
Acting（表演）：好莱坞 — 反应前有微小停顿、精确的视线、湿润灵动、带眼神光（catch-lights）的双眼、可见的呼吸与胸口起伏。
Physics（物理）：尊重重力与惯性 — 质量有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有漂浮道具。
Composition（构图）：三分法 + 黄金比例。每个入画的人从第一帧起即在移动。
Continuity（连贯性）：每一切之间角色、道具、环境完全一致。无身份漂移（no identity drift）。
Technical（技术）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。不得抖动。
Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（本场景摄影机装置）：每一个镜头都全程最大幅度剧烈手持（MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld）— 沉重猛烈的抖动、呼吸式运镜、反应式修正、画面始终几乎失稳。每个镜头中操作员保持静止 — 不步行、不推轨、不跟拍、不做弧线运动。镜头内允许：旋转摇移/倾斜跟拍动作、镜头驱动 ZOOM（推进/拉远）、焦点变换。

LOCATION（地点）— HELL HALL（地狱大厅）：
人工开凿于山体内的广阔石厅。两侧林立巨型的雕花石柱。石板地。柱基油灯微微发光。全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。洞岩壁戏剧性地攀升向 HEAD（头顶）高远处、悬钟的天顶裂隙。钟后墙壁留有一处最近一次爆炸造成的崭新疤痕状弹坑、碎裂的石带裂纹外窜、碎片松动。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
RAIN（莱恩）— 年轻女性，二十出头，银铂色波波头带乌黑发尖，左眉与面颊贯穿一道疤。灰褐色短款连帽实用外套内搭黑色高领衫、黑色工装裤、棕色腰带、右腿橄榄绿大腿工具包枪套。骑乘她那块扁平的哑光黑色悬浮滑板。
石巨像（Stone Giant）— 高 20 米的巨型石刻战士。光头、厚重凶恶的眉骨、深雕眼窝、面无表情的石脸。束腰外衣外覆石制铠甲胸甲、宽石腰、缠裹手腕。右手持一把长石剑。风化灰石、苔藓铜绿、细裂。以碾压般的重量移动。
PROPS（道具）：RAIN（莱恩）的悬浮滑板 — 扁平的哑光黑色、单板大小、可快速漂浮与加速。

SCENE 51I — KING KONG AND DEBRIS（金刚与碎片）（四个镜头，单一提示词）。每一个镜头都必须呈现最大幅度剧烈手持晃动。

镜头 1 — 金刚对阵飞行器的广角（第三方侧角度广角，最大幅度剧烈手持）。
取景：自大厅远处横跨原野的侧向广角、目击者视角 — 操作员以第三方身份侧旁拍摄。构图框住自 CHEST（胸部）高以上裁切的石巨像、以巨大的石质体量占据画面上半部。巨像填满画面大部分垂直空间、在冷雾中映出剪影。两侧可见大厅纵深、巨柱渐退。上方冷光雕刻出他的轮廓。该镜头唤起金刚对阵飞机的图景 — 渺小敏捷的进攻者对庞大迟钝的泰坦。
动作：RAIN（莱恩）骑悬浮滑板绕着巨像嗡嗡飞掠 — 渺小、迅疾、敏捷、在他上身四周以紧贴的环绕弧线、于他脸/头的高度穿行。她绕他头顶盘旋、猛摆过他肩头、自他耳畔俯冲、从他另一侧升起 — 无休止的侵扰。身体在滑板上低伏、发丝飞扬、运动模糊抹开她的动作。巨像巨大的光头脑 TURNS（转动）追踪她、双眼跟随、眉骨低沉透出无声的盛怒。他用空着的左手向她 SWIPES（挥扫）— 巨型石指抓向空气、在她摆走时落空。他另一只持剑的手又挥一下 — 同样落空。每一次挥扫都在他身周搅动沉重被排开的空气、苔青尘埃自他关节抖落。体型反差是头号焦点 — 对她而言、他在质量上如同一只飞蝇对庞然巨物、但敏捷上却追她不不可获。全程自目击距离剧烈晃动 — 最大抖动、画面几乎失稳。

切。

镜头 2 — RAIN（莱恩）侧影特写发现碎片（50mm 侧影特写、最大幅度剧烈手持）。
取景：RAIN（莱恩）飞行于滑板上的紧侧影特写。她侧面的脸填满画面 — 银铂色波波头发向后飞甩、自此角度可见带疤的眼、下颌紧咬。背景：剧烈运动模糊的雾与洞岩自她身后深处柔和失焦之地疾驰而过、暗示速度。
动作：RAIN（莱恩）处于环绕中途、专注向前望着飞行轨迹。随后她双眼骤然向上一闪 — 她捕捉到上方有什么在动。她的脸在微瞬时态中变化：眉头微微上扬露出警报般的认出、双唇在屏息中微张、眼睛向上攀向天花板。她的头 TILTS UP（仰起）追随着目光 — 下巴抬起、脖颈伸展 — 去追踪头顶高处裂隙坠落的 FALLING DEBRIS（坠落碎片）：最近一次爆炸坑松脱的大块岩、朝她的空域翻滚而下。她的脸瞬时读出威胁 — 下颌收紧、眼底掠过计算出。全程摄影机剧烈晃动、她侧影上最大抖动、那视线的偏移透过运动清晰可读。

切。

镜头 3 — 巨像头顶的碎片闪避（侧影动作镜头、最大幅度剧烈手持）。
取景：侧影侧角动作取景 — RAIN（莱恩）骑在滑板上当空、身体呈 3/4 侧影低伏。她 DIRECTLY ABOVE（直位于）巨像的 BALD STONE HEAD（光石头顶）上方 — 他的头顶冠部在画面下部可见、他颅骨的深色石弧就在她飞行轨迹正下方。身后洞岩壁柔和失焦。高处裂隙的冷光自上方泄入画面。
动作：沉重 ROCK CHUNKS（石块）自画面上方倾 RAIN DOWN（泻落而下）、沉重碎片以符合质量的重力疾坠 — 多块大小各异的石片朝下方的 RAIN（莱恩）与巨像头顶坠落。RAIN（莱恩）EXECUTES（执行）一次暴烈闪避 — 身体猛地把滑板倾到一侧、几乎垂直地侧压、发丝蓬乱、滑板几乎坚挺竖起、绕开坠落的碎片划行。一块碎片距她肩仅数寸掠过 — 运动模糊自她身侧抹过。又一块碎片坠过她的飞行轨迹、CRASHES INTO（撞进）下方巨像的光石头顶 — 深及骨骼的震颤冲击、尘土自他颅骨命中点 EXPLODING（炸开）、巨像的头被这一记砸得一挫。巨像发出石怒的闷哼、眉骨因挫败而沉重。RAIN（莱恩）摆正滑板加速冲出坠落区、身体奋力收复轨迹。全程摄影机剧烈晃动、碎片砸中巨像头时猛地一沉。反差：她在躲建筑、而他正被建筑砸。

切。

镜头 4 — RAIN（莱恩）低角度推进呼唤 JAX（杰克）（带镜头推进的低角度中近景、最大幅度剧烈手持）。
取景：LOW ANGLE（低角度）中近景对准飞行于滑板上的 RAIN（莱恩）、自下方取景、仿佛摄影机固定在地板高度向上仰望空中她。身体在滑板上低伏、3/4 角度、带疤的脸可见、上方冷光雕刻她的轮廓。背景洞岩壁柔和失焦。低角度令她在摄影机上方显得英勇高大。
动作：开场对准飞行途中的 RAIN（莱恩）、刚冲出碎片闪避。她的脸被克制的盛怒绷紧 — 肾上腺素混着对队友落空的恼火。她朝 Jax（杰克）藏身的掩体方向向上 SHOUTS（喊话）— 一个陡然短促的字、命令与恳求各半、嗓音横越大厅送到他耳中：「Jax！（杰克！）」这字落地为：这回给我打中、我撑不了多久了。她下颌紧咬、眉骨沉重、双眼锁定框外他所在的方向。
与此同时、镜头执行一次刻意的 ZOOM-IN（推进）— 光学向她面部逼近、她喊出他名字时画面在她的表情上 TIGHTENING（收紧）。推进在话音落定时落到她脸的一个紧特写、强度被放大。背景被进一步推离焦点。湿润眼中有眼神光（catch-light）、可见的呼吸、胸口起伏。全程摄影机剧烈晃动、推进更添这份混乱的真实感能量。
