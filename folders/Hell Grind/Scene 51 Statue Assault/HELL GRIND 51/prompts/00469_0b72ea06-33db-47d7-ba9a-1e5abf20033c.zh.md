Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone-carved giant: weathered granite-grey stone, mossy patina in crevices, fine cracks, dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (all shots): Witness-style documentary handheld, amateur vérité aesthetic, 8K photorealistic rendering. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Only rotational moves and lens-driven zoom allowed. MAXIMUM violent handheld shake throughout. Imperfect raw framing. NO visible sound waves, NO heat-haze distortion — only physical effects of invisible acoustic energy.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns lining both sides. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. The hall walls are carved from ancient resonant stone. High up on the LEFT cave wall, set deep into the rock, there is a CARVED ALCOVE-NICHE — a small recessed opening like a meditation cell, framed by weathered carved stone, accessible only from above.

CHARACTERS:
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set eyes. Stone armored cuirass, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in right hand. Weathered grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks. Moves with crushing weight.
MONK — elderly Asian man, late 60s, short graying hair, lined weathered face, calm composed features. Deep burgundy monk's robe with orange-red waist sash. Sits cross-legged in lotus position inside the alcove, hands held in mudra at his chest, eyes closed in deep meditative trance. Lips move continuously, whispering an ancient mantra.

SCENE 51E — STRIKE AND RESONANCE (four shots, single prompt, in this exact order).

SHOT 1 — HORIZONTAL SWING (low-angle three-quarter close-up).
Framing: Low-angle close-up, camera planted at ground level looking UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE. Telephoto compression. Giant positioned slightly LEFT of center, cropped at the WAIST — only upper body visible: stone cuirass, wrapped right shoulder pad, bald head with heavy menacing brow. Right hand grips the long stone sword, blade held HIGH and to the side. Frame-RIGHT: dark vertical mass of an adjacent column. Frame-LEFT: hazy cave depth. Cold rim light catches the contour of his profile and edge of the raised blade.
Action: SLOW windup — the giant holds the sword raised high for a held beat, building tension. Then EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE — the blade WHIPS in a fast HORIZONTAL ARC across his body, sweeping sideways at chest-to-hip level with brutal speed. Heavy motion blur on the blade. The blade SMASHES into the lower portion of a column with sudden bone-deep concussive impact — dust explodes outward, fragments spray, frame jolts violently at maximum shake intensity.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — COLUMN CHUNK FALL (close-up on column).
Framing: Tight close-up on the LOWER section of a hall column near floor level — weathered carved stone, fine relief, mossy patina. Column fills frame vertically. Cold rim light catches the surface texture. Background: hazy hall depth, out-of-focus.
Action: The column stands intact for a held beat. Then SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT from off-frame, stone fractures violently. A LARGE CHUNK of stone breaks off, hairline cracks racing outward. The chunk TUMBLES forward and downward through frame with mass-correct gravity. Camera tilts down with it slightly. The chunk SLAMS into the stone-tiled floor at frame-bottom with a thunderous secondary impact — stone-on-stone shatter, breaking into pieces, dust kicks up violently. Frame jolts violently on both impacts. Dust hangs heavy after.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — WALLS VIBRATE (medium-wide on cave wall).
Framing: Camera planted at hall floor level, framed on a section of cave wall and adjacent column. Cold pale rim light grazes the rock texture. Hazy hall depth at the edges, out of focus. Oil lamp glowing at frame-bottom corner.
Action: The stone wall and column surface VIBRATE physically — fine micro-tremors visibly rippling across the rock face, carved relief patterns shaking in place. Sheets of fine dust cascade down the wall, drifting through cold light. Pebbles and small stone fragments shake loose from upper crevices, skittering down. The oil-lamp flame jolts and flickers violently. Frame trembles violently. NO visible sound waves, NO heat-haze.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — THE ALCOVE (high-angle perspective from above the niche, looking down at it).
Framing: High-angle perspective shot, camera planted on the cave wall above the alcove, looking steeply DOWN at the carved ALCOVE-NICHE set into the rock face. The frame is dominated diagonally on the LEFT side by the rough vertical mass of the cave wall — a large dark cliff-like surface receding downward, deep shadow and rugged stone texture. The alcove-niche itself is visible in the FRAME-CENTER to RIGHT — a clear DARK RECTANGULAR OPENING cut into the wall, weathered carved stone framing it like a doorway, the shadowed interior of the meditation cell readable inside. Visible inside the niche: the MONK seated in lotus position, deep burgundy robe with orange sash, hands held in mudra at his chest, head slightly bowed, eyes closed, lips moving in mantra. The opening is the unmistakable focal point of the frame — we are looking down into his hidden cell. Below the niche to the bottom-right of frame, a sliver of the hall floor far below glimmers faintly with oil-lamp light, but the giant is NOT visible in this shot. Cold pale daylight from the ceiling fissure rakes across the wall surface from above.
Action: The monk is mid-mantra inside the niche — calm and centered, lips shaping syllables in steady flow despite the chaos. Then the SHOCKWAVE from the column's collapse reaches the alcove — fine dust cascades down the carved stone framing the niche around him, pebbles and small fragments rattle loose from the rock face above and around the opening, the alcove itself visibly trembles. Inside the niche, the monk's CONCENTRATION BREAKS for a fraction of a second — his lips falter, the mantra stutters, closed eyes squeeze tighter, brow furrowing in strain. He fights to recover — lips resuming, syllables stumbling back into flow, but the steady stream is shaken. Dust drifts past his face inside the niche, settles on his shoulders. Knuckles whiten faintly on his mudra. The fragility of his control is the headline. Frame trembles violently throughout.

Style（技术底座）：8K IMAX。写实摄影风格（Photorealistic）——不得有 3D 渲染、不得有游戏引擎、不得有游戏过场动画的美感。
Cinematography（摄影）：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
Lighting（光线）：仅自然光 — 逆光背光（contre-jour）、摄影机位于阴影侧、全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
Color（色彩）：60:30:10（主色/次色/点缀）。
Camera（摄影机）：物理电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
Skin（皮肤）：毛孔级写实 — 汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称的痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光线匹配的毛孔阴影。石雕巨像：风化花岗岩灰石、缝隙中的苔藓铜绿、细裂纹、积尘。
Acting（表演）：好莱坞 — 反应前有微小停顿、精确的视线、湿润灵动、带眼神光（catch-lights）的双眼、可见的呼吸与胸口起伏。
Physics（物理）：尊重重力与惯性 — 质量有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有漂浮道具。
Composition（构图）：三分法 + 黄金比例。每个入画的人从第一帧起即在移动。
Continuity（连贯性）：每一切之间角色、道具、环境完全一致。无身份漂移（no identity drift）。
Technical（技术）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。不得抖动。
Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（全部镜头摄影机装置）：旁观者式纪录手持、业余真实感美学（amateur vérité aesthetic）、8K 写实渲染。每个镜头中操作员保持静止 — 不步行、不推轨、不跟拍、不做弧线运动。仅允许旋转动作与镜头驱动变焦。全程最大幅度剧烈手持晃动。不完美的原生取景。不得有可见声波、不得有热霾失真 — 只呈现隐形声学能量的物理效应。

LOCATION（地点）— HELL HALL（地狱大厅）：
人工开凿于山体内的广阔石厅。两侧林立巨型的雕花石柱。石板地。柱基油灯微微发光。一道苍白冷光自岩顶裂隙直落而下。尘埃微粒与大气雾感（atmospheric haze）遍布全程。大厅墙壁由上古共鸣之石雕凿而成。右墙高处、深入岩体之中，有一处 CARVED ALCOVE-NICHE（雕凿龛洞）— 一个如冥想室般的小型凹陷开口、由风化的雕石框住、仅可从上方进入。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
石巨像（Stone Giant）— 高 20 米的巨型石刻战士。光头、厚重凶恶的眉骨、深陷的眼。石制铠甲胸甲、宽石腰、缠裹手腕。右手持一把长石剑。风化灰石、苔藓铜绿、细裂。以碾压般的重量移动。
MONK（僧人）— 年迈亚洲男性，年近七旬，短发花白、沟壑纵横的风霜面容、沉着镇定的神情。深酒红色僧袍配橙红腰带。在龛洞内以莲花坐姿盘腿而坐，双手在胸前结法印（mudra），双眼闭阖进入深沉禅定。双唇不停翕动，低念古老真言。

SCENE 51E — STRIKE AND RESONANCE（打击与共鸣）（四个镜头，单一提示词，按此顺序严格排列）。

镜头 1 — 水平挥击（低角度 3/4 特写）。
取景：低角度特写，摄影机固定在地面高度，仰视处于 THREE-QUARTER ANGLE（3/4 角度）的巨像。长焦压缩。巨像微偏中心左侧，于 WAIST（腰部）处裁切 — 仅见上身：石胸甲、裹缠的右肩垫、厚重凶恶眉骨的光头。右手紧握长石剑，剑刃高高举向身侧。画面右侧：相邻巨柱的深色垂直体量。画面左侧：雾霾的洞穴纵深。寒冷轮廓光（rim light）勾住他侧影的轮廓与高举剑刃的边缘。
动作：SLOW windup（缓慢蓄力）— 巨像将剑高举、顿住一拍蓄势。随后 EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE（爆发式快速释放）— 剑刃 WHIPS（猛挥）出一道快速的水平弧线掠过他身前，以狂暴速度在胸至髋高度横扫侧向。剑刃带沉重运动模糊。剑刃 SMASHES（砸）入一根巨柱下半部，顿生深及骨骼的震颤冲击 — 尘埃向外炸开、碎片飞溅、画面在最高抖动强度下剧烈崩震。

切。

镜头 2 — 巨柱碎块坠落（巨柱特写）。
取景：大厅巨柱近地高度的下部紧特写 — 风化的雕石、细浮雕、苔藓铜绿。巨柱纵向填满画面。寒冷轮廓光（rim light）打亮表面质感。背景：雾霾大厅纵深、失焦。
动作：巨柱完好地顿立一拍。随后框外 SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT（骤然巨力冲击）、石面剧烈破裂。一大块巨石断裂、发丝般细纹向外急窜。碎块以真实质量重力向前向下 TUMBLES（翻滚）过画面。摄影机随它微微下移。碎块以雷霆般的二次冲击 SLAMS（猛砸）入画面底部的石板地 — 石碰石碎裂、崩成数块、尘土剧烈扬起。两次冲击都令画面剧烈崩震。事后尘土沉重弥漫。

切。

镜头 3 — 墙壁震动（洞壁中广景）。
取景：摄影机固定在大厅地板高度，框住一段洞壁与相邻巨柱。苍白冷轮廓光掠过岩石质感。边缘雾霾大厅纵深、失焦。画面底角一盏油灯发光。
动作：石墙与巨柱表面在物理上 VIBRATE（震动）— 细微震颤在岩石表面清晰可见地震漾，雕凿浮雕原地抖动。成片细尘沿墙泻落、于冷光中飘荡。小石卵与碎石片从上方裂隙松动掉落、簌簌滚落。油灯火苗剧烈震颤、摇曳。画面剧烈震颤。不得有可见声波、不得有热霾。

切。

镜头 4 — 龛洞（从洞窟上方俯瞰它的高角度透视）。
取景：高角度透视镜头，摄影机固定于龛洞上方的洞壁上，陡峭向下俯视嵌入岩面的雕凿 ALCOVE-NICHE（龛洞）。画面左缘被洞壁粗犷的垂直体量呈对角线支配 — 一大片深暗如悬崖的表面向下退缩、深重阴影与粗犷岩石质感。龛洞本身可见于 FRAME-CENTER（画面中央）偏右 — 一个嵌在墙中的清晰 DARK RECTANGULAR OPENING（深色矩形开口）、风化雕石如门框般框住它、阴影中冥想室的内部可辨。龛内可见：MONK（僧人）呈莲花坐、深酒红袍配橙带、双手于胸前结法印、头微垂、双眼闭阖、双唇翕动念诵真言。开口是画面不容置疑的焦点 — 我们在俯视他隐匿的静室。龛洞下方、画面右下，远处大厅地板的一线以油灯火微光闪烁，但本镜头中巨像 NOT（不）可见。来自天顶裂隙的苍白冷光自上方斜扫过墙面。
动作：僧人正在龛内念诵真言 — 沉着镇定、尽管周遭混乱、双唇仍以平稳节奏塑出音节。随后巨柱坍塌的 SHOCKWAVE（冲击波）抵达龛洞 — 细尘沿框住他的龛壁雕石泻落、上方与开口四周的岩石上碎石与小块喀啦松脱、龛洞本身清晰可见地震颤。龛内，僧人的 CONCENTRATION（专注）断裂了一瞬 — 双唇顿挫、真言磕绊、闭阖的双眼挤得更紧、眉头因用力而皱起。他奋力回神 — 双唇重续、音节跌撞着回到节奏，但平稳的经文被震动了。龛内尘埃飘过他眼前、落上他的肩。法印（mudra）上指节隐约发白。他这份掌控的脆弱是真正的头号焦点。全程画面剧烈震颤。
