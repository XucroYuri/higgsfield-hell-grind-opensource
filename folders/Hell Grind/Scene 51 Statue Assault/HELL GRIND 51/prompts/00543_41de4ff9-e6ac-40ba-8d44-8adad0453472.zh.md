Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (applies to every shot in this scene): VIOLENT handheld operator throughout — heavy aggressive jitter, visible reframing tremor, breathing rig, reactive whip-corrections. Action-urgency feel cranked up. Even in close-ups the frame shakes hard. The camera operator does NOT travel through space — no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. The camera holds its planted position; only handheld shake.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame glow at the bases of columns. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. To the right of the main column the wall transitions into raw rugged cave rock. Dominant tones: cold blue-gray stone (60), deep shadow black (30), warm amber lamp flame (10).

CHARACTERS:

ROKO — man, mid-20s, dark messy shoulder-length hair, faint mustache, red abraded marks across the bridge of his nose. Wears a full translucent blood-red crystalline armor suit with jagged crystal spikes erupting from shoulders and forearms. Crystal helmet RETRACTED — face fully exposed.
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips, thin red scar running through left eyebrow down across the cheek. Wears a grey cropped hooded utility jacket over a black turtleneck top, black cargo pants, brown leather belt, olive thigh pouch holster on right leg.
PROPS: RAIN's flat matte-dark hovering board parks autonomously beside her at hip-height. She does not touch it.

COLUMN STAGING (locked):
RAIN — pressed to the LEFT edge of the central column. Board hovering parked at her left hip.
JAX — back flat to the CENTER of the column.
ROKO — pressed to the RIGHT side of the column, body half hidden behind the column's right edge.

SCENE 51D — TREMOR AND REVEAL.

BEAT 1 (medium-wide on the trio at the column, planted camera, VIOLENT handheld shake): Open on the same anchored composition — RAIN frame-left half-tucked behind Jax in depth, JAX center back-to-column with head tilted up, ROKO frame-right half-hidden behind the column's right edge. A sudden massive impact hits off-frame — hard low rumble shudders the entire hall. Dust cascades from the tops of columns. Oil-lamp flames jolt and flicker, some snuffing out. Small stone pebbles bounce and skitter on the tile floor. The shaft of light flickers as dust thickens through it. RAIN's hovering board wobbles low at her hip from the shockwave. All three flinch hard, eyes snapping wide, heads jerking. JAX's gaze sweeps the ceiling, ROKO's gaze locks on the cave wall to his right, RAIN — first to recover — twists her torso left, head turning sharply toward the column's left edge as she begins to lean out to look. Her body weight shifts onto her front foot, hand catching the column for balance. Camera shudders violently throughout, settling with her motion as she initiates the peek.

BEAT 2 (cut, reverse angle, medium close on RAIN, planted camera, hard handheld shake): Camera is now planted on the OPEN side of the column, looking back toward Rain's hiding spot. The massive carved stone column dominates the RIGHT side of frame, vertical dark mass filling roughly one-third of the image. RAIN snaps out from behind the column's edge into frame-center-left in a SHARP, fast head-and-shoulder thrust — no hesitation. Three-quarter front toward camera, head and shoulders past the column edge. Background behind her: a deep dark rugged cave-rock wall, mostly black, faint oil-lamp glow tucked into the far bottom-left corner. Cold rim light catches the silver of her hair, left side of her face in soft shadow toward camera, scarred eye visible, lips parted with breath catching. Sub-beat A — SNAP PEEK: head whips out fast past the column edge, eyes lock straight ahead at eye-level, immediately registering something directly in front of her. Pupils tighten, jaw drops a hair, sharp inhale. Sub-beat B — UPWARD NOTICE: a fraction of a second later her gaze jumps UPWARD, chin lifting, eyes climbing as if catching something else higher up — alarmed recognition. Sub-beat C — WHIP BACK: she snaps her head and shoulders back hard behind the column, hair flaring with the motion, body slamming flat into stone behind cover, eyes wide. Three discrete head movements — out, up, back — sharp and reactive, not smooth. Frame trembles violently throughout, hardest on the snap-out and whip-back.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、带有眼神光（catch-lights）的湿润鲜活双眼、可见的呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，本场景每个镜头通用）：全程剧烈的手持操作者——频繁强烈的抖动、可见的重新取景震颤、呼吸式机架、反应性鞭扫纠正。动作紧迫感拉满。即便在特写中，画框也剧烈晃动。摄影操作者不在空间中位移——不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄。摄影机保持固定位置；仅手持抖动。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱分列两侧，向雾中延展没入。石板地面。柱基处有低矮橙色火焰的小油灯发光。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从高高在上的岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。主柱右侧的墙壁过渡为裸露粗犷的洞穴岩体。主色调：冰冷蓝灰色石面（60）、深阴影黑（30）、暖琥珀色油灯火焰（10）。

角色（CHARACTERS）：
JAX——黑人男性，二十多岁，漂白金黄寸头，透明裹式安全护目镜，前额一道新鲜的红色擦伤。身穿黑色战术连帽夹克带灰色拼接、橄榄绿工装裤、无指手套、带黄色拉链点缀的棕色腰带、磨损的战斗靴。
ROKO——男性，二十多岁，深色凌乱垂肩长发，淡胡须，鼻梁上红色擦伤痕迹。身穿通体红色半透明水晶装甲，肩部与前臂爆发参差水晶利刺。水晶头盔已收起（RETRACTED）——脸部完全暴露。
RAIN——年轻女性，二十岁出头，银白金波波头带深黑发尾，一道细红疤贯穿左眉垂过脸颊。身穿黑色高领内搭外罩灰色短款连帽工装夹克、黑色工装裤、棕色腰带、右腿束橄榄绿大腿收纳枪套。
道具（PROPS）：RAIN 的扁平哑光深色悬浮板自主停靠在她身旁齐髋高度处。她并不触碰它。

石柱走位（COLUMN STAGING，锁定）：
RAIN——紧贴中央石柱的左缘。滑板悬空停靠在她左髋旁。
JAX——后背平贴石柱中央。
ROKO——紧贴石柱右侧，身体半隐在石柱右缘之后。

场景 51D——震颤与揭示（TREMOR AND REVEAL）。

拍 1（BEAT 1，中全景对准石柱旁三人，固定摄影机，剧烈手持抖动）：开场保持同一锚定构图——RAIN 画面左侧半藏于 Jax 身后纵深处，JAX 居中背贴石柱、头仰起，ROKO 画面右侧半隐于石柱右缘之后。一阵突如其来的巨大冲击落在画框外——低沉重响震动整座巨厅。尘土自柱顶倾泻。油灯火焰猛晃闪烁，有些熄灭。小石砾在地砖上弹跳、飞滑。光柱随着尘土变浓而闪烁。RAIN 的悬浮板被冲击波震得在她髋边低低摇晃。三人全都猛地一颤，双眼圆睁，头颅猛甩。JAX 的目光扫向天花板，ROKO 的目光锁定他右侧的洞穴岩壁，RAIN——最先恢复——扭转躯干向左，头锐利地转向石柱左缘，开始探身张望。她的身体重心移到前脚，手抓住石柱保持平衡。摄影机全程剧烈震颤，随她发动窥望的动作而落定。

拍 2（BEAT 2，切。，反向角度，中近景对准 RAIN，固定摄影机，剧烈的手持抖动）：摄影机现在固定于石柱敞开的这一侧，回头望向 Rain 的藏身处。巨大的雕花石柱作为垂直的暗色体量占据画面右侧，约占整幅画面三分之一。RAIN 急剧地从石柱边缘后猛出，以锐利、迅疾的头肩探出动作进入画面中央偏左——毫无迟疑。3/4 正对镜头，头与肩探过石柱边缘。她身后背景：深黑粗犷的洞穴岩壁，大多漆黑，远左下角透进微弱的油灯光晕。冷轮廓光接出她银发的反光，左脸朝向镜头处于柔和的阴影中，带疤的眼睛可见，双唇张开、呼吸凝滞。子拍 A——猛然窥望（SNAP PEEK）：头快速甩过石柱边缘，双眼锁定齐眼高度的正前方，瞬间接收到正前方的某个东西。瞳孔收紧，下巴微张，锐利地吸气。子拍 B——向上察觉（UPWARD NOTICE）：零点几秒后她的目光跳出向上，下巴上抬，双眼攀升，仿佛接住另一个更高的东西——警觉地认出。子拍 C——鞭扫回头（WHIP BACK）：她猛烈地把头和肩甩回石柱后，头发随动作扬起，身体猛地平拍进掩体后的石面上，双眼圆睁。三次分明的头部动作——探出、向上、收回——锐利而反应本能，而非平滑。画框全程剧烈颤抖，在猛扫而出与鞭扫回头时最甚。
