Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): COMBAT JOURNALISM VÉRITÉ — as if filmed in real time by an embedded amateur operator hiding among the architecture, struggling to keep the action in frame. MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld throughout — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections, frame nearly destabilized at all times. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed within a shot: rotational pan/tilt to follow action (including overshooting, correcting, lost-and-found tracking), lens-driven zoom, rack focus.

VÉRITÉ FOREGROUND RULE: In every shot of this scene, the FOREGROUND of frame is partially obstructed by environmental elements out of focus — pieces of stone column, edges of cave-rock outcrops, falling dust particles, debris fragments, oil-lamp glow, hazy atmosphere — passing through or sitting at the edges of frame. The operator's position is partially blocked, and they are filming AROUND or PAST these foreground objects. This creates a layered cluttered vérité aesthetic — we feel the operator is hiding/embedded in the environment, not in a clear position.

VÉRITÉ TRACKING RULE: The camera frequently OVERSHOOTS the action, briefly LOSES the subject, then recovers — imperfect human tracking, not smooth professional follow. Pans correct themselves mid-motion, tilts overshoot and pull back, zooms hunt for the right framing.
