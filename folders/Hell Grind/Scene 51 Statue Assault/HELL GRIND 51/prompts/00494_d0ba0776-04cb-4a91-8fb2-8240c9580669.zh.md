## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone-carved giant: weathered granite-grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine hairline cracks, fine dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, visible breath. The stone giant is animated with weight and intent; menace reads through head angle, silhouette, deliberate stillness of stone catching cold light.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (all three shots in this scene): Witness-style documentary handheld, amateur vérité aesthetic, 8K photorealistic rendering. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Only rotational moves (tilt, minor pan) and lens-driven zoom allowed. VIOLENT handheld shake throughout each shot, peaking on impacts and on the roar. Imperfect raw framing.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set carved eyes, expressionless stone face. Stone armored cuirass over tunic, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in right hand, left hand empty. Weathered grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks. Moves with crushing weight, slow and deliberate.

SCENE 51E — STRIKE SEQUENCE (three shots, single prompt).

SHOT 1 — SWORD TRACKER (low-angle close-up).
Framing: Camera planted at ground level near the base of the column the giant is about to strike. Low-angle close-up. The base of a HALL COLUMN dominates the FOREGROUND of frame, slightly soft, vertical mass running up the side of the image. Beyond it in middle ground we see the giant's stone sword in close detail — fine cracks, weathered edge, mossy patina along the spine of the blade. The giant's wrist and gripped hand visible at the upper edge of frame, towering body implied above.
Action: The stone SWORD lifts in a slow methodical windup — camera tilts UP, tracking the blade as it rises high overhead. Held at the apex for a beat. Then the blade SLAMS DOWN in a slow heavy arc — camera tilts back DOWN with the motion, following the sword as it crashes into the column right next to camera. Bone-deep concussive impact at the moment of contact, dust explodes from the strike point, fragments spray outward past camera. Frame jolts violently with the shockwave. Cold rim light catches the edge of the descending blade.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — COLUMN CHUNK FALL (close-up on column).
Framing: Tight close-up on the upper-mid section of a hall column — weathered carved stone, fine relief patterns, mossy patina in crevices. The column fills frame vertically, dominant subject. Cold rim light from the ceiling fissure catches the surface texture. Background behind: hazy depth of the hall, soft out-of-focus.
Action: The column stands intact for a held beat. Then SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT — an unseen blade smashes into the column from off-frame, the stone surface fractures violently at the point of impact. A LARGE CHUNK of stone breaks off, hairline cracks racing outward across the surface. The chunk TUMBLES forward and downward through frame, falling with real mass-correct gravity, rotating slowly as it descends. Camera tilts down with it slightly, tracking the fall. The chunk SLAMS into the stone-tiled floor at the bottom of frame with a thunderous secondary impact — stone-on-stone shatter, breaking into smaller pieces on landing, dust kicks up violently, debris skitters across tiles. Frame jolts violently on both impacts. Dust hangs heavy in the air after.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — ROAR (low-angle close-up, three-quarter angle, heavy telephoto compression).
Framing: Extreme low angle from ground level, looking steeply UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE (semi-profile — neither pure front nor pure side, partial face and partial shoulder visible). HEAVY TELEPHOTO COMPRESSION — long focal length stacks the giant against the rock ceiling behind him, flattening depth. The giant is so massive that the frame CROPS HIM AT THE WAIST — we see only his upper body from waist up: belt, cuirass, shoulders, head. His lower body and the ground are off-frame below the bottom edge. The crop itself signals his scale — the camera cannot contain him. Cold pale shaft of light from the ceiling fissure silhouettes his back contour, rim catching the edge of his three-quarter profile. Surrounding architecture compressed and out of focus around edges. The angle is mythic, monumental, oppressive.
Action: The Giant tilts his huge stone head BACK, mouth gaping wide in carved stone, and unleashes a deep guttural ROAR upward at the ceiling. Stone face contorted in a death-mask howl, brow heavy, deep-set carved eyes wide. Dust shakes loose from his shoulders and the cracks of his armor, drifting down through cold light. Camera shake reaches peak intensity at the roar. Held for the full duration of the howl.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。石雕巨像：风化的花岗岩灰色石面、缝隙间青灰色的苔藓绿锈斑、细如毫发的裂纹、细微的积尘。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、可见的呼吸。石巨像被赋予重量与意志而活；威胁感通过头部的角度、剪影、石面接住冷光时那份刻意的静定来传达。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，本场景三个镜头通用）：目击式纪实手持，业余真实感（vérité）美学，8K 照片级写实渲染。操作者在每个镜头中都静止——不得走动、不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄。只允许旋转式移动（上下摇、轻微横摇）与镜头驱动的变焦。每个镜头全程剧烈的手持抖动，在冲击与咆哮时达到顶峰。不完美的原始取景。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱。石板地面。油灯在柱基处发光。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。

巨石像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨形雕石武士。光头，浓重凶悍的眉骨，深陷的雕凿双目，面无表情的石脸。束腰外衣之上披着石雕的铠甲胸甲，宽石腰带，缠绕的手腕。右手持一把长石剑，左手空空。风化的灰色石面，苔藓绿锈，细纹裂痕。以碾压般的重量活动，缓慢而从容。

场景 51E——挥击序列（STRIKE SEQUENCE，三个镜头，单条提示）。

镜头 1——剑轨跟拍（SWORD TRACKER，低角度特写）。
取景（Framing）：摄影机固定于巨像即将挥击的那根石柱底部附近的地面高度。低角度特写。一根大厅石柱的柱基占据画框前景，略柔焦，垂直的体量沿画面一侧向上延伸。越过它，在中景处我们看到巨像的石剑的近景细节——细纹裂痕、风化的刃口、刀刃背脊上的苔藓绿锈。巨像的手腕与被握住的手在画面上缘可见，上方是暗示出的高大体量。
动作（Action）：石剑以缓慢机械的蓄势抬起——摄影机向上摇，追踪刀锋高举过头顶。在最高点停驻一拍。随后刀锋以缓慢沉重的弧线狠狠劈下——摄影机随之向下摇回，跟随长剑砸进摄影机正旁的这根石柱。接触瞬间是直透骨髓的冲击，撞击点爆起尘土，碎片向摄影机外侧飞溅。画框随冲击波剧烈震荡。冷轮廓光接住下劈刀锋的边缘。

切。

镜头 2——碎石坠落（COLUMN CHUNK FALL，石柱特写）。
取景（Framing）：紧紧特写大厅一根石柱的上中段——风化的雕石、精细的浮雕纹饰、缝隙里的苔藓绿锈。石柱垂直填满画面，是主导主体。来自天花板裂隙的冷轮廓光接住表面纹理。身后背景：巨厅雾蒙的深处，柔和失焦。
动作（Action）：石柱完好地矗立一拍。随后突然爆发巨大的冲击——一柄看不见的刀锋从画框外砸进石柱，石面在撞击点猛烈炸裂。一大块碎石劈裂下来，毫发般的裂纹向表面四面八方疾驰蔓延。这块碎石向前下方翻落，穿过画面，带着真正符合质量的重力坠落，下落中缓缓旋转。摄影机随之轻微下摇，跟踪这次坠落。碎石以震耳欲聋的二次冲击轰然砸进画面底部的石板地面——石对石的碎裂，落地时碎成更小的碎片，尘土猛烈扬起，残屑在地砖上四散飞滑。画框在两记冲击处都剧烈震荡。之后尘埃滞重地悬在空气中。

切。

镜头 3——咆哮（ROAR，低角度特写，3/4 角度，重度长焦压缩）。
取景（Framing）：自地面高度的极端低角度，陡峻地向上对准处于 3/4 角度（半侧——非纯正面亦非纯侧面，可见部分面孔、部分肩膀）的巨像。重度长焦压缩——长焦焦距把巨像叠压在他身后的岩顶天花板上，压平纵深。巨像是如此庞大，以至于画框把他齐腰裁切——我们只看到他的上半身：腰带、胸甲、肩、头。他的下半身与地面都在画面下缘之下、画框外。这种裁切本身就在暗示他的尺度——摄影机装不下他。来自天花板裂隙的冰冷苍白光柱映出他背部轮廓的剪影，轮廓光接住他 3/4 侧脸的边缘。四周的建筑被压缩并在边缘处失焦。这个角度神话而宏大，压迫感十足。
动作（Action）：巨像把那巨大的石头头颅仰起，雕石嘴大张，朝天花板发出一声低沉的喉音咆哮。石脸扭曲成一副死亡面具般的嚎叫，眉骨沉重，深陷的雕凿双目圆睁。尘土自他肩头与铠甲裂隙处抖落，穿过冷光向下飘散。摄影机抖动在咆哮时达到最强。在整个嚎叫的完整时长内保持住。
