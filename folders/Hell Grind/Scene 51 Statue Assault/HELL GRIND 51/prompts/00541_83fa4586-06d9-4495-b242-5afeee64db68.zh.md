Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (applies to every shot in this scene): VIOLENT handheld operator throughout — heavy aggressive jitter, visible reframing tremor, breathing rig, reactive whip-corrections. The camera operator does NOT travel through space — no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. The camera holds its planted position; only handheld shake.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame glow at the bases of columns. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. To the right of the main column the wall transitions into raw rugged cave rock. Dominant tones: cold blue-gray stone (60), deep shadow black (30), warm amber lamp flame (10).

CHARACTERS:
JAX — Black man, mid-20s, bleached yellow buzz-cut hair, clear wraparound safety glasses, fresh red graze on his forehead. Wears a black tactical hooded jacket with grey paneling, olive cargo pants, fingerless gloves, brown belt with yellow zipper accents, worn combat boots.
ROKO — man, mid-20s, dark messy shoulder-length hair, faint mustache, red abraded marks across the bridge of his nose. Wears a full translucent blood-red crystalline armor suit with jagged crystal spikes erupting from shoulders and forearms. Crystal helmet RETRACTED — face fully exposed.
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips, thin red scar running through left eyebrow down across the cheek. Wears a grey cropped hooded utility jacket over a black turtleneck top, black cargo pants, brown leather belt, olive thigh pouch holster on right leg.
PROPS: RAIN's flat matte-dark hovering board parks autonomously beside her at hip-height. She does not touch it.

COLUMN STAGING (locked):
RAIN — pressed to the LEFT edge of the central column with her back and shoulder. Board hovering parked at her left hip.
JAX — back flat to the CENTER of the column.
ROKO — pressed to the RIGHT side of the column, body half hidden behind the column's right edge.

SCENE 51D — TREMOR AND REVEAL.

BEAT 1 (medium-wide on the trio at the column, planted camera, VIOLENT handheld shake — duration 2-3 seconds, brief and punchy): Open on the anchored composition — RAIN frame-left half-tucked behind Jax in depth, JAX center back-to-column head tilted up, ROKO frame-right half-hidden behind the column's right edge. A sudden massive impact slams off-frame — hard low rumble shudders the hall. Dust cascades from the column tops. Oil-lamp flames jolt, some snuffing out. Pebbles skitter across the tile floor. RAIN's board wobbles at her hip. All three flinch hard and snap their heads outward — JAX scans the ceiling, ROKO eyes the cave wall to his right, RAIN turns sharply toward the column's left edge. Camera shudders violently throughout. Brief, fast cut.

BEAT 2 (cut, reverse angle, medium close on RAIN over-shoulder peek, planted camera, MAXIMUM handheld shake — most violent in the entire scene, frame nearly destabilized): Camera planted on the OPEN side of the column. The massive carved stone column dominates the RIGHT side of frame as a vertical dark mass. RAIN does NOT lean her body out into the open — she stays tucked against the column, twisting only her upper body around its left edge. Her left shoulder leads first, jacket fabric scraping the stone, then her head pivots to look past her own shoulder — peeking around the column from over/behind her shoulder rather than emerging chest-first. We see her three-quarter, body still mostly behind cover, only shoulder, side of head and face visible past the edge. Background behind her: dark rugged cave-rock wall, mostly black, faint oil-lamp glow in the far bottom-left corner. Cold rim catches the silver of her hair, left side of face in shadow toward camera, scarred eye visible. Sub-beat A — SNAP PEEK: shoulder leads, head whips around fast past the column edge, eyes lock straight ahead at her own eye-level, instant registration of something directly in front. Pupils tighten, sharp inhale, lips part. Sub-beat B — UPWARD NOTICE: a fraction of a second later her gaze jumps UPWARD, chin lifting, eyes climbing as if catching something else higher up — alarmed recognition. Sub-beat C — WHIP BACK: she snaps her shoulder and head back hard behind the column, hair flaring with the motion, body slamming flat to stone, eyes wide. Three discrete head movements — out, up, back — sharp and reactive, not smooth. Frame shakes harder than any other shot in the scene, particularly violent on the snap-out and the whip-back.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、带有眼神光（catch-lights）的湿润鲜活双眼、可见的呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，本场景每个镜头通用）：全程剧烈的手持操作者——频繁强烈的抖动、可见的重新取景震颤、呼吸式机架、反应性鞭扫纠正。摄影操作者不在空间中位移——不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄。摄影机保持固定位置；仅手持抖动。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱分列两侧，向雾中延展没入。石板地面。柱基处有低矮橙色火焰的小油灯发光。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从高高在上的岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。主柱右侧的墙壁过渡为裸露粗犷的洞穴岩体。主色调：冰冷蓝灰色石面（60）、深阴影黑（30）、暖琥珀色油灯火焰（10）。

角色（CHARACTERS）：
JAX——黑人男性，二十多岁，漂白金黄寸头，透明裹式安全护目镜，前额一道新鲜的红色擦伤。身穿黑色战术连帽夹克带灰色拼接、橄榄绿工装裤、无指手套、带黄色拉链点缀的棕色腰带、磨损的战斗靴。
ROKO——男性，二十多岁，深色凌乱垂肩长发，淡胡须，鼻梁上红色擦伤痕迹。身穿通体红色半透明水晶装甲，肩部与前臂爆发参差水晶利刺。水晶头盔已收起（RETRACTED）——脸部完全暴露。
RAIN——年轻女性，二十岁出头，银白金波波头带深黑发尾，一道细红疤贯穿左眉垂过脸颊。身穿黑色高领内搭外罩灰色短款连帽工装夹克、黑色工装裤、棕色腰带、右腿束橄榄绿大腿收纳枪套。
道具（PROPS）：RAIN 的扁平哑光深色悬浮板自主停靠在她身旁齐髋高度处。她并不触碰它。

石柱走位（COLUMN STAGING，锁定）：
RAIN——背部与肩部紧贴中央石柱的左缘。滑板悬空停靠在她左髋旁。
JAX——后背平贴石柱中央。
ROKO——紧贴石柱右侧，身体半隐在石柱右缘之后。

场景 51D——震颤与揭示（TREMOR AND REVEAL）。

拍 1（BEAT 1，中全景对准石柱旁三人，固定摄影机，剧烈手持抖动——时长 2-3 秒，短促有力）：开场保持锚定构图——RAIN 画面左侧半藏于 Jax 身后纵深处，JAX 居中背贴石柱、头仰起，ROKO 画面右侧半隐于石柱右缘之后。一阵突如其来的巨大冲击猛砸在画框外——低沉重响震动巨厅。尘土自柱顶倾泻。油灯火焰猛晃，有些熄灭。小石砾在地砖上飞滑。RAIN 的滑板在她髋边摇晃。三人全都猛地一颤、猛甩头颅——JAX 扫视天花板，ROKO 注视他右侧的洞穴岩壁，RAIN 锐利地转向石柱左缘。摄影机全程剧烈震颤。短暂、快速的剪切。

拍 2（BEAT 2，切。，反向角度，中近景对准 RAIN 探肩窥望，固定摄影机，极致手持抖动——本场景中最剧烈的一枚，画面近乎失去稳定）：摄影机固定于石柱敞开的一侧。巨大的雕花石柱作为垂直的暗色体量占据画面右侧。RAIN 并不把身体探进敞开的空场——她贴身藏于石柱，只把上半身扭绕过它的左缘。她的左肩领先，夹克布料刮擦石面，随后她的头转动越过自己肩膀探看——从她肩头后绕过石柱窥望，而不是挺胸显身。我们看到她的 3/4身，身体仍大半藏在掩体后，只有肩膀、侧脸与面孔探出边缘。她身后背景：粗犷的黑暗洞穴岩壁，大多漆黑，远左下角透进微弱的油灯光晕。冷轮廓光接出她银发的反光，左脸朝向镜头处于阴影，带疤的眼睛可见。子拍 A——猛然窥望（SNAP PEEK）：肩领先行，头快速甩过石柱边缘，双眼锁定正前方与自身视线齐平处，瞬间接收到正前方的某个东西。瞳孔收紧，锐利地吸气，双唇张开。子拍 B——向上察觉（UPWARD NOTICE）：零点几秒后她的目光跳出向上，下巴上抬，双眼攀升，仿佛接住另一个更高的东西——警觉地认出。子拍 C——鞭扫回头（WHIP BACK）：她猛烈地把肩与头甩回石柱后，头发随动作扬起，身体猛地平拍到石面上，双眼圆睁。三次分明的头部动作——探出、向上、收回——锐利而反应本能，而非平滑。画面比本场景其他任何镜头抖得都更厉害，尤其猛扫而出与鞭扫回头时。
