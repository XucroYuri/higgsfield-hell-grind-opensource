Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld throughout in EVERY single shot — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections, frame nearly destabilized at all times, the most extreme shake of the entire scene. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed within a shot: rotational pan/tilt to follow action, lens-driven zoom, rack focus. Even in close-ups the frame trembles intensely without exception.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Atmospheric haze throughout. The cave-rock walls climb dramatically upward toward a deep ceiling fissure HIGH OVERHEAD — well above the stone hall's ceiling level, the BELL hangs in the upper crack of the rock, far above the heroes' position.

CHARACTERS:
JAX — Black man, mid-20s, bleached yellow buzz-cut hair, clear wraparound safety glasses, fresh red graze on his forehead. Black tactical hooded jacket with grey paneling, olive cargo pants, fingerless gloves, brown belt with yellow zipper accents.
BELL — antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern, small clapper hanging beneath. Hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored deep into the rock of the ceiling fissure. POSITIONED FAR HIGH OVERHEAD — above the level of the hall's stone ceiling, in the upper rock crack of the cavern, requiring an extreme high-angle shot to reach.

PROPS:
JAX's SLINGSHOT — heavy-duty modern tactical slingshot, machined dark metal frame, reinforced wrist brace forearm support, heavy elastic bands, leather pouch.
PLASTIQUE EXPLOSIVE — small lump of pliable putty-like military explosive, off-white/beige in color. Activated by being warmed and shaped — Jax keeps it in his mouth to soften it with body heat and saliva, then spits out the warmed pliable wad and quickly molds it into a ball-shaped charge in his hand before loading the slingshot.

SCENE 51H — JAX'S RUSHED SHOT (three shots, single prompt, in this exact order). EVERY SHOT FEATURES MAXIMUM VIOLENT HANDHELD SHAKE WITHOUT EXCEPTION.

SHOT 1 — JAX SPITS THE PLASTIQUE INTO HIS HAND (50mm CU on Jax behind column, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Tight close-up on JAX, back pressed flat against the side of a stone column, body tucked into cover. The vertical mass of the column behind him fills the background. Cold rim light from above catches the contour of his face, safety glasses reflecting faint cold light. Sweat beading at his temple, fresh graze on forehead.
Action: Jax's right hand is raised cupped in front of his mouth, palm open. His jaw is working — the plastique already in his mouth, having been warmed against his cheek with body heat. Eyes flicker with HESITATION and DOUBT — brow tight, breath quick and shallow, jaw clenched between chew motions. He is not calm; he is RUSHED, improvising under pressure, second-guessing the plan even as he executes it. Then in a hurried motion he SPITS the warmed pliable wad of plastique out of his mouth into his open palm — a small off-white lump landing wet in his hand. He glances down at the lump for a fast micro-beat — eyes narrow with uncertainty — then quickly works it between his fingers, rolling and pressing it into a rough BALL-SHAPED CHARGE in haste, fingers moving fast, not careful. He doesn't take a moment to admire it — he is already turning his attention back toward the column edge, breath quickening. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout — maximum handheld jitter in this tight close-up, frame never stabilizing.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — JAX LEANS OUT AND FIRES UPWARD (medium close-up, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Medium close-up on Jax at the column edge, planted camera at eye-level. The vertical mass of the column on frame-LEFT, Jax leaning out from cover into frame-CENTER-RIGHT. Hall depth visible in shallow soft-focus background.
Action: Jax raises his SLINGSHOT — heavy-duty tactical model, dark metal frame, his left forearm braced through the wrist support, the leather pouch loaded with the freshly-formed plastique ball. He LEANS OUT past the column edge in a hurried motion, exposing his upper body. He extends his left arm forward gripping the slingshot frame, raises his right hand to draw the elastic bands BACK, the heavy bands stretching taut, tension visible in his arm muscles. His aim is angled HIGH UPWARD — the slingshot pointed toward the ceiling fissure far above where the bell hangs, his head tilted up tracking the high target through his safety glasses. Brief micro-pause for the calibration — but rushed, doubt still flickering across his face. Then he RELEASES — fingers letting go, the elastic bands SNAP forward with brutal speed, the plastique ball LAUNCHING upward out of the pouch and shooting away skyward through the upper edge of frame in a streak of motion blur. Jax recoils slightly from the release, shoulder absorbing the snap. His eyes track the projectile's flight upward off-frame, breath held, jaw set in hope. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout — maximum handheld jitter.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — PROFILE CU OF BELL HIGH OVERHEAD, CHARGE MISSES, EXPLOSION CLIMAX (single continuous shot, profile CU with rack focus to background, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Profile close-up on the BELL hanging FAR HIGH OVERHEAD in the upper rock fissure of the cavern — the bell positioned well above the hall's ceiling level, suspended on its iron chain anchored deep into the rock crack above. Bronze body in sharp focus filling frame-CENTER, ornate engraved relief bands visible across its patinated surface, the small clapper hanging beneath. Cold pale daylight pours from the open fissure crack behind the bell, silhouetting it from above. Background behind the bell: the rugged cave-rock wall of the upper fissure in soft focus.

Sub-beat A — BELL HANGING HIGH: The bell hangs still in the cold daylight high above, swaying very faintly from distant tremors. Its altitude reads — we are looking at a target far above the heroes.

Sub-beat B — CHARGE MISSES: Suddenly the PLASTIQUE BALL streaks INTO frame from frame-BOTTOM at high velocity, traveling upward — small dark projectile cutting through the air past the bell, MISSING the bronze body by a margin, motion blur smearing the projectile across frame. The ball continues past the bell out toward the cave-rock wall in the upper fissure background.

Sub-beat C — RACK FOCUS TO WALL: Camera RACK FOCUS shifts — sharpness pulls off the bell in foreground (it goes soft, becoming a blurred bronze shape) and racks deep onto the rugged rock wall of the upper fissure behind, which comes into sharp focus, the ball mid-flight in the new sharp plane.

Sub-beat D — EXPLOSION CLIMAX: The plastique ball IMPACTS the rock wall — instantaneous ignition. The frame is DOMINATED by the EXPLOSION as the focal climax of the entire shot. A massive burst of orange-yellow FIRE erupts outward from the impact point, expanding into a chaotic billowing FIREBALL filling much of the frame, white-hot core at center, flames licking outward, thick black SMOKE plumes mushrooming. Heavy chunks of rock SHATTER and BLOW OUTWARD from the wall in violent shrapnel, fragments tumbling with mass-correct trajectories. Dust and debris CLOUD upward into the cold light from the fissure above. The cold rock face FRACTURES — crater carved into the wall, hairline cracks racing outward. The blurred bell in soft foreground REVERBERATES from the shockwave, swinging on its chain. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY at peak intensity, jolting hard on the explosion impact, the operator clearly thrown by the blast.

The explosion is the headline of the shot — visually overwhelming, the climax. The framing holds on the fireball as it billows. A clean miss — but the destruction is enormous.

CAMERA RIG（本场景）：全程每一个镜头都用「最大强度暴力手持（MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld）」—— 强烈、咄咄逼人的抖动、呼吸式机位、应激性纠正、画面几乎时刻濒临失稳，是全片最极端的晃动。每个镜头中摄影机操作员保持静止 —— 不走路、不推轨、不跟拍、不环移。镜头内允许：旋转式摇镜/俯仰跟随动作、镜头驱动变焦、跟焦。即使在特写中画面也无一例外地剧烈颤抖。

BELL（铜钟）—— 古董铜钟，暗锈蚀的绿铜色表面，饰有华丽凸起的经文与纹样浮雕环带，下方悬着一枚小钟舌。由一条深深锚固进顶部裂隙岩体的沉重铁链悬挂。位置高悬于正上方极远处 —— 高于大厅石天花板的高度，位于洞穴上方岩石裂缝中，需以极端俯视角镜头才能抵达。

PROPS：

SCENE 51H —— JAX 的仓促一射（三个镜头，单条提示词，严格按此顺序）。每一个镜头都无一例外地呈现最大强度暴力手持晃动。

SHOT 1（镜头 1）—— JAX 把塑胶炸药吐进手里（对柱后的 Jax 取 50mm CU，MAXIMUM VIOLENT 手持）。

Action：Jax 右手张开成杯状举在嘴前，掌心张开。他下颌在动 —— 塑胶炸药已经在他嘴里，已被贴着腮帮用体温捂热。眼神中闪过一丝犹豫与疑虑（HESITATION 与 DOUBT）—— 眉头紧锁、呼吸又急又浅、嚼动间隙里下颌紧咬。他并不平静；他是在仓促行事，在压力下即兴发挥，即使一边执行也一边对计划自我怀疑。然后他以一个急促的动作把这块温热可塑的塑胶胶体从嘴里吐进张开的掌中 —— 一团米白色小胶块湿乎乎地落进手里。他低头看了一眼这团胶块，只快速一个微节拍 —— 眯起眼带着不确定 —— 然后迅速在指间揉搓、滚动按压成一个粗略的球形装药，动作仓促、指头又急又不精心。他顾不上端详它 —— 已经把注意力转回柱缘，呼吸加快。摄像全程剧烈晃动 —— 这枚紧特写中的最大手持抖动，画面从不稳定。

SHOT 2（镜头 2）—— JAX 探身向上发射（中近景，MAXIMUM VIOLENT 手持）。
Framing：在柱缘对 Jax 取中近景，固定机位平视。画面左侧是柱子的垂直体量，Jax 从掩体中探身进入画面中右部。背景为浅景深柔和焦点下可辨的大厅纵深。
Action：Jax 抬起弹弓 —— 高强度战术型号、深色金属框架，他左前臂通过手腕支撑架固定，皮制弹囊里已装上刚塑形的塑胶弹球。他以一个急促的动作探出柱缘，暴露上身。他伸展左臂向前握紧弹弓框架，抬起右手向后拉开弹性皮筋，沉重的皮筋绷得笔直，臂上肌肉可见张力。他的瞄准角度大幅朝上 —— 弹弓指向远处上方铜钟悬挂的那道顶缝，头向上仰、隔着护目镜追踪高位目标。为校准做一次短暂的微停顿 —— 但很仓促，疑虑仍在他脸上闪烁。然后他松手 —— 手指放开，弹性皮筋以凶狠速度啪地向前弹出，塑胶弹球从弹囊中向上弹射而出，沿着画面顶端边缘化作一道运动模糊的轨迹射向高空。Jax 微微后坐，肩膀吸收了这股猛弹。他的目光追着射弹的飞行向上落到画面外，屏住呼吸，下颌坚定地带着一丝希望。摄像全程剧烈晃动 —— 最大手持抖动。

切。

SHOT 3（镜头 3）—— 高处正上方铜钟的侧面特写，弹丸落空，爆炸高潮（单次连续镜头，侧面特写配合向背景跟焦，MAXIMUM VIOLENT 手持）。
Framing：对悬在洞穴上方岩石裂隙极深处的铜钟取侧面特写 —— 钟明显高于大厅天花板的高度，由深深锚固在上方岩缝的铁链悬吊。青铜钟体锐利焦点填满画面中央，其锈蚀表面上可见华丽的錾刻浮雕环带，下方小钟舌悬挂。背后敞开的裂隙裂口倾泻下惨白的冷日，从上方勾出它剪影。钟身后方背景：上方裂隙崎岖的洞壁岩面，处于柔和焦点。

Sub-beat A —— 高悬的铜钟：铜钟在远处高空的冷日下静止悬垂，因远处震颤而极轻微摆动。它的海拔可读 —— 我们正看着一个远在主角们之上的目标。

Sub-beat B —— 弹丸落空：骤然间塑胶弹球以高速从画面底部射入，向上飞行 —— 一颗小小的深色射弹切开空气掠过铜钟，与青铜钟体擦肩而失（MISSING），运动模糊在画面中拖出射弹轨迹。弹球越过铜钟继续飞向背景中上方裂隙的洞壁岩面。

Sub-beat D —— 爆炸高潮：塑胶弹球撞上岩壁 —— 瞬时引燃。画面被这场爆炸主导，成为整个镜头的焦点高潮。一大团橙黄色火焰从撞击点向外爆起，扩张成一片混乱翻滚的火球几乎填满整幅画面，中央白热核心、火舌外舔、浓重黑烟柱翻腾升腾。大块岩石被震碎、化作猛烈弹片向外抛四散，碎片以质量正确（mass-correct）的轨迹翻滚坠落。尘土与碎屑在顶上裂隙投下的冷光中向上弥散。冷色岩面开裂 —— 墙上被凿出一个弹坑，发丝样裂纹向外奔蹿。柔和前景中模糊的铜钟被冲击波震得震颤，在链上摆动。摄像以峰值强度剧烈晃动，在爆炸冲击时被狠狠一颠，操作员明显被爆炸抛起。

爆炸是本镜头最重要的看点 —— 视觉上压倒一切，是高潮。构图锁定在翻滚翻腾的火球上。干净利落的一次脱靶 —— 但摧毁的规模是巨大的。
