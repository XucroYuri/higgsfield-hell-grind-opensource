Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld throughout in EVERY single shot — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections, frame nearly destabilized at all times. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed within a shot: rotational pan/tilt (including fast whip-pans), lens-driven zoom, rack focus. WITNESS-STYLE — operator filming from a third-party position to the side, reacting in real time, sometimes losing the action and finding it again.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. The cave-rock walls climb dramatically upward toward a deep ceiling fissure HIGH OVERHEAD — well above the stone hall's ceiling level, the BELL hangs in the upper crack of the rock, far above the heroes' position.

CHARACTERS:
JAX — Black man, mid-20s, bleached yellow buzz-cut hair, clear wraparound safety glasses, fresh red graze on his forehead. Black tactical hooded jacket with grey paneling, olive cargo pants, fingerless gloves, brown belt with yellow zipper accents.
BELL — antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands, small clapper hanging beneath. Hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored deep into the rock of the ceiling fissure. POSITIONED FAR HIGH OVERHEAD.

PROPS:
JAX's SLINGSHOT — heavy-duty modern tactical slingshot, machined dark metal frame, reinforced wrist brace, heavy elastic bands, leather pouch.
PLASTIQUE EXPLOSIVE — small lump of pliable putty-like military explosive, off-white/beige. Jax keeps it in his mouth to soften with body heat, then spits it into his hand and molds into a ball-shaped charge.

SCENE 51H — JAX'S RUSHED SHOT (three shots, single prompt). EVERY SHOT FEATURES MAXIMUM VIOLENT HANDHELD SHAKE.

SHOT 1 — JAX SPITS THE PLASTIQUE INTO HIS HAND (50mm CU, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Tight close-up on JAX, back pressed flat against a stone column. Sweat at temple, fresh graze on forehead. His right hand is raised cupped in front of his mouth, palm open, jaw working — the plastique already in his mouth, warmed against his cheek with body heat. Eyes flicker with HESITATION and DOUBT — brow tight, breath quick, jaw clenched. He is RUSHED, improvising under pressure, second-guessing the plan. In a hurried motion he SPITS the warmed pliable wad of plastique out of his mouth into his open palm — small off-white lump landing wet in his hand. Glances down for a fast micro-beat with uncertainty, then quickly works it between his fingers, rolling and pressing into a rough BALL-SHAPED CHARGE in haste, fingers fast not careful. He doesn't admire it — already turning attention back toward the column edge, breath quickening. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout — maximum handheld jitter, frame never stabilizing.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — JAX FIRES THE SLINGSHOT, LOW ANGLE HALF PROFILE CLOSE-UP (MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: LOW ANGLE HALF PROFILE CLOSE-UP. Camera planted near hip/waist height looking UP at Jax. The angle is HALF PROFILE — three-quarter oblique, his face turned partially toward camera at an angle, neither full front nor full side, showing one cheek, the bridge of his nose, the curve of his jaw, safety glasses at angled perspective. Tight CLOSE-UP framing — head and shoulders dominate, slingshot raised in upper portion of frame. Underside of his jaw catches cold light, cheekbone defined in cold rim, safety glasses reflecting cold daylight from the high fissure overhead. Vertical mass of column behind in heavy soft-focus blur. Low angle makes him loom above the camera, head tilted UP toward the high bell — vertical aim unmistakable.
Action: Jax has leaned out past the column in hurried motion, slingshot already raised. From the low angle in half profile we see his upper body in three-quarter — left forearm extended UPWARD braced through the slingshot's wrist support, right hand drawn BACK pulling elastic bands taut, leather pouch loaded with plastique ball. Head tilted UP, eyes narrowed behind safety glasses, locked on the bell far above. Half profile reveals doubt and hesitation — brow tight, jaw clenched, mouth set hard, breath shallow. He is shooting nearly STRAIGHT UP at a target high overhead — the low angle reinforces the vertical aim. Brief micro-pause for calibration, rushed, uncertain. He RELEASES — fingers let go, elastic bands SNAP forward with brutal speed, plastique ball LAUNCHING upward out of the pouch shooting away skyward through the upper edge of frame in motion blur streak. Jax recoils slightly, shoulder absorbing the snap, head tilting further back to track the projectile. Breath held, jaw set in hope. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout from the low angle.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — WITNESS WHIP-PAN CHASE FROM CROUCH FIRE TO WALL EXPLOSION (single continuous shot, witness-side angle, planted camera with violent whip-pan, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: WITNESS POV from the side — camera planted to the side of Jax's position, far enough to see his full body in the cover, as if a third-party operator filming from across the hall. Vérité documentary aesthetic, the operator clearly amateur-feeling, struggling to track the action in real time.

Sub-beat A — JAX IN CROUCH FIRES: Open on a side-angle MEDIUM-WIDE on JAX. He is in a CROUCH at the column edge — knees bent, body low to the ground, slingshot raised high in his arms angled steeply UPWARD toward the ceiling fissure overhead, elastic bands drawn taut, head tilted up tracking the high target. The vertical mass of the column visible behind him to one side, the open hall extending to the other. Cold rim light catches him from above. Then he RELEASES — elastic bands SNAP forward, plastique ball LAUNCHES upward out of the pouch and shoots away skyward. Jax recoils into his crouch, head jerking back to follow the projectile.

Sub-beat B — VIOLENT WHIP-PAN UP: Camera EXECUTES A VIOLENT WHIP-PAN UPWARD chasing the launched projectile — fast vertical rotational tilt that smears the frame in heavy motion blur. The hall walls and columns blur past as the camera pivots upward at speed. The operator is reacting in real time, scrambling to keep up — the pan briefly LOSES the projectile mid-flight, frame just smeared rock and haze, then catches it again as the camera tilts to find it. The pan is IMPERFECT — overshooting, correcting, jittering. Witness camera energy at maximum chaos.

Sub-beat C — PROJECTILE NEAR THE BELL, MISS: As the camera finds the projectile high in the air, the BELL comes into frame in the deep distance high above — small bronze body hanging from its chain in the upper fissure, cold daylight pouring around it. The plastique ball flies past the bell — MISSING the bronze body by a margin, the projectile clearly cutting through empty air beside it, motion blur smearing the trajectory. The ball continues past out toward the cave-rock wall behind the bell.

Sub-beat D — IMPACT AND EXPLOSION ON THE WALL: The plastique ball IMPACTS the rock wall behind the bell — instantaneous ignition. A massive burst of orange-yellow FIRE erupts outward, expanding into a chaotic billowing FIREBALL, white-hot core, flames licking outward, thick black SMOKE plumes mushrooming. Heavy chunks of rock SHATTER and BLOW OUTWARD in violent shrapnel, fragments tumbling with mass-correct trajectories. The cold rock face FRACTURES — crater carved into the wall, hairline cracks racing outward. The bell in the foreground swings violently from the shockwave on its chain. Camera SHAKES at peak intensity, jolting hard on the explosion impact, the witness operator clearly thrown by the blast — frame jerks back from the shock.

Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout the ENTIRE continuous shot — maximum handheld jitter from start to finish, heavy whip-pan motion, frame never stabilizing. The witness-style amateur framing reads through the imperfect tracking, the lost-and-found projectile chase, the operator's struggle to keep the action in frame. The headline: a clean miss — but the destruction is enormous.

Style：8K IMAX（不译）。照片级真实 —— 无 3D 渲染、无游戏引擎、无游戏过场动画质感。
Cinematography：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
Lighting：仅自然光 —— 逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于背光侧，全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
Color：60:30:10（主色/次色/点缀）。
Camera：物理电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
Skin：毛孔级真实感 —— 汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场灯光匹配的毛孔阴影。
Acting：好莱坞式 —— 反应前的微停顿、精准的眼线、湿润有神带眼神光（catch-lights）的眼睛、可见的呼吸与胸腔起伏。
Physics：尊重重力与惯性 —— 质量有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。无漂浮道具。

Technical：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
Audio：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（本场景）：全程每一个镜头都用「最大强度暴力手持（MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld）」—— 强烈、咄咄逼人的抖动、呼吸式机位、应激性纠正、画面几乎时刻濒临失稳。每个镜头中摄影机操作员保持静止 —— 不走路、不推轨、不跟拍、不环移。镜头内允许：旋转式摇镜/俯仰（包括快速的甩镜）、镜头驱动变焦、跟焦。目击者式（WITNESS-STYLE）—— 操作员侧位于第三方位拍摄，实时作出反应，有时把动作跟丢又重新找到它。

CHARACTERS：
JAX（杰克）—— 黑人男性，20 多岁，漂白金剃短寸头，透明环绕式护目镜，额头上有一道新鲜的红色擦伤。黑色战术连帽夹克配灰色镶板，橄榄色工装裤，露指手套，棕色腰带配黄色拉链饰条。
BELL（铜钟）—— 古董铜钟，暗锈蚀的绿铜色表面，饰有华丽凸起的浮雕环带，下方悬着一枚小钟舌。由一条深深锚固进顶部裂隙岩体的沉重铁链悬挂。位置高悬于正上方极远处。

PROPS：
JAX 的弹弓（SLINGSHOT）—— 高强度现代战术弹弓，机加工的深色金属框架，加固手腕支撑，重型弹性皮筋，皮制弹囊。
塑胶炸药（PLASTIQUE EXPLOSIVE）—— 一小团可塑、油腻子状军用炸药，米白/米色。Jax 将其含在口中利用体温软化，然后吐到手里捏成球形装药。

SCENE 51H —— JAX 的仓促一射（三个镜头，单条提示词）。每一个镜头都呈现最大强度暴力手持晃动。

SHOT 1（镜头 1）—— JAX 把塑胶炸药吐进手里（50mm CU，MAXIMUM VIOLENT 手持）。
对 JAX 取紧特写，背部平贴一根石柱。太阳穴挂着汗，额头有新鲜擦伤。他右手张开成杯状举在嘴前，掌心张开，下颌在动 —— 塑胶炸药已经在他嘴里，已被贴在腮帮上用体温捂热。眼神中闪过一丝犹豫与疑虑 —— 眉头紧锁、呼吸急促、下颌紧咬。他在仓促行事，在压力下即兴发挥，对计划自我怀疑。他以一个急促的动作把这块温热可塑的塑胶胶体从嘴里吐进张开的掌中 —— 一团米白色小胶块湿乎乎地落进手里。带着不确定低头瞥一眼，只一个快速微节拍，然后迅速在指间揉搓，滚动按压成粗略的球形装药，动作仓促、指头又急又不精心。他顾不上端详 —— 已经把注意力转回柱缘，呼吸加快。摄像全程剧烈晃动 —— 最大手持抖动，画面从不稳定。

SHOT 3（镜头 3）—— 目击者甩镜追猎：从蹲姿射击到岩壁爆炸（单次连续镜头，目击者侧位，固定机位配合暴力甩镜，MAXIMUM VIOLENT 手持）。
Framing：侧面目击者视角（WITNESS POV）—— 摄影机安置在 Jax 位置的侧方，远到足以看到他掩体中的全身，仿佛一位第三方操作员从大厅另一侧拍摄。写实纪录（vérité）纪录片美学，操作员明显业余、笨拙，实时艰难地追踪动作。

Sub-beat A —— 蹲姿 JAX 射击：以 JAX 的侧面中全景开场。他蹲伏在柱缘 —— 膝盖弯曲、身体贴地压得很低，弹弓高举双臂中、以陡峭角度朝上对准头顶的裂隙，皮筋拉得笔直，头后仰追踪高位目标。柱子垂直体量在一侧他身后可见，开阔的大厅向另一侧延伸。上空冷色轮廓光落到他身上。然后他松手 —— 弹性皮筋啪地向前弹出，塑胶弹球从弹囊中向上弹射、射向高空。Jax 缩蹲回去，头猛甩回头去看射弹。

Sub-beat C —— 射弹逼近铜钟、落空：当摄影机在高空中找回那颗射弹时，铜钟进入画面 —— 在上方裂隙深处，小小的青铜钟体从它的铁链上悬下，冷日环绕着它倾泻。塑胶弹球掠过铜钟 —— 与青铜钟体擦肩而失，射弹明显只是切过它旁侧的空间空隙，运动模糊拖开轨迹。弹球继续掠过，飞向铜钟背后洞壁岩面的方向。

Sub-beat D —— 击中岩壁与爆炸：塑胶弹球击中铜钟后方岩壁 —— 瞬时引燃。一大团橙黄色火焰向外爆起，扩张成一片混乱翻滚的火球，核心白热、火舌外舔、浓重黑烟柱翻腾升腾。大块岩石被震碎、化作猛烈弹片向外抛四散，碎片以质量正确（mass-correct）的轨迹翻滚坠落。冷色岩面开裂 —— 墙上被凿出一个弹坑，发丝样裂纹向外奔蹿。前景中的铜钟被冲击波拽得在链上剧烈甩摆。摄像以峰值强度晃动，在爆炸冲击时被狠狠一颠，目击者操作员明显被爆炸抛起 —— 画面被冲击震得向后猛抽。
