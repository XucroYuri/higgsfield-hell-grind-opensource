## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone-carved giant: weathered granite-grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks, dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): Witness-style documentary handheld, vérité aesthetic. MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld throughout — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections, frame nearly destabilized. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed within a shot: TILT (vertical rotation) to follow rising/falling action, rotational pan to track lateral action, lens-driven zoom. Imperfect raw framing — operator reacting in real time, sometimes losing the subject and recovering.

TREMOR RULE: When tremors hit, camera shake intensifies, but NO dust cascades on the heroes. Strike impacts produce localized debris at point of contact only.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Atmospheric haze throughout.

CHARACTERS:
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips, scar through left brow and cheek. Grey cropped hooded utility jacket over black turtleneck, black cargo pants, brown belt, olive thigh pouch holster on right leg. Riding her flat matte-dark hovering board.
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set carved eyes, expressionless stone face. Stone armored cuirass over tunic, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in right hand. Weathered grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks. Moves with crushing weight — slow and heavy by human standards but his swings carry massive air-displacement force.
PROPS: RAIN's hovering board — flat matte-dark, snowboard-sized, levitates and accelerates fast.

SCENE 51G — ORBIT AND DODGE (two shots, single prompt, in this exact order). EVERY SHOT FEATURES MAXIMUM VIOLENT HANDHELD SHAKE WITHOUT EXCEPTION.

SHOT 1 — RISING WIDE FROM FLOOR LEVEL THROUGH THE COLUMNS (single continuous shot, planted camera tilting up, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Camera planted at HALL FLOOR LEVEL, side angle to the giant, framed THROUGH the gap between two foreground hall columns — the dark vertical masses of two columns flank the LEFT and RIGHT edges of frame as foreground brackets. Beyond them, in middle ground, the GIANT's full body stands in three-quarter angle. Camera is initially aimed at his body-level — torso/cuirass region of the giant visible.
Action: RAIN ENTERS frame at brutal speed on her hovering board, threading the air at giant's torso level. As she passes, the camera begins a continuous upward TILT, climbing past his cuirass, shoulders, neck, finally landing on the level of his FACE. Rain ORBITS around his head — board curving in tight horizontal arcs, wrapping around his face from one side to the other and back, fast and unpredictable, body crouched and leaning hard into each turn. The giant's huge head TURNS to follow her, eyes tracking, brow lowering in mute fury. He REACHES with his free left hand, massive stone fingers grasping at the air where she just was — but she is already gone. Swipes again, missing again. On the next swipe, his stone hand BATS her aside through sheer mass — air-displacement and partial brush of his fingertips sends her board veering off-course, Rain LURCHING off her trajectory, body fighting to recover the board's balance as she flies off at a reckless angle. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout the entire continuous shot. Through the foreground columns we feel like spying witnesses.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — TELEPHOTO TRACKING RAIN PAST THE NAPE, MISS ON COLUMN (single continuous shot, planted camera with rotational pan, long lens telephoto compression, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Documentary witness camera filmed on LONG-FOCAL-LENGTH TELEPHOTO LENS — heavy spatial compression flattens depth, planes stacked together. Camera planted at distance, side view to the action. Tight framing close on RAIN as the centered subject — vérité close-up, raw documentary capture. The lens TRACKS her flight by ROTATIONAL PAN as she crosses the frame; she stays roughly centered while everything around her moves through frame.
Action: Open on RAIN in close-up on her board mid-flight, body crouched low, hair whipping back, scarred eye visible, jaw set hard, breath visible — vérité telephoto capture. Camera rotationally pans following her flight path. She THREADS PAST the back of the giant's head — the giant's NAPE/back-of-skull enters frame as a massive stone mass passing behind her in the background, telephoto compression making it feel close even though she's at distance from his head. The stone surface of his nape, mossy patina in the neck-folds, the back of his bald skull — all blur past in the depth of focus behind her crouched form.
Then the GIANT begins to TURN, registering her passage behind him — his huge bald head ROTATING in slow heavy delayed reaction, neck stone groaning. As his head completes the turn, his stone sword arm SWINGS UP and around in a wide arc following the rotation, the long stone blade WHIPPING through the space behind Rain — but again she is faster than he can track, already clearing the swing zone. The blade SWEEPS past empty air where she was a fraction of a second ago.
Camera continues panning with Rain. The blade carries through its arc and CRASHES INTO A HALL COLUMN behind her in the middle distance — bone-deep concussive impact, the column SHUDDERS, a chunk of stone shears off at the strike point, dust explodes outward, fragments spray. Telephoto compression makes the impact feel close to Rain even though she has cleared. The column miss reads as collateral damage from the swing he aimed at her. Rain accelerates further, board carrying her forward out of the strike zone. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY throughout, struggling at long focal length to keep her in frame as her movement against the compressed background creates a chaotic vérité visual.

Style（技术底座）：8K IMAX。写实摄影风格（Photorealistic）——不得有 3D 渲染、不得有游戏引擎、不得有游戏过场动画的美感。
Cinematography（摄影）：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
Lighting（光线）：仅自然光 — 逆光背光（contre-jour）、摄影机位于阴影侧、全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
Color（色彩）：60:30:10（主色/次色/点缀）。
Camera（摄影机）：物理电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
Skin（皮肤）：毛孔级写实 — 汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称的痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光线匹配的毛孔阴影。石雕巨像：风化花岗岩灰石、苔藓铜绿、细裂纹、积尘。
Acting（表演）：好莱坞 — 反应前有微小停顿、精确的视线、湿润灵动、带眼神光（catch-lights）的双眼、可见的呼吸与胸口起伏。
Physics（物理）：尊重重力与惯性 — 质量有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有漂浮道具。
Composition（构图）：三分法 + 黄金比例。每个入画的人从第一帧起即在移动。
Continuity（连贯性）：每一切之间角色、道具、环境完全一致。无身份漂移（no identity drift）。
Technical（技术）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。不得抖动。
Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（本场景摄影机装置）：旁观者式纪录手持、真实感美学（vérité aesthetic）。全程最大幅度剧烈手持（MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld）— 沉重猛烈的抖动、呼吸式运镜、反应式修正、画面几乎失稳。每个镜头中操作员保持静止 — 不步行、不推轨、不跟拍、不做弧线运动。镜头内允许：TILT（垂直旋转）追踪升降中的动作、旋转式摇移追踪横向动作、由镜头驱动变焦。不完美的原生取景 — 操作员实时反应，有时跟丢主体再找回。

TREMOR RULE（震动规则）：震动来袭时，摄影机抖动加剧，但不得有灰尘倾泻到主角身上。打击命中仅在接触点产生局部碎片。

LOCATION（地点）— HELL HALL（地狱大厅）：
人工开凿于山体内的广阔石厅。两侧林立巨型的雕花石柱。石板地。柱基油灯微微发光。一道苍白冷光自岩顶裂隙直落而下。全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
RAIN（莱恩）— 年轻女性，二十出头，银铂色波波头带乌黑发尖，左眉与面颊贯穿一道疤。灰褐色短款连帽实用外套内搭黑色高领衫、黑色工装裤、棕色腰带、右腿橄榄绿大腿工具包枪套。骑乘她那块扁平的哑光黑色悬浮滑板。
石巨像（Stone Giant）— 高 20 米的巨型石刻战士。光头、厚重凶恶的眉骨、深雕眼窝、面无表情的石脸。束腰外衣外覆石制铠甲胸甲、宽石腰、缠裹手腕。右手持一把长石剑。风化灰石、苔藓铜绿、细裂。以碾压般的重量移动 — 按人类标准缓慢而沉重，但其挥击携带巨大的位移空气（air-displacement）冲击。
PROPS（道具）：RAIN（莱恩）的悬浮滑板 — 扁平的哑光黑色、单板大小、可快速漂浮与加速。

SCENE 51G — ORBIT AND DODGE（环绕闪避）（两个镜头，单一提示词，按此顺序严格排列）。每一个镜头都必须呈现最大幅度剧烈手持晃动，绝无例外。

镜头 1 — 从地板高度穿过巨柱抬升的广角（单一连续镜头，固定摄影机向上抬头，最大幅度剧烈手持）。
取景：摄影机固定于大厅地板高度，位于巨像侧角度，透过两列前景巨柱之间的缝隙取景 — 两根巨柱的深色垂直体量作为前景框柱分别占据画面左右边缘。柱后、中景处，巨像全身以 3/4 角度站立。摄影机起初对准其身体高度 — 可见巨像的躯干/胸甲区域。
动作：RAIN（莱恩）以狂暴速度进入画面，骑在悬浮滑板上，于巨像躯干高度穿行。经过时，摄影机开始连续向上 TILT（抬头），越过他的胸甲、越过双肩、沿其脖颈而上，最终落在其 FACE（脸）的高度。RAIN（莱恩）环绕他的头部 — 滑板划出紧贴的水平弧线，从一侧绕其面庞至另一侧再折返，快速且不可预测，身体低伏并在每个转弯中猛烈倾斜。巨像巨大的头 TURN（转动）追随之，双眼追踪，眉骨低沉透出无声的盛怒。他用空着的左手 REACH（探抓）过去，巨型石指抓向她刚刚所在之处 — 但她已经离开。他再次挥抓，再次落空。在下一次挥抓中，他的石手以纯粹的质量将她扇开 — 位移空气与指尖的轻扫令她的滑板偏离航线，RAIN（莱恩）被甩离轨迹，身体奋力夺回滑板平衡，以鲁莽的角度飞出。在整个连续镜头中摄影机全程剧烈晃动。透过前景巨柱，我们如同潜伏偷窥的目击者。

切。

镜头 2 — 长焦跟拍 RAIN（莱恩）掠过其后颈、命中巨柱而落空（单一连续镜头，固定摄影机配旋转摇移，长焦长镜头压缩，最大幅度剧烈手持）。
取景：纪录式目击摄影机以长焦距长焦镜头（LONG-FOCAL-LENGTH TELEPHOTO LENS）拍摄 — 强烈的空间压缩压平景深、平面层层堆叠。摄影机于远处固定，侧观动作。紧凑取景，以 RAIN（莱恩）为中心主体 — 真实感特写、原生纪录捕捉。在她横穿画面时，镜头以旋转摇移追踪她的飞行；她大致保持居中，而四周一切在画面中掠过。
动作：开场即以特写对准飞行中、滑板上的 RAIN（莱恩），身体低伏、发丝向后飞甩、可见带疤的眼、下颌紧咬、可见的呼吸 — 真实感长焦捕捉。摄影机沿她的飞行轨迹旋转摇移。她紧贴着巨像后脑穿过 — 巨像的 NAPE（后颈）/后颅骨作为一块巨大石质体量在背景中自她身后掠过，长焦压缩使它虽隔着距离、却似近在眼前。他后颈的石面、颈褶间的苔藓铜绿、他光头后脑勺的背部 — 全部在她低伏的身形身后、景深之内模糊掠过。
随后巨像开始 TURN（转身），察觉她从身后掠过 — 他巨大的光头以缓慢沉重、迟滞的反应 ROTATING（旋转），颈部石骨发出呻吟。当他头完成旋动，他持剑的石臂沿旋转方向划出一道宽弧线上扬环绕，长石刃 WHIPPING（猛挥）穿过 RAIN（莱恩）身后的空间 — 但他又一次追不上她，她已冲出挥击区域。剑刃 SWEEPS（扫过）一瞬前她所在之处的空荡空气。
摄影机继续伴随 RAIN（莱恩）摇移。剑刃划过完整弧度，在她身后中景处 CRASHES INTO A HALL COLUMN（撞入一根大厅巨柱）— 深及骨骼的震颤冲击，巨柱 SHUDDERS（颤抖），打击点处齐整削下一块巨石，尘土向外炸开、碎片飞溅。长焦压缩令这记冲击感觉离 RAIN（莱恩）极近，尽管她已脱身。这根立柱的落空读作他瞄准她的那一挥的附带伤害。RAIN（莱恩）进一步加速，滑板带她向前冲出打击区域。全程摄影机剧烈晃动，在长焦距下竭力将她框入画面，她在被压平背景上的移动制造出混乱的真实感画面。
