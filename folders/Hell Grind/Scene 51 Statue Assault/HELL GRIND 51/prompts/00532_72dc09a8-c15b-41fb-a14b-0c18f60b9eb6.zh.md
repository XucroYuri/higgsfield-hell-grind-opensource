## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this shot): Witness handheld — as if filmed by an ordinary human standing in cover at floor level far from the action. The camera is PLANTED at hall floor level, far back from the giant, low ground-up angle looking UPWARD at the scene. Long focal length, slightly compressed perspective. Operator does NOT walk or move through space — stationary throughout. Allowed camera moves: ZOOM (in and out) and TILT (vertical rotation). VIOLENT handheld shake at all times — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections. Even at long focal length the shake is pronounced — telephoto amplifies it.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame at column bases. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. High up the rock wall on one side: a small carved alcove-niche cut into the stone. The ceiling fissure above suspends a heavy iron chain.

CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS:
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-meter-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald head, heavy menacing brow, deep-set angry eyes, thick-set muscular build. Stone-sculpted armored cuirass over a tunic and draped knee-length skirt-robe, wide stone belt with relief pattern, wrapped wrists. Wields two long stone swords, one in each hand. Stone sandals on huge feet. Weathered grey stone surface, mossy green-grey patina in carved crevices, fine cracks across the form. Animated — moves with crushing weight.
MONK — elderly Asian man, late 60s, short graying hair at the temples, lined weathered face, eyes closed or half-closed in meditative trance. Wears a deep burgundy monk's robe with orange-red waist sash, long sleeves. Sits cross-legged inside a small carved alcove in the rock wall. Lips move continuously, whispering a mantra. Calm and still amid the chaos.
BELL — massive antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern around its body, hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored deep into the rock of the ceiling fissure above.

SCENE 51E — THE REVEAL.

BEAT 1 (single continuous shot, distant low-angle witness handheld, planted at floor level, zoom and tilt only, MAXIMUM violent shake):

Sub-beat A — SMASH (low-angle wide on the giant): The shot opens at hall floor level looking UP toward the towering GIANT STONE STATUE in the distance, his full body filling the upper-center of frame, columns at the LEFT and RIGHT edges framing him. Extreme low ground-up perspective makes him look monolithic. He swings his two stone swords outward in a wide cleaving arc — both blades simultaneously crash through the columns at the frame edges. Stone explodes outward in massive chunks raining down, dust billows in thick clouds, debris ricochets across the floor in foreground. The entire frame jolts with the concussive impact, oil lamps below flicker. Subtle ZOOM-IN tightens during the impact, pulling closer to the giant's body.

Sub-beat B — ROAR: The Giant snaps his huge stone head back, mouth gaping wide, and unleashes a deep guttural ROAR upward at the ceiling. Stone face contorted in fury, deep-set eyes wide. Dust shakes loose from his shoulders. Camera holds the long-lens framing.

Sub-beat C — TILT TO MONK: Camera begins a continuous upward TILT from its planted floor position, rotating up past the Giant's roaring head, climbing the rough vertical rock wall behind him. Tilt reveals a small carved stone alcove-niche cut high in the wall. Inside the niche the MONK sits cross-legged in deep burgundy robes, eyes closed, lips moving in silent mantra, palms upturned on his knees. Calm pocket of stillness amid the violent shaking. Soft cold light catches the edge of his face. Camera holds on him.

Sub-beat D — TILT TO BELL: Tilt continues upward past the alcove, climbing toward the ceiling fissure. Tilt reveals the massive ancient BRONZE BELL suspended by its heavy iron chain in the wide rock fissure overhead, swaying very slightly from the impact tremors, cold pale daylight silhouetting it from behind, dust drifting through the shaft. Chain creaks faintly. Camera holds on the bell.

Throughout the entire shot: low-angle witness perspective from floor level, planted operator far from the action, long lens with violent handheld shake (telephoto amplifies the tremor). No walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing — only zoom and tilt rotation.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、带有眼神光（catch-lights）的湿润鲜活双眼、可见的呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，本镜头）：目击式手持——仿佛由一位站在楼层地面掩体中、远离动作的普通人所拍。摄影机固定于巨厅地面高度，远离巨像，自下而上、贴地仰视角望向场景。长焦镜头，透视频有轻微压缩。操作者不走上、也不在空间中位移——全程静止。允许的摄影机移动：ZOOM（推近与拉远）和 TILT（垂直旋转）。全程剧烈的手持抖动——频繁强烈的抖动、呼吸式机架、反应性纠正。即便在长焦端，抖动也依然显著——长焦放大了它。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱分列两侧，向雾中延展没入。石板地面。柱基处有低矮橙色火焰的小油灯。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从高高在上的岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。一侧岩壁高处：凿入石中的一个小型雕凿壁龛。上方的天花板裂隙吊挂着一条沉重铁链。

角色 / 主体（CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS）：
巨石像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨形雕石武士。光头，浓重凶悍的眉骨，深陷的愤怒双目，厚实魁梧的体格。束腰外衣之上披着石雕的铠甲胸甲，外罩垂及膝下的裙式长袍，一条带浮雕纹饰的宽石腰带，缠绕的手腕。双手各持一把长石剑。巨足上蹬着石制凉鞋。风化的灰色石面，雕凿缝隙间有青灰色的苔藓绿锈斑，形体表面布满细纹裂痕。有生命——以碾压般的重量活动。
僧人（MONK）——年迈的亚洲男性，接近古稀、年近七十，两鬓花白的短发，满是皱纹的风霜面孔，双眼在冥想入定中紧闭或半闭。身穿一袭深酒红色僧袍，腰系橙红腰带，长袖。盘腿坐在岩壁内一个雕凿的小壁龛中。嘴唇不停翕动，低声念诵真言。在一片混乱中沉静而安详。
钟（BELL）——一口巨大的古青铜钟，深色带绿锈的青铜表面，钟身四周环饰凸起浮雕的纹饰与铭文带，由一条深深锚固进上方岩顶裂隙岩石中的沉重铁链吊挂着。

场景 51E——揭示（THE REVEAL）。

拍 1（BEAT 1，单个连续镜头，远处低角度目击手持，固定于地面高度，仅做缩放与上下摇摄，极致剧烈抖动）：

子拍 A——轰砸（SMASH，低角度全景对准巨像）：镜头在巨厅地面高度开场，仰视远处高耸的巨石像，他整副身体填满画面上方中央，左右边缘的石柱将他框住。极端的贴地仰视视角令他显得宛如巨石。他双刀向外挥出一道宽阔的劈斩弧线——两把刀刃同时砸穿画面边缘的石柱。巨石向外爆裂成无数碎块如雨倾落，尘云翻涌成浓雾，碎屑在画面前景地面上四散弹跳。整幅画面被冲击震动，下方油灯摇曳闪烁。冲击瞬间有微妙的推近（ZOOM-IN）收紧，向巨像的身体拉近。

子拍 B——咆哮（ROAR）：巨像猛然把头往后一甩，石嘴大张，朝天花板发出一声低沉的喉音咆哮。石脸因暴怒而扭曲，深陷的双眼圆睁。尘土自他肩头抖落。摄影机保持长焦端取景。

子拍 C——上摇到僧人（TILT TO MONK）：摄影机从固定的地面位置开始一段连续的向上摇摄，旋转向上越过咆哮的巨像头部，沿他身后粗糙垂直的岩壁攀爬。摇摄揭示出高处凿入墙中的一个小型雕石壁龛。壁龛内僧人盘腿端坐，身披深酒红僧袍，双目紧闭，双唇无声地念动真言，双掌上翻置于膝头。暴力震晃之中一方沉静的宁静之所。柔和的冷光拂过他脸颊边缘。摄影机停驻在他身上。

子拍 D——上摇到钟（TILT TO BELL）：摇摄继续向上越过壁龛，朝向天花板裂隙攀爬。摇摄揭示出那口巨大的古青铜钟，由沉重的铁链吊挂在头顶宽阔的岩缝中，因冲击震动而微微晃动，背后有冰冷苍白的天光映出剪影，尘埃在光柱中飘荡。铁链发出细微的吱嘎声。摄影机停驻在钟上。

整个镜头贯穿：楼层地面的低角度目击视角，操作者远离动作立于原地，长镜头叠加剧烈的手持抖动（长焦放大震颤）。不得走动、不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄——只准缩放与上下摇转。
