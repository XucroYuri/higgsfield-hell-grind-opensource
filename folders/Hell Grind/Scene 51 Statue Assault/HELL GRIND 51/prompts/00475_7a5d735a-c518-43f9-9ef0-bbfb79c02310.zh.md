## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone-carved giant: weathered granite-grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine hairline cracks, fine dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, visible breath. The stone giant is animated with weight and intent; menace reads through head angle, silhouette, deliberate stillness of stone catching cold light.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (all shots): Witness-style documentary handheld, amateur vérité aesthetic, 8K photorealistic rendering. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Only rotational moves and lens-driven zoom allowed. MAXIMUM violent handheld shake throughout — peaking on impacts and on the resonance pulse. Imperfect raw framing. NO visible sound waves, NO heat-haze distortion, NO visible acoustic ripples — sound remains invisible; only its physical effects on solid matter are shown.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns lining both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. The hall walls and columns are carved from ancient resonant stone — they react to powerful sound by vibrating heavily.

GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set carved eyes, expressionless stone face. Stone armored cuirass over tunic, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in right hand, left hand empty. Weathered grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks. Moves with crushing weight.

SCENE 51E — STRIKE AND RESONANCE (four shots, single prompt, in this exact order).

SHOT 1 — HORIZONTAL SWING (low-angle three-quarter close-up).
Framing: Low-angle close-up, camera planted at ground level looking UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE. Telephoto compression. Giant positioned slightly LEFT of center, cropped at the WAIST — only upper body visible: stone cuirass, wrapped right shoulder pad, bald head with heavy menacing brow, expressionless mouth. Right hand grips the long stone sword, blade held HIGH and to the side, raised in preparation. Frame-RIGHT: dark vertical mass of an adjacent column with rugged relief. Frame-LEFT: hazy cave depth. Cold rim light catches the contour of his profile and the edge of the raised blade.
Action: SLOW windup — the giant holds the sword raised high for a held beat, building tension. Then EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE — the blade WHIPS in a fast HORIZONTAL ARC across his body, sweeping sideways through the air at chest-to-hip level with brutal speed. Heavy motion blur on the blade as it cuts through frame. The blade SMASHES into the lower portion of a column (hip-level strike) with sudden bone-deep concussive impact — dust explodes outward, fragments spray, frame jolts violently with the shockwave at maximum shake intensity. The contrast between slow buildup and fast release amplifies the impact.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — COLUMN CHUNK FALL (close-up on column).
Framing: Tight close-up on the LOWER section of a hall column near floor level — weathered carved stone, fine relief patterns, mossy patina in crevices. Column fills frame vertically. Cold rim light catches the surface texture. Background: hazy hall depth, out-of-focus.
Action: The column stands intact for a held beat. Then SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT — an unseen blade smashes horizontally into the column from off-frame, stone surface fractures violently. A LARGE CHUNK of stone breaks off, hairline cracks racing outward. The chunk TUMBLES forward and downward through frame, falling with real mass-correct gravity, rotating slowly. Camera tilts down with it slightly. The chunk SLAMS into the stone-tiled floor at the bottom of frame with a thunderous secondary impact — stone-on-stone shatter, breaking into pieces, dust kicks up violently, debris skitters across tiles. Frame jolts violently on both impacts. Dust hangs heavy after.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — WALLS VIBRATE (medium-wide on cave wall).
Framing: Camera planted at hall floor level, framed on a section of the cave wall and adjacent column — ancient carved stone surface filling most of frame. Cold pale rim light grazes the rock texture. Hazy hall depth at the edges, soft and out of focus. Oil lamp glowing at frame-bottom corner.
Action: In the wake of the giant's roar, the stone wall and column surface VIBRATE physically — fine micro-tremors visibly rippling across the rock face, the carved relief patterns shaking in place. Sheets of fine dust cascade down the wall, drifting through cold light. Pebbles and small stone fragments shake loose from upper crevices and skitter down the surface. The oil-lamp flame jolts and flickers violently from the airborne tremor. The whole wall is a living, trembling body of stone reacting to invisible acoustic pressure. Frame trembles violently with handheld shake. NO visible sound waves, NO heat-haze, NO air distortion — just the raw physical vibration of solid stone and falling dust.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — HIGH ANGLE WIDE (extreme high angle on giant's back).
Framing: EXTREME HIGH ANGLE WIDE shot — camera planted high near the ceiling fissure, looking steeply DOWN at the hall floor far below. The GIANT STONE STATUE is in the center of frame seen from above — we see the top of his bald head, the broad span of his stone shoulders, and the long ridge of his back receding toward his feet. He is facing AWAY from camera, looking down the hall in the direction the heroes are hidden. The vast hall stretches out in front of him from this top-down perspective: rows of massive columns flanking both sides, oil lamps lining the floor in long diminishing lines, the open stone floor with debris from the collapsed column scattered in a heap. Cold pale light from the fissure overhead casts a god-eye perspective.
Action: The giant stands still after his strike, his roar finished. Across the hall floor, the resonance manifests as physical disturbance only — dust cascading from the column tops in long curtains down through the cold light, oil-lamp flames along the floor jolting and sputtering in sequence as if a wave of pressure passes down the hall, fine debris and pebbles bouncing on the stone tiles, the columns themselves visibly trembling in place. The giant's shoulders shed dust in sheets. The whole architectural geometry registers the invisible sound through its physical reaction. Frame trembles violently throughout. NO visible sound waves, NO heat-haze, NO air distortion — only the physical effects of invisible acoustic energy on stone and flame.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Style（技术底座）：8K IMAX。写实摄影风格（Photorealistic）——不得有 3D 渲染、不得有游戏引擎、不得有游戏过场动画的美感。
Cinematography（摄影）：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
Lighting（光线）：仅自然光 — 逆光背光（contre-jour）、摄影机位于阴影侧、全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
Color（色彩）：60:30:10（主色/次色/点缀）。
Camera（摄影机）：物理电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
Skin（皮肤）：毛孔级写实 — 汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称的痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光线匹配的毛孔阴影。石雕巨像：风化花岗岩灰石、缝隙中灰绿苔藓铜绿、发丝般细纹、细积尘。
Acting（表演）：好莱坞 — 反应前有微小停顿、精确的视线、可见的呼吸。石巨像以重量与意图被赋予生命；威压透过头部角度、剪影、石块挡住冷光时那份刻意的静止显影。
Physics（物理）：尊重重力与惯性 — 质量有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有漂浮道具。
Composition（构图）：三分法 + 黄金比例。每个入画的人从第一帧起即在移动。
Continuity（连贯性）：每一切之间角色、道具、环境完全一致。无身份漂移（no identity drift）。
Technical（技术）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。不得抖动。
Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（全部镜头摄影机装置）：旁观者式纪录手持、业余真实感美学（amateur vérité aesthetic）、8K 写实渲染。每个镜头中操作员保持静止 — 不步行、不推轨、不跟拍、不做弧线运动。仅允许旋转动作与镜头驱动变焦。全程最大幅度剧烈手持晃动 — 在冲击与共鸣脉冲处达峰。不完美的原生取景。不得有可见声波、不得有热霾失真、不得有可见声学涟漪 — 声音保持隐形；仅呈现其对固态物体的物理效应。

LOCATION（地点）— HELL HALL（地狱大厅）：
人工开凿于山体内的广阔石厅。两侧林立巨型的雕花石柱，渐退入雾。石板地。柱基油灯微微发光。一道苍白冷光自岩顶裂隙直落而下。尘埃微粒与大气雾感（atmospheric haze）遍布全程。大厅墙壁与巨柱由上古共鸣之石雕凿而成 — 它们会对强大声响作出剧烈震动反应。

石巨像（Stone Giant）— 高 20 米的巨型石刻战士。光头、厚重凶恶的眉骨、深雕眼窝、面无表情的石脸。束腰外衣外覆石制铠甲胸甲、宽石腰、缠裹手腕。右手持一把长石剑、左手空着。风化灰石、苔藓铜绿、细裂。以碾压般的重量移动。

SCENE 51E — STRIKE AND RESONANCE（打击与共鸣）（四个镜头，单一提示词，按此顺序严格排列）。

镜头 1 — 水平挥击（低角度 3/4 特写）。
取景：低角度特写，摄影机固定在地面高度，仰视处于 THREE-QUARTER ANGLE（3/4 角度）的巨像。长焦压缩。巨像微偏中心左侧，于 WAIST（腰部）处裁切 — 仅见上身：石胸甲、裹缠的右肩垫、厚重凶恶眉骨的光头、面无表情的嘴。右手紧握长石剑，剑刃高高举向身侧、预备举起。画面右侧：相邻巨柱的深色垂直体量、带粗犷浮雕。画面左侧：雾霾的洞穴纵深。寒冷轮廓光（rim light）勾住他侧影的轮廓与高举剑刃的边缘。
动作：SLOW windup（缓慢蓄力）— 巨像将剑高举、顿住一拍蓄势。随后 EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE（爆发式快速释放）— 剑刃 WHIPS（猛挥）出一道快速的水平弧线掠过他身前，以狂暴速度在胸至髋高度横扫侧向空气。剑刃切过画面时带沉重运动模糊。剑刃以骤然深及骨骼的震颤冲击 SMASHES（砸）入一根巨柱下部（髋高处的打击）— 尘埃向外炸开、碎片飞溅、画面随冲击波在最高抖动强度下剧烈崩震。缓慢蓄势与快速释放之间的反差放大了冲击。

切。

镜头 2 — 巨柱碎块坠落（巨柱特写）。
取景：大厅巨柱近地高度的下部紧特写 — 风化的雕石、细浮雕纹样、缝隙中苔藓铜绿。巨柱纵向填满画面。寒冷轮廓光（rim light）打亮表面质感。背景：雾霾大厅纵深、失焦。
动作：巨柱完好地顿立一拍。随后 SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT（骤然巨力冲击）— 一柄看不见的刀刃自框外向横向砸入巨柱、石面剧烈破裂。一大块巨石断裂、发丝般细纹向外急窜。碎块以真实质量重力向前向下 TUMBLES（翻滚）过画面、缓慢自转。摄影机随它微微下移。碎块以雷霆般的二次冲击 SLAMS（猛砸）入画面底部的石板地 — 石碰石碎裂、崩成数块、尘土剧烈扬起、碎片嘎啦划过石板。两次冲击都令画面剧烈崩震。事后尘土沉重弥漫。

切。

镜头 3 — 墙壁震动（洞壁中广景）。
取景：摄影机固定在大厅地板高度，框住一段洞壁与相邻巨柱 — 上古雕花石面占据画面大半。苍白冷轮廓光掠过岩石质感。边缘雾霾大厅纵深、柔和失焦。画面底角一盏油灯发光。
动作：随着巨像吼声的余波，石墙与巨柱表面在物理上 VIBRATE（震动）— 细微震颤在岩石表面清晰可见地震漾、雕凿浮雕原地抖动。成片细尘沿墙泻落、于冷光中飘荡。小石卵与碎石片从上方裂隙松动、簌簌滑落表面。油灯火苗被空中震颤震得剧烈跳动、摇曳。整堵墙是一具活生生、震颤着、对无形声压作出反应的石质躯体。画面随手持晃动剧烈震颤。不得有可见声波、不得有热霾、不得有空气扭曲 — 唯有固态石头与坠落尘埃的赤裸物理震动。

切。

镜头 4 — 高角度广角（巨像背上的极限高角度）。
取景：EXTREME HIGH ANGLE WIDE（极限高角度广角）镜头 — 摄影机固定在天顶裂隙附近高处、陡峭向下俯视远方地面。石巨像居画面正中、自上方观看 — 我们看到他的头顶、阔展开的石宽肩、以及那微向后脚退缩的长脊背。他 AWAY（背）对摄影机、顺着英雄们藏身的方向朝大厅深处眺望。自这一鸟瞰视角，广阔大厅在他面前铺展开来：两侧林立的巨柱列、沿地排成漫长渐缩光线的油灯、开阔石地、以坍塌巨柱遗下的一堆散落碎片。头顶裂隙的苍白冷光投下一道上帝之眼般的透视。
动作：巨像挥击后静立、吼声已歇。横贯大厅地面，共鸣仅以物理扰动显影 — 尘埃自柱顶化作长幕沿冷光泻落、沿地油灯火苗仿佛一道压力波沿厅而过、依次跳动吞吐、细碎碎片与石卵在石板上弹跳、巨柱本身原地清晰可见地颤抖。巨像的双肩成片扬尘。整幅建筑几何透过其物理反应记录下这隐形的声音。全程画面剧烈震颤。不得有可见声波、不得有热霾、不得有空气扭曲 — 唯有隐形声学能量施加于石与火的物理效应。
