Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone-carved giant: weathered granite-grey stone, mossy patina in crevices, fine cracks, dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (all shots): Witness-style documentary handheld, amateur vérité aesthetic, 8K photorealistic. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Only rotational moves and lens-driven zoom allowed. MAXIMUM violent handheld shake throughout, peaking on impacts and on the resonance pulse. Imperfect raw framing.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns lining both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. The hall walls and columns are carved from ancient resonant stone — they react to powerful sound, absorbing and refracting acoustic energy.

GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set carved eyes. Stone armored cuirass over tunic and draped knee-length skirt-robe, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in right hand, left hand empty. Weathered grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks. Moves with crushing weight.

SCENE 51E — AFTERMATH AND RESONANCE (three shots, single prompt, in this exact order).

SHOT 1 — COLUMN COLLAPSES (medium close-up on the struck column).
Framing: Tight medium close-up on the LOWER section of the hall column that the giant just struck — weathered carved stone, fine relief patterns, mossy patina in crevices. The column fills frame vertically. Cold rim light catches the surface. A deep gash visible on its lower body where the blade impacted. Background: hazy hall depth, out-of-focus.
Action: The column stands wounded for a held beat — hairline cracks racing outward from the gash, fine dust shaking loose. Then it begins to FAIL — the cracks widen with audible groans, the upper portion of the column shifts as the lower body buckles. Massive STONE CHUNKS break apart and TUMBLE forward through frame, rotating with mass-correct gravity, falling toward the floor. The column collapses in slow-heavy stages, segments hitting the stone-tiled floor with thunderous concussive impacts, dust billowing in massive clouds, debris skittering across the tiles. Frame jolts violently with each impact. Dust hangs heavy in the air after.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — WALLS ABSORB SOUND (medium-wide on cave wall).
Framing: Camera planted at hall floor level, framed on a section of the cave wall and adjacent column — ancient carved stone surface filling most of frame. Cold pale rim light grazes the rock texture. Hazy hall depth at the edges, soft and out of focus. Oil lamp glowing at frame-bottom corner.
Action: In the silence after the column's collapse, the stone wall and column surface BEGIN TO VIBRATE — fine micro-tremors visibly rippling across the rock face, dust shaking loose in cascading sheets. Concentric ripples of HEAT-HAZE-LIKE DISTORTION emanate outward through the air in front of the wall, curving and bending light as sound waves are absorbed and refracted by the resonant stone. The visible distortion pulses in waves, acoustic energy made physical. Carved relief patterns shimmer and warp as air refracts in front of them. Dust drifts upward in the disturbed air. Frame trembles violently with handheld shake on top of the in-camera distortion. The wall is alive with sound made visible.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — HIGH ANGLE WIDE, SOUND WAVES DOWN THE HALL (extreme high angle on giant's back).
Framing: EXTREME HIGH ANGLE WIDE shot — camera planted high near the ceiling fissure, looking steeply DOWN at the hall floor far below. The GIANT STONE STATUE is in the center of frame seen from above — we see the top of his bald head, the broad span of his stone shoulders, and the long ridge of his back receding toward his feet. He is facing AWAY from camera, looking down the hall in the direction the heroes are hidden. The vast hall stretches out in front of him from this top-down perspective: rows of massive columns flanking both sides, oil lamps lining the floor in long diminishing lines, the open stone floor with debris from the collapsed column scattered in a heap. Cold pale light from the fissure overhead casts a god-eye perspective. The hall geometry reads instantly from this bird's-eye view — we see the full architecture of the trap.
Action: The giant stands still, his roar finished. Then a MASSIVE PULSE OF VISIBLE SOUND WAVES erupts from his position and travels DOWN THE HALL away from him — concentric heat-haze-like distortion ripples expanding outward and forward through the long axis of the hall, sweeping along the corridor between the columns at high speed, visibly bending the air, warping the columns in passing pulses, the oil-lamp flames jolting and sputtering as each wave sweeps past them. The waves travel as a directional propagation — energy moving FORWARD down the hall toward the heroes' position. Dust shakes loose from the column tops and rains down through the cold light. Frame trembles violently throughout — handheld shake plus the visible air distortion warping reality. The high-angle wide shot reveals the architectural scale of what is happening — the giant at the source, the hall as the conduit, the waves as the weapon.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Style: 8K IMAX。写实电影级——无 3D 渲染、无游戏引擎、无游戏过场动画审美。
摄影指导：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
灯光：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机位于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
色彩：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀色。
摄影机：实体电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
皮肤：毛孔级真实感——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称胎记、毛细血管潮红、与棚内光匹配的毛孔阴影。石雕巨像：风化花岗岩灰石、沟槽内苔质蚀斑、细密裂纹、尘垢堆积。
演技：好莱坞级——反应前微型停顿、精准视线与眼线、湿润活体之眼带眼神光（catch-lights）、可见呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理：遵守重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量，正确的接触阴影。无悬浮道具。
构图：三分法（rule of thirds）+ 黄金比例（golden ratio）。每个角色从头帧开始即移动。
连续性：角色、道具、环境在每一处切换间完全一致。无身份漂移。
技术：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（全部镜头）：见证式纪录手持，业余真实（vérité）审美，8K 写实电影级。每个镜头中操作者静止——不行走、不推轨推摄、不跟拍、不环绕。仅允许旋转性运镜与镜头驱动变焦。全程最大强度剧烈手持抖动，在撞击与共振脉冲处达峰值。不完美的裸拍构图。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
凿刻于山体内部的宏伟石厅。巨大华丽立柱沿两侧排列，向雾霭中退去。石板地板。油灯在柱基发光。一道冷白自然光柱自岩石天花板的裂隙倾泻而下。全程尘粒与大气雾感。巨厅墙壁与立柱以古老的共鸣石凿成——它们会对强声作出反应，吸收并折射声学能量。

石巨像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨硕雕刻石战士。光头、厚重威慑眉脊、深陷刻凿之眼。罩衫外覆石质盔甲胸甲并垂膝裙袍、宽石腰带、缠裹手腕。右手执一柄长石剑，左手空置。风化灰石、苔质蚀斑、细密裂纹。以碾压重量移动。

场景 51E——余波与共鸣（三个镜头，单条提示词，严格按此顺序）。

镜头 1——立柱坍塌（受击立柱的中特写）。
取景：对巨像刚击中的大厅立柱下部进行紧凑中特写——风化刻凿之石、精细浮雕纹样、沟槽内苔质蚀斑。立柱纵向填满画面。冷色轮廓光捕捉表面。其下部可见剑刃命中的一道深豁口。背景：雾霭厅深，虚焦。
动作：立柱带伤屹立一个节拍——发丝裂缝自豁口四散疾冲，细尘震落。随后它开始垮塌——裂缝伴可闻的呻吟声扩张，立柱上部随下部屈折而移位。巨硕石料碎块崩裂并向前翻滚穿画，以符合质量的重力自转，坠向地面。立柱以缓慢沉重的分阶段坍塌，各段砸中石板地面发出雷鸣般的震撼撞击，尘土翻涌成巨云，碎砾在地砖上乱窜。每次撞击画框均剧烈震荡。此后尘雾沉重弥漫于空气中。

切。

镜头 2——石壁吸收声音（洞壁中全景）。
取景：摄影机植于巨厅地面层，取景于一段洞壁与相邻立柱——古雕石面填满画面大部。冷白淡轮廓光擦过岩石纹理。边缘为雾霭厅深，柔和虚焦。画面底角油灯发光。
动作：在立柱坍塌后的寂静之中，石壁与柱面开始振动——可见的微震颤在岩面上荡漾，尘土成片成片震落。热浪状畸变的同心涟漪经壁前空气向外扩散，随声波被共鸣石吸收与折射而弯折光线。可见的畸变一波波脉动，声学能量化为实体。浮雕纹样因空气在它前方折射而闪烁扭曲。尘土在扰动空气中向上飘升。画框在机内畸变之上叠加剧烈手持抖动而猛烈震颤。石壁因可见之声活了过来。

切。

镜头 3——高角度宽幅，声波灌满巨厅（巨像背脊的极高角度）。
取景：极高角度宽幅——摄影机植于天顶裂隙附近高处，陡峻俯视下方遥远的巨厅地面。石巨像位于画面中央自上方看——我们见到他光秃头顶、石质双肩的宽阔跨度，以及向后背朝双足退去的厚长脊线。他背对摄影机，顺着巨厅望向英雄们藏身的方向。此俯视视角之下，巨大巨厅在他身前铺展：两侧排排巨柱、沿地板布成长长渐缩一线的油灯、开阔石质地面散布着坍塌立柱堆积的碎岩。裂隙上方冷白淡光投下一元神的俯瞰视角。这一鸟瞰视角令巨厅几何一目了然——我们看见这处陷阱的全貌建筑结构。
动作：巨像静立，咆哮已毕。随后一道巨大的可见声波脉冲自其所在爆发并自他身旁沿巨厅向下传播——同心热浪状畸变涟漪向外并向前沿巨厅长轴扩张，以高速沿柱间走廊冲刷，可见地弯折空气、以掠过脉冲扭曲立柱、每道波掠过时油灯焰光颠抖摇曳。声波以定向传播行进——能量向其身后英雄们的位置沿巨厅向前推进。尘土自柱顶震落，穿过冷光如雨洒落。全程画框猛烈震颤——手持抖动叠加可见的空气畸变扭曲现实。高角度宽幅揭示正在发生之事的建筑尺度——巨像是源头、巨厅是通道、声波是武器。
