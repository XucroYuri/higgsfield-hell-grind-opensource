Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone-carved giant: weathered granite-grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine hairline cracks, fine dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, visible breath. The stone giant is animated with weight and intent; menace reads through head angle, silhouette, deliberate stillness of stone catching cold light.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (all three shots in this scene): Witness-style documentary handheld, amateur vérité aesthetic, 8K photorealistic rendering. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Only rotational moves (tilt, minor pan) and lens-driven zoom allowed. MAXIMUM violent handheld shake throughout — the most extreme of the entire scene, peaking on impacts and on the roar. Imperfect raw framing.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set carved eyes, expressionless stone face. Stone armored cuirass over tunic, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in right hand, left hand empty. Weathered grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks. Moves with crushing weight, slow and deliberate.

SCENE 51E — STRIKE SEQUENCE (three shots, single prompt).

SHOT 1 — HORIZONTAL SWING (low-angle three-quarter close-up).
Framing: Low-angle close-up, camera planted at ground level looking UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE. Telephoto compression. The giant is positioned slightly LEFT of center, cropped at the WAIST — we see only his upper body from belt up: stone cuirass, wrapped right shoulder pad, bald head with heavy menacing brow, expressionless mouth. His right hand grips the long stone sword, blade held HIGH and to the side, raised in preparation. Background frame-RIGHT: a dark vertical mass of an adjacent column with rugged carved relief, deep shadow. Background frame-LEFT: dark hazy depth of the cave hall. Cold rim light from above catches the contour of his profile and the edge of the raised blade. Mossy patina visible in the deep crevices.
Action: From the held high-ready position, the Giant SWINGS the stone sword in a SLOW deliberate HORIZONTAL ARC across his body — the blade sweeps SIDEWAYS through the air at chest-to-hip level, not vertically downward. As the horizontal swing reaches its peak, the blade SMASHES into the lower portion of a column (hip-level strike — the column's lower body, not its upper section). Bone-deep concussive impact at the moment of contact, dust explodes from the strike point, fragments spray outward. Frame jolts violently with the shockwave — handheld shake spikes to maximum intensity. Cold rim light catches the sweeping arc of the blade.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — COLUMN CHUNK FALL (close-up on column).
Framing: Tight close-up on the LOWER section of a hall column near floor level — weathered carved stone, fine relief patterns, mossy patina in crevices. The column fills frame vertically, dominant subject. Cold rim light catches the surface texture. Background behind: hazy depth of the hall, soft out-of-focus.
Action: The column stands intact for a held beat. Then SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT — an unseen blade smashes into the column horizontally from off-frame, the stone surface fractures violently at the point of impact. A LARGE CHUNK of stone breaks off, hairline cracks racing outward across the surface. The chunk TUMBLES forward and downward through frame, falling with real mass-correct gravity, rotating slowly as it descends. Camera tilts down with it slightly, tracking the fall. The chunk SLAMS into the stone-tiled floor at the bottom of frame with a thunderous secondary impact — stone-on-stone shatter, breaking into smaller pieces on landing, dust kicks up violently, debris skitters across tiles. Frame jolts violently on both impacts. Dust hangs heavy in the air after.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — ROAR (low-angle close-up, three-quarter angle, heavy telephoto compression).
Framing: Extreme low angle from ground level, looking steeply UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE (semi-profile — neither pure front nor pure side, partial face and partial shoulder visible). HEAVY TELEPHOTO COMPRESSION — long focal length stacks the giant against the rock ceiling behind him, flattening depth. The frame CROPS HIM AT THE WAIST — we see only his upper body from waist up: belt, cuirass, shoulders, head. His lower body is off-frame below the bottom edge. The crop signals his scale — the camera cannot contain him. Cold pale shaft of light from the ceiling fissure silhouettes his back contour, rim catching the edge of his three-quarter profile. The angle is mythic, monumental, oppressive.
Action: The Giant tilts his huge stone head BACK, mouth gaping wide in carved stone, and unleashes a deep guttural ROAR upward at the ceiling. Stone face contorted in a death-mask howl, brow heavy, deep-set carved eyes wide. Dust shakes loose from his shoulders and the cracks of his armor, drifting down through cold light. Camera shake reaches peak intensity at the roar. Held for the full duration of the howl.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Style: 8K IMAX。写实电影级——无 3D 渲染、无游戏引擎、无游戏过场动画审美。
摄影指导：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
灯光：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机位于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
色彩：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀色。
摄影机：实体电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
皮肤：毛孔级真实感——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称胎记、毛细血管潮红、与棚内光匹配的毛孔阴影。石雕巨像：风化花岗岩灰石、沟槽内苔绿灰蚀斑、细微发丝裂缝、细尘堆积。
演技：好莱坞级——反应前微型停顿、精准视线与眼线、可见呼吸。石巨像以重量与意念被驱动；威胁感经由头部角度、剪影、石头承接冷光时的刻意静止而呈现。
物理：遵守重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量，正确的接触阴影。无悬浮道具。
构图：三分法（rule of thirds）+ 黄金比例（golden ratio）。每个角色从头帧开始即移动。
连续性：角色、道具、环境在每一处切换间完全一致。无身份漂移。
技术：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（本场景全部三个镜头）：见证式纪录手持，业余真实（vérité）审美，8K 写实电影级渲染。每个镜头中操作者静止——不行走、不推轨推摄、不跟拍、不环绕。仅允许旋转性运镜（倾斜、小幅摇摄）与镜头驱动变焦。全程最大强度剧烈手持抖动——为整个场景之最，在撞击与咆哮处达到峰值。不完美的裸拍构图。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
凿刻于山体内部的宏伟石厅。巨大华丽立柱。石板地板。油灯在柱基处发光。一道冷白自然光柱自岩石天花板的裂隙倾泻而下。全程尘粒与大气雾感。

石巨像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨硕雕刻石战士。光头、厚重威慑眉脊、深陷刻凿之眼、无表情石面。罩衫外覆石质盔甲胸甲、宽石腰带、缠裹手腕。右手执一柄长石剑，左手空置。风化灰石、苔质蚀斑、细密裂纹。以碾压力移动，缓慢而审慎。

场景 51E——挥砍序列（三个镜头，单条提示词）。

镜头 1——横扫（低角度 3/4 特写）。
取景：低角度特写，摄影机植于地面自下而上仰视巨像，呈 3/4 角。长焦压缩。巨像略偏画面左，于腰部裁切——我们只见其腰带以上的上半身：石质胸甲、缠裹的右护肩、带厚重威慑眉脊的光头、无表情的嘴。右手紧握长石剑，剑刃高举于身侧，蓄势待发。画面右侧背景：相邻立柱的黑暗垂直巨块，带粗犷的浮雕刻凿，深重阴影。画面左侧背景：洞厅的暗色雾霭纵深。来自上方的冷色轮廓光勾勒其轮廓与高举剑刃的边缘。深蚀沟槽内可见苔绿蚀斑。
动作：自持握式的高举预备姿态，巨像以缓慢审慎的横向弧线挥动石剑横越自身——剑刃于胸部至髋部高度水平横扫过空气，而非竖劈下砍。当横扫达到峰值，剑刃猛劈进一根立柱的下部（髋部高度之击——立柱的下半身，非其上部）。接触瞬间骨深震撼的撞击，尘土自打击点爆出，碎片向外飞溅。画框随冲击波剧烈震荡——手持抖动冲向最大强度。冷色轮廓光捕捉剑刃挥扫的弧线。

切。

镜头 2——立柱碎块坠落（立柱特写）。
取景：对巨厅立柱靠近地面的下部进行紧凑特写——风化刻凿之石、精细浮雕纹样、沟槽内苔绿蚀斑。立柱纵向填满画面，为主宰主体。冷色轮廓光捕捉表面纹理。背后背景：巨厅雾霭纵深，柔和虚焦。
动作：立柱完好定格一个节拍。随后突如其来的巨大撞击——一柄看不见的剑刃自画外水平猛劈进立柱，打击点石面剧烈龟裂。一大块石头崩落，发丝裂缝表面四散疾冲。碎块向前向下翻滚穿画，以符合质量的重力真实坠落，下落时缓慢自转。摄影机随之轻微下倾，跟拍其坠落。碎块轰然砸进画面底部石板地面，产生雷鸣般的次级撞击——石砸石迸裂，落地碎裂成更小块，尘土剧烈扬起，碎砾在地砖上弹跳乱窜。两处撞击时画框均剧烈震荡。此后尘雾沉重弥漫于空气中。

切。

镜头 3——咆哮（低角度特写，3/4 角，重长焦压缩）。
取景：地面极低角度，陡峻仰视巨像，呈 3/4 角（半侧面——既非纯正面亦非纯侧面，部分面部与部分肩部可见）。重长焦压缩——长焦距将巨像叠加于他身后的岩石天花板，压平纵深。画面于他的腰部裁切——我们只见其腰部以上身体：腰带、胸甲、双肩、头颅。其下半身位于底缘以下的画面外。这一裁切暗示其体量——摄影机无法容纳他。自天顶裂隙倾泻的冷白光束勾出他背脊轮廓，轮廓光勾勒其 3/4 侧面的边缘。此角度神话、恢弘、压迫。

动作：巨像将巨大的石首向后仰，雕凿石口大张，朝着天花板发出低沉浑厚的怒吼。石面扭曲成死亡面具般的嚎叫、眉头沉重、深陷刻凿之眼大张。尘土自他肩头与盔甲裂纹震落，穿过冷光飘落。摄影机抖动在咆哮处达到峰值强度。延续至嚎叫全程。
