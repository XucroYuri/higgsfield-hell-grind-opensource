Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (applies to every shot in this scene): Strong handheld operator — aggressive micro-jitter, breathing rig, slight reframing drift, reactive whip-corrections to action. Action-urgency feel. Never static, never locked-off. Even in close-ups the frame floats and trembles.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame glow at the bases of columns. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. Dominant tones: cold blue-gray stone (60), deep shadow black (30), warm amber lamp flame (10).

CHARACTERS:
JAX — Black man, mid-20s, bleached yellow buzz-cut hair, clear wraparound safety glasses, fresh red graze on his forehead. Wears a black tactical hooded jacket with grey paneling, olive cargo pants, black fingerless gloves, worn combat boots.
ROKO — man, mid-20s, dark messy shoulder-length hair, faint mustache, red abraded marks across the bridge of his nose. Wears a full translucent blood-red crystalline armor suit with jagged crystal spikes erupting from shoulders and forearms. In this scene his crystal helmet is RETRACTED — face fully exposed.
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips, thin red scar running through left eyebrow down across the cheek. Wears a grey cropped hooded utility jacket over a black turtleneck top, black cargo pants, brown leather belt, olive thigh pouch holster strapped to right leg.
PROPS: RAIN's flat matte-dark hovering board, roughly snowboard-sized.

COLUMN STAGING (locked for the whole scene):
ROKO — pressed to the LEFT side of the column.
JAX — pressed to the CENTER of the column.
RAIN — pressed to the RIGHT edge of the column, board tucked under her right armpit.

SCENE 51C — TAKE COVER AND EXCHANGE.

BEAT 1 (medium-wide, handheld converging): The three converge on a single massive column, each emerging from BEHIND a DIFFERENT neighboring column in the hall — three separate sprinting trajectories breaking from cover into cover. ROKO breaks from a column on the LEFT, crystal armor scraping stone, slams shoulder-first to the LEFT side of the central column — his crystal helmet plates fracture and retract in segments revealing his face, panting through open mouth. JAX breaks from a column directly BEHIND camera-line, sprints up and slams his back flat to the CENTER of the column, chest heaving. RAIN breaks from a column on the RIGHT, gliding low on her hovering board; mid-glide she snatches the board with her right hand, tucks it tight under her right armpit, and hits the RIGHT EDGE of the column with her shoulder, board gripped against her ribs. All three pressed to the same column in their locked positions. Cold rim light from above carves silhouettes. Dust kicked up catches the shaft of light. Camera jitters hard.

BEAT 2 (cut, 50mm CU on JAX, handheld floats): Back flat to the CENTER of the column. Sweat at his temple, jaw set hard. Eyes flick once to the others off-frame, micro-pause, then low and frustrated he speaks: "Whatever it is — charges have no effect on him." Visible breath. Chest rise. Soft cold rim from frame-left wraps the edge of his face. Frame breathes and trembles.

BEAT 3 (cut, 50mm CU on ROKO, handheld floats): Helmet retracted. Pressed to the LEFT side of the column. He exhales sharp through teeth, shakes his head once, eyes find Jax off-frame to his left: "Same with me. We need something else." Frustrated, breath visible. Cold rim catches the crystal edges of his pauldron, scattering faint red refractions across his jaw. Frame trembles.

BEAT 4 (cut, medium-wide on the trio at the column, handheld violently shakes): A sudden massive impact hits off-frame — hard low rumble shudders the entire hall. Dust cascades from the tops of columns. Oil-lamp flames jolt and flicker, some snuffing out. Small stone pebbles bounce and skitter on the tile floor. The shaft of light flickers as dust thickens through it. ROKO on the LEFT, JAX in the CENTER, RAIN on the RIGHT — all three flinch, eyes snapping wide, heads jerking outward to scan the hall beyond their cover. Bodies tense against the trembling column. Camera shudders violently in sync with the impact.

BEAT 5 (cut, 50mm CU on RAIN peeking, handheld trembles): RAIN at the RIGHT edge of the column, board still tucked under her right armpit. Hair falls past her scarred eye. Micro-pause — she inhales, slowly leans her face past the column's right edge, eyes narrow as they scan the hall beyond. Catch-light in wet living eyes. Subtle chest rise. Profile rotates to three-quarter as she turns. Cold backlight bleeds around the column edge across her cheek. Frame trembles in sync with her breath.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、带有眼神光（catch-lights）的湿润鲜活双眼、可见的呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，本场景每个镜头通用）：结实的手持操作者——激进的微抖动、呼吸式机架、轻微的重新取景漂移、对动作的反应性鞭扫纠正。动作紧迫感。绝不停滞，绝不锁死。即便在特写中，画框也在漂浮与颤抖。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱分列两侧，向雾中延展没入。石板地面。柱基处有低矮橙色火焰的小油灯发光。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从高高在上的岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。主色调：冰冷蓝灰色石面（60）、深阴影黑（30）、暖琥珀色油灯火焰（10）。

角色（CHARACTERS）：
JAX——黑人男性，二十多岁，漂白金黄寸头，透明裹式安全护目镜，前额一道新鲜的红色擦伤。身穿黑色战术连帽夹克带灰色拼接、橄榄绿工装裤、黑色无指手套、磨损的战斗靴。
ROKO——男性，二十多岁，深色凌乱垂肩长发，淡胡须，鼻梁上红色擦伤痕迹。身穿通体红色半透明水晶装甲，肩部与前臂爆发参差水晶利刺。本场景中他的水晶头盔已收起（RETRACTED）——脸部完全暴露。
RAIN——年轻女性，二十岁出头，银白金波波头带深黑发尾，一道细红疤贯穿左眉垂过脸颊。身穿黑色高领内搭外罩灰色短款连帽工装夹克、黑色工装裤、棕色腰带、右腿束着橄榄绿大腿收纳枪套。
道具（PROPS）：RAIN 的扁平哑光深色悬浮板，约滑板大小。

石柱走位（COLUMN STAGING，全程锁定）：
ROKO——紧贴石柱左侧。
JAX——紧贴石柱中央。
RAIN——紧贴石柱右缘，滑板夹在右腋下。

场景 51C——掩护与交换（TAKE COVER AND EXCHANGE）。

拍 1（BEAT 1，中全景，手持汇聚）：三人同时汇聚到一根巨型石柱上，各自从大厅中不同的相邻石柱后方冲出——三条各自独立的冲刺轨迹从掩体突破奔向掩体。ROKO 从左侧一根石柱冲出，水晶装甲刮擦石面，肩先撞上中央石柱的左侧——他头盔的水晶甲片碎裂并分段缩回，露出他的脸，张着嘴喘息。JAX 从相机线正后方的石柱冲出，冲刺上前，后背平拍在石柱中央，胸口剧烈起伏。RAIN 从右侧一根石柱冲出，在悬浮板上低掠滑行；滑行途中她右手一把抄起滑板，紧紧夹在右腋下，肩头撞上石柱右缘，滑板抵在肋侧。三人全都按各自锁定的位置紧贴同一石柱。上方冰冷的轮廓光雕出剪影。扬起的尘土被光柱映亮。摄影机剧烈抖动。

拍 2（BEAT 2，切。，50mm 特写对准 JAX，手持漂浮）：后背平贴石柱中央。太阳穴冒汗，下颌咬紧。眼神一次掠过画框外，微停顿，随后低沉沮丧地说：「不管那是什么——爆炸物对他来说毫无效果。」可见的呼吸。胸廓起伏。画框左侧柔和的冷轮廓光包裹住他脸的边缘。画面呼吸着、颤抖着。

拍 3（BEAT 3，切。，50mm 特写对准 ROKO，手持漂浮）：头盔已收起。紧贴石柱左侧。他透过牙缝猛地吐出一口气，摇了摇头，目光在画框外左侧找到 Jax：「我这边也一样。我们需要些别的手段。」沮丧，可见的呼吸。冷轮廓光接住他护肩的水晶边缘，在他下颌处散开淡淡的红色折光。画面颤抖。

拍 4（BEAT 4，切。，中全景对准石柱旁三人，手持剧烈晃动）：一阵突如其来的巨大冲击落在画框外——低沉重响震动整座巨厅。尘土自柱顶倾泻。油灯火焰猛晃闪烁，有些熄灭。小石砾在地砖上弹跳、飞滑。光柱随着尘土变浓而闪烁。左侧的 ROKO、中央的 JAX、右侧的 RAIN——三人全都一颤，双眼圆睁，头颅猛地向外甩去，扫视掩体外的巨厅。身体绷紧贴着颤抖的石柱。摄影机随冲击剧烈震颤，与之同步。

拍 5（BEAT 5，切。，50mm 特写对准 RAIN 窥望，手持颤抖）：RAIN 在石柱右缘，滑板仍夹在右腋下。头发垂过她带疤的眼睛。微停顿——她吸气，缓缓把脸探过石柱右缘，双眼眯起扫视眼前的巨厅。湿润鲜活的双眼里有眼神光（catch-lights）。细微的胸廓起伏。她转身时侧脸转入 3/4 角度。冰冷的背光沿石柱边缘渗过她的脸颊。画面随她的呼吸同步颤抖。
