## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): VIOLENT handheld throughout — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections. Operator stationary in both shots — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed moves: ZOOM and TILT only.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame at column bases. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS:
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips, thin red scar through left eyebrow down across the cheek. Grey cropped hooded utility jacket over black turtleneck top, black cargo pants, brown leather belt, olive thigh pouch holster. Pressed to the LEFT edge of the central column.
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-meter-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set angry eyes, thick-set muscular build. Stone-sculpted armored cuirass over tunic and draped knee-length skirt-robe, wide stone belt with relief, wrapped wrists. Two long stone swords, one in each hand. Stone sandals. Weathered grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine cracks. Moves with massive crushing weight, slow and deliberate.
BELL — massive antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern, hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored into the rock of the ceiling fissure.

SCENE 51E — THE REVEAL.

BEAT 1 (OVER-THE-SHOULDER on RAIN, planted camera, violent handheld, duration 2 seconds — brief and punchy): Camera planted close behind RAIN at the LEFT edge of the central column. We see her in foreground from behind — silver-platinum bob, the curve of her shoulder, edge of her grey hooded jacket, the nape of her neck. Her body occupies frame-RIGHT in soft-focus foreground silhouette. The vertical mass of the column edge frames the LEFT of frame. Beyond her, deep in focus, the open hall stretches in cold haze — and far in the distance, towering over everything, stands the GIANT STONE STATUE in full body, monolithic against the receding columns. Scale relationship reads instantly: she is small in cover, he is massive in open space. She holds frozen-still, only breath rising in her shoulders, hair barely shifting. Brief 2-second hold. Frame trembles with handheld shake throughout.

BEAT 2 (cut to RAIN's POV — distant low-angle witness camera, planted at floor level, MAXIMUM violent handheld shake): Camera position represents Rain's eye-line / vantage from behind her column edge. Long focal length, slightly compressed perspective. Documentary witness aesthetic — imperfect framing, raw, unsteady, telephoto amplifies the tremor.

Sub-beat A — SLOW STRIKE: Shot looks UP toward the towering GIANT STONE STATUE in the distance, his full body filling upper-center frame, columns at the LEFT and RIGHT edges framing him. Extreme low ground-up perspective makes him monolithic. He raises ONE stone sword in a SLOW, deliberate, methodical windup — heavy mass moving through the air, the blade lifting high. Held at the apex for a beat — pure threat. Then he swings DOWN in a slow heavy arc into ONE column at the frame edge. The blade strikes with bone-deep concussive impact — but the column does NOT break, it HOLDS. Burst of dust puffs out at point of contact, fine fragments shake loose, the column stands intact. Frame jolts with shockwave, oil lamps flicker, dust cascades from above. Subtle ZOOM-IN tightens during the impact. Restrained violence reads more menacing than destruction — he is showing power, not unleashing it.

Sub-beat B — ROAR: The Giant snaps his huge stone head back, mouth gaping wide, and unleashes a deep guttural ROAR upward at the ceiling. Stone face contorted in fury, deep-set eyes wide. Dust shakes loose from his shoulders.

Sub-beat C — TILT UP TO BELL: Camera begins a continuous upward TILT from its planted floor position, motivated by the Giant's upward roar — rotating past his head, climbing past the rough rock wall, reaching toward the ceiling fissure. Tilt reveals the massive ancient BRONZE BELL suspended by its heavy iron chain in the wide fissure overhead, swaying very slightly from the impact tremors, cold pale daylight silhouetting it from behind, dust drifting through the shaft of light, chain creaking faintly. Camera holds on the bell.

Throughout BEAT 2: low-angle witness perspective, planted operator far from the action, long lens with violent handheld shake. Documentary raw aesthetic. Only zoom and tilt rotation.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、带有眼神光（catch-lights）的湿润鲜活双眼、可见的呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，本场景全程）：剧烈的手持——频繁强烈的抖动、呼吸式机架、反应性纠正。两个镜头中操作者都静止——不得走动、不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄。允许的移动：仅 ZOOM 与 TILT。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱分列两侧，向雾中延展没入。石板地面。柱基处有低矮橙色火焰的小油灯。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从高高在上的岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。

角色 / 主体（CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS）：
RAIN——年轻女性，二十岁出头，银白金波波头带深黑发尾，一道细红疤贯穿左眉垂过脸颊。黑色高领内搭外罩灰色短款连帽工装夹克、黑色工装裤、棕色腰带、橄榄绿大腿收纳枪套。紧贴中央石柱左缘。
巨石像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨形雕石武士。光头，浓重凶悍的眉骨，深陷的愤怒双目，厚实魁梧的体格。束腰外衣之上披着石雕的铠甲胸甲，外罩垂及膝下的裙式长袍，宽石腰带带浮雕纹饰，缠绕的手腕。双手各持一把长石剑。石制凉鞋。风化的灰色石面、缝隙间青灰色的苔藓绿锈斑、细纹裂痕。以碾压般的重量活动，缓慢而从容。
钟（BELL）——一口巨大的古青铜钟，深色带绿锈的青铜表面，钟身四周环饰凸起浮雕的纹饰与铭文带，由一条锚固进天花板裂隙岩石中的沉重铁链吊挂着。

场景 51E——揭示（THE REVEAL）。

拍 1（BEAT 1，对准 RAIN 的过肩镜头（OVER-THE-SHOULDER），固定摄影机，剧烈手持，时长 2 秒——短促有力）：摄影机固定于中央石柱左缘、紧贴 RAIN 背后。我们从她背后望见她处于前景——银白金波波头、她肩头的弧线、灰色连帽夹克的边缘、她的后颈。她的身体以柔焦前景剪影占据画面右侧。石柱边缘的垂直体量框住画面左侧。越过她，纵深锐利处，敞开的巨厅在寒冷的雾中延伸——而在远方，高耸于万物之上，矗立着通体可见的巨石像，衬着退走的石柱显得巨石般屹立。尺度的关系一眼即读：她小巧地掩蔽着，他庞然地立于空场。她凝定不动，只有呼吸在肩头起伏，头发几乎不动。短暂 2 秒的定格。画框全程以手持抖动颤抖。

拍 2（BEAT 2，切到 RAIN 的视点（POV）——远处低角度目击摄影机，固定于地面高度，极致剧烈手持抖动）：摄影机位置代表 Rain 从她石柱边缘后的视线 / 视角。长焦镜头，透视频有轻微压缩。纪实目击美学——不完美的取景、原始、不稳定，长焦放大震颤。

子拍 A——缓慢挥击（SLOW STRIKE）：镜头仰视远处高耸的巨石像，他整副身体填满画面上方中央，左右边缘的石柱将他框住。极端的贴地仰视视角令他显得宛如巨石。他举起一把石剑，慢速、从容、机械地蓄势——沉重的质量划过空气，刀刃高举。在最高点停驻一拍——纯粹的威胁。随后他向下挥出一记缓慢沉重的弧线，砸进画面边缘的其中一根石柱。刀刃以直透骨髓的冲击击中——但石柱并未断裂，它撑住了。撞击点喷出一团尘雾，细小的碎片簌簌抖落，石柱依然完好。画框随冲击波震荡，油灯摇曳，尘土自上方倾泻。冲击瞬间有微妙的拉近（ZOOM-IN）收紧。受控的暴力读起来比破坏更令人胆寒——他是在展示力量，而非宣泄力量。

子拍 B——咆哮（ROAR）：巨像猛然把头往后一甩，石嘴大张，朝天花板发出一声低沉的喉音咆哮。石脸因暴怒而扭曲，深陷的双眼圆睁。尘土自他肩头抖落。

子拍 C——上摇到钟（TILT UP TO BELL）：摄影机从固定的地面位置开始一段连续的向上摇摄，被巨像向上的咆哮牵引——摇过他的头部，沿着粗糙的岩壁向上攀爬，伸向天花板裂隙。摇摄揭示出那口巨大的古青铜钟，由沉重的铁链吊挂在头顶宽阔的裂隙中，因冲击震动而微微晃动，背后有冰冷苍白的天光映出剪影，尘埃在光柱中飘荡，铁链发出细微的吱嘎声。摄影机停驻在钟上。

整个拍 2 贯穿：低角度目击视角，操作者远离动作立于原地，长镜头叠加剧烈的手持抖动。纪实原始美学。只做缩放下或上下摇转。
