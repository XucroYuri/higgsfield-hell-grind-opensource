## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this shot): Documentary witness camera — as if filmed by an ordinary human in cover at floor level, far from the action. The camera position represents RAIN's eye-line / vantage from behind her column. Long focal length, slightly compressed perspective. Operator stationary throughout — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed camera moves: ZOOM (in/out) and TILT (vertical rotation only). VIOLENT handheld shake at all times — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections. Telephoto amplifies the tremor. Documentary raw aesthetic — imperfect, reactive, unsteady.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame at column bases. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS:
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-meter-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald head, heavy menacing brow, deep-set angry eyes, thick-set muscular build. Stone-sculpted armored cuirass over a tunic and draped knee-length skirt-robe, wide stone belt with relief pattern, wrapped wrists. Wields two long stone swords, one in each hand. Stone sandals on huge feet. Weathered grey stone surface, mossy green-grey patina in carved crevices, fine cracks across the form. Animated — moves with massive crushing weight, slow and deliberate.
BELL — massive antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern around its body, hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored deep into the rock of the ceiling fissure above.

SCENE 51E — THE REVEAL.

BEAT 1 (single continuous shot, distant low-angle witness handheld, planted at floor level, MAXIMUM violent shake):

Sub-beat A — SLOW STRIKE (low-angle wide on the giant): Shot opens at hall floor level looking UP toward the towering GIANT STONE STATUE in the distance, his full body filling the upper-center of frame, columns at the LEFT and RIGHT edges framing him. Extreme low ground-up perspective makes him monolithic. He raises ONE stone sword in a SLOW, deliberate, methodical windup — heavy mass moving through the air, the blade lifting high. Held at the apex for a beat — pure threat. Then he swings the sword DOWN in a slow heavy arc into ONE of the columns at the frame edge. The blade strikes the column with a bone-deep concussive impact — but the column does NOT break. It HOLDS. A burst of dust puffs out at the point of contact, fine stone fragments shake loose, the column stands intact. The entire frame jolts with the concussive shockwave, oil lamps below flicker, dust cascades from the ceiling. Subtle ZOOM-IN tightens during the impact. The slow, restrained violence reads more menacing than destruction — he is showing power, not unleashing it.

Sub-beat B — ROAR: The Giant snaps his huge stone head back, mouth gaping wide, and unleashes a deep guttural ROAR upward at the ceiling. Stone face contorted in fury, deep-set eyes wide. Dust shakes loose from his shoulders.

Sub-beat C — TILT UP TO BELL: Camera begins a continuous upward TILT from its planted floor position, motivated by the Giant's upward roar — rotating past his head, climbing past the rough rock wall, reaching toward the ceiling fissure. Tilt reveals the massive ancient BRONZE BELL suspended by its heavy iron chain in the wide fissure overhead, swaying very slightly from the impact tremors, cold pale daylight silhouetting it from behind, dust drifting through the shaft of light, chain creaking faintly. Camera holds on the bell.

Throughout: low-angle witness perspective from floor level, planted operator far from the action, long lens with violent handheld shake. Documentary raw aesthetic. No walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing — only zoom and tilt rotation.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、带有眼神光（catch-lights）的湿润鲜活双眼、可见的呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，本镜头）：纪实目击摄影机——仿佛由一位躲在楼层地面掩体里、远离动作的普通人所拍。摄影机位置代表 RAIN 的视线 / 从她石柱后方的视角。长焦镜头，透视频有轻微压缩。操作者全程静止——不得走动、不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄。允许的摄影机移动：ZOOM（推近/拉远）和 TILT（仅上下垂直转动）。全程剧烈的手持抖动——频繁强烈的抖动、呼吸式机架、反应性纠正。长焦放大了震颤。纪实目击美学：不完美的取景、反应本能的操作者、原始而不稳定。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱分列两侧，向雾中延展没入。石板地面。柱基处有低矮橙色火焰的小油灯。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从高高在上的岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。

角色 / 主体（CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS）：
巨石像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨形雕石武士。光头，浓重凶悍的眉骨，深陷的愤怒双目，厚实魁梧的体格。束腰外衣之上披着石雕的铠甲胸甲，外罩垂及膝下的裙式长袍，一条带浮雕纹饰的宽石腰带，缠绕的手腕。双手各持一把长石剑。巨足上蹬着石制凉鞋。风化的灰色石面，雕凿缝隙间有青灰色的苔藓绿锈斑，形体表面布满细纹裂痕。有生命——以碾压般的重量活动，缓慢而从容。
钟（BELL）——一口巨大的古青铜钟，深色带绿锈的青铜表面，钟身四周环饰凸起浮雕的纹饰与铭文带，由一条深深锚固进上方岩顶裂隙岩石中的沉重铁链吊挂着。

场景 51E——揭示（THE REVEAL）。

拍 1（BEAT 1，单个连续镜头，远处低角度目击手持，固定于楼层地面，极致剧烈抖动）：

子拍 A——轰砸（SMASH，低角度全景对准巨像）：镜头在巨厅地面高度开场，仰视远处高耸的巨石像，他整副身体填满画面上方中央，左右边缘的石柱将他框住。极端的贴地仰视视角令他显得宛如巨石。他双刀向外挥出一道宽阔的劈斩弧线——两把刀刃同时砸穿画面边缘的石柱。巨石向外爆裂成无数碎块，尘云翻涌成浓雾，碎屑在画面前景地面上四散弹跳。整幅画面被冲击震动，下方油灯摇曳闪烁。冲击瞬间有微妙的推近（ZOOM-IN）收紧。

子拍 B——咆哮（ROAR）：巨像猛然把头往后一甩，石嘴大张，朝天花板发出一声低沉的喉音咆哮。石脸因暴怒而扭曲，深陷的双眼圆睁。尘土自他肩头抖落。

子拍 C——上摇到钟（TILT UP TO BELL）：摄影机从固定的地面位置开始一段连续的向上摇摄，被巨像向上的咆哮牵引——摇过他的头部，沿着粗糙的岩壁向上攀爬，伸向天花板裂隙。摇摄揭示出那口巨大的古青铜钟，由沉重的铁链吊挂在头顶宽阔的裂隙中，因冲击震动微微晃动，背后有冰冷苍白的天光映出它的剪影，尘埃在光柱中飘荡，铁链发出细微的吱嘎声。摄影机停驻在钟上。

全片贯穿：楼层地面的低角度目击视角，操作者远离动作立于原地，长镜头叠加剧烈的手持抖动。纪实原始美学——不完美、反应本能、不稳定。不得走动、不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄——只准缩放与上下摇转。
