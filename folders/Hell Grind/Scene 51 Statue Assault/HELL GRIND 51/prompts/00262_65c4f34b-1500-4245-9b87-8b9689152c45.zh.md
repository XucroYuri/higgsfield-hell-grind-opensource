Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

VÉRITÉ FOREGROUND RULE: In every shot the FOREGROUND is partially obstructed by environmental elements out of focus.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides — but the giant TOWERS even higher than the hall's columns. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Atmospheric haze throughout. Cave-rock walls climb dramatically toward the ceiling fissure HIGH OVERHEAD with cold pale daylight pouring down through the crack.

CHARACTERS:
ROKO — man, mid-20s. Full translucent blood-red crystalline armor with jagged crystal spikes on shoulders and forearms. Crystal HELMET FULLY SEALED OVER HIS FACE — translucent red faceted crystal helmet form with glowing internal refraction where his eyes are, jagged crystal points along the crown. Hands and forearms in articulated red crystalline gauntlets with jagged crystal spikes on the back of the forearm capable of piercing stone for purchase. Roko CARRIES NO WEAPON — he has no sword, no slingshot, no firearm. His only weapon and tool is his crystalline armor itself.

Stone armored cuirass over a tunic with weathered cracks across the chest and abdomen, ornate carved belt with patterned border, fabric-wrapped stone wrists. Holds the long stone sword RAISED in TWO HANDS BEHIND HIS HEAD when frozen — both stone hands gripping the hilt, arms locked aloft above his head. Weathered grey granite, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine cracks across cuirass and arms, dust accumulation.

His helmet's eye-glow burns intensely behind the translucent crystal, head tilted up slightly as if scanning the back of the giant's skull above him. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY THROUGHOUT — maximum handheld jitter, frame trembling intensely even on this static held moment.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — GIANT WAKES, ROARS UPWARD, SPINS TO SHAKE OFF (witness through-the-column dynamic shot, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Camera planted on the OPEN side of the hall, framed THROUGH a vertical slot/gap alongside a stone column — the dark vertical mass of a column dominates one side of frame as a soft-focus foreground bracket, partially blocking view, we are spying past it. Beyond the column gap: the 50-meter GIANT STONE STATUE in three-quarter angle, his full body visible through the frame. Roko is visible as a tiny red ant-figure clinging to the giant's BACK at the neck area, dwarfed by the colossal stone mass. Cold pale daylight from the high fissure pours down past the giant. Atmospheric haze throughout.
Action: Open on the giant FROZEN — eyes still dark, body locked in raised-sword pose, Roko clinging tiny on the back of his neck.
Then suddenly the GIANT'S EYES RELIGHT — the deep-set carved eyes IGNITE WITH RETURNING AMBER GLOW, the warm inner light SNAPPING BACK ON in both eye sockets, surging back to life. Consciousness returns in a single instant.
Immediately the GIANT THROWS HIS HEAD BACK and ROARS UPWARD — chin lifting toward the high cave ceiling, mouth gaping wide open in stone, his face TURNED UP TO THE SKY as he releases a deafening primal STONE ROAR straight upward into the cavern's heights. The roar is enormous, so loud it visibly trembles the cavern, dust shaking from the upper rock, oil lamps flickering. Jaw cracking open hard.
THEN — without any pause — the giant EXPLODES into VIOLENT MOTION, beginning to SPIN AND TWIST his massive body wildly to throw Roko off. He WHIRLS his torso hard side-to-side, shoulders rolling, body jerking and spinning in violent torque, head whipping around trying to spot the climber on his back. His FREE STONE HAND reaches behind toward his neck, grasping toward where Roko clings. He BUCKS, lurches, twists, every ounce of stone mass in chaotic motion to dislodge the parasite.
Roko, the tiny red ant, is FLUNG hard with each twist of the giant's body — body slammed against the giant's neck and back from each thrash, his crystalline gauntlets straining at their grip points, crystal spikes wedged deep but the entire body whipped around as the giant spins. Dust and stone fragments EXPLODING from the giant's joints from the violent motion.
The camera STRUGGLES to track the chaos through the foreground column slot — frame OVERSHOOTS, briefly LOSES the giant in motion blur, finds him again. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY THROUGHOUT — maximum handheld jitter, frame nearly destabilized.
