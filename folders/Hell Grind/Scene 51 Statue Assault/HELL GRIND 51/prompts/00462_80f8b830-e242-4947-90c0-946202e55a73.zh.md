## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): VIOLENT handheld throughout — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Even in close-ups the frame floats and trembles.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame glow at column bases. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. Dominant tones: cold blue-gray stone (60), deep shadow black (30), warm amber lamp flame (10).

CHARACTERS:
JAX — Black man, mid-20s, bleached yellow buzz-cut hair, clear wraparound safety glasses, fresh red graze on his forehead. Black tactical hooded jacket with grey paneling, olive cargo pants, fingerless gloves, brown belt with yellow zipper accents, worn combat boots.
ROKO — man, mid-20s, dark messy shoulder-length hair, faint mustache, red abraded marks across the bridge of his nose. Full translucent blood-red crystalline armor with jagged crystal spikes on shoulders and forearms. Crystal helmet RETRACTED — face fully exposed.
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips, thin red scar through left eyebrow down across the cheek. Grey cropped hooded utility jacket over black turtleneck, black cargo pants, brown belt, olive thigh pouch holster on right leg. NO hovering board in this scene.

COLUMN STAGING (locked):
RAIN — pressed to the LEFT edge of the central column.
JAX — back flat to the CENTER of the column.
ROKO — pressed to the RIGHT side of the column.

SCENE 51F — PLAN REVEAL (four shots, single prompt, in this exact order).

SHOT 1 — RAIN PEEK (50mm CU on Rain at column edge, handheld trembles).
Framing: Tight close-up on RAIN at the LEFT edge of the central column. Three-quarter front, body still tucked behind cover, only her shoulder, side of head and face leaning past the edge. The massive carved stone column dominates the RIGHT side of frame as a vertical dark mass. Background behind her: deep dark cave-rock wall, mostly black, faint oil-lamp glow in far bottom-left corner. Cold rim light catches the silver of her hair, scarred eye visible.
Action: She is leaned out past the column edge, eyes locked on something off-frame in the distance — alert, intent, processing. Lips slightly parted, breath visible. Catch-light in wet living eyes. Frame trembles violently.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — RAIN TURNS BACK, OTS WITH JAX (medium-close, handheld trembles).
Framing: Rain on frame-LEFT in three-quarter, in sharp focus, having just pulled back from the peek. Behind her in soft-focus foreground frame-RIGHT: JAX's shoulder and the side of his face, out of focus, anchoring the OTS angle. The column's stone surface fills the background between and behind them. Cold rim light defines Rain's profile.
Action: Rain whips her head back from the column edge to look at Jax (and Roko beyond him), hair settling against her scarred cheek. Micro-pause. Then low and urgent, eyes locked on his: "He moves on chant." Visible breath. Frame trembles.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — JAX REACTION CU (50mm CU on Jax, handheld trembles).
Framing: Tight close-up on JAX, back flat to the center of the column. Sweat at his temple, fresh graze on forehead, safety glasses catching faint cold rim from frame-left. Soft-focus column texture behind.
Action: Micro-pause as he processes — brow furrowing under the safety glasses, eyes flicking between Rain and Roko off-frame. Then incredulous, sharp: "WHAT?!" Eyes wide, mouth open on the word. Visible breath, chest heaves. Frame trembles.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — RAIN EXPLAINS THE PLAN (50mm CU on Rain, handheld trembles).
Framing: Tight close-up on RAIN at the column's LEFT edge, three-quarter front toward camera. Cold backlight wraps the column edge behind her. Hair past her scarred eye. Background: dark stone column and hazy depth.
Action: Rain holds steady eye-line off-frame on her teammates, intent, certain — the look of someone who has just figured something out. She speaks low and fast, almost a whisper, urgent and clear, laying out the plan: "He moves to the chant. And when something interrupts that chant — he stops listening." Beat. A faint flicker in her eyes — the second part of the realization landing. "And opens his mouth." She holds the look on Jax, waiting for it to register. Catch-light in wet eyes, breath visible, micro-pauses between phrases. Frame trembles.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、带有眼神光（catch-lights）的湿润鲜活双眼、可见的呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，本场景全程）：全程剧烈的手持——频繁强烈的抖动、呼吸式机架、反应性纠正。操作者在每个镜头中都静止——不得走动、不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄。即便在特写中，画框也在漂浮与颤抖。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱分列两侧，向雾中延展没入。石板地面。柱基处有低矮橙色火焰的小油灯发光。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从高高在上的岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。主色调：冰冷蓝灰色石面（60）、深阴影黑（30）、暖琥珀色油灯火焰（10）。

角色（CHARACTERS）：
JAX——黑人男性，二十多岁，漂白金黄寸头，透明裹式安全护目镜，前额一道新鲜的红色擦伤。黑色战术连帽夹克带灰色拼接、橄榄绿工装裤、无指手套、带黄色拉链点缀的棕色腰带、磨损的战斗靴。
ROKO——男性，二十多岁，深色凌乱垂肩长发，淡胡须，鼻梁上红色擦伤痕迹。通体红色半透明水晶装甲，肩部与前臂带有参差水晶利刺。水晶头盔已收起（RETRACTED）——脸部完全暴露。
RAIN——年轻女性，二十岁出头，银白金波波头带深黑发尾，一道细红疤贯穿左眉垂过脸颊。黑色高领内搭外罩灰色短款连帽工装夹克、黑色工装裤、棕色腰带、右腿束着橄榄绿大腿收纳枪套。本场景不出现悬浮滑板。

石柱走位（COLUMN STAGING，锁定）：
RAIN——紧贴中央石柱的左缘。
JAX——后背平贴石柱中央。
ROKO——紧贴石柱右侧。

场景 51F——计划揭示（PLAN REVEAL，四个镜头，单条提示，按此精确顺序）。

镜头 1——RAIN 窥探（SHOT 1——RAIN PEEK，50mm 特写对准石柱边缘的 Rain，手持颤抖）。
取景（Framing）：紧紧特写石柱左缘的 RAIN。3/4 正面，身体仍藏在掩体后，只有她的肩膀、侧脸与面孔探出边缘。巨大的雕花石柱作为垂直的暗色体量占据画面右侧。她身后背景：深黑的洞穴岩壁，大多漆黑，远左下角有微弱的油灯光晕。冷轮廓光接出她银发的反光，带疤的眼睛可见。
动作（Action）：她探身越过石柱边缘，双眼锁定远方画框外的某个目标——警觉、专注、在盘算。双唇微启，可见的呼吸。湿润鲜活双眼里有眼神光（catch-lights）。画框剧烈颤抖。

切。

镜头 2——RAIN 转回，与 JAX 过肩（SHOT 2——RAIN TURNS BACK, OTS WITH JAX，中近景，手持颤抖）。
取景（Framing）：Rain 在画面左侧呈 3/4 角度，锐利对焦，刚刚探视收身。她身后柔焦的前景画面右侧：JAX 的肩膀与他侧脸，失焦，锚定这组过肩（OTS）取景。石柱的石面填满两人之间与身后的背景。冷轮廓光勾出 Rain 的侧影。
动作（Action）：Rain 把头从石柱边缘猛甩回来，看向 Jax（及其身后的 Roko），头发垂落在她带疤的脸颊上。微停顿。随后低沉而急迫，双眼锁在他身上：「他靠念诵行动。」可见的呼吸。画面颤抖。

切。

镜头 3——JAX 反应特写（SHOT 3——JAX REACTION CU，50mm 特写对准 Jax，手持颤抖）。
取景（Framing）：紧紧特写 JAX，后背平贴石柱中央。太阳穴冒汗，前额新鲜的擦伤，护目镜接住画框左侧微弱的冷轮廓光。身后是柔焦的石柱纹理。
动作（Action）：他消化信息时微停顿——护目镜下的眉头皱起，眼神在画框外的 Rain 与 Roko 之间闪动。随后难以置信、尖利地：「什么？！」双眼圆睁，说出那字时嘴大张。可见的呼吸，胸口起伏。画面颤抖。

切。

镜头 4——RAIN 说明计划（SHOT 4——RAIN EXPLAINS THE PLAN，50mm 特写对准 Rain，手持颤抖）。
取景（Framing）：紧紧特写石柱左缘的 RAIN，3/4 正对镜头。冰冷的背光在她的石柱边缘勾勒。垂在她带疤眼睛前的头发。背景：暗色的石柱与雾蒙的深处。
动作（Action）：Rain 稳住画框外队友方向上的视线，专注、笃定——那种刚刚想通某件事的人的神情。她语速低快，近乎耳语，急迫而清晰，铺陈出计划：「他应念诵而行动。而一旦有什么打断了那念诵——他便停止聆听。」一拍。她眼里闪过一丝微光——觉悟的后半截落定。「然后张开他的嘴。」她顿住望向 Jax 的目光，等着信息被他接收到。湿润双眼里有眼神光，可见的呼吸，各短句之间有微停顿。画面颤抖。
