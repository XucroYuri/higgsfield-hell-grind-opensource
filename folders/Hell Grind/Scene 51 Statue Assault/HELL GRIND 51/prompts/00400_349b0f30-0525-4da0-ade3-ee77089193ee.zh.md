Jax's brow lifts faintly under the safety glasses, an almost-imperceptible nod-energy in his expression. Roko's jaw sets harder, eyes returning the lock with calm conviction. Catch-light in both pairs of wet eyes, breath visible, chest rises. The pact is silent and complete.
