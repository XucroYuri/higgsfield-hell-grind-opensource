The GIANT, fueled by frustration at the missed first strike, immediately winds up for ANOTHER attack — he RAISES the long stone sword UP and BACK, winding the blade up high above his head, sword arm climbing into the upper portion of frame, body coiling for a downward chop, head turning to track Rain off-frame, brow heavy with mute fury. Heavy stone weight in motion, mossy dust shaking loose from his joints.

Sub-beat C — FREEZE: Then suddenly — at the APEX of the second windup, sword RAISED high above his head, body tensed for the strike — the GIANT FREEZES. His DEEP-SET CARVED EYES go DARK in the same instant — the faint inner glow EXTINGUISHES, snuffed out, eyes becoming dull lifeless stone. His body LOCKS in the raised-sword pose — sword aloft above his head, arm fully extended upward, head turned mid-track, mouth set hard. He becomes a statue mid-strike, frozen in unnatural stillness, the second swing never completing. Faint dust still drifting off his joints from the aborted motion. Camera SETTLES on the frozen giant in disbelief. Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY THROUGHOUT — maximum handheld jitter.
