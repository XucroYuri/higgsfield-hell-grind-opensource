Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this shot): Witness-style documentary camera, amateur vérité aesthetic — as if filmed raw on a consumer camera by a person lying or crouched on the ground in cover. Despite the amateur look the rendering remains 8K photorealistic. Camera placed at GROUND LEVEL — extreme low angle, almost floor-eye-view. Operator stationary — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed moves: minor reactive reframing only. VIOLENT handheld shake throughout. Imperfect framing, raw and reactive.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame at column bases. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS:
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set eyes, thick-set muscular build. Stone armored cuirass over tunic and draped knee-length skirt-robe, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in his right hand, left hand empty. Stone sandals. Weathered grey stone, mossy green-grey patina, fine cracks. Moves with massive crushing weight, slow and deliberate. SCALE: surrounding hall columns reach only to his hip or chest — he dwarfs the architecture entirely.

SCENE 51E — GROUND LEVEL STRIKE (single continuous shot).

FRAMING (held throughout): Camera planted at GROUND LEVEL on the stone-tiled floor of the hall, almost lying-down POV. The view looks UPWARD and through a FOREGROUND COLUMN — the stone base and lower body of one of the hall columns occupies a portion of the LEFT side of frame in soft-focus close foreground, partially blocking the view, framing the shot like we are peeking past it. An oil lamp glows softly at its base in foreground. Beyond the foreground column, in the middle ground, stands the GIANT STONE STATUE in profile or three-quarter angle, his full towering 20-meter form rising from the floor up out of the top of the frame because of the extreme low angle. Surrounding regular hall columns visibly reaching only to his hip — instantly readable as a colossus. The dwarfed scale and looming low perspective make him feel mythic and oppressive.

Sub-beat A — HOLD AND WIND-UP: Open on the giant standing in the middle ground, sword in his right hand held low. He raises the sword in a SLOW deliberate methodical windup — heavy mass arcing through air, blade lifting high above his shoulder. Held at the apex for a held beat — pure threat. Camera shakes violently throughout, the operator on the ground trembling with the rig.

Sub-beat B — STRIKE: He swings DOWN in a slow heavy arc, the blade smashing into a column standing in the middle distance. SINGLE strike. Bone-deep concussive impact — the column does NOT shatter completely, but a LARGE CHUNK of stone breaks off from its surface where the blade hit. Burst of dust explodes outward at point of contact. Frame jolts violently with the shockwave, oil lamps flicker, fine debris rains.

Sub-beat C — CHUNK FALLS: The broken slab of stone, a heavy fragment, TUMBLES through the air from the column down toward the floor — falling with real weight, mass-correct gravity. After a beat of falling it CRASHES onto the stone-tiled floor with a massive secondary impact — a thunderous noise, stone-on-stone shatter, the chunk breaks into smaller pieces on landing, dust kicks up violently, debris skitters across the tiles. Frame jolts again with the second impact. The foreground column we are hiding behind trembles visibly. Oil lamp in foreground sputters.

Throughout: ground-level vérité POV through a foreground column, planted operator, violent handheld shake, telephoto reach. Two distinct impacts within the beat — the strike, and the chunk hitting the floor. Documentary raw aesthetic, imperfect reactive framing. The camera operator is a person hiding on the ground trying to film a monster.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、带有眼神光（catch-lights）的湿润鲜活双眼、可见的呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，两个镜头通用）：目击式纪实手持，业余真实感（vérité）美学，8K 照片级写实渲染。操作者在每个镜头中都静止——不得走动、不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄。只允许旋转式移动与镜头驱动的变焦。全程极致剧烈的手持抖动。不完美的原始取景。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱。石板地面。油灯在柱基处发光。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。石厅的墙壁与石柱由古老的共鸣石雕凿而成——它们对强力的声波起反应，使穿过大厅的声波产生振动与扭曲。

角色 / 主体（CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS）：
RAIN——年轻女性，二十岁出头，银白金波波头带深黑发尾，左眉与脸颊各有一道贯穿的疤。黑色高领内搭外罩灰色短款连帽工装夹克。
JAX——黑人男性，二十多岁，漂白金黄寸头，透明安全护目镜，前额一道新鲜的红色擦伤。黑色战术连帽夹克，橄榄绿工装裤。
ROKO——男性，二十多岁，深色凌乱长发，淡胡须，鼻梁上红色擦伤痕迹。通体红色半透明水晶装甲，带参差水晶利刺，头盔已收起（RETRACTED）。

场景 51E——共鸣（RESONANCE，两个镜头，单条提示）。

镜头 1——震颤的墙壁（SHOT 1——VIBRATING WALLS，中全景对准洞穴壁面）。
取景（Framing）：摄影机固定于巨厅地面高度，对准洞穴壁面的一段与相邻的石柱——古雕石质表面填满画面大部分。冰冷苍白的轮廓光擦过岩石纹理。巨厅雾蒙蒙的深处只在边缘隐约可见，柔和而失焦。画框底角地面有一盏低燃的油灯。
动作（Action）：在巨像咆哮的余波中，石墙与石柱的表面开始震颤——细微的微小震动肉眼可见地在岩面上涟漪般扩散，尘土成片簌簌抖落。一圈圈类似热霾的扭曲涟漪从墙前空气中向外扩散，在声波被共鸣石吸收与折射时令光线弯曲变形。可见的扭曲感如波涌动，仿佛把声能化为了实质。空气在石面浮雕前折射时，那些浮雕纹饰微微闪烁、扭动。尘土在被扰动的空气中向上飘移。画面在机内扭曲之上又叠加以剧烈的手持抖动而猛烈震颤。这面墙因被看见的声音而活了起来。

切。

镜头 2——主观沉浸（SHOT 2——SUBJECTIVE IMMERSION，中景对准石柱旁的三位主角，声波冲刷过他们）。
取景（Framing）：中景对准紧贴掩体石柱的 RAIN、JAX 与 ROKO——RAIN 在石柱左缘，JAX 在中央，ROKO 在右侧。他们的身体紧张地蹲伏在石面上。上方冰冷的轮廓光雕出他们的剪影。背景：巨厅雾蒙的深处。
动作（Action）：涟漪般的声学扭曲波动冲刷过他们、穿过画框——可见的类热霾涟漪令他们身体周围的空气弯曲，令身后石柱扭曲，令他们的剪影随之脉动变形。他们的脸对这股感知上的扰动明显作出反应——双眼紧闭、下颌咬紧、双手本能地抬起捂住耳朵以抵御难以忍受的共鸣。RAIN 退缩并咬紧牙关。JAX 皱眉，头垂向前。ROKO 面部抽搐，他水晶装甲的嗡鸣清晰可闻，肩上水晶利刺显示出细微的震动。尘土与小碎石从上方抖落，纷纷撒在他们身上。整幅画面以最大强度颤抖——手持抖动叠加可见的空气扭曲把现实撕得变形。他们的世界正被那声音震得四分五裂。
