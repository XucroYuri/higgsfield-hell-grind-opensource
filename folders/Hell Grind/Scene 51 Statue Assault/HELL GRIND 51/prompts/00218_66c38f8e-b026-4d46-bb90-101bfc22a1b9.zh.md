VÉRITÉ FOREGROUND RULE: In every shot the FOREGROUND is partially obstructed by environmental elements out of focus.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate stone columns flank both sides — but the giant TOWERS even higher. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Atmospheric haze throughout. Cave-rock walls climb dramatically toward the ceiling fissure HIGH OVERHEAD.

SHOT 3 — INSIDE THE MOUTH POV, RAIN DELIVERS THE PLASTIQUE, GIANT SUDDENLY MOVES (medium shot from inside the mouth, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: COMPLEX INSIDE-MOUTH POV. The camera is positioned INSIDE the giant's stone mouth cavity — looking out toward the mouth opening from within. ROKO becomes VISIBLE in MEDIUM SHOT FRAMING — his red crystalline-armored body silhouetted in the mouth opening at the front of frame, gripping the upper and lower lip edges with both crystal gauntlets, body squeezed into the opening, helmet's red faceted form catching the cold light. He has just opened the mouth from outside.

She is at the edge of the open mouth alongside Roko, board hovering steady, hair whipping. In her hand: the ROUND SPHERICAL PLASTIQUE — softball-sized, off-white pre-formed sphere of plastique explosive. Roko receives it.
SUDDEN MOVEMENT: At the moment of the transfer, the GIANT SUDDENLY BEGINS TO MOVE — the stone mouth around them STARTS TO TWITCH AND CLOSE, the carved stone jaw beginning to clench shut, the cavity around them BEGINNING TO SHIFT as the giant's head moves. The cold daylight from outside flickers/shifts as the jaw moves. ROKO STUMBLES — his footing on the lip edge slips as the jaw moves under him, body lurching, almost losing his grip. He scrambles to maintain position. Rain reacts with shock, banking her board hard to escape. The plastique remains in Roko's grip.
Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY THROUGHOUT — maximum handheld jitter, intensified as the mouth begins to move.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — CLOSE-UP, GIANT'S EYES RELIGHT, BEGINS TO MOVE (close-up on giant's face, MAXIMUM VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Tight close-up captured at three-quarter angle to the GIANT'S FACE — bald stone skull, heavy menacing brow, deep-set carved eyes filling the frame, nose, mouth at the lower edge (with Roko visible in extreme miniature inside/at the mouth opening, ear-sized scale anchor). Then suddenly the GIANT'S EYES RELIGHT — the deep-set carved eyes IGNITE WITH RETURNING AMBER GLOW, the warm inner light SNAPPING BACK ON in both eye sockets, surging back to life with a flare of intensity. Consciousness returns. The transition is fast and definitive.
Immediately the GIANT BEGINS TO MOVE — head jerking forward, brow lowering in renewed fury, jaw working/clenching, mouth beginning to close on Roko at the lower edge of frame. His body coiling for new motion, stone weight shifting, dust EXPLODING from joints as motion returns. The third revival lands — alive again, more dangerous than ever, with Roko now critically positioned inside or at the edge of his mouth.
Camera SHAKES VIOLENTLY THROUGHOUT — maximum handheld jitter, frame nearly destabilized as the giant surges back to life.
