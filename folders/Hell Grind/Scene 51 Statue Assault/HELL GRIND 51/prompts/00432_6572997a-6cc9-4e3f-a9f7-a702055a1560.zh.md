Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone-carved giant: weathered granite-grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine hairline cracks, fine dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, visible breath. The stone giant is animated with weight and intent; menace reads through head angle, silhouette, deliberate stillness of stone catching cold light.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (all shots in this scene): Witness-style documentary handheld, amateur vérité aesthetic, 8K photorealistic rendering. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Only rotational moves (tilt, minor pan) and lens-driven zoom allowed. MAXIMUM violent handheld shake throughout — the most extreme of the entire scene, peaking on impacts and on the roar. Imperfect raw framing.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald head with deep aged cracks running across the crown and forehead. Heavy stone brow with prominent horizontal wrinkles carved across the forehead, deep furrow between the brows, permanent fierce scowl. Broad flat nose, thick downturned mouth, square heavy jaw, visible stone ears. Deep-set eye sockets that GLOW with intense bright RED supernatural light — burning crimson luminescence radiating from deep within the sockets, casting strong red rim-light onto the brow ridge, bridge of the nose, and upper cheekbones, with a faint red bloom on the surrounding stone. Thick-set heavy muscular build. Layered stone shoulder pauldrons — multiple stacked plates on each shoulder. Stone armored cuirass over the torso with horizontal armor lines across the chest. Wide stone belt at the waist engraved with a raised-relief Greek-key meander pattern. Below the belt — a draped knee-length skirt-robe with heavy vertical folds wrapping the legs. Stone wrappings around both forearms and wrists like bandage bindings. Stone sandals. Holds ONLY ONE long stone sword in his RIGHT hand — heavy stone blade with grip wrapped in stone binding. His LEFT hand is empty, hanging as a closed stone fist. Weathered grey stone with mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine cracks across the body especially the head. Moves with crushing weight.

SCENE 51E — STRIKE, FREEZE, REIGNITION, ROAR (five shots, single prompt).

SHOT 1 — HORIZONTAL SWING (low-angle three-quarter close-up).
Framing: Low-angle close-up, camera planted at ground level looking UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE. Telephoto compression. Giant positioned slightly LEFT of center, cropped at the WAIST — only upper body visible: layered pauldrons, cuirass, wrapped right wrist, bald cracked head with heavy wrinkled brow, downturned stone mouth, deep-set sockets blazing with intense red glow. Right hand grips the long stone sword, blade held HIGH and to the side, raised in preparation. Left hand empty as a closed fist. Frame-RIGHT: dark vertical mass of an adjacent column with rugged relief. Frame-LEFT: hazy cave depth. Cold rim light catches the contour of his profile and the edge of the raised blade; red eye-glow bleeds onto the brow ridge.
Action: SLOW windup — the giant holds the sword raised high for a held beat, building tension, red eyes fixed and burning. Then EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE — the blade WHIPS in a fast HORIZONTAL ARC across his body, sweeping sideways through the air at chest-to-hip level with brutal speed. Heavy motion blur on the blade as it cuts through frame. The blade SMASHES into the lower portion of a column (hip-level strike) with sudden bone-deep concussive impact — dust explodes outward, fragments spray, frame jolts violently with the shockwave at maximum shake intensity. The contrast between slow buildup and fast release amplifies the impact.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — COLUMN CHUNK FALL (close-up on column).
Framing: Tight close-up on the LOWER section of a hall column near floor level — weathered carved stone, fine relief patterns, mossy patina in crevices. Column fills frame vertically. Cold rim light catches the surface texture. Background: hazy hall depth, out-of-focus.
Action: The column stands intact for a held beat. Then SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT — an unseen blade smashes horizontally into the column from off-frame, stone surface fractures violently. A LARGE CHUNK of stone breaks off, hairline cracks racing outward. The chunk TUMBLES forward and downward through frame, falling with real mass-correct gravity, rotating slowly. Camera tilts down with it slightly. The chunk SLAMS into the stone-tiled floor at the bottom of frame with a thunderous secondary impact — stone-on-stone shatter, breaking into pieces, dust kicks up violently, debris skitters across tiles. Frame jolts violently on both impacts. Dust hangs heavy after.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — SECOND WINDUP, POWER-DOWN FREEZE (low-angle wide three-quarter).
Framing: Low-angle wide shot, camera planted at ground level looking UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE. Telephoto compression. Full upper body and raised arm in frame — cropped at the knee-robe to read the windup silhouette in full. Background: hazy hall depth, broken column stumps faintly visible. Cold rim light from the ceiling fissure rakes across his back contour and pauldrons; red eye-glow burns strong from deep sockets, casting crimson wash on the brow.
Action: The Giant takes ONE heavy step forward and lifts the stone sword in a slow methodical windup — heavy mass arcing through air, blade rising HIGH above his right shoulder into the apex windup position, body coiled, torso turned, ready to bring it down. Left hand still empty at his side. AT THE APEX of the windup — sudden GLITCH: the burning red glow in his eye sockets ABRUPTLY EXTINGUISHES, snapping out clean as if a power source was severed, sockets going completely dark and lifeless, dead empty stone hollows. In the same instant his entire 20-meter body FREEZES solid — sword still held high in the windup position, right arm aloft, torso coiled, foot planted mid-step — locked stone-still, no breath, no micro-motion, mass gone inert. The freeze HOLDS for TWO FULL SECONDS — dead suspension, an industrial machine that lost signal mid-action. Dust from the previous impact continues drifting and settling around his motionless form. Oil lamps still flicker at column bases. Camera shake continues throughout — the only motion in frame is the trembling vérité tremor and drifting dust against his perfectly frozen mass.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — REIGNITION (extreme close-up on face).
Framing: Tight extreme close-up on the GIANT's FACE — heavy wrinkled stone brow, deep-set empty eye sockets, broad flat nose, downturned mouth fill the frame. Three-quarter angle, low looking up. Surface in macro detail: weathered granite with hairline cracks across the forehead and crown, horizontal wrinkles carved deep into the brow, mossy patina pooled in the deep furrow between the brows, fine dust on the cheekbones. Cold rim light rakes across the stone from above. The eye sockets are completely DARK at frame open — dead empty hollows, no glow, no light, just deep stone shadow.
Action: Held beat on the dark dead face — sockets completely lifeless, expression frozen in the scowl. Then — SHARP IGNITION: the burning red supernatural light SNAPS BACK ON inside the eye sockets, blazing crimson again — initial flash slightly OVERBRIGHT for a single frame before settling into the steady intense red glow. The reignition throws strong red rim-light across the brow ridge, the bridge of the nose, the wrinkle creases on the forehead, and a faint red bloom into the surrounding stone. Fine dust shakes loose from the brow as the connection re-establishes. The face reads as REAWAKENED — the same scowl, but now alive, hostile, locked-in. Held briefly on the burning red eyes.

CUT.

SHOT 5 — ROAR (medium close-up, low angle, three-quarter).
Framing: Medium close-up from low angle at ground level, looking UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE. Heavy telephoto compression stacks him against the rock ceiling behind. Frame CROPS at the upper chest — pauldrons, neck, and head dominate the frame, with the upper edge of the cuirass and the layered shoulder plates anchoring the bottom. Cold pale shaft of light from the ceiling fissure silhouettes his back contour and the curve of his crown; rim catches the three-quarter profile. Red eye-glow flares strong, casting crimson wash on the underside of his jaw, the inside of his open mouth, and the wrinkled brow above. Mythic, monumental, oppressive.
Action: The Giant tilts his huge cracked stone head BACK fully, mouth gaping wide open, and unleashes a deep guttural ROAR upward at the ceiling — fury and frustration. Stone face contorted in a death-mask howl, heavy wrinkled brow furrowed deep, deep-set red eyes blazing brighter with the exertion. Dust shakes loose from his shoulders, the seams of his pauldrons, and the cracks of his armor, drifting down through cold light. Inside the gaping mouth: pure black void with the red eye-glow spilling faintly onto the upper lip from above. Camera shake reaches peak intensity at the roar. Held for the full duration of the howl.

Style：8K IMAX（不译）。照片级真实 —— 无 3D 渲染、无游戏引擎、无游戏过场动画质感。
Cinematography：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
Lighting：仅自然光 —— 逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于背光侧，全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。

Camera：物理电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。

Acting：好莱坞式 —— 反应前的微停顿、精准的眼线、可见呼吸。石巨像以重量与意图被赋予生命；威胁感透过头部角度、剪影、岩石接住冷光时那份刻意静滞来传达。
Physics：尊重重力与惯性 —— 质量有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。无漂浮道具。
Composition：三分法 + 黄金比例。每个角色从第一帧起都在运动。
Continuity：每个镜头间角色、道具、环境完全一致。无身份漂移。
Technical：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
Audio：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（本场景全部镜头）：目击者式纪录片手持，业余写实纪录（amateur vérité）美学，8K 照片级真实渲染。每个镜头中操作员保持静止 —— 不走路、不推轨、不跟拍、不环移。仅允许旋转动作（俯仰、小幅摇镜）与镜头驱动变焦。全程最大强度暴力手持晃动 —— 全片最极端，在撞击与嚎叫时达峰。不完美的原生构图。

LOCATION —— HELL HALL（地下巨厅）：
山体内凿出的巨大石厅。大量华丽巨柱。石砖地面。油灯在柱基处发光。一道惨白的冷光轴从岩石天花板的一条裂隙竖直切下。全程尘粒与大气雾感。

石巨像（GIANT STONE STATUE）—— 20 米高的巨型雕琢石像武士。光头，头顶与额头横贯深刻的年代裂纹。厚重石眉，额头上雕着显眼的横纹，双眉之间一道深沟，一张永久的凶悍蹙眉。宽阔扁平鼻、粗厚下撇的嘴、方正厚重的下颌、可见的石头耳朵。深邃的眼窝透出强烈的亮红色超自然光（GLOW）—— 灼红的光芒从眼窝深处辐射而出，把强烈的红色轮廓光投到眉脊、鼻梁与上颧，周围石面投下一抹淡红晕。厚实沉重的肌肉体型。层叠石甲护肩 —— 每侧肩膀多块叠板。躯干罩石甲胸铠，胸口横纹甲线。腰部宽阔石带，刻着凸起的希腊回纹（Greek-key meander）装饰纹样。腰带之下 —— 一段垂落的及膝裙袍，厚重的竖褶绕腿盘旋。两前臂与手腕缠着像绷带绑扎的石裹。石凉鞋。右手只持一柄长石剑 —— 沉重的石刃、握柄缠着石绑。左手空空，垂着握成一只闭合的石拳。风化灰石，隙缝里积苔藓绿灰垢斑，全身遍布细密裂纹头尤甚。移动带着碾人的重量。

SCENE 51E —— 一击、冻结、复燃、咆哮（五个镜头，单条提示词）。

SHOT 1（镜头 1）—— 水平横扫（低角度 3/4 特写）。
Framing：低角度特写，摄影机安置在地面，向上看 3/4 角度的巨人。长焦压缩（Telephoto compression）。巨人略微偏左，在腰部（WAIST）被裁 —— 仅上身可见：层叠护肩、胸铠、缠扎的右手腕、布满裂痕的光头配厚重皱纹眉骨、下撇的石质嘴、灼着强烈红光的深邃眼窝。右手紧握长石剑，剑刃高举于身侧、举起处于预备状。左手空空、握成闭合的拳。画面右侧：相邻一根柱子的暗色垂直体量配粗犷浮雕。画面左侧：雾霭洞穴纵深。冷色轮廓光勾住他侧影的轮廓与高举剑刃的边缘；红眼的光芒晕上眉脊。
Action：缓慢蓄势（SLOW windup）—— 巨人把剑高举悬停一个保持拍，积累张力，红眼定定地望着、灼烧。然后爆发式快速释放（EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE）—— 剑刃在高速的水平弧线（HORIZONTAL ARC）中横贯他的身体，以凶狠速度在齐胸至齐髋的高度横向扫过空气。剑刃切开画面时带着厚重的运动模糊。剑刃以其猛然贯穿骨骼般的震荡冲击砸进一根柱子的下部（齐髋高度一击）—— 尘土向外爆开、碎片飞溅、画面被冲击波以最大晃动强度狠狠猛震。缓慢蓄势与快速释放之间的反差放大了一下这一击的冲击。

切。

SHOT 2（镜头 2）—— 柱石碎块坠落（柱身特写）。
Framing：对一根大厅柱子靠近地板的下段取紧特写 —— 风化雕石、精细浮雕纹样、隙缝内的苔藓垢斑。柱子竖直填满画面。冷色轮廓光接住表面纹理。背景：雾霭大厅纵深，失焦。
Action：柱子完整静止了一拍。然后突然的巨量冲击 —— 一柄看不见的剑从画面外横向砸进柱子，石面剧烈碎裂。一大块（LARGE CHUNK）石料断裂剥离，发丝样裂纹向外奔蹿。那块石料向前、向下翻转着穿过画面，以真实质量正确的重力坠落，缓慢旋转。摄影机略带随之下俯。石料砸进画面底部的石砖地面，带起雷鸣般的二次冲击 —— 石与石的碎裂、碎成数片、尘土猛烈扬起、碎屑在砖块间飞溅乱弹。画面在两次冲击处都剧烈猛震。之后尘土沉重悬挂。

切。

SHOT 3（镜头 3）—— 二次蓄势，断电冻结（低角度全景 3/4）。
Framing：低角度全景，摄影机安置在地面，向上看 3/4 角度的巨人。长焦压缩。完整的上身与举起的手臂在画面中 —— 在裙袍膝盖处裁掉，以完整读出蓄势剪影。背景：雾霭大厅纵深，隐约可见断裂柱桩。顶部裂隙投来的冷色轮廓光斜掠他背部的轮廓与护肩；从深陷眼窝烧起强烈的红眼之光，把绯红晕投到眉骨上。
Action：巨人向前重踏一步，以缓慢有条不紊的蓄势举起石剑 —— 沉重的巨躯在空中划弧，剑刃升到右肩上方高位进入蓄势顶点，身体蓄力蜷身、躯干扭转，准备向下砸落。左手仍空空垂在身侧。就在蓄势的顶点 —— 突然一顿故障（GLITCH）：他眼窝里灼烧的红芒骤然熄灭（ABRUPTLY EXTINGUISHES），像被切断电源一样干脆地啪地灭掉，眼窝变得全黑、空洞无声，成了死寂的空石眼窝。同一瞬间，他整个 20 米的身体僵冻成块（FREEZES solid）—— 剑仍高挂在蓄势位，右臂高举，躯干蓄力蜷身，脚落在半步中 —— 锁死得如同石头一样静止，无呼吸、无微动作、巨躯化作惰性。这冻结保持足足两秒（TWO FULL SECONDS）—— 死寂悬停，一台在动作中途失联的工业机器。上一次冲击扬起的尘土继续绕着他不动的身形飘移、沉降。柱基油灯仍闪烁。摄像晃动全程持续 —— 画面里唯一的动是那阵颤抖的写实震颤，以及对着他那块完美冻结的巨躯飘动的尘土。

切。

SHOT 4（镜头 4）—— 复燃（脸部极特写）。
Framing：对巨人的脸取紧极特写 —— 厚重皱纹的石眉、深陷空洞的眼窝、宽阔扁平鼻、下撇的嘴填满画面。3/4 角度、低位仰视。表面以微观细节呈现：风化花岗岩，额头与头顶有发丝样细裂纹、深雕进眉骨的横纹、双眉之间深沟里积聚的苔藓垢斑、颧上的细尘。冷色轮廓光从上方斜掠石面。画面开场时眼窝完全是黑的（completely DARK）—— 死寂的空洞、无光、无亮，只有深石影。
Action：在黑暗死寂的脸上一个保持拍 —— 眼窝完全无生命，表情冻在那道蹙眉里。然后 —— 锐利点火（SHARP IGNITION）：灼烧的红超自然光在眼窝内部嗡地重新亮起（SNAPS BACK ON），重新燃起绯红 —— 起初的一闪在稳定成那股稳定强烈的红焰前，先单个多帧地略微过亮（OVERBRIGHT）。这次复燃把强烈的红轮廓光抛过眉脊、鼻梁、额头上的皱痕，并在周围石面上投下一抹淡红晕。连接重新建立时，细尘从眉骨抖落。脸读起来是「苏醒」（REAWAKENED）—— 同一道蹙眉，但如今活着、敌对、锁定。短暂定在燃烧的红眼上。

切。

SHOT 5（镜头 5）—— 咆哮（中近景，低角度，3/4）。
Framing：从地面低角度取中近景，向上看 3/4 角度的巨人。厚重长焦压缩把他堆叠在身后的岩顶前。构图在上胸处裁切（Frame CROPS at the upper chest）—— 护肩、颈与头占主导画面，胸铠的上缘与层叠肩板锚定底部。顶部裂隙照来一道惨白冷光，在他背部的轮廓与头顶的弧线上勾出剪影；轮廓光接住 3/4 侧影。红眼之光强劲烧起，在下颌底面、张开嘴的内里以及上面那道皱纹眉骨上投下绯红晕。神话、恢弘、压抑。
Action：巨人把巨大开裂的头完全后仰，嘴大张，朝屋顶引出一声低沉喉音咆哮 —— 狂怒与挫败。石脸扭曲成死亡面具般的嚎叫，厚重皱纹的眉骨深皱，深邃的红眼随发力烧得更亮。尘土从双肩、护肩的接缝与铠甲裂缝里抖落，飘过冷光落下。张大的嘴里：纯粹黑色的虚空，上方红眼光芒淡淡漫到上唇。嚎叫时摄像晃动达到峰值强度。整个嚎叫期间保持。
