He WHIRLS his torso hard side-to-side, shoulders rolling, body jerking and spinning in violent torque, head whipping around trying to spot the climber on his back. His FREE STONE HAND reaches behind, grasping toward his own back, fingers clawing at where Roko clings. Dust and stone fragments EXPLODING from the giant's joints from the violent motion.
