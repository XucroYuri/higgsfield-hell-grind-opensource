## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): VIOLENT handheld throughout — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Even in close-ups the frame floats and trembles.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame at column bases. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. To the right of the central column the wall transitions into raw rugged cave rock.

CHARACTERS:
JAX — Black man, mid-20s, bleached yellow buzz-cut hair, clear wraparound safety glasses, fresh red graze on his forehead. Black tactical hooded jacket with grey paneling, olive cargo pants, fingerless gloves, brown belt with yellow zipper accents.
ROKO — man, mid-20s, dark messy shoulder-length hair, faint mustache, red abraded marks on the bridge of his nose. Full translucent blood-red crystalline armor with jagged crystal spikes on shoulders and forearms. Crystal helmet RETRACTED — face fully exposed.
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips, thin red scar through left eyebrow down across the cheek. Grey cropped hooded utility jacket over black turtleneck, black cargo pants, brown belt, olive thigh pouch holster on right leg. NO hovering board in this scene.

COLUMN STAGING (locked):
RAIN — pressed to the LEFT edge of the central column, partly tucked behind Jax in depth, leaning her head past the column edge to peek out toward the hall.
JAX — back flat to the CENTER of the column.
ROKO — pressed to the RIGHT side of the column, body half hidden behind the column's right edge, only one shoulder, head and crystalline armor pieces visible past the edge.

SCENE 51F — PLAN REVEAL (four shots, single prompt, in this exact order).

SHOT 1 — ESTABLISHING WIDE ON THE TRIO (anchor frame, planted camera, VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Static wide framing facing the central column from the front, eye-level, capturing the full trio against the column. Background frame-LEFT: long perspective of the hall extending into haze, oil lamps glowing along the floor receding into distance, drifting dust. Frame-RIGHT: rugged cave-rock wall transitioning from the column. An oil lamp glows on the stone floor in the foreground bottom-center.
Trio positions: RAIN on frame-LEFT in the depth plane, partly tucked behind Jax, silver-platinum bob, grey hooded jacket, leaning her head past the column's left edge — eyes locked off-frame to the LEFT, peeking out into the hall, scanning intently. JAX in center, back flat to the column, head tilted up scanning the ceiling, mouth slightly open in a held breath, sweat at his temple, fresh graze on forehead. ROKO on frame-RIGHT, body mostly hidden behind the column's right edge, only one shoulder, head and red crystalline armor pieces visible past the edge, head turned watching out into the cave to his right.
All three frozen in tense alertness, breath visible, chest rising. Cold rim light from above carves their silhouettes. Dust drifts in the cold shaft of light. Frame trembles violently with handheld shake throughout — pure jitter, no movement of camera through space.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — RAIN TURNS BACK, OTS WITH JAX (medium-close, handheld trembles).
Framing: Rain on frame-LEFT in three-quarter, in sharp focus, having just pulled back from her peek. Behind her in soft-focus foreground frame-RIGHT: JAX's shoulder and the side of his face out of focus, anchoring the OTS angle. Column stone surface in background between and behind them. Cold rim light defines Rain's profile.
Action: Rain whips her head back from the column edge to look at Jax (and Roko beyond him), hair settling against her scarred cheek. Micro-pause. Then low and urgent, eyes locked on his: "He moves on chant." Visible breath. Frame trembles.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — JAX REACTION CU WITH EMBEDDED TREMOR (50mm CU on Jax, handheld trembles, hard shake on tremor).
Framing: Tight close-up on JAX, back flat to the center of the column. Sweat at his temple, fresh graze on forehead, safety glasses catching faint cold rim from frame-left. Soft-focus column texture behind.
Action: Micro-pause as he processes the words — heard them clearly but the LOGIC doesn't track. His brow furrows under the safety glasses, head tilts a fraction toward Rain off-frame, expression of "what are you even talking about right now" reading on his face. He doesn't shout — he speaks the line low, baffled, slightly frustrated, as if asking her to make sense in the middle of a fight: "What?" The emphasis is on disbelief at her reasoning, not at her volume — he heard her, he just doesn't understand WHY she's saying it or what it means tactically. Eyes searching her face for follow-up. Immediately AFTER his line, an OFF-FRAME TREMOR HITS — a sudden hard low rumble shudders the hall, dust cascades down the column behind him into frame, fine debris rattles past, the cold light flickers as dust thickens. Camera shudders violently with the tremor. Crucially, JAX DOES NOT REACT to the tremor — he keeps his focus dead on Rain off-frame, expression locked on the question, treating the rumble as background noise they have all become numbed to. Visible breath, chest heaves.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — RAIN EXPLAINS THE PLAN (50mm CU on Rain, handheld trembles).
Framing: Tight close-up on RAIN at the column's LEFT edge, three-quarter front toward camera. Cold backlight wraps the column edge behind her. Hair past her scarred eye. Background: dark stone column and hazy depth. Faint dust still drifting in the air from the recent tremor.
Action: Rain holds steady eye-line off-frame on her teammates, intent and certain — the look of someone who has just figured something out. She speaks low and fast, almost a whisper, urgent and clear, laying out the plan: "He moves to the chant. And when something interrupts that chant — he stops listening." Beat. A faint flicker in her eyes — the second part of the realization landing. "And opens his mouth." She holds the look on Jax, waiting for it to register. Catch-light in wet eyes, breath visible, micro-pauses between phrases. She does not react to faint tremor aftershocks either — focused entirely on conveying the plan. Frame trembles.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Style（技术底座）：8K IMAX。写实摄影风格（Photorealistic）——不得有 3D 渲染、不得有游戏引擎、不得有游戏过场动画的美感。
Cinematography（摄影）：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
Lighting（光线）：仅自然光 — 逆光背光（contre-jour）、摄影机位于阴影侧、全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
Color（色彩）：60:30:10（主色/次色/点缀）。
Camera（摄影机）：物理电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
Skin（皮肤）：毛孔级写实 — 汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称的痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光线匹配的毛孔阴影。
Acting（表演）：好莱坞 — 反应前有微小停顿、精确的视线、湿润灵动、带眼神光（catch-lights）的双眼、可见的呼吸与胸口起伏。
Physics（物理）：尊重重力与惯性 — 质量有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有漂浮道具。
Composition（构图）：三分法 + 黄金比例。每个入画的人从第一帧起即在移动。
Continuity（连贯性）：每一切之间角色、道具、环境完全一致。无身份漂移（no identity drift）。
Technical（技术）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。不得抖动。
Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（本场景摄影机装置）：全程剧烈手持（VIOLENT handheld）— 沉重猛烈的抖动、呼吸式运镜、反应式修正。每个镜头中操作员保持静止 — 不步行、不推轨、不跟拍、不做弧线运动。即使在特写中画面也在漂浮震颤。

LOCATION（地点）— HELL HALL（地狱大厅）：
人工开凿于山体内的广阔石厅。两侧林立巨型的雕花石柱，渐退入雾。石板地。柱基小油灯燃着低矮的橙色火苗。一道苍白冷光自远处高空的岩顶裂隙直落而下。尘埃微粒与大气雾感（atmospheric haze）遍布全程。中央巨柱右侧，墙面过渡为裸露的粗犷洞岩。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
JAX（杰克）— 黑人男性，二十过半，漂白金寸头、透明的环绕式护目镜、前额一道新鲜红色擦伤。黑色战术连帽夹克配灰色嵌板、橄榄绿工装裤、露指手套、带黄色拉链装饰的棕色腰带。
ROKO（罗科）— 男性，二十过半，深色凌乱齐肩发、淡淡胡须、鼻梁红色擦痕。全身半透明血红水晶铠甲，肩与前臂带锯齿晶刺。水晶头盔 RETRACTED（回收）— 面部完全露出。
RAIN（莱恩）— 年轻女性，二十出头，银铂色波波头带乌黑发尖、左眉向下贯穿面颊的一道细红疤。灰褐色短款连帽实用外套内搭黑色高领衫、黑色工装裤、棕色腰带、右腿橄榄绿大腿工具包枪套。本场景无悬浮滑板。

COLUMN STAGING（立柱站位，锁定）：
RAIN（莱恩）— 贴靠中央巨柱左缘，在景深中半藏于 JAX 身后，将头侧过柱缘向大厅方向窥探。
JAX（杰克）— 背脊平贴于巨柱 CENTER（正中）。
ROKO（罗科）— 贴靠巨柱右侧，半个身体藏在柱的右缘之后，仅一侧肩、头与水晶铠甲部件探出柱缘。

SCENE 51F — PLAN REVEAL（计划揭示）（四个镜头，单一提示词，按此顺序严格排列）。

镜头 1 — 三人定场广角（锚定帧、固定摄影机、剧烈手持）。
取景：正对中央巨柱的静态广角取景、视平线高度、框住倚柱的全三人组。画面左背景：大厅纵深渐入雾中、地面油灯沿程渐远、飘浮的尘埃。画面右侧：自巨柱过渡出的粗犷洞岩壁。前景底部中央，石地上一盏油灯发光。
三人站位：RAIN（莱恩）居画面左侧的景深面、半藏于 JAX 之后、银铂色波波头、灰褐色连帽夹克、将头侧过巨柱左缘 — 双眼锁定框外 LEFT（左侧）、向大厅窥探、仔细扫视。JAX（杰克）居中、背贴巨柱、抬头扫视天花板、嘴微张屏息、鬓角有汗、前额新鲜擦伤。ROKO（罗科）居画面右侧、身体大半藏在巨柱右缘后、仅一侧肩、头与红色水晶铠甲部件探出柱缘、回头观望其右侧洞穴。
三人僵立、紧张警觉、可见的呼吸、胸口起伏。上方寒冷轮廓光（rim light）勾勒他们的剪影。尘埃在冷光柱中飘荡。全程画面随手持晃动剧烈震颤 — 纯粹抖动、摄影机不在空间中移动。

切。

镜头 2 — RAIN（莱恩）转头回看、与 JAX 过肩（中近景、手持震颤）。
取景：RAIN（莱恩）居画面左侧 3/4 角度、对焦锐利、刚自窥探抽身。她身后失焦的前景画面右侧：JAX（杰克）的肩与他侧面部失焦，锚定过肩（OTS）角度。背景中他们之间与之后是巨柱石面。寒冷轮廓光（rim light）勾勒 RAIN（莱恩）的侧影。
动作：RAIN（莱恩）自柱缘甩回头看向 JAX（以及他身后的 ROKO），发丝落向带疤的脸颊。微小停顿。随后低声急迫、双眼锁住他的双眼：「He moves on chant.（他一听到吟唱就动。）」可见的呼吸。画面震颤。

切。

镜头 3 — JAX（杰克）反应特写，内嵌震动（JAX 上 50mm 特写、手持震颤、震动时硬抖）。
取景：JAX（杰克）紧特写、背贴巨柱正中。鬓角有汗、前额新鲜擦伤、护目镜反着画面左方微弱冷轮廓光。身后是失焦的巨柱质感。
动作：微小停顿间他消化这句话 — 他听清了，但 LOGIC（逻辑）讲不通。护目镜下眉头皱起，头向框外 RAIN（莱恩）偏了一分，脸上读出一个「你现在到底在说什么」的表情。他没有喊 — 他低声说出这句台词、困惑、略带恼火，仿佛在激战中途请她把话说通：「What?（什么？）」强调落在对她推理的不信而非音量 — 他听到了她，只是不明白她为何这么说、或在战术上意味着什么。双眼在她脸上搜寻下文。就在他这句台词结束之后，一个 OFF-FRAME（框外）震动来袭 — 突然一阵低沉硬轰隆震颤大厅，他身后巨柱上尘埃落入画面、细碎碎片嘎啦掠过、尘埃变浓时冷光闪烁。摄影机随震动剧烈震颤。关键的是，JAX（杰克）对这震动毫无反应 — 他把注意力死死钉在框外 RAIN（莱恩）身上，表情锁定在那个疑问上，将这轰隆当作大家都已麻木的背景噪音。可见的呼吸、胸口起伏。

切。

镜头 4 — RAIN（莱恩）说明计划（RAIN 上 50mm 特写、手持震颤）。
取景：巨柱 LEFT 边缘的 RAIN（莱恩）紧特写，3/4 朝向摄影机前方。寒冷背光包裹她身后的巨柱边缘。发丝垂过带疤的眼。背景：深色石柱与雾的纵深。近期地震后的细微尘埃仍在空气中飘荡。
动作：RAIN（莱恩）沉稳地保持框外视线看向队友，专注而笃定 — 一副刚刚想通了什么的人的神情。她低声快速说出，近乎耳语、急迫而清晰，铺陈计划：「He moves to the chant. And when something interrupts that chant — he stops listening.（他随吟唱移动。而当有什么打断那吟唱 — 他就会停止聆听。）」顿一拍。她眼中闪过一丝微光 — 第二重醒悟落地。「And opens his mouth.（并且张开他的嘴。）」她保持看往 JAX 的目光，等他领会。湿润眼中有眼神光（catch-light）、可见的呼吸、句子之间微小停顿。她也对微弱震动余波毫无反应 — 完全专注在传达计划上。画面震颤。
