## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (applies to every shot in this scene): Strong handheld operator — aggressive micro-jitter, breathing rig, slight reframing drift, reactive whip-corrections to action. Action-urgency feel. Never static, never locked-off. Even in close-ups the frame floats and trembles.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame glow at the bases of columns. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout. Dominant tones: cold blue-gray stone (60), deep shadow black (30), warm amber lamp flame (10).

CHARACTERS:
JAX — Black man, mid-20s, bleached yellow buzz-cut hair, clear wraparound safety glasses, fresh red graze on his forehead. Wears a black tactical hooded jacket with grey paneling, olive cargo pants, black fingerless gloves, worn combat boots.
ROKO — man, mid-20s, dark messy shoulder-length hair, faint mustache, red abraded marks across the bridge of his nose. Wears a full translucent blood-red crystalline armor suit with jagged crystal spikes erupting from shoulders and forearms. In this scene his crystal helmet is RETRACTED — face fully exposed.
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips, thin red scar running through left eyebrow down across the cheek. Wears a grey cropped hooded utility jacket over a black turtleneck top, black cargo pants, brown leather belt, olive thigh pouch holster strapped to right leg.
PROPS: RAIN's flat matte-dark hovering board, roughly snowboard-sized.

SCENE 51C — TAKE COVER AND EXCHANGE.

BEAT 1 (medium-wide, handheld converging): The three converge on a single massive column from THREE DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS at once. JAX sprints in from frame-LEFT, slams his back flat against the column, chest heaving. ROKO crashes in from frame-RIGHT, his crystal armor scraping the stone with a hard contact, shoulder-first into the column — his crystal helmet plates fracture and retract in segments to reveal his face, panting through open mouth. RAIN drops in low from the BACKGROUND on her hovering board; mid-glide she snatches the board out from under her feet with her right hand, tucks it tight under her right armpit, and hits the column with her shoulder beside the others, board now gripped against her ribs. All three pressed to the same column, breathing hard. Cold rim light from above carves silhouettes. Dust kicked up catches the shaft of light. Camera jitters hard.

BEAT 2 (cut, 50mm CU on JAX, handheld floats): Back flat to the column. Sweat at his temple, jaw set hard. Eyes flick once to the others off-frame, micro-pause, then low and frustrated he speaks: "Whatever it is — charges have no effect on him." Visible breath. Chest rise. Soft cold rim from frame-left wraps the edge of his face. Frame breathes and trembles.

BEAT 3 (cut, 50mm CU on ROKO, handheld floats): Helmet retracted. He exhales sharp through teeth, shakes his head once, eyes find Jax off-frame: "Same with me. We need something else." Frustrated, breath visible. Cold rim catches the crystal edges of his pauldron, scattering faint red refractions across his jaw. Frame trembles.

BEAT 4 (cut, INSERT — environmental tremor, handheld violently shakes): Wide-medium of the hall mid-distance from the trio. A massive impact hits somewhere off-frame — a sudden hard rumble. Dust cascades from the tops of columns. Oil-lamp flames jolt and flicker, some snuffing out. Small stone pebbles bounce and skitter on the tile floor. The chain of an unseen mechanism rattles in the haze. The shaft of light flickers as dust thickens through it. Camera shudders violently. No people in this insert — pure environmental reaction shot.

BEAT 5 (cut, 50mm CU on RAIN peeking, handheld trembles): RAIN pressed to the column, board still tucked under her right armpit. Her head snaps slightly toward the source of the tremor — micro-pause, hair falls past her scarred eye. She inhales, slowly leans her face past the column's edge, eyes narrow as they scan the hall beyond. Catch-light in wet living eyes. Subtle chest rise. Profile rotates to three-quarter as she turns. Cold backlight bleeds around the column edge across her cheek. Frame trembles in sync with her breath.

## 中文对照 (zh)

风格（Style）：8K IMAX。照片级写实——非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎、非游戏过场动画美学。
摄影（Cinematography）：Emmanuel Lubezki × Roger Deakins。
布光（Lighting）：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗口。
色彩（Color）：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀。
摄影机（Camera）：实体电影镜头。180 度快门运动模糊。
皮肤（Skin）：毛孔级真实——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称黑痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场光匹配的毛孔阴影。
表演（Acting）：好莱坞式——反应前有微停顿、精确视线、带有眼神光（catch-lights）的湿润鲜活双眼、可见的呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理（Physics）：尊重重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。不得有无重感道具。
构图（Composition）：三分法 + 黄金比例。从第一帧起每个人都在运动。
连续性（Continuity）：角色、道具、环境在每一个切点上完全一致。不得有身份漂移。
技术（Technical）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频（Audio）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（CAMERA RIG，本镜头）：全程剧烈的手持操作者。摄影机固定于地面高度，侧对巨像。摄影机并不过空间位移——不得推轨、不得跟拍、不得弧线摇摄。摄影机在本镜头内只做一段连续向上的摇摄（TILT），从巨像的身体旋转式上攀至天花板。剧烈的手持抖动叠加上摇摄之上。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
山体内部开凿出的广袤石厅。巨大精美的雕花石柱分列两侧，向雾中延展没入。石板地面。柱基处有低矮橙色火焰的小油灯发光。一道冰冷苍白的自然光柱从高高在上的岩顶裂隙斜切而下。全程微粒尘埃与大气雾感。一侧岩壁高处：凿入石中的一个小型雕凿壁龛，深度足以坐下一人。上方的天花板裂隙宽阔到足以吊挂一条沉重铁链。

角色 / 主体（CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS）：
巨石像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨形雕石武士。光头，浓重凶悍的眉骨，深陷的愤怒双目，厚实魁梧的体格。束腰外衣之上披着石雕的铠甲胸甲，外罩垂及膝下的裙式长袍，一条带浮雕纹饰的宽石腰带，缠绕的手腕。双手各持一把长石剑。巨足上蹬着石制凉鞋。风化的灰色石面，雕凿缝隙间有青灰色的苔藓绿锈斑，形体表面布满细纹裂痕。有生命——以碾压般的重量活动。
僧人（MONK）——年迈的亚洲男性，接近古稀、年近七十，两鬓花白的短发，满是皱纹的风霜面孔，双眼在冥想入定中紧闭或半闭。身穿一袭深酒红色僧袍，腰系橙红腰带，长袖。盘腿坐在岩壁内一个雕凿的小壁龛中。嘴唇不停翕动，低声念诵真言。在一片混乱中沉静而安详。
钟（BELL）——一口巨大的古青铜钟，深色带绿锈的青铜表面，钟身四周环饰凸起浮雕的纹饰与铭文带，由一条深深锚固进上方岩顶裂隙岩石中的沉重铁链吊挂着。

场景 51E——揭示（THE REVEAL）。

拍 1（BEAT 1，单个连续镜头，侧角度中全景对准巨像，固定摄影机向上摇摄，极致剧烈的手持抖动）：

子拍 A——轰砸（SMASH）：镜头以中全景开场，侧角度侧面轮廓对准高耸的巨石像，他填满画面中央。两根巨大的雕花石柱立于画框左右边缘，将他框住。他双刀向外挥出一道宽阔的劈斩弧线——两把刀刃同时砸穿画面边缘的石柱。巨石向外爆裂成无数碎块，尘云翻涌成浓雾，碎屑在画面地面上四散弹跳。整幅画面随冲击震荡，下方油灯猛晃闪烁，上方寒冷的光柱因尘土而变得浓稠。

子拍 B——咆哮（ROAR）：巨像猛然把头往后一甩，石嘴大张，朝天花板发出一声低沉的喉音咆哮。石脸因暴怒而扭曲，深陷的双眼圆睁。尘土自他肩头抖落。

子拍 C——上摇到僧人（TILT TO MONK）：摄影机开始一段连续的向上摇摄，旋转式摇过咆哮的巨像头部，沿他身后粗糙垂直的岩壁向上攀爬。摇摄揭示出高处凿入墙中的一个小型雕石壁龛。壁龛内僧人盘腿端坐，身披深酒红僧袍，双目紧闭，双唇无声地念动真言，双掌上翻置于膝头。暴力震晃之中一方沉静的宁静之所。柔和的冷光拂过他脸颊边缘。摄影机在他身上停驻一拍。

子拍 D——上摇到钟（TILT TO BELL）：摇摄继续向上越过壁龛，沿岩壁进一步攀升，朝向天花板。摇摄揭示出那口巨大的古青铜钟，由沉重的铁链吊挂在头顶宽阔的岩缝中，因巨像的冲击震动而微微晃动，背后有外部射入的冰冷苍白天光映出剪影，尘埃在光柱中飘荡。铁链吱嘎作响。摄影机停驻在钟上。

在整个上摇的全过程：手持抖动始终沉重而剧烈，绝不平滑。摇摄是一段连续的旋转攀爬，不得有剪切，不得在空间中位移。
