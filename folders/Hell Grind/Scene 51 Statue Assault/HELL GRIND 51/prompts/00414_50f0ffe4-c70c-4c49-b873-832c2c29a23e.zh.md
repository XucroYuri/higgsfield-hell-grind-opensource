Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone-carved giant: weathered granite-grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine hairline cracks, fine dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, visible breath. The stone giant is animated with weight and intent; menace reads through head angle, silhouette, deliberate stillness of stone catching cold light.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (all shots): Witness-style documentary handheld, amateur vérité aesthetic, 8K photorealistic rendering. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Only rotational moves and lens-driven zoom allowed. MAXIMUM violent handheld shake throughout — peaking on impacts and on the bell strike. Imperfect raw framing. NO visible sound waves, NO heat-haze distortion, NO visible acoustic ripples, NO visible cracks racing through stone — sound and impulse remain invisible; only their physical effects on solid matter (dust, debris, vibration) are shown.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns lining both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set carved eyes, expressionless stone face. Stone armored cuirass over tunic, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in right hand, left hand empty. Weathered grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks. Moves with crushing weight.

BELL — large imposing antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern, hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored deep into the rock of the ceiling fissure — positioned high, practically against the rock ceiling above the hall.

SCENE 51E — STRIKE AND BELL TOLL (four shots, single prompt, in this exact order).

SHOT 1 — HORIZONTAL SWING (low-angle three-quarter close-up).
Framing: Low-angle close-up, camera planted at ground level looking UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE. Telephoto compression. Giant positioned slightly LEFT of center, cropped at the WAIST — only upper body visible: stone cuirass, wrapped right shoulder pad, bald head with heavy menacing brow, expressionless mouth. Right hand grips the long stone sword, blade held HIGH and to the side, raised in preparation. Frame-RIGHT: dark vertical mass of an adjacent column with rugged relief. Frame-LEFT: hazy cave depth. Cold rim light catches the contour of his profile and the edge of the raised blade.
Action: SLOW windup — the giant holds the sword raised high for a held beat, building tension. Then EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE — the blade WHIPS in a fast HORIZONTAL ARC across his body, sweeping sideways through the air at chest-to-hip level with brutal speed. Heavy motion blur on the blade as it cuts through frame. The blade SMASHES into the lower portion of a column (hip-level strike) with sudden bone-deep concussive impact — dust explodes outward, fragments spray, frame jolts violently with the shockwave at maximum shake intensity. The contrast between slow buildup and fast release amplifies the impact.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — COLUMN CHUNK FALL (close-up on column).
Framing: Tight close-up on the LOWER section of a hall column near floor level — weathered carved stone, fine relief patterns, mossy patina in crevices. Column fills frame vertically. Cold rim light catches the surface texture. Background: hazy hall depth, out-of-focus.
Action: The column stands intact for a held beat. Then SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT — an unseen blade smashes horizontally into the column from off-frame, stone surface fractures violently. A LARGE CHUNK of stone breaks off, hairline cracks racing outward. The chunk TUMBLES forward and downward through frame, falling with real mass-correct gravity, rotating slowly. Camera tilts down with it slightly. The chunk SLAMS into the stone-tiled floor at the bottom of frame with a thunderous secondary impact — stone-on-stone shatter, breaking into pieces, dust kicks up violently, debris skitters across tiles. Frame jolts violently on both impacts. Dust hangs heavy after.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — INVISIBLE PULSE TRAVELS UP WALL AND ACROSS CEILING (high-angle wide on upper wall and rock ceiling).
Framing: Camera planted HIGH up in the hall, well above the giant's head, looking outward at the UPPER SECTION OF THE CAVE WALL and across the ROCK CEILING. Wide angle. The vertical wall fills the lower-left of frame, rising up to meet the rough rock ceiling that arches overhead and fills the upper portion of frame — wall-and-ceiling junction running diagonally across the composition. Ancient pitted rock surface in detail, mossy patina in deep crevices, cold pale daylight from the distant ceiling fissure raking across the texture from upper-frame, atmospheric haze and slow-drifting dust catching the cold light. Hazy depth on frame-RIGHT looking down into the hall void.
Action: A held beat on the still rock face. Then — an INVISIBLE IMPULSE travels upward along the wall, completely unseen by the eye, registered ONLY through its physical effects in sequence as the pressure wave moves: a band of fine dust puffs out from the wall surface near the lower-frame, drifting outward into the air; a fraction of a second later, higher up the wall, another zone of fine dust releases from the rock; the propagation continues upward — micro-tremors visibly rippling across each successive section of the rock face, mossy crumbs and tiny pebble fragments shaking loose from crevices and beginning to fall, the carved relief surface trembling subtly. The impulse reaches the wall-and-ceiling junction and continues across the ROCK CEILING overhead — fine dust cascading down from the underside of the rock in delicate veils, drifting through the cold light, tiny fragments dropping past the camera. The whole rock body responds to invisible structural pressure traveling through it. NO visible crack, NO visible line, NO visible wave — only the dust release, the trembling, the falling debris reading the pressure as it passes. Frame trembles violently with handheld shake.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — FALLING DEBRIS STRIKES THE BELL (low-angle wide, distant view).
Framing: LOW ANGLE WIDE shot, camera planted on the hall floor far below the bell, looking steeply UP at it from a long distance away. The BELL is suspended high in the upper rock fissure, framed centrally but readable as a SMALL DISTANT OBJECT against the vast vertical reach of rock above — heavy iron chain rising from the crown of the bell up into the rock fissure, the patinated greenish-bronze body silhouetted against the cold pale daylight bleeding down from the fissure. The towering rugged rock walls of the fissure rise on both sides of the bell, receding upward. Atmospheric haze throughout, slow-drifting dust catching the cold light, the immense vertical scale emphasizing how high above the floor the bell hangs. Telephoto compression flattens the depth. Frame-edges show the upper portions of distant hall columns receding into haze.
Action: A held beat on the still distant bell — chain barely moving, dust drifting through cold light around it. Then — fine debris and SMALL, ALMOST IMPERCEPTIBLE PEBBLES rain down from the rock above, tiny fragments barely visible against the haze, scattering past the bell. A few of these tiny pebbles strike the bronze shoulder of the bell with sharp small TICKS — the bell takes the small impacts, the heavy bronze body beginning to LURCH on its chain from the cumulative force, the iron links snapping taut with a metallic groan barely audible from this distance. More tiny pebbles bounce off the bronze surface, almost lost in the haze. Then the bell BOOMS with a DEEP RESONANT TOLL — a massive metallic peal bursting outward across the vast space of the hall, sustaining and rolling on, the bell oscillating wildly on its chain, the heavy bronze body swinging through cold pale light high above. The deep ringing toll continues to sustain through the shot, the sound filling the entire stone hall, audible as a vast bronze peal rolling outward through the architecture. The bell sways visibly even at this distance — the only large motion in the upper portion of frame, the rest of the rock geometry holding still, dust drifting around the swinging bronze body. Frame trembles violently with handheld shake. Cold light catches the swinging bronze form. NO visible sound waves — only the bell's physical sway, the cumulative tiny impacts, and the sustained resonant ring as audio.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Style：8K IMAX（不译）。照片级真实 —— 无 3D 渲染、无游戏引擎、无游戏过场动画质感。
Cinematography：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
Lighting：仅自然光 —— 逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于背光侧，全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
Color：60:30:10（主色/次色/点缀）。
Camera：物理电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
Skin：毛孔级真实感 —— 汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场灯光匹配的毛孔阴影。石雕巨像（stone-carved giant）：风化花岗岩灰色石质，隙缝内沉积苔藓绿灰垢斑，细发丝样裂纹，细尘堆积。
Acting：好莱坞式 —— 反应前的微停顿、精准的眼线、可见呼吸。石巨像以重量与意图被赋予生命；威胁感透过头部角度、剪影、岩石接住冷光时那份刻意静滞来传达。
Physics：尊重重力与惯性 —— 质量有真实重量、正确的接触阴影。无漂浮道具。
Composition：三分法 + 黄金比例。
Continuity：每个镜头间角色、道具、环境完全一致。无身份漂移。
Technical：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
Audio：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（全部镜头）：目击者式纪录片手持，业余写实纪录（amateur vérité）美学，8K 照片级真实渲染。每个镜头中操作员保持静止 —— 不走路、不推轨、不跟拍、不环移。仅允许旋转动作与镜头驱动变焦。全程最大强度暴力手持晃动（MAXIMUM violent handheld shake）—— 在撞击与敲钟时达峰。不完美的原生构图。无可见声波、无热霾畸变、无可见声学涟漪、无可见裂纹穿越石面 —— 声音与脉冲保持不可见；只表现它们对固体物质的物理效应（尘土、碎片、颤动）。

LOCATION —— HELL HALL（地下巨厅）：
山体内凿出的巨大石厅。两侧林立着大量华丽巨柱，向雾中纵深退去。石砖地面。油灯在柱基处发光。一道惨白的冷光轴从岩石天花板的一条裂隙竖直切下。全程尘粒与大气雾感。

石巨像（GIANT STONE STATUE）—— 20 米高的巨型雕琢石像武士。光头、沉重压迫的眉骨、深邃雕琢的眼窝、面无表情的石脸。束腰短衣外罩石甲胸铠、宽阔石腰带、缠扎的手腕。右手握一柄长石剑，左手空空。风化灰石，苔藓垢斑，细密裂纹。动作带着碾人的重量。

铜钟（BELL）—— 巨大威严的古董铜钟，暗锈蚀的绿铜色表面，饰有华丽凸起的经文与纹样浮雕环带，由一条深深锚固进顶部裂隙岩体的沉重铁链悬挂 —— 位置很高，几乎贴着大厅上方的岩顶。

SCENE 51E —— 一击与钟鸣（四个镜头，单条提示词，严格按此顺序）。

SHOT 1（镜头 1）—— 水平横扫（低角度 3/4 特写）。
Framing：低角度特写，摄影机安置在地面，向上看 3/4 角度的巨人（THREE-QUARTER ANGLE）。长焦压缩（Telephoto compression）。巨人略微偏左，在腰部（WAIST）被裁 —— 仅上身可见：石甲胸铠、缠扎的右肩垫、光头配沉重威吓的眉骨、无表情的嘴。右手紧握长石剑，剑刃高举于身侧，举起处于预备状。画面右侧：相邻一根柱子的暗色垂直体量配粗犷浮雕。画面左侧：雾霭洞穴纵深。冷色轮廓光勾住他侧影的轮廓与高举剑刃的边缘。
Action：缓慢蓄势（SLOW windup）—— 巨人把剑高举悬停一个静止拍，积累张力。然后爆发式快速释放（EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE）—— 剑刃在高速的水平弧线（HORIZONTAL ARC）中横贯他的身体，以凶狠速度在齐胸至齐髋的高度横向扫过空气。剑刃切开画面时带着厚重的运动模糊。剑刃以其猛然贯穿骨骼般的震荡冲击（instant）砸进一根柱子的下部（齐髋高度一击）—— 尘土向外爆开、碎片飞溅、画面被冲击波以最大晃动强度狠狠猛震。缓慢蓄势与快速释放之间的反差放大了一下这一击的冲击。

切。

SHOT 2（镜头 2）—— 柱石碎块坠落（柱身特写）。
Framing：对一根大厅柱子靠近地板的下段取紧特写 —— 风化雕石、精细浮雕纹样、隙缝内的苔藓垢斑。柱子竖直填满画面。冷色轮廓光接住表面纹理。背景：雾霭大厅纵深，失焦。
Action：柱子完整静止了一拍。然后突然的巨量冲击 —— 一柄看不见的剑从画面外横向砸进柱子，石面剧烈碎裂。一大块（LARGE CHUNK）石料断裂剥离，发丝样裂纹向外奔蹿。那块石料向前、向下翻转着穿过画面，以真实质量正确的重力坠落，缓慢旋转。摄影机略带随之下俯。石料砸进画面底部的石砖地面，带起雷鸣般的二次冲击 —— 石与石的碎裂、碎成数片、尘土猛烈扬起、碎屑在砖块间飞溅乱弹。画面在两次冲击处都剧烈猛震。之后尘土沉重悬挂。

切。

SHOT 3（镜头 3）—— 无形脉冲沿墙而上、横穿岩顶（对上部墙面与岩顶的高位全景）。
Framing：摄影机高位安置在大厅里，远高于巨人的头顶，朝洞穴岩壁的上段（UPPER SECTION OF THE CAVE WALL）并横越岩顶（ROCK CEILING）望出。广角。竖直墙面填满画面左下侧，向上迎上头顶拱起的粗岩顶、填满画面上部 —— 墙与顶的接缝在对角线穿构图。古老麻坑的岩面细节、深隙缝内苔藓垢斑、远处顶部裂隙照来的惨白冷日从画面上方斜掠其纹理、大气雾感与缓缓飘移的尘土接住冷光。画面右侧雾霭纵深的空洞。
Action：静止岩面上一个保持拍。然后 —— 一道无形脉冲（INVISIBLE IMPULSE）沿墙向上传播，肉眼完全看不见，只能以其物理效应随压力波的移动逐一被辨认：一股细尘带在画面下部靠墙处从壁面喷出，向外飘进空气；一瞬间后，再往上，另一片细尘从岩上脱落；传播继续向上 —— 微颤肉眼可见地依次波及岩面每一段，苔藓碎屑与细小的卵石碎片从隙缝中抖落、开始坠落，雕饰浮雕表面细细颤动。脉冲抵达墙与顶的接缝，继续横越头顶的岩顶 —— 细尘从岩石下缘以轻纱般的幕帘曳下，飘过冷光，细小碎片掠过摄影机坠落。整块岩体回应着贯穿其中的无形结构压力。无可见裂纹、无可见线条、无可见波 —— 只有尘土脱落、那阵颤动、坠落的碎屑在压力掠过时被读出。画面以手持晃动剧烈颤抖。

切。

SHOT 4（镜头 4）—— 坠落碎屑击中铜钟（低角度全景，远景）。
Framing：低角度全景（LOW ANGLE WIDE），摄影机安置在大厅地面、远在钟下，从很远的距离陡峭向上看它。铜钟高悬于上方岩石裂隙，居中构图但可读为一块小小的、遥远的物体，衬着上方广袤恢弘的岩势 —— 沉重的铁链从钟冠拔起升入岩缝，锈蚀的绿铜色钟体在裂隙渗漏下的惨白冷日中描成剪影。裂隙两侧高耸崎岖的岩墙在钟两侧升起、向上退去。全程大气雾感，缓缓飘移的尘土接住冷光，无比宏大的垂直尺度突显钟悬挂在距地面多高的地方。长焦压缩压平了纵深。画面边缘可见远处大厅柱子在上方隐入雾中。
Action：静止遥远的钟上一个保持拍 —— 铁链几乎不动，尘土在它周围飘过冷光。然后 —— 细碎屑与细小、几乎不可察觉的卵石（SMALL, ALMOST IMPERCEPTIBLE PEBBLES）从上方岩体坠落，细小碎片在雾霭中几乎难以看见，从钟旁纷纷散落。这其几颗细小卵石带着清脆细微的「嗒嗒」声（TICKS）敲在钟的青铜肩部 —— 铜钟承受着小小的冲击，沉重的青铜钟体开始被累积的力拽得在链上踉跄（LURCH），铁链咔地绷直，从这距离几乎听不到一丝金属的呻吟。更多细小卵石从青铜表面弹开，几乎迷失在雾霭中。然后铜钟发出一声深沉共鸣的巨鸣（BOOMS）—— 一声恢弘的金属钟鸣越过大厅广袤的空间迸发而出，持续回荡翻涌，铜钟在链上疯狂摆动，沉重的青铜钟体在高处的惨白冷光中甩摆。深沉的钟鸣继续贯穿整段镜头响起，声音充满整座石厅，可听见为一片宏大的青铜钟鸣穿过建筑向外翻滚。即使隔着这段距离铜钟也肉眼可见地摇摆 —— 是画面上部唯一的大幅度运动，其余岩石几何保持静立，尘土绕着摆动的青铜钟体飘动。画面以手持晃动剧烈颤抖。冷光接住摆动的青铜形体。无可见声波 —— 只有铜钟物理性的摇摆、那些累积的细小冲击、以及一段持续共鸣的钟鸣作为音频。
