## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this shot): Documentary witness camera, amateur vérité aesthetic — as if filmed raw on a consumer camera. Despite the amateur look the rendering remains 8K photorealistic. EXTREME long-focal-length telephoto — heavy spatial compression, foreground and background visually stacked together. Operator stationary at hall floor level — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Allowed moves: ZOOM (in/out) and TILT (vertical rotation only). MAXIMUM violent handheld shake throughout — the longer the focal length, the more the tremor amplifies. Imperfect framing, reactive instinctive operator.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame line the bases of the columns and along the floor. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS:
RAIN — young woman, early-20s, silver-platinum bob with dark black tips. Grey cropped hooded utility jacket, hood partially up. Pressed against the right side of a column.
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set eyes, thick-set muscular build. Stone armored cuirass over tunic and draped knee-length skirt-robe, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Two long stone swords, one in each hand. Stone sandals on huge feet. Weathered grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine cracks. Moves with massive crushing weight, slow and deliberate. SCALE: he is enormous — the surrounding columns of the hall reach only to his hip or chest. He dwarfs everything in frame.
BELL — modest 2-3 meter antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern, hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored into the rock of the ceiling fissure. Small relative to the scale of the hall — almost human-scaled, dwarfed by the architecture.

SCENE 51E — THE REVEAL (single continuous shot).

FRAMING (held throughout): The shot is a wide telephoto from hall floor level. RAIN occupies the FAR RIGHT of frame in extreme close foreground, seen from behind / three-quarter back — silver-platinum bob, grey hood, soft-focus silhouette out of the depth-of-field. Immediately to her left in frame is the EDGE of her cover column with oil lamps glowing at its base, also slightly soft. Beyond her cover, the open hall stretches LEFT into the distance — receding columns, oil lamps lining the floor in a diminishing line, cold haze. Far in the middle-to-deep distance stands the GIANT STONE STATUE — turned with his BACK to camera, semi-profile back angle. Telephoto compression flattens the depth: Rain in foreground and giant in distance feel visually stacked, the regular hall columns near him reaching only to his hips, instantly readable as a 20-meter monster towering over a normal-scale environment.

Sub-beat A — SEARCHING APPROACH: The Giant is in the act of slowly searching the hall — his bald stone head turns deliberately as he scans the space, looking for something. He doesn't know about Rain. His back is to camera. We see massive shoulders and the back of his head shifting as he searches.

Sub-beat B — SLOW STRIKE WITH BACK TURNED: Still facing AWAY from camera, the Giant raises ONE stone sword in a SLOW deliberate methodical windup — his shoulder rolling, the blade lifting high. Held at the apex for a beat. Then he swings DOWN in a slow heavy arc into the nearest column at his side. The blade strikes with bone-deep concussive impact — but the column does NOT break, it HOLDS. Burst of dust puffs out at point of contact, fine fragments shake loose, the column stands intact. The frame jolts violently, oil lamps flicker along the floor, dust cascades from above. He continues facing away — his strike was casual, almost dismissive, part of his search. His scale is the point: even this restrained hit feels apocalyptic.

Sub-beat C — TILT UP AND ZOOM TO BELL: Camera begins a continuous upward TILT from its planted floor position — rotating up past the giant's distant figure, climbing past the rough rock walls and receding columns, reaching toward the ceiling fissure. Tilt reveals the modest 2-3 meter bronze BELL suspended by its iron chain in the fissure overhead — small, almost human-scaled, dwarfed by the surrounding rock, hanging quietly in the cold pale light. Camera holds briefly, then deliberate ZOOM-IN pushes closer onto the bell, tightening on its patinated bronze body, engraved relief bands becoming visible, the chain creaking faintly, dust drifting through the shaft of light around it. The bell sways very slightly from the impact tremors. Camera holds zoomed-in on the bell as the shot ends.

Throughout: maximum telephoto compression, planted operator far from action, RAIN's OTS silhouette anchoring frame-right in soft foreground, violent vérité handheld shake. Only zoom and tilt rotation. The 20-meter scale of the giant should read instantly through context — surrounding columns dwarfed beside him, the human-scaled bell dwarfed in the fissure above. Compression density makes the frame feel packed, oppressive, claustrophobic despite the vast architecture.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Style: 8K IMAX。写实电影级——无 3D 渲染、无游戏引擎、无游戏过场动画审美。
摄影指导：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
灯光：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机位于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
色彩：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀色。
摄影机：实体电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
皮肤：毛孔级真实感——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称胎记、毛细血管潮红、与棚内光匹配的毛孔阴影。
演技：好莱坞级——反应前微型停顿、精准视线与眼线、湿润活体之眼带眼神光（catch-lights）、可见呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理：遵守重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量，正确的接触阴影。无悬浮道具。
构图：三分法（rule of thirds）+ 黄金比例（golden ratio）。每个角色从头帧开始即移动。
连续性：角色、道具、环境在每一处切换间完全一致。无身份漂移。
技术：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（本镜头）：纪录见证摄影机，业余真实（vérité）审美——如同一台消费级摄影机裸拍。尽管是业余观感，渲染仍为 8K 写实电影级。超长焦距望远——重度空间压缩，前景与背景视觉层叠。操作者静止于巨厅地面层——不行走、不推轨推摄、不跟拍、不环绕。允许的运镜：ZOOM（推拉）与 TILT（仅垂直旋转）。全程最大强度剧烈手持抖动——焦距越长，震颤越被放大。不完美的构图，反应式的本能操作者。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
凿刻于山体内部的宏伟石厅。巨大华丽立柱分列两侧，向雾霭中退去。石板地板。小型油灯透出低橙火焰沿柱基与地板排列。一道冷白自然光柱自高远岩顶的裂隙倾泻而下。全程尘粒与大气雾感。

角色 / 主体：
莱恩（RAIN）——年轻女性，二十出头，银白波波头带深黑发梢。灰色短裁连帽战术夹克，兜帽半立。贴于一根立柱右侧。
石巨像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨硕雕刻石战士。光头、厚重威慑眉脊、深陷刻凿之眼、粗壮肌肉体型。罩衫外覆石质盔甲胸甲并垂膝裙袍、宽石腰带、缠裹手腕。两柄长石剑，一手一柄。巨足着石质凉鞋。风化灰石、沟槽内苔绿灰蚀斑、细密裂纹。以巨硕碾压重量移动，缓慢而审慎。体量：他巨大无匹——周围巨厅立柱仅及其髋或胸。他令画中一切显得渺小。
钟（BELL）——朴素 2-3 米古青铜钟，深蚀的绿青铜暗色表面，刻有经文与纹样的华丽凸起浮雕带，悬于一挂打入天顶裂隙岩石的重型铁链上。相对于巨厅体量很小——几近人尺度，被建筑反衬得渺小。

场景 51E——揭露（THE REVEAL）（单一连续镜头）。

取景（全程保持）：为自巨厅地面层的宽幅望远镜头。莱恩占据画面最右侧极端贴近前景，自背面 / 3/4 背面看——银白波波头、灰兜帽，失焦景深中轮廓朦胧。紧邻其左画中为她掩体柱的边缘，柱基油灯发光，亦略虚。越过其掩体，空旷巨厅向左延展至远处——退去的立柱、沿地板排列成渐缩一线的油灯、冷雾。中远纵深深处立着石巨像——背对摄影机转身，略带半侧面背向角。长焦压缩压平了纵深：前景莱恩与远处巨像观感上视觉层叠，巨像旁常规立柱仅及其髋，一目了然是一尊 20 米巨兽凌驾于常规尺度环境之上。

子节拍 A——搜寻逼近：巨像正在缓缓搜寻巨厅——他光秃的石首审慎转动，扫视空间，寻找什么。他不知莱恩的存在。背对摄影机。我们看到巨硕双肩与他搜寻时摆动的后脑。

子节拍 B——背对慢击：仍背对摄影机，巨像以缓慢审慎有条不紊的蓄势（windup）举起一柄石剑——肩部滚动，刃口高举。在顶点定格一个节拍。随后以缓慢沉重的弧线下劈，劈进身侧最近的立柱。剑刃以骨深震撼的撞击命中——但立柱并未断裂，它撑住了。接触点喷出一团尘土，细碎崩落，立柱完好屹立。画框剧烈震荡，沿地板油灯闪烁，尘土自上倾泻。他继续背对——他的挥击漫不经心，几乎是不屑一顾，是其搜寻的一部分。他的体量才是重点：即便是这般克制的重击观感都宛如末日。

子节拍 C——上倾并推镜至钟：摄影机自其植地的地板位置开始持续向上的 TILT——旋转越过巨像的远处身影，攀升掠过粗犷岩壁与退去的立柱，伸向天顶裂隙。倾斜揭示悬挂于头顶裂隙中铁链上那口朴素 2-3 米青铜钟——小巧、几近人尺度，被周围岩石反衬得渺小，静静悬于冷白淡光中。摄影机短暂定格，随后审慎的 ZOOM-IN 推近钟体，收紧其带蚀斑的青铜身，浮现刻文浮雕带，链条微弱吱嘎，灰尘穿过其周围光柱飘动。钟在撞击震颤影响下极轻微晃动。镜头定格于钟的推近画面收束本镜。

全程：最大限度长焦压缩、远离动作的植地操作者、莱恩的过肩剪影锚定右前景的柔和前景、剧烈真实手持抖动。仅变焦与倾斜旋转。巨像的 20 米体量应经语境瞬间读透——周围立柱在其旁显得渺小、人尺度的钟在上方裂隙中更显渺小。压缩密度令画面显得满实、压迫、幽闭，尽管建筑宏大无比。
