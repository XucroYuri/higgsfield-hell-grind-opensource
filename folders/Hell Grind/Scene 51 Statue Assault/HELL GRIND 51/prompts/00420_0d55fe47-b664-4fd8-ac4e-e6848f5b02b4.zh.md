Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light. Stone-carved giant: weathered granite-grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine hairline cracks, fine dust accumulation.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, visible breath. The stone giant is animated with weight and intent; menace reads through head angle, silhouette, deliberate stillness of stone catching cold light.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (all shots): Witness-style documentary handheld, amateur vérité aesthetic, 8K photorealistic rendering. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking, no arcing. Only rotational moves and lens-driven zoom allowed. MAXIMUM violent handheld shake throughout — peaking on impacts and on the bell strike. Imperfect raw framing. NO visible sound waves, NO heat-haze distortion, NO visible acoustic ripples — sound remains invisible; only its physical effects on solid matter are shown.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns lining both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Oil lamps glow at column bases. Cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set carved eyes, expressionless stone face. Stone armored cuirass over tunic, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Holds ONE long stone sword in right hand, left hand empty. Weathered grey stone, mossy patina, fine cracks. Moves with crushing weight.

BELL — large imposing antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern, hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored deep into the rock of the ceiling fissure — positioned high, practically against the rock ceiling above the hall.

SCENE 51E — STRIKE AND BELL TOLL (four shots, single prompt, in this exact order).

SHOT 1 — HORIZONTAL SWING (low-angle three-quarter close-up).
Framing: Low-angle close-up, camera planted at ground level looking UP at the GIANT in THREE-QUARTER ANGLE. Telephoto compression. Giant positioned slightly LEFT of center, cropped at the WAIST — only upper body visible: stone cuirass, wrapped right shoulder pad, bald head with heavy menacing brow, expressionless mouth. Right hand grips the long stone sword, blade held HIGH and to the side, raised in preparation. Frame-RIGHT: dark vertical mass of an adjacent column with rugged relief. Frame-LEFT: hazy cave depth. Cold rim light catches the contour of his profile and the edge of the raised blade.
Action: SLOW windup — the giant holds the sword raised high for a held beat, building tension. Then EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE — the blade WHIPS in a fast HORIZONTAL ARC across his body, sweeping sideways through the air at chest-to-hip level with brutal speed. Heavy motion blur on the blade as it cuts through frame. The blade SMASHES into the lower portion of a column (hip-level strike) with sudden bone-deep concussive impact — dust explodes outward, fragments spray, frame jolts violently with the shockwave at maximum shake intensity. The contrast between slow buildup and fast release amplifies the impact.

CUT.

SHOT 2 — COLUMN CHUNK FALL (close-up on column).
Framing: Tight close-up on the LOWER section of a hall column near floor level — weathered carved stone, fine relief patterns, mossy patina in crevices. Column fills frame vertically. Cold rim light catches the surface texture. Background: hazy hall depth, out-of-focus.
Action: The column stands intact for a held beat. Then SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT — an unseen blade smashes horizontally into the column from off-frame, stone surface fractures violently. A LARGE CHUNK of stone breaks off, hairline cracks racing outward. The chunk TUMBLES forward and downward through frame, falling with real mass-correct gravity, rotating slowly. Camera tilts down with it slightly. The chunk SLAMS into the stone-tiled floor at the bottom of frame with a thunderous secondary impact — stone-on-stone shatter, breaking into pieces, dust kicks up violently, debris skitters across tiles. Frame jolts violently on both impacts. Dust hangs heavy after.

CUT.

SHOT 3 — CRACK RACES UP WALL AND ACROSS CEILING (high-angle wide on upper wall and rock ceiling).
Framing: Camera planted HIGH up in the hall, well above the giant's head, looking outward at the UPPER SECTION OF THE CAVE WALL and across the ROCK CEILING. Wide angle. The vertical wall fills the lower-left of frame, rising up to meet the rough rock ceiling that arches overhead and fills the upper portion of frame — wall-and-ceiling junction running diagonally across the composition. Ancient pitted rock surface in detail, mossy patina in deep crevices, cold pale daylight from the distant ceiling fissure raking across the texture from upper-frame, atmospheric haze and slow-drifting dust catching the cold light. Hazy depth on frame-RIGHT looking down into the hall void. The bronze BELL is faintly visible at upper-frame edge in soft out-of-focus haze, hanging from its iron chain anchored into the rock — placed there as a destination the eye registers without yet focusing on.
Action: A held beat on the still rock face. Then — from the lower-frame edge a JAGGED HAIRLINE CRACK suddenly RACES upward across the wall surface with sharp velocity — a fast-propagating split tearing through the rock, splintering and forking as it travels, fine dust puffing out along its path. The crack hits the wall-and-ceiling junction and continues across the ROCK CEILING overhead, racing along the underside of the rock in a long jagged line, branching into smaller forks, fragments of stone shaking loose from the crack edges and beginning to drop. Pebbles and small debris start raining down past the camera. The crack continues toward upper-frame in the direction of the bell, propagating with relentless momentum. Frame trembles violently with handheld shake. Cold light catches the dust pluming from the fresh fracture. NO visible sound waves — pure physical structural failure, the wall and ceiling responding to the giant's strike like a living rock body splitting open.

CUT.

SHOT 4 — DEBRIS STRIKES THE BELL (close on bell).
Framing: Tight close-up on the BELL — the antique bronze body filling most of frame, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface in macro detail, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern catching cold rim light, the heavy iron chain rising from the crown of the bell up out of frame top into the rock fissure above. Background: rough rock of the upper ceiling fissure, cracked and freshly damaged from Shot 3, cold pale daylight bleeding from above silhouetting the bell, atmospheric haze and drifting dust. Frame-edges hint at the vast hall depth far below.
Action: A held beat on the still bell — chain barely moving, dust drifting around it. Then — STONE DEBRIS rains down from the freshly cracked ceiling above, small chunks tumbling through frame, and a LARGE FALLING ROCK SLAMS into the curved bronze shoulder of the bell with a sharp violent impact — the bell takes the hit hard, the bronze body LURCHING sideways on its chain, the iron links snapping taut with a metallic groan. A second chunk strikes the bell's flank. The bronze body BOOMS with a DEEP RESONANT TOLL — a massive metallic peal bursting outward, sustaining and ringing on, the bell oscillating wildly on its chain, swinging through cold light, the bronze surface trembling with visible micro-vibration. Smaller debris bounces off the patinated bronze, ricocheting away into the haze below. The deep ringing toll continues to sustain through the shot — the toll filling the entire hall, audible as a vast bronze peal rolling outward through the stone architecture. Dust around the bell stirs from its violent oscillation. Frame trembles violently with handheld shake. Cold light catches the swinging bronze body as the toll rolls on. NO visible sound waves — only the bell's physical sway, the metallic surface vibration, and the sustained resonant ring as audio.

Lighting（光线）：仅自然光 — 逆光背光（contre-jour）、摄影机位于阴影侧、全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。

Continuity（连贯性）：每一切之间角色、道具、环境完全一致。无身份漂移（no identity drift）。
Technical（技术）：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。不得抖动。
Audio（音频）：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（全部镜头摄影机装置）：旁观者式纪录手持、业余真实感美学（amateur vérité aesthetic）、8K 写实渲染。每个镜头中操作员保持静止 — 不步行、不推轨、不跟拍、不做弧线运动。仅允许旋转动作与镜头驱动变焦。全程最大幅度剧烈手持晃动 — 在冲击与撞钟处达峰。不完美的原生取景。不得有可见声波、不得有热霾失真、不得有可见声学涟漪 — 声音保持隐形；仅呈现其对固态物体的物理效应。

LOCATION（地点）— HELL HALL（地狱大厅）：
人工开凿于山体内的广阔石厅。两侧林立巨型的雕花石柱，渐退入雾。石板地。柱基油灯微微发光。一道苍白冷光自岩顶裂隙直落而下。尘埃微粒与大气雾感（atmospheric haze）遍布全程。

石巨像（Stone Giant）— 高 20 米的巨型石刻战士。光头、厚重凶恶的眉骨、深雕眼窝、面无表情的石脸。束腰外衣外覆石制铠甲胸甲、宽石腰、缠裹手腕。右手持一把长石剑、左手空着。风化灰石、苔藓铜绿、细裂。以碾压般的重量移动。

BELL（钟）— 大型威严的古董青铜钟，深斑锈绿青铜表面、文字与图案的华丽凸起浮雕带、由一条深入天顶裂隙岩石中的重型铁链悬吊 — 位置高高、几乎紧贴大厅上方的岩顶。

SCENE 51E — STRIKE AND BELL TOLL（打击与鸣钟）（四个镜头，单一提示词，按此顺序严格排列）。

镜头 1 — 水平挥击（低角度 3/4 特写）。
取景：低角度特写，摄影机固定在地面高度，仰视处于 THREE-QUARTER ANGLE（3/4 角度）的巨像。长焦压缩。巨像微偏中心左侧，于 WAIST（腰部）处裁切 — 仅见上身：石胸甲、裹缠的右肩垫、厚重凶恶眉骨的光头、面无表情的嘴。右手紧握长石剑，剑刃高高举向身侧、预备举起。画面右侧：相邻巨柱的深色垂直体量、带粗犷浮雕。画面左侧：雾霾的洞穴纵深。寒冷轮廓光（rim light）勾住他侧影的轮廓与高举剑刃的边缘。
动作：SLOW windup（缓慢蓄力）— 巨像将剑高举、顿住一拍蓄势。随后 EXPLOSIVE FAST RELEASE（爆发式快速释放）— 剑刃 WHIPS（猛挥）出一道快速的水平弧线掠过他身前，以狂暴速度在胸至髋高度横扫侧向空气。剑刃切过画面时带沉重运动模糊。剑刃以骤然深及骨骼的震颤冲击 SMASHES（砸）入一根巨柱下部（髋高处的打击）— 尘埃向外炸开、碎片飞溅、画面随冲击波在最高抖动强度下剧烈崩震。缓慢蓄势与快速释放之间的反差放大了冲击。

切。

镜头 2 — 巨柱碎块坠落（巨柱特写）。
取景：大厅巨柱近地高度的下部紧特写 — 风化的雕石、细浮雕纹样、缝隙中苔藓铜绿。巨柱纵向填满画面。寒冷轮廓光（rim light）打亮表面质感。背景：雾霾大厅纵深、失焦。
动作：巨柱完好地顿立一拍。随后 SUDDEN MASSIVE IMPACT（骤然巨力冲击）— 一柄看不见的刀刃自框外向横向砸入巨柱、石面剧烈破裂。一大块巨石断裂、发丝般细纹向外急窜。碎块以真实质量重力向前向下 TUMBLES（翻滚）过画面、缓慢自转。摄影机随它微微下移。碎块以雷霆般的二次冲击 SLAMS（猛砸）入画面底部的石板地 — 石碰石碎裂、崩成数块、尘土剧烈扬起、碎片嘎啦划过石板。两次冲击都令画面剧烈崩震。事后尘土沉重弥漫。

切。

镜头 3 — 裂纹沿墙上窜、直穿天花板（上墙与岩顶的高角度广角）。
取景：摄影机固定于大厅 HIGH（高处）、远在巨像头顶之上、向外望向洞壁 UPPER SECTION（上部区段）并横越 ROCK CEILING（岩顶）。广角。垂直墙壁填满画面左下、向上攀升、接住头顶拱起、填满画面上部的不规则岩顶 — 墙顶交界斜贯构图。古老坑蚀的岩面细节、深裂隙中苔藓铜绿、来自远处天顶裂隙的苍白冷日光自画面上方斜扫质感、捕捉冷光的大气雾感与缓飘尘埃。画面右部向下望向大厅空间的雾霾纵深。青铜 BELL（钟）在天顶裂隙处若隐若现于画面上缘的柔和失焦雾中、由深入岩石的铁链悬吊 — 作为观众已留意、却尚未聚焦的目标而置放。
动作：静岩面上顿住一拍。随后 — 自画面下缘、一道锯齿状 HAIRLINE CRACK（发丝裂纹）骤然以锐利速度沿墙面向上 RACES（疾窜）— 一道高速传播的裂口撕开岩石、行进间碎裂分叉、沿程迸出细尘。裂纹抵达墙顶交界、继续横越头顶的 ROCK CEILING（岩顶）、沿岩石下壁划出一条长长的锯齿线、分出更小的叉、岩稜碎片自裂纹边缘松脱、开始坠落。石卵与小碎片开始掠过摄影机纷纷坠下。裂纹朝画面上方、钟的方向继续传播、以穷追不舍的气势推进。画面随手持晃动剧烈震颤。冷光捕捉新鲜裂口中扬起的尘柱。不得有可见声波 — 纯粹物理的结构失效、墙壁与天花板像一具活生生、裂开的岩质躯体般响应巨像的一击。

切。

镜头 4 — 碎片击中钟（钟的特写）。
取景：BELL（钟）紧特写 — 古董青铜钟体填满画面大半、深斑锈绿青铜表面呈微距细节、映着寒冷轮廓光的文字与图案华丽凸起浮雕带、重铁链自钟冠向上探出画面上缘、没入上方岩缝。背景：上端天顶裂隙的粗岩、自镜头 3 起开裂的新损、自上方泄入的苍白冷日光剪出钟的轮廓、大气雾感与飘尘。画面边缘暗示远处下方浩瀚的大厅纵深。
动作：静钟上顿住一拍 — 链几乎不动、尘埃在其周飘荡。随后 — 自上方刚开裂的天花板、STONE DEBRIS（石碎）纷纷坠落、小块翻滚过画面、一块 LARGE FALLING ROCK（大坠岩）猛 SLAMS（砸）入钟的弧青铜肩、带一道尖锐暴烈的冲击 — 钟沉重地受了这一击、青铜钟体在其链上 LURCHING（猛甩）侧向、铁链环以一声金属呻吟骤然绷紧。第二块碎片命中钟的侧壁。青铜钟体 BOOMS（轰响）出一声 DEEP RESONANT TOLL（深沉共鸣的钟鸣）— 一波沛然金属震响向外迸出、持续鸣振不绝、钟在其链上疯狂震荡、荡过冷光、青铜表面以可见的微振颤抖。更小的碎屑撞开斑驳青铜、弹射开落入下方雾中。深沉的钟鸣余音在整枚镜头中不绝 — 钟鸣填满整个大厅、可闻为一波浩荡的青铜震响透过石质建筑向外滚涌。钟周围的尘埃被它剧烈的震荡搅起。画面随手持晃动剧烈震颤。钟鸣碾过时、冷光捕捉摆荡的青铜钟体。不得有可见声波 — 唯有钟的物理摆荡、金属表面振动、以及作为音频而持续存在的共鸣声。
