## 原文 (source)

Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.
Cinematography: Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins.
Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.
Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent.
Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this shot): Documentary witness camera, amateur vérité aesthetic — as if filmed raw on a consumer camera. Despite the amateur look the rendering remains 8K photorealistic. EXTREME long-focal-length telephoto — heavy spatial compression. Operator stationary at hall FLOOR level, camera very low — extreme LOW ANGLE looking UPWARD at the giant in the distance. Camera does not walk, dolly, track, or arc. Allowed moves: ZOOM (in/out) and TILT (vertical rotation only). MAXIMUM violent handheld shake throughout — telephoto amplifies the tremor. Imperfect framing, reactive instinctive operator.

LOCATION — HELL HALL:
Vast stone hall carved inside a mountain. Massive ornate columns flank both sides, receding into haze. Stone-tiled floor. Small oil lamps with low orange flame line the bases of columns and along the floor. A cold pale shaft of natural light cuts down from a fissure in the rock ceiling far above. Dust particles and atmospheric haze throughout.

CHARACTERS / SUBJECTS:
GIANT STONE STATUE — 20-METER-tall colossal carved stone warrior. Bald, heavy menacing brow, deep-set eyes, thick-set muscular build. Stone armored cuirass over tunic and draped knee-length skirt-robe, wide stone belt, wrapped wrists. Two long stone swords, one in each hand. Stone sandals on huge feet. Weathered grey stone, mossy green-grey patina in crevices, fine cracks. Moves with massive crushing weight, slow and deliberate. SCALE REFERENCE: the surrounding hall columns reach only to his hip or chest height — he dwarfs the regular architecture entirely.
BELL — modest 2-3 meter antique bronze bell, dark patinated greenish-bronze surface, ornate raised relief bands of script and pattern, hangs from a heavy iron chain anchored into the rock of the ceiling fissure. Small relative to the scale of the hall — almost human-scaled, dwarfed by the architecture.

SCENE 51E — TITAN REVEAL (single continuous shot).

FRAMING (held throughout): EXTREME LOW ANGLE from hall floor level, looking UPWARD into the depth of the hall. Telephoto lens, heavy spatial compression. The GIANT STONE STATUE stands at distance in the middle-to-deep ground, but because of the low angle and his 20-meter scale he OCCUPIES THE LARGE PORTION of the frame — his massive form rising from the floor and reaching toward the upper edge of frame. He is turned with his BACK to camera, semi-profile back angle. The surrounding hall columns near him visibly reach only to his hip or chest, instantly establishing he is a 20-meter colossus. The receding stone-tile floor stretches in foreground at our feet. Cold haze and the high ceiling fissure read above him. Composition is heroic, monumental, almost mythic — declaring his presence and grandeur.

Sub-beat A — STANDING TITAN: The Giant stands tall in the distance at the start of the shot, back to us, two stone swords held low at his sides. His massive silhouette dominates the frame from below, head almost touching the upper edge from this low angle. His shoulders shift slowly as he scans the hall in the direction he faces — searching, methodical. Ground tremor faintly registers as his weight settles. Scale is the headline: regular columns dwarfed, the man is a monument.

Sub-beat B — SLOW STRIKE: Still facing away, the Giant raises ONE stone sword in a SLOW deliberate methodical windup — shoulder rolling, blade lifting high. Held at the apex for a beat. Then he swings DOWN in a slow heavy arc into the column standing right next to him — a regular hall column reaching only to his hip. Blade strikes with bone-deep concussive impact — column does NOT break, it HOLDS. Burst of dust puffs out at point of contact. Frame jolts violently with shockwave, oil lamps along the floor flicker, dust cascades from the ceiling. Restrained casual violence — part of his search.

Sub-beat C — WALKS DEEPER: After the strike, the Giant resumes walking — slow heavy strides taking him FURTHER into the depth of the hall, away from camera. Each footfall sends a faint ground tremor through frame, lamps along the floor jiggling. He does not look back. His massive form recedes a fraction, but telephoto compression keeps him visually huge in the frame — still dominant, still towering. He passes the broken-but-intact column and continues searching the far end of the hall.

Sub-beat D — TILT UP AND ZOOM TO BELL: Camera begins a continuous upward TILT from its planted floor position — rotating up past the giant's distant figure, climbing past the rough rock walls and receding columns, reaching toward the ceiling fissure. Tilt reveals the modest 2-3 meter bronze BELL suspended by its iron chain in the fissure overhead, small and almost human-scaled, dwarfed by surrounding rock. Camera holds briefly, then deliberate ZOOM-IN pushes closer onto the bell, tightening on its patinated bronze body, engraved relief bands becoming visible, chain creaking faintly, dust drifting through the shaft of light. The bell sways slightly from the impact tremors. Camera holds zoomed-in on the bell as the shot ends.

Throughout: extreme low angle, maximum telephoto compression, planted operator at floor level, 20-meter giant occupying most of frame at distance, violent vérité handheld shake. Only zoom and tilt rotation. The scale of the giant must read instantly — heroic, monumental, mythic — through the low angle and dwarfed surrounding columns.

## 中文对照 (zh)

Style: 8K IMAX。写实电影级——无 3D 渲染、无游戏引擎、无游戏过场动画审美。
摄影指导：Emmanuel Lubezki x Roger Deakins。
灯光：仅自然光——逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机位于阴影侧，全程大气雾感。主光仅来自天空与窗户。
色彩：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀色。
摄影机：实体电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
皮肤：毛孔级真实感——汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称胎记、毛细血管潮红、与棚内光匹配的毛孔阴影。
演技：好莱坞级——反应前微型停顿、精准视线与眼线、湿润活体之眼带眼神光（catch-lights）、可见呼吸与胸廓起伏。
物理：遵守重力与惯性——质量具有真实重量，正确的接触阴影。无悬浮道具。
构图：三分法（rule of thirds）+ 黄金比例（golden ratio）。每个角色从头帧开始即移动。
连续性：角色、道具、环境在每一处切换间完全一致。无身份漂移。
技术：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
音频：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

摄影机装置（本镜头）：纪录见证摄影机，业余真实（vérité）审美——如同一台消费级摄影机裸拍。尽管是业余观感，渲染仍为 8K 写实电影级。超长焦距望远——重度空间压缩。操作者静止于巨厅地面层，摄影机极低——极低角度向上仰望远处巨像。摄影机不行走、不推轨推摄、不跟拍、不环绕。允许的运镜：ZOOM（推拉）与 TILT（仅垂直旋转）。全程最大强度剧烈手持抖动——长焦放大震颤。不完美构图，反应式本能操作者。

地点——地狱巨厅（HELL HALL）：
凿刻于山体内部的宏伟石厅。巨大华丽立柱分列两侧，向雾霭中退去。石板地板。小型油灯透出低橙火焰沿柱基与地板排列。一道冷白自然光柱自高远岩顶的裂隙倾泻而下。全程尘粒与大气雾感。

角色 / 主体：
石巨像（GIANT STONE STATUE）——20 米高的巨硕雕刻石战士。光头、厚重威慑眉脊、深陷刻凿之眼、粗壮肌肉体型。罩衫外覆石质盔甲胸甲并垂膝裙袍、宽石腰带、缠裹手腕。两柄长石剑，一手一柄。巨足着石质凉鞋。风化灰石、沟槽内苔绿灰蚀斑、细密裂纹。以巨硕碾压重量移动，缓慢而审慎。体量参照：周围巨厅立柱仅及其髋或胸——他彻底凌驾常规建筑之上。
钟（BELL）——朴素 2-3 米古青铜钟，深蚀的绿青铜暗色表面，刻有经文与纹样的华丽凸起浮雕带，悬于一挂打入天顶裂隙岩石的重型铁链上。相对于巨厅体量很小——几近人尺度，被建筑反衬得渺小。

场景 51E——泰坦揭露（TITAN REVEAL）（单一连续镜头）。

取景（全程保持）：自巨厅地面层的极低角度，向上仰望巨厅纵深。长焦镜头、重度空间压缩。石巨像立在中远纵深处，但因低角度与他的 20 米体量，他占据画面的较大区域——他巨硕的形态自地面升起，探向画面上缘。他背对摄影机，半侧面背向角。其旁周围巨厅立柱可见地仅及他髋或胸，瞬间确立他是 20 米巨像。退去石板地面在我们脚下前景延展。冷雾与高远天顶裂隙在他上方读来。构图是英雄式的、恢弘的、近乎神话般的——宣告他的存在与威严。

子节拍 A——挺立的泰坦：镜头开始时巨像在远处高挺站立，背朝我们，两柄石剑低垂于身侧。自这一低角度，他巨硕的剪影自下方主宰画面，头几乎触到上缘。他缓缓转动双肩扫视所朝方向的巨厅——搜寻着，有条不紊。他体重落定时地面震颤隐约传来。体量是头条：常规立柱渺小，此人为一座纪念碑。

子节拍 B——缓慢挥击：仍背对方向，巨像以缓慢审慎有条不紊的蓄势（windup）举起一柄石剑——肩部滚动，刃口高举。在顶点定格一个节拍。随后他以缓慢沉重的弧线下劈，劈进紧邻他身旁的立柱——一根常规巨厅立柱仅及其髋。剑刃以骨深震撼的撞击命中——立柱并未断裂，它撑住了。接触点喷出一团尘土。画框随冲击波剧烈震荡，沿地板油灯闪烁，尘土自天花板倾泻。克制的漫不经心之暴——其搜寻的一部分。

子节拍 C——向深处走去：挥击之后，巨像继续行走——缓慢沉重的大步带他进一步深入巨厅纵深，远离摄影机。每一步落足都令画框传来隐约地面震颤，沿地板灯焰随之颠动。他不回头。他巨硕的身影略微退远，但长焦压缩令他在画面中视觉上依旧巨大——依旧主宰、依旧高耸。他越过那根断裂却完好的立柱，继续搜寻巨厅远端。

子节拍 D——上倾并推镜至钟：摄影机自其植地位置开始持续向上的 TILT——旋转越过巨像的远处身影、攀升越过粗犷岩壁与退去的立柱、伸向天顶裂隙。倾斜揭示裂隙中以铁链悬挂的朴素 2-3 米青铜钟，小巧、几近人尺度，被周围岩石反衬渺小。摄影机短暂定格，随后审慎的 ZOOM-IN 推近钟体，收紧其带蚀斑的青铜身，浮现刻文浮雕带，链条微弱吱嘎，灰尘穿过光柱飘动。钟在撞击震颤下微微晃动。镜头定格于钟的推近画面收束本镜。

全程：极低角度、最大长焦压缩、植地操作者于地面层、20 米巨像在远处占据画面大部、剧烈真实手持抖动。仅变焦与倾斜旋转。巨像的体量必须瞬间读透——英雄式、恢弘、神话式——通过低角度与被反衬渺小的周围立柱。
