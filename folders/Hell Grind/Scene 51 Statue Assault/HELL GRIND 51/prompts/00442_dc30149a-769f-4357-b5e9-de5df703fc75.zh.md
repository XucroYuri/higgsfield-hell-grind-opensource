Style: 8K IMAX. Photorealistic — no 3D render, no game engine, no game-cutscene aesthetic.

Lighting: Natural light only — contre-jour backlight, camera on shadow side, atmospheric haze throughout. Key light from sky and windows only.

Camera: Physical cine lens. 180-degree shutter motion blur.
Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric moles, capillary flush, pore-shadow matching on-set light.
Acting: Hollywood — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with catch-lights, visible breath and chest rise.
Physics: Gravity and inertia respected — mass has real weight, correct contact shadows. No floating props.
Composition: Rule of thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one.
Continuity: Characters, props, environment identical across every cut. No identity drift.
Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail. No jitter.
Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music. No subtitles.

CAMERA RIG (this scene): VIOLENT handheld throughout in EVERY shot — heavy aggressive jitter, breathing rig, reactive corrections. Operator stationary in each shot — no walking, no dollying, no tracking. Allowed within a shot: rack focus, minor reframing pan/tilt rotation, lens-driven zoom. TREMOR RULE: When tremors hit, camera shake intensifies and a low rumble shudders the environment, but NO dust cascades, NO debris falls, NO pebbles rain into frame. Atmosphere stays clean during dialogue.

Then NEW CRYSTAL GROWTH begins — fresh translucent red crystals slowly extruding outward from between the knuckles and along the back of the gauntlet, growing in jagged elongating spikes, refracting cold light from within. Growth is unhurried, biological, the crystals lengthening visibly as if alive — quiet menacing demonstration of his power. The fist remains absolutely still. In the soft-focus background the three faces hold steady, watching. Frame trembles violently. No falling dust or debris.

SHOT 4 — ROKO FINAL LINE CU (50mm CU on Roko, confident, VIOLENT handheld).
Framing: Tight close-up on ROKO at the column's right edge, three-quarter front toward camera, red crystalline armor catching cold rim light, dark messy hair falling past his face. Cold backlight wraps the column edge behind him. Background: dark stone and hazy depth.
Action: Roko raises his gaze from the hand back up to find Jax and Rain off-frame, eyes settling on them with calm conviction. He delivers the line low, normal speaking volume — not a whisper — voice carrying certainty. He opens with a brief held breath on the conditional, a single deliberate pause on the ellipsis, then continues in a controlled flow:

The pause on the ellipsis is a measured drag breath, not theatrical — the rest of the line continues unbroken. His expression holds steady through the line, jaw set, eyes locked off-frame. Catch-light in wet eyes, breath visible. The crystalline armor catches cold rim light, fine internal refractions of red. Frame trembles violently throughout. No falling dust or debris.

Style：8K IMAX（不译）。照片级真实 —— 无 3D 渲染、无游戏引擎、无游戏过场动画质感。

Lighting：仅自然光 —— 逆光背光（contre-jour），摄影机置于背光侧，全程大气雾感（atmospheric haze）。主光仅来自天空与窗户。

Camera：物理电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。
Skin：毛孔级真实感 —— 汗毛（vellus hair）、不对称痣、毛细血管潮红、与现场灯光匹配的毛孔阴影。
Acting：好莱坞式 —— 反应前的微停顿、精准的眼线、湿润有神带眼神光（catch-lights）的眼睛、可见的呼吸与胸腔起伏。

Technical：24fps 平滑运动。8K 细节。无抖动。
Audio：仅环境音效（Environmental SFX only）。无音乐。无字幕。

CAMERA RIG（本场景）：全程每一个镜头都用「暴力手持（VIOLENT handheld）」—— 强烈、咄咄逼人的抖动、呼吸式机位、应激性纠正。每个镜头中摄影机操作员保持静止 —— 不走路、不推轨、不跟拍。镜头内允许：跟焦、小幅重构图摇镜/俯仰旋转、镜头驱动变焦。即使在特写中画面也剧烈地漂浮颤抖。

TREMOR RULE（震颤规则）：震颤袭来时，摄像晃动加剧、一股低沉的隆隆声令环境震颤，但绝无尘土簌簌落下、绝无碎片坠落、绝无小石子砸进画面。对白期间氛围保持干净。
