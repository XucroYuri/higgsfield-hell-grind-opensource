"and when something interrupts it," — her hand changes shape sharply, fingers chopping or slicing horizontally through the air, miming a cut or break in that rhythm.

A held beat. A faint flicker in her eyes — the second part of the realization landing.

"and opens his mouth." — both hands rise slightly, fingers spreading apart in a small opening gesture in front of her own face, miming an open mouth, eyes locking back hard on Jax. She holds the look, waiting for it to register.

Catch-light in wet eyes, breath visible, micro-pauses between phrases. The gestures are tactical-team-leader compact, not theatrical — small, fast, purposeful. Frame trembles violently throughout. No falling dust, no debris.
