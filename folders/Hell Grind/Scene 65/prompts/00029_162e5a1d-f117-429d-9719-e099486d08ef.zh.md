## 原文 (source)

Re-render this same exact location from a different camera angle: the camera moves to the left side of the scene, so the gnarled tree is now positioned on the right third of the frame, and the distant conical mountain is visible on the far left in the background. Show more of the snowy plain extending to the left, revealing additional rocks, red-leafed bushes, and frosted reed grass in the foreground stretching across the new composition. Keep the same tree species, the same twisted trunk character with exposed roots, the same rust-red and frosted white foliage, the same overall mood. Preserve identical lighting conditions: soft diffused backlight from behind the mountain, moderate snowstorm with diagonal motion-blurred snow streaks in the foreground, volumetric atmospheric fog. Maintain the cinematic anamorphic look: horizontal oval bokeh, subtle barrel distortion, vertical highlight stretch, mild chromatic aberration, organic film grain, gentle vignetting, no lens flares. Same muted cool color palette with rust-red accents. Cinematic 2.39:1 widescreen aspect ratio with letterbox bars.
