## 原文 (source)

medium close up shot of monster standing in front of tree, holding diamond

<<<image_3>>> — The akari artifact. A SMALL clear transparent crystal teardrop, only about 8-10cm tall (the size of a small egg or a lighter — easily fits in the palm of a hand with fingers wrapping around it). Multi-faceted like a cut gemstone — colorless, not red, not glowing. Mounted in a base of 5-7 weathered dark-bronze talons that grip the crystal from below, coiled like tiny roots holding it upright. The metal base is small, ancient and rough, with green oxidation between the talons. The crystal refracts ambient light through its facets, producing subtle rainbow caustics, but emits no light of its own. Snowflakes that touch it melt instantly. Always held in a hand or embedded in the Monster's chest. SCALE NOTE: this is a small intimate object — when held by the 4-meter Monster, it nearly disappears in his huge clawed hand, only visible because he holds it deliberately between his thumb and fingers, raised. NEVER render it as large or impressive in size — its power is in its smallness. Element reference only.
