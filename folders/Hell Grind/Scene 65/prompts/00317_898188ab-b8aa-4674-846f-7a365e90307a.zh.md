Photoreal cinematic shot. Dusk. Close on a three-meter humanoid figure standing on a flat bone-grey snowfield, the trunk of a dead black tree behind him going out of focus — Monster. Greyish dense scarred skin, long black bone spikes up his spine, long black hair in a high asymmetric topknot, faded blood-colored battle robes hanging open at the chest. His left arm is normal humanoid; his right arm hangs longer and blacker at his side with five half-meter razor claws of black bone.

His left hand rises slowly to his own chest, palm open. He does not unbutton anything. He pushes his fingertips into the skin of his bare chest just beneath the collarbone — and the skin OPENS for him. Not torn, not cut. The skin opens like a wet seam, a soft slit parts vertically across muscle, edges pink and glistening. There is no blood. The flesh has done this before.

His left hand reaches inside the seam. His fingers move once. He withdraws his hand holding a fist-sized teardrop-shaped crystal — deep crimson, internally faceted, semi-translucent, a soft pulse of light visible inside it. The crystal is wet from being inside him. Frost forms on its surface within the second despite the warmth of his hand.

He holds it in front of his chest at sternum height. The grin has returned to his face but smaller now — not amused, possessive. Eye contact with camera/Roco. He turns the crystal once between his fingers like a magician showing a coin.

MONSTER (the same soft tender voice, addressing camera/Roco): "You want it. Reach in and take it."

He pushes the crystal back through the open seam in his chest. The skin closes over it like wax cooling into smoothness — within two seconds there is no scar, no mark, just unbroken grey skin.

He raises both arms slowly out to his sides — left arm normal, right arm with its half-meter claws. Palms up. An invitation. His grin holds.

他的左手缓缓举到自己的胸口、掌心张开。他不解任何扣。他把指尖按进锁骨正下方赤裸胸口的皮肤——而那皮肤为他张开。不是撕裂、不是切割。皮肤如湿润的接缝般张开、一道柔软裂口在肌肉上纵向分开、边缘粉嫩湿亮。无血。这血肉曾做过这件事。

他的左手探入接缝内。他的手指动了一次。他缩回手、握着一颗拳头大小的泪滴形水晶——深绯红、内部多面切割、半透明、内部可见一缕柔和的光脉动。水晶因在他体内而潮湿。尽管他手掌温暖、冰霜仍在一秒内凝成于其表面。
