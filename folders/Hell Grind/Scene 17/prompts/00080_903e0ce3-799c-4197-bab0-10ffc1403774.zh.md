## 原文 (source)

8K IMAX. Photorealistic, photographed on a physical motion-picture camera. No 3D render, no game engine. Genre: Modern action blockbuster. References: cinematography of Emmanuel Lubezki and Roger Deakins. No CGI-heavy feel, no game-cutscene aesthetic. Lighting: Only natural light and practicals — camera always on the shadow side of subjects, backlit contre-jour with atmospheric haze throughout. Key light comes only from the sky, windows, or on-set fixtures. Color: 60:30:10 — dominant / secondary / accent. Camera: Physical cine lens. 180° shutter motion blur. No frame-interp smoothing. Realism: Photorealistic surfaces with real-world wear, organic asymmetry, and real material behavior — skin shows pores, vellus, asymmetric moles, capillary flush with pore-shadow matching on-set light; corner vignette and edge chromatic aberration from physical lens. Gravity, inertia, and follow-through respected — mass has real weight, every object casts correct contact shadows. No render-look, no floating or duplicated props. Continuity: All characters, props, environment, and lighting stay identical across every frame and every cut. No identity drift, no appearance changes. Acting: Hollywood-grade — micro-pauses before reactions, precise eye-line, wet living eyes with practical catch-lights and micro-saccades, visible breath and chest rise, pre-speech micro-swallow, inter-beat micro-expressions. No playing to camera. Composition: Thirds + golden ratio. Every person moving from frame one, no static figures. Technical: 60fps smooth motion, 8K detail. No jitter, no identity drift. Audio: Environmental SFX only. No music, no subtitles.

## 中文对照 (zh)

8K IMAX。写实摄影，实体电影摄影机实拍（physical motion-picture camera）。非 3D 渲染、非游戏引擎。类型：现代动作大片（action blockbuster）。参考：Emmanuel Lubezki 与 Roger Deakins 的摄影指导。无浓重 CGI 感、无游戏过场动画质感。灯光：仅自然光与实用光（practicals）——摄影机始终置于被摄主体的阴影一侧，全片逆光背光（contre-jour）配以大气雾感。主光仅来自天空、窗户或现场灯具。色彩：60:30:10——主色 / 次色 / 点缀色。摄影机：实体电影镜头。180° 快门运动模糊。无帧间插值平滑（frame-interp smoothing）。写实度：真实世界的磨损、有机的不对称与真实的材质物理行为——皮肤呈现毛孔、汗毛（vellus）、不对称痣、毛细血管潮红，毛孔阴影贴合现场光；实体镜头带来的暗角（vignette）与边缘色差（chromatic aberration）。遵循重力、惯性与动作余势（follow-through）——质量有真实分量，每个物体投射正确的接触阴影。无渲染质感、无漂浮或重复的道具。连贯性：所有角色、道具、环境与灯光在每一帧、每一刀（cut）间保持完全一致。无身份漂移、无外观变化。表演：好莱坞级——反应前的微停顿、精准视线、湿润有神且带实用眼神光（practical catch-lights）与微扫视（micro-saccades）的活眼睛、可见的呼吸与胸口起伏、说话前微吞咽、节拍间的微表情。绝不对镜头演出。构图：三分法 + 黄金比例。每个人从第一帧起即在运动，无静止人物。技术：60fps 平滑运动，8K 细节。无抖动、无身份漂移。音频：仅环境音效（SFX only）。无音乐、无字幕。
