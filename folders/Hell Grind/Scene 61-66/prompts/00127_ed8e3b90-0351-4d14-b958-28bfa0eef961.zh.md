<<<image_1>>> (Roco · STRIPPED) — ⚠️20岁混血亚白男性, 瘦削结实体型. ⚠️湿润凌乱中长黑发(垂到眉毛/眼睛), 稀疏黑色八字胡, 苍白皮肤+疲惫凹陷眼神, 鼻梁横向⚠️浅色已经愈合多年的旧伤疤约2-3cm(light-colored long-healed old scar, ⚠️NO redness, NO pink, NO red, very faded almost skin-toned). 黑色T恤(沾泥+撕裂)、黑色战术长裤+腰带+挂袋+devil钥匙扣、黑色无指手套、右手腕红头巾、沾泥战术靴, 双臂裸露+泥渍, 右手空空, 表情疲惫+不可置信。
<<<image_2>>> (Lulu · 怀孕的Lulu) — ⚠️20岁年轻女性, ⚠️深棕色短发bob, 头部右侧⚠️白色丝带蝴蝶结, 项链小水晶吊坠, 自然柔和妆容, 灰绿色眼睛, 皮肤自然偏白略偏暖+孕妇glow. ⚠️白色丝绸长裙带蓝色花朵图案(细肩带, 深V领, 露背, 伞形长裙), 右肩臂蝴蝶纹身. ⚠️怀孕5个月(腹部圆润隆起). 赤脚.
<<<image_3>>> (Sunlit meadow) — 远处明亮圆形空地. ⚠️SIZE: 直径约14米/~150平方米(Lulu在中心距边界约7米). 中央密集淡蓝色scilla花海+周围阴暗青木原森林+巨大杉树+黑竹+酒红色枫叶+边界倒下枯枝/树根/落叶+小水洼+岩石+薄雪斑+明亮冷色调垂直光柱从上方穿过浓雾直射到花地上.

multishot sequence

style: imax电影现实主义, 超真实实拍, Lubezki x Deakins; ⚠️非常浓厚DENSE的冷蓝灰雾(能见度5-10米); ⚠️持续轻雪飘落(amorphous shapeless snow, NOT hex stars); 阴沉天空; 60:30:10配色; 21:9, 8K, 60fps with 180-shutter; ⚠️⚠️⚠️CAMERA MANDATE: ⚠️呼吸式平滑手持机(always breathing handheld AND ALWAYS SMOOTH, gentle organic breathing drift, NEVER jerky, NEVER aggressive shake), 阴面拍摄; HARD CUTS. ⚠️音频: 寂静+对话+呼吸+脚步. Lulu声音轻微空间混响. ⚠️禁则: 禁3D/CG/游戏引擎、禁霓虹、禁正面光/补光板、禁桶形畸变/鱼眼、禁过饱和/HDR、禁漂浮道具、禁身份漂移、禁角色自发光、禁音乐/字幕.

lens: ⚠️Laowa 85mm T2.4 telephoto for CU shots(F2.0, swirl bokeh, anamorphic flares); ⚠️Laowa Cine 12mm T2.9 wide for WS+chase.

⚠️⚠️⚠️LULU POSITION MANDATE: Lulu⚠️始终严格站在空地的⚠️几何中心(strictly at the EXACT GEOMETRIC CENTER of the circular meadow ALWAYS — Lulu does NOT move from center until shot 5 of 63.2C when she walks back into fog), 双手温柔放在怀孕腹部上.

⚠️⚠️⚠️ROCO STRAIGHT-LINE MANDATE: Roco⚠️严格沿一条直线从空地南边界(他进入空地的入口点)朝向Lulu(空地中心)走(Roco moves in a STRAIGHT LINE from the south entry edge of the meadow directly toward Lulu at center, ⚠️NO zigzag, NO deviation, NO sideways movement). 这条南-北直线⚠️穿过空地中心.

⚠️⚠️⚠️LIGHTING MANDATE (关键): ⚠️冷色调银蓝白色光柱从空地正上方(略偏北侧)穿过浓雾直射下来照亮空地中心(cold silvery-blue-white light shaft pours down from above and slightly behind Lulu's position from the north — light source is above-and-behind Lulu from Roco's POV). ⚠️⚠️Lulu始终处于⚠️完全的逆光/contre-jour(Lulu always stands IN the cold silvery-blue-white sunbeam, ⚠️FULL CONTRE-JOUR BACKLIGHT — light comes from ABOVE-and-BEHIND her from Roco's perspective, illuminating the back of her hair/shoulders/dress with strong rim light, her face slightly in shadow side but still visible due to bright bounce from the surrounding light). ⚠️⚠️Roco的脸正对着Lulu, 因此他⚠️面对光源(Roco faces Lulu directly, therefore he is facing the light source) — ⚠️⚠️⚠️Roco一旦走入空地, 他的脸⚠️开始被冷色调银蓝白色光⚠️正面照亮(as soon as Roco steps into the meadow, his face begins to be illuminated FRONTALLY by the cold silvery-blue-white light coming from Lulu's direction/center, 正面光照亮他的整张脸/full frontal light on his face, 苍白冷调skin tone, 比他身后阴暗森林的环境光亮许多(noticeably brighter than the dim forest behind him)). ⚠️Roco的脸越接近Lulu越被光直接照亮(the closer Roco gets to Lulu, the more brightly his face is lit by the direct cold light).

⚠️⚠️⚠️POSITION & DISTANCE MANDATE: 圆形空地直径~14米, ⚠️Lulu始终严格在空地中心不动. ⚠️Roco沿⚠️南-北直线(strictly along straight S→N line)向Lulu走近. ⚠️镜头1开场: Roco距Lulu约5.5米. ⚠️镜头3 Roco沿同一直线再走一步, ⚠️距Lulu严格约3米. ⚠️镜头4结束: 距离~3米.

【镜头1】 ⚠️0.5秒 — 开场WS低角度, 12mm wide, 呼吸式平滑(breathing smooth), 阴面. ⚠️构图同时显示Roco(右下, 在花海中, ⚠️距Lulu约5.5米)和Lulu(左深处, 严格在空地正中心, 面朝Roco, 双手放腹部, contre-jour cold rim light). 浓雾环绕空地外围, 持续轻雪.
ROCO(soft, almost subaudible): "hm"
0.5秒后hard cut.

【镜头2】 3秒 — CU Lulu, 85mm, F2.0, 呼吸式平滑(breathing smooth). ⚠️Lulu⚠️缓慢抬眼看向Roco(slowly raises eyes to Roco), ⚠️带着悲伤的微笑(sad smile). ⚠️距Roco~5.5米.
LULU(温柔但带悲伤, slight reverb): "You're late."
⚠️说"late"时眼神⚠️空了一秒 — 然后恢复温柔.

【镜头3】 5秒 — CU Roco, 85mm, F2.0, 呼吸式平滑(breathing smooth). ⚠️Roco⚠️再走一步靠近Lulu(takes another step closer toward Lulu, ⚠️他现在距Lulu严格约3米/now exactly ~3m from Lulu). Lulu画外说话:
LULU(off-screen, 温柔knowing, slight reverb): "You said 'after.' Remember? On the roof. You said we will think about our future."
说话期间Roco的脸上⚠️一颗眼泪⚠️开始从眼睛流下(a single real tear starts flowing from his eye from emotional pain, slow real tear), 表情充满深深的悲伤+负罪感(deep sadness + guilt).

【镜头4】 4秒 — MS Roco, 35mm/85mm, F2.0, 呼吸式平滑(breathing smooth). ⚠️距Lulu~3米. ⚠️⚠️⚠️Roco⚠️双膝跪倒在花海中(falls to his knees onto the flower carpet, knees pressing into the pale-blue flowers, full body weight collapsing down) — ⚠️他的脸⚠️充满深深的痛苦和折磨(face filled with deep pain and suffering, mouth twisted in anguish, eyebrows pulled together, tears streaming down his face). ⚠️Roco低下眼睛(lowers his gaze guiltily, looking down at the flowers in front of his knees), 充满负罪感.
ROCO(几乎破碎, 充满巨大的内心痛苦+灵魂折磨, in English): "I'm so sorry"
⚠️说"sorry"时他的声音⚠️充满巨大的灵魂痛苦(voice filled with enormous emotional pain and soul-deep suffering, breaking with grief).

SFX only. No music, no subtitles.
