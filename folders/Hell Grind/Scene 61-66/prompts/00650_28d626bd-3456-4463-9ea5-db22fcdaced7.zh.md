@image2 (Aokigahara Forest · pathless dense) — pathless dense Japanese forest: tall cedars + black bamboo + twisted exposed tree roots + moss-covered rocks + wine-red maple leaves + sparse snow + dark wet soil. Heavy fog haze, visibility 15m max.

风格 / Style: 8K IMAX. Hyperreal but heavily OPTICALLY DISTORTED via vintage character lens. Lubezki × Deakins. Natural light only (diffuse, fog-filtered, weak). Heavy fog haze. Color: 60:30:10 — cold blue-grey fog / desaturated dark green / subtle red-green chromatic fringing on edges from optical aberration. 60fps, 8K. Audio: Environmental SFX only — distant muffled CRYSTALLINE RINGING (diffused, no clear direction, dampened by fog) + faint wind in branches + occasional creaking of trunks + low subtle heartbeat thump (~60-80 BPM, almost subliminal). No music. No subtitles.

【SNOWFALL · LIGHT】 Continuous LIGHT snowfall throughout · small sparse snowflakes drifting through the fog · clearly visible falling through the haze · disturbed by gentle wind · landing on ground/rocks/moss/roots · some melting on surfaces.

⚠️ THIS VIDEO IS 1 CONTINUOUS SHOT, 15 seconds. NO CHARACTER IN FRAME.

🎯 LENS / OPTICAL AESTHETIC — STRICT REFERENCE TARGET (matching reference images uploaded for 62.2):
The camera/optics combine THREE distinct vintage-creative-lens characteristics simultaneously. This is NOT a clean modern lens; it is a heavily-charactered organic distortion vortex:

(1) ⚠️ HELIOS 44-2 / PETZVAL SWIRL BOKEH (per reference image 3 — tree with swirling bokeh balls):
- Strong PETZVAL-STYLE / HELIOS-STYLE swirling bokeh in the out-of-focus areas;
- Background out-of-focus highlights render as soap-bubble / cat-eye / oval bokeh balls that visibly SWIRL CONCENTRICALLY around the optical center;
- Swirl most pronounced at frame edges/corners, calmer near optical center;
- Painterly, dreamy, dimensional bokeh — NOT modern clinical.

(2) ⚠️ RADIAL ZOOM-BLUR / VORTEX SMEAR (per reference images 2 & 4):
- World streaks/smears RADIALLY outward from center of frame toward edges, as if everything is being pulled into a vortex centered on frame center;
- Trees, foliage, fog wisps render as long radial smears at frame edges, progressively sharper toward optical center;
- The center of frame is the still eye of the optical storm; everything around it swirls/streaks/vortexes.

(3) ⚠️ HITCHCOCK VERTIGO DOLLY-ZOOM (per reference image 1):
- Classic dolly-in + zoom-out vertigo effect operating throughout — physical space appears to STRETCH AND CURVE;
- Foreground trunks stay similar size in frame while background stretches and warps;
- Walls of trees on left/right curve outward at edges (barrel-like distortion).

Combined effect: the forest looks like it's being SUCKED INTO A VORTEX centered on the optical axis — trees curving and stretching, fog spiraling, perspective warping, bokeh swirling concentrically. The forest LOOKS ALIVE and BREATHING.

Lens approximation: "Helios 44-2 58mm modified" / "Petzval swirl-bokeh lens with Hitchcock dolly-zoom" / "Lensbaby Twist 60 with anamorphic vertigo combo". F2.0 for shallow DOF maximizing swirl bokeh.

Camera: starts at chest-height looking into the foggy forest depth (cedars, roots, moss-rocks, wine-red maple foliage, drifting snow, heavy fog). Slow physical DOLLY FORWARD into the forest throughout the 15 seconds, synchronized with ZOOM-OUT to produce vertigo effect — foreground subjects stay similar size while background stretches/warps.

Action (15s):

[0-3s · ESTABLISHING] Calm misty forest view. Petzval swirl bokeh visible in out-of-focus background highlights and fog patches, gentle radial smear at edges, very subtle vertigo creep beginning. Light snow drifting.

[3-9s · VORTEX INTENSIFIES] Dolly-zoom builds: trees at frame edges curve outward and stretch radially; bokeh balls swirl harder concentrically around the optical center; radial smears at edges lengthen; chromatic aberration on tree-edges intensifies (red on one side, green on the other, fringing visibly on high-contrast trunk edges). Subtle heartbeat thump appears in audio (~60-80 BPM); the vortex pulses with each heartbeat — swirl intensity oscillates synchronously.

[9-15s · MAXIMUM VORTEX BREATHING] Effect peaks: the forest visibly seems to ROTATE/SPIRAL around the optical center, trees curve dramatically, fog wisps radiate outward as long smears, bokeh balls form a clear swirling pattern, the heartbeat-pulse is unmistakable in the visual breathing of the swirl. Crystalline ringing weaves through audio. The forest looks ALIVE, CONSCIOUS, and breathing — ⚠️ but it's purely OPTICAL ILLUSION (the trees don't actually move diegetically; only the lens + camera move warps the image). HARD CUT.

⚠️ ALSO PRESENT — additional aberration layers throughout:
- Anamorphic-style horizontal lens flares streaking across high-contrast edges;
- Subtle micro-shimmer / heat-haze-like ripple across mid-frame as if air itself is unstable;
- Soft corner-blur and slight focus shimmer at frame periphery.

15秒。21:9。
