## 原文 (source)

STUDIO V.O. RECORDING SESSION · OLD LAMA voice for 62.3 (auditory hallucination heard by Roco — voice of a Tibetan lama Roco killed in the Himalayas, returning as guilt-vision).

<<<image_1>>> (Voice talent · OLD LAMA) — elderly male voice actor, 70+ years old · ideally a native Tibetan speaker (or fluent Tibetan speaker with authentic delivery) · breathy hoarse rasping voice quality (sounds dying / damaged vocal cords) · capable of slow ritual delivery in Tibetan and heavily-accented English.

<<<image_2>>> (Recording setup) — same as V.O.-6: professional voice-over booth · large condenser mic + pop filter · closed-back headphones · acoustically dead recording.

DELIVERY DIRECTION:
- Tone: HOARSE DYING WHISPER · breath-rasping · trembling vocal cords · weight of a deathbed curse + spiritual warning;
- Emotion: NOT angry, NOT pleading — somber pronouncement of judgement · ritual gravity;
- Pace: VERY SLOW · long pauses between phrases · drawn-out vowels;
- Air: heavy breath quality · audible inhale-rasps · weak voice that almost cuts out;
- Language structure: opens with a phrase in TIBETAN (e.g. ཁྱེད་རང་གིས་བདུད་ལ་ཞབས་ཏོག་བྱེད་ཀྱི་ཡོད་... or similar — translator/lama consultant should provide authentic Tibetan for "You serve the devil") · then repeats the curse in heavily-accented English: "You serve the devil...";
- Performance feel: voice of someone speaking from beyond death · the curse he laid on Roco at the moment of his death.

LINE (Tibetan first, then English):
[TIBETAN whisper · "ཁྱེད་བདུད་ལ་ཞབས་ཕྱི་བྱེད..."]
"You serve the devil..."

POST-PROCESSING NOTES:
- Apply heavy ANCIENT TEMPLE REVERB (long tail, dampened by thick stone) layered with FOG REVERB;
- Spatial position: BEHIND Roco, slightly elevated, indistinct (no clear direction);
- Pitch may be subtly bent down to enhance the otherworldly feel;
- Layer a deep low-frequency drone underneath the whisper for occult unease;
- The voice should feel BOTH like it's coming from outside (the forest) AND from inside Roco's head simultaneously.

Record 3-5 takes with delivery variations.

15秒。21:9。
