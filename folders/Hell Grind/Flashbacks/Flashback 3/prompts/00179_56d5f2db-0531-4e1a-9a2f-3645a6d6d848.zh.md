## 原文 (source)

Change the character's outfit to a uniform-like ensemble while preserving face, hair, body proportions, pose, and lighting exactly. Dress the character in: a faded dusty pale blue short-sleeve cotton button-up shirt, simple collar, buttoned up to the second button from the top (top button undone), sleeves not rolled, well-fitted but soft, worn-in fabric that has clearly been washed many times. Pair with straight-leg cotton trousers in faded khaki / muted greige color, simple flat front, no visible pockets, slightly loose but clean silhouette, ankle length. Replace shoes with bare feet — completely barefoot, no socks. No accessories, no jewelry. Keep the same studio grey background, same neutral soft lighting, same pose, same expression. Photorealistic, timeless aesthetic reminiscent of Wes Anderson or Lindsay Anderson's "If....", institutional clothing but elegant. Full body front view and back view, same composition as original.
