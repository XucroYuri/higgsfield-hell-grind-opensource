## 原文 (source)

Change the character's outfit to a stylized uniform while preserving face, hair, body proportions, pose, and lighting exactly. Dress the character in: an oversized boxy-cut chambray work shirt in faded washed-out indigo blue color (heavily washed, soft, almost dusty grey-blue, with subtle natural fading on the folds), band collar / mandarin collar style (no traditional office collar), two large square patch pockets on the chest, wooden or bone buttons in ivory color, long sleeves with single-button cuffs at the wrist, sleeves fully extended down to wrists. The shirt is buttoned all the way up and tucked into the pants — clean, composed look. Pair with wide-leg straight high-waisted trousers in slightly darker faded indigo cotton, soft fabric, ankle-length cropped showing bare ankles, simple cloth tie belt or drawstring waist (no leather belt, no metal buckle), side seam pockets only. Bare feet, no shoes, no socks. No accessories. Keep the same studio grey background, same neutral soft lighting, same pose, same expression. Photorealistic, aesthetic reference: Japanese workwear (Visvim, Kapital), French chore jacket, faded boro textile, Aki Kaurismäki film palette. Full body front view and back view, same composition as original. The clothes should look heavily washed and worn-in, not new.
