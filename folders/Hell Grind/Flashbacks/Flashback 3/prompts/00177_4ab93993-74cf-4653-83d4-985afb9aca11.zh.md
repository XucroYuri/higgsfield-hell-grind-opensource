## 原文 (source)

Change the character's outfit to a stylized uniform while preserving face, hair, body proportions, pose, and lighting exactly. Dress the character in: the same oversized boxy-cut chambray work shirt in faded washed-out indigo blue (heavily washed, soft, dusty grey-blue tone), band collar style, two large square chest pockets, ivory wooden buttons, long sleeves with cuffs — but worn completely unbuttoned and open, sleeves rolled up messily above the elbows, revealing a simple plain white cotton tank top underneath. The shirt hangs open and is slightly slipping off one shoulder. Pair with wide-leg straight high-waisted trousers in slightly darker faded indigo cotton, ankle-length, cloth tie belt, sitting low on the hips rather than at the waist. Bare feet, no shoes, no socks. A single thin black string bracelet on one wrist. The bob hair slightly imperfect, a few strands tucked behind one ear, light asymmetry. Keep the same studio grey background, same neutral soft lighting, same pose, same expression. Photorealistic, aesthetic reference: Japanese workwear (Visvim, Kapital), boro textile, melancholic European cinema. Full body front view and back view, same composition as original. The clothes should look heavily washed and lived-in, not new.
