## 原文 (source)

Single continuous shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, 
50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of Terrence Malick's 
"The Tree of Life" — intimate connection dissolving into vast nature.

[CAMERA]
Begins as close handheld medium shot — their two heads filling the 
frame. Then SLOWLY, smoothly DOLLIES BACKWARDS through the tall grass, 
gradually pulling away from them. The motion is slow, deliberate, 
almost dreamlike — the camera receding while they stay still. As 
the camera retreats, more of the surrounding field, tree, sky, and 
distant Hong Kong skyline come into frame. The two figures get 
smaller in the center of frame, becoming small in the vast landscape.

The handheld is alive but the dolly-back is smooth and continuous — 
like the camera is being slowly pulled away by a gentle invisible 
force.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, warm low sun catching their hair and 
shoulders in rim light, dappled tree shadows on them and the grass, 
dust and seed particles floating in beams, lens flares from the low 
sun.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Tight close-up: Lulu and Roco standing in the grass, foreheads 
       pressed together, eyes closed, breathing slowing after running. 
       Lulu's hands rest on his chest. Roco's hands cup her elbows 
       gently. They are completely still, breathing each other in.

1.0s — Camera begins slow dolly back. They remain still, foreheads 
       touching. We see more of their shoulders, the tall grass 
       around them.

2.5s — Camera continues retreating. We see them now from a few meters 
       back — two small figures standing forehead-to-forehead in the 
       middle of the golden grass field, the large old tree to one 
       side, the city skyline far in the distance behind them.

4.0s — Camera continues to pull back. They are even smaller now, 
       silhouetted against the vast golden field and the soft hazy 
       skyline of distant white-and-red Hong Kong towers. Two small 
       figures, one shape, in the middle of the world.

5.0s — Frame ends with them small in the vast landscape, still 
       forehead to forehead, the world enormous around them.

[CHARACTERS — both 15]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length hair messy from running, pale skin 
  flushed pink, oversized blue-and-white vertical striped button-up 
  shirt over white t-shirt, white midi skirt, white sandals, thin 
  silver chain bracelets.
- ROCO: messy shaggy black hair, smooth cheeks (NO mustache, NO 
  stubble — too young), olive skin flushed, dark gray cotton zip-up 
  hoodie open over light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants with 
  red contrast stitching, dark Vans-style sneakers. Small red 
  adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose. No tattoos, 
  no jewelry, no chains.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Their slow synchronized breathing audible at the start, 
gradually getting quieter as the camera retreats. Gentle wind through 
grass becoming louder as the world opens up around them. Distant 
birds, faint distant city hum, the soft rustle of grass.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no kissing, no slow motion, no fast camera movement, no 
shaky camera, no zoom, no aerial drone shot, no tilt up to sky, no 
other people in field, no facial hair on Roco, no mustache, no 
stubble, no tattoos, no jewelry, no chains, no choker, no makeup, 
no removal of red nose bandage, no rapid focus changes, no broad 
smiling, no laughing, no talking, no dialogue, no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，50mm 镜头，浅景深，风格参照泰伦斯·马力克（Terrence Malick）的《生命之树》（The Tree of Life）——亲密联结消融于辽阔自然。

[CAMERA]
以贴近手持的中景开始——两颗头填满画面。随后缓慢、平滑地穿过高草向后推轨（dolly），逐渐远离他们。动作缓慢、刻意、近乎如梦似幻——镜头后撤而他们保持静止。随着镜头后退，更多的四周田野、树、天空与远处香港天际线入画。两个人影在画面中央变小，成为辽阔风景中的小小身影。

手持是有生命力的，但推轨后拉平滑而连续——仿佛镜头被一股轻柔无形的力量缓缓拖离。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，温暖低角度阳光以轮廓光勾勒发丝与肩膀，斑驳树影洒在他们与草丛上，光束中漂浮尘埃与种子颗粒，低角度阳光产生镜头耀斑。

[ACTION]
0.0s——紧特写：露露与罗科站在草丛中，额头相抵、双目紧闭，奔跑后的呼吸渐缓。露露的手搭在他胸口。罗科的手轻柔捧着她的肘部。他们完全静止，互相呼吸着彼此。

1.0s——镜头开始缓慢后拉。他们保持静止，额头相触。我们看到更多他们的肩膀、四周的高草。

2.5s——镜头持续后退。现在我们从几米外看他们——两个小小人影额头相对站在金色草丛中央，大树在一旁，城市天际线远远在其后。

4.0s——镜头持续后拉。他们现在更小了，在辽阔的金色田野与远处红白香港塔楼柔和的朦胧天际线前化作剪影。两个小身影，一个整体，立于世界中央。

5.0s——镜头以他们在辽阔风景中的渺小身影收尾，仍额头相对，环绕他们的世界无比辽阔。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 15 岁]
- 露露：奔跑后凌乱的深褐色齐肩发，苍白皮肤泛粉，宽大的蓝白竖条纹系扣衬衫内搭白 T 恤，白色中长裙，白色凉鞋，纤细银链手链。
- 罗科：凌乱蓬松的黑发，光洁的脸颊（绝对无胡须、无胡茬——太年轻），泛粉的橄榄色皮肤，深灰色棉质拉链连帽卫衣敞开内搭浅灰 T 恤，黑色工装裤带红色撞色缝线，深色 Vans 风格运动鞋。鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。开始时可闻他们缓慢同步的呼吸，随镜头后退逐渐变轻。随着世界在他们四周展开而渐强的穿过草丛的轻风、远处鸟鸣、轻微远处都市嗡鸣、草丛轻柔窸窣。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无接吻、无慢动作、无快速镜头运动、无抖动镜头、无变焦、无航拍无人机、无向天空俯仰、田野中无其他人、罗科无面部毛发、无胡须、无胡茬、无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰、无项圈、无妆容、不得移除红色鼻贴、无快速焦点变化、无大笑、无笑、无说话、无对白、无文字。
