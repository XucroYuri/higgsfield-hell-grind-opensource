## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the style 
of Wong Kar-wai's "Fallen Angels".

Active handheld camera at chest level, the operator running ahead of 
them facing back toward them. Lulu and Roco SPRINT around a corner 
into the frame, hand in hand, breathless laughter, hair flying. Wet 
asphalt under their feet, neon signs around them — pink, red, electric 
blue. They almost crash into the camera, swerve, keep running past 
toward the lens. Camera pans hard to follow, briefly loses them, 
catches them again from behind running away into the alley.

Lulu, 17: dark brown shoulder-length hair with side-swept fringe, 
pale skin, light wash denim jacket covered in vintage embroidered 
patches (aviation, retro badges) over a white cropped t-shirt, thin 
black velvet choker, blue cargo shorts, blue high-top canvas sneakers, 
thin silver chain bracelets.
Roco, 17: messy shaggy black hair, faint mustache forming, olive skin, 
burgundy and cream check pattern short-sleeve shirt open over a white 
t-shirt, gold chain necklaces with crosses, dark gray denim shorts, 
white socks, tan Timberland boots, faint tattoos on forearms, small 
red adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose.

NO music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no other people, no rapid 
focus changes that look mechanical, no removal of red nose bandage, 
no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续手持特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，50mm 镜头，浅景深。

手持镜头与他们在同一高度，侧面角度。他们靠着树坐在草地上，都还喘着奔跑的气。露露突然用肩膀猛地撞了罗科一下。他晃了晃，笑了，回撞她一下。她撞得更狠，几乎把他撞得失去平衡。他一把揽住她的肩膀，短暂地把她带进一个玩笑式的锁喉，两人都笑着，随即放开她。他们肩靠着肩重新靠回树干，呼吸渐缓，笑容渐渐沉入静默。

两人皆 15 岁。着装同前。身后树干、野花、背景虚化中的远处城市。斑驳金光穿过树叶。

无音乐、无慢动作、无接吻、无平滑稳定器、无其他人、罗科无面部毛发、无文字。
