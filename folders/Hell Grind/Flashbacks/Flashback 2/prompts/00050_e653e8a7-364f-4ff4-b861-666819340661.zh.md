## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of 
playful intimate moments.

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld, close to them on the sofa, operator following 
the wrestling motion, slight bounces, alive. Frame: medium close-up 
of both their faces close together, sofa cushions and warm interior 
in soft bokeh.

[LIGHT]
Same warm golden hour through curtains, dust particles, deep red wall 
behind them softly out of focus.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Roco grabs Lulu's wrists firmly but playfully, both still 
       laughing hard.
1.5s — He flips them — Lulu shrieks with laughter as he rolls her onto 
       her back, now he's leaning over her, pinning her wrists above 
       her head. Both gasping for air through laughter.
2.5s — Lulu, breathless and laughing, kicks her legs, tries to free 
       herself. "Get off, idiot!" she yells through laughter.
3.5s — Roco grins down at her, totally relaxed, also laughing — "Say 
       sorry first." She laughs harder, "Never!"
5.0s — Frame cuts mid-laugh, both still struggling playfully.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Their breathless laughter, her shriek, sofa creaking under 
their weight, his playful "Say sorry first," her "Never!", their 
gasping breaths.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no posed shots, no fake 
laughter, no kissing, no sexual content, no other people, no full 
body wide shot, no removal of red nose bandage, no rapid focus changes, 
no text.

[LIGHT]
同样温暖的黄金时刻穿过窗帘，尘埃颗粒，身后深红墙面柔和失焦。

[ACTION]

1.5s——他翻转了他们——露露笑着尖叫起来，他把她滚到她背上，此刻他俯身压在她上方，把她的手腕按在她头顶上方。两人隔着笑声上气不接下气。
2.5s——露露，喘着气、笑着，踢着腿想挣脱。“滚开啦，笨蛋！”她隔着笑声喊。
3.5s——罗科朝她咧嘴而笑，完全放松，也在笑——“先说对不起。”她笑得更厉害，“才不！”
5.0s——镜头切在笑声中途，两人都还在俏皮地挣扎。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。他们气喘吁吁的笑声、她的尖叫、沙发在重量下吱呀、他俏皮的“先说对不起”、她的“才不要！”、他们喘息的呼吸。
