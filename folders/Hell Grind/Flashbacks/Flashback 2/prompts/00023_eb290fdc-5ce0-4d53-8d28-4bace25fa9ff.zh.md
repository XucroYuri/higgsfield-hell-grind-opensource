## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld extreme close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 
2.39:1, 35mm film grain, 100mm telephoto, extremely shallow depth of 
field, in the style of intimate domestic detail shots.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, very close to the developing polaroid in 
Lulu's hand. Gentle natural breathing motion, occasionally bouncing 
slightly when she shakes the photo. Frame: extreme close-up of the 
white polaroid square filling most of the frame, held between Lulu's 
pale fingers. Edges of her burgundy striped shirt-dress visible in 
soft bokeh, warm golden hour light bathing everything.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour through gauzy curtains, warm dappled 
sunlight falling across the polaroid surface, dust particles floating 
in beams.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Close-up of the blank white polaroid square in Lulu's hand. 
       Pure white, slightly milky, just developed. Her thumb at the 
       white border edge.

1.0s — Lulu's hand SHAKES the polaroid playfully — quick flick of 
       the wrist, the photo waving back and forth in frame. The 
       camera follows the motion slightly. Roco's voice off-frame 
       laughs: "You're not supposed to shake it, that's a myth." 
       Lulu, also off-frame, laughs back: "Shut up, it works." She 
       shakes it again.

2.5s — She stops shaking. Faint shapes begin to emerge in the white 
       square — vague shadows, hints of color appearing through the 
       chemical haze. Two heads forming.

3.5s — The image becomes clearer — we can now see Lulu and Roco 
       cheek-to-cheek, faces emerging, their grins becoming visible. 
       Warm golden light from the moment captured. Lulu shakes it 
       once more for good measure.

4.5s — Image is now mostly developed — the photo shows Lulu laughing 
       with her cheek slightly damp from the lick, Roco grinning 
       wide beside her. A frozen warm moment, fully visible.

5.0s — Frame holds on the now-clear polaroid, image fully developed, 
       a perfect captured second of pure happiness in Lulu's pale 
       fingers, the photo finally still.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. The soft flick of the polaroid being shaken (paper sound 
in air), Roco's off-frame laugh and his line "You're not supposed 
to shake it, that's a myth", Lulu's off-frame laugh and "Shut up, 
it works", the soft chemical hiss of polaroid developing, the subtle 
rustle of fingers, faint sofa creak, distant ambient room tone.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no faces visible in real 
scene (only on the photo), no other people, no rapid violent shaking, 
no text, no captions, no watermarks on the polaroid, no logos, no 
extra fingers, no broken hand anatomy, no facial morphing of the 
people on the photo, no character identity drift on the photo.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续手持极特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照亲密的居家细节镜头。

[CAMERA]
轻微有机手持，极贴近露露手中正在显影的宝丽来。柔和自然的呼吸律动，她摇晃相片时偶尔轻微跳动。取景：白色宝丽来方片占据画面大部分，夹在她苍白手指之间。酒红色条纹衬衫裙边缘可见化作柔和虚化，温暖黄金时刻光线沐浴一切。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻透过薄纱窗帘，温暖斑驳的阳光洒落宝丽来表面，光束中尘埃漂浮。

[ACTION]
0.0s——露露手中空白白色宝丽来方片的特写。纯白、略带乳白、刚刚吐出。拇指抵着白色边框边缘。

1.0s——露露的手俏皮地摇晃宝丽来——手腕迅速一弹，相片在画面中来回摆动。镜头轻微追随这一动作。罗科画外音笑：“你不该晃它，那是谣言。”露露，同样画外，笑着回：“闭嘴，这招管用。”她又晃了一次。

2.5s——她停止摇晃。白色方片中开始浮现模糊形状——隐约的阴影、穿过化学雾气透出的色彩暗示。两颗头成形。

3.5s——影像更清晰——我们现在能看到露露与罗科脸颊相贴，面孔浮出，他们的笑意变得可见。捕捉瞬间的温暖金色光线。露露为保险起见又晃了一次。

4.5s——影像如今基本显影——照片显示露露因那一舔微湿的脸颊的大笑，罗科在一旁咧嘴大张的笑容。一个定格的温暖瞬间，完整可见。

5.0s——镜头停在这张现已清晰的宝丽来上，影像完全显影，纯粹快乐定格的一瞬在她苍白指间，相片终于静止。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。摇晃宝丽来的轻柔弹动（相纸破空之声）、罗科画外笑声与那句话“你不该晃它，那是谣言”、露露画外笑声与“闭嘴，这招管用”、宝丽来显影的柔和化学嘶声、手指的细微窸窣、轻微沙发吱呀、远处环境房间氛围声。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、真实场景中无可见面部（仅照片上有）、无其他人、无快速剧烈摇晃、无文字、无字幕、宝丽来上无水印、无标志、无多余手指、无畸形手部结构、照片上人物无面部变形、照片上无角色身份漂移。
