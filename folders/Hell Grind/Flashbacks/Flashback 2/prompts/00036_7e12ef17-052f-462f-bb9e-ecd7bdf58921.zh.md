Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of 
Terrence Malick.

Handheld camera at chest height circling slowly opposite to Lulu's 
spin. Lulu stands in the tall golden grass, arms spread wide, slowly 
SPINNING with her face tilted up to the sky, eyes closed, the small 
private smile of someone alone with the world. White midi skirt 
billowing in the spin, dark hair flying. Roco visible in soft focus 
in the background, sitting on the grass under the tree, just watching 
her. Wildflowers, golden hour light, dust in beams, distant Hong Kong

white midi skirt, white sandals barely visible. Roco, 15: dark gray 
hoodie over light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants, dark Vans, no 
mustache, red bandage on nose. He is far away in the frame in soft 
bokeh.

facial hair on Roco, no posed dancing, no full body of Roco, no
