## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld shot, 14 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1 aspect 
ratio, 35mm film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic lenses, 
shallow depth of field, neon-reflective night cinematography in the 
style of Wong Kar-wai's "Fallen Angels" and "Chungking Express". 
Wet-street energy, alive and breathing.

[CAMERA]
Active handheld camera following the action — running with them, 
breathing with them, dynamic but not chaotic. Camera at chest-to-shoulder 
height, occasionally lower. Operator visibly chasing the subjects. 
Lens occasionally catches reflections, neon flares, raindrops. The 
camera is alive and present, NOT a tripod, NOT smooth gimbal — it has 
weight, breath, urgency.

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
Late night after recent rain. Wet asphalt reflecting colored neon 
signs from above — pink, blue, red, yellow. Streetlights overhead 
casting pools of orange light. Steam rising from sewer grates. Misty 
distance. Color temperature mixed: cool blues in shadows, warm 
oranges and pinks in highlights. Saturated but cinematic.

[LOCATION]
Narrow Asian megacity backstreets — Hong Kong / Tokyo aesthetic. Tight 
alleys with hanging signs in Chinese/Japanese characters, neon shop 
signs glowing, puddles everywhere reflecting the lights, occasional 
parked scooter, no other people visible. The city feels like it 
belongs only to them in this moment.

[CHARACTERS — both 17 years old, full of life]
- LULU: 17, dark brown shoulder-length hair flying behind her as she 
 runs, side-swept fringe, pale skin flushed from running, oversized 
 blue-and-white vertical striped button-up shirt over a white t-shirt, 
 flowy white midi skirt, white sandals. Bright eyes, wild grin.
- ROCO: 17, messy shaggy black hair, olive skin, charcoal-gray t-shirt 
 under an open dark gray hooded zip jacket, black carpenter pants 
 with red contrast stitching, black-and-red sneakers. Small red 
 adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose. Lean, fast.

[ACTION — choreographed energy]
0.0s: Lulu runs ahead through a wet alley, laughing, looking back over 
her shoulder at Roco — hair flying, grinning wildly, mouth open in a 
shout we cannot quite hear over the city. She turns forward, keeps 
running.

3.0s: Roco catches up, grabs her hand on the run. They sprint together 
now, hand in hand, splashing through puddles, neon reflections 
shattering under their feet.

6.0s: Lulu stumbles slightly on a wet stone. Roco catches her without 
breaking stride — they keep running, both laughing harder now.

9.0s: They burst out of the alley into a wider open space — empty wet 
plaza or waterfront promenade, distant city lights, vast open sky. 
They slow down, then stop, panting, hands on knees.

11.0s: Lulu drops backward onto the wet asphalt, arms spread wide, 
laughing up at the sky. Roco stands a moment, breathing hard, smiling 
down at her.

13.0s: Roco lies down beside her on the wet ground. They both look up 
at the sky. Lulu turns her head sideways toward him, still smiling, 
chest rising and falling.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, NO MUSIC]
No score, no instruments. Their fast running footsteps splashing 
through puddles, their breathless laughter, their gasping breaths, 
the distant low hum of the megacity, occasional faraway car horn, 
the soft hiss of mist on wet pavement. Maximum life, maximum sound.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no instruments, no melody, no score, no slow motion, no 
dissolves, no fades, no cross-fades, no smooth gimbal, no static 
camera, no other people in the alleys, no cars driving past, no rain 
falling, no sad atmosphere, no melancholy, no danger, no fear, no 
character separating, no extra limbs, no duplicate characters, no 
text, no captions, no subtitles, no children, no adults over 25, no 
removal of the red nose bandage, no facial morphing, no character 
identity drift, no stiff acting, no posed running, no looking at camera.

## 中文对照 (zh)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持镜头，14 秒，变形 2.39:1 宽高比，35mm 胶片颗粒，用 Arri Alexa 配变形镜头拍摄，浅景深，霓虹之夜反射式电影风格，参照王家卫的《堕落天使》（Fallen Angels）与《重庆森林》（Chungking Express）。湿街的张力，生动、会呼吸。

[CAMERA]
活跃手持镜头追随动作——与他们一起跑、一起呼吸，动态但不混乱。镜头在胸到肩的高度，偶尔更低。摄影师明显在追逐主体。镜头偶尔捕捉倒影、霓虹耀斑、雨滴。镜头生动且在场，不是三脚架、不是平滑稳定器——它有重量、有呼吸、有急迫感。

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
刚下过雨的深夜。湿柏油路反射上方彩色霓虹招牌——粉红、蓝、红、黄。上方街灯投下橙色光圈。下水道井盖升起蒸汽。雾蒙蒙的远方。混合色温：阴影中清冷蓝，高光中温暖橙与粉。饱和但电影感。

[LOCATION]
狭窄的亚洲特大城市背巷——香港 / 东京美学。挂满中文 / 日文招牌的狭窄小巷、发光的霓虹店面招牌、到处是反射灯光的水洼、偶尔停靠的踏板车、没有其他人可见。此刻这座城市好似只属于他们。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 17 岁，充满生命力]
- 露露：17 岁，奔跑时深褐色齐肩发在身后飞舞，侧分刘海，奔跑后泛红的苍白皮肤，白 T 恤上宽大的蓝白竖条纹系扣衬衫，飘逸的白色中长裙，白色凉鞋。明亮的眼睛、狂放的咧嘴笑。
- 罗科：17 岁，凌乱蓬松的黑发，橄榄色皮肤，敞开深灰带帽拉链夹克下的炭灰色 T 恤，红色撞色缝线黑色工装裤，红黑运动鞋。鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。精瘦、迅捷。

[ACTION — 编排的张力]
0.0s：露露领先跑过一条湿巷，笑着，回头越过肩头望向罗科——头发飞舞、狂放咧嘴笑、嘴因一声我们隔着城市听不大清的喊叫而张开。她转回前方，继续跑。

3.0s：罗科追上，奔跑中抓住她的手。他们现在手牵手冲刺，踩过水洼溅水，霓虹倒影在他们脚下碎裂。

6.0s：露露在一块湿石头上轻微踉跄。罗科未乱步伐地将她扶住——他们继续跑，两人现在都笑得更欢。

9.0s：他们冲出小巷，进入一片更开阔的空间——空荡的湿广场或滨水大道，远处城市灯光、辽阔空旷的天空。他们放慢，然后停下，喘着气，双手撑着膝盖。

11.0s：露露向后仰倒到湿柏油路上，双臂大张，朝天空笑着。罗科站了片刻，喘着气，低头朝她微笑。

13.0s：罗科在她身旁的湿地上躺下。两人都望向天空。露露侧过头朝他，仍微笑着，胸口起伏。

[SOUND — 仅同期声，无音乐]
无配乐、无乐器。他们踩过水洼的快速奔跑脚步声、他们喘不过气的笑声、他们大口喘气、特大城市的远处低沉嗡鸣、偶尔遥远一声汽车喇叭、雾在湿路面上轻柔嘶嘶。最大化的生命力、最大化的声音。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无乐器、无旋律、无配乐、无慢动作、无溶接、无淡入淡出、无交叉淡化、无平滑稳定器、无静态镜头、小巷中无其他人、无驶过的汽车、无落雨、无悲伤氛围、无惆怅、无危险、无恐惧、无角色分离、无多余肢体、无重复角色、无文字、无字幕、无儿童、无超过 25 岁的成人、不得移除红色鼻贴、无面部变形、无角色身份漂移、无僵硬的表演、无刻意的奔跑摆拍、不得看向镜头。
