[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous static-but-breathing close-up shot, 5 seconds, 
anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic 
lenses, shallow depth of field with neon bokeh at edges, cinematography 
in the style of Wong Kar-wai's quiet aftermath moments.

[CAMERA]
Camera positioned directly above Lulu's face, top-down angle, very 
close — her face fills most of the frame. Subtle organic handheld 
breathing, like someone leaning over her. Almost no movement, but 
ALIVE — the operator is breathing, present. Frame: extreme close-up 
of Lulu's face, hair fanned out beneath her on wet asphalt, neon 
reflections in puddles framing her head from below.

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
Late night Asian megacity, after-the-run quiet. Lulu lies on her 
back on wet asphalt of an open promenade or empty plaza. Above her 
(above the camera's POV) — sky and distant city glow. Beneath her 
head — wet pavement reflecting scattered neon points from distant 
signs. Anamorphic lens flares from a single distant streetlamp catch 
in the corner of frame. Mist in the air.

[CHARACTER — Lulu, 17, in aftermath]
Lulu lying flat on her back on wet pavement, head turned slightly to 
one side. Dark brown shoulder-length hair fanned out on the wet 
asphalt around her head, wet strands sticking to her temples and 
cheeks. Pale skin flushed deep pink from running, sheen of sweat and 
mist on her forehead. Mouth slightly open, breathing hard, chest 
rising and falling visibly. Eyes open, looking up at the sky, 
unfocused, exhausted-happy. The denim jacket with patches splayed 
open beneath her, white cropped t-shirt visible, thin black velvet 
choker at her throat. Tiny droplets of water on her face from the 
mist.

[ACTION]
0.0s: Lulu's face in close-up, breathing hard, eyes looking up at 
the sky (past the camera). She is exhausted, alive, a faint smile 
ghosting on her lips.
2.0s: She blinks slowly, turns her head a fraction to the side — 
toward where Roco is lying beside her (off-frame). Her smile grows 
slightly.
3.5s: Roco's hand enters the frame from the side — his hand lowering 
onto the wet asphalt beside her head, palm up, gold chain bracelet 
at his wrist catching a glint of neon. We do NOT see his face.
4.0s: Lulu's hand enters the frame from the other side, slowly, and 
her fingers find his palm. They lace fingers on the wet pavement 
beside her head. Her smile deepens. She closes her eyes for a second, 
then opens them looking up at the sky again.
5.0s: Final frame — both hands joined on wet asphalt beside her face, 
her looking up at the sky, soft smile, deep breath.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music, no instruments. Her heavy breathing, his off-frame breathing 
beside her, distant city hum, very far car horn, the soft drip of 
water somewhere, mist hissing on pavement. The sound of being alive 
after running.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no instruments, no slow motion, no static dead 
camera, no smooth gimbal, no stabilization, no full body shot, no 
wide shot, no other people, no cars driving past, no rain falling, 
no melancholy, no fear, no death, no unconsciousness, no closing eyes 
fully for too long, no extra limbs, no facial morphing, no character 
identity drift, no Roco's face visible, no children, no adults over 
25, no text, no captions, no sharp focus on background.

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]

[CAMERA]
镜头直直置于露露脸部正上方，俯拍角度，非常近——她的脸填满画面大部分。轻微有机手持的呼吸，如同有人俯身看着她。几乎无运动，但生动——摄影师在呼吸、在场。取景：露露脸的极特写，头发在她四周铺散到湿柏油路上，水洼里的霓虹倒影从下方框住她的头。

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
亚洲特大城市的深夜，奔跑终了的静谧。露露仰躺在开放人行道或空荡广场的湿柏油路上。她上方（镜头视角上方）——天空与远处城市辉光。她脑后——反射远处招牌霓虹散点的湿路面。单一远处街灯在画面角落捕捉到变形镜头耀斑。空气中有雾气。

[CHARACTER — 露露，17 岁，奔跑后的余韵]
露露平躺在湿路面上，头微微偏向一侧。深褐色齐肩发在她头四周铺散到湿柏油路上，湿发贴在她太阳穴与脸颊。奔跑而深红的苍白皮肤，额上汗与雾光。嘴唇微张、大口呼吸，胸口可见地起伏。睁眼望向天空，失焦，疲惫而快乐。带补丁的牛仔夹克铺张在她身下，白色露脐 T 恤可见，喉间纤细黑色天鹅绒项圈。雾在她脸上凝成细小的水珠。

[ACTION]
0.0s：露露的脸特写，大口呼吸，望向天空的眼睛（越过镜头）。她疲惫、有生命力，一抹浅淡微笑挂在唇上。
2.0s：她缓缓眨了下眼，把头微微偏向一侧——转向躺在她身旁的罗科（画外）。她的微笑稍稍加深。
3.5s：罗科的手从侧面入画——他的手落在她头旁的湿柏油路上，掌心朝上，腕上金链手链捕捉到一丝霓虹微光。我们看不到他的脸。
4.0s：露露的手从另一侧缓缓入画，她的手指找到他的掌心。他们在她头旁湿漉漉的路面上交扣手指。她的微笑加深。她闭了一秒眼，又睁开望向天空。
5.0s：最终镜头——两双手在她脸旁湿柏油路上交握，她望向天空，浅淡微笑，深深的呼吸。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐、无乐器。她沉重的呼吸、他画外的呼吸在她身旁、远处都市嗡鸣、非常远的一声汽车喇叭、某处轻柔滴水、雾气在路面上嘶嘶作响。奔跑之后活着的声响。
