[ABSOLUTE TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS — HIGHEST PRIORITY]
This is a single continuous one-take shot. 15 seconds. No edits visible. 
No dissolves, no fades, no cross-fades, no flashes, no whooshes, no 
motion blur transitions, no light bursts, no morphing. The two match

shows the previous chapter, the very next frame shows the new chapter, 
with zero blending between them. The cut is invisible only because 
the camera is directly behind the figures and only their backs are 
visible at that exact moment, so silhouettes match across the frame 
boundary.

[CAMERA GEOMETRY — LOCKED FOR ALL 15 SECONDS]
- Distance from camera to embracing figures: CONSTANT. Never moves 
  closer, never moves farther. Same focal length, same focus distance, 
  same framing size from 0.0s to 15.0s.
- Rotation speed: CONSTANT 24 degrees per second, perfectly even. 
  Three full 360° rotations in 15 seconds. No acceleration, no 
  deceleration, no pauses, no slowdown at the end. The camera rotates 
  with mechanical precision, like a turntable.
- Height: CONSTANT eye-level. Never tilts up or down.
- Angle to subjects: CONSTANT. Camera is always horizontal, always 
  pointed at the embracing pair at the same vertical angle.
- Focal length: CONSTANT. No zoom in, no zoom out, no lens breathing.
- The camera behaves like it is mounted on a perfectly leveled 
  motorized rotating rig at fixed radius around the subjects.

[FRAMING — IDENTICAL ACROSS ALL THREE CHAPTERS]
Anamorphic 2.39:1. Intimate medium close-up: figures from chest up, 
centered in frame, occupying the same proportion of the frame in 
every single second of the 15-second take. 35mm film grain. Arri 
Alexa with anamorphic lenses. Shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh.

[CONSTANTS — NEVER CHANGE ACROSS CHAPTERS]

  closed. Roco's right hand cradles the back of Lulu's head, fingers 
  in her hair. Left arm wraps her back.

  same head positions across all three chapters.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, NO MUSIC]
Only natural environmental sound from each location. No score, no 
instruments, no melody, no transition sounds. Slow synchronized 
breathing audible throughout.
- Chapter 1: institutional silence, distant wind, dry leaves on concrete.
- Chapter 2: open rooftop wind, distant city hum, occasional metallic 
  creak from the antenna tower.

At each match cut: ambient acoustic space changes instantly with the 
visual cut. No audio crossfade, no sting, no whoosh.

[CAMERA GEOMETRY — LOCKED FOR ALL 15 SECONDS]
[镜头几何——整个 15 秒锁定]

- 焦距：恒定。无推进、无拉远、无镜头呼吸。
- 镜头的表现如同一台围绕主体以固定半径、装配在完全调平的电动旋转架上。
