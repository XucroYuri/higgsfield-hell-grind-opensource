## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic 100mm telephoto 
lens, extremely shallow depth of field (only 1-2 cm in focus), 
cinematography in the style of Wong Kar-wai's playful intimate moments 
and Christopher Doyle's handheld telephoto work.

[CRITICAL CAMERA BEHAVIOR]
Lulu and Roco are lying TOGETHER on their backs on wet asphalt, 
side by side, after running. Both physically present, lying close 
enough that their shoulders almost touch. BUT because the camera 
operator is using a long telephoto lens with extremely shallow depth 
of field, only ONE face is sharp at any given moment. The other 
character is heavily out of focus — a blurred shape of color and 
hair beside the in-focus one. The handheld operator hunts focus, 
racking between them as they interact, sometimes losing focus 
entirely for a fraction of a second when motion is too fast. They 
are clearly together, playing, touching, but the camera language 
visually separates them moment to moment.

[CAMERA POSITION]
Camera leaning over them from above-side angle, very close, slightly 
tilted toward whoever is in focus. Active organic handheld breathing — 
the operator is leaning over them, present, reacting to their 
movements. Subtle bounces and shifts as the operator follows the 
play. Frame: tight close-up of one face filling most of the frame, 
the OTHER character a soft blur of color in the corner or edge of 
frame.

[ACTION OVER 5 SECONDS]
0.0s — focus on LULU's face, eyes open, breathing hard, laughing 
       softly between gasps for air. Roco a blur of dark hair and 
       burgundy fabric in the lower edge of frame, also laughing, 
       his shoulder almost touching hers.

1.0s — Lulu turns her head sharply toward Roco, still laughing. 
       Quickly dips her hand into a small puddle of water on the 
       asphalt beside her — and FLICKS the cold water at his face. 
       Droplets fly across the frame. Focus tries to follow the 
       water mid-air, briefly catches the splash.

2.0s — Focus RACKS to ROCO's face, sharp on him — he flinches from 
       the water, eyes squeezed shut for an instant, then bursts 
       into a loud laugh. Water droplets on his cheek and in his 
       eyelashes catching neon reflections. He shouts something 
       playful — "Oi! Lulu, you idiot!" — fully laughing.

3.0s — Roco rolls slightly toward her, reaches out, his hand enters 
       the frame as a blur, GRABS her wrist mid-laugh. Focus loses 
       both for a beat as motion happens.

3.5s — Focus catches LULU again, sharp on her — she is laughing 
       hysterically now, eyes squeezed shut from laughing, her wrist 
       in his grip but she's not fighting, just laughing. Roco a 
       warm blur of motion and burgundy beside her.

4.5s — Their laughter softens slightly. Lulu opens her eyes, still 
       smiling, and turns her face toward him (still off-focus 
       beside her). The camera doesn't rack — Roco stays a soft 
       blur of warmth in frame. She is looking at him, smiling, 
       wet hair across her cheek. Her hand still in his grip on the 
       wet pavement, fingers now lacing into his.

5.0s — Frame cuts mid-moment, her looking at him with a smile, 
       still half-laughing, hands joined.

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
Late night Hong-Kong-style city, after-the-run, on an open promenade 
or empty plaza. Above them — sky and distant city glow visible in 
out-of-focus background. Wet asphalt beneath them reflecting 
scattered points of distant neon. Anamorphic horizontal lens flares 
from a distant streetlamp catching at the edge of frame. Mist in 
the air catching colored light. Warm distant city glow contrasting 
with cool sky. The water Lulu flicks catches a streak of red neon 
mid-flight.

[CHARACTERS — both 17 years old, lying side by side, playing]
- LULU: 17, dark brown shoulder-length hair wet and fanned out on 
  the asphalt around her, wet strands sticking to her temples and 
  cheeks, pale skin flushed deep pink from running and laughing, 
  sheen of sweat on her forehead, eyes alive and crinkled with 
  laughter, mouth open in laughter. Light wash denim jacket with 
  vintage embroidered patches splayed open beneath her, white 
  cropped t-shirt, thin black velvet choker.
- ROCO: 17, messy black hair wet on the asphalt, faint mustache, 
  olive skin flushed, eyes crinkled with laughter, mouth open 
  laughing loudly. Burgundy and cream check pattern short-sleeve 
  shirt open over white t-shirt, gold chain necklaces with crosses 
  glinting at his throat, small red adhesive bandage strip across 
  the bridge of his nose, faint tattoos on forearms.

They lie close — shoulders almost touching, heads at the same level, 
hands meeting between them on the wet ground.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music, no instruments. Both of them laughing — Lulu's higher 
giggling laugh, Roco's lower chest laugh. Water flicking through 
air, splash droplets. Roco's playful shout "Oi! Lulu, you idiot!" 
spoken through his laughter (or in another language, naturally 
intimate). Heavy breathing between laughs. Wet asphalt sounds as 
they shift slightly. Distant city hum, far car horn. The sound of 
two people fully alive and in love with being alive together.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no instruments, no slow motion, no static dead 
camera, no smooth gimbal, no stabilization, no deep depth of field, 
no both faces sharp at the same time, no melancholy, no sadness, no 
silence, no still motionless poses, no looking up at sky, no posed 
beauty, no fear, no death, no unconsciousness, no closing eyes for 
too long, no full body shot, no wide shot, no other people, no cars 
driving past, no rain falling, no extra limbs, no facial morphing, 
no character identity drift, no characters separated by distance, 
no children, no adults over 25, no text, no captions, no sharp focus 
on background, no fake laughter, no posed smiling, no kissing, no 
romance signals, no characters letting go of each other.

## 中文对照 (zh)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，用 Arri Alexa 与变形 100mm 长焦镜头拍摄，极浅景深（仅 1-2 厘米合焦），摄影风格参照王家卫俏皮亲密的时刻与杜可风（Christopher Doyle）的手持长焦作品。

[CRITICAL CAMERA BEHAVIOR]
露露与罗科仰躺在潮湿柏油路上，并排，奔跑之后。两人都在场，躺得近到肩膀几乎相触。但由于摄影师的镜头是长焦、极浅景深，任何时刻只有一张脸是锐利合焦的。另一位角色严重失焦——是一个位于合焦者身旁的色彩与头发的模糊轮廓。手持摄影师追寻焦点，在两人互动时在他们之间对焦摇摆（rack），遇到动作太快时会短暂地完全失焦瞬间。他们显然在一起、玩耍、相触，但镜头语言在分秒之间把他们视觉上分隔开来。

[CAMERA POSITION]
镜头从侧上方的俯身姿态对准他们，非常近，微微朝合焦者倾斜。活跃有机的手持呼吸——摄影师俯身在他们上方，在场，对他们的动作作出反应。摄影师跟随这场游戏时轻微弹跳与移位。取景：一张脸填满画面大部分的特写，另一位角色是画面一角或边缘处的柔和颜色模糊。

[ACTION OVER 5 SECONDS]
0.0s——对焦在露露脸上，眼睛睁着、大口呼吸、隔着喘气轻声笑。罗科是画面下缘的黑发与酒红色布料的一团模糊，也在笑，他的肩几乎触到她的肩。

1.0s——露露仍笑着猛地转头朝罗科。她迅速把手伸进身旁柏油路上的一个小水洼——把冷水往他脸上弹去。水珠飞过画面。对焦试图在空中追水，短暂抓住水花。

2.0s——对焦摇摆到罗科脸上，锐利地落在他身上——他因水而瑟缩，双眼瞬时紧闭，随即爆发出响亮的笑声。他脸颊与睫毛上的水珠捕捉霓虹倒影。他喊着什么俏皮话——“喂！露露，你个笨蛋！”——笑开了。

3.0s——罗科微微朝她滚过去，伸出手，他的手模糊地入画，大笑中一把抓住她手腕。动作发生时对焦短暂地跟丢两人。

3.5s——对焦再次抓住露露，锐利地落在她身上——她现在笑得快失控了，笑得双眼紧闭，手腕被他握着但她不挣扎，只是笑。罗科是她身旁一团暖色调的运动与酒红模糊。

4.5s——他们的笑声微缓。露露睁开眼，仍笑着，把脸转向他（在她身旁，仍未合焦）。镜头没有对焦摇摆——罗科保持着她取景中一团温暖柔和模糊。她看着他，笑着，湿发垂在脸颊。她的手仍在他握着她的湿漉漉的路面上，手指现在正与他交扣。

5.0s——镜头切在瞬间中途，她笑着看他，仍半笑着，双手交握。

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
香港式城市的深夜，奔跑终了，在开阔的人行大道或空荡广场上。他们上方——天空与远处城市辉光在失焦背景中可见。脚下潮湿柏油路反射着远处霓虹的散点。远处街灯在画面边缘捕捉到变形横拉镜头耀斑。空气中雾气捕捉彩光。温暖远处城市辉光与清冷天空形成反差。露露弹起的落水在空中化作一抹红色霓虹的流光。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 17 岁，并排躺着玩耍]
- 露露：17 岁，深褐齐肩发湿透、在她四周铺散到柏油路面上，湿发贴在她太阳穴与脸颊，奔跑与大笑而深红的苍白皮肤，额上汗光，双眼生动、因笑意而弯起，张嘴笑着。带复古刺绣补丁的浅色水洗牛仔夹克铺张在她身下，白色露脐 T 恤，纤细黑色天鹅绒项圈。
- 罗科：17 岁，湿透的凌乱黑发摊在柏油路上，浅淡胡须，泛红的橄榄色皮肤，因笑意而弯起的双眼，张嘴响亮大笑。白 T 恤上酒红与奶油格纹短袖衬衫敞开，喉间闪烁的金链十字项链，鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴，前臂浅淡纹身。

他们躺得很近——肩膀几乎相触，头在同一水平，双手在两人之间湿漉漉的地上交合。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐、无乐器。两人都在笑——露露更尖的咯咯笑、罗科更低的胸腔笑。水弹过空中、水花四溅。罗科俏皮的喊叫“喂！露露，你个笨蛋！”透过他的笑声喊出（或用另一种语言，自然亲密）。笑声间沉重的呼吸。他们微微挪动时潮湿柏油路的声音。远处都市嗡鸣，遥远的汽车喇叭。两个充满生命力、并爱上彼此生机勃勃之状的声音。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无配乐、无乐器、无慢动作、无静态死板镜头、无平滑稳定器、无稳定化、无深景深、无同时两张脸都锐利合焦、无惆怅、无悲伤、无沉默、无静止僵硬姿态、无仰望天空、无刻意的美感造型、无恐惧、无死亡、无失去意识、无长时间闭眼、无全身镜头、无宽景、无其他人、无驶过的汽车、无落雨、无多余肢体、无面部变形、无角色身份漂移、无角色被距离分离、无儿童、无超过 25 岁的成人、无文字、无字幕、无背景锐利合焦、无假笑、无刻意微笑、无接吻、无恋爱信号、无角色彼此松手。
