## 原文 (source)

Single continuous shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, 
50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of Terrence Malick's 
"The Tree of Life" — intimate moment dissolving into vast landscape.

[CAMERA]
Active organic handheld, the OPERATOR PHYSICALLY WALKING BACKWARDS 
through the tall grass, retreating step by step away from the two 
figures. Each footstep creates a subtle natural bounce in the frame. 
The operator occasionally sways slightly to navigate uneven ground 
or grass tufts behind them — real human walking, not a dolly. The 
camera is alive, breathing, organic — clearly attached to a person 
walking, not mounted on a rig. Occasionally the operator stumbles 
gently on the uneven field, the camera dips slightly, recovers.

The retreat is slow and deliberate, the operator walking step by 
step backwards while keeping the two figures centered in frame. As 
the operator walks back, more of the field, tree, sky, and distant 
Hong Kong skyline gradually come into frame. The two figures get 
smaller and smaller in the center.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, warm low sun catching their hair and 
shoulders in rim light, dappled tree shadows on them and the grass, 
dust and seed particles floating in beams, lens flares from the low 
sun.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Close handheld medium shot: Lulu and Roco standing in the 
       grass, foreheads pressed together, eyes closed, breathing 
       slowing after running. Lulu's hands rest on his chest. Roco's 
       hands cup her elbows gently. They are completely still.

1.0s — The operator takes the first step backward. The camera bounces 
       slightly with the step, frame jostles naturally. They remain 
       still, foreheads touching. We see more of their shoulders, 
       the tall grass around them.

2.5s — Operator continues walking backward through grass — each step 
       creating a small organic bounce. We see them now from a few 
       meters back — two small figures standing forehead-to-forehead 
       in the golden field, the tree to one side, distant city 
       skyline soft behind them.

4.0s — Operator keeps retreating, more steps, more grass between 
       camera and them. They are smaller now, silhouetted against 
       the vast golden field and the hazy skyline. Two small figures, 
       one shape, in the middle of the world.

5.0s — Frame ends with them small in the vast landscape, still 
       forehead to forehead, golden grass swaying between them and 
       the camera.

[CHARACTERS — both 15]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length hair messy from running, pale skin 
  flushed pink, oversized blue-and-white vertical striped button-up 
  shirt over white t-shirt, white midi skirt, white sandals, thin 
  silver chain bracelets.
- ROCO: messy shaggy black hair, smooth cheeks (NO mustache, NO 
  stubble — too young), olive skin flushed, dark gray cotton zip-up 
  hoodie open over light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants with 
  red contrast stitching, dark Vans-style sneakers. Small red 
  adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose. No tattoos, 
  no jewelry, no chains.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Their slow synchronized breathing audible at the start, 
gradually getting quieter as the camera retreats. The soft crunch 
and rustle of grass under the operator's footsteps as they walk 
backward, the operator's quiet breathing, gentle wind through the 
field becoming louder as space opens up, distant birds, faint city 
hum.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no kissing, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no smooth 
dolly, no mechanical dolly, no smooth track, no smooth pull-back, 
no stabilization, no aerial drone shot, no tilt up to sky, no zoom 
out, no other people in field, no facial hair on Roco, no mustache, 
no stubble, no tattoos, no jewelry, no chains, no choker, no makeup, 
no removal of red nose bandage, no rapid focus changes, no broad 
smiling, no laughing, no talking, no dialogue, no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，50mm 镜头，浅景深，风格参照泰伦斯·马力克（Terrence Malick）的《生命之树》（The Tree of Life）——亲密时刻消融于辽阔风景。

[CAMERA]
活跃的有机手持，摄影师真的在高草丛中倒着向后行走，一步步远离两个人影。每走一步都带来画面中细微的自然弹跳。摄影师偶尔微微侧身，以绕过后方不平的地面或草丛——真实的行走，不是推轨。镜头有生命、会呼吸、有机——清晰可见是系在行走的人身上，而非安装于某个支架。摄影师偶尔在崎岖田野上轻微踉跄，镜头微微下坠、随即恢复。

后退缓慢而刻意，摄影师一步步倒着走，同时让两个人影保持在画面中央。随着摄影师后退，更多的田野、树、天空与远处香港天际线逐渐入画。两个人影在画面中央越来越小。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，温暖低角度阳光以轮廓光勾勒发丝与肩膀，斑驳树影洒在他们与草丛上，光束中漂浮尘埃与种子颗粒，低角度阳光产生镜头耀斑。

[ACTION]
0.0s——贴近手持的中景：露露与罗科站在草丛中，额头相抵、双目紧闭，奔跑后的呼吸渐缓。露露的手搭在他胸口。罗科的手轻柔捧着她的肘部。他们完全静止。

1.0s——摄影师迈出第一步后退。镜头随脚步轻微弹跳，画面自然地晃动。他们保持静止，额头相触。我们看到更多他们的肩膀、四周的高草。

2.5s——摄影师继续穿过草丛后退——每走一步都带来细微的有机弹跳。现在我们从几米外看他们——两个小小人影额头相对站在金色田野中，树在侧旁，远处城市天际线柔和其后。

4.0s——摄影师持续后退，更多的步子，镜头与他们之间更多的草丛。他们现在更小了，在辽阔的金色田野与朦胧天际线前化作剪影。两个小身影，一个整体，立于世界中央。

5.0s——镜头以他们在辽阔风景中的渺小身影收尾，仍额头相对，金色草在他们与镜头之间摇曳。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 15 岁]
- 露露：奔跑后凌乱的深褐色齐肩发，苍白皮肤泛粉，宽大的蓝白竖条纹系扣衬衫内搭白 T 恤，白色中长裙，白色凉鞋，纤细银链手链。
- 罗科：凌乱蓬松的黑发，光洁的脸颊（绝对无胡须、无胡茬——太年轻），泛粉的橄榄色皮肤，深灰色棉质拉链连帽卫衣敞开内搭浅灰 T 恤，黑色工装裤带红色撞色缝线，深色 Vans 风格运动鞋。鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。开始时可闻他们缓慢同步的呼吸，随镜头后退逐渐变轻。摄影师倒步踩在草上的轻柔沙沙与窸窣声、摄影师轻微的呼吸、随着空间展开而渐强的田野轻风、远处鸟鸣、轻微都市嗡鸣。
