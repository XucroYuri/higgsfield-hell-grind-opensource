## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 35mm wide lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of 
Terrence Malick's "Days of Heaven" and "The Tree of Life" — natural 
golden hour wonder.

[CAMERA]
Camera placed at ground level in the tall golden grass, lens just 
above the grass tops. Subtle organic handheld breathing motion. The 
camera does not move from its position — it watches them run past. 
Frame: extreme low angle looking up through swaying grass and 
wildflowers in the immediate foreground, distant Hong Kong skyline 
small and hazy in the deep background.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, low warm sun streaming directly through 
the grass blades creating glowing rim light on every stem, dust and 
seed particles floating in golden beams, lens flares from the low 
sun, soft warm haze.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Empty grass field in foreground, distant gentle wind through 
       the grass, distant city. We hear running feet approaching 
       off-frame.
1.5s — Lulu BURSTS into the frame from the right, running through the 
       grass — we see her legs and lower body powerfully sweeping 
       through the tall grass, her white midi skirt flying around her 
       legs, blue sneakers hitting the dirt path between grass tufts. 
       She runs past the camera, laughing, looking back over her 
       shoulder.
3.0s — Roco follows a beat behind, also running through the grass, 
       his legs and dark Vans sneakers sweeping through the stems. 
       Camera follows him with a slight pan as he passes.
4.0s — Both running away from camera into the field, getting smaller, 
       silhouetted against the golden light, moving toward the large 
       old tree visible in the distance. Grass swaying in their wake.
5.0s — Frame cuts on them mid-stride running away.

[CHARACTERS — both 15]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length hair flying behind her, oversized 
  blue-and-white vertical striped button-up shirt over a white t-shirt, 
  flowy white midi skirt billowing as she runs, white sandals barely 
  visible. Thin silver chain bracelets, no choker, no makeup.
- ROCO: messy shaggy black hair, smooth cheeks (NO mustache, NO 
  stubble — too young), dark gray cotton zip-up hoodie open over a 
  light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants with red contrast stitching, 
  dark canvas Vans-style sneakers with red details. Small red adhesive 
  bandage strip across the bridge of his nose. No tattoos, no jewelry, 
  no chains.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Their fast running footsteps on dirt and grass, breathless 
laughter, the loud rustle of grass parting around their legs, distant 
birds, faint city hum, gentle wind.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no high angle, no full 
body framing from distance, no other people, no facial hair on Roco, 
no mustache, no stubble, no tattoos, no jewelry, no chains, no 
choker on Lulu, no makeup, no removal of red nose bandage, no posed 
running, no looking at camera, no rapid camera shake, no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续手持镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，35mm 广角镜头，浅景深，风格参照泰伦斯·马力克（Terrence Malick）的《天堂之日》（Days of Heaven）与《生命之树》（The Tree of Life）——自然黄金时刻的惊叹。

[CAMERA]
镜头置于金色高草丛中的地面高度，镜片刚高出草丛顶端。细微有机的手持呼吸律动。镜头不离开原位——它看着他们跑过。取景：极低机位透过眼前摇曳的草丛与野花向上看，远处香港天际线在纵深背景中很小、朦胧。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，低而温暖的阳光直直穿过草叶，让每一根草茎都泛着发光的轮廓光，金色光束中漂浮尘埃与种子颗粒，低角度阳光的镜头耀斑，柔和温暖的薄雾。

[ACTION]
0.0s——前景空旷的草地，远处微风穿过草丛，远处城市。我们听到奔跑的脚步从画外逼近。
1.5s——露露从右侧冲入画面，穿过草丛奔跑——我们看到她的双腿与下半身有力扫过高草，白色中长裙在她腿边飞舞，蓝色运动鞋踩在草丛间的泥土小径上。她跑过镜头，笑着，回头越过肩膀看了一眼。
3.0s——罗科隔了一拍跟在后面，也穿过草丛奔跑，他的双腿与深色 Vans 运动鞋扫过草茎。镜头在他经过时轻轻摇移跟随。
4.0s——两人背对镜头跑进田野，身影变小，在金色光线中化作剪影，朝远处可见的那棵大树跑去。草丛在他们身后摇曳。
5.0s——镜头切在他们大步跑远、脚步未落之时。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 15 岁]
- 露露（LULU）：深褐色齐肩发在身后飞舞，白 T 恤上宽大的蓝白竖条纹系扣衬衫，奔跑时飘摆的白色中长裙，白色凉鞋若隐若现。纤细银链手链，无项圈，不化妆。
- 罗科（ROCO）：凌乱蓬松的黑发，光洁的脸颊（绝对无胡须、无胡茬——太年轻），浅灰 T 恤上深灰色棉质拉链连帽卫衣敞开，红色撞色缝线黑色工装裤，带红色细节的深色帆布 Vans 风格运动鞋。鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。他们泥土与草上快速的奔跑脚步声、喘气的笑声、草丛在他们腿边分开的响亮窸窣、远处鸟鸣、轻微都市嗡鸣、轻柔的风。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、无高机位、无远处的全身取景、无其他人、罗科无面部毛发、无胡须、无胡茬、无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰、露露无项圈、无妆容、不得移除红色鼻贴、无刻意的奔跑摆拍、不得看向镜头、无镜头剧烈晃动、无文字。
