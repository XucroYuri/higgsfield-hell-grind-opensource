## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 15 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 50mm lens for the selfie portion shifting to 100mm telephoto 
extreme close-up for the polaroid, shallow depth of field throughout, 
in the style of intimate domestic moments.

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera, close to them on the dark blue velvet 
sofa. Frame begins as medium close-up of both their faces 
cheek-to-cheek, then in the second half pushes in tight on the 
polaroid in Lulu's hand. Gentle natural breathing motion throughout, 
slight bounces during the playful moments.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour through gauzy curtains, warm sunlight 
catching their faces, dust particles in beams, deep red wall with 
posters in soft warm bokeh behind them.

[CHARACTERS — both 28]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length wavy hair, pale skin warm in golden 
  light, oversized burgundy and black vertical striped button-up 
  shirt-dress, ear cuff earring catching light, holding a vintage 
  white-and-rainbow-stripe Polaroid OneStep camera in her outstretched 
  hand at arm's length.
- ROCO: lean toned shirtless torso with visible tattoos on arms and 
  ribs, longer messy black hair, faint mustache, deep brown eyes, 
  small red adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose. 
  Pressed cheek-to-cheek with Lulu, his arm wrapped around her 
  shoulders.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Lulu holds out the polaroid camera in front of them at arm's 
       length. Both leaning in close cheek-to-cheek, Roco grinning. 
       "One, two — " Lulu starts.

1.5s — Roco interrupts her by suddenly TURNING and licking her cheek 
       playfully — she SHRIEKS laughing, recoils, hits his arm.

2.5s — Roco laughs hard, pulls her back in. She wipes her cheek 
       dramatically on his shoulder, still laughing, gives him a fake 
       angry look.

3.5s — They both lean cheek-to-cheek again toward the camera. Lulu, 
       still laughing — "Don't you dare, idiot — " Roco grins wide 
       and innocent. CLICK — the polaroid camera's shutter fires, 
       small flash bursts.

4.5s — The polaroid film WHIRRS out of the bottom of the camera — a 
       blank white square. Lulu pulls it out of the slot.

5.0s — Camera pushes in close to Lulu's hand holding the blank white 
       polaroid. Pure white, slightly milky, completely undeveloped. 
       Frame is now extreme close-up of the photo in her fingers, 
       her shirt-dress in soft bokeh.

6.0s — Lulu FLICKS her wrist, shaking the polaroid playfully back 
       and forth in frame. The photo blurs with motion. Roco's voice 
       off-frame: "You're not supposed to shake it, that's a myth."

7.5s — She shakes it again, grinning off-frame. "Shut up, it works." 
       Another quick flick.

9.0s — She slows down, holds the polaroid still. The white surface 
       starts to ghost — faintest hints of shadow emerging.

10.5s — Faint outlines of two heads emerge through the milky haze. 
        Pale skin tones pushing through, the dark mass of hair forming.

12.0s — Image becomes much clearer — Lulu and Roco visible 
        cheek-to-cheek, grinning. Lulu's slightly damp cheek from 
        the lick, Roco's wide grin. Warm golden light from the 
        captured moment finally readable.

13.5s — Image is fully developed, fully sharp. A perfect frozen second 
        of pure happiness. Roco's hand enters frame from off-side, 
        his finger gently traces the edge of the photo. Lulu's quiet 
        "Look" off-frame.

15.0s — Frame holds on the fully developed polaroid in her hand, the 
        captured moment of laughter completely visible.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Lulu's "One, two —", her shriek of laughter, Roco's laugh, 
the click of the polaroid shutter, the small electronic whirr of 
film ejecting, the soft flick of polaroid paper through air, Roco's 
"You're not supposed to shake it, that's a myth", Lulu's "Shut up, 
it works", the faint chemical hiss of developing, Lulu's quiet 
"Look" near the end, Roco's soft satisfied laugh, sofa creak, 
distant room tone.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no posed shots, no 
modern smartphone, no DSLR camera, no digital camera, no other 
people, no kissing on mouth, no removal of red nose bandage, no 
facial morphing, no character identity drift, no extra fingers, no 
broken hand anatomy, no text, no captions, no watermarks on polaroid, 
no logos, no facial morphing on the photo.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续手持镜头，15 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，自拍部分用 50mm 镜头，随后切换为 100mm 长焦对宝丽来做极特写，全程浅景深，风格参照亲密的居家时刻。

[CAMERA]
贴近深蓝色丝绒沙发的活跃手持摄影机。画面以两人脸颊相贴的中近特写开始，后半段推近至露露手中的宝丽来。全程柔和自然的呼吸律动，俏皮时刻有轻微弹跳。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻透过薄纱窗帘，温暖阳光洒在两人脸上，光束中尘埃漂浮，身后深红墙面与海报化作柔和温暖的虚化背景。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 28 岁]
- 露露（LULU）：深褐色中长波浪发，苍白皮肤在金色光线中泛暖，宽大的酒红色与黑色竖条纹系扣衬衫裙，耳骨夹耳饰捕捉光线，一手伸直握着复古白底彩虹条纹的宝丽来 OneStep 相机。
- 罗科（ROCO）：精瘦有力的赤裸上身，手臂与肋骨处可见纹身，略长凌乱的黑发，浅淡胡须，深棕眼眸，鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。与露露脸颊相贴，手臂环抱她的肩膀。

[ACTION]
0.0s——露露伸直手臂，将宝丽来相机举在两人面前。两人脸颊相贴近靠，罗科咧嘴笑着。“一、二——”露露开口。

1.5s——罗科突然转头俏皮地舔她的脸颊，打断她——她惊声大笑、退开、捶打他的手臂。

2.5s——罗科开怀大笑，把她拉回身边。她戏剧化地在他肩头擦脸，仍笑着，给了他一个假怒的眼神。

3.5s——两人再次脸颊相贴朝向相机。露露仍笑着——“你敢，笨蛋——”罗科咧嘴笑得天真无邪。咔嚓——宝丽来快门按下，迸发小小闪光。

4.5s——宝丽来相纸从相机底部嗡鸣吐出——一张空白白片。露露把它从出口抽出。

5.0s——镜头凑近露露手中那张空白白色宝丽来。纯白、略带乳白、完全未显影。此刻画面是她指间的极特写，她的衬衫裙化作柔和虚化。

6.0s——露露弹动手腕，俏皮地来回晃动画面中的宝丽来。相片因运动而模糊。罗科画外音：“你不该晃它，那是谣言。”

7.5s——她又晃了一次，画外咧嘴而笑。“闭嘴，这招管用。”又快速弹了一下。

9.0s——她放慢动作，举稳宝丽来。白色表面开始显影——最微弱的阴影浮现。

10.5s——乳白雾气中浮现两颗头的朦胧轮廓。苍白肤色透出，黑色发团成形。

12.0s——影像清晰许多——可见露露与罗科脸颊相贴、咧嘴而笑。露露因那一舔微湿的脸颊，罗科大张的笑容。捕捉到的温暖金色光线终于可辨。

13.5s——影像完全显影、完全锐利。纯粹快乐定格的一瞬。罗科的手从画面侧方入画，手指轻描相片边缘。露露画外音轻声“看”。

15.0s——镜头停在她手中完全显影的宝丽来上，捕捉到的欢笑瞬间完整可见。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。露露的“一、二——”、她的惊声大笑、罗科的笑、宝丽来快门声、相纸吐出的轻微电子嗡鸣、宝丽来相纸在空中轻柔弹动、罗科的“你不该晃它，那是谣言”、露露的“闭嘴，这招管用”、显影的微弱化学嘶声、临近结束时露露轻声的“看”、罗科柔和满足的笑、沙发吱呀、远处房间氛围声。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、无摆拍、无现代智能手机、无单反相机、无数码相机、无其他人、无嘴对嘴接吻、不得移除红色鼻贴、无面部变形、无角色身份漂移、无多余手指、无畸形手部结构、无文字、无字幕、宝丽来上无水印、无标志、照片上无面部变形。
