## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic 100mm telephoto lens, 
extremely shallow depth of field (only 1-2 cm in focus at any moment), 
cinematography in the style of Wong Kar-wai's "Fallen Angels" and 
"Chungking Express", Christopher Doyle handheld telephoto chasing 
camera.

[CRITICAL CAMERA BEHAVIOR]
Lulu and Roco are running TOGETHER side by side through the alley — 
both physically present in the scene at all times. BUT because the 
camera operator is using a long telephoto lens with extremely shallow 
depth of field, the camera CANNOT hold both of them in focus at once. 
Only ONE face is sharp at any given moment. The other character is 
present in the frame but heavily out of focus — a blurred shape, a 
flash of motion, a hint of color passing through the foreground or 
background. The handheld operator is actively HUNTING focus, racking 
between them as they run, sometimes losing both of them entirely for 
a fraction of a second before catching one again. The camera struggles 
to keep up — it bounces, it sways, it occasionally drifts off-target 
and corrects. Alive, breathing, imperfect, beautiful.

[CAMERA MOVEMENT]
Active alive handheld, running alongside them at chest level. The 
camera operator is clearly chasing them. Frame: tight medium close-up 
range, faces filling most of the frame when caught, but with constant 
focus shifts and partial framings. Anamorphic lens flares streak 
across the frame when neon signs pass behind the in-focus subject.

[FOCUS CHOREOGRAPHY OVER 5 SECONDS]
0.0s — focus catches LULU's face mid-run, sharp on her, Roco a blur 
       of charcoal-and-burgundy in the right edge of frame
1.0s — Lulu turns her head, focus drifts as the camera tries to follow
1.5s — focus RACKS to ROCO's face, sharp on him, Lulu now a blur of 
       denim-and-pale on the left edge
2.5s — Roco grins, focus drifts, both briefly soft
3.0s — focus catches LULU again, she is laughing, hair flying, neon 
       on her cheek
4.0s — momentary loss of focus, the world is colored bokeh
4.5s — focus rack back to ROCO, his face caught for a beat
5.0s — frame cuts mid-motion, focus mid-rack

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
Late night Asian megacity backstreet, recently rained. Wet asphalt 
beneath them. Hong-Kong-style alley with red and pink neon signs, 
hanging Chinese-character signage glowing, white-and-red striped 
tower visible far in the deep background bokeh. Color streaks of 
neon — pink, red, electric blue, amber — splashing across both their 
faces as they run through different signage zones. Anamorphic 
horizontal lens flares bursting from each neon source. Misty air 
catching the colored light.

[CHARACTERS — both 17 years old, running together]
- LULU: 17, dark brown shoulder-length hair flying behind her, 
  side-swept fringe, pale skin flushed pink, bright eyes, wild grin, 
  wearing a light wash denim jacket covered in vintage embroidered 
  patches over a white cropped t-shirt, thin black velvet choker, 
  blue cargo shorts, blue high-top canvas sneakers.
- ROCO: 17, messy shaggy black hair flying, faint mustache, olive 
  skin flushed, deep brown eyes, wearing a burgundy and cream check 
  pattern short-sleeve shirt open over a white t-shirt, gold chain 
  necklaces with crosses, dark gray denim shorts, white socks, tan 
  Timberland boots, small red adhesive bandage strip across the 
  bridge of his nose. Tattoos faintly visible on forearms.

[ACTION]
Both running side by side, hand in hand. Laughing breathlessly. 
Occasionally glancing at each other. The camera catches these 
moments — when one of them turns their head toward the other, that 
face goes sharp; the other becomes blur. They are clearly running 
together, clearly bonded, but the camera language separates them 
visually moment to moment, then reunites them, then separates again.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music, no instruments. Two pairs of synchronized footsteps on wet 
asphalt — heavy Timberland boots and rubber sneakers — splashing 
through puddles, breathless laughter from both, fast breathing, 
distant city hum, far car horns, the rustle of denim and cotton.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no instruments, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, 
no static camera, no stabilization, no deep depth of field, no sharp 
focus on background, no both faces sharp at the same time, no other 
people in frame, no cars driving past, no rain falling, no melancholy, 
no fear, no extra limbs, no duplicate characters, no facial morphing, 
no character identity drift, no posed running, no fake laughter, no 
characters separating from each other, no characters letting go of 
hands, no children, no adults over 25, no text, no captions, no 
single character alone in frame, no characters running away from 
each other.

## 中文对照 (zh)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，用 Arri Alexa 与变形 100mm 长焦镜头拍摄，极浅景深（任何时刻仅 1-2 厘米合焦），摄影风格参照王家卫的《堕落天使》（Fallen Angels）与《重庆森林》（Chungking Express）、杜可风（Christopher Doyle）手持长焦追逐镜头。

[CRITICAL CAMERA BEHAVIOR]
露露与罗科并肩奔跑着穿过小巷——两人始终物理上都在场。但由于摄影师用的是长焦、极浅景深，镜头无法同时让两人都合焦。任何时刻只有一张脸锐利。另一位角色在画中但不在此刻合焦——一个模糊轮廓、一团运动闪光、一抹色彩划过前景或背景。手持摄影师主动追猎焦点，在他们奔跑时于两人之间对焦摇摆，有时在抓住某一人之前短暂地把两人都跟丢一瞬间。镜头挣扎着跟上——它弹跳、它摇摆、它偶尔偏离目标再修正。生动、会呼吸、不完美、美丽。

[CAMERA MOVEMENT]
活跃生动的手持，在胸高位与他们并排奔跑。摄影师明显在追逐他们。取景：紧中近特写范围，被抓住时脸填满画面大部分，但焦点不断切换、构图偶有残缺。霓虹招牌在合焦主体身后经过时，变形镜头耀斑横划画面。

[FOCUS CHOREOGRAPHY OVER 5 SECONDS]
0.0s——对焦在奔跑中抓住露露的脸，锐利落在她身上，罗科是画面右缘一团炭灰与酒红模糊。
1.0s——露露转头，镜头试图跟随而对焦漂移。
1.5s——对焦摇摆到罗科脸上，锐利落在他身上，露露此刻是左缘牛仔与苍白的一团模糊。
2.5s——罗科咧嘴笑，对焦漂移，两人短暂都柔和。
3.0s——对焦再次抓住露露，她在笑，头发飞舞，霓虹落在她脸颊。
4.0s——瞬时失焦，世界化作一片彩色虚化。
4.5s——对焦摇摆回罗科，他的脸被抓到一拍。
5.0s——镜头切在动作中途、对焦摇摆中段。

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
亚洲特大城市的深夜背巷，刚下过雨。他们脚下潮湿柏油路。香港式小巷有红与粉霓虹招牌、发光的悬挂汉字招牌，纵深背景虚化远处可见红白条纹高楼。霓虹的色彩拖影——粉红、红、电光蓝、琥珀——在他们跑过不同招牌区域时溅在两人脸上。每个霓虹光源爆出变形横向镜头耀斑。雾气空气捕捉彩光。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 17 岁，并肩奔跑]
- 露露：17 岁，深褐色齐肩发在身后飞舞，侧分刘海，泛粉的苍白皮肤，明亮的眼睛，狂放的咧嘴笑，穿着覆盖复古刺绣补丁的浅色水洗牛仔夹克内搭白色露脐 T 恤，纤细黑色天鹅绒项圈，蓝色工装短裤，蓝色高帮帆布运动鞋。
- 罗科：17 岁，凌乱蓬松的黑发飞舞，浅淡胡须，泛红的橄榄色皮肤，深棕眼眸，穿着白 T 恤上酒红与奶油格纹短袖衬衫敞开，金链十字项链，深灰牛仔短裤，白袜，棕色 Timberland 靴子，鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。前臂隐约可见纹身。

[ACTION]
两人手牵手并肩奔跑。喘着气笑。偶尔互相瞥一眼。镜头捕捉这些时刻——当其中一人转头朝向对方时，那张脸变得锐利；另一个变成模糊。他们明显在一起跑、明显有羁绊，但镜头语言在分秒之间把他们视觉上分开，随即再合二为一，然后又分开。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐、无乐器。两双同步的脚步落在湿柏油路上——沉重的 Timberland 靴与橡胶运动鞋——踩过水洼溅起，两人喘不过气来的笑声，快速的呼吸，远处都市嗡鸣，遥远汽车喇叭，牛仔与棉布的窸窣。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无配乐、无乐器、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、无静态镜头、无稳定化、无深景深、无背景锐利合焦、无同时两张脸都锐利合焦、画中无其他人、无驶过的汽车、无落雨、无惆怅、无恐惧、无多余肢体、无重复角色、无面部变形、无角色身份漂移、无刻意的奔跑摆拍、无假笑、无角色彼此分离、无角色松开手、无儿童、无超过 25 岁的成人、无文字、无字幕、无单独一个角色独处画中、无角色互相跑开。
