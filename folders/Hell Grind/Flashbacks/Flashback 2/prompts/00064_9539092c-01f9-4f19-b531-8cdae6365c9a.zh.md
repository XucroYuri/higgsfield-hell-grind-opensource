[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld extreme close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 
2.39:1, 35mm film grain, anamorphic 100mm telephoto lens, extremely 
shallow depth of field, cinematography in the style of Terrence Malick 
and Lukas Dhont's "Close".

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, very close to their hands resting on grass. 
Gentle natural breathing of camera. Frame: extreme close-up on two 
teenage hands lying on green grass between them, wildflowers and 
out-of-focus tree roots in foreground bokeh.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, dappled sunlight through tree leaves above, 
warm soft light on skin and grass.

[CHARACTERS — hands only, both 15 years old]
- LULU's hand: pale slender fingers, thin silver chain bracelets on 
  wrist, edge of white midi skirt visible.
- ROCO's hand: olive skin, slightly larger, faint scar on knuckle, 
  edge of dark gray hoodie sleeve visible. No jewelry, no tattoos, 
  no rings. Smooth young skin.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Both hands lying separately on grass, palms down, a small gap 
       between them. Lulu's fingers twitch slightly.
2.0s — Roco's hand begins to move slowly toward hers, hesitantly.
3.5s — His hand gently covers hers from above.
4.5s — Their fingers slowly interlace. Lulu's hand turns palm-up to 
       meet his. Fingers fully laced.
5.0s — Frame ends on their joined hands.

hum, the soft rustle of skin against grass.

no deep focus, no other people, no faces visible, no text, no 
captions, no kissing, no romance clichés.

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，上方树叶间斑驳的阳光，落在皮肤与草上的温暖柔和光线。

[CHARACTERS — 仅手，两人皆 15 岁]
- 露露的手：苍白纤细的手指，手腕上纤细银链手链，白色中长裙的边缘可见。
- 罗科的手：橄榄色皮肤，略大一圈，指节上一道浅淡伤疤，深灰卫衣袖口边缘可见。无珠宝、无纹身、无戒指。光滑年轻的皮肤。

[ACTION]

5.0s——镜头定格在他们交握的双手上。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。穿过草丛的轻柔风、远处鸟鸣、轻微远处都市嗡鸣、皮肤蹭过草的轻柔窸窣。
