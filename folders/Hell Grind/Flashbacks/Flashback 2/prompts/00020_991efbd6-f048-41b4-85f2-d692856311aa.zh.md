## 原文 (source)

Single continuous shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, 
100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the style of Lukas 
Dhont's "Close" — quiet sustained intimacy.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, the operator standing still in the grass. 
The camera does NOT move position — only gentle natural breathing 
motion of the operator, slight sways, alive but locked in space. 
Tight close-up of their two faces and shoulders, foreheads pressed 
together. Tall grass in soft foreground bokeh framing the shot. 
Distant Hong Kong skyline very softly out of focus in deep background.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, warm low sun catching their hair and 
the side of their faces in rim light, dappled tree shadows, dust 
and seed particles floating in beams, soft warm haze.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Tight close-up: Lulu and Roco standing in the grass, foreheads 
       pressed together, eyes closed, completely still. Lulu's hands 
       on his chest. Roco's hands cupping her elbows. Their breathing 
       calm, synchronized.

2.0s — They hold the position. The camera breathes gently. The world 
       is quiet around them.

3.5s — A small moment — Lulu's lips part slightly in a soft exhale. 
       Roco's thumb makes the tiniest stroke on her elbow. They don't 
       open their eyes. They don't speak. They just breathe.

5.0s — Frame ends still on them, foreheads touching, eyes closed, 
       held in this suspended moment.

[CHARACTERS — both 15]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length hair, oversized blue-and-white 
  vertical striped button-up shirt over white t-shirt, white midi 
  skirt, white sandals, thin silver chain bracelets, no makeup, no 
  choker.
- ROCO: messy shaggy black hair, smooth cheeks (NO mustache, NO 
  stubble — too young), olive skin, dark gray cotton zip-up hoodie 
  open over light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants with red 
  contrast stitching. Small red adhesive bandage strip across the 
  bridge of his nose. No tattoos, no jewelry, no chains.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Their soft synchronized breathing, gentle wind through 
grass, distant birds, faint city hum, the soft rustle of clothing 
in the breeze.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no kissing, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no dolly, 
no track, no pull-back, no zoom, no operator walking, no aerial 
shot, no tilt, no characters walking, no characters leaving frame, 
no other people in field, no facial hair on Roco, no mustache, no 
stubble, no tattoos, no jewelry, no chains, no choker, no makeup, 
no removal of red nose bandage, no characters turning away, no 
looking at camera, no broad smiling, no laughing, no talking, no 
dialogue, no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照卢卡斯·德霍特（Lukas Dhont）的《亲密》（Close）——静谧而延绵的亲密。

[CAMERA]
轻微有机手持，摄影师静止站在草丛中。摄影机不移动机位——仅有摄影师柔和自然的呼吸律动、微微摇摆，富有生命力却又锁定在空间中。两人脸部与肩部的紧特写，额头相抵。前景高草化作柔和虚化和框景。远处香港天际线在纵深背景中极度失焦。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，温暖低角度阳光以轮廓光勾勒发丝与侧脸，斑驳树影，光束中漂浮尘埃与种子颗粒，柔和温暖的薄雾。

[ACTION]
0.0s——紧特写：露露与罗科站在草丛中，额头相抵、双目紧闭、完全静止。露露的手搭在他胸口。罗科的手捧着她的肘部。呼吸平静而同步。

2.0s——他们保持这一姿势。镜头轻缓呼吸。周遭世界一片静谧。

3.5s——一个微小的时刻——露露的嘴唇在一记柔和呼气中微微张开。罗科的拇指在她肘部做了最轻的一抚。他们不睁眼。他们不说话。只是呼吸。

5.0s——镜头仍停在他们身上，额头相触、双眼紧闭，定格在这个悬停的瞬间。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 15 岁]
- 露露（LULU）：深褐色齐肩发，宽大的蓝白竖条纹系扣衬衫内搭白 T 恤，白色中长裙，白色凉鞋，纤细银链手链，不化妆，无项圈。
- 罗科（ROCO）：凌乱蓬松的黑发，光洁的脸颊（绝对无胡须、无胡茬——太年轻），橄榄色皮肤，深灰色棉质拉链连帽卫衣敞开内搭浅灰 T 恤，黑色工装裤带红色撞色缝线。鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。他们轻柔同步的呼吸，微风穿过草丛，远处鸟鸣，轻微都市嗡鸣，衣物在风中的轻柔窸窣。
