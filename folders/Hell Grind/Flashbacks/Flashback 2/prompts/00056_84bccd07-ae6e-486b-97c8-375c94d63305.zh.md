## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, 100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the 
style of Lukas Dhont's "Close" — awkward teenage tenderness.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, slight three-quarter angle on Roco's face, 
very close. Camera at his eye level. Gentle natural breathing motion. 
Frame: tight close-up of Roco's face, soft bokeh of green leaves and 
dappled golden light behind him, distant Hong Kong skyline barely 
visible in deep background bokeh.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, dappled warm light through tree leaves 
falling across his face in moving patterns, soft warm side-light from 
the low sun catching on his skin.

[CHARACTER — Roco, 15]
Awkward growing-into-his-body teen, messy shaggy black hair falling 
into his eyes, smooth cheeks (NO mustache, NO stubble — too young), 
olive skin flushed pink across his cheeks and ears, deep brown eyes, 
small red adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose, 
wearing a dark gray cotton zip-up hoodie open over a light gray 
t-shirt visible at the collar. No tattoos, no jewelry, no chains.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Roco looks down at his own hands in his lap, jaw tight, lips 
       pressed together. Cheeks visibly flushed.
1.0s — He bites his lower lip nervously, glances quickly to the side 
       (where Lulu is, off-frame), eyes flicking to her then immediately 
       back down to his hands. Caught looking.
2.0s — He swallows hard, the small movement of his throat visible. 
       Breathes out slowly through his nose, trying to calm himself.
3.0s — A tiny embarrassed smile twitches at the corner of his mouth — 
       he can't help it — and he immediately ducks his head lower to 
       hide it, hair falling further over his eyes.
4.0s — He runs a hand quickly through his hair, brushing it back, 
       still not looking up, ears bright red.
5.0s — Frame ends on him with his head still slightly down, the ghost 
       of a smile he can't suppress, blushing.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Soft wind through leaves, distant birds, faint city hum, 
his nervous swallow at 2.0s, his slow exhale.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no smiling broadly, no laughing, no looking at 
camera, no looking directly at Lulu for long, no confidence, no facial 
hair, no mustache, no stubble, no tattoos, no jewelry, no chains, no 
removal of red nose bandage, no rapid movement, no smooth gimbal, no 
deep focus, no other people, no facial morphing, no character identity 
drift, no posed shyness, no fake blushing.

[CAMERA]

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，透过树叶的斑驳暖光以流动的图案洒落他的脸，低角度阳光的柔和温暖侧光落在他的皮肤上。

[CHARACTER — 罗科，15 岁]
正笨拙地长进自己身体里的少年，凌乱蓬松的黑发垂进眼睛，光洁的脸颊（绝对无胡须、无胡茬——太年轻），脸颊与耳朵泛粉的橄榄色皮肤，深棕眼眸，鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴，穿着深灰色棉质拉链连帽卫衣敞开、领口可见浅灰 T 恤。无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰。

2.0s——他狠狠咽了口唾沫，喉结的小动作可见。从鼻中缓缓呼出气，试图让自己平静。
3.0s——一个尴尬的小微笑在他嘴角抽了抽——他控制不住——随即立刻把脑袋埋得更低遮掩，头发垂得更深盖住眼睛。
4.0s——他飞快用手把头发往后撸了一下，仍不抬头，耳朵通红。
5.0s——镜头以他仍微低着头的姿态收尾，一个压不住的笑影，脸红。
