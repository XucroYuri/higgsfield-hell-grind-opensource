## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic 100mm telephoto 
lens, extremely shallow depth of field (only 1-2 cm in focus), 
cinematography in the style of Wong Kar-wai's quiet aftermath moments.

[CRITICAL CAMERA BEHAVIOR]
Lulu and Roco are lying TOGETHER on their backs on the wet asphalt, 
side by side, after running. Both physically present, lying close 
enough that their shoulders almost touch. BUT because the camera 
operator is using a long telephoto lens with extremely shallow depth 
of field, only ONE face is sharp at any given moment. The other 
character is heavily out of focus — a blurred shape of color and hair 
on the wet ground beside the in-focus one. The handheld operator 
breathes softly, the camera drifts slowly between them, racking focus 
to catch first one, then the other. They are clearly together, but 
visually framed alone moment to moment.

[CAMERA POSITION]
Camera leaning over them from above-side angle, very close, almost 
top-down but tilted toward whoever is in focus. Subtle organic 
handheld breathing — the operator is leaning over them, present, 
quiet. Almost no large movement, but constantly alive, micro-shifts 
of focus. Frame: tight close-up of one face filling most of the 
frame, the OTHER character a soft blur of color in the corner or 
edge of frame.

[FOCUS CHOREOGRAPHY OVER 5 SECONDS]
0.0s — focus on LULU's face, eyes open looking up at sky, breathing 
       hard, faint smile. Roco is a blur of dark hair and burgundy 
       fabric in the lower edge of frame, his shoulder almost 
       touching hers but heavily out of focus.
1.5s — focus slowly racks toward ROCO. For a beat both are soft.
2.0s — focus catches ROCO's face, eyes open looking up at sky, 
       breathing hard, faint smile. Lulu is now a blur of denim and 
       pale skin and dark hair in the upper edge of frame.
3.5s — focus racks back toward LULU. Their hands meet on the wet 
       asphalt between them — we see the joining of fingers go in 
       and out of focus as the rack passes.
4.5s — focus settles on LULU's face again, her eyes turn slightly 
       toward Roco (off-focus beside her), her smile deepens.
5.0s — frame cuts mid-rack, focus mid-shift.

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
Late night Hong-Kong-style city, after-the-run quiet. They lie on 
wet pavement of an open promenade or empty plaza. Above them — sky 
and distant city glow. Wet asphalt beneath their heads reflecting 
scattered points of neon from distant signs. Anamorphic lens flares 
from a single distant streetlamp catch in the frame. Mist in the 
air. Warm distant city glow contrasting with cool sky.

[CHARACTERS — both 17 years old, lying side by side]
- LULU: 17, dark brown shoulder-length hair fanned out on wet asphalt 
  around her head, wet strands sticking to her temples, pale skin 
  flushed deep pink, sheen of sweat and mist on her forehead, mouth 
  slightly open breathing hard, eyes open looking up at the sky. The 
  patch-covered denim jacket splayed open beneath her, white cropped 
  t-shirt, black velvet choker.
- ROCO: 17, messy black hair wet on the wet asphalt, faint mustache, 
  olive skin flushed, mouth slightly open breathing hard, eyes open 
  looking up at the sky, deep brown eyes catching distant neon. 
  Burgundy check shirt open showing white t-shirt, gold chain 
  necklaces glinting on his collarbone, small red adhesive bandage 
  strip across the bridge of his nose.

They lie close — shoulders almost touching, heads on the same level, 
their hands meeting between them on the wet ground.

[ACTION]
Both lying on their backs, breathing hard, recovering. Eyes open to 
the sky. Faint smiles. At ~3.5s their hands find each other on the 
wet asphalt between them — fingers lacing slowly, deliberately, 
without looking. They continue breathing, looking up. The camera 
drifts focus between their faces throughout, never showing both 
sharp at once.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Two synchronized heavy breaths recovering from running. 
Distant city hum, very far car horn, the soft drip of water 
somewhere, mist hissing on pavement. The sound of being alive after 
running together.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no instruments, no slow motion, no static dead 
camera, no smooth gimbal, no stabilization, no deep depth of field, 
no both faces sharp at the same time, no full body shot, no wide 
shot, no other people, no cars driving past, no rain falling, no 
melancholy, no fear, no death, no unconsciousness, no closing eyes 
fully for too long, no extra limbs, no facial morphing, no character 
identity drift, no characters separated by distance, no characters 
on opposite sides of frame, no children, no adults over 25, no text, 
no captions, no sharp focus on background.

## 中文对照 (zh)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，用 Arri Alexa 与变形 100mm 长焦镜头拍摄，极浅景深（仅 1-2 厘米合焦），摄影风格参照王家卫的静谧余韵时刻。

[CRITICAL CAMERA BEHAVIOR]
露露与罗科仰躺在潮湿柏油路上，并排，奔跑之后。两人都在场，躺得近到肩膀几乎相触。但由于摄影师的镜头是长焦、极浅景深，任何时刻只有一张脸是锐利合焦的。另一位角色严重失焦——是一个躺在合焦者身旁湿地上、色彩与头发的模糊轮廓。手持摄影师轻柔呼吸，镜头在他们之间缓缓漂移，对焦摇摆先抓住这个、再抓住那个。他们显然在一起，但分秒之间在视觉上被单独框取。

[CAMERA POSITION]
镜头从侧上方的俯身姿态对准他们，非常近，近乎俯拍但朝合焦者倾斜。细微有机的手持呼吸——摄影师俯身在他们上方，在场，安静。几乎没有大幅度运动，但始终生动，焦点微移。取景：一张脸填满画面大部分的特写，另一位角色是画面一角或边缘处的柔和颜色模糊。

[FOCUS CHOREOGRAPHY OVER 5 SECONDS]
0.0s——对焦在露露脸上，睁眼看向天空，大口呼吸，浅淡微笑。罗科是画面下缘的黑发与酒红布料的一团模糊，他的肩几乎触到她的肩但严重失焦。
1.5s——对焦缓缓朝罗科摇摆。一拍间两人都柔和。
2.0s——对焦抓住罗科的脸，睁眼看向天空，大口呼吸，浅淡微笑。露露此刻是画面上缘牛仔与苍白皮肤与黑发的模糊。
3.5s——对焦摇摆回露露。他们的手在他们之间的湿柏油路上相会——我们看着交扣的手指随摇摆经过而进出焦点。
4.5s——对焦再次落在露露脸上，她的视线微微转向罗科（在她身旁，失焦），她的微笑加深。
5.0s——镜头切在对焦中途、焦点移位时。

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
香港式城市的深夜，奔跑终了的静谧。他们躺在开放人行道或空荡广场的湿路面上。他们上方——天空与远处城市辉光。他们脑后潮湿柏油路反射远处招牌霓虹的散点。单一远处街灯的变形镜头耀斑捕捉入画。空气中有雾气。温暖远处城市辉光与清冷天空形成反差。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 17 岁，并排躺着]
- 露露：17 岁，深褐齐肩发在她头四周铺散到湿柏油路上，湿发贴在她太阳穴，深红的苍白皮肤，额上汗与雾光，嘴唇微张、大口呼吸，睁眼望向天空。带补丁的牛仔夹克铺张在她身下，白色露脐 T 恤，黑色天鹅绒项圈。
- 罗科：17 岁，湿透的凌乱黑发摊在湿柏油路上，浅淡胡须，泛红的橄榄色皮肤，嘴唇微张、大口呼吸，睁眼望向天空，捕捉远处霓虹的深棕眼眸。酒红格纹衬衫敞开露出白 T 恤，锁骨闪烁的金链项链，鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。

他们躺得很近——肩膀几乎相触，头在同一水平，双手在两人之间湿漉漉的地上交合。

[ACTION]
两人仰躺，大口呼吸，恢复着。睁眼望向天空。浅淡微笑。约 3.5s 时他们的双手在两人之间的湿柏油路上找到彼此——手指缓缓、刻意、不看地交扣。他们继续呼吸、望着上方。全片镜头在他们的脸之间漂移对焦，从不同时让两人都锐利。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。两道从奔跑中恢复的同步沉重呼吸。远处都市嗡鸣，非常远的汽车喇叭，某处轻柔的滴水，雾气在路面上嘶嘶作响。一起奔跑之后活着的声响。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无配乐、无乐器、无慢动作、无静态死板镜头、无平滑稳定器、无稳定化、无深景深、无同时两张脸都锐利合焦、无全身镜头、无宽景、无其他人、无驶过的汽车、无落雨、无惆怅、无恐惧、无死亡、无失去意识、无长时间完全闭眼、无多余肢体、无面部变形、无角色身份漂移、无角色被距离分离、无角色分处画面两侧、无儿童、无超过 25 岁的成人、无文字、无字幕、无背景锐利合焦。
