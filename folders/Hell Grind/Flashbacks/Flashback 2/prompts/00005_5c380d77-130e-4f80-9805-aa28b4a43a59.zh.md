## 原文 (source)

Two-shot sequence connected by hard cut, 10 seconds total, anamorphic 
2.39:1, 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field throughout, in the 
style of intimate domestic moments.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT 1 — SELFIE AND CLICK — 0.0s to 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera close to them on the dark blue velvet 
sofa, slightly above shoulder level, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field. 
Frame: medium close-up of both their faces pressed together 
cheek-to-cheek, looking at the polaroid camera held in Lulu's 
outstretched hand at the front of the frame.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour through gauzy curtains, warm sunlight 
catching their faces, dust particles in beams, deep red wall with 
posters in soft warm bokeh behind them.

[CHARACTERS — both 28]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length wavy hair, pale skin warm in golden 
  light, oversized burgundy and black vertical striped button-up 
  shirt-dress, ear cuff earring catching light, holding a vintage 
  white-and-rainbow-stripe Polaroid OneStep camera in her outstretched 
  hand at arm's length, gripping it correctly with both hands at the 
  sides of the camera.
- ROCO: lean toned shirtless torso with visible tattoos on arms and 
  ribs, longer messy black hair, faint mustache, deep brown eyes, 
  small red adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose. 
  Pressed cheek-to-cheek with Lulu, his arm wrapped around her 
  shoulders.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Lulu holds out the polaroid camera at arm's length, gripping 
       it correctly. Both leaning cheek-to-cheek, Roco grinning. 
       "One, two —" Lulu starts.
1.5s — Roco suddenly TURNS and licks her cheek playfully — she 
       SHRIEKS laughing, recoils, hits his arm.
2.5s — Roco laughs hard, pulls her back. She wipes her cheek 
       dramatically on his shoulder, fake angry look.
3.5s — Both lean cheek-to-cheek again. Lulu still laughing — "Don't 
       you dare, idiot —" Roco grins wide. CLICK — shutter fires, 
       small flash bursts.
4.5s — The motorized whirring sound of the polaroid begins. The 
       polaroid camera held still in her hand.
5.0s — Frame ends on the camera in her hand, the photo just beginning 
       to slide out of the front slot.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT AT 5.0s — instant frame swap, no fade, no dissolve
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT 2 — POLAROID SLOWLY EJECTING FROM CAMERA — 5.0s to 10.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld extreme close-up, 100mm telephoto, extremely 
shallow depth of field. Almost no movement, just gentle natural 
breathing of operator. Frame: extreme close-up of the FRONT SLOT 
of the polaroid camera, the photo physically emerging out of the 
slot. The camera body fills part of the frame, soft warm interior 
bokeh behind.

[LIGHT]
Same warm golden hour light, dappled sun, dust particles in beams.

[ACTION]
5.0s — Close-up of the polaroid camera's front ejection slot. The 
       white edge of a polaroid photo is just beginning to emerge, 
       motorized whirr audible.
6.5s — The photo continues sliding out slowly, motorized mechanism 
       pushing it forward. Half of the photo now visible — the 
       image fully developed already, showing Lulu and Roco 
       cheek-to-cheek grinning, her cheek slightly damp from the 
       lick, his wide grin, warm captured moment.
8.0s — The photo continues emerging, almost fully out now, the 
       motorized whirr slowing. Most of the developed image 
       visible above the camera body.
9.0s — The photo slides the last bit and falls free of the camera 
       slot, Lulu's other hand catches it gently as it emerges. The 
       motor stops.
10.0s — Frame holds on the fully ejected developed polaroid in her 
        fingers, the captured moment of joy completely visible. 
        Roco's voice off-frame, soft satisfied laugh.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, CONTINUOUS ACROSS BOTH SHOTS]
No music. Shot 1: Lulu's "One, two —", her shriek of laughter, Roco's 
laugh, the click of the polaroid shutter, the start of the motorized 
whirr. Shot 2: the continuous motorized whirring of the polaroid 
ejection mechanism slowly pushing the photo out, the soft slide of 
photo paper through the slot, the small mechanical click when the 
motor stops, Roco's quiet satisfied laugh, Lulu's quiet "Look", 
sofa creak, distant room tone.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no fade between shots, 
no dissolve between shots, no soft transition, no posed shots, no 
modern smartphone, no DSLR, no digital camera, no other people, no 
kissing on mouth, no removal of red nose bandage, no facial morphing, 
no character identity drift, no extra fingers, no broken hand anatomy, 
no incorrect grip on camera, no holding camera by the front lens, 
no holding camera upside down, no text, no captions, no watermarks 
on polaroid, no logos, no shaking the polaroid, no waving the 
polaroid, no blank white photo, no undeveloped photo, no photo 
already in her hand at start of shot 2, no photo magically appearing.

## 中文对照 (zh)

硬切连接的连续双镜头序列，总长 10 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，全程浅景深，风格参照亲密的居家时刻。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
镜头 1——自拍与按下快门——0.0s 至 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
贴近深蓝色丝绒沙发的活跃手持摄影机，略高于肩部，50mm 镜头，浅景深。取景：两人脸颊紧贴的中近特写，望向露露伸出、置于画面前方的宝丽来（polaroid）相机。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻透过薄纱窗帘，温暖阳光洒在两人脸上，光束中尘埃漂浮，身后深红墙面与海报化作柔和温暖的虚化背景。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 28 岁]
- 露露（LULU）：深褐色中长波浪发，苍白皮肤在金色光线中泛暖，宽大的酒红色与黑色竖条纹系扣衬衫裙（shirt-dress），耳骨夹（ear cuff）耳饰捕捉光线，一手伸直握着复古白底彩虹条纹的宝丽来 OneStep 相机，双手正确握持相机两侧。
- 罗科（ROCO）：精瘦有力的赤裸上身，手臂与肋骨处可见纹身，略长凌乱的黑发，浅淡胡须，深棕眼眸，鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。与露露脸颊相贴，手臂环抱她的肩膀。

[ACTION]
0.0s——露露伸直手臂举起宝丽来相机，双手正确握持。两人脸颊相贴，罗科咧嘴笑着。“一、二——”露露开口。
1.5s——罗科突然转头俏皮地舔她的脸颊——她惊声大笑、退开、捶打他的手臂。
2.5s——罗科开怀大笑，把她拉回来。她戏剧化地在他肩头擦脸，摆出假怒的表情。
3.5s——两人再次脸颊相贴。露露仍笑着——“你敢，笨蛋——”罗科咧嘴大笑。咔嚓——快门按下，发出小小闪光。
4.5s——宝丽来的电动转动声响起。她手中的相机保持静止。
5.0s——镜头定格在她手中的相机，照片刚开始从前端出口滑出。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.0s 硬切——瞬间换帧，无淡入淡出、无溶接
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
镜头 2——宝丽来从相机中缓缓吐出——5.0s 至 10.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
轻微有机手持的极特写，100mm 长焦，极浅景深。几乎无运动，仅有摄影师柔和的自然呼吸。取景：宝丽来相机前端出口的极特写，照片正从出口中物理浮现。机身占据画面一部分，身后是柔和温暖的室内虚化。

[LIGHT]
同样温暖的黄金时光线，斑驳阳光，光束中尘埃漂浮。

[ACTION]
5.0s——宝丽来相机前端出片口的特写。一张宝丽来照片的白色边缘刚开始显现，电动转动声可闻。
6.5s——照片继续缓慢滑出，电动机构将其推出。照片一半已可见——影像已经完全显影，显示露露与罗科脸颊相贴咧嘴而笑，她的脸颊因那一舔略湿，他笑容大张，温暖定格的一瞬。
8.0s——照片继续吐出，现已几乎完全出来，电动转动声渐缓。大部分显影影像浮现于相机机身之上。
9.0s——照片滑动最后一段并从出片口脱离，露露另一只手轻柔接住浮现的照片。马达停止。
10.0s——镜头停在她指间完全吐出的、已显影的宝丽来照片，捕捉到的欢乐瞬间完整可见。罗科画外音，一声柔和满足的笑。

[SOUND — 仅同期声，横跨两镜头连续]
无音乐。镜头 1：露露的“一、二——”、她的惊声大笑、罗科的笑、宝丽来快门声、电动转动声开始。镜头 2：宝丽来出片机构缓慢推出照片的连续电动转动声、相纸滑过出口的轻柔滑动声、马达停止时的轻微机械咔哒声、罗科低低满足的笑、露露轻声的“看”、沙发吱呀、远处房间氛围声。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、镜头之间无淡入淡出、镜头之间无溶接、无柔和转场、无摆拍、无现代智能手机、无单反（DSLR）、无数码相机、无其他人、无嘴对嘴接吻、不得移除红色鼻贴、无面部变形、无角色身份漂移、无多余手指、无畸形手部结构、相机握持不得错误、不得持相机前端镜头、不得倒持相机、无文字、无字幕、宝丽来上无水印、无标志、无摇晃宝丽来、无挥舞宝丽来、无空白白色照片、无未显影照片、镜头 2 开始时她手中不得已有照片、无照片凭空出现。
