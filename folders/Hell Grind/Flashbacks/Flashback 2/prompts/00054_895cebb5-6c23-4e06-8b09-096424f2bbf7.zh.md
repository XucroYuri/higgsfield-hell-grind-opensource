## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld extreme close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 
2.39:1, 35mm film grain, 100mm telephoto, extremely shallow depth of 
field, in the style of intimate domestic detail shots.

[CAMERA]
Subtle handheld, very close to his hand in her hair. Gentle natural 
breathing.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, warm dappled sunlight through curtain 
falling across his hand, her hair, dust particles in beams.

[CHARACTERS — hand and hair, both 28]
Roco's hand: olive skin, lean fingers with visible faint tattoos on 
forearm, small silver rings on two fingers, slow gentle movement 
through Lulu's dark brown wavy hair spread across his thigh in burgundy 
fabric.

[ACTION]
His fingers slowly comb through her hair, gathering a strand, letting 
it fall, gathering another. Tender, unconscious, automatic — the gesture 
of someone who has done this thousands of times. Her hair catches 
golden light. After 3 seconds, his fingertips trace the line of her 
temple gently. We hear her soft sigh from off-frame.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Faint distant city hum, soft sound of fingers through hair, 
her quiet sigh.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no faces visible, no rapid movement, no smooth gimbal, no 
deep focus, no extra fingers, no broken hand anatomy, no other people, 
no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持极特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照亲密的居家细节镜头。

[CAMERA]
轻微手持，极贴近他的手在她的头发里。柔和自然的呼吸。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，穿过窗帘的温暖斑驳阳光洒在他的手、她的头发上，光束中尘埃颗粒。

[CHARACTERS — 手与发，两人皆 28 岁]
罗科的手：橄榄色皮肤，修长手指，前臂可见浅淡纹身，两根手指戴着小银戒，缓慢轻柔地穿过铺在他酒红色布料大腿上的深褐波浪发。

[ACTION]
他的手指慢慢梳理她的头发，聚起一缕，任其落下，再聚起另一缕。温柔、无意识、自动——一个做过千百次这番动作之人的手势。她的头发捕捉金色光线。三秒后，他的指尖轻轻描过她的太阳穴。我们听到她画外轻柔的叹息。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。微弱的远处都市嗡鸣、手指穿过头发的柔和声响、她轻轻的叹息。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无可见的面部、无快速运动、无平滑稳定器、无深焦、无多余手指、无畸形手部结构、无其他人、无文字。
