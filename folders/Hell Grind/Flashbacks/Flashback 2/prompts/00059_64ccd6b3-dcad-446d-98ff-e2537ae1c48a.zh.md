## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, 100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the 
style of Lukas Dhont's "Close".

[CAMERA]
Subtle handheld, side-back angle on their touching shoulders. Camera 
behind and to the side, framing both their shoulders, necks, partial 
hair from behind. Tree trunk visible behind them in soft focus.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, dappled tree shadows on their backs and 
necks.

[ACTION]
Lulu's blue-and-white striped shirt shoulder pressed against Roco's 
dark gray hoodie shoulder. Visible: her dark brown hair falling on 
her shoulder, his messy black hair, the back of their necks. They 
breathe softly. After 2 seconds, Lulu shifts a fraction closer — 
her shoulder presses slightly more into his. He doesn't move, but 
his shoulder leans back into hers. Quiet pressure.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Soft wind through leaves, distant birds, faint city hum, 
the soft rustle of cotton fabric.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no faces visible, no turning around, no embracing, no 
kissing, no rapid movement, no smooth gimbal, no deep focus, no text, 
no other people.

## 中文对照 (zh)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照卢卡斯·德霍特（Lukas Dhont）的《亲密》（Close）。

[CAMERA]
轻微手持，对他们相触肩膀的侧后方角度。镜头在后侧方，框取两人的肩、颈、些许后方发丝。身后树干柔和失焦可见。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，斑驳树影落在他们的背与颈上。

[ACTION]
露露的蓝白条纹衬衫肩贴着罗科的深灰卫衣肩。可见：她垂在肩上的深褐头发、他凌乱的黑发、两人后颈。他们轻柔呼吸。两秒后，露露往他那边挪近一分——她的肩微微更深地压入他的肩。他没有动，但他的肩朝她的肩回靠过去。安静的受力。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。穿过树叶的轻柔风、远处鸟鸣、轻微都市嗡鸣、棉布柔和的窸窣。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无可见的面部、无转身、无拥抱、无接吻、无快速运动、无平滑稳定器、无深焦、无文字、无其他人。
