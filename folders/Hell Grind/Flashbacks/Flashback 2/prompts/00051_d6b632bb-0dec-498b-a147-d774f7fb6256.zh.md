## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of intimate 
domestic chaos.

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera, close to the action on the sofa, operator 
reacting to their movement. Loose energy, slight motion blur, alive 
bouncing.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour through gauzy curtains, warm dappled 
sunlight, dust particles in beams, deep red wall with posters in 
background bokeh.

[CHARACTERS — both 28]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length wavy hair flying, pale skin flushed 
  pink, wide grin showing teeth, oversized burgundy and black vertical 
  striped button-up shirt as a long shirt-dress, barefoot, small ear 
  cuff earring.
- ROCO: lean toned shirtless torso with visible tattoos on arms and 
  ribs, longer messy black hair, faint mustache, olive skin, deep 
  burgundy red distressed jeans, small red adhesive bandage strip 
  across the bridge of his nose.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Roco lying on his back on the dark blue velvet sofa, scrolling 
       on his phone, relaxed.
1.0s — Lulu LUNGES into the frame from off-screen, jumps onto him 
       laughing wildly, pinning him.
2.0s — Roco's phone flies out of his hand, he laughs in shocked 
       surprise — loud, real, open laugh.
3.0s — Lulu starts tickling his ribs aggressively, hair falling into 
       her face, she's hysterical with laughter.
4.0s — Roco squirms, laughs louder, tries to grab her wrists.
5.0s — Frame cuts mid-action, both mid-laugh.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Loud uninhibited laughter from both, the thump of her landing 
on him, sofa creaking, phone falling onto cushions, their breathless 
gasping laughs.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no static camera, no 
posed action, no fake laughter, no other people, no kissing, no 
sexual undertones, no rapid focus changes, no full body shot from 
distance, no removal of red nose bandage, no facial morphing, no 
text.
