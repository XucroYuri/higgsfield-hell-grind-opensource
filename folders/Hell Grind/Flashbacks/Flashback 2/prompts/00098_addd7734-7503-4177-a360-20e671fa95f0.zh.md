[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous 15-second shot. Anamorphic 2.39:1. Three full 360° 
clockwise orbital rotations around two embracing figures — one rotation 
per life chapter (5s each). Smooth gimbal motion at eye-level, intimate 
medium close-up framing. Subtle organic breathing layered over the 
orbit — alive, never shaky. 35mm film grain. Arri Alexa with anamorphic 
lenses. Shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh. Two seamless hidden match 
cuts at 5.0s and 10.0s, executed when the camera is directly behind 
them and only their backs are visible. Final second: camera decelerates 
to a soft stop facing them.

[CONSTANTS — NEVER CHANGE ACROSS ALL THREE CHAPTERS]
- Embrace pose: Lulu's face pressed into Roco's right shoulder, eyes 
  closed. Roco's right hand cradles the back of Lulu's head, fingers 
  in her hair. His left arm wraps her back.
- Roco wears a small red adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of 
  his nose — present in every single frame, all three ages, never 
  removed, never altered.
- Frame composition: figures centered, same height ratio in frame, 
  same rotational speed across all three chapters.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, NO MUSIC]
Only natural environmental sound that exists in each location. No 
score, no instruments, no melody. Their slow synchronized breathing 
audible throughout all 15 seconds, the most consistent sonic thread.
- Chapter 1: faint institutional silence, distant wind, dry leaves 
  scraping concrete.
- Chapter 2: open rooftop wind, distant city hum far below, occasional 
  metallic creak from the satellite tower.
- Chapter 3: warm interior room tone, faint muffled city through 
  windows, soft creak of wooden floor.
At each match cut, ambient sound transitions seamlessly, no musical 
sting, no whoosh, just the natural shift of acoustic space.
