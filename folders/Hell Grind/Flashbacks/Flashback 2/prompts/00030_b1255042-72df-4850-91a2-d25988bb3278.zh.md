## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of 
chasing handheld energy.

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera, operator running BACKWARDS through 
the grass facing them, camera pointed at them as they run toward 
lens. The operator stumbles slightly through the grass — real, 
imperfect, alive. Heavy bouncing, occasional dips and swings as 
the operator navigates the field running in reverse. Frame: 
medium shot of both running TOWARD camera, golden field stretching 
behind them, distant tree and Hong Kong skyline behind them in 
soft focus.

[LIGHT]
Golden hour, warm low sun behind them creating dramatic backlight 
and golden halos around their hair and shoulders, lens flares from 
the sun catching in the lens.

[ACTION]
Both running toward camera through tall grass, hand in hand, laughing, 
hair flying. Lulu pulls slightly ahead, dragging Roco along by the 
hand, looking back at him laughing. Roco laughs, almost trips on a 
tuft of grass, recovers, keeps running. They get closer to camera, 
filling more of the frame, energy building.

Both 15, same outfits.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Running footsteps on grass and dirt, loud breathless 
laughter, grass rustling violently, the operator's breath and 
occasional grunts from running backwards, distant birds, faint city 
hum.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no stabilization, no 
static camera, no aerial shots, no other people, no facial hair on 
Roco, no jewelry, no choker, no removal of red nose bandage, no 
posed running, no looking directly at camera lens for too long, no 
text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续手持镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，50mm 镜头，浅景深，风格参照追逐式手持的能量。

[CAMERA]
活跃生动的手持摄影机，摄影师在草丛中倒着向后奔跑迎向他们，镜头指向朝镜头跑来的他们。摄影师在草丛中轻微踉跄——真实、不完美、有生命力。沉重的弹跳，摄影师倒着穿行田野时偶尔的下坠与摆动。取景：两人朝镜头迎面跑来的中景，身后金色田野延展、远处树与香港天际线在后柔焦。

[LIGHT]
黄金时刻，温暖低角度阳光在他们身后形成戏剧性的逆光，头发与肩膀周围金色光晕，阳光进入镜头产生耀斑。

[ACTION]
两人牵着手穿过高草朝镜头跑来，笑着，头发飞舞。露露稍稍领先，牵着他往前拽，回头看他笑着。罗科笑着，几乎被一丛草绊倒，稳住，继续跑。他们靠近镜头，填满更多画面，能量积聚。

两人皆 15 岁，着装同前。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。草丛与泥土上的奔跑脚步声、响亮的喘气笑声、草丛剧烈窸窣、倒跑摄影师的呼吸与偶尔的闷哼、远处鸟鸣、轻微都市嗡鸣。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、无稳定化、无静态镜头、无航拍、无其他人、罗科无面部毛发、无珠宝、无项圈、不得移除红色鼻贴、无刻意的奔跑摆拍、不得长时间直视镜头、无文字。
