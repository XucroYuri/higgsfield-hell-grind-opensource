[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, 100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the 
style of Sofia Coppola's tender portraits and Wong Kar-wai's golden 
hour intimacy.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, low angle from her side looking up at Roco 
sitting against the sofa back. Gentle natural breathing motion.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, warm sunlight from window-side hitting 
half of his face, the other half in soft warm shadow, dust particles 
floating in beams behind him, deep red wall with posters in soft bokeh 
behind.

[CHARACTER — Roco, 28]
Lean toned shirtless torso with visible tattoos on arms and ribs, 
longer messy black hair slightly damp and tousled, faint mustache, 
olive skin warm in golden light, small red adhesive bandage strip 
across the bridge of his nose, deep brown eyes looking down at Lulu 
(off-frame in his lap). Wearing only deep burgundy red worn-in 
distressed jeans. Hand visible in lower frame, in her hair.

[ACTION]
Roco looks down at Lulu on his lap (off-frame). His face is soft, 
relaxed, unguarded. After 2 seconds, the corner of his mouth lifts 
in a small private smile — barely there, just for himself, watching 
her. He slowly tilts his head back against the sofa, eyes still on 
her. Closes his eyes briefly, opens them again, still smiling 
softly. His chest rises and falls in slow content breath.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Faint distant city hum through window, his slow content 
breathing, soft creak of sofa.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no broad smiling, no laughing, no looking at camera, no 
turning toward camera, no removal of red nose bandage, no dialogue, 
no text, no rapid movement, no smooth gimbal, no deep focus, no 
other people, no facial morphing, no character identity drift.
