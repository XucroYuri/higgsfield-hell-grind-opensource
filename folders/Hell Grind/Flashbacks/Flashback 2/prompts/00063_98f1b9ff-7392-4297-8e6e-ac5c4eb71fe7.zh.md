[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, anamorphic 100mm telephoto lens, extremely shallow 
depth of field, cinematography in the style of Lukas Dhont's "Close" 
and Terrence Malick's intimate portraits.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, side angle on Lulu's profile. Camera at her 
eye level, very close. Gentle natural breathing motion. Frame: tight 
profile close-up of Lulu's face, dappled tree shadows on her skin, 
soft bokeh of green leaves and distant white-and-red striped Hong Kong 
skyscrapers far in background.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, dappled warm light through tree leaves 
falling across her face in moving patterns, soft warm side-light from 
the low sun.

[CHARACTER — Lulu, 15]
Awkward in-between teen age, dark brown shoulder-length hair with 
center part falling around her shoulders moving softly in breeze, 
soft rounded teen face, no makeup, pale skin lightly flushed, eyes 
looking forward toward distant city (off-frame), wearing oversized 
blue and white vertical striped button-up shirt open over white 
t-shirt visible at collar, thin silver chain bracelet on her wrist 
visible.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Lulu's profile, eyes looking forward at the distant city, 
       calm but inwardly alert.
2.0s — Her eyes flicker slightly, the smallest catch of breath as she 
       feels his hand on hers (off-frame).
3.0s — She does NOT turn toward him. She keeps looking forward. But 
       her lips part slightly, a slow deeper inhale.
4.0s — Her eyes close for half a second, then open. A faint ghost of 
       a smile at the corner of her mouth — almost invisible.
5.0s — Frame ends on her looking forward, lips slightly parted, soft 
       glow on her skin.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Soft wind through leaves, distant birds, faint city hum, 
her quiet deeper breath at 3.0s.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no smiling broadly, no laughing, no crying, no

makeup, no choker, no rapid focus changes, no smooth gimbal, no 
deep focus, no other people, no children, no adults, no facial 
morphing, no character identity drift, no posed beauty, no fashion 
shot.
