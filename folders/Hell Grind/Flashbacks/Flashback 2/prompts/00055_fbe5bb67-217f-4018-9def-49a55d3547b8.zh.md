## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, 100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the 
style of Sofia Coppola's warm intimate interiors.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, gentle natural breathing. Top-down close-up 
of Lulu's face resting on Roco's lap. Warm golden light pouring across 
her face in dappled patterns through curtain.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, warm sunlight streaming through gauzy 
curtains, dust particles floating in light beams, soft amber and warm 
peach color palette, deep wood and red bokeh in background.

[CHARACTER — Lulu, 28]
Dark brown shoulder-length hair with soft natural waves spread across 
the dark fabric of his thigh, pale skin warm in golden light, faint 
gentle lines around her eyes, small ear cuff earring catching light, 
wearing an oversized burgundy and black vertical striped button-up 
shirt as a long shirt-dress. Bare legs visible in lower frame, barefoot.

[ACTION]
Lulu lies on her back on the dark blue velvet sofa, head resting on 
Roco's thigh (his torso visible in soft focus above frame). She looks 
up — not at him, just at the ceiling, eyes soft and unfocused. After 
2 seconds, a slow real smile spreads across her face — small, private, 
as if remembering something good. Roco's hand visible entering frame, 
fingers slowly running through her hair. She closes her eyes briefly 
into the touch.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Faint distant city hum through window, soft creak of sofa 
fabric, her quiet exhale.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no sad atmosphere, no rapid movement, no smooth gimbal, no 
deep focus, no full body shot, no other people, no dialogue, no text, 
no broad laughing, no kissing, no facial morphing.

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照索菲亚·科波拉温暖私密的室内空间。

[CAMERA]
轻微有机手持，柔和自然的呼吸。对露露躺在罗科腿上的脸做俯拍特写。穿过窗帘的斑驳图案中，温暖金色光线流泻在她的脸上。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，温暖阳光穿过薄纱窗帘流射进来，光束中尘埃漂浮，柔和琥珀与温暖蜜桃色系，背景深色木质与红色虚化。

[CHARACTER — 露露，28 岁]
深褐色齐肩发带柔和自然波浪，铺在他深色布料的大腿上，金色光线中温暖的苍白皮肤，眼角淡而温柔的纹路，捕捉光线的小耳骨夹耳饰，穿着作为长衬衫裙的宽大酒红与黑色竖条纹系扣衬衫。下部画面可见光裸的腿、光着脚。

[ACTION]
露露仰躺在深蓝色丝绒沙发上，头枕在罗科大腿上（他的躯干在上方画面柔和失焦）。她向上看——不是看他，只是看着天花板，眼神柔和、失焦。两秒后，一个缓慢真实的微笑在她脸上漾开——微小、私密，仿佛想起了什么美好的事。罗科的手可见入画，手指缓缓穿过她的头发。她用闭眼回应这个触碰。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。透过窗户的微弱远处都市嗡鸣、沙发布料轻柔吱呀、她轻轻的呼气。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无伤感的氛围、无快速运动、无平滑稳定器、无深焦、无全身镜头、无其他人、无对白、无文字、无咧嘴大笑、无接吻、无面部变形。
