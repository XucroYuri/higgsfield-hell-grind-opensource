## 原文 (source)

Two-shot sequence connected by hard cut, 10 seconds total, anamorphic 
2.39:1, 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field throughout, in the 
style of intimate domestic moments.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT 1 — SELFIE AND CLICK — 0.0s to 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera close to them on the dark blue velvet 
sofa, slightly above shoulder level, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field. 
Frame: medium close-up of both their faces pressed together 
cheek-to-cheek, looking at the polaroid camera held in Lulu's 
outstretched hand at the front of the frame.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour through gauzy curtains, warm sunlight 
catching their faces, dust particles in beams, deep red wall with 
posters in soft warm bokeh behind them.

[CHARACTERS — both 28]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length wavy hair, pale skin warm in golden 
  light, oversized burgundy and black vertical striped button-up 
  shirt-dress, ear cuff earring catching light, holding a vintage 
  white-and-rainbow-stripe Polaroid OneStep camera in her outstretched 
  hand at arm's length.
- ROCO: lean toned shirtless torso with visible tattoos on arms and 
  ribs, longer messy black hair, faint mustache, deep brown eyes, 
  small red adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose. 
  Pressed cheek-to-cheek with Lulu, his arm wrapped around her 
  shoulders.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Lulu holds out the polaroid camera at arm's length. Both 
       leaning cheek-to-cheek, Roco grinning. "One, two —" Lulu starts.
1.5s — Roco suddenly TURNS and licks her cheek playfully — she 
       SHRIEKS laughing, recoils, hits his arm.
2.5s — Roco laughs hard, pulls her back. She wipes her cheek 
       dramatically on his shoulder, fake angry look.
3.5s — Both lean cheek-to-cheek again. Lulu still laughing — "Don't 
       you dare, idiot —" Roco grins wide. CLICK — shutter fires, 
       small flash bursts.
4.5s — The polaroid film WHIRRS out of the bottom of the camera — 
       a fully developed photo emerges, the picture of them already 
       visible on it. Lulu pulls it out of the slot.
5.0s — Frame ends on the developed polaroid emerging in her fingers.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HARD CUT AT 5.0s — instant frame swap, no fade, no dissolve
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SHOT 2 — HOLDING THE DEVELOPED POLAROID — 5.0s to 10.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld extreme close-up, 100mm telephoto, extremely 
shallow depth of field. Almost no movement, just gentle natural 
breathing of operator. Frame: extreme close-up of the polaroid in 
her pale fingers, edges of her burgundy striped shirt-dress in soft 
bokeh.

[LIGHT]
Same warm golden hour light, dappled sun, dust particles in beams.

[ACTION]
5.0s — Close-up of the fully developed polaroid in Lulu's hand. The 
       photo clearly shows Lulu and Roco cheek-to-cheek, grinning, 
       her cheek slightly damp from the lick, his wide grin, warm 
       golden light captured in the moment. White polaroid border 
       visible around the image.
6.5s — Lulu's wrist gives ONE small gentle wave — a soft single 
       motion. Roco's voice off-frame, quietly laughing: "You're 
       not supposed to shake it, that's a myth." Lulu, off-frame: 
       "Shut up."
8.0s — She holds the polaroid completely still now, both observing 
       the captured photo. Image fully readable, sharp.
9.5s — Roco's hand enters frame from off-side, his finger gently 
       traces the white border of the photo. Lulu's quiet "Look" 
       off-frame.
10.0s — Frame holds on the developed polaroid in her fingers, Roco's 
        finger at its edge. The captured moment of joy fully visible.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY, CONTINUOUS ACROSS BOTH SHOTS]
No music. Shot 1: Lulu's "One, two —", her shriek of laughter, Roco's 
laugh, the click of the polaroid shutter, small electronic whirr of 
film ejecting. Shot 2: a single soft flick of polaroid paper, Roco's 
quiet line "You're not supposed to shake it, that's a myth", Lulu's 
"Shut up", Lulu's quiet "Look", Roco's soft satisfied laugh, sofa 
creak, distant room tone.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no fade between shots, 
no dissolve between shots, no soft transition, no posed shots, no 
modern smartphone, no DSLR, no digital camera, no other people, no 
kissing on mouth, no removal of red nose bandage, no facial morphing, 
no character identity drift, no extra fingers, no broken hand anatomy, 
no text, no captions, no watermarks on polaroid, no logos, no 
violent shaking, no rapid shaking, no blank polaroid, no white 
undeveloped photo, no developing animation, no photo emerging from 
white to image, no chemical bloom transition.

## 中文对照 (zh)

硬切连接的连续双镜头序列，总长 10 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，全程浅景深，风格参照亲密的居家时刻。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
镜头 1——自拍与按下快门——0.0s 至 5.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
贴近深蓝色丝绒沙发的活跃手持摄影机，略高于肩部，50mm 镜头，浅景深。取景：两人脸颊紧贴的中近特写，望向露露伸出、置于画面前方的宝丽来相机。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻透过薄纱窗帘，温暖阳光洒在两人脸上，光束中尘埃漂浮，身后深红墙面与海报化作柔和温暖的虚化背景。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 28 岁]
- 露露（LULU）：深褐色中长波浪发，苍白皮肤在金色光线中泛暖，宽大的酒红色与黑色竖条纹系扣衬衫裙，耳骨夹耳饰捕捉光线，一手伸直握着复古白底彩虹条纹的宝丽来 OneStep 相机。
- 罗科（ROCO）：精瘦有力的赤裸上身，手臂与肋骨处可见纹身，略长凌乱的黑发，浅淡胡须，深棕眼眸，鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。与露露脸颊相贴，手臂环抱她的肩膀。

[ACTION]
0.0s——露露伸直手臂举起宝丽来相机。两人脸颊相贴，罗科咧嘴笑着。“一、二——”露露开口。
1.5s——罗科突然转头俏皮地舔她的脸颊——她惊声大笑、退开、捶打他的手臂。
2.5s——罗科开怀大笑，把她拉回来。她戏剧化地在他肩头擦脸，仍笑着，摆出假怒的表情。
3.5s——两人再次脸颊相贴朝向相机。露露仍笑着——“你敢，笨蛋——”罗科咧嘴大笑。咔嚓——宝丽来快门按下，小小闪光迸发。
4.5s——宝丽来相纸从相机底部嗡鸣吐出——一张已完全显影的照片浮现，上面两人的影像已然可见。露露把它从出口抽出。
5.0s——镜头定格在她指间滑出的已显影宝丽来照片。

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
5.0s 硬切——瞬间换帧，无淡入淡出、无溶接
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
镜头 2——手持已显影的宝丽来——5.0s 至 10.0s
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CAMERA]
轻微有机手持的极特写，100mm 长焦，极浅景深。几乎无运动，仅有摄影师柔和的自然呼吸。取景：她苍白手指间宝丽来的极特写，酒红色条纹衬衫裙边缘化作柔和虚化。

[LIGHT]
同样温暖的黄金时光线，斑驳阳光，光束中尘埃漂浮。

[ACTION]
5.0s——露露手中已完全显影宝丽来的特写。照片清晰显示露露与罗科脸颊相贴、咧嘴而笑，她的脸颊因那一舔略湿，他笑容大张，瞬间捕捉到温暖金色光线。影像四周可见白色宝丽来边框。
6.5s——露露的手腕做了唯一一次轻柔的晃动——一个柔和而简单的动作。罗科画外音，低声笑着：“不该去晃它，那是谣言。”露露，画外音：“闭嘴。”
8.0s——她现在完全静止地举着宝丽来，两人一同端详捕捉到的照片。影像清晰可读、锐利。
9.5s——罗科的手从画面侧方入画，他的手指轻轻描着照片的白色边框。露露画外音轻声“看”。
10.0s——镜头停在她指间已显影的宝丽来上，罗科的手指抵在边缘。捕捉到的欢乐瞬间完整可见。

[SOUND — 仅同期声，横跨两镜头连续]
无音乐。镜头 1：露露的“一、二——”、她的惊声大笑、罗科的笑、宝丽来快门声、相纸吐出的轻微电子嗡鸣。镜头 2：宝丽来相纸的一下轻柔弹动、罗科低声“你不该晃它，那是谣言”、露露的“闭嘴”、露露轻声的“看”、罗科柔和满足的笑、沙发吱呀、远处房间氛围声。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、镜头之间无淡入淡出、镜头之间无溶接、无柔和转场、无摆拍、无现代智能手机、无单反、无数码相机、无其他人、无嘴对嘴接吻、不得移除红色鼻贴、无面部变形、无角色身份漂移、无多余手指、无畸形手部结构、无文字、无字幕、宝丽来上无水印、无标志、无剧烈摇晃、无快速摇晃、无空白宝丽来、无白色未显影照片、无显影动画、无照片从白色渐显为影像、无化学显影过渡效果。
