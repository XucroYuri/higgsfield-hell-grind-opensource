## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field.

Handheld camera close to them on the dark blue velvet sofa. Lulu and 
Roco sit close together on the sofa, sharing one big bowl of noodles 
between them — both eating with chopsticks from the same bowl. Lulu 
takes a bite, immediately steals a noodle from Roco's chopsticks 
mid-bite — he laughs, mock-offended, tries to grab the noodle back 
with his chopsticks. They fight over the noodle laughing. Lulu wins, 
slurps the noodle triumphantly, kisses his cheek quickly. Roco 
laughs, shakes his head, takes another bite. Domestic chaos, small, 
real.

Both 28. Lulu: dark brown hair, burgundy striped shirt-dress, 
barefoot, ear cuff earring. Roco: lean shirtless torso with tattoos, 
longer messy black hair, faint mustache, deep burgundy distressed 
jeans, small red bandage on nose.

Warm golden hour through gauzy curtains, dust in beams, deep red 
wall with posters, soft warm bokeh.

NO music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no posed eating, no full 
body shot from distance, no other people, no removal of red nose 
bandage, no text.

单次连续手持特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，50mm 镜头，浅景深。

手持镜头贴近他们在深蓝色丝绒沙发上。露露与罗科紧挨着坐在沙发上，共分一大碗面条——两人都用筷子从同一只碗里吃东西。露露吃了一口，随即在罗科吃了一半时偷走他筷子上的一根面条——他笑了，假装被冒犯，想用筷子抢回来。他们笑着为这根面条争抢。露露赢了，得意地吸溜着面条，快速亲了他的脸颊一下。罗科笑了，摇摇头，又吃了一口。居家式的混乱，微小、真实。

两人皆 28 岁。露露：深褐色头发，酒红色条纹衬衫裙，光着脚，耳骨夹耳饰。罗科：带纹身的精瘦赤裸上身，更长凌乱的黑发，浅淡胡须，深酒红色做旧牛仔裤，鼻子上小红创可贴。
