## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of Wong 
Kar-wai's neon noir.

Handheld camera follows Lulu from low angle as she runs ahead of Roco 
through wet alley. She suddenly STOMPS BOTH FEET into a large puddle 
deliberately — water EXPLODES upward in a huge splash catching pink 
and red neon. She laughs wildly, turns to look back at Roco. Roco 
catches up, gets splashed, shouts laughing, splashes her back. They 
both stomp in the puddle together like kids, neon-lit water flying 
everywhere. Pure joy, pure chaos.

Both 15. Lulu in striped shirt and white skirt, blue sneakers soaking 
wet. Roco in gray hoodie, black pants, dark sneakers, red bandage on 
nose, no mustache.

NO music, no slow motion, no posed action, no smooth gimbal, no other 
people, no facial hair on Roco, no jewelry, no text.

手持镜头从低机位跟随露露穿过湿巷子，她领先罗科。她突然故意把双脚狠狠跺进一个大水洼——水向上炸开，巨大的水花捕捉到粉红与红色霓虹。她狂笑着，回头看向罗科。罗科追上，被溅到水，喊着笑着，回泼她。两人像孩子一样一起踩进水洼，霓虹照亮的水花四处飞溅。纯粹的欢乐、纯粹的混乱。

两人皆 15 岁。露露：条纹衬衫与白裙，湿透的蓝色运动鞋。罗科：灰卫衣、黑裤、深色运动鞋，鼻子上红色创可贴，无胡须。

无音乐、无慢动作、无刻意的动作摆拍、无平滑稳定器、无其他人、罗科无面部毛发、无珠宝、无文字。
