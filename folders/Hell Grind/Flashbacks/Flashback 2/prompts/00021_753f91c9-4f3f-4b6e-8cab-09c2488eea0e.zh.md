## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld extreme close-up shot, 10 seconds, anamorphic 
2.39:1, 35mm film grain, 100mm telephoto, extremely shallow depth of 
field, in the style of intimate domestic detail shots.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, very close to Lulu's hand holding the polaroid. 
Camera bounces slightly when she shakes the photo, then settles into 
gentle natural breathing as the image develops. Frame: extreme close-up 
of the white polaroid square in her pale fingers, edges of her burgundy 
striped shirt-dress in soft bokeh, warm golden hour light bathing 
everything, dust particles floating in beams.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour through gauzy curtains, warm dappled sunlight 
catching the white photo paper and her skin, dust particles in light beams.

[CHARACTER — Lulu's hand, 28]
Pale slender fingers holding a vintage Polaroid square photo by its 
white border, small ear cuff earring catching light at edge of frame, 
edge of oversized burgundy and black vertical striped shirt-dress sleeve.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Close-up of the blank white polaroid square in Lulu's hand. 
       Pure white, slightly milky, completely undeveloped. Her thumb 
       at the white border edge.

1.0s — Lulu FLICKS her wrist, shaking the polaroid playfully back and 
       forth in frame. The photo blurs with motion. Roco's voice 
       off-frame, laughing: "You're not supposed to shake it, that's 
       a myth."

2.5s — She shakes it again, more deliberately, grinning off-frame. Her 
       voice, also off-frame: "Shut up, it works." Another quick flick.

3.5s — She slows down, holds the polaroid more still, examining it. 
       The white surface still mostly blank, just the faintest hints 
       of shadow beginning to emerge.

5.0s — The polaroid now nearly still in her fingers. Faint outlines of 
       two heads emerging through the milky haze. Pale skin tones 
       starting to push through, the dark mass of hair forming.

6.5s — Image continues to develop. We start to see the shape of two 
       faces cheek-to-cheek emerging from the chemical haze.

8.0s — The image becomes much clearer — Lulu and Roco visible, 
       cheek-to-cheek, grinning. Lulu's slightly damp cheek from the 
       lick, Roco's wide grin. Warm golden light from the captured 
       moment finally readable.

9.0s — Image is fully developed, fully sharp. A perfect frozen second 
       of pure happiness. Roco's hand enters frame from off-side, his 
       finger gently traces the edge of the photo. Lulu's quiet "Look" 
       off-frame.

10.0s — Frame holds on the fully developed polaroid in her hand, the 
        captured moment of laughter completely visible. A small artifact 
        of joy.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. The soft flick of polaroid paper through air during the shaking, 
Lulu's off-frame laugh, Roco's off-frame line "You're not supposed to 
shake it, that's a myth", Lulu's reply "Shut up, it works", the faint 
chemical hiss of polaroid developing, the subtle rustle of fingers on 
paper, Lulu's quiet "Look" near the end, Roco's soft satisfied laugh 
off-frame, sofa creak, distant room tone.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no faces visible in real 
scene (only on the photo), no other people, no violent rapid shaking, 
no text, no captions, no watermarks on polaroid, no logos, no extra 
fingers, no broken hand anatomy, no facial morphing of the people on 
the photo, no character identity drift on the photo.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续手持极特写镜头，10 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照亲密的居家细节镜头。

[CAMERA]
轻微有机手持，极贴近露露手持宝丽来的手。她摇晃相片时镜头微微跳动，随后在影像显影时归于柔和的自然呼吸。取景：她苍白手指间白色宝丽来方片的极特写，酒红色条纹衬衫裙边缘化作柔和虚化，温暖黄金时刻光线沐浴一切，光束中尘埃漂浮。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻透过薄纱窗帘，温暖斑驳的阳光洒在白色相纸与她皮肤上，光束中尘埃漂浮。

[CHARACTER — 露露的手，28 岁]
苍白纤细的手指，捏着复古宝丽来方片照片的白色边框，画面边缘处小小耳骨夹捕捉光线，宽大酒红与黑色竖条纹衬衫裙的袖口边缘可见。

[ACTION]
0.0s——露露手中空白白色宝丽来方片的特写。纯白、略带乳白、完全未显影。拇指抵着白色边框边缘。

1.0s——露露弹动手腕，俏皮地来回晃动画面中的宝丽来。相片因运动而模糊。罗科画外音，笑着：“你不该晃它，那是谣言。”

2.5s——她又晃了一次，更刻意，画外咧嘴而笑。她的声音，同样画外：“闭嘴，这招管用。”又快速弹了一下。

3.5s——她放慢动作，将宝丽来举得更稳，端详着它。白色表面仍近乎空白，只有最微弱的阴影开始浮现。

5.0s——宝丽来此刻在她指间近乎静止。乳白雾气中浮现两颗头的朦胧轮廓。苍白肤色开始透出，黑色发团成形。

6.5s——影像继续显影。我们开始看到两张脸颊相贴的面部形状从化学雾气中浮现。

8.0s——影像清晰许多——可见露露与罗科，脸颊相贴、咧嘴而笑。露露因那一舔微湿的脸颊，罗科大张的笑容。捕捉到的温暖金色光线终于可辨。

9.0s——影像完全显影、完全锐利。纯粹快乐定格的一瞬。罗科的手从画面侧方入画，手指轻描相片边缘。露露画外音轻声“看”。

10.0s——镜头停在她手中完全显影的宝丽来上，捕捉到的欢笑瞬间完整可见。一件小小的快乐之物。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。摇晃时宝丽来相纸在空中轻柔弹动、露露画外笑声、罗科画外那句话“你不该晃它，那是谣言”、露露的回答“闭嘴，这招管用”、宝丽来显影的微弱化学嘶声、手指摩挲相纸的细微窸窣、临近结束时露露轻声的“看”、罗科画外柔和满足的笑、沙发吱呀、远处房间氛围声。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、真实场景中无可见面部（仅照片上有）、无其他人、无剧烈快速摇晃、无文字、无字幕、宝丽来上无水印、无标志、无多余手指、无畸形手部结构、照片上人物无面部变形、照片上无角色身份漂移。
