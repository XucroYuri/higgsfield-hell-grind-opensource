## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of 
intimate domestic moments.

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera close to them on the dark blue velvet 
sofa, slightly above shoulder level. Frame: medium close-up of both 
their faces pressed together cheek-to-cheek, looking at the polaroid 
camera held in Lulu's outstretched hand at the front of the frame.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour through gauzy curtains, warm sunlight 
catching their faces, dust particles in beams, deep red wall with 
posters in soft warm bokeh behind them.

[CHARACTERS — both 28]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length wavy hair, pale skin warm in golden 
  light, oversized burgundy and black vertical striped button-up 
  shirt-dress, ear cuff earring catching light, holding a vintage 
  white-and-rainbow-stripe Polaroid OneStep camera in her outstretched 
  hand at arm's length.
- ROCO: lean toned shirtless torso with visible tattoos, longer messy 
  black hair, faint mustache, deep brown eyes, small red adhesive 
  bandage strip across the bridge of his nose. Pressed cheek-to-cheek 
  with Lulu, his arm wrapped around her shoulders.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Lulu holds out the polaroid camera in front of them. Both 
       leaning in close cheek-to-cheek, Roco grinning. "One, two — " 
       Lulu starts.
1.5s — Roco interrupts her by suddenly TURNING and licking her cheek 
       playfully — she SHRIEKS laughing, recoils, hits his arm.
2.5s — Roco laughs, pulls her back in. She wipes her cheek dramatically 
       on his shoulder, still laughing, gives him a fake angry look.
3.5s — They both lean cheek-to-cheek again toward the camera. Lulu, 
       still laughing — "Don't you dare, idiot — " Roco gives a wide 
       innocent grin. CLICK — the polaroid camera's shutter fires, 
       small flash bursts.
4.5s — The polaroid film WHIRRS out of the bottom of the camera — a 
       blank white square. Lulu pulls it out, both leaning over to 
       look at the developing photo in her hand.
5.0s — Frame cuts on them looking down at the developing polaroid, 
       grinning.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Lulu's "One, two —", her shriek of laughter, Roco's laugh, 
the click of the polaroid shutter, the small electronic whirr of the 
film ejecting, their breathless laughter, the soft creak of the sofa.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no posed shots, no 
modern smartphone, no DSLR camera, no digital camera, no fake 
laughter, no other people, no kissing on mouth, no removal of red 
nose bandage, no facial morphing, no character identity drift, no 
text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续手持特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，50mm 镜头，浅景深，风格参照亲密的居家时刻。

[CAMERA]
贴近深蓝色丝绒沙发的活跃手持摄影机，略高于肩部。取景：两人脸颊紧贴的中近特写，望向露露伸出、置于画面前方的宝丽来相机。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻透过薄纱窗帘，温暖阳光洒在两人脸上，光束中尘埃漂浮，身后深红墙面与海报化作柔和温暖的虚化背景。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 28 岁]
- 露露：深褐色中长波浪发，苍白皮肤在金色光线中泛暖，宽大的酒红色与黑色竖条纹系扣衬衫裙，耳骨夹耳饰捕捉光线，一手伸直握着复古白底彩虹条纹的宝丽来 OneStep 相机。
- 罗科：精瘦有力的赤裸上身，可见纹身，略长凌乱的黑发，浅淡胡须，深棕眼眸，鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。与露露脸颊相贴，手臂环抱她的肩膀。

[ACTION]
0.0s——露露把宝丽来相机举在两人面前。两人脸颊相贴近靠，罗科咧嘴笑着。“一、二——”露露开口。
1.5s——罗科突然转头俏皮地舔她的脸颊，打断她——她惊声大笑、退开、捶打他的手臂。
2.5s——罗科笑了，把她拉回身边。她戏剧化地在他肩头擦脸，仍笑着，给了他一个假怒的眼神。
3.5s——两人再次脸颊相贴朝向相机。露露仍笑着——“你敢，笨蛋——”罗科咧嘴笑得天真无邪。咔嚓——宝丽来快门按下，迸发小小闪光。
4.5s——宝丽来相纸从相机底部嗡鸣吐出——一张空白白片。露露把它抽出，两人凑过来看她手中正在显影的相片。
5.0s——镜头切在两人低头看着正在显影的宝丽来、咧嘴而笑之上。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。露露的“一、二——”、她的惊声大笑、罗科的笑、宝丽来快门声、相纸吐出的轻微电动嗡鸣、她们气喘吁吁的笑声、沙发轻吱呀。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、无摆拍、无现代智能手机、无单反相机、无数码相机、无假笑、无其他人、无嘴对嘴接吻、不得移除红色鼻贴、无面部变形、无角色身份漂移、无文字。
