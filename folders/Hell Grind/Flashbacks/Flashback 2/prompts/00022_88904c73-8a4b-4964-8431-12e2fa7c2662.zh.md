## 原文 (source)

Single continuous shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, 
100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the style of Lukas 
Dhont's "Close" — intimate moment dissolving into absence.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, the operator standing still in the grass. 
The camera does NOT move position — only gentle natural breathing 
motion of the operator, slight sways, alive but locked in space. 
Tight close-up of their two faces and shoulders, foreheads pressed 
together. Tall grass in soft foreground bokeh framing the shot. 
Distant Hong Kong skyline very softly out of focus in deep background.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, warm low sun catching their hair and 
the side of their faces in rim light, dappled tree shadows, dust 
and seed particles floating in beams, soft warm haze.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Tight close-up: Lulu and Roco standing in the grass, foreheads 
       pressed together, eyes closed, completely still. Lulu's hands 
       on his chest. Roco's hands cupping her elbows. Their breathing 
       is calm, synchronized.

2.0s — They hold this position. The camera breathes gently. The 
       world is quiet.

3.0s — Slowly, without breaking the foreheads-together connection, 
       they begin to TURN TOGETHER as one body, pivoting in place, 
       still leaning into each other. Their movement is slow, calm.

4.0s — Still touching, they begin to walk SIDEWAYS out of frame to 
       the right (or left) — moving laterally across the camera's 
       view, not toward or away from it. Their faces and bodies 
       leave the frame slowly.

5.0s — Frame ends on the empty space they left behind — tall golden 
       grass swaying gently, dappled tree shadows, distant city 
       softly behind, the spot where they just stood now empty. 
       Their footsteps faintly receding off-frame.

[CHARACTERS — both 15]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length hair, oversized blue-and-white 
  vertical striped button-up shirt over white t-shirt, white midi 
  skirt, white sandals, thin silver chain bracelets.
- ROCO: messy shaggy black hair, smooth cheeks (NO mustache, NO 
  stubble), olive skin, dark gray cotton zip-up hoodie open over 
  light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants with red stitching, 
  dark Vans-style sneakers. Small red adhesive bandage strip across 
  the bridge of his nose. No tattoos, no jewelry, no chains.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Their soft synchronized breathing at the start, gentle 
wind through grass, distant birds, faint city hum. As they leave 
frame: the soft rustle of grass parting around their bodies, faint 
footsteps walking off-frame, becoming quieter. The empty frame 
sounds only of wind and distant world.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no kissing, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no dolly, 
no track, no pull-back, no zoom, no camera movement of the operator, 
no operator walking, no aerial shot, no tilt, no other people in 
field, no facial hair on Roco, no mustache, no stubble, no tattoos, 
no jewelry, no chains, no choker, no makeup, no removal of red nose 
bandage, no characters turning toward camera, no looking back at 
camera, no running, no broad smiling, no laughing, no talking, no 
dialogue, no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照卢卡斯·德霍特（Lukas Dhont）的《亲密》（Close）——亲密时刻消融于缺席。

[CAMERA]
轻微有机手持，摄影师静止站在草丛中。摄影机不移动机位——仅有摄影师柔和自然的呼吸律动、微微摇摆，富有生命力却又锁定在空间中。两人脸部与肩部的紧特写，额头相抵。前景高草化作柔和虚化和框景。远处香港天际线在纵深背景中极度失焦。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，温暖低角度阳光以轮廓光勾勒发丝与侧脸，斑驳树影，光束中漂浮尘埃与种子颗粒，柔和温暖的薄雾。

[ACTION]
0.0s——紧特写：露露与罗科站在草丛中，额头相抵、双目紧闭、完全静止。露露的手搭在他胸口。罗科的手捧着她的肘部。呼吸平静而同步。

2.0s——他们保持这一姿势。镜头轻缓呼吸。世界一片静谧。

3.0s——慢慢地，没有打破额头相抵的连接，他们开始如同一个身体般一起转身，原地转动，仍彼此依偎。动作缓慢、平静。

4.0s——仍相触着，他们开始向画面右侧（或左侧）横向侧移走出画面——横跨摄影机视野横向移动，既不朝它走近也不远离。他们的脸与身体缓缓离开画面。

5.0s——镜头定格在他们身后留下的空地——金色高草轻轻摇曳、斑驳树影、远处城市柔柔在后，他们方才站立之处如今空无一人。脚步在画外隐约远去。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 15 岁]
- 露露：深褐色齐肩发，宽大的蓝白竖条纹系扣衬衫内搭白 T 恤，白色中长裙，白色凉鞋，纤细银链手链。
- 罗科：凌乱蓬松的黑发，光洁的脸颊（绝对无胡须、无胡茬），橄榄色皮肤，深灰色棉质拉链连帽卫衣敞开内搭浅灰 T 恤，黑色工装裤带红色缝线，深色 Vans 风格运动鞋。鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。开始时的轻柔同步呼吸，微风穿过草丛，远处鸟鸣，轻微都市嗡鸣。他们离开画面时：他们的身体分开草丛的轻柔窸窣、走离画面的隐约脚步声、渐弱。空旷的画面只剩下风与远处世界的声音。
