## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, 100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field.

Handheld camera close to ground level beside them. Lulu and Roco lying 
on their backs in tall golden grass and wildflowers, heads close 
together facing the sky. Lulu turns her head to look at him in profile, 
plucks a small wildflower beside her, places it gently on his cheek 
without saying anything. Roco's eyes flicker — he reaches up slowly, 
takes the flower, twirls it in his fingers, then places it carefully 
behind her ear. They both look up at the sky again, small smiles, 
shoulders touching. Soft golden light, dappled tree shadows.

Both 15. Lulu: striped shirt, white t-shirt, white skirt spread on 
grass, hair fanned out. Roco: dark gray hoodie, light gray t-shirt, 
no mustache, red bandage on nose.

NO music, no slow motion, no kissing, no smooth gimbal, no other 
people, no facial hair on Roco, no romance clichés, no text.
