## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, anamorphic 100mm telephoto lens, extremely shallow 
depth of field, cinematography in the style of Lukas Dhont's "Close" 
and intimate teenage portraits.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, side angle on Roco's profile. Camera at his 
eye level, very close. Gentle natural breathing motion. Frame: tight 
profile close-up of Roco's face, dappled tree shadows on his skin, 
soft bokeh of green leaves and distant white-and-red striped Hong 
Kong skyscrapers far in background.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, dappled warm light through tree leaves 
falling across his face in moving patterns, soft warm side-light 
from the low sun.

[CHARACTER — Roco, 15]
Awkward growing-into-his-body teen, messy shaggy black hair moving 
softly in breeze, smooth cheeks (NO mustache, NO stubble — too 
young), olive skin lightly flushed, deep brown eyes looking forward 
toward distant city (off-frame), small red adhesive bandage strip 
across the bridge of his nose, wearing a dark gray cotton zip-up 
hoodie open over a light gray t-shirt visible at collar. No tattoos, 
no jewelry, no chains.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Roco's profile, eyes looking forward at the distant city, 
       holding his breath slightly.
2.0s — Slow exhale through his nose. His fingers (off-frame) tighten 
       slightly around hers.
3.0s — He does NOT turn toward her. Keeps looking forward. But his 
       jaw softens.
4.0s — The corner of his mouth lifts in the smallest possible smile 
       — barely there, private, internal. Eyes still forward.
5.0s — Frame ends on him looking at the city, soft smile, golden 
       light on his face.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Soft wind through leaves, distant birds, faint city hum, 
his slow exhale at 2.0s.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no smiling broadly, no laughing, no crying, no 
turning head toward camera, no looking at camera, no kissing, no 
mustache, no stubble, no facial hair, no tattoos, no jewelry, no 
chains, no removal of red nose bandage, no rapid focus changes, no 
smooth gimbal, no deep focus, no other people, no children, no 
adults, no facial morphing, no character identity drift, no posed 
beauty, no fashion shot.
