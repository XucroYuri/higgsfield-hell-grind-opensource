## 原文 (source)

Cinematic intimate close-up, anamorphic 2.39:1 aspect ratio, subtle 
organic handheld camera with gentle breathing motion — almost 
imperceptible micro-movements as if held on the operator's shoulder, 
no shake, no jitter, slow natural sway synchronized with the 
characters' breathing, eye-level, three-quarter angle from the right

A 12-year-old girl, Lulu, presses her face into the shoulder of a 
12-year-old boy, Roco. We see her face in three-quarter view, cheek 
pressed against the rough fabric of his charcoal-gray cotton t-shirt. 
She has a sharp dark-brown bob with straight bangs slightly tousled by 
the wind, pale skin, delicate features, freckles barely visible across 
her nose. Her eyes are squeezed shut, her brow tense, her lips slightly 
parted from breathing hard — she just ran. A single strand of her hair 
catches the wind. The collar of her dusty-blue knit cardigan and cream 
turtleneck visible at the bottom of frame.

We see Roco partially — the side of his jaw, his ear, the messy shaggy 
black hair falling across his temple, olive skin. His right hand is 
visible cradling the back of Lulu's head, fingers gently buried in her 
hair, holding her like she might disappear. His jaw is tight, his eyes 
closed.

The frame is filled with their faces and the embrace. Behind them, far 
out of focus, the soft suggestion of a brick institutional building 
and gray sky. Almost no movement — only the faintest tremor of her 
shoulders from breathing, a strand of hair stirring, the rise and fall 
of breath.

Cinematography in the style of Lukas Dhont's "Close" and Céline Sciamma's 
"Portrait of a Lady on Fire", 35mm film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with 
anamorphic lenses, very shallow depth of field, soft natural skin tones, 
intimate emotional framing.

NEGATIVE: no camera movement, no zoom, no pan, no characters separating, 
no smiling, no text, no saturated colors, no sunny weather, no facial 
morphing, no sharp background, no other people.

## 中文对照 (zh)

电影感亲密的特写，变形 2.39:1 宽高比，细微有机的手持摄影机，带柔和呼吸律动——几乎不可察觉的微动作，仿佛搁在摄影师肩上、无抖动、无晃荡，缓慢自然的摇摆与角色的呼吸同步，视平高度，来自右侧的四分之三角度。

一个 12 岁女孩露露，把脸埋进一个 12 岁男孩罗科的肩里。我们看到她四分之三视角的脸，脸颊贴着他炭灰棉质 T 恤粗糙的布料。她有一头利落的深褐波波头配直刘海，被风略微吹乱，苍白皮肤、精致的五官、鼻翼两侧几乎看不见的几点雀斑。她紧闭双眼、眉头微蹙、因为大口喘气而嘴唇微张——她刚跑完。她的一缕头发捕捉到风。她灰蓝针织开衫与奶油色高领的领口在画面底部可见。

我们部分看到罗科——他的下颌侧面、耳朵、垂落太阳穴的凌乱蓬松黑发、橄榄色皮肤。他的右手可见，托着露露的后脑，手指轻轻埋在她的发间，抱着她仿佛她会消失。他的下颌紧绷、双眼紧闭。

画面被他们的脸与拥抱填满。他们身后，远远失焦处，是一座砖砌机构建筑与灰天空的柔和暗示。几乎无动作——只有她因呼吸而肩头最微弱的颤抖、一缕发丝的扰动、呼吸的起伏。

摄影风格参照卢卡斯·德霍特（Lukas Dhont）的《亲密》（Close）与瑟琳·席安玛（Céline Sciamma）的《燃烧女子的肖像》（Portrait of a Lady on Fire），35mm 胶片颗粒，Arri Alexa 配变形镜头拍摄，很浅景深，柔和自然的肤色，亲密的情感取景。

负面（NEGATIVE）：无镜头运动、无变焦、无平摇、无角色分离、无微笑、无文字、无饱和色彩、无晴好天气、无面部变形、无锐利背景、无其他人。
