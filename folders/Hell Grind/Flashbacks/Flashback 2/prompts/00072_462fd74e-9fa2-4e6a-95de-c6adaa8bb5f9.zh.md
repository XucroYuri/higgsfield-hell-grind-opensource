## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld extreme close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 
2.39:1, 35mm film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic lenses, 
extreme shallow depth of field with creamy neon bokeh, cinematography 
in the style of Wong Kar-wai's intimate detail shots and Christopher 
Doyle's handheld closeups.

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera, low angle, following their joined hands 
running through the wet city. Operator running behind them, camera 
pointed slightly down at their interlocked fingers and the wet asphalt 
streaking beneath their feet. Loose handheld breath, alive motion, 
the camera bounces gently with each footstep. Frame: extreme close-up 
on their joined hands centered, their forearms visible, wet asphalt 
and neon reflections rushing past in extreme bokeh below.

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
Late night Asian megacity backstreet, recently rained. Wet asphalt 
beneath their running feet reflecting streaks of neon — pink, red, 
electric blue — moving past in motion. Each splash they kick up 
catches a different colored highlight. Streetlamps overhead create 
intermittent pools of warm orange light passing across their hands.

[CHARACTERS — partial view, hands and forearms only]
Their two hands tightly interlocked, fingers laced together, running. 
- LULU's hand: pale, slender fingers, thin silver chain bracelet on 
  her wrist, dark blue denim jacket sleeve with patches at the 
  forearm.
- ROCO's hand: olive skin, slightly larger, stronger grip, gold chain 
  bracelet at his wrist, dark tattoos faintly visible on his forearm, 
  reddish-brown plaid short-sleeve shirt sleeve at the elbow. His 
  thumb subtly stroking the back of her hand even as they run.

We do NOT see their faces. Only their joined hands and forearms 
filling the frame.

[ACTION]
Their hands stay tightly clasped throughout the shot. The motion of 
running creates rhythmic micro-bounces in the grip. Occasionally one 
hand grips slightly tighter, the thumb shifts. Wet asphalt streaks 
beneath them in motion blur. A puddle splashes — droplets fly past 
their hands. The neon reflections beneath flash through pink, red, 
blue as they run through different signage zones.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music, no instruments. Their fast synchronized footsteps splashing 
through puddles, the rhythm of two pairs of feet hitting wet asphalt, 
breathless laughter from above the frame, distant city hum, far car 
horns.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no instruments, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, 
no static camera, no faces visible, no full body shot, no wide shot, 
no other people, no cars, no rain falling, no extra limbs, no extra 
fingers, no broken hand anatomy, no character identity drift, no 
hands separating, no children, no adults over 25, no text, no 
captions, no sharp focus on background, no deep depth of field, no 
melancholy, no fear.

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持极特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，用 Arri Alexa 配变形镜头拍摄，带奶油般霓虹虚化的极浅景深，摄影风格参照王家卫的亲密细节镜头与杜可风（Christopher Doyle）的手持特写。

[CAMERA]
活跃生动的手持摄影机，低机位，跟随他们奔跑中穿过湿城交握的双手。摄影师在他们身后奔跑，镜头微微朝下指向他们交扣的手指与脚下拖出的湿柏油路。松散的手持呼吸、生动的律动，镜头随每一步轻柔弹跳。取景：居中交握双手的极特写，前臂可见，下方湿柏油路与霓虹倒影化作极虚化奔流而过。

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
亚洲特大城市的深夜背巷，刚下过雨。他们奔跑的脚步下方湿柏油路反射霓虹拖影——粉红、红、电光蓝——流动着。他们踢起的每一下水花捕捉一种不同的彩色高光。头顶街灯在他们手上投下一阵一阵温暖的橙色光圈。

[CHARACTERS — 局部取景，仅手与前臂]
他们交扣手指紧握的双手，奔跑着。
- 露露的手：苍白、纤细的手指，手腕上纤细银链手链，前臂深蓝色带补丁的牛仔夹克袖口。
- 罗科的手：橄榄色皮肤，略大一圈，握得更有力，手腕金链手链，前臂隐约可见深色纹身，肘部红棕色格纹短袖衬衫袖口。即便奔跑中他的拇指也在轻柔摩挲她的手背。

我们看不到他们的脸。只有交握的双手与前臂填满画面。

[ACTION]
全片他们的双手保持紧握。奔跑的动作在握手中产生节奏性的微小弹跳。偶尔一只手握得更紧一些，拇指移位。湿柏油路在下方化作运动模糊拖过。一个水洼溅起——水珠飞过他们的手。他们跑过不同招牌区域时，脚下霓虹倒影闪烁经过粉红、红、蓝。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐、无乐器。他们快速同步的脚印踩进水洼、两双脚拍打湿柏油路的节奏、画面上方喘不过气的笑声、远处都市嗡鸣、遥远的汽车喇叭。
