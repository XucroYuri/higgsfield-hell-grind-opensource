## 原文 (source)

Single continuous shot, 7 seconds (or 5 if model limits), anamorphic 
2.39:1, 35mm film grain, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the 
style of Terrence Malick's "The Tree of Life" — intimacy dissolving 
into world.

[CAMERA]
Active organic handheld, the OPERATOR PHYSICALLY WALKING BACKWARDS 
through the tall grass — but VERY VERY SLOWLY. One small hesitant 
step every two or three seconds. Each footstep creates a subtle 
natural bounce. The camera operator is barely moving, almost 
reluctant to leave. Real human walking, not a dolly, not a track. 
The retreat is so slow it feels like time itself slowing down. The 
two figures stay centered in frame, the world gradually opening up 
around them as the operator inches backward.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, warm low sun catching their hair and 
shoulders in rim light, dappled tree shadows on them and the grass, 
dust and seed particles floating in beams, lens flares from the low 
sun.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Medium shot: Lulu and Roco standing in the grass, foreheads 
       pressed together, eyes closed, completely still. Lulu's hands 
       on his chest. Roco's hands cupping her elbows. Their breathing 
       is calm, synchronized.

1.5s — The operator takes the first VERY slow careful step backwards. 
       Frame jostles slightly with the step. The two figures stay 
       still, foreheads touching. We see slightly more grass around 
       them.

3.0s — Another small slow step backward. They are still motionless, 
       silent. We see them now from a few meters back, the field 
       opening up around them, the tree to one side, distant city 
       skyline beginning to appear in deep background.

4.5s — After holding the position for a beat, Lulu and Roco — without 
       breaking apart — slowly TURN TOGETHER and start walking 
       AWAY FROM THE CAMERA, hand in hand, into the deeper field 
       toward the horizon. They move with quiet calm steps through 
       the tall grass.

5.5s — They continue walking away, getting smaller. The camera 
       barely moves now — almost stopped. We watch them leave.

7.0s — They are far now, two small figures walking together into 
       the golden field, becoming silhouettes against the distant 
       hazy Hong Kong skyline. The frame ends with them small in 
       the vast landscape, walking on into the world without us.

[CHARACTERS — both 15]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length hair, oversized blue-and-white 
  vertical striped button-up shirt over white t-shirt, white midi 
  skirt swaying as she walks, white sandals, thin silver chain 
  bracelets.
- ROCO: messy shaggy black hair, smooth cheeks (NO mustache, NO 
  stubble), olive skin, dark gray cotton zip-up hoodie open over 
  light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants with red stitching, 
  dark Vans-style sneakers. Small red adhesive bandage strip across 
  the bridge of his nose. No tattoos, no jewelry, no chains.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Their soft synchronized breathing at the start, the very 
quiet crunch of the operator's careful footsteps in grass, gentle 
wind through the field, distant birds, faint distant city hum. 
Their footsteps walking away into the field becoming fainter. The 
sound of the world expanding around them as they leave.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no kissing, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no smooth 
dolly, no mechanical dolly, no smooth track, no smooth pull-back, 
no stabilization, no aerial drone shot, no tilt up to sky, no zoom 
out, no fast camera movement, no other people in field, no facial 
hair on Roco, no mustache, no stubble, no tattoos, no jewelry, no 
chains, no choker, no makeup, no removal of red nose bandage, no 
characters turning toward camera, no looking back at camera, no 
running, no broad smiling, no laughing, no talking, no dialogue, 
no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续镜头，7 秒（若模型限制则 5 秒），变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，50mm 镜头，浅景深，风格参照泰伦斯·马力克（Terrence Malick）的《生命之树》（The Tree of Life）——亲密消融于世界。

[CAMERA]
活跃的有机手持，摄影师真的在高草丛中倒着向后行走——但极慢极慢。每两三秒一个迟疑的小步。每走一步都带来细微的自然弹跳。摄影机的运动微乎其微，几乎是不舍地离开。真实的行走，不是推轨、不是轨道。后退如此缓慢，仿佛时间本身慢了下来。两个人影始终居于画面中央，随着摄影师一寸寸后退，四周的世界逐渐展开。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，温暖低角度阳光以轮廓光勾勒发丝与肩膀，斑驳树影洒在他们与草丛上，光束中漂浮尘埃与种子颗粒，低角度阳光产生镜头耀斑（lens flare）。

[ACTION]
0.0s——中景：露露与罗科站在草丛中，额头相抵、双目紧闭、完全静止。露露的手搭在他胸口。罗科的手捧着她的肘部。呼吸平静而同步。

1.5s——摄影师迈出第一步极慢的后退。画面随脚步轻微晃动。两人依旧静止，额头相触。我们看到更多四周的草丛。

3.0s——又一个小步后退。他们依旧静止、沉默。现在我们从几米外看他们，田野在他们四周展开，树在侧旁，远处的城市天际线开始在纵深背景中浮现。

4.5s——在保持姿势一拍之后，露露与罗科——没有分开彼此——缓缓一起转身，开始朝远离镜头的方向、手牵手走向更深的田野、走向地平线。他们以安静平稳的步伐穿过高草。

5.5s——他们继续走远，身影变小。镜头此刻几乎不动——几乎停下。我们目送他们离开。

7.0s——他们已很远，两个小人影一起走进金色田野，在远处朦胧的香港天际线前化作剪影。画面以他们在辽阔风景中的渺小身影收尾，走向没有我们的世界。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 15 岁]
- 露露：深褐色齐肩发，宽大的蓝白竖条纹系扣衬衫内搭白 T 恤，走动时飘摆的白色中长裙，白色凉鞋，纤细银链手链。
- 罗科：凌乱蓬松的黑发，光洁的脸颊（绝对无胡须、无胡茬），橄榄色皮肤，深灰色棉质拉链连帽卫衣敞开内搭浅灰 T 恤，黑色工装裤带红色缝线，深色 Vans 风格运动鞋。鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。开始时的轻柔同步呼吸，摄影师小心翼翼脚步踩在草上极轻的沙沙声，微风穿过田野，远处鸟鸣，轻微远处都市嗡鸣。他们走进田野的脚步渐弱。他们离开时世界在他们四周展开的声音。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无接吻、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、无平滑推轨、无机械推轨、无平滑轨道、无平滑后拉、无稳定化、无航拍无人机、无向天空俯仰、无拉远、无快速镜头运动、田野中无其他人、罗科无面部毛发、无胡须、无胡茬、无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰、无项圈、无妆容、不得移除红色鼻贴、无角色转身朝向镜头、无回望镜头、无奔跑、无大笑、无笑、无说话、无对白、无文字。
