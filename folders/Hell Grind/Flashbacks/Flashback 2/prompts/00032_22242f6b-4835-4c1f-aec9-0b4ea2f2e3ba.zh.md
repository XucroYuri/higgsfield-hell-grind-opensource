## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 35mm wide lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of 
Terrence Malick's "Days of Heaven" — running through grass with the 
characters.

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera, operator running BEHIND Lulu and Roco 
through the tall grass, camera at chest level, the operator visibly 
chasing them. Heavy organic motion, real bouncing from running 
footsteps, occasional grass blades brushing the lens. The camera is 
PHYSICALLY part of the chase. Frame: their backs and shoulders fill 
the foreground, tall golden grass parting around them, wildflowers 
flying past, distant tree and Hong Kong skyline ahead in the deep 
focus.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, low warm sun ahead of them creating 
silhouettes and rim light on their hair and shoulders, lens flares 
from the low sun, dust and seed particles flying in the air.

[ACTION]
Both running ahead through tall grass toward the distant tree, the 
camera chasing right behind them, swaying with each step. Lulu glances 
back over her shoulder at the camera (at Roco's POV from behind), 
laughing, hair flying. She turns forward again. Roco runs slightly 
behind her, pushing through the grass.

Both 15. Lulu: dark brown hair flying, oversized blue-and-white 
striped shirt over white t-shirt, white midi skirt billowing, white 
sandals. Roco: messy shaggy black hair, smooth cheeks (no mustache), 
dark gray hoodie open over light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants 
with red stitching, dark Vans-style sneakers, small red bandage on 
nose.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Heavy running footsteps on dirt and grass, breathless 
laughter, loud rustle of grass parting, the operator's breath, 
distant birds, faint city hum.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no static camera, no 
stabilization, no aerial shots, no other people, no facial hair on 
Roco, no jewelry, no chains, no choker, no makeup, no removal of 
red nose bandage, no text.

[CAMERA]

[LIGHT]
