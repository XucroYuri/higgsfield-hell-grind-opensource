## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, 100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the 
style of Lukas Dhont's "Close".

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, side angle on Roco's profile, very close. 
Frame: tight profile close-up of Roco's face. Lulu off-frame to his 
side. Soft bokeh of green leaves and distant Hong Kong skyline behind.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, dappled warm light through tree leaves on 
his face.

[CHARACTER — Roco, 15]
Messy shaggy black hair, smooth cheeks (no mustache, no stubble), olive 
skin, deep brown eyes, dark gray hoodie open over light gray t-shirt, 
small red adhesive bandage strip across the bridge of his nose. No 
tattoos, no jewelry.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Roco's profile, eyes looking straight forward at the distant 
       city, jaw calm, breathing slowly.
1.5s — His eyes — only his eyes, not his head — slowly slide sideways 
       toward Lulu (off-frame). His head does not turn.
2.5s — He looks at her for one full second. Eyes lingering. Reading 
       her face.
3.5s — He catches himself, snaps his eyes forward again, looks at the 
       city. Slight tension in jaw — embarrassed at being caught 
       looking, even by himself.
5.0s — Frame ends on him looking forward, swallowing softly.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Soft wind through leaves, distant birds, faint city hum, 
his soft swallow.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no turning head toward Lulu, no looking at camera, no 
smiling, no facial hair, no mustache, no stubble, no tattoos, no 
jewelry, no removal of red nose bandage, no rapid movement, no 
smooth gimbal, no deep focus, no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照卢卡斯·德霍特（Lukas Dhont）的《亲密》（Close）。

[CAMERA]
轻微有机手持，对罗科侧影的侧面角度，非常近。取景：罗科脸的紧侧面特写。露露在他一侧画外。身后绿叶与远处香港天际线的柔和虚化。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，透过树叶的斑驳暖光落在他脸上。

[CHARACTER — 罗科，15 岁]
凌乱蓬松的黑发，光洁的脸颊（无胡须、无胡茬），橄榄色皮肤，深棕眼眸，浅灰 T 恤上深灰卫衣敞开，鼻梁上横贴一道小红创可贴。无纹身、无珠宝。

[ACTION]
0.0s——罗科的侧影，目光直视前方远处的城市，下颌平静，缓缓呼吸。
1.5s——他的眼睛——只有眼睛，不是头——缓缓向露露（画外）的方向侧滑。他的头没有转。
2.5s——他看了她整整一秒。目光停留。读着她的脸。
3.5s——他回过神来，目光猛地再次转向正前方，看着城市。下颌微紧——即便只是被自己逮到在看，也感到不好意思。
5.0s——镜头以他看向前方、轻轻吞咽收尾。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。穿过树叶的轻柔风、远处鸟鸣、轻微都市嗡鸣、他轻缓的吞咽。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、不得转头朝向露露、不得看向镜头、无微笑、无面部毛发、无胡须、无胡茬、无纹身、无珠宝、不得移除红色鼻贴、无快速运动、无平滑稳定器、无深焦、无文字。
