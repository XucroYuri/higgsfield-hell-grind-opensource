## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 35mm wide lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of 
Wong Kar-wai's "Fallen Angels".

Handheld camera at chest level following them from behind as they run 
through a narrow neon alley draped with hanging laundry, paper 
lanterns, and Chinese signage swinging overhead. They duck under low 
hanging banners, push past hanging fabric that whips back into 
camera. Lulu pulls a hanging sheet aside as she runs through, it 
flutters wildly. Roco follows close behind, pushes through the same 
sheet. Neon reflections on wet asphalt, red and pink light flashing.

Both 17. Lulu: light wash denim jacket with vintage patches, white 
cropped t-shirt, blue cargo shorts, blue high-top sneakers, black 
velvet choker. Roco: burgundy and cream check short-sleeve shirt over 
white t-shirt, dark gray denim shorts, tan Timberland boots, gold 
chain necklaces with crosses, faint mustache, small red bandage on 
nose.

NO music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no other people in alley, 
no romance signals, no removal of red nose bandage, no text.

单次连续手持镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，35mm 广角镜头，浅景深，风格参照王家卫（Wong Kar-wai）的《堕落天使》（Fallen Angels）。

胸高位手持镜头从背后跟随他们，穿过一条悬挂着晾洗衣物、纸灯笼、头顶摇摆的中文招牌的狭窄霓虹小巷。他们低头躲过低垂的横幅，推开会抽回镜头上的悬挂布料。露露跑过时拉开一块挂毯，它剧烈招展。罗科紧跟其后，穿过同一块挂毯。潮湿柏油路上反射霓虹，红与粉光线闪烁。

两人皆 17 岁。露露：带复古补丁的浅色水洗牛仔夹克，白色露脐 T 恤，蓝色工装短裤，蓝色高帮运动鞋，黑色天鹅绒项圈。罗科：白 T 恤上酒红与奶油格纹短袖衬衫，深灰牛仔短裤，棕色 Timberland 靴子，金链十字项链，浅淡胡须，鼻子上小红创可贴。

无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、巷内无其他人、无恋爱暗示、不得移除红色鼻贴、无文字。
