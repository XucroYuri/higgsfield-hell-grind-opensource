## 原文 (source)

Single continuous shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, 
100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the style of Lukas 
Dhont's "Close" — first kiss, suspended tenderness.

[CAMERA]
Subtle organic handheld, the operator standing still in the grass. 
The camera does NOT move position — only gentle natural breathing 
motion, slight sways. Tight close-up of their two faces and shoulders, 
foreheads pressed together. Tall grass in soft foreground bokeh, 
distant Hong Kong skyline very softly out of focus in deep background.

[LIGHT]
Late afternoon golden hour, warm low sun catching their hair and the 
side of their faces in rim light, dappled tree shadows, dust and 
seed particles floating in beams.

[ACTION]
0.0s — Tight close-up: Lulu and Roco standing in the grass, foreheads 
       pressed together, eyes closed, completely still. Lulu's hands 
       on his chest. Roco's hands cupping her elbows. Breathing calm, 
       synchronized.

1.5s — A tiny shift. Lulu tilts her head a fraction. Their noses 
       brush gently. Eyes still closed. The smallest deliberate 
       movement.

3.0s — Slow and tentative — their lips meet. A first kiss, soft, 
       awkward, real. Not passionate, not posed — careful, fifteen 
       and uncertain, like asking a question without words. Eyes 
       closed.

4.5s — They linger for a breath. Then their lips part softly. They 
       don't pull back — foreheads still touching, eyes still closed, 
       breathing each other in. A small private smile ghosts on 
       Lulu's mouth.

5.0s — Frame ends on them still close, eyes closed, foreheads 
       together, the kiss having just happened, time suspended.

[CHARACTERS — both 15]
- LULU: dark brown shoulder-length hair, oversized blue-and-white 
  vertical striped button-up shirt over white t-shirt, white midi 
  skirt, white sandals, thin silver chain bracelets, no makeup, no 
  choker.
- ROCO: messy shaggy black hair, smooth cheeks (NO mustache, NO 
  stubble — too young), olive skin, dark gray cotton zip-up hoodie 
  open over light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants with red 
  contrast stitching. Small red adhesive bandage strip across the 
  bridge of his nose. No tattoos, no jewelry, no chains.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Their soft synchronized breathing, the very faint catch 
of breath as their lips meet, gentle wind through grass, distant 
birds, faint city hum, soft rustle of clothing.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no dolly, no zoom, no 
operator walking, no aerial shot, no tilt, no characters walking, 
no other people, no facial hair on Roco, no mustache, no stubble, 
no tattoos, no jewelry, no chains, no choker, no makeup, no removal 
of red nose bandage, no passionate kissing, no open mouth kissing, 
no tongue, no aggressive kissing, no movie cliche kiss, no looking 
at camera, no broad smiling, no laughing, no talking, no dialogue, 
no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照卢卡斯·德霍特（Lukas Dhont）的《亲密》（Close）——初吻，悬停的温柔。

[CAMERA]
轻微的手持有机运动，摄影师静止站在草丛中。摄影机不移动机位——只有柔和自然的呼吸律动、微微摇摆。两人脸部与肩部的紧特写，额头相抵。前景高草虚化为柔和前景虚化，远处的香港天际线在纵深背景中极度失焦。

[LIGHT]
傍晚黄金时刻，温暖低角度阳光以轮廓光（rim light）勾勒发丝与侧脸，斑驳的树影，光束中漂浮着尘埃与种子颗粒。

[ACTION]
0.0s——紧特写：露露（Lulu）与罗科（Roco）站在草丛中，额头相抵、双目紧闭、完全静止。露露的手搭在他胸口。罗科的手捧着她的手肘。呼吸平静而同步。

1.5s——一次微小的变化。露露将头微偏一分。两人的鼻尖轻轻相蹭。眼睛仍闭着。一个最细微而刻意的动作。

3.0s——缓慢而试探地——他们的唇相遇。一个初吻，轻柔、笨拙、真实。不是激情，不是摆拍——是小心翼翼、十五岁、带着不确定，如同一个无声的提问。眼睛闭着。

4.5s——他们停留了一息。然后嘴唇轻轻分开。他们没有后退——额头仍相触，眼睛仍闭着，互相呼吸着彼此。一抹私密的小微笑在露露嘴角隐约浮现。

5.0s——镜头定格在两人依旧贴近：闭眼、额头靠拢，吻刚刚发生，时间悬停。

[CHARACTERS — 两人皆 15 岁]
- 露露（LULU）：深褐色齐肩发，宽大的蓝白竖条纹系扣衬衫内搭白 T 恤，白色中长裙（midi skirt），白色凉鞋，纤细银链手链，不化妆，无项圈（choker）。
- 罗科（ROCO）：凌乱蓬松的黑发，光洁的脸颊（绝对无胡须、无胡茬——太年轻），橄榄色皮肤，深灰色棉质拉链连帽卫衣敞开内搭浅灰 T 恤，黑色工装裤带红色撞色缝线。一道小红创可贴横贴在他鼻梁上。无纹身、无珠宝、无链饰。

[SOUND — 仅同期声（DIEGETIC ONLY）]
无音乐。他们轻柔同步的呼吸，嘴唇相触时极轻的屏息，微风穿过草丛，远处鸟鸣，轻微的都市嗡鸣，衣物轻柔的窸窣声。
