## 原文 (source)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld extreme close-up shot, 5 seconds, 
anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic 
100mm telephoto lens, extremely shallow depth of field, cinematography 
in the style of Wong Kar-wai's intimate detail shots and Christopher 
Doyle's handheld closeups.

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera, low angle, telephoto lens hunting their 
joined hands as they run. The depth of field is so shallow that only 
the point where their fingers lock is in sharp focus — wrists and 
forearms drift out of focus, asphalt below is total bokeh streaks of 
neon. The handheld operator is running behind them, the camera 
bounces with each step, occasionally loses the focus on the grip 
point and racks back to find it. Frame: extreme close-up on their 
joined hands centered, motion blur on edges.

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
Late night Hong-Kong-style alley, recently rained. Wet asphalt 
streaking below their running feet, reflecting neon — pink, red, 
electric blue — in motion. Each splash they kick up flashes a 
different color. Streetlamps overhead create intermittent warm 
orange pools of light passing across their hands as they run through 
them. Anamorphic horizontal flares from neon sources.

[CHARACTERS — partial view, hands and forearms only]
Their two hands tightly interlocked, fingers laced, running.
- LULU's hand: pale slender fingers, thin silver chain bracelet on 
  her wrist, edge of her patch-covered light wash denim jacket sleeve 
  visible at her forearm.
- ROCO's hand: olive skin, larger, stronger grip, gold chain bracelet 
  at his wrist, dark tattoos faintly visible on his forearm, edge of 
  burgundy check shirt sleeve at his elbow. His thumb subtly stroking 
  the back of her hand even as they run.

NO faces visible. Only their joined hands and forearms filling the 
frame, with the world streaming past in extreme bokeh.

[ACTION]
Their hands stay tightly clasped throughout. The motion of running 
creates rhythmic micro-bounces in the grip. One hand grips slightly 
tighter at one beat. A puddle splashes — droplets fly past their 
hands in slow visible motion. Neon reflections beneath flash through 
pink, red, blue. Camera focus occasionally drifts and snaps back to 
the grip point.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music. Two pairs of synchronized footsteps splashing through 
puddles — heavy boots and rubber sneakers — fast rhythmic breathing, 
breathless laughter from above the frame, distant city hum, far car 
horns.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no instruments, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, 
no static camera, no faces visible, no full body shot, no wide shot, 
no other people, no cars, no rain falling, no extra limbs, no extra 
fingers, no broken hand anatomy, no character identity drift, no 
hands separating, no children, no adults, no text, no captions, no 
deep depth of field, no sharp focus on background, no melancholy, 
no fear.

## 中文对照 (zh)

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
单次连续手持极特写镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，用 Arri Alexa 与变形 100mm 长焦镜头拍摄，极浅景深，摄影风格参照王家卫的亲密细节镜头与杜可风（Christopher Doyle）的手持特写。

[CAMERA]
活跃生动的手持摄影机，低机位，长焦镜头追猎他们奔跑中交握的双手。景深如此之浅，只有他们手指紧握的相触点锐利合焦——手腕与前臂滑出焦点，下方柏油路化作一片片霓虹的纯虚化拖影。手持摄影师在他们身后奔跑，镜头随每一步弹跳，偶尔丢失握点的对焦再摇摆找回它。取景：居中交握双手的极特写，边缘有运动模糊。

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
香港式城市的深夜小巷，刚下过雨。他们奔跑的脚步下方潮湿柏油路拖出霓虹倒影——粉红、红色、电光蓝——流动着。他们踢起的每一下水花闪出一种不同颜色。头顶街灯在他们跑过时，在他们手上投下一阵一阵温暖的橙色光圈。霓虹光源产生变形横向耀斑。

[CHARACTERS — 局部取景，仅手与前臂]
他们的双手紧锁交扣，手指相扣，奔跑着。
- 露露的手：苍白纤细的手指，手腕上纤细银链手链，前臂可见带补丁的浅色水洗牛仔夹克袖口边缘。
- 罗科的手：橄榄色皮肤，更大、握得更有力，手腕上金链手链，前臂可见浅淡深色纹身，肘部酒红格纹衬衫袖口边缘。即便奔跑中他的拇指也在轻柔摩挲她的手背。

无可见面部。只有交握的双手与前臂填满画面，世界化作极虚化从旁飞掠。

[ACTION]
全片他们的双手保持紧握。奔跑的动作在握手中产生节奏性的微小弹跳。某一拍一只手握得更紧一些。一个水洼溅起——水珠以可见的慢速飞过他们的手。脚下霓虹倒影闪烁经过粉红、红、蓝。镜头对焦偶尔漂移、随即弹回握点。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
无音乐。两双同步的脚步踏过水洼的脚步声——沉重的靴子与橡胶运动鞋——节奏快的呼吸、画面上方喘不过气的笑声、远处都市嗡鸣、遥远的汽车喇叭。

[NEGATIVE]
无音乐、无配乐、无乐器、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、无静态镜头、无可见的面部、无全身镜头、无宽景、无其他人、无汽车、无落雨、无多余肢体、无多余手指、无畸形手部结构、无角色身份漂移、无双手分离、无儿童、无成人、无文字、无字幕、无深景深、无背景锐利合焦、无惆怅、无恐惧。
