## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, in the style of 
Terrence Malick's nature shots.

Handheld camera follows Lulu from behind as she runs through tall 
golden grass toward the large old tree, laughing, looking back over 
her shoulder. Roco chases after her a few meters behind, also 
laughing, trying to catch up. Wildflowers in the grass, golden hour 
sun low, distant Hong Kong skyline behind them. She reaches the tree 
first, spins around laughing, breathing hard. Roco catches up, 
panting, hands on knees, also laughing.

Both 15. Lulu in oversized blue-and-white striped shirt over white 
t-shirt, white midi skirt billowing, white sandals. Roco in dark 
gray hoodie open over light gray t-shirt, black carpenter pants, 
dark Vans, small red bandage on nose, no mustache.

NO music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no other people, no 
facial hair on Roco, no posed running, no text.
