[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]
Single continuous handheld close-up shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 
35mm film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with anamorphic lenses, very 
shallow depth of field with creamy neon bokeh in background, 
cinematography in the style of Wong Kar-wai's "Fallen Angels" and 
"Chungking Express", Christopher Doyle handheld energy.

[CAMERA]
Active alive handheld camera close to Lulu's face, running alongside 
her, breathing with the motion. Operator visibly chasing her — slight 
weight, slight bounce, occasional motion blur. The camera is barely 
keeping up with her. NOT smooth gimbal, NOT stabilized — alive and 
human. Frame: tight close-up, Lulu's face fills most of the frame, 
her shoulder visible, everything behind her dissolved into colored 
neon bokeh.

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
Late night Asian megacity backstreet, recently rained. Neon signs 
above and behind her — pink, red, electric blue — splashing colored 
light across her face as she runs through them. Each step she takes 
catches a different colored beam. Wet asphalt reflections of neon 
visible at edges of frame. Streetlamps overhead. Misty air. 
Anamorphic lens flares streaking horizontally when bright signs pass 
behind her.

[CHARACTER — Lulu, 17 years old]
Lulu, 17, dark brown shoulder-length hair flying behind her as she 
runs, side-swept fringe falling across her forehead, pale skin 
flushed pink from running, bright eyes gleaming with neon reflections, 
mouth open in a wild laugh. Wearing a light wash denim jacket covered 
in vintage embroidered patches (aviation, retro, badges) over a 
white cropped t-shirt, thin black velvet choker around her neck 
visible at her collarbone. The choker and patches catch light as she 
moves.

[ACTION]
Lulu is mid-run, looking forward, hair flying. She glances over her 
shoulder, looking BACK toward the camera (toward where Roco is, 
chasing her), grinning wildly — eyes alive, completely free. She 
shouts something playful to him over the city noise, laughs at her 
own shout, then turns her face forward again and keeps running. The 
neon behind her shifts as she runs through different signs. Her hair 
catches a beam of red light, then pink, then blue.

[SOUND — DIEGETIC ONLY]
No music, no instruments. Her fast running footsteps on wet asphalt, 
her breathless laughter, the rustle of her denim jacket, distant city 
hum, faint car horns, the soft hiss of mist on pavement.

[NEGATIVE]
no music, no score, no instruments, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, 
no static camera, no stabilization, no full body shot, no wide shot, 
no other people in frame, no cars in foreground, no rain falling, 
no melancholy, no fear, no sadness, no extra limbs, no facial morphing, 
no character identity drift, no posed running, no fake laughter, no 
looking directly into camera lens, no sharp focus on background, no 
deep depth of field, no children, no adults, no text, no captions.

[CINEMATIC SPECIFICATION]

[CAMERA]
活跃生动的手持摄影机贴近露露的脸，与她并排奔跑、随动作呼吸。摄影师明显在追她——轻微的重量、轻微的弹跳、偶尔的运动模糊。镜头勉强跟得上她。不是平滑稳定器、不是稳定化——生动且人性化。取景：紧特写，露露的脸填满画面大部分，她的肩可见，她身后的一切都化作彩色霓虹虚化。

[LIGHT & ATMOSPHERE]
亚洲特大城市的深夜背巷，刚下过雨。她上方与身后的霓虹招牌——粉红、红、电光蓝——在她跑过去时把彩光溅在她脸上。她迈出每一步都捕捉一束不同的色光。湿柏油路反射的霓虹在画面边缘可见。头顶街灯。雾蒙蒙的空气。明亮的招牌在她身后经过时，变形镜头耀斑横向拖曳。

[CHARACTER — 露露，17 岁]
露露，17 岁，奔跑时深褐色齐肩发在身后飞舞，侧分刘海垂落额头，奔跑后泛粉的苍白皮肤，闪动着霓虹倒影的明亮双眼，张大狂妄大笑的嘴。穿着覆盖复古刺绣补丁（航空、复古、徽章）的浅色水洗牛仔夹克内搭白色露脐 T 恤，颈项锁骨处可见纤细黑色天鹅绒项圈。她动作时项圈与补丁捕捉光线。

[SOUND — 仅同期声]
