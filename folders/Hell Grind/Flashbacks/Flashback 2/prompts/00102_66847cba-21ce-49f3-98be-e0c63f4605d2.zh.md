## 原文 (source)

Cinematic medium shot, anamorphic 2.39:1 aspect ratio, static locked-off 
camera, eye-level, three-quarter angle from the right, overcast diffused 
daylight, no sun, muted desaturated palette of cold browns, dusty grays 
and washed blues, soft fog, gentle ambient wind.

Two 12-year-old children embrace tightly at the bottom of a wide cracked 
concrete staircase in front of a massive four-story brick institutional 
building. We see them from chest up.

The girl, Lulu, 12 years old, has a sharp dark-brown bob with straight 
bangs, pale skin, delicate features, wearing a dusty-blue knit cardigan 
over a cream turtleneck. She presses her face into the boy's shoulder, 
her eyes squeezed shut, fists gripping the back of his shirt. A small 
worn dusty-blue canvas backpack lies dropped on the ground beside them — 
she just ran back to him.

The boy, Roco, 12 years old, has messy shaggy black hair, olive skin, 
soft boyish features, wearing a charcoal-gray cotton t-shirt. His right 
hand cradles the back of Lulu's head, his left arm wraps tightly around 
her back. His eyes are closed, jaw tight, holding her like a promise — 
"I'm not letting you go again."

The embrace is desperate, raw, the hug of two children who almost lost 
each other and just found their way back. Almost no movement — only 
the faintest breath, a strand of Lulu's hair stirring in the wind, 
one dry leaf drifting past in the background.

Cinematography in the style of Andrey Zvyagintsev's "Loveless" and 
Lukas Dhont's "Close", 35mm film grain, shot on Arri Alexa with 
anamorphic lenses, shallow depth of field, soft bokeh of brick wall 
behind them.

NEGATIVE: no camera movement, no zoom, no pan, no characters separating, 
no smiling, no text, no saturated colors, no sunny weather, no facial 
morphing, no extra people in background.

两个 12 岁孩子在一座庞大的四层砖砌机构建筑前、宽阔开裂混凝土台阶的底部紧紧相拥。我们从胸部以上看到他们。

女孩露露，12 岁，利落的深褐波波头配直刘海、苍白皮肤、精致的五官，奶油色高领毛衣外罩灰蓝针织开衫。她把脸埋进男孩的肩里，紧闭双眼，双拳攥住他衬衫的后背。一只破旧的灰蓝色帆布小背包被丢在他们身旁地上——她刚刚跑回来找他。

男孩罗科，12 岁，凌乱蓬松的黑发、橄榄色皮肤、柔和的少年气五官，穿着炭灰棉质 T 恤。他的右手托着露露的后脑，左臂紧紧环住她的背。他双眼紧闭、下颌紧绷，抱着她如同抱着一句承诺——“我不会再让你离开了。”

这个拥抱绝望、赤裸，是差点失去彼此、刚刚找回彼此的两个孩子的拥抱。几乎无动作——只有最微弱的呼吸、露露一缕在风中扰动的头发、一片枯叶在背景中飘过。

摄影风格参照安德烈·萨金塞夫（Andrey Zvyagintsev）的《无爱可诉》（Loveless）与卢卡斯·德霍特（Lukas Dhont）的《亲密》（Close），35mm 胶片颗粒，Arri Alexa 配变形镜头拍摄，浅景深，身后砖墙化成柔和虚化。
