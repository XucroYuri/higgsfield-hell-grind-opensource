## 原文 (source)

Single continuous handheld shot, 5 seconds, anamorphic 2.39:1, 35mm 
film grain, 100mm telephoto, very shallow depth of field, in the style 
of Wong Kar-wai's "Fallen Angels".

Active handheld camera at chest level, the operator running ahead of 
them facing back toward them. Lulu and Roco SPRINT around a corner 
into the frame, hand in hand, breathless laughter, hair flying. Wet 
asphalt under their feet, neon signs around them — pink, red, electric 
blue. They almost crash into the camera, swerve, keep running past 
toward the lens. Camera pans hard to follow, briefly loses them, 
catches them again from behind running away into the alley.

Lulu, 15: dark brown shoulder-length hair, oversized blue-and-white 
striped shirt over white t-shirt, white midi skirt flying, blue 
high-top sneakers.
Roco, 15: messy black hair, gray hoodie open over light gray t-shirt, 
black carpenter pants with red stitching, dark Vans-style sneakers, 
small red bandage on nose, no mustache.

NO music, no slow motion, no smooth gimbal, no other people, no facial 
hair on Roco, no jewelry, no rapid focus changes that look mechanical, 
no text.

## 中文对照 (zh)

单次连续手持镜头，5 秒，变形镜头 2.39:1，35mm 胶片颗粒，100mm 长焦，极浅景深，风格参照王家卫的《堕落天使》（Fallen Angels）。

胸高位活跃手持镜头，摄影师跑在他们前方、面朝他们。露露与罗科手牵手猛冲过一个拐角冲入画面，喘着气笑着，头发飞舞。脚下潮湿柏油路，四周霓虹招牌——粉红、红色、电光蓝。他们几乎撞上镜头，侧身一闪，继续朝镜头跑过去。镜头猛烈摇移跟随，短暂跟丢他们，再在他们身后追住、跑进小巷。

露露，15 岁：深褐色齐肩发，白 T 恤上宽大的蓝白条纹衬衫，飞舞的白色中长裙，蓝色高帮运动鞋。
罗科，15 岁：凌乱黑发，浅灰 T 恤上灰色卫衣敞开，红色缝线黑色工装裤，深色 Vans 风格运动鞋，鼻子上小红创可贴，无胡须。

无音乐、无慢动作、无平滑稳定器、无其他人、罗科无面部毛发、无珠宝、无显得机械的快速焦点变化、无文字。
