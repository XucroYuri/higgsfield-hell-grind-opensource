[SINGLE CONTINUOUS SHOT] LOW ANGLE on REAR SEAT — LULU'S DECISION TO SPEAK
MEDIUM 85mm anamorphic LOCKED STATIC LOW ANGLE on the rear seat next to Lulu, camera positioned below her chin level looking slightly UP at her, Lulu <<<image_5>>> placed in LEFT third of frame (rule of thirds), three-quarter from her right side, body angled into seat. Cool overcast daylight from offscreen-LEFT window catches her face front; her right cheek (camera-side) in cabin shadow. Background OOF — soft cool blur of cabin interior on right side of frame. Single uninterrupted take.

ACTION ARC:
1) Lulu's face turned toward LEFT window offscreen, hands restless in her lap fidgeting — fingers picking at knuckles, thumb rubbing back of opposite hand, wringing slowly. Wet tear tracks glisten on her cheek. Uneven shaky breaths.

2) Slowly turns her head away from the window, rotating toward front of car. Eyes find mother <<<image_4>>> (offscreen-RIGHT, front passenger), holding the look with quiet desperation. Then gaze shifts further, finding father <<<image_3>>> at the wheel (offscreen-RIGHT, deeper). Hands keep fidgeting in lap, never stopping.

3) Lips part. Draws a shaky breath, gathering herself — mouth opens slightly, jaw tenses — then closes again. She doesn't dare. Chin trembles. Hands keep moving. Lowers her head, eyes dropping to her own lap, watching her fidgeting fingers. Shoulders curl inward small.

4) A held pause. Car keeps moving. Hands keep fidgeting. Held silence.

5) Then SUDDENLY she lifts her head sharply — eyes up, head straight, jaw set. Her hands STOP DEAD in her lap, frozen still — the moment of decision. She looks forward toward her parents (offscreen-RIGHT), eyes wet and brimming, gaze direct and clear. With a small confident voice trembling at edges, she says: "Please, stop the car." She holds the look after the words, eyes steady, fresh tears spilling. Car keeps moving. The weight of her request hangs in the cabin.

⚠️ 风格（STYLE）⚠️

Sound（声音）：车厢嗡鸣、轮胎碾过湿沥青、冷风、露露不均颤抖的呼吸、结尾一句支离破碎的对白。无音乐。

[单一连续镜头] 后座低位角度（LOW ANGLE on REAR SEAT）—— 露露开口的决定
MEDIUM 85mm anamorphic 锁定静态低位角度，对准露露身边的后座，摄影机低于她的下巴、略微仰视她，露露 <<<image_5>>> 置于画面左侧三分之一处（三分法），从她右侧的四分之三侧面，身体斜靠进座椅。阴冷阴天日光从画面外左侧车窗透进，落在她脸正面；她的右颊（朝向摄影机一侧）处于车厢阴影中。背景在景深之外——画面右侧车内的柔和冷调模糊。单次不间断拍摄。

动作弧线（ACTION ARC）：
1) 露露的脸转向画面外的左侧车窗，双手在膝上不安地躁动——手指拨弄指节，拇指揉搓另一只手的手背，缓慢地绞动。湿润的泪痕在她颊上泛着光。不均的颤抖呼吸。

2) 她缓缓把脸从车窗移开，转向车前。她的目光寻到母亲 <<<image_4>>>（画面外右侧，副驾座），带着静默的绝望锁住这目光。然后她的视线再移，寻到握着方向盘的父亲 <<<image_3>>>（画面外右侧，更深处）。双手始终在膝上躁动，从不停歇。

3) 她双唇分开。吸一口气，颤抖着，稳住自己——嘴微微张开，下颌绷紧——然后又闭上。她不敢。下巴颤抖。双手仍动着。她低下头，目光落到自己的膝上，望着自己躁动的手指。双肩向内蜷缩，小小的。

4) 一段凝住的停顿。车继续前行。双手仍在躁动。凝住的寂静。

5) 然后她猛地抬起头——目光抬起，头昂直，下颌绷定。她的双手在膝上戛然而止（STOP DEAD），僵住不动——这是决定的瞬间。她朝前方望向她的父母（画面外右侧），双眼湿润盈满，目光直接而清晰。以小小的、带着颤抖的、信心十足的声音说：「求求你，停车。」话后依旧锁着目光，眼神沉稳，新的泪水涌出。车继续前行。她请求的分量在车厢中悬着。
