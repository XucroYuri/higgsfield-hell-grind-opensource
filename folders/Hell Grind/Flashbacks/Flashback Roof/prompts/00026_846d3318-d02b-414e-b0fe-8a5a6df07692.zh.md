[8.0–9.5s] HELD BEAT + EYE-LINE SHIFT + INDIVIDUAL MICRO-RESPONSES (NOT synchronized squeeze).
Roko's gaze slowly moves around the inner space — meeting Lulu's wet eyes (R) first, holding for a beat (she gives back a tiny watery smile through her tears), then meeting Rein's glassy controlled eyes (across), then meeting Jax's set jaw and slow blink (L). Each receives him at their own moment.
SIMULTANEOUSLY but NOT SYNCHRONIZED: Lulu's fingers re-grip Roko's jacket fabric, Jax's fingers dig fractionally tighter on Rein's shoulder, Rein begins her slow thumb-stroke down Lulu's back. NO collective squeeze — each gesture is its own thing at its own micro-moment.

[9.5–12.0s] SECOND OATH DELIVERY — INSPIRED HUSHED, INDIVIDUAL RECEPTIONS.
ROKO speaks the line again, slightly slower cadence, slightly heavier weight, same hushed quiet volume — with a micro-catch of inspiration in his voice on "together":
"All of us, together, no matter what."
His voice has the smallest crack on "together" (NOT a sob — a crack of belief, the word almost too full to hold). Slight smile-trace flickers at corners of mouth between phrases.

During the line, each kid responds in their own way:
- LULU (~9.5s): hears "All of us," tilts her head slightly more toward Roko, eyes overflowing — first tear escapes and rolls down her cheek
- JAX (~10.2s during "together"): swallows visibly, nostrils flare faintly with held emotion, single slow nod with bent head
- REIN (~10.8s during "no matter what"): thumb-stroke on Lulu's back continues, eyes flick to Roko then back to Lulu, the wet-shine on her cheekbone scar becomes visible

[12.0–14.0s] SILENT TAIL — ASYNCHRONOUS QUIET RESPONSES.
Camera holds. The oath sits. Each kid in their own state:
- ~12.3s: Lulu BURIES her face briefly into Roko's neck/shoulder for ~1s, one shaky exhale, then lifts her face again with wet cheeks
- ~12.8s: Roko's eyes close for a half-second, deep breath through nose, opens eyes with quiet certainty
- ~13.1s: Jax sniffs sharply once (cold air + emotion blend)
- ~13.5s: Rein's thumb-stroke on Lulu's back continues slow, her own controlled tear-track catches faint warm rim
- Throughout: bodies still pressed in huddle, embraces unbroken, breath cloud rising warm
- ~13.7s: Lulu wipes her nose briefly with back of her hand without breaking the embrace

[8.0–9.5 秒] 停顿节拍 + 视线游移 + 个体微反应（非同步挤压）。
Roko 的目光在内圈缓缓游移——先迎上 Lulu 湿润的眼（右侧），短暂停留一个节拍（她透过泪回以一个极小水盈盈的笑），接着迎上 Rein 泛着泪光而克制的眼（对面），再迎上 Jax 咬紧的下颚与缓慢的一眨（左侧）。每个人都在自己的时刻接住他。
同时发生，但并非同步：Lulu 的手指重新抓紧 Roko 的夹克布料，Jax 的手指在 Rein 肩上略微更紧地按了一下，Rein 开始沿着 Lulu 的背缓缓拇指轻抚。没有集体的挤压——每个动作各自独立、发生在各自的微小时刻。

[9.5–12.0 秒] 第二次宣誓——灵感的低语，各自不同的承接。
ROKO 再次念出台词，节奏略慢、分量略重，音量同前般低——在 "together" 上有一丝灵感的微哽：
"All of us, together, no matter what."
他的声音在 "together" 上有极细微的一裂（不是抽噎——是信念的破裂，这个词几乎满得兜不住）。句间嘴角掠过淡淡笑意。

台词期间，每个孩子以各自的方式回应：
- LULU（约 9.5 秒）：听见 "All of us,"，头更向 Roko 一侧稍倾，双眼盈满——第一颗泪溢出，滑下脸颊
- JAX（约 10.2 秒，念到 "together" 时）：明显吞咽，鼻翼因压抑的情绪微微翕动，低垂着头缓缓点头一次
- REIN（约 10.8 秒，念到 "no matter what" 时）：在 Lulu 背上的拇指轻抚继续，目光先瞥向 Roko 又回到 Lulu，她颧骨疤痕上的湿光显现

[12.0–14.0 秒] 静默收尾——异步而安静的反应。
摄影机保持。誓约落定。每个孩子各在自己的状态中：
- 约 12.3 秒：Lulu 将脸短暂埋进 Roko 颈/肩约 1 秒，一次颤抖的吐息，随后抬起脸，双颊湿润
- 约 12.8 秒：Roko 双眼阖上半秒，经鼻深呼一口气，睁眼带着沉静的笃定
- 约 13.1 秒：Jax 轻吸一下鼻子（冷空气与情绪交织）
- 约 13.5 秒：Rein 在 Lulu 背上的拇指轻抚继续缓缓，她自己克制的泪痕承接微弱暖色的轮廓光
- 全程：身体仍在围抱中紧贴，拥抱不破，温暖的气息云向上升腾
- 约 13.7 秒：Lulu 不破拥抱地用手背匆匆擦了下鼻子
