Five-shot sequence in a dim industrial bedroom, 12 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose from Image 1. He is wearing only a plain black t-shirt in this scene — no jacket, bare arms exposed.

Use the composition and framing style from Image 3 — the visual language of off-center placement, broken composition, foreground obstruction, and asymmetric framing should be applied throughout this scene.

Setting: a dim sci-fi bedroom in an industrial building. Concrete walls, low bed with white sheets in shadow, large floor-to-ceiling glass windows on the right wall overlooking a dark industrial cityscape at night. A desk near the windows with two dark computer monitors that are on — the monitor screens emit a soft diffused cool blue-white glow that washes across the desk surface and surrounding area as the only main light source, no hard pools or directional beams, the light is wrapping and ambient. Faint additional cool glow filters through the windows from the city outside. Cool neutral palette overall, deep crushed blacks softened by gentle ambient screen wrap. On the desk: a single sheet of paper with handwriting on it (text not legible to camera), a pen. The paper remains on the desk, unmoved, throughout the entire sequence.

Shot 1 (2.5s) — OFF-CENTER ISOLATION:
Handheld wide medium shot, deliberately off-balance composition following the framing style of Image 3. The dark-haired man in a black t-shirt is pushed far to the LEFT EDGE of the frame, sitting at the desk hunched over the paper, only a small portion of his body visible, bare arms resting on the desk. The right two-thirds of the frame is dominated by deep empty soft-lit space — the dark room, the cool blue-white glow of the monitor screens washing the area, the windows showing the silent industrial city outside. He is small and isolated in his own space. Long-lens telephoto, shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh on the screen glow and distant window lights. Camera holds slightly tilted, breathing handheld. Soft diffused lighting wraps the scene gently.

Shot 2 (2s) — MACRO ON HAND WRITING ON PAPER:
Hard cut. Handheld macro extreme close-up at the desk, lens inches from his hand. Bare arm visible, the black t-shirt sleeve cropped at the edge of frame. Frame filled by his hand holding the pen, the back of his fingers and knuckles in razor-sharp focus, the loose sheet of paper below in extreme shallow softness — handwriting dissolved into unreadable blur, just a pale surface beneath the pen tip. The hand makes the writing motion, hesitates between strokes. Soft diffused cool blue-white light from the monitor screens wraps gently over the back of his hand from one side, no harsh shadow lines. Static intimate.

Shot 3 (2s) — TIGHT CROP CUTTING ACROSS HIS FACE:
Hard cut. Handheld extreme close-up on his face, deliberately broken composition matching the style of Image 3 — the camera frames so tightly that the TOP of his head is cut off severely above the eyebrows, and the BOTTOM of frame cuts at his upper lip, leaving only his eyes and nose bridge visible across the entire frame. No headroom, no breathing room — the crop is uncomfortable and intimate. His eyes hold a downward gaze, fixed on the paper off-frame, breath shallow. Soft diffused cool blue-white light from the monitors wraps his face gently from one side, the other side falling into soft shadow without hard edges. Long-lens, extreme shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh.

Shot 4 (2s) — PUTTING DOWN THE PEN, HAND RESTING:
Hard cut. Handheld extreme close-up shot from a low angle, framed THROUGH a foreground out-of-focus element on the left side as soft bokeh — composition style following Image 3, partially obscuring the view. Through this obscured framing we see his hand at the desk in the gently lit area — he finishes a final stroke, lifts the pen off the paper slowly, hesitates, then sets the pen down on the desk surface beside the paper. His hand remains resting on the desk for a beat, fingers still, breath quiet. The paper sits on the desk beside the pen, written but unmoved, exactly where he wrote it. Voyeuristic, observational. Long-lens, extreme shallow depth, the foreground bokeh dissolving into anamorphic oval shapes, soft wrapping cool screen light.

Shot 5 (3.5s) — THE PAPER STILL ON THE DESK, BROKEN COMPOSITION:
Hard cut. Handheld static-feel shot of the desk, deliberately off-balance composition following the framing style of Image 3. The single sheet of paper sits in the LOWER LEFT corner of the frame, small, the pen lying next to it, exactly as he left them. The rest of the frame is dominated by negative space — the dark room above and the right two-thirds empty, the cool blue-white glow of the monitors cropped at the upper edge of frame, partial. There is no headroom convention, no centering — the paper is almost falling off the bottom of the frame, vulnerable and remaining, written and present. Hold on this composition. The paper is the focus of the eye, content unseen, its placement in the frame emotionally heavy. Long-lens, extreme shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh from the screen glow and city windows beyond. Soft ambient light wraps gently across the empty desk.

Camera: handheld throughout with deliberately broken compositions matching the visual style of Image 3 — off-center framing, severe headroom-violation crop, through-foreground obstruction, asymmetric placement. Long-lens telephoto and macro cinematography with extreme shallow depth of field at f/1.4 aperture, anamorphic lens characteristic creating oval bokeh shapes in defocused highlights. Soft diffused lighting throughout — the cool blue-white glow of computer monitor screens emitting wrapping ambient light as the main source, faint cool city light from windows providing gentle fill, no hard pools or directional beams. Sounds: heavy stillness in the room throughout. Shot 1 — quiet ambient room tone, faint scratching of pen on paper from his direction, faint electronic hum of monitors, distant city ambient. ## 中文对照 (zh)

昏暗工业卧室中的五镜头序列，12 秒，16:9，照片写实（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊（motion blur），无音乐。

参照图 1 中那个留着凌乱深色头发、淡色小胡子、鼻子上贴着红色绷带（red bandage）的男子。本场景中他仅身穿一件素黑色 T 恤——无夹克（no jacket），裸露双臂。

采用图 3 的构图和取景风格——偏位放置、打破构图、前景遮挡和不对称取景的视觉语言应贯穿整个场景。

场景（Setting）：工业建筑内一间昏暗的科幻卧室。混凝土墙壁，阴影中的低矮床上铺着白色床单，右侧墙上有大面积的落地玻璃窗，俯瞰夜晚的黑暗工业都市景观（cityscape）。窗边有一张书桌，上面两个深色电脑显示器处于开机状态——显示器屏幕散发出柔和弥散的冷蓝白光，冲刷过桌面及周围区域，作为唯一的主要光源，无硬光池，无定向光束，光线是包裹性、弥散的。窗外城市透进微弱的额外冷光。整体为冷色中性色调，深邃的压黑阴影被柔和的环境屏幕光线所软化。在桌上：一张写有手写字迹的纸（text not legible to camera，文字对摄影机不可读），一支钢笔。这张纸在整个序列中始终留在桌上、一动不动。

镜头 1（2.5 秒）——偏位孤立（OFF-CENTER ISOLATION）：
手持广角中景镜头，刻意失衡的构图，遵循图 3 的取景风格。穿黑色 T 恤的深发男子被推向画面最左侧边缘（LEFT EDGE），弓身坐在书桌前俯身看着纸张，只有他身体的一小部分可见，光裸的双臂放在桌上。画面右侧三分之二被深邃空旷的柔和受光空间所主导——黑暗的房间，显示器屏幕的冷蓝白光冲刷着这片区域，窗户显出外面寂静的工业城市，阴影中的空床。他在自己的空间里显得渺小而孤立。长焦镜头，浅景深，屏幕辉光和远处窗外灯光上是变形镜头椭圆散景（anamorphic oval bokeh）。摄影机保持轻微倾斜，呼吸式手持。柔和弥散的光线轻裹整个场景。

镜头 2（2 秒）——纸上手部书写的微距（MACRO ON HAND WRITING ON PAPER）：
硬切（Hard cut）。在书桌处手持微距极端特写，镜头距他的手仅数英寸。可见光裸手臂，画面边缘裁掉黑色 T 恤袖口。画面被握笔的手充满，手指和指节背部处于极锐聚焦中，下方的松散纸张呈极端浅景深的柔和——手写字迹溶解成不可读的模糊，只是笔尖下的一片苍白表面。手做书写动作，在笔画之间迟疑。屏幕散出的柔和弥散冷蓝白光从一侧轻轻裹住他的手背，无生硬的阴影线。静态而亲密。

镜头 3（2 秒）——横切其面部的紧裁（TIGHT CROP CUTTING ACROSS HIS FACE）：
硬切（Hard cut）。对他面部的手持极端特写，刻意打破的构图，匹配图 3 的风格——摄影机框取极紧，以致他的头顶在眉毛上方被严重切掉，画面底部在他的上嘴唇处裁断，只留下他的双眼和鼻梁横跨整个画面。无头顶空间（no headroom），无呼吸空间——裁切令人不适且亲密。他的双眼保持下视的目光，注视画面外的纸张，呼吸浅。显示器散出的柔和弥散冷蓝白光从一侧轻轻裹住他的脸，另一侧落入无生硬边缘的柔和阴影中。长焦，极端浅景深，变形镜头椭圆散景（anamorphic oval bokeh）。

镜头 4（2 秒）——放下钢笔，手休息（PUTTING DOWN THE PEN, HAND RESTING）：
硬切（Hard cut）。低角度手持极端特写，透过前景中左侧一件失焦元素作为柔和散景来框取——构图风格遵循图 3，部分遮挡视野。透过这被遮挡的框取，我们看到他的手在柔和受光区域的桌上——他写完最后一笔，缓缓把笔从纸上提起来，迟疑一下，然后把它放在纸旁桌面上。他的手在桌上停留一拍，手指静止，呼吸安静。纸张位于笔旁，写过了却未被移动，就在他写的位置。偷窥式的、观察性的。长焦，极端浅景深，前景散景溶解为变形椭圆形状，柔软的包裹式冷色屏幕光。

镜头 5（3.5 秒）——纸仍在桌上，打破的构图（THE PAPER STILL ON THE DESK, BROKEN COMPOSITION）：
硬切（Hard cut）。桌台手持、静止感的镜头，刻意失衡的构图，遵循图 3 的取景风格。单张纸位于画面左下角（LOWER LEFT corner），很小，笔躺在它旁边，正如他留下的样子。画面其余部分被负空间（negative space）主导——上方黑暗的房间和右侧三分之二空荡，显示器冷蓝白光在画面上边缘被部分裁切。无头顶空间惯例，无居中——纸几乎要从画面底部掉落，脆弱而残留，写过了并存在着。保持这个构图。纸是视线的焦点，内容不可见，它在画面中的位置在情绪上沉重。长焦，极端浅景深，屏幕辉光和后方城市窗户投出变形椭圆散景。柔和的环境光线轻轻裹过空荡的书桌。

摄影机（Camera）：全程手持，带有刻意打破的构图，匹配图 3 的视觉风格——偏位取景、严重违反头顶空间的裁切、透过前景遮挡、不对称放置。长焦和微距电影摄影，f/1.4 光圈下极端浅景深，变形镜头特性在失焦高光处形成椭圆散景（oval bokeh）。全程柔和弥散的布光——电脑显示器屏幕的冷蓝白光发出包裹性的环境光作为主光源，窗外微弱的冷色都市光照提供轻柔的补光，无硬光池，无定向光束。冷色中性色调，压黑阴影被环境光包裹软化。

声音（Sounds）：房间内全程沉重的静止。镜头 1——安静的环境房间基调，他那个方向传来钢笔在纸上的细微刮擦声，显示器的细微电子嗡鸣，远方城市环境音。镜头 2——更响的钢笔刮擦声，纸张质感，迟疑的呼吸，笔尖停顿。镜头 3——他缓慢受控的呼吸，非常安静。镜头 4——纸上最后一下笔画，钢笔轻轻放到桌面，他的手仍放在桌上，他安静的呼气。镜头 5——寂静，显示器的细微环境嗡鸣，远方城市声音。无音乐。无对白。

总计：12 秒 / 5 镜头 / 16:9
