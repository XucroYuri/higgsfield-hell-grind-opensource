## 原文 (source)

Single continuous shot in a dim industrial bedroom, 5 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Setting: a dim sci-fi bedroom in an industrial building. Concrete walls, low bed in shadow, large floor-to-ceiling glass windows on the right wall overlooking a dark industrial cityscape at night. A desk near the windows with two dark monitors and a small desk lamp with a soft fabric shade emitting a soft diffused warm-cool glow that wraps gently around the desk surface — no hard pools, light is wrapping and ambient. Faint cool ambient glow filters through the windows from the city outside. Cool neutral palette, deep crushed blacks softened by gentle ambient wrap. On the desk: a single sheet of paper with handwriting on it (text not legible to camera), a pen lying beside it. The room is empty, the desk is the only point of light.

Camera: One continuous handheld take. The camera begins at chest height across the dim room from the desk, framed wide on the empty room with the desk in the deep background, the lamp glowing softly. The camera then DOLLIES IN slowly and steadily toward the desk, closing the distance through the dark room, foreground out-of-focus elements drifting past the lens (corner of furniture, edge of bed, soft shapes) as anamorphic oval bokeh. As the camera approaches the desk, it simultaneously TILTS DOWN, the angle gradually descending from chest level toward a 3/4 overhead view of the desk surface. The two movements (dolly in, tilt down) flow together as one fluid motion. By the end of the shot, the camera is close to the desk and angled down at the surface in a 3/4 perspective — looking partly down, partly forward — framing the folded handwritten letter and the pen lying on the desk surface in the soft pool of lamp light. The handwriting on the paper is intentionally out of focus and unreadable, only abstract ink lines suggesting cursive script. The letter is centered in the final frame composition.

Camera characteristics: long-lens telephoto cinematography, very shallow depth of field at f/1.4, anamorphic lens characteristic creating oval bokeh shapes in defocused highlights. Soft diffused lighting throughout — desk lamp with fabric shade emitting wrapping ambient glow, faint cool city light from windows providing gentle fill, no hard pools or directional beams. The camera holds with subtle handheld breathing throughout, intimate and observational.

Sounds: heavy stillness in the room. Quiet ambient room tone, faint hum of monitors and the lamp, distant city sounds through the windows. No movement, no breathing, no human presence audible. Just the empty room and the letter waiting. No music. No dialogue.

Total: 5s / 1 continuous shot / 16:9

## 中文对照 (zh)

昏暗工业卧室内单个连续镜头（single continuous shot），时长 5 秒，16:9，照片级写实（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊（motion blur），无音乐。

场景（Setting）：工业建筑内一间昏暗的科幻卧室。混凝土墙面，低床处于阴影中，右侧墙上有大型通顶落地玻璃窗，俯瞰夜晚暗沉的工业都市天际线。窗边有一张书桌，上面放着两台暗色显示器和一盏带柔软织物灯罩的小台灯，散发出柔和漫射的温冷光晕，温柔地包裹桌面——无生硬光斑，光线是包裹性的环境光。城市外的微弱冷调环境光透过窗户渗透进来。冷调中性色调，深沉压黑（deep crushed blacks）被柔和的环境包裹所软化。桌上：一张上面有手写文字的单页纸（相机无法读出文字），旁边躺着一支笔。房间里空荡荡的，书桌是唯一的光源。

摄影机（Camera）：一个连续手持（handheld）的长镜头。镜头从书桌对面昏暗房间的齐胸高度开始，以广角构图框住空房间，书桌在景深深处，台灯柔和地发光。随后摄影机（DOLLIES IN）缓慢而稳定地向书桌推进，穿过黑暗的房间拉近距离，前景失焦元素贴着镜头漂移而过（家具一角、床沿、柔和的轮廓），形成变形宽银幕镜头（anamorphic）椭圆光斑（oval bokeh）。当镜头靠近书桌时，它同时（TILTS DOWN）下摇，角度从齐胸高度逐步下降，直到书桌表面的四分之三俯视角度。两个动作（推进 dolly in、下摇 tilt down）融为一次流畅的运动。到镜头末尾，摄影机已贴近书桌，以四分之三透视向下倾斜——一部分下俯、一部分前视——在台灯柔和的光池中框住叠好的手写信和躺放在桌面上的笔。纸上的手写文字刻意失焦、无法辨认，只见抽象的墨线暗示笔迹。信处于最终画面的构图中心。

摄影机特性（Camera characteristics）：长焦远摄（long-lens telephoto）摄影，f/1.4 下极浅景深，变形宽银幕镜头（anamorphic）特性在失焦高光中形成椭圆光斑（oval bokeh）形状。全程柔和漫射光——带织物灯罩的台灯散发放射性的包裹环境光，窗外冷调微弱的城市光提供细腻补光，无生硬光斑或定向光束。全程摄影机持守着微妙的手持呼吸（handheld breathing），亲昵而观察式。

声音（Sounds）：室内沉寂厚重。安静的环境室内空间声，显示器与台灯的微弱嗡鸣，窗外来自城市的遥远声响。无动作、无呼吸、无人声可闻。只有空房间和一封等待中的信。无音乐。无对白。

总计：5 秒 / 1 个连续镜头 / 16:9
