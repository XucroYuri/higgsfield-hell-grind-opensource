## 原文 (source)

Five-shot sequence in a dim industrial bedroom, 12 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose from Image 1.

Setting: a dim sci-fi bedroom in an industrial building. Concrete walls, low bed with white sheets in shadow, large floor-to-ceiling glass windows on the right wall overlooking a dark industrial cityscape at night. A desk near the windows with two dark monitors and a small desk lamp with a soft fabric shade emitting a soft diffused warm-cool glow that wraps gently around the desk surface — no hard pools, light is wrapping and ambient. Faint cool ambient glow filters through the windows from the city outside. Cool neutral palette overall, deep crushed blacks softened by gentle ambient wrap. On the desk: a single sheet of paper with handwriting on it (text not legible to camera), a pen, the lamp.

Shot 1 (2.5s) — FOLLOWING DRIFT THROUGH FOREGROUND OBJECTS:
Handheld camera that DRIFTS slowly through the dark room toward him, lens floating past out-of-focus foreground elements — the corner of a piece of furniture, the edge of the bed in shadow, anything between camera and subject — these foreground shapes pass through the frame as soft anamorphic oval bokeh, partially blocking and revealing him as the camera moves. He sits at the desk far to the left of frame, hunched over the paper, only partially visible past the foreground blur. Very shallow depth of field at f/1.4, only a thin slice of focus on him in the deep background, foreground and surroundings dissolved into soft bokeh haze. The camera floats and breathes, finding him through the obstacles. Long-lens telephoto, anamorphic oval bokeh, soft diffused lamp glow.

Shot 2 (2s) — MACRO ON HAND, FOREGROUND BLUR FOLLOWS:
Hard cut. Handheld macro extreme close-up at the desk, lens inches from his hand. The pen and his fingers are in razor-sharp focus, but in the FOREGROUND of the frame the edge of the paper or a shadow of his other hand drifts as soft out-of-focus blur, partially obscuring the contact point. As the hand moves with his writing strokes, the camera DRIFTS slightly to FOLLOW the pen tip, the foreground blur shifting with the movement. Very shallow depth of field, only the immediate contact point in razor focus, everything else dissolved. Soft diffused light wraps gently. Anamorphic oval bokeh on every defocused highlight.

Shot 3 (2s) — TIGHT CROP THROUGH FOREGROUND, FOLLOWING HIS GAZE:
Hard cut. Handheld tight close-up on his face, framed THROUGH a foreground out-of-focus element — the soft glow of the lamp shade dominates the right side of frame as a diffused bokeh shape, partially obscuring his face from one side. The camera frames so tightly that headroom is violated — only his eyes, nose, and partial mouth visible, top of head cut off. As his eyes shift downward toward the paper off-frame, the camera DRIFTS subtly with the gaze direction, the foreground lamp glow shifting as we move. Very shallow depth, only his eye in razor-sharp focus, the rest of his face and the foreground dissolved into anamorphic oval bokeh.

Shot 4 (2s) — THROUGH-FOREGROUND VOYEURISTIC FOLLOW, FOLDING THE LETTER:
Hard cut. Handheld extreme close-up shot from a low angle, framed THROUGH the lamp itself in extreme foreground bokeh — the lamp's soft fabric shade dominates the left side of frame as a soft glowing shape, almost half the frame obscured. Through this voyeuristic framing we see his hands at the desk in the gently lit area — he picks up the loose sheet of paper, folds the letter in half once, folds again, sets it down on the desk. As his hands move through the action, the camera DRIFTS subtly to follow the folding motion, the foreground lamp shape shifting in parallax against the background. Very shallow depth, only the hands and paper in razor focus, the foreground lamp shade and surroundings dissolved into anamorphic oval bokeh.

Shot 5 (3.5s) — SLOW DRIFT TOWARD ABANDONED LETTER:
Hard cut. Handheld static-ish shot of the desk that begins with the camera framed on the EMPTY space of the desk surface, the folded letter in the lower left corner of the frame in shallow softness. Slowly, the camera DRIFTS forward and slightly to the left, the focus pulling subtly so that the letter comes into sharper focus as the camera approaches it. Foreground out-of-focus elements — the edge of a monitor, a cable, the rim of the lamp base — pass softly through the frame as the camera drifts. The composition remains broken — letter in the lower left, negative space dominating the rest. By the end of the shot, the letter is in clearer focus, small and abandoned, its placement emotionally heavy. Very shallow depth of field, anamorphic oval bokeh from the lamp glow and city windows beyond. Soft ambient light.

Camera: handheld throughout with following drift movements — the camera floats, drifts, follows the action and the gaze of the character, never static for long. Foreground out-of-focus elements consistently present in every shot — partial obstructions, edges of objects, lamp shades, shadows passing through frame as soft anamorphic oval bokeh. Long-lens telephoto and macro cinematography with very shallow depth of field at f/1.4 aperture, only thin slices of focus at any time, the rest of the frame dissolved into soft bokeh haze. Anamorphic lens characteristic, oval bokeh on every defocused highlight. Soft diffused lighting throughout.

Sounds: heavy stillness in the room throughout. Shot 1 — quiet ambient room tone, faint scratching of pen on paper from his direction, faint hum of monitors, distant city ambient. Shot 2 — louder pen scratching, paper texture, hesitation breath between strokes, pen tip pausing. Shot 3 — his slow controlled breath, very quiet. Shot 4 — quiet paper handling, sharp creases of folding, soft set-down on desk surface. Shot 5 — silence, faint ambient hum from monitors, distant city sounds, faint electrical buzz from the lamp. No music. No dialogue.

Total: 12s / 5 shots / 16:9

## 中文对照 (zh)

昏暗工业卧室中的五镜头序列，12 秒，16:9，照片写实（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，无音乐。

请参照 Image 1（图 1）中那位黑色凌乱头发、浅色胡须、鼻梁上缠着红色绷带的男性。

设定：工业建筑里一间昏暗的科幻卧室。混凝土墙壁，阴影中白色床单的低床，右侧墙壁上有大型落地玻璃窗，俯瞰夜晚黑暗的工业城市景观。窗边有一张桌子，摆着两台深色显示器与一盏带柔和织物灯罩的小台灯，发出柔和的漫射暖冷光，温柔包裹桌面——无硬光池，光带包裹性且具环境感。窗外城市透进隐约的冷调环境辉光。整体冷调中性色调，深沉的压缩黑（crushed blacks）被轻柔环绕的环境光柔化。桌上：一张写有手写字迹的纸张（文字对摄影机不可辨认），一支笔，那盏灯。

Shot 1（镜头 1，2.5s）——穿行前景物体的跟随漂流（FOLLOWING DRIFT THROUGH FOREGROUND OBJECTS）：
手持（handheld）摄影机在昏暗的房间中缓慢地向它漂移，镜头飘过失焦的前景元素——一件家具的角落、阴影中床的边缘、摄影机与主体之间的任何东西——这些前景形状化作柔和变形宽银幕椭圆光斑穿过画面，随着摄影机的移动部分遮挡又显露他。他坐在画面极左的桌边，弓身在纸上，越过前景模糊只部分可见。光圈 f/1.4 极浅景深，只有薄薄一片对焦落在深背景中的他身上，前景与周围溶解进柔和焦外光斑的雾霭。摄影机漂浮并呼吸，穿行障碍找到他。长焦镜头，变形宽银幕椭圆光斑，柔和漫射的灯辉光。

Shot 2（镜头 2，2s）——手的宏观特写，前景模糊跟随（MACRO ON HAND, FOREGROUND BLUR FOLLOWS）：
硬切。桌上手持宏观极端特写，镜头距他的手仅数英寸。笔与他的手指处于锐利清晰的对焦中，但画面前景中纸的边缘或他另一只手的阴影化作柔和的失焦模糊漂移，部分遮蔽触点。当他的手循着书写笔划移动时，摄影机轻微漂移以跟随笔尖，前景模糊随运动移动。极浅景深，只有直接的触点处于锐利对焦，其余一切溶解。柔和漫射光温柔包裹。每个失焦高光上变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

Shot 3（镜头 3，2s）——透过前景的紧裁切，跟随他的目光（TIGHT CROP THROUGH FOREGROUND, FOLLOWING HIS GAZE）：
硬切。他脸上的手持紧特写，透过一处失焦的前景元素取景——灯罩柔和的辉光化作漫射焦外光斑形状主导画面右侧，从他的一侧部分遮蔽他的脸。摄影机取景如此之紧，以至于头顶留白（headroom）被打破——只有他的眼睛、鼻子与部分嘴巴可见，头顶被裁掉。当他的目光向下移向画框之外的纸时，摄影机随目光方向微妙漂移，随着我们的移动前景灯辉光随之移动。极浅景深，只有他的眼睛处于锐利清晰的对焦中，他脸的其余部分与前景溶解进变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

Shot 4（镜头 4，2s）——透过前景的偷窥式跟随，折叠信纸（THROUGH-FOREGROUND VOYEURISTIC FOLLOW, FOLDING THE LETTER）：
硬切。手持从低角度拍摄的极端特写，透过灯本身以极端前景焦外光斑取景——灯柔软的织物灯罩化作柔和发光形状主导画面左侧，几乎遮蔽画面一半。透过这偷窥式的取景，我们看见他的双手在柔和照亮区域里的桌面上——他拿起那张松散的纸，把信对折一次，再对折，把它放在桌上。当他的手完成动作时，摄影机微妙漂移以跟随折叠运动，前景灯的形状以视差相对背景移动。极浅景深，只有双手与纸处于锐利对焦，前景灯罩与周围溶解进变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

Shot 5（镜头 5，3.5s）——缓慢漂移向被弃置的信（SLOW DRIFT TOWARD ABANDONED LETTER）：
硬切。桌子的手持静态感镜头，摄影机开始取景在桌面空旷的空间上，折好的信在画面左下角浅浅柔和。缓慢地，摄影机向前并略偏左漂移，对焦微妙拉动，随摄影机靠近这封信进入更清晰的对焦。失焦的前景元素——一台显示器的边缘、一根数据线、灯座的边缘——随摄影机漂移柔和地穿过画面。构图仍保持破碎——信在左下，负空间主导其余部分。镜头结束时，这封信处于更清晰的对焦，小而弃置，其位置情感沉重。极浅景深，来自灯辉光与窗外城市的变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。柔和环境光。

摄影机：全程手持，带跟随性的漂移运动——摄影机漂浮、漂移、跟随角色动作与目光，从不长时间静止。每个镜头中始终存在失焦的前景元素——部分遮挡、物体边缘、灯罩、化作柔和变形宽银幕椭圆光斑穿过画面的阴影。长焦镜头与宏观摄影风格，光圈 f/1.4 极浅景深，任何时刻都只有薄薄一片对焦，画面其余部分溶解进柔和焦外光斑的雾霭。变形宽银幕镜头特性，每个失焦高光上的椭圆光斑。全程柔和漫射的灯光。

声音：室内全程沉重的寂静。镜头 1——安静的环境室内声，他那边笔在纸上隐约的沙沙声，显示器隐约的嗡鸣，远处的城市环境音。镜头 2——更响的笔沙沙声，纸的质感，笔划之间犹豫的呼吸，笔尖停顿。镜头 3——他缓慢克制的呼吸，非常安静。镜头 4——安静的纸张处理，折叠的利落折痕，在桌面上轻柔的放下。镜头 5——寂静，显示器隐约的环境嗡鸣，远处的城市声，灯隐约的电嗡嗡声。无音乐。无对白。

总计：12 秒 / 5 镜头 / 16:9
