## 原文 (source)

Five-shot sequence in a dim industrial bedroom, 12 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose from Image 1.

Setting: a dim sci-fi bedroom in an industrial building. Concrete walls, low bed with white sheets in shadow, large floor-to-ceiling glass windows on the right wall overlooking a dark industrial cityscape at night. A desk near the windows with two dark monitors and a small desk lamp with a soft fabric shade — the lamp emits a soft diffused warm-cool glow that wraps gently around the desk surface and surrounding area, no hard pools or directional beams, the light is wrapping and ambient. Faint cool ambient glow filters through the windows from the city outside, adding gentle soft fill to the room. Cool neutral palette overall, deep crushed blacks softened by gentle ambient wrap. On the desk: a single sheet of paper with handwriting on it (text not legible to camera), a pen, the lamp.

Shot 1 (2.5s) — OFF-CENTER ISOLATION:
Handheld wide medium shot, deliberately off-balance composition. The dark-haired man is pushed far to the LEFT EDGE of the frame, sitting at the desk hunched over the paper, only a small portion of his body visible. The right two-thirds of the frame is dominated by deep empty soft-lit space — the dark room, the windows showing the silent industrial city outside in cool soft glow, the empty bed in shadow. He is small and isolated in his own space. Long-lens telephoto, shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh on the lamp's soft glow and distant window lights. Camera holds slightly tilted, breathing handheld. Soft diffused lighting wraps the scene gently.

Shot 2 (2s) — MACRO ON HAND WRITING ON PAPER:
Hard cut. Handheld macro extreme close-up at the desk, lens inches from his hand. Frame filled by his hand holding the pen, the back of his fingers and knuckles in razor-sharp focus, the loose sheet of paper below in extreme shallow softness — handwriting dissolved into unreadable blur, just a pale surface beneath the pen tip. The hand makes the writing motion, hesitates between strokes. Soft diffused light from the lamp wraps gently over the back of his hand from above, no harsh shadow lines, the lighting rounded and gentle. Static intimate.

Shot 3 (2s) — TIGHT CROP CUTTING ACROSS HIS FACE:
Hard cut. Handheld extreme close-up on his face, deliberately broken composition — the camera frames so tightly that the TOP of his head is cut off severely above the eyebrows, and the BOTTOM of frame cuts at his upper lip, leaving only his eyes and nose bridge visible across the entire frame. No headroom, no breathing room — the crop is uncomfortable and intimate. His eyes hold a downward gaze, fixed on the paper off-frame, breath shallow. Soft diffused light wraps his face gently from one side, the other side falling into soft shadow without hard edges. Long-lens, extreme shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh.

Shot 4 (2s) — THROUGH-FOREGROUND, FOLDING THE LETTER:
Hard cut. Handheld extreme close-up shot from a low angle, framed THROUGH the lamp itself in extreme foreground bokeh — the lamp's soft shade dominates the left side of frame as a soft out-of-focus glowing shape, partially blocking the view. Through this obscured framing we see his hands at the desk in the gently lit area — he picks up the loose sheet of paper, folds the letter in half once, folds again, sets it down on the desk surface. Voyeuristic, observational, as if we are watching from a hidden position. Long-lens, extreme shallow depth, the foreground lamp shade dissolving into anamorphic oval bokeh, soft wrapping light throughout.

Shot 5 (3.5s) — THE LETTER LEFT, BROKEN COMPOSITION:
Hard cut. Handheld static-feel shot of the desk, deliberately off-balance composition. The folded letter is positioned in the LOWER LEFT corner of the frame, small. The rest of the frame is dominated by negative space — the dark room above and the right two-thirds empty, the lamp's soft glow cropped at the bottom edge of frame, partial. There is no headroom convention, no centering — the letter is almost falling off the bottom of the frame, vulnerable and abandoned. Hold on this composition. The letter is the focus of the eye, content unseen, its placement in the frame emotionally heavy. Long-lens, extreme shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh from the lamp glow and city windows beyond. Soft ambient light wraps gently across the empty desk.

Camera: handheld throughout with deliberately broken compositions — off-center framing, severe headroom-violation crop, through-foreground obstruction, asymmetric placement. Long-lens telephoto and macro cinematography with extreme shallow depth of field at f/1.4 aperture, anamorphic lens characteristic creating oval bokeh shapes in defocused highlights. Soft diffused lighting throughout — the desk lamp with fabric shade emitting wrapping ambient glow, faint cool city light from windows providing gentle fill, no hard pools or directional beams. Cool neutral palette with crushed blacks softened by ambient wrap.

Sounds: heavy stillness in the room throughout. Shot 1 — quiet ambient room tone, faint scratching of pen on paper from his direction, faint hum of monitors, distant city ambient. Shot 2 — louder pen scratching, paper texture, hesitation breath, pen tip pausing. Shot 3 — his slow controlled breath, very quiet. Shot 4 — quiet paper handling, sharp creases of folding, soft set-down on desk surface. Shot 5 — silence, faint ambient hum from monitors, distant city sounds, faint electrical buzz from the lamp. No music. No dialogue.

Total: 12s / 5 shots / 16:9

## 中文对照 (zh)

一个昏暗工业卧室中的五镜头序列，12 秒，16:9，写实，厚重 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，无音乐。

参照图 1 中那名黑色头发凌乱、留浅胡须、鼻子上贴着红色绷带（bandage）的男子。

场景：工业建筑内一间昏暗的科幻卧室。混凝土墙，低矮床铺着深阴影中的白色床单，右侧墙上一扇大幅落地玻璃窗，俯瞰夜晚幽暗的工业城景。窗边一张书桌，放着两台深色显示器和一盏带柔软织物灯罩的小台灯——台灯发出柔和漫射的冷暖混合光晕，轻柔地包裹着桌面及周围区域，无生硬的光圈或定向光束，光线是包裹性渗透性的（ambient）环境光。城市外透过窗户渗入微弱的冷色环境光，为房间添上一层轻柔的柔和补光。整体为冷色中性色调，被轻柔的环境光包裹柔化了的深邃压黑。书桌上有：一张写着笔迹的纸（文字对摄影机不可辨读），一支笔，那盏灯。

镜头 1（2.5 秒）——偏心孤立（OFF-CENTER ISOLATION）：
手持广角中景，刻意失衡的构图。黑发男子被推到画面最左边缘，坐在书桌前伏身于纸上，身体只露出一小部分。画面右侧三分之二被深沉空旷的柔和受光空间主导——昏暗的房间，窗户显出窗外静默的工业城市散着冷色柔光，阴影中的空床。他在自己的空间里显得渺小而孤立。长焦镜头，浅景深，台灯的柔光与远处窗灯上可见变形的椭圆光斑（bokeh）。摄影机微微倾斜手持，手持呼吸。柔和漫射光轻柔地包裹场景。

镜头 2（2 秒）——纸上写字的手的特写（MACRO ON HAND WRITING ON PAPER）：
硬切。书桌前手持特写极端特写，镜头离他的手只有几英寸。画框被他的手握着笔填满，指背与指节处于锐利对焦，其下方松散的纸以极致浅柔的形式呈现——笔迹溶解成不可辨认的模糊，只是笔尖下那片苍白的表面。手做出书写的动作，在笔画之间迟疑。来自台灯的柔和漫射光从上方轻柔地包裹他的手背，无生硬阴影线，光线圆润柔和。静默的亲密。

镜头 3（2 秒）——横切其脸部的紧贴裁切（TIGHT CROP CUTTING ACROSS HIS FACE）：
硬切。手持极端特写落在他的脸上，刻意破碎的构图——摄影机取景如此之紧，以至他头顶在眉毛上方被严重切掉，画框底部又切在他的上唇处，只留下他的双眼与鼻梁横贯整个画面。无头部留白，无呼吸余地——这裁切令人不安而亲密。他的目光低垂，定在画框之外的纸上，呼吸很浅。柔和漫射光从一侧轻柔地包裹他的脸，另一侧坠入无生硬边缘的柔影中。长焦镜头，极致浅景深，变形椭圆光斑。

镜头 4（2 秒）——透过前景，折叠信件（THROUGH-FOREGROUND, FOLDING THE LETTER）：
硬切。手持极端特写，低角度拍摄，透过台灯本身在极致前景焦外光斑（bokeh）中取景——台灯的柔光灯罩在画面左侧占主导，成一团柔和失焦的发光形状，部分遮挡住视线。透过这片被遮挡的取景，我们看到他在温柔受光区里书桌旁的双手——他拾起那张松散的纸，把信件对折一次，再折一次，放到桌面上。偷窥式、观察式的（Voyeuristic），仿佛我们正从一处隐蔽位置旁观。长焦镜头，极致浅景深，前景灯罩溶解成变形的椭圆光斑（bokeh），全程柔和包裹的光线。

镜头 5（3.5 秒）——留下的信，破碎构图（THE LETTER LEFT, BROKEN COMPOSITION）：
硬切。手持静态感镜头落在书桌上，刻意失衡的构图。折叠的信件被置于画面左下角，很小。画面其余部分被留白主导——上方幽暗的房间与右侧空出三分之二，台灯的柔光在画面底缘被裁切，不完整。无头部留白惯例，无居中——这封信几乎要掉出画面底部，脆弱而被遗弃。停留在这一构图上。这封信是目光的焦点，内容不可见，它在画面中的位置带着情感上的沉重。长焦镜头，极致浅景深，台灯光芒与窗外城市窗灯形成变形的椭圆光斑（bokeh）。柔和的环境光轻柔地包裹空荡的书桌。

摄影机：全程手持，配以刻意破碎的构图——偏心取景、严重破坏头部留白的裁切、透过前景的遮挡、不对称的摆放。长焦与微距摄影，配合 f/1.4 光圈极浅景深，变形镜头特性在失焦高光中形成椭圆光斑形状。全程柔和漫射布光——带织物灯罩的台灯散发包裹性的环境光晕，窗外微弱的冷城光提供轻柔补光，无生硬光圈，无定向光束。冷色中性色调，被环境光包裹柔化的压黑。

声音：全程房间内浓重的寂静。镜头 1——安静的室内环境音，他方向传来笔在纸上细微的沙沙声，显示器微弱的嗡鸣，远处城市的环境声。镜头 2——更响的笔划沙沙声，纸张纹理，迟疑的呼吸，笔尖停顿。镜头 3——他缓慢而克制的呼吸，非常安静。镜头 4——安静的取纸声，叠纸时尖锐的折痕声，轻柔落在桌面上。镜头 5——寂静，显示器微弱的氛围嗡鸣，远处城市的声响，台灯微弱的电流嗡鸣。无音乐。无对白。

总计：12 秒 / 5 个镜头 / 16:9
