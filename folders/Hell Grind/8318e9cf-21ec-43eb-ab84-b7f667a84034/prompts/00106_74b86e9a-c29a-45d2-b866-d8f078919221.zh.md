Six-shot sequence in a dim industrial bedroom, 15 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose from Image 1.

Setting: a dim sci-fi bedroom in an industrial building. Concrete walls with rough stone outcrops embedded into them on the left side, low bed with white sheets, built-in storage cabinets in the center with one door open, exit door visible in the wall. Large floor-to-ceiling glass windows on the right wall overlooking a dark industrial cityscape at night. A workspace near the windows with two dark monitors and a small desk lamp pooling cool-white light onto the desk surface — the only light source in the room beyond faint window glow. Cool neutral palette dominated by deep blue-grey shadows, deep crushed blacks. The room is quiet, his personal space. On the desk: an open notebook with a half-written letter (text not legible to camera), a pen. On a low surface near the open closet: a handheld scanner with a small glowing screen, compact dark-grey power cells, an open backpack.

Shot 1 (3s) — WRITING THE LETTER, MACRO ON HAND AND PEN:
Handheld macro extreme close-up at the desk, lens inches from his hand. Frame is filled by his hand holding the pen, the back of his fingers and knuckles in razor-sharp focus, the pen barrel angled toward the page below. The page itself is in extreme shallow softness, completely out of focus — any writing on it is dissolved into unreadable softness, just a pale blurred surface beneath the pen tip. The pen makes the writing motion — we see the hand's micro-movements as he writes, the wrist shifting, the knuckles flexing. He pauses for a fraction of a second between movements. The desk lamp pools cool-white light from above, catching the back of his hand in chiaroscuro, leaving the page in shadow. Long-lens macro cinematography with extreme shallow depth at f/1.4, anamorphic oval bokeh on every defocused highlight. The hand finishes a movement, the pen lifts, hesitates, sets down on the desk beside the notebook out of frame. Held briefly on the still hand.

Shot 2 (1.5s) — MACRO CLOSE-UP ON HIS EYES:
Hard cut. Handheld macro extreme close-up on his face, lens inches from him, framed so only his eyes fill the frame from a low three-quarter angle. The desk lamp's cool-white light catches his eyes from below, the rest of his face dissolved into deep shadow and shallow softness. His eyes hold a downward gaze for a beat — fixed on something off-frame below (the closed notebook). Something private, decided. Then his gaze hardens with resolve. Long-lens macro, extreme shallow depth at f/1.4, only his iris and lashes in razor-sharp focus, anamorphic oval bokeh on any background highlight.

Shot 3 (2s) — HANDS AT THE BACKPACK:
Hard cut. Handheld macro extreme close-up on his hands at the open backpack on the surface near the closet. Long-lens macro, extreme shallow focus locked on the contact point of his fingers and the gear, depth dissolved into anamorphic oval bokeh. He picks up the handheld scanner, glances at it briefly, drops it into the backpack. Picks up two compact power cells, examines them for half a beat, drops them in with a soft thud against fabric. Methodical, silent.

Shot 4 (2s) — JACKET ZIPPER CLOSING:
Hard cut. Handheld macro extreme close-up on the zipper of his tactical jacket, lens inches from his torso. Long-lens macro, extreme shallow depth — only the metal teeth of the zipper at the contact point in razor-sharp focus, his torso fabric dissolved into shallow softness, the room behind into anamorphic oval bokeh shapes. His hand pulls the zipper UP from waist to chest in one steady firm motion, the metal teeth interlocking, sealing the jacket closed. Hold for a beat on the closed zipper at his chest level.

Shot 5 (3.5s) — LEAVING THE ROOM:
Hard cut. Handheld medium shot from behind him at low angle as he walks toward the exit door, backpack slung over his shoulder. The desk lamp glows in the empty room behind him, the closed notebook on the desk visible in the soft anamorphic oval bokeh of the deep background. His silhouette moves toward the door, jacket zipped, body composed. He reaches the door, his hand on the handle, pauses for a fraction of a second — does not look back. He pulls the door open, steps through into the darker corridor beyond, and closes the door behind him with a soft heavy click. Long-lens telephoto, extreme shallow depth, the room dissolved into deep cool bokeh.

Shot 6 (3s) — THE EMPTY ROOM AND THE NOTEBOOK:
Hard cut. Handheld static-feel medium shot of the empty room — the desk with the lamp still glowing in its small pool of light, the closed notebook on the surface beside it, the rest of the room dissolved into deep cool anamorphic oval bokeh and shadow. No movement, just the lamp light, the notebook, the empty space he left behind. Long-lens telephoto, extreme shallow depth at f/1.4, anamorphic oval bokeh shapes from the windows and monitors. Held in stillness. Faint city ambient from outside the windows. The notebook is the focus of the frame — small but central, content unseen.

Camera: handheld throughout, breathing with quiet intimacy. Long-lens macro and telephoto cinematography with extreme shallow depth of field at f/1.4 aperture, the lens often inches from the subject for macro intimacy. Anamorphic lens characteristic creating oval bokeh shapes in defocused highlights. Cool neutral palette with deep crushed blacks, the desk lamp as the single cool-white light source, faint window glow from the city beyond.

Sounds: heavy stillness in the room throughout. Shot 1 — quiet scratching of pen on paper, paper texture, hesitation breaths between strokes, soft set-down of the pen. Shot 2 — quiet click of notebook closing off-frame, his soft controlled breath. Shot 5 — soft footsteps on stone floor, jacket fabric rustling, hand grasp on door handle, soft heavy click of the door closing. ## 中文对照 (zh)

昏暗工业卧室中的六镜头序列，15 秒，16:9，照片写实（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，无音乐。

请参照 Image 1（图 1）中那位黑色凌乱头发、浅色胡须、鼻梁上缠着红色绷带的男性。

设定：工业建筑里一间昏暗的科幻卧室。左侧混凝土墙壁嵌入粗糙的石质凸起（stone outcrops），白色床单的低床，中央的嵌入式收纳柜有一扇门敞开，墙壁上可见出口门。右侧墙壁上有大型落地玻璃窗，俯瞰夜晚黑暗的工业城市景观。窗边有一处工作区，摆着两台深色显示器与一盏小台灯，在桌面之上汇成冷白光束——除窗外微弱的辉光外，这是整个房间唯一的光源。冷调中性色调由深蓝灰阴影与深沉的压缩黑（crushed blacks）主导。房间安静，是他私人的空间。桌上：一本摊开的笔记本，上面有一封写到一半的信（文字对摄影机不可辨认），一支笔。在敞开的壁橱附近的低矮台面上：一只带小发光屏的手持扫描仪，紧凑的深灰色电源块（power cells），一只敞开的背包。

Shot 1（镜头 1，3s）——写那封信，手与笔的特写（WRITING THE LETTER, MACRO ON HAND AND PEN）：
桌上手持宏观极端特写，镜头距他的手仅数英寸。画面被握住笔的手填满，指背与指节处于锐利清晰的对焦中，笔杆朝向下方的纸页倾斜。纸页本身处于极端的浅景深柔和，完全失焦——其上的任何书写都被溶解成不可读的柔和，只是笔尖下方一片苍白模糊的表面。笔做出书写的动作——我们看到书写时手的微运动，手腕移动，指节屈伸。他在两次动作之间停顿几分之一秒。台灯自上方汇成冷白光，以明暗对照（chiaroscuro）掠过他的手背，把纸页留在阴影中。长焦宏观摄影风格，光圈 f/1.4 极浅景深，每个失焦高光上都是变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。手完成一次动作，笔提起，犹豫，从画框之外放下在笔记本旁的桌上。对着静止的手短暂停留。

Shot 2（镜头 2，1.5s）——他眼睛的宏观特写（MACRO CLOSE-UP ON HIS EYES）：
硬切。在他脸上手持宏观极端特写，镜头距他仅数英寸，取景使只有他的双眼从低位的四分之三角度填满画面。台灯的冷白光自下方掠过他的眼睛，他脸的其余部分溶解进深影与浅景深柔和。他的双目向下凝定一拍——盯着画框之下某一物（那本合起的笔记本）。某种私密、已决意之事。随后他的目光在决心中变硬。长焦宏观镜头，光圈 f/1.4 极浅景深，只有他的虹膜与睫毛处于锐利清晰的对焦中，任何背景高光上都变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

Shot 3（镜头 3，2s）——背包处的手（HANDS AT THE BACKPACK）：
硬切。在壁橱附近台面上敞开的背包处，对他的手手持宏观极端特写。长焦宏观镜头，极浅对焦锁定在他手指与装备的触点，景深溶解为变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。他拿起手持扫描仪，匆匆扫了一眼，丢进背包。拿起两块紧凑的电源块，审视半拍，以轻撞织物的柔和闷响丢入。有条不紊，沉默。

Shot 4（镜头 4，2s）——夹克拉链闭合（JACKET ZIPPER CLOSING）：
硬切。在他战术夹克拉链上手持宏观极端特写，镜头距他躯干仅数英寸。长焦宏观镜头，极浅景深——只有触点处拉链的金属齿处于锐利清晰的对焦中，他躯干的织物溶解进浅景深柔和，身后房间成为变形宽银幕椭圆光斑的形状。他一手使力而稳地，一下把拉链从腰间拉到胸口，金属齿互锁，将夹克密封合拢。对着胸口高度的闭合拉链停驻一拍。

Shot 5（镜头 5，3.5s）——离开房间（LEAVING THE ROOM）：
硬切。从低角度在他身后手持中景，他走向出口门，背包斜挎在肩上。台灯在他身后的空房间中发光，桌上的合起笔记本在深背景的柔和变形宽银幕椭圆光斑中可见。他的剪影移向门，夹克拉好拉链，身体镇定。他到达门边，手搭在门把上，停顿几分之一秒——没有回头。他拉开门，迈步走进其外更晦暗的走廊，随一下轻柔厚重的咔哒声在身后关上门。长焦镜头，极浅景深，房间溶解进深沉的冷调焦外光斑。

Shot 6（镜头 6，3s）——空房间与笔记本（THE EMPTY ROOM AND THE NOTEBOOK）：
硬切。空房间的手持静态感中景——那张桌，灯仍在其一小片光池中发光，旁边台面上那本合起的笔记本，房间其余部分溶解进深沉的冷调变形宽银幕椭圆光斑与阴影。毫无动静，只有灯光、笔记本，以及他留下的一切空无。长焦镜头，光圈 f/1.4 极浅景深，自窗户与显示器长出变形宽银幕椭圆光斑形状。凝定在静默中。窗外传来隐约的城市环境音。笔记本是画面焦点——小却居中，内容不可见。

摄影机：全程手持，以安静的亲密感呼吸。长焦宏观与长焦摄影风格，光圈 f/1.4 极端浅景深，镜头常距主体数英寸以求宏观亲密。变形宽银幕镜头特性，在失焦高光中产生椭圆光斑形状。冷调中性色调带深沉压缩黑，台灯作为唯一的冷白光源，窗外来自城市微弱的辉光。

声音：室内全程沉重的寂静。镜头 1——笔在纸上安静沙沙的书写声，纸的质感，笔划之间的迟疑呼吸，笔的轻柔放下。镜头 2——画框之外笔记本合起的安静咔哒声，他轻柔而克制的呼吸。镜头 3——物品落入背包撞上织物的柔和闷响，电源块隐约的金属叮当，扫描仪屏幕隐约的嗡鸣。镜头 4——夹克拉链自腰间拉至胸口决断的金属拉链声。镜头 5——石地板上轻柔的脚步声，夹克织物窸窣，手抓住门把手，房门关闭轻柔厚重的咔哒声。镜头 6——寂静，显示器隐约的环境嗡鸣，窗外远处的城市声，台灯隐约的电嗡嗡声。无音乐。无对白。

总计：15 秒 / 6 镜头 / 16:9
