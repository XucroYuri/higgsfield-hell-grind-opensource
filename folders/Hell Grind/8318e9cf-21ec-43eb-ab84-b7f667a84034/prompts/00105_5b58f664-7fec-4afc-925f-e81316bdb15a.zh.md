## 原文 (source)

Seven-shot sequence in a dim industrial bedroom, 15 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose from Image 1.

Setting: a dim sci-fi bedroom in an industrial building. Concrete walls with rough stone outcrops on the left side, low bed with white sheets, built-in storage cabinets in the center with one door open, exit door visible. Large floor-to-ceiling glass windows on the right wall overlooking a dark industrial cityscape at night. A desk near the windows with two dark monitors and a small desk lamp pooling cool-white light onto the desk surface — the only warm light source in the room beyond faint window glow. Cool neutral palette dominated by deep blue-grey shadows, deep crushed blacks. On the desk: an open notebook with a half-written letter (text not legible to camera), a pen. On a low surface near the open closet: a handheld scanner, dark-grey power cells, an open backpack.

Shot 1 (2.5s) — SLOW PUSH-IN ON HIM AT THE DESK:
Wide medium shot of the bedroom from across the room, the dark-haired man sitting at the desk by the window writing in the notebook, hunched over the page, the lamp pooling cool light onto his hands. Camera SLOWLY PUSHES IN toward him through the dark room, the bedroom dissolving past the lens edges as we close the distance, his figure growing larger in frame. Long-lens telephoto, extreme shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh, the path between camera and him in deep cool bokeh.

Shot 2 (2s) — MACRO ON HAND WRITING, STATIC:
Hard cut. Handheld macro extreme close-up at the desk, lens inches from his hand. Frame filled by his hand holding the pen, the back of his fingers and knuckles in razor-sharp focus, the page below in extreme shallow softness — any writing dissolved into unreadable blur. The hand makes the writing motion, pauses, finishes a movement, pen lifts, hesitates, sets down off-frame. Camera held tight and intimate, breathing slightly.

Shot 3 (1.5s) — OVER-THE-SHOULDER, NOTEBOOK CLOSING:
Hard cut. Handheld over-the-shoulder shot from behind him, framed past his shoulder looking down at the desk. We see his hand on the closed notebook, the lamp light pooling on it, his other hand reaching off-frame for the gear. Camera DRIFTS slightly with his movement as he pushes away from the desk and stands, the frame following his rise. Long-lens, shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh on the desk surface and lamp glow.

Shot 4 (2s) — LOW ANGLE TILT-UP AS HE PICKS UP GEAR:
Hard cut. Handheld low-angle shot at the surface near the open closet, camera at the level of the gear, looking up at his hands as they enter frame from above. He picks up the handheld scanner, the camera TILTS UP with the rising motion of his hand following the scanner. The scanner enters the backpack with a thud. Camera tilts down again as he reaches for the power cells. The motion reveals his torso and a fragment of his face from below in chiaroscuro lamp light. Long-lens, shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh.

Shot 5 (2s) — MACRO ON ZIPPER CLOSING, STATIC:
Hard cut. Handheld macro extreme close-up on the zipper of his tactical jacket, lens inches from his torso. Long-lens macro, extreme shallow depth — only the metal teeth of the zipper at the contact point in razor-sharp focus. His hand pulls the zipper UP from waist to chest in one steady firm motion. Held briefly on the closed zipper.

Shot 6 (3s) — PULL-BACK REVEALS HIM READY, THEN TRACKS HIM TO DOOR:
Hard cut. Handheld medium shot framed on him from the front, fully geared, jacket zipped, backpack slung over shoulder. Camera SLOWLY PULLS BACK as he stands still for one beat — composed, ready. Then he turns and starts walking toward the exit door. Camera pivots and TRACKS BEHIND him as he walks, following his back through the room. The desk lamp glows behind him in the deep background, the notebook visible in soft anamorphic bokeh on the desk. He reaches the door, hand on the handle, pauses for a fraction of a second — does not look back. He pulls the door open, steps through into the darker corridor beyond, closes the door behind him. Camera holds at the closed door for a beat. Long-lens telephoto, shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh throughout.

Shot 7 (2s) — STATIC LOCKED-OFF ON EMPTY DESK AND NOTEBOOK:
Hard cut. Static medium close-up locked-off on the desk — the lamp still glowing in its small pool of cool light, the closed notebook sitting beside it in the lit area, the rest of the room dissolved into deep cool anamorphic oval bokeh and shadow beyond. No movement. No camera shake. Held in absolute stillness. The notebook is the focus of the frame, content unseen, lamp light catching its closed cover. Faint city ambient from outside the windows.

Camera: handheld throughout most of the sequence with active camera movements — push-in, drift, tilt, pull-back, tracking — except for the macro shots (held tight and breathing) and the final shot (locked-off static). Long-lens telephoto and macro cinematography with extreme shallow depth of field at f/1.4 aperture, anamorphic lens characteristic creating oval bokeh shapes in defocused highlights. Cool neutral palette with deep crushed blacks, the desk lamp as the single cool-white light source, faint window glow from the city beyond.

Sounds: heavy stillness in the room throughout the sequence. Shot 1 — quiet ambient room tone, faint scratching of pen on paper from his direction, faint hum of monitors. Shot 2 — louder pen scratching, paper texture, hesitation breath, soft set-down of pen. Shot 3 — quiet click of notebook closing, his soft breath, fabric shift as he stands. Shot 4 — soft thuds of objects landing in backpack, faint metallic clink of power cells, faint hum from scanner's screen. Shot 5 — decisive metallic zip of the jacket zipper. Shot 6 — soft footsteps on stone floor, jacket fabric rustling, hand grasp on door handle, soft heavy click of the door closing. Shot 7 — silence, faint ambient hum from monitors, distant city sounds, faint electrical buzz from the lamp. No music. No dialogue.

Total: 15s / 7 shots / 16:9

## 中文对照 (zh)

昏暗工业卧室中的七镜头序列，15 秒，16:9，照片写实（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，无音乐。

请参照 Image 1（图 1）中那位黑色凌乱头发、浅色胡须、鼻梁上缠着红色绷带的男性。

设定：工业建筑里一间昏暗的科幻卧室。左侧混凝土墙壁带粗糙的石质凸起（stone outcrops），白色床单的低床，中央的嵌入式收纳柜有一扇门敞开，可见出口门。右侧墙壁上有大型落地玻璃窗，俯瞰夜晚黑暗的工业城市景观。窗边有一张桌子，摆着两台深色显示器与一盏小台灯，在桌面之上汇成冷白光束——除窗外微弱的辉光外，这是房间中唯一的暖光源。冷调中性色调由深蓝灰阴影与深沉的压缩黑（crushed blacks）主导。桌上：一本摊开的笔记本，上面有一封写到一半的信（文字对摄影机不可辨认），一支笔。在敞开的壁橱附近的低矮台面上：一只手持扫描仪，深灰色电源块（power cells），一只敞开的背包。

Shot 1（镜头 1，2.5s）——对他缓慢推近，在书桌边（SLOW PUSH-IN ON HIM AT THE DESK）：
从房间对侧拍摄卧室的宽中景，黑发的男性坐在窗边的桌子前写笔记本，弓身在纸页上，灯在他的手上汇成冷光。摄影机缓慢地透过昏暗的房间向他推近（push-in），随着我们缩短距离，卧室的景致从镜头边缘溶解退去，他的身形在画面中变大。长焦镜头，极端浅景深，变形宽银幕椭圆光斑，他与摄影机之间的路径处于深沉的冷调焦外光斑中。

Shot 2（镜头 2，2s）——写字的宏观特写，静态（MACRO ON HAND WRITING, STATIC）：
硬切。桌上手持宏观极端特写，镜头距他的手仅数英寸。画面被握住笔的手填满，指背与指节处于锐利清晰的对焦中，下方纸页处于极端浅景深柔和——任何书写溶解成不可读的模糊。手做出书写的动作，停顿，完成一次动作，笔提起，犹豫，在画框之外放下。摄影机紧贴而亲密地保持，微微呼吸。

Shot 3（镜头 3，1.5s）——过肩镜头，笔记本合起（OVER-THE-SHOULDER, NOTEBOOK CLOSING）：
硬切。在他身后手持过肩镜头，越其肩取景俯视桌面。我们看见他放在合起笔记本上的手，灯光在其上汇成，他另一只手伸向画框之外去取装备。当他自桌边推开并站起时，摄影机随他的动作轻微漂移，画面跟随他起身。长焦镜头，浅景深，桌面与灯辉光上变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

Shot 4（镜头 4，2s）——他取装备时低角度上仰（LOW ANGLE TILT-UP AS HE PICKS UP GEAR）：
硬切。在敞开的壁橱附近台面处手持低角度镜头，摄影机在装备的高度，向上看着他的手自上方进入画面。他拿起手持扫描仪，摄影机随他的手抬起的运动上仰（tilt-up）跟随扫描仪。扫描仪砰一声进入背包。当他伸手去取电源块时，摄影机再次向下仰。运动以明暗对照（chiaroscuro）灯光自下方揭示他的躯干与脸部的片段。长焦镜头，浅景深，变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

Shot 5（镜头 5，2s）——拉链闭合的宏观特写，静态（MACRO ON ZIPPER CLOSING, STATIC）：
硬切。在他战术夹克拉链上手持宏观极端特写，镜头距他躯干仅数英寸。长焦宏观镜头，极端浅景深——只有触点处拉链的金属齿处于锐利清晰的对焦中。他一手使力而稳地，一下把拉链从腰间拉到胸口。对着闭合的拉链短暂凝停。

Shot 6（镜头 6，3s）——拉后揭示他准备就绪，然后追踪他到门边（PULL-BACK REVEALS HIM READY, THEN TRACKS HIM TO DOOR）：
硬切。手持中景从正面取景他，全副装备，夹克拉好拉链，背包斜挎在肩上。当他静立一拍时摄影机缓慢拉后（pull-back）——镇定，整装待发。然后他转身走向出口门。摄影机转轴并在他行走时于后方追踪（track），跟随他的背穿行房间。台灯在他身后的深背景中发光，笔记本在桌上的柔和变形宽银幕椭圆光斑中可见。他到达门边，手搭在门把上，停顿几分之一秒——没有回头。他拉开门，迈步走进其外更晦暗的走廊，在身后把门合上。摄影机对着闭合的门凝停一拍。长焦镜头，浅景深，全程变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

Shot 7（镜头 7，2s）——静态锁定在空桌子与笔记本上（STATIC LOCKED-OFF ON EMPTY DESK AND NOTEBOOK）：
硬切。静态中近特写，锁定在桌子上——灯仍在其一小片冷光池中发光，合起的笔记本坐在其旁被照亮的区域，房间其余部分溶解进深沉的冷调变形宽银幕椭圆光斑与阴影。毫无动静。无摄影机抖动。凝定在绝对的静止中。笔记本是画面焦点，内容不可见，灯光掠过它合起的封面。窗外传来隐约的城市环境音。

摄影机：序列大部分时间手持，带主动的摄影机运动——推近、漂移、仰摇、拉后、追踪——唯宏观镜头（紧贴而呼吸地保持）与最后一颗镜头（锁定静态）除外。长焦镜头与宏观摄影风格，光圈 f/1.4 极端浅景深，变形宽银幕镜头特性在失焦高光中产生椭圆光斑形状。冷调中性色调带深沉压缩黑，台灯作为唯一的冷白光源，窗外来自城市微弱的辉光。

声音：序列全程室内沉重的寂静。镜头 1——安静的环境室内声，他那边笔在纸上隐约的沙沙声，显示器隐约的嗡鸣。镜头 2——更响的笔沙沙声，纸的质感，犹豫的呼吸，笔的轻柔放下。镜头 3——笔记本合起的安静咔哒声，他轻柔的呼吸，他起身时织物的移动。镜头 4——物品落入背包的柔和闷响，电源块隐约的金属叮当，扫描仪屏幕隐约的嗡鸣。镜头 5——夹克拉链决断的金属拉链声。镜头 6——石地板上轻柔的脚步声，夹克织物窸窣，手抓住门把手，房门关闭轻柔厚重的咔哒声。镜头 7——寂静，显示器隐约的环境嗡鸣，远处的城市声，灯隐约的电嗡嗡声。无音乐。无对白。

总计：15 秒 / 7 镜头 / 16:9
