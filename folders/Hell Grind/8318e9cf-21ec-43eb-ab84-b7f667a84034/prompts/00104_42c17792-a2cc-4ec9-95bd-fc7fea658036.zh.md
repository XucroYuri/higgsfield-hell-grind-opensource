## 原文 (source)

Five-shot sequence in a dim industrial bedroom, 12 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose from Image 1.

Setting: a dim sci-fi bedroom in an industrial building. Concrete walls, low bed with white sheets in shadow, large floor-to-ceiling glass windows on the right wall overlooking a dark industrial cityscape at night. A desk near the windows with two dark monitors and a small desk lamp pooling cool-white light onto the desk surface — the only warm light source in the room. Cool neutral palette dominated by deep blue-grey shadows, deep crushed blacks. On the desk: an open notebook with a half-written letter on the page (text not legible to camera), a pen, the lamp.

Shot 1 (2.5s) — OFF-CENTER ISOLATION:
Handheld wide medium shot, deliberately off-balance composition. The dark-haired man is pushed far to the LEFT EDGE of the frame, sitting at the desk hunched over the notebook, only a small portion of his body visible. The right two-thirds of the frame is dominated by deep empty dark space — the cold dark room, the windows showing the silent industrial city outside, the empty bed in shadow. He is small and isolated in his own space. Long-lens telephoto, shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh on the lamp glow and distant window lights. Camera holds slightly tilted, breathing handheld.

Shot 2 (2s) — MACRO ON HAND WRITING:
Hard cut. Handheld macro extreme close-up at the desk, lens inches from his hand. Frame filled by his hand holding the pen, the back of his fingers and knuckles in razor-sharp focus, the page below in extreme shallow softness — any writing dissolved into unreadable blur, just a pale surface beneath the pen tip. The hand makes the writing motion, hesitates between strokes. The desk lamp pools cool light from above on the back of his hand. Static intimate.

Shot 3 (2s) — DUTCH ANGLE ON HIS FACE:
Hard cut. Handheld close-up on his face from a low three-quarter angle, the camera CANTED at a hard 25-degree dutch tilt. His face fills most of the frame off-axis, the lamp light catching half of him from below in chiaroscuro, the other half lost in deep shadow. His eyes hold a downward gaze, fixed on the page off-frame, jaw tight, breath shallow. The world is visibly off-kilter through the canted frame — the windows behind him slanted, the geometry of the room broken. He is unmoored. Long-lens, extreme shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh.

Shot 4 (2s) — THROUGH-FOREGROUND SHOT, FOLDING THE LETTER:
Hard cut. Handheld extreme close-up shot from a low angle, framed THROUGH the lamp itself in extreme foreground bokeh — the lamp's metal arm or shade dominates the left side of frame as a soft out-of-focus shape, partially blocking the view. Through this obscured framing we see his hands at the desk in the lit pool — he tears the page from the notebook, folds the letter in half once, folds again, sets it down on the desk surface. Voyeuristic, observational, as if we are watching from a hiding place. Long-lens, extreme shallow depth, the foreground lamp shape dissolving into anamorphic oval bokeh.

Shot 5 (3.5s) — THE LETTER LEFT, BROKEN COMPOSITION:
Hard cut. Handheld static-feel shot of the desk, deliberately off-balance composition. The folded letter is positioned in the LOWER LEFT corner of the frame, small. The rest of the frame is dominated by negative space — the dark room above and the right two-thirds empty, the lamp's pool of light cropped at the bottom edge of frame, partial. There is no headroom convention, no centering — the letter is **almost falling off the bottom of the frame**, vulnerable and abandoned. Hold on this composition. The letter is the focus of the eye, content unseen, its placement in the frame emotionally heavy. Long-lens, extreme shallow depth, anamorphic oval bokeh on the lamp glow and distant room beyond.

Camera: handheld throughout with deliberately broken compositions — off-center framing, dutch angle, through-foreground obstruction, headroom violation, asymmetric placement. Long-lens telephoto and macro cinematography with extreme shallow depth of field at f/1.4 aperture, anamorphic lens characteristic creating oval bokeh shapes in defocused highlights. Cool neutral palette with deep crushed blacks, the desk lamp as the single cool-white light source, faint window glow from the city beyond.

Sounds: heavy stillness in the room throughout. Shot 1 — quiet ambient room tone, faint scratching of pen on paper from his direction, faint hum of monitors, distant city ambient. Shot 2 — louder pen scratching, paper texture, hesitation breath, pen tip pausing. Shot 3 — his slow controlled breath, very quiet. Shot 4 — quiet tear of paper from notebook, sharp creases of folding, soft set-down on desk surface. Shot 5 — silence, faint ambient hum from monitors, distant city sounds, faint electrical buzz from the lamp, his absence a tangible thing. No music. No dialogue.

Total: 12s / 5 shots / 16:9

## 中文对照 (zh)

昏暗工业卧室中的五镜头序列，12 秒，16:9，照片写实（photorealistic），厚重的 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，无音乐。

请参照 Image 1（图 1）中那位黑色凌乱头发、浅色胡须、鼻梁上缠着红色绷带的男性。

设定：工业建筑里一间昏暗的科幻卧室。混凝土墙壁，阴影中白色床单的低床，右侧墙壁上有大型落地玻璃窗，俯瞰夜晚黑暗的工业城市景观。窗边有一张桌子，摆着两台深色显示器与一盏小台灯，在桌面之上汇成冷白光束——这是房间中唯一的暖光源。冷调中性色调由深蓝灰阴影与深沉的压缩黑（crushed blacks）主导。桌上：一本摊开的笔记本，书页上有一封写到一半的信（文字对摄影机不可辨认），一支笔，那盏灯。

Shot 1（镜头 1，2.5s）——偏中隔离（OFF-CENTER ISOLATION）：
手持广中景，刻意失衡的构图。黑发的男性被推到画面极左边缘，坐在桌边弓身在笔记本上，只有他身体的一小部分可见。画面右侧三分之二被深沉的空旷黑暗空间主导——冰冷黑暗的房间，窗上映出窗外静默的工业城市，阴影中空荡的床。在自己的空间里他渺小而孤立。长焦镜头，浅景深，灯辉光与远处窗光上变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。摄影机保持略倾斜，以持机的姿态呼吸。

Shot 2（镜头 2，2s）——写字的宏观特写（MACRO ON HAND WRITING）：
硬切。桌上手持宏观极端特写，镜头距他的手仅数英寸。画面被握住笔的手填满，指背与指节处于锐利清晰的对焦中，下方纸页处于极端浅景深柔和——任何书写溶解成不可读的模糊，只是笔尖下苍白的一片。手做出书写的动作，笔划之间犹豫。台灯自上方在他的手背上汇成冷光。静态而亲密。

Shot 3（镜头 3，2s）——他脸上的荷兰角（DUTCH ANGLE ON HIS FACE）：
硬切。从低位的四分之三角度在他脸上手持特写，摄影机以硬挺的 25 度荷兰角（dutch tilt）倾斜。他的脸在偏轴状态下填满画面大部分，灯光自下方以明暗对照（chiaroscuro）照亮他的一半，另一半迷失在深影中。他的双目向下凝定，盯着画框之外的纸页，下颌紧绷，呼吸浅促。透过倾斜的取景，世界明显失序——他身后的窗户歪斜，房间的几何破碎。他未系泊。长焦镜头，极端浅景深，变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

Shot 4（镜头 4，2s）——透过前景的镜头，折叠信纸（THROUGH-FOREGROUND SHOT, FOLDING THE LETTER）：
硬切。手持从低角度拍摄的极端特写，透过灯本身以极端前景焦外光斑取景——灯金属的臂或灯罩化作柔和的失焦形状主导画面左侧，部分遮蔽视野。透过这被遮蔽的取景，我们看见他的双手在灯光池中的桌面上——他把纸页从笔记本上撕下，把信对折一次，再对折，把它放在桌面上。偷窥式、观察式，仿佛我们正从一个藏身处观看。长焦镜头，极端浅景深，前景灯的形状溶解进变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

Shot 5（镜头 5，3.5s）——被留下的信，破碎的构图（THE LETTER LEFT, BROKEN COMPOSITION）：
硬切。桌子的手持静态感镜头，刻意失衡的构图。折好的信位于画面左下角，小而局促。画面其余部分由负空间主导——上方黑暗的房间与右侧三分之二空无，灯的灯光池在画面底部边缘被部分裁掉。没有头顶留白规范，没有居中——这封信几乎要从画面底部滑落，脆弱且被弃置。凝定于这种构图。这封信是视线焦点，内容不可见，它在画面中的位置情感沉重。长焦镜头，极端浅景深，灯辉光与远处房间之上变形宽银幕椭圆光斑。

摄影机：全程手持，带刻意打破的构图——偏中取景、荷兰角、透过前景的遮挡、头顶留白侵犯、不对称的放置。长焦镜头与宏观摄影风格，光圈 f/1.4 极浅景深，变形宽银幕镜头特性在失焦高光中产生椭圆光斑形状。冷调中性色调带深沉压缩黑，台灯作为唯一的冷白光源，窗外来自城市微弱的辉光。

声音：室内全程沉重的寂静。镜头 1——安静的环境室内声，他那边笔在纸上隐约的沙沙声，显示器隐约的嗡鸣，远处的城市环境音。镜头 2——更响的笔沙沙声，纸的质感，犹豫的呼吸，笔尖停顿。镜头 3——他缓慢克制的呼吸，非常安静。镜头 4——纸从笔记本上被撕下的安静撕裂声，折叠的利落折痕，在桌面上轻柔的放下。镜头 5——寂静，显示器隐约的环境嗡鸣，远处的城市声，灯隐约的电嗡嗡声，他的缺席是一种可触之物。无音乐。无对白。

总计：12 秒 / 5 镜头 / 16:9
