## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, arrival sequence in the immediate aftermath of an explosion, 15 seconds.

SCENE CONTEXT: this scene begins seconds after a violent explosion collapsed the passage above, sending the three characters falling/sliding down through the rubble into this narrow corridor below. They have just arrived. Rain and Roco are riding hoverboards (compact tech they had with them); Jax is INJURED and SEMI-CONSCIOUS from the blast, unable to ride his own board, and is being CARRIED on Roco's hoverboard with him — Roco supports Jax's weight, one arm wrapped around him, keeping him upright as they glide forward. Dust is still raining down, debris is still settling, ears ringing from the blast.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide with low ceiling around two and a half meters high. BEHIND the characters — a massive fresh rubble collapse blocking retreat, the way they fell through, dust pouring down from the breach above. AHEAD of the characters — an open carved stone gateway at the far end, faint cold blue light spilling through it from beyond.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars pressed close along both side walls, candles flickering in alcoves (some knocked over by the blast vibration), thick volumetric dust haze. Anamorphic widescreen, teal-shadow color grade with warm candle accents, naturalistic low-key lighting.

Characters (just survived a blast):
— Rain (@image_2): young woman, silver-blonde shoulder-length hair, small scar by eye, fresh small cut on temple, grey hooded jacket over black top, dark cargo pants, heavy dust on clothes, hair disheveled. RIDING her own hoverboard with faint purple glow underneath. Lead, alert, recovering fast.
— Roco (@image_4): young man, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage across bridge of nose, dark jacket, dust-covered. RIDING his own hoverboard with faint red glow underneath, ONE ARM WRAPPED AROUND Jax supporting his weight on the same board. Strained, focused, protective.
— Jax (@image_3): young man, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked, head lolling slightly, eyes half-open, semi-conscious from the blast. Boots dragging, supported entirely by Roco. INJURED.

Shot 1 (0–6s): Camera starts low at floor level pointed UP at the ceiling breach above the rubble pile behind the characters — broken stone, dust pouring down in thick shafts, faint daylight from the surface filtering through the chaos. Hold for a beat on the destruction overhead. Then camera CRANES upward and rotates 180 degrees, descending in front of the characters as they emerge — Rain on her hoverboard leading, Roco just behind on his board with Jax leaning heavily against him, semi-conscious. The crane lands at chest height in front of them, now tracking backward as they advance. Rain looks ahead toward the distant gateway, calculates, then says clearly with quiet authority: "Straight through. Forty feet." Single continuous move.

Shot 2 (6–10s): Cut to a tight Dutch-angle medium two-shot of Roco and Jax sharing the hoverboard. Roco's jaw is tight, sweat and dust on his face, his arm visibly straining around Jax's shoulders. Jax's head rolls against Roco's shoulder, eyes flutter, glasses crooked. Roco mutters under his breath, low and steady: "Stay with me. Stay with me." Jax's eyes begin to focus weakly. Camera slowly straightens out. Strain visible in every muscle.

Shot 3 (10–12s): Profile tracking shot of Rain on her own hoverboard ahead, camera locked to her shoulder in a tight arc-track. Carved pillars sweep past in background like vertical bars, strobing parallax. Her face stays in profile, jaw set, the cut on her temple catching candlelight. She glances back briefly — checking on the others — then forward again.

Shot 4 (12–15s): Cut to a vertical TILT-DOWN from the low ceiling — camera starts on carved glyphs above, descends slowly past a hanging candle still swaying from blast vibration, finds the trio from a high angle as they glide directly under the lens. Rain passes through frame first. Then Roco with Jax follows — Jax's head finally lifts, eyes focusing. He is coming back. Cut.

no music. sfx only — settling debris and trickling stone dust from the breach behind, distant rumble of secondary collapse far above, faint ringing tinnitus from the blast aftermath, dual hoverboard hum (Rain's and Roco's), Jax's ragged breathing, Roco's strained breath, Rain's clear "Straight through. Forty feet." with stone reverb, soft "stay with me" murmur, candle flicker, distant drip from ahead.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的远古石走廊，爆炸刚过后的抵达段落，15 秒。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：本场景始于一次剧烈爆炸塌陷上方通道、使三名角色顺着瓦砾坠落/滑入这条狭窄下方走廊之后的数秒。他们刚抵达。Rain 与 Roco 踏着悬浮滑板（他们随身携带的紧凑科技）；Jax 在爆炸中受伤、半昏迷（SEMI-CONSCIOUS），无法驾驶自己的滑板，正被 Roco 的悬浮滑板一并搭载运送——Roco 支撑着 Jax 的重量，一条手臂环抱住他，滑行前进时让他保持直立。灰尘仍在纷纷洒落，碎屑仍在沉降，耳鸣尚未散去。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（场地几何）：一条约两米宽的狭窄石头走廊，低矮的天花板约两米半高。角色身后——一大片新鲜的瓦砾塌落挡住退路、正是他们坠落穿过的路，灰土从上方破口倾泻而下。角色前方——最远端的敞开雕刻石门道，透出微弱冷蓝光。

Setting（环境）：湿石地面、刻满符文的廊柱紧贴两侧墙、壁龛中烛火闪烁（有些被爆炸震动撞倒）、浓稠的体积尘雾。变形宽银幕，青影调色带暖烛点缀，自然主义低调布光。

Characters（角色）（刚在爆炸中生还）：
— Rain（@image_2）：年轻女子，银金色及肩发，眼旁小疤，太阳穴小新伤口，灰色连帽夹克罩在黑色上衣外，深色工装裤，衣上厚尘，头发凌乱。踏着自己的悬浮滑板，底面泛着微弱的紫色辉光。领队、警觉、恢复迅速。
— Roco（@image_4）：年轻人，深色凌乱的及肩长发，淡胡须，一条红色绷带横过鼻梁，深色夹克，覆尘。踏着自己的悬浮滑板，底面泛着微弱的红色辉光，一条手臂环抱住 Jax、在同板上支撑他的重量。吃力、专注、护着。
— Jax（@image_3）：年轻人，漂白黄寸头，深肤色，一副清晰护目镜歪斜，深色连帽夹克，结满尘土，头略垂耷，双眼半阖，因爆炸而半昏迷。靴子拖着地，完全由 Roco 支撑着。受伤。

Shot 1（第 0–6 秒）：摄影机从地面低位起拍，向上指向角色身后瓦砾堆上方的天棚破口——断石、浓稠光柱般倾泻的灰土、从地表滤下透过这混乱的微弱天光。在头顶的破坏物上停一拍。然后摄影机摇臂上移（CRANES upward）并旋转 180 度，在他们现身的三人面前下落——领先的是踏板的 Rain，Roco 在她身后一点、就着他的板、Jax 半昏迷地重重倚靠在他身上。摇臂落在他们面前的胸口高度，转而随着他们推进而向后跟拍。Rain 朝远处门道望去，计算了一下，然后以沉稳的命令口吻清晰地说：「Straight through. Forty feet.（径直穿过。四十英尺。）」单次连续运镜。

Shot 2（第 6–10 秒）：切到 Roco 与 Jax 共用悬浮滑板的紧密荷兰角中景双人镜头。Roco 下颌绷紧，脸上有汗有尘，手臂环在 Jax 肩上、明显吃力。Jax 的头靠着 Roco 的肩滚动，眼帘颤动，眼镜歪斜。Roco 低声自言自语，低沉而平稳：「Stay with me. Stay with me.（跟我一起撑住。跟我一起撑住。）」Jax 的双眼开始微弱地聚焦。摄影机缓缓摆正。每块肌肉都可见吃力。

Shot 3（第 10–12 秒）：侧影跟拍镜头，前方的 Rain 踏在自己的悬浮滑板上，摄影机以紧凑的弧形轨道跟着她的肩。雕凿廊柱如竖直栅条在背景中掠过，带频闪视差。她的脸保持侧影，下颌绷定，太阳穴上的伤口接住烛光。她短暂回瞥——照看同伴——然后再次前望。

Shot 4（第 12–15 秒）：切到一个自低矮天棚向下的竖直下摇（TILT-DOWN）——摄影机从上方雕凿字形起拍，缓缓下降、掠过一盏仍因爆炸震动而摇摆悬挂的烛台，以高角度找到径直从镜头正下方滑过的三人组。Rain 先穿出画幅。随后 Roco 载着 Jax 跟随——Jax 的头终于抬起，双眼聚焦。他正在苏醒。剪。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——身后破口处沉降的碎屑与筛落的石尘，上方远处传来二次塌陷的轰隆，爆炸后缀带出的微弱耳鸣，双块悬浮滑板的嗡鸣（Rain 的与 Roco 的），Jax 粗重的呼吸，Roco 吃力的呼吸，Rain 清晰的「Straight through. Forty feet.（径直穿过。四十英尺。）」带着石廊混响，轻柔的「stay with me（跟我一起撑住）」低语，烛火闪烁，前方远处滴水。
