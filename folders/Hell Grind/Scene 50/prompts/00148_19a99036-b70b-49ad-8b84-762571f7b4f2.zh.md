Hard cut. Handheld medium shot at the pit edge, three-quarter angle, framed so the warmly lit left wall is visible behind the characters and the right wall stays in dark shadow. Dust falls in steady streams from the rough stone ceiling above the characters, particles raining down through the torch light, the ceiling visibly stressed. In the deep background through the atmospheric haze, the distant gateway is visible — the slowly descending stone slab clearly lower than before, the cold blue gap below it visibly smaller and shrinking in real time during the shot.

The dark-haired man on his knee at the edge, his entire body STRAINING under the friend's weight, every muscle pushed to its limit. He is barely making it — face contorted in pure effort, teeth bared, eyes squeezed shut for a moment, jaw clenched so hard the muscles spasm in his neck, veins bulging on his forehead and temple, sweat pouring down his face streaking through the dust. He pulls upward in violent jerking heaves — not one smooth motion but multiple desperate yanks, his arm shaking from the strain, body almost giving out and recovering, almost slipping forward into the pit himself. He lets out a raw guttural roar through gritted teeth, animal-like, his throat tearing with the effort. Camera SHAKES violently with each heave, frame jolting and dropping with every yank, the operator unable to keep stable, focus drifting on the chaos.

Jax dazed, swaying. Shot 4（3 秒）——MEDIUM SHOT OF THE HEAVE AND RISE（奋力拉拽与起身的中景）、摄影机剧烈颤抖：

硬切。手持中景于坑边、四分之三角、构图使温暖照亮的左墙可见于角色之后、右墙保持黑暗阴影中。尘土从角色头顶粗糙石顶成股落下、颗粒穿过火把光而下倾泻、天花板明显受压着。透过大气雾霭的纵深背景、可见远处拱门——缓慢下降的石板明显比之前低、其下冷蓝缝隙可见地更小且在镜头中实时缩小。

膝跪在边缘的深发男子、他整个身体在朋友的重量下 STRAINING（苦撑）、每块肌肉被推到极限。他勉强撑住——面容因纯粹用力而扭曲、龇着牙、双眼紧闭片刻、下颌紧咬到颈部肌肉痉挛、额头太阳穴青筋暴鼓、汗穿过脸上的尘土直流。他以暴烈的抽搐奋拉向上拽——不是一次平滑动作、而是多次绝望的一扯、手臂因紧绷而颤抖、身体几乎垮掉又撑回来、自己都险些向前滑进坑里。他隔着咬紧的牙关迸出一声 raw 喉底咆哮、兽性般、喉咙因用力而要撕裂。摄影机随每次奋拉 violently 剧烈 SHAKES（摇晃）、每拉一下画格颠震下坠、操机员无法稳住、焦点在混乱中漂移。
