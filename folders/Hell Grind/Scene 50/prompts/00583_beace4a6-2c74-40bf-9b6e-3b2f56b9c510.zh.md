## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, advancement and trigger sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: shot in the visual language of Greg Fraser's cinematography on The Batman — captured on large-format anamorphic glass (ARRI Alexa 65 with Panavision Ultra Vista lenses), 2.39:1 anamorphic widescreen with subtle organic lens distortion at frame edges, oval bokeh shapes in defocused highlights. Apertures held at f/2.8 to f/4 — multiple focal planes hold detail simultaneously, deeper field than typical shallow-focus work, but with selective focus pulls when needed. Practical motivated lighting only — torch flames are the sole visible light source, casting hard directional shadows that crush half of every face into deep angular darkness. Black levels extremely deep but never clipped — coal-black shadows that retain texture, ARRI-style deep latitude. Color palette: muted dark teals, earthy browns, oxidized amber from torch fire, no saturated colors — the world feels dirty, weathered, oppressive. Atmospheric haze is a physical presence: thick particulate dust catches every shaft of torchlight as solid visible beams, light physically dying as it travels through the air. Subtle 35mm film grain texture overlay. Camera movement disciplined and slow — measured dolly, static holds, controlled pans. No handheld shake, no rapid moves. Compositions favor heavy negative space — faces and subjects often pushed to one third of the frame with vast areas of sculpted darkness around them. Pacing slow, dense, weighted.

SCENE CONTEXT: continuing immediately from Rain's "Stay close" warning. The team begins walking forward through the corridor in tense formation. Rain leads, holding her tablet. Jax and Roco follow. After a few steps, Roco's eye catches something on the floor ahead — he tries to process what it is. Before he can speak, Rain's boot presses on a worn stone tile. The tile depresses with a dull click. The trap activates.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide, ceiling two and a half meters. BEHIND — fresh rubble collapse from recent blast, retreat impossible. AHEAD — open carved stone gateway with cold blue light, the only way forward. Iron torches lit along both walls, smoke and dust thick in the air.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars pressed close on both walls, lit iron torches every few meters casting hard directional shadows, dense atmospheric haze.

Characters (recently survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, black top, dark cargo pants, dusty. Hoverboard tucked under one arm. Tablet glowing dim cold blue in her other hand. Walking forward in tense formation.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket, dusty. Walking close behind Rain after her warning, no longer distracted, but uneasy.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red nose bandage, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm. Walks at the rear, eyes constantly scanning.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Static long-lens medium shot from low angle, looking up the corridor at the trio walking forward toward camera in tight formation. Composition pushes them to the right third of frame; the left two-thirds is sculpted darkness, broken only by a single torch in deep background throwing a hard wedge of orange light across one wall. Rain leads, tablet glow underlit on her face from below as a faint cold blue, torch light from above as warm rim. The atmospheric haze between them and camera makes the air visibly thick — distinct beams of torchlight cut through it as solid shafts. They move slowly, deliberately, dust on their faces, breath visible. Camera does not move. They advance toward the lens, growing slightly larger in frame, but the pace is funereal. No dialogue. Only footsteps, breathing, distant drip.

Shot 2 (4–7s): Cut to a long-lens close-up of Roco's face from a three-quarter angle, walking, his eyes scanning the floor ahead. The right side of his face is lit by a single hard torch source — angular shadow cuts across the bridge of his nose, the bandage catching warm light, his left side completely lost in coal-black shadow. Atmospheric haze between camera and his face softens contrast slightly without losing the hardness. His eyes narrow. Something on the floor catches his attention — focus pulls subtly past his face to the corridor floor in deep background, where a single stone tile sits among others (visually almost identical, but a millimeter different — a slightly worn pattern, a subtle seam). Focus pulls back to his eye. His mouth begins to open, about to speak, brow furrowing — he doesn't have the word yet.

Shot 3 (7–11s): Hard cut to a static long-lens low-angle medium shot of Rain in profile, walking forward, ahead of the others. The torch on the wall behind her throws a hard backlight, rim-lighting her silver hair into a halated edge against the deep blackness behind. Her face is mostly in shadow, only one eye and the cut on her temple catching faint side fill from another distant torch. Tablet glowing dim cold blue in her hand at hip level, casting upward fill. She does not look back. The atmospheric haze around her is dense, light beams visibly truncated by the dust. Then — her boot enters frame at floor level in soft focus foreground. The boot lowers. The tile DEPRESSES one centimeter under her weight with a faint dull CLICK. Rain freezes mid-step. Her face does not turn — only her eye widens slightly. Three seconds of held tension. The torches flicker. Dust drifts. From within the walls, faintly, a deep mechanical groan begins.

Shot 4 (11–15s): Snap cut to a static long-lens medium shot of Roco from the front, the trio's formation now broken — Rain frozen ahead in soft focus background, Jax in between, Roco in sharp foreground focus. Roco's eyes locked on Rain's foot. He registers the click he half-anticipated. His face reads the chain of events instantly — what he was about to warn about, he was too late on. He shouts, voice raw and cracking with self-blame: "Wait!" The word hangs in the heavy air. The torches flicker harder, mechanical groan inside the walls intensifying. Hold on Roco's face — the silence after the shout is heavier than the shout itself.

no music. sfx only — slow deliberate footsteps on wet stone with deep stone reverb, faint electronic hum from Rain's tablet, torches crackling with low hiss, breathing controlled but heavy, distant drip from ahead, faint settle of debris from rubble behind, boot pressing on stone, faint dull CLICK of depressed tile, dust sifting from seam, deep mechanical groan beginning within walls (subtle, building), Roco's "Wait!" raw with self-blame and stone reverb, torches flickering harder as the mechanism wakes.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄远古石廊，前进与触发序列，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：以 Greg Fraser 在电影《The Batman》的电影摄影语言拍摄——以大幅面变形宽银幕玻璃拍摄（ARRI Alexa 65 配 Panavision Ultra Vista 镜头），2.39:1 变形宽银幕，画框边缘保留微妙的有机镜头畸变，失焦高光呈椭圆 bokeh 形状。光圈保持 f/2.8 至 f/4——多个焦平面同时保持细节，比典型浅焦作品更深的景深，但在需要时选择性拉焦。仅实用动机光——火把火焰是唯一可见光源，投下硬向阴影、把每张脸的一半压进深深的角形黑暗。黑电平极深但绝不裁切——保留纹理的煤黑阴影、ARRI 式深宽容度。色彩板：暗哑深青（teals）、大地棕、火把焰的氧化琥珀，无饱和色——世界显得脏污、风化、压迫。大气雾霭是实际的存在：浓密颗粒灰尘拦住每道光束成为坚实的可见光柱，光在穿过空气时物理地衰亡。微妙 35mm 胶片颗粒纹理叠加。运镜纪律而缓慢——衡定的摄影车（dolly）、静态停留、有控制的摇镜。无手持晃动、无快速运动。构图偏好浓重负空间——面孔与主体常被推到画面三分之一处，四周是大片雕琢出来的黑暗。节奏缓慢、厚重、压秤。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：紧接 Rain 的"Stay close（靠近点）"警告之后。队伍开始以紧张的编队穿过走廊前进。Rain 领队，手持她的平板。Jax 与 Roco 跟随后面。几步之后，Roco 的目光捕捉到前方地板上有什么——他试图弄清那是什么。他还没来得及开口，Rain 的靴子便踩上一块磨损的石板。石板带着沉闷的咔哒声下陷。陷阱启动。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（场地几何）：约两米宽的窄石廊，天花板两米半。BEHIND（后方）——最近一次爆炸留下的新鲜石碴崩落，退路不可能。AHEAD（前方）——带冷蓝光的敞开雕石拱门，唯一的前进方向。两侧墙点亮铁火把，空气中烟尘厚重。

场景：湿石地面、两侧墙紧贴的符文覆盖石柱、每隔几米点亮的铁火把投下硬向阴影、浓厚的大气雾霭。

角色（刚从爆炸中生还、覆满尘土）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银金色头发凌乱，眼旁一道疤，太阳穴一道新伤口，灰色连帽夹克，黑色上衣，深色工装裤，覆尘。一块悬浮滑板夹在一臂之下。另一只手拿的平板泛着暗淡冷蓝光。以紧张编队向前走。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄板寸，深肤色，透明护镜，深色连帽夹克，覆尘。在她的警告后紧跟在她身后走，不再分心，但心神不宁。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱短发，小胡子，红鼻绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。滑板夹在臂下。走在队尾，双眼不断扫视。

Shot 1（0–4 秒）：静态长焦中景、低角、从走廊抬头望三人以紧凑编队向机位走来。构图把他们推到画面右三分之一；左三分之二是雕琢出的黑暗，仅被纵深背景一两根火把打破、向一面墙投出硬楔形橙光。Rain 领队，平板光从下方在她脸上兜底成暗淡冷蓝、上方火把光成温暖轮廓光。他们与机位之间的薄雾让空气明显稠密——清晰的火把光束以坚实物柱切穿它。他们缓慢、审慎地移动，脸上有尘、呼气可见。摄影机不动。他们向镜头推进、在画面中略微变大，但行进是葬礼般的。无对话。只有脚步声、呼吸、远处滴水。

Shot 2（4–7 秒）：切至 Roco 面部的长焦特写、四分之三角、行走中，眼睛扫视前方地板。他脸的右侧被单一硬火把光源照亮——角形阴影切过他鼻梁、绷带捕捉到暖光，他左侧完全消失在煤黑阴影中。他与机位之间的大气雾霭微微降低对比、但不失硬度。他眯起眼。地板上有什么引起他的注意——焦点微妙地掠过他的脸、落到纵深背景的走廊地板上，那里有一块石板混在其他石板之间（视觉上几乎完全相同，但有毫米之差——一块略磨损的纹样、一条微妙的接缝）。焦点拉回他的眼睛。他的嘴开始张开、刚要开口，眉头皱起——他还没有那个词。

Shot 3（7–11 秒）：硬切至 Rain 侧影的静态长焦低角中景、走在前、走在其他人前面。她身后墙上的火把投下硬背光、把她银发边缘框成一道 ha 晕光边（halated edge）、衬托身后深黑。她的脸大半处于阴影中，只有一只眼与太阳穴上的伤口捕捉到远处另一根火把的微弱侧补光。平板在她髋部高度的手里泛着暗淡冷蓝光、向上投补光。她不回头。她周围的大气雾霭浓密，光束被灰尘可见地截断。然后——她的靴在柔和虚焦前景中、地面高度进入画面。靴子落下。石板在她重量下降了一厘米、伴随一声微弱的沉闷 CLICK（咔哒）。Rain 在一步中段僵住。她的脸不回望——只有她的眼略微睁大。三秒紧绷的停留。火把闪烁。尘土飘移。从墙体内部，隐隐地，开始一声深沉的机械呻吟。

Shot 4（11–15 秒）：猛切至 Roco 正面的静态长焦中景，三人编队现已破碎——Rain 僵在虚焦背景前方、Jax 居中、Roco 在锐利前景对焦中。Roco 的双眼锁定 Rain 的脚。他辨出那半是预料之中的咔哒。他的脸瞬时读懂事件链——他本想警告的事，他太晚了。他呼喊，声音带着自责而 raw 迸裂："Wait！（等等！）"那个字悬在沉重的空气里。火把闪烁得更厉害，墙壁内的机械呻吟加剧。停在 Roco 的脸上——呼喊之后的寂静比呼喊本身更沉重。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——湿石上缓慢审慎的脚步声带深深石回响、Rain 平板的微弱电子嗡鸣、火把低嘶噼啪、克制却沉重的呼吸、前方远处滴水、身后瓦砾碎料微弱的沉降、靴踩石板、被踩石板微弱的沉闷 CLICK、接缝扬尘、墙内开始的深沉机械呻吟（微妙、渐强）、Roco 带着自责与石回响的 raw"Wait！"、机制苏醒时火把闪烁得更厉害。
