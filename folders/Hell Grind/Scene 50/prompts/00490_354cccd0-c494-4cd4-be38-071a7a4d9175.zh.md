## 原文 (source)

Handheld long-lens medium shot of a young man running through an ancient corridor and suddenly hearing a scream from behind, 3 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — the camera alive in the operator's hands, tracking alongside the running character, reactive but not jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from torch flames forming bokeh orbs, slight chromatic aberration on edges, occasional focus drift, faint film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm orange torch accents and a small amount of cold blue light visible in deep background. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — deep crushed blacks, the character's face half-lit half-shadowed.

LOCATION: a tight ancient underground stone corridor, approximately two meters wide with low stone ceiling. The walls are weathered carved stone with glyphs and engravings, lit iron torches mounted on both side walls every few meters casting flickering warm orange light. Wet stone floor reflecting dim highlights. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust particles. In the deep background of the corridor, far ahead at its end, a distant carved stone gateway with cold blue light spilling through from beyond it — but a massive carved stone slab is visibly descending slowly from the ceiling above this gateway, having descended about halfway, the gap below the slab still tall but visibly shrinking. Arrows are embedded in both side walls of the corridor from a previous trap volley.

CHARACTER: a young man, mid-twenties, with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, a red bandage strip across the bridge of his nose, dark jacket, dust-covered face. He is in mid-run, body in full sprint motion, breath ragged, focus locked ahead toward the distant gateway. He is running away from camera direction (forward through the corridor toward the gateway).

ACTION: handheld camera tracks alongside the running man at his pace from a three-quarter angle on his face and upper body. His features are caught in hard angular torch light from one side, the other half of his face in deep shadow. The bandage on his nose catches warm light. Sweat and dust streak his skin. Camera trembles intensely with the run. Suddenly he HEARS something — a distant scream of his name from off-frame far behind him in the corridor (the sound is heard by the audience as well: a faint cracking "ROCO!!" with stone reverb echoing from the depth). His stride HESITATES sharply, his foot hitting wet stone slightly out of rhythm, body's forward momentum disrupted. His head WHIPS back sharply over his shoulder toward the source of the scream behind him. His face shows brutal conflict in a single second — eyes wide, jaw tight, the pull between forward duty and what's behind him visible across his expression. He keeps moving forward but slower now, his focus split between ahead and behind, body in the moment of decision.

no music. sfx only — running boots pounding wet stone with deep stone reverb, ragged sprint breathing, the off-frame distant "ROCO!!" scream echoing from far behind with deep stone reverb, the running stride hesitating with audible pause and disruption in the footstep rhythm, sharp inhale of internal conflict, deep slow grinding stone-on-stone rumble from the distant slab descending, torches crackling on the walls.

一名年轻男子奔跑穿过古廊、突然听到身后一声尖叫的手持长焦中景、3 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影、强烈受控镜头晃动——摄影机在操机员手中鲜活、贴着奔跑角色跟移、有反应但绝不抖动。长焦远摄摄影、浅景深、f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜上可见的尘、火炬火焰形成光斑球的剧烈光晕（halation）、边缘轻微色差、偶发漂焦、微淡胶片颗粒。电影感宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和调色板、孤立暖橘火炬点缀 + 深景中可见的一小点冷蓝光。戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）布光——深沉的压碎黑、角色的脸半亮半影。

地点（LOCATION）：一条狭促的古地下石廊、约两米宽、低矮石顶。墙面为风化雕花石带符文与刻印、两侧墙每隔几米装有点亮的铁制火把、投出闪烁暖橘光。湿滑石板倒映暗淡高光。大气雾霾浓、漂浮尘粒。走廊深景处、尽头远处、一座远处石雕入口、冷蓝光自其彼侧泄出——但一块巨大石雕门板正从该入口上方天花板可见地缓缓下落、已降约一半、门板下缝隙仍高却可见地收窄。先前陷阱齐射的箭嵌在走廊两侧墙。

角色（CHARACTER）：一名年轻男子、二十几岁、深色凌乱齐肩发、浅胡须、鼻梁横贴一条红布带、深色夹克、覆尘的脸。他正在奔跑中、身体处于全速冲刺运动、呼吸粗重、双眼锁定前方朝远处入口。他正自机位方向跑开（向前穿过走廊朝入口）。

动作（ACTION）：手持机位以他步速自三分之四角度贴着他脸与上身跟移。他的五官被一侧硬棱角火炬光捕捉、另半张脸在深影中。鼻上的绷带捕捉暖光。汗与尘划痕他的皮肤。机位随奔跑剧烈颤抖。突然他听到某物——身后腔中远远出画处传来一声唤他名的尖叫（观众也听到：一声带石混响的细微崩裂「ROCO!!」自纵深回荡而来）。他的步幅剧烈犹豫、脚略失拍地落上湿石、身体前冲动量被打断。他的头猛地回头转向后、朝向身后那尖叫的来处。他脸上一秒内显出残酷的冲突——双眼圆睁、下颌绷紧、前方之责与身后之事的拉扯在他表情上可见。他继续向前移动但更慢了、注意力在前后间分裂、身体处决断瞬间。

无音乐。仅音效（SFX only）——奔跑靴带深沉石混响砸湿石、粗糙冲刺呼吸、出画处远处「ROCO!!」尖叫自远后方带深沉石混响回荡、奔跑步幅犹豫带脚步声节奏中可闻的停顿与中断、内心冲突的尖锐吸气、远处慢落门板低沉缓慢石对石磨擦轰鸣、墙上火把噼啪。
