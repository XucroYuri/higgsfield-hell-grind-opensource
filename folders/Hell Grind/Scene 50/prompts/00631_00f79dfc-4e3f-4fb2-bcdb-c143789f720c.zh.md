— <<<image_3>>>: young man, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Tight low-angle on the stone floor ahead of Rain, camera at ground level. Her boot enters frame from above, foot lowering toward a worn stone tile. She doesn't see what's beneath it. The boot presses down. The tile DEPRESSES slightly under her weight with a faint dull click, sinking maybe a centimeter. Dust sifts from the seam. Hold on the sunken tile.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Medium close-up of <<<image_3>>>, head snapping toward the sound, eyes narrowing behind his glasses, scanning the floor. He spots the depressed tile. His face drops. He shouts, voice cracking: "Wait!" Hand reaching out toward Rain. Handheld micro-shake, sharp push-in on his face.

The three characters freeze mid-step, heads turning toward the walls.

Shot 4 (9–12s): Hard cut to close-up of <<<image_4>>>, eyes wide, mouth opening — he screams "DOWN!" with raw urgency, throwing his body forward and downward. Camera shakes hard, push-in on the final syllable.

Shot 5 (12–15s): Wide low-angle along the corridor floor, all three characters DROP to the floor near-unison — Rain slides forward controlled, hoverboard clattering aside; <<<image_3>>> flattens with arms over his head; <<<image_4>>> tackles flat onto wet stone. Dust kicks up from impact points. Bodies still, breath held. Camera settles low.
