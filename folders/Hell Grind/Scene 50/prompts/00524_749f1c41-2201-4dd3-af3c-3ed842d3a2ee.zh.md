## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, suspended Jax and team divergence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with subtle controlled camera shake. Long-lens telephoto at f/1.2 aperture. Lens imperfections — dust on lens, halation, chromatic aberration, focus drift, film grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. Heavily desaturated palette with warm torch accents and cold blue gateway light from the still-open gap. Dramatic chiaroscuro. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor. Behind — rubble collapse. Ahead — distant gateway with cold blue light. The massive stone slab continues its slow descent from the ceiling above the gateway — it has descended about halfway, the opening below now reduced to roughly the height of a person standing fully upright, but still wide and clearly passable. The slab's descent is steady, slow, inexorable but not panicked-fast. Lit torches. Arrows embedded in walls. Mid-corridor floor now has hole where tile collapsed — Jax hangs from edge by fingertips, dark pit with stone spikes below.

CHARACTERS:
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair, scar by eye, cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Mid-run between Roco and Jax.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Hanging from edge of pit by fingertips, body dangling into darkness.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Running toward gateway, halfway there when click happens.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Handheld long-lens close-up from below — looking up at Jax's terrified face hanging from the pit edge, dust streaming past camera, dark pit walls in foreground bokeh framing him. His arms shaking, fingers gripping stone above. He looks past camera (downward, visible as ledge above frame) toward the corridor where Roco runs. He screams, voice cracking: "ROCO!!"

Shot 2 (3–5.5s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens medium shot of Roco mid-run, three-quarter angle, sprinting toward the gateway. Camera tracks with his motion. He registers Jax's scream — his stride hesitates, head turning back over shoulder, but he keeps running, conflicted. The slowly descending slab visible in deep background, opening still tall enough to walk through but shrinking gradually.

Shot 3 (5.5–8s): Handheld long-lens close-up of Rain mid-run between them, three-quarter angle. Camera tracks with her. She glances back — sees Jax's hands at pit edge in soft bokeh — then forward to Roco running ahead, then to the slab descending over gateway. She shouts forward to Roco, voice urgent: "Roko, the door!" She points forward toward the closing gap, urging him to keep going.

Shot 4 (8–11s): Handheld long-lens medium close-up of Roco from three-quarter angle, mid-stride. He has stopped running, body skidding to a halt, turning back. His eyes lock onto Rain's, then past her to Jax hanging from the pit. He shakes his head once, sharply. He shouts back, voice raw: "GO!" His command leaves no room. He plants his foot and TURNS, running BACK toward Jax, away from the gateway.

Shot 5 (11–13.5s): Handheld long-lens medium shot from low angle — corridor split between them. Rain in foreground sharp focus continuing to sprint forward toward the slowly closing gateway, Roco in deep background soft focus running back away from camera toward the pit. They diverge in opposite directions. Camera holds with subtle handheld breathing.

Shot 6 (13.5–15s): Handheld long-lens close-up from below at the pit edge — Jax hanging, fingers slipping slightly, looking up. From his angle, Roco's silhouette in the deep corridor running TOWARD camera position, growing larger fast, dust kicking up behind him.

no music. sfx only — slow deep grinding rumble of slab continuing (steady pace, not accelerating), torches crackling, Jax's panicked breathing and shaking arms, "ROCO!!" raw scream cracking with terror, Roco's running boots hesitating, three sets of urgency-driven breathing, Rain's "Roko, the door!" urgent command, Roco's boots skidding to halt, "GO!" raw command, Roco's boots turning and accelerating back, Rain's continued running boots fading toward gateway, Jax's fingers grinding on stone, dust falling into pit echoing distantly off stone spikes below.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄远古石廊，悬空的 Jax 与队伍分歧，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持电影摄影带微妙受控的摄影机晃动。长焦远摄 f/1.2 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜头上尘、辉散（halation）、色差、焦点漂移、胶片颗粒。变形宽银幕 2.39:1。重度降饱和调色板带暖火把点缀与仍开缝隙透来的冷蓝拱门光。戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）。灵感源自 Snyder 的《僵尸军团（Army of the Dead）》。

LOCATION（场地）：窄远古石廊。身后——碎石崩落。前方——带冷蓝光的远处拱门。巨大石板继续自拱门上方的天花板缓慢下降——它已下降约一半，下方开口现缩小到约一个人直立挺立的高度、但仍宽阔明确可通过。石板下降稳定、缓慢、不可阻挡但不慌不急。点亮火把。嵌入墙的箭。走廊中段地板上现有一处石板塌陷的洞——Jax 以指尖悬在边缘，下方是带石尖的黑暗深坑。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银金色头发，眼旁一道疤，太阳穴一道伤口，灰色连帽夹克，覆尘。奔跑中、处于 Roco 与 Jax 之间。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄板寸，深肤色，透明眼镜，深色夹克，覆尘。以指尖悬在坑沿、身体悬垂进黑暗。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱短发，小胡子，鼻上红绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。朝拱门奔跑、咔哒发生时已跑了一半。

Shot 1（0–3 秒）：自下方的手持长焦特写——仰望悬在坑沿的 Jax 惊恐的脸，尘在机位前飘流而过，前景 bokeh 里深坑壁框住他。他双臂颤抖、手指紧抓上方的石头。他越过机位望（向下、见为画框上缘的 ledge 石檐）向 Roco 奔跑的走廊。他尖叫、声音迸裂："ROCO！！"

Shot 2（3–5.5 秒）：硬切至奔跑中的 Roco 的手持长焦中景、四分之三角、朝拱门冲刺。摄影机随他运动跟踪。他辨出 Jax 的尖叫——他步幅迟疑、头回头过肩、但他继续跑、内心矛盾。缓慢下降的石板在纵深背景可见，开口仍高到可穿过、但逐渐缩小。

Shot 3（5.5–8 秒）：两人之间的 Rain 奔跑中的手持长焦特写、四分之三角。摄影机随她跟踪。她回头瞥——在柔焦 bokeh 里看见坑沿上 Jax 的双手——随即向前看见跑在前面的 Roco、再看见降在拱门上的石板。她朝 Roco 向前呼喊、声音急迫："Roko, the door！（Roko，那扇门！）"她向前指向正在关闭的缝隙、催他继续走。

Shot 4（8–11 秒）：四分之三角中 Roco 的手持长焦近特写、一步中段。他已停跑、身体滑止、转回身。他的双眼锁住 Rain 的，随后越过她锁住悬在坑上的 Jax。他用力猛摇头一次。他回喊、声音 raw："GO！（去！）"他的命令不留余地。他稳住脚、TURN（转身）、背弃拱门跑 BACK（回）向 Jax。

Shot 5（11–13.5 秒）：来自低角的手持长焦中景——走廊在他俩之间一分为二。前景锐利对焦的 Rain 继续朝正在缓慢关闭的拱门冲刺、纵深背景虚焦的 Roco 背弃机位跑回向坑。他们朝相反方向分歧。摄影机以微妙的手持呼吸稳住。

Shot 6（13.5–15 秒）：自坑沿下方的手持长焦特写——Jax 悬挂、手指微微滑移、向上望。从他的角度，深走廊里跑 TOWARD 机位方向的 Roco 的剪影、急速变大、身后扬起尘。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——石板深沉的缓慢研磨轰隆持续（恒定节奏、不加速）、火把噼啪、Jax 恐慌的呼吸与颤抖的臂、"ROCO！！"raw 的尖叫迸出惊惶、Roco 迟疑的跑靴、三组急迫驱动的呼吸、Rain 急迫命令的"Roko, the door！"、Roco 的靴子滑止、"GO！"raw 的命令、Roco 的靴子转身加速回奔、Rain 持续奔跑的靴声向拱门淡去、Jax 的手指在石上磨刮、尘坠入坑内、在下方石尖上遥遥回响。
