## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, advancement and trap activation sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: shot in the visual language of Greg Fraser's cinematography on The Batman — captured on large-format anamorphic glass (ARRI Alexa 65 with Panavision Ultra Vista lenses), 2.39:1 anamorphic widescreen with subtle organic lens distortion at frame edges, oval bokeh shapes in defocused highlights. Apertures held at f/2.8 to f/4 — multiple focal planes hold detail simultaneously, deeper field than typical shallow-focus work, but with selective focus pulls when needed. Practical motivated lighting only — torch flames are the sole visible light source, casting hard directional shadows that crush half of every face into deep angular darkness. Black levels extremely deep but never clipped — coal-black shadows that retain texture, ARRI-style deep latitude. Color palette: muted dark teals, earthy browns, oxidized amber from torch fire, no saturated colors. Atmospheric haze is a physical presence: thick particulate dust catches every shaft of torchlight as solid visible beams. Subtle 35mm film grain texture overlay. Camera movement disciplined — measured dolly, static holds, controlled pans. No handheld shake. Compositions favor heavy negative space — subjects pushed to one third of the frame with vast areas of sculpted darkness. Pacing dense and weighted.

SCENE CONTEXT: continuing immediately from Rain's "Stay close" warning. The team begins walking forward in tense formation. After several steps, Roco's eye catches a worn stone tile on the floor ahead of Rain — he recognizes the danger and shouts "Rein!" But Rain's foot is already mid-step. Her boot lands on the tile. The tile depresses with a dull click. In response, ten ancient stone ports embedded in BOTH SIDE WALLS GRIND open simultaneously along the corridor — five along the left wall, five along the right wall — revealing dark hollow apertures. Roco shouts "DOWN!" The three drop to the floor. The first arrows begin to fire from the ports.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide, ceiling two and a half meters. BEHIND — fresh rubble collapse from blast. AHEAD — open carved stone gateway with cold blue light. Iron torches along both walls. Ten ancient stone ports embedded in both side walls (five left, five right), currently sealed with weathered carved covers.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars on both walls, lit iron torches every few meters casting hard directional shadows, dense atmospheric haze.

Characters (recently survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, black top, dark cargo pants, dusty. Hoverboard tucked under one arm. Tablet glowing dim cold blue in her other hand.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark hooded jacket, dusty.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red nose bandage, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm. Eyes constantly scanning.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Static long-lens medium shot from low angle, looking up the corridor at the trio walking forward toward camera in tight formation. Composition pushes them to the right third of frame; the left two-thirds is sculpted darkness, broken by a single torch in deep background throwing a hard wedge of orange light. Rain leads, tablet glow underlit on her face, torch rim warm from above. Atmospheric haze makes air visibly thick — distinct beams of torchlight cut through as solid shafts. They move slowly. Footsteps and breathing only.

Shot 2 (3–5s): Cut to a long-lens close-up of Roco's face from a three-quarter angle, walking. Hard torch source on right side cuts angular shadow across his nose, the bandage catching warm light, his left side completely lost in coal-black shadow. His eyes narrow. Focus pulls subtly past his face to the corridor floor in deep background — a worn stone tile, slightly off pattern. Recognition crosses his face. Jaw clenches. He shouts, voice sharp and warning: "Rein!"

Shot 3 (5–8s): EXTREME CLOSE-UP — long-lens macro shot at floor level, the camera mere inches from a single worn stone tile, framed full-bleed in 2.39:1. Wet stone glistening, every crack and chip in sharp focus, surrounding tiles falling off into soft bokeh. A torch on the wall throws hard angular orange light across the surface, dust motes visible in the beam as solid particles. Rain's boot enters frame from above in soft focus, descending — the warning has come a half-second too late. The boot fills the upper half of the frame. The boot lands on the tile. The tile DEPRESSES one centimeter under her weight with a faint dull CLICK. Dust sifts from the seam between stones, individual particles glowing in the torch beam. Camera locks dead still. Hold on the sunken tile. From within the walls, faintly, a deep mechanical groan begins.

Shot 4 (8–11s): Hard cut to a long-lens low-angle shot looking down the length of the corridor at f/4 — both side walls in focus, depth stretching toward the distant gateway. Atmospheric haze thick, torch beams cutting through as solid shafts. Then — ancient stone ports embedded along BOTH SIDE WALLS GRIND open simultaneously, five along the left wall, five along the right wall. Weathered carved covers slide aside in unison with a heavy mechanical clack, dust pouring from the seams in thick streams catching torch light, dark hollow apertures revealed inside the stone. The trio frozen in the middle distance, heads beginning to turn toward the walls. Camera holds static — the corridor reveals itself as a weapon.

Shot 5 (11–13s): Snap cut to a static long-lens medium shot of Roco, half his face slashed by hard torch light, the other half coal-black. Eyes wide. Jaw tight. He shouts, voice raw and cracking: "DOWN!" He launches forward into frame, body lunging toward Jax to take him down.

Shot 6 (13–15s): One continuous long-lens shot, low-angle, camera at floor level looking down the corridor. Composition heavy with negative space — floor and lower walls in frame, ceiling cropped, depth stretching toward the gateway. The three DROP into frame — Rain dives forward and slides controlled, her hoverboard clattering aside, tablet skidding across stone with blue glow flickering; Roco's tackle drives Jax down with him, both hitting wet stone hard, Roco's hoverboard skittering away; all three flatten. Bodies in mid-foreground sharp focus, gateway in deep background also in focus thanks to f/4 depth. Above the prone bodies, the FIRST arrow CRACKS through frame in real-time speed — sharp wooden whoosh — passing horizontally across the corridor. The volley is beginning. Hold.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的古老石廊、推进与陷阱激活段落，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：以 Greg Fraser 在《新蝙蝠侠》（The Batman）中的电影摄影视觉语言拍摄——用大幅面变形宽银幕玻璃（ARRI Alexa 65 + Panavision Ultra Vista 镜头）捕捉，2.39:1 变形宽银幕，画面边缘带细微有机镜头畸变、失焦高光处呈卵形散景。光圈保持在 f/2.8 到 f/4——多个焦平面同时保持细节、景深比典型浅焦作品更深，但需要时可选择性拉焦。仅实用光源动因（practical motivated lighting）——火炬火焰是唯一可见光源、投下把每张脸的一半碾进深棱角黑暗的硬朗定向阴影。黑电平极深但从不裁切——保留质感的煤黑阴影、ARRI 式深宽容度。调色板：含蓄的暗青色、泥土棕、火炬火点锻出的氧化琥珀色，无饱和色。大气雾霾是一种物理在场：浓密微粒灰尘把每一根火炬光束都捕捉成清晰可见的实体光柱。覆盖一层淡淡的 35mm 胶片颗粒纹理。摄影机运动自律——克制的轨道（dolly）、静态保持、受控摇镜。无手持颤抖。构图偏好厚重负空间——主体被推到画面三分之一处、大片凿刻出的黑暗区域。节奏稠密而有分量。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：紧接着 Rain 的「Stay close」警告继续。小队以紧绷阵形开始前行。走出几步后，Roco 的目光扫到 Rain 前方地板上的一块磨损石砖——他认出危险、大喊「Rein！」但 Rain 的脚已经迈在半空。她的靴子落到砖上。石砖以一声沉闷咔嗒下陷。作为回应，嵌入两侧壁中的十个古老石口沿走廊同时碾开——左壁五个、右壁五个——露出黑暗空洞的孔口。Roco 大喊「DOWN！」三人扑倒在地。第一批箭开始从孔口射出。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（场地几何）：狭窄石廊，约两米宽、天花高两米半。身后——一次爆破遗留的新鲜废墟塌落。前方——敞开的凿花石门带冷蓝光。两壁沿线铁制火炬。两侧壁中嵌入十个古老石口（左五右五），当前以饱经风化的刻花盖板封住。

布景：湿滑石地板、两壁符文柱、每几米点亮的铁制火炬投下硬朗定向阴影、浓密大气雾霾。

角色（近期刚熬过一次爆破、蒙尘）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：凌乱的银金长发、眼侧一道疤、太阳穴新伤口、灰连帽夹克、黑色上衣、深色工装裤、带尘。滑板夹在一条臂下。另一只手里平板的暗淡冷蓝光发着光。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄板寸头、深肤色、清晰眼镜、深色连帽夹克、带尘。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱发、胡子、红色鼻绷带、深色夹克、带尘。滑板夹在腋下。眼睛不停扫视。

Shot 1（0–3s）：静态长焦中景、低角度、沿走廊上方望向三人以紧凑阵形朝镜头前行。构图把他们推到画面右三分之一；左边三分之二是被凿刻的黑暗、被背景深处一支独自的火炬打破、投下一支硬朗的橙色光楔。Rain 领队、平板光从下方衬托她的脸、火炬边缘暖光从上方来。大气雾霾让空气肉眼可见地浓稠——清晰的火炬光束如实体柱状切过。他们缓慢移动。只有脚步声与呼吸声。

Shot 2（3–5s）：切到 Roco 脸部的长焦特写、四分之三角度、行进中。右侧硬朗火炬光源在他鼻上切出棱角阴影、绷带捕捉到暖光、他左侧完全陷入煤黑阴影。他眯起眼。对焦从他脸上微妙拉走、落到背景深处的地板——一块磨损石砖、稍稍偏出纹样。领悟掠过他的脸。下颌咬紧。他大喊、声音尖锐而带警示：「Rein！」

Shot 3（5–8s）：极特写——长焦微距镜头、位于地板高度、摄影机离一块磨损石砖仅数英寸、以 2.39:1 满幅取景。湿滑石头泛着光泽、每一道裂纹与缺口都处于锐利对焦、周围石砖落进柔软散景。墙上一支火炬在表面投下硬朗棱角橙色光、光束中的尘埃微粒作为实体颗粒可见。Rain 的靴子自上方以柔焦进入画面、下降——警告迟到半秒。靴子填满画面上半部。靴子踩上石砖。石砖在她体重下内陷一厘米、带一声隐约的沉闷「咔嗒」。灰尘自石缝间筛落、个别微粒在光束中发光。摄影机死死锁住。定格在下陷的石砖上。墙体内部、隐约地、一声深沉的机械呻吟开始。

Shot 4（8–11s）：硬切到长焦低角度、以 f/4 沿走廊长度望去——两侧壁都在对焦、纵深伸向远处的石门。大气雾霾浓重、火炬光束作为实体柱状切过。随即——嵌入两侧壁的古老石口同时碾开、左壁五个、右壁五个。饱经风霜的刻花盖板以一声沉重的机械卡嗒齐齐滑开、灰尘以浓流从接缝倾泻、捕捉火炬光、石内露出黑暗空洞的孔口。三人僵在中间距离、头开始转向墙壁。摄影机保持静态——走廊显形为一件武器。

Shot 5（11–13s）：骤切到 Roco 的静态长焦中景、他半张脸被硬朗火炬光斜切、另一半煤黑。眼珠圆睁。下颌绷紧。他大喊、声音粗粝而碎裂：「DOWN！」他纵身扑进画面、身体朝 Jax 猛冲、要把他按下。

Shot 6（13–15s）：一个连续长焦镜头、低角度、摄影机在地板高度沿走廊望去。构图厚重负空间——地板与下部墙壁在画面中、天花板被裁切、纵深伸向石门。三人「扑」进画面——Rain 向前俯冲、控制地滑扑、她的滑板咔嗒滑开、平板带闪烁蓝光在石头上滑出、Roco 的扑倒把 Jax 带着一起按倒、两人重重砸上湿滑石头、Roco 的滑板飞滑开去；三人全部趴平。中前景的身体锐利对焦、背景深处石门也凭 f/4 景深处于对焦。俯卧身体上方、第一支箭以实时速度「嗖」地破空穿过画面——尖锐木制「嗖」——横穿走廊掠过。齐射开始了。定格。

无配乐。仅环境音效（SFX only）——湿滑石头上缓慢刻意的脚步声带深沉石壁混响、平板的隐约电子嗡响、火炬噼啪、克制克制的沉重呼吸、前方远处的水滴、身后废墟的隐约沉降、Roco 尖锐带石壁混响的「Rein！」警告、靴子压石、凹陷石砖隐约沉闷「咔嗒」、石缝筛落灰尘、墙内开始响起的深沉机械呻吟（微妙、不断建立）、十块古老石盖同时碾开、沉重的机械咔嗒、接缝倾泻灰尘、Roco 粗粝碎裂的「DOWN！」、身体扑撞的撞击声、三具身体重重砸上湿滑石头、两块滑板咔嗒响、平板带着电子故障音在石头上滑出、粗重呼吸、第一支箭以满实时速度「嗖」地破空穿过。
