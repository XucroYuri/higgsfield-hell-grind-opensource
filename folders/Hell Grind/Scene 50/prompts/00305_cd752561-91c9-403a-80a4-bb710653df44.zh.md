## 原文 (source)

Five-shot sequence in an ancient stone corridor — two young men sprinting toward a closing gateway exit while a young woman waits anxiously beyond it, the men reaching the gateway and one being thrown through, the other diving through at the last possible moment before the massive stone slab seals the passage. 14 seconds total, single continuous shot with five distinct framings, in the visual language of Emmanuel Lubezki and Greig Fraser.

SCENE CONTEXT: Roco and Jax have just escaped a deadly trap pit and are now sprinting together down a long ancient stone corridor toward a closing gateway exit. The exit is being slowly sealed from above by a massive descending stone slab — the gap below the slab has narrowed dramatically and is now only knee-to-waist height. Their teammate Rain is already on the safe side beyond the gateway, crouched at the threshold, watching her friends through the narrow gap with anxious terror. As the men reach the gateway, Roco grabs Jax from behind and THROWS him forward through the narrow gap — Jax flies through and is caught by Rain. The gap continues closing rapidly. Roco then DIVES himself in a horizontal slide through the now-very-narrow gap, sliding on his stomach across the wet stone in the final possible moment before the slab THUNDERS down to seal the corridor completely.

CINEMATOGRAPHY in the language of Emmanuel Lubezki and Greig Fraser: handheld cinematography with reactive camera shake, alive in operator's hands. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from torch flames and from the cold blue gateway light, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Practical motivated lighting only — iron torches on corridor walls as warm key sources, cold blue light from beyond the gateway threshold. Deep crushed blacks with detail in shadows. Heavily desaturated palette.

LOCATION: a long ancient underground stone corridor approximately two meters wide. Walls weathered carved stone with deep glyph carvings, lit iron torches mounted on both side walls casting warm orange light, halated bokeh orbs of flame on both sides receding into distance. Wet stone floor reflecting torch highlights. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust. At the end of the corridor: the closing stone gateway exit — a tall carved stone doorway frame, with a massive carved stone slab descending slowly from above, the remaining gap below the slab narrowing rapidly throughout the sequence (waist-height at start, knee-height by middle, ankle-height at end). Cold blue light streams from beyond the gateway through the narrow gap.

CHARACTERS:
— Roco: young man, mid-twenties, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, fresh bleeding wound on the bridge of his nose, dust-covered face, lighter olive/tan skin tone. Wearing a dark hooded jacket with grey panel inserts, dark t-shirt underneath, dark cargo pants, fingerless glove on his left hand with red bandana at the wrist. Both jacket sleeves intact.
— Jax: young man, mid-twenties, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin (visibly darker than Roco's), clear protective glasses askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked face, exhausted from his fall into the pit, stumbling in his run.
— Rain: young woman, mid-twenties, silver-blonde shoulder-length disheveled hair, fresh cut on her temple, grey hooded jacket, dark cargo pants, dust-covered. On the safe side beyond the gateway, crouched low at the threshold to see through the narrow gap, hands extended back through.

SHOT 1 — LOW ANGLE ON ROCO (0–2s):

Handheld extreme low angle close-up shot from floor level looking UP at Roco's face and torso as he runs toward camera direction (down the corridor toward the gateway). Camera position is on the floor between his feet, tilted up steeply. His face fills the upper portion of frame, dust-streaked, sweat-streaked, blood from his nose, jaw locked with determination, eyes locked forward. The dark corridor ceiling visible above him, torches flickering on the walls passing on both sides as bokeh orbs streaking by. His body looms massive over the camera at this angle — a heroic, dominant framing that emphasizes his commitment to the run. Camera moves backward at his pace, keeping him framed while he sprints over the camera position. His running boots impact wet stone immediately above the lens. Camera trembles intensely with his motion.

SHOT 2 — MEDIUM SHOT FROM BEHIND (2–4.5s):

HARD CUT to handheld medium shot from BEHIND the two running men, framing both their backs as they run together down the long corridor away from camera. Roco on the left, Jax on the right, slightly behind, Roco's right hand gripping Jax's jacket back/shoulder pulling him forward. Their hair flies, jackets whipping with motion. The corridor stretches before them — torches passing on both sides as bokeh streaks of warm orange. In deep background ahead: the closing gateway visible, the cold blue gap below the descending slab now low to the ground (knee-to-waist height), the dark slab pressing down from above. The viewer sees what the men see — the narrowing exit getting smaller as they sprint toward it. Camera tracks behind them at a slight handheld jog pace, the gap visibly shrinking during the shot. Both men's running boots pound wet stone. Audio: dual ragged sprint breathing, fabric whipping, distant slab grinding rumble ahead.

SHOT 3 — CLOSE-UP ON RAIN UNDER THE SLAB (4.5–6.5s):

HARD CUT to handheld extreme close-up on Rain's face from low angle on the safe side of the gateway. Rain is crouched LOW to the floor (because the gap is now low) on her side of the threshold, her face framed THROUGH the narrowing gap below the descending slab — the bottom edge of the descending stone slab visible as a horizontal line above her head crossing the upper portion of frame, the cold blue light from her side spilling around her face. Behind her, the cold blue ancient hall is visible in soft bokeh. Through the gap, in the deep distance behind/past the camera position, faint silhouettes of the running men can be seen but are far away. Rain's face shows pure anxious terror — eyes wide and locked on the men in the distance, brows furrowed, breath catching. The slab visibly continues its slow descent during the shot, the horizontal stone line at the top of frame slowly lowering, compressing her viewing frame. She SHOUTS through the gap with desperate urgency, voice straining: "COME ON!" Her voice carries through the corridor with deep stone reverb. Camera trembles with her anxious tension.

SHOT 4 — ROCO THROWS JAX (6.5–10s):

HARD CUT to handheld medium shot at the gateway threshold, ground-level angle. The two men have arrived at the gateway. The slab is now very low (knee-height or less), the gap dangerously narrow. Roco from BEHIND grabs Jax — both his hands gripping Jax's jacket at the shoulders/back FROM BEHIND. With a massive grunt of effort, Roco LIFTS and THROWS Jax forward through the narrow gap with violent force. Jax's body becomes airborne briefly, body horizontal, arms outstretched forward, flying through the gap below the descending slab. Camera tracks Jax's flying body as he passes through the narrow opening, his body sliding on wet stone after impact, sliding through the threshold to the safe side. On the other side, Rain immediately catches Jax's body, dragging him the last distance, both of them sprawling on the floor of the safe side. Camera holds at the threshold position briefly. Audio: Roco's massive grunt of throw effort, Jax's surprised gasp as he flies, body impacting and sliding on wet stone, Rain's grunt as she catches him, both bodies impacting the safe-side floor.

SHOT 5 — ROCO DIVES THROUGH AT LAST MOMENT (10–14s):

HARD CUT to handheld low-angle medium shot looking sideways at the gateway from the corridor side (the dangerous side). The gap is now ANKLE-HEIGHT — the slab almost touching the floor, the cold blue light reduced to a thin horizontal slit. Roco is alone on the dangerous side. He has only seconds. He BACKS UP a step, then LAUNCHES himself forward into a horizontal sliding dive — body parallel to the ground, arms outstretched forward, hitting the wet stone and SLIDING fast on his stomach toward the closing gap. Camera tracks his diving body. His body slides under the descending slab, his back grazing the bottom edge of the stone with friction sparks visible, jacket fabric scraping audibly on stone. His head, shoulders, torso pass through, then his legs whipping behind him as the slab continues its final descent. The instant his feet clear the threshold, the slab THUNDERS down the final inches and IMPACTS the floor with a massive deep stone-on-stone CRASH, sealing the corridor completely behind him. Dust explodes from the impact. The audio of the crash dominates, then immediate silence broken only by ragged exhausted breathing. Camera settles low on the safe side — Roco sprawled on the floor where he slid to a stop, gasping, alive. Rain and Jax visible beside him, also exhausted on the floor. The sealed gateway behind them, the cold blue light of this side now their only illumination. All three lying there breathing hard. Hold on the trio for the final beat. Cut to black on their continued breathing.

no music. sfx only — entire shot has dynamic audio building to slab impact then silence.

Specific beats:
— Roco's running boots impacting wet stone immediately above lens, his ragged sprint breathing, fabric whipping with run motion (Shot 1, 0–2s)

— DIVE: Roco's running boots accelerating in the final sprint, his grunt of effort as he launches into the dive, body becoming airborne briefly, then slamming onto wet stone with hard impact, continuous wet sliding sound of body on slick floor sliding rapidly toward the gap, jacket fabric scraping against the descending slab edge with high friction sound (Shot 5, 10–12s)
— SEAL: the slab THUNDERING down the final inches with grinding acceleration, then a massive deep stone-on-stone CRASH as it impacts the floor with full force, deep reverberating echo carrying through both spaces, dust impact rumble (Shot 5, 12–13s)

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：Roco 与 Jax 刚刚逃脱一处致命陷阱坑，现正并肩沿一条古老长石质走廊朝一处正在关闭的门状出口冲刺。出口正被一块巨大的下落石板自上缓缓封合——石板下方的缝隙已急剧收窄，现仅剩膝至腰高度。他们的队友 Rain 已处于门后的安全侧，蹲伏在门槛处，透过窄缝以焦急的恐惧望着她的朋友们。当两人抵达门洞，Roco 自后方抓住 Jax，将他向前抛穿过狭窄缝隙——Jax 飞穿过去，被 Rain 接住。缝隙继续快速闭合。Roco 随即自己以一个水平的滑跃俯身扑穿现已极窄的缝隙，在石板轰然降下彻底封死走廊前最后可能的一刻，以腹部滑过湿滑地面。

CINEMATOGRAPHY（摄影）采用 Emmanuel Lubezki 与 Greig Fraser 的语言：手持摄影带反应性摇晃，在操机员手中鲜活。长焦（long-lens telephoto）浅景深摄影，f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜片上可见的灰尘、火把火焰与冷蓝门光的强烈光晕（halation）、高对比边缘上轻微色差、偶尔温和的焦点漂移、隐约的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影感宽屏 16:9。仅实用动机照明（practical motivated lighting）——走廊墙壁上的铁火把作温暖主光源，门槛之外的冷蓝光。压碎浓黑且阴影内含细节。重度去饱和调色。

LOCATION（地点）：约两米的狭长古代地下石质走廊。墙壁是经风化凿刻的石墙，带深层符文（glyph）凿刻，两侧墙壁上点亮的铁火把投出摇曳的暖橙光，两边都带火焰辉散散景球（bokeh orbs），推向远方。湿滑石地反射火把高光。大气薄雾厚积漂浮尘。走廊尽端：正在关闭的石质门口出口——一座高大的凿刻石门框，一块巨大的雕刻石板自上方缓缓下降，石板下方的剩余缝隙在序列中快速收窄（开场齐腰高度、中途齐膝高度、结尾齐踝高度）。冷蓝光自门后透过窄缝倾泻而出。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
— Roco：年轻男子，二十多岁，深色凌乱齐肩长发，浅色小胡子，鼻梁上有道新鲜流血的伤口，蒙尘的脸，较浅的橄榄/棕褐色肤色。穿着深色连帽夹克带灰色插片，内搭深色 T 恤，深色工装裤，左手戴无指手套、腕部系着红色头巾。两条夹克袖都完好。

— Rain：年轻女子，二十多岁，银色金发齐肩凌乱长发，太阳穴有道新割口，灰色连帽夹克，深色工装裤，蒙尘。位于门后的安全侧，低蹲在门槛处以透过窄缝张望，双手反向探出伸回。

SHOT 1（镜头 1）——ROCO 上的低角度（0–2s）：

手持极端低角度特写，自地面高度仰视 Roco 的脸与躯干，他正朝摄影机方向奔跑（沿走廊朝门口而去）。摄影机位于他两脚之间的地面，陡峭上仰。他的脸填满画面上部，蒙尘、汗渍、鼻血，下颌咬定决意，目光向前锁定。他头上是深色走廊顶棚，两侧墙壁上的火把以散景球流光的形态掠过。在此角度下他的身体在摄影机上方显得庞大雄伟——一种英雄式、主导性的取景，强调他对奔跑的投入。摄影机以他的节奏后移，在他冲过摄影机位置时保持框住他。他奔跑的靴子即刻踏在镜头上方的湿滑石地上。摄影机随他的动作剧烈颤抖。

SHOT 2（镜头 2）——从身后的中景（2–4.5s）：
