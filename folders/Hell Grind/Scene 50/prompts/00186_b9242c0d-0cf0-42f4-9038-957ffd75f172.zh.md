## 原文 (source)

Two-character close up shot in an ancient stone corridor lit by torches, 7 seconds, single continuous shot.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, fresh cut on her temple, grey hooded jacket from Image 2.

Setting: tight stone corridor with carved walls and torches receding into warm bokeh on both sides, wet stone floor, atmospheric dust haze, slab grinding rumble in the distance.

Action: The man stands in the corridor facing camera, breathing hard, sweat and dust on his face. The woman runs into frame from behind him and slams to a halt beside him, breath ragged. She shouts at him, urgent and desperate: "Jax!" He grabs her by the arm, locks eyes with her, and shouts back, raw and final: "Run!" He releases her, turns sharply, and runs past camera back into the corridor depth. She watches his back recede for a beat, face hardening with the weight of his choice, then turns and sprints away from camera in the opposite direction toward the distant gateway. The two characters disappear in opposite directions.

Camera: tight handheld, breathing with the action, frame trembling with intensity, the operator close to the characters throughout. Telephoto lens, shallow depth of field, the corridor dissolves into warm torch bokeh behind them.

Photorealistic, 35mm film grain, motion blur, warm torch light with deep shadows.

Sounds: distant slab grinding throughout, torches crackling, ragged breathing, the woman's straining "Jax!", running footsteps slamming to halt, the man's raw "Run!", footsteps accelerating in opposite directions, fading. No music.
