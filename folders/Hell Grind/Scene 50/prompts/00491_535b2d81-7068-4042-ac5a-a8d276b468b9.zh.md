## 原文 (source)

Handheld high-angle close-up of a young man hanging in a stone pit screaming for help, 4 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — the camera alive in the operator's hands, breathing, reactive but never jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections embraced — visible dust particles on the lens catching light, halation bleeding from torch flames into surrounding darkness, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain texture. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated color palette with isolated warm orange torch accents. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from torches only — deep crushed blacks, the character's face partially in shadow with hard angular light from above, light falling off rapidly into darkness. Inspired by the cinematography of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: a deep dark stone pit inside the floor of an ancient underground corridor. The pit is vertical, narrow, hewn rough into bedrock — a tile from the corridor floor has collapsed and revealed this hole. The pit walls are wet, weathered stone, glistening with moisture. Stone spikes are barely visible in the deep darkness at the bottom of the pit, far below. At the top edge of the frame, the corridor floor above with warm orange torch light spilling down into the pit from the lit corridor. Atmosphere thick with floating dust particles caught in the descending light.

CHARACTER: a young man, mid-twenties, with bleached yellow buzzcut hair, dark skin, clear protective glasses on his face, wearing a dark hooded jacket. His face is dust-caked, streaked with dirt from a recent disaster. He has fallen partway into the pit and is now hanging from the upper edge of it by his fingertips alone, his body dangling into the darkness below. His arms are pulled fully extended above him, shoulders hyperextended, his entire weight on his fingers gripping the rough stone lip above. He is in complete physical desperation.

ACTION: the camera is positioned ABOVE the pit, looking steeply DOWN at the young man's face. His face fills the lower two-thirds of the frame, illuminated from above by warm torch light. His eyes are wide, locked UP toward the camera (toward where his unseen friends are running in the corridor above), pupils dilated with terror, dust on his eyelashes. Hard angular torch light from above carves shadow down his cheeks and jaw, the rest of his features lost in deep shadow. At the top edge of the frame, his arms are visible straining, fingers white-knuckled gripping the stone lip. The dark pit drops away below him into pure shadow, depth visible in the background. Camera trembles intensely with handheld shake throughout, as if the operator is also panicking. The young man SCREAMS upward, voice cracking with raw full terror, the single word: "ROCO!!" His mouth opens wide on the scream, throat strained, neck tendons visible, jaw locked open. The scream echoes off the rough pit walls and trails away into the darkness.

no music. sfx only — the young man's panicked rapid shallow breathing leading into the scream, his arms shaking from physical strain, his fingers grinding on rough stone, the raw "ROCO!!" scream cracking with full terror echoing off pit walls with deep stone reverb, dust falling past camera into the darkness below catching light, distant torches crackling above in the corridor, faint deep grinding stone rumble far in the distance from somewhere else in the corridor.

## 中文对照 (zh)

手持高角特写，一名青年男子悬挂在石门坑里冲着喊救命、4 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持电影摄影带激烈受控的摄影机晃动——摄影机在操机员手中是活的、在呼吸、有回应但绝不发跳。长焦远摄电影摄影配浅景深、f/1.8 光圈。拥抱镜头瑕疵——镜头上的可见尘埃粒遇光成辉光、火把焰光把辉散（halation）泄入周围黑暗、高反差边缘的轻微色差、偶发温柔焦点漂移、淡淡 35mm 胶片颗粒纹理。电影宽银幕 16:9。重度降饱和调色板带孤立的暖橙火把点缀。仅火把的戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）布光——深压黑影、角色面部分处阴影、自上方来的硬角形光、光线急速衰落进黑暗。灵感源自 Zack Snyder 的《僵尸军团（Army of the Dead）》电影摄影。

LOCATION（场地）：远古地下廊地层内一个深邃黑暗的石坑。坑垂直、狭窄、粗凿入基岩——廊地上的一块石板塌落、露出此洞。坑壁湿润、风化、泛着湿气粼光。深深坑底极远处勉强可见石尖。画框顶部边缘，廊地在上、带暖橙火把光自点亮的走廊泄坠入坑。大气厚积飘浮尘粒、被困在降下的光里。

CHARACTER（角色）：一位青年男子，二十多岁，漂白黄板寸头发，深肤色，脸上戴透明护镜，穿深色连帽夹克。他的脸结满尘、带最近一场灾难污痕。他已半坠入坑、现在仅凭指尖悬在坑的上缘、身体悬垂进下方黑暗。他的双臂在他上方被拉得完全伸展、肩膀过度外展、他全部重量都压在扣住上方粗糙石唇的手指上。他处于彻底的生理绝望中。

ACTION（动作）：机位位于坑 ABOVE（上方）、很陡地朝下张望男子的脸。他的脸填满画面下方三分之二、被上方暖火把光照亮。他的眼睛圆睁、向上锁定机位（向上朝向楼上走廊里朋友们奔驰之处）、瞳孔因惊惶放大、睫毛上有尘。自上方来的硬角形火把光切出阴影下他面颊与下颌、他其余五官消失在深影中。画框顶部边缘，他的双臂可见绷紧、手指白着指节扣住石唇。黑暗的坑在他脚下坠开进纯粹阴影、纵深在背景中可见。摄影机全程以手持晃动剧烈颤抖、仿佛操机员也在恐慌。青年男子向上 SCREAMS（尖叫）、声音以 raw 十足惊恐迸裂、只一个字："ROCO！！"他嘴大张着尖叫、喉咙绷紧、颈部肌腱可见、下颌锁开。尖叫在粗凿坑壁上回响并拖进黑暗。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——青年男子恐慌的急促浅呼吸引入尖叫、他因生理紧绷而颤抖的臂、他在粗石上磨刮的手指、raw 的"ROCO！！"尖叫以十足惊恐迸裂、在坑壁上带深沉石回响回响、尘在机位前飘落进下方黑暗捕捉光线、上方走廊远处火把噼啪、走廊别处某地在很远处传来的微弱深沉研磨石轰隆。
