## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, suspended Jax screams for help, 8 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — alive, breathing, reactive to action but not jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 to f/2 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on lens, halation from torch flames, chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional focus drift, faint film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with warm orange torch accents. Dramatic chiaroscuro from torches only — deep crushed blacks, faces partially in shadow. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead atmosphere.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse. Ahead — distant carved stone gateway with cold blue light. Massive stone slab continues slow descent from ceiling above gateway, descended about halfway, opening still tall. Lit iron torches on both walls. Mid-corridor, an open pit where a tile collapsed — Jax hangs from edge by fingertips, dark pit with stone spikes below.

CHARACTERS:
— Jax (<<<image_2>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Hanging from edge of pit by fingertips, body dangling into darkness, arms straining.
— Roco (<<<image_3>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Running mid-corridor toward gateway, halfway there.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Handheld HIGH-ANGLE close-up shot looking DOWN at Jax from above the pit edge — camera positioned above him, looking down into the pit, dark stone walls of the pit framing his face from above. His face fills the lower half of the frame, terrified, dust-streaked, eyes wide. His arms strain visible at the top of frame, fingers gripping the stone lip white-knuckled. The dark pit drops away below him into shadow. He looks UP toward camera, raw fear. He SCREAMS, voice cracking with full terror: "ROCO!!" Camera trembles intensely with operator's reaction.

Shot 2 (4–8s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens medium shot of Roco mid-run from three-quarter angle, sprinting toward the gateway. Camera tracks with his motion, intense shake. Hard torch light slashes across his features. He hears Jax's scream — his stride HESITATES sharply, head WHIPS back over his shoulder. His face shows the conflict — duty pulling forward, brotherhood pulling back. Slowly descending slab visible in deep background.

no music. sfx only — slow deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble of slab continuing, torches crackling, Jax's panicked rapid breathing and shaking arms, Jax's "ROCO!!" raw scream cracking with full terror with stone reverb, Roco's running boots pounding wet stone, his stride hesitating with sudden audible pause.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄古老石廊，悬垂的 Jax 尖声呼救，8 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影带强烈受控的摄影机颤动——「活着」、呼吸、对动作有反应但不抖晃。长焦远摄摄影带浅景深，f/1.8 至 f/2 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜头上的可见灰尘、火把火焰的光晕（halation）、高对比边缘的色差、偶发焦点漂移、淡淡胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和的调色板带暖橙火把点缀。仅来自火把的戏剧性明暗对照——压死深黑、面部局部入影。受 Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）氛围启发。

场景（LOCATION）：狭窄的古老石质走廊，两米宽。角色身后——新鲜碎石塌落。前方——远处雕石门廊透出冷蓝色光。巨大石板继续从门廊上方天花板缓缓下落、已下到约一半、开口仍很高。两面墙上点燃铁火把。走廊中段，一个石板塌落形成敞开的坑——Jax 指尖吊挂在边缘，下方黑暗坑底有石刺。

角色（CHARACTERS）：
— Jax（<<<image_2>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，透明眼镜，深色夹克，覆尘。指尖吊挂在坑边缘，身体悬荡入黑暗中，手臂使劲。
— Roco（<<<image_3>>>）：深色凌乱发，胡须，鼻上红绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。在走廊中段朝门廊奔跑、已在半途。

镜头 1（Shot 1，0–4s）：手持高角度（HIGH-ANGLE）特写，从坑沿上方朝下看 Jax——摄影机置于他上方、向下看入坑中，坑的黑暗石壁从上方框住他的脸。他的脸填满画面下半部、惊恐、尘土斑驳、双眼圆睁。他的手臂在画面上部可见地使劲、手指握石沿握到指节发白。黑暗的坑在他下方落空坠入阴影。他向上看向摄影机、赤裸的恐惧。他尖叫（SCREAMS），声音因十足的惊骇而破开：「ROCO!!」摄影机随操机员反应剧烈颤抖。

镜头 2（Shot 2，4–8s）：硬切至 Roco 奔跑中段的手持长焦中景，四分之三角，朝门廊冲刺。摄影机随他运动、剧烈颤抖。硬朗火把光斜劈过他五官。他听到 Jax 的尖叫——他的步伐猛地一顿（HESITATES）、头「唰」地甩回肩后。他的脸显出冲突——责任把他向前拉、兄弟情把他向后扯。深处背景中可见缓慢下落石板。

无音乐。仅音效——石板石怼石碾磨的缓慢深沉轰鸣持续、火把噼啪、Jax 惊慌的急促呼吸与颤抖的手臂、Jax 带十足惊骇破开、带石混响的沙哑尖叫「ROCO!!」、Roco 奔跑的靴子重踏湿石、他的步伐一顿带着突然可闻的停顿。
