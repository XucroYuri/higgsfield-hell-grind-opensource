## 原文 (source)

Two-shot sequence, 6 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur on impacts, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, sweat and blood streaking his dust-covered face, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.

Setting: ancient stone corridor lit by torches, wet stone floor. A massive stone slab descends from the ceiling above a stone gateway at the end, the gap below now reduced to a narrow horizontal slit at knee height — barely large enough to pass through. Cold blue light spills through the slit from the safe side beyond, mixing with the warm orange torch light of the corridor.

Shot 1 (3s) — TIGHT HANDHELD CLOSE-UP ON ROCO'S FACE, OPERATOR RUNNING WITH HIM:
The camera is body-mounted at chest level inches from his face, breathing handheld feel, the operator chases him at his pace as he sprints the last meters toward the gateway. Telephoto lens, extreme shallow depth of field, the corridor behind him dissolved into streaks of warm torch bokeh racing past. Frame trembles with running gait. We stay locked on his face — eyes wide and burning forward, pupils dilated, jaw clenched hard, nostrils flared, veins standing on his temple, sweat tracking through dust down his cheek. Pure desperate determination. He commits to the leap and launches himself forward, the camera following his motion as he goes airborne, his face filling the frame mid-flight.

Shot 2 (3s) — LOW REAR-WIDE FROM SAFE SIDE, ROCO MAKING IT THROUGH AT THE LAST POSSIBLE SECOND:
Hard cut. Camera positioned low at floor level on the safe side beyond the gateway, looking back through the narrow horizontal slit below the descending slab. The man passes through the slit at the very last possible moment before the slab seals shut. The instant he clears the threshold, the slab CRASHES down to the floor with a massive stone-on-stone impact directly behind him, dust exploding outward in a thick wave that washes forward over the camera. Frame shakes violently with the impact, then settles. He emerges on the safe side, gasping, alive in the cold blue light, his back to the sealed gateway.

Sounds: Shot 1 — ragged sprint breathing at full intensity, running boots pounding wet stone, fabric whipping with motion, slab grinding ahead growing louder. Shot 2 — body passing through with whoosh, body impact on stone, massive slab CRASH on stone behind him, dust impact wave, then sudden silence broken only by his ragged exhausted breathing in the new space.

Total: 6s / 2 shots / 16:9
