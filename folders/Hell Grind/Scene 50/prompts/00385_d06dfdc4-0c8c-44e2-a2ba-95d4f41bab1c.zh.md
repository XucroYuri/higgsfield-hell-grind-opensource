## 原文 (source)

Handheld extreme close-up on a young man's face and his prosthetic arm transforming, 3 seconds.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Roco has just tried to save his friend Jax with his cybernetic crystalline prosthetic arm, but the crystal surface is too smooth — Jax's fingers slipped off, and Jax fell back into the pit catching himself by the fingertips. Roco realizes the problem in a single second. He has a control mechanism on his prosthetic that can deactivate the crystalline outer layer, revealing a normal human flesh hand beneath. He slaps the control with his left hand and the crystal recedes, the cold-blue glow extinguishing, exposing his real hand underneath.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with controlled camera shake, intimately close on the character. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — dust on the lens, halation from the prosthetic glow and torch flames, slight chromatic aberration, occasional focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with warm orange torch tones and the cold blue prosthetic glow that fades to nothing during the shot. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting.

LOCATION: at the edge of a stone pit inside an ancient underground corridor. Warm torch light from torches mounted on the corridor walls. Wet stone floor.

CHARACTER: Roco, young man, mid-twenties, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage strip across the bridge of his nose, dark jacket, dust-covered face, sweat-streaked, kneeling at the pit edge. His RIGHT ARM is a CRYSTALLINE PROSTHETIC — translucent glass-like forearm and hand with internal cold-blue glow. His LEFT hand is a normal flesh hand.

ACTION: handheld camera close on Roco from a three-quarter angle, his face filling most of the frame with his right shoulder and prosthetic arm visible at the lower edge of frame. Camera trembles with subtle controlled shake.

(0–1s): Roco's face is locked in tactical decision, eyes wide with sudden problem-solving urgency. He has just realized the crystal arm cannot grip Jax. His jaw tightens. His LEFT hand SNAPS up to a control pad on his RIGHT shoulder where the prosthetic is integrated to his body. His thumb finds a recessed button.

(1–2s): He PRESSES the control. The crystalline arm responds — the cold-blue internal glow PULSES brightly once, then begins to FADE. The translucent crystal surface of the forearm and hand begins to RETRACT, the glass-like outer layer receding inward and DISAPPEARING. Mechanical transformation visible, the crystal segments folding back along the length of the arm.

(2–3s): The crystal layer is fully retracted now, completely disappeared. Beneath where the crystal was is a NORMAL HUMAN FLESH ARM — a bare forearm and hand, skin tone matching Roco's other arm, fingers, knuckles, the back of the hand, all the natural details of human flesh visible. Sweat and dust on the newly exposed skin. The cold-blue glow is completely gone. His expression shifts to renewed determination — the human hand will grip where the crystal failed. He immediately turns his attention back toward the pit (toward off-frame below), preparing to reach down again.

no music. sfx only — Roco's heavy controlled breathing throughout, his sharp exhale of decision before pressing the control, the THUMB pressing the recessed button on the shoulder control with a soft mechanical click, an electronic descending whine as the crystalline prosthetic deactivates, a rapid sequence of small mechanical clicks and crystal-on-crystal slides as the prosthetic layer retracts and folds away, the cold-blue resonant hum fading to silence, Jax's distant panicked breathing from off-frame below in the pit, deep slow grinding rumble of distant slab descending in the background, torches crackling.

## 中文对照 (zh)

手持极特写，一名年轻男子的脸与其义肢手臂的变形，3 秒。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：一名年轻男子 Roco 刚用他的机械水晶义肢手臂试图救他的朋友 Jax，但水晶表面太光滑——Jax 的手指滑脱，Jax 掉回坑中靠指尖接住自己。Roco 在一秒内意识到问题。他的义肢上有一个控制机构，可以解除水晶外层，露出其下正常的血肉人手。他用左手拍击控制器，水晶缩回，冷蓝辉光熄灭，露出他其下的真手。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持摄影带受控摇晃，亲密贴近角色。长焦（long-lens telephoto）浅景深摄影，f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜片上的灰尘、来自义肢辉光与火把火焰的光晕（halation）、轻微色差、偶尔的焦点漂移、隐约的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影感宽屏 16:9。重度去饱和调色带暖橙火把色调，与镜头中逐渐消散至无的冷蓝义肢辉光。戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）照明。

LOCATION（地点）：古老地下走廊内一处石坑边缘。走廊墙壁上火把投来的暖火把光。湿滑石地。

CHARACTER（角色）：Roco，年轻男子，二十多岁，深色凌乱齐肩长发，浅色小胡子，鼻梁覆着红色绷带条，深色夹克，蒙尘的脸，汗渍纵横，跪在坑缘。他的右臂是一支水晶义肢（CRYSTALLINE PROSTHETIC）——半透明玻璃般的前臂与手，内部带冷蓝辉光。他的左手是一支正常的血肉之手。

ACTION（动作）：手持摄影机自四分之三角度贴近 Roco，他的脸填满画面大部分，右肩与义肢手臂在画面下缘可见。摄影机带细微受控摇晃颤抖。

(0–1s)：Roco 的脸锁定在战术决断中，眼睛因突发的问题解决紧迫而大睁。他刚意识到水晶臂无法抓住 Jax。他的下颌绷紧。他的左手啪地抬起，按到右肩上义肢接合身体的操控板上。他的拇指找到一枚凹陷的按钮。

(1–2s)：他按下控制器。水晶臂作出响应——内部冷蓝辉光强力脉动一次，随即开始褪去。前臂与手的半透明水晶表面开始退缩（RETRACT），玻璃般的外层向内收缩并消失。可见的机械变形，水晶段沿臂长向后折叠。

(2–3s)：水晶层现已完全退缩，彻底消失。水晶原先处之下是一支正常的血肉手臂——一支裸露的前臂与手，肤色与 Roco 另一只手臂相符，手指、指节、手背，人类血肉的自然细节全部可见。新露出的皮肤上有汗与尘。冷蓝辉光完全消失。他的表情转为重新燃起的决意——血肉之手将握住水晶失败之处。他立即把注意力转回坑边（朝下方画外），准备再次探手下去。

no music. sfx only（无配乐。仅环境音效）——Roco 全程沉重受控的呼吸、按下控制器前决断的锐利呼气、拇指按下肩控器凹陷按钮时的柔和机械咔嗒、水晶义肢解除时一声电子下行尖啸、义肢层退缩折叠时一连串快速的小机械咔嗒与水晶磨水晶滑动声、冷蓝共振轰鸣渐弱至静默、Jax 自画外坑下的遥远恐慌呼吸、背景远处石板下降缓慢深沉的研磨轰鸣、火把噼啪作响。
