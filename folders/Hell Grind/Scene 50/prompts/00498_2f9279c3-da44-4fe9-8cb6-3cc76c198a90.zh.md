## 原文 (source)

Handheld high-angle close-up shot looking down at Jax in the pit, 4 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — alive, breathing, reactive but not jittery. Long-lens telephoto with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on lens, halation from torch flames, chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional focus drift, faint film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with warm orange torch accents. Dramatic chiaroscuro from torches only — deep crushed blacks, faces partially in shadow. Snyder's Army of the Dead atmosphere.

LOCATION: dark stone pit inside an ancient corridor floor — a tile collapsed and now this hole drops into darkness with stone spikes barely visible in the depths below. The pit walls are rough hewn stone, wet, cold. Above the pit at the top edge of frame, the lit corridor floor with warm torch light spilling down into the pit.

CHARACTER: Jax — young man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked face. Hanging from the edge of the pit by his fingertips, body dangling into the darkness, arms straining hard, shoulders pulled up by his grip on the stone lip above.

ACTION: handheld camera positioned ABOVE Jax, looking DOWN into the pit at his face. Camera angle steeply tilted downward from above the pit edge. Jax's face fills the lower two-thirds of the frame, dust-streaked, terrified, eyes wide and locked UP toward camera. His arms strain visible at the top of the frame, fingertips white-knuckled gripping the stone lip. The dark pit drops away below him into shadow, depth visible. Warm torch light from the corridor above casts hard angular light on his face from above, the rest of his features in deep shadow. Camera trembles intensely with handheld shake. He SCREAMS, voice cracking with full raw terror: "ROCO!!" His mouth open wide in the scream, throat strained, neck tendons visible. The scream echoes off the pit walls.

no music. sfx only — Jax's panicked rapid breathing, his arms shaking from strain, fingers grinding on rough stone, "ROCO!!" raw scream cracking with full terror, deep stone reverb echoing in the pit, distant torches crackling above, dust falling past camera into the darkness below, faint deep grinding rumble of distant slab descending in the corridor above.

地点（LOCATION）：古廊地面内的一处黑暗石坑——一块砖塌落、如今这道洞坠入黑暗、下方深处石钉勉强可见。坑壁粗糙劈凿的石头、湿、冷。坑上方画面顶缘、点亮的走廊地面、暖火炬光洒落坑内。

角色（CHARACTER）：Jax——漂白黄色平头、深肤色、透明护目镜、深色连帽夹克、结尘的脸的年轻男子。以指端悬吊在坑缘、身体悬入黑暗、手臂硬撑、肩膀被他抓握上方石缘的力拉起。

动作（ACTION）：手持机位置于 Jax 上方、朝下望入坑中他的脸。机位自坑缘上方陡斜朝下倾斜。Jax 的脸填满画幅下三分之二、覆尘、惊惧、双眼圆睁锁向上方机位。他绷紧的手臂在画面上端可见、泛白指节抓着石缘。黑暗的坑在他下方坠入阴影、纵深可见。上方走廊的暖火炬光自上方把硬棱角的光投在他脸上、其余五官在深影里。机位随手持晃动剧烈颤抖。他尖叫、嗓音以全然赤裸恐惧崩裂：「ROCO!!」他嘴在尖叫中大张、喉绷紧、颈部腱可见。尖叫声在坑壁间回荡。
