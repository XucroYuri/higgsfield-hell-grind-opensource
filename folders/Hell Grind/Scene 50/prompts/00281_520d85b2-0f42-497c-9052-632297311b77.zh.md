## 原文 (source)

Refer to the man with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, sweat-streaked dust-covered face, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.

Tight handheld close-up tracking shot following the man as he runs forward through an ancient stone corridor and dives horizontally through a closing stone gateway. The camera stays inches from his face throughout, breathing handheld feel, telephoto lens compression, extreme shallow depth of field with the corridor dissolved into soft warm bokeh of torch fires behind him.

The man sprints toward camera, then launches into a horizontal dive — his body going parallel to the floor, arms outstretched, sliding on his stomach. The camera matches his motion, dropping with him as he goes low, traveling beside his face as he slides under a massive descending stone slab through a narrow gap. We stay tight on his face — sweat tracking down, jaw clenched, eyes locked forward. As his body clears the threshold, the slab crashes down behind him with a massive impact, dust exploding past camera.

Photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur on the dive, focus stays on his face throughout while everything else dissolves into bokeh. Sounds: ragged breathing, body sliding on wet stone, fabric scraping stone, massive slab crash, dust impact wave. No music.


## 中文对照 (zh)

参照图 1 中那名男子——深色凌乱披肩发、一抹浅胡须、鼻上红绷带、汗痕纵横的覆尘的脸、深色连帽夹克。

紧密手持特写跟拍镜头，跟随这名男子穿过一条古老石质走廊向前奔跑、并水平俯冲穿过一扇正在关闭的石门。整场摄影机始终距他脸数英寸、带呼吸式手持感、远摄镜头压缩、极浅景深、走廊在他身后消散为火把的柔和暖色焦外光（bokeh）。

男子朝摄影机冲刺、随即纵身一个水平俯冲——他的身体变得与地面平行、双臂前伸、伏在肚上滑动。摄影机匹配他的运动、随他压低而一同落下、在他滑过一块巨大下落石板下方一道狭窄缝隙时贴着他脸并行。我们始终紧贴他脸上——汗珠下淌、下颌紧咬、双眼向前锁定。他身体一脱离门槛，石板便在他身后砸下、带一次巨大冲击、粉尘擦过镜头爆炸。

写实摄影（photorealistic）、厚重 35mm 胶片颗粒、俯冲处运动模糊、整场焦点停在他脸上而其他一切消散入焦外光（bokeh）。声音：沙哑呼吸、身体在湿滑石上滑动、织物刮擦石面、巨大石板砸落、粉尘冲击浪。无音乐。
