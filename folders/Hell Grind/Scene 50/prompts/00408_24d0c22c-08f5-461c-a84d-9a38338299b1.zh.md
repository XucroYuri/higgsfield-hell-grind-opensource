## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, kinetic arrow volley and slab descent beginning, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense reactive camera shake — the operator under fire, frame trembling, occasional brief loss of stability. Long-lens telephoto at f/1.2 aperture. Lens imperfections aggressive — heavy dust on lens, halation streaming from torches, chromatic aberration, focus drift, light leaks, barrel distortion at edges, film grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. Heavily desaturated palette with warm torch accents and cold blue gateway glow. Dramatic chiaroscuro. Camera behaves like a documentary cameraman caught in combat — reacting to every impact, ducking, following arrows, never composed.

LOCATION: tight stone corridor, two meters wide. Behind — fresh rubble collapse. Ahead — distant gateway with cold blue light. Lit torches on both walls. Wall ports actively firing arrows from both sides.

CHARACTERS (pinned flat):
— Rain (<<<image_1>>>): silver-blonde hair, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Pressed flat to wet stone, Roco's body covering hers.
— Jax (<<<image_2>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Pressed flat on his own a few meters back, arms over his head.
— Roco (<<<image_3>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Lying on top of Rain, shielding her, his back exposed.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Handheld camera at floor level on the line of fire between the two walls, camera shaking heavily — the operator low and exposed. An arrow EXPLODES from a wall port on the right and STREAKS across frame from right to left, the iron tip flying directly toward camera. The operator FLINCHES — camera jerks down hard reactively, then whip-pans LEFT to follow the arrow as it crosses the corridor and SLAMS into the opposite wall with a brutal thunk, embedding deep, wooden shaft vibrating. The whip-pan is messy, overshot, the operator's panic visible in the framing. Halated torch light streaks across the lens during the chaotic pan.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Handheld camera at floor level inches from Rain's prone face, low three-quarter angle. Camera trembling, the operator close and unstable. Shallow focus on her eye. An arrow CRACKS through frame in the IMMEDIATE foreground — the lens passing within inches of the shaft — and the camera operator JERKS BACKWARDS reactively, frame snapping back hard for a fraction of a second, then recovering to find Rain's face again. A second arrow snaps through behind her at a different depth. Camera flinches again, smaller jolt. A third at yet another depth. Each arrow triggers a micro-flinch from the operator, the framing deteriorating with the cumulative shock. Her hair lifts violently from the wakes. She does not blink.

Shot 3 (6–9s): Handheld camera mounted near a wall position, looking sideways across the corridor. An arrow FIRES from the opposite wall port directly toward camera — camera JOLTS BACKWARDS hard as the operator panics, frame destabilizing, the arrow growing rapidly larger in the unstable frame. Just before impact, hard cut to handheld extreme close-up of the arrow IMPACTING the wall behind camera position. Camera SHAKES hard from the impact concussion, dust exploding into the lens. Two more arrows slam into stone in rapid succession, each impact rocking the camera with increasing severity, the operator barely keeping the frame.

Shot 4 (9–12s): Handheld long-lens close-up of Roco's face from above, lying on top of Rain. Camera trembling, the operator close. Eyes squeezed shut, jaw clenched, breathing hard. Above and around them, arrow whooshes continue but begin THINNING. The camera shake gradually subsides — the operator's body settling as the danger phase ends. Roco cracks one eye open, lifts his head slightly. Camera follows his head movement organically. He looks past Rain toward the distant gateway off-frame. His expression shifts from survival to dread.

Shot 5 (12–15s): Handheld long-lens low-angle shot down the corridor, camera trembling but more controlled now — the operator regaining composure but still alive in the hands. Shallow focus pulled to the deep background. Foreground: prone bodies of Roco covering Rain, Jax behind, all in soft focus. Mid-ground: arrows embedded in both walls, wooden shafts still trembling. Deep background in razor focus: the open gateway with cold blue light streaming through. A massive carved stone slab BEGINS to descend from the ceiling above the gateway — slow, inexorable. The camera shake makes the descending slab appear even more menacing, the imperfect frame underscoring the helplessness. Deep grinding rumble. Hold.

no music. sfx only — sharp wooden whoosh as first arrow flies past camera lens (Shot 1), brutal thunk of arrow embedding, wooden shaft vibrating, three arrows whooshing past Rain's head at different depths and pitches (Shot 2), arrow leaping out of port and accelerating toward camera (Shot 3), arrow impacting stone full-bleed with wood splitting and stone chips falling, two more impacts in rapid succession with camera shake, scattered arrow whooshes thinning out (Shot 4), Roco's heavy strained breathing, Rain's controlled shallow breathing, Jax's ragged breathing further back, torches crackling, deep grinding stone-on-stone descent of slab beginning in the distance (Shot 5).

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的远古石走廊，暴烈的箭矢齐射与石板开始下降，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持摄影，带有强烈的反应性摄影机晃动——操机员正受箭雨压制，画幅颤抖，偶尔短暂失去稳定。长焦 f/1.2 光圈。镜头瑕疵猛厉——镜片上灰尘厚重，火炬光晕浩荡，色差，焦点漂移，漏光，边缘桶形畸变，胶片颗粒。变形宽银幕 2.39:1。高度去饱和的调色，带暖火炬点缀与冷蓝门道辉光。戏剧性明暗。摄影机表现得像一个身陷战场的纪录片摄影师——对每一次冲击作出反应、缩身躲避、追着箭矢走，从不从容摆拍。

LOCATION（场景）：约两米宽的狭窄石头走廊。身后——新鲜的瓦砾塌落。前方——带冷蓝光的远处门道。两侧墙上点着火炬。墙面石孔正从两侧持续发射箭矢。

CHARACTERS（角色）（贴地趴伏）：
— Rain（<<<image_1>>>）：银金色头发，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新伤口，灰色连帽夹克，蒙尘。贴平在湿石上，Roco 的身体罩着她。
— Jax（<<<image_2>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，清晰眼镜，深色夹克，蒙尘。孤身贴平在几米之后，双臂护在头顶。
— Roco（<<<image_3>>>）：深色凌乱头发，胡须，鼻上有红色绷带，深色夹克，蒙尘。躺在 Rain 之上，护着她的身躯，背部暴露在外。

Shot 1（第 0–3 秒）：手持摄影机在地面高度、处于两面墙之间的射击线上，摄影机剧烈摇晃——操机员压低身形、暴露在外。一支箭从右侧墙石孔炸出（EXPLODES），从右到左横掠画幅，铁镞直朝摄影机飞来。操机员瑟缩（FLINCHES）——摄影机猛地反应性向下一抽，然后猛甩向左（whip-pans LEFT）跟随那支箭横穿走廊、以一声野蛮的闷响（SLAMS into）撞进对面墙、深深嵌进，木杆震颤。猛甩狼狈而越界，操机员的惊慌暴露在取景中。混乱的甩动期间，带光晕的火炬光掠过镜头。

Shot 2（第 3–6 秒）：手持摄影机在地面高度、距 Rain 伏卧的脸仅数英寸，低四分之三角度。摄影机颤抖，操机员贴近而不稳。浅焦落在她的眼。一支箭在最前景（IMMEDIATE foreground）嘎地穿过画幅——镜头与箭杆擦身数英寸之内——操机员反应性地向后一抽（JERKS BACKWARDS），画面猛地弹回一瞬又恢复，重新找到 Rain 的脸。第二支箭在她身后以不同纵深嗖地穿过。摄影机又瑟缩一下，较小的一次猛跳。第三支在又一处纵深。每支箭都触发操机员一次微瑟缩，取景随累加冲击而恶化。她的头发被尾迹猛地扬起。她双眼不眨。

Shot 3（第 6–9 秒）：手持摄影机架在近墙的位置，横视角望向走廊对侧。对面墙石孔直接向摄影机射出一支箭（FIRES）——操机员惊慌中摄影机猛地向后猛弹（JOLTS BACKWARDS hard），画幅失稳，箭在不稳的画幅中急剧变大。就在击中前，硬切到手持极特写，箭射入摄影机位后方的墙（IMPACTING the wall）。摄影机随冲击震荡而剧烈摇晃（SHAKES hard），灰尘炸进镜头。又两支箭迅速接连撞进石头，每次冲击都让摄影机愈加剧烈地摇晃，操机员勉强守住画幅。

Shot 4（第 9–12 秒）：手持长焦特写，从上方俯视躺在 Rain 之上的 Roco 的脸。摄影机颤抖，操机员贴近。双眼紧阖，下颌紧咬，喘着粗气。他们上方与周围，箭的呼啸声持续但开始变稀（THINNING）。摄影机晃动逐渐平息——危险阶段结束，操机员的身体落定。Roco 睁开一只眼，微微抬起头。摄影机有机地随他头部动作跟动。他越过 Rain 望向画外的远处门道。他的神情从求生切换为恐惧。

Shot 5（第 12–15 秒）：手持长焦低角度镜头顺走廊望去，摄影机颤抖但此刻更为受控——操机员正恢复镇定，但双手仍活泛。浅焦拉向深背景。前景：Roco 伏身罩着 Rain、Jax 在其后，全在柔焦。中景：两支箭嵌在两面墙上，木杆仍在震颤。深背景剃刀般锐焦：敞开的门道透着冷蓝光。一块巨型雕刻石板开始从门道上方的天棚下降（BEGINS to descend）——缓慢、不可阻挡。摄影机的晃动让下降的石板显得愈发凶险，这残缺的画幅凸显出无力感。深处的研磨轰隆。定格。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——第一支箭飞掠摄影机镜头时尖锐的木制呼啸（Shot 1），箭嵌入的野蛮闷响，木杆震颤，三支箭以不同纵深与音高嗖过 Rain 的头（Shot 2），箭自石孔射出、朝摄影机加速扑来（Shot 3），箭全出血式扎入石头、木裂石屑飞落，随后两次迅速接连的冲击伴随摄影机晃动，零散箭呼啸声渐稀（Shot 4），Roco 沉重吃力的呼吸，Rain 克制的浅呼吸，Jax 在更后处粗重的呼吸，火炬噼啪作响，远处开始的深沉的石对石研磨下降（Shot 5）。
