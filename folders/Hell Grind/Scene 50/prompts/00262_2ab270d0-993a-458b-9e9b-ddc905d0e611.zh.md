## 原文 (source)

Two-shot sequence, 6 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur on impacts, no music.

Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, askew protective glasses, exhausted bloodied face, dark jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, fresh cut on her temple, grey hooded jacket from Image 2.

Setting: ancient stone corridor lit by torches, wet stone floor. A massive stone slab descends from the ceiling above a stone gateway at the end, the gap below now ankle-to-knee height, almost sealed. The woman is on the safe side beyond the gateway, lying flat on her stomach with her upper body forced back through the narrow gap into the corridor side, slab pressing on her shoulders, her hands extended through the gap reaching desperately back into the corridor.

Shot 1 (3s) — TIGHT HANDHELD CLOSE-UP ON JAX'S FACE, OPERATOR RUNNING WITH HIM:
The camera is body-mounted at chest level inches from his face, breathing handheld feel, the operator chases him at his pace as he sprints toward the closing gateway. Telephoto lens, extreme shallow depth of field, the corridor behind him dissolved into streaks of warm torch bokeh racing past. Frame trembles with running gait. We stay locked on his face — eyes wide and burning forward, glasses askew, jaw clenched hard, mouth open in a ragged exhausted gasp, sweat tracking through dust on his cheeks, blood at the corner of his lip. Pure desperate determination. He bursts toward the threshold and launches himself into a horizontal dive — the camera drops with him into the leap, his face filling the frame mid-flight, his eyes never breaking forward focus.

Shot 2 (3s) — LOW GROUND-LEVEL ON RAIN'S HANDS GRABBING JAX:
Hard cut. Camera at floor level very close to the threshold, focused on the woman's outstretched hands reaching out from under the descending slab in the foreground, palms up, fingers extended. The yellow-haired man slides into frame on his stomach across the wet stone, body horizontal, arms forward. The woman's hands SNAP onto his forearm and jacket with sudden grip — fingers digging into fabric, knuckles whitening with effort. She YANKS hard, his body jerks toward the gap and slides under the slab through the narrow opening. His face passes the camera inches away — eyes wide, dust streaked, the gasp of being saved. The camera holds tight on the contact between their hands, body weight transferring through the grip. Shallow depth of field, focus locked on the gripping hands while everything else dissolves into bokeh.

Sounds: Shot 1 — ragged exhausted breathing at full intensity, running boots on wet stone, fabric whipping with motion, slab grinding ahead growing louder, the leap impact. Shot 2 — body sliding on slick wet floor, the woman's effort grunt as she grabs and pulls, fabric of jacket scraping stone, body weight thudding through the gap, his gasp of relief.



## 中文对照 (zh)

两镜头序列，6 秒，16:9，写实摄影，厚重 35mm 胶片颗粒，撞击处运动模糊，无配乐。

参照图片 1（Image 1）中那名漂白黄色圆寸、深肤色、歪斜护目镜、疲惫带血的脸、深色夹克的男子。
参照图片 2（Image 2）中那名银色金发、太阳穴新割口、灰色连帽夹克的女子。

Setting（环境）：火把照明的古老石质走廊，湿滑石地。一块巨大石板自尽端石门上方顶棚下降，其下缝隙现已为踝至膝高度，几乎封死。女子在门后的安全侧，俯身趴平，上半身被迫穿过窄缝探回走廊侧，石板压住她的肩，双手穿过缝隙拼命探回走廊内。

Shot 1（镜头 1）（3s）——JAX 的脸的紧密手持特写，操机员随他奔跑：
摄影机胸戴式固定在距他脸数英寸处，手持呼吸感，操机员以他的步速追逐他，他正朝正在关闭的门口冲刺。长焦镜头，极端浅景深，他身后的走廊消解为竞速掠过的暖火把散景流光。画框随奔跑步态颤抖。我们锁定他的脸——眼睛大睁而燃烧般向前，护目镜歪斜，下颌强咬，嘴张开展出处死力竭的喘息，汗渍划过脸颊尘土，嘴角带血。纯粹绝望的决意。他冲向门槛，纵身扑入一个水平的飞跃——摄影机随他落入跃动，飞扑半途中他的脸填满画面，他的眼睛绝不断开前方焦点。

Shot 2（镜头 2）（3s）——RAIN 抓住 JAX 的双手的低地面机位：
硬切。摄影机在地面高度、非常贴近门槛，焦点集中在女子从前景下落石板下伸出的双手，掌心朝上，手指伸展。黄发男子以腹部横跨湿滑石地滑入画面，身体水平，双臂向前。女子的双手啪地抓上他的前臂与夹克——手指扣入织物，指节因用力而泛白。她猛地用力猛拽（YANKS hard），他的身体朝缝隙一抽，在窄口中滑到石板之下。他的脸在数英寸处掠过摄影机——眼睛大睁，尘土纵横，得救的喘息。摄影机紧紧停在两人双手的接触上，身体重量经由抓握转移。浅景深，焦点锁在紧握的手上，其余一切消解进散景。

Sounds（声音）：镜头 1——全强度处死力竭的呼吸、湿滑石地上的奔跑靴、织物随运动飘甩、前方石板研磨变响、飞跃撞击。镜头 2——身体在光滑湿滑地面滑行、女子抓拉时用力的咕哝、夹克织物刮擦石头、身体重量砰然穿过缝隙、他释然的喘息。
