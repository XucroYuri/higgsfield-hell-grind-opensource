## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, advancement and trap activation sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: shot in the visual language of Greg Fraser's cinematography on The Batman — captured on large-format anamorphic glass (ARRI Alexa 65 with Panavision Ultra Vista lenses), 2.39:1 anamorphic widescreen with subtle organic lens distortion at frame edges, oval bokeh shapes in defocused highlights. Apertures held at f/2.8 to f/4 — multiple focal planes hold detail simultaneously, deeper field than typical shallow-focus work, but with selective focus pulls when needed. Practical motivated lighting only — torch flames are the sole visible light source, casting hard directional shadows that crush half of every face into deep angular darkness. Black levels extremely deep but never clipped — coal-black shadows that retain texture, ARRI-style deep latitude. Color palette: muted dark teals, earthy browns, oxidized amber from torch fire, no saturated colors. Atmospheric haze is a physical presence: thick particulate dust catches every shaft of torchlight as solid visible beams. Subtle 35mm film grain texture overlay. Camera movement disciplined — measured dolly, static holds, controlled pans. No handheld shake. Compositions favor heavy negative space — subjects pushed to one third of the frame with vast areas of sculpted darkness around them. Pacing dense and weighted.

SCENE CONTEXT: continuing immediately from Rain's "Stay close" warning. The team begins walking forward through the corridor in tense formation. Rain leads, holding her tablet. After several steps, Roco's eye catches a worn stone tile on the floor ahead of Rain — he recognizes the danger. He shouts "Rein!" as a warning. But Rain's foot is already committed mid-step — the warning comes a fraction too late. Her boot presses on the tile. The tile depresses with a dull click. The trap mechanism awakens. Roco shouts "DOWN!" The three drop to the floor as the first arrows begin to fire from the walls.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide, ceiling two and a half meters. BEHIND — fresh rubble collapse from blast, retreat impossible. AHEAD — open carved stone gateway with cold blue light. Iron torches lit along both walls.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars pressed close on both walls, lit iron torches every few meters casting hard directional shadows, dense atmospheric haze.

Characters (recently survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, black top, dark cargo pants, dusty. Hoverboard tucked under one arm. Tablet glowing dim cold blue in her other hand.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark hooded jacket, dusty. Walking close behind Rain.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red nose bandage, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm. Walks at the rear, eyes constantly scanning.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Static long-lens medium shot from low angle, looking up the corridor at the trio walking forward toward camera in tight formation. Composition pushes them to the right third of frame; the left two-thirds is sculpted darkness, broken only by a single torch in deep background throwing a hard wedge of orange light. Rain leads, tablet glow underlit on her face from below as faint cold blue, torch rim warm from above. Atmospheric haze between them and camera makes the air visibly thick — distinct beams of torchlight cut through it as solid shafts. They move slowly, deliberately. Only footsteps, breathing, distant drip.

Shot 2 (4–7s): Cut to a long-lens close-up of Roco's face from a three-quarter angle, walking. Hard torch source on right side cuts angular shadow across his nose, the bandage catching warm light, his left side completely lost in coal-black shadow. His eyes narrow. Focus pulls subtly past his face to the corridor floor in deep background — a single stone tile sits among others, slightly worn pattern, subtle seam. Recognition crosses his face. Jaw clenches. He shouts, voice sharp and warning: "Rein!" The name is the warning — no second word needed.

Shot 3 (7–10s): Snap cut to a static long-lens low-angle profile shot of Rain mid-step. Her boot is already descending toward the tile in soft focus foreground — the warning has come a half-second too late. Torch backlight rims her silver hair into a halated edge against deep blackness. Her face is mostly in shadow. Her eyes flick toward Roco's voice — but her foot is committed. The boot lands on the tile. The tile DEPRESSES one centimeter under her weight with a faint dull CLICK. Rain freezes. Dust sifts from the seam, particles glowing in passing torch beam. From within the walls, faintly, a deep mechanical groan begins. Three seconds of held tension on her face — the realization that she stepped on it anyway.

Shot 4 (10–12s): Hard cut to a static long-lens medium shot of Roco from the front. His face fully in profile against an angular slash of torch light, half lost in black. His jaw tightens. He shouts, voice raw and cracking: "DOWN!" He launches forward into the frame, body lunging toward Jax to take him down with him.

Shot 5 (12–15s): One continuous long-lens shot, low-angle, camera at floor level looking down the corridor. Composition heavy with negative space — the floor and lower walls in frame, ceiling cropped, depth stretching toward the distant gateway in soft focus. The three DROP into frame — Rain dives forward and slides controlled, her hoverboard clattering aside, tablet skidding across stone with its blue glow flickering against the floor; Roco's tackle drives Jax down with him, both hitting wet stone hard, Roco's hoverboard skittering away; all three flatten. Bodies in mid-foreground sharp focus, gateway in deep background also in focus thanks to f/4 depth. Above the prone bodies, the FIRST arrow CRACKS through frame in real-time speed — sharp wooden whoosh — passing horizontally across the corridor and cutting into the dark beyond the frame edge. A second arrow follows. The volley is beginning. Hold.

no music. sfx only — slow deliberate footsteps on wet stone with deep stone reverb, faint electronic hum from Rain's tablet, torches crackling with low hiss, controlled heavy breathing, distant drip from ahead, faint settle of debris from rubble behind, Roco's "Rein!" sharp warning with stone reverb, boot pressing on stone, faint dull CLICK of depressed tile, dust sifting from seam, deep mechanical groan beginning within walls (subtle, building), Roco's "DOWN!" raw with urgency, body tackle impact, three bodies hitting wet stone hard, two hoverboards clattering, tablet skidding across stone, ragged breathing, first arrow whooshes cracking through air at full real-time speed.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄石头走廊，推进与机关触发段落，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：采用 Greg Fraser 在《The Batman（新蝙蝠侠）》中的摄影语言拍摄——大画幅变形宽银幕玻璃（ARRI Alexa 65 配 Panavision Ultra Vista 镜头），2.39:1 变形宽银幕，画幅边缘带微妙的有机镜头畸变，失焦高光呈椭圆形散景形状。光圈保持在 f/2.8 至 f/4——多个焦面同时保留细节，景深比典型浅焦作品更深，但需要时也作选择性焦点拉动。仅实用动机照明——火炬火焰是唯一可见光源，投出硬朗的定向阴影，把每个人的半张脸都碾进深层的棱角状黑暗。黑位极深但绝不裁黑——煤黑色阴影保留纹理，ARRI 式深宽容度。配色：柔和的暗青色（teals）、大地棕、来自火炬火的氧化琥珀色，无饱和色。大气尘雾是一种物理在场：浓密的微粒尘土把每道火炬光都截作实心可见的光束。微妙覆盖的 35mm 胶片颗粒纹理。摄影机运动克制——有节制的轨道车（dolly）、静态保持、受控的摇（pan）。无手持晃动。构图偏好重负空间（negative space）——主体被推到画幅的三分之一处，四周是广阔的雕琢黑暗。节奏密集而有分量。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：直接接续 Rain 的「Stay close（紧跟）」警告。团队开始以紧绷的阵形穿过走廊前行。Rain 领队，手握平板电脑。几步之后，Roco 的目光捕捉到 Rain 前方地面上的一块磨损石砖——他认出危险。他喊出「Rein!」作为警告。但 Rain 的脚已在半步中途、无法挽回——警告晚了一线。她的靴子踩上石砖。石砖以一声钝响受压下沉。机关苏醒。Roco 喊出「DOWN!」。三人扑向地面，第一批箭矢开始从墙壁射出。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（场地几何）：一条约两米宽的狭窄石头走廊，天花板高两米半。后方——爆炸造成的新鲜瓦砾塌落，无法撤退。前方——敞开的雕刻石门道透出冷蓝光。两侧墙上点着铁火炬。
Setting（环境）：湿石地面、刻满符文的廊柱紧贴两侧墙、每隔几米点着的铁火炬投出硬朗的定向阴影、浓稠的大气尘雾。
Characters（角色）（刚在爆炸中生还、满身尘土）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银金色头发凌乱，眼旁有疤，太阳穴有新伤口，灰色连帽夹克，黑色上衣，深色工装裤，蒙尘。腋下夹着悬浮滑板。另一只手中的平板电脑泛着昏暗的冷蓝光。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，清晰眼镜，深色连帽夹克，蒙尘。紧跟在 Rain 身后行走。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱头发，胡须，红色鼻带，深色夹克，蒙尘。腋下夹着悬浮滑板。走在殿后，双眼不停扫视。

Shot 1（第 0–4 秒）：静态长焦中景，低角度，顺走廊望去，三人以紧凑阵形朝摄影机走来。构图把他们推到画幅右侧三分之一处；左侧三分之二是雕琢的黑暗，只在深背景处被一盏独火炬打破，投出一块硬朗的橙色光楔。Rain 领队，平板光从下方映在她脸上，呈微弱的冷蓝色，火炬轮廓光从上方为暖色。他们与摄影机之间的大气尘雾使空气肉眼可见地浓稠——一道道鲜明的火炬光化作实心光柱穿过其中。他们缓慢、从容地移动。只有脚步、呼吸、远方的滴水。

Shot 2（第 4–7 秒）：切到四分之三角度行走中的 Roco 面孔的长焦特写。右侧硬朗火炬光源在他的鼻子上切出棱角状阴影，绷带接住暖光，他左侧完全陷入煤黑色阴影。他的双眼眯起。焦点微妙地拉过他脸，落向深背景的走廊地面——一块石砖夹杂在其它之间静止不动，纹样略磨损，接缝微妙。他脸上掠过认出的神情。下颌绷紧。他喊出，声音尖锐而警示：「Rein!」这个名字本身就是警告——无需第二个词。

Shot 3（第 7–10 秒）：快切到 Rain 半步跨出时的静态长焦低角度侧影镜头。她的靴子正朝石砖降下，处于柔焦前景中——警告晚到了半秒。火炬逆光把她的银发缘成一圈带光晕的边缘，衬着深黑。她的脸大半在阴影中。她的眼转向 Roco 的声音——但她脚已无法收回。靴子落在石砖上。石砖在她重量下受压下沉一厘米（DEPRESSES one centimeter），发出微弱的钝响 CLICK（咔嗒）。Rain 僵住。灰尘从缝间筛落，微粒在掠过的火炬束中发光。从墙壁内部，微不可闻地，一声深沉的机械呻吟开始了。她的脸上呈现三秒钟的绷紧张力——她终究还是踩上去了的醒悟。

Shot 4（第 10–12 秒）：硬切到正面 Roco 的静态长焦中景。他的脸侧影完整，对着一道棱角状火炬光切出，半面陷入黑色。他的下颌绷紧。他喊出，嗓音嘶哑破碎：「DOWN!」他纵身扑进画幅，身体朝他身后的上方扑，把 Jax 一起带到地上。

Shot 5（第 12–15 秒）：一个连续长焦镜头，低角度，地面高度顺走廊望去。构图充满负空间——画幅中是地面与下侧墙，天花板被裁去，纵深朝柔焦中的远处门道伸展。三人扑进画幅（DROP into frame）——Rain 前扑、作受控滑动，她的悬浮滑板哗啦滑到一边，平板电脑擦过石头滑开、蓝光辉光在地面上闪动；Roco 的扑击把 Jax 带着一起压下，两人重重砸在湿石上，Roco 的悬浮滑板飞弹出去；三人贴平。中前景的身体锐焦，深背景的门道因 f/4 景深同样受焦。俯伏的身体上方，第一支箭以实时速度嘎地穿过（CRACKS through）画幅——尖锐的木制呼啸——水平横穿走廊，切入画幅边缘之外黑暗中。第二支箭接踵而至。齐射开始了。定格。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——湿石上缓慢从容的脚步带着深沉的石廊混响，Rain 平板电脑传来微弱的电子嗡鸣，火炬以低嘶噼啪作响，克制的沉重呼吸，前方远处滴水，身后瓦砾碎屑轻微沉降，Roco 尖锐而带警示的「Rein!」夹着石廊混响，靴子踩压石板，凹陷石砖的微弱钝响 CLICK（咔嗒），灰尘从缝间筛落，墙内开始响起的深沉机械呻吟（微妙、渐强），Roco 嘶哑充满紧迫的「DOWN!」，身体扑击的冲击，三人身体重重砸在湿石上，两块悬浮滑板哗啦作响，平板电脑擦过石头滑开，粗细的呼吸，第一支箭嗖地以全实时速度嘎地穿过空气。
