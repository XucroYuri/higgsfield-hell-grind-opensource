## 原文 (source)

A continuous handheld long-take medium shot of a young man transforming his right arm into crystalline armor while running to save a friend dangling from a stone pit, the friend slipping and falling, the man shattering his crystal arm to catch him in mid-air with bare flesh, then immediately launching him toward a closing exit. 16 seconds, single continuous shot, no cuts, in the visual language of Emmanuel Lubezki and Greig Fraser.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Roco is racing to save his friend Jax who has fallen into a deadly trap pit in an ancient stone corridor and is barely holding on by his fingertips, on the verge of falling. Roco has the ability to transform his right arm into crystalline armor — sharp jagged blood-red crystal formations growing through his skin and clothing along the right arm only, with internal red glow. As Roco sprints toward the pit, he TRANSFORMS his right arm into the crystalline armor mid-stride, choosing strength for the rescue. He arrives, drops to his knee, extends the armored arm down to Jax just as Jax's grip fails. They clasp briefly but Jax's flesh fingers can't grip the rough faceted crystal — he SLIPS off entirely, his body FALLING backward into the pit toward stone spikes below. In a single instinctive moment, Roco SHATTERS the crystal armor on his right arm — explodes it away — and his bare flesh hand SHOOTS into the pit, catching Jax's wrist in mid-fall. Roco HEAVES him out of the pit, then immediately without rest launches Jax forward across the wet stone floor toward the closing gateway — no recovery time, the urgency continues.

CINEMATOGRAPHY in the language of Emmanuel Lubezki: continuous handheld long-take, the camera moving organically through the entire sequence as one fluid motion, never cutting, the operator chasing the action. Camera floats and breathes, varies between extreme close-ups on critical details (slipping fingers, hardening eyes, crystal growth), medium shots capturing physical action (running, kneeling, lifting), and brief wider compositions (the pit edge, the falling body). All in one continuous motion. Camera shake alive but never jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Focus pulls happen organically as the camera reframes. Lens imperfections: visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from torch flames forming bokeh orbs, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9.

LIGHTING in the language of Greig Fraser: practical motivated lighting only, iron torches on corridor walls as warm key sources, hard angular orange light. Deep crushed blacks with detail in shadows. Color palette progression — warm-only at start (Roco's flesh arms), warm + emerging red glow as the right arm transforms (mid-shot), then warm + full red glow until crystal shatters, then back to warm-only after the shatter. Cold blue light from the closing distant gateway in deep background visible throughout, providing a third color note.

LOCATION: tight ancient underground stone corridor approximately two meters wide. Walls weathered carved stone with deep glyph carvings, lit iron torches on both side walls casting flickering warm orange light, halated bokeh orbs of flame on both sides. Wet stone floor reflecting torch highlights. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust. In the middle of the corridor floor, an open trap pit — dark vertical shaft cut into stone, two meters deep, rough hewn walls dropping into darkness, stone spikes barely visible at the bottom far below. In deep background ahead, a stone gateway exit being slowly closed from above by a massive descending stone slab — the gap narrow but still passable.

CHARACTERS:
— Roco: young man, mid-twenties, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, fresh bleeding wound on the bridge of his nose with red blood streaming down, dust-covered face. Wearing a dark hooded jacket with grey panel inserts, dark t-shirt underneath, dark cargo pants, fingerless glove on his left hand with a red bandana tied at the wrist. AT START of shot: BOTH arms are bare flesh under the jacket sleeves. He has the ability to transform his RIGHT arm only into crystalline armor — sharp jagged faceted blood-red crystal formations that grow through his skin AND through the fabric of his jacket sleeve, the crystal erupting through and around the cloth, asymmetric and aggressive shards covering his right shoulder, bicep, forearm and hand, translucent with internal red glow visible through the facets. The crystal is organic in appearance, like solidified blood crystallized into shards, not geometric or mechanical.
— Jax: young man, mid-twenties, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked face, terror in his eyes. Hanging from upper edge of pit by his fingertips alone, body fully extended into darkness below, arms shaking from strain, on the very edge of falling.

ACT 5 — INSTINCT (11–13s):

(11–12s) ACCENT: the shatter. In a fraction of a second, Roco's crystalline right arm SHATTERS — red crystal shards EXPLODE outward in all directions, fragments bursting away from his arm with violent force, the deep red glow extinguishing instantly. Camera holds on the explosion of crystal in extreme close-up — sharp red fragments raining down past frame. Beneath the shattered crystal: BARE HUMAN FLESH ARM, hand, fingers free.

(12–13s) ACCENT: the catch. Camera tracks Roco's now-bare flesh right hand SHOOTING DOWN into the pit at maximum speed, reaching for the falling Jax in mid-air. Audio explodes back: violent flesh-on-flesh slap, body weight catching with severe jolt, Jax's gasp of relief mixed with pain.

ACT 6 — NO REST, IMMEDIATE LAUNCH (13–16s):

(13–14.5s) ACCENT: physical peak. Without ANY pause for recovery, Roco IMMEDIATELY HEAVES Jax up out of the pit with one violent motion, using his still-active body's full strength. His back muscles strain, jaw locked, neck tendons popping. Audio rises with effort — massive grunt building into roar. Jax's body comes up over the pit edge, dragged up by the wrist.

(14.5–16s) ACCENT: the launch. The MOMENT Jax is clear of the pit, Roco doesn't pause — he uses the lifting momentum to PIVOT and SHOVE Jax forward across the wet stone floor toward the distant closing gateway visible in deep background. Roco shouts a single raw word: "GO!" The shove uses Roco's flesh right arm and his shoulder, redirecting Jax's body like a thrown spear. Jax LAUNCHES forward and CRASHES onto the wet stone, body sliding across the slick floor toward the gateway, propelled by Roco's force. Camera tracks Jax's body as it slides several meters across the corridor, his sliding body kicking up small water splashes, body skidding fast on the wet stone toward the closing gateway in deep background. The shot ends on Jax sliding away, Roco still kneeling at the pit edge in foreground, the urgency continuing — no resolution, no rest.
