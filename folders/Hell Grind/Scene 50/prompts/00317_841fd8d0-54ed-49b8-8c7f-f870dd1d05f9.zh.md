A continuous handheld long-take medium shot of a young man transforming his right arm into crystalline armor while running to save a friend dangling from a stone pit, the friend slipping and falling, the man shattering his crystal arm to catch him in mid-air with bare flesh in extreme close-up, then both men rising and running together toward a very distant closing exit. 16 seconds, single continuous shot, no cuts, in the visual language of Emmanuel Lubezki and Greig Fraser.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Roco is racing to save his friend Jax who has fallen into a deadly trap pit in an ancient stone corridor, hanging by his fingertips on the verge of falling. The corridor is VERY LONG — extending many tens of meters from the pit position to the closing gateway exit at the far end. The exit is so far away that it appears as a small distant point of cold blue light at the end of the long corridor, visible only through the heavy atmospheric haze of dust and smoke filling the passage. Roco has the ability to summon crystalline armor on his right arm — sharp jagged blood-red crystal formations that materialize on top of his jacket sleeve as energy-based external armor with internal red glow. The crystal does not damage his clothing. As Roco sprints to the pit, he summons the crystal armor on his right arm. He arrives, drops to his knee, extends the armored arm down to Jax just as Jax's grip fails. Jax slips off the rough crystal and falls. Roco DISMISSES the crystal armor (it shatters into red energy and dissipates leaving the jacket sleeve intact) and his bare flesh hand catches Jax's wrist in mid-fall in extreme close-up. Roco heaves Jax up out of the pit. Both men stand up together at the pit edge, then both BREAK INTO A RUN TOGETHER down the long corridor toward the very distant gateway, sprinting side by side or one slightly behind the other, racing the closing exit.
