## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, exploration and trigger sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: shot in the visual language of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead — long lens cinematography (85mm to 135mm equivalent), shallow depth of field at f/1.2 aperture, extreme bokeh with soft circular highlights, foreground and background dissolving into creamy out-of-focus haze, only razor-thin slices of frame in sharp focus, dynamic focus pulls between subjects. Heavy lens characteristics: subtle barrel distortion in close shots, slight chromatic aberration at high-contrast edges, halation bleeding from torch flames into surrounding darkness. Compressed perspective from telephoto lens flattens corridor depth. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm orange torch accents. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — deep crushed blacks, faces partially in shadow, single-source torch motivation, light falling off rapidly into darkness. Cinematic anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1.

SCENE CONTEXT: continuing immediately from previous beat. Rain has just told the team something feels off and to stay close. They begin moving forward. Rain leads, holding her tablet. Jax — DESPITE Rain's warning — is again drawn to the carved glyphs on the wall and stops to touch them. Roco hangs back near Jax, watchful. Rain keeps walking, focused. Her boot then presses on a worn stone tile that DEPRESSES under her weight with a dull click. Roco hears the click and notices.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide, ceiling two and a half meters. BEHIND — fresh rubble collapse, dust still drifting. AHEAD — open carved stone gateway with cold blue light, the only way forward. Iron torches lit along both walls, flickering warm orange.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars pressed close on both walls, lit iron torches every few meters, thick volumetric dust haze, claustrophobic.

Characters (recently survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, black top, dark cargo pants, heavy dust. Hoverboard tucked under one arm, deactivated. Tablet in her other hand, its screen glowing cold blue against her face. Walking forward, focused on the path.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket, dusty. Curious, drawn back to the carvings on the wall — IGNORING Rain's warning. No board.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red nose bandage, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard tucked under arm. Hangs back near Jax, watchful, jaw tight with mild frustration that Jax stopped again.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Long-lens tracking shot of Rain walking forward in profile, shot at f/1.2 — only her face in sharp focus, the corridor ahead and behind dissolved into wash of orange torch bokeh orbs and floating dust. Cold blue tablet glow on her face from below, mixing with warm torch light from the side, dual-tone contrast. She doesn't look back. Her eyes flick down to the tablet, then up to the path ahead, then down again — checking the schematic against reality. Off-frame from behind, the soft sound of fingers brushing stone. Rain's expression flickers — she heard it but doesn't stop, doesn't turn. Her pace stays steady. The faintest tightness in her jaw — she knows Jax stopped. She's choosing not to confront him right now.

Shot 2 (4–8s): Hard cut to a long-lens extreme close-up of Jax's fingertips on the carved glyphs, shot at f/1.2. Only the points of contact are in razor-sharp focus — the carvings, his fingertips, individual grains of dust shifting under his touch — the rest dissolved into creamy bokeh. The nearest torch flickers warm halated light across the stone, dust motes glowing as bokeh particles in the beam. His fingers slide laterally across the carvings, fascinated. Pull focus from the stone surface up to his eye behind glasses — wide, locked on the carvings, completely tuned out from the rest of the world. He has stopped walking entirely.

Shot 3 (8–12s): Hard cut to a tight long-lens low-angle on the wet stone floor ahead of Rain, camera at ground level, shot at f/1.2. Only the surface of one stone tile is in sharp focus — surrounding tiles, the corridor walls, Rain's silhouette above all dissolved into deep soft bokeh. The torch behind throws warm halated highlights across the wet stone, reflections shimmering as bokeh streaks. Rain's boot enters frame from above, descending toward the tile in soft focus. The boot presses down. The tile DEPRESSES one centimeter under her weight with a faint dull CLICK. Dust sifts from the seam between stones, individual particles catching firelight as glowing micro-bokeh. Camera locks dead still on the sunken tile. Three full seconds of nothing but the depressed stone and dust falling. The world holds its breath.

Shot 4 (12–15s): SNAP cut to a long-lens tight insert of Roco's eye behind dust-streaked skin, shot at f/1.2 — only his iris in razor-sharp focus, his pupil dilating as he registers the click. Warm torch halation glowing across the side of his face. His head whips toward Rain — camera whip-pans with him, the world smearing into streaked bokeh and halation, snapping into focus on Rain's silhouette ahead with one foot still on the depressed tile. Roco shouts, voice sharp and urgent, cracking on the word: "Wait!" One word. Push-in on Roco's face. From within the walls, faintly, a deep mechanical groan begins — old stone machinery awakening for the first time in centuries.

no music. sfx only — Rain's footsteps echoing, faint electronic hum from her tablet, soft tap of her fingers on the screen, fingers brushing carved stone with grit (off-frame in Shot 1), torch flames crackling, boot pressing on stone, faint dull CLICK of depressed tile, dust sifting from the seam, sharp "Wait!" with urgency and stone reverb, deep mechanical groan beginning within the walls, hoarse breathing, distant drip ahead, faint ongoing settle of debris from breach behind.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的古老石质走廊，探索与触发序列，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：以 Zack Snyder《亡者之师》（Army of the Dead）的视觉语言拍摄——长焦摄影（85mm 至 135mm 等效），f/1.2 光圈浅景深，带柔和圆形高光的极端散景，前景与背景消解为奶油状失焦薄雾，仅画面上剃刀般细薄的切片处于锐焦点，主体间动态焦点拉动。重镜头特征：近景中细微桶形畸变、高对比边缘处轻微色差、火把火焰向周围黑暗晕渗的光晕（halation）。长焦镜头带来的压缩透视压平走廊纵深。重度去饱和调色，仅暖橙火把的颜色孤立点缀。戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）照明——压碎浓黑，面部部分没入阴影，单源火把动机光，光迅速衰减进黑暗。电影感变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：紧接前一节拍继续。Rain 刚告诉小队这里有些不对劲、要跟紧。他们开始前行。Rain 带头，手拿平板电脑。Jax——尽管 Rain 已警告——再次被墙上的凿刻符文吸引，停下脚步去触碰它们。Roco 靠在 Jax 近旁殿后，保持警惕。Rain 继续走，专注。随后她的靴子压上一块磨损的石板，石板在她的体重下嘎然一声闷咔嗒下沉。Roco 听到咔嗒声并注意到了。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（地点几何）：约两米宽的紧走廊，顶棚两米半高。身后（BEHIND）——新鲜塌落碎石堆，尘土仍在飘散。前方（AHEAD）——敞开的凿刻石门泛冷蓝光，是唯一的前路。沿两侧墙壁点亮的铁火把，摇曳暖橙光。

Setting（环境）：湿滑石地，两侧墙壁紧贴地立着遍布符文的立柱，每隔几米点亮的铁火把，浓重的体积尘土薄雾，幽闭。

Characters（角色）（刚经历爆炸幸存，蒙尘）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银色金发凌乱，眼睛旁有道疤，太阳穴新割口，灰色连帽夹克，黑色上衣，深色工装裤，厚积尘土。一块熄机的悬浮滑板夹在腋下。另一只手拿着平板电脑，屏幕冷蓝光映在她脸上。向前走，专注于路径。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：漂白黄色圆寸，深肤色，清晰护目镜，深色连帽夹克，蒙尘。好奇，被墙上雕刻拉了回去——无视 Rain 的警告。无滑板。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱头发，胡子，红色鼻子绷带，深色夹克，蒙尘。腋下夹着悬浮滑板。靠在 Jax 近旁殿后，警惕，下颌因轻微的挫败而绷紧——Jax 又停了。

Shot 1（镜头 1）（0–4s）：Rain 侧影前行的长焦跟拍镜头，f/1.2 拍摄——仅她的脸处于锐利焦点，前后走廊消解为一片暖橙火把散景球与漂尘的色洗。来自下方的冷蓝平板辉光照在她脸上，与侧面暖火把光混合，双色调对比。她不回头。她的眼睛瞟向平板，随即抬向前方路径，随即又落下——把构架图与现实对照。身后画外，传来手指拂过石面的柔和声。Rain 的表情一闪——她听见了，但不停步、不回头。她的步速保持平稳。下颌有最轻微的绷紧——她知道 Jax 停了。她选择此刻不去质问他。

Shot 2（镜头 2）（4–8s）：硬切至 Jax 指尖在刻凿符文上的长焦极特写，f/1.2 拍摄。仅接触点处于剃刀锐利焦点——雕刻、他的指尖、在他触摸下移动的一粒粒尘土——其余消解进奶油散景。最近的火把在石上摇曳暖辉散光，尘点化作光束中泛光的散景粒子。他的手指在雕刻上横向滑动，入迷。焦点从石面拉上他护目镜后的眼睛——睁大，锁死在雕刻上，完全与世界隔绝。他已完全停下脚步。

Shot 3（镜头 3）（8–12s）：硬切至 Rain 前方湿滑石地上的紧密长焦低角度，摄影机在地面高度，f/1.2 拍摄。仅一块石板的表面处于锐利焦点——周围石板、走廊墙壁、上方 Rain 的剪影全部消解进深层柔散景。身后火把在湿滑石上投出暖辉散高光，倒影化作散景流光闪烁。Rain 的靴子自上方入画，以柔焦下落朝石板而去。靴子压下。石板在她的体重下下沉一厘米，伴随一声微弱的沉闷咔嗒。尘土自石缝筛落，个别尘粒捕获火光化作泛光的微散景。摄影机死死锁定在凹陷的石板上。整整三秒，只有这块凹陷的石板和落下的尘土。世界屏住了呼吸。

Shot 4（镜头 4）（12–15s）：啪切至 Roco 蒙尘皮肤下眼睛的长焦紧密插入镜头，f/1.2 拍摄——仅他的虹膜处于剃刀锐利焦点，随他察觉咔嗒声而瞳孔放大。暖火把光晕沿他脸颊一侧铺亮。他的头猛甩向 Rain——摄影机随他猛甩镜头（whip-pan），世界糊成条纹状散景与光晕，啪地聚焦在前方 Rain 的剪影上，她一只脚仍踩在凹陷的石板上。Roco 喊道，声音锐利而紧迫，在该词上破音：「Wait！（等等！）」一个字。推近 Roco 的脸。自墙壁内部，隐约地，一声深沉的机械呻吟开始——古老的石质机关数百年来首次苏醒。

no music. sfx only（无配乐。仅环境音效）——Rain 的回响脚步声、她平板电脑的微弱电子嗡响、她手指在屏幕上轻敲、手指拂过凿刻石面带沙砾（镜头 1 中画外）、火把火焰噼啪、靴子压上石板、被压石板的微弱沉闷咔嗒、尘土自石缝筛落、带着紧迫与石混响的锐利「Wait！」、墙内开始的深沉机械呻吟、沙哑的呼吸、前方远处滴水、身后破口处碎石持续的轻微沉淀。
