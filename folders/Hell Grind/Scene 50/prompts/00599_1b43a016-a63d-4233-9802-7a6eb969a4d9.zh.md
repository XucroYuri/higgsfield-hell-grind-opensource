## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, opening sequence with awakening torches and recovering survivors, 15 seconds.

SCENE CONTEXT: this scene begins seconds after a violent explosion collapsed the passage above, sending the three characters falling/sliding down through the rubble into this narrow corridor below. They have just arrived and are catching their breath, dust thick in the air, ears ringing. As they appear in this ancient place, the corridor itself seems to RESPOND — wall-mounted torches along its length IGNITE one by one in sequence, deep into the corridor toward the distant gateway, as if waking up to the presence of intruders. The characters are too disoriented to register this immediately.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide with low ceiling around two and a half meters high. BEHIND the characters — a massive fresh rubble collapse blocking retreat, the way they fell through, dust pouring down from the breach above. AHEAD of the characters — wall-mounted iron torch brackets along both side walls, currently dark; at the far end, an open carved stone gateway with faint cold blue light spilling through from beyond.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars pressed close along both side walls, iron torch brackets every few meters along both walls, thick volumetric dust haze. Anamorphic widescreen, teal-shadow color grade with warm orange torch accents (once lit), naturalistic low-key lighting.

Characters (just survived a blast, exhausted and disoriented):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): young woman, silver-blonde shoulder-length hair, small scar by eye, fresh small cut on temple, grey hooded jacket over black top, dark cargo pants, heavy dust on clothes, hair disheveled. Hoverboard tucked under her arm, deactivated. Breathing hard. Lead.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): young man, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked. Hands on knees, catching his breath, recovering from the blast.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): young man, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage across bridge of nose, dark jacket, dust-covered. Hoverboard tucked under his arm, deactivated. Breathing hard, watching Jax to make sure he's okay.

Shot 1 (0–6s): Long static low-angle shot looking down the length of the corridor toward the distant carved gateway. The corridor is dark, only faint cold blue light from the gateway visible at the far end. Then — the FIRST iron torch nearest camera IGNITES with a soft whump and a burst of warm orange flame, flickering. A beat. The NEXT torch deeper down the corridor ignites — whump. Then the next. Then the next. The torches light in sequence, one after another, traveling away from camera deeper into the corridor, each new flame revealing more of the carved walls and pillars previously hidden in darkness. Camera holds completely still throughout. The corridor itself is awakening. By the end of the shot, the entire corridor is bathed in flickering warm orange light against the cold blue glow at the far gateway.

Shot 2 (6–10s): Hard cut to a tight medium shot of the three characters standing close together in the now-illuminated corridor, dust still thick in the air around them, debris settling. Rain leans one hand against a pillar, head down, chest rising and falling hard. Jax is bent forward, hands on knees, coughing softly. Roco wipes dust from his face with the back of his hand, his other arm half-extended toward Jax in case he stumbles. The newly lit torches flicker on their dust-covered faces. Camera is still, holding on the moment of recovery. No dialogue. Just breathing, coughing, the aftermath.

Shot 3 (10–13s): Slow push-in on Rain's profile as her breathing steadies. She straightens up, lifts her head, and only now seems to notice the corridor she's standing in — eyes scanning the carved walls, the lit torches, the gateway in the distance. The cut on her temple catches the firelight. She inhales once, deep and controlled, and then says with quiet authority: "Straight through. Forty feet." The line is delivered as a tactical assessment, not an emotion. She is back.

Shot 4 (13–15s): Cut to a wider shot from behind the trio, looking forward down the now-fully-lit corridor toward the gateway. Rain stands a half-step ahead. Jax straightens up next to Roco, finally lifting his head, finally taking in the place. The torches flicker on both walls leading the eye to the distant gateway. The shot holds the promise of forward movement — they're about to begin.

no music. sfx only — settling debris and trickling stone dust from the breach behind, distant rumble of secondary collapse far above, faint ringing tinnitus from blast aftermath, sequential whump of each torch igniting one by one (six or seven distinct ignitions, evenly spaced through Shot 1), crackling flames, heavy ragged breathing from all three, soft cough from Jax, Rain's clear "Straight through. Forty feet." with stone reverb, faint distant drip from ahead.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄远古石廊，火把苏醒与幸存者恢复的开场序列，15 秒。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：本场在一次暴力爆炸震塌上方通道、把三名角色震落 / 滑落穿过瓦砾进下方这段窄廊的几秒后开始。他们刚抵达、正喘口气，空气里尘浓、耳朵嗡嗡。他们现身于这远古之地时，走廊本身似乎做出 RESPONDS（反应）——沿其长度点燃墙挂火把、一个接一个按序 IGNITE（点亮）、深入走廊朝远处拱门、仿佛对闯入者的降临苏醒过来。角色们太晕头转向、一时没察觉。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（场地几何）：约两米宽的窄石廊、约两米半高的低顶。角色身后——覆盖退路的巨大新鲜碎石崩落、他们坠落穿过之处、尘土自上方缺口倾泻而下。角色前方——两侧墙沿壁的铁火把支架、目前黑暗；远端一座带微弱冷蓝光泄入的敞雕石拱门。

场景：湿石地面、两侧墙紧贴的符文覆盖石柱、两墙每隔几米一个铁火把支架、浓厚体积尘土雾霭。变形宽银幕、青影调色板带暖橙火把点缀（点亮后）、自然主义低调布光。

角色（刚爆炸中生还、精疲力竭而晕头转向）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：青年女子，银金色齐肩头发，眼旁一道小疤，太阳穴一道新鲜小伤口，黑色上衣外罩灰色连帽夹克，深色工装裤，衣服覆满重尘，头发凌乱。一块已关闭的悬浮滑板夹在臂下。粗重喘息。领队。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：青年男子，漂白黄板寸，深肤色，透明护镜歪戴，深色连帽夹克，结满尘。双手撑膝、喘气、从爆炸中恢复。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：青年男子，深色凌乱齐肩长发，浅色小胡子，鼻梁上红色绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。一块已关闭的滑板夹在臂下。粗重喘息，看着 Jax 确认他没事。

Shot 1（0–6 秒）：沿走廊长度望向远处雕石拱门的长静态低角镜头。走廊黑暗，只有远端拱门微弱的冷蓝光可见。随后——最靠近机位的第一根铁火把带一声轻柔的 whump（闷燃声）和一阵温暖橙焰 IGNITES（点亮）、闪烁。一个节拍。走廊更深处的下一根火把点亮——whump。再下一根。再下一根。火把按序点亮、一根接一根、自机位向外更深地行入走廊，每一簇新火焰都揭示出更多此前隐于黑暗中的雕墙与石柱。摄影机全程完全稳住。走廊本身正在苏醒。到本镜头结尾，整个走廊沐浴在闪烁的暖橙光里、衬托远端拱门的冷蓝辉光。

Shot 2（6–10 秒）：硬切至三名角色在现已照亮的走廊中紧密站在一起的密切中景，他们身周空气里尘仍浓、碎料落定。Rain 一只手抵着石柱、低着头、胸口剧烈起伏。Jax 前倾、双手撑膝、轻轻咳嗽。Roco 用手背擦去脸上的尘，他另一只臂半伸向 Jax、以防他踉跄。新点亮的火把在他们覆尘的两人面孔上闪烁。摄影机静止、停在这恢复的时刻。无对话。只有呼吸、咳嗽、余波。

Shot 3（10–13 秒）：缓慢推近 Rain 的侧影、她的呼吸渐渐平稳。她直起身、抬起头、直到此刻才似乎注意到自己正站在一条廊里——眼睛扫视雕墙、点亮的火把、远处的拱门。她太阳穴上的伤口捕捉到火光。她吸气一次、深沉而克制，然后以安静的权威说："Straight through. Forty feet.（直穿过去。四十英尺。）"这句台词以战术评估的方式说出、不是情绪。她回来了。

Shot 4（13–15 秒）：切至三人组后方的更宽镜头、向前望向现已完全照亮的走廊、朝拱门。Rain 领先半步而立。Jax 在 Roco 身旁直起身、终于抬起头、终于注意到这个所在。两侧墙上闪烁的火把引着视线到远处拱门。本镜头承住了前进的许诺——他们即将启程。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——身后缺口落定的碎料与滴落的石尘、上方远处二次崩塌的遥远轰隆、爆炸余波的微弱耳鸣、每根火把一根接一根点亮的连续 whump（六或七次分明点亮、在 Shot 1 中均匀间隔）、噼啪火焰、三人浓重粗重的呼吸、Jax 的轻咳、Rain 带石回响清晰的"Straight through. Forty feet."、前方远处微弱的滴水。
