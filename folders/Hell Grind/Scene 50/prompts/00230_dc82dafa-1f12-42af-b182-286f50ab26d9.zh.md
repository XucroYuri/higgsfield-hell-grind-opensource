Narrow ancient stone corridor, escape sequence beginning, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with subtle controlled camera shake — alive in operator's hands, breathing, never jerky. Long-lens telephoto at f/1.2 aperture with extreme shallow depth of field. Lens imperfections — visible dust on lens, halation aggressive from torch flames, chromatic aberration on edges, occasional gentle focus drift, faint film grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. Heavily desaturated palette with warm torch accents and cold blue gateway light. Dramatic chiaroscuro from torches only. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse from earlier blast. Ahead — distant carved stone gateway with cold blue light spilling through, but a massive stone slab is now slowly descending from the ceiling above the gateway, sealing it. Lit iron torches on both walls. Arrows embedded in both walls from previous volley. Wet stone floor with depressed pressure tile.

CHARACTERS (recovering from arrow volley, dust-covered, in defensive prone positions):
— Rain (<<<image_3>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Lying flat on the wet stone, on her stomach, head turned sideways against the floor.
— Jax (<<<image_2>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Lying CURLED in fetal position on the wet stone, face down, arms wrapped around his head protectively, his yellow buzzcut visible from above, body compact and low.
— Roco (<<<image_3>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Lying on his stomach on the wet stone, propped up slightly on his forearms, head raised to look forward down the corridor, the most active of the three. Jax is visible behind him in soft bokeh, also prone.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Handheld long-lens low-angle shot down the corridor at floor level, focus on the distant gateway — a massive carved stone slab is visibly descending from the ceiling above the gateway, the bright cold blue light beyond compressing as the slab lowers. Halation streaming off the diminishing aperture. In foreground bokeh, prone bodies of the three on the wet stone floor — Jax curled in fetal position with yellow buzzcut visible, Rain prone next to him, Roco propped on forearms with head raised. Camera trembles subtly. Deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble dominates.

Shot 2 (3–5s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens close-up of Jax curled on the floor, his yellow buzzcut visible as he lifts his head slightly, dust-streaked face emerging from his arms. He breathes hard. His eyes lock forward toward the distant gateway off-frame. He says, voice rough with realization: "Bro. The door." Camera close, subtle shake.

Shot 3 (5–7s): Handheld long-lens close-up of Roco propped on his forearms next to Rain, his head raised, hard torch light slashing across his features. Jax visible in soft bokeh behind him, curled. His eyes assess the situation forward — gateway, slab, distance — for one beat. Then he commits. Voice flat with command: "On me." He pushes up off the floor.

Shot 4 (7–10s): Handheld long-lens medium shot from low angle as Roco SPRINGS to his feet first and starts running toward the gateway, body launching forward through the corridor. Rain follows immediately — pushing up, snatching her hoverboard and tablet from the floor, sprinting after him. Jax is last — uncurling from his fetal position, scrambling up from the floor, getting his feet under him. Camera tracks with their motion forward, dust kicking up from their boots, arrow-cage walls passing in rapid bokeh on either side.

Shot 5 (10–12.5s): Handheld long-lens close-up tracking with Jax as he runs, a beat behind the others. Camera trembling with the run. His breath ragged, eyes forward on Roco's back ahead. Boots pounding wet stone. Then suddenly — a sharp CLICK from beneath his feet.

Shot 6 (12.5–15s): Hard cut to handheld extreme close-up at floor level — a worn stone tile DROPS AWAY beneath Jax's foot mid-stride, the entire tile collapsing into a dark hole below. Jax PLUNGES downward, body dropping out of frame as the floor opens. His hands SCRABBLE at the edge of the hole — fingertips catching the stone lip — his body slamming hard against the inner wall of the pit. He hangs by his fingertips. Camera trembles with the impact, holds tight on his fingers gripping the edge, knuckles white, dust falling past into the darkness below.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭长古石廊，逃跑序列开端，15 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影、细微受控镜头晃动——在操机员手中鲜活、呼吸着、绝不猛震。长焦远摄 f/1.2 光圈、极端浅景深。镜头瑕疵——镜上可见的尘、火炬火焰剧烈光晕（halation）、边缘色差、偶发柔和漂焦、微淡胶片颗粒。变形宽银幕 2.39:1。高度去饱和调色板、暖火炬点缀 + 冷蓝入口光。仅火炬的戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）。灵感源出 Snyder《活死人军团》。

地点（LOCATION）：狭促古石廊、两米宽。角色身后——早前爆炸的新鲜碎石坍堆。前方——远处的石雕入口、冷蓝光从中泄出、但入口上方天花板如今一块巨大石门缓缓下落、封合着它。两侧墙点亮的铁制火把。先前齐射的箭嵌在两侧墙。湿滑石板地面、带一块下陷的感应石砖。

角色（CHARACTERS，正从箭齐射中恢复、覆尘、处防御俯卧姿势）：
— Rain（<<<image_3>>>）：银金发凌乱、眼旁疤痕、太阳穴新割口、灰连帽夹克、蒙尘。平躺在湿石上、趴着、头侧转贴地。
— Jax（<<<image_2>>>）：黄色平头、深肤色、透明护目镜、深色夹克、蒙尘。蜷成胎儿姿势伏在湿石上、面朝下、双臂防护性地抱头、自上可见他的黄色平头、身体紧凑而贴地。
— Roco（<<<image_3>>>）：深色凌乱发、胡须、鼻上红绷带、深色夹克、蒙尘。趴在湿石上、以前臂微微撑起、头抬起朝前望走廊、三人中最活跃。Jax 在他身后柔光斑中可见、也俯卧。

镜头 1（0-3s）：地面层顺走廊的手持长焦低角度镜头、对焦远处入口——一块巨大的石雕门板正从入口上方天花板可见地降下、门板降低时其后的明亮冷蓝光被压缩。光晕（halation）自缩小中的光孔流出。前景光斑中、三人俯卧身体在湿石地面——Jax 蜷成胎儿姿势带可见黄色平头、Rain 俯卧在他旁边、Roco 以前臂撑起头抬着。机位细微颤抖。深沉石对石磨擦轰鸣主导。

镜头 2（3-5s）：硬切至蜷地 Jax 的手持长焦特写、他把头微微抬起、覆尘的脸自双臂中露出、可见黄色平头。他粗喘。他的眼锁定前方出画的远处入口。他说道、声音因恍然而粗沉：「Bro. The door.（哥们儿。那门。）」机位近、细微晃动。

镜头 3（5-7s）：撑在 Rain 旁前臂上的 Roco 的手持长焦特写、头抬起、硬火炬光斜劈过他的五官。Jax 在他身后柔光斑中可见、蜷着。他的眼评估前方局势——入口、门板、距离——一瞬。随即他投入。声音沉着带命令：「On me.（跟我来。）」他自地面推起身。

镜头 4（7-10s）：低角手持长焦中景、Roco 率先弹起身、开始朝入口跑去、身体向前跃出经走廊。Rain 立即跟上——撑起、自地面抄起她的悬浮滑板与平板、追在他身后冲刺。Jax 最后——自胎儿姿势展开、自地面爬起、双腿撑稳站住。机位跟他们的前进运动、他们的靴踢起尘、两侧的箭墙快速光斑掠过。

镜头 5（10-12.5s）：手持长焦特写跟着奔跑的 Jax、落后他人一拍。机位随奔跑颤抖。他呼吸粗重、双眼锁定前方 Roco 的背。靴砸湿石。随即突然——他脚下传来一声尖锐咔哒（CLICK）。

镜头 6（12.5-15s）：硬切至地面高度的手持极特写——Jax 半步中、一块磨损石砖在他脚下塌落、整块砖坍进下方一个黑洞。Jax 向下猛坠、地面张开时身体掉出画面。他的手在洞口边缘乱抓（SCRABBLE）——指端勾住石缘——身体重重撞上坑的内壁。他以指端悬吊。机位随撞击颤抖、紧钉在他抓紧缘沿的手指上、指节泛白、尘掠过落入下方黑暗。
