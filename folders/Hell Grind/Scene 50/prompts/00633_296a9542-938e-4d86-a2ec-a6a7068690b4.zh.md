## 原文 (source)

Ancient stone corridor, slow arrival sequence, 15 seconds. Setting: massive rubble collapse blocking the far end of a long carved stone corridor, faint cold blue light bleeding through a narrow gap in the debris, wet stone floor reflecting dim highlights, glyph-covered pillars on both side walls, candles flickering in alcoves, volumetric dust haze, oppressive silence. Anamorphic widescreen, teal-shadow color grade with warm candle accents, naturalistic low-key lighting.

Characters:
— Rain (<<<image_1>>>): young woman, silver-blonde shoulder-length hair, small scar by eye, grey hooded jacket over black top, dark cargo pants. Lead, cautious, observant.
— <<<image_2>>>: young man, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket. Tech-focused.
— <<<image_3>>>: young man, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage across bridge of nose, dark jacket. Wary, instinct-driven.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Extreme wide low-angle establishing shot looking down the corridor's full length toward the rubble. The three figures are tiny silhouettes at frame entrance, dwarfed by the towering pillars and ceiling. Rain glides in first on her hoverboard with faint purple glow, the other two walk in behind her on foot. Camera holds completely static. Their footsteps and the hoverboard hum are the only sounds.

Shot 2 (4–8s): Side profile tracking close-up of Rain on the hoverboard, slow dolly alongside her. She decelerates gradually, head turning slightly to take in the carved walls. She steps off, the board hovers stationary at knee height. She picks it up, tucks it under her arm. Boots land on wet stone with a soft echo.

Shot 3 (8–12s): Medium shot of <<<image_2>>> and <<<image_3>>> catching up to Rain, all three now standing close together. They stop. Heads slowly turn — looking up at the ceiling, scanning the pillars, taking in the scale. Nobody speaks. <<<image_3>>>'s hand instinctively drifts toward his side. Camera slowly pushes in on the group. Tension building in silence.

Shot 4 (12–15s): Close-up on Rain's face, silver hair half-shadowed, eyes scanning, lips parted. She exhales slowly, controlled. Her gaze locks toward the rubble at the far end, calculating. Camera holds on her stillness. Distant drip. A candle flickers off-frame, light shifting subtly across her face.

no music. sfx only — hoverboard hum fading to silence, footsteps echoing on wet stone, soft scrape of the board being picked up, controlled breathing, distant drip, candle flicker.
