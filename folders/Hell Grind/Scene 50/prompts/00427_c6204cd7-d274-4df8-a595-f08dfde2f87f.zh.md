## 原文 (source)

Handheld long-lens close-up shot of an ancient stone gateway being slowly squeezed shut from above by a massive descending stone ceiling, 2.5 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — alive in operator's hands, the operator drawn to the threat, frame trembling with grim recognition. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from cold blue light beyond the gateway forming a bright bloom that bleeds into surrounding darkness, slight chromatic aberration on the high-contrast edge between cold light and dark stone, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm orange torch accents in the foreground edges and intense cold blue light from beyond the gateway. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — deep crushed blacks, the gateway as a bright cold portal in the darkness ahead. Inspired by the cinematography of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: the deep end of an ancient underground stone corridor, looking forward at a carved stone gateway that opens to somewhere beyond filled with cold blue light. The gateway opening is being SQUEEZED shut from ABOVE — what was once a tall doorway is being compressed by the ceiling lowering onto it. The opening is currently still tall enough to walk through but visibly shrinking, the upper portion of the gateway already obscured by the descending ceiling above it, the lower portion still wide and open with cold blue light streaming through. The descent is VERY SLOW — almost imperceptible, but visible enough to show real-time progression over the duration of the shot, the ceiling lowering perhaps an inch during the 2.5 seconds. Dust and small stone fragments cascade in steady waves from where the ceiling meets the top of the gateway, falling down through the cold  light. The stone walls of the gateway are weathered, cold, ancient. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust caught in the cold blue light.

ACTION: handheld camera positioned in the corridor at chest height, framed on a long-lens close-up of the closing gateway in the deep distance — telephoto compression making the gateway appear closer than it is, the cold blue light bleeding through the still-tall but shrinking opening and washing the foreground stone in cool fill. The camera trembles with subtle handheld shake, the operator caught in the moment of recognizing the slow inexorable threat. The opening is visibly shrinking from ABOVE in real time during the 2.5 seconds — the descending ceiling slowly grinding lower over the top of the gateway, the bright cold blue opening compressing in height from above (not from the sides). Halation streams off the diminishing aperture and stretches across the lens. Dust pours down past the gateway from above in steady waves, individual particles glowing as bokeh against the cold blue light. The slowness of the descent makes it more menacing, not less — there is no urgency in the movement itself, just inevitability. Camera holds the gateway in frame, still, observing the threat with controlled tension.

no music. sfx only — slow deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble dominating from above as the ceiling descends, the rumble louder than other shots because we are closer to the gateway threat, cascading dust and small stone fragments falling in steady waves from where the descending ceiling meets the top of the gateway, faint cold hum or resonance from the cold blue light beyond the gateway, distant torches crackling somewhere behind in the corridor.

## 中文对照 (zh)

手持长焦特写镜头，拍一座古老石门被上方一块巨大下落石质天花板缓缓压紧关闭，2.5 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影带强烈受控的摄影机颤动——在操机员手中「活着」、操机员被威胁吸引、画面带着阴郁认知颤抖。长焦远摄摄影、浅景深、f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜头上的可见灰尘、门廊之外冷蓝色光的强烈光晕（halation）形成一道渗入周围黑暗的明亮光晕，冷光与暗石之间的高对比边缘轻微色差、偶发温和的焦点漂移、淡淡的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和的调色板，前景边缘带孤立的暖橙火把点缀、门廊之外带强烈的冷蓝色光。戏剧性明暗对照照明——压死深黑、门廊作为前方黑暗中的明亮寒冷通道。受 Zack Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）摄影启发。

场景（LOCATION）：古老地下石质走廊的深处末端，向前看到一座雕石门廊，通向某处、填满冷蓝色光。门廊开口正从上方（ABOVE）被压紧关闭——曾经的极高门洞正被上方低下的天花板压缩。开口目前仍高到足以步行穿过但明显在缩小，门廊上部已被上方低下的天花板遮蔽，下部仍宽阔敞开、冷蓝色光倾泻穿过。下落非常缓慢（VERY SLOW）——几乎不可察觉，但可见程度足以在镜头时长内显示实时进程，天花板在 2.5 秒内或许降低一英寸。粉尘与小石碎块从天花板与门廊顶部相接处成稳定浪涌倾泻、穿过冷色光落下。门廊的石墙风化、寒冷、古老。大气雾霭浓重，冷蓝色光中捕捉到的飘浮尘埃。

动作（ACTION）：手持摄影机置于走廊中胸高，取景为深远距离处关闭门廊的长焦特写——长焦压缩使门廊显得比实际更近，冷蓝色光透过仍高但缩小的开口渗出、以冷色补光洗过前景石头。摄影机带微妙的手持颤动颤抖，操机员陷入认出这缓慢不可阻挡威胁的时刻。开口在 2.5 秒内以实时自上方（ABOVE）可见地缩小——下降的天花板在门廊顶部缓慢碾低，明亮的冷蓝色开口从上方（非两侧）压缩高度。光晕（halation）从不断缩小的孔径流布、横跨镜头伸展。粉尘从上方成稳定浪涌掠过门廊落下，个别颗粒在冷蓝色光前作为焦外光（bokeh）发光。下落的缓慢使它更令人不寒而栗，而非反之——运动本身没有急切，只有必然。摄影机把门廊收入画面、静止，以受控的张力观察这威胁。

无音乐。仅音效——天花板下落时石怼石碾磨的缓慢深沉轰鸣从上方主导、轰鸣比别的镜头更响因为我们更贴近门廊威胁、下降天花板与门廊顶部相接处粉尘与小石碎块成稳定浪涌下落、门廊之外冷蓝色光发出微弱的冷色嗡鸣或共振、身后走廊某处远处火把噼啪。
