## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, trap activation sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: cinematic anamorphic 2.39:1, long-lens telephoto cinematography, shallow depth of field, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from torch flames, deep blacks with halated warm orange highlights, atmospheric dust haze. Heavily desaturated palette. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide, low ceiling. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse. Ahead — distant glowing gateway. Lit iron torches on both walls. Ten stone ports embedded in the walls (five left, five right), currently sealed.

CHARACTERS:
— Rain (<<<image_1>>>): silver-blonde hair, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm, glowing tablet in hand.
— Jax (<<<image_2>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty.
— Roco (<<<image_3>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Long-lens medium close-up of Rain walking forward, three-quarter angle. Shallow focus on her face. Behind her, the corridor dissolves into orange torch bokeh. She glances back over her shoulder, opens her mouth to say something. From off-frame behind, Roco shouts urgently: "Rein!" Her expression freezes mid-word.

Shot 2 (4–8s): Extreme close-up at floor level, camera inches from a single stone tile. Rain's boot descends from above and lands on it. The tile depresses one centimeter with a dull CLICK. Dust sifts from the seam. Hold still. A deep mechanical groan begins inside the walls.

Shot 3 (8–11s): Extreme close-up of one stone wall port. The carved cover GRINDS open with a heavy mechanical clack, dust exploding outward in a thick plume, revealing a dark hollow aperture. From off-frame in all directions, the simultaneous clacks of nine more ports opening at once.

Shot 4 (11–13s): Long-lens close-up of Roco mid-scream, voice cracking: "DOWN!" Hard torch light slashes across his face. He launches forward into frame.

Shot 5 (13–15s): Long-lens close-up of Rain. Roco's body CRASHES into her from off-frame, his shoulder slamming into her midsection, his arm wrapping around her as both bodies are driven down out of frame. As they fall, dozens of arrows EXPLODE from the wall ports — sharp wooden whooshes filling the air, motion-blur streaks slicing across frame.

no music. sfx only — slow footsteps echoing on wet stone, torches crackling, "Rein!" sharp warning, boot pressing on stone, dull CLICK of depressed tile, dust sifting, deep mechanical groan inside walls, ten stone covers grinding open simultaneously, "DOWN!" raw scream, body tackle impact, dozens of arrows whooshing through the air at full real-time speed.

狭窄的远古石走廊，机关触发段落，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：电影级变形宽银幕 2.39:1，长焦摄影，浅景深，来自火炬火焰的戏剧性明暗布光，深黑带带光晕的暖橙高光，大气尘雾。高度去饱和的调色。灵感来自 Snyder《Army of the Dead（活死人军团）》。

LOCATION（场景）：约两米宽的狭窄远古石走廊，低矮天花板。角色身后——新鲜的瓦砾塌落。前方——远处发光的门道。两侧墙上点着铁火炬。墙内嵌着十个石孔（左五、右五），目前封闭。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
— Rain（<<<image_1>>>）：银金色头发，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新伤口，灰色连帽夹克，蒙尘。腋下夹着悬浮滑板，手里是发光的平板电脑。
— Jax（<<<image_2>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，清晰眼镜，深色夹克，蒙尘。
— Roco（<<<image_3>>>）：深色凌乱头发，胡须，鼻上有红色绷带，深色夹克，蒙尘。腋下夹着悬浮滑板。

Shot 1（第 0–4 秒）：向前行走的 Rain 的长焦中近景，四分之三角度。浅焦落在她的脸。她身后，走廊溶成橙色火炬散景。她回头瞥了一眼，张嘴要说些什么。从身后画外，Roco 急切地喊：「Rein!」她的表情在话说到一半僵住。

Shot 2（第 4–8 秒）：地面高度的极特写，摄影机距一块石砖仅数英寸。Rain 的靴子从上方落下、踩在上面。石砖以一声闷响受压下沉一厘米。灰尘从缝间筛落。定住。墙内响起一声深沉的机械呻吟。

Shot 3（第 8–11 秒）：一处石墙石孔的极特写。雕凿的孔盖以沉重的机械咔嗒声磨绞张开（GRINDS open），灰尘向外炸开、化作浓稠烟柱，露出黑暗空心的孔口。从四面八方画外，另外九个石孔同时传来的咔嗒声。

Shot 4（第 11–13 秒）：Roco 呼叫中途的长焦特写，嗓音破碎：「DOWN!」硬朗火炬光斜切过他的脸。他纵身扑进画幅。

Shot 5（第 13–15 秒）：Rain 的长焦特写。Roco 的身体从画外猛撞进（CRASHES into）她，他的肩猛撞进她的腹部，他的臂环抱住她，两人身体一起被带出画幅。他们坠落时，数十支箭从墙面石孔炸出（EXPLODE）——尖锐的木制呼啸填满空气，运动模糊拖影划过画幅。
