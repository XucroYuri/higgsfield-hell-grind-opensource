## 原文 (source)

Two-shot sequence, 6 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur on impacts, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, sweat and blood streaking his dust-covered face, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.

Setting: ancient stone corridor lit by torches, wet stone floor. A massive stone slab descends from the ceiling above a stone gateway at the end, the gap below now ankle-height, almost sealed. Cold blue light beyond the gateway, warm torch light in the corridor.

Shot 1 (3.5s) — TIGHT HANDHELD CLOSE-UP ON HIS FACE, OPERATOR RUNNING WITH HIM:
The camera is inches from his face, body-mounted at chest level, breathing handheld feel, the operator chases him at his pace as he sprints toward the closing gateway. Telephoto lens, extreme shallow depth of field, the corridor behind him dissolved into streaks of warm torch bokeh racing past as they run. Frame trembles with running gait. We stay locked on his face — eyes wide and burning forward, pupils dilated, jaw clenched hard, nostrils flared with heavy breathing, veins standing out on his temple, a single line of sweat tracking through dust down his cheek. He bursts toward the threshold and launches into a horizontal dive — the camera drops with him into the leap, his face filling the frame mid-flight, his eyes never breaking forward focus. Pure physical determination.

Shot 2 (2.5s) — LOW REAR-ANGLE FROM BEHIND, BODY SLIDING UNDER THE SLAB:
Hard cut. Camera positioned low at floor level behind him, looking forward at his back as he slides on his stomach through the narrow gap under the descending slab. We see his back, his arms outstretched forward, his legs trailing, body parallel to the wet stone. The slab presses inches above his back. Beyond the gap, cold blue light spills from the safe side. The instant his feet clear the threshold, the slab crashes down to the floor with a massive stone-on-stone impact directly behind the camera, dust exploding forward in a thick wall, frame shaking violently then settling. His silhouette continues sliding into the cold blue light beyond.

Sounds: Shot 1 — ragged breathing at full intensity, dual running boots on wet stone, fabric whipping with motion, slab grinding ahead growing louder. Shot 2 — body sliding on slick wet floor, fabric scraping stone slab edge above, massive slab CRASH on stone behind camera, dust impact wave, then sudden silence broken only by the slide and breath continuing into the safe side.

Total: 6s / 2 shots / 16:9

## 中文对照 (zh)

双镜头序列、6 秒、16:9、写实摄影、厚重 35mm 胶片颗粒、撞击上运动模糊、无音乐。

参照图 1 中那名深色凌乱齐肩发、浅胡须、鼻上红绷带、汗与血划痕覆尘脸庞、深色连帽夹克的男子。

布景（Setting）：火炬照亮的古石廊、湿滑石板地面。一块巨大石门自尽头石制入口上方天花板降下、其下缝隙如今齐脚踝高、几乎封闭。入口彼侧冷蓝光、走廊中暖火炬光。

镜头 1（3.5s）——他面部的手紧特写、操机员随他奔跑（TIGHT HANDHELD CLOSE-UP, OPERATOR RUNNING WITH HIM）：
机位距他脸仅数寸、以胸前高度机身背负、呼吸式手持感、操机员以他步速追他、他正朝正在关闭的入口冲刺。远摄镜头、极端浅景深、他身后走廊溶解为奔跑冲刺而过的暖火把光斑流。画面随奔跑步态颤抖。我们锁在他脸上——双眼圆睁、向前燃烧、瞳孔放大、下颌死咬、鼻孔因粗重呼吸而张大、太阳穴青筋凸起、一线汗穿过尘土沿颊滑下。他冲向门槛、纵身成水平跳水——机位随他坠入这一跃、他的脸在飞行中途填满画面、双眼绝不离开前向焦点。纯粹的肉体决意。

镜头 2（2.5s）——身后低位后角、身体滑过门板下（LOW REAR-ANGLE FROM BEHIND, BODY SLIDING UNDER THE SLAB）：
硬切。机位低置他身后的地面位、朝前望他的背、他正俯卧滑过下落门板下那道窄缝。我们看到他的背、他前伸的双臂、拖行的双腿、与湿石平行的身体。门板在他背上数寸处压着。缝隙彼侧、冷蓝光自安全一侧泄出。他双脚刚越出门槛的一瞬、门板带着一声巨大石对石撞击直砸在机位正后的地面上、尘以厚墙向前爆开、画面剧烈晃动随即安定。他的剪影继续滑入彼侧的冷蓝光。

声音（Sounds）：镜头 1——全强度的粗糙呼吸、双跑步靴踏湿石、织物随动甩动、前方门板磨擦渐响。镜头 2——身体在湿滑地面滑行、织物刮蹭上方门板石缘、镜头后门板巨大石对石撞击（CRASH）、尘冲击波、随即骤然静默、只被滑行声与呼吸声打破、延续进入安全一侧。
