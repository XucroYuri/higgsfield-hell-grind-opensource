## 原文 (source)

Two-shot sequence, 6 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur on impacts, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, sweat and blood streaking his dust-covered face, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.

Setting: ancient stone corridor lit by torches, wet stone floor. A massive stone slab descends from the ceiling above a stone gateway at the end, the gap below now reduced to a narrow horizontal slit at knee height — barely large enough to pass through. Cold blue light spills through the slit from the safe side beyond, mixing with the warm orange torch light of the corridor.

Shot 1 (3s) — TIGHT HANDHELD CLOSE-UP ON ROCO'S FACE, OPERATOR RUNNING WITH HIM:
The camera is body-mounted at chest level inches from his face, breathing handheld feel, the operator chases him at his pace as he sprints the last meters toward the gateway. Telephoto lens, extreme shallow depth of field, the corridor behind him dissolved into streaks of warm torch bokeh racing past. Frame trembles with running gait. We stay locked on his face — eyes wide and burning forward, pupils dilated, jaw clenched hard, nostrils flared, veins standing on his temple, sweat tracking through dust down his cheek. He plants his foot hard at the threshold and LEAPS forward into the air, body launching through the gap. The camera follows the leap, his face stays in frame mid-flight as he goes airborne, eyes never breaking forward focus.

Shot 2 (3s) — LOW REAR-WIDE FROM SAFE SIDE, BODY VAULTING THROUGH THE SLIT:
Hard cut. Camera positioned low at floor level on the safe side beyond the gateway, looking back toward the corridor through the narrow horizontal slit below the descending slab. The man's body LAUNCHES through the slit horizontally — body crouched compact, arms forward, knees tucked — passing through the narrow opening with inches of clearance above his back and below his belly. He hits the safe-side floor in a forward roll, body tumbling once across the stone, rolling out of the roll into a low crouch. The instant his feet clear the threshold, the slab CRASHES down to the floor with a massive stone-on-stone impact directly behind him, dust exploding outward in a thick wave that washes forward over the camera. Frame shakes violently with the impact, then settles. He kneels gasping in the cold blue light, his back to the sealed gateway behind him.

Sounds: Shot 1 — ragged sprint breathing at full intensity, running boots pounding wet stone, fabric whipping with motion, slab grinding ahead growing louder, the leap takeoff. Shot 2 — body passing through the air with whoosh, body impact on stone with hard slap, tumbling roll on stone, massive slab CRASH on stone behind him, dust impact wave, then sudden silence broken only by his ragged exhausted breathing in the new space.

## 中文对照 (zh)

双镜头序列，6 秒，16:9，写实摄影（photorealistic），厚重 35mm 胶片颗粒，冲击处运动模糊，无音乐。

参照图 1 中那名男子——深色凌乱披肩发、一抹浅胡须、鼻上红绷带、汗与血纵横划过他覆尘的脸、深色连帽夹克。

环境：古老石质走廊被火把照亮、湿滑石地面。一块巨大石板从尽头石质门廊上方天花板下落，此时下方缝隙已缩成一道膝盖高的狭窄水平窄缝——勉强够一个人钻过。冷蓝色光从安全侧穿过窄缝渗入、与走廊的暖橙火把光混合。

镜头 1（3 秒）——ROCO 面部的紧密手持特写，操机员与他一同奔跑：
摄影机以胸高身体固定，距他脸仅数英寸、带呼吸式手持感，他朝门廊冲刺最后几米时操机员以他的步速追逐他。远摄镜头、极浅景深、他身后走廊消散为擦身掠过的暖火把焦外条纹。画面随奔跑步态颤抖。我们保持锁定在他脸上——双眼圆睁灼灼向前、瞳孔放亮、下颌紧咬、鼻孔张开、太阳穴青筋暴起、汗痕穿过尘土顺颊而下。他在门槛处猛地踏地、向前跃（LEAPS）入空中、身体穿过缝隙发射而出。摄影机跟随这一跃，他腾空时脸仍留在画面中段，目光从不脱离向前的焦点。

镜头 2（3 秒）——来自安全侧的贴地后广角，身体跃穿窄缝：
硬切。摄影机低置于门廊另一侧安全侧的地面高度，透过下落石板下方的狭窄水平窄缝向走廊回看。男人的身体水平地发射（LAUNCHES）穿过窄缝——身体蜷缩紧凑、双臂前伸、双膝收拢——以背上方与腹下方各剩数英寸的间隙穿过狭窄开口。他以一个前滚翻落在安全侧地面，身体在石上翻滚一圈、从翻滚中滚起为低位蹲伏。他双脚一脱离门槛，石板便在他正后方带着一次石怼石的重击砸到（CRASHES）地面，粉尘向外爆炸成一道浓密的浪、向前覆盖在摄影机上。画面随冲击剧烈颤抖、随即安定。他在冷蓝色光中跪着喘息，背对身后已封死的门廊。

声音：镜头 1——满强度的沙哑冲刺呼吸、奔跑靴子重踏湿石、织物随动作噼啪甩动、前方石板碾磨声渐响、起跳。镜头 2——身体带「嗖」声穿空而过、身体撞石带硬脆的啪击、在石上翻滚、身后巨板砸石的重击、粉尘冲击浪、随即蓦然寂静、只被他在新空间里沙哑疲惫的呼吸打破。
