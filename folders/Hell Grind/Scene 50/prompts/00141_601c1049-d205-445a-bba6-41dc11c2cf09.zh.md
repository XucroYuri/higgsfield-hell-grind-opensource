## 原文 (source)

Single shot in an ancient stone corridor or chamber, 4 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, fresh cut on her temple, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.

Setting: a stone passage on the safer side of a doorway, dust haze, cold blue ambient light from above mixed with warm bokeh from somewhere behind. Wet stone floor.

Action: Handheld tracking shot at chest height as the woman runs forward into frame from behind camera, pushing through the threshold into the chamber. She passes the camera and continues a few steps forward, breathing hard, ragged. Then she stops and TURNS sharply on her heel, body rotating to face back the way she came, hair flying with the motion. She stares back toward the threshold off-frame behind camera, eyes wide, breath catching, face tense — searching for something or someone. Camera follows her motion, settles on her face as she holds the look back, intimate and trembling.

Camera: handheld, 85mm telephoto lens, shallow depth of field, the chamber dissolving into bokeh behind her. Camera moves with her motion, then settles when she turns. Trembles with her breath.

Sounds: her running boots thudding onto stone as she enters, fabric whipping, her ragged exhausted breathing. The boots scuff to a halt and she pivots on stone with a sharp scrape. A held breath as she turns. Distant rumble of stone from off-frame behind camera. No music.



## 中文对照 (zh)

古老石质走廊或石厅中的单镜头，4 秒，16:9，写实摄影，厚重 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，无配乐。

参照图片 1（Image 1）中那名银色金发、太阳穴新割口、灰色连帽夹克的女子。

Setting（环境）：门洞相对安全侧的一处石质通道，尘土薄雾，自上方来的冷蓝环境光与身后某处的暖散景混合。湿滑石地。

Action（动作）：齐胸高度的手持追踪镜头，女子自摄影机身后向前跑入画面，穿过门槛进入石厅。她越过摄影机，继续向前几步，呼吸沉重、粗重。随即她停下，以脚后跟锐利转身，身体旋转回望向自己来路，头发随动作飞扬。她朝摄影机方向身后画外的门槛往回瞪视，眼睛大睁，呼吸屏住，脸面紧绷——搜寻某物或某人。摄影机跟随她的动作，落定在她保持回望时脸上，亲昵而颤抖。

Camera（摄影机）：手持，85mm 长焦镜头，浅景深，她身后的石厅消解进散景。摄影机随她动作移动，她转身时落定。随她呼吸颤抖。

Sounds（声音）：她进入时奔跑靴子踏击石头的声响、织物飘甩、她处死力竭的呼吸。靴子摩擦刹停，她以一声锐利刮擦在石上转身。她转身时屏住的呼吸。摄影机方向身后画外传来的远处石鸣。无配乐。
