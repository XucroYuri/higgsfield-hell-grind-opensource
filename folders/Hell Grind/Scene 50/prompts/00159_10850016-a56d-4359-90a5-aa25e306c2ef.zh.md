## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, opening sequence with awakening torches and team briefing, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography in the visual language of Snyder's Army of the Dead — 85mm telephoto lens, shallow depth of field, extreme bokeh with soft circular highlights, foreground and background dissolving into creamy out-of-focus haze, dynamic focus pulls between subjects. Halation bleeding from torch flames. Heavily desaturated palette dominated by deep amber-brown earth tones, isolated warm orange torch accents in pure black surrounding darkness, faint cold blue glow from Rain's tablet on her face. Dramatic chiaroscuro — deep crushed blacks dominating most of the frame, faces partially in shadow, single-source torch motivation, light falling off rapidly into darkness. Cinematic widescreen 16:9.

SCENE CONTEXT: this scene begins seconds after a violent explosion collapsed the passage above, sending the three characters falling/sliding down through the rubble into this corridor below. As they appear, the corridor itself RESPONDS — the wall-mounted torches along its length IGNITE one by one in sequence, deep into the corridor, as if the place is waking up to the presence of intruders.

LOCATION: a long ancient underground stone corridor with low rough-hewn stone ceiling. Walls covered in horizontal bands of carved ancient script — rows of weathered glyph-letters running along the entire length of both walls. Iron torch brackets mounted at angle on the walls every few meters, holding short flame-torches. Flagstone floor of irregular cracked stone slabs. Small carved stone tablets and rubble propped against the bases of the walls. BEHIND the characters: a large fresh rubble pile of broken stone blocking the passage they came from, dust still settling. AHEAD into the distance: the corridor stretches into deep blackness, no visible end.

CHARACTERS (just survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_1>>>): young woman, silver-blonde shoulder-length hair, small scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket over black top, dark cargo pants, dust on clothes. Hoverboard tucked under one arm, deactivated. Holding a small handheld tablet device in her other hand, screen casting cold blue light up onto her face.
— Jax (<<<image_2>>>): young man, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked. Curious, drawn to the carvings.
— Roco (<<<image_3>>>): young man, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage across bridge of nose, dark jacket, dust-covered. Hoverboard tucked under arm, deactivated. Hangs close to Jax.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Long-lens static shot looking down the length of the corridor into deep blackness, telephoto compression flattening the corridor. The corridor is dark — only a hint of foreground stone visible. The FIRST iron torch nearest camera IGNITES with a soft whump, flame blooming into a halated orange flare on the carved wall, illuminating a section of the script glyphs. The NEXT torch ignites deeper down the corridor, then the next, then the next — successive ignitions traveling away from camera into the depth, each one revealing another segment of carved wall and stone floor. Camera holds static, focus subtly shifting through the depth as new lights appear. By the end of the shot, a chain of warm flickering torches recedes into the corridor.

Shot 2 (4–8s): Hard cut to long-lens tight close-up of Rain's face from a low three-quarter angle. Only her eyes in razor-sharp focus, cheekbones falling off into soft focus. Cold blue light from her tablet (held below frame) glows up onto her face from below, mixing with warm orange torch light from behind, creating dual-tone contrast across her features. The corridor behind her dissolves into a wash of orange torch bokeh and floating dust. Her eyes flick down to the tablet, then back up. She says with quiet authority: "Final stretch. Forty feet." Her breath fogs faintly in the cold air, drifting through the bokeh.

Shot 3 (8–11s): Hard cut to extreme close-up of Jax's hand approaching the carved script on the wall. Only his fingertips at the moment of contact in sharp focus, the rest dissolving into soft creamy bokeh. The nearest torch flickers warm halated light across the carved letters, individual dust motes glowing as bokeh balls in the beam. His fingers slide across the surface, dust shifting beneath them. Pull focus from the stone surface to his eye behind glasses — wide, fascinated. Off-frame his voice, slightly rough from inhaled dust: "Yo. These markings. Look at 'em."

Shot 4 (11–15s): Hard cut to long-lens medium close-up of Rain in profile. Her face in sharp focus, the corridor ahead and behind dissolved into orange bokeh of distant torch flames. She lowers the tablet slightly, eyes narrowing. Blue tablet glow fades from her face, leaving only warm torch light from one side. She turns her head slightly, scanning the corridor. Her instinct kicks in. She says, lower and more urgent: "Guys. Something's off." A beat. Eyes flick to where Jax is off-frame. "Stay close." The line lands quiet but heavy. Hold on her face. Cut.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的古老石廊、开篇段落——苏醒的火炬与小队简报，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：以 Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）的视觉语言手持电影拍摄——85mm 远摄镜头、浅景深、带柔和圆形高光的极散景、前景与背景溶解进奶油般失焦雾霭、主体之间动态拉焦。火炬火焰漏出的光晕（halation）。高度去饱和的调色板、由深琥珀棕土地色调主导、在纯粹黑色的周围黑暗中孤立暖橙火炬点缀、Rain 脸上平板的隐约冷蓝光。戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）——深重碾碎的黑色主导画面大半、面部部分入影、单一火炬光源动机、光线迅速衰减入黑暗。电影宽银幕 16:9。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：本场景始于一次猛烈爆炸塌陷上方通道、把三名角色抛坠 / 下滑穿过废墟、落进下方这条走廊、几秒钟之后。他们出现时、走廊本身「回应」——沿其长度的墙挂火炬一支支按序点燃、深入走廊、仿佛这地方正在对外来者的到来苏醒。

LOCATION（场地）：一条长长的古老地下石廊、低矮粗凿石顶。墙壁覆满水平带状的古老刻写——一排排饱经风化的符文字母沿两壁全长延伸。铁制火炬托架自墙壁以斜角每隔几米装设、捧着短焰火炬。石板地面为不规则开裂石板。小块雕花石板与废墟斜靠在墙壁基座上。角色身后：一大堆新鲜碎石块堵住他们来时的通道、灰尘仍在沉降。前方远处：走廊伸进深黑、不见尽头。

CHARACTERS（角色）（刚熬过一次爆破、蒙尘）：
— Rain（<<<image_1>>>）：年轻女子、银金齐肩长发、眼侧小疤、太阳穴新伤口、黑上衣外罩灰色连帽夹克、深色工装裤、衣服带尘。一支滑板夹在一条臂下、已关闭。另一只手握着一台小型手持平板装置、屏幕把冷蓝光向上照在她脸上。
— Jax（<<<image_2>>>）：年轻男子、漂白金黄板寸头、深肤色、清晰护目镜、深色连帽夹克、蒙尘。好奇、被雕刻吸引。
— Roco（<<<image_3>>>）：年轻男子、深色凌乱齐肩长发、淡淡胡子、鼻梁上红绷带、深色夹克、蒙尘。滑板夹在腋下、已关闭。守在 Jax 附近。

Shot 1（0–4s）：长焦静态镜头沿走廊长度望向深黑、远摄压缩压平走廊。走廊黑暗——只有一点前景石头可见。距镜头最近的第一支铁制火炬带一声柔和「轰」点燃、火焰在刻花墙上绽成一团带光晕橙色光爆、照亮一段符文刻写。下一支火炬在更深处点燃、再下一支、再下一支——逐次点燃远离镜头深入纵深、每一支揭示又一段刻花墙与石地板。摄影机保持静态、随着新灯光出现、对焦在纵深中微妙移动。到镜头结束、一串温暖闪烁的火炬退入走廊。

Shot 2（4–8s）：硬切到 Rain 脸部长焦紧特写、低四分之三角度。只有她的双眼处于刺刀般锐利对焦、颧骨滑入柔焦。她平板（握在画下）的冷蓝光从下方照上她脸、与身后暖橙火炬光混合、在她的五官上制造双色调对比。她身后走廊溶解成橙色火炬散景与漂浮尘埃的洪流。她的目光闪下平板、又抬起。她以安静的命令腔说：「Final stretch. Forty feet.」（最后一程，四十英尺。）她的呼吸在冷空气中隐约起雾、飘过散景。

Shot 3（8–11s）：硬切到 Jax 手趋近墙上刻写文本的极特写。只有接触瞬间的指尖处于锐利对焦、其余溶解进柔软奶油散景。最近的火炬把温暖的带光晕光闪在刻写字母上、个别尘埃微粒在光束中作散景光球发光。他的手指滑过表面、尘土在指尖下移动。对焦从石面拉到他眼镜后的眼睛——圆睁、着迷。画外他的声音、因吸入灰尘而略显粗哑：「Yo. These markings. Look at 'em.」（哟。这些符号。看这些。）

Shot 4（11–15s）：硬切到 Rain 侧影的长焦中近景。她的脸锐利对焦、前方与后方走廊溶解进远处火炬火焰的橙色散景。她略微放下平板、眯起眼。蓝色平板光从脸上淡去、只留下一侧的暖火炬光。她微微转头、扫视走廊。她的本能在运作。她说、更低、更急迫：「Guys. Something's off.」（各位。有哪里不对劲。）一顿。目光闪向画外的 Jax。「Stay close.」（跟紧了。）这句台词落地安静而沉重。停在她的脸上。切。

无配乐。仅环境音效（SFX only）——角色身后废墟堆的沉降废墟与淌流石尘、远处上方的二次塌落远轰隆、爆炸余波而来的隐约耳鸣、火炬逐支点燃的顺序「轰」声（贯穿 Shot 1 的五个清晰点燃）、带柔和嘶吼声的噼啪火焰、Rain 平板的隐约电子嗡响、她指尖轻敲平板屏幕、Rain 带长廊深沉石壁混响的「Final stretch. Forty feet.」、手指拂过雕花石带砂砾、Jax 的「Yo. These markings. Look at 'em.」（画外）、Rain 带安静急迫的「Guys. Something's off. Stay close.」、前方某处远处的滴水。
