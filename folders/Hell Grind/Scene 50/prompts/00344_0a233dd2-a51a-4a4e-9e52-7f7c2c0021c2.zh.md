## 原文 (source)

A continuous handheld long-take medium shot of a young man transforming his right arm into crystalline armor while running to save a friend dangling from a stone pit, the friend slipping and falling, the man shattering his crystal arm to catch him in mid-air with bare flesh in an extreme close-up moment, then immediately launching him toward a closing exit. 16 seconds, single continuous shot, no cuts, in the visual language of Emmanuel Lubezki and Greig Fraser.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Roco is racing to save his friend Jax who has fallen into a deadly trap pit in an ancient stone corridor and is barely holding on by his fingertips, on the verge of falling. Roco has the ability to transform his right arm into crystalline armor — sharp jagged blood-red crystal formations growing through his skin and bursting through the fabric of his right jacket sleeve, with internal red glow. As Roco sprints toward the pit, he TRANSFORMS his right arm mid-stride — the crystal punches through his right jacket sleeve, tearing holes in the dark fabric but the sleeve itself remains on his arm, the crystal protruding through the rips. He arrives, drops to his knee, extends the armored arm down to Jax just as Jax's grip fails. They clasp briefly but Jax's flesh fingers can't grip the rough faceted crystal — he SLIPS off entirely, his body FALLING backward into the pit toward stone spikes below. In a single instinctive moment, Roco SHATTERS the crystal armor — the sharp shards explode away leaving only his bare flesh arm beneath, but the torn jacket sleeve REMAINS on his arm, ragged and ripped where the crystal had punched through. His bare flesh hand SHOOTS through the ripped sleeve and into the pit, catching Jax's wrist in mid-fall. The catch is shown in extreme close-up — flesh-on-flesh contact, Roco's lighter-toned hand gripping Jax's darker wrist. Roco then HEAVES him out of the pit and immediately launches Jax forward across the wet stone floor toward the closing gateway — no recovery time.

All in one continuous motion. Camera shake alive but never jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Focus pulls happen organically. Lens imperfections: visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from torch flames, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Color palette progression: warm-only at start, warm + emerging red glow as the right arm transforms (mid-shot), then warm + full red glow until crystal shatters, then back to warm-only after the shatter.
