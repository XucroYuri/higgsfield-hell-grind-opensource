## 原文 (source)

Handheld close-up looking down into a stone pit at a young man's hands gripping the edge with terror, 3 seconds.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Jax has just tried to grip the crystalline armored hand of his friend Roco to be pulled out of a deadly pit, but the rough faceted crystal surface gave him nothing to hold onto — his fingers slipped off the sharp edges, and he fell back, catching himself again on the pit edge with his fingertips. Now he hangs from the pit edge by his fingertips alone, terrified, his hands raw from the crystal contact, looking up at Roco above for the next chance.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — alive in operator's hands, intimately close. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on the lens, halation from torch flames above, chromatic aberration on edges, occasional focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with warm orange torch tones from above and deep shadow of the pit. Dramatic chiaroscuro from torches above the pit only. Inspired by the cinematography of Emmanuel Lubezki and Greig Fraser.

LOCATION: inside a dark stone pit two meters deep with rough hewn walls dropping into darkness below. Stone spikes barely visible at the bottom far below. The pit edge above lit by warm torch light from the corridor.

CHARACTER: Jax, young man, mid-twenties, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked face streaked with sweat and fear. Hanging from the pit edge by his fingertips, body dangling into the darkness below, arms fully extended above him, shoulders hyperextended.

ACTION: handheld camera positioned ABOVE the pit looking steeply DOWN at Jax — high angle close-up framing his face and his hands gripping the stone lip above. Warm torch light from the corridor above casts hard angular light onto his face from above. Camera trembles with subtle shake, the operator above the pit witnessing the desperation.

(0–1s): Jax has just slipped off the crystal arm and caught himself on the stone lip below. His hands are SLAPPED against the rough stone, fingertips white-knuckled gripping the edge. His face contorts in pure terror — eyes bulging, mouth open gasping, throat strained. The realization: the crystal hand couldn't save him.

(1–2s): His arms shake violently with strain, his weight pulling him down. His feet kick against the dark pit walls below trying to find purchase, finding none. His body SWINGS from his fingertips. His fingertips begin to SLIP slightly on the wet stone — he fights to maintain grip, regripping a fraction lower than before, knuckles bleeding from the rough stone.

(2–3s): He throws his head back to look UP toward camera (toward where Roco is above), eyes wide with full bug-eyed terror, his bottom lip trembling. He gasps — a desperate sound, half-scream half-sob. He tries to call out but his voice fails. His arms shake harder. Then his face HARDENS — he's not giving up, he's holding on.

no music. sfx only — Jax's hands SLAPPING the stone lip with grip impact, fingers grinding against rough wet stone, panicked rapid shallow breathing escalating, his arm muscles trembling audibly with strain, his boots kicking against pit walls below trying to find a foothold, finding none, his body shifting against the pit wall with cloth scraping, his desperate gasping sob, dust falling past camera into the darkness below catching torch light, distant deep slow grinding rumble of slab descending from above somewhere in the corridor, faint torch crackling from above.

## 中文对照 (zh)

手持特写俯视石坑——一个年轻男子的手带恐惧死抓边缘，3 秒。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：一名叫 Jax 的年轻男子刚才试图抓住他朋友 Roco 的晶体甲胄手、让后者把他拉出致命陷阱坑，但粗糙的多面晶体表面让他无从握持——他的手指从锐利边缘滑脱、他跌回去、再次仅凭指尖在坑沿接住自己。现在他仅凭指尖挂在坑沿、惊恐万分、双手因晶体接触而磨破、抬头仰望上方、等待下一次机会。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持电影摄影、带强烈而受控的摄影机颤抖——在操机员手中活着、亲密地近。长焦远景电影拍摄、浅景深、f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜头上可见尘埃、上方火炬火焰光晕（halation）、边缘色差、偶发对焦漂移、淡淡的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和的调色板、带上方暖橙火炬色调与坑的深影。仅由坑上方的火炬产生的戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）。参考 Emmanuel Lubezki 与 Greig Fraser 的电影摄影。

LOCATION（场地）：一个两米深、粗凿井壁沉入下方黑暗的暗色石坑内部。底部深处处几不可见的石尖。上方坑沿被走廊的暖火炬光照亮。

CHARACTER（角色）：Jax、年轻男子、二十多岁、漂白金黄板寸头、深肤色、清晰护目镜歪斜、深色连帽夹克、蒙尘的脸带汗痕与恐惧。仅凭指尖挂在坑沿、身体吊垂进下方黑暗、双臂在他上方完全伸展、肩部过度伸展。

ACTION（动作）：手持摄影机位于坑「上方」、陡峻地「俯视」Jax——高角度特写取景他的脸与他死抓上方石沿的手。上方走廊的暖火炬光从他上方投下硬朗棱角的光、打在他脸上。摄影机以微妙颤抖发颤、坑上的操机员目击着这绝望。

(0–1s)：Jax 刚滑脱晶体臂、在下方坑沿接住自己。他的双手「啪」地拍贴粗糙石头、指尖发白死抓边缘。他的脸因纯粹恐惧而扭曲——眼珠鼓出、嘴巴张开喘气、喉咙绷紧。领悟：那只晶体手救不了他。

(1–2s)：他的手臂因用力而暴烈颤抖、体重把他往下拽。他的脚朝下方暗色坑壁蹬打、试图找到踏脚、却无处可落。他的身体从指尖「荡」起。他的指尖开始在湿滑石头上微微「滑」——他奋力维持抓握、在比之前低一点的位置重新抓住、指节被粗糙石头磨出血。

(2–3s)：他把头仰起、朝上望向镜头（望 Roco 上方的位置）、眼珠瞪圆、十足瞳孔放大的恐惧、下唇颤抖。他倒吸一口气——一声绝望的声响、一半尖叫一半啜泣。他试图喊叫、但嗓子失声。他的手臂抖得更狠。随即他的脸「硬化」——他没有放弃、他在坚持。

无配乐。仅环境音效（SFX only）——Jax 的双手「啪」地拍上石沿的抓握撞击、手指磨刮粗糙湿滑石头、惊惶的快速浅呼吸不断升级、他手臂肌肉随用力可闻地颤抖、他的靴子朝下方坑壁蹬打试图找踏脚、却无处可落、他的身体贴坑壁磨蹭带布料刮擦、他绝望的倒吸啜泣、灰尘从镜头旁落入下方黑暗、捕捉火炬光、走廊某处上方远处下降石板的深沉缓慢碾磨轰隆、上方隐约火炬噼啪。
