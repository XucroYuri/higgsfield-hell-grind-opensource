## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, advancement and trap activation sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: shot in the visual language of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead — long lens cinematography (85mm to 135mm equivalent), shallow depth of field at f/1.2 aperture, extreme bokeh with soft circular highlights, foreground and background dissolving into creamy out-of-focus haze, only razor-thin slices of frame in sharp focus, dynamic focus pulls between subjects. Heavy lens characteristics: subtle barrel distortion in close shots, slight chromatic aberration at high-contrast edges, halation bleeding from torch flames into surrounding darkness, warm orange light blooming organically. Compressed perspective from telephoto lens flattens corridor depth. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm pops from fire. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — deep crushed blacks, faces partially in shadow, single-source torch motivation, light falling off rapidly into darkness. Cinematic anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1.

SCENE CONTEXT: continuing immediately from Rain's "Stay close" warning. The team walks forward through the corridor, spaced apart — Rain leads several meters ahead, Jax in the middle, Roco at the rear. Rain glances back over her shoulder, beginning to say something. Roco's eye catches a worn stone tile under Rain's descending foot — he shouts "Rein!" as a desperate warning, but her foot is already committed. The boot lands, the tile depresses, mechanical groan begins in the walls. Ten stone ports along both side walls grind open simultaneously. Roco shouts "DOWN!" — Jax drops on his own, hitting the floor hard. Roco SPRINTS forward and tackles Rain, throwing his body over hers to shield her as the first wave of arrows fires from the wall ports.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide, ceiling two and a half meters. BEHIND — fresh rubble collapse from blast. AHEAD — open carved stone gateway with cold blue light. Iron torches lit along both walls. Ten ancient stone ports embedded in both side walls (five left, five right).

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars on both walls, lit iron torches every few meters, thick volumetric dust haze.

Characters (recently survived a blast, dust-covered, walking spaced apart):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, black top, dark cargo pants, dusty. Hoverboard tucked under one arm. Tablet glowing dim cold blue in her other hand. Walking ahead.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket, dusty. Walking in the middle.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red nose bandage, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm. At the rear, eyes scanning.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Long-lens static low-angle shot looking down the corridor from Roco's position, the team strung out ahead — Rain in the deep distance as a small silhouette in sharp focus thanks to telephoto compression, Jax in the middle distance dissolving into soft bokeh, Roco's shoulder in extreme out-of-focus foreground. Telephoto compression flattens depth so all three appear stacked at different focal planes. Torches lining both walls render as massive halated bokeh orbs receding into perspective. Atmospheric haze thick. They walk forward at a slow tense pace, spacing clearly readable. Footsteps from three distances, breathing.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Hard cut to long-lens medium shot of Rain in three-quarter angle, walking forward ahead of the others. Razor-thin focus on her face, the corridor behind her dissolved into wash of orange torch bokeh. She glances back over her shoulder — focus subtly racks as her face turns slightly. She begins to open her mouth, about to call back to the team. Her foot is mid-step beneath her in completely soft foreground bokeh, descending toward the floor unseen.

Shot 3 (6–8s): Hard cut to long-lens medium close-up of Roco's face from a three-quarter angle. Hard torch source carving angular halated light across his features, half his face lost in coal-black shadow. His eyes are scanning the corridor floor ahead — focus pulls past his face to the deep background where Rain's foot is descending toward a worn stone tile in soft focus. Roco's eyes lock onto it. Recognition floods his face. He shouts, voice sharp and desperate: "Rein!" The warning is the very last second possible.

Shot 4 (8–10.5s): EXTREME CLOSE-UP — long-lens macro shot at floor level, the camera mere inches from a single worn stone tile, framed full-bleed in 2.39:1 anamorphic. Razor-thin focus on the tile surface, every crack and chip rendered with sharp detail, surrounding tiles dissolving into deep creamy bokeh. Rain's boot descends from the upper half of frame in soft halated focus and lands. The tile DEPRESSES one centimeter under her weight with a faint dull CLICK. Dust SIFTS from the seam between stones, individual particles glowing as halated bokeh in passing torch light. Hold. From within the walls, faintly, a deep mechanical groan begins to build.

Shot 5 (10.5–12.5s): EXTREME CLOSE-UP — long-lens macro shot of one stone wall port in maximum detail, the camera mere inches from the carved stone surface. Razor-thin focus on the seam of the port cover, deep weathered glyph carvings around it falling off into creamy bokeh. The carved cover GRINDS open, sliding aside with a heavy mechanical clack — dust EXPLODES from the seam in a thick visible plume, individual particles glowing as halated bokeh in torch light. The dark hollow aperture inside the stone is revealed. Behind in soft bokeh, the other ports along this wall and on the opposite wall open in unison, registered as flickers of dust eruption — nine more openings, all simultaneous.

Shot 6 (12.5–13.5s): Snap cut to long-lens extreme close-up of Roco — full face mid-scream, voice raw and cracking: "DOWN!" His body launches forward, arm reaching — but NOT toward Jax. He lunges PAST Jax (who is already dropping on his own), sprinting forward toward Rain in the deep background.

Shot 7 (13.5–15s): Long-lens low-angle at floor level, camera looking down the corridor. Multiple actions happen simultaneously in the same frame at f/1.2 with shifting focus — Jax DROPS on his own in mid-foreground, hitting the floor hard with arms over his head; Roco is mid-sprint past Jax, body launching through the air toward Rain ahead; Rain stands frozen with foot still on the depressed tile, beginning to react too late. Roco reaches Rain — TACKLES her by the waist, his body wrapping over hers as they BOTH crash to the floor, Roco's body shielding her from above, his back exposed to the corridor. As they hit, the FIRST WAVE of arrows EXPLODES from the wall ports — DOZENS of arrows streaking horizontally across the corridor in both directions simultaneously, sharp wooden whooshes filling the audio, motion blur streaks slicing through the bokeh in every plane of depth. Hold.

no music. sfx only — slow deliberate footsteps on wet stone with deep stone reverb (spaced from three distances), faint electronic hum from tablet, torches crackling, controlled heavy breathing, distant drip from ahead, faint settle of debris from rubble behind, Rain beginning to inhale to speak (cut off by the click), Roco's sharp desperate "Rein!" with stone reverb, boot pressing on stone, faint dull CLICK of depressed tile, dust sifting from seam, deep mechanical groan beginning within walls, one ancient stone cover grinding open in foreground with heavy mechanical clack and explosive dust burst, nine more grinding open in unison, Roco's "DOWN!" raw and cracking, Jax's body hitting wet stone hard, Roco's running footsteps sprinting forward, body tackle impact as Roco hits Rain, both crashing to the floor together, hoverboards clattering, tablet skidding, dozens of arrows whooshing simultaneously through the air at full real-time speed in overlapping waves.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄古老石廊，推进与陷阱激活序列，15 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：以 Zack Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）的视觉语言拍摄——长焦镜头摄影（85mm 至 135mm 等效），f/1.2 光圈浅景深，极端焦外光点（bokeh）带柔和的圆形高光，前景与背景消散为奶咖啡状失焦雾霭，只有剃刀般细薄的帧切片处于锐焦，主体间动态拉焦。沉重的镜头特性：近距离镜头的微妙桶形畸变、高对比边缘的轻微色差、火把火焰渗入周围黑暗的光晕（halation）、暖橙光有机化晕开。长焦镜头带来的压缩透视压平了走廊纵深。高度去饱和的调色板，从火中孤立出几个暖色小爆炸。戏剧性的明暗对照照明（chiaroscuro）——压死深黑、面部局部入影、单一火把光源动机、光迅速衰减入黑暗。电影变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

场景情境（SCENE CONTEXT）：紧接 Rain「Stay close（别走散）」的警告。队伍向前穿过走廊，彼此拉开间距——Rain 在前方数米领头，Jax 居中，Roco 殿后。Rain 回头越过肩看了一眼，正要开口说什么。Roco 的目光捕捉到 Rain 落下的脚下有一块磨蚀的石板——他嘶吼出「Rein!」作为绝望的警告，但她的脚已经踏实。靴子落地，石板下陷，墙内开始传来机械的呻吟。两侧墙上的十个石口同时碾磨着打开。Roco 吼出「DOWN（趴下）！」——Jax 自己伏下，重重砸到地面。Roco 向前猛冲，扑倒（tackles）Rain，把身体盖在她身上护住她，就在第一波箭矢从墙口射出之时。

场景几何（LOCATION GEOMETRY）：狭窄石廊，约两米宽，天花板两米半。身后——爆炸造成的碎裂废墟塌落。前方——一座敞开的雕石门廊，透出冷蓝色光。两侧墙上点燃铁火把。两面侧壁内嵌十个古老石口（左五、右五）。

环境（Setting）：湿滑石地面，两侧墙面符文覆盖的石柱，每隔几米点燃铁火把，浓重的体积粉尘雾霭。

角色（刚爆炸幸存、覆尘、拉开间距行走）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银白金色头发凌乱，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新割伤，灰色连帽夹克，黑上衣，深色工装裤，覆尘。悬浮滑板夹在一臂之下。另一只手握着辉光暗淡冷蓝色的平板。走在前方。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，透明防护眼镜，深色连帽夹克，覆尘。走在中间。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱发，胡须，红色鼻部绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。悬浮滑板在臂下。在殿后，眼睛扫视。

镜头 1（Shot 1，0–3s）：长焦静态低角度镜头，从 Roco 的位置沿走廊向前看，队伍在前方拉成一串——Rain 在深远距离处成为一个小的剪影，得益于长焦压缩而锐焦，Jax 在中距离处消散为柔和焦外光，Roco 的肩膀在极端失焦前景。长焦压缩压平纵深，使三人看起来叠在不同焦平面上。两侧火把渲染成巨大带光晕的焦外光球退入透视。大气雾霭浓重。他们以缓慢紧绷的步态前行，间距清晰可读。来自三个距离的脚步声、呼吸声。

镜头 2（Shot 2，3–6s）：硬切至 Rain 长焦中景，四分之三角，在其他人前方前行。剃刀般细薄的焦点在她脸上，她身后走廊消散为橙火把焦外光的流。她回头越过肩一瞥——脸微转时焦点微妙地拉移。她开始张开口，正要回头叫队伍。她的脚在她身下处于完全柔和的失焦前景中、正迈着一步，无声地向地面落去。

镜头 3（Shot 3，6–8s）：硬切至 Roco 的面部长焦中近景，四分之三角。硬朗火把光源在他脸上刻出棱角、带光晕的光，半张脸迷入煤黑阴影。他的眼睛扫视着前方走廊地面——焦点越过他脸拉向深处背景，那里 Rain 的脚正朝一块磨蚀石板落下、处于柔焦。Roco 的目光锁定它。认出感涌上他脸。他嘶吼，声音尖锐而绝望：「Rein!」这警告已是可能的最后一秒。

镜头 4（Shot 4，8–10.5s）：极特写——贴地长焦微距镜头，摄影机距一块磨蚀石板仅数英寸，以 2.39:1 变形宽银幕满画幅取景。剃刀般细薄焦点在石板表面，每条裂缝与缺口都渲染以锐利细节，周围石板消散为深奶咖啡焦外光。Rain 的靴子从画面上半部以柔和带光晕的焦点下落、落地。石板在她重量下沉陷一厘米，发出微弱的沉闷咔哒（CLICK）。粉尘从石缝间筛落，个别颗粒在掠过的火把光中作为带光晕的焦外光点发光。停格。墙内，隐约地，一道深沉的机械呻吟开始积聚。

镜头 5（Shot 5，10.5–12.5s）：极特写——个石墙口最大细节的长焦微距镜头，摄影机距雕石表面仅数英寸。剃刀般细薄焦点在孔盖的接缝上，周围饱经风化的符文雕刻落入奶咖啡焦外光。雕刻的盖板碾磨着打开（GRINDS open），伴随沉重的机械咔嗒滑向一侧——粉尘从接缝带着一道浓密的可见冲柱爆炸开来，个别颗粒在火把光中作为带光晕的焦外光点发光。石头内部黑暗镂空的孔径显露。身后柔和焦外光中，这面墙与对面墙上的其他孔口同时打开，记为一阵阵粉尘爆发的闪烁——另外九处开口，全部同时。

镜头 6（Shot 6，12.5–13.5s）：骤切至 Roco 长焦极特写——全脸尖叫中段，声音嘶哑迸裂：「DOWN（趴下）！」他身体前扑，手臂探出——但不对 Jax。他越过（PAST）Jax（他自己已经趴下）猛冲，朝深处背景中的 Rain 冲刺。

镜头 7（Shot 7，13.5–15s）：贴地长焦低角度，摄影机沿走廊向前看。多个动作在 f/1.2 同一帧内同时发生、焦点切换——Jax 在中前景自己趴下（DROPS），重重砸地、双手抱头；Roco 冲刺越过 Jax 中段，身体腾空扑向前方 Rain；Rain 僵立原地，脚仍踩在下陷的石板上，开始做出反应已太迟。Roco 够到 Rain——从腰部扑倒（TACKLES）她，他的身体包覆在她之上，两人一起（BOTH）砸到地面，Roco 的身体从中上方护住她，他的背暴露向走廊。他们着地的当口，第一波箭矢从墙口爆炸射出（EXPLODES）——数十支箭同时沿走廊朝两个方向水平疾飞，尖锐的木制嗖嗖声灌满音频，运动模糊的条纹在每个纵深平面上切开焦外光。停格。

无音乐。仅音效——湿石上缓慢刻意的脚步声带深沉石混响（来自三个距离、拉开间距）、平板发出的微弱电子嗡鸣、火把噼啪、被压制住的深重呼吸、前方远处滴水、身后废墟里的碎石微落声、Rain 开始吸入预备说话（被咔哒声打断）、Roco 尖锐绝望带石混响的「Rein!」、靴子踩压石板、下陷石板的微弱沉闷咔哒、粉尘从缝隙筛落、墙内开始升起沉重的机械呻吟、前景一块古老石盖带着沉重的机械咔嗒与爆炸般的尘柱碾磨打开、另外九块同时碾磨打开、Roco 沙哑迸裂的「DOWN!」（趴下！）、Jax 的身体重重砸在湿石上、Roco 向前冲刺的奔跑脚步、Roco 撞上 Rain 时的身体扑倒冲击、两人一起倒地、悬浮滑板叮当作响、平板滑开、数十支箭以全实时速度、相叠的波次同时嗖嗖穿空而过。
