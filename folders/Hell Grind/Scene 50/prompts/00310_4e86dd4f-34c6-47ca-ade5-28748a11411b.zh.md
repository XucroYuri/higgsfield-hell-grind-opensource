## 原文 (source)

A continuous handheld long-take medium shot of two young men running together toward a closing stone gateway exit while a young woman waits anxiously at the threshold, the men reaching the gateway and diving through the narrowing gap one after another in the final seconds before the massive stone slab seals the doorway. 14 seconds, single continuous shot, no cuts, in the visual language of Emmanuel Lubezki and Greig Fraser.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Roco and his friend Jax have just escaped a deadly trap pit in an ancient stone corridor and are now running together down the long corridor toward the closing gateway exit at the far end. The exit is being slowly sealed from above by a massive descending stone slab — the gap below the slab has narrowed dramatically and is now only knee-to-waist height, requiring desperate ducking, sliding, or diving to pass through. Their teammate Rain is already on the safe side beyond the gateway, waiting at the threshold, watching her friends sprint toward her with terror that they won't make it in time. Roco supports the exhausted Jax as they run, then at the gateway he SHOVES Jax forward, sending him diving through the narrow gap. Rain catches Jax on the other side. Then Roco, in the last possible moment, LAUNCHES himself into a horizontal sliding dive under the rapidly descending slab, his body sliding through the gap on the wet stone just before the slab THUNDERS down to the floor with a final stone-on-stone impact, sealing the corridor behind him.

CINEMATOGRAPHY in the language of Emmanuel Lubezki: continuous handheld long-take, the camera moving organically through the entire sequence as one fluid motion, never cutting. Camera floats and breathes, varies between tracking the running men, framing on Rain's anxious face, capturing the closing slab, and finally settling on the aftermath. All in one continuous motion. Camera shake alive but never jittery, the operator chasing the desperate action, occasionally losing tracking briefly as bodies move fast. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture — telephoto compression making the long corridor distance feel both vast and oppressive. Focus pulls happen organically. Lens imperfections: visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from torch flames and from the cold blue gateway light, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9.

LIGHTING in the language of Greig Fraser: practical motivated lighting only, iron torches on corridor walls as warm key sources receding into deep distance like a string of bokeh orbs, hard angular orange light. Deep crushed blacks with detail in shadows. The closing gateway in the deep distance emits cold blue light through its narrowing gap — the cold blue light contrasting with the warm orange of the corridor torches. As characters approach the gateway and pass through, the light shifts from warm corridor tones to cold blue gateway tones on their bodies. After the slab seals, only the warm corridor side is visible (the cold blue cuts off completely with the seal).

LOCATION: a VERY LONG ancient underground stone corridor approximately two meters wide. Rows of iron torches mounted on both side walls every few meters, creating long perspective of warm flame light receding into distant haze. Walls weathered carved stone with deep glyph carvings along the entire length. Wet stone floor reflecting torch highlights, stretching far in both directions. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust. At the far end of the corridor: the closing stone gateway exit — a tall carved stone doorway frame, with a massive carved stone slab descending slowly from above, currently the slab has descended significantly so that the remaining gap below it is only knee-to-waist height (perhaps 60-80 cm tall), requiring crouching, ducking, sliding, or diving to pass through. Cold blue light streams from beyond the gateway through the narrow gap, illuminating the threshold area. Beyond the gateway visible through the gap: an ancient stone hall with cold blue atmospheric lighting, stretching deep into another space.

CHARACTERS:
— Roco: young man, mid-twenties, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, fresh bleeding wound on the bridge of his nose, dust-covered face, lighter olive/tan skin tone. Wearing a dark hooded jacket with grey panel inserts, dark t-shirt underneath, dark cargo pants, fingerless glove on his left hand with red bandana at the wrist. Both jacket sleeves intact. Running with effort.
— Jax: young man, mid-twenties, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked face, exhausted from his fall into the pit. Running but stumbling, supported by Roco.
— Rain: young woman, mid-twenties, silver-blonde shoulder-length disheveled hair, fresh cut on her temple, grey hooded jacket, dark cargo pants, dust-covered. Already on the safe side beyond the gateway, crouched at the threshold, anxiously watching her friends approach. Her hands extending into the narrowing gap reaching back toward them.

(10–11.5s) ACCENT: the dive. Roco LAUNCHES himself into a horizontal sliding dive, body parallel to the ground, arms outstretched forward toward the gap. His body becomes airborne briefly then slams onto the wet stone floor and SLIDES on his stomach toward the closing gap. Camera tracks his diving body, capturing the desperate horizontal slide. The gap is now mere centimeters above his back as he slides through.

(11.5–12.5s) ACCENT: scraping through. Roco's body slides through the closing gap on the wet stone, his back grazing the bottom edge of the descending slab as it lowers — his jacket scraping against the descending stone with sparks of friction. His head, shoulders, torso pass through, then his legs whipping behind him just as the slab continues its final descent.

(12.5–13.5s) ACCENT: the slab seals. The instant Roco's feet clear the threshold, the slab THUNDERS down the final inches and IMPACTS the floor with a massive deep stone-on-stone CRASH, sealing the corridor completely behind him. Dust explodes from the impact. Audio: the sealing impact dominates, then immediate silence after.

(13.5–14s) ACCENT: the aftermath silence. Camera settles on the safe side now — Roco sprawled on the floor where he slid to a stop, gasping, alive. Rain and Jax visible beside him, also exhausted on the floor. The sealed gateway behind them, the corridor on the other side now inaccessible. All three of them lying there breathing hard in heavy ragged gasps. The cold blue light of this side now their only illumination. Hold on the trio for the final beat. Cut to black on their continued breathing.
