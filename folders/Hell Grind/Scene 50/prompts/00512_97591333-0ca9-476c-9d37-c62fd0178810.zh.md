## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, suspended Jax and team divergence sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — alive, breathing, reactive to action but not jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography (85mm to 135mm equivalent) with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 to f/2 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on lens, halation from torch flames, chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional focus drift, faint film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with warm orange torch accents and cold blue gateway light from the still-open gap. Dramatic chiaroscuro from torches only — deep crushed blacks, faces partially in shadow. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead's atmosphere and lighting.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse. Ahead — distant carved stone gateway with cold blue light. The massive stone slab continues its slow descent from the ceiling above the gateway — descended about halfway, opening still tall enough to walk through but shrinking gradually, never accelerating. Lit iron torches on both walls. Arrows embedded in walls from previous volley. Mid-corridor, an open pit where a tile collapsed — Jax hangs from its edge by his fingertips, dark pit with stone spikes in darkness below.

CHARACTERS:
— Rain (): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Mid-run between Roco and Jax.
— Jax (<<<image_2>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Hanging from edge of pit by his fingertips, body dangling into darkness, arms straining.
— Roco (<<<image_3>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Running toward gateway, halfway there when click happens.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Handheld HIGH-ANGLE close-up shot looking DOWN at Jax from above the pit edge — camera positioned above him, looking down into the pit, the dark stone walls of the pit framing his face from above. His face fills the lower half of the frame, terrified, dust-streaked. His arms strain visible at the top of frame, fingers gripping the stone lip. The dark pit drops away below him into shadow. He looks UP toward camera (toward where Roco runs), eyes wide with raw fear. He SCREAMS, voice cracking with full terror: "ROCO!!" Camera trembles intensely with the operator's reaction to his scream.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens medium shot of Roco mid-run from a three-quarter angle, sprinting toward the gateway. Camera tracks with his motion, intense shake. Hard torch light slashes across his features. He hears Jax's scream — his stride HESITATES, head WHIPS back over his shoulder. His face shows the conflict — duty pulling forward, brotherhood pulling back. The slowly descending slab visible in deep background, opening gradually shrinking but still tall.


no music. sfx only — slow deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble of descending slab continuing throughout (steady, not accelerating), torches crackling with low hiss, Jax's panicked rapid breathing and shaking arm muscles, Jax's "ROCO!!" raw scream cracking with full terror with stone reverb, Roco's running boots pounding wet stone, his stride hesitating, three sets of urgent breathing at different distances, Roco's boots planting and turning, running boots accelerating in opposite direction back toward pit, Rain's continued running boots fading toward gateway, Jax's fingers grinding on stone, dust falling into pit echoing distantly off stone spikes.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄古老石廊，悬垂的 Jax 与队伍分道扬镳序列，15 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影带强烈受控的摄影机颤动——「活着」、呼吸、对动作有反应但不抖晃。长焦远摄摄影（85mm 至 135mm 等效）带浅景深，f/1.8 至 f/2 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜头上的可见灰尘、火把火焰的光晕（halation）、高对比边缘的色差、偶发焦点漂移、淡淡胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和的调色板带暖橙火把点缀与仍敞开缝隙透出的冷蓝门廊光。仅来自火把的戏剧性明暗对照——压死深黑、面部局部入影。受 Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）的氛围与照明启发。

场景（LOCATION）：狭窄的古老石质走廊，两米宽。角色身后——新鲜碎石塌落。前方——远处雕石门廊透出冷蓝色光。巨大的石板继续从门廊上方天花板缓缓下落——已下到约一半，开口仍高到足以步行通过但逐渐缩小、从不加速。两面墙上点燃铁火把。此前一轮齐射的箭矢嵌入墙中。走廊中段，一个石板塌落形成敞开的坑——Jax 以指尖吊挂在边缘，下方黑暗的坑底有石刺。

角色（CHARACTERS）：
— Rain（）：银白金色头发凌乱，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新割伤，灰色连帽夹克，覆尘。在 Roco 与 Jax 之间奔跑中段。
— Jax（<<<image_2>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，透明眼镜，深色夹克，覆尘。指尖吊挂在坑边缘，身体悬荡入黑暗中，手臂使劲。
— Roco（<<<image_3>>>）：深色凌乱发，胡须，鼻上红绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。朝门廊奔跑，咔哒声响起时已在半途。

镜头 1（Shot 1，0–3s）：手持高角度（HIGH-ANGLE）特写，从坑沿上方朝下看 Jax——摄影机置于他上方、向下看入坑中，坑的黑暗石壁从上方框住他的脸。他的脸填满画面下半部、惊恐、尘土斑驳。他的手臂在画面上部可见地使劲、手指握着石沿。黑暗的坑在他下方落空坠入阴影。他向上看向摄影机（朝 Roco 奔跑的方向），双眼圆睁带着赤裸的恐惧。他尖叫（SCREAMS），声音因十足的惊骇而破开：「ROCO!!」摄影机随操机员对他尖叫的反应而剧烈颤抖。

镜头 2（Shot 2，3–6s）：硬切至 Roco 奔跑中段的手持长焦中景，四分之三角，朝门廊冲刺。摄影机随他运动、剧烈颤抖。硬朗火把光斜劈过他五官。他听到 Jax 的尖叫——他的步伐一顿（HESITATES），头「唰」地甩回肩后。他的脸显出冲突——责任把他向前拉、兄弟情把他向后扯。深处背景中可见缓慢下落石板，开口逐渐缩小但仍很高。


无音乐。仅音效——下落石板石怼石碾磨的缓慢深沉轰鸣全程持续（稳定、不加速）、火把低沉嘶嘶地噼啪、Jax 惊慌的急促呼吸与颤抖的臂肌、Jax 带十足惊骇破开、带石混响的沙哑尖叫「ROCO!!」、Roco 奔跑的靴子重踏湿石、他步伐一顿、不同距离的三组急切呼吸、Roco 的靴子踏地转身、奔跑的靴子朝坑反向加速、Rain 持续的奔跑靴朝向门廊淡去、Jax 的手指在石头上摩擦碾扎、粉尘落入坑中隐约在远处石刺上回响。
