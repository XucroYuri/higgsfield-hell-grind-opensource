Single shot in an ancient stone chamber on the safer side of a closing gateway, 4 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Through the gap behind her, the warm orange torchlit corridor on the other side is visible, dust falling, the slab continuing to descend slowly. Wet stone floor.

Camera follows her turn and settles on her face, the warm orange light from the corridor visible behind her through the closing gap, mixing with the cold blue light of her side.

Distant sounds of running and turmoil from the corridor beyond. No music.
