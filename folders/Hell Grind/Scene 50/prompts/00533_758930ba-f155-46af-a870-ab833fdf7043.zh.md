## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, escape sequence beginning, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with subtle controlled camera shake — alive in operator's hands, breathing, never jerky. Long-lens telephoto at f/1.2 aperture with extreme shallow depth of field. Lens imperfections — visible dust on lens, halation aggressive from torch flames, chromatic aberration on edges, occasional gentle focus drift, faint film grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. Heavily desaturated palette with warm torch accents and cold blue gateway light. Dramatic chiaroscuro from torches only. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse from earlier blast. Ahead — distant carved stone gateway with cold blue light spilling through, but a massive stone slab is now slowly descending from the ceiling above the gateway, sealing it. Lit iron torches on both walls. Arrows embedded in both walls from previous volley. Wet stone floor with depressed pressure tile.

CHARACTERS (recovering from arrow volley, dust-covered, urgency rising):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty.
— Roco (): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Handheld long-lens low-angle shot down the corridor, focus on the distant gateway — a massive carved stone slab is visibly descending from the ceiling above the gateway, the bright cold blue light beyond compressing as the slab lowers. Halation streaming off the diminishing aperture. Foreground bokeh of arrow shafts protruding from walls. Camera trembles subtly, alive. Deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble dominates the audio.

Shot 2 (3–5s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens close-up of Jax's face on the floor, dust-streaked, eyes locked forward toward the gateway. He breathes hard. He says, voice rough with realization: "Bro. The door." Camera close, subtle shake.

Shot 3 (5–7s): Handheld long-lens close-up of Roco's face on the floor next to Rain. Hard torch light slashes across his features. His eyes assess the situation — gateway, slab, distance — for one beat. Then he commits. Voice flat with command: "On me." He pushes up off the floor.

Shot 4 (7–10s): Handheld long-lens medium shot from low angle as Roco SPRINGS to his feet first and starts running toward the gateway, body launching forward through the corridor. Rain follows immediately — pushing up, snatching her hoverboard and tablet from the floor, sprinting after him. Jax is last — scrambling up from his prone position, getting his feet under him. Camera tracks with their motion forward, dust kicking up from their boots, arrow-cage walls passing in rapid bokeh on either side.

Shot 5 (10–12.5s): Handheld long-lens close-up tracking with Jax as he runs, a beat behind the others. Camera trembling with the run, the operator chasing. His breath ragged, eyes forward on Roco's back ahead. Boots pounding wet stone. Then suddenly — a sharp CLICK from beneath his feet.

Shot 6 (12.5–15s): Hard cut to handheld extreme close-up at floor level — a worn stone tile DROPS AWAY beneath Jax's foot mid-stride, the entire tile collapsing into a dark hole below. Jax PLUNGES downward, body dropping out of frame as the floor opens. His hands SCRABBLE at the edge of the hole — fingertips catching the stone lip — his body slamming hard against the inner wall of the pit. He hangs by his fingertips. Camera trembles with the impact, then holds tight on his fingers gripping the edge, knuckles white, dust falling past into the darkness below.

no music. sfx only — deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble of the descending slab continuing throughout, settling debris from rubble behind, faint ringing tinnitus residue, torches crackling, three sets of ragged breathing, "Bro. The door." quiet rough, "On me." flat command, three pairs of running boots pounding wet stone with deep stone reverb, sudden sharp mechanical CLICK beneath Jax, tile dropping away with stone-grinding crack, Jax's body falling with dust cascade, hands SLAPPING stone hard as he catches the edge, his body slamming against the inner pit wall, ragged panicked breathing.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄古老石廊，逃生序列开始，15 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影带微妙受控的摄影机颤动——在操机员手中「活着」、呼吸、从不抽搐。长焦远摄 f/1.2 光圈带极端浅景深。镜头瑕疵——镜头上的可见灰尘、火把火焰的强烈光晕（halation）、边缘色差、偶发温和的焦点漂移、淡淡胶片颗粒。变形宽银幕 2.39:1。高度去饱和的调色板带暖火把点缀与冷蓝门廊光。仅来自火把的戏剧性明暗对照照明。受 Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）启发。

场景（LOCATION）：狭窄的古老石质走廊，两米宽。角色身后——先前爆炸造成的碎石塌落。前方——远处雕石门廊透出冷蓝色光，但一块巨大石板正从门廊上方天花板缓缓下落、封死它。两面墙上点燃铁火把。先前齐射的箭矢嵌入两面墙中。湿滑石地面有一块下陷的压力砖（pressure tile）。

角色（从箭矢齐射中恢复、覆尘、急切感上升）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银白金色头发凌乱，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新割伤，灰色连帽夹克，覆尘。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，透明眼镜，深色夹克，覆尘。
— Roco（）：深色凌乱发，胡须，鼻上红绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。

镜头 1（Shot 1，0–3s）：手持长焦低角度沿走廊向深处看，焦点在远处门廊——一块巨大雕刻石板明显正从门廊上方天花板下落，远处的明亮冷蓝色光随石板降低而压缩。光晕（halation）从不断缩小的孔径流布。前景焦外光中是从墙上突出的箭杆。摄影机微妙颤抖、「活着」。石怼石碾磨的深沉轰鸣主导音频。

镜头 2（Shot 2，3–5s）：硬切至 Jax 伏地脸的手持长焦特写，尘土斑驳，双眼向前锁定门廊。他喘着粗气。他以带着醒悟的粗哑声音说：「兄弟。门。」摄影机贴近、微妙颤抖。

镜头 3（Shot 3，5–7s）：手持长焦 Roco 的脸的特写，躺在 Rain 旁边。硬朗火把光斜劈过他五官。他的眼睛评估局面——门廊、石板、距离——花一拍。随即他下定决心。声音平静带着命令：「跟我走（On me）。」他撑身离开地面。

镜头 4（Shot 4，7–10s）：手持长焦低角度中景，Roco 第一个「唰」地站起来开始朝门廊跑去，身体向前扑穿过走廊。Rain 立刻跟上——撑起身、从地面抓起她的悬浮滑板与平板、在他身后冲刺。Jax 最后——从伏卧姿势慌忙爬起、把脚踩实。摄影机随他们向前的运动跟拍，他们靴下尘土扬起，两侧箭笼般的墙在迅速焦外光中掠过。

镜头 5（Shot 5，10–12.5s）：手持长焦特写跟随奔跑的 Jax，落后其他人一拍。摄影机随奔跑颤动、操机员追逐。他呼吸沙哑、双眼看着前方 Roco 的背。靴子重踏湿石。随即突然——他脚下传来一声尖锐的咔哒（CLICK）。

镜头 6（Shot 6，12.5–15s）：硬切至手持贴地极特写——Jax 脚下的一块磨蚀石片在步伐中途向下塌落（DROPS AWAY），整块石片塌入下方一个黑暗的洞。Jax 猛地（PLUNGES）下坠，地面裂开、身体脱出画面。他的双手在洞口边缘乱抓（SCRABBLE）——指尖扣住石沿——他的身体重重砸在坑的内壁上。他靠指尖吊挂着。摄影机随冲击颤抖，随即紧贴在他握着边缘、指节发白的手指上，粉尘擦过落入下方黑暗中。

无音乐。仅音效——下落石板石怼石碾磨的深沉轰鸣全程持续、身后废墟的沉降碎屑、微弱的耳鸣余音残留、火把噼啪、三组沙哑呼吸、「兄弟。门。」轻声粗哑、「跟我走。」平静命令、三双奔跑的靴子带深沉石混响重踏湿石、Jax 脚下一声突然尖锐的机械咔哒、石片带着石碾碎裂声塌落、Jax 的身体带粉尘倾泻下落、他接住边缘时双手重重「啪」地砸在石上、他的身体撞上坑内壁、沙哑惊慌的呼吸。
