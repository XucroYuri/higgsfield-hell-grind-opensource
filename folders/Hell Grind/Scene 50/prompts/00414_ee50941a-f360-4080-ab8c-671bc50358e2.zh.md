Narrow ancient stone corridor, trap activation sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with constant camera shake and reactive movement, camera always alive in operator's hands. Long-lens telephoto at f/1.2 aperture with extreme shallow depth of field. Lens imperfections — visible dust particles on lens, halation aggressive from torch flames, chromatic aberration on edges, occasional focus drift, light leaks, subtle barrel distortion, faint film grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. Heavily desaturated palette with warm torch accents. Dramatic chiaroscuro from torches only. The camera reacts instinctively to action — it dodges, follows, shakes with impacts, like a human witness in the corridor.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide. Behind — fresh rubble collapse. Ahead — distant glowing gateway. Lit iron torches on both walls. Ten stone ports embedded in walls (five left, five right), currently sealed.

CHARACTERS:
— Rain (<<<image_1>>>): silver-blonde hair, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm, glowing tablet in hand.
— Jax (<<<image_2>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty.
— Roco (<<<image_3>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Handheld long-lens medium close-up of Rain walking forward, three-quarter angle. Camera moves with her at her pace, operator stepping with her, micro-shake constant from the steady-but-imperfect tracking. Shallow focus on her face, occasionally drifting between her eye and her cheekbone with the shake. Behind her, the corridor dissolves into orange torch bokeh. She glances back over her shoulder, opens her mouth to say something. From off-frame behind, Roco shouts urgently: "Rein!" Camera SNAPS reactively toward his voice for a fraction of a second — a sudden whip of the operator's head — then pulls back to Rain's frozen face mid-word.

Shot 2: Close up shot of <<<image_3>>>. He looks around then stops, and yells: "Rein!"

Shot 3 : Hard cut to handheld extreme close-up at floor level, camera unsteady in operator's hands inches from a single stone tile. Heavy shake — the operator is low, balancing awkwardly. Shallow focus on the tile surface, focus drifting with the shake. Rain's boot descends from above and lands on it. The tile DEPRESSES one centimeter with a dull CLICK. Dust sifts from the seam. Camera reacts — a small jolt of operator surprise, frame shifting briefly, then settling back on the sunken tile. A deep mechanical groan begins inside the walls. Camera tightens, holding focus.

Shot 4: Hard cut to handheld extreme close-up of one stone wall port, camera shaking, the operator pressed close to the wall. The carved cover GRINDS open with a heavy mechanical clack — camera JUMPS reactively from the impact, frame jolting hard then recovering, focus briefly lost then reacquired. Dust EXPLODES from the seam outward, particles flying past the lens, some catching directly on the lens as new dust additions. From off-frame in all directions, the simultaneous mechanical clacks of nine more ports — camera flinches with each clack, micro-jolts from every direction, like the operator is being startled by sounds on all sides.

Shot 5: Snap cut to handheld long-lens close-up of Roco mid-scream, voice cracking: "DOWN!" Hard torch light slashes across his face. Camera shake intense — the operator reacting to the scream, frame jerking. He launches forward into frame and the camera operator steps back instinctively, the camera pulling back with his motion, frame destabilizing with the rapid retreat.

Shot 6 : Handheld long-lens close-up of Rain, camera shaking violently as the operator has lost stability in the chaos. Roco's body CRASHES into her from off-frame — his shoulder slamming into her midsection, his arm wrapping around her — camera HARD SHAKE from the impact, frame tilting and dropping as both bodies are driven down out of frame together. As they fall, dozens of arrows EXPLODE from the wall ports — camera DUCKS instinctively with operator reaction, frame dropping low as if the operator is also taking cover, sharp wooden whooshes filling the air, motion-blur streaks slicing across the destabilized frame. Hard cut as if camera lost tracking entirely.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的古老石廊、陷阱激活段落，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持电影摄影、带持续的摄影机颤抖与反应性移动——摄影机在操机员手中始终活着。f/1.2 光圈的远摄长焦、极浅景深。镜头瑕疵——镜头上可见尘埃微粒、火炬火焰咄咄逼人的光晕（halation）、边缘色差、偶发对焦漂移、漏光、微妙桶形畸变、淡淡胶片颗粒。变形宽银幕 2.39:1。高度去饱和的调色板、带暖火炬点缀。仅由火炬产生的戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）。摄影机对动作本能地反应——它闪避、追随、随撞击颤抖、如同走廊里的一名人类目击者。

LOCATION（场地）：狭窄古老石廊、两米宽。身后——新鲜废墟塌落。前方——远处发光的石门。两壁点亮的铁制火炬。墙壁中嵌入十个石口（左五右五）、当前封闭。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
— Rain（<<<image_1>>>）：银金发、眼侧疤痕、太阳穴新伤口、灰连帽夹克、带尘。腋下夹着滑板、手里发光平板。
— Jax（<<<image_2>>>）：黄板寸头、深肤色、清晰眼镜、深色夹克、带尘。
— Roco（<<<image_3>>>）：深色凌乱发、胡子、红鼻绷带、深色夹克、带尘。腋下夹着滑板。

Shot 1（0–4s）：Rain 向前走的手持长焦中近景、四分之三角度。摄影机以其步速随她移动、操机员与她同步迈步、因稳定但不太完美的跟拍而微颤不停。她脸上浅对焦、随颤抖在对焦中偶发在她眼与颧骨之间漂移。她身后、走廊溶解进橙色火炬散景。她越肩回望、张嘴要说些什么。从身后画外、Roco 急迫地喊：「Rein！」摄影机朝他声音的方向反应性地「啪」地甩过零点几秒——操机员头的一次骤然一摆——随即拉回 Rain 说到一半冻住的脸。

Shot 2：<<<image_3>>> 的特写镜头。他环顾四周、然后停住、大喊：「Rein！」

Shot 3：硬切到地板高度手持极特写、摄影机在操机员手中不稳、离一块石板仅数英寸。强烈颤抖——操机员蹲得很低、别扭地维持平衡。石砖表面上浅对焦、对焦随颤抖漂移。Rain 的靴子自上方降下、踩上去。石砖以一记沉闷「咔嗒」内陷一厘米。灰尘自石缝筛落。摄影机反应——操机员受惊的一小下、画面短暂偏移、随即落回下陷石砖。墙内响起一声深沉机械呻吟。摄影机收紧、保持对焦。

Shot 4：硬切到一面石墙孔口的手持极特写、摄影机颤抖、操机员紧贴墙壁。刻花盖板以一声沉重的机械咔嗒「碾」开——摄影机被冲击「惊」得一跳、画面狠狠一颠后恢复、对焦短暂丢失后又找回。灰尘自接缝「爆」出、微粒飞过镜头、有些直接糊在镜头上成为新的灰尘。从四面八方画外、其余九个孔口同时的机械咔嗒——摄影机随每一声咔嗒都一缩、每个方向来的微跳、如同一名被四面八方的声响惊吓到的操机员。

Shot 5：骤切到 Roco 尖叫中的手持长焦特写、声音碎裂：「DOWN！」硬朗火炬光斜切过他的脸。摄影机颤抖强烈——操机员对尖叫作出反应、画面猛抽。他纵身扑进画面、操机员本能后退、摄影机随他的动作后拉、画面因这急速退让而失稳。

Shot 6：Rain 的手持长焦特写、混战中操机员失去稳定性、摄影机猛颤。Roco 的身体自画外「撞」进她——他的肩撞上她腹部、他的手臂环住她——摄影机被撞击「硬颤」、画面倾斜下坠、两具身体一起被压出画面。他们坠落之际、成打的箭从墙上的孔口「爆」出——摄影机随操机员反应本能地「缩低」、画面俯低、仿佛操机员也在找掩护、尖锐的木制「嗖嗖」声充满空气、运动模糊条纹斜切过失稳的画面。硬切、仿佛摄影机彻底失去追踪。

无配乐。仅环境音效（SFX only）——湿滑石头上回荡的缓慢脚步声、火炬噼啪、画外尖锐的「Rein！」警告、靴子压石、凹陷石砖的沉闷「咔嗒」、灰尘筛落、墙内深沉机械呻吟、十个石盖以沉重咔嗒同时碾开、「DOWN！」粗粝尖叫、身体扑撞的撞击声、成打的箭以满实时速度嗖嗖穿空。
