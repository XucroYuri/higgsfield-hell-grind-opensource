## 原文 (source)

Handheld medium shot in an ancient stone corridor lit by torches, two characters meeting in the middle of an escape, exchanging words, then running in opposite directions. 7 seconds, single continuous shot.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Roco is escaping a deadly trap. The exit is a stone gateway at the far end of the corridor where a massive stone slab is slowly descending from above, sealing the doorway. His friend Jax has fallen into a pit further back in the corridor and needs rescue. Roco is torn — he had been running toward the exit but stopped, conflicted. Now his teammate Rain catches up to him from the direction of the exit. She has just been at the gateway and seen it closing — she yells at him to keep going to the door. But Roco has already decided to go back for Jax. He grabs her hands, tells her to go through the gate without him, then turns and runs back the way he came toward Jax. Rain watches him go for a beat, then turns and runs forward toward the closing gateway.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — the camera alive in the operator's hands, breathing with the action, reactive but not jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from torch flames forming bokeh orbs along both walls, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette dominated by warm orange torch tones with deep crushed blacks. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from torches only — characters' faces lit warmly from the side torches, half in shadow, the corridor stretching into receding bokeh of torch flames on both walls. Inspired by the cinematography of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: a tight ancient underground stone corridor approximately two meters wide with stable stone ceiling at normal height. Walls are weathered carved stone with deep glyph carvings, lit iron torches mounted on both side walls every few meters, casting flickering warm orange light. The torches recede into the depth of the corridor on both sides as bokeh orbs of warm light. Wet stone floor reflecting dim torch highlights. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust particles caught in the torch beams.

CHARACTERS:
— Roco: a young man, mid-twenties, with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage strip across the bridge of his nose, dark hooded jacket with gray shoulder panels, dust-covered face, sweat-streaked. Standing in the middle of the corridor at the start of the shot, breathing hard, body tense.
— Rain: a young woman, mid-twenties, with silver-blonde shoulder-length disheveled hair, small old scar near her eye, fresh small cut on her temple, grey hooded jacket over black top, dark cargo pants, dust on her clothes and face. Running into frame from behind Roco's position (from the direction of the exit).

ACTION: handheld camera positioned in front of Roco at chest height, medium shot framing him centered in frame. Camera trembles with intense controlled shake throughout the entire sequence.

Beat 1 (0–1s): Roco standing in the middle of the corridor facing camera, breath ragged, eyes wide and focused, sweat and dust on his face, the warm torch light from both sides catching his features, deep shadows where the light doesn't reach. He has just made a decision and is about to act on it. The corridor stretches behind him into receding bokeh of torch flames.

Beat 2 (1–2s): Rain RUNS into frame from behind Roco (entering from the deep background, running toward camera and toward Roco), her hair flying with motion, breath ragged. She closes the distance to him quickly and SLAMS to a halt directly behind/beside him, her body coming into focus next to his. She is breathing hard, her eyes wide.

Beat 3 (2–3s): Rain immediately SHOUTS at Roco, voice straining with urgency, arm pointing back the way she came (toward the exit behind her, away from camera direction): "Roco, Jax!" Camera shakes intensely with the urgency.

Beat 4 (3–4.5s): Roco's response is immediate and physical — he TURNS toward Rain, GRABS BOTH her hands firmly with his hands, locks his eyes onto hers with absolute certainty. His face is set, decision made. He shouts back at her, voice raw and final: "GO!" His grip on her hands is tight, transferring his certainty to her. He gives her hands a brief squeeze of finality.

Beat 5 (4.5–5.5s): Roco RELEASES her hands and TURNS sharply, body twisting away from her toward camera direction (back toward where Jax fell), his back filling the frame as he begins to RUN past camera in the opposite direction from where Rain came, sprinting toward the back of the corridor. Camera tracks slightly with his motion as he runs past lens, halated torch light streaking, dust kicking up.

Beat 6 (5.5–7s): Rain remains standing where she was, now alone in frame, the camera holding on her face as she watches Roco's back receding behind camera off-frame. Her face shows the weight of the moment — she understands his choice, processes it for a beat, eyes briefly close in acknowledgment. Then her face HARDENS — she turns sharply on her heel toward the deep background (toward the exit she came from) and SPRINTS away from camera back toward the closing gateway. Her hair flies as she runs into the depth of the corridor, body shrinking in scale as she recedes into the receding bokeh of torch flames. The two characters now move in opposite directions in the empty corridor.

no music. sfx only — slow deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble of the descending slab over the gateway audible from the deep distance ahead (where Rain came from), torches crackling on the walls throughout, Roco's heavy ragged breathing in Beat 1, Rain's running footsteps approaching from the deep background and slamming to a halt next to him in Beat 2, Rain's straining urgent "Roco, Where's JAX?!" with deep stone reverb, brief beat of two breaths held, hands clasping with the firm grip, Roco's raw final "Run!" with deep stone reverb, hands releasing, Roco's boots pivoting on wet stone as he turns, his running boots accelerating away past camera with Doppler shift from foreground to background behind camera, brief moment of stillness as Rain holds, her sharp inhale of acknowledgment, her boots pivoting on wet stone, her running boots accelerating away from camera into the deep distance with footsteps fading.

## 中文对照 (zh)

手持中景于火把照亮的远古石廊中，两名角色在逃生的中途相遇、交换话语、随后朝相反方向跑开。7 秒，单次持续镜头。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：一名叫 Roco 的青年正在逃离一个致命陷阱。出口是走廊远端的一座石拱门，一扇巨大石板正从上方缓缓降下封死门洞。他的朋友 Jax 掉进了走廊更后面的一处坑里，需要营救。Roco 纠结——他一直在跑向出口，但停住了，内心矛盾。此刻他的队友 Rain 从出口方向追了上来。她刚才路过拱门、看见它正在关闭——她朝他吼叫要他继续去门口。但 Roco 已决定回去救 Jax。他抓住她的手，告诉她不要管他通过石门，然后转身循原路往回跑向 Jax。Rain 看着他离去一个节拍，然后转身向前跑向正在关闭的拱门。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持电影摄影带强烈可受控的摄影机晃动——摄影机在操机员手中是活的、与动作一同呼吸、有回应但不发抖。长焦远摄电影摄影配浅景深、f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜头上的可见尘土、火把焰光沿两侧墙形成光晕球（bokeh orbs）的强烈辉散（halation）、高反差边缘的轻微色差、偶发的温柔焦点漂移、淡淡的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。重度降饱和的调色板、由暖橙火把调主导并带深压的黑影。仅火把的戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）布光——角色面孔被侧面火把温暖照亮、半脸在阴影中，走廊向两侧墙退隐的火把 bokeh 延伸。灵感源自 Zack Snyder 电影《僵尸军团（Army of the Dead）》的电影摄影。

LOCATION（场地）：约两米宽的幽闭远古地下石廊、正常高度的稳固石顶。墙壁是带深深符文雕刻的风化雕石，两侧壁每隔几米点亮铁火把，投下闪烁的暖橙色光。火把在走廊两侧向深处退隐成暖色光的 bokeh 球。湿石地面映着暗淡火把高光。大气雾霭厚积、飘浮尘粒被困在火把光束中。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
— Roco：青年男子，二十多岁，深色凌乱齐肩长发，浅色小胡子，鼻梁上一条红色绷带条，深色连帽夹克带灰色肩镶板，脸上覆尘、带汗痕。镜头开场时站在走廊中央，粗重喘气，身体紧绷。
— Rain：青年女子，二十多岁，银金色齐肩凌乱头发，眼旁一道旧小疤，太阳穴一道新鲜小伤口，黑色上衣外罩灰色连帽夹克，深色工装裤，衣服脸上覆尘。从 Roco 身后位置跑进画面（自出口方向而来）。

ACTION（动作）：手持摄影机位于 Roco 前胸高位置，中景把他居中框住。摄影机全程以强烈可受控的晃动颤抖。

Beat 1（第 0-1 秒）：Roco 站在走廊中央面向机位，呼吸粗重、眼睛圆睁而专注，脸上有汗有尘，两侧暖火把光捕捉他的五官，光照不到之处是深影。他刚做了一个决定、正要付诸行动。走廊在他身后延伸进火把焰火退隐的 bokeh。

Beat 2（第 1-2 秒）：Rain 从 Roco 身后 RUNS（跑）进画面（自纵深背景进入、跑向机位、跑向 Roco），头发随动飞甩、呼吸粗重。她迅速拉近与他之间的距离，SLAMS 重重地嘎然急停在他正后方 / 旁侧，她的身体在他身旁进入对焦。她粗重喘息、双眼圆睁。

Beat 3（第 2-3 秒）：Rain 立刻朝 Roco SHOUTS（大喊），声音带着急迫而绷紧，一臂指向她来时的方向（她身后、背离机位的方向）："Roco, Jax！（Roco，Jax！）"摄影机随这种急迫猛烈晃动。

Beat 4（第 3-4.5 秒）：Roco 的回应立时且身体性——他 TURNS（转向）Rain，双手牢牢 GRABS（握住）她的双手，以绝对的笃定锁住她的眼睛。他面容坚毅、决定已定。他朝她吼回话，声音 raw 而终局："GO！（去！）"他握着她的手很紧，把他的笃定传递给她。他给她一个短暂的最终一握。

Beat 5（第 4.5-5.5 秒）：Roco RELEASES（松开）她的手、尖锐地 TURNS（转身），身体从她那里扭向机位方向（转回向 Jax 跌落之处），他的背填满画面、他开始与 Rain 来路相反的方向 RUN（跑）过机位，向走廊深处冲刺。他跑过镜头时摄影机稍微随他运动跟踪，带光晕的火把光拖成条痕、尘土扬起。

Beat 6（第 5.5-7 秒）：Rain 仍站在原地，此刻独据画面，摄影机停在她脸上、她望着 Roco 的背影在机位之后出画远去。她的表情承载这一时刻的分量——她理解他的选择，用一瞬消化它，双眼短暂闭上以示领受。随后她的面容 HARDENS（变硬）——她脚跟一转朝纵深背景（她来时的出口）转身，SPRINTS（猛冲）背离去机位、奔回正在关闭的拱门。她跑进走廊深处时头发飞甩、身体随她隐入退隐的火把 bokeh 而缩小。两名角色如今在空廊里朝相反方向移动。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——拱门上方降下石板的深沉缓慢石磨石轰隆在远处前方（Rain 来处）可闻、两侧墙火把全程噼啪、Beat 1 中 Roco 沉重的粗重呼吸、Beat 2 中 Rain 自纵深背景接近的奔跑脚步、重重嘎然急停在他身旁、Rain 绷紧急迫的"Roco, Where's JAX？！"带深深石回响、两口气共抬的短暂一瞬、双手牢牢扣握交握、Roco 终局的 raw"Run！（跑！）"带深深石回响、松手、Roco 转身时靴子在湿石上旋转、他的跑靴加速越过机位远去、带从前景到机位后背景的多普勒位移、Rain 停留的短暂静止时刻、她领受的尖促吸气、她的靴子在湿石上旋转、她的跑靴加速背离机位远去、脚步渐隐。
