Six-shot sequence in an ancient stone corridor, 15 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, askew protective glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.

Setting: a long ancient underground stone corridor with low rough-hewn stone ceiling, walls covered in horizontal bands of carved ancient script, flagstone floor of irregular cracked stone slabs. Iron torch brackets mounted at angle ONLY on the LEFT wall every few meters — the right wall is dark and bare, no torches, only carved stone in shadow. Thick atmospheric dust haze. In the middle of the corridor: an open trap pit with the dark-skinned yellow-haired man hanging from the edge by his fingertips. In the very far distance through the haze: a closing stone gateway with a slowly descending stone slab.

Roco's ability: he summons crystalline armor on his right arm — sharp jagged blood-red crystal formations materializing on top of his right jacket sleeve as energy-based external armor with internal red glow.

Shot 1 (3s) — STYLISH HERO RUN TOWARD CAMERA:

He runs through the long perspective of the torchlit left wall, distant figure growing larger as he approaches, body in full athletic motion, cape of his jacket flying behind him, dust kicking up from each boot strike. Halated torch light flares as he passes each torch on the left, his silhouette punching through pools of warm light and dark void in alternation. He looks DETERMINED — jaw clenched, eyes burning forward locked on his goal. Camera trembles with anticipation, holding low. The right wall stays in deep dark void.

Shot 2 (2.5s) — SLIDING ARRIVAL WITH CRYSTAL ACTIVATION:
Hard cut. Handheld three-quarter angle medium shot at floor level. The dark-haired man arrives at the pit edge at full sprint and DROPS into a power slide on his knee, body sliding the last meter across the wet flagstones with sparks of friction, leg extended behind for balance, body angled forward. The MOMENT his sliding knee impacts the pit edge, sharp red crystalline shards EXPLODE into existence on his right jacket sleeve from shoulder to fingertips in one violent burst — the armored arm forming instantly with internal red glow blazing alive, dust kicking up around him from the slide impact. He freezes for a fraction of a second at the pit edge in this dynamic pose — knee down, crystal arm extended forward ready to reach, face set with determination. Camera shudders from the impact of his arrival.

Shot 3 (2.5s) — HIGH ANGLE LOOKING DOWN AT JAX IN THE PIT:
Hard cut. Handheld extreme high angle from above the pit looking down at the yellow-haired man hanging from the edge. His face fills the frame — eyes wide and terrified, glasses askew, mouth open in a panicked gasp. His arms above frame strain visibly, hands gripping the stone lip with white knuckles, fingers trembling, slipping a fraction. He looks UP toward camera with raw fear. Dark void of the pit drops away below him. Camera trembles with his desperation. He gasps a single cracked word: "Roco!"

Shot 4 (2.5s) — EXTREME CLOSE-UP ON THE HANDS MEETING:

The crystal-armored hand SLAMS down into frame from above — sharp red shards, internal glow, alien and powerful. Jax's flesh hand reaches up from below to meet it. Their hands CLASP — flesh fingers wrapping around the crystal-armored hand, gripping desperately, tactile and physical. The two contrasting textures locked together — sharp red crystal facets against dark-skinned flesh. Camera holds tight on the contact, trembling. Held briefly to let the gravity of the moment land.

Shot 5 (3s) — MEDIUM SHOT OF THE HEAVE AND RISE, CAMERA SHAKING VIOLENTLY:

Dust falls in steady streams from the rough stone ceiling above the characters, particles raining down through the torch light, the ceiling visibly stressed. In the deep background through the atmospheric haze, the distant gateway visible — the descending stone slab lower than before, the cold blue gap visibly shrinking in real time during the shot. The dark-haired man on his knee straining under the friend's weight, every muscle pushed to its limit, barely making it — face contorted in effort, teeth bared, jaw clenched so hard muscles spasm in his neck, veins bulging on his temple, sweat pouring through dust on his face. He lets out a raw guttural roar through gritted teeth, throat tearing with effort. Camera SHAKES violently with each heave, frame jolting and dropping with every yank. Jax's body finally comes up over the edge in the last desperate yank, dragged out by the wrist, body THUDDING hard onto wet stone. He grips friend's shoulder, glances toward the distant gateway — registering how close to closed it is — then turns Jax forward urgently.

The row of torches on the left wall blurs past as bokeh streaks from the running motion, the right wall stays in deep dark void. The distant gateway visible far ahead through the atmospheric haze, still small, the cold blue gap shrinking from above. Camera trembles with the chase, barely keeping up.

Shot 1（3 秒）——时髦的英雄向摄影机冲来：

Shot 3（2.5 秒）——高角度俯视坑中的 Jax：
