## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, suspended Jax and team divergence sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — alive, breathing, reactive to action but not jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography (85mm to 135mm equivalent) with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 to f/2 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on lens, halation from torch flames, chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional focus drift, faint film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with warm orange torch accents and cold blue gateway light from the still-open gap. Dramatic chiaroscuro from torches only — deep crushed blacks, faces partially in shadow. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead's atmosphere and lighting.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse. Ahead — distant carved stone gateway with cold blue light. The massive stone slab continues its slow descent from the ceiling above the gateway — descended about halfway, opening still tall enough to walk through but shrinking gradually, never accelerating. Lit iron torches on both walls. Arrows embedded in walls from previous volley. Mid-corridor, an open pit where a tile collapsed — Jax hangs from its edge by his fingertips, dark pit with stone spikes in darkness below.

CHARACTERS:
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Mid-run between Roco and Jax.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Hanging from edge of pit by his fingertips, body dangling into darkness, arms straining.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Running toward gateway, halfway there when click happens.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Handheld HIGH-ANGLE close-up shot looking DOWN at Jax from above the pit edge — camera positioned above him, looking down into the pit, the dark stone walls of the pit framing his face from above. His face fills the lower half of the frame, terrified, dust-streaked. His arms strain visible at the top of frame, fingers gripping the stone lip. The dark pit drops away below him into shadow. He looks UP toward camera (toward where Roco runs), eyes wide with raw fear. He SCREAMS, voice cracking with full terror: "ROCO!!" Camera trembles intensely with the operator's reaction to his scream.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens medium shot of Roco mid-run from a three-quarter angle, sprinting toward the gateway. Camera tracks with his motion, intense shake. Hard torch light slashes across his features. He hears Jax's scream — his stride HESITATES, head WHIPS back over his shoulder. His face shows the conflict — duty pulling forward, brotherhood pulling back. The slowly descending slab visible in deep background, opening gradually shrinking but still tall.

Shot 3 (6–9s): Handheld long-lens close-up of Rain mid-run, three-quarter angle. Camera trembling intensely with her motion. She glances back over her shoulder — her eyes register Jax's situation in soft bokeh deep behind her — then her head SNAPS forward toward Roco running ahead of her. She SHOUTS forward at Roco's back, voice straining with urgency: "The door!" She stabs her finger forward toward the slowly closing gateway, commanding him to keep going.

Shot 4 (9–12s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens medium close-up of Roco from three-quarter angle, mid-stride. He has STOPPED running, body skidding to a halt with dust kicking up, body turning back to face Rain. His eyes lock onto hers behind him. He SHAKES his head once, sharply — refusal. He shouts back at her, voice raw and absolute: "GO!" His command leaves no room. Camera shakes intensely with the emotional weight.

Shot 5 (12–15s): Handheld long-lens medium tracking shot from low angle, the camera catching Roco as he plants his foot and TURNS, body launching back into a sprint, running BACK toward the pit, away from the gateway. Camera trembles, tracking with his reverse motion. Halated torch light streaks past as he runs back into the depth of the corridor, dust kicking up behind him. In deep background bokeh, the closing gateway recedes — Rain's silhouette continues running forward toward it. They move in opposite directions in the same frame.

no music. sfx only — slow deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble of descending slab continuing throughout (steady, not accelerating), torches crackling with low hiss, Jax's panicked rapid breathing and shaking arm muscles, Jax's "ROCO!!" raw scream cracking with full terror with stone reverb, Roco's running boots pounding wet stone, his stride hesitating, three sets of urgent breathing at different distances, Rain's "The door!" straining urgent shout with stone reverb, Roco's boots skidding to halt on wet stone, "GO!" raw absolute command, Roco's boots planting and turning, running boots accelerating in opposite direction back toward pit, Rain's continued running boots fading toward gateway, Jax's fingers grinding on stone, dust falling into pit echoing distantly off stone spikes.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的古老石廊、悬挂的 Jax 与小队分歧段落，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持电影摄影、带强烈而受控的摄影机颤抖——活着、呼吸着、对动作有反应但不忙乱。长焦远景电影拍摄（等效 85mm-135mm）浅景深、f/1.8 到 f/2 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜头上可见尘埃、火炬火焰光晕（halation）、高反差边缘色差、偶发对焦漂移、淡淡胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和的调色板、带暖橙火炬点缀与仍敞开的缝隙漏出的冷蓝石门光。仅由火炬产生的戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）——深重碾碎的黑色、面部部分入影。参考 Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）的氛围与布光。

LOCATION（场地）：狭窄古老石廊、两米宽。角色身后——新鲜废墟塌落。前方——远处凿花石门带冷蓝光。石门上方天花板那块巨石板持续缓慢下降——已下降约一半、开口仍高得能走人、但逐渐收缩、从不加速。两壁点亮的铁制火炬。墙壁上嵌着上一轮齐射的箭。走廊中部、一个坑——一块石砖塌落后翻开、Jax 仅凭指尖挂在其沿、深坑下方黑暗中的石尖。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：凌乱银金发、眼侧疤痕、太阳穴新伤口、灰连帽夹克、带尘。在 Roco 与 Jax 之间跑着。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄板寸头、深肤色、清晰眼镜、深色夹克、带尘。仅凭指尖挂在坑沿、身体吊垂进黑暗、双臂绷紧。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱发、胡子、红鼻绷带、深色夹克、带尘。朝石门跑去、咔嗒声响起时已跑到半途。

Shot 1（0–3s）：手持高角度特写、从坑沿上方俯视 Jax——摄影机位于他上方、望入坑中、坑的暗色石壁自上方框住他的脸。他的脸填满画面下半部、惊恐、蒙尘。他绷紧的手臂在画面上缘可见、手指死抓石沿。深坑在他下方坠入阴影。他向上望向镜头（望 Roco 跑的方向）、眼珠因原始恐惧而圆睁。他「尖叫」、声音因十足恐惧而碎裂：「ROCO！！」摄影机随操机员对他尖叫的反应而强烈颤抖。

Shot 2（3–6s）：硬切到 Roco 奔跑中的手持长焦中景、四分之三角度、朝石门冲刺。摄影机追踪他的动作、强烈颤抖。硬朗火炬光斜切过他的五官。他听见 Jax 的尖叫——他的步伐「顿住」、头「嗖」地甩过肩头。他的脸显出冲突——职责把人往前拽、兄弟情让人往回拉。背景深处可见缓慢下降的石板、开口逐渐收缩但仍挺高。

Shot 3（6–9s）：Rain 奔跑中的手持长焦特写、四分之三角度。摄影机随她的动作强烈颤抖。她越过肩头回望——她的目光在身后深处、于柔和散景中辨认出 Jax 的处境——随即她的头「嗖」地转回、朝跑在她前方的 Roco。她朝 Roco 的后背向前「喊」话、声音因急迫而绷紧：「The door！」（门！）她朝缓慢关闭的石门猛戳手指、命令他继续走。

Shot 4（9–12s）：硬切到 Roco 的手持长焦中近景、四分之三角度、迈步途中。他已「停」下奔跑、身体擦着灰尘滑停、身体回转向 Rain。他的双眼锁住她。他猛地摇一次头——拒绝。他朝她喊回去、声音粗粝而绝对：「GO！」（走！）他的指令没有余地。摄影机随这情绪重量强烈发颤。

Shot 5（12–15s）：低角度手持长焦中景跟拍镜头、摄影机捕捉 Roco 定脚、「转」身、身体弹回冲刺、朝坑「跑」回去、背离石门。摄影机颤抖、追踪他的逆向动作。他跑回走廊深处时、带光晕的火炬光从他身侧曳过、身后扬起灰尘。在背景深处散景中、关闭中的石门退去——Rain 的剪影继续朝它向前奔跑。同一画面中他们朝相反方向移动。

无配乐。仅环境音效（SFX only）——下降石板贯穿全程的缓慢深沉磨石-磨石轰隆声（稳定、不加速）、低嘶声噼啪的火炬、Jax 惊惶的快速呼吸与颤抖的手臂肌肉、Jax 带十足恐惧碎裂的「ROCO！！」粗粝尖叫带石壁混响、Roco 奔跑中靴子重踏湿滑石头、他的步伐顿住、三组不同距离的急迫呼吸、Rain 绷紧急迫喊出带石壁混响的「The door！」、Roco 的靴子在湿滑石头上擦停、粗粝绝对的「GO！」、Roco 的靴子定脚转身、跑步靴反向朝坑加速、Rain 持续的跑步靴朝石门渐远、Jax 的手指磨刮石头、灰尘坠入坑中、在石尖上远远地回响。
