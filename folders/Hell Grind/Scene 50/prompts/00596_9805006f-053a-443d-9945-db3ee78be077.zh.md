## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, opening sequence with awakening torches and team briefing, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: shot in the visual language of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead — long lens cinematography (85mm to 135mm equivalent), shallow depth of field at f/1.2 aperture, extreme bokeh with soft circular highlights, foreground and background dissolving into creamy out-of-focus haze, only razor-thin slices of the frame in sharp focus, dynamic focus pulls between subjects. Heavy lens characteristics: subtle barrel distortion in close shots, slight chromatic aberration at high-contrast edges, halation bleeding from torch flames into surrounding darkness, warm orange light blooming organically. Compressed perspective from telephoto lens flattens the corridor depth. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm pops from fire and the cold blue glow of Rain's tablet screen. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — deep crushed blacks, faces partially in shadow, single-source torch motivation, light falling off rapidly into darkness. Cinematic anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1.

SCENE CONTEXT: this scene begins seconds after a violent explosion collapsed the passage above, sending the three characters falling/sliding down through the rubble into this narrow corridor below. They have arrived. As they appear in this ancient place, the corridor itself RESPONDS — wall-mounted torches along its length IGNITE one by one in sequence, deep into the corridor toward the distant gateway, as if waking up to the presence of intruders. Rain consults a small handheld tablet showing schematics of the location. Jax becomes distracted by carved glyphs on the wall. Rain feels something wrong about the place but cannot name it.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide with low ceiling around two and a half meters high. BEHIND the characters — a massive fresh rubble collapse blocking retreat, dust pouring down from the breach above. AHEAD — wall-mounted iron torch brackets along both side walls; at the far end, an open carved stone gateway with faint cold blue light spilling through.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars pressed close along both side walls, iron torch brackets every few meters, thick volumetric dust haze. Teal-shadow color grade with isolated warm orange torch accents and a small cold blue tablet glow on Rain's face.

Characters (just survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): young woman, silver-blonde shoulder-length hair, small scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket over black top, dark cargo pants, heavy dust on clothes. Hoverboard tucked under one arm, deactivated. Holding a small handheld tablet device in her other hand, its screen casting cold blue light up onto her face. Lead.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): young man, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked. Curious, drawn to the carvings.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): young man, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage across bridge of nose, dark jacket, dust-covered. Hoverboard tucked under arm, deactivated. Hangs close to Jax.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Long-lens static shot looking down the length of the corridor toward the distant gateway, telephoto compression flattening the corridor into stacked planes. The corridor is dark — only faint cold blue bokeh from the gateway visible at the far end. Foreground dissolved into out-of-focus blackness. The FIRST iron torch nearest camera IGNITES with a soft whump, flame blooming into a halated orange flare, bokeh balls scattering across the frame from dust particles. The NEXT torch ignites deeper down — appearing first as a defocused glowing sphere, then snapping into resolution as focus racks to it. Each successive ignition deeper in causes a focus shift, the camera rack-focusing through the depth of the corridor torch by torch. By the end of the shot, the corridor is bathed in flickering halated warm orange light against the cold blue gateway glow.

Shot 2 (4–8s): Hard cut to a long-lens tight close-up of Rain's face from a low three-quarter angle, shot at f/1.2 — only her eyes are in razor-sharp focus, her cheekbones and the cut on her temple falling off into soft focus. Cold blue light from her handheld tablet (held just below frame, glowing up onto her face) mixes with warm orange torch light from behind, creating dual-tone contrast across her features. The corridor behind her dissolves into a wash of orange torch bokeh orbs and floating dust. Her eyes flick down to the tablet screen, then back up to the corridor ahead. She says with quiet authority: "Final stretch. Forty feet." Her breath fogs faintly in the cold air, drifting through the bokeh.

Shot 3 (8–11s): Hard cut to a tight extreme close-up of Jax's hand approaching the carved glyphs on a side pillar, shot on long lens at f/1.2. Only his fingertips at the moment of contact are in sharp focus, the rest of his hand and the wall surface dissolving into soft creamy bokeh. The nearest torch flickers warm halated light across the carvings, individual dust motes glowing as bokeh balls in the beam. His fingers slide across the surface, dust shifting beneath them. Pull focus from the stone surface to his eye behind glasses — wide, fascinated, distracted. Off-frame his voice, slightly rough from inhaled dust, almost wonderstruck: "Yo. These markings. Look at 'em."

Shot 4 (11–15s): Hard cut to long-lens medium close-up of Rain in profile, shot at f/1.2 — her face in sharp focus, the corridor ahead and behind dissolved into orange bokeh. She lowers the tablet slightly, eyes narrowing. The blue tablet glow fades from her face, leaving only the warm torch light from one side. She turns her head slightly, scanning the corridor. Her instinct kicks in. She says, lower and more urgent than before, with a tightness in her voice: "Guys. Something's off." A beat. Eyes flick to where Jax is off-frame at the wall. "Stay close." The line lands quiet but heavy. Hold on her face. Cut.

no music. sfx only — settling debris and trickling stone dust from the breach behind, distant rumble of secondary collapse far above, faint ringing tinnitus from blast aftermath, sequential whump of torches igniting (five or six distinct ignitions through Shot 1), crackling flames with soft roar, faint electronic hum from Rain's tablet, soft tap of her fingers on the tablet screen, Rain's "Final stretch. Forty feet." with stone reverb, fingers brushing carved stone with grit, Jax's "Yo. These markings. Look at 'em." (off-frame), Rain's "Guys. Something's off. Stay close." with quiet urgency, distant drip from ahead.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的古老石质走廊，苏醒火把与小队简报的开启序列，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：以 Zack Snyder《亡者之师》（Army of the Dead）的视觉语言拍摄——长焦摄影（85mm 至 135mm 等效），f/1.2 光圈浅景深，带柔和圆形高光的极端散景，前景与背景消解为奶油状失焦薄雾，仅画面上剃刀般细薄的切片处于锐焦点，主体间动态焦点拉动。重镜头特征：近景中细微桶形畸变、高对比边缘处轻微色差、火把火焰向周围黑暗晕渗的光晕（halation）、暖橙光有机地绽放漫延。长焦镜头带来的压缩透视压平走廊纵深。重度去饱和调色，仅火光的孤立暖色跳动与 Rain 平板屏幕的冷蓝辉光。戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）照明——压碎浓黑，面部部分没入阴影，单源火把动机光，光迅速衰减进黑暗。电影感变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：本场景始于一次暴力爆炸压塌上方通道、令三名角色沿碎石滑落掉入下方这条狭窄走廊的几秒之后。他们已抵达。当三人在这个古老之地现身，走廊本身作出响应——沿其长度的墙壁火把一字排开地一支接一支点燃，一直深入到朝远处门口的走廊更深处，仿佛因闯入者的存在而苏醒。Rain 查看一只手持平板电脑，其上显示此地的构架图。Jax 被墙面凿刻符文分了神。Rain 感到这地方有些不对劲，却说不出来。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（地点几何）：约两米宽的紧走廊，低顶棚约两米半高。角色身后（BEHIND）——一处巨大的新鲜塌落碎石堆封住退路，尘土自上方破口倾泻而下。前方（AHEAD）——沿两侧墙壁安装的铁火把架；远处末端，一处敞开的凿刻石门透着微弱的冷蓝光。

Setting（环境）：湿滑石地，两侧墙壁旁紧贴地立着遍布符文的立柱，每隔几米的铁火把架，浓重的体积尘土薄雾。青影（teal-shadow）色调分级，带孤立的暖橙火把点缀与照在 Rain 脸上小片冷蓝平板辉光。

Characters（角色）（刚经历爆炸幸存，蒙尘）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：年轻女子，银色金发齐肩长发，眼睛旁道小疤，太阳穴新割口，黑色上衣外套灰色连帽夹克，深色工装裤，衣服上厚积尘土。一块熄机的悬浮滑板夹在腋下。另一只手持一只小型平板电脑，屏幕冷蓝光上照她的脸。领队。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：年轻男子，漂白黄色圆寸，深肤色，清晰护目镜，深色连帽夹克，蒙尘结垢。好奇，被雕刻吸引。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：年轻男子，深色凌乱齐肩长发，浅色小胡子，鼻梁覆着红色绷带，深色夹克，蒙尘。腋下夹着熄机的悬浮滑板。紧贴着 Jax 站近。

Shot 1（镜头 1）（0–4s）：长焦静态镜头沿走廊长度望向远处门口，长焦压缩将走廊压平为层叠平面。走廊黑暗——仅远处末端可见微弱的冷蓝散景。前景消解进失焦漆黑。距摄影机最近的第一个铁火把以一声柔和的噗燃声点燃（IGNITES），火焰绽放为一片辉散的橙色光耀，尘土粒子在画面上散射出散景球。下一支火把在更深处点燃——先呈现为一个失焦的光亮球体，随后随焦点拉向其而啪地解析清晰。每一支更深处逐次点燃都引起一次焦点转移，摄影机循着火把逐一贯穿走廊纵深变焦对焦。镜头结束时，走廊沐浴在摇曳辉散的暖橙光中，反衬冷蓝门辉光。

Shot 2（镜头 2）（4–8s）：硬切至低三平面角度下 Rain 的脸的长焦紧密特写，f/1.2 拍摄——仅她眼睛处于剃刀锐利焦点，她颧骨与她太阳穴上的割口落到柔焦。手持平板（恰好握在画面下方，光上照她的脸）的冷蓝光与她身后暖橙火把光混合，在她五官上制造出双色调对比。她身后的走廊消解为一片暖橙火把散景球与漂尘的色洗。她的眼睛向下落到平板屏幕，随即抬回前方走廊。她以安静的权威说道：「Final stretch. Forty feet.（最后一段。四十英尺。）」她的呼吸雾在冷空气中微微凝出，飘过散景。

Shot 3（镜头 3）（8–11s）：硬切至 Jax 的手接近侧柱上凿刻符文的紧密极特写，f/1.2 长焦拍摄。仅他在接触瞬间的指尖处于锐利焦点，他手的其余部分与墙壁表面消解进柔软奶油散景。最近的火把在雕刻上摇曳暖辉散光，个别尘点在光束中化作散景球泛光。他的手指滑过表面，指下尘土移动。焦点从石面拉到他护目镜后的眼睛——睁大，入迷，分了神。画外他的声音，因吸入尘土而略显沙哑，几近惊叹：「Yo. These markings. Look at 'em.（哟。这些刻纹。看呐。）」

Shot 4（镜头 4）（11–15s）：硬切至侧影中 Rain 的长焦中近景，f/1.2 拍摄——她的脸处于锐利焦点，前后走廊消解为橙色散景。她略微放下平板，眼睛眯起。蓝色平板辉光自她脸上退去，仅剩来自一侧的暖火把光。她微微转头，扫视走廊。她的本能被触动。她以比此前更低、更急、声音带着紧绷的语气说：「Guys. Something's off.（伙计们。有点不对劲。）」一个节拍。目光瞟向画外 Jax 在墙边之处。「Stay close.（跟紧。）」这句落下，安静却沉重。保持她的脸。切。

no music. sfx only（无配乐。仅环境音效）——身后破口处沉淀的碎石与流淌的石尘、更高处二次塌落的遥远轰鸣、爆炸余波后的轻微耳鸣锐音、火把依次点燃的连续性噗燃声（镜头 1 中有五或六次不同的点燃）、噼啪火焰带柔和喷鸣、Rain 平板电脑的微弱电子嗡响、她手指在平板屏幕上的轻敲、Rain 的「Final stretch. Forty feet.」带石混响、手指拂过凿刻石面带沙砾、Jax 的「Yo. These markings. Look at 'em.」（画外）、Rain 的「Guys. Something's off. Stay close.」带安静的紧迫、前方远处的滴水。
