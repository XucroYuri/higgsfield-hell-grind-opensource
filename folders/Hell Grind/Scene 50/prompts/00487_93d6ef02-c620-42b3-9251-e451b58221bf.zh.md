## 原文 (source)

Handheld long-lens medium shot of a young man running through an ancient corridor and suddenly hearing a scream from behind, 3 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — the camera alive in the operator's hands, tracking alongside the running character, reactive but not jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from torch flames forming bokeh orbs, slight chromatic aberration on edges, occasional focus drift, faint film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm orange torch accents and a small amount of cold blue light visible in deep background. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — deep crushed blacks, the character's face half-lit half-shadowed.

LOCATION: a tight ancient underground stone corridor, approximately two meters wide with low stone ceiling. The walls are weathered carved stone with glyphs and engravings, lit iron torches mounted on both side walls every few meters casting flickering warm orange light. Wet stone floor reflecting dim highlights. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust particles. In the deep background of the corridor, far ahead at its end, a distant carved stone gateway with cold light spilling through from beyond it — but a massive carved stone slab is visibly descending slowly from the ceiling above this gateway, having descended about halfway, the gap below the slab still tall but visibly shrinking. Arrows are embedded in both side walls of the corridor from a previous trap volley.

CHARACTER: a young man, mid-twenties, with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, a red bandage strip across the bridge of his nose, dark jacket, dust-covered face. He is in mid-run, body in full sprint motion, breath ragged, focus locked ahead toward the distant gateway. He is running away from camera direction (forward through the corridor toward the gateway).

ACTION: handheld camera tracks  the running man at his pace from behind. Camera trembles intensely with the run. Suddenly he HEARS something — a distant scream of his name from off-frame far behind him in the corridor (the sound is heard by the audience as well: a faint cracking "ROCO!!" with stone reverb echoing from the depth). His stride HESITATES sharply, his foot hitting wet stone slightly out of rhythm, body's forward momentum disrupted. His head WHIPS back sharply over his shoulder toward the source of the scream behind him. His face shows brutal conflict in a single second — eyes wide, jaw tight, the pull between forward duty and what's behind him visible across his expression. He stops, his focus split between ahead and behind, body in the moment of decision.

no music. sfx only — running boots pounding wet stone with deep stone reverb, ragged sprint breathing, the off-frame distant "ROCO!!" scream echoing from far behind with deep stone reverb, the running stride hesitating with audible pause and disruption in the footstep rhythm, sharp inhale of internal conflict, deep slow grinding stone-on-stone rumble from the distant slab descending, torches crackling on the walls.

## 中文对照 (zh)

手持长焦中景，一名年轻男子跑过一条古老走廊时突然听到身后传来一声尖叫，3 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持摄影带强烈受控的摇晃——摄影机在操机员手中鲜活，沿奔跑角色侧旁追踪，有反应但不跳抖。长焦长镜头摄影带浅景深，f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜片上可见灰尘、火把火焰形成散景球的强烈光晕（halation）、边缘轻微色差、偶尔的焦点漂移、隐约的胶片颗粒。电影感宽屏 16:9。重度去饱和调色带孤立的暖橙火把点缀与深背景中可见的一小点冷蓝光。戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）照明——压碎浓黑，角色脸一半照亮一半没入阴影。

LOCATION（地点）：约两米宽、低石质顶棚的狭窄古老地下石质走廊。墙壁是带符文与雕刻的经风化凿刻石墙，每隔几米在两侧墙壁上点亮的铁火把投出摇曳的暖橙光。湿滑石地反射昏暗高光。大气薄雾厚积漂浮尘粒子。走廊深背景、远端尽头，一处遥远的凿刻石门透着门后的冷光——但一块巨大的雕刻石板可见地自门上方顶棚缓缓下降，已下降约一半，石板下缝隙仍高但可见地收窄。走廊两侧墙上嵌着此前陷阱齐射的箭矢。

CHARACTER（角色）：一名年轻男子，二十多岁，深色凌乱齐肩长发，浅色小胡子，鼻梁覆着红色绷带条，深色夹克，蒙尘的脸。奔跑中，身体全力冲刺姿态，呼吸粗重，注意力锁定前方远处的门口。他正背朝摄影机方向跑（向前穿过走廊朝门口而去）。

ACTION（动作）：手持摄影机自背后以其步速追踪奔跑男子。摄影机随奔跑剧烈颤抖。突然他听到什么——画外他身后走廊远方传来一声他名字的遥远尖叫（观众也听到这声音：一声从纵深传来、带石混响的微弱破音「ROCO!!」）。他的步幅锐利迟疑（HESITATES sharply），脚略微失拍地踏上湿滑石地，身体的前行动量被打乱。他的头隔着肩锐利甩向身后尖叫的来源。他的脸在一秒内显出残酷的冲突——眼睛大睁，下颌绷紧，前方职责与身后之物的拉扯在他整个表情中可见。他停下，注意力分裂在前方与后方之间，身体处于决断的瞬间。

no music. sfx only（无配乐。仅环境音效）——奔跑靴子踏击湿滑地面带深石混响、粗重的冲刺呼吸、自远后方回荡的画外遥远「ROCO!!」尖叫带深石混响、奔跑步幅迟疑带脚步节奏中可闻的停顿与扰乱、内心冲突的锐利吸气、远处石板降下深层缓慢的石磨石轰鸣、墙壁上火把噼啪作响。
