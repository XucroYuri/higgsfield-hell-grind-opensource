## 原文 (source)

Handheld long-lens close-up shot of an ancient stone gateway slowly being squeezed shut by a descending stone ceiling, 1.5 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense reactive camera shake — alive in operator's hands, the operator drawn to the threat, frame trembling with anticipation. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from cold blue light beyond the gateway forming a bright bloom that bleeds into surrounding darkness, slight chromatic aberration on the high-contrast edge between cold light and dark stone, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm orange torch accents in foreground and intense cold blue light from beyond the gateway. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — deep crushed blacks, the gateway as a bright cold portal in the darkness ahead. Inspired by the cinematography of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: the deep end of an ancient underground stone corridor, looking forward at a carved stone gateway that opens to somewhere beyond filled with cold blue light. The MASSIVE STONE CEILING of the corridor is descending — the entire ceiling is grinding downward, and the part of it directly above the gateway is lower than the rest, having descended nearly down to the top of the gateway itself. The opening through the gateway is being SQUEEZED — what was once a tall doorway is now a horizontal slit of cold blue light, only the lower portion of the gateway opening still visible beneath the descending ceiling above it. The slit continues to narrow as the ceiling lowers. Dust and small stone fragments cascade from the descending ceiling at the gateway's top edge, falling into the slit of light. Carved glyphs visible on the underside of the descending ceiling. The stone walls of the gateway are weathered, cold, ancient. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust caught in the cold blue light beam.

ACTION: handheld camera positioned in the corridor at chest height, framed on a long-lens close-up of the closing gateway in the deep distance — telephoto compression making the gateway appear closer than it is, the cold blue light bleeding through the narrowing slit and washing the foreground stone in cool fill. The camera is unstable, trembling, the operator caught in the moment of recognizing the threat. The slit of cold blue light at the gateway is visibly NARROWING in real time during the 1.5 seconds — the descending ceiling slowly grinding lower, the bright opening compressing horizontally, halation streaming off the diminishing aperture and stretching across the lens. Dust pours down past the gateway from above in steady waves, individual particles glowing as bokeh against the cold blue light. The narrowing of the exit is the focal point — visible, urgent, threatening. Camera holds the gateway in frame, slight shake.

no music. sfx only — slow deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble dominating from above as the ceiling descends, the rumble louder than other shots because we are closer to the gateway threat, cascading dust and small stone fragments falling in waves from the descending ceiling at the gateway's top edge, faint cold hum or resonance from the cold blue light beyond the gateway, distant torches crackling somewhere behind in the corridor.

## 中文对照 (zh)

手持长焦特写镜头，拍一座古老石门被一块下落石质天花板缓缓挤压关闭，1.5 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影带强烈反应性摄影机颤动——在操机员手中「活着」、操机员被威胁吸引、画面带着期待颤抖。长焦远摄摄影、浅景深、f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜头上的可见灰尘、门廊之外冷蓝色光的强烈光晕（halation）形成一道渗入周围黑暗的明亮光晕，冷光与暗石之间的高对比边缘轻微色差、偶发温和的焦点漂移、淡淡的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和的调色板，前景带孤立的暖橙火把点缀、门廊之外带强烈的冷蓝色光。戏剧性明暗对照照明——压死深黑、门廊作为前方黑暗中的明亮寒冷通道。受 Zack Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）摄影启发。

场景（LOCATION）：古老地下石质走廊的深处末端，向前看到一座雕石门廊，通向某处、填满冷蓝色光。走廊的巨大石质天花板正在下落（MASSIVE STONE CEILING）——整片天花板在向下碾磨，其中正对门廊上方的部分低于其余部分，已几乎降到门廊顶部本身。穿过门廊的开口正被挤压（SQUEEZED）——曾经的极高门洞现在是一道水平窄缝（slit）的冷蓝色光，在它上方下落天花板之下，只有门廊开口下部仍可见。缝隙随天花板降低而继续收窄。粉尘与小石碎块从门廊顶缘的下落天花板处倾泻、落入光缝中。下落天花板底面可见雕刻的符号。门廊的石墙风化、寒冷、古老。大气雾霭浓重，冷蓝光束中捕捉到的飘浮尘埃。

动作（ACTION）：手持摄影机置于走廊中胸高，取景为深远距离处关闭门廊的长焦特写——长焦压缩使门廊显得比实际更近，冷蓝色光透过不断收窄的缝隙渗出、以冷色补光洗过前景石头。摄影机不稳、颤抖，操机员陷入认出威胁的时刻。门廊处的冷蓝光缝隙在 1.5 秒内以实时可见地收窄（NARROWING）——下降的天花板缓慢碾低、明亮的开口水平地压缩、光晕（halation）从不断缩小的孔径流布、横跨镜头伸展。粉尘从上方成稳定浪涌掠过门廊落下，个别颗粒在冷蓝色光前作为焦外光（bokeh）发光。出口的收窄是焦点所在——可见、迫切、构成威胁。摄影机把门廊收入画面、轻微颤抖。

无音乐。仅音效——天花板下落时石怼石碾磨的缓慢深沉轰鸣从上方主导、轰鸣比别的镜头更响因为我们更贴近门廊威胁、门廊顶缘下落天花板处粉尘与小石碎块成浪涌下落、门廊之外冷蓝色光发出微弱的冷色嗡鸣或共振、身后走廊某处远处火把噼啪。
