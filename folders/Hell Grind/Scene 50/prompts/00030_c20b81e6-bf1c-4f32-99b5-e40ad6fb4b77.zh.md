Narrow ancient stone corridor, arrow volley impact and slab descent beginning, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: cinematic anamorphic 2.39:1, long-lens telephoto cinematography, shallow depth of field, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from torch flames, deep blacks with halated warm orange highlights, atmospheric dust haze. Heavily desaturated palette. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide, low ceiling. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse. Ahead — distant glowing gateway with cold blue light. Lit iron torches on both walls. Wall ports actively firing arrows from both sides.

CHARACTERS (pinned flat on the floor):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Pressed flat to wet stone, Roco's body covering hers protectively.
— Jax (): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Pressed flat on his own a few meters back, arms over his head.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Lying on top of Rain, shielding her with his body, his back exposed.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Long-lens close-up on Rain's face turned sideways against the wet stone, only her eye in sharp focus, silver hair fanned across the floor. Roco's shoulder visible in soft foreground bokeh covering her. Above her, arrows STREAK through frame in real-time speed in overlapping waves, motion-blur streaks slicing through the orange torch bokeh. Her hair lifts from the wakes. She does not blink.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Extreme close-up of the stone wall, camera inches from the carved surface. An arrow SLAMS into the stone full-bleed in frame, iron tip splintering rock as it embeds, wooden shaft VIBRATING violently in front of the lens. A second arrow slams in beside it. A third. Stone chips spalling outward, dust puffing.

Shot 3 (6–9s): Long-lens medium shot of the opposite wall in Dutch angle. Multiple arrows continue SLAMMING into the carved stone in staggered volley, wooden shafts vibrating, dust clouds erupting. Camera slightly canted, micro-shake on each impact. One arrow embeds in Roco's deactivated hoverboard lying on the floor in foreground bokeh — the board jerks.

Shot 4 (9–12s): Long-lens close-up on Roco's face from above, lying on top of Rain. His eyes squeezed shut, jaw clenched, breathing hard. The arrow whooshes around them are thinning out — the volley is ending. He cracks one eye open and lifts his head slightly. He looks past Rain toward the distant gateway off-frame. His expression shifts from survival to dread.

Shot 5 (12–15s): Long-lens low-angle shot down the corridor at shallow focus. Foreground: prone bodies of Roco covering Rain, Jax behind, all in soft focus. Mid-ground: arrows embedded in both walls, wooden shafts still trembling. Deep background in razor focus: the open gateway with cold blue light streaming through. A massive carved stone slab BEGINS to descend from the ceiling above the gateway — slow, inexorable. The bright cold blue light begins to compress as the slab lowers. Deep grinding rumble. Hold.

LOCATION（场景）：约两米宽的狭窄远古石走廊，低矮天花板。角色身后——新鲜的瓦砾塌落。前方——带冷蓝光的远处发光门道。两侧墙上点着铁火炬。墙面石孔正从两侧持续发射箭矢。

CHARACTERS（角色）（伏在平地上）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银金色头发，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新伤口，灰色连帽夹克，蒙尘。贴平在湿石上，Roco 的身体护着她。
— Jax（）：黄色寸头，深肤色，清晰眼镜，深色夹克，蒙尘。孤身贴平在几米之后，双臂护在头顶。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱头发，胡须，鼻上有红色绷带，深色夹克，蒙尘。躺在 Rain 之上，用身体护着她，背部暴露在外。

Shot 1（第 0–3 秒）：长焦特写，Rain 的脸侧转贴住湿石，只有她的眼处于锐焦，银发如扇子般铺开在在地板上。Roco 的肩在柔和前景散景中可见、罩住她。她上方，箭矢以实时速度、重叠波次横掠画幅，运动模糊拖影切开橙色火炬散景。她的头发被尾迹扬起。她双眼不眨。

Shot 4（第 9–12 秒）：长焦特写，从上方俯视躺在 Rain 之上的 Roco 的脸。双眼紧阖，下颌紧咬，喘着粗气。周围箭的呼啸正在变稀——齐射临近结束。他睁开一只眼、微抬抬头。他越过 Rain 望向画外的远处门道。他的神情从求生切换为恐惧。
