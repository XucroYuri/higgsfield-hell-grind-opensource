Refer to the man with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, dust-streaked face, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, askew protective glasses, exhausted bloodied face, dark jacket from Image 2.
Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, fresh cut on her temple bleeding, grey hooded jacket from Image 3.

Multi-shot escape finale, 14 seconds, 5 shots, 16:9, ARRI ALEXA cinematic photorealism, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur on impacts, sharp focus through sweat and dust, no music.

Scene: ancient stone corridor, torches flickering on both walls, wet stone floor reflecting fire. At the far end, a massive stone slab descends slowly from the ceiling above a carved gateway, the gap below now ankle-to-knee height, almost sealed. The woman is on the safe side beyond the gateway, her body forced flat onto her belly, upper torso shoved back through the narrowing gap into the dangerous corridor side — slab pressing down hard on her shoulders pinning her, her face in the gap, arms reaching desperately toward the running men. The two men are sprinting the last meters. Cold blue light spills through the gap from the safe side, mixing with the warm orange torch light of the corridor — creating a hard split-tone at the threshold. Thick atmospheric dust drifts through both light sources catching as visible particles in every beam.

Shot 1 (3.5s) — DUTCH-ANGLE EXTREME CLOSE-UP ON RAIN'S FACE IN THE GAP:
Camera tilted hard at 30 degrees, positioned inside the corridor at floor level pointing forward at her face squeezed under the descending slab. Cold blue light underlights her face from behind through the gap, warm torch light catches her brow from above. Tears mix with sweat and dust on her cheeks. Her hair fans out across the wet stone floor in the gap. The bottom edge of the descending stone slab visible across the upper third of frame, slowly grinding lower in real time, dust raining from where the slab meets stone. Veins stand out on her neck. She SCREAMS forward, voice cracking, raw: "COME ON!" Saliva flecks. Camera trembles violently with her scream as if recoiling.

Shot 2 (2s) — LOW BEHIND-SHOULDER ON ROCO, JAX HURLED FORWARD:
Camera at hip height immediately behind Roco's right shoulder, his shove shot through over his shoulder as Jax launches forward into deep frame. Roco's hands shove hard into Jax's back. Jax's body pitches forward, foot catches on a raised stone, body tipping into uncontrolled fall, arms windmilling. The slab and gap visible in deep background ahead, getting closer. Camera lurches forward with the shove energy, then snaps to a halt. Dust kicks up from boot impact.

Shot 3 (3s) — GROUND-LEVEL TRACKING SHOT ALONGSIDE JAX'S TUMBLE:
Camera on the wet stone floor, low as Jax's hip, racing alongside his rolling body as he crashes and tumbles across the last meters of corridor. Frame shakes hard with each impact of his body against stone — shoulder, hip, shoulder, hip — water splashing from wet floor onto lens. As he reaches the gap, Rain's hands SLAM into his jacket from inside the threshold and YANK him hard sideways under the slab through the gap. His body whips out of frame to safe side. Camera holds at threshold momentarily, stunned by the sudden absence.

Shot 4 (1.5s) — UNDER-SLAB TIGHT CLOSE-UP ON ROCO'S EYES:
Camera mounted very low at the threshold ground level, looking back into the corridor at Roco standing alone meters away. The bottom edge of the slab cuts across the upper frame, framing Roco within the shrinking horizontal slit. Roco is rim-lit by torches behind him, his face mostly in shadow, only his eyes catch the cold blue light from behind camera. Sweat drips from his chin. He breathes once, hard, jaw clenches. The slab continues lowering — the horizontal frame shrinking around him. He bursts forward toward camera. Camera holds dead still.

Shot 5 (4s) — GROUND-LEVEL SIDE PROFILE ON THE DIVE, IMPACT, SILENCE:
Camera at the threshold from the side, ground level looking horizontally across the gap. Roco LAUNCHES into the air, body horizontal, arms extended forward Superman-style, sliding through the air toward the gap. He hits the wet floor with a heavy slap and skids hard on his stomach toward the slit. His back grazes the descending slab as he slides under — fabric tearing audibly against stone, sparks of friction. His head, shoulders, torso pass under. As his feet clear the threshold the SLAB CRASHES DOWN with massive stone-on-stone impact, dust exploding violently outward in a wave that washes over the camera lens. Frame shakes hard, then falls completely still. Audio drops to total silence. Slowly, only the sound of three sets of ragged exhausted breathing emerges from beyond the sealed gateway. Hold on the sealed slab in stillness.

Camera: handheld with intensity that escalates per shot — Shot 1 trembles violently with the scream, Shot 2 lurches with the shove, Shot 3 races and shakes with body impacts, Shot 4 holds dead still in held breath, Shot 5 explodes with the slab impact then freezes into total stillness. Lighting: practical torches cast warm orange with hard angular shadows, cold blue from beyond gateway pierces through the gap creating split-tone at threshold, deep crushed blacks throughout, dust visible in every beam. Sound design: continuous slab grind throughout building to climax, Rain's raw "COME ON!" with stone reverb, body impacts against wet stone, fabric scraping descending slab edge, final massive slab CRASH with dust impact wave, then total silence broken only by ragged breathing.


## 中文对照 (zh)

参照 Image 1 中那名深色凌乱齐肩长发、浅色小胡子、鼻上红色绷带、脸上带尘痕、深色连帽夹克的男子。
参照 Image 2 中那名漂白黄板寸、深肤色、护目镜歪戴、疲惫带血的脸、深色夹克的男子。
参照 Image 3 中那名银金色头发、太阳穴一道流血新伤口、灰色连帽夹克的女子。

多镜逃命终局，14 秒，5 镜头，16:9，ARRI ALEXA 电影写实，浓重 35mm 胶片颗粒，撞击上运动模糊，穿透汗与尘的锐利对焦，无配乐。

场景：远古石廊，两侧墙火把闪烁，湿石地面映着火。远端，一扇巨大石板自一座雕凿拱门上方的天花板缓缓降下，其下缝隙现为踝到膝高、几乎封死。女子在拱门另一侧的安全侧，身体被迫平趴成肚贴地，上躯干被硬塞回收窄的缝隙回到危险走廊侧——石板沉重地压在她肩上把她钉住，她的脸在缝隙里，双臂绝望地够向奔跑中的男人们。两名男子正冲刺最后几米。冷蓝光穿过从安全侧的缝隙泄出、与走廊温暖橙火把光混合——在门槛处造就一道硬邦邦的分裂色调（split-tone）。浓厚大气尘土穿过两束光源漂移、在每道光束中作为可见颗粒被捕获。

Shot 1（3.5 秒）——DUTCH-ANGLE EXTREME CLOSE-UP ON RAIN'S FACE IN THE GAP（荷兰角缝隙中 Rain 面部的超特写）：
机位硬倾 30 度、置于走廊内地面高度、朝前指向她被压在降下石板下的脸。冷蓝光透过缝隙从她身后兜底照亮她的脸、暖火把光自上方捕捉她的眉。泪水与汗、尘混在她面颊上。她的头发在缝隙里的湿石地面上扇展开。降下石板的底缘在画面最上方三分之一可见、实时缓缓研磨得更低，石板与石相接处下灰如雨。她颈部青筋凸起。她向前 SCREAMS（尖叫），声音迸裂、raw："COME ON！（加油！）"唾沫星子。摄影机随她的尖叫剧烈颤抖、仿佛退缩。

Shot 2（2 秒）——LOW BEHIND-SHOULDER ON ROCO, JAX HURLED FORWARD（Roco 的低肩后机位，Jax 被抛向前）：
机位在 Roco 右肩正后方的髋部高度、他的猛推隔过他肩头拍到，Jax 向前射出进深画面。Roco 双手使劲推进 Jax 的背。Jax 的身体向前倾倒、脚被一块凸起的石头绊住、身体倾进失控的摔倒、双臂风车般乱挥。石板与缝隙在纵深背景前方可见、越来越近。摄影机随猛推的能量踉跄前冲、随即啪地顿停。靴撞击扬起尘土。

Shot 3（3 秒）——GROUND-LEVEL TRACKING SHOT ALONGSIDE JAX'S TUMBLE（贴着 Jax 翻滚的地面高度跟踪镜头）：
机位在湿石地面上、低到 Jax 髋部、在他撞倒并翻滚过最后几米走廊时并排追着他的翻滚身体疾驰。画框随他身体每次撞石——肩、髋、肩、髋——而猛烈晃动，湿地板的水溅上镜头。他到达缝隙时，Rain 的双手从门槛内部 SLAM（猛拍）进他的夹克、把他猛拽向侧、从缝隙石板下硬拽过去。他的身体甩出画面到了安全侧。摄影机在门槛处停了一瞬、被这突然空出的身影怔住。

Shot 4（1.5 秒）——UNDER-SLAB TIGHT CLOSE-UP ON ROCO'S EYES（石板下 Roco 双眼的紧凑特写）：
机位装得很低、在门槛地面高度、回望走廊里数米外独站的 Roco。石板底缘横切过画面最上方、把 Roco 框在逐渐收窄的水平裂隙里。Roco 被他身后火把勾出轮廓光、他脸大半处于阴影中、只有眼睛捕捉到机位后的冷蓝光。汗从他下巴滴落。他困难地呼吸一次、牙关紧咬。石板继续下降——水平画框在他四周收窄。他朝机位爆冲向前。摄影机死寂地稳住。

Shot 5（4 秒）——GROUND-LEVEL SIDE PROFILE ON THE DIVE, IMPACT, SILENCE（俯冲的地面高度侧影，撞击，寂静）：
机位在门槛旁侧、地面高度、水平望过缝隙。Roco LAUNCHES（纵身）腾空、身体水平、双臂超人身姿前伸、滑过空气朝缝隙飞去。他带一声沉重拍击撞上湿地、用腹部朝裂缝硬滑去。他的背擦着降下的石板掠过——布料贴石可闻地撕裂、带摩擦火花。他的头、肩、躯干钻过去。他脚一离门槛，石板以巨大的石撞石撞击 CRASHES DOWN（轰然砸下）、尘土以一波猛烈的浪爆开、淹过镜头。画框剧烈晃动、随即完全静止。音频跌至全寂。缓慢地，只有三组粗重精疲力竭的呼吸声自封死的拱门另一侧浮出。静止中停在封死的石板上。

Camera（摄影机）：手持、强度逐镜头升级——Shot 1 随尖叫剧烈颤抖、Shot 2 随猛推踉跄、Shot 3 随身体撞击疾驰摇晃、Shot 4 屏息死寂稳住、Shot 5 随石板撞击爆发随即冻结进全静。Lighting（光照）：实用火把投暖橙带硬角形阴影、拱门外的冷蓝穿透缝隙在门槛处造就分裂色调、全程深压黑影、尘在每道光束中可见。Sound design（声音设计）：持续石板研磨全程推到高潮、Rain raw 的"COME ON！"带石回响、身体撞击湿石、布料刮擦降下石板边缘、最终巨大石板 CRASH 带尘土撞击波、随后全寂仅被粗重呼吸打破。
