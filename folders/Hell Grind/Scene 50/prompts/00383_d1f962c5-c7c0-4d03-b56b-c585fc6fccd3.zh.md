## 原文 (source)

A continuous handheld long-take medium shot following a young man rescuing his friend from a stone pit in an ancient corridor — the camera moves with the action through the entire sequence, capturing the rescue as one breath. 9 seconds, single continuous shot, no cuts.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Roco is rescuing his friend Jax who has fallen into a hidden pit in an ancient stone corridor. Jax is hanging from the pit edge by his fingertips, dangling above stone spikes far below, terrified. Roco reaches him at full sprint, drops to his knee at the pit edge, extends his cybernetic crystalline prosthetic right arm — a translucent glass-like limb with cold blue internal glow — down to Jax. Their hands meet but the crystal surface is too smooth and frictionless: Jax's fingers slip off, and he drops back, catching himself again on the pit edge by the fingertips, even more terrified. Roco realizes the problem instantly. He slaps a control on his shoulder and the crystalline prosthetic deactivates — the cold blue glow fades, the glass-like outer layer retracts and folds away, exposing his real human flesh hand beneath. He reaches down again with the bare flesh hand, grips Jax's wrist with full friction this time, and HEAVES him out of the pit with one violent motion of pure strength. Both bodies crash onto the wet stone floor of the corridor, sprawled and gasping. Throughout, a massive stone slab is descending slowly over a distant gateway exit, the time pressure constant.

CINEMATOGRAPHY: shot in the visual language of Emmanuel Lubezki — a continuous handheld long-take with the camera moving organically through the action, never cutting, the operator following the bodies and emotions like a witness in the room. The camera floats and breathes, sometimes close on faces, sometimes pulling back to capture full action, sometimes drifting in to find a detail, but always in one fluid motion. Camera shake is alive but never jittery — it's the operator's body in the space. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture, focus pulls happening organically as the camera reframes through the action — focus searching, finding, occasionally drifting before catching the subject again. Lens imperfections: visible dust on the lens catching light, aggressive halation from torch flames forming bokeh orbs, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges between warm torchlight and the cold blue prosthetic glow, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9.

LIGHTING in the language of Greig Fraser — practical motivated lighting only, the iron torches on the corridor walls as the sole warm key sources casting hard angular orange light from the sides, deep crushed blacks dominating the frame with detail preserved in the shadows (ARRI-style deep latitude). The cold blue glow from the prosthetic adds a contrasting cool fill on Roco's face and the pit edge during the early portion, then disappears as the prosthetic deactivates. After deactivation, the lighting collapses entirely to warm torch tones — the shift in color temperature itself is part of the storytelling. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm orange torch tones and brief cold blue prosthetic glow. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust.

LOCATION: a tight ancient underground stone corridor approximately two meters wide. Walls weathered carved stone densely covered in deep glyph and rune carvings, lit iron torches mounted on both side walls every few meters casting flickering warm orange light, wet stone floor reflecting dim torch highlights. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust particles caught in the torch beams. In the middle of the corridor floor, a hidden trap pit has opened — a dark vertical shaft cut into the stone, about two meters deep, with rough hewn stone walls dropping into darkness, stone spikes barely visible at the bottom far below.

His RIGHT ARM from the elbow down is initially a CRYSTALLINE PROSTHETIC — a translucent glass-like sculpted forearm and hand made of crystal-like material with faint internal cold-blue glow visible inside the crystal, the surface smooth and reflective like polished glass, alien against his organic body, integrated at the elbow with a visible mechanical seam. A small control pad is on his right shoulder where his left hand can reach.
— Jax <<<image_1>>>: young man, mid-twenties, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses partially askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked face. Hanging from the upper edge of the pit by his fingertips, body dangling into darkness, arms straining hard.

ACTION (one continuous handheld camera movement, 9 seconds):

(0–1s) — The shot OPENS with the camera at chest height in the corridor, framed on Roco approaching at full sprint from background depth toward camera. The torchlight from both walls catches him as he runs, halated bokeh orbs of flame on both sides receding behind him. Camera tracks his approach with subtle handheld breathing.

(1–2s) — As Roco reaches the pit edge, the camera DROPS WITH HIM as he goes to one knee — the camera lowers organically to follow his body downward in one fluid handheld motion, ending up at the level of his shoulder beside the pit edge, three-quarter angle on his face and the dark pit opening before him. His knee slams onto the wet stone with force, dust impacts, his body folding into position.

(2–3s) — Camera reframes slightly as Roco extends his crystalline right arm DOWN into the pit. The arm enters the lower portion of the frame, the cold blue glow lighting up the pit walls and Roco's face from below. Camera tilts down slightly to follow the arm into the pit, then back up as Jax's hand reaches up from below to meet the crystal hand. The cold blue glow and the warm torch light battle for color across Roco's intent face.

(3–4s) — Their hands clasp briefly. Camera pushes slightly closer to capture the contact. Then immediately the failure — Jax's fingers SLIDE on the smooth crystal surface with audible friction-glide. His weight pulls him down, fingers slipping, and he DROPS out of the lower frame. Roco's eyes widen — the camera catches his face in tight close-up as the failure registers. A second later, the slap of Jax's hands catching the pit edge below again, his terrified gasp from off-frame.

(4–5.5s) — Roco reacts instantly. Camera holds tight on his face as his LEFT hand snaps up to a control pad on his right shoulder. Camera reframes slightly to capture both his face and the prosthetic shoulder area. He PRESSES the control. The crystalline arm responds — the cold blue internal glow PULSES once, then begins to FADE rapidly. The translucent crystal outer layer RETRACTS, glass-like segments folding back along the length of the arm, mechanical transformation visible. The cold blue light dies, the entire warm orange torch palette taking over. Beneath where the crystal was: a NORMAL HUMAN FLESH ARM and HAND, bare skin, sweat and dust on the newly exposed flesh.

(5.5–7s) — Camera follows Roco's now-flesh hand as he REACHES BACK DOWN into the pit with renewed urgency, the camera tilting to track his arm's motion. His bare flesh hand SLAMS DOWN, GRIPS Jax's wrist hard, flesh-on-flesh contact with full friction this time. Roco's other hand braces on the pit edge for leverage. He GRUNTS with maximum effort, his entire body straining as he begins to HEAVE upward.

(7–9s) — In one violent pulling motion, Roco HAULS Jax up out of the pit. The camera pulls back slightly to capture the full action — Jax's body emerging from below the frame edge, dragged upward by the wrist, first arm, then shoulder, then head and torso coming up over the pit edge. The momentum of the heave throws BOTH bodies BACKWARDS away from the pit. The camera tracks their fall, both bodies crashing down hard onto the wet stone floor of the corridor in a sprawled exhausted heap. Dust explodes on impact. The camera settles low on the floor with them, capturing the two bodies side by side, both gasping for breath, chests heaving violently. Hold on the exhausted pair for the final beat. Camera trembles with their breathing.

## 中文对照 (zh)

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：一名叫 Roco 的年轻男子正在拯救坠入古老石廊隐藏陷阱坑的朋友 Jax。Jax 仅凭指尖挂在坑沿，悬在深处石尖之上，惊恐万分。Roco 以全速冲刺抵达，单膝跪在坑沿，把机械感晶体假肢的右臂——一条带冷蓝内部光的半透明玻璃般肢体——伸向 Jax。他们的手相遇，但晶体表面太光滑、毫无摩擦：Jax 的手指滑脱，他跌落回去、再次仅凭指尖在坑沿接住自己，更加惊恐。Roco 瞬间明白了问题。他拍了一下肩上的控制装置，晶体假肢解除——冷蓝光淡去，玻璃般外壳收回折叠、露出他底下真实的人类血肉之手。他再次以赤裸的血肉之手伸下，这次以全摩擦握住 Jax 的手腕，以一次纯力量的暴烈动作把他「猛拽」出坑。两具身体重重砸在走廊的湿滑石地板上，瘫作一团、大口喘息。全程，一块巨石板正缓慢降过远处通道出口，时间压力持续不断。

CINEMATOGRAPHY（电影摄影）：以 Emmanuel Lubezki 的视觉语言拍摄——连续手持长镜头，摄影机有机地穿过动作移动、从不剪辑，操机员如同房间里的一名目击者追随身体与情绪。摄影机漂浮呼吸，有时贴近脸部、有时拉远捕捉完整动作、有时飘入寻找一处细节，但始终处于一种流体的运动中。摄影机抖动活生生但从不忙乱——那是操机员的身体在场。长焦远景电影拍摄，浅景深，f/1.8 光圈，随着摄影机在动作中重构图、拉焦有机发生——对焦搜寻、抓住、偶尔漂移、随后再次追上主体。镜头瑕疵：镜头上可见尘埃捕捉光线、火炬火焰聚成弧状散景光球的咄咄逼人光晕（halation）、暖火炬光与冷蓝假肢光之间高反差边缘的轻微色差、淡淡的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。

LIGHTING（灯光）——以 Greig Fraser 的语言——仅实用光源动因（practical motivated lighting），走廊壁上的铁制火炬是唯一暖色主光源、从两侧投下硬朗棱角橙色光，深重碾碎的黑色主导画面、阴影中保留细节（ARRI 式深宽容度）。早期部分，假肢的冷蓝光在 Roco 的脸与坑沿上加了对比的冷色补光，随后随假肢解除而消失。解除之后，灯光完全坍缩为暖火炬色调——色温的转变本身就是叙事的一部分。高度去饱和的调色板，带孤立暖橙色火炬色调与短暂的冷蓝假肢光。大气雾霾厚重、漂浮尘埃。

LOCATION（场地）：一条狭窄的古老地下石廊，约两米宽。墙壁为饱经风化的凿花石、密布深埋的符文与雕饰，两侧壁上每几米装有照明的铁制火炬、投下闪烁的暖橙色光，湿滑石地板反射暗淡火炬高光。大气雾霾厚重、漂浮尘埃微粒被火炬光束捕捉。走廊地板正中，一个隐藏的陷阱坑已经翻开——一条切入石中的暗色竖井，约两米深，粗凿的石壁落入黑暗，深长下方几不可见的石尖。
