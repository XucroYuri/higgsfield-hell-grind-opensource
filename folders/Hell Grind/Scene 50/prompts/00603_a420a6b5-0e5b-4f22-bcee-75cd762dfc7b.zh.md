## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, arrival sequence in the immediate aftermath of an explosion, 15 seconds.

SCENE CONTEXT: this scene begins seconds after a violent explosion collapsed the passage above, sending the three characters falling/sliding down through the rubble into this narrow corridor below. They have just arrived. Rain and Roco are riding hoverboards (compact tech they had with them); Jax is INJURED and SEMI-CONSCIOUS from the blast, unable to ride his own board, and is being CARRIED on Roco's hoverboard with him — Roco supports Jax's weight, one arm wrapped around him, keeping him upright. Dust still raining down, debris settling, ears ringing.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide with low ceiling around two and a half meters high. BEHIND the characters — a massive fresh rubble collapse blocking retreat, the way they fell through, dust pouring down from the breach above. AHEAD of the characters — an open carved stone gateway at the far end, faint cold blue light spilling through it from beyond.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars pressed close along both side walls, candles flickering in alcoves (some knocked over by blast vibration), thick volumetric dust haze. Anamorphic widescreen, teal-shadow color grade with warm candle accents, naturalistic low-key lighting.

Characters (just survived a blast):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): young woman, silver-blonde shoulder-length hair, small scar by eye, fresh small cut on temple, grey hooded jacket over black top, dark cargo pants, heavy dust on clothes, hair disheveled. RIDING her own hoverboard with faint purple glow underneath. Lead, alert, recovering fast.
— Roco (@image_4): young man, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage across bridge of nose, dark jacket, dust-covered. RIDING his own hoverboard with faint red glow underneath, ONE ARM WRAPPED AROUND Jax supporting his weight. Strained, focused, protective.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): young man, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked, head lolling, eyes half-open, semi-conscious. Boots dragging, supported entirely by Roco. INJURED.

Shot 1 (0–6s): Camera starts low at floor level pointed UP at the ceiling breach above the rubble pile behind the characters — broken stone, dust pouring down in thick shafts, faint daylight from the surface filtering through. Hold for a beat on the destruction overhead. Then camera CRANES upward and rotates 180 degrees, descending in front of the characters as they emerge — Rain on her hoverboard leading, Roco just behind on his board with Jax leaning heavily against him. The crane lands at chest height in front of them, tracking backward as they advance. Rain looks ahead toward the distant gateway, calculates, then says clearly with quiet authority: "Straight through. Forty feet." Single continuous move.

Shot 2 (6–10s): Cut to a tight Dutch-angle medium two-shot of Roco and Jax sharing the hoverboard. Roco's jaw tight, sweat and dust on his face, arm visibly straining around Jax's shoulders. Jax's head rolls against Roco's shoulder, eyes flutter, glasses crooked. Roco mutters, low and steady: "Stay with me. Stay with me." Jax's eyes begin to focus weakly. Camera slowly straightens out.

Shot 3 (10–12s): Profile tracking shot of Rain on her own hoverboard ahead, camera locked to her shoulder in a tight arc-track. Carved pillars sweep past in background like vertical bars, strobing parallax. Her face stays in profile, jaw set, the cut on her temple catching candlelight. She glances back briefly — checking on the others — then forward again.

Shot 4 (12–15s): Cut to a vertical TILT-DOWN from the low ceiling — camera starts on carved glyphs above, descends slowly past a hanging candle still swaying from blast vibration, finds the trio from a high angle as they glide directly under the lens. Rain passes through frame first. Then Roco with Jax follows — Jax's head finally lifts, eyes focusing. He is coming back. Cut.

no music. sfx only — settling debris and trickling stone dust from the breach behind, distant rumble of secondary collapse far above, faint ringing tinnitus from blast aftermath, dual hoverboard hum (Rain's and Roco's), Jax's ragged breathing, Roco's strained breath, Rain's clear "Straight through. Forty feet." with stone reverb, soft "stay with me" murmur, candle flicker, distant drip from ahead.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的古老石质走廊，爆炸刚刚结束后的抵达序列，15 秒。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：本场景始于一次暴力爆炸压塌上方通道、令三名角色沿碎石滑落掉入下方这条狭窄走廊的几秒之后。他们刚刚抵达。Rain 与 Roco 骑着悬浮滑板（他们随身带的紧凑科技）；Jax 在爆炸中受伤且半昏迷，无法骑自己的滑板，正与 Roco 同乘 Roco 的悬浮滑板——Roco 撑住 Jax 的体重，一只手臂环抱住他，让他保持直立。尘土仍在倾泻，碎石沉淀，耳鸣回响。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（地点几何）：约两米宽的紧走廊，低顶棚约两米半高。角色身后（BEHIND）——一处巨大的新鲜塌落碎石堆封住退路，即他们坠落的来路，尘土自上方破口倾泻而下。角色前方（AHEAD）——远端一处敞开的凿刻石门，门后透出微弱的冷蓝光。

Setting（环境）：湿滑石地，两侧墙壁旁紧贴地立着遍布符文的立柱，壁龛中摇曳的蜡烛（几支被爆炸震动震倒），浓重的体积尘土薄雾。变形宽银幕宽屏，青影（teal-shadow）色调分级带暖烛点缀，自然主义低调照明。

Characters（角色）（刚经历爆炸幸存）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：年轻女子，银色金发齐肩长发，眼睛旁道小疤，太阳穴一道新小割口，黑色上衣外套灰色连帽夹克，深色工装裤，衣服上厚积尘土，头发凌乱。骑着她自己的悬浮滑板，板下带微弱紫色辉光。领队，机警，快速恢复。
— Roco（@image_4）：年轻男子，深色凌乱齐肩长发，浅色小胡子，鼻梁覆着红色绷带，深色夹克，蒙尘。骑着他自己的悬浮滑板，板下带微弱红色辉光，一只手臂环抱着 Jax 撑住他的体重。勉强、专注、护护。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：年轻男子，漂白黄色圆寸，深肤色，清晰护目镜歪斜，深色连帽夹克，蒙尘结垢，头耷拉着，眼睛半睁，半昏迷。靴子拖着，完全由 Roco 支撑。受伤。

Shot 1（镜头 1）（0–6s）：摄影机始自地面高度低位，上指角色身后碎石堆上方顶棚的破口——断裂的石头、以浓密光柱倾泻的尘土、地表渗下的隐约日光。在头顶的破坏上保持一个节拍。随即摄影机向上摇起（CRANES upward）并旋转 180 度，在三人现身时下降到他们前方——Rain 骑滑板领前，Roco 稍后在滑板之上，Jax 重重靠向他。摇臂落在他们胸前高度，随他们前进而后退追踪。Rain 望向远处的门口，估算了一下，随即带着安静的权威清楚说道：「Straight through. Forty feet.（直穿过去。四十英尺。）」单一连续移动。

Shot 2（镜头 2）（6–10s）：切至 Roco 与 Jax 共用滑板的紧密荷兰角（Dutch-angle）中景双人镜头。Roco 下颌绷紧，脸上汗尘，手臂可见地绷紧环住 Jax 的肩。Jax 的头枕着 Roco 的肩滚动，眼睛颤动，护目镜歪斜。Roco 低声、低沉而平稳地说：「Stay with me. Stay with me.（撑住。跟我撑住。）」Jax 的眼睛开始微弱聚焦。摄影机缓缓扶正。

Shot 3（镜头 3）（10–12s）：Rain 独立滑板在前的侧影追踪镜头，摄影机锁定她的肩做一道紧密弧线追踪。凿刻立柱如垂直的条杆在背景中掠过，频闪视差。她的脸保持侧影，下颌咬定，太阳穴上的割口接住烛光。她短暂回头——查看其他人——随即又转向前方。

Shot 4（镜头 4）（12–15s）：切至自低顶棚向下的一道垂直俯拍（TILT-DOWN）——摄影机始自上方的凿刻符文，缓缓下落掠过一支仍在因爆炸震动而摇晃的悬挂蜡烛，自一个高角度找到三人正滑行到镜头正下方。Rain 先穿过画面。随后带着 Jax 的 Roco 跟上——Jax 的头终于抬起，眼睛聚焦。他正在回来。切。

no music. sfx only（无配乐。仅环境音效）——身后破口处沉淀的碎石与流淌的石尘、更高处二次塌落的遥远轰鸣、爆炸余波后的耳鸣锐音、双悬浮滑板轰鸣（Rain 与 Roco 的）、Jax 粗重的呼吸、Roco 勉强的呼吸、Rain 清晰的「Straight through. Forty feet.」带石混响、轻柔的「stay with me」低语、蜡烛噼闪、前方远处滴水。
