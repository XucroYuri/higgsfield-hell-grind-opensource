## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, opening sequence with awakening torches and team briefing, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with constant subtle camera shake and organic operator movement, the camera always alive and breathing in the operator's hands. Long-lens telephoto cinematography (85mm equivalent) at f/1.2 aperture, extreme shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh. Lens imperfections embraced — visible dust particles on the lens catching light flares, halation bleeding aggressively from torch flames into surrounding darkness, chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional brief focus drift as camera shifts in operator's hands, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with deep amber-brown earth tones, isolated warm orange torch accents in pure black surrounding darkness, faint cold blue glow from Rain's tablet on her face. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from torches only — deep crushed blacks dominating most of the frame.

SCENE CONTEXT: this scene begins seconds after a violent explosion collapsed the passage above, sending the three characters falling into this corridor below. The corridor RESPONDS to their presence — wall-mounted torches IGNITE one by one in sequence deeper down the passage. Each character begins examining the location in their own way. Rain consults a handheld tablet showing schematics. Jax is drawn to carved script on the wall. Roco scans the corridor tactically. Then Rain feels something wrong, warns the team, and begins walking forward.

LOCATION: a long ancient underground stone corridor with low rough-hewn stone ceiling. Walls covered in horizontal bands of carved ancient script — rows of weathered glyph-letters running along the entire length of both walls. Iron torch brackets mounted at angle on the walls every few meters, holding short flame-torches. Flagstone floor of irregular cracked stone slabs. Small carved stone tablets and rubble propped against the bases of the walls. BEHIND the characters: a large fresh rubble pile of broken stone blocking the passage they came from, dust still settling. AHEAD into the distance: the corridor stretches into deep blackness, no visible end, no door, just darkness.

CHARACTERS (just survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Hoverboard tucked under one arm. Holding a small handheld tablet, screen casting cold blue light up onto her face.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Handheld long-lens shot looking down the corridor into deep blackness, camera held low and unstable, micro-shake constant. The first iron torch on a wall bracket nearest camera IGNITES with a whump, flame blooming into a halated orange flare against the carved script wall, illuminating a section of glyph-letters and the rough stone ceiling above. The camera operator subtly shifts weight, the frame drifting. The next torch ignites deeper down — the operator naturally adjusts focus as flame appears, focus catching it imperfectly at first then resolving. Each successive ignition deeper into the corridor pulls the operator's focus organically, the focal plane drifting through the depth. Visible dust particles on the lens catch the new light. By the end of the shot, a chain of warm flickering torches recedes into the corridor's depth on alternating walls.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens close-up of Rain's face from a low three-quarter angle. Camera held close, breathing with the operator, micro-shake constant. Cold blue tablet light underlit on her face from below, warm torch light from behind catching the carved script wall in soft bokeh. The shake makes the focus drift slightly between her eyes and her cheekbones, never quite locked. She glances down at the tablet, then back up. She says with quiet authority: "Final stretch. Forty feet." Camera shifts slightly with her movement.

Shot 3 (6–8.5s): Hard cut to handheld extreme close-up of Jax's hand approaching the carved horizontal bands of script on the wall. Camera trembling, the lens moving with the operator's micro-adjustments to maintain frame. Shallow focus on his fingertips at the moment of contact with the weathered carved letter-glyphs, but the shake causes focus to subtly drift in and out as he moves his fingers across the carvings. The nearest torch flares aggressive halation across one edge of the lens. Pull focus to his eye behind glasses — the pull is slightly imperfect, finding his eye after a half-second hesitation. Off-frame his voice, slightly rough: "Yo. These markings. Look at 'em."

Shot 4 (8.5–10s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens medium close-up of Roco. Hard torch light slashes across his features, half his face in coal-black shadow. Camera shake constant, the operator staying close to him. His eyes scan — looking up toward the rough stone ceiling, then down toward the flagstone floor, then to a dark recess between sections of carved wall. As his head turns, the camera operator subtly follows the movement, frame drifting with his attention. Tactical, silent.

Shot 5 (10–15s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens medium close-up of Rain in profile. Camera close, shake breathing constant. She lowers the tablet, blue glow fading from her face. Warm torch light catches one side, the other in coal-black shadow. She turns her head slightly, scanning. The camera operator adjusts to keep her face framed, micro-shifts of the lens. She says, lower and more urgent: "Guys. Something's off." A beat. Eyes flick to where Jax is off-frame, then briefly toward Roco off-frame. "Stay close." Hold on her face. Then her gaze locks forward into the dark depth of the corridor. Her jaw sets. She turns her head fully forward and TAKES THE FIRST STEP — her body begins moving into the depth of the corridor. The camera operator instinctively follows her motion for a fraction of a second, the shake intensifying briefly, then the camera holds back, letting her advance into the bokeh as the clip ends.

Forty feet." with deep stone reverb, fingers brushing carved stone with grit, Jax's "Yo. These markings. Look at 'em." ## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄古老石廊，带苏醒火把与队伍简报的开场序列，15 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影带恒定微妙摄影机颤动与有机的操机员运动，摄影机在操机员手中始终「活着」、呼吸着。长焦远摄摄影（85mm 等效）f/1.2 光圈，带奶咖啡焦外光的极浅景深。拥抱镜头瑕疵——镜头上捕捉光晕的可见灰尘颗粒、火把火焰侵入周围黑暗的强烈光晕（halation）、高对比边缘的色差、摄影机在操机员手中挪动时的偶发短暂焦点漂移、淡淡的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和的调色板带深沉琥珀棕大地色系，纯净黑周围暗影中孤立的暖橙火把点缀，Rain 平板的微弱冷蓝辉光照在她脸上。仅来自火把的戏剧性明暗对照照明——压死深黑主导画面大半。

场景情境（SCENE CONTEXT）：本场景始于一次剧烈爆炸塌陷了上方通道、把三名角色坠入下方这条走廊之后的数秒。走廊对它们的存在作出回应——壁挂火把从更深处沿通道一盏接一盏依序点燃（IGNITE）。每个角色开始以各自的方式探查这处地点。Rain 查看着一块显示示意图的手持平板。Jax 被墙上的雕刻文字吸引。Roco 战术性地扫视走廊。随后 Rain 感到有什么不对，警告队伍，开始向前走。

场景（LOCATION）：一条漫长的古老地下石质走廊，低矮粗凿的石质天花板。墙壁覆满横向带状的雕刻古文字——成排饱经风化的楔形字母沿两面墙整段延伸。铁火把托架以一定角度安装在墙上，每隔几米一支，托着短火焰火把。石板地面由不规则开裂的石板拼成。小块的雕刻石牌与碎石靠在墙基边。角色身后：一堆新鲜的大块碎石废墟堵住他们来时的通道，粉尘仍在沉降。前方远处：走廊伸入深不见底的黑，无可见尽头、无门，只是黑暗。

角色（刚经历爆炸幸存、覆尘）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银白金色头发，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新割伤，灰色连帽夹克，覆尘。悬浮滑板夹在一臂之下。握着一块小手持平板，屏幕把冷蓝色光投到她脸上。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，透明眼镜，深色夹克，覆尘。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱发，胡须，鼻上红绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。悬浮滑板在臂下。

镜头 1（Shot 1，0–3s）：手持长焦镜头沿走廊看入深不见底的黑，摄影机低持而不稳，微颤恒定。离摄影机最近的墙上托架上的第一支铁火把「呼」（whump）地点燃，火焰绽放成带光晕（halated）的橙色火光，映在雕刻文字的墙上，照亮一段楔形字母与上方粗糙石质天花板。操机员微微换重心，画面漂移。下一支火把在更深处点燃——火焰出现时操机员自然地调整对焦，焦点起初未完全咬住、随后对准。每一次向更深处依次点燃都把操机员的焦点有机地拉过去，焦平面穿过纵深漂移。镜头上的可见灰尘颗粒捕捉到新光。镜头结束时，一长串温暖的闪烁火把在走廊纵深中、在交替的墙上退去。

镜头 2（Shot 2，3–6s）：硬切至 Rain 面部的手持长焦特写，低四分之三角。摄影机贴近、随操机员呼吸，微颤恒定。冷蓝平板光从下方在她脸上留打底光，身后暖火把光在柔焦中捕捉到雕刻文字的墙。颤抖使焦点在她眼睛与颧骨间轻微漂移，从不完全锁定。她低头瞥一眼平板，再抬起。她带着安静的权威说：「最后一段。四十英尺。」摄影机随她动作微微挪动。

镜头 3（Shot 3，6–8.5s）：硬切至 Jax 的手接近墙上雕刻的横向带状文字的手持极特写。摄影机颤抖，镜头随操机员的微调维持画面。指尖在接触饱经风化的雕刻楔形字母的瞬间浅焦，但颤抖使焦点在他手指划过雕刻时微妙地进进出出漂移。最近的火把在一侧镜头边缘爆出强烈的光晕。拉焦到他眼镜后方的眼睛——这次拉焦略有小瑕疵，在半秒迟疑后找回他的眼睛。画外他的声音、略带沙哑：「哟。这些刻痕。看看它们。」

镜头 4（Shot 4，8.5–10s）：硬切至 Roco 的手持长焦中近景。硬朗火把光斜劈过他的五官，半张脸在煤黑阴影中。摄影机颤恒定，操机员贴近他。他的眼睛扫视——看向粗糙石质天花板，再向下看向石板地面，再看向一段雕墙隔区之间的幽暗凹处。他转头时，操机员微妙地跟随动作，画面随他的注意力漂移。战术性，沉默。

镜头 5（Shot 5，10–15s）：硬切至 Rain 侧面的手持长焦中近景。摄影机贴近，呼吸颤抖恒定。她放下平板，蓝辉光从她脸上淡去。暖火把光照到一侧，另一侧在煤黑阴影中。她微微转头，扫视。操机员调整以让她的脸保持取景，镜头的微移。她更低、更急切地说：「各位。有点不对劲。」一寂。目光闪向 Jax 所在的画外，再短暂朝向 Roco 的画外。「别走散。」定格在她脸上。随后她的目光向前锁定进走廊的黑暗纵深。她下颌绷紧。她头完全转向正前，迈出第一步（TAKES THE FIRST STEP）——她的身体开始移入走廊纵深。操机员本能地随她动作跟随几分之一秒，颤抖短暂加剧，随后摄影机停住撤回，让她在前行时退入焦外光，短片随之结束。

无音乐。仅音效——身后废墟堆的沉降碎屑与流淌的石粉、高处远处二级坍塌的低沉轰鸣、爆炸余波微弱的耳鸣倒响、火把依序点燃的连续「呼」声（镜头 1 中五下清晰点燃）、噼啪火焰、Rain 平板的微弱电子嗡鸣、她手指轻敲屏幕的轻响、Rain 带深沉石混响的「最后一段。四十英尺。」、手指轻刷雕石带沙粒声、Jax 的「哟。这些刻痕。看看它们。」（画外）、Roco 克制的战术呼吸、他转头时衣物的细微移动、Rain 带着安静迫切感的「各位。有点不对劲。别走散。」、前方某处的远处滴水、短片结束时 Rain 踏在石板上的第一下刻意脚步。
