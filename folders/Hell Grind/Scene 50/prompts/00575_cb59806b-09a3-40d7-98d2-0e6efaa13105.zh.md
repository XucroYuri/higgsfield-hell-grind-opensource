## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, advancement and trap activation sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: shot in the visual language of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead — long lens cinematography (85mm to 135mm equivalent), shallow depth of field at f/1.2 aperture, extreme bokeh with soft circular highlights, foreground and background dissolving into creamy out-of-focus haze, only razor-thin slices of frame in sharp focus, dynamic focus pulls between subjects. Heavy lens characteristics: subtle barrel distortion in close shots, slight chromatic aberration at high-contrast edges, halation bleeding from torch flames into surrounding darkness, warm orange light blooming organically. Compressed perspective from telephoto lens flattens corridor depth. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm pops from fire. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — deep crushed blacks, faces partially in shadow, single-source torch motivation, light falling off rapidly into darkness. Cinematic anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1.

SCENE CONTEXT: continuing immediately from Rain's "Stay close" warning. The team begins walking forward through the corridor, but they are spaced apart — Rain leads several meters ahead, Jax in the middle, Roco at the rear. Rain glances back over her shoulder to say something to the team. In that exact moment of distraction, her boot lands on a worn stone tile — she did not see it. The tile depresses with a dull click. Roco, who was scanning the corridor, sees it happen and shouts "Rein!" — but the click has already triggered the mechanism. Ten ancient stone ports embedded in BOTH SIDE WALLS GRIND open simultaneously. Roco shouts "DOWN!" The three drop to the floor as the first arrows begin to fire.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide, ceiling two and a half meters. BEHIND — fresh rubble collapse from blast, retreat impossible. AHEAD — open carved stone gateway with cold blue light. Iron torches lit along both walls. Ten ancient stone ports embedded in both side walls (five left, five right), currently sealed with weathered carved covers.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars on both walls, lit iron torches every few meters, thick volumetric dust haze.

Characters (recently survived a blast, dust-covered, walking spaced apart):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, black top, dark cargo pants, dusty. Hoverboard tucked under one arm. Tablet glowing dim cold blue in her other hand. Walking ahead of the others by several meters.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket, dusty. Walking in the middle, a few meters behind Rain.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red nose bandage, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm. Walking at the rear, eyes constantly scanning the walls and floor.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Long-lens static low-angle shot looking down the corridor from Roco's position, the team strung out ahead — Rain in the deep distance as a small silhouette in sharp focus thanks to telephoto compression, Jax in the middle distance dissolving into soft bokeh, Roco's shoulder in extreme out-of-focus foreground silhouette. Telephoto compression flattens the depth so all three appear stacked but at different focal planes. Torches lining both walls render as massive halated bokeh orbs receding into perspective. Atmospheric haze thick. They walk forward at a slow tense pace, the spacing between them clearly readable. No dialogue. Only footsteps, breathing.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Hard cut to long-lens medium shot of Rain in three-quarter angle, walking forward ahead of the others. Razor-thin focus on her face, the corridor behind her dissolved into wash of orange torch bokeh. She glances back over her shoulder — focus subtly racks as her face turns slightly toward camera-rear. She begins to say something, mouth opening — about to call back to the team. Her foot continues forward beneath her, unseen, in completely soft foreground bokeh. The boot lands on something. A faint dull CLICK rises from below the frame. Rain's expression freezes mid-word. Her eyes drop. She has just registered what she stepped on — but only after.

Shot 3 (6–8s): EXTREME CLOSE-UP — long-lens macro shot at floor level, the camera mere inches from a single worn stone tile, framed full-bleed in 2.39:1 anamorphic. Razor-thin focus on the surface of the tile, every crack and chip rendered with sharp detail, surrounding tiles dissolving into deep creamy bokeh. Rain's boot is already pressed down on it from the upper half of frame, in soft halated focus. The tile is DEPRESSED one centimeter — the seam between stones visible as a dark line. Dust SIFTS from the seam, individual particles glowing as halated bokeh in passing torch light. Hold dead still on the sunken tile. From within the walls, faintly, a deep mechanical groan begins to build.

Shot 4 (8–10s): Hard cut to long-lens medium close-up of Roco's face from a three-quarter angle. Hard torch source carving angular halated light across his features, half his face lost in coal-black shadow. His eyes register what just happened — focus pulls past him to Rain in deep background, frozen with foot on tile, then snaps back to his face. He shouts, voice sharp: "Rein!" The warning is too late and he knows it.

Shot 5 (10–12s): EXTREME CLOSE-UP — long-lens macro shot of one stone wall port in maximum detail, the camera mere inches from the carved stone surface. Razor-thin focus on the seam of the port cover, deep weathered glyph carvings around it falling off into creamy bokeh. The carved cover GRINDS open, sliding aside with a heavy mechanical clack — dust EXPLODES from the seam in a thick visible plume, individual particles glowing as halated bokeh in torch light. The dark hollow aperture inside the stone is revealed, the inner mechanism barely visible as deep shadow. Behind in soft bokeh, more ports along the same wall and on the opposite wall open in unison, registered as flickers of dust eruption in the out-of-focus background — nine more openings, all simultaneous. Camera locks dead still on the one port in foreground.

Shot 6 (12–13.5s): Snap cut to long-lens extreme close-up of Roco — only his mouth and chin in frame at first, scream beginning: "DO—" Camera PULLS BACK rapidly with motion blur and chromatic aberration, revealing his full face mid-scream "—WN!" His body launches forward into camera, arm reaching toward Jax.

Shot 7 (13.5–15s): One continuous long-lens shot, low-angle at floor level. The three DROP into frame — Rain dives forward and slides controlled on her own, hoverboard clattering aside, tablet skidding across stone with blue glow flickering; Roco's tackle drives Jax down with him, both hitting wet stone hard, Roco's hoverboard skittering away; all three flatten. Camera arc reveals each body as it falls in turn at f/1.2, focus shifting between subjects — only one sharp at any moment, the rest in soft bokeh. Above the prone bodies, the FIRST arrow CRACKS through frame in real-time speed — sharp wooden whoosh — passing horizontally across the corridor as a streak of motion blur through the bokeh. Hold.

no music. sfx only — slow deliberate footsteps on wet stone with deep stone reverb (spaced footsteps from three different distances), faint electronic hum from Rain's tablet, torches crackling with low hiss, controlled heavy breathing, distant drip from ahead, faint settle of debris from rubble behind, Rain beginning to speak (cut off by the click), boot pressing on stone, faint dull CLICK of depressed tile, dust sifting from seam, deep mechanical groan beginning within walls (subtle, building), Roco's sharp "Rein!" warning with stone reverb (too late), one ancient stone cover grinding open in foreground with heavy mechanical clack and explosive dust burst, nine more grinding open in unison from background, Roco's "DOWN!" raw and cracking, body tackle impact, three bodies hitting wet stone hard, two hoverboards clattering, tablet skidding across stone, ragged breathing, first arrow whooshing cracking through air at full real-time speed.

狭窄的古老石质走廊，推进与陷阱触发序列，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：以 Zack Snyder《亡者之师》（Army of the Dead）的视觉语言拍摄——长焦摄影（85mm 至 135mm 等效），f/1.2 光圈浅景深，带柔和圆形高光的极端散景，前景与背景消解为奶油状失焦薄雾，仅画面上剃刀般细薄的切片处于锐焦点，主体间动态焦点拉动。重镜头特征：近景中细微桶形畸变、高对比边缘处轻微色差、火把火焰向周围黑暗晕渗的光晕（halation）、暖橙光有机地绽放漫延。长焦镜头带来的压缩透视压平走廊纵深。重度去饱和调色，仅火光的孤立暖色跳动。戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）照明——压碎浓黑，面部部分没入阴影，单源火把动机光，光迅速衰减进黑暗。电影感变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：紧接 Rain「Stay close（跟紧）」的警告继续。小队开始沿走廊前行，但彼此拉开间距——Rain 领先数米，Jax 居中，Roco 殿后。Rain 回头越过肩膀想对队伍说些什么。恰在这一分神瞬间，她的靴子踏上一块磨损的石板——她没有看到。石板以一声沉闷咔嗒声下沉。一直在扫视走廊的 Roco 看到这一幕，喊道「Rein！」——但咔嗒声已经触发了机关。嵌入两侧墙壁中的十处古老石孔同时研磨开启。Roco 喊「DOWN！（趴下！）」三人扑倒于地面，第一批箭开始射出。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（地点几何）：约两米宽的狭窄石质走廊，顶棚两米半高。身后（BEHIND）——爆炸造成的新鲜塌落碎石堆，撤退不可能。前方（AHEAD）——敞开的凿刻石门泛冷蓝光。沿两侧墙壁点亮的铁火把。嵌入两侧墙壁中的十处古老石孔（左五、右五），目前以风化凿刻的盖板封合。

Setting（环境）：湿滑石地，两侧墙壁上遍布符文的立柱，每隔几米点亮的铁火把，浓重的体积尘土薄雾。

Characters（角色）（刚经历爆炸幸存，蒙尘，以间距行走）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银色金发凌乱，眼睛旁有道疤，太阳穴有新割口，灰色连帽夹克，黑色上衣，深色工装裤，蒙尘。一块悬浮滑板夹在腋下。另一只手里一块平板电脑泛着昏淡冷蓝光。领先其他人数米行走。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色圆寸，深肤色，清晰护目镜，深色连帽夹克，蒙尘。走在中间，落后 Rain 数米。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱头发，胡子，红色鼻子绷带，深色夹克，蒙尘。腋下夹着悬浮滑板。走在殿后，眼睛持续扫视墙壁与地面。

Shot 1（镜头 1）（0–3s）：长焦静态低角度镜头，自 Roco 的位置沿走廊望去，队伍在前方拉开——Rain 在深远距离呈小而清晰的剪影（得益于长焦压缩），Jax 在中景距离消解进柔散景，Roco 的肩膀在极端失焦前景剪影中。长焦压缩压平纵深，三人看似前后层叠但处于不同焦平面。沿两侧墙壁排列的火把化作巨大的辉散散景球退向透视纵深。大气薄雾浓重。他们以缓慢紧绷的步速前行，彼此间距清晰可读。无对白。仅脚步与呼吸。

Shot 2（镜头 2）（3–6s）：硬切至三平面角度 Rain 的长焦中景，在其他人之前前行。她的脸处于剃刀细薄的焦点，身后走廊消解为一片暖橙火把散景的色洗。她回头越过肩膀扫视——随她脸颊微微转向镜头后方，焦点微妙地变焦拉动。她开始开口，欲回头朝小队喊话。她的脚在她身下继续前探，完全处于柔散前景散景中，不可见。靴子落到某物上。一声微弱的沉闷咔嗒自画面下方升起。Rain 的表情在说话中途冻结。她的目光落下。她刚意识到自己踩到什么——但只是事后。

Shot 3（镜头 3）（6–8s）：EXTREME CLOSE-UP（极特写）——地面高度的长焦微距镜头，摄影机距一块磨损石板仅数英寸，以 2.39:1 变形宽银幕全幅框出。石板表面呈剃刀细薄焦点，每条裂纹与缺口都以锐利细节呈现，周围石板消解进深层奶油散景。Rain 的靴子早已从画面上半部压在其上，处于柔和辉散焦点。石板下沉了一厘米——石缝以一道暗线可见。尘土自石缝筛落，个别粉尘粒子在掠过的火把光中化作辉散散景。死死保持静定在凹陷的石板上。自墙壁内部，隐约地，一声深沉的机械呻吟开始积聚。

Shot 4（镜头 4）（8–10s）：硬切至三平面角度 Roco 的脸的长焦中近景。坚硬的火把光源在他五官上切出棱角状辉散光，半张脸没入煤炭般的黑影。他的眼睛注意到了刚才发生的事——焦点拉过他，落到深背景中定格在石板上的 Rain，随即啪地拉回他的脸。他喊道，声音锐利：「Rein！」警告已太迟，他知道。

Shot 5（镜头 5）（10–12s）：EXTREME CLOSE-UP（极特写）——一处石墙孔的最细致长焦微距，摄影机距凿刻石面仅数英寸。孔盖接缝呈剃刀细薄焦点，周围深风化符文凿刻落到奶油散景中。凿刻盖板研磨开启，以一声沉重的机械咔嗒滑开——尘土自接缝喷爆成浓密可见的羽流，个别粉尘粒子在火把光中化作辉散散景。石内深暗的空洞孔径现出，内部机构仅以深影略可见。后方柔散景中，同墙与他墙的更多石孔齐齐开启，在失焦背景中以尘土喷发的闪烁被感知——另外九处开口，全部同时。摄影机死死锁定前景这一处石孔。

Shot 6（镜头 6）（12–13.5s）：甩切至 Roco 的长焦极特写——画面起初仅他的嘴与下巴，尖叫开始：「DO—」摄影机带运动模糊与色差快速后拉（PULLS BACK），揭示他尖叫中「—WN！」的整张脸。他的身体纵身扑向镜头，手臂伸向 Jax。

Shot 7（镜头 7）（13.5–15s）：一个连续长焦镜头，地面高度低角度。三人跌入画面——Rain 前扑并受控地自行滑开，悬浮滑板哐当甩到一旁，平板电脑拖着闪烁蓝光在石上滑脱；Roco 的扑倒将 Jax 拽下同坠，两人重重砸上湿滑石地，Roco 的悬浮滑板踉跄滑走；三人全部贴地压扁。摄影机弧线逐一揭示每具落下的身体，f/1.2 下焦点在主体间移动——任何时刻仅一个清晰，其余在柔散景中。俯身横陈的身体上方，第一支箭以实时速度锐穿画面——锐利的木质嗖声——以一道运动模糊流光横向掠过走廊穿进散景。保持。

no music. sfx only（无配乐。仅环境音效）——湿滑石地上缓慢迟疑的脚步带深石混响（三个不同距离的间隔脚步）、Rain 平板电脑的微弱电子嗡响、火把带低嘶噼啪、受控的重呼吸、前方远处滴水、身后碎石堆残片隐约沉降、Rain 开始说话（被咔嗒声打断）、靴子压上石板、被压石板微弱的沉闷咔嗒、尘土自石缝筛落、墙内开始的深沉机械呻吟（细微，积聚中）、Roco 锐利的「Rein！」警告带石混响（太迟）、前景一处古老石盖以沉重机械咔嗒研磨开启并喷爆尘土、背景中另外九处齐齐研磨开启、Roco 的「DOWN！」嘶哑破裂、扑倒冲撞、三具身体重重砸上湿滑石地、两块悬浮滑板哐当作响、平板电脑在石上滑脱、粗重呼吸、第一支箭以全实时速度嗖锐穿空气。
