## 原文 (source)

Refer to the man with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, sweat-streaked dust-covered face, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.

Operator running alongside the man through an ancient stone corridor lit by torches. Tight handheld close-up — the camera is body-mounted and chest-level, inches from his face, breathing handheld feel, the operator chases him at his pace, frame trembling with each running step impact, motion blur from the running gait. Telephoto lens compression, extreme shallow depth of field, the corridor dissolved into streaks of warm torch bokeh on both sides as they race past.

The man sprints toward a closing stone gateway with a massive descending slab, the gap below now ankle-height. As he reaches the threshold he launches into a horizontal dive — and the operator dives with him. The camera drops to floor level mid-flight, traveling forward at the same speed as his body, staying tight on his face throughout the leap. Both the man and the camera slide on their stomachs through the narrow gap under the slab. The lens almost touches his cheek. Just as the operator clears the threshold the slab crashes down behind them with a massive impact, dust exploding outward, the camera tumbling into a roll on the safe side, frame spinning wildly before settling.

Photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur on the dive, focus stays locked on his face. Sounds: ragged breathing of both subject and operator, dual sets of running boots on wet stone, fabric scraping stone slab edge, massive slab impact, camera tumbling on stone, dust wave. No music.
