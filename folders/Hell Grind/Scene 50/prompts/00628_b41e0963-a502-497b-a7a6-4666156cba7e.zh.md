Setting: massive rubble collapse at far end, faint cold blue light through debris gap, wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars on both side walls, candles flickering, dense volumetric dust haze from the activated trap. Anamorphic widescreen, teal-shadow color grade with warm candle accents, low-key lighting.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Tight slow-motion close-up tracking a single iron-tipped arrow with a weathered wooden shaft — it SHOOTS horizontally from a side wall port and streaks across frame, passing inches from Rain's silver hair, the fletching grazing strands of her hair as it flies past her face. Her one visible eye is wide, locked forward, breath frozen, lips slightly parted. The arrow continues out of frame. Other arrows blur past in the background, whistling.

Shot 2 (4–7s): Side-on medium shot of the opposite stone wall — arrows SLAM into the carved surface in rapid staggered volley, embedding deep, aged wooden shafts vibrating violently, stone chips spalling outward, dust puffing from each impact. Glyphs pierced clean through. Camera flinches with the first impact, holds steady through the rest.

Shot 3 (7–10s): Close-up of Rain's face pressed against wet stone, cheek to the ground, silver hair fanned out across the floor, one eye wide and locked forward toward the rubble. Ragged controlled breathing. A vibrating arrow shaft is embedded in the side wall behind her in soft focus. She doesn't blink. Slow push-in on her eye.

Shot 4 (10–15s): Rain's POV, low ground-level looking down the corridor's full length — through the narrow gap in the rubble at the far end, a massive carved stone slab DESCENDS from the ceiling, slow and inexorable, sealing the only exit. Arrow shafts protrude from both side walls in the foreground, framing a lethal canopy across the shot. Dust cascades from the slab's edges as it lowers. The bright cold blue gap of light narrows steadily. Deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble. Hold on the shrinking light until the slab nearly seals the gap.
