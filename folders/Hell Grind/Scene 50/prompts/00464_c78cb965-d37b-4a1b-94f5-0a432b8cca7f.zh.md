## 原文 (source)

Handheld high-angle close-up of a young man fighting to hold on in a stone pit and screaming for help, 4 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with violent reactive camera shake — the camera is alive in the operator's hands, alarmed, jerking with each spasm of the character below, breathing hard. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust particles on the lens, halation aggressively blooming from torch flames, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges, occasional sharp focus loss as the operator struggles to maintain frame, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm orange torch accents. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from torches above — deep crushed blacks, hard angular light from above the pit cutting down across the character's face. Inspired by the cinematography of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: a deep dark stone pit inside the floor of an ancient underground corridor. Pit is vertical, narrow, hewn rough into bedrock — a tile collapsed, revealing this hole. Pit walls wet, weathered stone. Stone spikes barely visible in the deep darkness far below. At the top edge of frame, the corridor floor above with warm torch light spilling down into the pit. Dust falls past camera in waves from the corridor above where something heavy is grinding (a ceiling slowly descending in the corridor — heard but not seen here in the pit).

CHARACTER: a young man, mid-twenties, with bleached yellow buzzcut hair, dark skin, clear protective glasses on his face partially askew, dark hooded jacket. Face dust-caked and streaked with dirt and sweat. Hanging from the upper edge of the pit by his fingertips alone, body dangling violently into darkness. His arms are pulled fully extended above him, shoulders hyperextended, his entire weight on his fingers gripping the rough stone lip above. He is in complete physical desperation.

ACTION: the camera is positioned ABOVE the pit, looking steeply DOWN at the man's face. He is NOT just hanging passively — his body is JERKING with violent spasms of effort, kicking his legs against the dark pit walls trying to find a foothold, finding none, his body SWINGING from his fingertips. His shoulders pull upward in convulsive jerks as he tries to muscle his way up, fails, drops back down. His fingertips SLIP on the wet stone and he frantically REGRIPS, tightening his hold a fraction lower than before. Sweat streams from his forehead, eyes bulging with panic, every muscle in his face strained. The camera shakes violently with each of his spasms, as if the operator is being thrown by his struggle. Hard angular torch light from above carves shadow across his contorted face. He throws his head back to look up at the camera with full bug-eyed terror — his neck strained — and SCREAMS upward at full lung capacity, voice tearing into a raw cracking shriek: "ROCO!!" His mouth opens wide on the scream, throat strained beyond control, the scream ripping out of him with desperation. The scream echoes off the rough pit walls. Immediately after the scream, he lets out a panicked sob and tries again to muscle his way up, kicking the pit walls, finding no purchase.

no music. sfx only — the man's panicked rapid breathing escalating, his body slamming against the pit walls with thuds, his fingers grinding violently on rough stone with stone-on-flesh scraping, the raw cracking "ROCO!!" scream tearing out of his throat with full terror echoing off pit walls with deep stone reverb, panicked sob immediately after, his boots kicking against pit walls trying to find a foothold, dust falling past camera into darkness below catching light, distant slow deep grinding rumble of a ceiling descending somewhere above in the corridor, distant torches crackling above.

手持高角度特写：一名年轻人在石坑中拼命攥住不放、尖叫求救，4 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持摄影，带有暴烈的反应性摄影机晃动——摄影机在操机员手中鲜活、惊惶，随下方角色每一次痉挛而抽搐，喘着粗气。长焦摄影，浅景深，f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜片上可见尘埃颗粒，火炬火焰凶猛地勃发光晕（halation），高反差边缘轻微色差，操机员挣扎着维持画幅时偶尔瞬间失去锐焦，淡淡的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和的调色，带孤立的暖橙火炬点缀。来自上方火炬的戏剧性明暗布光——深碎的黑色，坑上方的硬朗棱角光向下斜切过角色的脸。灵感来自 Zack Snyder《Army of the Dead（活死人军团）》的摄影。

LOCATION（场景）：远古地下走廊地面内的一处深黑石坑。坑竖直而窄，粗凿掘入基岩——一块石砖塌落，露出这个洞。坑壁湿滑、风化石头。深暗遥远的坑底几乎看不见石刺。画幅顶端边缘，上方的走廊地面有暖火炬光泻入坑中。灰尘从摄影机旁一波波落下，来自走廊上方某处沉重的东西在研磨（走廊里一座天棚正缓缓下降——在这里的坑中只闻其声不见其影）。

CHARACTER（角色）：年轻人，二十多岁，漂白黄寸头，深肤色，脸上戴一副清晰护目镜、部分歪斜，深色连帽夹克。脸上结满尘土、脏污汗渍斑斑。仅凭指尖挂在坑的上缘，身体暴烈地悬荡进黑暗。双臂被拉得完全伸展在他头顶，肩部过度伸展，全部重量都压在抓扣上方粗糙石缘的手指上。他处于彻底的肢体绝望之中。

ACTION（动作）：摄影机位于坑上方，陡峭地向下俯视该男子的脸。他不只是消极地挂着——他的身体以暴烈的用力痉挛抽搐（JERKING），双腿踢蹬黑暗的坑壁、试图找到落脚点、一无所获，他的身体在指尖上荡摆（SWINGING）。他痉挛性地拱肩向上、试图勉强把自己拉上去，失败，回落。他的指尖在湿石上打滑（SLIP），他疯狂地重新抓扣（REGRIPS），把抓握收紧到比先前略低一点的位置。汗从他额头流下，眼珠因恐慌而凸出，脸上每块肌肉都绷紧。摄影机随他每一次痉挛而剧烈摇晃，仿佛操机员正被他的挣扎甩动。上方硬朗棱角火炬光在他扭曲的脸上切下阴影。他猛仰头、以完全的暴突眼恐惧朝上看向摄影机——脖子绷紧——拼尽全力向上尖叫（SCREAMS upward），嗓音撕裂成嘶哑破碎的嚎叫：「ROCO!!」他尖叫时嘴大张，喉咙失控地绷紧，尖叫携着绝望破腔而出。尖叫在粗糙坑壁间回响。尖叫甫落，他发出一声恐慌的呜咽，再次试图勉强自己上去，踢蹬坑壁，找不到可凭借之处。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——该男子升级中的恐慌急促呼吸，身体砰砰撞击坑壁，手指在粗糙石上暴烈研磨、发出石对肉的刮擦声，撕裂的「ROCO!!」尖叫携着十足恐惧从他咽喉破出、以深沉石廊混响在坑壁间回响，随后立刻是一声恐慌呜咽，他的靴子踢蹬坑壁试图找落脚点，灰尘从摄影机旁落入下方黑暗、接住光线，下方高高的走廊上方某处天棚下降的远处缓慢深沉研磨轰隆，上方远处火炬噼啪作响。
