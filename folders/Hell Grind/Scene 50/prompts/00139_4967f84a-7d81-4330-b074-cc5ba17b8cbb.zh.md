## 原文 (source)

Single shot in an ancient stone chamber on the safer side of a closing gateway, 4 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the woman with silver-blonde hair, fresh cut on her temple, grey hooded jacket from Image 1.

Setting: a stone chamber on the safer side of a stone gateway. Cold blue ambient light from above. The gateway visible behind camera direction — a massive stone slab that has descended halfway from above, leaving a horizontal gap below it at chest height through which the woman has just passed. Through the gap behind her, the warm orange torchlit corridor on the other side is visible, dust falling, the slab continuing to descend slowly. Wet stone floor.

Action: Handheld tracking shot at chest height. The woman bursts through the half-closed gateway, ducking under the descending stone slab to enter the chamber, body bent low to clear the gap. She straightens up after passing through, runs a few steps forward into the chamber, then stops and TURNS sharply on her heel to face back toward the gateway behind camera. Hair flies with the motion. She stares back through the half-closed gap, eyes wide, breath catching, face tense — watching the slab continue to descend, searching the corridor beyond for her teammates. Camera follows her turn and settles on her face, the warm orange light from the corridor visible behind her through the closing gap, mixing with the cold blue light of her side.

Camera: handheld, 85mm telephoto lens, shallow depth of field. Camera moves with her motion through the gateway, then settles when she turns. Trembles with her breath.

Sounds: her running boots thudding onto stone as she ducks through, fabric of her jacket scraping against the stone slab edge above her as she passes under it, ragged exhausted breathing. Boots scuff to a halt, sharp pivot on stone. Held breath as she turns. Loud continuous slab grinding rumble from the gateway behind, the slab descending. Distant sounds of running and turmoil from the corridor beyond. No music.



## 中文对照 (zh)

一座远古石厅中的单镜头，位于一座正在关闭的门道较安的一侧，4 秒，16:9，写实摄影，浓重 35mm 胶片颗粒，运动模糊，无音乐。

参照 Image 1 中那位银金色头发、太阳穴新伤口、穿灰色连帽夹克的女子。

Setting（环境）：一座石门道较安一侧的石厅。上方是冷蓝色环境光。门道在摄影机方向之后可见——一块巨型石板已自上方下降过半，留下胸下高度的一道水平缝隙，女子刚从中穿过。她身后缝隙那头、另一侧暖橙色火炬照亮的走廊可见，灰尘落下，石板继续缓缓下降。湿石地面。

Action（动作）：胸口高度的手持跟拍镜头。女子冲过半合的门道，在下降石板下低头闯入厅中，身体大幅低下以让过缝隙。穿过后她直立起身，向前跑了几步进入厅中，然后停下、在脚跟上一转（TURNS）回身面向摄影机后方的门道。头发随动作飞扬。她透过半合的缝隙回望，双眼圆睁，呼吸一滞，脸绷紧——看着石板持续下降，在更远处的走廊里搜寻着她的队友。摄影机跟随她转身、落在她的脸上，她身后透过正在关闭的缝隙可见走廊的暖橙光，与她那侧的冷蓝光相混。

Camera（摄影机）：手持，85mm 长焦镜头，浅景深。摄影机随她穿过门道的动作移动，她转身时稳住。随她呼吸而颤抖。

Sounds（声响）：她俯身穿过时奔跑的靴子重重踏上石头，她通过时夹克布料刮擦头顶上方石板边缘，粗重力竭的呼吸。靴子拖着刹停，石上一个尖锐的转体。她转身时屏着呼吸。身后门道传来响亮连续的石板研磨轰响，石板在下降。更远走廊传来奔跑与动荡的远处声响。无音乐。
