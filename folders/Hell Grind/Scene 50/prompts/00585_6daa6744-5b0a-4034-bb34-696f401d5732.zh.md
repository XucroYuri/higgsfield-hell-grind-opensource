## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, trap activation sequence, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: shot in the visual language of Zack Snyder's Army of the Dead — long lens cinematography (85mm to 135mm equivalent), shallow depth of field at f/1.2 aperture, extreme bokeh with soft circular highlights, foreground and background dissolving into creamy out-of-focus haze, only razor-thin slices of frame in sharp focus, dynamic focus pulls between subjects. Heavy lens characteristics: subtle barrel distortion in close shots, slight chromatic aberration at high-contrast edges, halation bleeding from torch flames into surrounding darkness. Compressed perspective from telephoto lens flattens corridor depth. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm orange torch accents. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — deep crushed blacks, faces partially in shadow, light falling off rapidly into darkness. Cinematic anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1.

SCENE CONTEXT: continuing immediately from Rain's "Stay close" warning. The team is now advancing forward through the corridor. Rain leads, holding her tablet. Jax has lingered behind, again drawn to the carvings on the wall. Roco hangs near him, watchful. Then Rain's boot presses on a worn stone tile that DEPRESSES with a dull click. Roco hears it. The trap activates.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide, ceiling two and a half meters. BEHIND — fresh rubble collapse from recent blast, retreat impossible. AHEAD — open carved stone gateway with cold blue light. Iron torches lit along both walls.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars pressed close on both walls, lit iron torches every few meters, thick volumetric dust haze, claustrophobic.

Characters (recently survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, black top, dark cargo pants, dusty. Hoverboard tucked under one arm. Tablet glowing cold blue in her other hand. Walking forward.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark hooded jacket, dusty. Lingering behind, hand on the wall carvings, body half-turned toward the wall — out of position.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red nose bandage, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm. Hangs back near Jax, jaw tight.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Long-lens low-angle on the wet stone floor ahead of Rain, camera at ground level, shot at f/1.2. Only one stone tile is in razor-sharp focus — surrounding tiles, walls, Rain's silhouette dissolved into deep soft bokeh. Torch behind throws halated warm highlights across wet stone, reflections shimmering as bokeh streaks. Rain's boot enters frame from above, descending toward the tile in soft focus. The boot presses down. The tile DEPRESSES one centimeter with a faint dull CLICK. Dust sifts from the seam, individual particles catching firelight as glowing micro-bokeh. Camera locks dead still. Three full seconds of nothing but the depressed stone and dust falling. The world holds its breath.

Shot 2 (4–6s): SNAP cut to long-lens tight insert of Roco's eye, shot at f/1.2 — only his iris in razor-sharp focus, pupil dilating as he registers the click. Warm torch halation glowing across the side of his face. His head whips toward Rain — camera whip-pans with him, world smearing into streaked bokeh and halation, snapping into focus on Rain's silhouette ahead with one foot still on the depressed tile. Roco shouts, voice cracking: "Wait!" Push-in.

Shot 3 (6–9s): Hard cut to long-lens profile shot of the side wall, shot at f/1.2. Camera begins to PUSH IN as ancient stone ports embedded in BOTH SIDE WALLS GRIND open along the corridor. Five along the left, five along the right. Carved covers slide aside with a heavy mechanical clack, dust pouring from seams, dark hollow apertures revealed. Push-in stops with a dark aperture filling frame center, completely sharp while everything else dissolves into bokeh. From within the walls, deep mechanical groan rising.

Shot 4 (9–11s): Hard cut to long-lens extreme close-up of Roco — only his mouth and chin in frame at first, scream beginning: "DO—" Camera PULLS BACK rapidly with subtle motion blur and chromatic aberration to reveal his full face mid-scream "—WN!" His body launches forward into camera, arm reaching toward Jax. The pullback continues into a wide low-angle as he tackles Jax away from the wall and down to the floor.

Shot 5 (11–15s): One continuous long-lens shot, no cuts. Low-angle tracking arc — camera at floor level, sweeping across the corridor at f/1.2 as the three DROP. Rain dives forward and slides on her own, her hoverboard clattering aside, tablet skidding across stone with its blue glow flickering; Roco's tackle drives Jax down with him away from the wall, both hitting the floor together, Roco's hoverboard skittering across wet stone; all three flatten. Camera arc reveals each body as it falls in turn, focus shifting between them — only one subject sharp at any moment, the rest in soft bokeh. Ends with a static low frame looking down the length of the corridor — three prone bodies in soft focus foreground, the gateway glowing distantly in sharp focus deep background, dust suspended in air catching torch light as floating bokeh particles. Held silence. Deep mechanical groan inside walls intensifies — release imminent.

no music. sfx only — Rain's footsteps echoing on wet stone, faint electronic hum from tablet, torch flames crackling, boot pressing on stone, faint dull CLICK of depressed tile, dust sifting from seam, sharp "Wait!" with urgency and stone reverb, ancient stone covers grinding open on both walls in unison, mechanical clack, dust pouring from seams, "DOWN!" scream raw and cracking, body tackle impact, three bodies hitting wet stone hard, two hoverboards clattering, tablet skidding across stone with electronic glitch, ragged breathing, deep mechanical groan within walls building toward release.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄古老石廊，陷阱激活序列，15 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：以 Zack Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）的视觉语言拍摄——长焦镜头摄影（85mm 至 135mm 等效），f/1.2 光圈浅景深，极端焦外光点（bokeh）带柔和的圆形高光，前景与背景消散为奶咖啡状失焦雾霭，只有剃刀般细薄的帧切片处于锐焦，主体间动态拉焦。沉重的镜头特性：近距离镜头的微妙桶形畸变、高对比边缘的轻微色差、火把火焰渗入周围黑暗的光晕（halation）。长焦镜头的压缩透视压平了走廊纵深。高度去饱和的调色板带孤立的暖橙火把点缀。戏剧性的明暗对照照明（chiaroscuro）——压死深黑、面部局部入影、光迅速衰减入黑暗。电影变形宽银幕 2.39:1。

场景情境（SCENE CONTEXT）：紧接 Rain「Stay close（别走散）」的警告。队伍现在正向前穿过走廊推进。Rain 领头，握着她平板。Jax 落在了后面，又一次被墙上的雕刻吸引。Roco 靠在他近旁，机警着。随后 Rain 的靴子踩到一块磨蚀的石板，石板以一声沉闷咔哒下陷（DEPRESSES）。Roco 听到了。陷阱激活。

场景几何（LOCATION GEOMETRY）：狭窄石廊，约两米宽，天花板两米半。身后——刚爆炸造成的新碎石塌落、撤退不可能。前方——敞开的雕石门廊透出冷蓝色光。两侧墙沿线点燃铁火把。

环境（Setting）：湿滑石地面，两面墙上符文覆盖的石柱逼得很近，每隔几米点燃铁火把，浓厚的体积粉尘雾霭、幽闭。

角色（刚经历爆炸幸存、覆尘）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银白金色头发凌乱，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新割伤，灰色连帽夹克，黑上衣，深色工装裤，覆尘。悬浮滑板夹在一臂之下。另一只手握着辉光冷蓝色的平板。向前行走。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，透明眼镜，深色连帽夹克，覆尘。落在后面，手扶墙上雕刻，身体半转向墙——不在位上。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱发，胡须，红色鼻部绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。悬浮滑板在臂下。待在 Jax 附近稍后，下颌绷紧。

镜头 1（Shot 1，0–4s）：长焦低角度对准 Rain 前方的湿滑石地面，摄影机在地面高度，f/1.2 拍摄。只有一块石片处于剃刀般锐焦——周围的石板、墙壁、Rain 的剪影都消散入深柔焦外光。身后火把把带光晕的暖色高光投在湿石上，反光作为焦外条纹闪烁。Rain 的靴子自上方入画、在柔焦中朝石片下落。靴子下压。石片下陷一厘米，发出微弱的沉闷咔哒。粉尘从缝隙筛落，个别颗粒捕捉到火光成为发光的微型焦外光点。摄影机死一般静止。整整三秒，只有下陷的石片与落下的粉尘。世界屏住了呼吸。

镜头 2（Shot 2，4–6s）：骤切至 Roco 眼睛的长焦紧密特写（insert），f/1.2 拍摄——只有他的虹膜处于剃刀般锐焦，瞳孔在听清咔哒时放大。暖火把光晕横过他脸侧。他的头「唰」地甩向 Rain——摄影机随他甩镜（whip-pan），世界糊成条纹焦外光与光晕，随即骤停到焦点上：前方 Rain 的剪影，一只脚仍踩在下陷的石板上。Roco 嘶吼，声音破开：「Wait（等等）！」推近。

镜头 3（Shot 3，6–9s）：硬切至侧墙的长焦侧面镜头，f/1.2 拍摄。古老的石口嵌入两个侧墙，沿走廊碾磨着打开时，摄影机开始推近（PUSH IN）。左五个、右五个。雕刻的盖板带着沉重的机械咔嗒滑开，粉尘从缝隙涌出，黑暗镂空的孔径显露。推近在一个填满画面中央的黑暗孔径处停住，它完全锐利而其余一切消散入焦外光。墙内，沉重的机械呻吟上升。

镜头 4（Shot 4，9–11s）：硬切至 Roco 的长焦极特写——起初画框中只有他的嘴与下巴，尖叫开始：「DO——（趴—）」摄影机带着微妙的运动模糊与色差迅速拉回（PULLS BACK）以显露他在尖叫中段的整脸「—WN！（—下！）」他的身体向前扑入镜头，手臂伸向 Jax。拉回在把 Jax 撞离墙壁、扑倒地面的大范围低角度中继续。

镜头 5（Shot 5，11–15s）：一个连续长焦镜头，无剪辑。低角度跟踪弧线——摄影机在地面高度，三人趴下时以 f/1.2 横扫走廊。Rain 自己向前俯冲滑行，她的悬浮滑板哐啷歪到一边，平板擦过石头、蓝辉光闪烁；Roco 的扑倒把 Jax 带离墙边按到地上，两人一起砸到地面，Roco 的悬浮滑板在湿石上窜开；三人全部伏平。摄影机的弧线一个接一个在每个身体倒下时显露他们，焦点在它们之间切换——任何时刻只有一主体锐利、其余在柔焦外光中。以一幅沿走廊纵深看的静态低画框结束——前景三具伏卧的身体处于柔焦，远处门廊在深处背景中锐利发光，悬浮尘粒在空气中捕捉火把光成为飘浮的焦外颗粒。定格静默。墙内深沉的机械呻吟加剧——释放迫近。

无音乐。仅音效——Rain 脚步在湿石上回响、平板微弱电子嗡鸣、火把火焰噼啪、靴子压石板、下陷石板的微弱沉闷咔哒、粉尘从缝隙筛落、尖锐带急切与石混响的「Wait（等等）！」、两侧墙上古老石盖同时碾磨打开、机械咔嗒、粉尘从缝隙涌出、「DOWN！（趴下！）」尖叫沙哑迸裂、身体扑倒冲击、三具身体重重砸在湿石上、两块悬浮滑板哐啷作响、平板带电子杂音擦过石头、沙哑呼吸、墙内深沉机械呻吟积聚向释放。
