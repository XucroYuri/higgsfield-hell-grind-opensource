## 原文 (source)

Continuous handheld long-take in an ancient stone corridor, 12 seconds, 16:9.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, askew protective glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.

Setting: a narrow ancient stone corridor with low ceiling, two meters wide. Iron torches on both close walls. Carved hieroglyph walls. Wet stone floor. Thick atmospheric dust haze. In the middle of the corridor: an open trap pit with the dark-skinned yellow-haired man hanging by his fingertips, slipping, panicking. At the far end of the corridor in deep shadow: a massive stone slab grinding down loudly from above, closing the dark gateway, dust cascading from its edges in heavy waves. The slab's grinding rumble is loud, immediate, threatening — the men have seconds.

Action: The dark-haired man comes in fast, body skidding hard at the pit edge, almost falling in himself. He drops to his knee with violent force, throws his hand down into the pit without looking, no time to aim. The yellow-haired man's hand snaps up and catches it on the first try — both gripping desperately. The dark-haired man instantly hauls back with a sharp grunt, ripping his friend up out of the pit in one violent jerk. The yellow-haired man's body comes up over the edge fast, almost flung, landing hard on his hands and knees on the wet stone. The dark-haired man is already up — he grabs his friend's jacket and yanks him upright, no pause. They sprint together immediately, no recovery time, the dark-haired man's hand gripping his friend's back propelling him forward. Both men sprint away from camera into the corridor depth, fleeing the loudly grinding gateway behind them.

Camera: handheld throughout but UNSTABLE — the operator is running in the action, frame jerking violently, occasionally losing the subjects briefly before snapping back, camera tilts hard with each running impact. Pushes in extreme tight on the meeting of hands at the contact point when they catch each other — frame fully filled by gripping fingers, shaking with the desperate energy. Camera barely keeps up as bodies move fast through frame. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field with natural telephoto compression, the corridor blurring past in chaotic warm bokeh streaks from the running motion, faces rendered with portrait-lens flatness, subjects separated cleanly from soft background.

Photorealistic, 35mm film grain, motion blur smeared on every action, warm orange torch light against deep crushed blacks, dust cascading visibly from the gateway slab in the deep distance.

Sounds: LOUD continuous slab grinding rumble dominating the audio, urgent and threatening — the slab is closing fast. Both men's fast hyperventilating breath, short panicked gasps not full inhales. Running boots SLAMMING wet stone hard at full sprint. Skidding halt with explosive dust impact at the pit edge. Knee SLAMMING stone with bone impact thud. Friend's panicked shout from the pit cut off mid-breath. Hands SLAPPING with violent grip impact, loud and physical. Dark-haired man's hard grunt of effort RIPPING through gritted teeth as he hauls. Friend's body THUDDING up over the edge with cloth scrape on stone. Dark-haired man's sharp shouted grunt: "GO!" Both men's boots slamming back into a sprint, hurried unsynced rhythm, loud, urgent, footsteps pounding away into the distance. No music.

Total: 12s / 1 continuous shot / 16:9

参照图片 2（Image 2）中那名漂白黄色圆寸、深肤色、歪斜护目镜、深色夹克的男子。

Setting（环境）：两米宽的狭窄古老石质走廊，低顶棚。两侧近墙上有铁火把。凿刻象形文字墙壁。湿滑石地。浓重大气尘土薄雾。走廊正中：一处打开的陷阱坑，深肤色黄发男子靠指尖悬着，正滑脱，恐慌。走廊远端没入深影：一块巨大石板正震耳地从上方研磨下行，关闭那个黑暗的门洞，尘土以厚重浪涛自其边缘倾泻。石板的研磨轰鸣响亮、逼近、危险——这两人只有几秒。

Action（动作）：深色头发男子快速冲近，身体在坑缘狠滑刹停，险些自己也栽进去。他以暴烈力量单膝砸落，不看来路地把手向下抛入坑中，没有瞄准的时间。黄色头发男子的手啪地探上、首试便接住——两人都拼命抓握。深色头发男子随即以一个锐利咕哝猛地后拉，在一个暴烈扯动中把他的朋友自坑中拽扯而出。黄色头发男子的身体快速越上边缘，几乎是被抛出的，重重落在湿滑石地上的手与膝上。深色头发男子已站起——他抓住朋友的夹克把他一把提起站稳，毫不停顿。二人立即一同冲刺，毫无恢复时间，深色头发男子的手抓着朋友的背把他向前推。两人背朝摄影机奔入走廊纵深，逃离身后那震耳研磨的门洞。

Camera（摄影机）：全程手持但不稳定——操机员在动作中奔跑，画框剧烈抽搐，偶发短暂滑脱主体随即啪地拉回，摄影机随每次奔跑冲击大幅倾斜。在他们相握时推向接触点双手相握的极致紧密——画面被紧紧抓握的手指完全填满，随这绝望的能量而颤抖。身体快速穿过画面时摄影机勉强跟进。85mm 镜头，浅景深带自然的远摄压缩，走廊随奔跑运动以混沌的暖散景流光糊过，面孔以肖像镜头的平直感呈现，主体与柔和背景利落地分离。

Photorealistic（写实摄影），35mm 胶片颗粒，每个动作上都涂抹运动模糊，暖橙火把光反衬压碎浓黑，深远距离门洞石板降落的尘土可见地倾泻。

Sounds（声音）：响亮的持续石板研磨轰鸣主导音频，紧迫而危险——石板正在快速关闭。两人快速过度换气的呼吸，短促恐慌的喘息而非完整吸气。全速冲刺下奔跑靴子重重踏击湿滑石地。坑缘带爆散尘土冲击的滑刹停落。膝盖带骨击石的闷响砸击石头。朋友自坑中的恐慌喊叫在呼吸半途被切断。掌击带暴烈抓握撞击，响亮而物理。深色头发男子拖拽时从咬紧牙关挤出的用力硬咕哝。朋友的身体带织物在石上的刮擦砰然越上边缘。深色头发男子尖锐喊出的咕哝：「GO！（走！）」两人靴子重新砸入冲刺，急促不合拍的节奏，响亮、紧迫，脚步声叩入远方。无配乐。
