## 原文 (source)

Side-angle ground-level shot in an ancient stone tomb corridor, in the visual style of the opening cave sequence in Indiana Jones and the Last Crusade — heavy atmospheric dust, single hard torchlight from one wall, deep crushed blacks dominating the frame, warm amber on skin against pure black background.

Refer to the man with dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, sweat-streaked dust-covered face, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.

A massive ancient stone slab descends from the ceiling above a carved stone gateway, the gap below now reduced to a horizontal slit at ankle height. The man dives horizontally through the air, body parallel to the wet stone floor, arms outstretched forward in a Superman pose. He hits the slick stone with a hard slap and slides on his stomach under the descending slab through the slit, his jacket back grazing the stone with friction sparks. The instant his feet pass the threshold, the slab crashes down with a massive impact, dust exploding outward in a thick wall toward camera, partially obscuring the lens.

Visible heavy 35mm film grain, anamorphic lens flares from the torch when his body crosses it, slight focus breathing as he moves through depth, motion blur on the dive impact, lens fogging from dust at the end. No CGI smoothness, no clean digital look — physical, raw, dirty cinematography.

Sounds: deep stone grinding, body slap on wet stone, sliding on slick floor, fabric tearing on stone edge, massive slab impact, silence with breath. No music.
