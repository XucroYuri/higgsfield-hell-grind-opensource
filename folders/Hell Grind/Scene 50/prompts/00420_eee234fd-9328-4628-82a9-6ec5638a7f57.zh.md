## 原文 (source)

Low-angle medium close-up handheld shot of an ancient stone gateway being slowly closed from above by a massive descending stone slab, 3 seconds.

SCENE CONTEXT: this is the only exit from a deadly ancient trap inside an underground stone corridor. A massive stone slab is grinding down from above the doorway, slowly sealing the gateway shut. If anyone doesn't make it through in time, they are trapped inside forever. The descent is slow, inexorable, but the opening is still passable for now — narrowing but not yet closed.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with intense controlled camera shake — the camera alive in the operator's hands, trembling with grim recognition of the threat, breathing but not jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on the lens, aggressive halation from torch flames, slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges between fire and shadow, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm orange torch accents and deep blackness beyond the gateway. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from the two torches only — deep crushed blacks dominating the frame, hard angular warm light carving out the carved stone walls and floor. No flares.

LOCATION: an ancient underground corridor with massive stone walls covered in deeply carved hieroglyph and rune-like glyphs. The walls are weathered, rough, ancient — every surface densely covered in carvings, the wall texture organic and tactile. Two iron torches mounted on the side walls flanking the gateway — one on the left, one on the right — each casting a warm orange flame and halated glow that illuminates the carvings around it but leaves most of the corridor in deep shadow. Wet stone floor with rough flagstones, reflecting dim torch highlights. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust particles caught in the torch light. The center of the frame is a tall rectangular stone GATEWAY — a doorway carved into the massive wall, framed by stone pillars on both sides and a stone lintel above carved with ancient glyphs. Beyond the gateway is pure deep darkness, a void of black with no detail visible. Above the gateway, a massive stone slab is descending VERY SLOWLY from the ceiling — grinding downward inch by inch, pressing down on the top of the gateway opening from above. The upper portion of the gateway is already partially obscured by the descending slab, the carved lintel beginning to be hidden as the slab covers it. The lower portion of the doorway is still tall enough to walk through but visibly shrinking from above. Dust and small stone fragments cascade in steady waves from where the slab grinds down against the lintel, falling through the gateway into the darkness beyond. The descending slab itself has carved glyphs on its underside matching the wall carvings — clearly part of the ancient mechanism, not random rubble.

ACTION: handheld camera positioned at a LOW ANGLE on the corridor floor, looking UP at the gateway as a medium close-up — the bottom of the gateway opening (the floor threshold) at the bottom of the frame, the stone pillars framing the opening on both sides, the descending slab above filling the upper portion of frame as it grinds downward. The two torches on the side walls are visible at the edges of frame, their warm halated flames flickering. The cold dark void of the doorway dominates the center of the frame as a black rectangle compressed at the top by the descending slab. Camera trembles with intense controlled handheld shake throughout — the operator's nerves visible in every micro-jolt of the frame. The slab descends VERY SLOWLY in real time during the 3 seconds — a visible inch or two of grinding lower over the duration of the shot, the top of the doorway compressing measurably from above, dust pouring down in fresh waves as the stone grinds. The slowness of the descent is what makes it terrifying — there is no panicked speed, just inexorable inevitability. Halation streams off the torch flames at the edges. Atmospheric dust drifts through the warm torch light, individual particles catching as bokeh.

no music. sfx only — slow deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble of the descending slab dominating the audio, low and heavy, the rumble felt as much as heard, cascading dust and small stone fragments falling in steady waves from where the slab grinds against the lintel above the doorway, individual stones clattering faintly as they fall and bounce, the two torches on the side walls crackling with low hiss, faint deep echo within the corridor space, distant atmosphere of ancient stone settling.

## 中文对照 (zh)

低角中近景手持镜头、一座正被上方巨大下落石门缓缓封合的古石入口、3 秒。

场景情境（SCENE CONTEXT）：这是地下石廊中一处致命古老陷阱的唯一下出口。一块巨大石门自门洞上方磨擦下降、缓缓将入口封合。若有人未能及时通过、便会被永远困死其中。下落缓慢、不可抗拒、但开口目前仍可通过——在收窄、却尚未关闭。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影、强烈受控镜头晃动——摄影机在操机员手中鲜活、以对这威胁的阴沉认出而颤抖、呼吸着但不抖动。长焦远摄摄影、浅景深、f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜上可见的尘、火炬火焰剧烈光晕（halation）、火与影之间高反差边缘的轻微色差、偶发柔和漂焦、微淡 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影感宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和调色板、孤立暖橘火炬点缀 + 入口之外深沉漆黑。仅两支火炬的戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）布光——深沉的压碎黑主导画面、硬朗棱角的暖光雕凿出雕花石墙与地面。无耀光（flare）。

地点（LOCATION）：一条古地下廊、巨大石墙上满覆深刻象形文与符文样的纹样。墙风化、粗糙、古老——每寸表面都密刻雕纹、墙体纹理有机而具触感。两侧墙夹峙入口处装有两支铁制火把——左一、右一——各投一团暖橘火焰与带光晕辉光、照亮其周围的雕刻、却令走廊大半沉入深影。湿滑石板地面、粗糙石板、倒映暗淡火炬高光。大气雾霾浓、漂浮尘粒被火炬光捕获。画面中心是一座高大体矩形的石制入口——一处刻进巨墙的门洞、两侧石柱框沿、上方一道刻有古文符的石楣。入口之外是纯粹深沉黑暗、一处黑墟、无可见细节。入口上方、一块巨大石门正从天花板极缓下落——寸寸磨擦下行、自上压着入口开口的顶部。入口上半部已被下落门板部分遮蔽、石楣随门板盖上而开始被掩起。门洞下部仍高可行走、却自上明显收缩。门板与石楣磨擦处、尘与细小碎石以稳定波次倾泻、穿过入口落入彼方黑暗。下落门板的下侧刻有与墙上相配的符文纹样——显然是古老机关的一部分、绝非随意碎石。

动作（ACTION）：手持机位置于走廊地面的低角、朝上望向入口作中近景——入口开口底部（地面门槛）在画面底端、石柱自两侧框住开口、上方下落门板占满画面上部、磨擦下行。侧墙上两支火把在画面边缘可见、暖带光晕火焰闪烁。门洞的冷黑墟以一块顶端被下落门板压缩的黑色矩形主导画面中心。机位全程以强烈受控手持晃动颤抖——操机员的神经在画面的每一下微震中都可见。门板在这 3 秒内以实时极缓下落——镜头持续时间内可见地磨低了约一两寸、门洞顶部自上可测地压缩、石磨时尘以新波次倾泻。下落之慢正是它可怖之处——没有慌乱的急、只有不可抗拒的必然。光晕（halation）自边缘火炬火焰流出。大气尘在暖火炬光中飘动、单个微粒被捕捉为光斑。

无音乐。仅音效（SFX only）——下落石门缓慢深沉的石对石磨擦轰鸣主导音频、低而沉、轰鸣与其说是听到不如说是感到、门板与门楣磨擦处尘与细小碎石以稳定波次倾泻而落、单个石块落下弹跳时微弱磕响、侧墙两支火把带低咝声噼啪、走廊空间内微弱的深沉回声、远处古老石材沉降的氛围。
