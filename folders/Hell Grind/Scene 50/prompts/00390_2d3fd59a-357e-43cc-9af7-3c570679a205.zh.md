## 原文 (source)

Handheld medium shot of a young man pulling another young man out of a stone pit with raw strength, both crashing to the corridor floor afterward, 4 seconds.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Roco has just deactivated the crystalline outer layer of his cybernetic prosthetic arm, exposing his real human flesh hand beneath. He immediately reaches down into the pit toward Jax, his friend who has fallen into the trap and is hanging by his fingertips. The flesh hand grips Jax's wrist with full friction this time. Roco hauls Jax up out of the pit with one violent motion of pure strength, both of them crashing onto the wet stone floor of the corridor afterward, sprawled and gasping.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with violent reactive camera shake — alive in operator's hands, capturing the kinetic effort of the rescue. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — dust on the lens, halation from torch flames, slight chromatic aberration, occasional focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Heavily desaturated palette with warm orange torch tones, no more cold blue prosthetic glow. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from torches only.

LOCATION: a tight ancient underground stone corridor approximately two meters wide. Walls weathered carved stone with glyphs, lit iron torches on both walls casting flickering warm orange light. Wet stone floor reflecting torch highlights. In the middle of the corridor floor, an open trap pit — a dark shaft dropping into darkness with stone spikes barely visible far below. Atmospheric haze with floating dust.

CHARACTERS:
— Roco: young man, mid-twenties, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage strip across the bridge of his nose, dark jacket, dust-covered face. Kneeling at the pit edge. His RIGHT ARM now a normal human flesh arm and hand (the crystalline prosthetic outer layer has been deactivated and retracted), bare skin, sweat and dust on the newly exposed flesh.
— Jax: young man, mid-twenties, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked face. Hanging from the upper edge of the pit by his fingertips below the frame edge.

ACTION: handheld camera at chest height beside the pit, capturing the rescue moment in profile/three-quarter angle. Camera trembles violently throughout with the kinetic action.

(0–1s): Roco's flesh right hand SLAMS DOWN into the pit toward Jax (toward off-frame below), the bare flesh palm and fingers visible, no more crystal glow. The hand finds Jax's wrist and GRIPS it tight, flesh-on-flesh contact, fingers wrapping around the wrist with full friction this time. Roco's left hand grips the pit edge for leverage.

(1–2.5s): Roco GRUNTS with maximum effort, his entire body straining as he HEAVES upward in one violent motion, pulling Jax's full body weight up out of the pit. His back muscles strain visible through his jacket, jaw locked, neck tendons popping, every muscle committed to the lift. Jax's body emerges from below frame edge, dragged up — first his arm, then his shoulder, then his head and torso coming up over the pit edge, his face dust-caked and shocked, glasses askew.

(2.5–4s): The momentum of the heave throws BOTH bodies BACKWARDS away from the pit. Roco falls back hard onto the wet stone floor of the corridor, dragging Jax with him. Both bodies CRASH down hard onto the wet stone, sprawled together in an exhausted heap, dust exploding around them on impact. They both gasp for breath, chests heaving violently, the camera trembling with their physical exhaustion. Jax is alive, on solid ground. The two of them lie there breathing for the final beat. Camera holds on the exhausted pair.

no music. sfx only — Roco's bare flesh hand SLAPPING down into the pit and gripping with skin-on-skin friction sound, Roco's massive grunt of effort filling the audio, his back and arm muscles straining audibly through controlled breathing turning to roar of effort, Jax's body being dragged up out of the pit with his clothing and body scraping against the rough stone edge, the heave of body weight, both bodies crashing down hard onto wet stone with dual heavy thuds, dust exploding on impact, both characters gasping for breath in heavy ragged exhausted relief, deep slow grinding rumble of distant slab descending in the background, torches crackling.

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：一名叫 Roco 的年轻男子刚刚解除了他机械假肢臂的晶体外壳、露出底下真实的人类血肉之手。他立即探身入坑、伸向已坠入陷阱、仅凭指尖挂着的好友 Jax。这次血肉之手以全摩擦握住 Jax 的手腕。Roco 以一次纯力量的暴烈动作把 Jax 拽出坑、随后两人一起砸上走廊的湿滑石地板、瘫作一团、大口喘气。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：手持电影摄影、带暴烈反应的摄影机颤抖——在操机员手中活着、捕捉救援的动能艰辛。长焦远景电影拍摄、浅景深、f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜头上尘埃、火炬火焰光晕（halation）、轻微色差、偶发对焦漂移、淡淡的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影宽银幕 16:9。高度去饱和的调色板、带暖橙火炬色调、再无冷蓝假肢光。仅由火炬产生的戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）布光。

LOCATION（场地）：一条狭窄的古老地下石廊、约两米宽。墙壁为饱经风化、带符文的凿花石、两壁点亮的铁制火炬投下闪烁的暖橙色光。湿滑石地板反射火炬高光。走廊地板正中、一个敞开的陷阱坑——一条暗色竖井坠入黑暗、深处过远处几不可见的石尖。大气雾霾带漂浮尘埃。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
— Roco：年轻男子、二十多岁、深色凌乱齐肩长发、淡淡胡子、鼻梁上一条红色绷带条、深色夹克、蒙尘的脸。跪在坑沿。他的右臂现在是一只正常的人类血肉手臂与手（晶体假肢外壳已解除并收回）、赤裸皮肤、新暴露皮肉上带汗与尘。
— Jax：年轻男子、二十多岁、漂白金黄板寸头、深肤色、清晰护目镜歪斜、深色连帽夹克、蒙尘的脸。仅凭指尖挂在坑上沿、在画下缘之下。

ACTION（动作）：手持摄影机在坑边、胸高位置、以侧面 / 四分之三角度捕捉救援时刻。摄影机全程随这动能动作暴烈颤抖。

(0–1s)：Roco 的血肉右手「啪」地猛拍入坑、伸向 Jax（伸向下方画外）、赤裸的肉掌与手指可见、再无晶体光。手找到 Jax 的手腕、咬紧抓住它、皮肉对皮肉接触、这次手指以全摩擦缠绕手腕。Roco 的左手抓住坑沿借力。
