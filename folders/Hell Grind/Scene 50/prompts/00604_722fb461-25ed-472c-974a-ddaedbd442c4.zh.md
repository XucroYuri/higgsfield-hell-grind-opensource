## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, exploration sequence with trigger event, 15 seconds.

SCENE CONTEXT: continuing seconds after the explosion. Jax has now regained consciousness and is back on his feet — Roco helped him off the hoverboard moments ago. All three are now walking on foot. Hoverboards deactivated and tucked under arms. Tinnitus fading. Going back is impossible. Jax becomes distracted by the carved glyphs on a side wall. He touches them. Something clicks. Roco notices.

LOCATION GEOMETRY: tight stone corridor approximately two meters wide, ceiling two and a half meters. BEHIND — fresh rubble collapse from the recent blast, dust still drifting down. AHEAD — open carved stone gateway with cold blue light, their only way forward.

Setting: wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars pressed close on both walls, candles flickering, thick volumetric dust haze still suspended from blast aftermath, claustrophobic. Anamorphic widescreen, teal-shadow grade with warm candle accents, low-key lighting.

Characters (recently survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, black top, dark cargo pants, heavy dust. Hoverboard tucked under her arm, deactivated. Lead.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket, dusty. Recently regained consciousness, slightly unsteady, curious, easily distracted by the carvings.
— Roco (): dark messy hair, mustache, red nose bandage, dark jacket, dusty. His hoverboard tucked under arm. Stays close to Jax, watchful.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Tracking shot of Rain walking ahead, profile, camera arcs alongside her. She doesn't look back, eyes locked forward toward the distant gateway. The other two are barely visible in soft focus behind. Then, off-frame from behind, Jax's voice — slightly rough from inhaled dust, distracted, almost wonderstruck: "Yo. These markings. Look at 'em." Rain's expression flickers — she heard him but doesn't stop. Camera holds on her profile.

Shot 2 (3–8s): Hard cut to a tight extreme close-up of Jax's hand approaching the carved glyphs on a side pillar — the camera is millimeters from the stone, his fingertips entering frame slowly. The carvings are deep, weathered, alien. His hand makes contact. Fingers slide across the surface, dust shifting beneath them. Camera follows his fingers laterally. Then, as his palm passes over a specific glyph — a faint dull CLICK from inside the stone, a tiny shift of dust falling from the seam between two stones. The camera holds dead still on his hand, frozen on the carving. Jax's fingers don't move. Three full seconds of stillness on the click.

Shot 3 (8–11.5s): SNAP cut to a tight insert of Roco's eye, pupil dilating as he registers the click. His head whips toward Jax — camera whip-pans with him, blurring stone, snapping into focus on Jax's silhouette frozen with hand on the wall. Roco shouts, voice sharp and urgent, cracking on the word: "Wait!" One word. Push-in on Roco's face.

Shot 4 (11.5–15s): Hard cut to medium shot — Jax's hand still on the carving, head slowly turning toward Roco's voice, expression shifting from curiosity to dawning realization. Behind him, the corridor stretches deep, gateway glowing far ahead. The camera begins a slow PUSH IN on Jax's face. From within the walls, faintly at first, a deep mechanical groan begins — old stone machinery awakening for the first time in centuries. Jax's eyes widen. Cut.

no music. sfx only — Rain's footsteps, Jax's "Yo. These markings. Look at 'em" with stone reverb, fingers brushing carved stone with grit, faint dull CLICK from inside the wall, dust sifting from the seam, sharp "Wait!" with urgency, deep mechanical groan beginning within the walls, hoarse breathing, candle flicker, distant drip ahead, faint ongoing settle of debris from breach behind.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄远古石廊，含触发事件的探索序列，15 秒。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：爆炸后数秒续接。Jax 现已恢复意识、重新站起——Roco 片刻前把他扶下悬浮滑板。现在三人都步行。滑板已关闭、夹在臂下。耳鸣渐退。回去不可能。Jax 被一面侧墙上雕凿的符文分了心。他触碰它们。有什么咔哒一声。Roco 注意到。

LOCATION GEOMETRY（场地几何）：约两米宽的窄石廊、两米半天花板。身后——最近爆炸的新鲜碎石崩落，尘仍飘落。前方——带冷蓝光的敞雕石拱门，他们唯一的前进方向。

场景：湿石地面、两侧墙紧贴的符文覆盖石柱、蜡烛闪烁、爆炸余波悬浮起的浓厚体积尘土雾霭、幽闭。变形宽银幕、青影调色板带暖烛光点缀、低调布光。

角色（最近爆炸中生还、覆尘）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银金色头发凌乱、眼旁一道疤、太阳穴一道新伤口、灰色连帽夹克、黑色上衣、深色工装裤、覆重尘。一块已关闭的滑板夹在臂下。领队。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：漂白黄板寸，深肤色，透明护镜，深色连帽夹克，覆尘。最近恢复意识、略微不稳、好奇、易被雕刻分心。
— Roco（<img_roco>）：深色凌乱短发、小胡子、红鼻绷带、深色夹克、覆尘。他的滑板夹在臂下。紧贴 Jax、警惕。

Shot 1（0–3 秒）：Rain 在前面行走的跟踪镜头、侧影、摄影机弧线随她移动。她不回头、目光向前锁死远处拱门。另两人在身后柔焦里几乎不可见。随后，自画外身后，Jax 的声音——因吸入尘土而略哑、心不在焉、近乎惊叹："Yo. These markings. Look at 'em.（唷。这些符号。看看它们。）"Rain 的神情一闪——她听见了但不停下。摄影机停在她侧影上。

Shot 2（3–8 秒）：硬切至 Jax 的手接近侧柱上雕凿符文的手持紧凑超特写——机位距石只有几毫米、他的指尖缓慢进入画面。雕刻深沉、风化、异样。他的手相触。手指划过表面、指下尘在滑动。摄影机横向跟随他的手指。随后，当他的掌扫过一处特定符文——石头内部传来一声微弱的沉闷 CLICK（咔哒）、一阵微小的尘从两块石之间接缝崩落。摄影机死寂地停在他的手上、冻结在雕刻上。Jax 的手指不动。在这声咔哒上整整三秒静止。

Shot 3（8–11.5 秒）：SNAP 猛切至 Roco 眼睛的紧凑插叙、他辨出咔哒声时瞳孔放大。他的头 whips（猛甩）向 Jax——摄影机随他 whip-pan（甩镜）、石头模糊、砰地撞进对焦、落定在 Jax 手按着墙、冻住的剪影上。Roco 呼喊、声音尖锐而急迫、在字上迸裂："Wait！（等等！）"一个字。推近 Roco 的脸。

Shot 4（11.5–15 秒）：硬切至中景——Jax 的手仍按在雕刻上、头缓缓转向 Roco 的声音、神情从好奇转为渐起的领悟。他身后走廊深深延伸、远处拱门发光。摄影机开始对 Jax 的脸缓慢 PUSH IN（推近）。自墙内部，起初隐隐地，一声深沉机械呻吟开始——古老的石器机械数百年来首次苏醒。Jax 的眼睛睁大。切。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——Rain 的脚步、Jax 带石回响的"Yo. These markings. Look at 'em"、手指带砂砾刷过雕石、墙内微弱的沉闷 CLICK、接缝筛尘、尖锐带急迫的"Wait！"、墙内开始深沉机械呻吟、嘶哑的呼吸、蜡烛闪烁、前方远处滴水、身后缺口碎料微弱持续的沉降。
