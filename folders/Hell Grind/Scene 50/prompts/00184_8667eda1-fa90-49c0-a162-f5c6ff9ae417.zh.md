## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, escape sequence beginning, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with subtle controlled camera shake — alive in operator's hands, breathing, never jerky. Long-lens telephoto at f/1.2 aperture with extreme shallow depth of field. Lens imperfections — visible dust on lens, halation aggressive from torch flames, chromatic aberration on edges, occasional gentle focus drift, faint film grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. Heavily desaturated palette with warm torch accents and cold blue gateway light. Dramatic chiaroscuro from torches only. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse from earlier blast. Ahead — distant carved stone gateway with cold blue light spilling through. A massive stone slab is slowly descending from the ceiling above the gateway — the slab has only just begun its descent and is still high, leaving a wide vertical opening of cold blue light through which the gateway is clearly visible. Lit iron torches on both walls. Arrows embedded in both walls from previous volley. Wet stone floor with depressed pressure tile.

CHARACTERS (recovering from arrow volley, dust-covered, in defensive prone positions):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair disheveled, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Lying flat on the wet stone, on her stomach, head turned sideways against the floor.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Lying CURLED in fetal position on the wet stone, face down, arms wrapped around his head protectively, yellow buzzcut visible from above, body compact and low.
— Roco (): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Lying on his stomach on the wet stone, propped up slightly on his forearms, head raised to look forward.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Handheld long-lens low-angle shot down the corridor at floor level, focus on the distant gateway. The massive carved stone slab is descending slowly from the ceiling above the gateway — it has descended only about a third of the way, the gap below it still tall and wide, cold blue light streaming through clearly. The slab's progress is gradual, deliberate, threatening but not imminent. Halation streams off the bright opening below. In foreground bokeh, prone bodies of the three on wet stone — Jax curled, Rain prone, Roco propped on forearms with head raised. Camera trembles subtly. Deep grinding stone-on-stone rumble of the descending slab.

Shot 2 (3–5s): Hard cut to handheld long-lens close-up of Jax curled on the floor, yellow buzzcut visible as he lifts his head slightly, dust-streaked face emerging from his arms. He breathes hard. His eyes lock forward toward the distant gateway off-frame. Voice rough with realization: "Bro. The door."

Shot 3 (5–7s): Handheld long-lens close-up of Roco propped on his forearms, head raised. Jax visible in soft bokeh behind him. Hard torch light slashes across Roco's features. His eyes assess forward — gateway, slowly descending slab, distance — for one beat. Then he commits. Voice flat with command: "On me." He pushes up off the floor.

Shot 4 (7–10s): Handheld long-lens medium shot from low angle as Roco SPRINGS to his feet first and starts running toward the gateway. Rain follows immediately — pushing up, snatching her hoverboard and tablet from the floor, sprinting after him. Jax is last — uncurling from fetal position, scrambling up, getting his feet under him. Camera tracks with their motion forward, dust kicking up, arrow-cage walls passing in rapid bokeh.

Shot 5 (10–12.5s): Handheld long-lens close-up tracking with Jax as he runs, a beat behind the others. Camera trembling with the run. Breath ragged, eyes forward on Roco's back. Boots pounding wet stone. Then suddenly — a sharp CLICK from beneath his feet.

Shot 6 (12.5–15s): Hard cut to handheld extreme close-up at floor level — a worn stone tile DROPS AWAY beneath Jax's foot mid-stride, the entire tile collapsing into a dark hole below. Jax PLUNGES downward, body dropping out of frame. His hands SCRABBLE at the edge — fingertips catching the stone lip — his body slamming against the inner wall of the pit. He hangs by his fingertips. Camera trembles, then holds tight on his fingers gripping the edge, knuckles white, dust falling past into darkness below.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭长古石廊，逃跑序列开端，15 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影、细微受控镜头晃动——在操机员手中鲜活、呼吸着、绝不猛震。长焦远摄 f/1.2 光圈、极端浅景深。镜头瑕疵——镜上可见的尘、火炬火焰剧烈光晕（halation）、边缘色差、偶发柔和漂焦、微淡胶片颗粒。变形宽银幕 2.39:1。高度去饱和调色板、暖火炬点缀 + 冷蓝入口光。仅火炬的戏剧性明暗对照（chiaroscuro）。灵感源出 Snyder《活死人军团》。

地点（LOCATION）：狭促古石廊、两米宽。角色身后——早前爆炸的新鲜碎石坍堆。前方——远处的石雕入口、冷蓝光从中泄出。一块巨大石门正从入口上方天花板缓缓下落——门板才刚刚开始其下落、仍高、留出一道宽阔的冷蓝光竖直开口、入口透过它清晰可见。两侧墙点亮的铁制火把。先前齐射的箭嵌在两侧墙。湿滑石板地面、带一块下陷的感应石砖。

角色（CHARACTERS，正从箭齐射中恢复、覆尘、处防御俯卧姿势）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银金发凌乱、眼旁疤痕、太阳穴新割口、灰连帽夹克、蒙尘。平躺在湿石上、趴着、头侧转贴地。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色平头、深肤色、透明护目镜、深色夹克、蒙尘。蜷成胎儿姿势伏在湿石上、面朝下、双臂防护性地抱头、自上可见黄色平头、身体紧凑而贴地。
— Roco：深色凌乱发、胡须、鼻上红绷带、深色夹克、蒙尘。趴在湿石上、以前臂微微撑起、头抬起朝前望。

镜头 1（0-3s）：地面层顺走廊的手持长焦低角度镜头、对焦远处入口。巨大的石雕门板正从入口上方天花板缓缓下落——它才刚下落到三分之一处、其下缝隙仍高而宽、冷蓝光清晰流泄。门板的进展渐变、从容、带有威胁但非迫在眉睫。光晕（halation）自下方明亮开口流出。前景光斑中、三人俯卧身体在湿石上——Jax 蜷着、Rain 俯卧、Roco 撑在前臂上头抬起。机位细微颤抖。下落门板深沉的石对石磨擦轰鸣。

镜头 2（3-5s）：硬切至蜷地 Jax 的手持长焦特写、他把头微微抬起、覆尘的脸自双臂中露出、可见黄色平头。他粗喘。他的眼锁定前方出画的远处入口。声音因恍然而粗沉：「Bro. The door.（哥们儿。那门。）」

镜头 3（5-7s）：撑在前臂上、头抬起的 Roco 的手持长焦特写。Jax 在他身后柔光斑中可见。硬火炬光斜劈过 Roco 的五官。他的眼评估前方——入口、缓缓下落门板、距离——一瞬。随即他投入。声音沉着带命令：「On me.（跟我来。）」他自地面推起身。

镜头 4（7-10s）：低角手持长焦中景、Roco 率先弹起身、开始朝入口跑去。Rain 立即跟上——撑起、自地面抄起她的悬浮滑板与平板、追在他身后冲刺。Jax 最后——自胎儿姿势展开、爬起、双腿撑稳站住。机位跟他们的前进运动、尘踢起、两侧箭墙快速光斑掠过。

镜头 5（10-12.5s）：手持长焦特写跟着奔跑的 Jax、落后他人一拍。机位随奔跑颤抖。呼吸粗重、双眼锁定前方 Roco 的背。靴砸湿石。随即突然——他脚下传来一声尖锐咔哒（CLICK）。

镜头 6（12.5-15s）：硬切至地面高度的手持极特写——Jax 半步中、一块磨损石砖在他脚下塌落、整块砖坍进下方一个黑洞。Jax 向下猛坠、身体掉出画面。他的手在洞口边缘乱抓（SCRABBLE）——指端勾住石缘——身体重重撞上坑的内壁。他以指端悬吊。机位颤抖、随即紧钉在他抓紧缘沿的手指上、指节泛白、尘掠过落入下方黑暗。
