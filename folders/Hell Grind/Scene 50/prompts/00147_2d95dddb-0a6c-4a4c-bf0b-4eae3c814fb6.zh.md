Six-shot sequence in an ancient stone corridor, 15 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, heavy 35mm film grain, motion blur, no music.

Refer to the man with dark messy hair, light mustache, red bandage on his nose, dark hooded jacket from Image 1.
Refer to the man with bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, askew protective glasses, dark jacket from Image 2.

Setting: a long ancient underground stone corridor with low rough-hewn stone ceiling, walls covered in horizontal bands of carved ancient script, flagstone floor of irregular cracked stone slabs. Iron torch brackets mounted at angle ONLY on the LEFT wall every few meters — the right wall is dark and bare, no torches, only carved stone in shadow. Thick atmospheric dust haze. In the middle of the corridor: an open trap pit with the dark-skinned yellow-haired man hanging from the edge by his fingertips. In the very far distance through the haze: a closing stone gateway with a slowly descending stone slab.

Roco's ability: he summons crystalline armor on his right arm — sharp jagged blood-red crystal formations materializing on top of his right jacket sleeve as energy-based external armor with internal red glow.

Shot 1 (3s) — STYLISH HERO RUN TOWARD CAMERA:
Handheld low-angle shot at floor level looking up the corridor at the dark-haired man sprinting full speed toward camera through the long passage. He runs through the long perspective of the torchlit left wall, distant figure growing larger as he approaches, body in full athletic motion, cape of his jacket flying behind him, dust kicking up from each boot strike. Halated torch light flares as he passes each torch on the left, his silhouette punching through pools of warm light and dark void in alternation. He looks DETERMINED — jaw clenched, eyes burning forward locked on his goal. Camera trembles with anticipation, holding low. The right wall stays in deep dark void.

Shot 2 (2.5s) — SLIDING ARRIVAL WITH CRYSTAL ACTIVATION:
Hard cut. Handheld three-quarter angle medium shot at floor level. The dark-haired man arrives at the pit edge at full sprint and DROPS into a power slide on his knee, body sliding the last meter across the wet flagstones with sparks of friction, leg extended behind for balance, body angled forward. The MOMENT his sliding knee impacts the pit edge, sharp red crystalline shards EXPLODE into existence on his right jacket sleeve from shoulder to fingertips in one violent burst — the armored arm forming instantly with internal red glow blazing alive, dust kicking up around him from the slide impact. He freezes for a fraction of a second at the pit edge in this dynamic pose — knee down, crystal arm extended forward ready to reach, face set with determination. Camera shudders from the impact of his arrival.

Shot 3 (2.5s) — HIGH ANGLE LOOKING DOWN AT JAX IN THE PIT:
Hard cut. Handheld extreme high angle from above the pit looking down at the yellow-haired man hanging from the edge. His face fills the frame — eyes wide and terrified, glasses askew, mouth open in a panicked gasp. His arms above frame strain visibly, hands gripping the stone lip with white knuckles, fingers trembling, slipping a fraction. He looks UP toward camera with raw fear. Dark void of the pit drops away below him. Camera trembles with his desperation. He gasps a single cracked word: "Roco!"

Shot 4 (2.5s) — EXTREME CLOSE-UP ON THE HANDS MEETING:
Hard cut. Handheld extreme close-up at the pit edge, frame fully filled by the moment of contact. The crystal-armored hand SLAMS down into frame from above — sharp red shards, internal glow, alien and powerful. Jax's flesh hand reaches up from below to meet it. Their hands CLASP — flesh fingers wrapping around the crystal-armored hand, gripping desperately, tactile and physical. The two contrasting textures locked together — sharp red crystal facets against dark-skinned flesh. Camera holds tight on the contact, trembling. Held briefly to let the gravity of the moment land.

Shot 5 (3s) — MEDIUM SHOT OF THE HEAVE AND RISE, CAMERA SHAKING VIOLENTLY:
Hard cut. Handheld medium shot at the pit edge, three-quarter angle, framed so the warmly lit left wall is visible behind the characters and the right wall stays in dark shadow. Dust falls in steady streams from the rough stone ceiling above the characters, particles raining down through the torch light, the ceiling visibly stressed. In the deep background through the atmospheric haze, the distant gateway visible — the descending stone slab lower than before, the cold blue gap visibly shrinking in real time during the shot. The dark-haired man on his knee straining under the friend's weight, every muscle pushed to its limit, barely making it — face contorted in effort, teeth bared, jaw clenched so hard muscles spasm in his neck, veins bulging on his temple, sweat pouring through dust on his face. He pulls upward in violent jerking heaves — multiple desperate yanks, arm shaking, body almost giving out and recovering, almost slipping forward into the pit himself. He lets out a raw guttural roar through gritted teeth, throat tearing with effort. Camera SHAKES violently with each heave, frame jolting and dropping with every yank. Jax's body finally comes up over the edge in the last desperate yank, dragged out by the wrist, body THUDDING hard onto wet stone. The dark-haired man collapses backward briefly from released tension, gasping. Without pause he forces himself up, grabs Jax's jacket, yanks him to his feet. Both men stumble upright. He grips friend's shoulder, glances toward the distant gateway — registering how close to closed it is — then turns Jax forward urgently.

Shot 6 (2s) — TRACKING SHOT BEHIND BOTH MEN SPRINTING AWAY:

The row of torches on the left wall blurs past as bokeh streaks from the running motion, the right wall stays in deep dark void. The distant gateway visible far ahead through the atmospheric haze, still small, the cold blue gap shrinking from above. Camera trembles with the chase, barely keeping up.

场景：一条长长的远古地下石廊、低矮粗凿石顶，墙壁覆满横向带状雕凿的古代文字，地面是凹凸不规则开裂的石板（flagstone）。铁火把支架带角度只装在 LEFT 墙（左墙）上，每隔几米一根——右墙黑暗而裸露，无火把，只有阴影中的雕石。浓厚的大气尘雾。走廊中央：一个敞开的陷阱坑，那名深肤色黄发男子正抓着边缘悬在那里。透过雾霭极远处：一座正在关闭的石拱门，上方缓缓降落的石板。

Roco 的能力：他在右臂上召唤水晶甲胄——尖锐锯齿状血红水晶构造，作为附在右夹克袖上的能量型体外甲具现，带内部红辉光。

Shot 1（3 秒）——STYLISH HERO RUN TOWARD CAMERA（风格化英雄奔向机位）：
手持低角仰拍，位于地面高度，仰望走廊里那名深发男子沿长通道全速冲向机位。他穿过火把照亮左墙的悠长透视，远处的身影随他接近而变大，身体处于完整运动状态，夹克披风在他身后飞扬，每只靴一踏就扬尘。他掠过左侧每根火把时带光晕的（halated）火光爆发，他的剪影交替穿过温暖光池与黑暗虚空。他神情 DETERMINED（坚定）——下颌紧咬，双眼灼灼向前锁死目标。摄影机带着期盼而颤动，保持低位。右墙保持深深黑暗虚空。

Shot 2（2.5 秒）——SLIDING ARRIVAL WITH CRYSTAL ACTIVATION（带水晶激活的滑身抵达）：
硬切。手持四分之三角中景，位于地面高度。深发男子全速冲刺抵达坑边，DROPS（落下）成膝上的力量滑行，身体带着摩擦火花滑过最后一块湿石板，一条腿向后伸展平衡，身体前倾。他滑行的膝一撞上坑边的那一刻，锋锐血红水晶碎片在他右夹克袖上从肩到指尖以一次猛烈爆发 EXPLODE（炸现）成形——甲胄臂瞬间成型、内部红辉光灼灼迸亮，他周围因滑行撞击而扬尘。他在坑边以这个动态姿势定格了零点几秒——膝跪地、水晶臂前伸随时可探、面容带着坚定定格。摄影机因他抵达的撞击而颤抖。

Shot 3（2.5 秒）——HIGH ANGLE LOOKING DOWN AT JAX IN THE PIT（俯视坑中 Jax 的高角仰拍）：
硬切。手持极高角、从坑上方俯看悬在边缘的黄发男子。他的脸占满画面——双眼圆睁满是惊恐、护镜歪斜、嘴张开发出恐慌的抽气。他上方的双臂明显绷紧，双手白着指节紧扣石唇，手指颤抖、微微滑移。他带着 raw 恐惧仰望向机位。坑的黑暗虚空在他脚下坠开。摄影机随他的绝望而颤抖。他迸出一个破碎的字："Roco！"

Shot 4（2.5 秒）——EXTREME CLOSE-UP ON THE HANDS MEETING（双手相接的超特写）：

Shot 5（3 秒）——MEDIUM SHOT OF THE HEAVE AND RISE, CAMERA SHAKING VIOLENTLY（奋力拉拽与起身的中景，摄影机剧烈颤抖）：
硬切。手持中景于坑边、四分之三角，构图使温暖照亮左墙可见于角色之后、右墙保持黑暗阴影中。尘土从角色头顶粗糙石顶的缝隙成股落下，颗粒穿过火把光而下倾泻，天花板明显受压着。透过大气雾霭的纵深背景，可见远处拱门——降下的石板比之前更低，冷蓝缝隙在镜头中实时可见地缩小着。膝跪地的深发男子在朋友的重压下苦苦撑着，每块肌肉都被推到极限、勉强支撑——面容因用力而扭曲、龇着牙、下颌紧咬到颈部肌肉痉挛、太阳穴青筋暴鼓、汗穿过脸上的尘土直流。他以猛烈抽搐的奋拉向上拽——多次绝望的一扯、手臂发抖、身体几乎垮掉又撑回来、自己都险些向前滑进坑里。他隔着咬紧的牙关迸出一声 raw 喉底咆哮，喉咙因用力而要撕裂。摄影机随每次奋拉剧烈颤抖，每拉一下画格都颠震下坠。Jax 的身体终于在上一次绝望的猛拉中被拽着手腕拉上边缘，重重地 THUDDING（砰）砸上湿石。深发男子因张力释放短暂向后瘫软、喘着气。绝不停顿他强迫自己起身，抓住 Jax 的夹克，把他拽起来。两名男子都踉跄站直。他握住朋友的肩，瞥向远处拱门——察觉它离关闭有多近——然后急迫地把 Jax 转向前方。

Shot 6（2 秒）——TRACKING SHOT BEHIND BOTH MEN SPRINTING AWAY（两名男子跑远之后的追踪镜头）：
硬切。手持追踪镜头自后方、低角，跟随两名男子一起爆冲成冲刺、背离去往走廊纵深、奔向极远处正在关闭的拱门。他们的背影快速远去，深发男子的手按在朋友背上推他向前。左墙那排火把因奔跑运动化作 bokeh 残影掠过眼前，右墙保持深深黑暗虚空。远处拱门透过大气雾霭遥遥可见，仍然很小，冷蓝缝隙自上方缩小。摄影机随这场追逐而颤抖，勉强跟上。

声音：所有镜头全程都有连续响亮的远处石板研磨轰隆、音频里的紧迫时钟。Shot 1——全速冲刺中跑靴 SLAMMING（重砸）湿石、夹克布料随动甩动、Roco 艰涩的冲刺呼吸、每次撞击远处扬起尘土。Shot 2——靴子在湿石上带摩擦尖叫硬滑、膝盖撞上坑边的砰响、水晶甲胄以爆发性机械有机共鸣具现、深红共振嗡鸣爆炽点燃、尘土撞击波。Shot 3——Jax 恐慌惊悚的急促呼吸、手指在粗糙石上磨刮、他单个破碎的"Roco！"带深深石回响。Shot 4——水晶甲胄手以 grip 撞击 SLAPPING 拍下、双手带摩擦声锁合、共同粗重的呼吸。Shot 5——石尘不断从天花板滴落飘落、石板研磨更大更近、Roco 的野兽发力的咆哮从闷哼升级为撕裂的嘶喊、多次随闷哼撞击的猛烈奋拉、身体险些带布料刮石沿滑倒、Jax 重重砸上湿石、Roco 喘息的恢复、迅速扭身强撑起来、他一看到拱门便恐慌地尖促吸气。Shot 6——两名男子奔跑的靴子加速成急迫不同步的冲刺、脚步咚咚远去跌入走廊纵深、布料随动甩动、呼吸淡入远处。
