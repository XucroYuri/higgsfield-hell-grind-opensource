## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, gateway sealing finale, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography with controlled trembling camera — the operator surviving the chaos, breath returning, frame trembling from exhaustion and adrenaline rather than panic. Long-lens telephoto at f/1.2. Lens imperfections — dust on lens, halation, chromatic aberration, focus drift, light leaks, barrel distortion, film grain. Anamorphic 2.39:1. Heavily desaturated palette with warm torch accents and cold blue gateway glow that shrinks. Dramatic chiaroscuro. Camera shake gentler than previous clip but still alive — the operator alive but shaken.

LOCATION: tight stone corridor, two meters wide. Behind — fresh rubble collapse. Ahead — distant gateway with cold blue light, now being SEALED by descending slab. Lit torches. Arrows embedded in both walls.

CHARACTERS (pinned, watching the exit close):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Pressed flat to floor, head forward.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Pressed flat.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, pressed flat next to Jax.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Handheld long-lens close-up on Rain's face pressed to wet stone, camera trembling close — operator low to the ground. Shallow focus on her eye, occasional drift with the shake. Silver hair fanned across the floor as halated strands catching warm torch light. Her eye locked forward toward the gateway. The cold blue glow reflects in her iris as a soft bokeh highlight. The reflection in her eye begins to compress into a horizontal slit. Her pupil dilates. Off-frame, deep grinding rumble. Camera holds on her eye as the realization travels across her face without her moving — only the eye holds it.

Shot 2 (4–8s): Match-cut to handheld Rain's POV from floor level, looking forward through the corridor toward the gateway. Camera trembling — the operator embodying Rain's prone perspective. Foreground arrow shafts protruding from both walls render as massive soft bokeh shapes framing the shot. Razor-sharp focus deep at the distant gateway, where cold blue light streams through. A massive carved stone slab DESCENDS from the ceiling above the gateway. Slow. Inexorable. The bright cold blue light begins to compress into a horizontal slit. Halation streams off the diminishing light, bleeding into the dust-laden air. Camera holds with subtle shake — Rain breathing, the camera breathing with her. Deep grinding rumble dominates.

Shot 3 (8–12s): Handheld long-lens slow PUSH-IN along the floor toward the gateway, the operator crawling forward with the camera. Foreground arrows still framing as bokeh canopy. The descending slab continues lowering, blue gap narrowing to a thin horizontal slit, halation intense around the diminishing aperture, lens flares stretching horizontally across the anamorphic frame. The push-in is unsteady, the operator's effort visible in the shake. Dust cascades from the slab's edges, particles glowing in the shrinking light, dust on lens catching glare.

Shot 4 (12–15s): Handheld long-lens tightest close-up on Rain's eye, camera close and trembling. Only her iris fills the frame, the cold blue gateway light reflected in it shrinking to a thin horizontal line as the slab seals. Halation intense. The reflected slit grows thinner. Then — the reflected line disappears. Her eye in total darkness now, only warm torch light from the side of her face remaining. A final DEEP THUD of stone meeting stone. Camera holds for a beat in the new darkness — operator's controlled breathing audible. Cut to black.

no music. sfx only — deep grinding stone-on-stone descent of slab, dust cascade, ragged shallow breathing, torches crackling, faint glitch from cracked tablet screen, distant rumble of secondary debris from blast site behind, cold blue light's faint hum diminishing as gap closes, final heavy THUD of stone meeting stone on the cut to black.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭长古石廊，入口封合终局，15 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：手持摄影、受控颤动的摄影机——操机员自混乱中幸存、呼吸渐渐平复、画面因疲惫与肾上腺素而非恐慌而颤抖。长焦远摄 f/1.2。镜头瑕疵——镜上尘、光晕（halation）、色差、漂焦、漏光、桶形畸变、胶片颗粒。变形宽银幕 2.39:1。高度去饱和调色板、暖火炬点缀 + 不断缩小的冷蓝入口辉光。戏剧性明暗对照。镜头晃动比上一片段更柔和但仍鲜活——操机员活着但被震动了。

地点（LOCATION）：狭促石廊、两米宽。身后——新鲜碎石坍堆。前方——带冷蓝光的远处入口、如今正被下落门板封合。点亮的火把。箭嵌在两侧墙。

角色（CHARACTERS，紧贴地面、望着出口关闭）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银金发、眼旁疤痕、太阳穴新割口、灰连帽夹克、蒙尘。紧贴地面、头朝前。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色平头、深肤色、透明护目镜、深色夹克、蒙尘。紧贴地面。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱发、胡须、鼻上红绷带、深色夹克、紧贴 Jax 旁边。

镜头 1（0-4s）：Rain 紧贴湿石的脸部手持长焦特写、机位逼近颤抖——操机员低贴地面。她眼上浅焦、随晃动偶发漂移。银发铺散地面、带光晕的发缕捕捉暖火炬光。她的眼锁定前方入口。冷蓝辉光作为柔和光斑高光映在她虹膜上。她眼里的倒影开始压缩成一道水平缝。她瞳孔放大。出画处、深沉磨擦轰鸣。机位端在她眼上、恍然漫过她的脸而她不动——只有眼承住它。

镜头 2（4-8s）：匹配切（Match-cut）至地面层的 Rain 手持主观视角（POV）、朝前穿过走廊望向入口。机位颤抖——操机员具身 Rain 的俯卧视角。两支箭杆自两侧墙凸出、渲染为框住镜头的大量柔光斑形状。深处远处入口处剃刀锐焦、冷蓝光从中流泄。一块巨大石雕门板自入口上方天花板降下。缓慢。不可抗拒。明亮冷蓝光开始压缩成一道水平缝。光晕（halation）自缩小中的光流出、渗进载尘的空气。机位以细微晃动端着——Rain 呼吸、机位随她一同呼吸。深沉磨擦轰鸣主导。

镜头 3（8-12s）：手持长焦沿地面缓慢推近（PUSH-IN）朝向入口、操机员带机俯爬前行。前景箭仍以光斑帘幕框着。下落门板继续降低、蓝缝收窄为一道细水平缝、缩小中的光孔周围光晕（halation）强烈、镜头耀光（flare）横向拉过变形银幕画面。推近不稳、操机员的吃力在晃动中可见。尘自门板边缘倾泻、微粒在缩小中的光里发亮、镜上尘捕捉眩光。

镜头 4（12-15s）：Rain 眼部的手持长焦最紧特写、机位逼近而颤抖。仅她的虹膜填满画面、其中倒映的冷蓝入口光随门板封合缩成一道细水平线。光晕（halation）强烈。倒影的缝越收越细。随即——倒影的线消失了。她的眼此刻陷于全然黑暗、只剩下她脸侧一方的暖火炬光。一声最终的沉闷石对石闷响（THUD）。机位在新黑暗中端住一拍——操机员受控的呼吸可闻。切至黑。

无音乐。仅音效（SFX only）——门板深沉石对石磨擦下落、尘倾泻、粗重浅呼吸、火把噼啪、碎裂平板屏幕的微弱故障嗡、身后爆炸现场二次碎屑的远处轰鸣、缝隙合拢时冷蓝光微弱嗡鸣渐微、切黑时那一声最终沉重石对石闷响（THUD）。
