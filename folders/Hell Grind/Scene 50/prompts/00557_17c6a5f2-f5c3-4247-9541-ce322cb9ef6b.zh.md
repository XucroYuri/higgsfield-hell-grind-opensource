## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, opening sequence with awakening torches and team briefing, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: cinematic anamorphic 2.39:1, long-lens telephoto cinematography, shallow depth of field, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from torch flames, deep blacks with halated warm orange highlights, atmospheric dust haze. Heavily desaturated palette with isolated warm pops from fire and cold blue glow of Rain's tablet screen. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead.

SCENE CONTEXT: this scene begins seconds after a violent explosion collapsed the passage above, sending the three characters falling into this narrow corridor below. The corridor RESPONDS to their presence — wall-mounted torches IGNITE one by one in sequence. Each character begins examining the location in their own way. Rain consults a handheld tablet showing schematics. Jax is drawn to carved glyphs on the wall. Roco scans the corridor tactically. Then Rain feels something wrong about the place but cannot name it — she warns the team and begins walking forward.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide, low ceiling. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse from breach above. Ahead — wall-mounted iron torch brackets along both side walls; at the far end, an open carved stone gateway with faint cold blue light spilling through.

CHARACTERS (just survived a blast, dust-covered):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Hoverboard tucked under one arm. Holding a small handheld tablet, screen casting cold blue light up onto her face. Lead, analytical.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Curious, drawn to the carvings.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Hoverboard under arm. Tactical, scanning constantly.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Long-lens static shot looking down the length of the corridor toward the distant gateway, telephoto compression flattening depth. The corridor is dark — only faint cold blue bokeh from the gateway visible at the far end. The FIRST iron torch nearest camera IGNITES with a soft whump, flame blooming into a halated orange flare. The NEXT torch ignites deeper — appearing first as a defocused glowing sphere, then snapping into resolution as focus racks to it. Each successive ignition causes a focus shift, the camera rack-focusing through the depth of the corridor torch by torch. By the end, the corridor is bathed in flickering halated warm orange light.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Hard cut to long-lens close-up of Rain's face from a low three-quarter angle. Cold blue light from her tablet (held just below frame) underlit on her face from below, mixing with warm torch light from behind. Shallow focus on her eyes. Corridor behind her dissolved into orange torch bokeh. Her eyes flick down to the tablet, then back up to the corridor ahead. She says with quiet authority: "Final stretch. Forty feet."

Shot 3 (6–8.5s): Hard cut to extreme close-up of Jax's hand approaching the carved glyphs on a side pillar. Shallow focus on his fingertips at the moment of contact. The nearest torch flickers warm halated light across the carvings. His fingers slide across the surface, dust shifting beneath them. Pull focus from the stone surface to his eye behind glasses. Off-frame his voice, slightly rough, almost wonderstruck: "Yo. These markings. Look at 'em."

Shot 4 (8.5–10s): Hard cut to long-lens medium close-up of Roco from a three-quarter angle. Hard torch light slashes across his features, half his face lost in coal-black shadow. His eyes scan methodically — looking up toward the carved ceiling, then down toward the floor ahead, then to a dark recess between pillars. Tactical, silent. Brow tight, jaw set. He doesn't speak.

Shot 5 (10–15s): Hard cut to long-lens medium close-up of Rain in profile. She lowers the tablet slightly, eyes narrowing. The blue tablet glow fades from her face. Warm torch light catches one side of her face, the other in coal-black shadow. She turns her head slightly, scanning. She says, lower and more urgent than before, with a tightness in her voice: "Guys. Something's off." A beat. Eyes flick to where Jax is off-frame, then briefly toward Roco off-frame. "Stay close." Hold on her face for a moment. Then her gaze locks forward toward the distant gateway. Her jaw sets. She turns her head fully forward and TAKES THE FIRST STEP — her body begins moving into the depth of the corridor, the camera holding static as she advances slightly out of the frame's sharp focal plane, her silhouette beginning to soften into bokeh as she moves deeper. The motion of her stepping continues the moment off-frame as the clip ends.

no music. sfx only — settling debris and trickling stone dust from the breach behind, distant rumble of secondary collapse far above, faint ringing tinnitus from blast aftermath, sequential whump of torches igniting (five distinct ignitions through Shot 1), crackling flames, faint electronic hum from Rain's tablet, soft tap of her fingers on the screen, Rain's "Final stretch. Forty feet." with stone reverb, fingers brushing carved stone with grit, Jax's "Yo. These markings. Look at 'em." (off-frame), Roco's controlled tactical breathing, faint shift of his clothing as he turns his head, Rain's "Guys. Something's off. Stay close." with quiet urgency, distant drip from ahead, then Rain's first deliberate footstep on wet stone with deep stone reverb as the clip ends.

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄的远古石走廊，火炬苏醒与队伍简报的开场段落，15 秒。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：电影级变形宽银幕 2.39:1，长焦摄影，浅景深，来自火炬火焰的戏剧性明暗布光，深黑带带光晕的暖橙高光，大气尘雾。高度去饱和的调色，仅火的孤立暖色点缀与 Rain 平板屏幕的冷蓝辉光。灵感来自 Snyder《Army of the Dead（活死人军团）》。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：本场景始于一次剧烈爆炸塌陷上方通道、把三名角色送入这条狭窄下方走廊之后的数秒。走廊对他们的在场作出回应（RESPONDS）——墙挂火炬一盏接一盏依序点燃（IGNITE）。每名角色开始以自己的方式检视这处所在。Rain 查看一台显示着简图的手持平板。Jax 被墙上的雕凿字形吸引。Roco 以战术眼光扫视走廊。随后 Rain 觉得这地方有什么不对劲却说不出名目——她警告队伍，开始向前走。

LOCATION（场景）：一条约两米宽的狭窄远古石走廊，低矮的天花板。角色身后——上方破口塌落的新鲜瓦砾。前方——两侧墙上的墙挂铁火炬支架；最远端一座敞开的雕刻石门道，透着微弱的冷蓝光。

CHARACTERS（角色）（刚在爆炸中生还、蒙尘）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银金色头发，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新伤口，灰色连帽夹克，蒙尘。一臂下夹着悬浮滑板。握着一台小手持平板，屏幕把冷蓝光辉投到她脸上。领队、理性分析。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，清晰眼镜，深色夹克，蒙尘。好奇，被雕纹吸引。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱头发，胡须，鼻上有红色绷带，深色夹克，蒙尘。腋下夹着悬浮滑板。战术警惕，不停扫视。

Shot 1（第 0–3 秒）：长焦静态镜头，顺走廊长度望向远处门道，长焦压缩压平纵深。走廊昏暗——只有最远端门道透出的微弱冷蓝散景可见。离摄影机最近的第一盏铁火炬以一声轻柔的扑响点亮（IGNITES），火焰绽成一团带光晕的橙色爆闪。下一盏火炬在更深处点亮——先是显现为一个失焦的发光球体，然后随焦点拉向它而骤然进入清晰。每一次相继点亮都引发一次焦点移位，摄影机逐盏火炬穿过走廊纵深进行焦点推移（rack-focus）。到末段，走廊被闪烁交叠、带光晕的暖橙光照亮。

Shot 2（第 3–6 秒）：硬切到低四分之三角度 Rain 面孔的长焦特写。她平板（握在画幅边缘稍下方）的冷蓝光从下方映在她脸上，与身后的暖火炬光相混。浅焦落在她的双眼。她身后走廊溶成橙色火炬散景。她的眼闪落到平板上，继而抬回前方走廊。她以沉稳的命令口吻说：「Final stretch. Forty feet.（最后一程。四十英尺。）」

Shot 3（第 6–8.5 秒）：硬切到 Jax 的手伸向一侧廊柱上雕凿字形的极特写。接触那一刻浅焦落在他指尖。最近的火炬以带光晕的暖光在雕纹上闪烁。他的手指滑过表面，指尖下灰尘移位。焦点从石面拉到他那镜片后的眼上。画外传来他的声音，略粗哑、近乎惊叹：「Yo. These markings. Look at 'em.（哟。这些刻痕。瞧它们。）」

Shot 4（第 8.5–10 秒）：硬切到四分之三角度 Roco 的长焦中近景。硬朗火炬光斜切过他的五官，半张脸埋在煤黑色阴影。他的双眼有条不紊地扫视——抬头看雕凿的天花板，继而望向前方地面，然后到廊柱间一处幽暗凹处。战术性、沉默。眉头紧锁，下颌绷定。他不说话。

Shot 5（第 10–15 秒）：硬切到侧影中 Rain 的长焦中近景。她略微放下平板，双眼眯起。蓝色平板辉光从她脸上淡去。暖火炬光接住她半边脸，另半边沉入煤黑色阴影。她微微转头扫视。她开口，比先前更低、更急迫，嗓音里带着一丝紧绷：「Guys. Something's off.（各位。有哪里不对劲。）」顿一拍。眼闪向画外 Jax 所在处，继而短暂朝画外 Roco。她接着：「Stay close.（跟紧。）」在她脸上停一瞬。然后她的目光锁向前方远处门道。下颌绷定。她把头完全转向正前方，跨出第一步（TAKES THE FIRST STEP）——她的身体开始移入走廊纵深，摄影机保持静态，她略移出画幅的锐利焦平面，随着她更深入、她的剪影开始柔和地溶入散景。她迈步的动作在画幅之外延续，镜头就此收尾。

无音乐。仅环境音效（sfx only）——身后破口处沉降的碎屑与筛落的石尘，上方传来二次塌陷的远处轰隆，爆炸后缀带出的微弱耳鸣，火炬依序点亮的连续扑响（Shot 1 中五次明确的点亮），噼啪的火声，Rain 平板的微弱电子嗡鸣，她指尖在屏幕上的轻敲，Rain 的「Final stretch. Forty feet.（最后一程。四十英尺。）」带着石廊混响，指尖拂过雕石带沙砾声，Jax 的「Yo. These markings. Look at 'em.（哟。这些刻痕。瞧它们。）」（画外），Roco 克制的战术呼吸，他转头时衣料的轻微摩挲，Rain 的「Guys. Something's off. Stay close.（各位。有哪里不对劲。跟紧。）」带着安静的急迫，前方滴水，随后镜头收尾时 Rain 在湿石上踏出的第一记从容脚步、带着深沉石廊混响。
