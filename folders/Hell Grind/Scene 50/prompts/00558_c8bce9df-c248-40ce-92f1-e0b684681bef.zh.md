## 原文 (source)

Narrow ancient stone corridor, kinetic arrow volley sequence and slab descent beginning, 15 seconds.

VISUAL STYLE: cinematic anamorphic 2.39:1, long-lens telephoto cinematography, shallow depth of field, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from torch flames, deep blacks with halated warm orange highlights, atmospheric dust haze. Heavily desaturated palette. Inspired by Snyder's Army of the Dead.

LOCATION: tight ancient stone corridor, two meters wide, low ceiling. Behind characters — fresh rubble collapse. Ahead — distant glowing gateway with cold blue light. Lit iron torches on both walls. Wall ports actively firing arrows from both sides perpendicularly across the narrow corridor.

CHARACTERS (pinned flat on the floor):
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): silver-blonde hair, scar by eye, fresh cut on temple, grey hooded jacket, dusty. Pressed flat to wet stone, Roco's body covering hers protectively.
— Jax (<<<image_3>>>): yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear glasses, dark jacket, dusty. Pressed flat on his own a few meters back, arms over his head.
— Roco (<<<image_4>>>): dark messy hair, mustache, red bandage on nose, dark jacket, dusty. Lying on top of Rain, shielding her with his body, his back exposed.

Shot 1 (0–3s): Camera positioned at floor level on the line of fire between the two walls, looking down the corridor. An arrow EXPLODES from a wall port on the right side and STREAKS across frame from right to left, the iron tip flying directly toward camera before whipping past inches from the lens with a sharp wooden whoosh — fletching nearly grazing the lens. Camera whip-pans LEFT to follow the arrow's flight as it crosses the narrow corridor and SLAMS into the opposite wall with a brutal thunk, embedding deep, the wooden shaft vibrating. Single continuous arc — vibration to flight to impact in one whip-pan. Halated torch light streaks across the lens during the pan.

Shot 2 (3–6s): Camera at floor level inches from Rain's prone face, low three-quarter angle. Her cheek pressed to wet stone, silver hair fanned out, eyes wide and locked forward. Suddenly an arrow CRACKS through frame in the IMMEDIATE foreground, the wooden shaft passing within inches of the lens just above her head — so close the motion blur fills half the frame for a single fraction of a second. Sharp wooden whoosh. Her hair LIFTS violently from the wake. She does not blink. A second arrow snaps through behind her at a different depth. Then a third at yet another depth. Each arrow in a different focal plane, each whoosh a different pitch — the volley is layered in space.

Shot 3 (6–9s): Camera mounted to a wall position, looking sideways across the corridor. An arrow FIRES from the opposite wall port directly toward camera — we see the dark aperture, the flash of dust, the arrow leaping out and growing rapidly larger as it accelerates toward the lens at full real-time speed. Just before impact, hard cut to the arrow IMPACTING the wall behind camera position — extreme close-up, the iron tip splitting carved stone full-bleed in frame, wooden shaft vibrating violently in foreground, stone chips spalling outward, dust puffing. Two more arrows slam into stone beside it in rapid succession from off-frame, each impact shaking the camera with micro-jolts.

Shot 4 (9–12s): Long-lens close-up of Roco's face from above, lying on top of Rain. Eyes squeezed shut, jaw clenched, breathing hard. Above and around them, arrow whooshes continue but begin THINNING — the volley is ending, the cadence slowing from rapid-fire to scattered. He cracks one eye open, lifts his head slightly. He looks past Rain toward the distant gateway off-frame. His expression shifts from survival to dread.

Shot 5 (12–15s): Long-lens low-angle shot down the corridor, shallow focus pulled to the deep background. Foreground: prone bodies of Roco covering Rain, Jax behind, all in soft focus. Mid-ground: arrows embedded in both walls, wooden shafts still trembling, forming a horizontal cage above the bodies. Deep background in razor focus: the open gateway with cold blue light streaming through. A massive carved stone slab BEGINS to descend from the ceiling above the gateway — slow, inexorable. The bright cold blue light begins to compress as the slab lowers. Deep grinding rumble. Hold.

no music. sfx only — sharp wooden whoosh as first arrow flies past camera lens (Shot 1), brutal thunk of arrow embedding in opposite wall, wooden shaft vibrating after impact, three arrows whooshing past Rain's head at different depths and pitches (Shot 2), arrow leaping out of wall port and rapidly accelerating toward camera (Shot 3), arrow impacting stone full-bleed with wood splitting and stone chips falling, two more impacts in rapid succession with camera shake, scattered arrow whooshes thinning out (Shot 4), Roco's heavy strained breathing close above Rain, Rain's controlled shallow breathing, Jax's ragged breathing further back, torches crackling, deep grinding stone-on-stone descent of slab beginning in the distance (Shot 5).

## 中文对照 (zh)

狭窄古老石廊，动态箭矢齐射序列与石板下落开始，15 秒。

视觉风格（VISUAL STYLE）：电影变形宽银幕 2.39:1，长焦远摄摄影，浅景深，来自火把火焰的戏剧性明暗对照照明，深黑带带光晕（halated）的暖橙高光，大气粉尘雾霭。高度去饱和的调色板。受 Snyder《活死人军团》（Army of the Dead）启发。

场景（LOCATION）：狭窄的古老石质走廊，两米宽，低矮天花板。角色身后——新鲜碎石塌落。前方——远处发光的门廊透出冷蓝色光。两面墙上点燃铁火把。墙口正从两侧垂直地朝狭窄走廊发射箭矢。

角色（扑伏在地）：
— Rain（<<<image_2>>>）：银白金色头发，眼旁有疤，太阳穴新割伤，灰色连帽夹克，覆尘。平贴湿滑石面，Roco 的身体护住她的身体作保护。
— Jax（<<<image_3>>>）：黄色寸头，深肤色，透明眼镜，深色夹克，覆尘。独自平伏在数米之后，双臂抱头。
— Roco（<<<image_4>>>）：深色凌乱发，胡须，鼻上红绷带，深色夹克，覆尘。躺在 Rain 身上，以身体护她，背部外露。

镜头 1（Shot 1，0–3s）：摄影机置于贴地高度、处在两面墙之间的射击线上，沿走廊向前看。一支箭从右侧墙口爆炸射出（EXPLODES）、横贯画面从右到左疾飞（STREAKS），铁箭头在甩过离镜头数英寸之前直接朝摄影机飞来——尾羽（fletching）近乎擦过镜头，伴着一声尖锐的木制嗖嗖声。摄影机向左甩镜（whip-pans）跟随箭矢飞行穿越狭窄走廊、「砰」地（SLAMS）重重砸进对面墙、深深嵌入，木箭杆震颤。单条连续弧线——颤振到飞行到冲击在一个甩镜中完成。甩镜期间带光晕的火把光擦过镜头成条纹。

镜头 2（Shot 2，3–6s）：摄影机在贴地高度距 Rain 的伏地脸数英寸，低四分之三角。她面颊贴湿石、银白头发披散、双眼圆睁向前锁定。突然一支箭从正前方远景（IMMEDIATE foreground）劈过画面（CRACKS），木箭杆在紧贴她头顶上方划过离镜头数英寸处——近到运动模糊在短短几分之一秒填满半帧。尖锐木制嗖嗖声。她的头发被尾流暴力掀起。她不眨眼。第二支箭在她身后的不同深度「啪」地穿过。随后第三支在又一个不同深度。每支箭在不同的焦平面、每声嗖嗖音高不同——齐射在空间中层叠。

镜头 3（Shot 3，6–9s）：摄影机固定于墙面位置，侧向横看走廊。一支箭从对面墙口朝摄影机直接发射（FIRES）——我们看到黑暗的孔径、粉尘的闪光、箭跃出后随它以全实时速度朝镜头加速而急剧变大。刚要冲击前，硬切至箭射中（IMPACTING）摄影机位置后方的墙——极特写，铁箭头满画幅劈开雕石，木箭杆在前景暴力震颤，石屑向外剥离，粉尘喷涌。另两支箭从画外迅速接连射入它旁边的石头，每次冲击都以微颤摇动摄影机。

镜头 4（Shot 4，9–12s）：Roco 面部自上而下的长焦特写，躺在 Rain 之上。眼睛紧紧闭拢、下颌紧咬、喘着粗气。上方与周围，箭嗖嗖声持续但开始变稀（THINNING）——齐射正在结束，节奏从速射放缓为稀疏零落。他睁开一只眼，微微抬头。他越过 Rain 看向画外远处的门廊。他的神情从求存转为惊惧。

镜头 5（Shot 5，12–15s）：贴地长焦低角度沿走廊向深处看，浅焦拉向深处背景。前景：Roco 伏身盖住 Rain、Jax 在后，全在柔焦中。中景：两支墙上的嵌箭、木箭杆仍在轻颤，在身体上方形成一道水平牢笼。深处背景剃刀般锐焦：敞开的门廊透出冷蓝色光。一块巨大雕刻石板开始（BEGINS）从门廊上方天花板下落——缓慢、不可阻挡。明亮的冷蓝色光随石板降低而开始压缩。深沉的碾磨轰鸣。停格。

无音乐。仅音效——首支箭飞过镜头时尖锐的木制嗖嗖声（镜头 1）、箭嵌入对面墙的暴力「咚」声、冲击后木箭杆震颤、三支箭在差异深度与差异音高下掠过 Rain 头顶的嗖嗖声（镜头 2）、箭跃出墙口迅速朝摄影机加速（镜头 3）、箭满画幅射中石头带木头劈裂与石屑落下、随后两次迅速连续的冲击带摄影机颤抖、零落箭嗖声渐稀（镜头 4）、Roco 紧紧压在 Rain 上方的沉重用力的呼吸、Rain 受控的浅呼吸、更后方 Jax 的沙哑呼吸、火把噼啪、远处石板石怼石的深碾磨下落开始（镜头 5）。
