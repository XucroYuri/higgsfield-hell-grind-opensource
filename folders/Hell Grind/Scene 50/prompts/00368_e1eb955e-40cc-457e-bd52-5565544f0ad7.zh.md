## 原文 (source)

Handheld medium shot of a young man arriving at a stone pit edge and reaching down with a crystalline armored arm to save a friend hanging below, 4 seconds.

SCENE CONTEXT: a young man named Roco is rescuing his friend Jax who has fallen into a hidden trap pit in an ancient stone corridor. Roco's arms are encased in heavy crystalline armor — sharp jagged blood-red crystal formations grown over both arms from shoulder to fingertips, like solidified lava or grown shards, asymmetric and aggressive. The crystals are translucent deep red with internal red glow, beautiful but dangerous — sharp ridges, faceted shards, jagged edges. They are armor, grown over his real flesh underneath, not a replacement of his arms. Roco runs to the pit edge at full sprint, drops to one knee, and reaches down toward Jax with his crystal-armored arm.

VISUAL STYLE: handheld cinematography in the visual language of Emmanuel Lubezki — alive in the operator's hands, breathing with the action, organic camera movement, never jittery. Long-lens telephoto cinematography with shallow depth of field, f/1.8 aperture. Lens imperfections — visible dust on the lens, halation from torch flames, slight chromatic aberration on the high-contrast edges between warm torchlight and cold red crystal glow, occasional gentle focus drift, faint 35mm film grain. Cinematic widescreen 16:9. Lighting in the language of Greig Fraser — practical motivated lighting only from iron torches mounted on the corridor walls, warm orange key from sides, deep crushed blacks with detail preserved in shadows, the red crystal glow adding contrasting cool red fill on Roco's arms and face. Heavily desaturated palette dominated by warm orange torch tones with isolated deep red glow from the crystal armor.

LOCATION: a tight ancient underground stone corridor approximately two meters wide. Walls weathered carved stone with deep glyph carvings, lit iron torches mounted on both side walls casting flickering warm orange light, wet stone floor reflecting torch highlights. Atmospheric haze thick with floating dust. In the middle of the corridor floor, an open trap pit — a dark vertical shaft cut into stone, two meters deep, rough hewn walls dropping into darkness, stone spikes barely visible far below at the bottom.

CHARACTERS:
— Roco: young man, mid-twenties, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage strip across the bridge of his nose, grey sleeveless tank top, dark red jeans, dust-covered, sweat-streaked. BOTH HIS ARMS from shoulder to fingertips are encased in heavy CRYSTALLINE ARMOR — translucent deep blood-red crystal formations grown over his arms, sharp jagged faceted shards, asymmetric crystalline texture covering shoulders, biceps, forearms, hands, fingers. The crystal is rough and aggressive, with sharp ridges and points, NOT smooth or aerodynamic. Internal red glow visible through the translucent crystal facets, light pulsing faintly within.
— Jax: young man, mid-twenties, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses askew, dark hooded jacket, dust-caked face. Hanging from the upper edge of the pit by his fingertips, body dangling into darkness, arms straining hard.

ACTION: handheld camera at chest height beside the pit edge, three-quarter angle. Camera trembles intensely throughout.

(0–1.5s): Roco RUNS into frame from background depth, sprinting toward camera at full speed. The crystal armor catches the torch light along its sharp faceted ridges, the deep red internal glow pulsing faintly. He reaches the pit edge and SKIDS to a halt, dust kicking up around his boots. He immediately DROPS to one knee at the pit edge — the camera follows his motion downward in one fluid handheld movement, settling at his shoulder level beside the pit. His knee impacts wet stone with force.

(1.5–2.5s): Roco extends his crystal-armored RIGHT ARM down into the pit toward Jax. The jagged crystal forearm and hand enter the lower frame, the red internal glow lighting up the pit walls below from within the translucent crystal. The hand opens, sharp crystal fingers spreading. Jax's flesh hand reaches up from below to meet it.

(2.5–4s): Their hands meet — but immediately the contact fails. The CRYSTAL SURFACE is jagged, faceted, with sharp ridges that cannot be gripped. Jax's flesh fingers cannot close around the rough sharp shards — they hit hard crystalline edges, slip across the faceted surface, find no purchase. Jax's hand SLIDES across the rough crystal surface trying to grip, finding only sharp edges, his fingers unable to hold. His weight pulls him down, his hand SLIPPING off the crystal armor entirely. He drops out of frame back toward the pit. Roco's eyes widen with shock.

no music. sfx only — Roco's running boots pounding wet stone reaching the pit, sharp skid to halt with dust impact, knee impacting wet stone hard with bone-on-stone thud, low resonant hum and faint pulsing tone from the crystalline armor with deep red frequency, Jax's panicked breathing from below the pit edge throughout, flesh-on-crystal contact with rough scraping sound (skin sliding across faceted sharp shards), Jax's panicked grunt as his fingers can't grip the rough surface, his hand SLIPPING off the crystal entirely with a final scrape, his body dropping out of frame, his hands SLAPPING stone hard as he catches the pit edge below again, ragged terrified breathing from below, deep slow grinding rumble of distant slab descending in background, torches crackling.

## 中文对照 (zh)

手持中景，一名年轻男子抵达石坑边缘，以水晶装甲臂向下探去营救悬在其下的朋友，4 秒。

SCENE CONTEXT（场景情境）：一名年轻男子 Roco 正在营救他的朋友 Jax——Jax 坠入古老石质走廊中一处隐蔽的陷阱坑。Roco 的双臂覆以沉重的晶甲——尖锐锯齿状的血红色水晶结构，自双肩至指尖长满双臂，如凝固的熔岩或生长的碎片，不对称而富有攻击性。水晶呈半透明深红，内部泛红光，美丽却危险——锐利棱脊、多面碎片、锯齿状边缘。它们是甲胄，长在他底层真实血肉之上，而非替换他的双臂。Roco 全速冲刺到坑缘，单膝跪落，以水晶装甲臂向下朝 Jax 探去。

VISUAL STYLE（视觉风格）：以 Emmanuel Lubezki 的视觉语言手持摄影——在操机员手中鲜活、随动作呼吸、有机的镜头移动、绝不跳抖。长焦摄影带浅景深，f/1.8 光圈。镜头瑕疵——镜片上可见灰尘、火把火焰的光晕（halation）、暖火把光与冷红水晶辉光之间的高对比边缘处轻微色差、偶尔温和的焦点漂移、隐约的 35mm 胶片颗粒。电影感宽屏 16:9。Greig Fraser 语言的灯光——仅来自走廊墙壁上铁火把的实用动机照明，来自两侧的暖橙主光，压碎浓黑且阴影内保留细节，红色水晶辉光在 Roco 双臂与脸上增添形成对比的冷红补光。以暖橙火把色调为主、孤立的深红光晶辉光衬托的重度去饱和调色。

LOCATION（地点）：约两米宽的狭窄古代地下石质走廊。墙壁是经风化凿刻的石墙，带深层符文（glyph）凿刻，两侧墙壁上点亮的铁火把投出摇曳的暖橙光，湿滑石地反射火把高光。大气薄雾厚积漂浮尘。走廊地面正中，一处打开的陷阱坑——深切入石的垂直暗井，两米深，粗糙开凿的井壁沉入黑暗，远端底部隐约可见石刺。

CHARACTERS（角色）：
— Roco：年轻男子，二十多岁，深色凌乱齐肩长发，浅色小胡子，鼻梁覆着红色绷带条，灰色无袖背心，深红牛仔裤，蒙尘，汗渍纵横。他的双臂自双肩至指尖覆以沉重的水晶甲胄——半透明深血红水晶结构长在手臂上，尖锐锯齿多面碎片，覆盖肩、二头肌、前臂、手、指的不对称水晶纹理。水晶粗糙而富有攻击性，带锐利棱脊与尖端，不平滑、不流线。内部红辉光透过多面水晶晶面可见，光在其内微弱脉动。
— Jax：年轻男子，二十多岁，漂白黄色圆寸（buzzcut），深肤色，清晰护目镜歪斜，深色连帽夹克，蒙尘结垢的脸。仅以指尖悬在坑上缘，身体悬垂入黑暗，双臂极力绷紧。

ACTION（动作）：手持摄影机在坑缘旁齐胸高度，四分之三角度。全程摄影机剧烈颤抖。

(0–1.5s)：Roco 自背景纵深跑入画面，以全速朝摄影机冲刺。水晶甲胄沿其锐利多面棱脊接住火把光，深红内部辉光微弱脉动。他抵达坑缘并刹车滑停，靴子周围尘土扬起。他立即在坑缘单膝跪落——摄影机在一个流畅的手持动作中随他下落，停定在他肩部高度、坑旁。他的膝盖重重撞上湿滑石地。

(1.5–2.5s)：Roco 将水晶装甲右臂向下探入坑中伸向 Jax。锯齿状水晶前臂与手进入画面下部，红色内部辉光从半透明水晶内部照亮下方坑壁。手张开，锐利水晶手指张开。Jax 的血肉之手自下方探上来迎它。

(2.5–4s)：他们的手相遇——但接触立刻失败。水晶表面锯齿多面，带无法抓握的锐利棱脊。Jax 的血肉手指无法环拢粗糙锐利的碎片——它们撞上坚硬水晶棱边，在多面表面滑过，寻觅不到着点。Jax 的手在粗糙水晶表面滑过试图抓握，只触到锐利边缘，手指无法握住。他的体重把他向下拉，手完全从水晶甲胄上滑脱。他坠出画面落回坑中。Roco 的眼睛因震惊而睁大。

no music. sfx only（无配乐。仅环境音效）——Roco 奔跑靴子踏击湿滑石地抵达坑边、带尘土冲击的锐利刹车滑停、膝盖重重撞上湿滑石地带骨击石之声的闷响，水晶甲胄带低共振轰鸣与微弱脉动音，深红频率，坑缘下 Jax 全程恐慌的呼吸，血肉触水晶带粗糙刮擦声（皮肤在多面锐利碎片上滑动）、Jax 因手指握不住粗糙表面而恐慌地咕哝、他的手以一记最终刮擦完全滑脱水晶、他的身体坠出画面、他的手重重拍击石头以重新抓住坑下缘、下方传来粗重力竭的恐惧呼吸、背景远处石板下降缓慢深沉的研磨轰鸣、火把噼啪作响。
