## 原文 (source)

Ancient stone corridor, exploration and dialogue sequence, 15 seconds. Setting: massive rubble collapse blocking the far end, faint cold blue light through debris gap, wet stone floor, glyph-covered pillars on both side walls, candles flickering in alcoves, volumetric dust haze. Anamorphic widescreen, teal-shadow color grade with warm candle accents, low-key lighting, mounting tension.

Characters:
— Rain (<<<image_2>>>): young woman, silver-blonde shoulder-length hair, small scar by eye, grey hooded jacket, black top, dark cargo pants.
— <<<image_3>>>: young man, bleached yellow buzzcut, dark skin, clear protective glasses, dark hooded jacket.
— <<<image_4>>>: young man, dark messy shoulder-length hair, light mustache, red bandage across bridge of nose, dark jacket.

Shot 1 (0–4s): Medium shot of <<<image_3>>> stepping toward a side pillar, his fingers tracing carved glyphs slowly, eyes narrowing as he tries to read them. The carvings are deep, weathered, alien. He murmurs, almost to himself: "These markings... they're old. Older than the maps said." Handheld, breathing-close camera, shallow focus.

Shot 2 (4–8s): Reverse medium shot on <<<image_4>>> standing a few steps behind, scanning the corridor with sharp eyes, jaw tight. He glances at <<<image_3>>>, then at the rubble ahead, voice low and tense: "I don't like this. Place feels wrong." A beat. He looks back at Rain off-frame: "We shouldn't linger." Camera holds on him as he speaks, micro-shake.

Shot 3 (8–12s): Two-shot of Rain and <<<image_4>>>, Rain in the foreground out of focus, <<<image_4>>> in the background. Rain slowly turns her head toward him, silver hair shifting, voice quiet but firm: "Then we move. Carefully." Camera rack-focuses from <<<image_4>>> to Rain as she speaks. She takes a slow step forward into deeper focus.

Shot 4 (12–15s): Tracking close-up following Rain's profile as she walks slowly forward, eyes scanning the floor and walls ahead. The other two follow behind in soft focus. Her breathing is controlled, every step deliberate. Camera matches her pace exactly. Faint distant drip. The corridor seems to stretch longer than it should.

no music. sfx only — fingers brushing carved stone, low tense dialogue with stone-room reverb, footsteps on wet stone, controlled breathing, distant drip, candle flicker, fabric movement.
